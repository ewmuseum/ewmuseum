USWF — Card

Promo Card

11 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is our first card, to give you a feel for what the USWF is like. Join us now and get in on all the excitement!
Write to: [detail withheld] (The USWF logo appears superimposed over a camera shot of the capacity crowd that is cheering wildly, awaiting the start of USWF action. Spotlights pan quickly over the crowd.) (The shot switches to Tony DiMera and Steve Hendrix who are sitting at the broadcast table, wearing headsets. DiMera is dressed in a suit and tie, while Hendrix wears a flashy Hawaiian shirt and shorts.)
TONY: Welcome, fans and thanks for tuning in for the greatest in wrestling entertainment... The United States Wrestling Federation. Excitement is in the air tonight as 12 superstars prepare to make their USWF debut here tonight.
STEVE: Superstars? You call this bunch of losers superstars? From what I've seen so far, we're a little lacking in the star department. Now if you want true star quality, look no further than right here. "Stunning" Steve Hendrix is where it's at, baby!
TONY: Whatever you say, Steve. As I was saying, fans, tonight you will get a chance to take a look at each USWF star as we await the announcement from President Clubbs about the upcoming title matches. Everyone is wondering how the first USWF champion will be crowned, and we're going to have that big announcement later for you in this broadcast.
STEVE: If you ask me, DiMera, we should just have a Battle Royal. Throw all these guys into the ring and when one is left standing, hand him the belt.
TONY: Well many people would agree with you, Steve, but we'll just have to wait and see. (The music of Space Odyssey 2001 begins to play loudly over the PA system as the lights dim.)
TONY: Well, it looks like we're ready for action! STEVE: And we couldn't have started off in a better way! Here comes a man who knows what wrestling is all about!
RING ANNONCER: Ladies and gentlemen, in our first contest... hailing from the Gateway City of St. Louis, Missouri... weighing in at 225 pounds... here is Mark Williams!! (Williams comes through the curtain. He is wearing a red robe, on the back are written the words "The Greatest." He walks confidently to the ring.)
RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, weighing 237 pounds.... Barry Horowitz! (Horowitz pats himself on the back.)
STEVE: Now as you know, DiMera, Mark Williams claims to be the greatest wrestler of all times. Now if that includes everybody but me, I guess he can go ahead and say that.
TONY: He is indeed a talented individual. Mark Williams has a great deal of technical ability, but he's not afraid to rough it up with his opponents!
STEVE: There's the bell and these two lock up. Williams starts things off with a kneelift into the midsection of Horowitz. He then locks him in with a side headlock. Horowitz pushes Williams off and sends him bouncing off the ropes, Williams comes back with a big shoulderblock that sends Horowitz down.
TONY: And see how quickly Williams caught Horowitz in that spinning toe hold? Williams loves submission moves and he is a master of them. He's really wearing down the right leg of Horowitz.
STEVE: Big elbowdrops right onto the thigh of Horowitz. Horowitz now tries to stand, but Williams kicks the leg out from under him. Horowitz is down!
TONY: And just like lightning, Williams locks him into his patented finisher, the STF! Horowitz isn't getting out of this one!
STEVE: No he's not, and he wasted no time in submitting. That is one painful hold, let me tell you!
RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner.... MARK WILLIAMS! TONY: A strong showing there from the St. Louis native. ********************************************************************* (The music of "Breathe" by Prodigy fills the arena as spotlights begin to swirl over the crowd. A knockout blode beauty wearing a skimpy black swimsuit enters through the curtain, blowing kisses to the crowd.)
STEVE: Whoah, momma! TONY: Here comes Krystal Powers, and where there's Krystal, Steve Shine can't be far behind... ("Hollywood" Steve Shine enters. He is wearing a black robe with the world "Hollywood" emblazened across the back in glittery gold letters. His long blonde hair falls over his shoulders.)
RING ANNOUNCER: Our next contest is scheduled for one fall. Accompanied to the ring by Krystal Powers... he hails from Hollywood, California... weighing 260 pounds.... "Hollywood" Steve Shine!
TONY: Ummm, you're drooling there, Hendrix.... STEVE: Where did Krystal come from? She's gorgeous! TONY: Well, you would expect nothing less from a former Miss California! RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, weighing 245 pounds... Jimmy Mercer! TONY: Jimmy Mercer looks like he's paying more attention to Krystal Powers than to Steve Shine. And Shine capitalizes on it! He goes after Mercer before the bell, hammering him on the back with vicious forearm blows!
STEVE: He really caught Mercer off guard there, and Mercer is suffering for it! You can't blame him for checking out Krystal Powers, though.
TONY: A big clothesline takes Mercer down, and Hollywood follows it up with a big legdrop. A couple of well-placed knees to the forehead of Mercer really has him reeling!
STEVE: Mercer's back up again... OH! But Hollywodd catches him in a tight sleeperhold! That's really draining the life out of him!
TONY: But Shine doesn't wait for the ref to check Mercer's arms, he releases the hold... he's going up to the top rope...
STEVE: Mercer's not going anywhere, he's out on his feet. TONY: It looks like Hollywood's going for... YES! The "Shine Line!" A big flying clothesline takes his opponent down... 1.....2....3!!!!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match.... "Hollywood" Steve Shine!!!!! **********************************************************************
TONY: Fans, we're ready for our next match! RING ANNOUNCER: This contest is scheduled for one fall. Now entering the arena, weighing 300 pounds... from Staffordshire, England.... here is "Critical" Bill Vaughn! (Vaughn enters to the sounds of "Fuel" by Metallica. He is a muscular man with long black hair and a look for fierce determination is on his bearded face.)
STEVE: What the heck kind of name is "Critical." TONY: Well, since you ask, it seems that he earned that name while wrestling overseas. Vaughn sent so many opponents to the hospital in critical condition, that he nickname stuck.
STEVE: Yeah, but that was while he was wrestling a bunch of pansy Englishmen. Let's see how he does here in the UNITED STATES Wrestling Federation.
RING ANNOUNCER: And hailing from Las Vegas, Nevada... at 289 pounds... Greg Gambit! STEVE: Gambit doesn't seem to be bothered by Vaughn's reputation. He's taking it to him before the bell! Some big roundhouse punches there.
TONY: But Vaughn battles back. This man loves to fight! He will mix it up with anybody! Some hard chops to the chest of Gambit really slow him down!
STEVE: BOOM! Vaughn really nailed that atomic drop. I think Gambit will be singing soprano in the shower. He follws it up with a vicious head butt... and OH! A BIG DDT! Gambit is out of it!
TONY: Vaughn's picking him up, though. It looks like he's not satisfied with a win unless he gets to do his patented finisher. He picks Gambit up, take off running and LANDS THE BIG POWERSLAM. He calls that one "The Critical Mass."
STEVE: That's an awesome slam! TONY: The ref counts 1...2....3.... he could've counted to 20! RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner.... "Critical" Bill Vaughn! (Critical Bill Vaugn grabs the microphone and speaks.)
CRITICAL BILL: Well it didn't take long did it? The challenges are coming in thick and fast, just the way I like it! Spider, listen to me. You want the match you got it. If you need someone to put you in line, then I'm your man. If you need Steve Shine to hide behind, then that's cool too. (Bill rubs his hands in anticipation).
CRITICAL BILL: Steve Shine seems to have put it best. I am a bada$$! He's not far wrong calling me 'Clinical' Bill either. I can take an opponent apart in a very 'Clinical' manner. After I've faced people you'd think they'd just had an autopsy.
CRITICAL BILL: Only time will tell who's the best. I'm not going anywhere for a while, so I have all the time in the world. As soon as the Critical Mass hits you, you'll be running home to momma. Untill I get in the ring and start cracking some heads you'll have to take my word for it. See you in the emergency ward! ************************************************************************* (Camera switches to Tony DiMera, who is on the interview platform.)
TONY: All right, thank you Steve. Joining me at this time is a man who made his fortune as a professional rodeo promoter all over the southern U.S. Now while wrestling and rodeo are certainly different, this man claims he has news that will effect both sports. Here he is, "Colonel" Carl Perkins. ("Colonel" Carl emerges from backstage. He is dressed all in white, from his white cowboy boots all the way up to his white cowboy hat. He carries a white walking stick. The only thing about him that is not white is the big brown cigar in his mouth and the black smoke he's exhaling.)
TONY: Welcome, Colonel. What brings you to the USWF? COLONEL CARL: Well, boy, let me tell you something. In all my years of runnin' around rodeos, I have seen a lot of tough son-of-a-guns. I have been boys get stomped on my bulls and get up and walk away. I have seen fellas kicked in the face by broncs and the next night, they're right back out there ridin' 'em again. But let me tell you somethin. I found one old boy who is tougher than anybody I have ever seen before. I have seen him wrestle steer to the ground so fast it'll make your head spin. I have seen him stare down a mad old bull and watched the bull back away. Let me tell you somethin. As soon as I saw this boy, I knew that I had to get him into the ring so he can whoop some butt. And that's why I'm here today. I'm here to unleash this man into the world of professional wrasslin'. Here he is, "Backwoods" Bob Wheeler! (Backwoods Bob comes out of the curtain. He looks like he just crawled out of a ditch. He is wearing a dirty, beat-up plaid shirt; torn, stained jeans; and muddy cowboy boots. His hair is greasy and his face covered with thick stubble. In his hand is a long bullwhip, which he cracks at the fans as he makes his way to the platform.)
TONY: Ummmm... welcome, Backwoods Bob. BACKWOODS BOB: (spitting chewing tobacco on Tony's shoes) Shut up, boy. I just got one thing to say. I don't care nothin about no titles, I don't care nothin about no rules, and I don't care nothing about you stinkin fans.... (the crowd boooos) I only care about one thing... and that's hurtin all you pansy-*ssed boys who call yourself wrasslers. When you get in the ring with me, you're gonna get yourself hurt. Now get out of my face. (Wheeler shoves Tony aside as he leaves the interview platform. Colonel Carl laughs at Tony and follows his man out of the arena.)
TONY: Well... Ok.... let's go back to the ring for our next match. **************************************************************************8 (The camera switches to the ring, focused on the announcer.)
RING ANOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, in the ring at this time... from Tampa, Florida... weighing 270 pounds... Chris Cabanna.... His opponent hails from Dublin, Ireland. He weighs 238 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Heather.... he is Justin Credible!!!! (The lovely Heather appears, dressed in a red sequined dress that glitters in the spotlights. Justin Credible has his arm over her shoulder, and smiles. As he makes his way down the aisle, he gives high-fives to some of the fans.)
TONY: There you go again, Hendrix. I'm going to have to wire your mouth shut if you keep drooling over these ladies.
STEVE: I can't help it, DiMera! Just take a look at her asssssssets. TONY: Assets, indeed! This former dancer is certainly a sight to behold! And the man that she manages should not be overlooked! Justin Credible is a former Olympic athlete turned pro wrestler, and his conditioning is absolutely amazing! He is sure to be a favorite here in the USWF!
STEVE: There's the bell. TONY: Justin Credible quickly locks a front facelock on Cabanna. Credible is an excellent submission wrestler, he prefers keeping his opponents locked in holds as much a possible.
STEVE: HE's got that facelock on tight. He finally lets go and Cabanna runs against the ropes and comes charging back with a clothesline, but Justin Credible ducks down. By the time Cabanna turns around, he's met with a big back kick to the chin! Cabanna is down!
TONY: Justin quickly goes and grabs a leg and hooks a nice half boston crab! Cabanna is really hurting! Eventually, Credible lets go and drops down with an elbow to the leg of Cabanna.
STEVE: He continues to work on the legs, kicking them out from under him and then dropping knees and elbows onto the leg. He hooks a spinning toe hold, and then moves into a figure four!
TONY: Cabanna was too close to the ropes there, and Credible had to break he hold. STEVE: WHOAH! Justin Credible just nailed him with a big clothesline and now he's hooking in his finisher!
TONY: The Four Leaf Clover! That's Justin Credible's version of the Texax Clover Leaf. Cabanna submits and we have a winner!
RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner.... Justin Credible! **************************************************************************** (The arena goes black and the sound of thunder if deafening.)
STEVE: Oh great, DiMera. Looks like lightning has knocked out the power. (Suddenly, a strobe light starts up, lighting the fog of the entrance runway.)
DIMERA: It's not lighting, Steve. But it is a force just as powerful.... (Death appears on the runway, looking totally ominous through the fog. He is dressed all in black, from the black leather mask over his face, to his black t-shirt and jeans. As the camera zooms in on him, the tatoo of the "Grim Reaper" on his left forearm becomes visible.)
RING ANNOUNCER: Coming to the ring at this time is Death, weighing in at 375 pounds and from parts unknown!
STEVE: Look at the size of that monster! TONY: As I check my notes here, I see that Death stands over SEVEN feet tall! STEVE: I'm glad I'm not the poor fellow in the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent is from Macon, Georgia and weighs 233 pounds... William Baker.
STEVE: His name might as well be "sucker" as far as I'm concerned. Who talked him into getting in the ring with Death?
TONY: There's the bell and Death walks right up to Baker. He picks him up effortlessly and tosses him out of the ring. Baker hits the floor hard.
STEVE: Look at Death, he's not showboating at all, just waiting for his opponent to return.
TONY: When Baker comes back, he's met with awesome roundhouse rights to the face. He's already groggy!
STEVE: I think he's already setting him up! TONY: Yes! He is! There it is! The Apocalypse Drop! Death's awesome super power bomb! 1....2....3! This match is over. I think that was less than a minute!
STEVE: That man is awesome.... RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner.... DEATH! **********************************************************************
TONY: Next up we have a wrestler with an interesting story. Some of you longtime wrestling fans may remember a star from about 10 years back by the name of "Crunch." (Tape rolls of one of Crunch's matches while Tony speaks) Well, after quitting the wrestling business, Crunch went to work in a steel will in Ontario, Canada. He was very successful, and his charismatic personality led him to become the leader of the local steelworkers union.
STEVE: Oh, you're right, DiMera. This is sooooo interesting. TONY: Would you just listen for one minute, Hendrix? As I was saying... Crunch talked to his fellow workers about his days as a pro wrestler, and one of them showed a great deal of interest. Crunch began training this fellow, and now he is ready to make his professional debut here tonight in the USWF.
STEVE: I can hardly wait. TONY: Will you have some respect? Crunch is now known as Remington Steele and will serve as the manager for his protege, Steely Eyed Dan. Let's take you to ringside for the action. (The lound sound effects of a Steel Mill operation pay over the loud speaker as the ring announcer gets in the ring.)
RING ANNOUNCER: This contest is scheduled for one fall. Accompanied to the ring by his mananger, Remington Steele.... hailing from Hamilton, Ontario, Canada... weighing 250 pounds... Steely Eyed Don! (Don wears his steel mill uniform as his wrestling attire. Steele looks a bit different than he did in his wrestling days, a big heavier and he has lost a bit of his hair.) His opponent is from Nashville, Tennessee... he weighs 270 pounds....Johnny Jones.
TONY: All right, here comes Jones with several forearm blows to the chesk of Steely Eyed Don, Don takes them in stride and returns him with a few kicks to the midsection. This doubles Jones over, and Don executed several double-axehandle blows to Jones' kidney area, which takes Jones down to the floor.
STEVE: And there goes Don, dropping down with a big elbow to the back of his opponent's head. HE catches him in a headlock down on the mat. Jones eventually works his way up to his knees, and Don lets him go.
TONY: Don runs into the rops and comes off with a big clothesline, which staggers Jones. Done repeats the process, and this time Jones goes down. Don doesn't waste any time with some well placed kicks to the stomach of Jones while he's down. He then scoops him up for a BIG bodyslam. He really hit hard with that one.
STEVE: He picks him up again, and looks like he's setting him up for... YES a big piledriver. Whoah that really knocked the sense out of Jones. But Steely Eyed Don isn't through. He picks him up.
TONY: He lands his finisher, The Steel Worker's Cruch! That's a big crucifix power bomb! An excellent move, and it's enough for the three count!
RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner is Steely Eyed Don! TONY: And this newcomer picks up a win in his debut professional bout! ******************************************************************* (Lights go dim as a cloud of smoke emits from the entrance area, the thundering sound of Iron Man pierces the arena, the crowd goes nuts! Andrea enters in a black leather bathing suit with a silver V on the front. Sporting spiked heels and a barbed wire belt, she leads her man Viper to the interview stand. Standing 6'4" & weighing in at 275 his size is only overshadowed by his ability.)
STEVE: I would like to welcome you to the USWF. What do you think of the competition so far?
ANDREA: Competition?!? What compitition? Do you actually think anyone has a chance against the ultumate fighting machine? Nobody has seen an aerial attack from a big man like this before. (putting her hands around his 25" bicep) He can military press you right into the audience or drive you right through the mat with his Vipers Strike!
STEVE: Vipers Strike? ANDREA: That's his little version of the powerslam, OFF THE TOP ROPE!!!! Every bit of his muscle bound body crashing down on you. Nobody can survive.
STEVE: That sounds devastating. And your matching crew cut hair seems to match your no-nonsense style.
ANDREA: Absolutely. We are here to take no prisoners & welcome all challengers. I am determined Viper will be the first thing that comes to mind when someone mentions USWF. He will be champion!
STEVE: Sounds like you two are ready to make your mark. Is there anything that Viper would like to say? (VIPER flexes his entire upper body his face is intense and his eyes are cold as night)
VIPER: (in a deep grumbling voice) HOLLYWOOD!!!!!!! (VIPER AND ANDREA turn to make their exit, as "Iron Man" plays over the speakers again.)
TONY: Ok fans, some very determined words from Viper. That man is sure to make an impact here in the USWF. While we wait for Steve Hendrix to get back to the broadcast area, let's go to the ring for the announcement of a tag team match. **************************************************************************
RING ANNOUNCER: The following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall.... Introducing first... from Parts Unknown, weighing 245 pounds.... The Missing Link! ("Outsider" by Chumbawumba begins to play as the Missing Link comes through the curtain. He is a frightening looking individual wearing a silver costume, covered in silver chains. Odd red face paint is smeared on his face. He threatens to hit some of the fans as he comes down the aisle.)
RING ANNOUNCER: His partner is from New York, New York. He weighs 234 pounds... he is Spider! (The music changes to "Sweet Dreams" as Spider appears. He wears a full body costume and a "spider" mask, there is a large picture of a spider on the right leg of his costume. He carries a baseball bat to the ring.)
RING ANNOUNCER: Their opponents... first from St. Paul, Minnesota... weighing 430 pounds... he is James The Hitman! (The Hitman comes out, the raucous sounds of "Whoop, There It Is!" playing over the loudspeakers. He is an angry looking man in a black costume with black hair. He is making choking gestures with his hands as he approaches the ring.)
RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, weighing 360 pounds... Sha Dog!! (She dog wears a collar, has red tights, and even appears to have blood red eyes. He has a look of determination on his face.)
TONY: OK! All four of these men are in the ring now, and there's the bell! The men show no signs of moving. The bell has rung, but they are not wrestling. What's the problem?
STEVE: Don't look at me, DiMera! You're the one who's supposed to know everything. TONY: The match should be underway, but these men are not figthing. Fans, I don't know what to say. Wait a minute.... here comes somebody. There's a man coming down the aisle wearing a baseball cap. He also has some kind of strange blood-like makeup smearing his face. I have no idea who this is.
STEVE: Well, he's got the microphone.. it looks like we're about to find out. SCORP: (with the mic) LISTEN UP! My name is Scorp and we're here to tell you something. I SAID LISTEN UP! There will be no match tonight! We are here for one reason and one reason only. Take a good look at the men in this ring. You are looking at the baddest mothers in the USWF. These are some HARDCORE men in the ring right now. These men stick together. They watch each other's backs. They are HC-2000, the hardcore wrestlers of the future. And all you wimps in the USWF just better get used to it. The HC-2000 TAKES NO PRISONERS! Tell 'em, Spider!
SPIDER: (Taking the mic) That's right, Scorp. We're not going to play any games. Especially not with some loser like "Hollywood" Steve Shine. Listen you me, you need to stop running your mouth and get in the ring with me! I'm calling you out for a challenge right now! WE TAKE NO PRISONERS! Isn't that right, Sha Dog?
SHA DOG: You know it, boy! All you losers may be wondering why we're all up here together. Well let me tell you something, we've been watching each other's backs every since we were kids, and it's not about to change now. We're gonna take over the USWF and take no prisoners!
JAMES THE HITMAN: You better believe it! And I can't wait to start counting how many losers I get in the CHOKE ANGER! (makes choking gestures with his hands.)
MISSING LINK: That's right! We're declaring WAR on the USWF! HC-2000 is about to take over. We're not taking any prisoners! We're gonna take the USWF title and beat the crap out of anybody who gets in our way!
SCORP: So remember the name: HC-2000, you're gonna all regret the day you ever showed up in the same place as us! (All of them pose for the cameras, shout, and rant as the HC-2000 theme music plays.)
TONY: All right, you heard it fans! We have a very powerful force forming here in the the USWF in the form of HC-2000. I'm sure that they will play a very dangerous role in the future of the USWF.
STEVE: Look at the size of that big monster, The Hitman! TONY: You've got that right! He's 7 feet tall and 430 pounds. He's the master of the choke slam, he calls his version the Choke Anger!
STEVE: I'm sure we haven't seen the last of these guys! TONY: Certainly not, fans! Wait a minute... I've just received word that we're ready to go live to the USWF Head Office for an important announcement from USWF President Bob Clubbs. *************************************************************************** (The camera switches, and is zoomed in on the USWF logo. As the camera backs up, we see President Clubbs seated at his desk.)
PRESIDENT: Good evening. It's my pleasure to announce our upcoming matches which will determine our very first USWF Champion. 12 competitors will face-off, but only one will be crowned USWF Champion. The Championship will be determined by way of a modified tournament. We will hold three "mini-tournaments," and the winner of each tournament will then advance to a final triangle match which will determine who will be the first USWF Champion. The matches are as follows.
In tournament one: Mark Williams VS The Missing Link Bill Vaughn VS Bob Wheeler
In tournament two: Spider VS Steely Eyed Don Death VS James The Hitman
In tournament three: Jusin Credible VS Viper Sha Dog VS Steve Shine Obviously, the two winners of each first round match will then face off to determine who will advance to the final Championship Triangle Match. Good luck to all of our superstars. May the best man win. (Camera zooms back to USWF logo, and then switches back to Tony DiMera and Steve Hendrix in the broadcast booth.)
TONY: All right fans, after what we've seen here tonight, the road to the USWF championship is sure to be an exiciting one. We'll be there to call all the action. For Steve Hendrix, I'm Tony DiMera saying "good night, everybody!" ************************************************************************
