Why Join USWF?

1 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As an ewrestling player, you may be thinking "with so many potential leagues to join, why should I join the USWF?" I believe there are several reasons: 1. We are experienced 2. We are personal 3. We emphasize quality roleplaying The USWF is headed up by two guys who each had their own league for a while and had good intentions, but found the workload and stress to be too much. We have decided that by working together, we will be able to create a more exciting league and respond to our players needs. We have a central email address ( [detail withheld] ) that we both check regularly, in order to answer your questions or forward your flashes in a timely manner. We will do our best to answer any questions that you have, and incorporate your roleplaying strategies into the league. As such, we encourage our players to come up with exciting, creative, and original roleplays. We emphasize roleplaying in our league and hope that our players will also. Not everyone can win every match, but everyone can develop an excellent character that they enjoy roleplaying. If you are more interested in participating in an exciting, active, league than in winning every match, the USWF is for you. Please email us today for more information about how to join. It's free, it's fun... it's the USWF. [detail withheld] Bob Clubbs [detail withheld]
