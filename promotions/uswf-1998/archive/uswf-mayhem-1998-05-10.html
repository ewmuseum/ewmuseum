MAYhem

Here is another sampling of USWF action. These are the title matches from our first Pay Per View, MAYhem, which was held this past Sunday. To join us, or for more info, write to: [detail withheld] ################################################## USWF Tag Team Championship Match Rail Road Express (Freight and Diesel) w/ Sammy VS The Gods (Mars and Apollo) w/ Hades ################################################## (match joined in progress)
TONY: And what a contest we're seeing here! After twenty grueling minutes of back and forth action, neither team has really gained the upper hand!
STEVE: Diesel and Apollo now going for a test of strength. What an evenly matched pair we have here!
TONY: As I check my notes, I can tell you that Disel is 6'6" and weighs 280 pounds, while Apollo is 6'5" and weighs 285 pounds! You can't get much closer than that!
STEVE: And these two have about equal stregth, DiMera. Each is jockeying for the upper hand, the fans are really getting into this!
TONY: I think that even the crowd is divided about halfway in their loyalty to these teams! I think it's safe to say that both teams have won the respect of the fans here tonight.
STEVE: And Diesel brings up a knee into the midsection of Apollo, doubling him over. That ends the test of strength. Diesel now locking Apollo in a front facelock and dragging him back to his corner, he makes the tag to Freight.
TONY: Freight going up to the top turnbuckle now, and here he comes down with a big double axehandle to the kidney area of Apollo. Apollo is down in his knees now, as Diesel leaves the ring.
STEVE: Mars and Hades are shouting encouragement to their man from their corner, but Freight is continuing to work on Apollo. He's stomping now on the back of Apollo, continuing to work on the back. He hooks his right leg and puts Apollo in a half Boston! That will take its toll on even a god!
TONY: Mars and Hades get the crowd behind Apollo, who manages to make it to the ropes. The ref makes Freight break the hold. Freight now tagging his partner, who comes in. They whip Apollo into the ropes, he rebounds off... AND APOLLO WITH A BIG DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Whoah!
STEVE: Where did he find the strength for that, DiMera? TONY: Who knows! He's going over now to make the tag, and here comes Mars! Mars coming in now, ready for War! He's attacking Diesel with a series of right hands, and a quick dropkick to Freight sends him out of the ring. Mars now whipping Diesel into the ropes, he catches him on the rebound for a big powerslam!
STEVE: Here comes Freight back into the ring now, ooooh a vicious kick to the knee sends Mars down and puts a stop to his offensive. Having done his damage, Freight goes back to the corner.
TONY: Diesel puts a headlock on Mars, slowing him down. Throughout this match, Rail Road Express has done their best to keep The Gods from using their aerial skills, they've tried to keep them grounded.
STEVE: And it's working, DiMera. Rail Road Express is trying to out-fight The Gods, and their strategy is working!
TONY: Diesel goes over for the tag, and Freight comes in with a kick to the midsection of Mars. Freight taking him now, setting him up for a big snap suplex!
STEVE: Who's that coming down the aisle? It's Subway! TONY: Subway coming down the aisle, and you know that this isn't going to be pretty! STEVE: The Rail Road Express don't see their enemy yet, and they look to be putting the finsishing touches on Mars. Freight tags out to Diesel, and it l ooks like they're setting him up for the Runaway Train, their big finishing double powerslam.
TONY: Oh wait, now they see Subway. As you know, there is apparently some bad blood between Rail Road Express and Subway from encounters they have had in other federations! They put down Mars and turn to yell at Subway, who is stading at the end of the aisle, taunting them.
STEVE: Rail Road Express' manager, Sammy, isn't not happy. He's trying to get his guys to finish the match.
TONY: Apollo sees his chance, and he runs across the ring to hit Freight and Diesel in the back of their heads with a double clotesline. Diesel turns around to retaliate with a big right hand... OH! HE JUST CAUGHT THE REF!
STEVE: Diesel just slammed the ref with a big roundhouse right, and the ref is down. He's down!
TONY: I think we're going to see some mayhem here! STEVE: Hades jumps up into the ring, making it 3 on 2... Rail Road Express is standing their ground. But here comes Subway!
TONY: The express' old enemy is in the ring now... we've got 4 on 2! This is certainly NOT going to be good for the Rail Road Express. We've basically got a stare down doing here...
STEVE: Subway's making his move... WHAT?!?! Subway just nailed Hades with an awesome clothesline! Subway has just attacked Hades!! Why in the world would he do that??
TONY: Subway throws Hades out of the ring and continues to attack him on the outside... Freight and Mars are attacking each other, and Sammy just reached under the rope to trip Apollo and drag him out of the ring. Diesel and Freight are setting Mars up for their finisher again... YES! They nail it this time. That awesome running double powerslam, The Runaway Train! Mars is down... Freight makes the cover, Diesel goes over to the recovering ref and makes sure that he sees it.... 1.....2.....3!!!!! They got it!
STEVE: Rail Road Express just won the tag belts.... but this still doesn't explain why Subway helped them!!!
ANNOUNCER: Here are your winners.... and NEW USWF Tag Team Champions.... Rail Road Express!!!!
TONY: Rail Road Express now taking the tag belts, Subway is in the ring with them, as is Sammy.... the Gods are regrouping outside the ring, looking stunned.
STEVE: Sammy's taking the mic.... SAMMY: How do you like THAT?? Ha ha ha.... I want you to take a good look at the three men in the ring right now... Freight, Diesel, and Subway... they are the new force in the USWF, so all of you better get used to it. Ladies and gentlemen, they are REVOLUTION ZERO!
TONY: What?? You mean to tell me that Subway is teaming up with Rail Road Express and Sammy? It looks like we have a new stable here in the USWF, folks....
SAMMY: And don't worry, we've got some more surprises in store for all of you! ############################################### USWF TV Title Match
CHAMPION: "Hollywood" Steve Shine VS CHALLENGER: John McKenzie ############################################### (match joined in progress)
STEVE: And McKenzie is out for revenge here tonight, Tony. Sure, he wants that TV Title, but I think the also wants to teach Steve Shine a lesson.
TONY: I think you're right, Steve. McKenzie obviously feels that "Hollywood" robbed him on the TV title at the battle royal, and tonight he wants to take it away from Shine.
STEVE: And a facerack by Shine slows down McKenzie. Shine takes a minute to wink at Krystal Powers before hammering McKenzie in the face with a big forearm. Shine sends McKenzie across and catches him on the rebound with a dropkick. McKenzie is down, Shine drops and elbow, and then another.
TONY: Shine now running across the ring, bouncing off the ropes, going up for a splash... STEVE: Nobody home! McKenzie rolls out of the way and Shine lands flat on his face. TONY: Now McKenzie is in charge of the match as he lands some kicks to the ribs of Shine. These two have been wavering between scientific wrestling and brawling, but as the match as progressed it's descended more and more into an out-and-out fight!
STEVE: Shine's getting back up to his feet, but McKenzie grabs him in a sleeper! He's got it locked on in the middle of the ring, Shine's got nowhere to go!
TONY: He's starting to fade, Steve! McKenzie's really got that sleeper on tight! STEVE: Here comes Krystal Powers! She knows her man is in trouble! She's up on the apron, screaming at Shine, trying to encourage him.
TONY: The ref's checking Shine... he lifts his arm once.... twice... Shine's about to lose the belt!
STEVE: Krystal Powers is in the ring, she's running toward McKenzie... TONY: Oh! She just slapped John McKenzie hard across the face. It looks like she's trying to save the belt for her man.
STEVE: The ref hasn't called for the bell! He's not going to disqualify Shine. McKenzie drops the hold and Shine flops to the mat. McKenzie is smiling at Powers, who's now trying to apologize. She's down on her knees, scooting away from the big Australian.
TONY: He's got her backed into a corner, and he's got that big right hand cocked back into a fist! He's looking to the crowd, seeking their approval!
STEVE: I can't believe it! These fans actually want McKenzie to hit her! TONY: Powers escapes by ducking under the bottom rope. STEVE: This has given Shine time to recover! He's on his feet, coming up behind McKenzie...
TONY: McKenzie turns around, sees him, locks him in a quick facelock! STEVE: THE DETONATION! His big DDT! TONY: He makes the cover, hooks the leg.... 1..... 2..... 3!!!!! STEVE: We have a new TV Champion! ANNOUNCER: Ladies and getlemen, your winner and NEW TV CHAMPION.... John McKenzie!! TONY: Krystal Powers is irate! McKenzie's got the belt... he's going onto the turnbuckle, displaying the belt for the fans, who give him their approval. ################################################ ******************MAIN EVENT******************** USWF CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH
CHAMPION: Mark Williams VS CHALLENGER: "Critical" Bill Vaughn ################################################# (match joined in progress)
TONY: And Williams, the crafty veteran, ducks under the bottom rope to regroup. STEVE: DiMera, I've never seen anyone as focused as Vaughn is here tonight. He's got his mind set on the USWF title, and Williams knows it.
TONY: Vaughn has had a counter for every offensive Williams has put forth in this match, so it looks like the champ has just decided to take a moment to gather his wits.
STEVE: But Vaughn doesn't look ready for a time out! He's going out after Williams, coming up from behind to scoop him up for a big atomic drop!
TONY: The momentum of that is carrying Williams forward, OH he just slammed into the ringpost.
STEVE: Vaughn isn't letting up, he's picking the champ up and introducing him to the floor with a big bodyslam. Williams is hurting.
TONY: Oh my! It looks like Vaughn is setting him up for... YES a piledriver right on the floor. Oh my! The champ is in definite trouble.
STEVE: Vaughn back up in the ring now as the ref continues to count. Williams is crawling around on the floor, obviously disoriented. He's crawling toward the ring, but he's so groggy that he crawled right under the ring.
TONY: The ref has stopped his count now as Mark Williams is under the ring. I think Vaughn's laughing a little bit in the ring now as he is clearly in control of this match.
STEVE: Here comes Williams out the other side of the ring, still disoriented. Vaughn is going out after him now. He takes Williams by the hair and throws him back into the ring, under the bottom rope.
TONY: Vaughn's getting back into the ring now, but Williams is up! Williams just sprang to his feet and catches Vaughn with a vicious kick to the face as he gets back into the ring. Was Williams playing possum the whole time?
STEVE: I don't know, but he's got his second wind, and then some! TONY: He's continuing to kick and stomp Vaughn now, really hammering away at the challenger. Vaughn gets to his feet, but William catches him with a quick snap suplex.
STEVE: And now Williams is setting him up, in the middle of the ring... with the figure four??
TONY: I haven't seen Mark Williams using a figure four very often, that doesn't seem to be one of his favorites. It is however the finisher of.... wait a minute....
STEVE: That's right! The figure four is CHRIS WILLIAMS' FINISHER! Could it be that we've witnessed another Williams switch?
TONY: Whichever Williams it is, he's got Vaughn caught in the figure four right in the center of the ring. It's going to be tough for Vaughn to get to the ropes.
STEVE: Take a look, DiMera! Look who's crawling out from under the ring! TONY: You're right, Steve! It's Mark Williams! He's trying to sneak back to the locker room area, the ref doesn't see it! I can't believe Vaughn is going to get robbed this way.
STEVE: Wait a second, someone's stepping over the guardrail out of the crowd. Who is that?
TONY: That is one big fan! That guy must be 7 feet tall! He's wearing dark sunglasses and a dark trenchcoat, I can't tell who it is!
STEVE: Whoever he is, he just scooped up Mark Williams. He's got Williams in a fireman's carry and is bringing him back to the ring.
TONY: He just tossed Mark Williams back into the ring! The ref can't believe his eyes, but I think that Chris Williams is even more shocked!
STEVE: Chris Williams lets go of the figure four and runs over to the ropes, trying to get a look at whoever that is that just tossed his brother back into the ring.
TONY: The mystery man is grabbing Chris by the foot and dragging him out of the ring. He's hooking him in for a suplex... NO! A brain buster! A vicious brain buster on the floor... Chris Williams is down!
STEVE: Mark Williams is looking on from the ring, helpless, as Bill Vaughn gets back to his feet. He turns Mark Williams around, hammering him in the face with powerful right hands. He whips Williams into the ropes... catches him... THE CRITICAL MASS! That awesome trademark power slam!
TONY: He's going for the cover.... 1.....2....3!!!! STEVE: He got him! TONY: Vaughn pinned him!!! STEVE: I can't believe it! TONY: We've got a new champion!! ANNOUNCER: Your winner, and the NEW USWF CHAMPION.... "Critical" Bill Vaughn! TONY: That guy in black, whoever he is, just tossed the USWF belt into the ring and is stepping back over the guardrail and leaving through the crowd. Who was that, anyway?
STEVE: Who know, and at this point, who cares? There's a new champion! TONY: That's right, Bill Vaughn just won the USWF Championship! Fans, what a way to end this amazing night! We've seen three new champions crownded tonight, but there's so much that's left unsaid. We'll bring you updates as soon as they become available, and we'll see you soon for Wednesday War. For Steve Hendrix, I'm Tony DiMera... good night everybody!
