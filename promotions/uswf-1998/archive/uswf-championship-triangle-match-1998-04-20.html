USWF Championship Triangle Match

19-20 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The jam-packed arena awaits the USWF Championship Triangle Match! Some of the fans are wearing "HC-2000" T-Shirts, others sport black t-shirts with the gold letters "HOLLYWOOD" across the front, while still others hold up signs that say "The Great One!") (Tony DiMera and Steve Hendrix, both wearing suits, are at the broadcast booth.)
TONY: Fans, this is the night we've all been waiting for! In just a matter of minutes we will know who will be the first ever United States Wrestling Federation Champion! It's been a long road for the three men in tonight's match, but they've made it! Each one is determined to take home the gold!
STEVE: That's right, DiMera! The intensity in this place is unbelievable! Mark Williams, Spider, and "Hollywood" Steve Shine are all incredible athletes, and there's no way anybody can predict who will walk away with the belt tonight!
TONY: Right now, fans, we're going to bring you some pre-recorded comments from Spider... I spoke to him earlier in his dressing room. Let's roll that tape now. <DiMera> Fans, I'd like to introduce to you know one of the 3 men who could become the first USWF Champion tonight. He is the leader of HC-2000.... Spider! (Spider enters.) <DiMera> Spider, we've heard a lot about HC-2000 recently, including the fact that one of your members, The Missing Link, has left the group. Wat can you tell us about that? <Spider> Well he was always wanting to quit but I talked him out of it and I think Crow had a talk with him. <DiMera> So are you telling us that the Missing Link is still a member of your group? <Spider> No I'm saying Crow beat the crap out of him so he being Crow could join. <DiMera> So Crow has taken his place in HC-2000? <Spider> Yes sir it took him a while to pass the test but on that test he made an a + <DiMera> All right, fans! A very determined Spider with some harsh words!
TONY: Well, as you know, fans... there is ceratinly no love lost between Death and the members of HC-2000, particularly Spider! And we'll have to see how... (The lights fall dark and thunder rumbles in the air.)
STEVE : Well, speak of the devil! That sounds like DEATH is in the building. (DEATH walks up from behind the two broadcasters. He stands over them with an evil look on his face.)
DEATH : I'm not real sure how these three men will come into this match tonight, but I know how one of them will be leaving. (Tony stands up with a microphone trying to get a word from this dark man.)
TONY : What are you saying... you know who will win tonight.? DEATH : No... but I know who won't !! (DEATH turns and walks back toward the locker room) STEVE : That is one man who is certainly not a people person. TONY : Well we'll have to see what the dark man has in store for us or I should say them, whoever it is. I wouldn't want to be on that man's hit list.
STEVE: DiMera, I don't know about you... but I'm through with all of this talking! I'm ready for this match!
TONY: For once, I agree with you, Steve! And it looks like everything's in place and we're ready to begin! (The lights fade and a spotlight picks up the RING ANNOUNCER. The fans explode!)
ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen! The following contest is for the USWF CHAMPIONSHIP! (The crowd's noise swells even louder!) This match is a Triangle Match. Only two men are allowed into the ring at a time, and any man may tag the third man to take his place in the ring. If tagged, the wrestler must enter! Two men will be eliminated by pinfall, submission, countout, or disqualification... and the remaining man will be declared the USWF CHAMPION! ("Breathe" by Prodigy begins to play.) Introducing our first competitor! He is accompanied to the ring by a Former Miss California, Krystal Powers! He hails from Hollywood, California and weighs 260 pounds.... ladies and gentlemen, here is "Hollywood" Steve Shine!
TONY: Close your mouth, Hendrix. You're drooling. STEVE: Get a load of Krystal Powers, DiMera! I think that's the skimpiest swimsuit yet! And take a look at those spike heels!
TONY: Well, it looks like "Hollywood" has a few words for us.... SHINE: Tonight is the night! I, "Hollywood" Steve Shine, will be crowned YOUR World Champion! *crowd boos* I think everyone knows how I feel about my opponents tonite. But there's someone else I have a problem with. Bill Vaughn. You sit at home and whine about how everyone mistreats you. How I turned my back on you. Well, shut up! You're a washed up nobody and you expect a title shot after I win? Not a chance! I don't see anyone worthy of a shot in the USWF. In fact, I might as well retire the championship because no one is going to beat me for it. Now it's time to make history. Start your VCR's, get out your cameras, call Billy Joe next door and tell him to watch. Tonight is the night that "Hollywood" begins his legacy. Thank you!
STEVE: I love it, DiMera! TONY: Well, say what he will... I believe that "Critical" Bill Vaughn has a legitimate complaint! Shine turned his back on Vaughn after the two had an agreement to watch each others' backs! If it weren't for Vaughn, Shine wouldn't be here tonight. And for that matter, Vaughn WOULD be here tonight in not for Shine's interference!
STEVE: Quit your whining, DiMera! That's history! The fact is, "Hollywood" is here and he's ready to fight! ("Sweet Dreams" begins to play as the house lights change to a red glow.)
ANNOUNCER: Our second challenger is from New York City. He is a member of HardCore 2000... he weighs 234 pounds.... Spider!
TONY: And here comes Spider, the leader of HC-2000. He is carrying his trademark baseball bat with him as he comes down the aisle.
STEVE: His eyes creep me out, DiMera. Look at that weird red glow! What causes that? TONY: You've got be, Steve. He is a man of mystery! One also has to wonder if this is the man Death was referrring to when he said that he had something in store for one of tonight's competitors. We have certainly heard harsh words exchanged between these two.
ANNOUNCER: And the third competitor.... (Space Oddyssey plays loudly) hailing from St. Louis, Missouri.... weighing 225 pounds... he is the self proclaimed "Greatest of All Time" Mark Williams! (Lights go out. One spotlight focuses on the entryway as TWO people come out, both looking the same and wearing red tights. One has a red robe that says The Greatest and the other has a robe exactly the same, only blue.)
STEVE: What?!?! There's two of them! (Williams walks into the ring and picks up a mic.) WILLIAMS: Before we start the match, I would first like to introduce the only man equal to me in talent, and the newest member of The Great Ones, my twin brother, Chris Williams!! (Both men take off their robes and they look exactly alike, except Chris is 1/2 an inch taller and five pounds heavier)
STEVE: TWINS! What the heck is going on here, DiMera?!!? TONY: Fans, this is a shocker! I mean, how can you tell them apart? Aside from about one-half inch, they look the same?
STEVE: EXACTLY the same, DiMera! This is too much! TONY: Well, it may be too much, but it looks like this is the way it's going to be! Chris Williams is remaining at ringside to support his brother Mark! Krystal Powers does not like this one bit! She's already complaining to the ref! Spider is just starting at the two of them ominously. (The bell rings to start the match.)
TONY: All right, remember fans: only two men in the ring at once, they tag out to the third man, who must come in to take their place. It looks like Steve Shine won the draw, so Mark Williams and Spider will start things off. We're underway! They lock up. Mark Williams takes him down with a spinebuster slam. Mark Williams executes a headlock takedown. Spider catches him in an inside cradle.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. Spider nails him with a slingshot legdrop. Spider executes a vertical suplex. Spider nails him with a kick to the midsection. Spider runs into the ropes. Spider nails him with an elbowdrop. Mark Williams takes him down with a dropkick to the knee. Mark Williams goes for a kneebreaker, but Spider blocks it. Spider whips Mark Williams into the ropes. Spider misses with a kick. Spider misses with a shoulderblock. Mark Williams nails him with a spinebuster slam.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. TONY: And we're off to a very even contest here as Williams and Spider are measuring each other up. Mark Williams executes a crotch slam. Mark Williams nails him with a double-axhandle chop. Mark Williams throws Spider out of the ring.
Juan Duarte counts: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, Spider reenters the ring. STEVE: Smart move there by Spider as he took some time to regroup outside the ring. Mark Williams whips Spider into the ropes. Spider executes a double-axhandle chop. Spider tags out to Steve Shine.
TONY: And there's our first tag as Shine will come in to face Williams. Steve Shine takes him down with a fallaway slam. Steve Shine nails him with a reverse flying elbowdrop. Steve Shine throws Mark Williams out of the ring. Steve Shine goes through the ropes. Steve Shine whips Mark Williams into the guardrail. Steve Shine throws Mark Williams back into the ring.
STEVE: Shine has really taken it to Williams! Here is a man determined to capture the gold! Steve Shine executes a single-leg takedown. Steve Shine goes for a slingshot facebuster, but Mark Williams counters it witha vertical suplex. Mark Williams whips Steve Shine into the ropes. Mark Williams misses with a kick. Steve Shine hits Mark Williams with a shoulderblock. Steve Shine tags out to Spider.
TONY: And Shine does his damage and gets back out of the ring. Mark Williams has been in the ring since the match started, taking on both of these men! Spider executes a fisherman suplex.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. Mark Williams nails him with a crotch slam. Spider throws Mark Williams out of the ring. Spider goes through the ropes. Spider knocks Mark Williams into the ringpost. Mark Williams reenters the ring. Spider follows him back in. Mark Williams tags out to Steve Shine.
STEVE: A smart move by Williams as he will finally get some rest here! He was really hurting after that last brawl out of the ring. Here comes "Hollywood." Steve Shine nails him with a flying lariat. Steve Shine nails him with a spinning leg lariat. Spider climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Steve Shine nails him in the stomach. Spider falls onto the top turnbuckle.
TONY: OH! That had to hurt... Steve Shine nails him with a Russian legsweep. Steve Shine nails him with a standing wristlock. Steve Shine lets go after 11 seconds. Spider tags out to Mark Williams.
STEVE: Williams back in now.... Mark Williams takes him down with a spinebuster slam. Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. Mark Williams catches him in a belly-to-belly suplex. Mark Williams pulls the tights.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, he sees it. Mark Williams hits Steve Shine with a shoulderblock. Mark Williams takes him down with a European uppercut. Mark Williams is going for the pin.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, kickout. TONY: A couple of near falls here as Williams is in control! Steve Shine whips Mark Williams into the ropes. Krystal Powers trips Mark Williams. Juan Duarte warns Krystal Powers.
STEVE: And a nice move there by Powers! She took the momentum away from Williams and gave her man a chance to recover! Shine takes advantage and goes to tag out to Spider! Spider goes for a moonsault bodyblock, but Mark Williams counters it with a side step. Mark Williams takes him down with a stomp. Mark Williams is going for the pin. Mark Williams pulls the tights.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, he sees it. TONY: It looks like Williams is up to his old tricks. STEVE: Hey, this is for all the marbles, DiMera! You do whatever it takes! Mark Williams places Spider on the turnbuckle. Mark Williams takes him down with a superplex. Mark Williams hits Spider with a kick. Mark Williams is going for the pin.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, kickout. Mark Williams executes a stomp. Mark Williams nails him with a standing wristlock. Spider breaks the hold after 9 seconds. Mark Williams tags out to Steve Shine.
TONY: Spider is taking a bit of a beating here, and here comes Steve Shine, who's been resting outside the ring. Steve Shine whips Spider into the ropes. Steve Shine takes him down with a double-axhandle chop. Steve Shine throws Spider out of the ring. Steve Shine goes through the ropes. Steve Shine whips Spider into the guardrail. Krystal Powers comes from behind and distracts Spider. Steve Shine grabs Spider and nails him with a DDT on the floor!
STEVE: Whoah! Shine really caught Spider with that one! I think Spider may be knocked out! The ref is counting...... 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, Spider is getting up, trying to shake it off, 8, 9, 10!
TONY: Spider has been counted out! Spider has been eliminated! Only two men remain! Whoah! That big DDT on the floor by Shine really knocked Spider for a loop, and now he has been eliminated from this match!
STEVE: And look at Spider, he can't believe what has happened! (The arena lights go black, and a strobe light flashes. The sounds of thunder can be heard.)
TONY: Oh no! Ladies and gentlemen, we have an unexpected visitor! (Through the fog and the flashing, Death can be seen on the runway. His deep laughter is heard, amplified over the PA system. Enraged, Spider charges down the runway towards him!)
STEVE: It's Death, and Spider is NOT happy! Those two are really pounding away at each other on the runway! Steve Shine and Mark Williams don't seem to mind, it's a bit of an intermission for them, as they both take this time to rest!
TONY: I've never seen two men fight with such ferocity! This is just a street brawl between Death and Spider!
STEVE: Wait a minute!!! What the ?!?!?!? TONY: Something is descending from the rafters! I (A figure, dressed all in white, slides quickly down from the rafters. He is wearing a large white cape that billows in teh air as he descends. He lands directly behind Spider!)
STEVE: Who is THAT, DiMera! TONY: I have NO IDEA! (The white figure hits death hard on the back of the head with a furious double axehandle blow. Spider drops to his knees as the two figures pound away on him.)
STEVE: I don't know who he is, but it looks like he's allied with Death! TONY: I think you're right.... and here comes HC-2000 to support their leader! Sha Dog and James The Hitman have just appeared from backstage!
STEVE: HOLY COW! Would you look at that! (The mysterious white figure has unhooked a wire from his belt and DEATH has attatched it to his belt. Suddenly, both death and the mysterious man are hoisted up into the rafters, the amplified sound of their laughter ringing through the arena. HC-2000 looks on, amazed!)
TONY: What an exit! Death and the man in white have disappeared into the rafters! I can't even see them up there!
STEVE: And HC-2000 are NOT happy! They're taking Spider backstage with them to assess the damage!
TONY: We have NOT heard the last of this, fans! We will try to bring you some kind of update on this situation soon. (The lights return to normal.)
TONY: Take a look at Steve Shine and Mark Williams! They look just as confused as the rest of us! (Williams and Shine look at each other, shrug their shoulders, and lock up.)
STEVE: And we're back into action as one of these two men will soon become the USWF champion.
TONY: And each man has support! Mark Williams' twin brother Chris is at ringside, as is the lovely Krystal Powers!
STEVE: I'd love to get a little support from Krystal Powers any day! TONY: I'm sure you would, Steve! They lock up. Steve Shine executes a spinning leg lariat. Steve Shine nails him with a chokehold.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, three. Steve Shine nails him with a standing wristlock. Mark Williams manages to escape the hold after being trapped for 9 seconds. Steve Shine whips Mark Williams into the ropes. Krystal Powers trips Mark Williams.
TONY: And for the second time tonight, we've seen Powers interfere in this match! The ref is having a few words with her! Steve Shine nails him with a standing wristlock. Mark Williams reaches the ropes after being trapped for 15 seconds. Steve Shine executes a fallaway slam. Steve Shine goes for a chokehold, but Mark Williams blocks it. Steve Shine is going for the pin.
Juan Duarte counts: One, kickout. Steve Shine complains about a slow count. Mark Williams catches him in an inside cradle.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. Mark Williams executes a double-axhandle chop. Mark Williams executes a belly-to-back suplex. Mark Williams whips Steve Shine into the turnbuckle, but Steve Shine reverses it. Steve Shine runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Mark Williams moves out of the way. Mark Williams takes him down with a double chickenwing submission. Steve Shine reaches the ropes after being trapped for 13 seconds. Mark Williams executes a spinebuster slam.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, kickout. TONY: Williams appears to have the upper hand here! Mark Williams takes him down with a kneebreaker. Mark Williams nails him with a dropkick to the knee. Mark Williams executes a fist to the midsection. Mark Williams executes a short lariat.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. Mark Williams throws Steve Shine out of the ring. Steve Shine goes for a slingshot facebuster, but Mark Williams counters it with a vertical suplex. Steve Shine whips Mark Williams into the ropes. Steve Shine hits Mark Williams with a clothesline. Mark Williams falls out of the ring. Steve Shine goes through the ropes. They're brawling inside the ring area. Steve Shine takes him down with a Samoan Drop. Steve Shine whips Mark Williams into the guardrail. Steve Shine nails him with a single-leg takedown. Steve Shine nails him with a piledriver.
TONY: WHOAH! A big piledriver right on the arena floor! Mark Williams is hurting, but Shine didn't really connect with the move the way he should have, so Williams is not suffering the full effects. Still, he's groggy! Steve Shine nails him with a bodyslam. Steve Shine nails him with a standing wristlock. Mark Williams manages to escape the hold after being trapped for 20 seconds. Mark Williams throws Steve Shine out of the ring.
STEVE: And Williams is taking a few seconds to catch his breath. He's still hurting from that piledriver! Steve Shine reenters the ring. Mark Williams whips Steve Shine into the ropes. Mark Williams hits Steve Shine with a clothesline. Mark Williams nails him with a fist to the midsection. Mark Williams hits Steve Shine with a DDT and goes for the pin.
Juan Duarte counts: One, two, shoulder up. TONY: Chris Williams is not happy with that count! He's complaining that the ref's count was too slow! He's so insistent about it that he's even up on the apron! The ref has gone to talk to him about it...
STEVE: And here comes Krystal Powers! I love it! TONY: Powers is removing one of her spike heels, and coming up behind Williams, who is busy watching the argument between the ref and his brother!
STEVE: Nobody sees her coming! BAM! TONY: She just nailed Mark Williams in the back of the head with that heel! Williams is down! Shine is still down and out from that DDT, but Powers is draping Shine's arm over Mark Williams! Chris Williams has seen it all and is irate! He's trying to get into the ring, but the ref is trying to push him out! Powers drops the shoe and escapes!
STEVE: The ref's turning back around to make the count! ONE, TWO.... WHY DID HE STOP? TONY: He has seen he shoe! He sees the shoe Krystal Powers left behind! He's going to pick it up, and is going over to have a little chat with Ms. Powers! She is, of course, denying everything!
STEVE: Wait a minute, what's Chris Williams doing?? TONY: Chris Williams is coming up into the ring! He's rolled Mark Williams out of the ring, and he's coming back.... He's got Steve Shine in a small package!
STEVE: The ref's turning back around! TONY and STEVE: ONE....... TWO..... THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! STEVE: I can't believe it!!!! TONY: Incredible!!! (The bell rings. Krystal Powers is livid.) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen... your winner... and the NEW United States Heavyweight Champion..... MARK WILLIAMS!
STEVE: But the ref is raising CHRIS WILLIAMS' HAND IN VICTORY! TONY: This is unbelievable, fans! I've never seen anything like it before! STEVE: So is our champion Mark Williams or Chris Williams? TONY: Fans, this is totally incredible! We're out of time here, but obviously there is much to talk about! We will try to bring you more information soon about all that has taken place here tonight! We'll try to find out more about Death and the mystery man who came from above, as well as the controversial end to tonight's match! For Steve Hendrix, I'm Tony DiMera saying "good night, everybody!"
STEVE: UNBELIEVABLE!
