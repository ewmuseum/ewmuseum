USWF Talent

6 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a sampling of the talent we have in the USWF. If you would like to get in on the action, just email us at [detail withheld] ************************************************* Mark Williams *************************************************
Tony DiMera: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome to the USWF a man who is bound to create a quite stir in the ranks here. He calls himself The Greatest, please welcome, Mark Williams! (Lights go out, except for one spotlight on the entrance way as Space Oddessy 2001 begins to play. A tall, man, wearing a red sequined robe steps out of the back and slowly makes his way to the ring as the spotlight follows. Tony stands in awe in the center of the ring)
Tony: Wow! That's quite an entrance, Mr. Williams. Williams: Let me tell you something, Tony. If you think that is an entrance, wait until you see me in the ring. I am the greatest in-ring performer of all time, and I have chosen the USWF to prove it. As soon as the first cards come out and I get a contract, I'll show you all who the man is here. I will become the first USWF champion, and I will hold on to the belt until I decide to retire. I will walk through the competition in this federation. Anone who is foolish enough to step in the ring with The Greatest will feel the STF and will know just who the greatest is. ( Hands mic to Tony and walks off to a mixture of cheers and boos)
Tony: Well, that's one man we will definitely keep our eyes on in the future. Looks like Mark Williams has already stirred up some mixed feelings in this crowd. ***************************************** Justin Credible ***************************************** (Lights start to flicker....Music is blaring in the background...pyrotechnics blast form everywhere.)
Tony: Please welcome my guest at this time. He is form New York CIty formerly hailing form Dublin, Ireland.....Please welcome Justin Credible. (Justin and his valet Heather walk out side my side. All of the sudden they both stop. Justin takes a step forward and starts strutting. The crowd goes wild.)
Tony: Man-O-Man with this intensity Justin is sure to be a sure fire hit in this federation. (Justin helps Heatehr into the ring and climbs on himself.)
Tony: Justin. How do you think you will cope with the wrestlers here in the USWF? Justin: Well Tony, I think I will do just fine. I've been wanting to be a wrestler ever since I was a wee little lad, now my dreams are finally becoming true, and I will demolish everyone who steps in my way No matter how big you are...No matter how strong you are, I will give you a night to remember.
Tony: So Just......... (Heather takes the mike from him.) Heather: Sorry I had to do that but I need to say something to all of our fans. Justin Credible will rule the USWF. No matter who the opponent is. See Justin and I have been training for this for 2 years now and you can imagine how pumped up we are.
Justin: That is exactly right Heather. I have waited for this day for 7 grueling years and nothing is gonna get in my way now. (Justin and Heather exit the ring area.) If you would like to get in on the action, just email us at [detail withheld] ******************************************** Death ******************************************** AND NOW DOWN TONY DiMera STANDING BY IN THE RING...
TONY: "Good evening ladies and gents, I'm Tony DiMera. In a couple of seconds, I'm going to bring down a man who's very name sends chills down your spine" (AT THAT THE LIGHTS FALL DARK A GIANT HUSH COVERS THE CROWD. THUNDER FILLS THE AREA AND STOBE LIGHTS FLASH ON AND OFF. A LARGE SHADOW MOVES DOWN THE WALK WAY...)
TONY: "I would like to wecome DEATH to the USWF " (AS DEATH STEPS OVER THE TOP ROPES INTO THE RING DiMera STEPS BACK. SNATCHING THE MIC FROM Tony AND LOOKING OUT AT EVERYONE IN A DEEP TONE DEATH SPEAKS...)
DEATH: "Listen and listen well. There is about to be a new plague sweeping over the USWF and that plague is DEATH. I have but one thing on my mind and that is becoming the USWF champion as quickly as possible. I have no problem taking anyone out that gets in my way." (DROPPING THE MICROPHONE DEATH TURNS FOR THE ROPES. Tony PICKS UP THE MIC TO RE-GAIN CONTOLL OF THE INTERVIEW)
TONY: "Aren't you over looking some great opponents here in the USWF ? " (SHARPLY TURNING AROUND AND BENDING DOWN TO THE MIC DEATH ANSWERS...)
DEATH: "I'm not looking over or around anybody I'm looking straight through them. And I await the challenges" (STEPPING OVER THE ROPES HE HEADS FOR THE LOCKER ROOM.)
TONY: "Well folks their you have it one angry and deranged man" *********************************************** Viper *********************************************** (The thundering sound of Black Sabbath is almost drowned out by the cheer of the fans. Some for,some against. But they all agree that this is no ordinary man. Led to the ring by his manager ANDREA, is VIPER!!!!!!!!!)
ANDREA: (in a loud demanding voice) SHUT UP!!!!! I would like to introduce you to VIPER!!!!! The warrior of tomorrow.(turning around & slapping him in his chest & running her hand over her own buzzed head) We want to open a challenge to anyone who will get in the ring with the perfect fighting machine. (flexing his upper body muscles while rubbing his fist into his palm ,he stands silent,starring foward) Together we will destroy each & every opponant on our way to the top! Nothing will stand in the way of VIPER owning the gold! ( ANDREA throws the mike into the audience & draws mixed cheers as they walk up the platform through the smoke) If you would like to get in on the action, just email us at [detail withheld] ************************************************* "Hollywood" Steve Shine ************************************************* (camera fades in on a California beach with Steve Shine and Krystal Powers laying on the sand)
SS: You know, I'm looking forward to knocking some heads in the USWF. The losers that are crazy enough to get in the ring with me are in for a butt whooping!
KP: Steve, you know you're the best. SS: Yeah, I am. I've been the best all my life. When I was in junior high, when the bully came to take my lunch money, I socked him in the mouth! As a senior, I was All-State in football. After high school, I began to work on my exquisite body. I won Mr. California three years in a row. Now, I want to get in the wrestling ring and show everyone why I'm still the best at everything I do. My goal is to win titles and keep them. I'll put them in my trophy case at home.
KP: But Steve, it's already poured into two rooms! SS: What's two rooms when you have 30? Beware USWF, if you have something I want, I'm going to make it mine. Watch out, it's time to SHINE!!!! (camera fades to black) *********************************************** "Critical" Bill Vaughn *********************************************** (The camera pans acrossa typical gym to find a monster of a man furiously working away on a machine. Hearing the camera crew Bill Vaugn turns to give his opening address.)
Critical Bill: You must be from the USWF. Well I've got a few things that I think you should hear. First. I've come here because some damn fool told me that the USWF was the place to find a good fight. England ran out of people who'd face me. So I've come here to lay down a challenge, and I don't want to be disapointed. Second. I want the gold that the USWF has to offer. They don't call me 'Critical Bill' for nothing. Every guy I face ends up in 'critical' condition. I haven't been told who else is in the fed yet, but there had better be some decent opposition. I haven't beaten the crap out of anyone for a while. So my appologies to the first person who faces me. It's not personal I hate everyone. (Bill closes to the camera.)
Critical Bill: I don't care who I take on, you're all meat for the beast. The time has come for 'Critical Bill' to lay down the law in the USWF. Bring on the challenge, I can't wait. If you would like to get in on the action, just email us at [detail withheld]
