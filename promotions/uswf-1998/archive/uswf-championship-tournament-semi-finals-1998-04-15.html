[TONY] Ok fans, as you can see, the action is fast and furious here in teh USWF. Well for the latest news, you can always call the USWF hotline! All of the latest news for the low-low cost of $2.99/minute. So yeah, folks, if you want to latest inside scoops on USWF goings-on, call the USWF Hotline, and dial 2 for Tony. Kids under 19, call with or without your parent's permission. (Tony's speech is cut all too short by the dimming of the arena lights; a hush comes over the crowd . . . ) (the hush resonates, until it loudly is interrupted by the opening notes of Nirvana's "Heart Shaped Box")
[TONY] Ladies and gentlemen, I, I'm not sure that I can explain just what is going on right now. Perhaps some children are coming out to speak about the merits of calling the USWF Hotline . . . (just then, a yellow light emanating from the entrance to the locker room area penetrates the arena darkness, just enough to reveal the contours of a man making his way down the aisle to the interview area, located halfway between the ring and the locker room) (the man, standing just over 6 feet tall, wears a black trenchcoat and a black beret; a white scarf hangs down off the man's shoulders)
[TONY] Ladies and gentlemen, it, it, well, give it up for some guy! (the music, as though on cue, stops; the man grabs the microphone from Tony; Tony scurries to the locker room area, evidently frightened by the appearance of the mysterious man)
[MAN] To all the wrestlers of the United States Wrestling Federation. As of right now, consider yourselves served noticed that what it boils down to is that for this man standing before you, Snuff isn't something you chew.
[MAN] It's something I do. And to all you ladies who feebly try to entertain wrestling fans though you are unable to entertain even a doubt: Snuff is something that I do to you.
[MAN] Back to your regularly scheduled program. Snuff said. *********************************************************************** ***** **********
