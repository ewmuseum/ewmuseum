Here is all of the information you need to join up with the USWF. If you have any questions, feel free to email us. Thanks! Thank you for your interest in the USWF. We are a brand new play by email wrestling leauge, and we are excited that you will be joining us. All you need to do in order to get started is send in the information requested by this form. You will be creating a FICTIONAL wrestler whose career you will guide in our fantasy league. While you may draw from real wrestling in creating your character, we ask that you not send in an obvious clone of a real pro wrestler. Also, be original in choosing your character's name. If we feel that you are copying too much, we will ask you to fill out a new form. Please be thoughtful and thorough as you create your character. This will be the wrestler you will play in our league. Create a distinct personality that you will enjoy roleplaying; create a character that you can be consistent with. Create an interesting, diverse, rich character rather than one you will have difficulty roleplaying as. Remember, the way to win in this league is be effective roleplaying. So concentrate on creating a character that you can breathe LIFE into, rather than sending in a 7ft, 500 monster and thinking that he will automatically win every match. We are also allowing you to create a MANAGER character. This is completely optional, but some of you may find this to be fun or necessary. If you are creating a character that will not be able to speak much on his own (Think of Zeus, Kane, etc) you will want a manager. If you have any questions, feel free to ask. Otherwise, send in the information and get started.
