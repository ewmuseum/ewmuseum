To give you an idea of the high-quality roleplaying we have in the USWF, here is a copy of one of our latest daily "flash packets." If you'd like to join in on e-wrestling action like this, write us at: [detail withheld]
********************** Storm ********************** (Storm is seated in the locker room after Sunday Night Slam. He is dressed in street clothes and appears to be deep in thought. USWF VP George Deeney walks up to him and they begin to speak to each other.)
Storm: Hey George, what's going on? George Deeney: Alot, Alot. We hope to have a tag title tourney soon but we need teams. Is there someone you can bring in to form a team with you?
Storm: Yes, there is. I have a cousin who is dying to find somewhere to wrestle. He'll be glad to come here and join me in trying to win the tag titles. First things first, however. At Wednesday WAR, I will become USWF TV Champ. Why? Because I possess the strength, skills and stamina to eliminate all 12-men. You know, I see that HC-2000 is in this matchup. I can't help but wonder what would happen if (and its an emormous if) HC-2000 were alone in the ring. Would they fight it out? My guess is no. They would probably lay down for Spider, that egomaniac.
Deeney: I noticed you seem to think HC-2000 isn't quite what it claims it is. Storm: You're right George I don't. Here's why? I heard Spider claim he is fighting for good, for disadvantaged wrestlers yet every time I hear him talk now all he does is go on about how he's the best and how HC-2000 is the best and how anyone not in HC-2000 isnt the best, etc. If he were truly a good person he would give credit to any wrestler who stands up to rulebreakers and such. HC-2000 unfortunately makes me think of the nwo. Now I know there aim is different but certain things make HC-2000 like the nwo: 1)Spider thinks he is greater than any other wrestler. 2)HC-2000 thinks it is better than anyone. 3)HC-2000 members grovel at Spider's feet. I could go on, but why?
Deeney: (sees Kristi Rayne walk up.) Hi Miss Rayne. Kristi Rayne: It's Kristi, please. I have a question. Would the USWF sanction a match between women?
Deeney: I think so. Id have to talk to Bobby (Pres. Clubbs) though. Kristi: Okay, I want to challenge any female wrestler in USWF to step in the ring with me. I really don't care who decides to step in the ring with me. However, I do know this. That bimbo that follows Steve Shine around all the time needs to watch out. Because sooner or later, I will challenge her to a match.
Storm: Look, we need to go. But I want all 11 opponents in Wednesday's match to make sure they are prepared to get their <beep> kicked. They will fall victim to the Lightning Strike or whatever I neeed to use to beat them. And I will beat them. And USWF will rise after I win the TV title. (George Deeney walks out of the locker room. Storm notices the cameraman all of a sudden. He sees a note taped to his locker. It reads....."We are taping this so all the USWF can see that there is a conspiracy between you and Deeney against HC-2000. Watch out. You have been warned.......HC-2000." Storm notices the cameraman turn his head for a second and nails him with the Lightning Strike (diagonal superkick). The camera falls and is caught quickly by Kristi Rayne. Kristi sets it down as Storm glares at it.)
Storm: HC-2000. There is no conspiracy between George and I. I used to be in another fed that he was vp of and that is the extent of the connection between us. I will win that 12 man on Wednesday and will prove to the USWF that I can get things done. And remember, If anyone wants to get in the ring with me, just say so. (the picture fades as Storm smashes the camera.) ******************************* "Critical" Bill Vaughn ******************************* (The camera show "Critical" Bill Vaughn standing in front of the USWF logo with a huge smile on his face).
CRITICAL BILL: Well, when I first came to this federation I said that I came here for the challenge. That's just what I got and I love every second of it. I have faced my opponents and seen them fall under the power of the "Critical Mass". I am well on my way to a shot at the championship. Now on to other matters. This Wednesday night, a Battle Royal is set to take place. The finest talent in the world will assemble for one reason and one reason alone. The USWF TV Championship. Now it's no secret that I had HC-2000 in my corner against Steve Shine. However when it comes down to it all bets are off. It's every man for himself, at least that's the way it's suppost to be. There's new blood in the USWF and like everyone else they all want the gold. Well I've got news for you and the rest of the fed. If you want the belt, you gotta get through me! Wednesday night the USWF will crown it's new TV champion. "Critical" Bill Vaughn. _______________________________________________________________________ __ Vaughn want's a shot at the champion ship belt. Vaughn says it's every man for himself. Vaughn tells the new guys to keep out of his way. ************************************ "Hollywood" Steve Shine ************************************ (Steve hendrix is standing in the ring awaiting "Hollywood" Steve Shine.)
HENDRIX: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages, put your hands together and welcome my guest at this time, "Hollywood" Steve Shine!!!!!! (Shine and his valet, Krystal Powers, make an entrance to many boos from the crowd.)
HENDRIX: Mr. Shine, you've run into a slimp in the ring lately. Anything you'd like to say about it.
SHINE: (very solemnly) Steve, it seems like I've hit a brick wall on my way to the USWF title. First, I lose in the final triangle match to Mark, or should I say Chris, Williams. No problem. I've got family to watch my back also. Then Bill Vaughn puts me down with his "Critical Mass". I'm telling you, Steve, that hurt like hell. In fact, my back is still hurting! And then, for all of you who watched the intrafed supercard, I was defeated.
HENDRIX: Yeah, you sure have taken a beating lately. SHINE: And with all of the new talent coming in, it sems like it's only going to get tougher. This Wednesday, I'm going to take part in my second title shot. I'm going to win this battle royal and get a title around my waist. And I have a couple of challenges to make. First, my nephew, Bolt, and I want to enter the tag team tourney. Second, if USWF President Bob Clubbs would allow it, I would like a one-on-one match with Mark Williams at the upcoming PPV. I want a cage match so that no one can interfere.
HENDRIX: I'm not so sure that you're still the number one contender anymore. SHINE: Well, if not, I will be by the time the PPV arrives. Count on it! Let's go, Krystal! (Shine and Powers leave the ring as the camera fades to black) ************************************ Bolt ************************************ (camera fades in on Tony Dimera wandering backstage.)
TONY: I'm trying to get an exclusive interview with the young nephew of Steve Shine, Bolt. (As Tony approaches the young man's locker room, the loud blasts of the grunge band Nirvana can be heard.)
TONY: (knocking on the door) Bolt? This is Tony Dimera. I was hoping to get an interview with you.
BOLT: (opening the door) Sure! C'mon in! (Tony steps inside and looks around. Clothes are scattered all over. It looks like a typical 18 year old's room.)
TONY: First off, I would like to know your immediate plans here in the USWF. BOLT: Well, I'm very thankful to be here. I'm glad that uncle Steve got me on here and I'm going to help him claim the tag titles here.
TONY: So, you're going to be a tag wrestler? BOLT: No. I just want to help him get some gold around his waist. TONY: Not many people like your uncle's ways. BOLT: I don't agree with most of them either, but he has really stepped forward in getting me a job here. And like they say, blood is thicker than water.
TONY: That may be true. What about the upcoming battle royal? BOLT: I'm going to give it my best, but I'm not a very big man. But I have a lot of heart and I'm going to try and last as long as I can.
TONY: Thanks again, Bolt. I'm sure we'l be seeing a lot of you in the future. BOLT: Thanks, Tony.
