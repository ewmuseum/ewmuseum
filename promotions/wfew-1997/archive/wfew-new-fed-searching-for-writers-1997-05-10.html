[WFeW] New Fed Searching for Writers

10 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Members of RSPW-F My name is [name withheld], and in the next couple of months, I am hoping to launch a brand new e-federation called the World Federation of e-Wrestlers. Right now, I am in search of writers for the fed. If you are hired, you will be required to write one card per week, as well as helping to write PPV's, which will happen every other month. You will also be involved in our selection committee, in which you can vote on who we bring into the fed. If you are interested, please send me the following: Your Name Your E-mail E-wrestling Experience -How long have you been involved with e-wrestling? -Any fed head experience? -Any writing experience? Do you have experience with simulators? -If yes, which ones? -If no, would you be willing to learn to use one? Send us a piece of your work. Be creative. I want to see what you're style is like. I thank you all for your time.
