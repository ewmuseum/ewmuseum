PWF, 28 March 1997 — the fed's first card

28 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all ! Here is a copy of the PWF's first ever card, which was held last Wednesday night. If you like what you see and would like to join, just e-mail me at [detail withheld]. Our first PPV is on April 12th and is called "In Search of a Champion". Our 3 titles will be awarded that night through tournaments. Enjoy ! The PWF's first ever card was held tonight at the Pond in Anaheim, California, and already things are starting to erupt. . . Here's a look back at this great moment in PWF history: MATCH 1: KEN SHAMROCK vs YOKOZUNA (w/Ted Dibiase) ************************************************* Yokozuna really put on a show for the fans. He just kept suplexing the UFC champ over and over and over . . . The "world's most dangerous man" was really out of his league against a man over two times larger than he is. Yokozuna won the match at 15:24 with his traditionnal finisher: the Banzai Drop. However, the story is what happened after the final bell. Yokozuna was joined by "Stone Cold" Steve Austin in an attack on Shamrock. But, who should come to the UFC champ's aid, but Bret "Hitman" Hart. The Hitman brought a chair to the ring and cleaned house. He then helped Shamrock up and congratulated him for his performance. Then, as soon as Shamrock turned his back, Bret whacked him upside the head with he chair. The Hitman offered no explanation for his actions tonight, but maybe we'll find out what the story is, 'cause there's more to this then meets the eye. RESULT: Yokozuna wins by pinfall at 15:24 with the Banzai Drop. MATCH 2: "STONE COLD" STEVE AUSTIN (w/Ted DiBiase) vs CHRIS BENOIT ****************************************************************** This was really a see-saw battle that could have been won by either wrestler. The Canadian Crippler almost ended the match in the first minute as he followed up a Northern Lights Suplex with a very near fall. Midway thru the match is when Austin really took over. He tried almost every pinning combination in the book, but just couldn't put it away. Then, at the 20-minute mark, Benoit hit his finisher, the Dragon Suplex, but with the referree KO'd, he couldn't pick up the victory. When the ref finally awoke, he counted 2, but DiBiase put Austin's foot on the rope without the zebra noticing, and the match continued. A few moments later, Benoit climbed to the top turnbuckle but was pushed off by the Million $ Man. After the fall, Austin put the Horseman away with the Stone Cold Stunner. RESULT: "Stone Cold" Steve Austin wins by pinfall via the Stone Cold Stunner at 24:38. MATCH 3: RAVEN (w/Sunny & Missy Hyatt) vs VADER (w/Ted DiBiase) *************************************************************** This match never really got started. Raven charged onto Vader as he was making his way to the ring and nailed him with a chair. He was really doing a number on Vader when Ted DiBiase flashed about a dozen $100 bills, which grabbed his attention, of course. As Raven approached the Million $ Man, Vader hit him with a running clothesline and then started slamming and power-bombing him on the concrete floor. A group of officials then came out to stop the brawl. This may be the beginning of a very intense feud. RESULT: Draw (Double Countout, I guess) MATCH 4: "RAVISHING" RICK RUDE (w/Sunny & Missy Hyatt) vs JEFF JARRETT ********************************************************************** Double J had many opportunities to defeat the Ravishing One, but simply couldn't get it done. He hit the sleeper very early in the match, but for some unknown reason, let go of the hold after Rude dropped to the canvas. Jarrett attempted to dive onto Rude when he was outside the ring, but the Ravishing One moved out of the way, and Jarrett smashed his body on the concrete. Moments later, Jarrett hooked Rude with a sunset flip, but Rude kicked out. Jarrett then complained to referee that the count was slow. During this time, Rude regained his energy and hooke Double J with an inside cradle and got the 3-count for the win. Jarrett complained about a fast count after the match was over. RESULT: Rick Rude wins with an inside cradle at 18:08 MATCH 5: STEINER BROTHERS vs CAN-AM EXPRESS ******************************************* Despite the fact that the Steiners dominated most of the match, it seemed like they just weren't in good enough physical shape. Scott, however, did manage to kick out of a massive Tiger Driver LaFon towards the mid-point of the match. There really wasn't that much to this relatively boring contest, despite the high expectations. Furnas pinned Rick with a small package to seal the victory for the Express. RESULT: Can-Am Express won when Furnas pinned Rick Steiner after a small package at 20:23 MATCH 6: TOMMY DREAMER vs DIAMOND DALLAS PAGE (w/Kimberley) *********************************************************** This was a very one-sided affair, marked by the surprising use of chokeholds by Page. The Horsemen were out to watch this match, as they are looking for the fourth man to complete their group. At the moment though, DDP is a member of the Scavengers. Will he prove to be a turn coat ? Only time will tell. DDP really out on a show for FLair, Benoit and the Giant, defeating Dreamer in just over 5 minutes. RESULT: Diamond Dallas Page wins with the Diamond Cutter at 5:19 MATCH 7: RIC FLAIR vs LEX LUGER ******************************** Luger was in total control early on, hitting the Nature Boy twice with his patented Running Forearm, but it wasn't enough to put Slick Ric away . . . yet. However, after getting out of a bearhug and kicking out of another Running Forearm, Flair simply could not handle the pain of the Torture Rack. RESULT: Lex Luger made Flair submit to the Torture Rack at 16:48. MATCH 8: SABU (w/Sunny & Missy Hyatt) vs KEVIN NASH (w/Ted Dibiase & Scott Hall) ********************************************************************* This was, by far, the most exciting match of the night. Surprisingly, none of the 4 people ever got involved in the match. Nash no-sold about 5 or 6 of Sabu's moves early on. Nash hit the the Outsider Bomb, used the ropes for added leverage, but still Sabu kicked out. The big man really tried everything, even the Outsider Bomb ON THE FLOOR. After 15 minutes of unbelievable action, Sabu went for a bodyslam, but Nash rolled him into a small package and got the 3. RESULT: Nash wins with a small package at 15:09. MATCH 9: HART BROS. (w/Jimmy Hart) vs ELIMINATORS (w/Sunny & Missy Hyatt) ****************************************************************** This bout was marked by missed move after missed move. From dropkicks to flying cross body presses, without forgetting DDTs, none of the 4 men could hit a move to save their life. For a little while it seemed as if the first person to get a headlock in would win the match. Later on, the custom changed to double-teaming on both sides. The match ended in an odd manner as Kronos pinned Owen Hart after what seemed to be an innocen little short clothesline, however, it was enough to put the self-proclaimed King of Harts on the shelf. RESULT: The Eliminators win after Kronos pinned Owen Hart, following a short lariat at 21:12. MATCH 10: TAZ (w/Bill Alphonso & Team Taz) vs STING *************************************************** This was a ver rough and tough contest. In fact, the referee must've warned both [arties about 5 times each to quit choking and break when he said so. Taz attempted to hook the Tazmission on many occasions, but simply couldn't do it. Then, as a complete surprise to everyone, out came the Horsemen ! Who were they there to see, Taz or Sting ? It didn't take long for the world to find out. Taz tossed the Stinger out of the ring and as the referee was admonishing Taz, Flair DDTed the master of the Scorpion Deathlock on the concrete. The ref counted 10 and Taz was awarded the decision. He wasn't very pleased with the idea, 'cause he felt he could've won without anyone's help. RESULT: Taz wins by countout at 10:56. MATCH 11: SYCHO SID vs THE GIANT ******************************** This was, without a doubt, the most and anticipated match of the night. Unfortunately for the fans, it was also the shortest match of the night, lasting under 4 minutes. The Giant simply never got started in this one as Sid mounted the only offence of the bout. Sid hit the choke slam, the Giant's finisher, and it was enough to put him away. RESULT: Sycho Sid wins with the choke slam at 3:49.
