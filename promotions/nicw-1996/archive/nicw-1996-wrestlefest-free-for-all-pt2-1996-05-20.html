Fenton - BACK BODY DROP BY COPPERDUST! Anderson - The Rappin' Rabbi went to the outside! Fenton - Copperdust is trying to get to the top ropes! Anderson - He fell off the second one, he's trying again. Director - TAKE 2! Fenton - Copperdust fell down again! Anderson - This time he is up but so is The Rappin' Rabbi, Copperdust leaps, The
Rappin' Rabbi moves out of the way, they are both on the outside brawling it out, the referee is counting, it's a double countout!
DING DING DING Fenton - The Rappin' Rabbi can't believe it! He grabs a chair and nails Copperdust with it but it was a low blow and Copperdust wants more, The Rappin' Rabbi has taken off! Anderson - Copperdust has grabbed a mike! Copperdust - Ummm... Tom Ace, you have not seen my last flick yet! R.A. This match as a result of a double countout is a draw! Fenton - Tom Ace..? What does HE have to do with Copperdust? Anderson - No idea, let's get back to Parker, I am outta here before he comes this way! Fenton - Hey, didn't you sit next to him on the bus? Parker - Thank you guys, and now for all you folks at home, I'm going to discuss the Main Event. The biggest, most important Ironman match in NICW history, it is for the NICW World Title, and it is 30 minutes long! It is between Falcon and
Mike D'Angelo! During an interview, he had something to say about that..
--------------- Parker - We are almost out of time.. Any closing thoughts? You have that upcoming
Ironman Match against Falcon at Wrestlefest, and he promises it will be beyond what has happened in any other league so far! What do you plan to do?
Angelo - I plan on winning my first title. The NICW World Heavyweight Championship. I'm sure it'll be Falcon who I wrestle, just because he's got ways of retaining the belt. You see, I know Falcon. I've taken him to the limits. An
Iron Man match? Every match I wrestle is an Iron Man match. 30 minutes against the Man Who Refuses to Lose is gonna be hell for you Falcon.
<pauses for a second or two> Wrestlefest will be one of the greatest cards in history, and I intend on wrestling the greatest match in wrestling history.
Falcon, adjust the straps on that gold belt! Because it's gonna fit just right around my waist! Falcon, you've been warned!!!!!!!
Parker - Mike, thanks for taking the time to give this interview, I'm sure the folks at home are impressed!
Angelo - You know they are baby... --------------- Parker - We have less then 30 seconds left.. Folks make that call now. The greatest E-Wrestling match in NICW HISTORY is about to take place! Be there!
<Scene fades to video, with the pictures of Mike D'Angelo, and Falcon, both superimposing themselves on each other, in a cyclic pattern>
