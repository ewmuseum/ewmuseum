The Best of Mike D'Angelo As compiled by Jawaad `Daredevil' Mahmood - Out of Character - `The Master Ace' Mike D'Angelo is probably the best `new' wrestler in the NICW, with the possible exception of `Downtown' Doug Sullivan. Controled by [name withheld], who has been one of the most enthusiastic controlers in the NICW, D'Angelo built up a believable personna, not invincible, but just a tough nut to crack. He was originally created, because I was complaining of the lack of Faces in
the NICW. I didn't have Hightower, or any other such High-level face in the fed (except for Sabin Figaro, who is another story). I needed a character like him, and when Lawrence came through with such a good character, with proper point allocations all throughout the board, gold was right around the corner. However, now, with all the faces I have,
D'Angelo might end up on the short end of the stick once Wrestlefest rolls around.. Or will he? All that I can say is that I've got the match down pat. And you all will like it,
- In Character - <The scene opens to the NICW control center, where the generally show PPV previews, etc.. John Parker is in the middle, and in the background Videowall, it shows Mike D'Angelo. The last few strings of `Enter the Sandman' play, and Parker begins> Parker - Hello NICW Wrestling fans, thank you for purchasing the BEST of Mike D'Angelo! I promise, this will show ALL of the Master Ace's matches, and since they were all fought by the same man, D'Angelo, I can also guarantee their content, 100% sweat, blood and tears! Before we get to the matches themselves, I had a heart-to-heart talk with Mike, while he was at home, in California. Let's go to the interview
---------------------------- <Scene focus' on the videowall, and all the `lines' in the image disapear. D'Angelo is in the middle, John Parker is on the side. Both of them are in comfortable sofas, in Mike's Piedmont residence>
Parker - Mike, thank you for doing this interview... Angelo - My pleasure John, I want the fans of the NICW to know as much about me as the want to..!
Parker - Let's start off by your childhood.. Where were you born? What did you do back then?
Angelo - I was born and raised in Piedmont, California. I used to watch wrestling on television, and really respected the real tough, rugged types. You know, Curt Henning, Ric Rude, Ric Flair, Magnum T.A. They were all great.
So at a young age, maybe eleven or twelve, I began to get in shape. You know, traditional stuff. Pull ups on a tree branch. Push ups. Sit ups.
Stomach Crunches. I lifted free weights and jogged a lot. Back then I did a lot of little league and eventually went on to play a little football.
Sports have always been in my life. Parker - That was your early childhood, but what about when you were a teenager? Did you keep your flair for the sports?
Angelo - Let me tell ya a story. When I entered high school, I tried out for the wrestling team....and didn't make it. That was a key juncture in my life.
I mean, I was crushed. Ready to quit and just stick with academics. Then, you know, something happened that changed my life. I was at a local match, and saw a man with just stubbs for legs pin this 160 pound guy. I mean, he grappled him with AWESOME SKILLS!
<pauses for a second> So, my sophmore year, I gave up on Football, the Swimteam, and Baseball. I dedicated myself to wrestling and made the team. I model my wrestling style even to this day after that courageous, disadvantaged wrestler. I focus on a man's body parts, and tear it apart. Or I mat grapple him till he doesn't know up from down. Nothing fancy. I don't show boat. Just work ethics.
Parker - Interesting.. You say that you concentrated on sports, but what did you do in school?
Angelo - I wrestled in college, won a couple trophies and awards. But even after I earned my 2 degrees in Literature and Business, I knew I was bound for wrestling. I love the sport. The whole science of it!
Parker - A science..??? Angelo - (Laughs, then pauses to think) Yes, wrestling is a science. Any 400 pound idiot can come off the street and fight. But a professional wrestler can manipulate the ring. Force an opponent to do things he doesn't want to do. Yeah, the science of wrestling can be mastered. And you are talking to the master in the NICW.
Parker - Mike, what influenced you the most in your life..? Angelo - I would say first and foremost God. He deserves all praises. I would also say the fans are rather influential. They appreciate hard working, tough guys who don't cry or whine because they aren't getting the push they want.
Shit, be a man. Go out and wrestle the best you can. As I always say, I Refuse to Lose. Irreguardless of whether I have money in my pocket or not, I go out every night to prove that I'm the best! Parker - Mike, who did you really learn from the most in wrestling..? Angelo - Actually, Bad Boy Mark Bagwell was the first e-wrestler I REALLY observed. I didn't like how he handled himself, but he could really wrestle. I mean, the Bad Crab is really something. I can tell ya that this is my first MAJOR fed, and I believe the NICW is the best one there is! Bar none!
Parker - How about friends inside and outside the ring? I doubt that Bagwell would be one of them <Smiles> Angelo - Sabin Figaro has proven to be a true friend. Ever sice he and I wrestled in two time limit draws, I've earned a HELL OF A LOT of respect for the man. You want to talk about a legend in the ring, he's done it all. Our styles exemplify the great dichotomy in professional wrestling. (Laughs)
<pauses for a second> Dichotomy means a polar differnce between 2 things. He's pretty much a big brawler, and I'm supposed to be a little technical wrestler. 255 pounds isn't little in any weight room. It all depends what your packing behind your weight. (Laughs again, thinking about those matches against Sabin Figaro.) Sabin should be World
Champion right now. If I were champ, I'd give him a title shot tommorrow. I'm not the kind of guy who hides behind anything.
There should never be any dispute over who is the best in a give promotion. Sabin arguably is the most renowned and greatest wrestler in the NICW. A true legend. He's also a great guy. We've talked things over in the locker room, and he's helped out a lot in terms of my development as a professional. The Returners in general are great guys. Great athletes.
Parker - Okay, let's get to the recent history... Who did you wrestle in your first NICW match..?
Angelo - First man I wrestled was the Tax Collector. Yup, it was February 2nd that I beat that big dummy. You know, I think I've single handedly beaten the entire Billion Dollar Team. (Big laugh as he calms himself down and drinks some water.....) Monster Heels, yeah right! I can at least respect Falcon for his wrestling ability. My own personal opinion is that the big man with no skills has no business in our 'SPORT'. Tax
Collector and anybody else who can't discern an Indian Death Lock from a Figure Four Leg Lock should go join the circus. Skills get you over in wrestling. Nothing else! (Becomes rather reflective, then continues....) Mr. Tax Collector.....Yeah, I remember my first match real well! His fat a$$ tossed me around the ring for a long while!
That's back when I was as green as a head of lettuce. But guess what? I am a wrestler. Always have been, always will be. All I have to do is WIN. I don't want to really beat anyone up. Wrestling is all about wins/losses and who's the best! And it don't get no better than the ACE!
Parker - That is for sure... Mike, what do you think was your BEST match in your life? Angelo - My Submission Lumberjack match is my favorite match ever. I took Falcon to school. I beat him. I was then referred to as the
Rookie Sensation. Now you know why. You know, Falcon says I've never pinned him. Very true. But the difference between Falcon and myself is twofold. First, no man has ever cleanly pinned "Masta Ace" Mike D'Angelo. And second, every victory I attain is definite. No one who falls to the CLAW can say they SHOULDA won their match with me. And I can slap that baby on any man, big or small. Young and old alike. <Pauses> Sure, Falcon has given me my only loss in professional wrestling. If you go back to that match, you'll know why. If he hadn't cheated, I would have made the greates upset in wrestling history. That scares an old man like Falcon. That's why he loathes the idea of an Iron Man match with 'Masta ACE'.
Parker - Speaking about `great matches', here's a great moment, you winning the UWC, in Bagdad Iraq..! What did you think of that Mike, as you think of it now?
Angelo - That was the greatest night in my life. I was on nation wide on pay per view! I mean, I trained so hard for that match. It's the kind of training
I'm doing now for the World Title match against Falcon, or whomever will be the World's Champ. Falcon, bird of prey, get ready for the ACE!
Parker - We are almost out of time.. Any closing thoughts? You have that upcoming Ironman Match against Falcon at Wrestlefest, and he promises it will be beyond what has happened in any other league so far!
What do you plan to do? Angelo - I plan on winning my first title. The NICW World Heavyweight Championship. I'm sure it'll be Falcon who I wrestle, just because he's got ways of retaining the belt. You see, I know Falcon. I've taken him to the limits. An Iron Man match? Every match I wrestle is an Iron Man match. 30 minutes against the Man Who Refuses to Lose is gonna be hell for you Falcon.
<pauses for a second or two> Wrestlefest will be one of the greatest cards in history, and I intend on wrestling the greatest match in wrestling history. Falcon, adjust the straps on that gold belt!
Because it's gonna fit just right around my waist! Falcon, you've been warned!!!!!!! Parker - Mike, thanks for taking the time to give this interview, I'm sure the folks at home are impressed!
