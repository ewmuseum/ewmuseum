NICW Returns: Call for Handlers

1 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all, for those of you who don't know me (probably most of you), let me introduce myself. My name is Mason Becker. You may know me as the handler of the Killing Machines. But now myself and "Midnight"
Jawaad Mahmood have decided to revive one of the truly great e-feds of the past, New Internet Championship Wrestling. NICW represented tradition, not gratuitous violence or bloated cards. And this is what we intend to bring back to e-wrestling...down to earth traditional wrestling on a weekly basis. While we can't promise that NICW is going to be "the best ever", we can say that we'll try our best to make this an enjoyable original wrestler e-fed for all involved. Myelf and Jawaad have more than a couple years of e-wrestling experience between us, and we feel confident that with support from you the handlers, NICW can approach the excellence it once stood for.
We need handlers ('cause you can't have a fed without wrestlers). We would also GLADLY accept anyone who is willing to help write matches.
