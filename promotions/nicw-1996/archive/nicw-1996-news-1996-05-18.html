New Internet Championship Wrestling — News

"[E-Wresling INFO] NICW"

18 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi all, here is the latest from the NICW! Comings and Goings -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Junaid `Jackknife' Mannan has been resigned to the NICW, but it is unclear as to his role in the league. Shawn Riser has been given a try-out contract. Goldshaw has been rejected by the NICW The American Dreams, The Royal Knights and several other teams are leaving the NICW for a short time Cardiac has rejoined the NICW, but only in a managerial position Guardian and Vindicator (Alpha Flight) are leaving the NICW, due to time constraints. Falcon will probably leave the NICW after Wrestlefest, to enter the ICW. News -=-= Look for Doug Sullivan to become the next NICW champion, if Mike D'Angelo is not `over' with the E-Wrestler handlers in the NICW. King Curtis is suffering from an incurable cancer. He might have to leave. Copperdust has a thing or two in common with Tom Ace, if you believe him.. Dark Master, the T.C. champion, is going through a major change at NICW Wrestlefest, to go with a more violent gimmick, and a less `invincible' gimmick. The Last Prophet is not interested in the NICW title anymore, he is more interested in defeating Dark Master by DQ or countout, because of personal reasons Upcoming Cards Monday - NICW WRESTLEFEST Main Event - 30 minute Ironman Match - NICW World Title match Falcon vs. Mike D'Angelo
