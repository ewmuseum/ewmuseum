IWF News Letter (10/12/99)

10 December 1999

Hello and Welcome to the first IWF Newsletter. This weeks action saw the IWF superstars making their debuts on both the
Monday Night Scream and the Thursday Night Fight cards, as well as two comptitions to decide the 2 contenders for the Vacant IC title, which will be decided at 'The Gathering' on the 26th of December.
For a full look at all of the results go to http://www.wrestlinguk.co.uk and have a look in the 'Events' section. Please also have a look around our website http://www.wrestlinguk.co.uk and pass on any comments or suggestions you have. ============================== Brief Results. Monday Night Scream (6/12/99) Ares defeated J. A. Broni by pin Beast Sanders defeated N. D. Punk via submission Goldberg defeated B. F. Nobody via pin. Tournament Matches - To decide the No 1 slot for the Vacant IC title. Goldberg Defeated Ares via Pin The British Bulldog defeated Beast Sanders via pin. Tournament Final Goldberg defeated The British Bulldog by pin. For full results go to http://www.wrestlinguk.co.uk click on 'Events' and Monday Night Scream. =============================== Brief Results. Thursday Night Fight (9/12/99) Goldberg defeated Ares via pin. Chris Jericho defeated N.D. Punk via submission The Undertaker defeated B. F. Nobody by pin Beast Sanders defeated the British Bulldog via submission The Rock defeated J. A. Broni via pin. Over the Top Battle Royal, with the last 2 competitors facing each other in a ladder match for the 2nd IC championship slot.
B. F. Nobody was eliminated by the Undertaker The Undertaker was eliminated by The British Bulldog Ares was eliminated by HHH The British Bulldog was eliminated by N. D. Punk N. D. Punk was eliminated by HHH HHH was eliminated by The Rock J. A. Broni was eliminated by the Rock The Rock was eliminated by Chris Jericho Y2J Vs Beast Sanders in a ladder match with the papers for the 2nd Championship slot hanging over the ring. Beast Sanders go to the top stair, but the case was raised into the ceiling by an unknown man.
Go to http://www.wrestlinguk.co.uk click on 'Events' and Thursday night fight for more details. ================================ IWF newsletter interview with .... IWF Owner John Walsh [Mathew Campbell is in the ring holding a microphone] Mathew Campbell - Ladies and Gentlemen, could you please put your hands together for the man who has made tonights entertainment possible JOHN WALSH
[The English National Anthem plays as John Walsh walks down to the ring. The
Crowd are cheering and shouting as he climbs into the ring]
Mathew Campbell - Thanks for coming here tonight Mr Walsh, and for talking to us now John Walsh - That's no problem Mat, to be honest my ass was getting a little sore sitting in the commentary spot!
Mathew Campbell - Errrrrrrmmm. OK. Let me ask you this, what do you hope to achieve with the IWF?
John Walsh - That's simple. I wanted to create a Federation that rewards hard work and commitment with titles, rather than a standard fed that awards the gold to friends and ass kissers! I've been around and I've seen a few of both types of fed, and as far as I'm concerned ones like the IWF are always going to be a lot more popular!
[The Crowd Roar their approval] Mathew Campbell - How do hope to make the IWF the No 1 Federation in this sport? John Walsh - I already have some very talented stars here, and I hope that with the help of my front office we can create more stars everyday! We are here to help wrestlers with angles and their mic work, and we can also help them with their abilities in the ring. The harder they work, the more they will receive. When you add all of that up, you get a fantastic federation.
Mathew Campbell - How do you see the future of the IWF working out? John Walsh - Well that depends on the kind of athletes we get, we have a limited roster at the moment but we are always looking for new talent. Our scouts go around daily to see if they can find the kind of people we are looking for, people with raw talent, people who have a wrestling brain, but don't know how to use it, and finely honed athletes who can enter straight into the race.
Mathew Campbell - OK, we'll try to keep this short and allow the matches to continue, so do you have any other comments?
John Walsh - Only that if people want to join the IWF, then they should check out our website at
http://www.wrestlinguk.co.uk and see what vacancies we have at the moment. ======================================= Roster Additions this week.
