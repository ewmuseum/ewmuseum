"NGEN World/IC Tournament First Round Results from Augusta, Maine!"

28 June 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here are the results of the TV-Taping of the World-IC Tournament from Augusta, Maine.... NGEN's first stop on its ten stop captial tour!!! ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - HIDEOUS ZUKI VS "THE AMBUSHER" MIKE TRENT - The Hideous Zuki, a heavy favorite in the tournament, got DQed when he was caught by the ref blowing dust into the eyes of Mike Trent. The Hideous Zuki looked good early but Mike Trent took Hideous Zuki out of his match and made it into a street fight and got Hideous Zuki to lose his cool costing him the match. ******************************************************** FIRST ROUND - EL SANTANICO vs RAZOR - A devious plan by El Santanico took Razor out of the tourney early. Big Evil distracted the ref and blew fire into Razor's eyes... then he powerbombed him through the announcer's table. El Santanico rolled in and the ref turned around and counted a ten count on Razor. ********************************************************* FIRST ROUND - SLEEPWALKER vs GODZILLA - Sleepwalker SUPRISED the monster Godzilla with a quick three! Godzilla was giving Sleepwalker the beating of his life and had just setup his Godzilla bomb with his devastating powerslam. Godzilla picked him up and Sleepwalker nailed him with a quick spinning kick out of no where to stun him enough to give him enough time to go to the top rope and deliver the sunset flip to get the 1---2---3! ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - STEEL CITY WARRIOR vs BEAST - The three time former YWF Champion, Beast gave Steel City Warrior a first round beating. Steel City Warrior had the clear size advantage but Beast was vicious and had a look in his eye of a champion. Beast controlled this match totally and got Steel City Warrior to submit to the figure-four leg! ********************************************************* FIRST ROUND - DETHLOK THE ASSASSIN vs EL BRUJO - El Brujo fought his heart out but even that was not enough. Dethlok at times looked beyond human kicking out after two top rope tombstones. He refused to be pinned.... He He made his comeback and nailed El Brujo with several DABP before he decided to finish him off with the Demolisher (The Top Rope Brainbuster) and get the pin! ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - EARL OF DOOM vs "GLADIATOR" GREGG ANDERSON - Earl of Doom made his NGEN return and took a battle with the Gladiator! He won it after several minutes out on the floor by countout after he nailed the Gladiator with a DOOMSDAY piledriver into the concrete floor around ringside. The place exploded after the count. The Commisioner Chris Wink came out and announced the EOD will advance to the third round due to an odd number of wrestlers in the tourney!! ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - The SUCIDE SLAMMER vs BIG BALLS BELLO - Big Balls Bello became outraged when the Sucide Slammer started chasing and making fun of the Fat Hermaphodite Nicole and nailed the Sucide Slammer from behind with a chair!! The ref called for the DQ but the battle did not end there. They started to fight into the audience and a heel crowd started chanting the Sucide Slammer. Police came in and they we finally seperated. ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - MR. HARDCORE vs THE STAMPEDER - The Tease pressed her luck and got caught THE SECOND TIME after she nailed the Stampeder with the barbed wire baseball bat! The Stampeder got hit early in the match when The Tease distracted the ref long enough for Mr. Hardcore to knock the Stampeder down...and out to the floor with the barbed wire baseball bat. Mr. Hardcore took it to the Stampeder on the floor and took him back inside for the pin. Some how the Stampeder kicked out and launched a counter attack. The ref became distracted by a Mr. Hardcore fan and centered his attention on him. The Stampeder bounced off the ropes and The Tease took a major league swing at him. The ref however caught it from the side of his eye and called for the DQ. Mr. Hardcore didn't stop though. He started using the bat to make the Stampeder's back all bloody. Some jobbers came down and pulled the Stampeder out of the ring before he was hurt further. ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - "BEAUTIFUL" BRENT FOLEY vs OL' DIRTY BASTARD - "Beautiful" Brent Foley beat the Ol' Dirty Bastard by pinfall after he nailed him with a Bootysault! The Ol' Dirty Bastard tried to cheat with every chance he had but "Beautiful" Brent Foley survived countless times. Ol' Dirty Bastard had deleivered a couple of hard bumps to "Beautfiul" Brent Foley. After Brent Foley pinned Ol' Dirty Bastard he recieved the one of the biggest pop's of the night, only second to EOD. ******************************************************* FIRST ROUND - EXECUTIONER vs NINJA BLADE - The Executioner pinned Ninja Blade after deleivering a Deathbow Powerbomb! The Alabama Stud only got envolved when it looked like his man was in trouble to give the Executioner a breather. This ruined Ninja Blade's moment several times and got Ninja Blade angry. In fact, It was the Alabama Stud's yelling that distracted Ninja Blade and gave the Executioner an extra second to move out of the way of the top rope legdrop. From there, the Executioner took the match and terminated Ninja Blade's chances. ******************************************************* NEBULOUS GETS THE BYE TO THE SECOND ROUND!!!! ******************************************************* FIRST INTERMISSION - Second round coming up later!!!!!
