A Louisiana-based Usenet booking promotion, revived at least twice after closing in the late 1990s, that crowned new champions on its 2004 comeback broadcast in Bossier City before its documented run goes quiet again in early 2007. Seven title lineages, 24 shows and 119 wrestlers survive. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Kick Ass Wrestling Federation dated itself back to 1996 in its own 2004 recruitment pitch: “The KAWF started back in 1996 and ran till about 1999 before problems started to occur in scheduling... Slowly the federation shut its doors in late 99 and opened them back up in 2001... this will be the third time.” It billed its main offices as New Orleans, Louisiana, while touring “throughout North America” and claiming shows as far afield as Japan and London.

Its in-fiction ownership belonged to Mike “The Always Right” Johnson, a 49-year-old face-playing owner determined to spend “ALL” his money making the third relaunch work. He inherited a Vice-President he hadn't chosen: his own son's wife, Kristen Roberts, whose vote had helped close the promotion down the previous time and whose contract kept her in the role over his objections.

That relaunch ran through 2004 and into 2005 on a weekly Saturday Night Fever broadcast and a biweekly War Stories, staging house shows across Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas and building toward a September 2004 supercard, Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash, out of Miami.

It returned again in mid-2006 under a new on-air owner, Mike Pace, running a lighter schedule its own operator pitched as aiming for “more of an ROH feel” than the promotion's earlier “WWE feel.” The Global Championship was discontinued in this run and two new titles, a Women's Championship and the Mason Dixon Championship, were introduced.

Its last surviving document, 10 January 2007, reports fresh champions crowned at a supercard in Destin, Florida — Lazz Sterling's win of the Promotional Title among them — and previews a further supercard already booked for Little Rock, Arkansas that April. No closure notice follows.
