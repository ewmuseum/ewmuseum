KAWF — Special Press Conference

KAWF Special Press Conference

2 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WE INTERRUPT YOUR ORIGINALLY SCHEDULED PROGRAM TO BRING YOU THIS OH SO SPECIAL KAWF PRESS CONFERENCE Well special enough to interrupt the Golden Girls. Thank us later if you l ike. You have probally been watching the show so long Sophia was looking hot. [Don't even act like you don't think she's F'n hot] [New Orleans, Louisiana.] [The camera fades into a large room decked out with chairs, and a refreshment table the size of LezKanic's arm [Which is big by the way] in the back of the room filled with goodies galore. At center stage in the room is a podium where KAWF President Mike Johnson is seen talking. Mike as usual is decked out in a nice Armansi suit, and is being as professional as possible infront of the "hawks" which are the media of New Orleans. We join him already in progress.] Mike Johnson : I am guaranteeing that my Birthday Bash on the 19th of September will be one of the biggest events of the year. We have ladders lined up, an Ironman match, and so much more with our solid list of wrestlers. Plus there will be quite a few suprises, were going all out for this event. I have been known to be a bit of a tight wad when it comes to spending cash, but for one night, and one night only the KAWF will seem like the IGA as far as how they throw money around. Don't worry however, the talent will still be KAWF level, and not anywhere near the garbage IGA puts out on a weekly basis. [Mike pauses, cracking a smile as one of the media people step in.] Media Man : You said earlier that this IGA vs KAWF event has the makings of a huge supercard event. If IGA is such garbage, then why will this be such an event, and why should we all watch it next week ? Mike Johnson : Well, my problem with the IGA is with there management, and not there wrestlers. There shows are garbage because of the brass, not cause of the peasents. Jazz Madison is what ruins the IGA on a weekly basis, nobody else. I think they have great talent on there roster. Guys like Tommy Danger, Buddy Drifter, Cyril Lesley, and Lazz Sterling just to name a few are top notch talents. I would love to have them on my roster, but Jazz does his best to ruin them on a weekly basis. He thinks spending all his money will win the crowd over, and deep down the crowd really just wants to see the wrestlers preform. Not the president trying to change things at every turn, for some kinda shock value pop. [A bit of buzz from the audience.] Mike Johnson : With that said however if you asked me which roster I would want to work with, I would pick the KAWF everytime. IGA is good, but the KAWF is _BETTER_ from what I can tell. I guess we will see next Thursday though for sure. I might be wrong, who knows. It just seems that the guys we have on our roster only come along once a lifetime, and I have been lucky enough to get them. Media Woman : Speaking of next Thursday, why did the event get moved back from this coming Sunday till then ? Mike Johnson : Well, the IGA has a PPV coming up, and there wrestlers wouldn't be ready that short of a time period after an event such as Chance Encounters to take on the KAWF in a supercard. I understand that completly. I know what goes into a pay per view event.I just wish Jazz would of brought that up orginally instead of juggling my plans, but I don't expect anything less from Mr Madison. I have adjusted already. Media Man 2 : Can you give us a hint of some of the matches ? Mike Johnson : << Smiling >> IGA will be announcing the line up AFTER there pay per view, so I better not say, but I can tell you that both federations tag team titles will be on the line, Each World Title will be up for grabs, and there is rumors of a fatal four way match which will be one of the stiffest matches of the year bar none. KAWF Representiive : Mr Johnson, what about the rumors of you, and Kristen's backstage battles getting more heated as of late ? There is even some thought going around that you pushed her against the wall at the last Fever. Mike Johnson : I knew somebody would touch on that, figures it would be one of my own guys. << Pauses >> Kristen, and I don't see eye to eye very often. We both want the KAWF to be a bigtime federation once again, but we go about it in different ways. Our battles have got hot as of lately, and I am hoping SOMETHING happens very soon to put some distance between the two. I need to remind her I am the _PRESIDENT_ , and she is the _VICE PRESIDENT_ of the KAWF. That's how the chain of command works. I am not going to let her put rubbish on my show very often, and get away with it. I plan on dropping some kinda hammer down on her very soon. We are wrestling first, sports entertainment second. Not vice versa. I have Hayden Grayson, Johnny Sledge, Boys Next Door, and the True All Stars on my roster for a reason. Media Man 3 : Is it true KAWF will be doing an east coast swing after your Birthday ? Mike Johnson : As of now, yes. Things may change, but it looks almost set in stone. Media Man 3 : What about the rumors of a Japan house show in November ? Mike Johnson : Also again true, for now. I plan on setting up a special KAWF match for the Japanese crowd when were over there. Media Woman 2 : Why has the KAWF been so active in non KAWF events ? Such as J*Star, Sky's the limit, Wicked IV, Super 8, IGA, Rumble Pro Invitational, Revelations and rumors of the upcoming Chicago Cup. Doesn't that take away from what you are trying to do when the KAWF airs on Saturdays ? Mike Johnson : I don't think so. The more people that can see the KAWF the better. I do this to help out other federations, and to spotlight my own. It works for both sides really. We bring a _KICK ASS_ no pun intended match to the table for there fans, and then we also win us some new fans in the process. I like to be an active member of the wrestling community. I know I might be cheap when it comes to spending expenses sometimes, but I do my best to pay my guys enough they will want to work as much as possible for the cash, love, and desire which comes with this sport. I am excited about the J*Star, and Revelations. I think we have a good chance in both to be honest with you all. I also am looking into the Chicago Cup as an oppurtunity to let some of the Northern states see us, The south is our base of operation, and always will be, but I don't want to cheat the rest of the country ethier. They deserve to see the KAWF, and the best in the sport as well. They can't help there Yankees. [Light laughter.] Mike Johnson : I have to be on my way to Atlanta now for the upcoming Fever, which might I add should be a very special show with two titles on the line, and some suprises. If you all have any more questions feel free to ask Mr Cunningham who will now be fielding questions up until dinner time. I thank you all for coming, and hope I said something which sparked your intrest this evening. [Mike nods to the crowd, and then back peddles allowing Russell Cunningham to step up. The two shake hands, and then Mike walks off stage to the right leaving Cunningham alone at center stage.] Russell Cunningham : Are there any more questions for the KAWF today gentleman ? [A bunch of hands raise, waiting to be called on by the KAWF announcer.] Russell Cunningham : I see, well let's start . .. [Russell points one of the media people out to begin his portion of the conference, but the camera we are viewing from pans behind the curtains to a view of smiling Mike Johnson standing infront of KAWF commish Shane Martin, and KAWF bodyguard the legendary Outlaw.] Mike Johnson : I'm excited about this guys. I hope KAWF pounds the IGA next Thursday. Shane Martin : Me, and Outlaw aren't wrestling for IGA in that event we should be fine. Outlaw : I would of jobbed, I should ot put my name in the hat. Mike Johnson : Ha ha . .I want to do it the right way Outlaw. The _KAWF_ way. Were not gonna stoop to there level, and still come out on top. Shane Martin : Now if we can just get VVV, and Kristen out of our hair everything would be fine. Mike Johnson : I got plans for both of those people. Let's get out of here, and I'll fill you in on the way to the airport. Shane Martin : Works for me. Outlaw : Can we stop and get some beer ? [Mike looks at Outlaw who just responds with a simple innocent shrug, and the camera slowly pans out with a KAWF logo popping back up on the screen, before we return to the Golden Girls.] [Fade.]
