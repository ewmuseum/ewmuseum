I don't have the emotional attachment to KAWF you do. I'm sorry... I don't. I have all the respect in the world for what Mike Johnson's done, and I'm sure that, over time, I'll defend it as fiercely as yourself. I'm not here to be the shining knight for KAWF and bring back its rightful name... for two reasons. [Turning away from the mirror, Sabby's icy blue eyes meet the camera lens.] Sabbath: Number one, it's not my place. You'd be a far better candidate... or Hayden Grayson... or even Outlaw. I'm the new guy. For me to step up and retake KAWF's image would be overstepping my bounds. [Holding up his free hand to indicate "two," Sabby adds.] Sabbath: Number two, while I understand that the "Suck Ass" name is an affront to the KAWF faithful, it comes down to the fact that the spirit of the KAWF, and thus Mike Johnson, is still alive and kicking. To the fans, this will *always* be KAWF. "A rose by any other name..." and all that. [Lowering his hand to his side, Sabby sits on the California king bed in the center of the ring, moving aside a twisted bunch of blankets.] Sabbath: Or are you asking where I stand in regards to one Vile "Vince" Viper? [A sigh, and another pull on his mug.] Sabbath: I'd have hoped this would be answered by now. I'm not Vile's baby- sitter, Johnny, as much as you'd like me to be. He's a grown man, and he's going to make his own decisions whether I agree with them or not. All I can do is stop him from going over the line. But you have to understand: Vile is a stubborn old bastard, and won't let anyone get in his way when he sets his mind to something, even me. Such is the nature of the business, Johnny... I'd hope a veteran like yourself would be able to appreciate that. [The raven haired man sits quiet for some time, pondering his next thought as he looks out towards the French doors.] Sabbath: "Where do I stand?" [And another, much briefer, pause.] Sabbath: I also said that was a very simple question... and the simple answer, regardless of the details is that I stand where I've always stood: [Turning his gaze back to the camera, he adds...] Sabbath: I stand in my own corner. I'm my own man, and I don't need Vile to make my decisions for me. I stand by the fans I've devoted my life to entertaining. And lastly... [...Sabby's eyes harden, as he pushes himself off the bed, rising to his full height.] Sabbath: ...I stand by my word. I've asked you to trust me on that. You gave your terms, and I stood by those. You're still unconvinced. Tell me, Johnny .... what can I do? Ball's in your court, chief. [And one last final sip of the mug, as Sabby sets it atop an ornately carved beureau.] Sabbath: It's up to you to do decide what to do with it. See ya on Saturday, Johnny. [The plain white walls and the cold linoleum of this hospital room are very unsettling. There sits Johnny Sledge with his left arm elevated on an aliminum table top. There is a fresh cast covering the broken wrist and Sledge looks a little less than thrilled about it.] Johnny Sledge: Viper... Do you see my left hand??? Do you see that I was willing to break my own wrist to get my hands on you??? Are you beginning to understand that there is nothing on this planet that will keep me from reaching that goal??? [Sledge looks down at the cast, and lets out a small laugh.] Johnny Sledge: This is not the first time I have put my own welfare on the line to get my hands on a person. Wearing this cast is just a small price to pay for seeing the look on your face when I broke free from those handcuffs. It was like you had almost seen a ghost. Viper, you are looking at a man who has no fear. You might have thought up until last Saturday night that you had faced the toughest men in the sport... Well, that is until you stood toe-to-toe with me at Fever. Since day one, you have manipulated everybody around you to get things you do not deserve to have. You know have the number one contender slot for the KAWF PROMOTIONAL TITLE. Something I earned by giving it my all against every man I stepped in the ring with. When people stand in awe of LezKanic, I stare him in the eye and tell him to bring his best. When people think that Tommy Flames is going to handle a person who just came out of retirement, I stood my ground and made many a person eat crow. I was looking forward to the challenge of facing Kai Lewis in a great match to see who is the better man... [Sledge pauses as he takes a deep breath, trying to calm himself down.] Johnny Sledge: That all changed, Viper, when you thought it was funny to mess with the honor of the KAWF. You then went and stole my number one contender's slot. You know what is funny, Viper, I never really liked you... and now I have a reason to want to rip your arms off and beat you to death with them. You and Sabbath love to wax poetic about the bloody wars the two of you have fought. Well, you are not the only one with a past like that. If you name it, Viper, I have done it. Barbed Wire... Razor Wire... Scaffolds... Ladders... Bullropes... Dog Collars... Viper, I can get just as violent as anybody else here in the KAWF. I am not affraid to lose a little blood to prove a point. Not in a long time, Viper, have I truly despised a person the way I do you. This cast is a symbol of my hatred toward you. Every time I look at this, I remember seeing you cheat at every chance you got, and how badly I needed to break away from those handcuffs. I remember hearing the snap. I remember feeling the blood rush to my wrist as it began to swell. But... like I already said, it was all worth it to see the look on your face when you thought there was no chance in hell I was going to get into that ring with you. All the pain I felt after hitting you with that left hand was well worth it. [Sledge takes another look at the cast.] Johnny Sledge: Viper... I consider this a present from you. And what a present it is. For the next six to eight weeks I will remember that look on your face, and I will cherish the moment when I finally get you in the ring one on one. At that point, Viper, I will return the gift to you ten-fold. [Sledge stops and clears his thoughts before going on.] Johnny Sledge: Now I know I have a match with Sabbath on the Special Christmas Edition of Saturday Night Fever. But before I talk about that I want to personally apologize to Garrett Jax. Jax, I am sorry about what happened at War Stories. You got stuck in a horrible situation and there was no good that could have come from that. I should have acted with a cooler head, but I did not, and it cost us the match... and for that I am sorry. Let me tell you this, Garrett, you are a man of your word and I respect that. So any time you need somebody to back you up, just give me a ring and I'll be there to back you up. [Sledge turns, again, to the cast. This time he taps it a couple times to make sure it is dry. He then stretches his left arm a bit. He continues speaking as he puts has casted hand back on the table.] Johnny Sledge: So on Saturday night while all the little kids will be playing with the gifts that Santa brought them, I will be getting in the ring with Sabbath. A man, who I would need to be at two hundred percent to be able to fight... let alone the fact that I am going to be getting in that ring with a broken left wrist. Sabbath, I am still not sure where you stand. You continue to try and play the peacemaker. I can see that you would like to me to see Viper the same way you do. I hate to break it to you Sabbath, but that is never going to happen. On Saturday, at Fever, I am going to treat you like any other opponent I step into the ring with. Make this a challenge for me... Sabbath... Bring your A game... Because win, lose, or draw... I am not going to use excuses... And I hope you will not either.
