KAWF — Awards Presentation

KAWF Awards Presentation

21 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[New Orleans, Louisiana.] [The camera fades in on a filled room with some of the best names in professional wrestling setting all across the room in there Sunday's best, along with some of the most devoted fans of the business directly behind them. Each section of the lower level of the room has tables setting on it, all of which our decorated nicely. The back portion of the lower level of the room has some single chairs meant for the most diehard fans, and the upper level of the building has more tables set up, also decorated nicely, but for the fans, not the wrestlers.] Voice : Welcome to New Orleans people. Female Voice : It's good to have you all here tonight, it really is. [The camera begins to pan to the front part of the room where a stage has been set up. As all eyes turn towards the voices, we now see Frankie Tha Pimp, in a dark purple suit, and the lovely White Rose, who is wearing a long silver dress that screams "I look to good, don't even bother asking me dude" to any man within eye range standing on the stage behind a podium. Behind the two you can see yet another table, which is set up with some awards. The _KAWF_ awards for the year to be exact which our gold plated, and giving off a very well polished glow.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Yea it's good to have you all here tonight. She's the lovely White Rose, and I'm the lovely Frankie Tha Pimp. White Rose : Lovely ? Frankie Tha Pimp : Yea. There's ladies here tonight to Rose. White Rose : But lovely ? Frankie Tha Pimp : Oh _SHAD UP_ White Rose : I know most of you our used to only seeing me on pay per views, but it's been a few months, and I had this itch. Frankie Tha Pimp : Better get that checked out. I've had an itch before to. White Rose : <<Ignoring Frankie>> I had this itch to get back infront of the camera, and again express my love for this company. Whether it's called the KAWF, or the SAWF it's the best federation in the business. [The crowd applauds, and as soon as they finish Frankie chimes back in.] Frankie Tha Pimp : We have Eight awards to hand out tonight, and I know alot of our roster is somewhat new, and might fill a bit left out by not getting anything, but don't worry. Next year these could be yours. White Rose : IF . .. .And only _IF_ you do what these men did over the course of 2004, which is give it 100 percent. If your sick, if your injured, if your the under dog, the favorite, or having family troubles. You STILL have to give it 100 percent at all times to be something in the KAWF. The competition is that good in this federation. A federation filled with wrestling vets, and upcoming superstars. A federation with the BEST mix of both in the wrestling business. Frankie Tha Pimp : Or if waiting isn't one of your strong points, just be quiet, and look at Rose. Damnnnnnn... White Rose : I did not come here to get harassed. Frankie Tha Pimp : But your gonna be anyway. White Rose : And on that note our first award tonight is for the _MOST UNDER RATED_ wrestler of the year. Frankie Tha Pimp : For the people who our damn good, but don't get the props of being damn good all the time. White Rose : Exactly. Us that follow the KAWF closely know that they our awseome talents, but not the average person. The casual wrestling fan, or the guy who just flicks on Fever for the final 30 minutes of the show. Frankie Tha Pimp : This awards for you. White Rose : This award is for you Mr .. .TOMMY FLAMES !!!!!!!!! [The crowd mostly applauds, with a few scattered boo's as the young Texan wearing a dark Armani outfit begins to walk towards the stage, slicking his long hair back on the way. As he walks the two host shout off some of his acomplishments for 04.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Tommy was the man in 2004, and it's ashame people didn't notice it more until now. He went from being goodie goodie that got him close to the Promotional title, but not quite there, to being the cocky talent he deserves to be each, and every week. White Rose : Tommy also acquired the talents of Ronnie Jamieson this year to guide him in that new cocky direction, while also making his finisher, the Flames Kick one talked about in the back by the boys each week after some poor soul in the ring got his head knocked off. Frankie Tha Pimp : He also took Johnny Sledge to his limits at Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash, and will be your next cruserweight champion. White Rose : Possibly. Frankie Tha Pimp : _WILL BE_ your next Cruserweight Champion. [Rose reaches back onto the table behind the podium, as Tommy steps up onto the stage with the two host. Rose hands Flames the title, who gives her a hug, followed by quickly lowering his hand to her ass which she quickly moved his hand away from causing the hug to be released ackwardly. Tommy then shakes hands with Frankie, who is in shock.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Can I do that, and only get pushed away ? White Rose : I'll slap you. Frankie Tha Pimp : Life isn't fair. Tommy Flames : It sure isn't, and I can vouch for that. I should be the Promotional Champion right now, and the Cruserweight champion for that matter. Hell, I should be one half of the tag team champions to with my buddy Lezkanic, but Noooo .. .Instead here I am accepting an award for MOST UNDER RATED. [Tommy shakes his head.] Tommy Flames : I'll take the award. I'll keep the award. Hell I'll even hang it up, right beside the toilet paper in my bathroom. Thanks, thanks for nothing people. [Tommy pauses, then mumbles.] Tommy Flames : Most under rated .. How pathetic. [Tommy then storms off the stage, behind some curtains causing him to no longer be seen as the crowd boo's loudly, even some of the wrestlers can be heard. Guys like Sammy Griffin, Johnny Sledge, and Kai Lewis can be heard above the crowd being negative towards the upstart.] White Rose : Well then. Frankie Tha Pimp : He was insulted. Give him a break. White Rose : I suppose. Frankie Tha Pimp : Which one is next ? Announcer of the year ? White Rose : There isn't one for that this year. Frankie Tha Pimp : AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH. White Rose : However we do got one for _ROOKIE OF THE YEAR_. The best newcomer to not only the SAWF, but the business. Frankie Tha Pimp : No announcer of the year award ? White Rose : Not this year, maybe next. Frankie Tha Pimp : So who wins the damn rookie of the year award ? White Rose : None other than the new leader of the Con$ortium . .. KYLE SOMMERS !!!!!! [The crowd applauds, as Kyle Sommers decked out in a custom made suit from God knows where begins to walk towards the podium with his lovely girlfriend Jasmine Green following shortly behind him.] Frankie Tha Pimp : CON$ORTIUM BABY !!!!!!! [Jasmine giggles at Frankie's excitement, which even gets a smile from Mr Sommers. Rose then begins to spout off some of his acomplishments.] White Rose : If we didn't tell you he was new, you would never guess it. His in ring talents, and energy he brings to the microphone when he speaks has won over many crowds in his limited days. Frankie Tha Pimp : He can wrestle singles, or tag team. White Rose : While also keeping a beautiful girlfriend, and leading a stable which has been around for years. Frankie Tha Pimp : Not to mention he's one half of the current Tag Team Champions the Perfect Storm. White Rose : Which makes him a perfect fit for this year's Rookie of the Year. [Rose grabs another trophy, and hands it to Kyle as he gets to the stage. Kyle gives Rose a quick hug, not wanting to anger Jasmine, then shakes hands with Frankie.] Frankie Tha Pimp : CON$ORTIUM BABY !!!!!!!!! [Frankie steps back with his eyes as big as a kid in the candy store, as Kyle takes center stage along side his lovely valet.] Kyle Sommers : What can I say about this thing ? Thanks I guess is the right word. [Kyle licks his lips.] Kyle Sommers : It's gonna get better though. I'm gonna get better. I'm gonna get so much better that this Rookie thing is going to be questioned by so many people that your prolly gonna see investigators trailing me at every turn I make in this company. I'm gonna get that much better people. [Jasmine nudges Kyle.] Kyle Sommers : Were gonna get better. [Another nudge.] Kyle Sommers : Shit, what Jasmine ? Jasmine Greene : The _CON$ORTIUM_ is going to get better. [Kyle pauses, then manages to force a smile before speaking.] Kyle Sommers : Indeed . ..The Con$ortium is going to make more money, and beat more people up than ever before. [Kyle gives a final nod, and then grabs Jasmine by her hand with his free hand leading her off stage as the crowd applauds for the civilness of Mr Sommers, for the sake of the event.] White Rose : Two awards down .. . Six more to go. Frankie Tha Pimp : CON$ORTIUM !!!!!! White Rose : I'm not sure where Jarrod Storm, Tender Smith, and Mark Steiner our, but Kyle Sommers is definitely in the house representing the Con$ortium here tonight. Frankie Tha Pimp : Jarrod is hurt, as you should know, but I'm not sure about the other two. White Rose : Could be something to look into. Frankie Tha Pimp : Yea if we didn't have six more awards to give out before we can all get to eating, and drinking. I love New Orleans food. White Rose : As do we all, so I'll stay on track. Up next we have _MOST IMRPOVED_ wrestler of the year. Frankie Tha Pimp : So somebody who sucked last year, and don't now ? White Rose : Not nessecarily, it could of been somebody who was good, and is now great. Frankie Tha Pimp : Uh huh. White Rose : He is our current Promotional Champion, and the winner of this years MOST IMPROVED award. Here is Brooklyn's finest KAI LEWIS !!!!!!!!!! [The fans applaud, and showing his Promotional Title on his shoulder we see the well built African American frame of Kai A Lewis heading towards the stage.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Doesn't take much to improve yourself if your from Brooklyn. Hell moving about does it. White Rose : Stop it .. . Kai has always been considered a good mat wrestler, but last year Kai took it to that next level. He showed the world he has intensity, speed, and can go hardcore if need be. There's so much more to Mr Lewis than just mat wrestling. Frankie Tha Pimp : Lucky for him, or else he would of got beat up alot in the SAWF. White Rose : Recently Kai brought in some of his old wrestling buddys to help guide them into the pro ranks, and scored victorys over Karl Ferguson in a match of the year candidate, while also making Vile Vince Viper pass out in the middle of the ring. [Rose hands Lewis his award, who gives Rose a quick hug, followed by a hard handshake on Frankie whose eyes get big up until the exact point Lewis releases his hand.] Kai Lewis : I'm a man of action, and not words as most of you know so I'll make this quick. I appreciate the thing, but I'm gonna keep doing what I do. I'm gonna keep getting respect, and keep trying to get to be number one around here. [Kai raises the trophy up.] Kai Lewis : If I get better doing it then great. I try to get better everytime I step into the ring, and I thank yall for noticing it. [Kai pumps the trophy a few times getting the crowd a bit rowdy before walking behind the curtains.] Frankie Tha Pimp : I need some ice for my hand. White Rose : Your such a baby. Frankie Tha Pimp : But a gorgeous one remember ? White Rose : So you say. Frankie Tha Pimp : Oh _SHAD UP_ White Rose : Next we have manager of the year. Frankie Tha Pimp : No hype needed here Rose. Come on down Ronnie Jamieson. White Rose : Ronnie did indeed win the award, but voting was close. . ..Ronnie has his hands full now with people like Jasmine Greene, Faraji, Mr Davis, and more managing in the SAWF. Frankie Tha Pimp : Ronnie WOULD like to have his hands full with Jasmine, but when it comes to managing he's the best there is. Come get your award RONNIE JAMIESON !!! [The crowd again applauds, being cordial as Ronnie begins to walk up towards the stage, also wearing a dark purple suit like Frankie. Behind Ronnie you can see Lezkanic, wearing a sleeveless tux. Looking like a penguin on _roids_, but nobody would dare tell him such.] White Rose : Ronnie is currently leading men like Lezkanic, M.E.M, and Tommy Flames. Like him, or not those four men our definitely doing better with Ronnie than they would be doing without him. Frankie Tha Pimp : Damn right. White Rose : He is definitely a manager who will do whatever he can to get his boys where they need to be, and shows no signs of slowing down next year. Thus making him a likely candidate for this same award next year. Frankie Tha Pimp : Go ahead, and mark it down babe. [Ronnie is handed his award by Rose, but doesn't even bother with her going straight to a handshake with Frankie, and then to the microphone. Rose looks insulted, but only briefly as she changes it back to a smile as Kanic eyes her.] Ronnie Jamieson : I TOLD YOU ALL I WOULD WIN THIS AWARD. I TOLD YOU ALL. [Ronnie begins to laugh hysterically.] Ronnie Jamieson : It's true to. I am the best manager in the business. The best manager here. The best manager in IGA. The best manager _anywhere_. Come on Kanic, let's get out of here, and go celebrate with Tommy. It's fun being the _BEST_ [Kanic nods, following his runt of an agent off stage to a bit of jeers from the crowd. Even Hayden Grayson can be heard in the crowd, giving Ronnie a hard time.] White Rose : Interesting as usual. Frankie Tha Pimp : He speaks the truth. White Rose : So you both say. Frankie Tha Pimp : All four of his guys our gonna get gold next year. Two of them real soon. Tommy is gonna spank Sammy Griffin at first oppurtunity, and Lezkanic should get the title shot at Crazed Intentions which means . .. .NEW GLOBAL CHAMPION LEZKANIC !!!!!!! [Boo's.] White Rose : Were not sure if Lezkanic is even getting the shot, and our next award winner might have something to say about Flames winning the cruserweight title. Our next award is for best cruserweight, and the winner of that award is of course. . . .the current champion SAMMY GRIFFIN !!!!!!!!! [A bunch of hollaring, and extra loud claps our heard as Sammy Griffin stands up from a table where he, Garrett Jax, and Johnny Sledge our setting along with some nameless young ladies. Decked out in a lime green suit Sammy runs towards the stage, sliding up onto it with a slight leap, before he can get any praise from the host.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Woah . . . White Rose : Here you go Sammy. Congratulations. You impressed everybody in 2004 with your cruserweight tournament victory, keeping the title for months, and competing in various tournaments taking everybody to there best. [Sammy gently accepts the award from Rose, then gives her a big hug lifting her up. He then sets her down, and ignores Frankie going to the microphone.] Sammy Griffin : Yeppers. . .. I'm in charge of this division. [Applauds can be heard.] Sammy Griffin : I have seen the names come, and go. I have seen Tommy Flames, Maddy Skillz, Matthew Beecham, Tyler Rose, Peter Hilo, and more come at me. I'm sure I'll be seeing more in 05, but one thing will remain the same. I WILL BE ON TOP OF THIS DIVISION. [Sammy hollars out to the crowd with a yeeehaw getting a nice reaction, before adding in a final note.] Sammy Griffin : Oh yea .. . _BOYS NEXT DOOR WILL BE REGAINING THE KAWF GOLD THIS YEAR BAY - BEEEEEEEEEEEEEE_ [Sammy bobs his head like crazy to the crowd, and then exits stage right.] White Rose : Three awards left folks. Frankie Tha Pimp : And then we eat. White Rose : And drink. Frankie Tha Pimp : And go to Bourbon Street to see Rose tittys. . . White Rose : And no. Frankie Tha Pimp : And damnit. White Rose : Our next award is for _STABLE OF THE YEAR_ .. .Not many ran for this, but even if they did this group might of won it. Frankie Tha Pimp : No mights to it babe. White Rose : They went through some controversy this year with members comings, and going, but they always remained a force in the KAWF. This year's STABLE OF THE YEAR .. .THE CON$ORTIUM. [Cordial applauds our heard, as from behind the curtain we again see Kyle Sommmers who has his other trophy in hand.] Kyle Sommers : I'll take that. [Kyle grabs the trophy from Rose, and begins to walk behind the curtain.] White Rose : Umm Kyle ? [Kyle turns around, and see's Rose pointing to the microphone.] Kyle Sommers : Oh. [Kyle chuckles, and walks back to the microphone holding up both trophys.] Kyle Sommers : Looks like I'm the only one to get TWO of these things. To bad for everybody else. White Rose : Where's your stablemates that helped you get those awards ? Kyle Sommers : Who knows, but I'll see you all on Bourbon later. [Kyle walks off quickly, as Rose remains speechless.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Should of known you seeing Kyle walk off would leave you speechless. White Rose : Oh stop it. Frankie Tha Pimp : Be careful, or Jasmine might hurt you. White Rose : Anyways, our next award is for tag team of the year. Frankie Tha Pimp : Does Kyle need to come back out ? White Rose : No, his team got second for this one. This year's TAG TEAM OF THE YEAR is the team from Japan, errr the former team. The TRUE ALL STARS !!!!!!! [The crowd is somewhat silent, as nobody gets up.] Frankie Tha Pimp : Since there not a team, I guess they didn't want to come. I don't blame them. I wouldn't want to accept an award with somebody I would rather punch in there mouth. White Rose : Well the team of the True All Stars showed that when they our on the same page they our one of the best teams in the business. Winning both the IGA, and the KAWF titles this year. Frankie Tha Pimp : Zodiak is gonna get beat up by Orion. White Rose : Zodiak has been in a slump lately, but he also has beaten Orion before if you can recall the J*Star Festival. Frankie Tha Pimp : Fluke. White Rose : We will just put there award back here for safe keeping, and go on to our last award of the evening .. . .WRESTLER OF THE YEAR !!!!!!!!! [All stand, and begin clapping. Over the noise Frankie can be heard whining, while Rose can be heard promoting.] Frankie Tha Pimp : BOOOOOOOOOOOOOO.. RE - VOTE. RE - VOTE. RE - VOTE. White Rose : This year's wrestler of the year, the current GLOBAL CHAMPION of our federation. A man who has went to battle for this comapny since day one. A man who has bled, cried, and fought for us all at some point, or another. He has went toe to toe with Karl Ferguson, Vile Vince Viper, Derek Douglas, Lezkanic, and more in epic battles ranging from cages, to blood baths. Here is HAYDEN GRAYSON !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Frankie Tha Pimp : Somebody should be his new agent. [The place errupts, with all clapping, even most heel wrestlers. Luckily Kanic is already in the back, or else there might be a few boo's. Hayden Grayson then stands up, shaking hands on his way to the stage. He gets up there, hugging Rose, kissing her on the cheek, and then shaking hands with Frankie, even though nethier really wanted to.] Hayden Grayson : You know she's right. I did do all that, but there is a bunch of other people on the roster who would do the same. Guys like Holtz, Lewis, Sledge, even Fenriz. I'm just glad I got this award over all those other top notch guys. [Hayden pauses.] Hayden Grayson : Really this ranks near the top of my acomplishments out of all things I have done in my lifetime involving pro wrestling. I'm not just saying that. Any award means alot, but to be considered the BEST overall wrestler on this roster, is breathetaking.. [Suddenly, a large man stands up in the audience pointing towards the stage. Not just a large man ethier, a 370 pound man of almost seven foot stature.] Lucifer Magnusson : I WANT YOU !!!!!!!!!!! I FUCKING WANT YOU !!!!!!!!! [Cardinall Synn can be seen at his side now, as Hayden just chuckles on stage, mainly cause he is a good bit of distance from Lucifer, and would have time to react.] Hayden Grayson : Yea well I want you to big boy, but that's not gonna happen tonight so sit your ass back down in your seat. Your gonna have your hands full with Jimmy Haynes, might as well not worry about me yet buddy. [Magnusson remains standing, Synn remains eyeing Grayson.] Hayden Grayson : Or not . . Just figured your legs would need a break from carrying all that extra weight around. What do you all say we get out of here to have some fun, and leave tubby standing around ? [Applause.] Hayden Grayson : Let's go hit up Bourbon, and party like it's _MARDI GRAS_ . .Hand Grenades on me for everybody in this room. Frankie Tha Pimp : Is it Mardi Gras ? Hayden & Rose : No. Frankie Tha Pimp : Doh. Hayden Grayson : But they say it's like a year long party around here so let's go find out for ourselves. Frankie Tha Pimp : For once you made sense. Hayden Grayson : Bye Lucy .. Call me when you beat a few people. Till then I think I'll stick to worrying about the Contenders. [And with that we slowly begin to fade with people heading for the exits of the building, to the streets of Bourbon, possibly putting eating off till after they get intoxicated. Smart, smart people.] Frankie Tha Pimp :Buy my drink first, incase Lucifer follows you, and kills you. I don't want to be jipped on this thing. [Fade.]
