KAWF — Speak Your Mind

KAWF Speak Your Mind #1

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Speaking there mind Today . . . . . .. ********************************** 1 - "Primo" Chris Capri 2 - "The White Falcon" Kenzo Tagara 3 - "Mad dog" Mike Harris 4 - The Turnbow Brothers 5 - Zodiak ********************************* [Two of the five debut-week vignettes in this packet, for wrestlers not minted under this wing, are collected but not served; the remaining three follow in their own posted order.] 1 - "Primo" Chris Capri [ Fade In.. ] [ The cameras begin rolling as we see the back of a rather average sized man walking in a parking lot towards a black 2003 BMW M3. Over the right shoulder of the man lays a black Addidas gym bag. The man reaches into his pocket and raises his hand up a bit in the air.. ] "BEEP BEEP" [ The sound of the vehicle's car alarm goes off and the man slowly reaches to the door and opens it. He then takes the strap of his gym bag off his shoulder and places it on the passenger seat.. As soon as the man turns around it's obvious who it is. Chris Capri shuts the door and then leans up against the side of the car, quickly running his hand through his hair, and then putting his hands in his jean pockets.. ] "PRIMO" CHRIS CAPRI: Had a rough night out there tonight.. Big over the top rumble here in the good ol' NEWC. I've still got alot of work to do in this promotion, but it seems things suddenly have reached a detour. It has come to my attention that a old friend of mine has decided to bring out his old promoter boots and strap em' up. It wasn't but 15 minutes ago when I picked my cell phone up out of my locker and checked my message.. [ Capri smirks.. ] Mikey boy, ,Mikey boy, Mikey boy... You've called upon the modern day messiah once more to make your lil' promotion famous. Don't get me wrong at all though Mike, the KaWF has alot of history behind it, good old legacy about it. But, you and I both know that it just ain't the same without me. I mean, who knows, I might be stating the obvious, but you were the one who called my cell phone, you were the one who imeddiatly booked me in some kind of #1 Contendership match.. and I didn't do a god damn thing but check my voicemail, all I did was press 1. [ Capri takes his hands out of his pockets and crosses his arms, still with a wide grin on his face.. ] CC: So now that we know the only reason I'm in the KaWF is because you put all the effort into not only inviting me, but booking me in a big time match right off the bat, we can move onto the contest at hand. It seems that I have found my way into another little triple threat matchup. The big rumor around the campfire surrounding this match is that the winner gets Promotional Title consideration.. [ Capri starts laughing.. ] CC: Sorry, simply couldn't help myself Mr Johnson, who are you kidding? Do you think Chris Capri is Promotional Title material? Give me a break man, you know as well as I do that I'm far above the level of your piss poor Promotional Title. But you know what, I guess I'm just going to have to prove that to you once again. It seems that in the past you've always booked me pretty big in the beginning, but not big _ENOUGH_.. You make me work my way up the ladder, which I do very easy, and then you finally give me what I deserve. But let me ask you something Mikey boy.. What is the point? You know I'm going to climb up those rungs without much effort. Why do you keep placing these little stepping stones in my way? You know I'll just kick them to the side and walk all over em'. Kenzo Tagara, Mike Harris... Who? Who in the hell are these guys Mike Johnson ? Is this all you can supply me with right now? Do you think I'm this bad? Or.. _OR_ do you simply want to use me to get these guys over, to make them.. _FAMOUS_. Mike, you know more than anyone else that I can do just that, I can walk into a match with a guy.. [ Capri snaps his fingers.. ] CC: And just like that, make them famous. I'm just that damn good Josh, and you _KNOW_ this man. You know I have the skills that sell out the arenas, you know I've got the tools to dismantle any man in a wrestling ring, and above all, you know that I'm the prototype for the best competitor in the world.. [ Capri reaches into his shirt pocket and pulls out a pack of Parliament Lights.. Capri pulls a cigarette from the pack and then puts the cigarette in his right ear.. Capri puts the pack back in his pocket and then reaches into his jean pocket and pulls out a zippo lighter.. Capri pulls the cigarette from his ear, puts it to his lips and quickly lights it.. Chris then takes a long drag, and looks intently into the camera.. ] CC: This may come as a suprise to some, but this upcoming match does have alot riding on things. Yeah, it's not a match with yours truely against two other stars of the trade, but it still has some valid meaning behind it. This is the beginning of the newest KaWF saga, this one match could make or break you, I mean, it's opening night baby, shit is going to hit the fan. If you go out there and don't perform to the best of your ablitity and do something to grasp the fans attention, then man, just like that you can be dropped down to the bottom rung.. I'm not stating all of this for my own personal use, hell, I already know I'm good to go.. I already know that I'm headed to the top of the promotion and that I'll walk into that ring this weekend and get a win. But I'm simply just giving advice to these two opponents of mine. I want them to know that if they don't come out in that ring against me performing to the best of their ability then they just might sign their own pink slip that very night.. [ Capri takes a few more drags off his Parliament and exhales into the night.. ] CC: I'm not going to lie to you guys at all, I'm extremely good at making others look bad, and Kenzo, big "Mad Dog", I don't want to emberass you guys. I want you two to come out and give the best possible effort you can.. Because when you go up against Chris Capri in the squared circle, your already starting out at a disadvantage.. Thus, you two need to overcome such a disadvantage and try so ever hard to impress Mr.Johnson and the fans of the KaWF. I already know I'll impress them, I already know Johnson is impressed, but I'm pretty sure you two have alot of work to do.. It will be very interesting to see if you guys decide to be losers or winners.. Hopefully you decide to be winners and give me a pretty good match this weekend.. You'll still lose... But maybe, just _MAYBE_ one of you will do yourself some good and allow me to help you shine out there. [ Capri takes a long final drag off his cigarette and begins to walk around the car to the driver's side.. Capri folds his arms over the top of the car and looks into the camera smirking.. ] CC: Sadly, both of you will probably not heed my warnings and show up this weekend thinking that your both going to get a win. You two will probably both waltz right in with your little egos blaring, thinking that you can take out Chris Capri in the ring. Sadly enough your going to end up meeting someone who can walk the walk, talk the talk, and when it's all said and done, still have the energy to walk in your mother's house and show her what a real man is. Your going to meet someone who lays down for absolutely nobody, who doesn't lose to just anybody, and who laughs in the face of adversity.. You want to know who that someone is? [ Capri swings open the driver side door and looks back into the camera.. ] CC: It's me... It's _ME_.. It's god damn "Primo" Chris _CAPRI_. [ Fade To Black.. ] ********************************************************************************************************* 2 - "The White Falcon" Kenzo Tagara [ Fade in.] [ The first thing we hear is the quiet roar of a stream. We can also hear the wind softly whistling through the trees. The sounds provide for a very peaceful and relaxing picture to form in our mind's eye.] [ The scene opens up in the wilderness. The sun is starting to go down and the birds sing in the distance. Our shot is of a calmly flowing stream. A young Japanese man is kneeling down at the water's edge, staring into the crystal clear surface. When he speaks, it is in a soft tone.] KT: This is my favorite place to meditate. There is no better place to clear one's head than in the beauty of nature. The bird's lovely singing paired with the calming waters makes for a fabulous environment. [ He lifts his head from the stream. We now look into the eyes of KAWF's newest star, Kenzo Tagara.] KT: I have come here today to try and prepare for the journey that lies ahead of me. I have come to America to on a mission. The KAWF has been gracious enough to let me begin my journey in their ring. What is this mission, you may ask? [ The young man sighs. KT: The state of wrestling has drastically changed. The men who call themselves wrestlers today are full of greed. Instead of caring about competition, they have their mind set on the gold. Instead of showing sportsmanship, they bash you with a chair. What happened to the days when men wrestled to please the fans? Where have all the heroes gone? [ He tosses a small stone into the water, and it makes a soft splashing sound.] KT: Much like that stone fading into the depths of the water, the heroes have all mysteriously faded away. There is no one left for the children fans to cheer for. No one with any morals or respect. Wrestling is a disgrace and I bet the legends of the past are turning over in their eternal resting places in disgust. So where do I come in? My name is Kenzo Tagara. I am "The White Falcon." [ He stands up and we see the mask that covers his face.] KT: I will fight for those who cannot. Those who feel there is no hope. The children need someone to look up to. They need a man who will rid the wrestling world of the evil men. I will attempt to do this. I know that I cannot realistically win every match. What I can promise however is that I will not give up. There is no quit in Kenzo Tagara! If I lose, I will fight another day. My fighting spirit is strong and will not be extinguished. [ He goes over to sit on a log.] KT: My American wrestling career begins on the first edition of Fever. I am facing Chris Capri and Mike Harris. I do not know anything about either of these men. I hope we can have a nice well-contested match. I am entering this with respect for each of you. I hope you give the same in return. Good luck to the both of you. May the best man win. [ We fade.] ********************************************************************************************************* 3 - "Mad Dog" Mike Harris [Fade In..] "You want a War Story?" [The KAWF Camera fades in, The location is unknown But is reminiscent of a locker room. Standing in one Corner, a Dumbbell In one hand doing Curls, Is KAWF Worker "Mad Dog" Mike Harris. He looks at the camera with unforgiving Icy Blue eyes. He is wearing Stonewashed Blue jeans, and a Black T-Shirt.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: I'll tell you a War story. Hell.. I'll tell you a whole _SHITLOAD_ of war stories. [Pauses.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: Y'see this? [Harris motions to a Long Red Scar running from His Wrist to his Elbow on his right hand.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: That little number I received during an Inferno Match in Japan. That Match was a WAR a real WAR. [He attempts a chuckle but it sounds more like a grunt.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: I've been in many battles.. many wars in my career.. and I am fully prepared to make my KAWF Debut into a war.. *CLANG* [Harris drops the Dumbbell with an audible sound.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: Y'see I'm not the kind of man.. That fears a name.. I'm not the kind of man.. that fears a certain race.. Hell.. I'm not a man who fears _MUCH_ at all.. And let me say this right now.. I don't fear my opponents this week.. In the Triple Threat.. Who do we have here.. Chris Capri.. You've probably done a lot overrated bullshit in your career, that your going to gloat about. Let's get one thing straight.. I don't care what you've done.. What you plan to do.. or what you say.. clean slate. Let me get another thing straight.. you don't impress me. You can flap your gums to your hearts content but the fact is.. until we step into the ring.. and a Blood Bath ensues.. Your just another face.. [Pauses.] "MAD DOG" MIKE HARRIS: Then we've got ourselves "White Falcon" Kenzo Tagara. The same shit I said, Goes for you too. You think givin' yourself a nickname, like White Falcon, makes you special? Makes you someone to see? Hell.. Maybe I shouldn't talk.. You could be a tough bastard.. you could've earned your nickname, just like I earned mine. We're going to find out.. Just how tough you are, Tagara.. When Fever rolls around.. Because me, you, and Chris Capri.. We're gonna dance.. ...And from what A'hm told.. I dance _REAL_ Good. [Fade Out..] ******************************************************************************************************** Sup gentleman ? Pretty good showing .. . Missing one or two, but I have talked to both, and know what the deal was. So Im very pleased with this .. .If your doing an on card please get it in late tonight or early in the morning so I can be sure to get your work on the card tomorrow night. Remember I do angles around role plays, not role plays around angles. In other words if you out rp someone, unless you both come to me saying so - so wins you will get the win. There will be angles involved,. but I will work the angles around the winners, and not the other way around. Keep up the good work guys . .. We added a few more guys since the Update came out. Were looking good fellas. Much Respect, Josh * Results out Tomorrow Night . . .
