KAWF — New Owner

KAWF's New Owner Speaks

18 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
V/O : I don''t have deep pockets. I don't have a big reputation. I don't even have a DAMN television deal for us. Nor do I have wrestling experence of my own to draw on when the times get tough. _I have never been in a ring personally_ [Fade In.] [We quickly pan in on a steel chair placed in a seated position in the middle of an empty ring. The ring has nobody around it, nor any logo's on it to cover up an area of the ring. Some sweat stains can be seen near each turnbuckle as we zoom in closer. The voice continues, but the view remains of nothing more than the steel chair.] V/O : I have however been apart of a company which could change a man's life in a drop of a hat. I have seen a company take men who had no confidence when they walked into our view, and turn them into egotistical maniacs, only to have them in the next week, or two not even being able to look themselves in the mirror. This company had that much control over a man's mind. This company had that much heart N soul. I was a huge part of that. I loved being apart of that every single day. I loved my job back then. I loved this company. [Slight pause, as the voice raises to a different tone.] V/O : Which is why despite not having connections, money, or a huge past in the business I will not let it die. I have Mike Johnson's blessing to run this company, and with that blessing I promised him I would possibly change the style, but never the goal. Which is to be the best it can possibly be. Can we be the old KAWF ? No. No we can't, but we can be a damn good version of a new KAWF ran with the same principles, and goals, but a smaller market. We can do that. [Some chuckling.] V/O : Oh I promise you we can do that. I got rid of a few people, and kept a few people. The history will be remembered forever, but the future shall bring great history of it's own. I was the head booker of that past. Now I shall be the owner of this future. [Climbing into the ring via the steel steps, we see a nicely dressed Mike Pace, looking the same as ever as if two years hadn't passed.] MP : I am Mike Pace, and if you can hear these words . . . Welcome to not only my wrestling company but my damn home. [Mike Pace sets in the chair pointing directly towards the camera smiling as we begin to pull away from view.] [Fade.]
