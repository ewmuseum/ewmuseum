SAWF House Show, 15 December 2004

Suck Ass Wrestling Federation December 15th, 2004 "Can Hayden do it by himself ? " >From Jackson, Mississippi Bell Time : 8 : 00 PM Ticket Prices : 10 $ for Adults, $ 4 dollars for Kids under 15. Hosted by : Frankie Tha Pimp scheduled matches The Turnbow Brothers Versus Blunt Force Trauma Matthew Beecham Versus El Tigre' Larry Gamble Versus Martyr X Shane Martin & VVV Versus Hayden Grayson Guest Appearances by Lucifer Magnusson Phillip Johnson [Mike Johnson's son/ Kristen Roberts husband] Johnny Sledge Commish Outlaw Bonus Match Jay Turnbow Versus Local Indy star Well hello there, I'm back. I'm in Jackson, but none the less I'm back so I'm not gonna let being in Jackson ruin this for me . I am ready for the SAWF's trip to the eastcoast however though, cause the south is starting to bore me a bit. Sure, I love the hot girls, and how gulible they our to us big city guys, but I'm ready for some flash, and some pizza. I want more fake tits. I want more Asian people. I want some New York Pizza for christ sake. By the way It's me Frankie incase you couldn't figure it out by the sexy voice. I'm here to tell you guys about a house show, a house show which has a few matches we don't care about on it, and a main event which has some potential to be awseome. So were gonna skip right to the main event. Actually NO were not. I wish we could, but we can't. They would fire ol Frankie if I did that, and I need this job. I got a tiny lil problem with a loanshark in Miami I have to settle, which is going to be hard to settle if I'm jobless, besdies I want to stick around to see the Con$ortium take over this place, and the name change back to KAWF if humanly possible. I at least want to hang around to see Kristen Roberts take this company to places Mike Johnson never could. I'm not glad he's really sick, but I am glad he's not in charge. I just didn't think he had the balls to make us as good as we could be. Kristen might not have the balls, but she has the attitude. I can't wait to see what the main event at Crazed Intentions is. I'm sure it will be something sick, but here I am rambling about the future. So let's get this shin dig started before I start talking about something completly different than wrestling .. .We got a decent crowd here. Hmmmm a few cuties a few rows back, and they are selling alchol which is always good for my after the show chances, and a must when the opening bout of the show is the Turnbows, and Blunt Force Trauma. Before this had some spark, but it has fizzled as time passed. Blunt Force's manager is still in jail, and thinks that the Turnbows planted some drugs on him. I don't know if they did, or not, but I do know BFT is still without a manager. Something's got to give in this one, and I'm sure it won't be the mat when the Turnbows our sent into it by Blunt Force Trauma, or vice versa so maybe we can get that spark back for this thing. ********************************************************************************************************* Opening Bout Turnbow Brothers Vs Blunt Force Trauma ********************************************************************************************************* The guys tried to get the sparks back. Jay, and Will the Turnbows were on one side of the ring hollaring at George Wesson, and Adam Clarkson who were hollaring right back. Some of the ringside fans were laughing at the two teams, and some of the stuff they said. I think Wesson was having alot of fun with this arguing stuff. [name withheld] was the referee, and he tried to get one man from each team out of the ring, but he had no such luck. The two teams then stormed towards the middle of the ring, and began to trade blows. The Turnbows kept the advantage, and guided BFT into neutral corners. The Turnbows went to whip BFT towards each other in the middle of the ring, but BFT reversed, and sent the Turnbows into each other. The two brothers collided, and came back only to catch massive lariats from the angry tag team. Turnbows rolled out of the ring, did some basic stalling tactics to get the crowd into it, and then finally the older, but smaller brother Jay Turnbow slid back into the ring to do battle with a pumped up George Wesson. Jay did some stretching, and the two then locked up followed by some basic wrestling, with Jay clearly being the better wrestler of the two. After a few trade off's Jay put George into an arm ringer, and tagged in his brother Will who landed a kick to the midsection of Wesson, then took over the arm ringer. Will stayed on the arm until . ... Wesson poked Will in the eye, and then landed an arm drag which he was very proud of. George then stalked towards his corner, and tagged in Clarkson. Before Clarkson could attack Will, the younger Turnbow was up in his corner talking strategy while eyeing Adam. Jay then talked his brother into tagging him back in, and the oldest Turnbow hopped the rope to do battle with Adam Clarkson. They went to lock up, but Jay landed a kick to the midsection. The Turnbows then showed off there experence as a tag team, landing a bunch of quick low impact tag team moves, with alot of quick tags. They also screamed some stuff about Johnny Rifleman to try to get under BFT's skin. They never omitted guilt for his jail time, but they did get Wesson worked up on the outside while punishing Clarkson on the inside. Wesson was so angry if he would of been tagged in right then, he prolly would of ended up in a cell next to Rifleman. Eventually however the tide turned again, and Clarkson hit a beautiful counter swinging neckbreaker, both Jay Turnbow, and Clarkson were down .. .. Each man crawled to there corner making the tag causing some nice crowd pop, and stormed into the ring where Wesson got the advantage. Wesson ran with the advtange, and dumped Will over the top rope, he also managed to send Jay out there to join his brother on the outside of the ring. Brief low moment in the action, only to be risen up by Wesson who sprang to the top rope, and then springboarded over with a moonsault landing on both men who were up wabbled. Clarkson then rolled outside, and sent Will Turnbow into the ring. Clarkson not being the legal man helped his partner up, and sent him into the ring. Wesson slowly eased up, and landed a high impact brainbuster suplex, before tagging in Adam who hit a top rope tunrbuckle legdrop, and went for a pin. Will managed to kick out, but just barely. Clarkson then tagged in Wesson, and the two did a double spike piledriver on Will .. . .Wesson then went for a cover. Jay tried to roll back in, but Clarkson cut him off leading to a fairly easy three count. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Blunt Force Trauma Via : Pinfall at 12 : 33 into the match ********************************************************************************************************* The crowd somewhat enjoyed that tag match between the two lesser known KAWF teams, and gave each a nice reaction. Nethier team played to the reactions, but you could tell they were pleased with there performances tonight in Jackson, espically George Wesson who has had some complaints about his ring work as of late from the people in management. Trauma got over there stiff style, while the Turnbows got to show off there teaming experence, and work on some heel antics for when they get back on the big screen. The KAWF dancers then came out, and danced. Yea the crowd loved it, they were in Mrs Santa outfits for christ sake. I do love Christmas time that's fo sure. I wander how much it will cost me to set on one of there laps after the show ? Were gonna skip that part though, or I will have to go to the bathroom again. The next match consisted of mexican road horse El Tigre' versus the newest member of the KAWF cruserweight division . .. Mr Matthew Beecham. ********************************************************************************************************* Second Bout Matthew Beecham Vs El Tigre' ********************************************************************************************************* This quick paced match skipped the lock ups, stalling tactics, and what not which sometimes comes into play when the bell first rings. They jumped right into a criss cross action from one side of the ring to the other until Beecham landed a beautiful drop kick, which got a pop from the crowd it was so crisp. Tigre' got up quickly, and the two small guys traded wrestling holds, until Finally Tigre'e got the advantage with a drop down toe hold, followed by a crisp elbow to the back of Beecham's head. Matthew jumped up holding his head, and had a look in his eyes. A look of a champion might I add. Course I was drunk cause Mike Hard Lemonade gives me all the drinks I can handle, so I could of seen that with beer goggles. This time they did lock up, and Beecham guided Tigre' back into the corner, where he beat him like he stole something with knife edge chops, punches, and stiff kicks. Beecham guided him out of the corner with a snapmare, followed by a neck snap. Beecham stayed on him like white on rice, and dropped a few lightning leg drops across the back of Tigre's head returning the favor from the elbow. Beecham then took control for a few minutes using different high impact moves to weaken his opposition, and then set Tigre on the top rope. The crowd stood for the most part expecting something big, but Tigre' fought Beecham off, and managed to land a desperation whirlwind DDT. Both men were down, Tigre' got up first, and landed a few stiff kicks, but his offense was shut down as Beecham caught his leg, and landed an enzurgi kick which prolly gave him a concussion. Beecham then quickly set Tigre up again on the top rope, and caught with a beautiful top rope DDT in which he floated over for a cover attempt afterwards. The motion in which all this went down was flawless . ..He of course got the three, and definitely was impressive in his in ring debut for the KAWF/SAWF. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Matthew Beecham Via : Pinfall courtesy of the Majestic at 10 : 23 into the match ********************************************************************************************************* Beecham then worked the crowd, and headed to the back with a look on his face which was like a kid in the candy store. He was pleased to say the least, and still stoked. This is where I come in. I entered the ring. Hold your applause for later. I told a few jokes, got the crowd in the Christmas spirit of things, and then I hyped the SAWF. I lied, but none the less I got paid for doing it so it's okay. I hyped Crazed Intentions which will be in New York sometime early next year, Sabbath joining the SAWF which plays into the whole SAWF/KAWF thing in a weird twisted way, Kristen Roberts era of power, this weekends shows which will feature guys like Sledge, Sabbath, Lewis, Fenriz, VVV, Leonard Holtz and my man LezKanic, and of course our main event of the evening was hyped which puts Hayden against two hardcore legends. This got a heel pop, people just don't like violence like they used to. I was all for seeing Hayden get pounded on by two guys. I'm glad Outlaw won't fight Shane. It's best for us all. I then announced Phillip Johnson who came out to a nice crowd pop .. . He told us his father is hanging in there. Him and Kristen our having problems, and he don't know what to do. Yada Yada .. . Basically he told us in short he needs a marriage counselor, and his father would speak on Fever Saturday night. Simple. He just drawn it out for a while, some people will do anything for camera time. I finally got Phillip out of the ring, and we prepared for our third of four bouts of the evening. This one puts newcomer Larry Gamble in against hardcore thug Martyr X who we have seen several times on house shows all over the south. Iv'e had a beer with this guy after the show, he's so ugly that I had people come up to me reminding me "pets" weren't allowed in the bars around there particular location. Heh. He then would try to fight the people, but he's a house show wrestler. It's not like he's a "badass" or anything. He's been beat up more than a kid who says "no" at Michael Jackson's wonderland. ********************************************************************************************************* Third Bout Larry Gamble Vs Martyr X ********************************************************************************************************* Well both these guys came out eager, but they didn't get to show off there stuff on this particular tonight. I was kinda anxious to see what this Gamble guy was all about to, it's somewhat a shame. As they went to lock up a woman from the top of the ramp way said "Hold On" and I swear to everything holy that the arena got quiet, and the two wrestlers stopped. The lady stood on the top of the ramp way for a while just eyeing both guys who remained motionless. Finally from out behind the curtains a mammoth man came out. A man who was almost 7 foot, and weighed over 300 pounds easy. Not LezKanic size, but the closest to him I have ever seen. The girl smiled, the man did not .. .They both then headed to the ring, and the two guys in the ring remain froze. As they got closer, people realized who it was, well some of the educated one's did. Luckily for me one of them leaned over and told ME who this was. It was Lucifer Magnusson from Rumble Pro, and his manager Cardnial Synn . ..Kinda hot for a gothic chick, but I wasn't about to tell her that. I was froze to. Synn entered the ring courtesy of the steps, and Lucifer jumped up onto the apron, and then right over the top rope. Synn walked up to each man who looked frightened, and kinda sniffed them before frowning, and sicking Lucifer on them with nothing more than a look to her twisted "friend" . .. Lucifer then hit two powerbombs on each man back to back showing off amazing strength considering X is around 250 pounds, before tossing them over the top rope, and to the floor. THIS MAN IS FREAKIN HUGE, AND I'M GUESSING THIS IS THE SON OF SATAN WE HEARD FROM LAST WEEK ON FEVER. I only say that cause of the Tat on his back, not like I did my homework, or nuttin. Synn and Magnusson then eyed the crowd, who was still speechless. A few spoke out amongst themselves buzzing a bit, but most just watched there every move/breathe. Synn smirked after a minute or so, and the two then went to the back leaving Gamble, and X for the EMT's who waited for the evil duo to exit, before going down. Obviously they were afraid to. Our roster just got alot _BIGGER_ .. .Literally. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Ummm is there a winner here ? Via : You saw what I saw. _SHADUP_ ********************************************************************************************************* The EMT's finally got Gamble, and X out of there .. .Luckily Lucifer didn't want to attack anybody else, cause there is not enough security in Mississippi to hold that guy back from anything he wants to get his hands on. I think the crowd finally heel popped for what they saw right about . ... . . .. . _NOW_ . .. Way after Lucifer, and Synn were gone. The som bitch even scared the crowd. How many times have you seen that happen ? The boo's quickly turned to cheer's though as Hayden Grayson came out wearing his Global Title. He worked the mic and told VVV, and Martin to get there ass out there so he could kick it in a bit of flashier words, if Outlaw wouldn't team with him, then he would be happy to do it by himself tonight for [cheap pop] Jackson, Mississippi. Enter the heels .. . VVV, and Martin quickly came with smiles on there faces. Martin worked the mic while VVV worked the crowd, and as the bell rang .. .We were interrupted again before the match could start.. Not by Lucifer this time though, or that annoying Phillip Johnson. This time by Outlaw .. . He said HE wasn't going to fight, but he knew someone who would help Hayden tonight. He never promised Martin a handicap match, and there wouldn't be one tonight. Outlaw paused as the crowd popped, and Martin bitched at his old friend while VVV focused in on Hayden who was smiling, and anticpating who may be tagging with him on this cold December night. Outlaw then introduced Johnny Sledge who stormed down towards the ring to join Grayson. Outlaw then watched from the top of the ramp way with a smile as the crowd errupted, and the bell rang . .._AGAIN_. ********************************************************************************************************* MAIN EVENT Vile Vince Viper & Shane Martin Vs Hayden Grayson & Johnny Sledge ********************************************************************************************************* Sledge charged down the aisle and slid head 1st into the ring as Vile turned around to see what was going on and ran into a solid right, Martin charged towards Grayson who ducked underneath the clothesline and waited on Shane kicking him in the midsection and sending forearm shot after forearm shot into the side of Shane's face. Vile stumbled backwards from the stiff shots of Sledge only to fall backwards out of the ring to the floor! Johnny climbed out of the ring after him, after several shots Martin came in with a knee lift to the stomach of Hayden doubling him over and driving his head into the top turnbuckle. Sledge continued to hammer away on VVV on the floor trying to let out some of his frustrations before an eye poke stopped him in his track, Triple V grabbed a chair and brought it around into the side of Sledge's face knocking him down. Got to love house shows. . .It seems you get away with more stuff. More chair shots please ? Martin took Grayson over with a belly to back suplex covering Grayson for a 2 count, Martin threw Hayden to the floor into the arms of the waiting VVV who drove Hayden into the railing and into the crowd as Shane took over on the recovering Sledge. Martin backed Johnny against the post and charged forward with a clothesline that was ducked out of the way of, Martin winced in pain as Sledge went to work on Shane's arm slamming it against the post, apron and railing without mercy. VVV kicked away at Grayson as they moved through the crowd while people scattered, VVV took a moment to taunt Grayson which only lit a fire and caused VVV to find himself on the wrong end of a face plant into an open chair. Vile grabbed his face and stumbled away through the crowd into the cheap seats with Hayden throwing forearms and elbows. Martin and Sledge fought back up the ramp way. Martin regaining some ground as the referee was confused with what was occuring. Yea it's not really a wrestling match buddy. What did you expect with these four guys ? Hayden hates VVV . . .Sledge hates VVV . . .VVV hates both Sledge, and Hayden. While Martin is just evil. I could of told you this was going to be a brawl, and remember im drunk. Hayden continued to win the fight against VVV who could be our next Promotional Champion in the stands, while Shane and Sledge continued to trade blows in the middle of the ramp way. Finally the referee called for the bell, ending the match, but not the fight. It kept going. And going . .. And going . . . Security broke things up with a little help from Hayden knocking down VVV, and Sledge knocking down Martin. Outlaw wasn't thrilled though. He said "Fuck that" in those exact words, and re started the match. Being commish he could do that, the referee wasn't thrilled, but he went with it, and tried to get security to get everybody back in the ring. Again security had help from the team of Grayson, and Sledge. Grayson dragged Vile back down to ringside and threw him over the railing and into the ring while Sledge pulled Martin back to the ring and rolled him inside with security shortly behind. By now the crowd was hopping. VVV, and Shane quickly rolled under the bottom ropes trying to get away from the high intensity of the opposing team. Martin, and Viper regrouped outside both busted open slightly while Sledge and Grayson stood side by side calling both men back into the ring. Martin and Vile waved the match off and headed back up the aisle as the referee laid down the 10 count again with Vile and Martin steady heading back. Each man passed Outlaw at the top of the ramp way without even glancing his directionn, as again the bell was sounded for the match to end. An irritated Grayson and Sledge paced the ring before exchanging a look and shaking their heads in disgust. The crowd roared as both men shook hands and played to the crowd as Outlaw joined them with a microphone. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Grayson & Sledge Via : Countout at 21 : 56 into the match ********************************************************************************************************* Outlaw started a roast .. . All three men then took turns on the microphone cussing, and poking fun at VVV and Martin trying to get them back to the ring, but couldn't achieve there goal considering there enemies have very thick skin to say the least. Outlaw then thanked the crowd for coming out, and said he was sorry for the ending, but in due time there would be a real ending. A true ending . .. Grayson then took the microphone, and said he wouldn't stop this battle, his battle until the SAWF was KAWF again, and VVV was gone forever . .. Graysons theme song then started playing, and all three men bailed to a standing ovation. Since the crowd was still popping Outlaw sent Jay Turnbow out there to wrestle a typical dark match style match against some local indy which he won in about seven minutes after a springboard hurricanna, but that was it . .. Jackson, Mississippi was offically over with, for now at least. Merchandise sold good, crowd was good, matches were solid, and entertainment wise all did there best. Thus I'm sure there will be more of these in the future, and I'll see you then cause I am the BEST DAMN ANNOUNCER IN THE BUSINESS .. . Sincerly, Frankie Tha Pimp P.S - I would watch Fever if I was you guys .. . I am thinking the heat from this house show is gonna carry over, but that's just me. Maybe I'm wrong. Okay I'm never wrong.
