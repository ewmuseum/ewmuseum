Sup guys ? It's me again. As alot of you know I am a big fan of tag team wrestling, and because of that I refuse to NOT have a tag division in the KAWF. For the first time ever I am having a hard time getting tag teams. Plenty of singles, not enough tag teams. If you handle a tag team, drop me a line. If you and a buddy handle a tag team, drop me a line. If you know of some guys who handle a tag team, tell them to drop me a line. [name withheld] . . .I'm not hard to find. I allow tag team wrestlers to do some single wrestling to, so your not restricted to just tag team wrestling. Nor will you be forgotten about like alot of tag divisions from various feds. I will give you your due amount of time. Much Respect, Josh Not really looking for single wrestlers, but I'll eye a good app. Also could use a woman, or two. Other than that were kinda booked up.
