Sup gentleman ? Was just gonna drop a quick line plugging the upcoming KAWF shows this weekend. Hopefully some of you guys will check it out. Most weekends the KAWF puts out pretty entertaining reads. The Role play packet just came out, and man oh man have some words been said. There is definitely some heat going around right now amongst the KAWF guys. We got two big shows coming up in Mississippi this weekend. The Tag Team Champs are in non title action. Youth Gone Wrong Is _PISSED_ Hayden Grayson speaks on the IGA vs KAWF feud, and the Global Title. Cruserweights galore compete for the Number 1 contendership to that title. Plus some info on Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash which will be the next KAWF ppv. It will take place in mid August. All that and more gentleman. Give it a read. Much Respect, Josh P.S - Be on the lookout for a KAWF review from Grant in a few days. I think he's going to be reviewing our PPV which occured a few weeks back _THE RETURN_. Hopefully he likes, and will encourage some of you guys to read up a bit.
