KAWF "The best mix of old fashion,and extreme wrestling in E - Wrestling" Update # 14 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup gentleman ? Sorry that the supercard was a bit late, but I think it was well worth the wait, better than the last supercard in my humble opinion. I did not want to rush it, nor did I feel rushed with it being the holiday season knowing the rest of you guys schedule was hectic as well. I know there was some controversial actions which might not of rubbed everybody the right way, but everything done has a method to the madness in the long run I promise you. Plus as a whole I think everybody should be able to find things they enjoy on the supercard, whether it be the angles, the commentary, or the quality of the matches. If not, well are you really in the right damn hobby ? -=) Moving along though we have another cycle to focus on which will end in April. This also should be a damn good cycle. I have a lot of the pre booking done for it, and feuds I am trying to set up listed in outline format, and if they go smoothly. My god. Great cycle. I do my booking really tight leaving scenarios for you guys to decide on cards, wins, and losses with me just stirring it towards a supercard match up, and if the supercard I have outlined happens, or even almost happens that way it will be amazing. We have cut majority of the deadweight, added some veteran guys, and were really ready to become a player in this great hobby of ours with the guys we got. With that said don't forget the E WRESTLING AWARDS our in the process now. Get your vote in. Represent the federation. Don't put us as number one if you don't feel we deserve it, but put us where you think we belong. Be fair. Also remember we can't always win our matches, but you don't have to win to tell a story. All the handlers here work hard, your not the only one.Keep your head up if things don't go the way you want them in the win/loss category. Email me if you have any questions. I'm not hard to find. I don't like complaining, but I don't mind having a discussion. On with the update. Much Respect, [name withheld] * I'll be at Raw on January 15th near the entrance. Look for a short guy, tall guy, and a bobby hill look a like holding signs. I'll be the short guy with a cap turned backwards. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * Congratulations is in order to all the new title holders, and people who managed to maintain there titles at the supercard. Adam Lazarus, and the Obsolete Army defended there titles in hard fought matches. Johnny Pain, and Josie Saito won vacant titles in crowd pleasing affairs, and Lazz Sterling was able to win the Promotional Title in very shocking fashion. * Little Rock, Arkansas on April 21st will be the next supercard. More to come as the event get's closer. A name has not yet been decided. * Owner / President Mike Pace was very pleased with the effort, and outcomes of "A Cold Day in Paradise". He left Florida with a smile on his face by all accounts of the people close to him. Outlaw was also said to be please, as the attacks have cutdown somewhat in recent times putting more focus on the matches. * Lazz Sterling left Destin quickly after escaping with the Promotional Title, feeling that the boys in the back might deliver a back lash to him for showing up, and winning the strap. * The KAWF is putting some money into Nightstalker, Ben Tuckwell, and Corey Hart hoping they have drawing power this next cycle, promising them more wrestling time than before. We shall see if this plays out good for the company considering the formula's they were using seemed to be working. * Corey Hart has now been cleared to wrestle by all EMT's, and doctors on the company's staff. * All wrestlers are cleared to go for the company, but Angela Cheetem will need some time off from the Def Bi Posse after the post match attack by the Obsolete Army at the supercard. * Three more house shows remain on the foreign soil house show tour the KAWF has put together. No news on if they plan on trying it again afterwards. So far the tour has been a major success for the company. * Xavier Orion has been let go from his contract by the KAWF, as they seemed to think he wasn't the same Orion as before, and his skills won't back up the trouble he will stir outside of the ring. The former Streetlife Champion was quickly replaced by another former Streetlife champion Lark Fenriz who seems to be the personal trainer of Foodstamp, and new female wrestler Tamara Jackson. * Tommy Flames has also returned to the roster after being inactive since the last KAWF. * Kai Lewis was thrilled backstage after winning the loser leaves town match, and even threw some trash talking the way of Mikey Mckay as he was packing his bags. Very unlike Brooklyn's finest, but you can't blame him on this one. * Mike Foyer got into it with a Santa Clause getting arrested while he was in Paris, France for the house show tour. * Damian Payne rented his Santa Clause suit he used for the supercard, and did indeed return it with a nice tip for management considering how handy it came in against the man who showed him up the last time we had a Fever. * Johnny Spades has requested a limited work schedule next cycle due to personal reasons, and has been granted his request by the office. Nobody is sure exactly how much ring action Spades will see, but we do know he will be fired up after the ending of the supercard when we do get to see him. * Rumors are stirring an SPW superstar has signed a long term contract with the KAWF. Many names have been mentioned so far. * Carson Ray left Destin, Florida without his mentor Ben Tuckwell. People who saw Carson leave said he seemed very upset, but more so with himself than anybody else. * The KAWF is planning on having more technically sound matches in the next few shows, to see who can really work in the squared circle, and plan for the long term when it comes to angles, and drawing power. The KAWF only has room for people who can when called for, put on clinics in the ring. The money is limited, and no need to waste it on people who can't be here in the long term for the company. * KAWF Calendars sold very well. Only a few remain at the offices for people who really want one for there walls. * New Years Resolution for Sunny Powell [KAWF office member] is to find a boyfriend. Word is she likes wrestlers, but which one ? What type ? * Tommy Danger was seen backstage at the supercard talking with some "friends" of his. Nothing can be confirmed as far as Danger wanting to work with the KAWF. * The office just wanted to point out a few special spots during the show that they were pleased with to encourage more of this activity. The technical aspect of Hood / Pain. Brady Lowell flying all over the place. Kai Lewis beautiful dragon sleeper grapevine, Lazz Sterling's promo, Carson Ray's big spot on Leon Corella, The hardcore flow of the Mexican Deathmatch,and the overall intensity of the men who partook in the show. * We hope everybody had a happy holiday, and are ready to get back to work here in two more weeks for what is sure to be more exciting action in the KAWF as we continue to try to grow. Some people have even rumored trying to get a television deal in the Arkansas, Louisiana, and Mississippi area with the current names on the roster within the next six months. Some very influential backers have been mentioned with wanting to help. ******************** 3 - Roster ******************* CURRENT KAWF MAINSTAYS Jimmy Cancer Appearance : 5'11 195 Style : Brawler We took sometime away from Louisiana, but you knew we wouldn't be gone for long with the women looking this good, and the environment being so rabid. Destin, Florida is behind us, and were ready to return with a bang. A _real_ bang as everything you saw must be explained, and everything you heard must be understood. This is where we try to give you answers to questions we don't even know the answers to you yet. We all might not like what we hear, but we promise things will be said. Monroe - You haven't seen anything yet. We still have some stories left to tell you. In the opening bout of the night the "STREET SAMURAI" SPADE will make his FEVER debut to take on "PRIMO" CHRIS CAPRI who held the PROMOTIONAL title back when the GLOBAL title was our main title. A man who performed in some of the greatest matches the KAWF has seen in the last five years. This match very well could be one who decides who is in for match time, and who will be on the back burner of the roster. With the current size of the roster, and the growth of the KAWF a match like this becomes very important. CAPRI knows all about performing inside of a KAWF ring, but SPADE has gotten hit feet wet recently in very impressive fashion. We continue to move forward with a very special tag team match that will pair up one of the newest teams on the roster. A team built out of respect for each other due to the time limit draw match up they had. DAVID IRVINE & EDDIE JAMES will take on CARSON RAY & BEN TUCKWELL a team in possible turmoil after A COLD DAY IN PARADISE. We don't know what will happen here in this special tag team attraction, but we do have a verbal commit from RAY saying he will be here for this match. Will it be to collect a payday ? Or get back on the winning track ? Look for the pace in this match to be set very high. The women get some time to shine next as two more women get to debut inside the ring. APATHY of IGA fame will take on TAMARA JACKSON in a match which could put one of them in line for a chance to do battle with the new Women's champion JOSIE SAITO. We assume both women will look at that as a reward, instead of a punishment. Both women have very different styles in the ring, but styles we feel that will lead to a very impressive match. Don't think there worried about there make up fellas, these women are coming to the South to be anything, but lady like. Before we go to Intermission we will take some time out to hear from the NEW PROMOTIONAL CHAMPION, yes we said NEW PROMOTIONAL CHAMPION. The biggest shocker of the night's story will be told by the man who lived it first hand. LAZZ STERLING with title in hand will be live, and in our faces to tell us why he was at the supercard, and why he decided to really stick his nose into the match. The man knows all will be gunning for him, but does LAZZ even fucking care ? The safe money say's he could care less. The man's ego as most know is one of the biggest in the business. Revenge is in the air, but this time revenge will be up for grabs as LEON CORELLA will take on the man who out lasted his main man in a Mexican Deathmatch. That of course being "GREENCARD" JUAN RODRIGUEZ. Both men have some bumps, and bruises from the supercard, but have promised to have just as much effort on the 20th as they did at the supercard. JUAN feels he still has more to prove, and LEON wants to continue his winning ways. Something has got to give, or better yet somebody will have to give. Let the war begin. The MAIN EVENT of the night will feature a FATAL FOUR WAY in which to the winner goes the spoils. The first pin wins, and the two men watching get a loss just like the man who had his shoulders pinned. NIGHTSTALKER will put his undefeated streak on the line against the likes of BRADY "BOLTZ" LOWELL who looked very impressive at the supercard, VINCENT CASE the former Promotional Champion, and the debuting JIMMY CANCER. All four men have different mind sets going into this huge match up, but that's what makes this a main event. NIGHTSTALKER will have the words of JOSIE SAITO in his head, LOWELL will have PAYNE in his mind, VINCENT CASE is still angered about the title no longer being his, and CANCER is being forced to debut in the spotlight. I talk to most of you in emails, or instant messages, but if I don't get around to each one of you lemme just say great job last cycle. You all put in some good work, and got your characters over the way they needed to. There were some minor bumps on the way, but nothing some quick thinking couldn't solve. Everybody is doing a good job of not just getting YOUR character over, but more importantly THE OTHER characters over. The roster size has gotten a bit bigger, and more active than before so be patient with your angles. Use on cards, and role plays to your advantage. YOU WON'T BE BOOKED EVERY SHOW so use the tools you have at your side to progress things. This will make the angles more important in the long run, just takes some patience. If your not happy. Talk to me. If you are happy. Talk to me. I like to hear both. I like to know where my guys are coming from. Let's continue on making the KAWF a fun place to be, maybe not the best, but definitely a fun quality place to further your character, and work on your craft. We have a lot of new guys in the KAWF, and we have to work them in the mix with open arms. Help me do so. Remember to run on cards, and angles by me before you do them. I'm pretty flexible, but I have final say, and don't want you to type something I can't fit onto the show. We have ALOT OF handlers here we have to juggle, so don't waste your time, keep me in the loop. I usually don't change things, but sometimes I have to. I also plan on allowing people to help me write a bit more than I was to try to get shows out on time. Contact me if you can match write for me. It does help. All fed heads will tell you the same. Adios for now lady, and gentleman. Again thanks. Much Respect, [name withheld] * Please fill out the survey I am about to send. It's a bit long, but it really does help me.
