SUCK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents **************************** 1 - Notes from the desk - Out of Character 2 - News - In Character 3 - Roster & Records 4 - Various Rankings, and Title Holders 5 - Line ups 6 - Final Notes ***************** 1 - Notes from the Office ****************** Well guys the pay per view took us much longer than we thought to get out, as did the IGA/KAWF supercard. I think the supercard, and the PPV could of been better, but they were still decent reads which showed bright spots about why both the IGA, and the KAWF have such a dedicated fan base. I know the next month or so has poiental to be shaky, but I thank all of you for sticking it out. It means alot. I love this hobby, whether yall choose to believe that, or not, and appreciate the things you all do to help make this hobby an enjoyable one. We had fun before, and in due time we will be having fun again. Same story lines, same people, and same end result. Just give it time to get back to where it was. With that said let's put our brains together and come up with some storylines. I have a few in mind, but am always looking for more. If they can't be used now, I betcha they can be used later. Much Respect, Josh * Shoot me an email if you have any questions. I know there probally will be some. ************************ 2 -News from the rumor bin ************************* * The KAWF is offically now the SAWF until further notice due to the shady ordeal between Shane Martin, and Vile Vince Viper at the pay per view. The sponsers of this very federation, and the wrestlers our not happy with the name change obviously, but will do there best to get things back to the KAWF as soon as possible. HBO is also very interested in having the name change back as soon as possible. *Mike Johnson is sick, and stationed at his home in New Orleans, Louisiana for the time being. More on him, his family, and his health in the weeks to come. Kristen Roberts will be the acting president until Mike Johnson gets back, with Shane Martin moving up to vice president, and Outlaw moving up to commish. * Please welcome Two fisted Tony, The Minute Men, Riverhawks, Mathew Beecham, Larry Gamble, Bungle and Sabbath to the KAWF/SAWF. * The True All Stars got into a fight backstage after there loss to Perfect Storm at the PPV, and stormed out of the arena seperately. A woman was seen following Zodiak out. * Kristen Roberts has promised that next Saturday Night Fever contenders for the Global Title, and Promotional Title will be named. * VVV vs Hayden Grayson at the Darkness Visible supercard is being seen as a possible match of the year canidate by most people in the wrestling community. * Among the many roster changes in the past few weeks, Misery has decided not to participate in the KAWF/SAWF for the time being. No news has been heard on Spikyjim, or Tristan Fate currently. * All tour dates, house shows, and arena bookings which were made before the departure of Mike Johnson have been canceled, and will be named as we go by Kristen Roberts in the recent weeks. * Crazed Intentions will still be the next KAWF/SAWF pay per view, but the date is currently unknown. * Congrats to Hayden Grayson, Lark Fenriz, Kai Lewis, Perfect Storm, and Sammy Griffin for there PPV title victorys. * Derek Douglas is slowly recovering from his shoulder Injury which occured at the hands of LezKanic months ago. * Lenoard Holtz was taken to the hospital after his match with Lezkanic to get stiched up, and X rays. * The KAWF/SAWF is currently accepting wrestlers ranging from tag teams, to cruserweights. It is limited space however so all entries should contact the KAWF/SAWF management team quickly. * The Hollywood Syndiated is rumored to be discussing a come back with the KAWF/SAWF now that Mike Johnson is out of the office. * Ratings for Mike Johnson's Birthday bash taped showing was alot better than recently expected. * Public Relations consultant Mike Pace has been really busy as of late with various things involving wrestling. More on this as we go, but there is some rumor that the SAWF name has been the focus of most of it. * Again the KAWF would like to send out thanks to those who have particpated in out of federation events. This list includes Garrett Jax, Orion, Zodiak, Sammy Griffin, Johnny Sledge, LezKanic, Hayden Grayson, and recently retired Billy Bodeen. * Remember to keep Mike Johnson in your prayers, and thoughts over the holiday season. * Kristen Roberts has promised to do a KAWF show Christmas day, and to bring some "presents" with her. * Shane Martin, and VVV left the arena together after there caused ruckus,and have not been heard from even by Outlaw [Shane's teacher] since that day in Miami, Flordia. * Tristan Fate, Ethan McBride, and Maxime St Jean were all seen backstage at Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash. * The KAWF is currently trying to get in touch with Johnny Malibu, Brian Floyd, Frankie Cash, and Domination about working some possible future KAWF/SAWF matches. * Adam Caine not only walked out of his last KAWF match, but the KAWF in general bringing Tyler Rose with him after the show in Miami. More released on that in the near future. * One of the two KAWF stables will be getting a major push now that Kristen Roberts has the reigns.
