KAWF House Show, 9 June 2004

Kick Ass Wrestling Federation June 9th 2004 The house show which will PREVIEW WHAT'S TO COME From New Orleans, Louisiana Bell Time : 8 : 00 PM Ticket Prices : 10 $ for Adults, $ 4 dollars for Kids under 15. Hosted by : Vinny Pascarelli Live from The KAWF arena on downtown Canal Street Hey it's me again. Vinny here, and boy did we have a good house show for the fans in New Orleans tonight. This was probally the best house show the KAWF has ever had, and I'm not just saying that cause I work here. Okay I am saying that cause I work here, but none the less it was a hell of a show. As I entered the building I saw Boys Next Door on one side of the entrance doors, and Youth Gone Wrong on the other greeting people in there own little special ways. Occasionally the two teams would shout at each other, and even at one point ran across the crowd to get there hands on the other team, but was quickly broke up by security, luckily for the fans sake cause somebody definitely would of got hurt. Then once you got in the building you saw Lark Fenriz staring at a picture of Xavier Daniels without even blinking which gave me chills, and Xavier Orion was signing autographs for fans. I noticed his knee was taped up pretty big time. From there I went to my table at ringside, and I saw Kenzo Tagara in the ring trying to get a good feel for the ring by running back and forth, while stopping on a dime. Boy is he a freak .. I hope Karl Ferguson makes him tap out at the pay per view, and in tonight's main event. Kenzo then exited the ring, and we slowly got ready for the offical start of the show. Ringside people began to do last minute things, people filed into there seats, and Juan Delgado the brother of Jessica Delgado entered the ring to ring announce this shin dig. * DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING * The bell rang like 40 times trying to get everybody in the KAWF arena's attention, and then we started the show off with a bang. Juan announced Xavier Orion and he came out to a nice crowd pop. Orion worked the mic a bit about Chris Capri, his knee injury, and his past hunt for the Promotional title .. He then asked Kenzo to join him in the ring. Kenzo then came out, and the two shook hands before Orion told him that he wouldnt be teaming with him tonight cause of his knee. He wanted to be sure he was ready for the pay per view. Kenzo nodded out of respect, but Orion then went on to say he did find a replacement for himself though. So Kenzo wouldn't have to do a handicap match. Kenzo looked a little happier now than he did when Orion first told him he wouldn't be teaming with him tonight. Orion didn't tell us who the partner was though . .. He left us hanging, and the two faces went to the back with a nice pop from the crowd. MATCH 1 The Business Men Versus Blackbirds Referee : [name withheld] We then went from alot of fans popping to alot of fans booing as we got set for our opening bout. The black gangsters who call themselves the Blackbirds came out bickering with the crowd, and everybody knows the Business Men could care less about anything. Tommy Taylor started the match off for the Business Men being there ring leader, and Tony T started it off for the blackbirds. He is the smaller of the two birds. Both men began to lock up, but from the top of the ramp way we heard. HOLD THE F[CENSORED ON WILL YA . . . Both men turned to the top of the ramp way, and we saw Kai Lewis at the top with a microphone in hand. You can insert a nice heel pop here cause that's definitely what Kai gets. Kai then came down and entered the ring. He asked the Business Men if he could take there place, they said no. He then offered the business men a 1,000 dollars, and gave them the location of a sweet penthouse party on Bourbon later that night . . Tommy took the money and told Kai they were all his. He threw the mic out of the ring, and began to beg for both men to come at him at the same time while Business Men eased to the back. So our new match became Kai Lewis versus both members of the Blackbirds. Tony T, and JJ Steele .. . .Tony T ran at Kai who caught him with a spinning heel kick. JJ then tried to clothesline Kai being a bit bigger than the former title contender, but Kai ducked under and reached back hooking JJ for a hangman's neckbreaker. Kai hopped up to his feet quickly and screamed out to the crowd who responded with more boo's. Kai then picked Tony up and pushed him into the turnbuckles. Kai mounted Tony and began to pound him with hard right hands, from behind JJ grabbed Kai and tried to outsider edge him down to the floor, but Kai wiggled out getting himself back on the ropes, and jumped off turning in mid air hitting the bigger man with a middle rope Bull dog. The crowd popped even though they didn't like Kai at all. Kai nodded to the crowd, and went for a cover on JJ, but it was broke up by Tony T .. . .Tony then eased Kai up and chopped him across the chest. Kai absorbed the chop, and returned with one of his own. Tony backed up, and it gave Kai enough room to shoot a front kick into his chest, followed by a nice tilt o whirl slam. Kai went for another cover, and this time JJ broke it up .. .JJ picked Kai up and scoop slammed him down. JJ tried to follow with an elbow to the stomach, but Kai moved out of the way and got to his feet in time to catch JJ as he got up with a hard right hand, followed by a nice EXPLODER suplex. Kai was once again the only man standing in the ring. Kai grabbed Tony and pulled him over on top of JJ . .. .Kai then hit the ropes, and came back with a mini frog splash trying to pin them both. The Birds managed to kick out at two, and Kai just smiled. From there Kai grabbed Tony and threw him right over the top rope to the floor. Maybe these guys are birds after all cause he damn sure did fly. Kai then picked JJ up and began to slap him in the top of the head .. .. JJ tried to fight back with a few shots to the midsection, but after about three of them Kai caught his arm, and drove him down applying a crossface in the middle of the ring . .. JJ tried to fight it, but eventually tapped out to Brooklyn's finest. Winner : Kai Lewis Via : Crossface at 5 : 01 into the match Vinny's Thoughts : I wanted to see the Business Men pull there trickery, but I can't blame them for leaving since they knew they were going to win anyway. As for Kai, I wasn't positive he would squash these guys since it was a handicap, but oh boy was I wrong. Riley Young better watch out at the return. Kai Lewis is not a very happy man, and seems to be prime to take control of the Global Title division know matter who wins out of Kanic and Hayden. Kai left the ring to a nice heel pop, and the Blackbirds eventually gathered themselves enough to leave the ring also. The KAWF dancers then came out to try and get the positive reaction going again from the crowd, led by that girl Princess who has been seen with both the Natural Criminals out, and about . .. Boy do I love these girls, I mean every bone in there body I love, espically the one I have. They were just about done with there song, and out comes that stupid, stupid man Ronnie Jamieson. Without Lezkanic, which makes it even more stupid. He scared the girls out of the ring .. .Worked the mic a bit .. Hyping Hayden vs LezKanic part 1,000 and then left. I HATED THAT .. . I REALLY HATED THAT. We quickly hopped to our next match. MATCH 2 Scott Haggarty Versus El Tigre' Referee Zach Ward We then got into what we all knew would be El Tigre'e first actual match since he joined the KAWF travel team in three attempts at having one. All his others had been ruined by outside interferance, but we knew Haggarty would be more than happy to do battle with him cause of his past track record. Before the match got started there was a buzz in the crowd, as security cleared four front row seats. We then saw Tyler Rose, a lovely blonde, Adam Caine and Kyle Sommers take there seats in the front row. Tyler was into tje girl, Caine sat motionless eyeing the ring, and Sommers tried to start an El Tigre' chant which failed, before it even started. Haggarty rolled his shoulders blowing off the guys on the outside, and focused in on Tigre. Haggarty tried to lock up with Tigre', but El ducked under and did a go behind on Haggarty. Haggarty quickly reversed it and did a go behind of his own. He then lifted Tigre up and slammed him down face first on the mat. Haggarty rolled over to the front of Tigre'e body and put him in a front face lock. Tigre' got back to his feet, and Haggarty did a beautiful STALLING SUPLEX on the small mexican cruserweight. Haggarty then got up and began to stomp on Tigre' as the crowd really gave Con$ortium a hard time at ringside. Haggarty eventually picked Tigre up and guided him into the corner. Haggarty landed a chop to the chest of Tigre' followed by a hard clothesline which shot Tigre up onto the ropes in a laying position . ... .Haggarty backed up and then slammed a few ax handles into the chest of the mexican cruserweight before yanking him off the ropes, and snapping him down with a snapping DDT. Haggarty went for the cover, and Tigre' kicked out at two .. . Sommers stood up on the outside trying to root Tigre' on , but nobody else joined him in his efforts. Haggarty yanked Tigre' up and went for a body slam, but Tigre'e wiggled free and kicked Haggarty in the head dazing him. Tigre then did another Yazuka kick . .. Followed by another one .. Haggarty dropped to a knee, and Tigre'e caught him with a nice spinning kick to the head knocking him down. Tigre' went for a cover, but got shoved off at one. Sommers then began to hollar "Three more days" to Haggarty who was put in a chin lock in the middle of the ring, with his face being pointed towards the Con$ortium. Haggarty eventually got to a vertical base, and Tigre' broke the hold and landed a few chops across Haggarty's chest. Tigre then hit the ropes and came back with a flying crossbody attempt, but Haggarty caught him and did a beautiful _FALL AWAY SLAM_ on the smaller man. Both men were down briefly, but Haggarty got up fairly quick and began to stomp away on Tigre' again. Haggarty then lifted Tigre up who came back with a thrust chop .. . Tigre then tried for a suplex, but Haggarty blocked and landed a beautiful _BRAINBUSTER_ on the smaller man, quickly hooking the leg for a cover attempt, and victory. Winner : Scott Haggarty Via : Brainbuster suplex at 6 : 45 into the match Vinny's thoughts : Haggarty is gonna be a force in the KAWF, and that girl with Tyler Rose was winking at me I think. I wander if I can hit that ? Haggarty then quickly rolled out of the ring and stared at the Con$ortium. Caine stood up in his chair, as did Sommers while Rose almost without even seemingly noticing Haggarty rose his middle finger up against his cheek as he was talking to the girl, flipping Haggarty off, and at the same time keeping his focus on the lovely girl at his side. Sommer bickered with Haggarty, but he just turned around and headed to the back. Caine, and Sommers then sat back down in there seats awaiting for the show to continue. At this point I wandered where Jarrod Storm was . .. Ya know the guy who won the other day on Fever for this stable ? Hmmmmmmmmmm .. . I wander if the groups fighting, or maybe if Jarrod is trying to be an extra in one of Rose flicks. MATCH 3 Christopher Scarlet Versus Brath Referee Mike Riquiah As Scarlet, and the modern day warrior Brath entered the ring Kyle Sommers begin to boo both of them loudly standing out deeply amongst the crowdf. Scarlet exited the ring and begin to bicker with the Con$ortium, even catching the attention of Tyler Rose for a moment. Finally the referee convinced Scarlet to come back into the ring, and the two guys locked up. Brath threw Scarlet back, but as he charged in at the Golden Boy he moved out of the way, and pushed Brath into the turnbuckles. Brath then backed up and Scarlet caught him with a roundhouse kick to the back of the head which knocked him head first into the turnbuckles. Scarlet then grabbed Brath and began banging his head into the turnbuckle. Scarlet released Brath and as he wabbled out of the corner Scarlet hopped up on the middle turnbuckles, and jumped off landing a double ax handle on the crazily dressed man. The crowd popped, but Sommers was still very vocal against the Golden Boy who was standing over Brath very happy with how the match was going so far. Scarlet then helped Brath up, and nailed him with a beautiful butterfly suplex. The match went on for four more minutes of Scarlet showing he could suplex, and toss around Brath like he was a small bag of chips. Eventually though Brath countered with an eye poke, and a desperation inverted Russian Leg Drop. Brath caught his breathe and then did a beautiful corkscrew splash from the top rope on the Golden Boy, almost getting a three count . .. Brath then stayed on top of Scarlet with a chin lock trying to wear the Golden Boy down. Eventually the Golden Boy got up to a veritcal base, and managed to counter of the move straight into the Scarlet Stretch . .. F'n crosswing .. . Brath is screwed. Why couldn't Scarlet just counter with an elbow to the stomach or a belly to back suplex like most people ? The kid eventually tapped out after putting up a decent effort to get out of the hold. I mean where could he go ? He was hooked in that crosswing right in the middle of the ring, with even his mom at ringside going " Please give up son "..... Winner : Christopher Scarlet Via : Scarlet Stretch at 9 : 10 into the match Vinny's Thoughts : Get him Con$ortium . .Get him . .. Scarlet quickly released Brath and slid out of the ring on the opposite side of the Con$ortium, and headed up the ramp way trying to avoid the situation . .. Con$ortium then got up and left there seats, heading to the back I suppose. . .Maybe to go find Jarrod Storm. Sheez .. . . Before our next scheduled match the KAWF put on a six man tag match between three local indys by the names of Cedman, Riley Valentine, and Kevin Klass against three Japanese stars who were in town for a TOP reunion show to do the job to the main guys from TOP .. . These three guys were Koji Kumata, Shizo TanjI' and The Dark Dragon . .. The Japanese team won in thirty minutes, and the crowd was hot .. . Those japs are good. They carried the locals in my opinion, but that Riley guy did have a few good moves on the ropes using his balance. Once those guys cleared the ring Hayden Grayson came out, and thanked the crowd for keeping there support behind him. Hayden also worked the mic on Lezkanic, and Jean Paul Valley showing up last week in Fever. ... . Thus of course .. . JEAN PAUL VALLEY SHOWED UP AGAIN IN HIS HOMETOWN . .. The two then worked the mic together, doing a good job actually, and then left with the crowd chanting both there names. Valley also plugged the Super 8 tourney which he won the first time it came around. Cheap F'n plugs. Doesn't he know the KAWF is focused on there PPV right now ? He could of mentioned the tag team title match, or maybe the possibility of the KAWF getting a new cruserweight title. MAIN EVENT Karl Ferguson & Chris Capri Versus Kenzo Tagara & Xavier Daniels Referee Wayne West Ya you heard me right . .Daniels was Kenzo's new partner, and boy did it get a pop when the STREETLIFE champion came out to join forces with the japanese superstar. Daniels already has a win over Ferguson by DQ, and Ferguson was kinda happy to see a guy who he has unfinished business with come out, and join the show. The fans weren't to let down now by Orion not particpating .. . Capri and Tagara started the match off .. . They put on a wrestling clinic before Capri got the losing end and bailed on it tagging in Ferguson. Ferguson then entered, and quickly took Tagara down with a rolling leg takedown, followed by a spinning toe hold keeping the quick man off his feet. Ferguson kept it there for a while, but eventually Tagara maneuvered out and tagged in Daniels rolling away from the ex shoot fighter. Daniels came in as a power house. . Really dominating Fergy with high powered tosses, and Capri when he tried to stick his nose into the business got some as well. Daniels was really over tonight in Nawlins.. Daniels then tagged Kenzo back in and he went to work on Ferguson's head with alot of stiff kicks .. . Out of nowhere Lark Fenriz ran down and pulled Daniels off the apron. The two began to do battle as Tagara went for the tag in an empty corner . .. Tagara then turned around in time to catch a T - Bone from Ferguson who slowly rolled over and tagged in Capri. Capri tried to roll Kenzo up three different times and all three failed .. .Capri then went for a piledriver, but Tagara back body dropped him over. On the outside you heard * CRASHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH * The referee including everybody else in the arena looked over, and we saw Xavier Daniels was now added to the steel guard rail as a permanent piece thanks to Lark Fenriz. The referee begged for Lark to go to the back, and while his back was turned Kenzo Tagara hit the TALLOM STRIKE on Capri knocking him out .. . However Ferguson came into the ring with a steel chair handed to him by his trainer, and nailed Tagara with it. CRACK .. . . Ferguson then put Capri on top of Kenzo and the referee turned around to make the count. HEEL POPPPPPPPPPPPPPPP Kenzo did kick out at two though . . .Capri shook the cob webs out and slapped Kenzo in the Primo Press .. . Kenzo was still wounded from the sneak chair shot, and didn't tap, but indeed did pass out .. On the outside Daniels was fighting back now with Lark, and they hade made there way into the crowd to trade blows. Winner : Karl Ferguson & Chris Capri Via : Primo Press at 10 : 11 into the match Vinny's Thoughts : Good job cheating guys . .Real good cheaters you are. I was also glad to see Lark come out and continue stalking X just about everywhere he goes. The crowd continued to buzz as Karl Ferguson re entered the ring with that chair, and was about to nail Kenzo as Capri held him . .. But Xavier Orion slid into the ring with a barbwire baseball bat in hand, and ran both guys off .. .. Orion then helped Tagara up . .. and they dared Capri and Ferguson to enter the ring. The duo had been working well together, but they declined the offer and went to the back. Thus the show ended with the faces winning. See yall again one day after the _RETURN_ Enjoy * For the fans who stuck around after the show was apparently over The Business Men came out and did indeed wrestle this time against two of the Japanese men we saw earlier. They defeated Koiji and the Dark Dragon in a ten minute match in which both teams appeared to have alot of fun.
