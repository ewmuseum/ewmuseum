KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 8 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Well that was an interesting show wasn't it guys ? Lots of CLOSE matches, and tons of hard work being put into that thing. Congratulations to everybody who contributed, you did a damn good job. I loved it. Had some story line progress, good spots, and most importantly for that much material the show flowed really well. Now we move onto the next cycle. Most feuds are decided, but some are not. We still got some room to work some stuff out, so if you don't have anything drop me a line. I prolly got a rough draft of something cooked up for you. I lost my rough draft bookings when my AOL went crazy, but I had some of it mapped out in my mind already. That should stay the same. Let's just keep things interesting . .. No need to let the momentum of that show die down. I don't think it should be much of a problem. Also know that if your not active, I will cut you from the roster. You can stay on the list, but I have a waiting list now. . .No need to keep you on if you don't got the desire, or the time to participate. Others are willing to help us achieve our goal. Much Respect, Josh ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * The next supercard will be an outside event from Destin, Florida. The name of the event has yet to be released, and of course matches will be signed closer to the actual show occurring. Look for this to happen in December. * A nice warm thank you goes out to Sabbath, Lezkanic, Ben Tuckwell, and Ethan McBride for showing up at the supercard last night even though there not on the full time active rosters of the KAWF. * Please welcome Xavier Orion, Ricky J, JRG, Veronica Ludart, Roland Dean, Corey Hart, Boltz, and Mike Foyer to the active roster. I'm sure you will recognize at least one, or two of these names which now make us one of the most well rounded rosters in the business. * Orion, J, and JRG of course made an impact at Welcome Home. Orion with his light bulb fetish, and the other two making sure the Army knew they were coming for them. * There will be a KAWF women's title match at the next supercard, and some rumors are stirring saying there could be a men's title added to the mix as well. * The boys backstage praised Adam Lazarus, and Foodstamp for there effort in the Streetlife title match setting a good solid backdrop for the Promotional Title match which followed. * The KAWF calendar will go on sale at the end of November in time for the Christmas rush of shopping. A brand new KAWF hoodie will also debut around that time. In gray, or red. * The New number one contender Carson Ray will get his shot at the title at the October 28th show against new champion Vincent Case, of the prestigious Case wrestling Family. * Mike Pace was seen backstage at Welcome Home, and did indeed break down into tears at the half way point of the show. Outlaw, and Prison were seen at his side most of the night. * White Rose did a wonderful job as she returned to the microphone for the first time in over a year. * The crowd was great at the KAWF arena, and we will be back sooner rather than later. Plans are to make that our home once again. * The house show tour will feature Tokyo, Japan, Mexico City, Mexico, Melbourne, Australia, London, England, and Paris, France in the next few months. Many of your favorite KAWF stars will be working at least two of the shows, some might even do three out of five. * Welcome Home will be ready for DVD release by the end of the month. Special post match reactions will be on the DVD release. Also a bonus match which happened earlier in the day with an empty arena between soon to debut Roland Dean, and former KAWF commish Shane Martin. * Surprisingly no real injuries occurred at the supercard, just lot's of bumps, bruises, and some stitches. * The KAWF is planning an awards show for early next year. The KAWF is also in the process of planning the building of a youth center in downtown Monroe, in which the wrestlers will be encouraged to help with. * Jasmine Greene needed a police escort out of the building cause of ruckus fans, and angry KAWF wrestlers last night. * Carson Ray was spotted at a night club later on that night. On the other side of town Johnny Spades, and Foodstamp were seen at waffle house, even pausing from there meal to sign some autographs. * The KAWF is expecting big things from Orion, and Roland Dean who will both be making there in ring returns this month for the company. Both have history in the KAWF. * Rumors are circulating that Josie Saito might go into the valet game as well as wrestling in the KAWF very soon, but who would she manage ? One of the new guys ? Tuckwell ? Frankie Cash ? . . .. .hmmm ? * A lot have challenges have been issued, and probably will be booked, but nobody knows when the challenges will be booked. Juan has a Mexican deathmatch challenge out to Ben Tuckwell, while Leon Corella / Michael Keathley are wanting to have a body bag match. It seems Nightstalker will also continue his open challenges since he remain undefeated at the supercard. * The KAWF is currently only accepting tag teams, and women wrestlers.
