PAY PER VIEW LINE UP _THE RETURN_ Live from Houston, Texas on June 13th Expected to take place infront of a sold out arena. Announcers are the lovely White Rose, Frankie Tha Pimp, and Russell Cunningham Opening Bout Strict Rules Match Karl Ferguson Versus Kenzo Tagara Insight - Two of the best technical wrestlers in the KAWF will go head to head to solve there problems with one another the old fashion way. By trying to out wrestle the guy they have a problem with. Faraji will be in the corner of Ferguson, but has stated he will stay out of things, and let the boys settle this one on there own. All wrestling rules will be strictly enforced, as we try to find out exactly who is the _BETTER_ Wrestler. Special Attraction Match Six man action Adam Caine, Kyle Sommers, Tyler Rose Versus Scott Haggarty, Christopher Scarlet, Denny Langham Insight - 75 percent of the Con$ortium will take on the man in which they have targetted Chris Scarlet, and two of the newer members of the KAWF Scott Haggarty, and Denny Langham who were begged to come in by the KAWF President himself to help up hold some justice so to say. This will be a spotlight match basically showcasing what bright future the KAWF has in the upcoming months. All six of these men could be future champions of some sorts. Third Bout Grudge Match Kai Lewis Versus Riley Young Insight - If Kai wins, he will also be special referee in the Main Event thanks to the orders coming straight from Mike Johnson. Both these men have a strong disliking for one another, and this match should be the most intense match on the show which is not for a title, and you might could even include the matches which are for a title. Riley has been looking good as of late, and we all know who love KAWF wrestling just how dangerous Kai Lewis can be. The punches and kicks will definitely be flying at a rapid pace in this one. Both men want to show the world they are indeed better than the other one. Expect Kristen Roberts to be watching this one very close. Fourth Bout Streetlife Title Match Seven Tables of Fear Lark Fenriz Versus Mike Harris Versus Xavier Daniels [C] Insight - This will take place VIA Satellite at the empty KAWF arena in New Orleans, Louisiana. The stips to this match are crazy, and won't be known to the wrestlers till they show up at the empty arena, but none the less look for this to be the craziest match of the week as nobody is happy with what has been going on in this psycotic division. Whether it be the screw job endings, or the personal life actions of Lark Fenriz against the others. Nobody is happy, and that could leave to alot of happy KAWF fans if you know what I am saying. Fifth Bout Tag Team Title Elimination Match Boys Next Door Versus Youth Gone Wrong Versus True All Stars Versus Natural Crminals Insight - The Tag Team titles will finally be on the line for the first time since the KAWF re opened it's doors, and these our the four teams which made it to this point by scheming, and hard work. Boys Next Door our former KAWF Champions, and the odds on favorite going in, but none the less the three other teams are hungry, and looking to get there hands on some KAWF gold for the very time. Don't be suprise if BND doesn't come out of the Return with the gold over there shoulder..Any of these four teams could be the next KAWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS OF THE WORLD. Sixth Bout Four Corners Match Promotional Title Match Chris Capri [C] Versus Xavier Orion Insight - Capri is forced to put up, or shut up against the not quite completly healthy Xavier Orion. Capri will defend his title in a four corners match against one of the men he has disrespected, bad mouthed, and even attacked in the early goings of the KAWF. Can Orion overcome his knee, and return to his winning ways in which in the last verion of the KAWF almost had him capture this same title ? Or will Capri be to much and simply laugh in the Bi Polar brawlers face ? MAIN EVENT Steel Cage Match Special referee Riley Young Global Title on the line Lezkanic Versus Hayden Grayson Insight - Finally we will have a Global Champion in the KAWF, and the only way we will know is if somebody climbs the cage, or executes a pinfall. There will be NO EXITING THREW THE DOOR OF THE CAGE for this one as both warrior's are being forced to put up there best efforts. Riley Young will be calling the match, and that could add even more fireworks to this already fire enduced match up for the biggest title in the wrestling game to date.
