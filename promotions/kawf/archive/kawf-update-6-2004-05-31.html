KICK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents **************************** 1 - Notes from the desk - Out of Character 2 - News - In Character 3 - Roster & Records 4 - Various Rankings, and Title Holders 5 - Line ups 6 - Final Notes ***************** 1 - Notes from the Office ****************** Not to bad of a week, but for this particular show I was hoping for even better. That could of been the best show yet, and we dropped the ball as a team just a bit, but none the less it was still a fairly good end product. We had a few guys not flash, and a few guys not get in segments, but I think we did a good job of covering up the few slip ups, and making it a good show. For that I say thank you to all the guys. There is only one more show before the PPV, and then we will see what all of our hard work this cycle has led us to. Were not sure when the next PPV will be after this one, so this is definitely time to make an _IMPACT_. The _RETURN_ should be one of the best KAWF ppvs of all time if you just look at the poiental, and talent on the card. On with the Update. Yall have had your horns tooted enough this week =) Much Respect, Josh ************************ 2 -News from the rumor bin ************************* * At the PPV one lucky fan will win a date with "The Golden Boy" Christopher Scarlett who will be on the Return line up in six man action. The auction for this will occur this week before Fever, more than likely at the KAWF arena on Thursday Night. The person will have lunch with Christopher the day of the PPV, get to go backstage with him in his locker room. * The KAWF is currently trying to get in touch with Brian Floyd, Ktulu, Rosco Riggins, Frankie Cash, and Shane Quartermaine about possibly working some future KAWF matches. * The KAWF will continue there southern tour after the PPV mainly cause of all the sold out arenas, and hype they have been getting in the south. Little Rock is being considered for the next big KAWF Supercard or PPV. * KAWF is still in talks with Japan, and Mexico about working some tours there in the future. * The Hollywood Syndicate's contract has been terminated for the time being cause of a loop hole found by the President himself. There has been nothing said on what Mike is going to do with them in the weeks to come. Some are saying he wants them out the door, and some are saying he will resign them. * Sammy Griffin will be representing KAWF in the Rumble Pro Inviational next month. * HBO is very happy with the KAWF ratings, and progress so far. * Tommy Flames and Xavier Orion will both be wrestling this week at 80 percent. * Rumor is the KAWF will be bringing in the Cruserweight Title after the PPV, and a very big name in the business is the main reason Mike is bringing it in. * Congratulations to the True All Stars on earning the fourth, and final spot in the PPV fatal four way match for the titles. * Kristen Roberts has been quoted as saying " Kai Lewis is a dead man " After what he did on Fever last weekend to her main man Riley Young. * Kenzo Tagara has flown home this week to concentrate, and focus for his match up with Karl Ferguson at the PPV. * Jean Paul Valley said he might return to a KAWF show in the very near future. * KAWF is currently looking into signing some more cruserweight talent to add to there already very good crop of wrestlers in that area. The weight range has been said to be 225 pounds and under. * Xavier Daniels has reporter to the KAWF offices that Lark Fenriz has been stalking him, but no charges have been brought up. X is wanting to handle this on his own, which could be good or bad news. * The parts for the Steel Cage that will be used in the main event at the PPV arrived today from a place in Detroit, Michigan. * Jazz Madison was not pleased with Mike Johnson's words to him over the phone at the Start of Fever. Something big could happen if those two ego's ever meet in person. * The NEW stable which formed last week in Monroe, Louisiana has already caused a huge stir amongst the KAWF locker room. Christopher Scarlet, Tommy Flames, and The Boys Next Door have been real vocal saying that they would clean house if Mike Johnson needed them to. * Ethan McBride was once again seen backstage at the KAWF house show last week in Monroe. * Karl Ferguson was seen throwing chairs, tipping tables, and cussing loudly backstage in New Orleans last week after Kenzo's confrontation with him. * Both members of the Natural Criminals were seen with KAWF dancer Princess on Bourbon after the show. * Ronnie Jamieson has refused treatment from KAWF doctor Jeremy Rivers. * Denny Langham & Scott Haggarty were brought into the KAWF to help Chris Scarlet hold up the honor in which is part of being on the KAWF roster against the newly arrived heel stable in the KAWF. * KAWF War Stories will still once again re start after the KAWF PPV. It will be hosted by Frankie Tha Pimp, and Russell Cunningham with Samuel Smith backstage doing interviews. * Former KAWF cruserweight star Bungle the Gay Bear recently got married in London, England. * White Rose will be joining the boys at the Pay Per View to help announce. ************************** 3 -
