KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 9 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information That wasn't one of our best shows, but it put us to a good transition point for this next Fever. After this next show just about all of you will have a feud of some sorts. The feud that we will focus on leading us to the next supercard which will be at the end of December. Lord knows I want everybody to be involved. It might not be the feud you WANT, but I won't keep you on the on the sidelines if your active. I don't care what I have to do. The main thing was it came out at a decent time [I think with mid terms, and weather problems], had some good segments, you guys got see where you stand, and where the company stands. Now we can move on out of the shadows of Welcome Home, and build another good solid cycle. I have no doubts in my mind that we will be able to do that. We added another tag team, and I am in talks with another female wrestler. Were really starting to get a packed roster. This is becoming one of the better rosters we have had in the KAWF. I mean that one, not bull shitting you . .. this time at least. Read the update. Yall know I like to put lots of goodies in here. This company wishes him nothing, but the best/ * Ben Tuckwell has also joined the SPW, and decided to wrestle there full time while also doing a bit more for the KAWF. Good to see Tuckwell get his wrestling drive back, while also taking Carson Ray under his wing to help him reach the next level. * Xavier Orion has still not officially signed a KAWF contract, but is rumored to be at the show in Monroe to take on Lazarus anyway. Leads us to believe the contract will be signed during the week. Kai Lewis was very upset backstage about how his match ended with Mckay cause of Orion. Look to see if that blows up anymore as well, since Orion is definitely not here looking for friends. Adam Lazarus also was very upset about some of the backstage actions. * Rumors have it Leon Corella sent Mike Foyer after Juan Rodriguez at Fever. Juan still has a challenge out to Tuckwell for a mexican deathmatch, but now has a bigger man fresh on his tail for the time being. * Leon Corella has been fined two pay days for his actions against the referee at the start of last week's Fever. He will also not be in action in Monroe, Louisiana because of this. Actions like that will not be tolerated. The fine would of been more, but the video tape did indeed show the referee was wrong. Next time let us handle it in house Mr Corella. If you were only coming to the show to see Corella, and you want your money back the KAWF is offering that option. * More on Leon Corella who is slowly becoming the most hated man on the roster. He was seen leaving the arena with Josie Saito at the end of the night. Camera's tried to follow, but had no such luck on finding out details. More on that to come we believe. * Rumors were flying that the KAWF would show up on the next ICWF pay per view to take part in a special royal rumble event, but these rumors have been shot down by Mike Pace saying "Were gonna focus in house for a while, and let the other company's do there thing. We have a lot on our plate till the end of the year." * Roland Dean seemed to be distracted in his return to the ring. Personal issues could be the reasoning behind that says people close to the situation. * Same match. Different spin. Red Hood on the other hand seemed very focused in that triple threat match, and apparently has the bite to go along with his bark. * Adam Lazarus recently won the SPW universal title. Currently Adam is holding gold in both KAWF, and SPW. The man from Baton Rouge is really making his mark this year on the wrestling scene. * Johnny Spades stayed after the show in New Orleans, and talked to a lot of the fans about Katrina, Wrestling, and life in general. No fees, just Spades donating his time. * Both Brady Lowell, and Corey Hart put on a hell of a match. Office members are already screaming for a rematch considering Hart's knee got hurt, causing the obvious end to that match up. Hart is fine, but did seek medical treatment after the match. * Speaking of medical treatment surprisingly the roster is doing well after Welcome Home with just some minor injuries. The medical staff was worried about McKenna, but x ray's showed up negative. * The KAWF is currently only accepting tag teams, and women wrestlers. The rest of the roster is closed. * The location of the next supercard has already been given. That being Destin, Florida, but now it has been brought to our attention that the name of the Supercard will be given next update. This will be an out door event of course in December of all months, so bring your blankets. * The boys backstage have been really impressed by Foodstamps in ring production as of late. Sighting how much he has improved since he first started, but Foodstamp hasn't made to many friends from all accounts. * Mike "The always Right" Johnson could be showing up at a future event to say hello to some of his old friends. Mike is the original owner, and president of the KAWF for those that didn't know. * The KAWF is considering having a tournament really soon for a new KAWF title. The title would be ranked second in the single men's division, only behind the Promotional Title. * The house show tour kicks off really soon in Japan. More information will be given on future house shows soon as the KAWF lets the rest of the world know there back in the business. * Kyle Sommers & Jasmine Greene were not at Fever, but Frankie Cash was seen by the entrance door all night waiting for them. Were not sure if to ask questions, or to get revenge. * Carson Ray was seen getting thrown out of a local establishment on Bourbon Street after Fever. Ben Tuckwell was able to talk to the police, and not get any charges filed on his new protege, and the current number one contender. Let's hope his head is in the right place by the time he get's to Monroe. * Most KAWF wrestlers took a sight seeing tour of New Orleans before the show. You could tell all were truly touched by what they saw. Michael Keathley of all people, even gave some money to one of the foundations down there which encouraged other guys like Frankie Cash, Johnny Spades, Adam Lazarus, and Johnny Pain to do the same. * Be sure to look at the line up dates closely here in the next few weeks. Suddenly he would grab a random intern wandering past and yank him by his collar until they were face to face.] Corella- Where is the Referee from Welcome Home, Ref West ? [The pimple faced kid would fumble with words before Corella suddenly snapped at him.] Corella- ANSWER ME DAMN YOU! [The kid stuttered in fear as he spoke.] Intern- He's down the hall by the locker room, prepping for his spot tonight! PLEASE DON'T HURT ME! [Corella slung the intern hard into the wall and walked past the kid, his lip twitching with anger.] [The Camera would suddenly cut to a view of a middle aged man wearing a black and white striped referee shirt, black khakis, and a good pair of sneakers. He was sitting on a stool reading the KAWF Referee guideline manual as he had done a thousand times over before his spot. Just as he reached into a nearby Krispy Kreme box to grab a donut, a shiney leather shoe would collide with the side of his head, sending him flying out of the chair and sprawling to the floor, donuts flying everywhere!.] [The ref's eyes would widen as Corella stepped into view on camera, hulking over him.] Ref- Mr. Corella! You gotta' understand! I didn't see your foot on the rope until AFTER the third count! I SWEAR! [Corella swooped down, grabbing the man by his collar and wrenching him to his feet. Quickly slamming his back against the wall, he would get right in his face. His words dripped with poison, and hung in the air like frozen ice.] Corella- You... dumb... blind... ignorant piece of shit... Do you have ANY idea how much that match meant to me? DO YOU?! [The ref nodded quickly.] Ref- It was a number one Contender spot! You gotta' understand I... URK! [Corella quickly belted a tightly curled fist into the man's guts.] Corella- NO YOU DON'T UNDERSTAND! Don't you EVER pretend you understand ANYTHING you ignorant shit! [The Perfect One now shook with rage as he stared the man eye to eye.] Corella- That was the biggest night YET for KAWF... A win there would have elevated my Career to new HEIGHTS! and you can't even look to see if a FUCKING FOOT IS ON THE ROPES! [The Ref suddenly shot Corella a stern glance.] Ref- WELL IF YOU WERE A BETTER WRESTLER YOU WOULD HAVE KICKED OUT INSTEAD OF LOSING AND COMING HERE TO BEAT ON SOMEBODY WHO CAN'T FIGHT BACK! [Corella's head snapped back at bit, shocked by the sudden show of courage. He then whipped his head forward with a bone jarring, nose crushing headbutt to the man's face. Blood immediately began pouring down the ref's face. Corella then grabbed a fistfull of the man's grey hair, and began walking him down the hall. Throwing him through a set of double doors the two walked into the parking lot!] Corella- You just chose the WRONG fucker to get brave with... [Belting the ref in the ribs with a stiff body blow, Corella walked the Ref to a nearby car. Rearing the man's head back he slammed his face down hard on the hood, a cry of pain his reward. Gripping the back of the man's pants, Corella turned a full 360 degrees, stepping back from the car and then right into it, slinging the Referee head first into the driver's side window.] [There he'd leave him to hang, bleeding, unconscious, and possibly comatose.] Corella- Now, time to have a chat with the boss.... *************************************************************************************************************** * ADAM LAZARUS REACTION [As Saturday Night Fever draws to a close, we find ourselves backstage, inside the locker room of Adam Lazarus.
