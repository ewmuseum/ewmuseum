KICK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents **************************** 1 - Notes from the desk - Out of Character 2 - News - In Character 3 - Roster & Records 4 - Various Rankings, and Title Holders 5 - Line ups 6 - Final Notes ***************** 1 - Notes from the Office ****************** Boy did I need those few days off after the Fourth of July. Sheez. I love the summer. God almighty do I love the summer. Time to get back to work though. The mini vacation is over for now. I got a trip for Mexico planned in a month or so, but until then Im going to be saving alot of money, and keeping my nose out of trouble. After this weekend I prolly need to lay low, I think I pissed alot of people off actually. Grouches. I liked how the results came over. I liked almost every segment, and thought every match did what it was supposed to do. I'm usually never happy with the shows, thinking they can always be better, but with that said I think this was one of our smoothest Fever's yet. Not every match was an epic or anything, but were a weekly fed. NOT EVERY MATCH IS SUPPOSED TO BE A DAMN EPIC. If it was by the time we got to PPV's you guys would be spoiled. We would hate to do that, now wouldn't we ? For the record as well I changed the end to that Rose and Griffin match about 20 times. You guys both deserve a shot at the cruserweight title. Keep up the good work fellas. You guys make my job hard, but truthfully that's something you won't ever see me complain about. Much Respect, Josh * Note that Fever this week will be an outside event. Strat accordingly. ************************ 2 -News from the rumor bin ************************* * Johnny Sledge has STILL not signed an actual KAWF contract. There has been no word as to whether or not he wants to sign a contract after what occured on Fever with Flames, and Ronnie Jamieson stealing his promised spotlight. * IGA superstar Todd Bishop was seen backstage at Fever last Saturday to talk to a few of the fellas. * The eastcoast has been confirmed as the next tour swing for the KAWF. Some dates later in the year have also been confirmed for Japan, and Mexico. * Mike Johnson's birthday bash will feature a Ironman match as a main event instead of the previously rumored steel cage. * Christopher Scarlet is out on medical leave after the attack Con$ortium did to him at the house show in El Dorado. * Congratulations to all the guys on the KAWF roster who made the Mid Year awards was sent out via EMAIL by Mike Johnson. He was espically proud of upcomers like Xavier Daniels, Kenzo Tagara, Chris Capri, and Karl Ferguson. * When Fever went off the air the brawl continued, and was highlighted by a stage dive off the top of the ramp way into the crowd by Riley Young who landed a twisting moonsault on both Hayden Grayson, and Karl Ferguson. * The _RETURN_ should be ready for DVD in about two months. The production team is working very hard to get it out as quickly as possible, while re doing all the imaging, and adding some post match interviews from several of the KAWF superstars. * Boys Next Door, and Youth Gone Wrong fought in the parking lot Saturday Night in Birmingham, and was almost arrested by Birmingham police until Kristen Roberts showed up, and talked them out of doing so. Each team was handcuffed though. All this occured after the tag team title match, and cruserweight matches. Which could explain why YGW didn't come down to help Riley Young at the end of the show. * Colt Montana just came back to the states from promoting future KAWF events in Mexico. It has been said stuff went very well with our friends below the border. * Rumor is a heel stable will debut next week, or the week after on KAWF television. The KAWF comitee isn't to thrilled about this. * The KAWF arena in New Orleans is currently getting 2,000 more seats added on for the next time KAWF comes home to it's headquarters. * The Turnbow Brothers got kicked out of yet another charity golf tournament last weekend, this one being in Augusta. Luckily for the KAWF it wasn't the Masters golf course, or there could of been hell to pay. Thank God for local golf courses. * Frankie Tha Pimp will be sponsering a Mrs Nude KAWF event in Miami, Flordia the week of the Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash PPV in late August. Mikes Hard Lemonade has signed on to help promote it since Frankie does so much promoting for them. Outlaw's wife Lady Love, and Steve Greedy's ex wife Ginger Dupree have both signed on to be apart of the event. We will let you know more in the near future. * KAWF is currently being very picky with who they accept to the active roster for the time being. The offices feel they have pretty much reached there maximum number of people for the time being. Only certain exceptions will be made. * Tommy Flames passed a physical with flying colors the other day showing the world his attitude isn't the only thing that's changed. So has his knee. * An IGA vs KAWF supercard is almost offically worked out, and is in the final stages of discussion. * KAWF has recently tried to contact Frankie Cash, Rick Marley, Ktulu, Shane Destiny,and Mad Hayashi about working some future matches for the company. * Kristen Roberts has stated the KAWF might have a diva calander in the works by mid September for the 2005 season. * Natural Criminals will return to action in two weeks. They haven't been hurt, or suspended, just very occupied with the KAWF dancers. * Congrats to the True All Stars who became Tag Team champions on Fever. Same can be said to Big Smoothy for getting accepted to the KAWF roster in the place of the injured Christopher Scarlet. * Randy Livingston's nose was indeed broke by Kenzo Tagara's palm strike to the face when he was trying to catch Randy coming in. Randy stated he should be back in a month or so to work more KAWF shows if they like. * Hayden Grayson was seen taking batting practice with the Birmingham Barons while the KAWF was in town for Fever. We do not have to worry about Hayden leaving wrestling for baseball. That's for sure. * The monster LezKanic recently won a toughman contest in Little Rock, Arkansas in which afterwards he got into a verbal spat with newfound enemy Derek Douglas who was in the audience. * Hollywood Syndicate recently tried to contact Mike Johnson about renewing there contracts, but were put on hold for over an hour before they eventually hung up. * There is still a contract out for 250,000 dollars for anybody who can pin Karl Ferguson. Even though he lost on Fever, he did not get pinned. * Tyler Rose is in the process of making a new Adult Film. * Justin Cage back is currently bothering him after the stage dive he took courtesy of BND's Garrett Jax. ************************** 3 -
