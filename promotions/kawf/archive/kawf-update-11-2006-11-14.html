| | / / / \ \ | ___| | |/ / / /\ \ \ /| |_ | \ / /__\ \ \ __ / | _| | KICK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION | |__| \__\__/ \__\___/\___/ |__| Update # 11 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup all ? I got to do my pre writing this week. My school work wasn't to hectic, and I have been broke so spending some time at the house, but my new sports betting problem caused the results to be a tad late, not to be bad though with some help from Rob, and Shayne. I am hoping to get a hold of my results once this semester ends. Next school semester I am only going Tuesday, and Thursday should help.. I cleaned out some of the dead weight this week as well. Cutting a few guys, and signed three fresh faces. All three are very good writers so I'm looking forward to the upgrade in talent. I also have two guys in mind I'll prolly cut next week. Should _sure_ up the roster. I am still accepting women, and tag teams. I'd like to get two more a piece, and I might even allow a singles wrestler to come in if they have a good solid angle to discuss. I do have my ears open. We have two Fevers', and two house shows till the supercard. Let's make things come together. I know we will, but for some reason it still makes me feel good to say it. Let's continue on with the update now, but as always you know how to get in touch with me if you need me. I'm not hard to find. Much Respect, Josh * I need some match writers on a more consistent basis please contact _ME_ if you can help. I didn't get any responses last week, so I'm going to leave this up here one more week. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * Two huge matches have been signed for "A Cold Day in Paradise" already, and two more will be signed next update. This supercard is shaping up to be a really good show. Let's just hope the weather stay's good in Destin. KAWF wants to bring the storm, not Florida for this out door event. * Xavier Orion disappointingly has not signed a contract as of yet. Thus him not being booked on any of the upcoming shows. We apologize for this, but were trying to cut down on our risk. We will continue to effort this. * Outlaw has promised he was serious on Fever when he said he wants more discipline on the shows. His comments seem to be directed towards the group of Corella, Saito, and Foyer more so than some of the other guys. He is very un - happy with some of the backstage antics, and post match attacks. * Corey Hart was visually disappointed when he was told he would not be in the Mason Dixon Title tourney cause of his knee, but did brighten up somewhat when he was told He would get first shot at the champion, and be the special referee for the title match. At least he knows the company is willing to put some stock in a healthy version of Corey Hart. * The company is very close to signing a triple threat women's match for the title at the supercard cause of the warfare amongst the three ladies over the last few shows. * Frankie Cash has indeed walked out on the company as you saw on Fever. This was not a work. Frankie is gone. No news on whether, or not the company will allow him to come back if he changes his mind down the road. * Tony Touch, and The Russian Powers have been released. They were quickly replaced by Cyn Bailus, David Irvine, and Eddie James. Two more people are rumored to be on the KAWF hot seat. * Wayne West, the referee in which came back from the ruthless attack of Leon Corella returned last week, but surprised us all when he did what he did in the main event. The KAWF is not going to fire him, since he is more than likely scared for his well being, but is no longer allowing him to work Carson Ray, or Leon Corella matches. Johnny Spades may also have a problem with him after Fever. * The KAWF has officially signed a former WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT champion to there roster, but there is currently no news on when this former champion will debut. * The KAWF is currently viewing applications for wrestlers, but for limited space only. We know we what we want, and our looking for THAT in particular. * The KAWF issues an apology to Amy Vickers for letting the false news out of she is Adam Lazarus girlfriend. That should of been edited before the update went to print by the staff. * The KAWF will take part in Wrestlebowl this year. They will be apart if the company challenge matches, and will have at least one showcase match. IW, IGA, and SPW are also apart of Wrestlebowl so far. * Nobody is sure who hit who with a chair during the Michael Keathley vs Ricky J match when the lights went off, but the victory will stand. Rumors are circulating it could have been Keathley, and J themselves .. or possibly there tag team partners. Maybe even a _new_ team, nobody is sure. * On related news the KAWF doctors have still not been able to confirm the injury report the doctor of Mark McKenna sent them, but don't think the case is worth going to court over considering the Army is still active members of the roster. The X rays we have though from the home grown doctor of McKenna are negative. Take it for what it's worth. * Next Cycle Ben Tuckwell has agreed to work a lot more shows for the company. Help giving it some much needed star power, to go along with a few of the names we already have. To get your new names seen, you have to have some people the fans already want to see. * New merchandise, and dvds will be coming out soon. We hope the sells pick up a bit from last month with Welcome Home now being available for release. With the buzz about the Promotional, and Streetlife title matches on that show we are almost positive they will be. * Carson Ray seemed in a panic after Fever. The vibe we were getting didn't revolve around wrestling either. He left with Ben Tuckwell trying to keep his emotions under control. * Knee High, former wrestling manager has been hired to do some janitor work. The famous midget has been out of work for a few years now, and we are trying to help the old fan favorite out as much as we can with our limited money situation. We hope you all caught a glimpse of him backstage at Fever. * Vincent Case broke kayfabe after the show Saturday to sign autographs for six mentally challenge kids who came to the show, and also allowed them to play with his Promotional Title. * The KAWF calendars come out next week. Were not telling you which month these people are, but the twelve people involved include - Vincent Case, Jasmine Greene, Adam Lazarus, Obsolete Army w Dee, Leon Corella, Nikki Glass, Josie Saito, KAWF announcing crew, Outlaw, Johnny Pain, Johnny Spades, and the now gone Frankie Cash. * Tickets sells are going good for future KAWF house shows in France, and Australia. We are really looking forward to having the Mason Dixon Title tournament be apart of the house show in France. Vincent Case also might defend his title at this show. ******************** 3 - Roster ******************* CURRENT KAWF MAINSTAYS Daddy Appearance : 6'2 218 Style : Stiff The Red Hood Appearance : 6'0 NOVEMBER 25TH FROM WESTMINISTER, SOUTH CAROLINA THE KAWF WILL BE HOSTING SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER FROM BIBB STREET ARENA MAXIMUM ATTENDANCE - 5,000 EXPECTED ATTENDACE - 2,000 Deadlines : On cards /Strats will be due November 22nd by 11 PM Central time Role plays will be due by November 23rd by 8 PM Central time . Well lookie here, lookie here. The KAWF going to a place they have never been to in there rich history of pro wrestling. Westminister, South Carolina here we come. We hope to see some Southern Hospitality continue as we take our company's business to the south east for the time being. A cold Day in Paradise is not very far away, and from here on out business is going to pick up. Guarantee. In the opening bout we will have a _special attraction_ double DEBUT match between two men the KAWF has not seen with there own eyes, but did very extensive research on which made there eyes open wide. "WHITE GANGSTA" EDDIE JAMES will bring his rugged, tough style to the arena as he takes on a South Carolina man by the name of DAVID IRVINE. Will the crowd support be enough for IRVINE ? Or will EDDIE show him how they do it in the hood ? Rough n tough baby. The night will continue as we see the women of the KAWF return to the ring. Last show we got to see them all go to war verbally, and mentally, but this week we see them physically. NIKKI GLASS comes back to the ring, fresh off of a win over the vicious JOSIE SAITO, and will take on the debuting VERONICA LUDART. We say debuting, but she has been with the company for over a month jocking for good position to get THIS match. Now we have it, and now we see more of what these women bring to the table. Then we will have a STREETLIFE title match as ADAM LAZARUS defends his title for the time since winning it, this time against KAI A LEWIS who is looking to regain some of his old dominance by picking up another title for his resume. Some are saying ORION will ruin this match, but were going to try our damndest to make sure that doesn't happen. We want to see a winner as bad as you all do we promise. MIKEY MCKAY will also be watched closely cause of the tension he has with both men. Then right before we go to intermission we will hear from the team of EXTREME INSANITY as they prepare for the MASON DIXON TITLE TOURNY. Both just two weeks from there matches, we assume will have a lot to say. Thoughts on the rumble ? On the tournament ? On the thought of fighting each other possibly ? Should be very interesting for the viewers at home. We come back from intermission into a straight SHOWCASE match. This six man tag match will keep the crowd on the edge of there seat, and make there voices go hoarse the next day. On one side of the ring you will see LEON CORELLA, MIKE FOYER, & BRADY LOWELL then on the other you have JUAN RODRIGUEZ, CARSON RAY, & BEN TUCKWELL. Making matters even more exciting at the booth we will have special guest JOHNNY SPADES, and Promotional Champion VINCENT CASE. Yes, both of them at the same time. No were not sure what will happen. Then in the main event the MASON DIXON TITLE TOURNAMENT starts off. Round One will finish on Next Fever in Lakeland, Florida, but this Fever will end with possibly the best match of the first round. THE RED HOOD will take his charisma into the ring with a man he has already done battle with on a few occasions the unique FOODSTAMP has seemingly changed in the last few weeks, but has it been for the better ? You come be the judge. There you have it. Good solid card line up. Lot's of star power. Possibility for some blood. And hot women. If that's not enough to get you to spend a LITTLE bit of money, well be a smook, and buy it on DVD when you hear the Internet buzz. Fine by us, but we do recommend you going. Remember a COLD DAY IN PARADISE isn't far away. The words will be shouted, and the intensity will be high. Again we guarantee.
