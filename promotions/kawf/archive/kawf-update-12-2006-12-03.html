KAWF "The best mix of old fashion, and extreme wrestling in E - Wrestling" Update # 12 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information One more Fever, and one more house show until "A Cold Day in Paradise" let's make sure we wrap up what angles we need to. We got some big plans for early 2007, but we can't look past this supercard to that stuff. We have some story lines here that I would put up against any story lines in the hobby right now. We might not be the best federation, or the most known federation, but we make sure the majority of the roster is having fun. We also put out a lot more stuff than most federations out there. You all should be proud. I am. Which is why I don't think we will look past this supercard, and make sure it's as good as Welcome Home. I trimmed some roster a little more this week, it's almost there. We are almost set from top to bottom. It's really shaping up to be one of the better rosters we have had in the history of the KAWF if you look at it from top to bottom. That makes a lot of the matches hard to judge, but I can't think of any fed heads who would complain about that problem. Much Respect, Josh * I'm still addicted to sports gambling. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * Little Rock, Arkansas has been named as the location for the next KAWF supercard in 2007. Both wrestlers lips are sealed. * Vincent Case will not arrive in Florida until the 29th of December electing to skip the Lakeland show, and stay in Paris up until the house show. Remember this is an Independent federation. We get promises for a certain amount of bookings, but nobody is booked for every single show. Nobody. * The KAWF has not named the people who will represent them in Wrestlebowl, but has promised they will have a team represent them at the event in January. * Apathy has become the newest female to the KAWF roster. Chris Capri has also came back to the KAWF. Capri is a former secondary title holder in the KAWF. * Ben Tuckwell & Carson Ray did leave the arena together even amongst the problems. Carson Ray is apparently in over his head with some gambling problems. * The KAWF arena is receiving some small upgrades. The lighting, and sound system should be better by the time the KAWF returns to Monroe, Louisiana. * Roland Dean has been released from the KAWF due to personal problems. Jarrod Storm has left the KAWF now that Frankie Cash has walked out on the promotion. Storm apparently just came to help Sommer's get his hands on Cash. * Kyle Sommers has took Jarrod's place in the Mason Dixon Title Tournament. He will now face Johnny Pain in what should be a wrestling clinic. * Perfection was very upset when they left the arena earlier tonight cause of the actions of Spade, and Vincent Case. * Johnny Spades was quoted as saying " I promise you I'm gonna beat Vincent like he's never been beaten before" to the South Carolina papers early Friday morning. He went on to say he really wants to represent the KAWF as there top wrestler. * Xavier Orion has officially signed a KAWF contract. If he does NOT honor his contract extreme measures will be taken. * The KAWF has said they will have at least seven active tag teams next cycle making there tag team division one of the best in the sport. * The KAWF calendars are now out for the public to buy. Please make them apart of your Christmas shopping. * If you have not picked up your holiday ham, and turkeys you still can at the KAWF headquarters in Monroe, Louisiana. All wrestlers, and employees get one of each courtesy of Mac's Fresh Market. * Various rumors are circulating backstage about who the Red Hood may be, and who Foodstamp's personal trainer could possibly be. To many names to even speculate have been mentioned. * The KAWF is looking to sign a handful of foreign wrestlers, and women wrestlers to help with the diversity of there roster. * Tickets are selling great for a Cold Day in Paradise on the 30th of December, and three more matches are still to be named. * The KAWF house shows have been doing really good as of late. Four more stops remain before the over seas tour ends. Those spots being Paris, London, Toronto, and Mexico City. Tokyo, and Sydney were very good to us financially. * All KAWF shows are now available for sale on the myspace. DVD, or VHS orders accepted. Look for a christmas special to go on place soon. * Irvine / James wrestled to the second time limit draw to start Fever since the KAWF has returned to the wrestling market. You might think the company would be upset without a finish, but are very pleased with the competitive match to start the night. Look for a lot more from both of these upcoming talents. * The KAWF is still promising they have signed a former world champion to there roster who will be at the supercard. * Mark McKenna has been cleared to wrestle. Angela Cheetem has helped the KAWF find out the X Rays were indeed false sent to us from the Obsolete Army camp. * Corey Hart, Ben Tuckwell, Johnny Spades, and KAWF assistant Sunny Powell were all seen eating at Chile Verde in West Monroe, Louisiana on the day before thanksgiving. * Juan Rodriguez was seen doing some conspicuous activity's on a park bench in South Carolina with a young blonde on Friday Night. * Backstage at Fever Ricky J got into a confrontation with Michael Keathley, helping add more heat to an already fueled fire. ******************** 3 - Roster ******************* It's not Destin, Florida, but the KAWF has touched down in the sunshine state for the first time in years. Tonight we show Lakeland what we bring to the table, and soon we show Destin what might be the best night of wrestling to happen in there city ever. Yes ever. You have the right to your own opinion, but if your own opinion differs from that one you might need to get checked out for possible insanity. VINCENT CASE won't be here tonight. He is staying away from Florida until the night of the supercard, electing to stay in FRANCE until the time to leave. Don't let this stop you from coming though, cause this is the _last_ fever before A COLD DAY IN PARADISE, and we promise we won't take one easy leading up to the big show, instead were going to try to promote the hell out of this thing. Cause that's how the KAWF rolls. Andddddddd starting it out tonight we will have more first round action of the Mason Dixon Title tournament as CYN BAILUS will make his KAWF debut in the ring against the promising upcomer DADDY. If you were a betting man you might would leave this one alone, but if your the KAWF you start the night off with this match to set the tone, and pace for the evening. The winner goes on to face RED HOOD in the second round at the PARIS, FRANCE house show. Best of luck to both title hopefuls. First round title action continues as on the other side of the bracket we will see the undefeated BRADY "BOLTZ" LOWELL take on "SADISTIC INSANITY" DAMIAN PAYNE. Both men are no stranger to title belts, and big time matches. This could end up being the best match of the first round, even topping last week's main event. BRADY didn't want to be in this tournament, but now that he's here anything could happen. We do mean anything. Moving right along on this night of opportunity we will have a GAUNTLET match which features KAI LEWIS, MIKEY MCKAY, ADAM LAZARUS, & XAVIER ORION. Yes ORION has finally signed a KAWF contract so we have been told, and thus making this match something to behold. LAZARUS will be the man trying to run the gauntlet, let's see if the current STREETLIFE champion can do so as he continues to try to make sure his talents are not over looked in this company. One of these men asked for THIS match, but the company is not giving out details as to which one did. Just to make sure it's known the title is _not_ on the line in this match. Special Interview time will be given to the newest woman to sign a contract to compete in the KAWF. That woman being APATHY of IGA fame. Word has just been released that there will be a women's title match at A Cold Day In Paradise with the other three women under contract. We don't know what she will say, but knowing the background of APATHY it could be something worth listening to. Plus the other women might have something to say considering there not booked for tonight's event. The final Mason Dixon Title match of round One will be what we come back to from intermission. This could be a wrestling clinic to say the least. Dad's if you want your kids to see how it's done, let them come watch this one. "EXTREME" JOHNNY PAIN will take on "PERFECT CREATION" KYLE SOMMERS in a one on one encounter. PAIN could possibly face his partner if he wins this match, and KYLE took the place of JARROD STORM his former tag team partner who left the KAWF since he saw FRANKIE CASH leave the company with his very own eyes. KYLE being an opportunist went from Promotional Title consideration to Mason Dixon title consideration just like that ...Thus giving us this possible dream match. We might of lost JARROD STORM, but we gained a possible match of the year.
