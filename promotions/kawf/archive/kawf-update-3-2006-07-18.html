KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 3 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup gentleman ? Hope all of you enjoyed the show. Please drop me a _line_ to let me know what you thought about the show. I love hearing your input. It helps me get a feel for what is going good, what is going bad, and what is working, but could use some minor adjusting. I don't want to try to fix something that's not broken, but then again I do want to tweak things which aren't getting over to well. I want this to be enjoyable for as many people as humanly possible. To do that I need YOUR help of course. After this next show I really want to focus on storylines. I have one more show I want to do which focuses in on Heel / Face to get that over to all of our readers, but after that I want to get the storylines cranked out. I have a few in mind, but can always listen to what you got to say so feel free to drop me a line. I think we have some real solid stuff lined up, and since the federation has the indy feel to it we can be very flexible with what we want to do on each show. I love having that at our control. Let's get on with this thing though, shall we ? Continue on for the goodies. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * Larry Gionet has decided to step away from the KAWF due to his busy schedule with the SPW. Well at least that's what the rumors our. Rumor has it that Gionet didn't think the KAWF would take up to much of his training time since it was a small time federation, but after being in front of the ruckus crowd, and in the ring with great competition in his debut Gionet has re - thought things leading to his departure from the company. The KAWF has already paid him for his performance wishing him the best in his future. All other wrestlers will receive there checks on Thursday Morning. * It's official that the KAWF promotional title will be up for grabs in a four way at the supercard show on September 30th at the old KAWF arena, but the gimmick for the match has not yet been announced, nor has all the competitors. Kyle Sommers is already in cause of his main event win on the comeback show, but the other three spots remain empty as of press time. * The Obsolete Army has been fined for there post match attack on Damian Payne for the sum of three appearances. The KAWF is hoping this will say they are strongly AGAINST people coming down to the ringside when they are not scheduled to be apart of the match. Drama will occur, but un - necessary drama will be stamped out. * Tommy Danger was seen backstage at the show in Crossett, Arkansas, but obviously was not used. It's said he was just visiting some friends. Not sure as to who in specific those friends were. * Mike Pace has stated that "All three titles will be on the line at the supercard show in some sort of fashion, and currently all titles, including the tag team titles are vacant". Which disputes some of the things one Kyle Sommers has said recently to the viewers of this company. * Merchandise sold very well at the Crossett show, leading the KAWF production crew to work double time for the upcoming show to have even more stuff lined up for it. The old KAWF pay per view Lovers Quarrel sold very well, as did the new KAWF shirt. Frankie Cash, and Adam Lazarus are said to be next in line to get a shirt. * Adam Lazarus will be at the Vicksburg bowling alley on Friday July 28th one day before the big show to sign autographs, and play one game of bowling with the first three lucky fans to show up for the signing. * Nightstalker will be joining the KAWF at the Augusta, Georgia show, but nobody is sure as to what he will be doing at the show. Nightstalker is a former world, and tag team champion for various federations. * The KAWF lawyers finally expressed the reason the name change occurred legally. As some know when the KAWF was last seen on television it was being called the SAWF cause of a previous stipulation, but a loop hole was found in that contract which stated as long as Mike Johnson wasn't running the company, it could have it's old name back. Which is part of the reason the man who created the KAWF is currently not around. * Former KAWF champion Hayden Grayson recently lost to Art Blake on the SPW pay per view Iconoclasm in a hard fought match up. Just thought some of the diehard fans might want to know what the old champion has been up to. You can email Hayden at [detail withheld] if you want to wish him best. * The KAWF has stated they will release a report card of the company, the venues, and the wrestlers a few days after the supercard at the end of September. This could be very controversial similar to some of the stuff the OLD KAWF would allow to be leaked out to the public. As of now many are saying the KAWF is very pleased with MUKA, DAMIAN PAYNE, and ADAM LAZARUS, while being upset with CZON YOZON, SANTANA SOBRAL, and the OBSOLETE ARMY. * Vincent Case lost some respect with the boys backstage by not accepting the challenge of Adam Lazarus at the Crossett show. Case does not seem to care much however as he continues to be his confident self backstage walking around with a smile the size of Texas. * In an effort to get the return of KAWF more spotlight in the wrestling business Mike Pace, and the Outlaw are trying to get a KAWF showcase match on an upcoming show of IGA, SPW, or UWF. IGA seems like the most likely choice cause of the history they have with each other, and the fact Outlaw is currently wrestling there. * The office is also trying to get a hold of a small screen to be placed on top of the ramp way so the fans in the audience can see some of the backstage happenings which are being taped for the DVDS. Hopefully by the time the supercard rolls around, a small screen will be in place. Till then, we recommend you purchase the DVD even if you went to the show live so that you don't miss _anything_ * Public relations in Monroe, Louisiana the original hometown of the KAWF headquarters has already begun as this week three of the wrestlers will be coming in to do special things for the city. Kai Lewis will be at the recreational center to referee one of the 12 - 14 year old basketball games on Tuesday. Thursday Johnny Spades will be at the Guidos gym to work out with some of it's members, and Saturday morning Frankie Tha Pimp will be guest speaker for the Louisiana delta community college in an effort to promote there broadcasting classes which just became available. Look for more of this in the near future leading up to the big supercard in September. * The KAWF is considering bringing in another title which will be for strict rule wrestling, or possibly a title defended on every single show depending on what the public wants. * More matches have been signed for upcoming shows. Please check below to see what matches might be occurring in your town before you purchase tickets to the upcoming events.
