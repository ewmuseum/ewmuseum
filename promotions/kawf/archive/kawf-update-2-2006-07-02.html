KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 1 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup gentleman ? The first show is now out, and it wasn't the _BEST_ show by any means, but I do feel like it was a solid show. It was a good show for us to grow on. I am still trying to get my match writers groove back, but none the less I feel it's an adequate starting ground with some good solid action, and storyline ideas being tossed around. The first two or three shows I want to focus on Heels / Faces, letting the fans know who they should cheer for, and who they should jeer for. There will be some storylines going on already obviously, but the stories, and titles will come into play a lot more after show three. Read the update to find out a bit more about the title situations. As you can probably tell by the results I like action / crowd interaction, but I focus mainly on the flow of the show, and the announcers making the story make sense. I want to tell a consistent story, so a lot of times I will talk about something which happened in the past, or earlier / later in the show. I want it to flow like a real commentary booth would. So sometimes I get a bit wordy with the announcers, but that's just my style. I think there is plenty of action mixed in there to, It's just so many places just put together these great matches, do some copying, and pasting .. . Then it's ready to be sent out. That's good n all, I mean those matches are superb, but there is no crispness to the show, or no flow to the show. I hope to change that for someone trying to read the KAWF results. As always if you have any questions shoot me an email at [name withheld]. We are also still looking for a few tag teams, so send them my way if you got some ideas. I love tag team wrestling as most of you already know. I grew up loving the Road Warriors, and the Freebirds. Now enjoy the rest of the update. Feel free to send me some news tidbits for the update, or thoughts on the shows/lineups/updates. I love hearing from the people on the roster. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * The KAWF will be selling merchandise at there next show in Crossett, Arkansas. More merchandise will be rewarded soon, as the staff is working as hard as possible with the limited budgeting for the company. * Czon Yozon tweaked his shoulder at the show in Longview, Texas doing his _amazing_ shooting star press to the outside, but the doctors have said he is okay to continue wrestling for the time being. They will be keeping a close eye on his shoulder in the next few weeks. *The KAWF arena which has been cut down in size due to problems with the city of Monroe will not be ready for the August 26th show as originally hoped for, but will be ready by the middle of September thus giving us a place to run our first supercard show since returning to the wrestling scene on September 30th . .. titled "Welcome Home" fittingly enough. * MUKA defeated "Brash" Anthony Kutler in a pre show match not to be recorded for Fever in Longview, Texas earlier tonight. The official time for this match was 7 : 20. The KAWF has said there will always be one "dark" match before Fever to give the crowd there money's worth, and get them pumped up for the start of the show. * All KAWF wrestlers were available for autographs after the show earlier today, and the same will be said for the Arkansas show coming up as Mike Pace has promised all wrestlers will sign autographs till the fans go away happy. Arriving early in the day will also be Johnny Pain, and Kai Lewis to do a "Care about kids" reading session at the Crossett library for ages 5 - 9 from 3:00 central till 4:30 central. * Rumors have slipped out of the front office meetings that the tag team titles, and the Promotional Title will be on the line at the supercard show at the KAWF arena on the 30th of September, but nobody knows as of yet who will participate in these match ups. * The KAWF was very impressed with the Johnny Pain versus Kai Lewis match to open the show in Longview, even with the interference of Vincent Case, and is hoping that the MUKA versus Mikey Mckay match can set somewhat of the same tempo for the night with there upcoming opening bout match up in Crossett. * Two matches have been signed for events down the road. The KAWF office plans on doing this kind of booking on a regular basis, so please remember when purchasing a ticket for any KAWF event that the card is subject to change, and you should keep up with the newswire to have an IDEA about what may occur. * The KAWF has said every singles match will be watched very close to help decide who gets title shots, and high rankings leading up to the big _welcome back_ show at the old KAWF arena. (Camera fades to black as BFKL heads toward the locker room..) ************************************************************************************************************ 2 - Foodstamp caught doing his thing backstage [In the back is where the KAWF Camera Crew is busy rolling film. The subject matter is Foodstamp, the six foot tall masked KAWF soon-to-be superstar... hopefully. He's sitting on the cement floor somewhere at the Paula M. Jones Recreational Center, wearing camo cargos and a black and red tye dye shirt. His dreads are actually pulled back into a ponytail, and his mask is securely fastened to his skull. In his hands is a toy he's busy playing with... a rusty nail. His stare is focused on this nail, and not on the camera.] FOODSTAMP: Rust... It's double edged sword, I'd say. <Nod.> On on hand, rust is an inhibitor. It makes thing slow, worthless and unusable. It turns things, slowly over time, into the color of shit, ruining them for humans enjoyment. <Another nod.> Rust does the same thing to wrestlers. It creeps into their joints and makes even the simplest of moves hard. It sneaks it's way into the mind, slowing down reaction time, ruining a wrestler's much needed instincts... [He pauses, flipping the nail over his fingers, never taking his eyes off of it.] FOODSTAMP: On the other hand, rust, at least in wrestling, can help a wrestler out almost as much as it can hurt them. Yes, a rusty wrestler might not have the same speed he had, but no one expects him too. All the fans expect of a rusty veteran is a few snapshots of former brilliance and a hope that the squirrelly vet can actually overcome the odds. The same goes for most wrestlers... They look upon the old guard as dinosaurs, men who cannot possibly hold their own in today's wrestling world. They underestimate and underprepare... [As he continues to stare at the nail, an mocking, ironic laugh escapes his lips.] FOODSTAMP: I am _NOT_ one of those fools! <The laughter increases in volume.> I do not expect Johnny Spades to come out and lay a egg. I am not counting on Czon Yozon coming out and making a bigger fool out of himself than Bush does at a press conference! And I am certainly not depending on Frankie Cash to save the day! [The laughter subsides as he whips the nail across the room. It's bounces can be heard, just barely, as Food turns his attention straight onto the camera. His eyes breathing a loveable crazy, even though they're hidden behind his mask!] FOODSTAMP: Instead, I am training as if I have no partner to rely on, no teammate to assist me. I am training as if Spades and Yozon aren't rusted shit buckets, and actually resemble their championship form. <Soft, mocking laugh.> And how are they preparing for me? They _aren't_. They're sitting around, actin as if they can walk on water, laughing at me... Talking about how there is no way they can lose to a _freak_ name Foodstamp... That it's _just_ _not_ _possible__! [He shakes his head, taking a pause.] FOODSTAMP; SO you tell me... Who is going to win this fight? The young, inexperienced warrior who prepared himself for battle better than any warrior before him? Or the old, crusted, comfortable warriors who feel no threat and thus have no motivation to train? <Outstretches his arms and hands, in a "Who? Tell me who?" way> Exactly. You hate to say it, but the answer is... [More of that mocking laughter.] _FOODSTAMP_~!
