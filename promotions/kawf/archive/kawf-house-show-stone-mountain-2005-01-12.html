Suck Ass Wrestling Federation January 12th, 2005 "The night some people had to stop, and think" >From Stone Mountain, Georgia Bell Time : 8 : 00 PM Ticket Prices : 10 $ for Adults, $ 4 dollars for Kids under 15. Hosted by : Frankie Tha Pimp scheduled matches The Short Bus Gang Versus Mark Steiner & Tender Smith Prison Versus Mitch Hanson of the RiverHawks NO DQ MATCH - Bungle Versus Mike Harris Kai Lewis defends his Promotional Title against Garrett Jax Special guest appearances by Commish Outlaw Vice President Shane Martin Lark Fenriz Vile Vince Viper Iowa State Cyclones Special Bonus Match Max Doosledorf Versus Jay Turnbow Ahh another day, another house show. For the first time ever the SAWF was in Stone Mountain, Georgia, and I must admit these crazy people did there homework. They knew who everybody was. I had to sign autographs, the referee's had there pictures taken, and even some of our ringside crew was called by there first name, instead of "Hey you" by these people. Stone Mountain did there homework, or maybe there's just nothing to do in Stone Mountain, so they had nuttin better to do than study the SAWF from top to bottom before the event came knocking on there door. The event started on time, as usual for an SAWF event, and when the clock struck 8:00 PM "Outlaw" By Tupac Shakur began to play, and out came the commish of the SAWF Outlaw, slapping hands with everybody. Outlaw did the normal "Thanks for coming" speech, and then quickly turned his attention towards the whole KAWF/SAWF deal. He was upset that Mike Johnson wasn't around to right the wrong. He wasn't comfortable with Kristen's leadership of the company even though ratings have been high as of late, and more importantly he was hurt by Shane Martin siding with VVV to help degrade the company they had both worked so hard for in the past. Thus Shane Martin came out . .. The Vice President, and Commish went nose to nose for a minute, and then backed off embracing for a moment drawing a pop from the crowd. The two then spoke about various things, not nessecarily agreeing with each other, but not nessecarily disagreeing with one another. Outlaw seemed to be getting Shane to listen to him for the first time since Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash about the evils in which would be coming both there ways,. until from the top of the ramp way we heard "Sssssssstop it.." VVV was up there, and the crowd errupted with Boo's. Viper nodded, pleased with the reaction, and walked down towards the ring to join the two office members. A "you tapped out" chant was heard as he did so. Viper ranted on Kai Lewis for a moment reacting to the chant which was being tossed his way cause of his recent tap out loss to Kai Lewis, but didn't touch the Sabbath issue as of yet leaving it out there for another day, in another city. The three men then argued bout the name SAWF, with Viper winning the debate if we were juding by points before security had to storm the ring to stop Outlaw, and Viper from fighting. Martin look confused, but didn't side ethier way on this occurence. Security slowly cleared the ring of the three hardcore legends who were all seemingly in another world thinking about the words which had been said just moments ago, and Juan Delgado entered the ring to get the first bout of the night started. The crowd was still buzzing from the opening Outlaw, Martin, and VVV segment so Juan jumped on it .. . helping the new team of the Short Bus Gang get a good crowd pop, along with the two Con$ortium members getting a nice heel pop. ********************************************************************************************************* Opening Bout Short Bus Gang Versus Mark Steiner & Tender Smith Referee [name withheld] ********************************************************************************************************* Mr Davis huddled with his two Short Bus gang members, "Tiny" Tommy Toosmall's, and "Short" Steve Delittle for a moment after the bell rang, and then exited leaving the self proclaimed "greatest" tag team in the business in the ring along against Mark Steiner, and Tender Smith. Steve exited the ring, as did Steiner. Smith then eased out towards the middle of the ring ,and went to lock up with Tommy, but he backed up into his corner. Talking with his partner. Tommy then exited the ring, and Steve exited the apron. They looked a bit nervous, and Mr Davis quickly went in for more words of encouragement sensing his debut team might not be ready for there debut. Tender was laughing on the inside, and Davis used this to fuel Tommy Toosmall's. ...Tommy slid back into the ring with the courage of a virgin boy going in for the first time, and this time they did indeed lock up. Tommy won the lock up, and the Short Bus gang won the next five minutes of the match showing off good tag team talents in there against Smith, really cutting the ring off. Steve, and Tommy went for a double suplex, but Tender managed to land on his feet from the sloppy attempt by the SAWF newbies. . .Davis was screaming on the outside, but by the time his team turned around Tender caught them both with a double drop kick. Tender used this as his first chance to tag out....With a desperate crawl he did indeed do so. Steiner cleared the ring with high impact moves, and then went to the outside where he won the fight briefly, before the numbers game became to much. Steve nailed Steiner from behind, and after using the ring apron the Short Bus gang rolled Steiner in. Tommy followed and landed a beautiful overhead suplex before going for the cover. Tender managed to recooperate in time to make the save. Steve entered the ring to do battle with Tender, and the referee lost control. Con$oritum took control of there own reversing a double irish whip sending the Gang crashing into each other, followed by double superkicks. Smith then rolled Steve out of the ring, and Steiner went to the top rope to land a high body splash on Tommy which got him a long two count. Steiner tagged back in Tender, who quickly began to wear Tommy Toosmall's down with nerve holds, clutches, and slow paced wrestling holds. Tommy was on his way to his feet, when Tender used the hair to get him back down, and tagged in Steiner who took over where Tender left off. This time when Tommy made his way back to his feet Steiner caught him with a russian leg sweep, and tagged in Tender who went to the top rope himself .. .Tender tried for a splash as his partner did with high elevation, but Tommy rolled out of the way. Both men were down for a good eight seconds teasing the crowd, but Tommy with a few rolles tagged out to Steve. Delittle then entered the ring, and cut Tender off just before he could make a tag .. Steiner entered the ring, but the referee pushed him out. Steve then hit an eye poke while smiling,much to the crowd's delight, followed by a field goal kick to the lower area, which oddly enough the crowd also applauded. Probally cause Steve was laughing, and smiling as he did it. Steve then pulled Tender towards there corner, and tagged back in Tommy. The Short Bus gang then executed a stiffly done spike piledriver which had there manager giving them a thumb's up, and got the three count with Mark Steiner not being able to do anything about it except wander why he was working the opening bout to a house show when he has a finisher which can put any man away, at any time as we saw on Fever last week. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Short Bus Gang Frankie's Opinion : This Short Bus duo is definitely unique. The Con$ortium people might be a family, but it's obvious the Perfect Storm is the only tag team in there ranks right now. I don't know if the Short Bus fellas have the brain capacity to beat the top teams in the SAWF, but it's definitely going to be interesting to see them try. The freakin fans applauded an eye poke by these morons for christ sake. ********************************************************************************************************* As the ring cleared the crowd remained hyped. Don't these people use the bathroom ? Or shut up long enough to get drunk ? My god. There was a "We want Hayden" chant from the crowd during the brief downtime, but of course Grayson wasn't here tonight.He's preparing for that challenge Kristen Roberts has set up for him, and the Global Title Contenders.. . .Which is part of the reason why the ratings are so high right now much to the dismay of Outlaw as noted earlier in the show. Plus the whole fact she is putting good people against good people unlike Mike Johnson who used to baby people. I mean right now we have Jimmy Haynes feuding with Magnusson, Gregorson feuding with Fenriz, Sledge feuding with Viper, and Sabbath. Outlaw, and Shane feuding, even if just verbally, a bunch of top notch guys including Lezkanic heading for Hayden Grayson, plus the cruserweight division is picking up better than ever with new recruits, and the SAWF has signed a few tag teams to help round out there division with TWO more teams for sure coming in the soon to be future. GO KRISTEN . .. GO KRISTEN . .. GO KRISTEN . .. I think it helps to have a hot chick in charge, over an uptight old man. I'm sad it took cancer to see the change in power occur, but I am glad to see the change of power none the less. As the Hayden chant finally died Juan Delgado introduced the next match up which put my ring announcing partner on Fever, and former KAWF world champion Prison in the ring against one of those crazy RiverHawks guys .. ..This one being Mitch Hanson, who has been apart of a few jobber beat downs, and also an attack on a few of SAWF's staff. I must say I give Prison a hard time on Fever, but he is my buddy. Don't tell him that, but I do like him. I hope he kicks this dudes ass. ********************************************************************************************************* Special Attraction Match Prison Versus Mitch Hanson Referee Mike Riquiah ********************************************************************************************************* The bell rang, and Prison didn't back down even though he hadn't been in a ring for years. Hanson bull rushed him, and Prison fought back. However after a few punches landed Hanson was on top of the former Global Champion, and Prison could do nothing, but try to defend off some of the punches. Hanson then traded his punches for kicks, and helped ease Prison out of the ring to the floor. Hanson joined, and did some damage on the outside using the concrete, guard rail, and steel post to his adavantage. Hanson then tossed Prison back into the ring, and went for a cover ,but lifted him up at two .. .Not wanting the three count. Hanson then landed back to back high impact drops causing Prison to hit his head, and went for another cover. Agin helping him up at two as the crowd boo'd loudly. Hanson returned to the kicks, and really began to beat Prison like he stole something from him. Mitch was obviously pleased with his work, and new his teammate Tommy Gibson, and there good friend Ceaser Risley would be please to on the beat down of one of the past KAWF icons. Bad knee, and all the gutsy former champion got back up using the ropes, and Mitch whipped him across the ring. Prison fired back with a lou thiez press, and landed some rapid fire punches. Hanson pushed him off, and quickly got up, but only to catch a clothesline. Then another, and another .. .This time Hanson stayed down, and the crowd popped as Prison held his ribs, but was in control. Prison then landed a few kicks of his own before helping Mitch up which was a mistake. Coming up Mitch caught Prison with a shot to the throat, followed by an inverted Russian leg sweep. Mitch then went for a REAL cover, and got a long two count. Mitch then picked Prison up, powerbombing him down before going for another cover. Again a two count. Mitch yanked Prison back up, trying for another powerbomb, but this time Prison wiggled free getting behind Mitch, and backsliding him over. LONGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGG two count. Both men got to there feet, and Mitch swung wildly which led to Prison ducking, and landing a jaw breaker. Both men were down for a moment, but this time Prison got up first. He landed a few punches, and then whipped Hanson across the ring where he caught him coming back with his old finisher the "get out of jail free card" which just happens to be a snake eyes move dropping the opposition on the top rope throat first. Prison hooked the leg, and got the three count much to the suprise of everybody. The crowd popped, and Prison got the hell out of dodge knowing Mitch would not be a happy camper losing to a retired wrestler, and KAWF fan favorite. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Prison Frankie's Opinion : Prison beat an active SAWF wrestler ? Maybe the old man's still got it, or maybe he just got lucky. Ethier way Mitch Hanson, and Tommy Gibson our gonna hear an ear full from Ceaser Risley who sent them here to destroy the company. Hard to do when one of the announcers beat you in a match, even if that announcer is a former wrestler who weighs 260 pounds. ********************************************************************************************************* Mitch stormed out of the ring angrily, and ashamed. Losing a bunch of the awe in which they had built for themself backstage from there past violent sneak attacks. Once Mitch got backstage, Lark Fenriz then came out. Lark wasn't wearing his trenchcoat, or his streetlife title however. Lark was shirtless, with some blue jeans on, and only had that piece of paper in his hand. The piece of paper which says Werewolf Gregorson Versus Lark Fenriz at the pay per view. Lark entered the ring to a nice heel pop, and didn't say a word to the crowd. He just looked at the contract, even squinting he was looking at it so hard. Lark remained in this trance, until finally the stunned crowd began chanting "Sign it" . ..the chant got louder as Lark remained looking at the contract. This went on for a good two minutes before finally Lark looked up towards the crowd stopping there chants. . .Lark then smiled, even laughed, and exited the ring just like that. The crowd was confused, and dealt with there confusion by booing. The mindgames had been continued, and the crowd didn't like it whatsoever. They were really thinking about Lark had just did to them, when in reality there probally wasn't much to think about. Juan Delgado, the younger brother of the lovely Jessica then prepared us for our next bout of the evening. A hardcore match which very well could end up with us seeing _BLOOD_ which is one of my favorite things to see unless it's on my sheets at the house after a crazy morning on the town. ********************************************************************************************************* NO DQ MATCH Bungle Versus Mike Harris Referee Wayne West ********************************************************************************************************* The gay bear Bungle from England, and former Streetlife Champion Mike Harris who we hadn't seen in a while prepared to do battle. They went to lock up, and Bungle quickly turned it into a side headlock, followed by a drop to his knee, and a single leg takedown which got him a One cover as he pressed onto Harris. An angry Mike Harris kicked out, and reminded Bungle what kinda match this was supposed to be. Bungle nodded, and the two locked up again. Again Bungle turned it into a side headlock, and went for a single leg takedown, but this time couldn't get the bigger man down. Harris slapped Bungle in the back of the head, and again reminded him that this was a hardcore match. Bungle shook his head, and walked over to Harris corner where he grabbed Louis....Harris trademark baseball bat. Mike shook his head, and snatched the bat from Bungle who shrugged playfully. Harris then swung the bat at Bungle, but the cruserweight ducked out of the ring to the floor. Mike followed, obviously wanting to take the action outside the ring to begin with. Bungle tried to run, but got caught half way up the ramp way when he stumbled. Harris landed a few hard punches, and then draped Bungle across the guard rail where Louis got to be introduce to Bungle's back, and bear suit. The crowd popped as Harris went for a pin on the ramp way, but only got a two count. Harris then continued the onslaught on the ramp way exeucuting a double underhook piledriver, followed by a single arm DDT, and ended the fury with a brainbuster suplex. Again Harris only got a two count. Both men our fan favorites, but with the abuse he had taken, and the near pinfalls Bungle had the crowd encouraging him to get up. . .Harris used the ringside apron a few times, and rolled Bungle into the ring. Harris grabbed a steel chair, and entered the ring himself. Harris set the chair up in the corner, and irish whipped Bungle towards it, but the CW stopped before reaching the chair. Harris then took off towards Bungle, who side stepped causing Harris to hit the chair ribcage first. .. .Harris bounced back, and Bungle sprung to the middle rope, then the turnbuckles before leaping off, and hitting a whirlwind DDT. Bungle got a two count. Bungle then set the chair up in a seated position, and whipped Harris into the ropes, apon coming back Bungle landed a drop down toe hold onto the chair, and went for another cover. Again we got a two count. I think that's the only number our referee knows this evening. Bungle helped Harris up, and tried to snap suplex him over onto the chair, but Harris blocked. The bigger man then hit a stalling suplex on Bungle, of course landing him on the chair. The third pinfall attempt for Harris was indeed not the charm, as Bungle barely kicked out at the last possible instant. Maybe if Harris would of hooked the leg, but Mike Harris never hooks the leg. He is a brute. A brute who gets hurt sometimes, and takes a few weeks off to recooperate, but always comes back just as much as a brute as before. Harris then went for the dead end powerbomb he has beat a few people with in the SAWF, but Bungle flipped over, and hit the ropes .. .As Harris turned around Bungle caught him with a flying forearm, which he followed up with a standing shooting star press. Bungle then quickly went to the top rope, and leaped off with a frogsplash which would have got a three count, but Harris was able to get his foot on the rope. A frustrated Bungle then went back to the chair sensing that the cruserweight style was not going to put the hardcore style of Mike Harris away, and drilled Harris with a inverted Russian leg sweep on the chair which busted the former champion open. Again Bungle got a two count. The fans popped as Bungle paused for a moment thinking about what he might can do to finish Mike Harris off .. .As he was thinking Harris got to his feet, and Bungle moved back in before finishing his thought .. .Harris blocked a few punches, and landed a high impact tear drop suplex as both men were down . .Bungle had took to much time, and didn't have a plan in mind. Harris was up first, and landed a few stomps before going for the chair, as he reached for the chair however Bungle rolled him up using the tights. TWO COUNT AGAIN. Harris stormed up with the chair this time, and swung wildly, but missed leaving himself vulernable to a low blow by the gay bear who prolly had been wanting to touch Harris there the whole time anyway . ... Harris bent over, Bungle grabbed the chair position it on the mat infront of Harris head, and drove him down with a rocker dropper onto the chair. Bungle then got the three count, and got the hell out of the ring before the"Maddog" got up again to continue this hardcore battle. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Bungle Frankie's Opinion : I can't believe Bungle won this match, but he did. Congrats to the gay bear, who if he can pull off a win on Fever this week might indeed get a cruserweight title shot against Sammy Griffin, but he's gonna have to beat three other men to do it. One of those three men being my boy Maddy Skillz, who has game playah . .. Fo Shizzle. ********************************************************************************************************* The fans were pleased with that match since both men put on a good seasaw performance, and when the ring was cleared Outlaw came back out. The commish apologized for his actions earlier, admitting VVV gets under his skin more than he should. Outlaw then announced that he would love to face Viper in a match, and got a cheap pop. Outlaw kept ranting, and raving killing time until finally VVV made his way back out. They had the typical anytime, any place kinda argument, and then went to blows. Outlaw was winning the battle, but security stormed the ring again before there was any real outcome. Won't these two ever learn that less its a sanctioned match these security guys our gonna stop you ? I mean c'mon .. Your BOTH vets, and BOTH have office pull. Book the damn match, or else don't even bother. That buzz faded slowly, and then it was time four our main event of the evening. A title match. A promotional Title match. Yea I know it's just a house show, I know what you people are thinking at home, but none the less this Stone Mountain crowd got to see a title on the line in the middle of the damn week. ********************************************************************************************************* MAIN EVENT PROMOTIONAL TITLE MATCH Kai Lewis [C] Versus Garrett Jax Referee Patrick Finley ********************************************************************************************************* Kai Lewis came to the ring with those two guys we saw hold his towel last week, and have sinced learned our the tag team known as the Iowa State Cyclones, while Garrett Jax came to the ring by himself. We don't know much about the Cyclones, but we do know they have a huge match coming up on Fever against NEO legends Tobias Chapel Smith, and Scrayper. I don't know if Griffin was in the building watching his partners back, or not, but I do know Jax wasn't scared of the three on one appearance of this match at all, he was locked in on the title till the bell rang, and then once it rang Mr Kai A. Lewis. Griffin has had the cruserweight title for a while in the SAWF, and maybe it was time for Garrett Jax to get himself some gold to ? The Cyclones stayed out of this match from the start to fhe finish letting Lewis, and Jax have a fair match believe it, or not. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO .. . However the match wasn't to bad. Real intense, real crisp, and real even except for when they mat wrestled. Kai was definitely the better wrestler, with Jax being a bit stronger, but both of them being equally as stiff. The early advantage went to Kai showing off his wrestling techniques, and getting several near pinfall roll ups which came in so many styles that I would need a Japanese announcer to help me discuss em. That lasted a lil while, but of course Jax got his oppurtunity when he caught Lewis with a desperation powerslam. Lewis tried to fight back from that quickly, but Jax caught him again as he was getting up with some strong man moves. Jax then went high power, while Lewis went to the defensive. It didn't work often, but once it finally did work. Once Lewis was finally able to counter something he slapped Jax into a crossface, trying to make him tap as he had did Viper just a few nights before. Jax almost tapped on two occasions, but the tough cowboy managed to tough it out getting to the ropes. The Cyclones applauded Jax effort, and even Kai was impressed, but stayed on the attack. Kai went for the Ebberts field drop twice, and was blocked by the big man struggling to keep his oppurtunity at getting some SAWF gold alive, but the third time was the charm for Kai. Kai hit the Ebberts field drop on the third attempt about 20 minutes into this thing, and got the pinfall, even grape vinning the leg to show off how technically sound he truly is inside the squared circle. Kai then helped Jax up, as the Cyclones joined them in the middle of the ring...All four played to the crowd with Kai holding his title high in the air, and the show was ovah on a very positive note. ********************************************************************************************************* Winner : Kai Lewis Frankie's Opinion : Kai is changing, I don't know if he is changing for the better, but none the less he is changing. He seems to like the crowd, the other wrestlers, and everybody,but VVV now. Him, and his new friends have took a new direction here in 2005, and I think he could be the Promotional Champion for a while, sorry Vipes. He's just so damn good in that ring. ********************************************************************************************************* Or was the show over ? Oh yea the Cyclones, Kai, and Jax went to the back leaving us with happy thoughts, instead of chair bashing thoughts like we usually try to end the shows on. Most people left, but some stayed. . .Some wrestling vets noticed that I was still hanging around ringisde, a referee was down there talking to Juan. Yea those people got another match... Georgia native, and newcomer to the SAWF Max Doosledorf came out, and wrestled former tag team wrestler Jay Turnbow who just recently retired, and moved onto our announcing staff. They did a good technical match with lots of reversals, counters, and holds which would of had your average fan chanting "BORING" while your diehard fan would be going "GOOD CHEMISTRY" .. .This match ended with Max hitting the LADYKILLER for a victory, and getting some ring wok in before his match on Fever which could give him a chance at the cruserweight title. Bungle looked good in his hardcore match earlier, we all know Maddy Skillz has skill, Matthew Beecham is a hot young prospect, but this Max guy could be the long shot. It pays to stick around people after shows. It pays to stick around. Now the show is over for real though, so go drink some Mikes Hard Lemonade, and tell them Frankie sent you. GO NOW GO !!!!!!!!!!!!!! The end .. .For now. See you all Saturday Night for Fever.
