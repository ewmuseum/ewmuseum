KAWF Update #1: We're Back

31 December 2002

KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation *1* Out of Character Notes *2* Out of Character News *3* In Character Notes *4* In Character News *5* Rosters & Champions *6* New lineups *7* End Notes *~*OUT OF CHARACTER NOTES *~* Well, I would like to start by welcoming you all to the KAWF. This is the first of many updates to come. I hope you are all here for the long haul, 'cause I promise you that I am. We will go through bad times, but hopefully we will go through a lot more good times. Pretend like the KAWF is like one of your love relationships. There will be times when you get upset, there will be times when you are not happy; but overall does the good things out - weigh the bad things??? If the answer to that question is yes, then I'm sure you will enjoy your stay at the KAWF and will not want to go anywhere. If the answer to that question is no, come to me and let's see how we can make the answer to that question yes. I promise you all I will do my best to make sure you are happy with what's going on in that KAWF. Now enough talk let's get down to business.... Sincerely- [name withheld] *~* OUT OF CHARACTER NOTES *~* * Since the card is coming out on a Wednesday instead of a Sunday this Saturday we will be doing a special press conference and the following Saturday will be our first show. To give you all adequate time to role play. * We are hoping to have our first pay perview around Valentine's Day. The name of it is yet to be decided but it will be a Valentine's Day type theme. * I will be posting everything I send out on the RSPWF message board. This will bring some attention to the federation, but fill free to mention the KAWF to others at any time. Our doors are always open. Especially to tag teams and Cruserweights. I would love to keep both those divisions loaded. * Strategy & Role play deadlines are listed below at the end of the card line up's. * Have fun and feel free to IM &/or E-mail me or [detail withheld] anytime. We will be more than happy to talk to you about anything in KAWF or life in general. Were just real swell people aren't we ? =) *~* IN CHARACTER NOTES *~* Written by: KAWF sports writer, Justin Thomas Recently the bayou news star caught up with owner of the upcoming federation KAWF. That of course being owned by the same man who ran the old KAWF, Mike Johnson. People in the South will remember Mike Johnson and the KAWF for some of the great pay per views they had in the New Orleans and Baton Rouge areas. Mainly being the bayou brawls one through five. That same man and that same federation are making a come back to the wrestling scene. Not just in the Southern Part of the United States, but all around the world. Mike recently told me that he wouldn't let me in on all of his plans, but "This KAWF will be just as good if not better than the old KAWF." With that being said this bayou reporter will be sure to keep himself and you all out there up - dated on the federation. All I can give you for sure right now is January 4, 2003, they will have a press conference live from New Orleans, Louisiana. No more details have been released to the press as of yet. *~* IN CHARACTER NEWS *~* *Special note - some of the following may be rumors* * The KAWF is looking to have their first pay perview somewhere overseas and not in the United States.Upsetting some of the financial backers of the KAWF who live in the states. * The KAWF announcer 'White Rose' has signed a huge contract to only let her work pay perviews. * Former wrestling legend Todd Van Rooyen has finally signed a contract with the KAWF after long talks over the phone and in person. * Wife of owner Mike Johnson's son and also Vice President of the KAWF Kristen Roberts has been seen late at night with many of the KAWF employees and wrestlers.More on this in upcoming news updates. * Japanese superstar, Akira Yoru, is in talks with KAWF and might be coming over to wrestle in the near future. * KAWF is willing to pay an extra $1,000 dollars to whomever does a commercial ad for Saturday Night Fever or Sunday Night War Stories. *~* ROSTERS & CHAMPIONS *~* * Roster 1 - 3 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 7 - 8 - 9 - 10 - *~* NEW LINE - UP'S *~* *SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER* January 11, 2003 Live from: Folsom Field Stadium in Boulder, Colorado Arena holds - 53,425 Announcers: Frankie tha Pimp, Russell Cunningham, & Prison *Opening Bout* Hein Bloeck & Tommy Flames VS. 'Vinny 'Iceman' Cortez & 'Black master' Kai Tun Insight: In the first ever match in the return of KAWF we put two of the fan favorites in the ring together against two people that the fans really hate. What better way to start some drama? Bloeck & Flames haven't met each other before but knowing both these men they should be able to work very well together to put on a good show in the return of the KAWF. *Special Interview* Mike Johnson talks to Hayden Grayson *Second Bout* Bungle VS 'The Business Man' Tommy Taylor Insight: These two men have a past together and the KAWF plans to make sure they have a future together. Both are known as great mat wrestlers. Last time they met in the SAW Bungle came out on top, but as you can tell this is a different federation which means it's also a different day. *Third Bout* 'Legend VS Legend' 'God's Champion' Jim Fraser VS Todd Van Rooyen Insight: Both these men have proven themselves in the wrestling ring time and time again. Now they will have to do it one more time in front of the great KAWF fans. More than likely Jim will keep the match high tempo and Todd Van Rooyen will try and keep the match on the mat at a slow pace. Which style will win? Start placing your bets now! *Main Event* * Winner in for a special Suprise * Hayden Grayson VS Lark Fenriz Insight: Two men who the KAWF think are going to be blue chips face off in the main event of the first show. Hayden is from Colorado so this will be a homecoming for him. It'll be interesting to see if Lark can ruin Hayden's homecoming. *Plus* More information on how the promotional title will be decided. Many surprise guests and much, much more. Strat & Roleplay deadline: Thursday, January 9th at midnight central time!!!!! *SUNDAY NIGHT WAR STORIES* January 12, 2003 Live from Chilson Recreation Center in Loveland, Colorado Center holds - 1,800 Announcers: Russell Cunningham & Mike Pace *Opening Bout* Kai Tun VS El Tigre' *Second Bout* 'I QUIT MATCH' Tommy Taylor VS James Monroe *Special Interview* Vice President, Kristen Roberts *Main Event* 'Random Tag Match' Bungle & Tommy Flames VS Hayden Grayson & Lark Fenriz *Plus* How will Lark & Hayden get along after their match on Fever? What will Kristen Roberts have to say? Will the KAWF have any new members show up? And much more... Strats & Roleplay deadline: Thursday, January 9th at midnight central time!!!!! * Note - The KAWF independent wrestlers are what we call "jobbers," but occasionally they will send out a roleplay. So be sure to mention them in your roleplays for your fever matches if you wish to win your match up _FOR SURE_. This show is also smaller than Fever, but just as important to get over angles and to start heat. The first role play packet will be sent out Tuesday January 7th. *~* END NOTES *~* In closing, I feel these shows are good to get things going in the right direction. All bookings have a deeper meaning in the over-all goal of the federation. If your clueless as to why your fighting someone, or where your character is going. Feel free to IM me, but remember this is still a young federation, and in the end I am the booker. One of the perks of the job, I guess. Be sure to read the press conference which will come out Saturday Night, and feel free to start role playing and sending in your strats for the show. I believe Kristen and I can make this a good show, and more importantly a good federation. Your loving President, [name withheld] P.S - Have a damn good New Years guys. . . .
