KAWF "The best mix of old fashion, and extreme wrestling in E - Wrestling" Update # 13 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup all ? We put in the hard work over the last three months, and now it's time to relax. To see your angles come to a conclusion, or reach a new higher climax. It's time to really let the characters tell the stories. I'm sure there will be some distractions, and what not on the supercard, but I'm gonna try my damndest to keep the matches as clean as possible. Before, after, interviews who knows, but the actual match I would like for the wrestlers to solve it as much as humanly possible. This is entertainment, but when you read the shows I want you to be entertained by the story telling of a match, and the words one say, not some screw job finish, or beat down attack. We have Raw for that kind of amusement now don't we ? Let's be better than that, especially this show. Put in one more good flash for this cycle ladies, and gentleman then we will re boot, and come strong for another cycle. The women's division has gotten better. The tag team division is better. I'm really looking forward to everything. I hope you all enjoyed the results being in your box exactly on time, not a minute late technically =). I hope we can continue on with that. I would like to see that trend get set for the KAWF. I really tried to focus on pre writing, and what not to make sure we got it done. Plus I didn't want to send Shayne any money. I think plenty has been said. If you need me you know how to contact me by now .. . Let's get on with the update. Much Respect, Josh * Don't forget to please send me an ENTRANCE for the supercard. We have also signed yet another top female by the name of Tamara Jackson. Both should be great additions to the best independent roster in the sport. * This is the last weekend to get your holiday ham, and turkey's from the KAWF headquarters. Just 8 shopping days left till Christmas so please stop by, and see us before we take YOUR food down to the homeless shelter. * Daddy has been handed his walking papers by the KAWF in an effort to make room for some of the newer faces we have recently signed. Best of luck to him in all his future endeavors. Kyle Sommers is rumored to be on the hot seat. * The former champion KAWF has been rumored to have signed for the last two months will be at "A Cold Day in Paradise" * Rumors are circulating the Red Hood's rib injury suffered post match a month ago against Foodstamp is worse than originally thought. He will continue to work. * Outlaw was very pleased that there were not any pre match attacks by the wrestlers, or heated moments backstage. The end of Fever was expected by all in charge, and somewhat encouraged to help hype the supercard. * KAWF is shooting around ideas for the next supercard in Little Rock to be in March, or April. We already know there will be a special anniversary show on July 1st to represent a year back in the business. * The official wrestlebowl roster for the KAWF includes Josie Saito, Vincent Case, Leon Corella, Michael Keathley, Red Hood, Nightstalker, and Johnny Spades. Johnny is possibly not going to be able to be at the show, and if that occurs Carson Ray will take his place. * Damian Payne had to get stitches after the vicious attack by Brady Lowell during there match. Some of the people at the end of Fever also had to get some light work done, but no major injuries. * There are four more house shows scheduled on the mini tour the KAWF put together. The next one being considered very important with the supercard happening shortly after it, and France being one of the tougher sells out of the places we decided to travel to. Thus a lot of big time matches have been signed for it. * Former manger of Brian Floyd, the mute midget Knee High was again doing janitorial services for us at Fever this weekend in Lakeland. Please help him with his lack of funds by stopping, and getting a picture taken with him, or an autograph. * The KAWF calendars have been selling well thus far. More merchandise will be out at the first of the year so please keep your eye open for that. * Rumors are circulating Perfection might have one more member in mind to help round out there already devastating group. * Carson Ray's gambling problems we can confirm have reached six figures. Ray really needs to beat Corella at the supercard, even more so than before. * Nobody is sure who is NEXT in line for a shot at the Promotional Title. Some are saying there could be a rematch, or maybe even the undefeated Nightstalker would be given the opportunity. * TNT called out a local tag team from Florida before Fever started, and beat them in less than three minutes continuing to look very impressive since joining the KAWF. Todd Van Rooyen got the flash victory on the local fan favorite. * Three more huge matches have been signed for "A Cold Day in Paradise" on December 30th. Kai Lewis will take on Mikey Mckay in a loser leaves town match. The Women's title will be on the line in a triple threat match up, and Brady Lowell will take on Todd Van Rooyen one on one in a special attraction match. * Tommy Danger was seen talking to Mike Pace at the KAWF headquarters during the week. Details have not been found out as of yet. * Paradise City by Guns N Roses is the official theme song for the upcoming supercard. * The company is currently being very picky about who they let into the federation due to the size of the roster, and great talent already located here. Five people have already been denied to join the company sadly for those reasons. * Mumblings have already started to occur from the boys in the back about the KAWF trying to expand. Outlaw has shot all rumors down quickly saying "We need to go slow", but many are still talking about a possible television deal, or pay per view deal with the list of names we have. * Vincent Case was seen enjoying the night life in Paris, France on more than one occasion during the week while the majority of the boys were in Lakeland, Florida putting on a hell of a show. Let's hope he is ready for his upcoming matches. * The Mason Dixon Title finals should be a good one know matter who reaches the end. Cyn Bailus has shown he is a fighter. Red Hood is KAWF alumni by all accounts of the masked man. Johnny Pain is one of the best technical wrestlers not only on the roster, but in the world, and his tag team partner Damian Payne is as strong as they come. * Both the DEF BI POSSE, and OBSOLETE ARMY ripped up the restraining orders backstage after Fever to help set the table for there tag team title match. * Lucifer Magnusson of RPW fame is one of the name's being tossed out there to be the personal trainer of Foodstamp. Whomever it is has caused Foodstamp to go from a fan favorite to a person the crowd jeer's at his presence. * Steve Greedy of SPW, and IGA fame has agreed to work a future match with Damian Payne here in the KAWF. Nobody is sure of the location, or date as of yet. * The KAWF has sent possible contract offers to Rosco Riggins, Eric Vanguard, Sammy Knight, and Johnny Malibu in an effort to get them to work limited matches for the company. Possibly even just house shows to help get some more name recognition for the KAWF. * The KAWF is looking forward to using ring vets Corey Hart, Ben Tuckwell, and David Irvine more next cycle. * The KAWF has promised in 07 they will make some changes to the KAWF myspace to help encourage more activity from the fans, wrestlers, and casual viewers. Also hoping to keep it more up to date with the current surroundings of the company. * January 13th Adam Lazarus, Johnny Spades, and Ben Tuckwell will be at the KAWF headquarters doing a one hour free wrestling clinic. The following week for 50 bucks per person they will be doing a one day clinic at the headquarters to help all people wanting to get a break into the business. No love loss, or hugs being handed out during this match. We have seen words of anger, attacks, and even blood shed when these ladies meet up. Expect more of the same for this match up. As a result of this his bank balance has sky-rocketed and now he has decided to get into the business side of the profession as well as also still competing regularly. Upon discovering that former IXWA owner Neil Bonser had hit hard times, Payne contacted him and purchased the IXWA for an undisclosed sum. Manager: Neil Bonser - Neil was once the owner of the IXWA but made the choice to close it down after he was offered a position with the ICWF. Now, with his ICWF stint over, Neil has been appointed the manager of the IXWA whilst Damian Payne continues his in-ring career. Neil Bonser doesn't have the money that Payne has, but he does have the knowledge which is needed to guide the IXWA to the next level. Bonser tends to support the fan favorites. When he appears on screen he wears business attire (suits and ties). The IXWA's first show will be on January 12, 2007 and fortnightly thereafter. To be involved with the IXWA head on over to http://z11.invisionfree.com/ixwa and fill out an application. A tournament will be starting on January 12 to crown the IXWA International champion, however other titles will be up for grabs in the coming weeks. ****************************************************************************************************************************** THOUGHTS FROM APATHY Everyone has asked me why I wanted to get into the ranks of the KAWF. Why would I want to further intensify my career. Prolong the retirement that I should have went into. And it really is simple if you look at it from my perspective. The talent that the KAWF has. A lot of wrestlers within these confines are considered to be some of the greatest in the world. Some of the most high flying, most technical, best brawlers that are known world wide. And that caught my eye. But still doesn't answer my purpose. I want to show you all something. No, it isn't a little nipple, its that you are nothing. I'm not trying to be cocky, but when you have had a career like I have had, then you can afford to be it. I am here to prove that there is only one woman that can tangle with whatever is thrown before her. That there is only one that is good enough to dominate a woman's division, as well as a men's. This entire company.This sport for that matter. I'm here, plain and simple, to plant the seed of surrealism. The reality being that things just got a little more complicated. I have a goal for my career. That goal being that I will not rest until my name is both known and feared on every continent, every town and every city from one coast to another. KAWF is just one more name on the rung of this ladder I'm climbing. ****************** 7 - Closing ***************** Woooooooo now it's time to produce your best role play to date as a lot of these matches should be very close by the looks of things.
