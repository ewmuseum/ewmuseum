KICK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents **************************** 1 - Notes from the desk - Out of Character 2 - News - In Character 3 - Roster & Records 4 - Various Rankings, and Title Holders 5 - Line ups 6 - Final Notes ***************** 1 - Notes from the Office ****************** Gentleman, gentleman, gentleman you did excellent this week with the pay per view knocking on all of your door steps. I was very impressed with the effort, and ideas sent my way for the show. I hope you all enjoyed the show, I think it came over quite well actually. Not the BEST KAWF PPV of all time, but definitely one in which we can grow on, and be proud that we were apart of. People flashed well, strat'd well, sent in just enough on cards, and ideas to make it an entertaining read. Plus all the matches were a pretty solid read to say the least thanks to guys like Pat, Rick, Jon, AJ, and Ryan stepping up to the plate. Let's keep up the good work, and see if we can't do even better this next cycle which will end with Mike Johnson's Birthday Bash in August. Don't forget we will be taking next week off to give myself a chance to recooperate from all the writing this week, and help make the debut of War Stories a good one as well. Since the pay per view came out a little bit late, I am moving the rp, and strat deadline back a bit, but none the less don't be expecting a show this weekend, cause well there isn't one.
