KAWF Returns

Howdy gentleman, Most of you know me, some of you don't. I had to dig for some screen names so I'm sure not all of you have been horrified by my role playing talents in the last ten years. I have handled guys like Horo Atashi, Outlaw, Steve Greedy, and Jason Jones amongst a few others in federations such as IGA, SPW, IWF/WOW, OWE, DHWS, and many crappy other ones. I'm just trying to get it known that I am gonna be re - starting KAWF in roughly a month which ran for a total of seven years, off and on . ... off and on . . .off and on in the late 90's, and some of the 2000's .. .. You get the picture. So tell your friends, your girlfriends, your babies mommas, your lovers, and your gay uncle to please email me at [name withheld] if interested. You can go ahead, and send an old application if you want, or a simple email stating you are interested, and I shall get back to you asap. In the next few days I'll be sending out an application with some details to those interested, and posting them up wherever I can. Hope some of you are interested. . . It's going to be a BIT different than the old KAWF . .. with a bit of a lighter schedule, but it should be just as entertaining at a slower pace. We used to do weekly shows, and I must say that's not happening. Were also gonna go smaller budget than we used to . .. and not try for a wwe feel, but more of an ROH feel. I know it's been done, but it hasn't been done by me, yada yada .. . Those who know me will tell ya I'm easy to work with, and give you a good bit of creative freedom so I think this will be fun. Much respect, [name withheld] NOW GO SPREAD THE WORD MOFOSSSSSSSSSS
