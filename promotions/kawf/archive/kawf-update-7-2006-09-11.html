KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 7 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Alright guys this is the week we have been building to. I have no clue who is going to win any of these matches, or where we are going afterwards. This is the _end_ of a cycle, and I am expecting things to easily transition into next cycle with some of the minor things we already have planned for the supercard. All of our hard work has led us to this point. All of the building we have done, has led us to this event. Myself, and the guys who I have lined up to write a match all plan on doing the best we can to make this supercard an event, your happy to promote, and happy to get together to talk about. Most importantly an event your happy to be APART of. I'm excited. I hope you are to cause your what makes the KAWF the KAWF. Without you it's just me pudding around ideas in my free time, with you . .. this is one of the more entertaining, and fun federations on the map. I'm not the only one who thinks that. So with that said. Remember for this show we do have a tron so you can do any sort of segment you want, and it go on the show, and please do your best role play. All matches will be decided by role plays, in close matches [WHICH MOST OF THEM WILL BE] I will also look into strats, and on cards to help sway my final judgement. Remember that not all of you can win. I know people will be upset, and want to win, but the fact of the matter is we all can't. I do want to assure you though none of your characters will be written in a way in which you can't redeem the loss. All characters will be portrayed in a light they can move in a direction after the event in which will help them become a valuable part of the KAWF. I promise you that.
