The Actual Application ---------------------------------- 1. The Purpose of KAWF --------------------------------- Have fun, and give an alternative federation to those who want something a little different than what's out there. In short, that's what the purpose of this federation is. We give a good amount of creative freedom to the handlers, while booking top notch matches amongst solid role players as much as humanly possible for the best level of excitement / competition. Nine out of ten people who have been on the KAWF roster over the last seven years have enjoyed themselves, I don't think this time will be much different on that side of things. There will be some differences with this version of the KAWF, from the old days of the KAWF, but I don't think creativity, or level of fun will be one of them. The main differences will include lighter schedule, more of an indy feel than big time federation feel, and some office member / storyline situations which have changed. Mainly the last time we were seen we were called the SAWF as part of an angle, well that's gone now, and the story will play out over for the first month or so of shows. Now keep reading. ---------------------------- 2. Office Members of KAWF ---------------------------- * Owner - Mike Pace - Mike was once a KAWF booker, the main booker infact. Mike developed a love for the KAWF which only could be matched by it's past owner / president Mike "The Always Right" Johnson who ran a big budget promotion cause of his deep pockets. Mr Pace doesn't have the pockets that Johnson had, but he does have the knowledge, and love for the KAWF in which is needed to guide it to the next level. Mike knows business as well as Paris Hilton knows men. *Commish - Outlaw - Outlaw has always been a face in the KAWF to keep justice, but normally he was a bodyguard for Mr Johnson. Pace has decided to give Outlaw some power, and let him be in charge of running the shows, while he focuses more on dealing with the business aspect of things, and the public relations. Outlaw has been apart of KAWF for all seven years of its existence, so if anybody can keep the order around here. It's Outlaw. *Gopher - Sunny Powell - If something needs to be done, the crew sends the hoe to do it. Sunny is not evil like most the past women of the KAWF, mainly that of the old VP Kristen Roberts, but she does get a lil sick n tired sometimes of being treated like a . .. well . ..a woman. She seeks more power, but currently she's nothing more than a cute errand girl with a side role of keeping track of arenas, dates, and gossip. She is considered an office member, but doesn't have an official title as of yet. -------------------------------------- 3. Tradtional Rules and Regulations -------------------------------------- 1. The KAWF will be an all Fake e-mail federation of course, so the only person you will send things to, for shows or when it comes to flashing, is yours truly. But I do highly encourage the getting together of handlers to make things come out on air a little bit better than they would have without the actual handlers getting in touch with each other. So anytime you want a role players E-mail Addy you let me know and I'll see what I can do .. 2. We are here to have fun...So please express all ideas, opinions, or even what you may consider facts. I am pretty level headed and easy to get along with. Part of this is also though, when you lose....cause it will happen....Everybody loses sometime or another..Please don't complain, Just take it like a man and re-group for the next go around.. 3. No More than One Flash per show. Feel free to do an on card, or a special segment for the actual presentation of Fever, but don't send me more than one flash for Speaking Your Mind. --------------------- 4. KAWF Titles --------------------- * Promotional Champion - This is the big dog. The Promotional title has always been apart of the KAWF, sometimes it was the secondary belt behind the Global Title, and sometimes it was the number one belt, but make no mistake about it this time .. .This is the top dog belt, and shall be showcased as the top dog's trophy. Were not going Global this time around so that title was axed, and this title has been placed on a pedestal. *Tag Team Champions - I love tag teams, and my friends this will be a strong division even if I have to blow a bunch of dudes to get the tag team division going. I love me some tag team wrestling, so don't let me down. Send me some applications. *Toughman Champion - This will be our hardcore title. This is where the gimmicks, blood, and shady ideas will come into play for the most part. This is where my warped mind gets to have some fun. For now that's it. I want to keep things fairly small if possible. ----------------------------------- 5. Schedule and Shows ----------------------------------- * Saturday Night Fever - This show will be bi weekly with about three to five matches on it. Most of your stuff will happen on this show, and can take place from anywhere in North America. Supercards - These will have spiffy names, and happen whenever I see fit. When these do occur it will happen three weeks after the last show, thus canceling a fever to give us more time to work on the big shin dig to make it high quality. Bonus Shows - You don't have to role play for em, every now and then I'll just spitout a bonus show which will be done in old school house show format as just a lil bonus for you fellas out there. Speak Your Mind - This is the role play packet. This will be sent out the Thursday before Saturday Night Fever. The deadline for these will probably be on that Wednesday thus giving you a lil over a week to role play on a consistent basis. ----------------------------------------- 6. Roleplaying and Strats ----------------------------------------- Roleplays will be sent out on "Speak Your Mind" a few days before the show. Please remember only one role play per RP packet. I want this to completely be about quality, and not quantity. When it comes to role playing I Just hope we can see some serious realistic stuff, along with some witty humor...I love creativity, but remember we are striving for realism here in the KAWF... I want to have fun, and expand our minds, but I want to do things which we could really see if the ROH, or somebody scratched there noggins. Strats, These are meant to help develop your characters, Help the match writers, and get story lines over in good detail...I highly encourage these, Tell me some good spots for your characters, Against your characters, Give me there mindset, attitude, feelings...Nothing to fancy, but lemme know what you want done and I'll do the best to try and make you happy...I'm not perfect, but I can try to be eh ? In a close match this could be the difference. --------------------- 7. Character choices ---------------------- The KAWF wants you to be creative when you decide your character for the federation....I love Gothic, I love cowboys, the Rich cocky guy we love to hate, Thugs, Hitmen....Whatever, but remember we want to keep it realistic, and we want to cut back on out rageous gimmicks...Maybe one or Two, but I want the fed to bring back an old feeling...Which puts excitement in the in rings, announcing and action instead of on the piss your guy left in all of the KAWF locker rooms =).... Winners will be decided on role plays, but storylines will be developed for both guys majority of the time. Angles will be focused around winners, not winners focused around angles if that makes sense. ----------------------------------------- 8. KAWF Announce Team ----------------------------------------- Fever Announcers Frankie Tha Pimp - Saturday night fever will be a three man booth, as what is a Mike Johnson fed without Frankie Tha Pimp..Long time NMW AND KAWF announcer Frankie Tha Pimp has been with Mike Johnson since there youth....Don't let the name fool you, He knows this sport like the back of his hand, but Frankie is one of the most controversial announcers in the history of the sport and will only be working strictly for the KAWF, once again. Once the new KAWF rolls around. Russell Cunningham - The very experienced DWHS announcer has decided to bring his work to Mike Pace and the KAWF. Like Frankie The Pimp knows the sport like the back of his hand, but he is more of your traditional type announcer. Nowhere near the arrogance and cockiness that Frankie has. Russell has never worked with Frankie before, but from now on Russell's going to get to see first hand why Frankie the Pimp was the most controversial announcer in the game for six years. Prison - The man known for sipping 40's is back at the booth with Frankie. The two who made us laugh, the two who made us cry, the two who shocked us are both back to try to do the same damn things once again. For any wrestlers who get in the face of Prison just remember he was the first ever KAWF heavyweight champion and the only reason he ever released the belt was because of leg problems. He is no slouch still to this day. Supercard Announcers Frankie the Pimp - See Above. Russell Cunnigham - See Above. White Rose - The lovely Rose is the only female announcer in the new KAWF. She will be lucky enough to get to work the big shows with our boys Russell and Frankie. It will be interesting to see how our guys react to this lovely young lady. She will only work supercard shows, or anniversaries Interviewers * Fat Mike - The loveable stuttering chubby interviewer is back, and ready to be made fun of once again. He is a face, but even the faces sometimes pick on this chubby lil guy. Have fun with him. * James Fontenot - This is our more serious announcer. He will ask the tough questions, and be persistent if need be. Ring Announcer * Jessica Delgado - The lovely mexican announcer has stayed on with the federation, and decided to continue being the ring announcer. She will also do the national anthem at shows for us. Head of Security Jay Trisler - One of Mike Johnson's old buddy's, Has signed on to try and keep things under tradtional rules and regulations..With the help of Outlaw, Hopefully he can...He is an older man, with a lil bit of a beer gut, but he is a former Army Ranger who is willing to do whatever is necessary if put in a situation...Hopefully the situations will not come up, but if they do so...We are confident Jay can stop them for us... ----------------------------------- 9.
