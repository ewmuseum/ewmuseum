KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 4 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup gentleman ? I liked this show. Best one out of the three IMO. Starting to get some storylines, character development, and my match writing talents back to a degree. Not quite back, but the flow /pace of the matches are getting better. I really wanted to focus on the heel/face situations which I think we did with these first three shows. Most of you are doing a great job. I have talked with all of you guys, and I'm glad your as excited about all this as I am. To the one's I haven't spoken with, look me up. Let's discuss, let's talk, let's male bond. Remember guys In it for the long haul fellas, in it for the long haul. A win doesn't make you, or break you. Consistency will. I need developed storylines, with handlers patient enough to develop the aforementioned. I would like for you all to keep giving me feedback so we get to the point we want with the federation. Plus if possible make a myspace for the KAWF stuff. You don't have to have photos, or nothing like that. Just something simple so you can see the bulletins, the blogs, the calendar stuff, and be apart of the comment board. I want us to interact. I think it would help. Getting a myspace account won't take you, but a second. Then if you decide you want to add stuff to it later, you can. For now all you need is an account so you can get in on the behind the scenes stuff of the KAWF that you can only see if your on the friend list. That link is http://www.myspace.com/kawf incase you haven't been by there yet. We have the calendar up for all events leading to payday after the supercard, some of the old KAWF matches, a blog from Mike Pace himself, the IGA showcase match between Mikey Mckay & Kyle Sommers, plus a brief interview with Chris the handler of Kyle Sommers to see what he loves about this hobby will be up shortly. My next interview will be in character with Devon Case. Then I'll prolly do an out of character interview with handlers of the Obsolete Army to go up onto myspace. They amuse me. So please .. . Join in . . .Help make it as fun as possible. The small details like this is what makes a federation above average. Much Respect, Josh. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * Czon Yozon received a tongue lashing backstage from the commissioner to try to encourage him not to ruin any more matches, much less main events. Even if the hatred is deep for the man whose match you interrupted, but was not fined since it occurred DURING the match. Unlike the post match attack from the Obsolete Army at the show in Crossett. * When the main camera's stopped taping Fever Johnny Spades got on the microphone angrily, and tried to get Yozon in the ring. Our small camera's caught Yozon coming back out, but bailing on the fight with Spades getting heel heat from all who were left near the ring. This will be available on the DVD in a hand held camera mode. Spades get's really vulgar with his nemesis. For mature audiences. * The final two spots of the four way for the Promotional Title match on September 30th will be filled at the next show. Kyle Sommers & Frankie Cash of course have already secured there spots. * The KAWF is very pleased with there newest members Leon Corella, and Carson Ray. Carson's in ring work was fun to watch, and Corella was easy to talk with backstage, plus cut a nice heel promo. * Vincent Case will be interviewed after getting upset in the three way last show. This interview will go up on Myspace later this week. Along with a blog from the Owner Mike Pace, and another old KAWF match from a few years back. * The KAWF fans have been sending in emails left, and right mad about Larry Gionet not showing up at the recent show, but we assure you he did not do it out spite. Just prior commitments with the SYKO SUMMER series starting up on the East Cost the day before. * Johnny Pain had his stitches re - opened by Mark McKenna [The man who gave him the stitches to begin with] during there match, but is doing fine. Some fans were concerned with the heavy blood loss of real damage being done, but it was the simple case of not letting an old wound heal. *Stipulations for the three title matches at _WELCOME HOME_ will be announced at the upcoming Monroe, Louisiana show at the old Paradox club. That show is shaping up to the biggest one since returning to the wrestling scene. * The Mckay Versus Sommers match drew good ratings for the IGA in what was a very back, and forth match up, but it also formed bad blood amongst the two causing a rematch to occur in Augusta on KAWF battlegrounds. If you saw what Jasmine Greene did to Mikey Mckay you know why there fighting again. * Tickets are selling nicely in all three upcoming Louisiana spots. It appears the KAWF will again make there headquarters, and stomping grounds in the North Louisiana area. * The Adam Lazarus, and Frankie Cash T - shirts will debut in Augusta to join the Johnny Spades, and KAWF shirts already made. Kyle Sommers, and MUKA shirts will debut at the Monroe show. After that the office is currently split on making a Vincent Case shirt, or a Damian Payne shirt. * For the first time ever the KAWF has signed a woman, with intent of her not just being a valet, or announcer. That woman being Nikki Glass. This could encourage the KAWF to bring a women's title in instead of another title for the boys. Time will tell * Nightstalker is not booked for the Augusta show, but is on the payroll for being at the event. Interesting isn't it people ? I think the time for the Stalker to debut is apon us. * A contract signing between Adam Lazarus & Foodstamp will take place next show setting up there weapon versus weapon streetlife title match for the following show. Special guest Lark Fenriz will be on hand for the contract signing. Lark is a former KAWF streetlife champion. If the match comes across as good as planned, many are saying there could be a rematch at the supercard, or possibly a three way luring Fenriz out of retirement. * The KAWF is still working hard to try to gather a big screen by the Supercard event so backstage things can be shown to the live public. Until then keep purchasing the DVD's even if you went to the event so you don't miss _anything_ at all which happens on Fever. * The KAWF is in talks with a former KAWF champion to come back to the company on a full time basis. No one shots, no interview spots, but to wrestle a full schedule. The fans in GEORGIA damn sure aren't as this could be a show to remember. As in a show your gonna wish a year from now you attended if at all possible. _so be there_ With the KAWF going back to there stomping grounds after this one, who know's how long it will be before the KAWF comes back to GEORGIA, or any other state for that matter. If you don't live in Louisiana, and want to view the KAWF life this could be your last chance for a few months. C'mon down from Alabama, Arkansas, or even Flordia. We don't mind. In the opening bout LEON CORELLA will make his in ring KAWF debut against a man who main evented the return show in LONGVIEW, but has not had an easy time of things since being put in that main event. That man being PETE WHEALDON. Mr WHEALDON is one of the more stiff workers on the roster, but can't seem to put matches away near the end. CORELLA is going to give him another oppurtunity, but if the rumors are true about this guy, one mistake, and WHEALDON could be in the loss column once again. The night will continue as we have a rematch from a bout which occurred on IGA television earlier in the week. A bout which went back, and forth before KYLE SOMMERS defeated MIKEY MCKAY. The rematch was signed for KAWF however cause of a post match low blow by the lovely JASMINE GREENE that could be heard from the cheapseats of the arena. Can the rapper beat the superstar ? Can he slap a ho? Well we won't know until the rematch occurs, but this time it's KAWF instead of the IGA which means _anything_ is possible folks. KYLE has earned himself a spot in the upcoming Promotional Title match, he can not afford a loss, but MIKEY needs to get a victory to take his name value to the next level in this business. _STRICT RULES MATCH_ will take place next changing the tone a bit from the first two bouts. A rematch from the opening bout of the return show. This match will feature a match in which all the rules will be strictly enforced. One mess up, and you can loose. No closed fist, no tight grabbing, rope using, eye poking, or anything else for that matter. KAI LEWIS will return to the ring to face a man he has grown to respect. A man he battled to a draw a little over a month ago. A man by the name of JOHNNY PAIN in what could be the match of the night. Hopefully the Obsolete Army will keep there noses out of this one, and we will have a winner. Nobody wants to see a sloppy finish, or another draw for that matter. CARSON RAY will then be given time to "speak his mind' about his debut performance. CARSON will be given a mic, and a stage in the center of the squared circle to speak about his own talents, his own goals, his debut victory that ended in a bitter sweet way I'm sure, and future plans involving the company. Nobody is sure exactly what kind of mood one Mr RAY will be in come Fever. This match could of been the main event, but it's safe to say Augusta, Georgia is getting two big time matches to end the night. The _THIRD_ spot in the Promotional Title match will be filled as JOHNNY SPADES takes on the man who cost him his chance last show against FRANKIE CASH the one, and only former WLW champion CZON YOZON. Since there is bad blood there for this one, the KAWF has agreed to make this a no dq, pinfalls count anywhere match up so the score can be settled, and the spot can be filled. They both WANT to main event here in the KAWF, but now we find out exactly who will be in the main event. One spot remains for the Promotional Title four way, and that spot will remain no longer. We know the other three, and now it's time to get to know the last one. VINCENT CASE will take on DAMIAN PAYNE in the main event of the evening to see who moves on for the biggest prize in the KAWF. VINCENT is coming off of a shocking loss in a threeway where FOODSTAMP of all people pinned him with a little help from ADAM LAZARUS, but must rebound to take on the man who some say ran LARRY GIONET out of town DAMIAN PAYNE. DAMIAN has had his problems so far in the KAWF with the OBSOLETE ARMY, but now must put that all behind him, and focuses on CASE. The betting odds are even on this one, and the discussion has already started about who will come out on top. Want to know who wins ? We bet you do. Were hoping you do. Were counting on it actually. We want you there on August 12th to find out. See you then ladies, and gentleman.
