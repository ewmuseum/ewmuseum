KAWF Titles 5. Schedule and Shows 6. Roleplaying and Strats 7. Character choices 8. KAWF Announce Team 9. KAWF Referee Team 10. The Actual Application ---------------------------------- 1. The Purpose of KAWF ---------------------------------- January 1, 2003 marks the return of something that once put smiles to our faces and tears in our eyes. Yes, you people guessed it January 1, 2003 marks the return of the KAWF. . . . What ? Did you think I was talking about another Carmen Electra porn ? Of course I'm not why in the hell would that make you cry. Now in all seriousness I am going to bring back the KAWF. Which has had several good runs in the past. One in which lasted three years and another which lasted just over a year. Most of you know me some of you don't but none of that really matters. The KAWF is going to have a rich history, but at the same time we do plan to start fresh. Newbies, veterans, and old time KAWF stars are all invited to participate in the federation which we hope will be at the top come the end of 2003. Some of you hate trying out new federations and I understand that cause I'm the same way. But if you would please give me a chance and I really believe the KAWF won't be a waste of your time. ---------------------------- 2. Office Members of KAWF ---------------------------- *Owner/President : Mike Johnson - The man known as Mike 'Always Right' Johnson is back to run the federation which has had his name on the ownership papers for seven years now. He briefly tried to run SAW and HIWO but towards the end of their runs he realized his heart was still with the KAWF. Some things have changed since we last seen the president, but hopefully he will still be the fair man we all once knew. *Bodyguard for the president: Outlaw - The man known as Outlaw has wrestled, worked with, and went against Mike Johnson all through his legendary career. Outlaw's wrestling days have been over with for at least a year now, but Mike Johnson still knows what this man is capable of. In the near future you will see that Mike Johnson's life is not what it used to be, but with the hiring of the legendary Outlaw hopefully things can still come out good. He's not part of the security team in the KAWF, but if you ever cross the boss this will be the man you have to go eye to eye with. *Vice President/ Public Relations Specialist : Kristen Roberts - She may look innocent and sweet but this red - headed bombshell has recently married her way in to the Mike Johnson family and into the legacy which is KAWF. Surprisingly, she does have some executive experience in small independent federations. But never has she been on a stage as big and grand as the KAWF. It will be interesting to see what happens when the Father - in - law disagrees with the fiery daughter - in - law. *Head Booker/Public Relations Specialist: Lady Love - Yes that's right the wife of the Outlaw is once again part of the KAWF office. Lady Love along with Kristen Roberts will be in charge of all public relations and marketing in the KAWF. What will happen when blonde bombshell meets red - headed bombshell ? Who the hell knows but it should be fun to watch. *Commish: Jay Wheery - The former Vice President of the KAWF, which got fired , due to the lying and cunning skills of former wrestler Steve Gill, is back back where he belongs. In the past five years Mike and Jay have sat down and had a long talk at the end of this conversatin they decided to renew their old friendship and once again go into business with one another. Everybody who knows Jay knows he will do anything for the ratings. -------------------------------------- 3. Tradtional Rules and Regulations -------------------------------------- 1. The KAWF will be an all Fake e-mail federation of course, so the only person you will send things to, for shows or when it comes to flashing, is yours truly. But I do highly encourage the getting together of handlers to make things come out on air a little bit better than they would have without the actual handlers getting in touch with each other. So anytime you want a role players E-mail Addy you let me know and I'll see what I can do . 2. We are here to have fun...So please express all ideas, opinions, or even what you may consider facts. I am pretty level headed and easy to get along with. Part of this is also though, when you lose....cause it will happen....Everybody loses sometime or another..Please don't complain, Just take it like a man and re-group for the next go around.. 3. No More than Two Flashes a week per character, and Please no Logo's, colors or cute little fonts [ I put that in there for the AOL guys, since this is going to be a net fed]..... --------------------- 4. KAWF Titles --------------------- The KAWF is gonna start out with three titles, and more may be added later when we see it necessary or beneficial to the federation, but for now the following are the only titles you can win, Which makes them that more special to have them.. 1 - The KAWF Promotional Championship - This is the impact player of the federation, He is the man currently holding the key to the success of the federation. Our top belt, Held by our top guy at that current time in the federation.. 2 - The KAWF Global Championship : This is the man sitting by the throne, Being the backbone of the Federation..He represents the World and this company the best he can..He will perform a little more often than our champion, As all three office members like to make title fights special, So the belts won't be defended every week, But since there is only two single titles (for now)...This man will be hunted by many and probably stalked by some. 3 - The KAWF Tag Team Championship - Office Member Jay Wheery loves tag team action to the fullest extent. Most federations have a weak Tag division, but Wheery stated in a news article involving the KAWF's opening " He will do whatever, It takes to make the tag division Prosper"...So we will just have to see what he means by that... Possible belts for the Future 1 - The KAWF cruiserweight championship - For the little guys, who make sure we don't blink during there matches and make us go blind with there speed...have to be under 225 pounds 2 - The KAWF Television championship - The man best for marketing and the man who will probably be the busiest wrestler in the company if you know what I am saying. 3 - The KAWF Streetlife Championship - This is for those chair swinging, Blood dripping, Snot nosed tough guys out there....The guy's who don't want rules and won't follow them anyway fit in right here... ----------------------------------- 5. Schedule and Shows ----------------------------------- The KAWF will have A review/ House show type show Every Sunday which is called War Stories, A live interview session called "Speak your Mind" on Tuesday's and Thursday's, Along with a weekly show on Saturday's called "Saturday Night Fever"...Occasionally a house show might appear, and once a month or maybe every two months the KAWF will have a supercard which will rock the wrestling world... Tuesday and Thursday = Flash Packet days Saturday = Our Main show Sunday = A review of the past, A Preview of the future and some House show type matches for the present.. Deadline's and dates will be given later when the federation technically "Starts"... ----------------------------------------- 6. Roleplaying and Strats ----------------------------------------- Roleplays will be sent out on "Speak Your Mind" On Tuesday's and Thursdays...Don't get these confused with In ring's or Interview's though..They will of course be posted on the results or the review show depending on what day they are meant for.. When it comes to role playing I Just hope we can see some serious realistic stuff, along with some witty humor...I love creativity, but remember we are striving for realism here in the KAWF... Strats, These are meant to help develop your characters, Help the match writers, and get story lines over in good detail...I highly encourage these, Tell me some good spots for your characters, Against your characters, Give me there mindset, attitude, feelings...Nothing to fancy, but lemme know what you want done and I'll do the best to try and make you happy...I'm not perfect, but I can try to be eh ? --------------------- 7. Character choices ---------------------- The KAWF wants you to be creative when you decide your character for the federation....I love Gothic, I love cowboys, the Rich cocky guy we love to hate, Thugs, Hitmen....Whatever, but remember we want to keep it realistic, and we want to cut back on out rageous gimmicks...Maybe one or Two, but I want the fed to bring back an old feeling...Which puts excitement in the in rings, announcing and action instead of on the piss your guy left in all of the KAWF locker rooms =).... ----------------------------------------- 8. KAWF Announce Team ----------------------------------------- Fever Announcers Frankie Tha Pimp - Saturday night fever will be a three man booth, as what is a Mike Johnson fed without Frankie Tha Pimp..Long time NMW AND KAWF announcer Frankie Tha Pimp has been with Mike Johnson since there youth....Don't let the name fool you, He knows this sport like the back of his hand, but Frankie is one of the most controversial announcers in the history of the sport and will only be working strictly for the KAWF, once again. Once January 1st rolls around. Russell Cunningham - The very experienced DWHS announcer has decided to bring his work to Mike Johnson and the KAWF. Like Frankie The Pimp knows the sport like the back of his hand, but he is more of your traditional type announcer. Nowhere near the arrogance and cockiness that Frankie has. Russell has never worked with Frankie before, but from now on Russell's going to get to see first hand why Frankie the Pimp was the most controversial announcer in the game for six years. Prison - The man known for sipping 40's is back at the booth with Frankie. The two who made us laugh, the two who made us cry, the two who shocked us are both back to try to do the same damn things once again. For any wrestlers who get in the face of Prison just remember he was the first ever KAWF heavyweight champion and the only reason he ever released the belt was because of leg problems. He is no slouch still to this day. War Stories Announcers Russell Cunningham - See above. Mike Pace - Mike has been demoted from Booker back to announcer where he origianlly came from. The reasons for this is still unknown, but hopefully soon Mr. Pace will open up and tell us why he is back behind the booth. Him and Russell should make a very solid team for War Stories. Supercard Announcers Frankie the Pimp - See Above. Russell Cunnigham - See Above. White Rose - The lovely Rose is the only female announcer in the new KAWF. She will be lucky enough to get to work the Pay per views with our boys Russell and Frankie. It will be interesting to see how our guys react to this lovely young lady. Interviewers Johnny Jackson - The only Black worker in the KAWF offices. Johnny will ask the questions most won't, and will not be scared to do so as he is an ex bouncer / Martial Artist trainer....None of the talent will push this man around,and hopefully he can do some pushing and shoving to get us the info we all want... Samantha Owens - This Beautiful Blond bombshell was told she couldn't wrestle anywhere in the United States by the powers higher than even Mike Johnson, cause of an incident of her posing nude in her younger days...So instead of wrestling in some trashy wrestling federation, Or going into hardcore wrestling, this lovely 21 year old lady will not wrestle in the KAWF, But will be given a chance to show what she is made of as a mic will be in her hand here in the KAWF.... Ring Announcer Stacey Normand - One of the few red heads you will ever see with a good tan, Will keep you posted with the height's, Weights, Locations and so on of your favorite KAWF wrestlers as she will be the one bringing them to the ring night after night here in the KAWF... Head of Security Jay Trisler - One of Mike Johnson's old buddy's, Has signed on to try and keep things under tradtional rules and regulations..With the help of Jay Wheery, Hopefully he can...He is an older man, with a lil bit of a beer gut, but he is a former Army Ranger who is willing to do whatever is nessecary if put in a situation...Hopefully the situations will not come up, but if they do so...We are confident Jay can stop them for us... ----------------------------------- 9.
