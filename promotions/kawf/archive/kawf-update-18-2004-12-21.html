I hope you liked the results, I think they were nice building blocks. This week's Christmas show, and the New Years show Im hoping to make a bit heavier on action, instead of storylines. Between the two shows, I would like to see two or three match of the year quality matches. I hope I can pull that off for you guys. The last flash packet I sent out was one of the best packets we have had in months, keep up the good flashes, and on cards. It makes my job a TOUCH harder as far as picking winners, but it sure does make it a hell of alot more fun for all of us. This is a hobby I have been doing for along time now, and it's a much better enviroment to be in when your not sure if your going to win, or not .. . When competition is stiff. It makes it THAT more interesting, and that much more appealing when you do win, or get your angle to come across just as you had it in your head. God I love this hobby. Let's get on with the update shall we ? Merry Christmas to each, and everyone one of you guys, and to the one gal we have on the mailing list.
