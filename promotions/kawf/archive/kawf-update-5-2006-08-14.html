KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 5 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Sup gentleman ? This was a good transition show, and came across about how I wanted it to. The show wasn't really about the action, but more so about setting up some feuds. Lot's of segments, and matches that will help lead us to where we need to be for the supercard. There is going to be _eight_ instead of five matches on the supercard. So please drop me a line if your interested in writing. So far I have two guys. I need about two more guys who are willing to write a good, solid back, and forth match. Not asking you to slop something together last minute for me. I am asking you to write a real match. Otherwise I'd rather struggle, and do an extra one myself. The handlers deserve it for this show. Obviously we can't all win, but I think we can all have our character portrayed in a good light. Maybe that's just me. As for the rest of things. So far so good. Brought in some new talent, shows are flowing pretty good now. Getting good feedback from you guys, and football season is about to start. All need is some tag teams, and a little more myspace activity, and were set. So no real complaints from yours truly, just having a blast helping you guys portray your characters. Now on with the update. Be sure to read this thing close. It gives you lot's of goodies IMO. Look's like one of the better technical wrestlers on the roster has healed. * Merchandise sells are still going good at the events, and doing average on the website keeping us under our monthly budgets by a nudge. MUKA, and Kyle Sommers shirts will be on sell starting at the show in Monroe. Currently for sell we have Frankie Cash, Adam Lazarus, Johnny Spades, and KAWF shirts to go along with old kawf events, the first four shows, and a baseball cap for the KAWF commish the Outlaw. One of them is rumored to have hold KAWF gold in the past. Safe to say it's not the lady considering the KAWF is just now for the first time ever starting that division up. * The KAWF has parted ways with Pete Whealdon, and Santana Sobral. We wish them the best of luck in the future. Pete says he loves the KAWF atmosphere, but is not ready for the intensity the KAWF brings. Santana has been having constant troubles with his VISA, and has decided to remain in his hometown to wrestle instead of continuing the battle in court. * On a different note the KAWF would like to welcome the following people to the active roster. The Red Hood, Jarrod Storm, Josie Saito, Nikki Glass, Nightstalker and Keith Dylan James. Jarrod Storm was of course the old partner of Kyle Sommers who helped win the KAWF tag team titles before the federation shut it's doors last time. * Congratulations to Vincent Case, Johnny Spades, Frankie Cash, and Kyle Sommers on making it to the four way for the Promotional Title. The stips will be named at the next show in Monroe, Louisiana. * In an effort to make the next show a great one with the KAWF returning to Louisiana for the final promoting of there upcoming supercard. The KAWF is putting the streetlife title on the line, plus having all four promotional title contenders in action, and speaking of all four KAWF divisions with announcements regarding stips, and matches for the Welcome Home event. Mike Pace has promised not to show his face on the camera, but rumors has it the commish will finally be making an appearance. Out of five shows this will be his first appearance, unlike other company's we try to let the wrestlers do the talking. * Rumors are swirling as to who the Red Hood could be. Some of the names which have popped up our Sammy Griffin, Sabbath, Hayden Grayson, Vile Vince Viper, Steel, or Travis Lagrange. He has really caught the eye of some people. * KAWF office members were really impressed with the Vincent Case & Damian Payne match. Like the wrestlers or not they put on a very intense battle for the final spot in the four way. A small amount of extra cash will be in there checks this week. * The ICWF is going to help the KAWF get a big screen for the upcoming KAWF supercard. Also with the help of the live auction the KAWF should be able to keep the big screen on a full time basis letting the live crowd see the backstage happenings. However the live crowd should steal purchase the DVDS cause there will be a bonus match on each one, and at least one post match segment with the wrestlers. Ya might even see your face on TV to, come on man . . purchase the DVDS. * The KAWF is looking to have another showcase match on the SPW Versus IGA supercard coming up early next month. Look for this to be done considering Outlaw is friends with both the owners. * Kyle Sommers got mega heat from the crowd when he used the tights to pin Mikey Mckay. The Augusta crowd really hated Mr Sommers. In news revolving Sommers his woman Jasmine Greene has been approached about doing a photo spread, along with Outlaw's wife Lady Love, and newly signed KAWF wrestler / valet Nikki Glass. * It was unknown to the KAWF office how Nightstalker would debut at the show in Augusta, but his appearance got positive feedback from the people with pull in the company. Even some of the backers like Tommy Flames, and Ronnie Jamieson spoke well of it. * Be sure to read below for matches signed for the supercard, and the event before it.
