KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 10 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - DVD extras 7 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information I hope all enjoyed the show. I think this show came across really well. Surprisingly a lot of you commented on the last show we had saying how much you liked it. I hope I get the same feedback on this one. My main concern was getting it out on time, cause the last two shows had a delay. I did that, I just hope I didn't hurt the quality of the show. I now have a real good idea of the booking for the rest of this cycle, and can be detailed with you guys discussing where your future is heading for the time being. Just remember guys sometimes less is more. Not everybody can get the big push all the time. If you continue to work hard though, and tell a story in the end you will be rewarded. We can't always win. We can't always have a title. There is other guys busting there ass to when it comes to the role playing. You have to understand that in a competitive federation like this. Most of you do, some of you are still trying work on that aspect of things. The only thing we can always do is tell a story about our character. Nobody can ever take that from you,. and I damn sure won't. Character development is a big part of the reason I do this hobby. Enjoy the update. Holla at me if you need me. If you need help with your time constraints email me as well we can work around it. I know finals are coming up guys. I'll work with you. Much Respect, Josh * I need some match writers on a more consistent basis please contact _ME_ if you can help. ***************** 2 - News from the rumor bin ***************** * The KAWF is really disappointed they have not got a signature from Xavier Orion yet. He will NOT be booked again till we do. What happened Saturday will not happen again on Fever. * Mark McKenna's "injury" is not being noted by the KAWF, only respected. By that we mean our Doctors checked him out, and decided he is cleared to wrestle, but McKenna was able to find a doctor stating he is only good for light duty until Christmas Day. Thus the titles won't be defended until the supercard, but the team will be booked. We promise you that. * Carson Ray & Leon Corella got into yet another argument backstage after Fever. Ray was more upset this time than ever before, it even seemed like it was more than just losing the title he was upset about. * The KAWF calendars are almost ready to be sent out to the stores. Just two more weeks. * Be sure when your ordering tickets below you check out the dates of the shows. They had to be changed up due to holiday schedules, and flight plans. * December 30th's supercard from the beaches of Destin, Florida will be titled " A cold day in Paradise" I believe you are all smart enough to figure out the meaning, and the reasoning. * Corey Hart's knee is still in below average shape, but the doctors have stated if he wants to wrestle he can. It's at his discretion. * The KAWF will be apart of the epic event WRESTLEBOWL in January of 2007. Mike Pace said he wanted to stay focused on KAWF until the end of the year, but was quick to get the company a piece of the Wrestlebowl pie in 07. Some of the rumored names to be representing the KAWF our Frankie Cash, Carson Ray, Michael Keathley, Leon Corella, Vincent Case, Brady Lowell, Corey Hart, Johnny Spades, and Red Hood. Of course not all those names will make it. * The house show in Japan was a success. Johnny Pain was really over, as was DEF BI POSSE. We hope to return in 07, and hope that the Australia show does just as well. Paris, London, Mexico, and Toronto have also been signed for future house shows. After the first tour we will way the pros, and cons, and decide if another is a good idea. * Nobody has a clue as to who the new "personal trainer" is for Foodstamp. Many have noticed a change in attitude however, more than likely due to this trainer. * The KAWF has decided not to count losses for the rumble, considering the nature of the event. Only the winner will be receiving a bump on his record. Congratulations to Mr Johnny Spades for winning that hard fought rumble. The KAWF was also impressed by some of the new talent like Corey Hart, Brady Lowell, and Ricky J. * Keith Dylan James has been released from the roster due to failure to meet KAWF standards. Spade was quickly put in his spot, as noted on Fever, but rumors are circling Spade was already going to be apart of the roster, so there could be one more individual coming in. * In Birmingham the name of the NEW KAWF title, and tournament participants will be named. Good luck to all involved in this event. This will be the secondary title for the company, and we hope you all make us proud raising the prestige of the belt up to as close to the Promotional Title as possible. * The KAWF is only accepting women, and tag teams for there roster right now. The rest is closed. * Outlaw has made it known he wants to get more order, and discipline at the shows. Less backstage attacks, and run ins. More straight forward action, and come to the booker if you got a problem. * It seems Josie Saito has formed alliance with the Hated Leon Corella. Mike Foyer is in the same boat. * Amy Vickers could possibly be working some of the KAWF house shows in the very near future. Adam Lazarus told us himself about his girlfriend wanting to be apart of that. * The New wrestling magazine "Squared Circle" which covers SPW, IW, and KAWF for the time being just came out earlier today. More federations will be added to the magazine as it expands to different readers. We hope you enjoy the effort put into that thing. * The KAWF is looking forward to heading east for there next few events. We hope the Southern hospitality stays the same as we get closer to the Ocean. * The referee Leon Corella attacked just recently got out of the hospital. He will be reporting back to his duties when the KAWF reaches South Carolina. * Some people are saying the Red Hood might be Vile Vince Viper under a different moniker. Lord we all hope not, or else the wool has definitely been pulled over our eyes. * The holidays are coming, everybody please get your free ham, and turkeys at the KAWF headquarters in Monroe, Louisiana.
