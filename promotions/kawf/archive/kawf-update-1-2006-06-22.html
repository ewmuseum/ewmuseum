KAWF Update #1

KAWF Kick Ass Wrestling Federation Update # 1 ****************** 1 - OOC Notes 2 - News from the rumor bin 3 - Roster 4 - Rankings 5 - Line up 6 - Closing ***************** 1 - Out of Character information Well guys, the first show is now upon us. Be sure to read these updates very closely, I want them followed as much as possible. The updates will answer 99 percent of your questions, but if you read the update, and still have a question please shoot me an instant message, or an email. I'll get back to you ASAP. I check my email on a daily basis. I'm a pimp like that. Updates will include deadlines, news [some fictional, some not], line ups, small details you might not know otherwise, and the updated roster. Remember this is being ran as INDY federation, a small time federation with a small time budget. This is not like the old KAWF. I repeat this is NOT LIKE THE OLD KAWF. So please don't come at me with huge entrances, stuff for a titan tron, or anything of that nature. We do not have a TV deal, we go straight to DVD to make our money. I want all on cards to take place at the arena, or around the arena unless it's mentioned that it was pre recorded. We will also have DVD extras on the updates sometimes, when I can't fit something on the show, but a wrestler has took the time to turn it in. Your flashes for Speak Your Mind can be done however you like for them to be done. They will of course not be seen on the actual show, so this is where you can be real creative. Let your mind wander, and come up with something to entertain the masses. We are assuming the viewer would only see these on the KAWF website, or on the EXTRA section of a KAWF DVD. I want to keep things as realistic, and edgy as possible. We might push some buttons, or stir some controversy, but we will be realistic. In the ring, and out of the ring. I am gonna avoid gimmick matches when possible, except for supercards, and try to have as much fun as possible doing so. I know it's kind of popular right now to like "ROH", but this federation will be ran more like ROH than WWE. It's not going to be a copy cat, or rip off by any means, but just think of that when your coming up with ideas. Remember sports, instead of entertainment. I know Mike is going to have to work from the ground up, but if anybody can do it, he is the man. I hope everybody in the Southern parts of the United States try and make it to one of their upcoming shows." * As of June 21st Hayden Grayson, Lezkanic, and Boys Next Door have not joined the KAWF, much to the surprise of the diehard KAWF following. * The original idea was to have the hardcore division title be called the toughman title, but that idea has been scrapped for the old title name. The division will once again be called the Streetlife division. All Streetlife matches will be NO DQ, but unless stated otherwise pinfalls must take place in the ring. * "The White Hope" Mikey Mckay has recently put out an underground rap albumn, and is hoping that wrestling in the South will help his record "blow" up as he so eloquently put it to us on Monday morning. * Former KAWF Vice President Kristen Roberts was recently seen in the company's home base area Monroe, Louisiana working as a cocktail waitress at the Library Lounge. Rumors have it she has gained about twenty pounds since we last saw her. To much free time for the once firey red head. * A special "dream match has been rumored for the first show at the old KAWF arena in Monroe, Louisiana featuring Hayden Grayson, and Karl Ferguson. That is if both wrestlers agree, and the damn building meets inspection. The same old announcing crew of RUSSELL CUNNINGHAM, FRANKIE THA PIMP, and PRISON are back to call the action as the KAWF makes its not so grand return to the wrestling scene in LONGVIEW, TEXAS in front of about three percent of the fans they used to perform for. This isn't the old KAWF where you see superstar _after_ superstar, fancy gimmicks, television, pay per views, huge arenas, and money spent like it grows off trees, this is the KAWF where the product will have to speak louder than the money. The _TEXAS_ fans will be greeted by something a little different than what there used to when they see that KAWF logo in the middle of the ring, but let's hope they like what they see cause the wrestlers would hate to have to stomp a stampede down in the middle of the center if somebody, or some people got a little out of hand. We know how you damn TEXAS people can be . .. Yeeeeeeeeee hawwwww, and all that jazz. In the opening bout, the match which will start the rebirth, and set the table for all things to come, two of the better pure wrestlers on the roster will be going head to head as "BROOKLYN'S FINEST" KAI A LEWIS will take on "THE EXTREME" JOHNNY PAIN. Lewis has been in the KAWF on two separate occasions, and knows the in's, and outs of the company. Pain on the other hand has only heard of the KAWF, and never experienced it. This could indeed be a show stealer. In the second match of the evening we will have a _forced tag team match_. Yea you heard me, these two teams weren't formed cause there all chummy, or wanted to go on double dates after the show with there little girlfriends. These two teams were picked by the office, for entertainment value only. Neither of these two teams would be together if they had a choice. We will see FRANKIE CASH make his in ring return, along with his partner the unique FOODSTAMP as they will take on CZON YOZON former WLW world champion, and JOHNNY SPADES. If you haven't heard Spades name in a while, that's cause well, he's been out of the business just like Mr Cash. If these men aren't rusty, we could be in for a helluva battle. Then we will give a man in which none thought would be signing the dotted line to compete in the KAWF a chance to speak. "SADISTIC INSANITY" DAMIAN PAYNE of IGA, and UKWA fame will be standing in front of the small KAWF crowd to as we like to say "speak his mind". We all know small crowds can get bored, or entertained very easily. I'd hate to see somebody heckle this 295 pound monster. Once the talking is over with, we will have the match all of the wrestling world is looking forward to the most on this particular card. "THE PHOENIX" ADAM LAZARUS will be looking across the ring at "THE SCOURGE OF VEGAS" VINNY CASE. Both these men have a deep history, and not with each other, but in this sport. All the tricks of the trade have been learned, and all the stops will be pulled out . .. These two men can flat out go. Then we do things how KAWF likes to do them to end the show, and send the fans home wanting more. We will have former KAWF tag team champion "THE PERFECT CREATION" KYLE SOMMERS along with his PHAT valet JASMINE GREENE taking on PETE WHEALDON in a NO DQ PINFALLS COUNT ANYWHERE type match up. And why ? Cause that's how we roll. . . .. Plus A whole hell of a lot more cause this is the debut show people Look for suprises, plots, story lines, good wrestling action, debuts, and more ******************** 6 - Closing ******************* Well that's it guys, the first update. I appreciate all of you joining, and I think we can have a lot of fun. Holla at me if you have any questions, and I'll be happy to answer them. The KAWF is my baby . .. just like IGA is Jon's baby, SPW is AJ's baby, EMWC is Blue's baby, and NEO is Wallie's baby. I have fun with the KAWF, but I want to put out a good product. So help me do it . .. Cause I damn sure can't do it alone. Remember don't use Mike Pace on screen for any reason, I want to try something different, and never have the owner be visible. Now I know the commish will prolly have to be used, but I think we can be creative enough to keep Pace off camera for a while, at least. Looking forward to reading everybody's work. Much Respect, Josh
