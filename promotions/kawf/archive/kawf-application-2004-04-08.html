Sup gentleman ? Long time no see . .. Just recently found out that the RSPWF was back, and im glad to see it. Below is the application to the KAWF. Some of you have heard of it, and some of you haven't, but I would appreciate it everybody just gave it a glance. Most people who have joined in the past said it was a really fun federation. For those who know me, yall know im pretty fair. So usually things run pretty smooth. Josh ******************************************************************************** KICK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents *************************** 1 - Purpose of KAWF 2 - Who runs the thing in Character 3 - Who runs the thing out of Character 4 - What do you have to do in KAWF 5 - List of titles, events, and people you might wish to know 6 - Application Federation F.A.Q. & Information * Some of this was taken from the IGA application. If you want another federation to join I suggest that one. Just don't go after the TV Champion . .He's a nice fellah from what I hear =) 1. What's the purpose of KAWF First things first, KAWF stands for Kick Ass Wrestling Federation. The KAWF started back in 1996 and ran till about 1999 before problems started to occur in scheduling and what not. Slowly the federation shut its doors in late 99 and opened them back up in 2001. Since then twice the KAWF has been opened, and this will be the third time. Probally the last time [ Something I didn't say the other times.]. It has become a high budget federation, and has a pretty good cult following. Alot of people have come and gone threw the KAWF doors, but one thing has always been for certain. NO BS TOLERATED. KAWF's main offices are located in New Orleans, Louisiana for the record but tours throughout North America with frequent shows and tours overseas as well making it an Internationally reconized promotion. Some of the best shows in KAWF history have been in Japan, and London. KAWF is a traditional organization with a modern feel, the flash and glamor mixed with straight solid worked wrestling matches. Gimmick matches are meant to be on special occassions not every time you turn around. KAWF is open to any kind of wrestler. Although, we would like a diverse group of wrestlers from Canada, United States, and from around the International scene. The roster size will be about 20-25 along with as many tag teams as we can find. I love tag team wrestling. Most importanly, it's all about fun. I strive to communicate with EVERYBODY on a regular basis, and even though we can't win everytime we step into the ring . . .I do believe we can keep most people happy by storylines, and communication purposes. That doesn't mean u will be getting a world title shot, or that every angle you want will be executed, but it does mean that we will work together to do the best we can to make yourself, and the KAWF come out looking better than it did going into it. 2. Who runs this thing? (Fantasy) The Owner, and President of the KAWF has always been, and will always be Mike "The always right" Johnson. It just wouldn't be right without this man calling the shots. He's usually playing the face role, and trys to do whats right. Sure his judgement gets thrown off a bit when the ratings are involved, but for the most part Mike is a good guy who knows the business very well. The last time KAWF shut its doors Mike was very sad, and went into a bit of depression. The ratings were high, the fan support was great, and the wrestling crew was as good as it got, but the staff got over whelmed by all the attention, work and hype. This time around Mike is willing to spend not just some of his money to make things work, but ALL of it if nessecary. This 49 year old man knows he doesn't have the time to mess around in this business with all the new up and comers, but wants to make his mark this time around in the wrestling business. He wants to be remembered for being the sharp, witty, easy going guy who led a low budget federation to the top of the wrestling world. Don't be shocked if he does it. As always there is a Vice President . . .Except this time Mike is stuck with a Vice President. He didn't get to choose one. His son's evil red headed wife who helped make the final descion of shutting down the KAWF last time is back, and oh boy did she have an evil grin on her face when Mike announced to the public KAWF was coming back. Mike tried to get this trouble maker out of the VP role this time around, but her contract held strong in court. Yep, Thats right . .. Kristen Roberts is back as Vice President of the KAWF. 4. What do I have to do in KAWF ? Roleplays/Flashes: The most important thing in KAWF is role playing. Plain and simple, if you don't role play, you don't win, and your story lines won't devlop very far if at all. I'm not saying every role play has to be your best, but it all starts with flashing. Fun is just as important as role playing, but if ya ain't role playing. I bet you aren't having ANY fun in the KAWF.There are some rules for role playing which are: - SWEARING. HBO and of course the occasional PPV's is where we do our shows. So we can stretch things a bit. Language, brief nudity, and some angles can be done in the KAWF that couldn't be done elesewhere cause of us being on HBO, but try not to cuss to much. Espically in your flashes .. Just remember that please. I like to leave most things open to you guys, but there is some limits to things. - NO ATTACKS: No physical attacks on announcers/reporters/staff/Wrestlers are allowed without the permission of the powers-that-be... a.k.a. Josh or Pat..If you do not ask for permission your flash most likely won't run. - KAYFABE: No mention of real wrestlers will be allowed... But other e-wrestlers and feds may be mentioned in your RPs IF they allow you too. Also, please keep the mention of "outside-KAWF" things to a minimum. It's fine to know that you gained the XYZ World belt, but we shouldn't remember it at every RP! -FUN. This is a rule . .. You must have fun. I want all of you to be as excited about everything as I am. I want you guys to lemme know when im messing up, and read all the shows. Get as excited as you used to get when RAW was coming on TV when you know a KAWF is about to come out. Stratagies & Angle Work: The KAWF stresses on card work, and Strategys. I love strats. They also help me decide who wins a close match. It shows that bit of extra effort that alot of people don't put in. Plus it helps match writers who might not know alot about your character. You don't want your guy high flying ? SEND IN A STRAT SO WE KNOW .. . Unlike alot of federations KAWF winners our only based on role plays. Unless the handlers both come to me saying they want Billy Joe to win there match to help develop a curtain story line, I will go on role plays. Angles will play apart in matches, but if someone out role plays you then your angle will just have to take a twist. I believe angles can be worked around winners, and not the other way around. This method has seemed to work so far for the most part. On cards don't help you win a match [ Less it's close then maybe ], but these our used to develop your characters and storylines. Nuttin like some good ol fashion air time to get something off your chest, or shock the crowd. 5. Titles, events, and People you might wish to know about. The following is a list of titles currently in the KAWF GLOBAL TITLE - This is our heavyweight championship. This is the one in which everybody wants to hold, and everybody will do anything in the world to get. The last person to hold this was the monster LezKanic, and he found out that being the Global Champion wasn't as easy as he thought it would be. This is defended at least Once every two months, but usually a bit more than that. We consider our Global Title to be a very big deal so it is extremly special for the title to be put on the line. PROMOTIONAL TITLE - This is our secondary title, and the champion in which the fans ethier love or hate the most. They see this guy in person, and on air alot more than the Global Champion, and learn alot more about him. There has been times in the federation that our Promotional Title was considered the top title by the public cause of the man who held it. This title is at least defended every 30 days, and must be defended on every other show done over seas when we go on those tours. STREETLIFE TITLE - This is for those tough, rugged, bad to the bone boys in which don't give a Sh[censored] about anything. Basically this is our extreme belt, and these matches are all No dq, pinfalls count anywhere. Sometimes there is more stips added, but you can almost guarantee to see some blood by the end of these things. This title must be defended every 30 days. We know some champions will defend it more, but remember these guys go threw alot. Give em a break will ya ? TAG TEAM TIT;LES - In alot of federations the tag team division, and titles go over - looked. Not here gentleman,not here. I love, I repeat I _LOVE_ tag team wrestling, and will not let the division go down the drain. Storylines will be developed for the tag team division, and the champions will be looked at as a big deal in the KAWF. The titles must be defended at least every 30 days. Titles in which might come at a later date - Television Title, Womens Title, Cruserweight Title, and Submission Title. More information will be given on these at a later date, but for now focus on the one's up above for they our your prize. KAWF Events SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER - This is the big weekly event for the KAWF. There is usually 4 - 6 matches on the card, and most of the bigger names show up on this particular show. The announcers for this show our Legendary KAWF announcers Prison, Russell Cunningham, and Frankie Tha Pimp. Bigger arenas in bigger cities are also used for this show. SUNDAY NIGHT WAR STORIES - This is used as a recap show for the most part, but does have anywhere from 2 -4 matches on it live. This always takes place at the KAWF arena in New Orleans, Louisiana unless we our on an over seas tour. Story lines usually develop very far on War Stories, as there is more time to devote to them on this particular show. Announcers for this show our Russell Cunningham, and Frankie Tha Pimp. The new UPDATE is always sent out after War Stories. PAY PER VIEWS - These our done every couple months, or when the President see's fit. These our as big as big gets in the KAWF. The announcer's for these shows our White Rose, Frankie Tha Pimp, and Russell Cunningham. More detail will be given on these at a later date. Current Pay Per View's scheduled KAWF PRESENTS - The RETURN On June 13TH from Houston, Texas KAWF PRESENTS - DARKNESS VISIBLE On December 26TH from the KAWF arena in New Orleans, Louisiana More will be given at a later date. SUPERCARDS - These aren't pay per views, but maybe they should be. This should remind you of the Clash of champions and such events as that. These our very special events which can fall on any day when Mike Johnson wants them to. Big things occur when these things go down. HBO loves for these to happen, and encourage KAWF to pull out all the stops. Current Supercards scheduled KAWF PRESENTS - MIKE JOHNSON'S BIRTHDAY BASH ON AUGUST 27TH FROM MIAMI, FLORDIA. More will be given on a later date. KAWF PEOPLE Owner/ President : Mike "The Always Right" Johnson - This of course is the top dawg in the KAWF. He plays a face role, and has the final say on anything that happens in the KAWF. He does his best to keep things going smoothly in the KAWF, and trys to stay off camera. Vice President : Kristen Roberts - She is a beautiful red headed bombshell who is married to Mike's daughter, but doesn't really give a damn. She just wants the power which is the KAWF in her hands. She will do anything, and anybody [ ya you heard me ] to get what she wants. She plays a heel role, and loves to get on the air. Bodyguard to President : Outlaw - This man is also an advisor in the KAWF when it comes to board meeting situations, and what not. He plays a very special role in keeping the peace around the KAWF, and looking out for his be - loved president. The man is a wrestling legend in his own right and isn't afraid to duke it out with anybody on the roster at any given time. Commish : Shane Martin - The man who makes alot of the matches in KAWF, and watches closely over the do's and don't's of the federation so he can report to Mike Johnson. He prolly ranks third in command on the chain in the KAWF. Not a man to cross, or you will have to deal with his wrath, and sick sense of humor. He also plays a face role. Head Booker : Mike Pace - Mike used to be head announcer, but decided he would take a more behind the scenes approach in the KAWF over the last few years. He books most of the matches, and comes up with the locations for all the events. He is also in charge of public relations in the KAWF, and over seas recruiting/ scouting. He of course plays a face role. Creative Specialist : Shawn and Chuck Cross - These two brothers used to have there own show in the KAWF called crossfire, but now there the men who write the script. You might never see there name mentioned again, but we just thought you all should know who writes the storylines. Shawn plays a face role. Chuck plays a heel role. Together we assume they equal out to neutral. VP's errand boy : Jay Wheery - Jay used to be a commish in the KAWF, but was replaced over a year ago by Shane Martin. He has been re - duced to keeping an eye on Kristen Roberts. He was assigned this job by Mike Johnson. He trys to play the face role, but gets forced to do alot of heel duties while dealing with the devil herself. Head Announcer : Russell Cunningham - Russell puts all the formats together, and trys to do his best job everytime he is infront of a camera representing the KAWF. He is the head announcer in the KAWF, and can tell you something about everything. He plays a face role. Fever Announcer : Prison - Prison is a former wrestler turned announcer. He was the first ever KAWF world heavyweight champion, and loves KAWF with all of his heart. He is a neutral announcer which leans both ways depending on the subject. He can always be seen with his 40 in hand, and always mentions possibly returning to the ring, but then Frankie reminds him of his bad knee. Prison is also one of the few black males in the announcing business currently. Color Commentary - Frankie Tha Pimp - THE BEST DAMN ANNOUNCER IN THE BUSINESS. Least thats what he calls himself. This man has a heart, but its covered deep, deep down . . .He is as heel as heel as an announcer can get. He loves blood, women, and cheating. Pay per view Announcer : White Rose - This sexy face announcer only works pay per views, and hates Frankie with a passion. She tends to flirt with Russell on occasion, some think the bomb shell has a crush on the mild mannered Russell Cunningham. Back up Announcer : Chris "The spirit" Florence - This cocky young announcer was last seen getting power bombed threw a table, but kept his contract with the KAWF anyways. He is Frankie's pupil, and well . ... He's learned from the worst, so you can expect the worsr from this cat. Heel all the way. House Show Reporter : Vinny Pascarelli - This Italian lover gets sent out to watch all the KAWF house shows, and report them on air to the public. He highlights each match, and mentions the crowd actions as well. He's a heel, but in a nice way I suppose. Fever Interviewer : Fat Mike - He is short, overweight, and has a stuttering problem. He is an easy targer for the wrestlers, but the fans love him cause he has one of the biggest hearts you will ever see. He will stand up to anybody, anywhere and ask that oh so crucial question. Face War Stories Interviewer : Samuel Smith - Well educated, and hard nose black man from Compton. He is straight up, and prolly leans towards a heel role cause he acts so much like a hard ass.. Back Up Interviewer : Sarah Many - This beautiful brown haired girl with glasses is the back up interviewer in the KAWF. If Samuel or Fat Mike our doing other task or under the weather this beautiful face will grace you with her beauty. She also sings the National Anthem at the house shows when Jessica is not the ring announcer. Beautiful voice, and beautiful body indeed. Ring Announcer : Jessica Delgado - This lovely mexican delight is the head ring announcer in the KAWF, and also sings the National Anthem for the KAWF at EVERY LIVE SHOW. Back up ring Announcer : Juan Delgado - Jessica's little brother can be seen at the House Shows, and occasionaly live working for Jessica. He isn't nearly as pretty, but does a damn good job of announcing when he is called apon. Head of Security : Rocky Evans - This man is in charge of security. He trys to keep the number high enough to tackle anything which happens, and listens very closely to Mike Johnson, but also listens very closely to Kristen Roberts. He trys to make both of them happy cause damnit . .. he don't want to lose his job. He's got kids ya know. Head Referee : Atasi Hokouma - A very strict referee from Iwate, Japan who has been put in charge of the referee's. He will DQ you in a heartbeat, and go to the office members and ask for wrestlers to be fined if he thinks it is nessecary. Secondary Referee : Patrick Finley - This is the second highest referee in the chain of command, and the only one who can even remotely think about saying something to Atasi or trying to overturn another referee's descion. He is a good ol southern boy who trys to call thinks exactly by the book. Referee : Mike Riquiah - Longtime KAWF referee who isn't to quick to call DQ's, but does call things down the middle. Outside interferance will lead to DQ's, but as for count outs, and to many punches being thrown you can forget about it. He also has a habit of kinda leaning towards the face side in a match. Which is what prolly has kept him out of being promoted to a higher referee position or position in the federation. Referee : Wayne West - Crazy as crazy can get .. .This big ol boy is almost as wild as the wrestlers, and won't take no Shit. He likes to let the boys go at it, and mainly likes to call the streetlife matches. Referee : Zach Ward - This referee lets just about everything slide. He is also kinda corrupt. He can be bribed, and is the weakest link of the referee chain. Doctor : Jeremy Rivers - The guys call him lifeline, and thats cause he has saved many of there wrestling carrears. He's a good all around guy, and a ladies man at that. EMT : Christy Rogers - The very polite, and shy EMT which usually gets hit on backstage by the boys. EMT : Brad Rogers - Christy's husband who is also an EMT, and wishes she would get a new damn job. Both Christy and Brad work under Jeremy. THE KAWF DANCERS : Lucky, Princess, Savannah, Red, Cherry, and Raine - These six girls dance whenever the KAWF lets them, and boy do they keep the crowd excited.
