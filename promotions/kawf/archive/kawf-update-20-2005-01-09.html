SUCK ASS WRESTLING FEDERATION " The continuation of the federation in which gives you the best mix of extreme, old fashion, and entertaining wrestling to ever be seen in Ewrestling." **************************** Table of Contents **************************** 1 - Notes from the desk - Out of Character 2 - News - In Character 3 - Roster & Records 4 - Various Rankings, and Title Holders 5 - Line ups 6 - Final Notes ***************** 1 - Notes from the Office ****************** Howdy boys ? Hope you all had a merry Christmas, and a happy New Year as the saying goes. We got threw the holidays pretty good, alot better than I thought we would. The results were a bit timely considering the holidays, and the activity was very nice indeed. Impressive. Everything is going smoothly right now. Roster is looking good. 90 percent of the deadweight is gone, the angle build up is mounting, and the results have been pretty solid reads with a good mix of action, story telling, and on cards. The _ONE_ problem is match writers. I'm not getting a good response. Gentleman, Iv'e said it a many a times, but it is important. I need them. Were doing something most E - feds don't do, and doing it fairly well. Were trying to be a weekly fed like the good ol days, and to do that, which is more fun to me than one show a month, or even Bi weekly I need some guys to step up. So get back to me on that. Anyways on with the Update. Much Respect, Josh * Mr Jarrod Storm your in our prayers. I hope everything works out good for your family. ************************ 2 -News from the rumor bin ************************* * Riley Young, Kid Cicero, Two Fisted Tony, and Blunt Force Trauma have all been released from there SAWF contract. Each for there own various reason. * Riley Young was released by Kristen Roberts personally, cause of an argument before the show, about Riley having to work the upcoming house show. Kristen did change her mind about the house show, but also changed her mind about Riley. * Kid Cicero was also let go personally by Roberts, because of his backstage antics. Showing up drunk _to the shows_ will not be tolerated, espically by the "newbies". * Tony was released after a very lack luster performance against Lark Fenriz, and a temper tantrum backstage leading to him saying the words "I quit" which can't be took back in the SAWF. * Blunt Force Trauma was released cause well, there all three in jail. When BFT gets out of jail the SAWF is willing to discuss things with them, but it does not look good for the company to have one of there tag teams in the slammer. * Legal issues for the name KAWF returning to the company started last week, and should continue on well into the 2005 year. * The SAWF recently signed two new tag teams, and have two more on in the soon to be horizon, really trying to heat up the division. * Mike Johnson is doing good, resting at his home in New Orleans, trying to do battle with cancer. Godspeed to our boss back at home in Louisiana. * Matches will start being booked shortly for the February 20th Pay per view from New York. * Leonard Holtz is currently in the lead for the Global Championship shot. Followed by LezKanic, Derek Douglas, and then Zodiak. Most our saying the small man from Japan has a slim shot, if any on catching up to the other guys after last weekends performance. * Mark Steiner is apparently in the SAWF to a cure a virus which of all things is _INSIDE_ the Con$ortium. More on this topic, as we get it. *The SAWF is very pleased with there new crop of wrestlers. So pleased in fact merchandise is already in the works for ninety percent of them. * Hayden Grayson is still leading the merchandise area of things, but is now being closely followed by Derek Douglas, Sabbath, and Johnny Sledge. The top heel is Lezkanic by a smidget over VVV, and Perfect Storm. * A former world champion will be joining the ranks of the SAWF very soon. * Currently the SAWF is looking for some hardcore wrestlers, and cruserweights to help round out there respective divisions. * Vile Vince Viper did not meet up with Sabbath after last Fever, but did report in a passing manner " He wasn't happy" with his old friend. * Johnny Sledge has to keep the cast on his arm for another 6 - 8 weeks so says Doctors orders. * The SAWF is currently trying to contact November, Brody Thunder, Frankie Cash, Hannibal Carver, and Rick Marley about working some future matches. * "Mr Main Event" Chris Douglas was seen backstage talking to cruserweight Champion Sammy Griffin at War Stories. * Ratings for both War Stories, and Fever were very good. HBO has said the SAWF is "Right on par, with where they need to be currently." * Lucifer Magnusson & Jimmy Haynes continued there brawl backstage in Savannah, eventually being broke up by police, and enough security guards to form a small army. * Mark Palmer, Karl Ferguson, and Mike Harris will be returning to SAWF action very soon. All three our suffering minor injuries, and have been informed to take there time getting healthy again. * The Short Bus Gang will be debuting very soon in an SAWF ring. Many our questionable this teams antics, and ring talents. * The True All Stars have "offically" broke up for the time being. The SAWF received the paperwork early this morning. * The SAWF has scheduled an awards banquet for January 21st to take place back at the base of operations for the SAWF New Orleans, Louisiana. * Some of the possible rumors for the main event match at Crazed Intentions include a steel cage match, a double ladder match, a Timebomb match, and a stairway to hell match among others. * Kristen Roberts, and Mike Johnson's son our currently having martial issues. Most say it revolves around his sick dad. *The SAWF is currently being sued by two unknown wrestling companys cause of the "roadkill" tour being done by Lezkanic. * Outlaw, and Shane Martin have not spoke in weeks cause of Shane turning his back on the company. Things our expected to end very ugly amongst these two men when all is said, and done. * Lark Fenriz has not yet signed the contract laid before him by Werewolf Gregorson on Fever. * The prints for the Christmas Day photo shoot amongst the Global Title holders will be for sell very soon. Get your very own copy of all four men, doing something there not used to doing to say the least. ************************** 3 - Current KAWF Roster & Alignments ************************** 1 - "Bulletproof" Jimmy Haynes -
