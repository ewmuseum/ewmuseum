CAL - the split from the NWC

October 15, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings! My name is [name withheld] and I am a long-time e-wrestler. My entire career, however, was within the confines of the National Wrestling
Council (which, by my understanding, isn't very well liked in the RSPWF world). Anyway, I just thought I'd post a little thing here about the split within the NWC.
In around January of this year, the NWC started an 'affiliate league' program that was called the NWCal. This Affiliate program was to prepare leagues for NWC entrance and what-not. It, in total, promoted three leagues from January to August. In August, though, I took over the NWCal because it had one active federation left and was days away from death.
In a month I turned it around to have six active federations and we got to the point where we were no longer really considered a
'training' fed to the NWC. Thus, the two groups (NWC and NWCal) decided to split off and become two different groups of federations. So, as of Thursday this past week, the CAL (Coalition of Affiliated Leagues) was created to serve as competition for the NWC. Now, for those of you that don't know what the NWC is and how it is structured (since the CAL uses the same structure), allow me to explain. It is a coalition of regional federations (web-based roleplaying federations, open flash system) that come together and recognize one group of World Champions between them (think, old-school
NWA). Each territory within the coalition is run as its own entity and interacts with the others in either a friendly/competitive/etc.
manner. In short, it brings six leagues together into one large community and it has a great atmosphere.
For anyone who would like more information, please visit our web site at http://thecal.hypermart.net/ or even just stop off at our OOC board, The Collective: http://members4.boardhost.com/thecal/ . Feel free to respond in any capacity as well.
