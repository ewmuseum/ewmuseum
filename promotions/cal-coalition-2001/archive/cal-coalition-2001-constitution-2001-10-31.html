CAL - Our Constitution

October 31, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After breaking away from the NWC, one of the first things the Coalition of Affiliated Leagues wanted to do was write a Constitution to keep the CAL from becoming the void of ego and politicking that the
NWC has become... thus, here is our Constitution. Feel free to comment upon it, we'd like as much feedback as possible! -[name withheld] [detail withheld] AOL IM Screen Name: [detail withheld] Creator of Toonopolis - http://www.toonopolis.tv/ Brooder of Chaotica - http://chaotica.hypermart.net/ CEO of the CAL - http://thecal.hypermart.net/ ----------------------------------------- "We the people of the Coalition of Affiliated Leagues, in an order to form a fun union, prevent injustice, insure peace, and do all that other stuff, do ordain and establish this Constitution of the
Coalition of Affiliated Leagues." Article. I. (Executive Board) Section. 1. (Powers) All powers regarding decisions of World Title match winners, new Coalition of Affiliated League (herein referred to as the CAL) territories, and amendments to this Constitution are placed in the hands of the Executive Board (EB).
Section. 2. (Executive Board Positions) The Executive Board shall consist of no greater than nine persons (persons herein can be synonymous with 'votes', thus 'co-president' positions being counted as one person) at one time, limited to the positions of Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Director of Publicity
(DoP), Webmaster, and one vote per currently affiliated territory. Current EB members will be listed on the About the CAL page on the website. Section. 2.a. (Powers and limitations of the CEO) The CEO is granted one vote in all decisions equal to the vote of every other EB member. At no time is the CEO allowed to make a decision regarding CAL-wide affairs without calling a vote amongst the entire EB.
The CEO is in charge of organizing inter-territorial events. The CEO is also called to ask for and tabulate all votes of the EB. Section. 2.b. (Powers and limitations of the DoP) The DoP is granted one vote in all decisions equal to the vote of every other EB member. The DoP is in charge of promoting for the good of the entire CAL and not for any specific territory unless he/she feels that it will benefit the CAL as a whole.
The DoP is in charge of the CAL Loose Affiliate program, whereby he/she seeks out potential future CAL member territories or independent leagues that would like to correlate with the CAL as a whole.
Section. 2.c. (Powers and limitations of the Webmaster) The CAL Webmaster is granted one vote in all decisions equal to the vote of every other EB member.
The Webmaster is in charge of keeping the CAL website in all facets updated and free from navigational errors.
The Webmaster may have as many assistants as he/she deems necessary for any website features they feel would assist the CAL but all updates and voting privileges are ultimately the responsibility of the
Webmaster him/herself. The Webmaster is not limited to which and how many features are on the website but the minimal site features are: the Constitution, About the
CAL, an Application, Title Histories, Event Calendar, Promo-Net, Collective, and Links. A list of all current territories and current CAL champions must appear (at least) on the main page. Section. 2.d. (Powers and limitations of the territorial presidents) The territorial management is granted one vote in all decisions equal to the vote of every other EB member. In the case of co-ownership/co-presidency, only one vote is allowed per territory.
How the vote is used is determined by the territory. The territorial presidents are free to run their federations as they see fit, so long as they do not interfere with CAL-wide prestige or damage the image of the CAL.
Section. 3. (Limitations of EB status) Any one person may not occupy more than two (2) EB positions simultaneously unless no suitable replacement/alternative can be found. In the event that one person needs to occupy more than two positions, they are only interim and must try to find a replacement to limit themselves back to two positions.
The other EB members are also charged to suggest replacements and seek them out. New EB members (in the instance of CEO, DoP, and Webmaster) must be approved unanimously by the remainder of the EB. Territorial presidency is a volunteer position and as such is to be filled based on each territory's wants and needs.
Any person holding two (or more) positions within the EB is only granted one (1) vote in EB decisions.
Article. II. (CAL Territorial Membership) Section. 1. (Joining) All new territorial applications must be presented to the DoP. If the DoP feels the territory is worthy of joining the CAL, he/she will call a vote amongst the entire EB.
New territories must be approved in a two-thirds majority by the EB to be granted membership.
Upon being granted membership, the territory will be given a spot on the rotation of the CAL World Title defense schedule as well as a vote on the EB.
Section. 2. (Limitations) There must never be more than six (6) territories in the CAL at one time. Any potential territories must be treated as a loose affiliate and they are allowed CAL event appearances only by CEO approval and are not to be in the CAL Title rotations.
Any territory joining the CAL must be territorially based (which is flexible) as well as roleplay-board based (no e-mail based federations need apply).
No CAL territory is allowed to have a sanctioned 'World' or otherwise all-encompassing title. The 'World' title stigmas are to be limited strictly to CAL Titles.
Section. 3. (Expulsion) Any territory will be expelled from CAL membership should they have more than two weeks of complete inactivity without any statistical updates.
Any territory may be subject from CAL expulsion if they participate in activities that degrade the CAL as a whole or taint the overall image of the CAL. This issue must be voted on by all EB members (not including the territorial president of the offending territory).
Expelled leagues may re-apply for CAL membership, but they can only be accepted via unanimous EB vote.
Article. III. (CAL Championships) Section. 1. (Definition) The CAL currently sanctions two World Championships, the CAL World Title (eligible to any territorial representative) and the CAL World Tag Team Titles (eligible to any territorial representatives). Challengers to CAL Titles must be publicly announced at least one full defense in advance. Section. 2. (Schedule of Defense) The CAL World Titles are to be defended regularly in a schedule set on the website in the Title Histories section. Any new territory joining is to be placed immediately at the end of the rotation. If a territory re-locates or is 'bought out' by an outsider and re-located, they are to keep their spot in the rotation.
The schedule is to be a date-based rotation, with each territory's shots being planned within a certain ten-day rotation. The CEO is in charge of keeping this up to date and sending updates to the Webmaster for posting.
If a territory does not have a show within that ten-day period due to their own scheduling conflicts, their shot is skipped and not 'pushed'
back. Shots may be switched if two territories agree on a switch, but the CEO and Webmaster must be alerted in advance to this change.
If a show is scheduled for release in between the ten-day period, the results of the match will be released, without the show itself if need be, on the last day of the ten-day period in order to keep the schedule moving.
Section. 3. (Voting) CAL World Title matches are to be voted on by five (5) CAL EB members. These five must be made up of, in order of required voting, by territorial presidents, DoP, Webmaster, CEO, alternate voters.
The vote must be called and tabulated by the CEO, regardless of whether or not the CEO him/herself has voted.
The CEO also selects, based on the above order of required voting, who will vote in each match.
Winners are decided on simple majority basis. If there is a draw in the voting, the champion retains.
Section. 3.a. (Voting Limitations) No territorial president may vote in a CAL Title Match where a representative of his own territory is a participant.
No EB member may vote in a CAL Title Match where their own character is a participant. No EB member may vote in a CAL Title Match where there is an obvious pre-determined bias. This clause is at CEO discretion and must be used liberally.
No one can be granted more than one vote on each match, regardless of the number of EB positions held.
Section. 3.b. (Alternative Voters) In the event that five EB members are not eligible for voting based on any number of circumstances, the CEO can call upon alternative voters.
These voters do not have to be fixed nor are they required to be a member of the CAL, although it is preferred that they are. All non-voting rules regarding their own characters and pre-determined bias apply.
Section. 4. (New CAL Titles) New CAL titles may be sanctioned, but only by a unanimous decision by all current EB members. No more than one (1) extra title may be sanctioned at any given time. There may be no more than four (4) CAL
Titles at any given time. The de-sanctioning of titles must also be done via unanimous voting.
Under no circumstances, however, are 'specialty' titles such as Cruiserweight, Womens, Hardcore, etc. are ever to be sanctioned by the CAL as a whole. These titles are restricted to territorial presidents within their own leagues.
Article. IV. (Pay Per Views) Section. 1. (Number and Dates) The CAL will have four inter-fed pay per views per year. They will take place within the final ten days of September, December, March, and June.
The CEO is in charge of organizing these events. If he/she feels the need, however, to place someone else in charge of one event he/she has the privilege. Ultimately, though, the CEO is responsible for CAL pay per view events.
Section. 2. (September PPV) The September pay per view will be a ten-match, one-night showcase event with no set name. The focus will not be on inter-fed matches but on territorial 'showcase' matches, whereby each territory is allotted a certain number of matches based on necessity. The main event will be the CAL World Title match.
Section. 3. (December PPV) The December pay per view will be a one-night inter-fed event entitled Reindeer Games (a suitable sub-title will be added each year). The showcase event will be a one-night tournament between all current heavyweight (or respective top tier) champions from each territory and the CAL World Champion. The main event will be the finals of the tournament and the title can change hands if the CAL Champion loses earlier in the tournament.
Section. 4. (March PPV) The March pay per view will be a one-night inter-fed event with no set name. The specialty event will be a fifteen-wrestler battle royal
(entitled Chance to Shine) between lower/middle card wrestlers with the prize being a World Title shot to be granted at a later date. The main event will be the CAL World Tag Team Title match.
Section. 5. (June PPV) The June pay per view will be a two-night inter-fed event entitled Coronation (a suitable sub-title will be added each year). The showcase event of the first night will be a one-night tournament between all current tag team champions from each territory and the CAL
World Tag Team Champions. The main event will be the finals of the tournament and the title can change hands if the CAL Champions lose earlier in the tournament. The specialty event of the second night will be a 'team-based' event that will fluctuate with each year. The main event will be the CAL World Title match.
Section. 6. (CAL PPV Title Shots) Challengers for the CAL World Titles on pay per views will be decided upon by a vote amongst the EB unless an already promised shot via tournament or special event has been determined.
Article. V. (Out Of Character Board) Section. 1. (Definition) Herein, the CAL out of character board shall be known as The Collective. All CAL and non-CAL members are welcome to post freely within the confines of the below stated limitations.
The only three people allowed access to the administration of the Collective will be the CEO, the DoP, and the Webmaster. Territorial presidents, unless also occupying one of the previous three positions, are not to have administrative access.
Section. 2. (Posting Limitations) The following content will be deleted from the Collective: racism, excessive hatred, harassment, adult content, nudity or pornography, any illegal content, anything that defames or taints the image of the
CAL (constructive criticism does not fall into this category if handled properly), anything considered not 'out of character', and any material deemed otherwise overly offensive by the administrators of the Collective.
Repeat offenders of these limitations will be banned from posting and asked by the administrators to be dealt with by their territorial president if they are a member of the CAL.
Article. VI. (Amendment Process) Section. 1. (Propositions) Any and all proposed amendments from any member of the CAL must be passed to the EB through their territorial president. Only territorial presidents may present new amendment for approval.
Section. 2. (Approval) All Constitutional amendments must be approved by a two-thirds majority of the EB before they can take effect. Any new amendment will take effect immediately, however, will not be retroactive so any differences set in place before the amendment (and this Constitution in its entirety) need not be changed.
