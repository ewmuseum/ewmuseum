WWO bio card — The Firm

The Firm Real Name: Hoover Platt Height: 6'8 Weight: 280 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Washington D.C Entrance Music: "Da Funk" by Daft Punk Manager: Big S-Ron &nsbp Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Psychological Makeup Strengths Power-- Firm's a strong guy, and his big-man offense is among the best looking in the game. Mentally speaking, Firm's damn near a genius, and that power enables him to survive the hurly-burly of sports entertainment with his ego intact. Firm doesn't go quite as far over the edge as his partner, which enables them to get out of tough situations caused by Rax overextending himself. When momentum is on UCD's side, Firm is able to really pour it on and destroy their opponents, either verbally or physically. Firm will go way over the top to prove a point. Weaknesses Firm's much less emotional than Rax, which can be a problem in big matches. Firm will respond to pain by generally becoming even less emotionally involved in matches. When he's really hurting, he struggles to keep intensity and focus. Wrestling means less to him than it does Rax--he's got a profession. Biographical Information Firm's history is nowhere near as sordid as Rax's--he's went to Gannon University in Erie as an undergrad, did his J.D at Duke, and worked as a public defender until meeting Rax Justice. Firm's always been the most principled member of UCD--not that that means much, it's like being the poorest billionaire or the smartest Canadian--but he's willing to support Rax's wild schemes and plays a bigger part in formulating them than he's given credit for (see: "Fun Loving Criminals"). Firm's generally more interested in accolades than Rax is, and that causes occasional friction between the two men.
