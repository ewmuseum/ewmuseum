WWO bio card — Brick

28 July 2003

Brick Wrestler Name: Beau Jackson Date of Birth: August 23, 1977 Height: 5'8 Weight: 386 pounds Alignment: Taoist Hometown: Jersey City, New Jersey Entrance Music: "The Meaning of Life" by Offspring Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Brick is a big, big man. He's a 'solid' for his weight, meaning that not all rolls of fat. Basically, his frames more like that of the Big Show rather than Rikishi. Brick has a weird little beard that runs under his chin as well as a small mohawk. Ring Appearance In the ring, Brick wears a pair of blue tights with black stripes that run down the sides. He also has black wrestling boots, elbow pads, and fingerless gloves. He also has a single shoulder shirt. Psychological Makeup Strengths Power - Brick is a strong man for his size. Last time they checked, he could bench press TWICE his weight, and with how big he is, that's a lot. Sumo - Brick spent about a year in Japan, and when he wasn't ogling the high school girls wearing uniforms, he spent his time wisely, learning the ancient art of Sumo Wrestling. Weaknesses Speed - Brick is maybe THREE TIMES too big, and with that much extra fat loading him down, he isn't going very quickly. Food - Brick is a man obsessed with food. He sees even just a little crumb and that's were his attention is. Of course, that adds to his size and his slow moving in the ring. Biographical Information Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Poor High-Flier Poor Martial Arts Fair Power Very Good Technical Poor Submission Very Good Cheating None Brawling/Hardcore Good Escapability Poor Stamina Fair Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Excellent Top Wrestling Moves The Brick Blaster - A Flapjack lift into a Powerbomb-like Takedown. The Shattered Glass - A huge barrage of swift and powerful palm strikes ended with a huge Palm Strike to the temple. "Bing~!" - The opponent runs at Brick and tries to hit him with some running move, but instead of it working, an appendage of the opponent gets stuck in Brick's stomach. Belly-to-Belly Overhead Suplex Throat Toss (Out of the corner.) Bearhug Back-to-Back Full Nelson - Get back-to-back with the opponent, lock arms, and lift the up on Brick's back. Neck Wrench DDT - DDT the opponent, and hold on with a Head Lock submission. Corner Body Splash Exploder Suplex Finishing Maneuver The Raise the Roof Bomb A modified Military Press. Basically, he whips the opponent into the rope, and as they run back, Brick lifts them into the air with a Military Press. He then begins to raise and lower them a few times (like the "raising the roof" motion) before slamming them down like in a Michinoku Driver.) Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Nex Tag Team Partner: Pax Tag Team Moves The NeXtreme 2000 (The NeX's original finisher. Brick lifts the opponent in a Power Bomb as Pak flies off the top rope with a Bull Dog Lariat.) Savate Kick (Brick)/Super Kick (Pak) combo. Power Bomb (Brick)/Reverse Mat Slam (Pak) combo. Brick lifts the opponent in a Double Arm Choke Lift as Pak spikes them down with a Spear from the top rope. Raise the Roof & Bring It Down (A combination of Brick and Pak's finishers.) Tag Team Finisher The NeXtreme 2002 Brick lifts the opponent up in a Bear Hug while Pak climbs up to the second rope. Pak leaps off spiking the opponent with an Inverted DDT while Brick slams the opponent down with a Forward Spine Buster [A.k.a., The Rock's Spine Buster.]
