WWO bio card — Ultimate Thrasher

31 March 2002

Ultimate Thrasher Real Name: Steve Hanneman Birthdate: 11/19/75 Height: 6'5 Weight: 280 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Duluth, MN Entrance Music: Manager: Sweet Melissa - Managerial Profile PHYSICAL APPEARANCE Thrasher, essentially, looks like a cross between an NFL offensive lineman and a heavy metal musician....he is tall and muscular, but is possessed of good looks which turn females' heads. He has shoulder-length blond hair, icy blue eyes and fair skin, fairly typical for someone of Scandinavian descent. On his right bicep is a large tattoo of a guitar, with a skull-and-crossbones tatooed underneath, while his left bicep simply has the letters "PACKERS SUCK" tattooed in purple. Typically, he wears a black t-shirt to the ring, with either the logo of one of his favorite heavy metal bands or something mocking his opponent or a WWO inside joke of some sort, along with black trunks, black elbow pads, and black knee pads, along with brown Skechers boots and a Gibson Les Paul guitar slung over his shoulder, which usually gets used on his opponents. Psychological Makeup Ultimate Thrasher may vary between being in the good and bad graces of the fans, but he really has loyalty to only one person, that being his girlfriend/manager Sweet Melissa. He has been involved with 7 stables thus far in his WWO career, and it has been he who has broken up six out of those seven stables with callous disregard for the other wrestlers involved. Thrasher typically views alliances with other wrestlers as simple marriages of convinence for furthering his own goals, and while he won't always go out of his way just to stab the other people involved in the back, he laughs at the concept some people have of loyalty. He is, for the most part, a loner, and sneers at those who need to surround themselves with lackeys in order to do their dirty work. Not that Thrasher doesn't believe in dirty tricks, but he'd rather pull them off himself, or with Melissa's help. Thrasher certainly enjoys beating people up, but at other times he'd rather just pull practical jokes for the sake of amusement-it all depends on the target. Strengths Experience: Although still a young man, Thrasher has been around the WWO for a longer span of time than almost anyone on the current roster and has been in almost every type of situation imaginable in the federation. If anyone in the federation knows the ins and outs of the federation and how to properly manipulate its rules, it's him. Patience: Thrasher almost always knows exactly what he wants (and if he doesn't, Melissa decides for him), but knows that his position in the WWO is as secure as anyone's and that there's no need to really do anything rash. Thrasher is a man who makes his move when the time is exactly right, never exactly before....he has been known to remain allied with wrestlers for months after becoming displeased with them, while plotting all the while to turn against them. Melissa: More than mere eye candy, Sweet Melissa is both brainy and willing to get involved in Thrasher's matches if the need arises. She never hesitates to set her man on the right course to success and to question his actions if she doesn't like them. It is doubtful Thrasher would be the superstar he is today if not for Melissa's presence. Weaknesses Stubbornness: When Thrasher gets an idea into his head to do something, it is hard for Melissa and nearly impossible for anyone else to convince him to switch courses.....even if it means walking into an ambush. Untrustworthiness: Thrasher has turned on so many people over the years that one would have to be totally naive to trust him completely. His history states that, even if you're a stablemate of his, never totally turn your back. Melissa: Thrasher becomes angered easily if somebody insults or threatens Melissa, and flies into a rage if any wrestler actually strikes her. Targetting Melissa can throw Thrasher off of his game. Biographical Information Ultimate Thrasher has frequently referred to himself as "the only living legend of the WWO". While that may be a bit of an exaggeration, the fact of the matter is that this Minnesotan heavy metal guitarist-turned-wrestler is one of the very brightest stars in the long, proud history of the federation. Thrasher, always accompanied by his trademark Gibson Les Paul guitar and his beautiful, brainy manager/girlfriend Sweet Melissa, is a straight-ahead, hard-working power wrestler who came into the WWO as a complete greenhorn in June 1997 and began a slow, painstaking climb to the top of the federation which included membership in some of the most famous and infamous stables in WWO history including the Hall of Fame and the Society Of Notoriety, heartbreaking defeats and glorious triumphs, intense feuds with Tyrant, Johnny Detson, Roundhouse and Randy Bryson, shocking turns against assumed allies Andros and Roundhouse, and finally a World title. He consistently refused to allow his lack of wrestling skill or great size to keep him from carving out quite a niche for himself in the WWO's first run, and nothing suggests this will change in the reborn WWO. What is unknown, however, is which path to success he will take....will he abide by the rules as he did in his first two years in the WWO, or will he be the conniving, cold-blooded schemer he eventually became? No one knows yet....but either way there's one gurantee: it will be interesting.
