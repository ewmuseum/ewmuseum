[detail withheld] !!! *#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*#*# The camera opens on a dark purple screen with red swirls, slowly taking form, behind the swirls, photographs of various wrestlers come across the screen in from all different directions, including Vader X, Ric Studd, Dark Ninja, Chris Stonebreaker, Derrick Michaels, King $$ Perfect... The swirls begin to form one letter at a time... (Insrt. WWO asterick Image) SATURDAY NIGHT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Suddenly the screen folds out from the center, showing your hosts of the show, Mickey Ralph and Michael Shoestrom. [[MR]] (screaming to be heard over a dark but noisy auditorium): Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to WWO Saturday Night, LIVE from Baton Rouge Louisiana!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Where the food is spicy, and the wrestling even spicier! [[MS]] And the women... Woooooooo!!!!! [[MR]] I'm Mickey Ralph and standing with me is Michael Shoestrom here bringing you all the action tonight from teh Baton Rouge Civic Auditorium! (Crowd pops on cue chanting WWO! WWO! WWO!) [[MR]] The question of the night, will Hell's Angel and Death make it back to peaceful terms any time soon, or has one of the most perfect groups in the WWO had a permament split. We have some comments from Hell's Angel this evening, and we're hoping to get a response from Death and or his minions. [[MS]] And then Total Package will lose any future they have in this federation when the two smartest men in wrestling, the Business Men take them to the bank tonight! [[MR]] I'm not sure about that one Michael, as the total Package knows what's on the line for them. If they lose, they get no more shots at the tag titles, and that's just as well saying you don't belong in this federation. [[MS]] And Total Package doesn't.... [[MR]] And Joseph Culhicky is down at ringside ready to give us the introductions for our first match. Take it away Joe! [[JC]] (Standing in the middle of the ring, with the house microphone in his hand) Ladies and Gentlmen, welcome to Baton Rouge, Louisiana and the WWO Saturday Night Extravaganzaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!!!!!!!! ((Crowd pops as some fireworks go off in the air)) [[ starts playing over the loudspeakers, and the lights go onto the ramp, as Blade starts to make his way down to Ringside. A pretty good crowd pop goes out for Blade, with a number of kids trying to reach out and hi-5 him, but Blade gives them no notice at the moment]] [[JC]] Ladies and gentlemen, now making his way down to ringside, standing 5 ft, 10 inches, weighing in at 228 lbs, here he is, BLLLLLLAAAAAAAAADDDDDDDDDDDEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!! Blade climbs up on to the ring apron, and steps in between the top two ropes, he looks around the audience, with some of them booing him, and a number of them giving him a fairly good pop. The music then stops and changes to the music of Ric Studd's unusual beat. Blade looks at the announcer and he can be heard saying something about "Bring them both on, it doesn't matter" [[JC]] (backing away from Blade), and ladies and gentlemen, his opponent for this match, THHHHORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The crowd has a slightly less enthusiastic reaction for Thor, but not by much., who makes his way down to the ring never taking his eyes off Blade. [[MS]] The bodyguard is going to need a body bag tonight! [[JC]] This match is for one fall or 20 minute time limit. JC climbs out of the ring, and the two men face off on each other, chest to chest. Blade is heard saying something about beating the crap out of Thor's boss, and he's going to do the same to Thor [[MR]] Whatever Blade is mouthing off to Thor, it doesn't look like it's phasing the former body guard of Ric Studd. [[MS]] Thor probably can't even understand the English langauge that Blade is using, so it won't phase the dolt. Thor pushes Blade all the way to the mat, who rolls up and looks up at Thor. [[MR]] He understood it well enough to cause that. [[MS]] Blade was probably being nice... Blade charges at Thor and the two go into an immediate lockup, when Blade hits with an elbow to the midsection. [[MR]] You were saying something about nice Mike? [[MS]] Yeah, nice and painful! Blade catches Thor with a kneelift, who reels back to the turnbuckle, and Blade slowly follows him in, apparently to slowly, because Thor catches the attacker with a big foot to the chin. [[MS]] Time for the plastic surgeon!! A chin-endectomy... Thor comes charging out and catches Blade with a clothesline that sends the man for a 360 degree flip, and then follows it up with an elbowdrop. [[MR]] Thor's showing Studd that his position as a bodyguard wasn't simply an honorific one. [[MS]] Studd and honor in teh same sentence? How far off of reality are you Ralph? The bodyguard picks blade up and hits a shoulderbreaker over his knee, and dumps Blade to the canvas. He then walks over to the cameraman at ringside [[Thor]] This is how we protect the real men in this world. Thor walks over and again picks Blade back up, and power slams him. [[MS]] Who is he calling the real men? He's not up here protecting me from having an inept partner, so what kind of job can he be doing? Thor bounces off the ropes, and tries to come down with another elbowdrop but nobody's there as Blade rolled out of the way, and almost all the way out of the ring. [[MR]] Looks like Thor shouldn't worry about protecting anyone but what advantage he has against Blade. [[MS]] Now you see why I don't have him as a bodyguard. Too stupid to do the job he's paid for. Of course, Studd was stupid to hire him in the first place. Blade uses the rope to climb to his feet, and catches Thor with a punch to the midsection as he comes over to try to resume an attack. Blade whips Thor into the turnbuckle and charges immediately, catching Thor with a splash in teh corner. [[MR]] Blade is trying to make a statement tonight. The question is, is he trying to make the statement to Studd or someone else? [[MS]] Well whatever the statement is, who'd want to listen to him anyway? Blade starts to whip thor to the other corner, but then sends thor back again into the same turnbuckle, adn then catches the man coming out with a forearm. [[MR]] And the bodyguard is now off his feet and on the canvas! Blade climbs up on the first rope, and dives off, but Thor lifts his knees to catch Blade in a very uncompromising situation. [[MS]] Soprano!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Blade falls through the rope and outside of the ring to the floor, Thor is slowly getting to his feet. Thor grabs Blade by the top of the head and starts to pull him in, but Blade rams his shoulder into Thor's midsection, allowing Blade to climb in the ring untouched. Thor recharges Blade, but despite being apparently woozy from the fall outside the ring, Blade catches Thor in a drop toe takedown, and applies pressure to the left leg of Thor. [[MR]] Blade has Thor too close to the ropes, and the ref is already signalling for a break. [[MS]] And Blade is dumb enough to do it, without even waiting for the start of a count. And I though Thor was the only idiot in the ring. Blade turns Thor over, who is still holding on to the bottom ring rope, and "catapaults" him off the rope. [[MS]] Okay, maybe Blade is a little smarter than i gave him credit for [[MR]] Everyone is smarter than you give them credit for Mike, except maybe you. Blade goes for the cover, but Thor kicks out at 2. Blade get Thor to his feet, and whips him to the rope, and attempts to shoudler block the big man, but neither of them budge. Blade bounces off the rope again, Thor expecting another shoulder tackle attempt braces for the impact, but Blade slides underneath Thor and catches him in a reverse neckbreaker and goes for the cover and gets the three count. [[MS]] Told you Thor is just to dumb of an ox to know what to do in the wrestling circle. [[JC]] And the winner of your match in 9:15, BLAAAAAADDDDDDDDEEEEEEE!!! [[MR]] Would you like me to have someone tell Thor that you said that? [[MS]] Sure when you wake him up from his little nap there in the ring. And you can Studd I say he's dumb for ever hiring the imbecile. MR just looks away: We'll be back right after these messages..
