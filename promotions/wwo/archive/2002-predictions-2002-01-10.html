And in Conclusion...

by James Knight

As I said previously, I'll now preview the 2002 year with a set of 20 predictions. So without further delay (is that an overused statement), here they are.

1. [name withheld] will return and not be himself. Instead, he'll be James Schwartz, Accountant from Phoenix, who can't understand why we think he's [name withheld], owner of the WWO. He then will sort the books, have the WWO running at an all time high in the summer until Stone regains his identity, at which point the WWO will plummet into IRS hell.

2. Chris Stringer will lose the IP Title only to win...the Tag Titles? Yes, expect Stringer to team with Glen Peeps to prove he has more talent than anyone in the tag team division. After winning the titles in typical Stringer fashion, he'll learn that Peeps is more over than him, attack Peeps, and then the ultra climatic Peeps/Stringer feud we've _ALL_ been waiting for will commence.

3. Tyrone Hayes will also lose the World Title. The twist is that he'll lose it and turn face. He'll become a reformed Christian and join R.A.P.E. to fight the evils of mirth and all that jazz.

4. Chris Hopper vs. Yours Truly will headline next year's Anarchy. I'm already training, you jackass.

5. Sam Bradley will become a spokesman for GLAD and make the front cover of the Gay Advocate. Mickey Ralph will be shocked and demand his resignation.

6. Midnight Assassin will return to the WWO a reformed alcoholic and druggy. He'll become coherent. And nobody will care. But then, he brings Candy Malone back into the WWO. And people will care. Just not a lot.

7. William Craven and Melissa will have a stormy breakup after Craven in a drunken stupor sleeps with Cherry Bomb. This will end the Stringer/Craven relationship, but wait, there's more. Cherry Bomb's pregnant! Next time, on One Life to Fuck Up.

8. "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian will begin an online petition to be brought back into the WWO. When Stone catches wind of this, he'll have Sebastian lynched, marking the first non racist thing Stone has ever done.

9. James Knight will win the Pulitzer for journalists and be labeled the Walter Cronkite of this generation. But, he won't grow a big head. Even after purchasing Playboy and all its riches, he'll remain the consummate humanitarian and will star in his own one-man show. James Knight: His Own One-Man Show. Look for it to hit Broadway by the end of the year.

10. After being passed up for the full-length feature of James Knight: The Man Behind the Man That Was James Knight and Yes, That's a Metaphor, Johnny Detson will become so distraught that he'll accept his small part on Friends. He'll play Matt Le Blanc's lost twin brother. It's uncanny how they type cast these days.

11. Nate Knowitall will feud with Sean Collins over rights to a particular vocabulary.

12. Miguel Quesada will turn on Silkk. The move will make Silkk a house- hold name for the first time in the WWO and he will win the Title of America's by September. Quesada will labor in heel hell until finally turning face to battle Uncle Tom.

13. P.A.I.N. will get their own talk show on TLC. They'll discuss medical procedures and how to bend the rules. Look for it to be the lead in show to Junkyard Wars.

14. Another tag team, Vegas Connection, will dissolve in 2002. Not because of a split, but because Lester Leary will finally get his dream job of the in-house entertainment at the Ramada Inn outside of Paris, Texas.

15. Papa Legba's 2002 will be very special. After feuding with Alex Extreme, he'll finally be noticed for something other than being a weird guy. He'll be the weird guy who is sorta face.

16. Ultimate Thrasher will return to the WWO following a trip to Betty Ford. He'll fall off the wagon shortly thereafter after the Packers deal Brett Farve for draft picks to the Texans.

17. Lion Tamer will market the PLUNGER OF DOOM~!, a great Christmas gift, and become one of the hottest selling merchandising members of the WWO. So what if no one takes him seriously, he'll be raking in the dough. The WWO is already setting up their own version of Bat Night, Plunger Night, for some future Showcase. WWO wrestlers, especially members of the Upper Crust, are showing their displeasure for such an event because it gives the fans too much to throw.

18. Donny McClamrock will try to take over the Source. He'll fail because come on...what kind of name is Donny for an editor?

19. Apex will test positive for drugs. Surprised? Why should you be? Have you witnessed what this guy does?

20. Yet another list of predictions.

So, there you have it. Look forward to each event, especially the ones with me involvement. Next week, I'll discuss how ugly Chris Hopper is. Until next time, have a happy and safe week.
