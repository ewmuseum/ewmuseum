Anywho, see ya in a few. ======================================== An Interview with Tigress By: Kevin Maxx comments: [detail withheld] ============================================= [NOTE: As a columnist, I've covered professional wrestling for some time now and have conducted interviews in all sorts of interesting places. But when I recently got to talk with WWO newcomer, Tigress, I knew things were going to be a little different. The setting seemed ordinary enough, the living room of her spacious home where Tigress sat on a black leather couch next to a big bay window overlooking her front lawn. It was when the real, live tiger padded into the room that I knew this was no ordinary wrestler! - K. Maxx] KEVIN MAXX: Hello Tigress, thanks for taking the time to see me. If it's OK with you, I'd rather not start off with the usual questions where people ask about your gender and how it affects your professional wrestling career. Is that all right with you? TIGRESS: Gracia Dios. MAXX: My first question would have to be about WWO in general. I know you haven't been signed with that organization for a long time just yet but... now that you have a couple of matches under your belt... what do you think? TIGRESS: I would give it a lukewarm type of reaction. Some people I am a disappointed in, like Union Jack for beating up his father or the hoopla over Roundhouse giving his first defense to Andros. Others I am impressed with, such as Dark Soul, who is a decent enough guy, and a few others that I have been able to talk to in the back. MAXX: Hmmm, well you've kind of addressed this a little although you've mentioned people you're not scheduled to face. How would you rate the level of competition in the WWO? Higher than other organizations you've been in? Or lower? TIGRESS: I wouldn't say that it's higher or lower. But I would say that everyone is a lot more relaxed and not as concerned with playing backstage politics. MAXX: Good to hear. I wonder if I can re-phrase my question from a workrate point of view? Does the WWO strike you as a particularly technical wrestling organization? Or one where brawling gets greater results? TIGRESS: I think the technical stuff edges out. And I enjoy that more. While brawling and "gimmick" matches have their entertainment value, I will take the actual wrestling any day. That makes the gimmicks more special. MAXX: Many have been impressed by your own workrate. But you don't seem afraid to brawl whenever you have to. Has that ever backfired on you? TIGRESS: (drawn out) Oooooooohhhhhhhhhhhh, yes. I've been on the receiving end of quite a few submission holds where I picked a fight that I shouldn't have. There was one time that I fell unconscious and didn't wake up until two weeks later. And there was one fight I got into that got me the scar under my eye. MAXX: Care to give us some details on those? TIGRESS: Hmmm... Well, the submission hold was when I was starting out in the WWA. Vyolynce attacked a guy I was dating and I confronted him. Then I threw a punch and he put me in a surfboard. The hospital stay resulted from several beat-downs and I wouldn't take care of myself. MAXX: How about the scar? TIGRESS: My scar resulted from a knifefight over some stupid reason. A woman thought I was after her boyfriend. MAXX: Ow! A knife-fight? TIGRESS: Yeah. It was just a stupid situation that got out of hand. MAXX: Hmmm... nasty. Getting back to the WWO for a minute, people have been asking about what's going on with you and Jack Lantern. Are the two of you allies? Have you been asked to join The Trailer Park Mutants? TIGRESS: (sighs) I don't really know myself. I just give Mackey Jay a comment that I'm impressed with the fruitface and now everyone is trying to pair us off. MAXX: But... both of you have helped each other out recently. You're saying that's coincidental? TIGRESS: Hey, he helped me. So I owed him one. MAXX: One of the reasons people might think of you and Lantern as allies is because of the recent match where you helped him out against Vyolynce. Will you and Vyolynce be resuming your past feud? TIGRESS: I hope not. I barely came out of our first feud with my head intact. And now... MAXX: If you were looking for allies in the WWO, and I'm not saying whether you are or aren't... who would you trust? Or better yet, who would make a good ally for you? TIGRESS: I can't say. I'm not really looking for one. Let's just say that I haven't had a lot of luck with allies lately. MAXX: All right... but speaking conjecturally... who on the roster is someone you'd consider if they ever approached you? I'm leading up to another question with this one. TIGRESS: I guess Jack. I dunno. Or Apex or Tracy or something. MAXX: Here's the flip side of that question... who would you definitely *NOT* want to be teamed with in... say... a random partner tag team match? TIGRESS: Oh, that's obvious, isn't it? MAXX: For the record... humor me. TIGRESS: Of course, Girard. And the Ultimate Misogynist. Or Mike Sebastian and his partner, Fang. Basically anyone that hates Andros for losing to me. MAXX: You don't think Sebastian and Fang are honest? They're gaining a lot of fan support lately. TIGRESS: I just don't trust them. Call me crazy, but anyone that concentrates on money is someone that I don't trust. MAXX: Hmmm... what about some of the newer guys? Trixxster, for instance, is someone that people are making a lot of noise about. Know anything about him? TIGRESS: No. But something in my gut tells me that he's not someone I should get involved with. MAXX: I promised not to deal with the gender issue because that's been overdone. But do you see yourself as an outsider still? TIGRESS: I'm always going to be an outsider. The taboo on female athletes and warriors dates back to prehistoric times. While we have made some powerful strides in the past fifty years, we are still a long way away from acceptance. MAXX: In a related question, do you think there's a place in wrestling for gimmicks like Sparkling Dan's? You're breaking down certain stereotype barriers. Should all barriers in wrestling be challenged? TIGRESS: Why not? Homosexuality exists and it would be stupid to deny that it does. MAXX: Very true. But do you see Sparkling Dan as promoting gay rights or hurting them with the depiction of his character? TIGRESS: Hmm... no more than I would with myself. I think the best route for him is to just be himself and not make a big deal about his choice of lifestyle. MAXX: Related to that question, do you see yourself as a role model? Not as a woman, perhaps, but as a person in general? Or is that placing too much responsibility on a professional wrestler? TIGRESS: Professional wrestlers, like any celebrity, have a responsibility to be a role model. People look at us as example of how to act. I don't know about the others, but I take that responsibility seriously and do charity work and make contributions to organizations that benefit children. MAXX: Here's my last question... where would you like to see yourself in the WWO six months from now? In title contention? TIGRESS: Of course. Perhaps scrapping around for the TV title. But wherever I am, my main goal is to enjoy what I'm doing. That's how I measure my success. MAXX: Thanks very much for your time, Tigress. I wish you the best of luck. TIGRESS: No problem. Tag Teams. For those of you who know me well in the WWO, you might also know that I participate in the EMWC as = of Strictly Business, the current EMWC Tag Team champions (shameless plug). Yes, for those of you who had no clue who that team was that won the RSP-WF Top 200 Tag Team poll, that was us. Strictly Business is the team of Andrew "Flash" Tucker and "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, and if any of you know the guy, [name withheld] is the one who handles Tucker. Anyhow, enough of that. I had a point, remember? :) The point is, you look at the 200 and you don't see 200. It was what? 60? The Top 60 of '99? That's sad. It really is. In years past, there was no problem whatsoever in obtaining 60 tag teams. Now, EMWC is allegedly the "best fed on the net" if any of you have heard that. From the WWO, Christopher Stonebreaker, Mike Sebastian, Fang, and Tracy Hudson have all participated there. Stonebreaker and Sebastian have been there roughly the same amount of time, and have worked together for a good portion of their respective tenures. Now, coming from a guy that has been in EMWC about as long as he has in the WWO, I will openly say the WWO is clearly the most entertaining out of the two. Why? We'll save that for a later discussion. But for me, personally, I enjoy participating in the WWO more, because well, it better suits what I am looking for in a federation. I am by no means saying either federation is better than the other, but that I have more fun in the WWO. No harm, no foul. The one thing I will say, and have said for months is the World Wrestling Organization has _the_ best tag team roster in all of e-wrestling, bar none. Now, I don't want to sound like I have some chip on my shoulder because I am = of the "best tag team in the best federation on the 'net," which is all an opinion anyhow, on both counts. But, relating the EMWC tag scene to the WWO's is no comparison, really. Comparing the WWO's tag roster to anyone's is just unfair to anyone but the WWO, to be honest. Let's take a look at the WWO's tag team roster and compare them to some of the higher rated teams around: WWO: 1) The Armed Forces Yes, they pulled in a very impressive #19 ranking in the 60, but rankings have really never meant much to NavCom and DefCon. The first team ever to reach the 20-win plateau in he Double Eye truly was the most dominant team of 1999 in the WWO -- no question. However, the team is yet to hold the Tag Team gold, even though they have dominated most every team to walk through the federation this year... that is, except one. 2) The Outlaws While the Forces brought home #19, the Outlaws pulled in #5. Yes, the UEW is resposible for that mainly, but no one will argue the fact the Outlaws have run roughshod through the WWO's ranks since coming in, but the Armed Forces seem to be returning from beyond as of late. The Outlaws have proven they can run with anyone, and if we thought an Armed Forces/UCD feud was going to be good, the best is yet to come. 3) Fire and Ice The heel turn was unexpected, and added a bit more finesse to the team, and because of their long tenure with the federation, FnI will always be a threat to the tag straps. However, now that UCD has departed and the Outlaws are laying prominence to the gold, FnI may just find themselves on the outside, looking in. Their #40 placing on the 60 was a bit low, I thought, but Fire and Ice have also proved they can hang with the big boys, and did so up in Canada. 4) Thunderstorm "Finally!" That's what we heard when Thunderstorm grabbed the titles in '99. A team that has been around for ages finally, and still remain the top team in the WWO status-wise. However, match them up against the best the world has to offer, and I am afraid they fall a bit short; depending upon whom they are facing. #44 sure ain't too shabby, though. 5) Les Canadians Are they destined to feud with the Armed Forces based on country boudaries? Whether that holds any truth or not, if they can keep with the consistency, Les Canadians will be a team for the future and could have quite the feud with FnI and/or Thunderstorm in the very near future. Runner-ups: Best of the Best, Precious Moments, Good, Bad, and the Ugly, Uncivil Disbobedience [gone] (editors note: UCD owns all other tag teams... hey?! Where the hell is Almost Better?! Damn it!), Ultimates [gone] ELSEWHERE: 1) Strictly Business [EMWC] Sebastian is known to the WWO, and his partner, Tucker, is most likely the man most similar to him in the entire sport. The team's only to defeat was in an impromptu match to a returning Down Boys team, while Tucker and Sebastian have knocked off such duos as the Epitome of Cool, Down Boys, "Diamondback" Chris Myers and "Epitome of Evil" Serge Annis, "Real Deal" Luke Steele and Scott "Hotspot" Daniels, "Spotlight" V.D. Vitrone and "Ironman" Eddie Jacobs, and of course their unbeleivable feud with the Bounty Hunters. 2) The Bounty Hunters [Retired] Get them to return and you have the best team ever assembled. Period. However, Hardin has headed off into retirement once more, while Thunder is said to be doing his thing back in Arizona. But when they were active, they were the best around. No question. 3) The Epitome of Cool [GLCW] The former EMWC Tag Team champions surprised everyone when they resurfaced in GLCW, claiming it was the "cool" place to be. Dan Thomas and Andrew Sterling, however, just may be one of the best teams ever created, even if they do not get the clout they perhaps deserve. One of the more underrated teams around. 4) The Token White Boyz [GLOW] Whether it be hitting the Nintendo skids or trading Pokemon cards Tuesday nights at Burger King, Billy Kane and Corey Atari have to be perhaps the most entertaining ensemble ever created in the tag team scene. They don't generally see too much action, but from their stints in GLOW and UWF, they have ruled like Luigi rules. 5) Toss-up: The Down Boys [EMWC]/The Machines [UWF] While both teams enjoyed success in the Double Eye alongside the Armed Forces, both have gone on to have success elsewhere. The Down Boys have become two-time EMWC Tag Team champions, while the Machines have essentially been _the_ team of the UWF, alongside The Harlequins. It's really a matter of preference of whom to list here. Runner-ups: The Harlequins [UWF], Fraternity Boys [UWF], El Punstereo [GLCW], American Dragons [UWF] FREE AGENTS: 1) The Benjamins [Formerly of IIWF, EMWC] While openly admitting to loving the WWO, Bobby and Joe never have been seen since being defeated for the EMWC Tag Team Titles. Word has it, they were reviewing the WWO at one point. 2) Rich and Infamous [Formerly of MLWO, EMWC] The two ripped apart both federations before up and leaving. If the team were to return, one would presumably expect them to head back to the land of extreme. 3) Nine Inch Males [Formerly of LWC] "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson has made GLCW his new home, while the WWO's favorite son, "Dios Locos" Rob James, has been without a home for months. Two of the other James' remain active (Tommy in GLCW and Casey in EMWC), but Rob and Kyle remain unheard of. 4) Prophets of Rage [Formerly of IIWF] A return of the Prophets would certainly spark the Armed Forces' career, but the likeliness of this is next to nothing, since they haven't been seen for nearly an eternity. 5) Toss-up: Team Sychosys/Alphabet Boys [Formerly of IIWF] For Team Sychosys, Petrow is dead and 4M is tearing apart the re-opened NLWP. For the Alphabet Boys, they haven't been heard from since the Prophets were, so both teams are certainly out of this picture. COMPARISON (judging on ten total matches between the two teams): 1) The Armed Forces vs. Strictly Business This would be one of those matches where you wouldn't know who wins each time. No squashes. Ever. NavCom and DefCon have their squabbles with one another, and they openly admit that. Strictly Business, however, seem perfect with one another all the time. There must be trouble in paradise at some point. The Armed Forces seem more like an actual team, but then again, Strictly Business must be the best for a reason. WINNER - Draw (5-5) 2) The Outlaws vs. The Bounty Hunters This really depends on the activeness of the Bounty Hunters. With Thunder and Hardin, one must keep in mind that James, Claw, and must not be too far behind, and this would cost the Hunters some victories via DQ. But, the Outlaws certainly could hang. Yet, these still are the Bounty Hunters. WINNER - The Bounty Hunters (6-4) 3) Fire and Ice vs. The Epitome of Cool Fire and Ice always seem to have some type of problem with themselves or how things are going, while the Epitome of Cool are always content with their surroundings. After all, they're cool... and you're not. But, FnI have that experience factor, as well. WINNER - The Epitome of Cool (7-3) 4) Thunderstorm vs. Token White Boyz Kane and Atari must be two of the most entertaining guys around. However, comedy does not always bring success. Thunderstorm would likely remain focused, and after getting in some trouble, the Boyz would probably call for some help with a big man of some kind. In a shocker... WINNER - Thunderstorm (6-4) 5) Les Canadians vs. The Down Boys/The Machines As much as I like the Canadians, they're not ready for the Down Boys. And they're sure not ready for the Machines. If the WWO had UCD involved, things might be different. But for now, no such luck. WINNER - The Down Boys (8-2) WINNER - The Machines (9-1) As bad as that may look, what other federation could even come _close_ to that? We talk a lot about how the WWO could do against the top talent of some of the "top" federations. I am one to think as far as tag teams go, the WWO cannot hang with anyone. It's everyone hanging with us. As for that 5th and final "lock" team that President Stone needs to complete his great pool of tag talent, he should take a look at the free agent list and see who has the #1 position...
