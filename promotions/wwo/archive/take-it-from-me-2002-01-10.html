Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

Well, volume 4 begins with something I think the WWO will like. The Roundtable Discussion. I'd like to thank Dave, Rich, Jeremy, YetiJosh, and I guess I *should* also thank Stone for his involvement in that discussion. Next time, I'll try to keep it more organized by having a more moderated discussion. Next week's topic (subject to change) is "The Championships." How they are being used, what should happen, and so forth.

We have recognition. From the Fan Site Rankings(http://thefansite.efeduniverse.com/Erankings.html), the following were ranked in singles competition. Alex Extreme (10), Tyrone Hayes (21), William Craven (25), and Chris Stringer (29). Tracy Hudson was mentioned as a somewhat honorable mention to the Top 35. In tag team ranks, Vegas Connection was ranked second and in federation ranks, the WWO was ranked 5th.

And then rspwf.com released their rankings. Congrats to Hayes (tied for 11), Extreme (22), Chris Hopper (30), and Hudson (33). Craven also received votes, but was not in the top 35. WWO as a federation was ranked 4th. I guess we can't complain too much on being completely overlooked.

Also, I'd like to congratulate everyone who won an award. Because of limitations, [name withheld] did not put together a show, but the awards are "handed out" via this article. For everyone who won an award or was nominated, thanks for putting together a solid WWO output for 2001 and hopefully, we can continue to build on that for 2002.

So, anyway, back to this issue of the Source, the first of volume 4 of the WWO mag. Those contributing this week are [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], Hopper, YetiJosh, and HootieJosh. Much appreciatation to each of these fine upstanding human beings.

If you would like to take a step to a better generation, please do send articles and assorted e-mails to [detail withheld] even if it happens to be bad stuff about my characters.
