ATTITUDE In 1998, then WWO Commissioner AJ Howard attempted to put together a weekly program for the WWO, hosted by Lenny Wacus and Bruce 'Bruise' Firestone. Booking decisions and productions by Howard were so bad, that wrestlers refused to be part of the Attitude show, and the project was scrapped after four episodes, as the slow lasted only through the month of May.
