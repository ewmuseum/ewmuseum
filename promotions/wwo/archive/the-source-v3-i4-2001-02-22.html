Having lived through the torturous delays of late 1999 and early 2000, I never thought I'd again see the day when the WWO was actually back to a weekly schedule AND staying up on the same level of quality we've all come to demand and expect, but here we are. Kudos to Mark and everyone who helps out writing matches for making it happen. It looks like there's very little dead weight on the roster right now, and the WWO is slowly but surely becoming one of the top forces in the e-wrestling world once again. And can I possibly report anything better than that?
