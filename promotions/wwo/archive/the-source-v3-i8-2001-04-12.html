The Problem Child ------------------------------------------------------------- An Opinion To Be Heard by [name withheld] Volumn 1, Issue 2 Let's be honest here, the WWO's depth took a well-needed shot in the arm recently and it should NOT go underappreciated. Holy Roller became a signed WWO wrestler and R.A.P.E. becomes the second biggest four-letter acronym in the WWO (P.A.I.N. still reigns supreme) (wow...that rhymed). Now, Roller has a tough road ahead. It's hard to mix religion and wrestling, but so far, Roller has been up to the challenge. Now, would it make sense for him to make a major run at the WWO Title, for he is now in the Tournament? Not particularly. He could be impressive and in a upcoming issue, I'll enter my World Title predictions. I have Roller listed as a hell of a match for Dark Soul, but I still don't think he'll get passed the Quesada for what Roller is going for, he doesn't need the distraction of gold just yet. Icon-X, to me, could have a hell of an alliance with Marky Marvelous if he was still around, even if Icon-X isn't supposed to be an outright loser. Icon-X considers himself a voice of a generation, but time will show if he represents the violent part of today's generation or the uncaring one. Lion Tamer comes in, quickly insults WWO good guys, Dark Soul and Apex, and becomes an immediate heel. I could almost see Tamer a possible member of the OSE, but from what I have heard, Tamer's ego combined with that of Chris Stringer will make Starr/Stringer seem like peas and carrots, to quote a movie. Tamer's not a match just yet for Dark Soul or Apex, which is not meant as a slight. Dark Soul and Apex are part of a defined feud, even if Apex seems in and out of the action biweekly. Not many WWO wrestlers even have a quasifeud happening. Genocide helps shore up a CruiserWeight division that needs a dependable fourth and quickly. Who wants to see a long-term situation of Stringer, Quesada, and Tucker passing the title off back and forth? Can Genocide help accomplish what Chris Johnson, Steven Erickson, and Lord Keeblor have failed at? Let's hope so. But all is not lost for the CruiserWeight division if Genocide fails. Ethan Love adds a new element to the division. For that matter, the CruiserWeight division could easily put the WWO HeavyWeight division as most in need of new bodies. With Icon-X, Genocide, and Love in the CruiserWeights, the tag division coming through on a weekly basis, the HeavyWeight roster continues to lag behind. A lack of true faces can do that. To add to Lion Tamer, Michael Priest hopes to follow another recent newcomer to the WWO, Major Damage, and his rise to the top of the fed. However, Priest and Tamer will have the unfortunate circumstance of entering the WWO while the fed is under major transistion. With the World Title Tournament becoming a weekly event, Tamer and Priest may only be able to get action from the losers of the Tournament for awhile, but once WWO Inquistion becomes a thing of the past, the entering of new wrestlers could help keep the 80's sure winner, sure loser matches of Dark Soul/Sean Eden, Major Damage/Lachance, and Romanis/Status Quo from ever polluting the WWO television screen. Course, the WWO hasn't had the greatest luck in recruiting wrestlers of late, but of the six newest WWOers, if three comes through as dependable wrestlers, the WWO is given the aforementioned shot in the arm.
