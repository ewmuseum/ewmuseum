WWO bio card — Andrew "Flash" Tucker

28 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Andrew "Flash" Tucker Height: 6'0 Weight: 225 1/4 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Oakland, California Entrance Music: Ambitionz as a Ridah - Tupac Shakur Manager: None Psychological Makeup Outside of the ring, Tucker is seen as a no-nonsense type of guy who takes his job quite seriously, and a man whose path you may regret crossing, but due to his smaller size, opponents tend to test their luck, usually regretting doing so. Inside the ring, however, it's a totally different story. Tucker is out to give the fans a show worth paying for while remaining a consumate professional. Despite what he'll tell you, he isn't out to injure anyone inside the ring, it's merely entertainment, not anything to make someone not be able to provide for their family. His ringwork, not so much his attitude and big mouth, is what Tucker will be remembered for when he retires. Strengths _Great_ highflyer - Andrew Tucker is definitely one of the best highflyers in the game. He's been consistantly listed in the top 10 of high-flying awards for years. Tucker, though, tends not to consider himself a cruiserweight, despite his physical attributes. Tucker likes to think of himself more as a guy who will go toe-to-toe with anyone in a fist fight. He knows, though, that his ability to fly is a huge asset inside the ring and utilizes it as such. Stamina - Tucker has endless amounts of energy, and just when you think you've beaten the hell out of him, he'll pop right back up into your face, and it tends to get opponents very frustrated. In other words, Tucker can take a licking and k eep on ticking. Respect - Andrew Tucker has been inside some of the most competitive federations in the world with some of the top talent anyone has ever seen. He's wrestled the best, he's beaten the best. The one thing that Tucker refuses to do, is become too proud. Tucker knows that he was once an up-and-coming rookie that shocked a lot of veterans by pinning them and refuses to take any opponent lightly. He sees every man across the ring from him as the next rising star, and wrestles them as such. Tu cker also wants to give people a show that they'll remember, so you'll never see Tucker lagging off in a match or taking an opponent easily, regardless of what he'll say in an interview. Weaknesses Intelligence - Tucker definitely isn't the most colorful crayon in the box, as is shown by the way he goes into widely outnumbered fights, throws himself through tables, etc. This may seem as if it's a strength to fans, who love a good show, but doing things without implementing common sense can cost him. Left knee - Tucker injured his left knee early in his career, torn ligaments to be more specific, and while it usually doesn't show, opponents who do their homework on Tucker tend to go straight for that knee. If they can take out Tucker's aerial arsenal, they've got a much better chance of getting the win. Mouth - Tucker's mouth tends to run him into some trouble. He'll tell a guy twice his size to screw off, and he'll get planted for it. But, Tucker (for what reason we'll never know) would rather be hurt physically than to have his pride dented. And that is a major weakness in this career field. Biographical Information Andrew Tucker was born February 26th, 1974 in Beverly Hills, California to Laura and William Tucker, two very successful, and very rich, investors. Andrew had everything he could ever want in life. Anything he could desire, he could have. Except one thing - attention. With his parents gone 95% of the time, he and his younger sister Angelina had a close bond, and all they ever wanted was their parents to spend some time with them instead of trying to buy their love So, one day, when Andrew was 15, and Angelina was 12, they finally had had enough, and they decided to run away. To where, they didn't know. So, they took the money they had in their stashes and hopped on a bus and told the driver to take them as far as their money would go. That place was Oakland, California. When they got there, they didn't exactly know what they were going to do, so they hopped from homeless shelter to homeless shelter until Andrew got himself involved in a gang. The gang may not have been the nicest group of people, but they gave Andrew and Angelina the love and attention that they desired. They barely got by, which was a stark contrast to the life they had previously lived, but they were happy. All things were going well until one day (DUM, DUM, DUMMMMMMM!! :).. While in a brawl with a rival gang, Andrew was stabbed in the side. When all was done, the gang Andrew had called family had left him there to bleed to death. Nobody heard Andrew's cries for help except one person - his sister. She got him to the hospital, and although Andrew had lost a lot of blood, he survived. Andrew was 16 years old then, and while lying in a hospital bed, he was clueless to what he was going to do next. But, by a stroke of good luck, a local wrestling promoter in the area named P.J. Christiansen walked into his room, mistaking it for someone else's, and they got to talking. Andrew told him his story, and P.J. only had one idea that he could possibly do to help the boy in need. He got him into wrestling. After a few weeks of recuperating from his stab wound, he was in shape to start training for what he dreamed of as a huge wrestling career. What Andrew found instead of a quick buck', was the hardest thing he had ever had to do. The workouts, the long days, everything was a lot more than he had expected. But, Andrew had never met a problem he couldn't solve, and he was hell bent on becoming successful at this as well. After two and a half years of training, P.J. told him that he thought he still had a lot to learn, yet he was ready to start wrestling. So, Andrew started to jump from small fed to small fed. And thus began his wrestling career. Tucker started his wrestling career in a small federation named the AWA, and there he found absolutely NO success. He went back to Oakland down and dejected, but as they say, after you fall off of the horse, you have to jump right back on. So, Tucker went back to wrestling, and signed a small contract with another small federation called the ECWO. There, Tucker had some minimal success, winning a few matches here and there. So, after being in the federation for about a month, the suits of the company decided that he would be a good warm up for the then World Heavyweight Champion, Da Rapper. Tucker, however, looked at it as his shot at stardom. So, one day Andrew Tucker got his shot at the World Champion - and made the best of it. When he pinned the World Champion for three, not only did he take home a big belt, but he shocked the World, and shot to the top of the federation instantly. He held that title for three months, longer than any Champion before him, and when he did drop it, he captured it back soon after. After Tucker dropped the title for the second time, he decided that he'd go for a little bigger competition. Tucker jumped from feds like the WecW, UCW and the wWw, and won the World titles in all three feds as well. He was also ranked as the 58th best wrestler in the World in 1997. After accomplishing all this, Tucker decided it was time to move up to the big leagues, so to speak. Andrew signed contracts with the INWI, N.E.W., and CNWF² and was ready for the competition that was thrown at him. After some mediocre success in all three feds, he started to get on a roll and captured the Lightheavyweight Championship in the INWI, and became the longest reigning Lightheavyweight Champion ever at over five months. He alsocaptured the CNWF² United States Championship and held it proudly until the federation folded. Tucker also secured the N.E.W. Cruiserweight Title and held it before a rift came between Tucker and the N.E.W. and he left the organization. As he concentrated mainly on the INWI, he captured the INWI World Heavyweight Title, his biggest prize to date, and held it from October 1998 to January of 1999. During this time, Andrew Tucker was also ranked as the 13th best wrestler in the World today. 1999 was the year that Tucker took a back seat in singles wrestling, only participating in the INWI in early 1999 to its closure, then moving to the IWE for a short while until that too, closed. From there, he formed a tag team with his best friend, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, better known as Strictly Business. Sebastian and Tucker soon topped the EMWC Tag Team Division, capturing the World Tag Team Titles from the longest reigning champions to date, the Epitome of Cool. From there, the duo held the titles for about 3 and a half months before dropping them to two masked luchadors, which turned out to be the Down Boys, returning to the EMWC. Strictly Business was not to be denied, however, and they recaptured the titles from the Down Boys on New Year's Eve 1999. They held the titles for another three months before a rift between the EMWC and themselves formed and they soon lost the titles to the Foundation and made their exit from the EMWC. About the same time, Tucker returned to the UKCW once again in singles action, and was the number one contender to the Heavyweight Title and was in the midst of a dangerous feud between the champion Nick Demola when the federation folded. Strictly Business then moved on to the UWF, where they immediately captured the World Tag Team Championships, and defended them against all comers. They succeeded in holding on to them after a bitter feud with Too Damn Nasty, and then again when two teams simultaneously put their bid in for the titles, the Brotherhood, and Strictly Too Sexy, a spoof of Tucker and Sebastian. Tucker has also had short stints in federations such as the TOP, CWC and others. He has also been seen in inter-fed events such as: The Jay Ralston Memorial Cup, Tag Team Hell, Master of the Ring II and III, WrestleBowl II, The Gordon Solie Memorial Cup and The International Cruiserweight Tournament of Champions.
