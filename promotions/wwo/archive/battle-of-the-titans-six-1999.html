Battle of the Titans Six (excerpt)

10 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. The promotion's own last European card of its touring run, broadcast from Madrid, Spain. Only the three title segments below are reproduced here in full; a seven-team battle royal and a four-chairs match remain held and are not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, defends against "The Future of Pro-Wrestling" Chris Johnson:] Ralph: Johnson back up to his feet...and a sleeper!! Johnson with a sleeper of his own on Sebastian!! And the champ back to the corner!! And that forces a quick break!! And we've got to be close to the time limit!
Bradley: Hey, anything that keeps Sebastian as the champion is just fine in my book. Just fight it off for another minute or two!!
[…] Ralph: TWO!!! The crowd trying to find someway to get Johnson up... THREE....and it stays down!! The arm stays down!! And Sebastian with the successful defense of the TV title!!
Bradley: Just goes to show you, that the more you throw at the champ, the more he can take!
[…] Calhoun: Your winner of the match, and _STILL_ your World Wrestling Organization Television Champion... "MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN!! WWO INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, "Latin Lightning" Al Perez, defends against "Playboy" Jude St. Claire, with St. Claire's own Showmen stablemates barred from ringside:] Ralph: Perez back up to his feet, and back around... Kick from Keeblor!! And he's hooking the man up!! Keeblor hooking Perez up... KEEBLOR BOMB!!
[… this exchange belongs to the undercard; the title match resumes below …] Ralph: And what do you think you're seeing! Bradley: Perez cannot be fighting back like this!! You had him Jude!! You were the champion!!
Ralph: Perez up to his feet..and St. Claire staggered... Perez charging in.. Monkey flip!! And over goes the Playboy!! The playboy driven up and over with it!! And the crowd roaring their approval, as "Hot Property" signalling to the crowd!!
Bradley: No! Come on Jude you don't want in a foot race with the man! Ralph: And St. Claire back around... Dropkick by Perez!! And St. Claire sent flying back into the corner!!!
Bradley: This isn't what I'm seeing! This can't be! Ralph: And what do you think you're seeing! […] Ralph: Perez turning St. Claire over!! And the cover!! ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!! Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of this match, and _STILL_ YOUR INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPION... "LATIN LIGHTNING" AL PEREZ!!! WWO WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP — TRIANGLE ELIMINATION MATCH ["Blockbuster" Johnny Detson, the man with the title to defend, against Ultimate Thrasher and "White Hot" Darren Morgan, with a returning Randy Bryson as special guest referee:] Ralph: Detson pulling himself back in the ring, and look at the smile on Detson's face. You can almost see the look of appreciation. He's got Randy in his corner! And Thrasher hobbling around on the floor now. That leg is hurt!!
[…] Ralph: Randy in place!! ONETWOTHREE!! HOLY!!! Bradley: What the!!! Randy!! What are you doing??? [The crowd erupts as Randy gets up and signals the three count to the timekeeper and Calhoun.]
Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match, and _NEW_ WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION WORLD CHAMPION, ULTIMATE THRASHER!!
Ralph: I don't believe it!! We all thought Randy was teaming up with Detson...and Bryson motioning for the title!! Thrasher up to his feet, and Randy's handing it over to him!!
[…] Ralph: Bryson holding the hand of Thrasher up.... What the!! Randy just levelled Thrasher with a boot of his own!! Diamond cutter on Thrasher!! What is going on!! Randy on top of Thrasher!! And he's registering the count himself!! ONETWOTHREEE!!!!
Bradley: Say it, Mickey!! New world Champion!!! Enjoy your 7 second reign Thrasher!! You're in the history books!!
Ralph: The hall of Fame is in the ring!! And Thrasher is laid out!! Folks, we're out of time!!
