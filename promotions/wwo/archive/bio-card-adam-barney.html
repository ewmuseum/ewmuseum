WWO bio card — "Action" Adam Barney

27 April 2001

"Action" Adam Barney Real Name: Adam Barney Height: 6'3 Weight: 236 Date of Birth: Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Palm Bay, FL Entrance Music: "Back in Black" by AC/DC Manager: Valerie Dougan-Barney Managerial Profile Psychological Makeup Strengths Intelligence - Adam is very smart. Like 1380's on his SAT smart. Attended Brown for two years, then dropped out to go into wrestling Knowledge of Wrestling - Barney is a genius of ring knowledge. Has probably watched more tapes than most wrestlers have had 'ringrats.' Has his main styles, but will pull of something you wouldn't expect from him at a moment's notice. Willpower - Barney will simply not give out. Sure, he may lose matches by submission, but only if he is knocked out. Weaknesses His wife - You touch Valerie, all of Adam's focus is on you. Period. Forget the match, forget breathing. Adam won't give up until he gets his hands on the person who attacks his wife. Not a 'Fighter' - By that, I mean, while, in a falls count anywhere match, his partner takes control while Adam picks up random foreign objects. Adam is a wrestler, not a brawler. Ring Rust - Hasn't wrestled since Spring of '99 regularly, so has to get back in ring shape. Injuries that have occurred over the years of wrestling haven't been really "worked out" and may never be back at 100%. Style Notes: Biographical Information Adam Barney has your normal middle-class upbringing in San Francisco, California until the age of twelve. Then his parent's divorced and he moved with his mother to her home of Palm Bay, Florida. There, he met up with Mike Hall. For year's, up until their graduation from high school, they were best friends. While Adam went to Brown, Mike went on a football scholarship to Maryland. Two year's later, Adam got the wrestling bug and went into training in Southern Maryland. There, who should he find but his old buddy Mike. At the end of 1994, they graduated and for the next few years, went into the smaller independents, making a name for themselves and generally having some fun and beating up the bad guys. Originally, they were known as the Florida Connection, but as their exploits of battle over 'heels' spread, they began to be called Final Justice. Their big break came in Feb. of 1998 when they got the call to go into the world-famous NPWA. This was their big break, and it showed. Even though they never quite made it to the top, they had good matches and got plenty of exposure. When all was said and done when the NPWA closed in early 1999, Final Justice only had one North American title reign, but memorable battles against Heaven & Hell and other great teams. However, since then, other than a brief stopover in a international fed in the summer of '99, Adam and Mike quit the wrestling business. They lived off the big severance pay from Mr. Dutt, and having been living the good life, on a giant yacht in the Caribbean (sp?). But, funds are running low, so they've decided to get back in the business.
