WWO Tag Teams Welcomed

8 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, ladies and gentlemen, the World Wrestling Organization is looking for those tag teams who are looking to make their mark in the sport of professional wrestling. The WWO has long prided itself on the fact that we give the tag team divisions in our federation the same indepth attention that most federations are renowned for giving their singles divisions only. With a recent cleaning of the roster, and unfortunate retirement of Uncivil Disobedience, the WWO is looking to fill up the last few slots on our roster. We're looking for tag teams ready to compete with the high-flying gypsies known as the Amazing Romanis, the old-school Blood Brothers, or even the rough and tumble Les Canadians and FIre and Ice. Or maybe the gimmickry is what you're looking to compete with? Well, what better duos are there to comepte against than facing off with Physicians Advocating Innovative Neoprocedure and the Vegas Connection. Or perhaps the hardcore antics of Dark Haven? Could it be you're just looking for some good old fashioned wrestling against teams such as Final Justice and Damage Incorporated? We've got the roster slots, we give you the attention... Now is your chance to get in with the federation that truly hasn't forgotten the tag team division, the World Wrestling Organization. Drop by and check out our operations at http://wwowrestling.virtualave.net We don't think you'll be disappointed.
