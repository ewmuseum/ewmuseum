Rumours - 10 January 2002

13 May 2003

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

-Tyrone Hayes is bringing in Steve Spurrier to help "clean up" Todd Johnstone's reputation

-Cherry Bomb isn't really Irish.. she's CANADIAN!

-Made In America will get a title shot before the Romanis.

-Mike Horan was seen lost on the streets of Seattle, mumbling something about the Packers blowing the division.

-Look for Holy Roller to return to WWO TV in some capacity during this cycle.

-Dave Olvera is running the WWO in [name withheld]'s chair-induced absence, and plans on manipulating the title scene to bring the World belt around the waist of a returning Jude St. Claire or "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian by as early as February.

-By the way, Jude St. Claire is rumored to have won the WFO, a fantasy football league for the WWO. From his deathbed, [name withheld] has called for the Cleveland Browns fans to track him down claiming St. Claire is a brother of an NFL ref.

-Joe Gonzo will be the next booker of the Tag Team Division. Title shots will be decided by games of "Quarters."

-[name withheld] has issued an edict barring anyone from writing-in The Chair That Hit [name withheld] as Announcer Of The Year on the WWO Year End Award ballots. Currently, it is believed that over a half-million ballots have been thrown out with The Chair having been voted for Announcer Of The Year on all of them. The majority of these votes were collected in Florida in an interesting tidbit of information

-Chris Stringer was reportedly peeved when he found out Fyodor Samsonov's broom closet was bigger than his hotel room.

-Look for Stringer's entourage to dwindle in the coming weeks... the WWO may be looking to cut some "peripheral" characters. Reasons are still unknown, but there are rumors that the WWO is trying to lock up Candy Malone from leaving the WWO for greener pastures and the money it will take for the young lady is reported to be causing [name withheld] to report to the intensive care unit on a regular basis.

-The long-rumored Dark Haven split will not happen until their feud with Blood Brothers is over, and may not happen at all.

-[name withheld] still has a sweet ass.

-Rumor has it Mike Horan's recent "unavailability" has the WWO looking for a tag team vice president again. Candidates include Al Sharpton, Julie Stone, and Josh Rodriguez, who has already reported that any band that changes their entrance music to Poison will be considered very favorably.

-[name withheld] does not have a sweet ass, but he makes up for it by having nice tits. [name withheld] has given no comment on such matters.

-The WWO front office was quite pleased with how well received the William Craven/Chris Hopper match was. They've now decided that Chris Hopper doesn't blow or suck, but now has a future as Craven's dominatrix. Apparently, the drugs Stone has to take are beginning to seriously alter his WWO vision.

-There are rumors Craven and Apex may end up feuding if Thrasher's backstage problems continue to worsen...

-Rumors are that Dark Soul will done the Latex Lex mask and return to torture his Upper Crust enemies in true Lex style.

-No word on what combination of feuds will come out of the group of wrestlers including Matt Cole, Jonas King, Xaiver Feyr and Marvin Patch, but you probably can count on the outcome being bloody. Bring a towel.

-Sean Collins and Tre Jordan are reported to be headed to a title feud with Vegas Connection.
