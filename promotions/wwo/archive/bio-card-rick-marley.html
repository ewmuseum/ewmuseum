WWO bio card — "Showtime" Rick Marley

27 May 2003

"Showtime" Rick Marley Wrestler Name: Rick Marley Date of Birth: Height: 5'10 Weight: 215 lbs Alignment: Principled Hometown: Allentown, Pennsylvania Entrance Music: "Ride the Wind" by Poison Manager: Not Applicable Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Rick Marley is a fairly short man for a wrestler. He has dark hair that reaches the area between his shoulder blades, moderately pale skin, and blue eyes. He has no facial hair, or scars. Ring Appearance While competing, Marley wears a pair of black wrestling boots, long legged black trunks with blue-white spotlight streaks running up the legs, and the word "Showtime" across the butt, a pair of black elbowpads, and black wrist tape. Psychological Makeup Strengths Quick as a hiccup: Marley is extremely quick, allowing him to dodge and/or block/counter moves from his opponent. Tough: Being the little guy, you get caught fairly often. Marley has the resliency to be able to take a beating and come back for more. Good in a brawl: With all of the heavyweights and superheavyweights that Marley has faced over the past two years, he's become a very accomplished brawler. Weaknesses It's called high risk: Many of Marley's high flying moves can hurt him as much, or more than his opponent. Boy...he's heavy: Marley is simply incapable of lifting anyone that weighs over 240 pounds. He's just not strong enough. Size: Let's face it...at 5'10", Marley isn't gonna have any kind of reach advantage on just about any opponent...or any leverage. Biographical Information Rick Marley is a second generation wrestler. His fater Sean was a high flyer on the indy scene back in the 70's, who retired and opened up a wrestling school. Rick has been in and around the sport since he can remember at this point. His first pro match was at 17 in a promotion his dad ran, which he lost horribly. After years of training, Rick struck out on his own and joined a fed called Dark Horse Wrestling, where he'd go on to become the champion, capturing the fans' hearts after 5 months of hard work there...and proceeded to capture the attention of the UEW at the same time. He was signed to a lucrative contract. After laboring in the UEW's undercard for a while, Marley was recruited by Magnus Colby for his new Widwomakers Inc. All Marley had to do was to betray most of his friends and family, and Colby would help him to get ahead. Marley sold out, and proceeded to win the UEW Cruiserweight title, then, upon betraying Colby and taking the stable for his own, Marley became a two time UEW American Champion as well. Upon losing the belt for the second time, Marley realized that he'd sold everything that used to mean anything to him, and has attmepted to change his ways. One of the first steps is to try to get back to the basics in wrestling. Getting back to a wrestling style where your work in the ring meant more than the goons you had to run in on your match...so Marley struck it out on his onw upon the closure of UEW, to make a name for himself away from all of his former compatriots.
