WWO bio card — Magnus Colby

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Magnus Colby Wrestler Name: Magnus Colby Date of Birth: Height: 6'6 Weight: 325 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Entrance Music: "I Want It All" By Queen Manager: Thomas J. Smith and Chicago's Finest Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Magnus Colby is a large man. He's a tad shorter than Lex Luger, his physique is starting to resemble that of Scott Steiner though, as Magnus has added quite a bit of muscle to his frame in an attempt to lengthen his career (by protecting his spinal column from further injury). Typically, Magnus wears his hair short, cut military style Ring Appearance Simple black tights and boots. Magnus wears a long black overcoat to the ring. Psychological Makeup Magnus has really grown apathetic towards the fans. He's learned that they are very fickle, cheering you one minute, and booing the next. He knows he can't count on them any more for support, so he doesn't. He does seem to take some joy in taunting the crowd when they are particuliarly negative towards him, and really seems to thrive when they are. Strengths Loyalty Once an ally, it's an ally for life... or close to it. Weaknesses Aging: Once able to do a number of aeriel assualts, the years are slowly taking it's toll on Colby. Where he once was able to compete with the smaller high-flying wrestlers, now, he finds himself on the losing end of those battles. Biographical Information Magnus Colby attended high school in Northwest Suburban Chicago where he wrestled and was named All Conference four straight years, and All-State twice. Recruited heavily for college, eventually decided on the University of Illionis at Champaign Urbana. Magnus was All Conference three straight years, and All America as a junior. Late in his junior year though, Magnus blew out his ACL in a freak accident. He would miss his entire senior year after major reconstructive knee surgery. During this layoff, Magnus experienced a rare growth spurt. This brought Magnus up from an even six foot to the six foot, six inches he now stands at. With the growth spurt, Magnus suddenly found himself being recruited by the major professional wrestling organizations. Magnus nearly made the change, but during the last few months of his senior year, Magnus' father passed on. Magnus turned away from the non-guaranteed money the wrestling organizations were offering, and instead went into the family business, essentially retiring before he ever began. Magnus spent a few years running the business, and took the business from a mom and pop organization to the upper portion of the Fortune 500 list. Finally, as the company grew more and more successful, Magnus started relinquishing some of the control of the organization to his assistants. Slowly, the bug started to return. Magnus started to wonder what he missed. Around this time (Early '97), a man by the name of [name withheld] took over the UEW. Jay wanted to upgrade the UEW, and in the process of looking for more advertising revenues, met Magnus Colby. Magnus helped supply the capital Jay would need to improve the UEW into what would eventually become the #3 federation. In exchange Magnus took a silent front office position. As Magnus got more and more involved in the operations of the UEW, the lust for competition got the better of him. Magnus drew up a contract with the UEW running for a three year period, and managed to slip it past Jay. In the fall of 1997, Magnus Colby entered the UEW, and began ripping off victories. He helped bring two wrestlers together to form the hugely successful tag team, the Circle of Death. Magnus served as manage to the duo and guided the two men to the UEW's Ultimate tag titles. Magnus wanted to be a part of seemingly everything in the UEW, be it announcing, wrestling or managing. He also founded the stable known as Widow Makers Inc. This stable would go on to be one of the more successful stables in recent UEW history. In December of 1997, Rob Magnum won the UEW's Ultimate title, vacating the Television title he had worn for nearly a year. Magnus was one of 16 wrestlers selected to compete in a tournament to crown a new Television champion. Magnus would reach the final four of this tournament, and fight for the title on a Sunday Night Special in mid February. In a four corners match against Eric Smart, Angel of Death and Alex Extreme, Magnus would reach the final two before being pinned by Extreme. Alex Extreme was a childhood of friend of Magnus' but jealousy got the better of Magnus. Magnus would launch a bitter assault on Alex, using the Widow Makers to dish out 2, 3 and 4 on one assaults. Finally, at the Ides of March Pay Per View, Magnus would engage in a Chicago Street Fight match for the Television title against Extreme. Magnus and Alex fought all throughout the United Center, and out into the streets. The fight finally culminated in a bar (Chelli's Chilli) down the street. Again, Extreme came out on top. In the summer of '97, Magnus won his first world title in a small regional promotion, named the IWC. He would go on to have the longest world title reign in that federation's history, as well as be the only two-time World Champion ever. He also was named the number one wrestler in the history of the federation, as voted on by his peers. Back in the UEW, Magnus Colby would suffer a series of setbacks, against wrestlers like Jeff "Madfox" Matthews and "Southern Gentlemen" Tommy Lane. Finally, Magnus got things going though, and moved into the top 5 in the federation's rankings. This would garner him a shot at the UEW's American Champion, Professor Payne. Payne was the longest reigning American champion in UEW history when he entered the ring that night. When he left, he left a beaten man, who would never be the same again. Magnus, had won his first major championship. Unfortunately, Magnus got in the way of "Ladykiller" Chris Tyler. Tyler was in the middle of the longest winning streak in the UEW's history, and he would overmatch Colby on two occasions to become the American champion. Just one month, and again, Magnus was faced with adversity. Magnus would look to the New Alliance and Rick Styles to try and give his career a boost. Unfortunately, Rick would double cross Magnus and instead, introduce Alex Martinez as the new New Alliance member. Martinez powerbombed Colby through a table, and a feud was born. Finally, in August of 1998, Magnus would meet Alex Martinez in the ring face to face. The two men would meet in an Ultimate Death Match. A match where the ring ropes were replaced with barbed wire, and a variety of weapons would be found in the ring, such as baseball bats, thumb tacks and bricks. The match would go down as one of the single most violent in the history of the UEW. No winner was declared, as neither man would answer a 10 count. However, Martinez had clearly won the war, as it was learned that Magnus Colby had suffered severe spinal injuries. In the fall of '98, Magnus Colby would retire from wrestling, and return to the UEW's front office as a Vice President. Martinez would go on to win both the American and Ultimate championships, having near record length streaks with each. Clearly, Martinez was the top star in the UEW from late-98 to mid-99. Meanwhile, Colby was done. Or was he? He was told by doctors, that he would never wrestle again. But, one morning in early '99, Magnus woke up pain-free. Think what you will, but Magnus claims to have been healed by the touch of God. When Magnus sold the UEW to John Joseph in early '99, he would re-enter active competition. After a few tune up victories, Magnus would enter the UEW's Rumble in the Bronx battle royal. The stipulations of this 50 man over the top rope battle royal are that the first 20 men eliminated, are banned from the UEW for life. The final 5 receive shots at each of the 5 UEW titles, with the winner getting an Ultimate title shot. Magnus would out last 49 other UEW stars to win the match, and with it, an Ultimate title shot. October 31, 1999, Magnus Colby steps into the ring with 5-time Ultimate champion "Top Dog" Rick Styles, Alex Martinez, and "Candyman" Damian Rose, in an elimination match. After Alex Martinez eliminated Rick Styles, Colby would pin Rose and then Martinez to become the Ultimate champion. In January of 2000, Magnus outlasted one of the strongest challenges of his life, pinning Damian Rose and retaining the Ultimate title. Unfortunately, the Widowmakers turned on Magnus, taking away some of his glory. Abandoned by the stable he created, Magnus once again found himself a fan favorite. He took the name "Peace Maker" and attempted to go it alone for one of the few times in his career. Unfortunately, a strong challenge from Trixxster proved to be too much and Magnus' title reign came to a screeching halt. After an unsuccessful attempt to regain the title at Ides of March, Magnus again took leave of the UEW ring to enter the UEW's front office. It is believed that Magnus completed a purchase of the UEW at this time, but it has never been substantiated. It should be noted that in January of 2000, that Magnus reportedly had some financial trouble. Insiders speculate that this simply reflected that Magnus had indeed purchased the UEW, but some still say that someone had embezzled quite a bit of money from one of Magnus' overseas ventures. At any rate, it appeared once again that Magnus' inring career was over... or was it? In the fall of 2000, a giant masked man entered the UEW under the name Arsenous. This giant would make quite a splash in a very limited time. He annihilated the UEW's Job Squad, and also raised quite a fuss as Ace of Hearts' Dream Partner in the UEW's annual Dream Partner Tournament. After helping Ace get by the team of Tracker and Ketiak, Arsenous assaulted his team mate, a sign of things to come. In the second round, numerous outside attacks would take their toll, threatening to derail the seemingly insurmoutable team of Ace and Arsenous... who suddenly were working together. The giant masked man turned to underhanded tactics in an attempt to even the odds, wielding a fireball and hurling at one of his opponents. Unfortunately, the opponent moved, and Ace was hit full on in the face. Ace would fall to defeat, and a war was started. In the months to comes, Ace and Arsenous would engage in a bloody feud, Ace of Hearts often drawing the worst of these encounters. At one point, Ace nearly lost his vision, but the youngster fought back and went on to defeat the behemoth at Holiday Horror. Along the way, Arsenous fought in a Wargames match, fighting with friends and foes alike, before submitting in controversial fation to supposed rival Alex Extreme. Arsenous would make enemies of such UEW luminaries as Ketiak, Tyrone Parker, Steve Douglas, Doc Holliday and Jason Keening. At Holiday Horror, Arsenous was revealed to be Magnus Colby in disguise. Also, the most unholy of alliances was formed as Magnus would team up with lifelong friend Alex Extreme in a stable for the first time. Magnus also reached into his past and plucked his first ever ally, Marcus Manson, out from under the Widowmakers. To round out the stable, Magnus turned to his new found associate Thomas J. Smith, who himself had a stable of young stars growing... Ace of Hearts and the Outer Dimensions. With a blend of upper echelon stars and up and comers, this new stable figures to be among the deadliest in UEW history.
