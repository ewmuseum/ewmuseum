An In Depth Psychological Profile -- by "World Famous" Doctor Victor Schwartz, MD.

Welcome to the second installment of my regular article in the WWO's Source magazine. Yes, I, Victor Schwartz, WWO's most successful (and only) psychiatrist, am here, brightening each and every one of you lovely reader's lives by tearing your heroes a new one.

This week, we examine a man of questionable temperature. That's right boys and girls, this week we look deep into the psyche of one--

--"TOO COOL" CHRIS HOPPER--

Amazing, no?

Overview: A brief statement, "pidgeonholing" the subject.

Silly. A classic nice guy who panders to the fans. Or is he? Deep beneath the surface of Chris Hopper there lays the fundamentals of a killer. You know that nice, quiet boy in the back of every classroom? Sure, he raises his hand, does his homework, takes his vitamins and says his prayers, but after school, where does he go?

Down to the Devil Temple to worship that horny guy in the red jumpsuit. No, not Santa Clause, SATAN CLAWS~! I mean, honestly, AC/DC? TNT? That's Devil music right there.

Breakdown: The subject's individual neuroses and (gasp!) psychoses are explored in more detail.

Penguin-o-phobia: A deep-rooted fear of penguins that rocks the very foundations of this retard's soul. Sure, they're cute, they're cuddly, but penguins are mean! Ever have one latch onto your nose? Hurts like a bitch. Hopper knows that even a small penguin could take him out in a heartbeat, so what he really needs is lots of iron in his diet to build muscle. For this disorder, I recommend red meat, and lots of it. The man needs to quit eating chicken. I mean, you are what you eat.

Murderous impulse: Unlike a simple murderous nature, the odd impulse is more difficult to deal with. Suddenly, a nice boy of questionable sexual orientation (EG: Chris Hopper) feels the need to stab a superior talent with an icepick (EG: William Craven). And why? Just to win a match. How sad is that? For this disorder, I'd recommend a straight jacket and regular beatings with a singapore cane. After six months of this, Hopper will be sleeping in a coffin at night and changing his name to Maurine. Guaranteed.

Obsession with cold: Aren't humans supposed to be warm-blooded animals? I know I am. I don't like cold. It's cold in New Jersey right now, which is why I'm glad I'm in a hotel on the WWO's dollar right now. Hopper believes himself to be "Too Cool", which is all well and good (until you realize that assholes are cool and nice guys are called, and try to keep up with me now, "geeks" nowadays. Hey, it's not my generation, friend. Remember, I'm old.), but Ice Breaker? Ice Bomb? Icy Icy Ice... Is it your fault my driveway's frozen over!? If so, for this disorder I recommend a stabbing with a snowshovel! I can't get my garage door open, you monster!

Too many finishing moves: Again, Ice Breaker, Ice Bomb, Cold Crusher, Frosty Chocolate Milkshake, you get the idea. This split-infinitive dilution of maneuvers keeps any one from really being believable as a match ender. His inability to focus on a single finisher leads me to believe that Hopper suffers from ADD (Attention Defecit Disorder) and could benefit from a prescription of Ritalin. Nah, on second thought, just beat him with a sack full of doorknobs. Why be serious now?

Stupidity: Whoops, did I say that? Where's the backspace key on this new-fangled computer thingie?

Diagnosis: A closing statement.

A lack of intelligence, fear of fluffy arctic birds, and violent moodswings would get most people labelled "Sling Blade" back in my day, but now, all we can do is throw some prozac Chris Hopper's way and hope for the best. With any luck, the drugged-up Hopper will feel a little warmer, stop calling everything cold, and start coming to the ring to some actual _good_ theme music. I'm thinking, maybe, some Beethoven. Mozart. Something along those lines. Until next time, this is your "World Famous" Doctor Victor Schwartz, wishing you all good mental health.
