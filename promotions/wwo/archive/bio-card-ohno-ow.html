WWO bio card — "Dr." Ohno Ow

27 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Dr." Ohno Ow Height: 5'11" Weight: 221 lbs. Alignment: Selfish Hometown: The Kevorkian Institute Of Painless Medicine Entry Music: None... Just irritating Ambulance Sirens Phsyical Appearance Looks: Ohno, is a Chinese man, who appears to be at least nearing middle aged. His facial feature appear somewhat worn, and he has hints of gray hear and there in his hair. His most distinguishing facial feature, is the scar through his right eye, which can just be seen at the edges of the eyepatch he wears. Despite his apparent age, he seems to be in excellent physical condition, with a small but very well chiseled, muscualr frame. Clothing: Ohno wears white, full length tights with a the red, snake staff symbol of the mecial profession both legs. He also wears a loose fitting white shirt, that looks like the one he would wear to surgery. Ohno has a black eyepatch over his right eye. He also wears black boots, and a pair of surgical gloves that he wears at all times. (Yes... ALL TIMES.) Biographical Information Dr. Ohno Ow, is originally from some obscure village in southern China. He was a surgeon in the service of the Communist Army in China. Having accompanied the army into hostile territories before (no one is quite sure how old he is) he learned martial arts so as to defend himself when drawn into conflicts, like the one where he lost his right eye. He fled China after he failed to meet up to the proper health standards for medical practices in that county, and was both figuratively, and literally run out of the country. (Yes, he had to swim out and catch a boat.) Eventually he ended up in Auburn, New York, where his medical "expertise" landed him a job at the Kevorkian Institute for Painless Medicine. He is currently the head of Pain Management at Dr. Mal's Institute. Wrestling Information Ohno practices an unorthodox wrestling style that has a heavy leaning towards martial arts strikes, throws, and holds. He is very quick, and surprizingly storng, but due to his missing right eye, has a little trouble with depth perception, making high flying moves very inaccurate and throwing off his timing when opponents attempt the same. While he is fully capable of pulling off aerail moves when in full contact with his opponent throughout the move, (like his finisher "Anasthesia") he misses moves like missile dropkicks, moonsaults, etc. about 75% of the time. (He's agile enough to do all the twists and flips, but just can't quite seem to hit.) When he gets carried away, which happens a lot with his short temper, he forgets about this problem, much to the frustration of his tag partner/partner in cri... I mean fellow humanitarian Dr. Mal. Aside from this, however, he is very effective in the ring, being both quick, and strong, with knowledge of various martial arts escapes that come in handy in the ring. People just can't seem to keep a hold of him. Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Poor High-Flier Poor Power Good Technical Fair Submission Good Cheating Very Good Brawling/Hardcore Fair Martial Arts Very Good Escapability Very Good Stamina Fair Ring Awareness Fair Teamwork Good Top Wrestling Moves Gut-wrench Brainbuster "CPR" (repeated palm strikes to the heart of a downed opponent... yes like repeated heart punches) "Neck Brace" (Dr. Ow, snap suplexes his opponent into a neckbreaker) Thrusting Sidekick (to the groin) Half-Nelso Suplex Palm Strike/ Spinning Back Fist/ Revers-Sidekick to the Groin/ Axe-Kick Combo Spinning Neckbreaker Stun Gunv Full Nelson Clutch (Locks a full Nelson on prone opponent, then leans back like a camel clutch) Spinebuster Finishing Moves "Anasthesia" Dr. Ow grabs his opponents head from behind as if for a reverse DDT, but instead jumps and flips over his opponent, while holding on to his opponents head, and hits a flip-over stunner. (This move can be done from the top-turnbuckle as well, as it is used in "The Cure". (See Tag Team Finisher) "Morphine" Ohno jumps up onto his opponents shoulders from behind, and hits a reverse hurranrana. He holds onto his opponents head with his legs, and then grabs the legs of his opponent, leaning forward. This bends his opponent's back the wrong way, and looks and in fact IS very painful for Ohno's opponent.) Tag Team Information Team Name: Physicians Advocating Innovative Neo-procedures (P.A.I.N.) Tag Team Partner: Dr. Mal Practice, MD Tag Team Moves Double Swinging Neckbreaker. Ohno actually leaps over Mal's back with this maneuver. Doctor's Orders - Mal lifts the opponent up for a back suplex, and Ohno catches the head of the opponent, executing a Reverse Neckbreaker along with the Back Suplex. Shock Therapy - Mal hoists the opponent over his knee after a side backbreaker. Dr. Ow launches a vicious Scissors Kick to the face of the opponent, flipping him off the knee and onto his face. Mal holds onto one of the guy's wrists as he flips, and crosses over into a crossface submission (Crippler Crossface) immediately. Ohno then turns, and smacks the guy's face and head with repeated hard thrust kicks, making the hold even worse. Defribluator - Dr. Ow hammers the opponent in the face with a rushing roundhouse kick, sending him snapping back into Dr. Mal, who snatches him with a reverse tilt-a-whirl suplex. Ow does not stop running, as he bounces off the far rope with a big windup kick to the head of the downed opponent. Traction - Dr. Ow places an opponent on the top rope, grabs his arm and head, and executes a martial arts shoulderthrow on the opponent off the ropes at Mal. Mal catches the upside down opponent and piledrives him out of the air. Finishing Moves The Cure - Dr. Mal lifts his opponent up onto his shoulders in a seated position, and stands in the corner facing out. Dr. Ow climbs to the top rope behind him, and hooks his head under his arm, as if for a reverse DDT. Ow executes a front flip Stunner (his finisher, Anathesia) off of Mal's shoulders. This is not the end of it, however, as Mal keeps ahold of the opponents' legs. He immediately throws the opponent back over his head in a Release> Wheelbarrow Suplex... into the turnbuckles. The back of the opponent's head will impact the top of the ringpost, and he'll fall into the ring, VERY likely unconscious.
