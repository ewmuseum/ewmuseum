WWO bio card — The Spectre

28 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Spectre Real Name: Unknown Height: 6'4 Weight: 270 Alignment: Diabolic Hometown: NYC, NY Entrance Music: "The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson Manager: N/A Psychological Makeup What IS known is that The Spectre is a certified Sociopathic Sado-Masochist. He flat out enjoys suffering, whether in others, or himself. And physical suffering is only part of it. One of the things he most consistently claims is that he exists for one thing only...to tear down those heroes that the fans love...to lay low the objects of their affection. Strengths Masochist: - The Spectre is capable of absorbing damage that would leave other men lying writhing in pain and come back for more. On top of that, he'll never tap out to a submission hold (but he is capable of being caused to pass out from the pain...he'll just like it.) Demon in a brawl: - When it comes to trading shots and just roughing it up, The Spectre will very rarely come out second best in a brawl. Finishers that terminate: - Once The Spectre lands his finisher (or the tag team finisher for The Kai'Lin Collective) you just don't get up again. Weaknesses Sadist: - The Spectre likes to hurt people...a lot. Due to this fact, he will often not go for a cover, or will release a successful submisson hold in order to injure his opponent more. Flys like a brick: - The Spectre has no business trying aerial moves. He never actually lands his aerial moves. That being said, quite often, The Spectre will try an aerial move. Prone to DQ: - Referees hate to be ignored. They hate to be hit even more than being ignored. As The Spectre doesn't care about any of this, he gets disqualified quite a bit. Biographical Information Not much is known of The Spectre's past. He has obviously competed in the martial arena in other places, but nothing concrete has been determined at this time. Whispered rumors abound of him competeing in the underground shoot fighting circut in such places as New York, Miami, and Mexico City, but to date no one has been able to unearth any records of this. His association with Dehan Annubis is as much of a mystery as anything else...only time will tell what this macabre man is really about... Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier None Martial Arts Poor Power Very Good Technical Excellent Submission Fair Cheating Good Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability Poor Stamina Very Good Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Very Good Top Wrestling Moves Destiny's Grip: Katahaijime (aka. Tazmission) Destiny Dropper: Inverted Rocker Dropper Guillotine Neckbreaker: Inverted fireman's carry into a reverse neckbreaker Fata Morgana: Fireman's Carry lift into an Inverted DDT Soul's Fate:Combination of punches into a backfist strike-Diamond Cutter combo Twisting Jumping Splash into the corner (ala Raven) Bite and chew Dragon Sleeper into an Inverted DDT Gordbuster across the top rope Hot shot onto turnbuckle, or guardrail, or ring steps, or announce table etc. Finishing Maneuver The Rebirth: Hook opponent for a fisherman suplex, lift him up, and when he reaches the apex of the suplex, sit down and piledrive the hell out of him in one smooth motion. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Kai'Lin Collective Tag Team Partner: "The Egyptian Enigma" Dehan Annubis Tag Team Moves Perdition: Double whip to ropes followed by a Double boot to midsection, and as Anubis propels himself backwards to the ropes, Spectre moves behind their prey and synches in a Full Nelson hold, which is followed by an Anubis Running Front Thrust Kick to prey's face that adds extra momentum to the Spectre Dragon Suplex. Blood Sacrifice: Double whip to ropes --->Spectre dropdown, and as foe steps over, he runs headfirst into a debilitating Anubis Earringer --->Spectre's Dragon Sleeper into an Inverted DDT. Sandwich Spear Tackles: Spectre hits high from the front, while Dehan hits low *legs* from the back at the very same instance. Wrath of the Heavens: Spectre places the prey in a Dragon Sleeper facing the corner, Annubis climbs to the top rope and hits a top rope heart puch on the prey's exposed chest. Curse of the Gods: A Spectre Shoulder Mount Crossed Arm Suplex---> an Anubis Bulldog Lariat from the top rope. Finishing Move Doomsday: - Dehan brings the victim up for a powerbomb, but instead of smashing the opponent forward, Dehan propels himself backwards as the victim sits on his shoulders, while Spectre catches the opponent in a 3/4 facelock bulldog (a stone cold stunner).
