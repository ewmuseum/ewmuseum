Managers of the WWO Charlie Real Name: Charlie Platt Agent for: Apex Background: Charlie is 5'5" and around 120 lbs. of bum. He's in his early 60's, but appears to be in his late 70's. The top of his head is bald and wrinkly, while the back and side are thinly covered with long greasy white hair. His arms are extremely bony, especially at the elbows. His fingernails are cracked and permanently a light tent of yellow, from his niccotine habit. His sternum is sunken and most of his ribs are easily visible, even through a shirt. His legs are equally weak and feeble. Charlie, a former street bum, typically dresses as such. He almost always wears the same dirty old green trench coat, and nobody is really sure how often he wears anything underneath it. When he does wear something underneath it, it's usually old and torn, as if to keep the worn down look at all times. Glen Peeps Manager for: Problem Child Chris Stringer Background: Peeps, who is six months older than Stringer, was a fellow student at Steve Starr's wrestling school. Peeps is about 5'10", 185 lbs., nothing special physically or athletically. Caucasian, short dark brown hair, brown eyes. Peeps has developed into Stringer's number one lackey. He's the guy who takes the vicious bumps intended for Stringer and is constantly putting himself in harm's way for the benefit of Stringer's plans. Very early in his wrestling training, he realized he would never make it in the business unless he latched onto a star and rode his coat-tails to a comfortable living. He believes he's found that star in Chris Stringer, and shows faith in Stringer that borders on blind loyalty. Stringer, for his part, generally disparages his less athletic and less intelligent friend, which Peeps handles in much the same way Ed McMahon handled Johnny Carson's insults. John Collins Birthdate: 2/2/76 Height: 6'1" Weight: 150 lbs. Hometown: Amherst, Virginia Theme Music: "All Star" by Smashmouth Agent/Reporter for Dark Soul John Collins grew up as Dark Soul's best friend. He left his job as a computer programmer when Dark Soul asked for his help. Since his WWO tenure began, his main job has been to keep Dark Soul happy and focused, not easily distracted on inflicting pain as he once was. He has been a good influence on Dark Soul in keeping the wrestler on the right track toward success both in and out of the ring. In addition to serving as an advisor and reporter for Dark Soul, Collins also is one of three agents for Dark Soul and Dark Soul's sole accountant. Collins had his first bit of wrestling action at the WWO Mega-Double Pay-Per-View, Prelude to the War and Inquisition. He fought in the Battle Royal that decided the new Champion of the Americas, eventually won by Papa Legba. Often, Collins plays a role around the ring of taking a hit or trying to distract Dark Soul's opponent if he feels it will help Dark Soul out. However, for the most part, he stays out of the match and plays the role of keeping Dark Soul focused. Collins infamously never changes his attire. Almost always, he wears a white polo shirt and a pair of khaki's. Candy Kisses Valet for: Ace of Hearts Real Name: Unknown Appearance: Candy is a very seductive woman. Her beauty is just as breathtaking as her quick wit and ferocious desires. Profile: Candy Kisses is the epitome of the perfect woman. She is soft spoken and very seductive. She has a awesome body that every man with a heartbeat desires. Yet she is any woman's nightmare. As she can be quite protective of her man. Candy has a definite fetish for lingerie and sexually revealing outfits. She is as gentle as can be but can go from zero to bitch in 1.2 seconds flat if provoked. She is quite capable of taking care of herself and has shown so on various occasions. Her great looks and analytical mind gives her the ability to out-think her opponents. She analyzes the opposition leaving nothing to chance. Then swiftly and efficiently executes the perfect gameplan. taking her opponents as well as Ace's by complete surprise. Having been trained in the sport and being a past champion. She is definitely not to be taken lightly. History: Candy worked for the Canadian Woman's Championship Wrestling federation until they closed in late 1997. It is there, where she won the CWCW tag team championship belts with her partner and sister in-law, Isis. Early in 1998 she met Ace and fell desperately in love. The two were married in October of 1998 as Ace was in training and looking for his future federation. Candy moved to Las Vegas with her husband and became his Valet when they both entered the GCWA. She followed Ace through the AWR, GCWO, and the UEW and is prepared to support her man here in the UWF. Putting her personal career goals aside to help lead her man to a championship title reign, she will stop at nothing to help him attain his goals. Sweet Melissa REAL NAME: Melissa Pulford BIRTHDATE: 10/11/74 Valet/Manager for: Ultimate Thrasher Known by some as "The First Lady of the WWO" and by others as "That Damn Bitch", Sweet Melissa is the brains and beauty behind Ultimate Thrasher's brawn. Standing 5'6 and weighing 120 pounds, this gorgeous blue-eyed brunette believes in a fair fight but will not hesitate to give her man a hand if he needs it, especially by spraying the opponent with a mysterious chemical known as the "Minnesota Myst" which causes temporary blindness. Really, though, she'd rather restrict herself to living in the fast lane and tormenting other managers of the WWO, especially women and Todd "The Rod" Johnstone. A native of Tierra del Fuego, but raised in Thunder Bay, Ontario, Melissa grew up well-educated in an upper middle-class household and graduated from the University of Winnipeg with a degree in musical theory before becoming vocalist/keyboardist in a Lake Superior-area progressive metal band which included guitarist Steve Hanneman. The band failed to make the big time in the wussified musical climate of the mid-90's, but the romance which blossomed between her and Hanneman did not flame out (incidentally, neither will comment on whether or not they are actually married). When her man was signed to a tryout contract by the WWO, Melissa eagerly followed along as his manager, and the rest is history. Melissa handles herself decently physically and is not a coward, but she's no wrestler. Her typical ring attire consists of a tight white #69 Minnesota Vikings road jersey, low-cut black shorts, and red go-go boots. Dr. Victor Schwartz Real Name: Dr. Victor Schwartz. Manager for: William Craven Background: The man formerly known as General Mayhem, "World Famous" Dr Victor Schwartz, still maintains his puppeteer-like control over William Craven, even now that the secret of Major Damage's identity is out. Always having been a rabid fan of wrestling, even participating in some activities in the now-defunct Atlantic Coast Wrestling Alliance, Schwartz quickly took advantage of his patient's fragile state to get himself back into the world of wrestling. He is not just a manager in front of the camera, but behind the scenes as well, acting as his man's eyes and ears, and guiding his career. Candy Malone Birthdate: "A woman who tells her age will tell anything." Height: 6'2" Weight: "HA! As if you would get that from me!" Hometown: (She refuses to let this be known, but we know she has a good portion of family in Canada.) Joined the WWO and has since turned the fed upside down to her liking. Playing the perfect mix of the fed's other two incredibly popular bombshells, Melissa and Cherry Bomb, she plays the perfect bitch and yet the perfect friend. Ms. Malone (as she's referred to as) was partnered up with Dark Soul and John Collins to give the two a kick into the right direction. Fans couldn't understand Dark Soul for the most part, but since Ms. Malone's joining, the three have been a hit with the fans and a hit in the ring. It's often thought that Ms. Malone has saved Dark Soul's lagging career, and while that might be true, Ms. Malone doesn't buy it. She stands behind the thought that Dark Soul just needed a push into the right direction and along with Collins, a push was created. She also believes that not one person in the WWO gives more each match than Dark Soul and that work ethic may be enough to put him over the top. Outspoken, God help those who get on her bad side. Her verbal wars with Dark Soul are well documented, though the two always reconcile. Rumors of a relationship more than business are always present, though neither confirms (nor declines) such rumors. Ms. Malone came in and was immediately the target of Chris Stringer's affections. However, she thinks even less of Stringer than Dark Soul, possibly angering Stringer, but the man seems to have moved on. Candy Malone is the classic blonde beauty with all the curves in the right places. She does let her measurements known, but one would be an idiot her body anything less than a body for Baywatch, or a classy porn movie. Unlike others, however, she uses those looks to get her way by whatever formula she chooses, never falling to someone else's game. She wears tight clothes when in the arena, or on occasion, a fabulous gown or dress, but is more likely to wear jeans and a bulky shirt while outside the arena. As for her personality, Malone has a sarcastic demeanor and can kid around with the boys. She does not let anything past her and can be as ruthless as needed. She still likes to do things the "right" way, or not breaking rules, but she wants to win more. Malone looks at this Dark Soul assignment as a way to make a name for herself and maybe show that women can be as knowing of the business as men. Time will tell if that thought is correct in her case. Cherry Bomb Real Name: Shanna McDonough Valet for: Problem Child Chris Stringer Background: 5'7", 125 lbs. of Irish, redheaded, green-eyed, fiery 38D-24-34 female. Although this 23-year-old beauty is not above using her sex appeal to help Stringer, or get her own way, she is more than just a pretty face. Very intelligent in her own right, and with average athleticism for a female. Stringer generally counts on her to carry out the key parts of his devious plans, as she has good "street smarts" and can improvise when a plan breaks down. A schoolmate of Stringer during his brief time in college, there are rumors that their relationship goes beyond business, and that this has caused some tension between the two. Most of these rumors are discounted, however, as Stringer is generally considered the type of man who will never be happy with just one woman, and Cherry is considered too intelligent to not realize that. Rimshot Manager for: Lester Leary and Larry Lowbrow Rimshot is a legless man in a wheelchair with a drumset attached. Wears a white suit with a black shirt, has sunglasses and is never seen without an unlit cigarette dangling out of the corner of his mouth. Not averse to throwing a drumstick into the ring to be used as a foreign object. While usually heels, it's a rule of thumb that beating on Rimshot is an act of serious heelism considering he's crippled. Rimshot was born in Cleveland, and found each of his charges in different casino cities. He saw potential in all of them and brought them to wrestling. Since then they were exiled from Vegas until they win belts. And so they wait to regain past glory. Todd realized that, with the success of women like Sweet Melissa and Candy Malone in the WWO, his new super-stable, The Upper Crust, could do well to have as much tits and ass in it's ranks as humanly possible. A few calls to the old GLCW offices later, and he was able to sign Moongirl on board the group. Of course, Moon knows who Tracy Hudson is...and really REALLY dislikes him. But hey, it's a job. And it pays better than GLCW did, so why should she complain?
