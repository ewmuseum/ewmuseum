WWO Scouting Report, Part 2

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

With this issue, I'll report on everyone from Nate Knowitall to the Ultimate Thrasher. Reminder to newbies like Dwayne Rogers and the guy with the weird Russian name, I have nothing to base an opinion on so I'll pass. I might add a fifth part (part 3 = tag teams, part 4 = other personalities, valets, and more) for the newbies. Maybe.

Nate Knowitall

What did he do in 2001? Was an early favorite behind the scenes when he showed up, but started off extremely slow. Seemed bored or simply didn't care, but either way, his early feud with Latex Lex had no heat (course, Lex had no heat) and then he took part in the heatsucking Greatness Personified. Only recently with a feud with Miguel Quesada and part of yet another stable, the Upper Crust, has he gained a significant role on WWO television.

What to expect in 2002? Knowitall right now is overshadowed by the people he hangs out with, but that could change. Knowitall will by year's end be one of the most hated heels in the WWO.

Latex Lex

What did he do in 2001? Hmm...got freaky with Glen Peeps and had people like Tracy Hudson and Apex put on the latex in his honor...I guess.

What to expect in 2002? How is this guy getting a paycheck? What dirt does he have on Stone?

Papa Legba

What did he do in 2001? Entered quietly shortly before Inquisition and shocked the WWO by winning the Battle Royal to decide the newly created Title of the America's. Then began a positive feud with Eric Stryker in which he would become victorious in the blowoff. And now, feuding with Alex Extreme. If it wasn't for the O:SE members, could be a frontrunner for the Rookie award in the WWO.

What to expect in 2002? To be perfectly honest, I see a work in progress with Papa Legba. He's not a legitimate heel or face in my opinion. He just swings to whatever side he's feuding with, very much like his good buddies, the Amazing Romanis. My feeling there is unless he sticks to one side, he'll be in the mid-card for awhile. Now, if he can establish himself as a face or as a heel, I see great things. Maybe in the IP race by this time next year.

Lion Tamer

What did he do in 2001? Lion Tamer is the heel that comes over in a face way. He came in by speaking bad of Apex and Dark Soul but soon forget both as he bumped into Steve Dumars. That began legal proceedings and one of the better second cycle feuds in the league finishing at the Showcase following Anarchy.

What to expect in 2002? Can he really be taken seriously? If he can, I project he'll be in the Title of America's chase within a few months.

"Showtime" Rich Marley

What did he do in 2001? He was one of the UEWers to come into the WWO who remained after Anarchy. Not much else to say.

What to expect in 2002? The UEWers are hit or miss so far. Hitting are Alex Extreme and Chris Hopper. Missing is pretty much everyone so far. No one doubts their talent, they just haven't had the time to make a big enough impact just yet. So, I'm reserving my opinion for some time.

Midnight Assassin

What did he do in 2001? Debuted quietly by sending a message to Dark Soul that their feud was over. Dark Soul countered by humoring him some but mostly ignoring him. He repeatedly seemed to not care about what was taken place in the WWO, choosing not to feud and the closest thing he had of a feud with The Choof ended at Inquisition as Choof left the WWO. Made many WWO fans irritated with his longwinded promos.

What to expect in 2002? Many times, we have thought that he would be on the outs. And then he somehow ended up not being on the outs. Who knows? His only victory came in a trios match so I doubt he'll be part of the WWO come April. But I have been wrong before.

"Trixxster" Marvin Patch

What did he do in 2001? The Trixxster returned at Anarchy and ruined the career of Totally 80's co-founder and current WWO columnist Tony Antony. He then tried to do the same to Midnight Assassin.

What to expect in 2002? I admit it. I'm a Patch mark. Looking into my crystal ball of booking, if he keeps his current track, look for him to be feuding with the big boys by next summer.

"Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada

What did he do in 2001? Returned to the WWO last January and established a face style by helping Dark Soul against Chris Stringer and costing Steve Starr a singles encounter against Johnny Detson. He tagged with his brother before putting the first notch in the Chris Stringer loss column. In fact, the Quesada family share the only two loses Stringer has suffered. He then tagged with Dark Soul making the semi-finals before falling to future stablemates Vegas Connection. He fought Stringer yet again in one of the best technical matchups in WWO history at Inquisition. Joining with Dark Soul the next Showcase against Stringer and William Craven, he turned on DS and brought DS's sister Stacy into the WWO. After a couple of weeks of serious heeldom, his stock fell rapidly and his feud with DS turned into Greatness Personified vs. Team Dark Soul, a far cry from what it started as. After losing the War Games match at Anarchy, Quesada was attacked by stablemate Nate Knowitall, beginning Quesada vs. Knowitall.

What to expect in 2002? As long as he stays face, he has a chance at coming off very well in 2002. I just don't think he has enough evil in him to be a great heel. A good one, maybe. He does have the charisma to be a great face, though. And when he's on, he has the whole crowd loving him.

Chris Stringer

What did he do in 2001? Took part in what was the best match of the early going stages in Stringer/DS to make a name for himself. Dispatched Steve Starr after Starr indivertibly cost him to lose against Miguel Quesada. Replaced him with Major Damage. Won the WWO CruiserWeight Title tournament and at Inquisition, had the best night this side of Tyrone Hayes. Showed the world that Damage was really William Craven and then defeated Quesada to become the WWO International People's Champion. After a Andrew Tucker/Ethan Love/Stringer feud fell through, made Matt Cole a household name by feuding with him and eventually winning the blowoff match. Now, involved in a feud with Apex and Johnny Detson.

What to expect in 2002? If this guy doesn't win the World Title, something tragic happened. Well, maybe not that far, but if he goes over Detson and Apex, he might put some stack to being one of the better IP Champions of all time. With friends like William Craven and Cherry Bomb, can we say this guy won't be in our faces if he chooses to be?

"Millionaire Marval" Eric Stryker

What did he do in 2001? One of the hardest workers since January, Stryker is a slowly rising prospect. Began the year without anyone to really feud with, something he was rewarded for by making the semifinals in the WWO World Title tournament, mentioned in the same sentence as Tyrone Hayes, Ultimate Thrasher, and Apex. Following Inquisition, found himself involved in another title chase, the Title of America's. Just fell short of that title and now is involved in a confrontation with Chris Hopper.

What to expect in 2002? More improvement. Those he can't buy off, he will soon be able to take down with his ability alone.

Ultimate Thrasher

What did he do in 2001? One of the marquee names returning to the WWO, Thrasher had a largely disappointing first half of the year. Joining forces with Johnny Detson against Tyrone Hayes kept him from being a main event face or heel and then Detson's disappearance put him limbo. With a bogus record contract scrapped, Thrasher turned back to the face side and got set for another run at the World Title. At Inquisition, he fought what could be the WWO Match of the Year with Tyrone Hayes. Shortly following Inquisition, he became enamored in one of the best feuds of 2001 (and into 2002, apparently) with William Craven. By 2001's end, he was on his last wit after losing Melissa to Craven.

What to expect in 2002? Rumors persist that Thrasher has fell apart and had disappeared into God-knows-where, only showing up to WWO events just to show up. I really don't know what to say but Thrasher needs to get everything working back in that head of his. He's just too valuable.
