WWO Card Archive

Donald McClamrock - Showcase Review

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

Okay, Hammond is attending to some personal issues, so guess what? You've got you're favorite WWO intern reviewing Showcase again!

A couple of things, first, lemme step OOC for a second....

-- The match ratings aren't meant to denigrate the match writers, or the handlers involved in the matches. The ratings are a combination of match writing, how much "heat" the guys have, how close the angles involved are to the "top of the card," etc. I do try to be as fair as possible with the ratings, but hey, I'm new at this. :) But even if a match you wrote or were involved with gets a DUD rating, it doesn't mean that the work you put in is not appreciated. Also, all other comments are written _in character_ from a "smark" perspective, although most of the time you can probably accurately read them as an amplification of my opinion. Okay, now back into character...

-- It's been brought to my attention that maybe people might like to give me some feedback on these reviews. Fair enough. If you have any comments, praises, flames, bribes, phone numbers of hot chicks, etc., you'd like to pass along, send them to [detail withheld]. (Read "Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas" to explain the nick.)

-- Oh, and what theme music would I choose for Ace of Hearts? Well, "I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said Fred would be the cliche choice, although I'd plug for "I Want You To Want Me" by Letters to Cleo, or maybe "The Power" by Snap or something that plays into a power-style wrestler.

-- We open Showcase with a rather nicely done video package of the Dark Soul-Tracy Hudson feud. Loser leaves the WWO match tonight, which is just _SCREAMING_ for a screwjob finish.

-- We're at the IIWF Arena in Portland, Oregon. Your hosts, as always, are Mickey and Sam. Once, just once, I'd like to see The Evil Masked Broadcaster do a guest Showcase.

-- Some shilling for the main event, then we cut to outside, to see Lion Tamer arriving, and looking for all the world like a pretty-boy heel. Apparently, he's upset at having to fight Marley.

-- And cue Johnny Detson, and for the first time in a long time, a Showcase doesn't start with KTFH, although Sam is pleased all the same and the crowd is still hot as hell. Detson comes down to the ring, and says he thinks he _may_ have been too hard on [name withheld]. Because it's usually not a good idea to brain your boss with a steel chair. So he put together a tribute video for [name withheld], set to "Hero" by Bette Midler... which lasts all of about 20 seconds until Detson is laughing so hard he has to have the video cut off, and replaced with a video of [name withheld] getting beat down, set to an uproarious parody of "Bang the Drum All Day." But someone cuts it prematurely... and that someone is....

-- APEX! A few backhanded compliments for Detson, then says he thinks Detson is a selfish prick because he was born that way. And, because Detson cost Apex the World Title, Apex basically says he's going to make Johnny's life hell. Yes, he's going to make him watch WIF videotapes. Oh, no, wait, he just says he's going to follow Detson all over the place and get back at him. Apex is about to leave, but then Detson starts insulting him, causing Apex to turn back around. And while those two are jawing in the ring, two random non-union construction guys build an f'n _podium_ in front of the entranceway. And out comes Luke Morningstar, and out comes Glen Peeps, and I'm thinking, "Hey guys? Shouldn't we end one segment before we start the next one?"

-- The P.R. guy announces that this is the "Liars' Club" roast, and the guest of honor is "The Pride of Portland" Chris Stringer. And Cherry Bomb is the roastmaster, and I wish all chicks that were going to formal occasions wore that get-up. Cherry gets some cheap heat by ripping on the Trail Blazers and rips on Stringer for a bit. By the way, Apex and Detson are just looking totally confused in the ring, and no one up at the entranceway is paying any attention to them. Then Luke gets in a few digs at Stringer, Glen is going to read from a huge loose-leaf binder, but Cherry stops him after one word. Poor Glen. One of these days, he's just going to snap and sacrifice a nursery school class or something. And just as Cherry is about to present Chris with a plaque proclaiming him the greatest IP champ of all-time, Detson buts in. Cherry takes exception, Detson and Stringer trade verbal jousts, and Stringer almost gets sent to hell in a handbasket for mentioning the "name," and long story short, we've got Apex-Detson for the #1 contender to the IP title tonight. Of course, seeing as Detson was also insulting Apex -- while somehow forgetting he was _in_the_ring_ with Apex -- Apex goes off and throws Detson to the floor, then challenges Stringer to get into the ring, which Stringer wants nothing to do with.

-- Okay, you know what, the segments have all been good so far, but I could use a match right about now.

-- And damned if we don't go to another backstage segment. Rick Marley's door, in specific. Lion Tamer and Tiger Lily are walking by, slipping $20 bills to passerby so they'll root for him tonight. Tamer then uses his "psychic powers" to get Marley to open his door. Tamer tries to make up with Marley, and Marley gets off the line of the night so far --"How the hell did I end up in the right place for toilet plunger attack?" The good feeling only lasts so long, as Marley gets blindsided by a masked man with a plunger, and Tamer leaves him a T-shirt for his trouble. Funny segment, when Tamer is on, he can be one of the most entertaining heels in the WWO. And Marley held his own in it, I've got a feeling this is going to be a great feud.

-- Singles match: The Guardian w/Gentleman Joe Littrell vs. Magnus Colby w/J.T. Smith and two pairs of tits. And we've got a match!!! YES!!! And do not stare directly into Calhoun's jacket! My God, that's hideous. Guardian comes out to a good pop for a debut match, and out comes Magnus Colby to "Sirius" by the Alan Parsons Project (bet you didn't know that was the name of the music the Chicago Bulls use for their intros!), which morphs into Queen's "I Want It All" ... with... oh my God here it comes.... GRAND MAL SEIZURE!!!! AAAAAAHHHHH!!!! You know, I really expected better of Colby than to have him come out to a video wall graphic telling about all these titles he's won in all these federations that the WWO fans give exactly two flying shits about. Yeah, we get the point. You're a big "name" in the business. I mean, seriously, his Anarchy run-in and his promos established him enough that he doesn't need to pull that bullshit with his intro. And really, someone in the WWO's video-editing department should have killed this intro and recorded a new one. Not to mention the fact that this intro is totally incongruous with Colby's promo from last Showcase, where he took Ace and the other ex-UEWers to task for living in the past and off glories from other feds. Is it too much to ask for just a _little_ continuity? Okay, I'll get off my soapbox now. Colby also comes out with a manager, which, to my knowledge, is the first time we've ever even heard his manager alluded to, much less seen, as well as two bimbos, who aren't even given names.

-- And the match starts with the Guardian _saluting_ Colby, then offering a handshake. Apparently, the Guardian is going to be the WWO's new "Boy Scout" face... literally. Sam, of course, is apoplectic about all this, until Colby takes the handshake and turns it into a short clothesline. Okay, now I'm totally confused. At this point, is Colby supposed to be a face or a heel? Brawling from Colby, but Guardian ducks a punch and takes him down with a legsweep and drops the elbow, then puts on a camel clutch, which Colby _stands up_ out of and breaks by rolling snake-eyes on the Guardian. More brawling, then a belly-to-belly by Colby. Damn, Colby's fuckin' _strong_. Full nelson by Colby, and he tries to turn it into a Dragon Suplex, but Guardian blocks it three times before catching Colby with a surprise roll-up for 1 1/2. Guardian then reverses a clothesline into a backslide for 2. Colby fights back with a back elbow, then lifts up Guardian in a Gorilla Press and lets him drop. Ouchie. Colby goes to pick up the Guardian, but the Guardian catches him with a surprise small package for... 3? Yes, 3. Maybe a _bit_ short, and a weird mix of styles, but they made it work, and the finish won't harm Colby's credibility. Wasn't bad for an opener. Rating -- *1/2. Too short to really go much higher than that. Both guys shake hands after the match, sending Sam into an epileptic fit.

-- Blood Brothers arrive, and cut a promo on the weirdness in the tag team division, and how they plan to take advantage of it. Decent stuff.

-- Singles match: "20 Quid Killer" Nathaniel Knowitall w/Shelly vs. Jonas King. Sam starts in with the homosexual innuendo early tonight, and Mickey retorts with some masturbation innuendo that goes right over Sam's head. Kick, kneelift, DDT from Nate to start, and Sam finally figures out Mickey was insulting him. Mudhole stomping from Nate as he yells trash to Miguel loud enough for the camera to hear. As an aside, what is the English translation for "a wee poof?" Irish whip from Knowitall, but King reverses and hits a lariat. Apparently, if you're from Texas, it's a lariat. From anywhere else, it's a clothesline. King headbutts Nate, then goes for the pin and gets 1 1/2. Mickey: You know, King's only 39. Sam: What is that in dog years? Hi-larious. Nate beautifully counters a short lariat with a German suplex, but eschews the pin in favor of more mudhole stomping, then Hotshots him into the corner and follows it up with a Northern Lights SUPERPLEX. Surprised Nate didn't break his own neck with that one. Pin gets about 2.2. Nate and King go toe-to-toe for a second, but King gets the advantage then takes Nate down for 1. King blocks a cheap shot and locks in the sleeperhold, which is his finisher. Nate tries to break it by falling back into the corner, but King holds on, before Nate finally breaks it with a jawbreaker. Powerslam by Nate into a modified Camel Clutch, then lets go and hits the BCL, then hits Straight Outta Wal-Mart to end it. Not bad for what was basically a squash, both men worked it pretty hard. Rating -- **.

-- And out of the crowd comes... more UEW guys! Be still my bleeding heart. It would be nice if Mick would stop informing us when guys are from UEW, seeing as the fed's dead and the Invasion angle (which, in my opinion, ended up sucking a big fatty, because by the end of it you had almost 50% turnover of the people involved in it. CONTINUITY, people!) is dead. Specifically, it's Xaiver Feyr, who grabs a chair and kills Jonas King, kills Henri Cordon, then bulldogs King _throat-first_ on a standing steel chair, which brings out Matt Cole to save the day and cut a _great_ old-school hardass face promo, saving most of his venom for Ace of Hearts. Loved the promo, Matt, but please, stop dancing. You have all the rhythm of a white boy from Mississippi. I've only seen Xaiver Feyr live once, during the Gauntlet match at WTR2, and I wasn't all that impressed, but if this is setting up Cole/Feyr, well, that feud should be one hell of a bloodbath, if nothing else. And if Cole can kick Ace's ass in the process, well, so much the better.

-- Mackey Jay catches up with Apex backstage, who promises Alex Extreme that he hasn't forgotten about him, and he's going to beat Detson and take Stringer's title. And maybe even Cherry, for old time's sake.

-- Tag Team Match: Amazing Romanis vs. Final Justice w/Valerie Dougan-Barney. And your special guest commentator is.... Dr. Ohno Ow! New theme music for the doc, a hilarious parody of Ted DiBiase's "Everybody Has a Price." Apparently, Mal would be here too, but his cursed travel plans took him to T[Edited]do, and who knows if he'll ever get out? Could be worse, it could've been Buffalo. Ow continues to set up an eventual PAIN/Romanis feud by cutting on the gypsies as they make their entrance to a somewhat mixed pop. Romanis bitch about Fire & Ice getting the tag title shot tonight as their heel turn edges forward. And "Back In Black" plays, which means, either F&I is coming out to answer the Romanis, or Final Justice is coming out. See, this is what happens when you let two teams have the same damn theme music. Actually a bit of a pop for Final Justice. And Ow introduces another guest member of the broadcast team, The Chair That Hit [name withheld], who I immediately nominate for Best Commentator of the year. Damn, I'm rambling. Okay, it's Ravnos and Mike Hall (who doesn't look _quite_ as shitty as he did last week) to start. Hall tries to brawl, but Ravnos dodges everything and counters with a throat thrust, then a chokehold (called by Ow as a "sidewalk slam") and a punch to the back of the neck (called by Ow as "Whatamove!"). Ravnos ropeburns Hall, then a blind tag by Niklos into a beautiful Total Elimination-style move with Niklos coming off the top with a bodypress. Quick DDT by Niklos, and Ravnos knocks Adam Barney off the apron, and Mickey says we're only 15 seconds in, but when I replayed the tape, it was at least 30.

-- Niklos snapmares Hall into the turnbuckle and then comes off with what I can only describe as a springboard kneedrop, but it was so much cooler than that term would imply. Damn, if we had a bunch of cruisers who could work like Niklos, we might actually still have a cruiserweight division. I think he's _definitely_ the most exciting worker in the WWO today. Ravnos schmoozes Valerie on the outside, but Adam chases after him, and the Romanis do some amazing circus rolls and dives to get out of the way of the enraged Barney and make him crash into Hall. I bow to the superior ability that is the Amazing Romanis. Double gourdbuster eliminates Barney, tumbling shoulderblock by Niklos on Hall, and I can't keep up. Pickpocket DDT, Gaje Guillotine, the Romanis just playing now, basically. Ravnos chokes Hall with his bandana, then whips him across the ring, Barney apparently still out. Double running reverse neckbreaker, and this one was academic long ago, but we'll let the Romanis show their entire arsenal on TV. Barney finally back up, but Ravnos eliminates him with some flash powder he pulled out of nowhere, then a _SWEET_ wheelbarrow suplex-clothesline to the back of the head combo move by the Romanis. Barney back up again, but Working Without A Net eliminates him for good, and a double kneedrop to Hall's neck by Ravnos (called the "Dance of Knives" by Mickey, called "the cure for a stiff neck" by Ow.) ends it. Ow says the match went one minute and 32 seconds, I counted 2 1/2 minutes, but that's quibbling. Basically a JTTS squash, so I can't go any higher than this rating, but it was the Romanis, so I can't go any lower than this, either. Rating -- **.

-- Cut backstage to the Vegas Connection, who take a delivery of eggplant tuxedos... with maple leafs on the back. They are not amused.

-- Tre Jordan snuck his way into the IIWF Arena in a garbage can, along with blind manservant Billy McWilliams. Apex and Dark Soul walk by, and Tre apparently knows them, but Apex doesn't remember him, and Darky apparently wants nothing to do with him, and when Apex finally remembers Tre, he decides he didn't like him, either. Please note how Apex and Dark Soul avoided mentioning the name of the fed all three were in previously. Anyway, Tre _wants_ Apex and Dark to sell him because he'd be more marketable than Joshua LaChance. Well, hell, who wouldn't be? Dark doesn't want to do it, but finally agrees to sell Tre if he gets a WWO contract. Billy, meanwhile, has gotten tangled up in some electrical wires. Nothing like making fun of the blind to get a cheap laugh.

-- Singles match: Ultimate Thrasher vs. Lion Tamer w/Tiger Lily. Thrash looks a little bit "off" tonight. I can't decide if it's part of the angle with him losing his chick to Craven or if he's back on the sauce. Bradley is joyous over the possibility of a WWO without Dark Soul and with a Thrasher who is just a shell of his former self. Tamer comes out, and plays up the pretty boy heel gimmick by making a ring worker sweep the aisle before walking down it... priceless. Tamer gets in the ring, but Thrasher gets ready to charge, and Tamer pulls Tiger Lily in the way. Tamer on the mic, ripping on Thrasher for losing Melissa to Craven and for hitting a woman, causing Thrasher to charge and Tamer to roll out of the ring hastily. Ringside chase, but Tiger Lily gets in the way, and Thrasher threatens to hit her (which actually draws a mixed reaction from the crowd), before settling for just verbally threatening her, letting Tamer bring THE TOILET PLUNGER OF DOOM~! into play. Sam and Mick doing some weird back and forth switching between color and play-by-play. Hiro finally rolls out to take the plunger away from Tamer and Tamer rolls Thrasher back into the ring. Swinging neckbreaker and leg drop by Tamer, and Sam isn't doing too bad with the PBP, but Ralph just _sucks_ at color. Backdrop by Tamer, then a half Boston Crab... which Thrasher appears to be thinking about selling at some point. Thrasher reaches the rope, Tamer refuses to let go until Hiro's count reaches four, then he DDT's Thrasher. Tamer goes up to the top for a frog splash, but misses, and both men are down. It's not _technically_ a double knockout spot, so I won't bitch.

-- Thrasher up first, toe-to-toe brawling, high knee by Thrasher and then that "Kiss that Doesn't Miss" punch (should he be using that now that Melissa is snogging Craven?), and Hiro actually tries to enforce the no-closed fist punch rule and gets shoved by Thrasher for his trouble. I think I actually heard some boos out there. Then, Thrasher stomps Tamer, then chokes him, and Hiro puts the count on, and actually reaches the five count and tries to physically _pull_ Thrasher off. Thrasher _finally_ lets go, and Thrasher and Hirosaki are screaming at each other, until Thrasher remembers he has an opponent and clotheslines Tamer, then Tombstone's him, and picks him up at the 2 count. Thrasher sets up for the double underhook powerbomb, but Tiger Lily gets up on the apron and distracts him, and Mickey actually calls Tiger Lily a "bitch" on the air. What, did she refuse to blow him or something?

Oh God, we're going full Psycho Thrasher now as he pulls Tiger Lily into the ring and is going to give _her_ the double underhook powerbomb. He starts to lift her up... then realizes what he's doing, gently lets her down, and _begs_ her forgiveness as Tamer finally comes to life and rolls up Thrasher for 2. Tamer tries for a suplex on Thrasher, but Thrasher reverses it and follows it up with a big elbow drop, then another chokehold, and this time, when Hiro pulls him off, Thrasher just shoves Hiro threw the ropes and to the floor. Hiro's out (second week in a row that's happenend), Thrasher hotshots Tamer then delivers three Power Chord Slams, but as soon as Hiro wakes up, he DQ's Thrasher. This, of course, enrages Thrasher, who tries to hang Tamer in the ropes... until Sweet Melissa appears at the entranceway, and Thrasher's off like a shot. Welllllll... the psychological aspect with Thrasher is interesting, but it would have been nice if he would have sold something. Tamer did his best to carry the match. The weird commentating didn't help. Rating -- *1/2.

-- Uh-oh, Marvin Patch has decided this would be a good time for his regularly scheduled beatdown. He lets Tamer out of the ropes so he can better bloody him with a chair, but then Xaiver Feyr comes out of the crowd, and basically offers to hold Tamer so Patch can get a better shot... and then Patch _levels_ Feyr. WTF?? Patch goes back to work on Tamer, but Team Whoopass (Matt Cole and Jonas King) come out from the back to save Tamer. Yes, save Tamer, who takes advantage of the chaos to bail out of the ring, only to run into a Rick Marley sledgehammer shot. Fighting in the ring takes us out to commercial. This was some odd, odd, ODD post-match booking. Tamer taking a beatdown from two heels and getting saved by two faces? It would have made _far_ more sense for Thrasher to take the beat down... or for this beatdown to occur after a match where the face lost. And Marley probably still could have back-jumped Tamer after this match, anyway.

-- Lester Leary Reese's commercial. He's a corporate whore, doncha know?

-- Cut back to Mickey, who informs us a 6-man tag will take place next week... Patch, Feyr and Marley against King, Cole and Tamer??? Okay, I see the point... the beatdown perps take on the guys they wanted to beat down. It's still frickin' odd.

-- Singles match: Miguel Quesada w/Silkk Mason and Dark Soul's hot sister vs. Alex Extreme. New music, outfit and entrance for Miggy. Fans still not quite sure how to take to him. Y'know, I bet if he hadn't turned heel, this match would be hella hot. Extreme has a pretty damn cool entrance. Extreme with some trash-talking, and Miguel slaps him and cue the brawling. Extreme hits a kneelift, but Miggy gets a school boy for 2. Running clothesline from Alex, which Miguel oversells masterfully. Nice roundhouse kick from Alex puts Miguel down again. Legdrop from Extreme, Miguel's back up and reverses an Irish whip and hits a very nice flying headscissors take down and follows it up with a judo throw. Both men back up, lock up into a side headlock by Extreme. And he pulls the hair to keep Miguel from pushing off, bringing a smile to my face. Sam continues to drool over Stacy Werner, and Cordon finally sees Extreme holding on by the hair, and makes him let go. Extreme argues with Cordon and Miggy slips up behind him and hits him with a back suplex. Quesada misses an elbow, then Alex misses a legdrop, and Miguel levels Alex with a flying shoulderblock and tries to get the crowd into it, but they're being relatively silent. Dammit, cheer people. Both these guys are working it. Miggy does his little Ali thing, but Extreme ducks a punch and comes back with a few body blows and a handspring elbow, then hits a HUGE flying bulldog. Alex follows it up with a dropkick, then an ax-kick gets 2. Russian leg sweep, then a big elbow drop... and out comes "Da Superior Tahlent" Sean Collins, apparently to do ringside commentary.

Extreme body slams Quesada in the ring, then lets him get up to hit a roundhouse kick, but I'm having trouble concentrating on the match due to Collins' commentary. Extreme misses another kick, and Miggy remembers his heel roots and nails a Greco-roman forearm shot to the nuts. Quesada with a nice belly-to-back superplex, and he manages to float over so he's standing on his feet. As Hammond once said, when motivated, Quesada rules the friggin' Earth. Collins: "Quesada's still beez a playa hater." Sam: "Yeah! Whatever that means!" 450 splash by Miggy gets 2 1/2. Miggy argues with Cordon for a second, then executes a beautiful tilt-a-whirl backbreaker that almost gets 3. _Sweet_ flying armdrag takedown by Miguel, and Alex decides now would be a good time to take a breather. Miguel follows him out, but Extreme gets up on the steps and pulls off a Tornado DDT onto the floor! Holy fuck, that rocked. Alex with some hard shots and a headlock, but Miggy shoves him into the ring post _hard._

Fans starting to get behind Miguel a little more now. Collins says he could take both of these guys out, but his "rotating cuff" is hurt. Alex in first, Miguel tries to follow but Alex snaps Miguel's neck on the top rope. Miggy rolls in and gets to his feet, but Alex puts him right back down with a clothesline then drops an elbow from the second rope for two. Brainbuster gets 2 1/2. Miggy reverses an Irish whip into the corner and charges Alex, Alex tries to backdrop him out of the ring, but Miguel lands on the apron and tries the springboard dropkick, but Alex ducks and Miguel ends up taking out Cordon. _Stiff_ kicks from Extreme, and a Fisherman's buster leaves Quesada laid out. And Papa Legba comes to ringside... wearing a referee shirt... with a fucking PANTHER. Extreme hits To The Extreme (the coolest finisher in the WWO right now, in my opinion), and Legba counts the three? Extreme thinks he has it won, the announcers are confused, and Extreme goes to leave, but Legba is still blocking the aisle with his panther.... oh, I see what's gonna happen! Quesada gets up and rolls up Extreme just as Cordon is waking up to get the duke. Hell of a match. Both guys brought their working boots, Extreme continues to be the breakaway star from the UEW contingent, and Quesada is slowly starting to get his face heat back. And yes, it was a screwjob finish, but it was a clever screwjob that'll further Extreme/Legba along. Rating -- ****.

-- Cut to the back, where Mackey Jay goes to talk to Chris Hopper, who is promptly jumped by a masked man with a chair. After a few shots, the man takes the cameraman's camera and starts nailing Hopper with it. Hmm... not good to come into a match with Craven bruised. Wonder if that's the same masked guy that jumped Marley earlier?

-- Singles match: "Too Cool" Chris Hopper vs. William Craven. Let me start out by saying I'd love to see the match work like this: Craven kicks Hopper's ass all match, Hopper fights back and pulls out a fluke win, and Craven kicks Hopper's ass some more. Hopper actually makes it out for the match, to a pretty good pop. Not Apex level or anything, but decent. Craven's intro hits, but he's not coming out, and just as Bradley mentions something about seeing Craven go into the kitchen earlier, a fucking FREEZER, one of those one's you see in school cafeterias, rolls down the ramp and slams into the ring, startling everyone, then Craven comes down, pushing a wheelbarrow full of ice. Craven gets to ringside, pulls a microphone out of the ice, and proceeds to cut a promo that is both hilariously funny and quite disturbing, as only Craven can. I think my favorite line was "Icy Road, Ice Crusher, HOW ABOUT A NICE ICE PICK BETWEEN THE EYES, BOY!?" Craven utters his catchphrase, and it's on. Brawling right away to start. Craven takes advantage quickly, and Bradley inaccurately says Hopper is bigger than Craven. Hopper is slightly _taller_ than Craven, but Craven is about 30 or so pounds of muscle heavier. Hopper rolls out to try to collect himself, but Craven _dives_ through the ropes to the floor and lands on Hopper _hard_. Craven then tries to pull Hopper toward the freezer as Crisper starts a 10-count... as if _he_ can keep any control of this one.

Craven working on Hopper's legs for some reason, then wheelbarrow suplexes Hopper on the fucking floor. Poor Hopper, it looks like Bill forgot his Prozac today. He pulls Hopper over to the freezer, opens the lid, and pulls out... STUFFED PENGUINS??? Craven slides in to break the count, then slides back out, but Hopper's recovered (well, as much as one can recover against Craven) and fights back with a few punches and a kick to the nuts. Now Ralph stumbles and says that Hopper has Craven by the hair, even though Craven is bald. Rough night for the announce crew. DDT on the floor by Hopper, then he breaks the count and goes back outside to work on Craven. Match finally goes back into the ring, and Hopper follows up an Irish whip with a belly-to-back suplex, actually, that was closer to a backdrop driver. 2-3 inches more vertical, and Craven might have actually sold it. Crowd loved it, though. But, instead of going for the win, Hopper poses, throws Bill into the ropes, and tries to backdrop him to the floor, but Craven holds on, and pulls the top rope down when Hopper tries to charge, sending him to the floor.

Craven then tosses a _big_ block of ice from his wheelbarrow into the ring at Hopper, and just barely misses. Then he pulls out an ice pick. What does he think this is, the Stringer Hardcore Invitational Tournament??? If I'm Crisper, at this point, I'm calling for the bell and hightailing it. Fuck it, let security handle the madman, y'know? But no, Crisper apparently is too busy cleaning up the shit in his pants to do that. Craven actually rolls back into the ring, _with the icepick_, to break the 10 count, then rolls out and tries to STAB Hopper, but Hopper comes to and quickly scrambles away. Holy shit, it really looked like Craven was going to stab him. Hopper, however, runs into the freezer and falls down, and Craven and Hopper are now fighting over the icepick and Hopper accidentally _stabs Craven in the shoulder_. Folks, it didn't look like that was worked at all. Crowd is... it's tough to describe their reactions right now. Ever since that icepick came out, they've just been scared shitless. And they ain't the only ones.

Craven, however, just calmly removes the icepick from his shoulder and tosses it away, then gets back into the ring to finish the match. Holy shit... if there's a tougher man on the planet than William Craven, I have not seen him. Powerslam (pretty much one-armed) by Craven gets 2. TURNBUCKLE PUNCHES OF DOOM~! from Craven, but Hopper stops it at six, and counters with a powerbomb, then holds onto the legs and tries to turn it over into a Boston Crab. I'm impressed Hopper's even able to go on with the match, never mind pull a sweet counter like that out of his ass. Hopper gets Craven turned over in the Boston Crab, but Craven quickly gets to the ropes. Powerload slam gets 2.8, and the crowd almost exploded there. If Hopper pulls this one out, the fans are going to react as if the Blazers won the NBA title. Craven briefly thinks about selling two shots to his injured shoulder, because there's still a steady trickle of blood coming from it, but Hopper clotheslines him to the floor... then pins him to the apron with the freezer! HOLY SHIT!!! Okay, this is turning out to be one hell of a fucking match. Hopper hops up on the freezer and delivers a short dropkick to Craven's face. The Final Word:

Hell of a Showcase. HELL of a Showcase. Undercard was worked pretty crisply, mid- to upper-card matches were all pretty hot, the secondary and tag team angles are really starting to clarify themselves. Stringer/Detson/Apex over the IP Title looks to be the plan, which could restore quite a bit of heat to the title. Extreme/Legba is looking real good, Hopper/Stryker is looking real good, and I still maintain Marley/Tamer is going to be the feud this cycle that will come out of nowhere and shock people. Taggers starting to look better, as the Canadian Legacy/Vegas Connection and PAIN/Romanis feuds are getting a nice slow build. Craven/Thrasher appears to be idling a bit at this point, along with Knowitall/Quesada, and for the first time in recent memory, no KTFH on a Showcase. This was the time for the "second echelon" of WWO stars to step up, and they did so admirably.

Thumbs _way_ up.
