WWO bio card — Behemoth

29 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Behemoth Real Name: Markus Griff Height: 7'3 Weight: 330 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: White Sands labs, NM Entrance Music: "Mr. Crowley" by Ozzy Osborne Manager: Dr. Jarod Stykes Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Behemoth's ring attire is a Black body stocking that is padded to look like armor. He also has black fingerless gloves and white forearm pads. The black boots he wear strap up to the knee. He also wears a white goalies mask to protect his face. Psychological Makeup Stengths Great Strength: Behemoth is one of the strongest if not the strongest man in the WWO. Power moves are almost second nature to this giant. High Pain threshold: Behemoth can take a lot of punishment almost to the level of the old fashioned "kip-up" after taking a huge beating, but he can be hurt it just takes a little more than a normal man can dish out. Fear: Behemoth is a big scary fella. Some of the more less stout of heart would be scared of facing this 7'3 monster. It usually comes at an advantage to start a match, and very often during the "kip-up" process. Weaknesses Flashbacks: Behemoth has flashbacks when triggered by various stimlus. Ie army like fatiuges, women that look like his wife and so on. These flashbacks can cause Behemoth to lose focus on what he his doing..untill he his struck or hit. Loyal to Stykes: If Stykes is harmed durning a match Behemoth will set after the attacker which can cause him to lose the match. No aerial Moves: Because of his size he can not preform even 2nd rope moves. Biographical Information Dr Jarod Stykes was one of the hot shots of the science world. Stykes was on the fast track to be one of the best biochemists in the US. That changed when Biotech, the corporation which Stykes worked for, passed Stykes over for the head of R&D in the bio Chemistry dept. He was passed over for the Senior VP's nephew which made Stykes very angry and he payed them back by selling off all the secrets that Biotech was working on to their rivals save one...the "Avatar project" which he kept for himself. Stykes was paid handsomely for his corporate turncoating and he started a lab in White Sands, New Mexico. Stykes worked and slaved on his project, gathering DNA of various people from soldiers, shootfighters to wrestlers. Stykes started to assemble his DNA samples to graft them together to form a drug that would be the steroid of the new 21st century . Stykes found his test subject in Markus Grif, a desert storm vet down on his luck. Stykes started Grif on his treatment two years ago and many changes happened to Grif. The first was Grif sprouted up to 7'3 and 330 pounds. His strength increased along with his size but Grif started losing patches of his memories like his family, his wife and various others. Stykes started to feed Grif his new memories for him... not calling him Markus but Behemoth. The treatment was a sucess except in the memory loss which Stykes vowed to correct. The money from his sellout was running thin and he was worried that his greatest creation would go unknown and his ego could not stand. Then Stykes saw a WWO broadcast asking for new wrestlers. A cruel smile crossed his lips as the thought of showcasing his genius and the money he could earn by using Behemoth as a wrestler and in that way he could continue his work on the drug. So Stykes unleashes his Behemoth on the WWO and hopes that his dream can come true and that his place in science will be insured for all eternity. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier None Power Excellent Technical Good Submission Fair Martial Arts Fair Cheating Poor Brawling/Hardcore Fair Escapability Good Stamina Excellent Ring Awareness Excellent Teamwork None Top Wrestling Moves Fallaway drop-slam Inner forarm shots in the corner One hand choke slam Two handed choke hoist and toss. Bear hug Head crush Haymaker like punches High-power throw into the corner Three hard headbutts in a row to the face The big boot Finishing Maneuver Double Helix Bomb: Two high jackknife powerbombs in a row.
