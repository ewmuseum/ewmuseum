We'll work our way up. The Title of America's...this is a belt that to me should never go through the new Spotlight/Showcase combo without being defended. With the depth at that level behind Papa Legba (Extreme, Colby, Ace of Hearts, Marley, Tamer, Stryker, even Hopper), why not? The depth is unmatched by any level in the WWO. Now, there could be a growing concern that if the title is defended each week, it'll hinder a wrestler's ability to build a feud and have a big blowoff match, but I combat that by saying, that is possible, but the Title of America's should be a circulation belt. It needs to find itself a new owner every now and then. If someone can keep the belt despite the tough challenges, it is time to see that person rise up the card. Like, say Legba ran through all the man I mentioned. It's time he takes on the Detson's, Stringer's, and so forth to see if he is ready to charge up the roster. First, it was brought back with a great match (Quesada/Stringer) and then Stringer did well with limited material (after Stringer/Love/Tucker fell through, he followed with Stringer/Cole) and now is in the hands of someone who I feel should be allowed a lengthy title reign.
