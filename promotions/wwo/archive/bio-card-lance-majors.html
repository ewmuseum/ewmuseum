WWO bio card — Lance Van Majors

Lance Van Majors Real Name: Lance Van Majors Height: 6'3 Weight: 271 Date of Birth: ?/1959 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Huntsville, AL Entrance Music: "Celtic Aggression" by Tonic Manager: Lexus Anderson Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Think of your aging wrestler. He's still in good physical condition, but he's added on the love handles and the fat padding that seems to come with all the old wrestlers. He's got blonde, now turning white, hair and blue eyes. He's got the look of someone who's over-the-hill. Think Ric Flair in his later years. He wears standard green trunks, green elbowpads, greene kneepads, white wrist tape, and white boots with green laces and soles. Psychological Makeup Strengths Experience: You know what comes along with this. He's got 20 years in the business. He knows the game. He's got the tools. Weaknesses He's old. His body's been through the grinder a dozen times over and it shows. HE doesn't have a bad wheel persay, but he just doesn't have what he use to have 10 years ago. He's lost the ability to perform a lot of his power moves, which pretty much hampers him into almost a one-dimesional style of wrestling. He brawls and uses basic tech moves a lot. A lot of today's "showstoppers" can easily combat his wrestling style. It's just shaking him mentally that's hard. Doesn't perform well as a singles competitor. Doesn't have the gas to take a high pace match, though he does know how to reverse his energy. Still, if forced to wrestle at a high pace for long, he's gonna hit empty. Biographical Information I'll make this short. He's 42 now and has been wrestling for around 20 years or more. He's got a lot of experience. In his life, he's suffered woes as well as joys. He and his wife couldn't have children. His wife ended up dying. He adopted Lexus Anderson. He's never really made it "big" in wrestling, but has wrestled around the world -- literally. Lance kind of took Kyle under his wing and trained the younger kid. Before that, he got him interested in sports and staying in good physical condition. When Kyle graduated from high school, he trained Kyle to wrestle. The two debuted in WWO in April of 99 as the New Generation. They team did fairly well, but after a few months, the two left WWO. Kyle went back to Mobile while Lance actually headed out west to book for a promotion out there. The two happened to hear of the reopening of WWO and decided to give the team another shot.
