WWO bio card — "The Wild Card" Ravnos Romani

27 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Wild Card" Ravnos Romani Real Name: Ravnos Romani Height: 6'2 Weight: 238 Date of Birth: Alignment: Taoist Hometown: The Gypsy Carivan Entrance Music: "Rebel Heart" by Corrs Manager: Physical Appearance Ravnos is very much a piece of Eurotrash, complete with the hairy chest, moderate tan, short-cut curly black hair, short-shaved goatee, and boyish charm; his two most striking features are his eyes, which are either grey or silver depending on how the light catches them and frequently hidden behind a set of mirrored sunglasses, and his grin, which is practically radiant but filled with hidden dangers, like a predator's. He typically dresses in road-worn cutoff jeans, a pair of comfortable and sturdy hiking boots, an open dress shirt (of various colors and/or patterns), and leather jacket, not to mention several earrings, gold chains, rings, and other "trophies". This also doubles as his wrestling attire, save for the accessories and jacket. He almost always carries a rucksack with him, containing various items including his Tarot deck, provisions for those long travels, and other miscellany, and which is used to keep his personal possessions during his matches... and occasionally those of others as well. Psychological Makeup Strengths Strength #1: Ravnos knows that he isn't the biggest dog in the fight, nor the most talented, but he makes up for his difficiencies in... other ways. To put it bluntly, Ravnos cheats like a bastard. A thief all his life, Ravnos has rarely if ever been caught; he knows how to make sure the authority figures are looking the other way at *just* the right time, and he's parlayed this talent into one of the most underhanded movesets in the business. There are no rules as far as this gypsy is concerned... at least, none that can be enforced. The less "legal" a fight becomes, the more Ravnos is in his element. Strength #2: While he may not be the strongest competitor, Ravnos certainly knows how to use his head (something other wrestlers use as just another blunt object). Especially adept at playing enemies off one another and other forms of diversion/distraction, Ravnos does his best to stay one step ahead of things (and a little fortune-telling doesn't hurt, either). By the time people figure out what's really going on, it's usually too late to stop him. Strength #3: If all else fails... RUN! Ravnos has also cultivated a servable amount of foot-speedand agility to high-tail it out of situations where he seems to be in over his head. Part of never being caught breaking the law is never being *caught* at all. Weaknesses Weakness #1: While Ravnos may be one of the world's best cheaters (if not *the* best), he has a slight impulse control problem. Part of the reason he's become such a good thief is because he simply can't help himself. This isn't quite kleptomania, but rather a conscious descision that Ravnos has yet to turn down. Jewlery, money, fine foods, extravagant clothing, gold, women -- *especially* gold and women -- nothing is safe from this gypsy's voracious appetite for thievery. More often than not, this lands him in a heap of trouble by attracting unwanted attention to himself... In addition, he has cultivated a helathy contempt for authority figures, who he generally considers to be useless and/or irredemably stupid, although he usually keeps this buried under a jovial demeanor towards them. Weakness #2: Outside of his illegal tactics, a few moves taught to him by an unknown acquaintance, and some aireal tricks he picked up hanging around circus folk most of his life (and later, his brother), Ravnos is woefully unskilled and easily outclassed in a "fair" fight. Weakness #3: A showman born and bred, Ravnos is easily distracted and occasionally makes some stupid decisions. He takes time to showboat to the crowd. He taunts his opponent relentlessly. He makes advances on his opponent's valet -- DURING THE MATCH. Entertaining? Yes. A good way to win? Not usually. Style Notes: Biographical Information History: Born in a gypsy caravan travelling from town to town and carnival to carnival, Ravnos had an... unique upbringing, to say the least. All his life, his mother (and several top contenders for his father) taught him how to make a living by swindling the general populace and generally bending the law to it's breaking point. Ravnos excelled in the arts of thievery and sleight-of-hand, and also picked up a few other useful tricks like fortune-telling and the like with which he could better employ his larcenous talents. In time, he struck out on his own to persue other goals, the life of a sideshow performer never really appealing to him (at the time). As he grew up into a fine-looking young man, with a perpetual Mediterranean tan to his skin, intriguing grey eyes, swarthy black hair, and charming grin, Ravnos ran afoul of the "gaje law" more times than he could count, but always slipped away unscathed through his quick wit and (if necessary) quick feet. Over time, he amassed an impressive collection of rings, necklaces, bracelets, watches, trinkets, and -- especially -- women, many with former (or in the cases of more than a few women, current) owners wondering exactly what happened. But by the time they suspected Ravnos, he was always a dot on the horizon, laughing to himself (and occasionally still accelerating). The only two forces holding Ravnos back from becoming the world's greatest thief were his preference for small-time "jobs" and his carefree nature. Nothing truly mattered to Ravnos but the next challenge, the next con, the next prize. Born and raised a showman, Ravnos needed... an audience. But what audience would be suitable for his talents? He could have become a televangelist, but the gypsy people have never been too fond of such religions. A politician's life, while rife with opportunites for some grand-scale thievery, held too much responsibility to his fickle natue. Perhaps the circus, where he could work the midways and side shows in fleecing the rubes of their hard-earned cash? The decision was agonizing, but like so many things in his life, Ravnos literally stumbled into the right choice when he "accidently" collided with a large man while pretending to not look where he was going. After the requisite mumbled apology and picking himself off the sidewalk, Ravnos dusted himself off. Once the large man had turned the corner, Ravnos inspected his newly-acquired wallet and discovered that his latest mark was a professional wrestler for the World Wrestling Association. In a flahs of insight, Ravnos knew what he wanted to do with the rest of his life... After all, Ravnos reasoned, he and wrestling shared so much in common, he'd have to be a stupid gaje to ignore this baxt. And so, under the pretext of returning the wrestler's "lost" wallet, Ravnos made his first step into the world of professional wrestling. His tenure with the WWA didn't last very long (only a month or so) before he and management had a "disagreement" and Ravnos left. But with even such short a time in the ring, Ravnos was hooked. The spotlight, the fans, the women, the gold... he needed more. Unfortunately, he had no idea where to go. Needing the time to do some research, he took a position in the small, independent New Classic Wreslting Alliance as color comentator, inflicting his garish lifestyle and mannerisms on central North Carolina. During that time, he had heard about the WWO legend Wuzho and reasoned that the WWO was a more "gypsy-friendly" federation, although at the time it had regretably shut its doors. The NCWA faced a similar fate, going underground when their TV deal went sour and depriving Ravnos of the exposure he so desparately craved. When the rumors of the WWO's reopening began circulating the internet, Ravnos decided to take action. As fate would have it, at the same time, his family's troupe was forced to fold, leaving his brother Niklos unemployed, alone, and angry. In a flash of insight, Ravnos recruited his youner brother and vowed to resurrect the name of the Amazing Romanis in the wrestling world; their goal, in Ravnos's own words, is to "steal the show," although the unsettling way in which he maintains that "I-know-something-you-don't-know" grin makes one wonder just what he means by that... Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Amazing Romanis Tag Team Partner: Niklos Romani Tag Team Moves "Gypsy Switch" -- Niklos will fake an injury, drawing the referee's attention, during which time Ravnos sneaks in the ring and does something *highly* illegal to the unsuspecting opponent, slipping out just as Niklos "recovers" Knee-strike sandwich (Ravnos to the back, Niklos to the gut) Double-team front vertical layout suplex Ravnos delivers an inverted atomic drop, followed by a Niklos dropkick from behind "Gaje Guillotine" -- Ravnos "flapjacks" the opponent into the ropes, accentuated by Niklos grabbing the opponent's head in mid-air and dropping to the floor (sort of a "spike hot-shot") See Niklos Romani's application for more tag team manuevers available to this team. Tag Team Finishing Maneuver "Working Without a Net" Niklos sits the opponent on the top turnbuckle and tags in Ravnos, who crouches down in front of the corner in a "ten fingers" pose. Niklos then runs the length of the ring, and is launched into the air by Ravnos, delivering a flying clothesline to the opponent that sends both the victim and Niklos sailing to the floor almost 10 feet below. The Romanis prefer a count-out victory (Ravnos, the legal man, is in the ring; Niklos tries to get up and to the corner), but if a pin is necessary (e.g. title match), one or the other will retrieve the usually KO'd opponent back into the ring; the Romani who actually does the retrieval depends on how good a landing Niklos had.
