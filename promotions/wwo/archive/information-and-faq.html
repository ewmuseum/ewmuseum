Information and FAQ

9 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks a lot fans for checking out the WWO homepage! Below is the WWO in a nut shell, while reading this, we hope you will become aquanted with the WWO daily operations. Enjoy! What makes the WWO so special compared to the many e-wrestling federations on the net? The following display, we hope, will explain to you what makes us so special. One Word: Fun. If you aren't having fun in this game, well somone's doing something wrong, and you're definitely in the wrong place. The one thing the World Wrestling Organization prides itself on is that win or lose, you're going to have fun doing it. Honestly, that's what kind of "forced" our first shutdown. I'll be honest, I never wanted to close the doors in the first place. And after putting up a lot of my own time and energy in four years, it seemed the only way to send a message (and to save my sanity). But it seems that some people forgot the purpose of this is to have fun. For four long years, with only a few exceptions, people never forgot that you shouldn't care if you win or lose in E-Wrestling. You're supposed to do it because you enjoy it, not because you're worried about an imaginary piece of gold that won't buy you coffee on the street. If you think you're wasting your time for nothing, then you're obviously in this hobby for all the wrong reasons. Don't plan on winning all your matches. You'll even lose matches that you swear up and down you out-rp'ed for, that you couldn't have done anything better. It's all a giant story. The hero doesn't always "get the girl", but even in those great books of htat sort, you still don't put it down, because it's a great story. That's what we've got here. Now, what is the World Wrestling Organization? With headquarters in Biloxi, Mississippi, the WWO tries to live up to it's motto of being "Simply the Best" by giving our handlers maximum daily interaction, and that interaction determines the wins and losses of the federation. As you can tell, we are a roleplay based, or as some people prefer to call it, a booked federation. Now unlike most organizations who state that the RP's are the final outcome, in the World Wrestling Organization, the storylines drive the federation. Anyone can write solid interviews week in and week out, but to come up with the storylines that carry you to the top takes that little bit extra. Of course, Roleplays are essential. Simply put, no roleplays means no story. No story means no World Wrestling Organization. And that's not what we want, now is it? We are also proud to state that we are one of the few federations that do not go around promising the most "Extreme" matches available, or the most "violent" or "colorful" action. (Even though some of our handlers do... don't ask about the urinal cakes and Fat Chinese Roundhouse.) We simply promise good clean fun for everyone involved, and we like to think that's why you, the people and fans of e-wrestling, got involved in the first place, for the fun and excitement, and those two things we promise. Who is the World Wrestling Organization? The WWO is led by our President, [name withheld]. He is the man who make the final say in all decisions, and understands that the idea of this federation is fun. But while President [name withheld] is the head of the organization, he wouldn't be able to do a think without his staff and the handlers of the WWO. But the President and the Staff is only part of the equation. The WWO roster itself consists of what President [name withheld] refers to as some of the best unknown players in the sport. Up to 59 other wrestlers to compete with for a limited number of belts make the WWO one of the most varied and active organizations in the e-wrestling world. And the variety of characters.... We definitely have some fairly interesting ones of our own either currently or in the past. A former CIA spy turned professional wrestler, business men who were out for profit only, Nazis, ex-hockey players, rock climbers, chess masters, and of course career wrestlers, we have them all, and look forward to your possibly joining us. Rules of the World Wrestling Organization The WWO rules are made to be as flexible as possible, however some rules just cannot be avoided. 1) We are a closed RP system. All Roleplays are directed to our Roleplay director, TV interviews and planned strategies to President [name withheld]. Sending an open RP to the entire mailing list of the federation is immediate grounds for expulsion if a review of the situation deems it non-accidental. 2) We simply ask that you limit the vulgarity. We understand that there are a number of younger handlers on the net, and while some people find it necessary to use "improper" language as every other word, we at the WWO look down on it, and even attempt to run our own censors if need be. A handler who doesn't use four letter words over and over can be just as effective if not more so than one who curses with every sentence he states. If it won't make it on TV, it won't make it here. 3) While we understand that shooting sometimes happens, as it has been seen done by the front office on a number of occasions, we remind our handlers that this is done entirely in fun, and that personal attacks between handlers are not tolerated and is grounds for immediate expulsion. For instance, the WWO front office will tolerate no RACISM whatsoever in it's ranks. There are other areas which we strongly look down on, and could be grounds for dismissal. Using other handler's characters without permission could also be considered a No-no, depending on the severity of the charge. 4) HAVE FUN! Oops..Did we say that already? Well, I guess we can't say it enough. The front office tries extremely hard to put out an enjoyable product, and we are definitely not doing our jobs if the handlers are not enjoying themselves, win or lose. Although we know you prefer to have your wrestlers win, you can't go 60-0 in the World Wrestling Organization, but everyone gets their shot at the win column. 5) [name withheld] is the final say on all decisions and outcomes. See the first section above, and ask yourself if you're doing this for the right reasons? Because I have already suffered one stress related ulcer, and don't plan on having anymore. How do I become a part of the World Wrestling Organization? There are two ways to be come a major part of the federation, and become a great part of e-wrestling history. First, check out the web page at http://wwowrestling.virtualave.net/index.html While the federation has limited it's focus down to becoming the Best of the Best, we're always looking for hot new talent, or the known superstars who want to prove their abilities in this part of the world. You think you have what it takes to be part of "Simply the Best"? There's only one way to find out... fill out the application and then our Talent coordinators will go over both you and the application with a fine tooth comb. If you don't have form ability, feel free to email us your own pre-created form. We'll do what we can to work with it and you're application to make it compatible with what we work with. And remember, we have all levels of wrestlers, so you will always have someone that you could find yourself competing with and have a great chance at some major feuds. And perhaps learn what it takes to get to the next level of roleplay based e-wrestling. Any final reasons why I should join the World Wrestling Organization? Can we say it enough times? You want to have Fun? This is definitely the place you're looking for. You will be in constant contact with the President, either via email, the home page chat system for those of you with Java capabilities, or even on irc where President [name withheld] and a number of our handlers can usually be found on various evenings. And most importantly of all, you will be looking at what we think would be one of the most exciting federations in the e-wrestling world, and you will be able to say that you are among "SIMPLY THE BEST". And for those of you who just aren't sure, we post our cards on the usenet under rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy (RSP-WF), or feel free to request from President [name withheld] that you would like to join our mailing lists, so that you can see for yourself why we are one of the best federations in the e-wrestling community. Let's find out...Do you have what it takes to been known as what is "Simply the Best?" ©1999 World Wrestling Organization Productions All Rights Reserved
