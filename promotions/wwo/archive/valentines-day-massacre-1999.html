Valentine's Day Massacre 1999 (excerpt)

25 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. This broadcast, from Aberdeen, Scotland, is a single long posting to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy; only the night's one title match and the main event finish below are reproduced here in full. The undercard remains held and is not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPIONSHIP [No-disqualification, falls-count-anywhere. The challenger, "Latin Lightning" Al Perez, already hobbled by a pre-match attack, takes on the reigning champion, Night's Harbinger, with the reigning World Champion Fury inserting himself into the match at ringside rather than staying a spectator. The fight spills out to the parking lot:] Ralph: Perez was trying to get Harbinger over in the neckscissors! Harbinger tried to throw Perez off!! And they both go over!! Both men down on the sedan!! And down to the ground!! Both men down to the ground!! And there goes Crisper down to the ground!!
Bradley: They're dead..it's official! Ralph: Crisper dropping to his feet, and the arms over on the man!! Crisper with the count!! ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!! That's three!!! We've got a three count!!!
[Almost as Crisper gets to his feet, and signals the three count, Calhoun's voice is heard over the intercom system, being told immediately of the outcome.]
Calhoun: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNER OF THE MATCH, AND... _NEW_ WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPION, "LATIN LIGHTNING" AL PEREZ!!!!
[The crowd erupts as Crisper immediately begins to signal for people to come out to the parking lot, as neither Al Perez, whose arm is draped over the chest of Harbinger, nor the man of the Shadows is moving that much on the ground.]
Ralph: What a win!! And we've got a new champion!! Bradley: At what price? I mean how much does he think he's going to be able to do with broken arms, broken legs, and broken ribs? MAIN EVENT — THE MASSACRE MATCH [A multi-team steel-cage submission match, with the reigning World Champion Fury working as a special guest referee rather than a competitor:] Ralph: Fury and Keeblor both checking!! And both Thrasher and Duncan in trouble...and .. they're submitting!! They're both submitting!! What the...
Bradley: What?? You mean we don't have a winner?? [… a dispute over which submission came first delays the decision …] Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, a member of both teams submitted at the end of this match. Ralph: We've got a draw?? Calhoun: However, according to the senior official in this match, Calhoun, they were not simultaneous submissions!! Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match, the team of...
Calhoun: Jude St. Claire, Johnny Detson, Marshall Duncan, and Mark Rock, THE SHOWMEN!!!
