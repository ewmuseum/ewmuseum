WWO bio card — Matt Cole

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Cole Wrestler Name: Matt Cole Date of Birth: Height: 6'5 Weight: 245 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Crossroads, MS Entrance Music: "Got No Shame" by Brother Cane Manager: Tank Holland Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Cole tends to wear normal biker clothes. Anything from dark colored collared shirts hen he is dressed up to Harley T-shirts when he is relaxing and casual. He also wears a Long leather coat with his Club's Symbol on the back. He wears Cowboy boots with spurs or biker boots. Ring Appearance Cole wears a blue collared workshirt(cut off at the bicep) Cole has a Black chinese dragon tattoo on his right bicep and a Marine Devil dog tattoo on his left bicep. On his right forearm he has blue thunderbolt tattoo. his wrists are taped and he is wearing a pair of padded fingerless black gloves. He wears his long black hair in a braided ponytail when in the ring he also has a goatee. Under his right eye he has a black tear tattoo. Over the blue shirt he wears a black leather vest with the Sword and blood circle symbol of his club. He wears slighly ripped blue jeans and black kneehigh marine jumpboots. Psychological Makeup Cole is a Lone wolf. Tank is his only "family" except for his club "The BloodCircle". Out of the ring, Cole is the kinda guy you can get drunk with and doesnt mind hanging with fans. He has been known to go to the nearest bar after the event and buy a couple of rounds and shoot the breeze with a couple fans. Cole dislikes the "superstar" image a lot. He feels that the fans pay him by showing up after a hard week of work. In a funny way the fans consider him "Their fist" since he goes after heels and faces that are too full of themselves that seems "above" them. Cole has never forgot his roots, On his offdays he works at his garage In Crossroads. The Garage is the last bit of his father he has and he likes being there. In the ring, Cole is focused and he is there to fight. He will take bumps and even blade if need be. Cole's moves do change from time to time to give the fans a show. Cole is a man that fights his own fights and he hates run ins. If Cole sees a face getting jumped by a lot of folks he will hit the ring and try to clean house, even if he doesnt like the face in question. Cole is a hard worker in the ring and is good seller. Since cole by nature is not a highflyer he tends to not go for top rope moves except a 2nd rope axe kick and this move can be done with a chair....one of the few in the biz that can do the move perfect. Strengths Hands of Stone: Cole is very dangerous with his fists. They are quick as twin vipers and hit like a brick wall. Cole is one of the most deadliest punchers in the business. All on One: Because of his rough life in a cycle club, Cole has a kind of sixth sense about more than one guy trying to jump him. If faced with a two or three on one fight Cole knows how to get the upper hand. Tough as nails: Cole has lived a hard life in and out of the ring. Cole can suffer a lot of punishment either by submission holds or gettting beat down. Weaknesses Hatred of Big mouths: Cole is an action not words kind of man. Cole doesnt like wrestlers talking about how good they are or what a force they are in wrestling or any other if that crap. Cole does tend to go after them harder.and sometimes that cause him to loss focus or get Dqed. Loner: Cole may be a common guy but he is not easy to befriend or earn respect from. save his cycle club or family Cole tends to go at things alone. Mat wrestling: Cole does is fighting on his feet and that leaves him open for a weakness to mat wrestling. Biographical Information Matt Cole is the only surviving member of his family. His Mother and father have been dead since he was 10 but before their deaths, John, Matt's father instilled in his baby boy the love of the open road and of freedom. That left the care of him to his older brother Darren. Darren was a Marine and took Matt to every post he was stationed. Cole got in trouble where he went. Everything for stealing cars to fighting in public. His rebel blood was hard to cool but in japan he found a place his fists could be used. When Darren was stationed in Japan, Matt turned 16. Darren took Matt to his first shootfight and after that Matt found his talent. With his brother's recon training and Cole's talent with his fists Matt was a sucess in the ring and seemed to clean up his act. In 1991 Darren was sent to Desert Storm to do his duty. Sgt Darren Aaron Cole was killed in action when he tried to save a tank crew that was hit by a rocket. Among the tank crew he saved was an older Sgt by the name of Bill "Tank" Holland. Holland swore that he would pay Darren back . Matt took the death of his brother hard. Cole when back home to the U.S. and into wrestling. Cole lost his rebel soul and became the "baby face". Cole at this time would job for milk money and had no pride like he did when he was shooting. Tank tracked Cole down to a small indy fed in the midwest. Tank saw what Cole was and was sick to his stomach. This couldn't be the brother of a piss and oil marine...So Tank did what any Marine would do... he gave the boy a dose of reality. Tank took Cole to a place he hasn't been in years, His father's garage. In that small garage in Crossroads Mississippi Matt Cole found his soul again. Tank trained Cole in the hard and fast style of street fighting. Cole, under the eye of Tank, fought in any fight he could ( think of the fights in the underground circuit ie Lionheart and such). Cole was in the WWA for some time where very few could match the tireless will and stone fists of Cole. Cole's last bout was with the current WWA Champion ,Bedrock. Cole was hurt bad but so was Bedrock, and after that match Cole had to find his fire again because Bedrock knocked it out of him. Cole told his friend and mentor that he had to go an "find his fist again". Cole traveled the world and took fights where he could. [name withheld] heard that Cole left the WWA and went to search out Cole to bring him into the WWO. He found Cole in a small farming village in South Korea. [name withheld] asked this fellow Mississippian to join his rebirth of a fed. Cole was going not to join until he heard all of the talk coming from its "superstars" and Cole looked [name withheld] in the eyes and said " Ok [name withheld]...I am in." Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier None Martial Arts Excellent Power Fair Technical None Submission Good Cheating Very Good Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability Fair Stamina Good Ring Awareness Excellent Teamwork Top Wrestling Moves Muay Thai knees to the face or stomach Hi impact DDT German suplex into the corner body blow combo ending with a hard right to the jaw in the corner short arm clothesline axe kick (can be done from 2nd rope) Nosebreaker: Cole drives the nose of his foe into shoulder as he drops to both knees) Road Rash Slam: face first full nelson drop to the mat...and then Cole rubs their face in the Mat for further damage) KnuckleDuster: Cole sits on his foe chest and rains fists on his face till the ref backs Cole off or his foe submits) Suicide Shift: reverse brainbuster DDT Gearjammer: Cole takes his foe and does one spinning toe hold. When the toe hold is in he jumps up slightly and jams their knee into the mat. Cole will do this move till they submit or is backed off by the ref Finishing Maneuver The Heart Punch: Powerful punch to the heart.
