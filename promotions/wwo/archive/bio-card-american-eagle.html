The American Eagle Wrestler Name: Brian Stephenson Date of Birth: Height: 6'6 Weight: 265 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Independence, Missouri Entrance Music: "Thunderstruck" by Sprung Monkey Manager: Lady Liberty - Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ring Appearance Eagle has a short blonde crew cut and wears patriotic-colored face paint. Eagle wears black wrestling tights with red, white, bue, and gold symbols such as an eagle, a flag, stars, etc. on the sides and back. He now also dons a blue star tattooed on his left shoulderblade. Eagle wears white wrestling boots. Psychological Makeup The attitude of the American Eagle has also changed with the dawn of the new millenium. The world of pro wrestling has changed significantly since his leaving the WWO. The mass populum of wrestling fans now do not want a squeaky-clean hero to adore and love and Eagle recognizes that. Eagle has vowed to remain the American hero, only this time around, he's gonna do it HIS way. Strengths Physical strength -- Eagle at one time claimed to be the strongest man in the WWO; it remains to be seen if he actually is. Regardless, Eagle's strength can allow him to overpower his opponent. The crowd -- Eagle always has and prtobably always will feed off the crowd as good or better than anyone. When the crowd is digging him and really supporting him, he's awful tough to beat. Nothing makes Eagle more upset than letting thousands of American fans watch him lose in HIS country. Arrogance -- Eagle has an arrogance about him that says "Here I am...come and get it if you want it." That's very intimidating. Weaknesses Arrogance -- The same arrogance that can be a strength can also serve as a weakness. Eagle can tend to get involved in situations (especially physical conforntations) he cannot handle all by himself. Also, his strong feelings for his country allow him to involve himself in the business of others who don't appreciate the U.S. of A. Aerial/acrobatic wrestling -- Every now and then, for whatever reason, Eagle can get the idea that he's some kind of aerobatic wrestler and will attempt some radical aerial turnbuckle manuver. This VERY RARELY works. Eagle wrestles at his best when he is on the ground with leverage over his opponent, and he gets himself into to trouble by trying something too risky aerially. Women -- Eagle can interestingly become distracted by women. In fact, the glimpse of a pretty woman may be the one thing that can distract Eagle from his in-ring opponent. Biographical Information The American Eagle (real name Brian Stephenson) was a professional hockey player long before he ever dreamed of professional wrestling. As a top prospect in the minor league system of the Washington Capitals, Stephenson was considered one of hockey's top prospects until a severe knee injury ended his hockey career in 1993. After extensive rehab, Stephenson decided to (half-jokingly, he now admits) pursue a career in his second boyhood love, professional wrestling. Wrestling abroad in Japan, Mexico, and across Europe and South America, Stephenson learned the tricks of the wrestling business. Wrestling under such names as Battlekat, Aaron Ajaxx, and Farmer Ferocious, Stephenson never really caught his break. Stephenson decided in late 1996 to bring his wrestling talents to an American-based wrestling promotion. After some negotiations, Stephenson was offered a contract with his then-tag team partner, "Solid Rock" Rusty Bowman, to join the World Wrestling Organization. Together, Bowman and Stephenson (Eagle) formed the Allies. Although showing much potential as a top-level tag team in the promotion, the tag team never got a chance to succeed, thanks to Stephenson's victory over Christopher Stonebreaker to win the WWO's Extreme Title in only his second WWO match. It marked the first time anywhere that Stpehenson had held pro wrestling gold. Stephenson was also affiliated the prestiguous Continental Connection during his WWO career. As the American Eagle, Stephenson had numerous historic battles with the likes of King $$$ Perfect, "Prime Time" Phill Massacre, Vader X, "Solid Rock" Rusty Bowman, Andros, the Master and Ruler of the World, and Lord Robert d'Artois, whom he defeated in a historic flag match at Season's Beatings in Denver, Colorado in 1997. He then followed up his victory by then winning the New Year's Bash battle royale. Stephenson left the WWO in early 1998 and actually left the world of wrestling. Stephenson and his wife, Karen (known to WWO fans as Lady Liberty, Eagle's beautiful valet), settled down and had a child, Brianne, who is now two. Stephenson wroked some as a color analyst for the Washington Capitals. In the ring, the American Eagle has always lived and died by the fans. As a performer whose gimmick was that of an all-out American patriot (Stephenson got the idea from his days playing in the Capitals' farm system), he needed the fans for support. However, upon his pending WWO return, the best way to describe this American Eagle is as the "Eagle for the New Millenium."
