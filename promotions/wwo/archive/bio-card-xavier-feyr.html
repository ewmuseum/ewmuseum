WWO bio card — "Blood Lust" Xavier Feyr

29 May 2003

"Blood Lust" Xavier Feyr Wrestler Name: Dr. Javier Fernandez Date of Birth: Height: 6' Weight: 238 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Parts Unknown Entrance Music: "Down with the Sickness" by Disturbed Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Despite Xavier's average height, he still manages to be very imposing. His physique is very-well defined, and the combination of tattoos of various predators, and numerous scars all over his body make him very intimidating. Xavier knows his appearance is intimidating to most people and plays on it further by dying his hair a crimson red, and leaving it in a tangled mess as if he's just bathed in the blood of some fallen enemy. In contrast he has lucifer-like mustache and goatee that he keeps very well groomed, just to complete that little evil incarnated look. ;P He's often known for having a wide cheshire cat like grin on his face that exposes his canines, giving him a predatory look even when he's in a good mood. Ring Appearance Xavier Feyr wears the same clothes both in and out of the ring. He wears a black wife-beater with black jeans and boots. Over this he wears a black trenchcoat, which he may or may not remove when he enters the match depending on the type of match and, more importantly, how seriously he takes his opponent. He also wears black open-fingered gloves. Outside the ring he almost always wears a pair of mirror shades. Psychological Makeup Strengths Speed: Xavier is incredibly fast, even by cruiser standards. He uses this in a fully offensive manner, hitting from multiple directions and angles in quick succession. Xavier isn't the strongest guy in the fed, but the sheer number of times he can hit consecutively is devastating Iron Willed: Xavier isn't immune to pain, but he's certantly relentless. Xavier is very VERY good at pushing pain to the back of his mind, so that he can press on when others could hardly even move. As a result he always seems to have a little something left to kick-out or fight off the submission hold. Getting a legitimate submission out of Xavier Feyr is ALMOST impossible. Eye of the Storm: Xavier thrives in the middle of chaos. The more chaotic the situation in a match, the more it goes in Xavier's favor. In a truly anarchic environment it's survival of the fittest, and Xavier is a survivor. When things get out of control Xavier always seems to be several steps ahead of everybody else, and revleing in the carnage. Weaknesses Blood Lust: When a match starts to get really bloody, Xavier may lose control of himself, and go on a rampage of destruction. He will attack whoever is bleeding the most, or whoever gets in his way. There is no exception for allies or referees. Until this rage dissipates he will not use any move that requires finesse. (IE: No technical holds.) He will also not attempt any pin-falls, nor will he deliberately attempt a submission during this time. After a while Xavier shakes off this mindless rage, but he may already have been disqualified by this time. Warrior Spirit: Xavier Feyr sees this as a contest to prove who the better man is, and as result disdains any actions that he feels bring this into question. He does not tolerate outside interference on either side, except to eliminate existing interference and may actually attack someone attacking his opponent on his behalf. He will NEVER under any circumstances attempt a pinning combination move, as he considers it the cowards way out. (No sunset flips, school boy roll-ups, etc.) This last part gives him a disadvantage against skilled technical wrestlers. Doesn't Pace Himself: Xavier's relentless style can take it's toll on him. While he may be hard to finish off, he does have a tendency to wind himself. As a result he has to stop and catch his breath every so often, which can give his opponent an opening. Biographical Information That Xavier Feyr ever ended up a wrestler would be a suprise to virtually everyone who knew him prior. He was a professor of Anthropology. Like many in his field, he spent his time teaching to earn up the money to do his research into the study of other cultures, in his case many now extinct cultures. Xavier also spent some time living among some of the native peoples of South America. Everything was turned upside-down after he failed to return from his studies of one particular, very obscure people. When some colleagues of his sought him out, they found that Xavier had assumed leadership of the people as a "God king" of sorts. Since birth Xavier had a slight defect which made the pupil's of his eyes narrower, making them somewhat like those of a cat. This isolated culture saw this as a sign of divinity in him (The son of the jaguar was the closest translation anyone could get), and Xavier, it would seem, had let it go to his head. In an attempt to "save" their former colleague from himself, Xavier was taken back to the United States by force. During the trip he severely injured several of his former friends, and upon return he was quickly institutionalized. After several years in this asylum, he was released into the custody of a then well known, but now defunct fed known as UEW. This foolish attempt to boost ratings backfired when it was learned that Lilith, Xavier's psychiatrist who had been brought in to supervise him, began encouraging his vicious streak. Unfortunately, it would seem the asylum was not anxious to take him back, as Xavier continued to leave a sickening number of broken opponents in his wake, ending countless careers during his short span of a little over a year in the fed. Spending most of his time in UEW's Hardcore Division, he effectively dominated it, applying his only rule to everyone else in the division whether they liked it or not... SURVIVAL OF THE FITTEST. With the closing of the fed, however, Xavier was sent back to the asylum, and once again locked away from the world. Or at least he was until someone decided to buy up his old contract...
