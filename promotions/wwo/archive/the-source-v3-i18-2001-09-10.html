Props to Josh Yetman and [name withheld] for contributing stuff this week, the rest of you are cordially invited to burn in hell :) I know this issue is somewhat bare-bones, but I'm a busy man between school and valuable time spent on seeing how much further I can go over the top in my character's feud with Craven, getting drunk, watching football, and figuring out how to be even more of a fantasy football god than before. If you would like to fix this situation by contributing an article of your own, or would just like to kvetch and whine pointlessly like my good friend [name withheld], the address for stuff is [detail withheld] . -------------------------------- RUMORS by Rumor Will -A just-recently-formed stable may be on the verge of breaking up already -One of the men in the World title match may be retiring soon -At least two more War Games matches will be announced this coming Showcase -The WWO may be, yet again, negotiating to take over yet another federation. -The UEWar Games match has rankled a lot of feathers backstage among the old line WWOers. Chris Stringer is said to be among the most pissed off, due to the fact he feels the UEW faction, as well as Wuzho, are intruding on what he believes to be his rightful place as the center of the WWO's attention. Some quotes recently heard in the backstage area (attribution withheld to protect my sources.) "If Fucking Wuzho wasn't involved, the UEW guys wouldn't be getting the time of day. Stone will bend over and spread his cheeks for Wuzho, whether it's right or wrong, and everyone knows it." "I don't have any problem with the UEW guys. Hell, they've worked their asses off since they've been here, and they've all got the talent. But Stone, for whatever reason, is insisting on forcing the invasion angle down everyone's throats, and NO ONE is buying it." "If it wasn't for Extreme and Wuzho, those guys would be jerking the curtain on Showcase, even if you put them in a cage with a wild bear." "The sad thing is, these guys didn't _need_ an invasion angle to get over. Extreme is top shelf no matter where you put him. Hopper's come alive, even if he insists on living in the past a bit. Lewis, Douglas, Parker, Marley and Ace are all solid. I wish they would have gotten the chance to get over on their own. Because, once this 'invasion' ends, where do they go? Extreme goes to the top of the card, and the rest go back to where they were before the invasion angle, and have to build themselves up again." "Invasions suck. Period. It sucked when we tried to do it, it didn't come _close_ to working when we tried to do it, and it sucks now." -A third type of gimmick match may be added to the Anarchy card, but some members of the WWO front office feel this would be overkill and make the gimmick matches seem less special. -One member of Chris Stringer's entourage is angry with Stringer over a betting dispute. The Hot List is the top ten personalities in the WWO, according to their total amount of Hot Five points. If you'd like to vote for the Hot Five, send a list of your top five WWO personalities or angles to [detail withheld] Adding comments to your votes is not required, but appreciated. Some of the point totals may not exactly match the total sum of all the previous hot fives. This is because of my back and forth method of tabulating points. I've had a hard time with votes that can count towards more than one person's Hot Five points total. The problem was in votes that read like: 3. Quesadaville vs. Operation Scorched Earth. When this feud was in its prime, that included Dark Soul, Miguel Quesada, Apex, Chris Stringer, Major Damage, and their assortment of managers. For me to take the sum of everyone in this feud and divide three by that sum, it would've been a negligible total for each wrestler. Seeing that as the case, I've decided to only split votes for votes that list exact wrestlers. For example: 4. Ultimate Thrasher/William Craven This would be a point splitting vote, where each wrestler gets half of the normal points. In this case, being 4th ranked, Thrasher and Craven would both get one point. Something like: 5. Greatness Personified vs. Pain, Dark Soul, and Union Jack Is no longer a point splitting vote. Greatness personified is now a stable, and gets points on the Hot Five as a separate entity. In this case, I'd take the total points (1) and divide them in half. Giving Greatness Personified 1/2 a point and adding the new group (Pain/DS/UJ) to my Hot Five list and give it a single point. It should be noted that: Miguel Quesada/Nate KnowItAll/Vegas Connection vs. Pain, Dark Soul, and Union Jack ... would still not be a point splitting vote, as the combination of the first group of wrestlers makes up the stable Greatness Personified. That information may or may not effect the way you vote, but I thought I'd pass it your way anyhow. This did effect the way I added the vote totals up, and it caused me to make a few slight modifications in the overall total. Anyhow, enough of this point calculation talk.
