WWO Recruitment Drive

27 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In April 2000, I made one of the toughest decisions I had to in this hobby, I took a break from the game. It was something I enjoyed, and due to real life, I was forced to take a long break from the hobby. When the WWO closed, there was an avenue that closed up with us. For a long time, the WWO prided itself on not depending on your status in other federations as to whether or not you were acceptable to the WWO roster. If you had a decent grasp of the english language, and were willing to put in a little effort, the WWO was more than happy to give you a chance to try out for a roster slot with the organization. In August 2000, a number of handlers had asked me to reopen the organization, but due to real life committments I had to continue to put off their request, although I had been missing the fed-heading routine. In January of 2001, I _had_ to get back in the hobby and announced the official reopening of the World Wrestling Organization. And while the WWO has kept some of it's "personal stars" such as Tyrone Hayes, Apex, Johnny Detson, Ultimate Thrasher, and Miguel Quesada to name a few, I've tried to insure that the one thing that made the WWO stand out was there... equal opportunity for any handler who was willing to put some effort into their roleplaying. Well, once again, we are "opening" that channel to those handlers who may have not been declared as the "best" to compete in other federations. As such, ladies and gentlemen, the World Wrestling Organization is on a recruitment drive. We've been an e-fed that has been in operation since late 1995, early 1996 (not counting the 8 months I took off due to real life problems in 2000). under a grand total of two different presidents, with myself being the co-president since 1996 and sole president of the organization since 1997. We're not a federation that will promise you titles or title shots, but what we do promise is a lot of friendly fun, and for those who are strong participants, plenty of fun storylines and enjoyment. The WWO was a regularly recognized name in some of the best e-feds prior to our closure; and with you on board, who knows, we might just get back to the top again. I'll be the first to admit that our application process is not one of the simplest in the hobby, but our staff goes over a lot of details in your character to help both yourself and us get the most out of your professioanl wrestler. For those of you who are interested in seeing some of our action, you can check out our website at http://wwowrestling.virtualave.net . All of our old cards from 1997 on can be found on the site in our card archives, and if you'd like to talk to some of our handlers to get their opinions on why you should join the WWO, drop by starchat on IRC almost any night from 6PM CST and on, and you should find someone there who's willing to explain why the WWO has long proclaimed to be "Simply the Best'. The WWO has proclaimed itself as being one of the federation that "made the history of the sport" and we'd like you to consider us as the federation that will "redefine the future of the sport". If you're looking for a federation that has a mix of old and new school of wrestling, and you're willing to try to put some effort into your writings, perhaps you're looking for the World Wrestling Organization?
