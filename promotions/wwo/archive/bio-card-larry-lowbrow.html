WWO bio card — Larry Lowbrow

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Larry Lowbrow Real Name: Larry Chesterton Height: 6'9 Weight: 331 Date of Birth: Apr 1, 1974 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: Reno, NV Entrance Music: "Viva Las Vegas" sung by Lester Leary (Lowbrow's Partner) Manager: Rimshot Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Like a huge Joe Piscopo with straight hair. Ring AttireFaded brown leather jacket, white T-shirt, faded jeans Psychological Makeup Strenghts Unpredictability - He can keep even the most astute wrestler guessing. Iron Will - you'll hardly ever find this guy submitting to anything; Surprising agility and flexibility for a man his size. Weaknesses Extraordinarily big shoulders make it easier to pin him; Easily distracted by a pair of big breasts (stand-up comedy life is lonely). Not big on ring geography. "I never studied geography in high school." Biographical Information Larry is a comedian, but hates the observational humour of late. All his jokes are generally lame and quite bawdy. Perfect for casino entertainment, especially where people have to go outside the city limits to buy sex acts. If anyone tries to heckle or outwit him he resorts to mother jokes. The Vegas Connection have been all over the map, from the ECCWF to the STWF to the MBC to the PMCW/RCCW/BDCW (which kept changing its name for some reason). They won belts in all but the ECCWF which closed after they had about one match (coincidence?) Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Fair High-Flier Poor Martial Arts None Power Very Good Technical Good Submission Poor Cheating Fair Brawling/Hardcore Very Good Escapability Excellent Stamina Good Ring Awareness None Teamwork Excellent Top Wrestling Moves Call for the test-o'-strength Lariat Belly-to-belly suplex 4-count-chokehold Big chops (Whoo!), Fistdrop Head scissor submission with the old hold-the-rope-then-deny-it bit Bearhug Death Valley Driver "I've Got a Million of 'em" Sleeperhold Chokeslam!Chokeslam!Chokeslam! (TM ) Finishing Maneuver The Punchline - Lowbrow lifts opponent in elevated belly-to-belly position at top rope, then does simplified moonsault/superplex leap so opponent lands on head - preceded by saying "Stop me if you've heard this one before..." Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: Vegas Connection Tag Team Partner: "Lounge Lizard" Lester Leary Tag Team Moves Double DDT spike piledriver spike facebuster Finishing Move The Vegas Deathwish - involves a quick tag - Lester puts on the Boston Crab and Larry comes in for a Camel Clutch. It's close quarters but it can be done. Especially effective on really tall guys.
