Thanksgiving Special 1999 (excerpt)

25 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. Three titles changed hands on this card, broadcast from the Delta Center in Salt Lake City, UT, and posted to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 1 December 1999. Only the four segments below are reproduced here in full. The rest of the broadcast remains held and is not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, Dark Soul, defends against "Smooth as Silkk" Miguel Quesada:] MR: Silkk spinning himself over into a full vertical... DDT!! Silkk got it that time! SB: Third time is the charm. MR: Silkk planting Dark Soul with the DDT!! And the cover!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREEE!!!! He got it!! Silkk got him with the DDT! SB: The one armed bandit strikes. MR: Hirosaki calling for the bell, and there goes the title into the ring! Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match, and NEW WWO TV CHAMPION... SILKK!!! WWO UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson, defends against "Latin Lightning" Al Perez, a masked ally throwing Hudson the title belt itself mid-match:] MR: Hudson nailing Perez across the back of the head with the belt and now dropping it on the canvas. He's dragging Perez to his feet. He's bent him, the arms are hooked .. NO!
SB: This is just too good! Hudson nailing Perez with the Lightning Strike right down onto the US belt! Poetic justice!!!
[…] ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! SB: This one's all over and Hudson has held onto that US Championship belt. Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match, and STILL YOUR UNITED STATES CHAMPION, "THE PRODIGY" TRACY HUDSON!!! WWO INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPIONSHIP [A three-way match: reigning champion "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson against "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian and "Playboy" Jude St. Claire, with St. Claire's own interference backfiring onto his stablemate Detson:] MR: St. Claire into the back of Detson!! St. Claire just nailing his own partner!! St. Claire just driven into his own partner!! And that'll force the sleeper break!!!
[…] MR: And who's fault would it be for St. Claire getting into the ring in the first place? And why did St. Claire wait to leap.
SB: That's just it!! He didn't!! He was pushed!! […] Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match.... [The crowd eruptions continue, and outside the ring, St. Claire is pulling himself away from the ring railing.]
Calhoun: And NEW WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPION, "MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN!!! WWO WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP — NO DISQUALIFICATION [The reigning champion, Ultimate Thrasher, defends against Roundhouse (billed "the Big Unit") in a brawl that spills through the arena's back hallways:] Calhoun: SALT LAKE CITY, ARE WE READY FOR THE MAIN EVENT??? Calhoun: The following contest is set for one fall, and the fall can take... Calhoun: And it is for the WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION'S WORLD... […] Calhoun: [Mine by The Hoodoo Gurus begins to play sending the already…] Calhoun: THE NEW WWO WORLD CHAMPION!!! "THE BIG UNIT"...
