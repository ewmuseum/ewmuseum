I'm Donny McClamrock, the star intern here at the WWO. I do it all, video editing, ring assembly, farm and market reports, accounting, etc., and I'm adding the Showcase Review to my resume. So let's get to it, shall we? -- We open with Louie (not Louis) the Lip and Rimshot talking about a career change for the Lip. Hmmmm.... maybe he can book the tag team division? -- And we're LIVE at the Key Arena in Seattle. Mickey and Sam are hosting, as usual. Sam is struggling with wrapping his Christmas gifts, which include some thoughtful presents for the faces in the WWO. Some obligatory shilling, and then.... -- King Tyrone Fuckin' Hayes and Todd come out. Not a bad way to start the show, got the crowd hot right away. Johnstone looks like he raided Glen (not Glenn) Peeps' closet. The Rod gives a brief promo only slightly less offensive than a Klan rally, then turns it over to KTFH. Apex chant almost immediately. KTFH runs down all the people who could legitimately have a shot at beating him. "Tyra" chant, for some reason. Hammond would probably know why. So, he starts talking about the last time he was in Seattle and blaming Stone for screwing him over and makes a Colussus reference -- again, probably something Hammond would know about, before my time and all -- and how he recruited a group of people to make sure he is not "torn down." Apparently, the group is called Upper Crust, hates Mark Stone, and the first member is... Nate "20 Quid" Knowitall. -- New music for Nate! And fog. Lots and lots of fog. And Shelly.... mmmm.... Shelly.... anyway, yeah. He takes credit for running off Lord Keeblor and Union Jack. No, Nate, shitty ratings and merchandise sales ran them off. And for running off Latex Lex, which I've heard actually may have happened. Blames the people for not being behind him, the WWO for not giving him any merchandise (what, he didn't get a cut of the Greatness Personified stuff?), blah blah blah. Then, Tyrone brings out... -- Tracy Hudson, complete with Moongirl. Brief, beautiful promo blaming the fans for destroying the WWO's heroes, and all that's left is the bastards like Upper Crust, but "We're YOUR bastards!" MAJOR heel heat. Damn right. And this just leaves... -- Johnny Detson, with his traditional over the top movie star entrance. Runs down Stoney, claims to have saved the career of the Ultimate Thrasher (um, no, the UTD angle crashed and burned, _Craven_ saved Thrasher, gotta call him on that one). Anyhow, he hates Stone, so do the other guys, and they're not friends, this is strictly about business. Then, The Rod cuts an absolutely vicious promo on Stoney, bringing out Dark Soul and Apex. Yay! Anyway, they challenge Tracy and Detson to a tag match tonight. Works for me. -- Cut to Kimberly Campbell, who is expecting Matt Cole.... but gets the Amazing Romanis, on a pair of cool looking motorbikes. Romanis cut their usual slightly obtuse and entertaining promo, then dash off. -- Tag team match: Final Justice vs. Blood Brothers. Normally, I'd be bitching about having that much non-wrestling stuff at the start of the show, but if it postpones this crap, well, all the better. I hereby embargo any more WWO wrestlers from using "Back in Black" as a theme song. Jesus, Hall looks like shit. Thunderwolf and Barney to start. Old school power offense from Thunderwolf, Barney counters with an inside cradle for 1. More old school stuff, which Sam is getting increasingly bored with. Thunderwolf tags in Thomas, who goes to work on Barney with kicks. Double Flapjack from the Brothers. Crowd not really into this one so far. Big Boot from Thomas, Thunderwolf back in, WOOOO chops. Couple of hip tosses from Thunderwolf, but Barney manages to counter one with a Russian Leg Sweep and get Hall in, finally. Brawling from Hall, a neckbreaker, then more brawling. Sam is complaining about the lack of wrestling, but neither team in here is really known for their workrate. More old school offense from Thunderwolf. Hall resorts to an eye gouge, but continues to job to the technical wizardry that is Michael Thunderwolf. You should have noticed the sarcasm there, because I was laying it on pretty thick. Tag in to Thomas, Nice flip out of a suplex by Hall, tag into Barney... and, as Sam says, "WHAT IS IT WITH THESE DROP TOE-HOLDS?" Thunderwolf back in with a rather odd-looking wristlock suplex... cool. Kind of a chicken-wing suplex gets one. Tag into Thomas, another double flapjack, and Thomas belly-to-bellied his partner onto Barney! Okay, THAT was pretty frickin' cool. Cover gets 2.5 before Hall decides to earn his paycheck. Thomas finishes off a sequence where he worked on Barney's arm with a pumphandle slam, then some nice double team work. Hall tries to interfere, but eats a spinebuster by Thomas. Barney with some weak offense... MY GOD WHEN ARE THEY GOING TO FINISH THIS THING???? Finally! Thunderwolf with a flying legdrop to get the duke. I'm warming up to the BB, but this was WAAAAAY too long for a JTTS squash. Rating -- 1/2* -- Louie (not Louis) the Lip and tries to threaten Stonebreaker into giving him a job as a ref. Feeling out stuff to start so Sam can get out his quota of homosexual innuendo. Chicken-wing into a full-nelson faceslam by Patch to start. Weird looking judo takedown by MA, and he takes time to showboat, allowing Patch to nail him with a clothesline and put him in a Texas Cloverleaf. Ouch. Shooting Star Press by Patch _would_ have gotten three, but he pulled him up. Hey, if it means MA takes more punishment, fine by me. MA in the Tree of Woe, mudhole stomping from Patch, Patch slides out of the ring, grabs MA (who is still hanging in the turnbuckle) by the _head_, and rams his head into the post a couple of times. Assassin took a few nasty bumps, and Patch looks like he doesn't have very much (if any) ring rust to knock off. Rating -- **. Don't worry Apex, I couldn't figure out the answer to that riddle, either. Wonder if Dumars will make it out for the match, or if that was to write him out of the storylines? There's been rumors he's out the door. -- Singles match: Ace of Hearts vs. Steve Dumars (maybe). Okay, can I ask one fucking question here? I can? Good. Chops and choking from Ace, but Dumars fights back, and a clothesline forces Ace to roll out of the ring. Good, solid, OSE style heel tactics with Ace and Candy Kisses with Ace choking Dumars on the ropes and Candy slapping him. Dumars comes back with a low blow, then a clothesline, and we have our first WWO-patented and copyrighted double knockout spot. Both men get up, toe-to-toe brawling, then Dumars ducks a punch and hits Ace with a kneelift/bulldog combo for his first real offense of the match. Belly-to-belly by Dumars, and Ace tries to escape, but Dumars catches him by the leg and drags him into a Knuckle Duster. Tilt-a-whirl slam gets a solid 2. Overhead head-and-arm suplex by Dumars, but then Candy Kisses trips him again. Dumars is out of the ring after Candy Kisses... grabs her by the arm... and then slaps her on the ass! Extreme comes out to keep Legba from slitting Ace's throat or something, and they brawl up the aisle, until Eric Stryker comes out of nowhere to lay out Legba, which incidentally starts the TOTA title match. Not a bad exit for Dumars if he's gone, and it was a decent big man match, but I'm still not sold on Ace yet. Rating -- **1/2 -- TOTA title match: Papa Legba vs. Eric Stryker. Stryker dominates Legba in the aisle and fires him back toward the ring. Stryker continues dominating Legba in the corner. Irish whip reversed, but Legba eats an Idiot Charge and a lariat. Damn, that was impressive. Got a two-count, and the crowd, Stryker and Sam were just about as shocked as I was. Legba tries for a tie-up, but Stryker thinks better of it and catches him with a few quick punches. Another charge from Legba, this time countered with a nice belly-to-belly suplex from Stryker. Stryker drops the elbow for 1. Then a snap suplex from Stryker gets 1 1/2. I like Legba, but I wouldn't mind seeing Stryker come out of here with the belt. Headlock from Stryker while he pounds on Legba's neck. At least when Stryker uses rest holds, he does something else while he's got it locked on to get heat. Bradley tries to take all the credit for the WWO's increase in ratings since 1997 before finally conceding "Maybe Tyrone and Stringer helped a little, too." Legba tries to push Stryker off, but Stryker just rides it into a bulldog and follows up with a ballstomp. Ouchie. Diving headbutt from Stryker gets two. Double underhook power bomb, followed up by the Wall Street Crash for 2.99999 and Legba's looking to be in real trouble. Legba selling really well tonight, incidentally. An enraged Stryker fires Legba outside, then goes to town with a chair. Of course, Crisper wouldn't do something logical like order a DQ at this point now, would he? Stryker tries to whip Legba into the guard rail, but Legba reverses and absolutely _mashes_ Stryker with a clothesline. Stryker comes back with a ballshot and tosses Legba into the ring, but Legba hits him in the midsection with a shoulder then brings him in with a brainbuster. Leg drop followed by a knuckle duster, and Stryker's in trouble and the crowd is into it. Crisper for some reason notices Legba choking Stryker, and Legba threatens him before spinning around just in time to take Stryker's head off with a clothesline. Double-hand chokeslam by Legba, and he refuses to go for the pin, just whipping him from pillar to post before planting him with a MASSIVE spinebuster for 2.7. Nice sequence of backdrop counters and reversals ends up with Stryker taking a Ragga Bomb, then Stryker misses a wild right and takes an atomic drop. Legba with a cover (and not a one-footed one) for the obligatory three-count... .... and Stryker gets a foot on the ropes!!! The crowd and Legba are both having a hairy conniption. Which, in this case, is an excuse to bring out Alex Extreme to get a little revenge on Legba. Roots from Legba on Stryker, and he goes for the Voodoo Spell From Hell, but Extreme nails him with the ring bell. EVERYONE is out cold. Then Legba _labels_ Stryker with a chairshot -- which Crisper somehow managed to not see -- for the win. A bit of an overbooked mess at the end, and I could have done without all the voodoo pyro, but it was a very good match all-in-all. Stryker's ready for some gold, though, and it won't be long before Legba's challenging for the IP belt. And if this set up Stryker/Hopper and Legba/Extreme, well, you could do a hell of a lot worse than that.
