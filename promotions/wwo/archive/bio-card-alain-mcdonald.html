WWO bio card — "Great White" Alain MacDonald

"Great White" Alain MacDonald Wrestler Name: Alain MacDonald Date of Birth: Height: 6'9 Weight: 325 Alignment: Hometown: "The Great Country of Canada" Entrance Music: Oh Canada Manager: Don Cameron Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Short blonde hair with thick dark goatee. Very muscular, but his body is well rounded, carrying all his weight. Ring Appearance Blank white t-shirt with a pair of red tights. Index and middle finger on his right punching hand is bandaged. Psychological Makeup MacDonald uses force and pure intimidation to get what he wants. If you don't get afraid of him raising his voice at you and looking at you with those wide eyes, he will make sure you are afraid once you stand into the ring with him. Once known as "The Canadian Grim Reaper", MacDonald wants you to always remember standing in the ring with him. Strengths Strength Experience Weaknesses Too much heart Sometimes foolhardy Biographical Information The Canadians started wrestling together in 1992 after both men ended their singles careers in the now-defunct WCWA. Both men feuded against each other in that league and gained respect for each other. They changed their name to Les Canadians, one word French the other English to show their loyalty to both English and French Canada. Denis, known for always fighting men bigger than he, is commonly known as The Little Big Man. He goes up against incredible odds (like fighting and beating MacDonald for the WCWA World Title) and either comes out a winner or gaining the respect of his opponent. He is now 37 years old. In the NCCW he feuded briefly with Lew Spectre and the rest of The Firm, but was ultimately humilated in Lew's hometown of Miami, where Denis challenged Spectre. He never got his chance against Spectre, the man who held the NCCW TV Title for over a year. However, once the CCW opened up, Denis got his chance and was destroyed by Spectre, in a match he wasn't prepared for. Set up by his former manager Don Cameron (who made a heel turn) Denis was sent to the hospital, with Alain MacDonald at his side. He hadn't been heard from since. MacDonald is the monster. He has turned on Denis a few times during their career, almost crippling him the last time. Only 32 years old now, he was once a member of NCCW's Tag Team Champions Dominatrix with Angus MacDougall, the top heel team in the NCCW, and NCCW Hall of Famers. He is known for his size and strength as well as his ring smarts. He was the faux Don Cameron in the NCCW after Cameron disappeared for awhile, and has always feuded with The Dominator, never being very close friends with him.
