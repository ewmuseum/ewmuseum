WWO bio card — "Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada Real Name: Miguel Quesada Residence: Miami, FL Height: 6' Weight: 225 lbs Date of Birth: 12/19/79 Manager: Cristine - Managerial Profile Entrance Theme Music: "Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top Alignment: Selfish Physical Appearance Psychological Makeup Strengths Intelligence Miguel is a brilliant wrestler. He knows when to attack and where. Showing a weakness to him is like a shark smelling blood. He basically paints a target on the weakness and aims straight for it, whether it be physically or mentally. His Mouth Miguel loves to run his mouth. Constantly. Even during the matches he continuously talks trash and throws insults at his opponents which irritates them more. This usually helps him in a fight especially when the opponent is too irritated or angry to concentrate on how to catch him while he's using hit and run tactics. Quickness Miguel may not be the fastest man in the fight, but he's quick enough to get away when needed. If fighting a bigger man, he can easily avoid the opponent. Against guys his size, he uses the quickness to avoid high flying moves and the likes. Weaknesses Ego Miguel hates to be shown up. Simple as that. He gets real frustrated in matches that aren't going his way. Being shown up makes him resort to cheating more often just to get the win and prove he's the better man. Sense of Humor Miguel takes only a few things seriously, and his opponents aren't any of those few things. His opponents tend to be just big jokes to him, which sometimes tends to have him lose a few matches due to underestimating. His Mouth Sometimes, Miguel's mouth gets him in more trouble than he hoped for. He knows he's doing it to irritate, but occasionally, he's caught by the opponent, and, especially when making fun of a bigger man, the results aren't always pretty. Biographical Information Miguel Quesada was basically born into wrestling. His father was not only a professional wrestler, but was abusive to Miguel's mother and older brother. His older brother became a professional wrestler by the name of Apex and used to lash out his aggressions at his younger brother just for being there. Once Miguel separated from his brother in Miami, he tried life on his own. Hanging out with the locals down there and trying his best to fit in eventually led him into somewhat of a wannabe gang life. He'd start hanging around any gang that would actually let him stay there without beating the life out of him. Eventually, he found one that didn't mind him constantly being around them all the time. He even met a girl in the gang, named Naza. Miguel tried his hardest to impress Naza, bringing her flowers that he saw growing in other people's yards and scrounging up change to buy the occasional candy bar (no one ever said he was rich). After months of trying and trying, Naza eventually started liking Miguel and went out with him on a date. The next day, everyone had dubbed Miguel Silkk, a name which he loved hearing and probably even forgot that he even had the name Miguel Quesada. Silkk had forgotten all about his past, all about his abusive father, his torn mother, and his insane brother..he had a new family now. But all that didn't last. One day while watching tv with Naza and a few friends, Silkk was flipping through channels and saw someone familiar. With a closer look, he recognized the man as his brother, Apex. Apex was wrestling somebody, and kept talking to some imaginary person in the corner. Silkk was shocked to say the least. He sat there and watched his brother's insanity lead him into the wrestling life their father was obsessed with. Silkk didn't want the same thing happening to his brother, especially in that state of mind. So Silkk took Naza, and they drove to find his brother. Upon finding him in a federation called the World Wrestling Organization, Silkk knew the only way to help his brother get out of the wrestling business, was to get in it himself. Apex had probably forgotten he had a brother, and Silkk knew Apex would only listen if he were talking to another wrestler. Maybe Apex did remember about Miguel though, and wouldn't want his baby brother, the kid he basically raised himself, getting hurt. Silkk entered the WWO and immediately went to help his brother, but Apex had bigger things going for him at the time, and almost completely ignored the new arrival. Eventually, Silkk got his attention by chronic insults and reminders of their childhood, but it also got the attention of someone else. Dark Soul, a man who knew Apex very well, didn't really take kindly to Silkk, especially once Silkk's insults actually started firing at Dark Soul. Once Silkk won the Television title from Dark Soul, he basically forgot about why he had joined the WWO in the first place. The wrestling life had done the inevitable, and had taken over his life as well. After a fallout with his friends back home in Miami, Silkk changed his name to "Smooth As Silk" Miguel Quesada. His feud with Dark Soul started getting more heated and eventually brought in another man by the name of "Epic" Eric Travers to the mix. Miguel took on all challengers for his title, but never took his mind off of his main foes. He completely forgot about his brother and why he drove all the way to the WWO in the first place. Then, the WWO closed it's doors and Miguel returned home with his Television title and Naza and tried to regain his old life back. Unfortunately, things didn't go so well at home. All his old friends had abandoned him. They kept saying he thought he was better than them and just laughed when he tried talking to any of them. Even Naza left him, going back with the original gang and leaving Miguel all alone. But not all hope was lost for Miguel. He found another girl by the name of Cristine who he immediately fell in love with. Now she accompanies him in his return to the World Wrestling Organization and to reunite with his older brother..to return to the life that he couldn't escape if he tried.
