Rumour has it that a WWO-known federation could be a targetted WWO acquisition. -Ilya Olenskaya's rendition of Cherry Pie has broken into the Billboard Top 40 in the Ukraine record sales. -The front offices are not thrilled with the "hesistancy" of current WWO members to compete with some of the UEW-signees. - Look for another stable to show signs of it's forming in the upcoming weeks, and a champion may be involved. - Could WWO commissioner Christopher Stonebreaker be stepping down from his position? Guzzling free beer there and THEN staggering over to Bradley Center to see Superstar Showcase, live. Worth a two-hour drive up the Tri-State Tollway, says I. The crowd was super-hot there as well, much more than in Indy a couple weeks back. -Quesada-mart sells a bulk selection of the idiots who just left the fed...but problems arise when John Collins can't figure out if he's Tucker or Eden, Charlie doesn't know if he's Ugly American, Lord Keeblor, Michael Priest, or Mike Dugan. Christ, how do these guys manage how to figure out new ways to outdo themselves every week? -We're LIVE from the Sam Bradley Center in Milwaukee, WI. Your hosts are Sam Bradley and Mickey Ralph. Card shilling follows....and then out comes Todd, dressed as tastefully as ever and drawing HUGE heel heat before he even says anything. As a rule, anyone ever associated with the WIF and/or NLWA and not named "Ultimate Thrasher" is absolutely HATED in the midwest, for various reasons. And Todd cranks it up a notch tonight, with an ultra- rambling, obscenity-laden, vintage 1998 Johnstone promo. People were actually trying to attack him on his way back to the locker room, something I haven't seen in quite a while. -UCD is in their apartment, with an INTELLIVISION~! and S-RON~! Evil fire fighters try to get them to join the dark side of the fire fighting force, and destroy their apartment. *Sniff* Another Intellivision wasted, but at least they name-dropped me. And at least they're like, alive again and stuff. -The Nobodies get a new dressing room, inside some psycho janitor's closet -And finally, es tiempo para la lucha libre. -No, wait, Les Canadians come out first and kill the crowd with one of the most fundamentally flawed in-rings I've ever seen in my life. Seriously, is there supposed to be something funny about a "I challenged the Romanis" t- shirt? And why would a CANADIAN compliment Milwaukee, or ANYWHERE in America, on its beer? Isn't one of the few things that keeps Canada from just being an oversized version of Vermont good beer? Coyote love, wow, what a fucking comedian Marc Denis is. Nice Lanny McDonald mustache though. I'm probably the only American who even knows who Lanny McDonald is and can name all three teams he played for, but whatever. With the crowd sufficiently dead, they introduce Colton, for the -Four Corners JTTS match: "Riverside Heatkiller" Chris Colton (w/Les Canadians) vs. Eric Bradley vs. Jonas King vs. "Sweet as Silkk....I mean Sugar" Ramon Que...Garcia: Oh, THIS should rock my world. They flip and flop and hit highspots no one pops for and Holy SHIT, is that Tyrone Parker and Brandon Franklin and Jim Lewis and Steve Douglas? And are they dressed in....UEW shirts. Oh no, no, no, no, NO. This is my nightmare. This is every sane WWO fan's nightmare. They clear the ring of the deadwood, then get on the mic and clear things up.....they're here to invade. Just what we fucking need, to see what failed in 2000 there fail in 2001 here. The crowd was as pissed as it looked on TV, believe me, but it was half heel heat and half "suck" heat. And out comes Mr. UEW himself, Chris Hopper....but he doesn't side with them, and instead spears Parker, but winds up recieving a bloody beatdown from the other UEWers. They challenge him to a six-man match tonight, with any two WWOers of his choice as partners. Ugh. This better be some kind of swerve. Invasion angles blow. I mean, really, they suck ass. Sort of like that stupid little match that happened before we were invaded. Rating: 1/4* -The Nobodies take over some psycho janitor's dressing room, but then get attacked by RAPE. Winner: "Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada 4.
