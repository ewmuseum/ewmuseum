WWO bio card — Ace of Hearts

23 May 2003

Ace of Hearts Wrestler Name: Date of Birth: Height: 6'3 Weight: 269 Alignment: Hometown: Calgary, Alberta, Canada Entrance Music: Bodies by Drowning Pool or My Way by Limp Bizkit Body Guard: "Sadistic" Simon Shade Valet: Candy Kisses Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ace stands 6' 3" and weighs in at 269 lbs. He has light brown, shoulder length hair. Bright blue piercing eyes and a very deep golden tanned, muscular body. His upperbody is very defined and developed, second to no-one! His muscular body looks as if it is chiseled in stone. He works hard on his physical appearance as he expects nothing short of perfection. He has a small tattoo that sits on his upper right arm. It is a black Ace of Spades with a deep red heart inset in the spade. It is hardly ever seen. Ring Appearance Ace usually is dressed in a black federation t-shirt bearing his likeness and a pair of black wrestling tights with red emblazoned hearts. Will include description of this in the strat. Psychological Makeup Having had the ability to train in the best gyms money could buy. Having had the greatest teachers that money could obtain. Ace had nothing but time, desire, and drive.... so he was fed the finest the wrestling business had to offer. Devouring this knowledge it made him quite the handful. He's cocky, arrogant and expects only the best and will be satisfied with nothing less. Strengths Superior wrestling & technical skills God given talent & cockiness Freakish strength and toughness Weaknesses Women (Candy Kisses) Arial manuvers Agressiveness Biographical Information Ace's history is known only from the time he joined the KSGA. (Keening School of Grappling Arts) He is quite secretive about his life prior to the KSGA. Enrolling into the KSGA in Seattle Washington, Ace moved into one of the twelve first class living suites. Studying vigorously under "Fireball" Ken Keening, Ace mastered the science of the grappling arts. Graduating at the head of his class, Ace became the sixth overall graduate from the KSGA Satisfied with this glowing accomplishment, Ace still desired to learn more. So, seeking a different approach at training and some more ring experience, Ace proceeded to enroll in the Shawn Michael's Wrestling Academy in San Antonio, Texas. It was here that Ace was instructed personally by Shawn Michael's and Jose Lothario. Excelling here, just as well as he did at the KSGA, Ace mastered what he now calls his finisher. The C.R.A.P.S. This maneuver, the charging reversed ass-punishing suplex, is one of the most devastating maneuvers in the business. Graduating from the SMWA, with even more ring experience than he had obtained at the KSGA, Ace now felt he was ready to step into the ring professionally. Thus he began scouring the nation searching for a federation to join. While scouting a fed at a show in Detroit, Michigan, meet up with another independent wrestler by the name of Nemisis. After some negotiations with this man, he and Ace formed an alliance and joined the Gulf Coast Wrestling Alliance. Really looking to make a name for himself, Ace came into the GCWA with a fire lit under his ass. Set to wrestle in his debut, he was attacked by another wrestler. Big mistake! Ace not only made him pay for sticking his nose where it didn't belong, but he also single-handedly ran Alexander Strakoi and Pierre out of the GCWA. Sending Strokoi packing, Ace lit the fed up with a viciousness unheard of around these parts. That is until that fateful night Ace garners his only loss to a debuting "Dashing" Damien DeMarco. Rallying from this loss, this embarrassment, he then went on to defeat the very first GCWA Coastal Champion "Reverend LaVey" in a non-title match. The fed was shocked as Ace was the very first man in the fed to accomplish this feat. Doing something nobody else had, he was granted a title shot at the next GCWA PPV "Out Of This World". On August 21, 1999, Ace's back was injured severely in an automobile accident just outside Las Vegas, Nevada. This accident nearly cost him his career. After a long surgery to repair the damage in his spine, Ace left the hospital in Vegas and went back home. He returned to his birth place where he slowly recovered regained his strength and stamina with the help of his brother. Vowing that he would return to the ring. Ace set forth into a very strenuous training program with his brother. The weeks that followed were torturous. Each week tested Ace's desire and skills to combat this rigorous path back to the ring. With his families encouragement every step of the way, Ace fully recovered and was ready to return to the ring. With the help of his many acquaintances along the way, he began working off the ring rust in many training bouts. Gaining momentum as he nears his professional in-ring return, he has been scouting out his opponents. Seeking out his enemies weaknesses and thus plotting his revenge. Ace successfully returned to the Gulf Coast Wrestling Alliance ring in mid-October and maintained his greatness by going 9-2-1. Having executed a masterful plan upon his return to action. Ace infuriated many by aligning himself with the legendary "House of Holiday". It was here that these elite men formed and feuded with the rival stables, "The South Coast Mafia" and the "One Bad Ass Army" for ultimate fed supremacy. Showing little signs of any ring-rust in his return, Ace pushed forward earning his way towards the top and thus garnering himself a title shot. While preparing for his shot at the unified title the GCWA closed its doors on January 22, 2000. Furious that the GCWA title strap had just eluded him, he talked to his agents and manager and rapidly began seeking out work. This time, he was centered upon his goal. Determined and destined to earn his share of the Wrestling World's Championship Gold. Ace, under advisement from "Ace's High Productions", joined the American Wrestling Revolution and the Global Championship Wrestling Organization. This endeavor was quite trying on him as the travel involved running between the two feds was monotonous. Flying from show to show in his private jet he slowly adapted to this fast paced life-style and thus the rewards were slowly starting to show. Ace debuting in an AWR ring on January 30th 2000, set the fed upon its ass! He immediately joined with a few old friends to form a stable called the "Blood Revolution". Assaulted their way up the ranking, they created a great many enemies. Within twenty-one days of his arrival, Ace finally obtained championship gold. He won the AWR Television Title in a grueling championship match where he captured this honor from the previously undefeated, undisputed, legend in the AWR, Kurt "The Wolf" Keizmen. Having to defend his newly obtained title in a bar-room brawl just nights later, at the AWR PPV "Final Stand." Ace displayed a ferociousness unlike nothing the AWR had ever seen. This was a match that will truly go down as one of the greats in the feds history. However, soon after this match, the AWR experienced financial problems and was unwilling to pay their talent. Refusing to work for next to nothing, Ace left the AWR to focus solely upon his other federation, the GCWO... Having entered the GCWO on February 7th 2000, Ace had almost came back home sort to speak. The fed was run by and treated as a second coming of the GCWA. Many of the his old competitors and allies alike were there competing for the shot at the Global Heavyweight Championship strap. However, this fed was now being run by Ace's arch nemesis, "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky. Ready to accept the challenge of excelling in his arch rivals federation, Ace put his nose to the grindstone and amassed a record of 8-0 in the GCWO. Running through his competition Ace obtained the GCWO Global Heavyweight Championship. Doing so, infuriated the feds President, his former rival, thus their feud had heated up yet again. Kopecky did everything in his power to void the Championship reign of Ace but was unsuccessful. Soon there-after, weather it be coincidence or not, the GCWO went on hiatus. So...Ace still reigns its undisputed, undefeated, Global Heavyweight Champion! Having gained the stamina from running in a few feds at the same time and adjusting to the grueling schedules, Ace joined not only the fabled UEW but also the upstart NeWF. His debuts in both fed were victorious as were the matches thereafter. In the NeWF, Ace joined with Brandon Franklin to spearheaded a revolution in the NeWF. Their goal...to squash the hostile takeover of the NeWF by Colt Anderson and his company, Sundown Productions. Ace and Brandon took the fight to Colt Anderson and company until Brandon and the NeWF came into a contract dispute. Pledging to eradicate Anderson, Ace trudged along until surprisingly Anderson was indited by the federal government. Ace turning his attention back to business after the arrest of Anderson was poised to excel to the very top of the NeWF as he remained undefeated until the federation abruptly closed its doors. Taking the closure of the NeWF in stride, Ace re-focussed his attention to the much fabled UEW. Making a splash upon his arrival into the UEW as he immediately called out the Ultimate Champion Trixxster. But as Trixxster feared Ace's ability, he shrugged off the calling out and proved himself not worthy his championship reign. Trudged along in the UEW, Ace made an alliance with the President of the UEW in Thomas J. Smith. Doing so, Ace did Smith's dirty business! During this time, Ace quickly rose in the ranking till he was due to face the Television Champion Steven Douglas. Defeating Douglas, Tracker, and The Giant of Nod. Ace took the UEW Television Championship and defended it proudly in his first title defense against the two time UEW TV Champion Liam Rogan. Victorious, Ace walked right into the next Monday Night Heat and was controversially defeated for the Television strap by "Mr Ratings" Warren Hayes. Not single-handly mind you, as the new UEW President Andy Sand's special guest referee and Warren Hayes cronies had allot to do with the loss. Ace angered and looking for revenge, had Tracker, Hayes, and Sand all within his sites for a little retribution. That is until Sand was injured in a snowboarding accident and the Hayes had left the federation after dropping the title. Even though Ace's revenge had just eluded him. His future in the UEW was about to pick-up as Ace was approached soon there-after by a former UEW Ultimate Champion, Magnus Colby. His goal, recruit Ace into a newly forming stable called the "Unforgiven." This stable consisting of Magnus Colby, Alex Extreme, Marcus Manson, the Ace of Hearts, and the Outer Dimensions rocked the UEW with it's formation. Setting the UEW afire, the Unforgiven fought for fed supremacy as they feuded with the aging Widowmakers Inc. As the "Unforgiven" were working to eradicate the UEW of the Widowmakers. Ace was personally succeeding very well as he was working his way up the rankings and was set to meet "The Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening for an UEW American Title shot. However, this match was never to take place. As just prior to this match, Ace was viciously injured by a pre-meditated attack at the hands of Jason Keening and Doc Holiday. Laid up in the hospital for the last two and a half to three months. Ace has been plotting his return to the ring and his revenge upon the Keenings. Released just recently from the hospital, Ace has been slowly recovering from the numerous surgeries upon his back. Training very hard in seclusion since this recent release, Ace is now ready for his professional in-ring return. However, as he returned to the UEW offices he was told of the feds impending closure. Now ready for action but without a home, Ace seeks to once again make a name for himself in yet another fed. Could the WWO be the place to do this? Only time will tell... Finishing Maneuver C.R.A.P.S. - Charging Reversed Ass Punishing Suplex [The finisher of Ace is called C.R.A.P.S. It is best used when his opponent is dazed from Ace's relentless attacks. The set up for this finisher is a kick to the mid-section. Although it can be preformed at anytime so long as his opponent is dazed from a previous move. The finisher starts as Ace grabs his stunned opponent. Wrapping his right arm around the neck of his opponent, Ace bends his opponent backward into a Inverted DDT position. (aka Sting's Scorpion Deathdrop) Then with his free left hand, Ace grabs a hand-full of tights and lifts his opponent into the air. His opponent is upright and facing Ace.(ala inverted) As soon as the opponent is upright, Ace charges the corner turnbuckles. Thus driving his opponents back into the corner, this takes the breathe from his opponent. Still holding the limp opponent, Ace takes a few steps backward and turns to the ropes. He then drops his opponents legs over the top rope. This sling-shots the opponents legs into the air. Using this sling-shot, Ace repositions his hands so that his right hand is just below the opponents neck on his face side. He moves his left to the mid-back section of his opponent. (All of this occurs as the opponent is being sling-shot from the ropes.) Using his opponents own weight against him, as well as all of Ace's weight. He drives the opponent, face first, into the mat with full force. Ace then rolls his opponent over to his back for the pin and the rest is academic.] Alternate Finishers "Snake Eye's" is an inverted rocker dropper which needs to be set up by stunning or dazing the opponent with a move that leaves him standing. Such as a European Uppercut or forearm smash. "The Payoff" - Superkick "The Bank Roll" - quickly executed small package close to the ropes. this move is strongly used along with the ring ropes and a handful of tights to secure a cheap and easy three count!
