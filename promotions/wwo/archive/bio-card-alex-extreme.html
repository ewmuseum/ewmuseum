WWO bio card — "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme

23 May 2003

"Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme Wrestler Name: Alexander Epstein Date of Birth: Height: 6'3 Weight: 240 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Entrance Music: Eminem 's "The Way I Am" Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Icy Blue Eyes, Long Black Wavy Hair with blonde streaks, Goatee, Well Tanned, Atheletic semi-defined build. Extreme mixes his attire between formal and casual. Something like a Black Armani suitjacket, neon colored mock t-shirt, jeans, shades, and Air Jordans. Ring Appearance Black leather jacket with neon purple string and a neon blue X on the back, a black t-shirt with a big huge clumsly splashed big X in neon purple on it, Ray Ban wafer shades and a pair of neon blue, neon purple, and black swirled spandex pants. Psychological Makeup Strengths Weaknesses Biographical Information Ever since he saw Randy Savage come off the top rope, young Alexander Esptein knew he wanted to fly. He wanted to be a pro wrestler. He wrestled in High School and was an All-American at Northwestern University but never finished. Instead, Alex ventured into a career in pro wrestling. Originally Alex was to team with his long time friend Magnus Colby. However, at the last minute, Magnus recieved an offer from Havard's graduate school of business. Colby took it and Esptein ventured to Montreal alone. On December 1, 1995, [name withheld] met with Canadian Fantasy Wrestling Federation owner [name withheld] in Montreal. During the meeting, Epstein convienced Griffith to give an opportunity and a contract offer. During this meeting, Alex met Dan Kauffman. Kauffman, who was their current champion at the time, saw something in college dropout because he offered to train the 20 year old kid from Chicago in the ways of pro wrestling. Epstein's traininng lasted one month. On Janurary 2, 1996, Alex Espstein became "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme and began his pro career. Six weeks later, Alex Extreme had won his first major title: The CFWF North American title. Extreme held the title until the CFWF closed in mid-March of 1996. Only days after the closing of the CFWF, Alex Extreme signed contracts with FIVE different organizations. This organizations included GWF spin-off Extreme to the Max, WHWF, EUWF, WOW (later merged to become IWF/WOW), and the famed WLW. Extreme worked on seven days a week schedule which saw him barnstorming arenas across the country. He quickly captured the EUWF IC title in a Ladder Match over "Spartan" Troy Walters, and nearly won Extreme to the Max TV and WOW titles. But it was in the WLW where Extreme was really causing a stir. He somehow became part of a controversy of breaking up the popular tag team the American Dreams, which featured Brett Young and Mr. Excitment Dropkick. It was also at this time that Alex Extreme would first run into Rick Styles. The two feuded until Extreme headed off to Hollywood to pursue a movie career. Universal Studios gave Alex a three-picture multi-million dollar deal. Extreme took it and walked away from wrestling in May of 1996. He was scheduled to be the male lead opposite Sandra Bullock in Speed 2. That ended when Alex got into a disagreement with the director of film and sent him flying through a 50,000 dollars worth of lights. Alex's days in big time movies were over before they even began--or so he thought. It seems that his contract, which still needed to be fufilled, had a clause stating he couldn't return to wrestling until it was finished. This combined with the director's multi-million dollar lawsuit and brash over spending, and Alex Extreme was in trouble. From penthouse to outhouse, Extreme found himself living in the slums of LA amongst the rich and famous backdrop of Hollywood, California. To finish up his contract, Extreme made 3 B action movies for Universal as part of the original deal. To make extra cash, he worked as a bouncer for numerous clubs. It was there he got his first taste of drugs. A month into the new year, Extreme could be found strung out on the corner of Hollywood and Vine like any other junkie. He was so deseperate for the extra cash, Alex ended up doing X-rated pornographic films. Alex Extreme had indeed hit the bottom. In April of 1997, Magnus Colby, a long time friend of Extreme, finished conquering the business world and decided to return to wrestling. But it had been a long time since Colby had wrestled and it he never on the professional level. He needed someone to help him break into the business. And so Magnus Colby set out to find his long time friend, Alex Extreme. He found Extreme, strung out in a back alley wigged out of his mind. Colby took in his friend, helping him get clean, and back on his feet. In turn, Extreme readied Colby for the world of pro wrestling. By late July, "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme was ready to return to the world of pro wrestling with Magnus Colby shorltly joinning him by September. On July 28th, 1997, Alex Extreme returned to wrestling signing with two smaller federations: The SPWA, a Chicago based organization, and the AeWF owned by [name withheld]. Shortly after, he was invited to other federations including his old stomping grounds: the WOW--now the IWF/WOW. It was there that he once again ran into Rick Styles. The two parted ways as enemies in early 1996 but were somehow able to smooth out the differences. As a show of good faith, Styles opened the door for Alex Extreme and later Magnus Colby to arrive in the UEW. He joinned Rick Styles as part of the New Alliance. Slowly Mr. Excitement began to return to past glory. In 1998, he captured the AeWF World Championship twice, IWF/WOW Tag Team Championship Reign with Magnus Colby, and a 5-month UEW TV title reign. In UEW, Extreme went from jeered to cheered during his TV title reign. This included an evil attack lead by Rick Styles that cost him the UEW TV title. Feuds in this time included Robski, Magnus Colby, Chris Hopper, Jeff Matthews, Rick Styles, and Alex Martinez. During this time, struggled with his new found popularity with the fans, logged numerous trips to the hospital, and seemed to find himself in some of the most dangerous matches allowed by law. He now had one quest: to win the UEW title. Extreme battled for the title on three occasions losing all of them. His inability to win the UEW title saw Mr. Excitement snap and go off the deep end. Alex began to use steroids to bulk up but soon began mixing recreational drugs in as well. The UEW suspended Alex for most of the summer. Extreme spent most of the summer touring with the WWO, IWE, and ICW to keep in wrestling shape. Extreme returned to the UEW after surving a match where he faced six men in 60 minutes. Extreme then worked the Rumble In The Bronx main event between Rick Styles and Alex Martinez as a referee but overdosed at a critical point in the match costing Martinez the title. Alex spent 12 days in a coma. Extreme promptly checked himself into the Betty Ford Clinic when he awoke. Extreme returned to action in early 2000. He formed a short lived tag team with his cousin Ryan Rumble in the IWF/WOW then returned to the UEW where he finally accomplished his long time goal: the UEW title. He held it defending against all comers until he was defeated by Doc Holliday at Rumble In The Bronx. At the end of that match, Extreme suffered a sevre head injury. The injury gave him a case on amensia during which he thought he was the Golden Flash and Mercenary. When his memory returned, he once again went after Doc Holliday and the title at Holiday Horror 5. Alex once again saw the title slip through his fingers but not before he and Magnus Colby formed the old school group known as Unforgiven. Lately, Alex Extreme has trouble with goverment officials in Portland, Oregon. They claimed Extreme was once again using steroids and had the drugs and tests to prove it. It was later found to be Xavier Feyr, Tyrone Parker, and Tracker out to destroy his career. Extreme has sworn revenge beginning with the main culprit, Xavier Feyr at Ides of March. Alex joined the UWF to work with his long time friends Magnus Colby and "Candyman" Damian Rose. The three joinned up with former UEW member Rick Styles as members of the Foundation. The association didn't last as Extreme is now working by himself. Things have been slow for Alex in UWF. He's made few friends and tons of enemies. He was recently a part of the EIT tournament and fought both Sabbath and Nick Demola at Gold Rush. The biggest buzz surrounding Alex Extreme has been since he started working in a new promotion called River City Wrestling out of St. Louis for long time legendary promoter Fletch. Extreme captured the RCW National title becoming it's first ever champion. Holding the title for 5 months as the fans cheered to no end. This was followed by a vicious feud with former friend Chris Gotham. Currently, he's fighting with Vincent Sandora an Legacy of Malice. Extreme is also proud of one other recent development. Five years after dropping out of college, Alex has spent most of his free time keeping a promise he gave to his family. In December of 2000, Alex completed his final course towards the degree started working towards since 1993. Extreme know has a Journalism with broadcasting minor with honors from Northwestern University.
