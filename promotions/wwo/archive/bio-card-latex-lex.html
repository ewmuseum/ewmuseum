WWO bio card — Latex Lex Also known as "Public Enema #1", "Yours Cruelly"

Latex Lex Also known as "Public Enema #1", "Yours Cruelly" Real Name: Unknown Height: 6'4 Weight: 289 Alignment: Diabolic Hometown: Los Angeles, California Entrance Music: "Be Aggressive" by Faith No More Manager: Todd "The Rod" Johnstone Managerial Profile Psychological Makeup Latex Lex is the consummate "trouble-maker". He thrives on catching the heat of the crowds and playing up to the deepest hatreds and emotional triggers of fans and foes alike. All actions and comments in the ring and out have a psychological motive. Everything is to get under the skin into the heads and cause the chaos that will bring their worlds crashing down and their shoulders to the mat. Latex Lex calls himself "Public Enema #1" for his uncanny ability to cause s*** for everyone in his path. Style Notes: Tends to favor submission moves over pin falls. It goes with his philosophy then bending of bodies is key to bending their minds. Although more of a technical wrestler, he is not afraid of getting into a brawl or wrestling a more hardcore style, he sees hardcore as foreplay, bringing back golden memories of his parents and their s&m swinger binges. He likes to take punishment almost as much as he likes to receive it. When coming to the ring, Lex makes rude gestures, suggestions, and inclinations towards the fans. He also uses veiled insults about them as fans and human beings in general. When in the ring he further uses insults and lewd behavior to catch the ire of those in attendance, usually with the "assistance" of his opponent who he uses as an unwilling prop in his gestures and body language. Everything he does is determined by the fans distaste, which he uses as a gauge to determine if he's being offensive enough or needs to be even more so. Physical Appearance Looks: - The wrestler is 6'4", 280 plus pounds making him of an average wrestling build. His physique is fairly muscular, more muscle than bulk anyway. He wears a black mask in the ring but its obvious that hes a white male in his mid to late 20's,innocent "baby blue" eyes, about the only thing innocent about him and his appearance, which makes your average leather fan look like Mr. Rogers. Clothing: In addition to a black leathery mask with a zipper in lip area, Lex is outfitted into a black stretchy latex bodysuit that leaves next to nothing to the imagination complemented by the odd metal stud here and there, and two areas of the material cutout where his butt cheeks would be. In contrast to the bawdy blackness of him is a large purple "J" airbrushed into the groin area. All of this is capped off with a shiny gold tongue piercing also of a J shape, which he frequently darts out at fan and foe alike. Biographical Information Latex Lex, believed to be his given name, was born and raised in The Sunny but seedy and just plain sick side of California. The product of Swinging S&M pill-popping parentage, Lex was blessed with a unique outlook and perspective what we know to be the big picture of life. He was imbued with a unique outlook towards his fellow humans, with a unique understanding of their rights and sensitivities. Given this understanding when it was time for Lex to leave the family home, due to the condemned notice on the house, and the heat he might have from his siblings for turning Ma and Pa in for the reward money, the two life directions he saw before were, Wrestling and the adult film industry. Lex opted for wrestling, feeling the dialogue and creative opportunities to be more receptive to him as well as the violent action and clashing and physical torment of wrestling to be more appealing to his "appetites". With this after a six week crash course at a wrestling school, unable to be named here due to an out of court settlement, Lex entered the squared circle for the first time on Sept 30, 1997. He lost his first match but showed great promise on the mat and the microphone. Due to the potential he showed and the skills he honed in the first few feds he was in were closed down by orders of the FCC and local state sanctioning bodies. After a brief period of financial instability he finally able to get steady work in promotion based out of Calgary, Alberta Canada. It was there where he really began to learn the tricks of his trade and strut his stuff, much to the dismay of the fans and foes of the self proclaimed Public Enema #1. This stint eventually resulted in his first championship titles and his first true experiences of being a "team player". Lex did not see eye to eye with the promoter, his executive committee or the the promotions own standards and practices committee, which cost him key opportunities on several occasions. Not being exactly management friendly or "marketable" would be a general theme in the career exploits of this Latexed one. It could be said Lex had an in cooperative attitude, and attitude that would only be amplified when he signed on with the equally uncooperative Todd "The Rod" Johnstone. Lex had an ability to offend to say the least, which was discouraged by most he met until meeting up the portly manager, who not only tolerated but encouraged it with the pairing of Tyrone "Purple" Hayes. These three individuals were devious on their own dynamically disgusting as a group, a group which came to be known as C.A.T., Carnage A Trois, which redefined, ravaged and ridiculed the standards of both good and bad taste. No one was safe in any fed they stepped foot in especially if they were Australian. Lex's exploits came to an abrupt end, when he suffered a serious groin injury in a top rope move that went horribly awry it what can only be described as "The chair shot felt around the world". This sidelined Lex for many months before falling into a coma, a coma which he never came out of,and subsequently died in due to the apparent turning off the life support by the promoter he currently worked for who refused to pay for any further hospital coverage. He was pronounced dead a few days later, although no autopsy was done and the body went missing reportedly misplaced by the hospitals morgue unit.
