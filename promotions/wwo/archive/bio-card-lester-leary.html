"Lounge Lizard" Lester Leary Real Name: Lester Leary Height: 6'3 Weight: 257 Date of Birth: July 12, 1968 Alignment: Taoist Hometown: Las Vegas, NV Entrance Music: "Viva Las Vegas" sung by Lester himself Manager: Rimshot Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Lester has a slick black pompadour haircut, white, clean shaven, blue eyes. Ring Attire: usually an eggplant-coloured ruffle tuxedo. For less formal occasions, has a lime-green shirt, black pants, black jacket with lime-green trim and matching lime green socks. Psychological Makeup Strenghts Ability to distract ref so as to cheat without getting caught; A juggernaut when his opponent is in the turnbuckle. He loves the concept of controlling a guy with no escape; As an entertainer, can feed off the crowd's reactions whether good or bad depending on whether he's face or heel. Both will energize him. Weaknesses Showboating, thus tends to telegraph moves; Hates his hair being mussed and will get disoriented if someone touches it (or occasionally go berserk); Is a little prone to martial arts attacks: He never did understand that "chinky jive" as he'd call it. Biographical Information Lester Leary is a lounge singer whose prized possession is the Golden Mic(TM). Often seen having to ply his trade at Casino Rama, an Indian casino in central Ontario. His music isn't to everyone's tastes but he doesn't care. Sleeperhold Rope burn Kneeling piledriver Boston Crab reverse DDT Uranage Nevadan Death Grip (Tonga Death Grip) Finishing Maneuver Paisley Powerbomb - Spinning bomb from middle rope Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Vegas Connection Tag Team Partner: Larry Lowbrow Tag Team Moves Double DDT spike piledriver spike facebuster Finishing Move The Vegas Deathwish - involves a quick tag - Lester puts on the Boston Crab and Larry comes in for a Camel Clutch. It's close quarters but it can be done. Especially effective on really tall guys.
