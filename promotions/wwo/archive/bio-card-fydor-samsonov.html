WWO bio card — "The Russian Bear" Fydor Samsonov

"The Russian Bear" Fydor Samsonov Wrestler Name: Fydor Samsonov Date of Birth: Height: 6'1 Weight: 302 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Zosmina, Russia Entrance Music: "Flight of the Bumblebee" by Rimsky Korsakoff Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Fydor is a 41 year-old graying man, who is built heavily in his upper-torso, arms, and neck. Heavily built even to a degree that makes his lower, black booted torso look dwarfed in comparison. He wears gray and brown army fatigues, being the only style that 'old-school 'communist Soviets abide by. Standard black singlet and complementing elbow pads show Fydor's long commitment to his craft, having been the same equipment he's owned throughout his extensive career.With a thick graying-brownish beard, and bushy brown eye-brows, Samsonov looks similar to any American generalization of a middle-aged dirty Russian bastard. Ring Appearance He wears gray and brown army fatigues, being the only style that 'old-school 'communist Soviets abide by. Standard black singlet and complementing elbow pads show Fydor's long commitment to his craft, having been the same equipment he's owned throughout his extensive career, such as his fingerless black gloves. Psychological Makeup Apathetic towards most matters that Fydor is ignorant of, which is nearly everything. His general apathy warrants him owning a low temper, as rarely does Samsonov lose his cool. However, Fydor can be known to react harshly regarding the few ideals that he holds dearly, which usually revolvearound his motherland. Samsonov understands little of American culture and values, and shys away from anything related. His opinions may be considered harsh in their bluntness, but is mostly considered by his total lacking of worldly knowledge. While Fydor may be uneducated in American standards, his basic principles and natural instinct have kept him well in the past. While occasionally having to wrestle across the United States' Independent wrestling scene, Fydor has been mostly received by jeering from the crowds or attendance on hand. Rarely will he receive a few cheers from specific fans in the complex looking to support the underdog, or just trying to play against the majority of crowd support. For the most part, people don't boo Samsonov for being an incredibly hated persona, as they would simply rather choose to cheer the more fan friendly or 'charismatic' opponent, regardless of him/her being a fan favorite or heel. Either way, The Russian Bear could care less. While he may be aware of the crowds, interaction or responding to their insults or occasional cheers is a moot point. Fydor Samsonov is there to stomp someone, plain and simple. Strengths Toughness. Having spent his entire life in a lackluster environment, where fighting solves more problems than negotiation, Samsonov has grown quite the shell around himself from physical and mental attack. Of course, some might add that his mental shell also is derived by his lacking of general wits. Regardless, Fydor is as tough as they come. Workings stiff is the only way he knows how. Strength. Gaining "The Russian Bear" moniker for a reason, Samsonov middle-aged exterior takes little away from his brute strength. Don't underplay his large upper torso as being his only positive, as Fydor's technical merit as a Suplex specialist comes fully with a 23 year background. Natural Instinct. Most certainly, Fydor never immerses himself in the 'mental game of chess' that American workers have found themselves taking part in. American soap opera needs for dramatic mental games between opponents make The Russian Bear sick to his stomach. Professional wrestling is still a sport to Fydor, and having pure natural instincts for the sport is what he excels at. Weaknesses Any style of wrestling that veers away from the tough / mat / street fight combination Samsonov is used to confuses Fydor. To put it simply, "Luchidor" is a word The Russian Bear didn't know even existed until his move to the United States. And a "Moonsault" is something he first thought had to be executed using some form of magic. Teamwork. In Professional wrestling, acquiring allies, or making alliances is a great part in achieving success. This could cause for problems in Samsonov's American wrestling experience, as the words "teamwork" or "tag-team" are things he doesn't believe in. Samsonov needs no relationships other than his own with himself, regardless of if it puts him into an unsatisfactory position. Cockiness. Cockiness by American wrestlers and fans alike greatly annoys Fydor, and can occasionally cause him to sometimes react without thinking. Wresling is a serious matter to The Russian Bear, and should be treated as such. Fydor can sometimes steer way from his goal of winning the match, simply to inflict some 'education' on those he sees as disrespectful to the sport he's honored and been apart of for so long. Biographical Information Fydor is an aged Russian wrestler, who traveled to America after his hometown wrestling territory was broken up due to the government collapse of the former USSR. His hope was to find the "Russian Dream": a communist riddled society with a militanary supremacy. Unfortunately for Fydor, who was none too educated as a child, had the depression to discover that America was in fact, a democratic based government set on enlightening the world in 'peaceful' coexistence. Disgusted with his findings, "The Russian Bear" Fydor Samsonov is now attempting to flee back to his fatherland, and rejoin the still politically shaken and economically ravaged country. Though, to complete this feet Fydor must find hopeful income in the possibility of joining The WWO: an American Oriented Wrestling Organization, as his trip to The United States has left his pants pockets stricken empty. With 23 years wrestling experience behind him and a hopeful prayer, we might just see Fydor make it back to his fatherland yet or die trying. Because The Russian Bear would almost rather be dead than be in America.
