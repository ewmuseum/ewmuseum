You've got you're favorite WWO intern reviewing Showcase again! A couple of things, first, lemme step OOC for a second.... -- The match ratings aren't meant to denigrate the match writers, or the handlers involved in the matches. Also, all other comments are written _in character_ from a "smark" perspective, although most of the time you can probably accurately read them as an amplification of my opinion. Okay, now back into character... -- It's been brought to my attention that maybe people might like to give me some feedback on these reviews. Fair enough.
