Vegas Connection Physicians Advocating Innovative Neoprocedures July 29, 2001 Milwaukee, WI Notes World Leaders were challenged immediately following their contest with the Lethal Weapons, and lost the belts to Mean Mason and Department of Corrections. DYWYWU defeated the Bionic Team the previous week in a non-title match. Instead of signing a title contract, the Bionic Team retired as "champions", and Moshpit was put in their place. Search and Destroy wrestled as the Centurions, before unmasking after winning the titles. Fire and Ice won a four corners tag match, pinning the Armed Forces in the final fall to become the new champions. Federation closed, Thunderstorm declared the final WWO tag team champions of 2000.
