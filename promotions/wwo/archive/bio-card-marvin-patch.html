WWO bio card — "Trixxster" Marvin Patch

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Trixxster" Marvin Patch Wrestler Name: Marvin Patch Date of Birth: Height: 5'10 Weight: 220 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: New Orleans, Louisiana Entrance Music: "Brain Stew" by Green Day Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance He is a short young man (25 years old) with long red hair, beady green eyes, and a pointed chin. He has beefed up since last in the wrestling world; adding fifteen pounds of muscle to his small body. He also sports a scruffy red goatee on his chin now. Ring Appearance "Trixxster" Marvin Patch no longer wears the trademark red and black jester's outfit. Instead he wears a black singlet with an image of a red "Tragedy" mask on the front and a red "Comedy" mask on the back. He also wears black wrestling boots with red laces and has his hair tied back in a pony-tail. Psychological Makeup Strengths Speed. "Trixxster" was always a fast man and he still possesses that speed. He may not be as fast as he was a year and a half ago due to the gained muscle, but he is still one of the fastest men in wrestling. Marvin Patch is a ruthless, non-forgiving man now. Nobody is really sure what made him change his attitude, but he is now much more physical in the ring and doesn't really care about disqualifications and such. He will take it to his opponents with vicious hits and will use a foriegn object A LOT more now than before. He has also increased his submission repetoire by about 300%. In the past four years "Trixxster" has maybe used a total of three submission moves in his matches, but now has about eight or nine deadly holds in his arsenal. Weaknesses He is not the strongest man in the ring, which is obvious due to his size. He is somewhat stronger now than before, but is still no strongman in the ring. So when he is in there with the big heavyweights and 300 pounders, he can not match strength with strength. With his new ruthless style, this can also lead to more losses as a result of disqualification. With his new "Who cares" attitude, he risks DQ's and count-outs in his matches. His body is getting very worn down. He may only be 25, but he has been competing at the highest level for nearly five years and his body has taken a beating. He can't fly as good as he used to when he was a rookie, and the decrease in mobility is a definite weakness. Biographical Information He met two older men (in their late 40's) down in Mexico who were somewhat "crazy" one could say. They took young Marvin into their home as they had lost their sons and wives in a tragic car accident a year prior in hopes of grooming him to be a fine adult. It was at this point that they sent young Marvin Patch to a luchador wrestling school, and after four long years, Marvin was out wrestling his instructors. It was at this time that he returned to America (aged 21 now) and arrived in New Orleans at the time of Mardi Gras. The splendor and excitement of the festival overwhelmed Marvin, and he then found something that would change his life forever; a red and black checkered jester's outfit. With the influence of the two crazy old men in Mexico and this newfound jester's outfit, Marvin transformed himself into an identity he would carry on for the next four years... Trixxster. Trixxster started wrestling in America, and quickly rose to stardom. He went undefeated in IWC (Insane Wrestling Corporation) for a few months and captured both the Can-Am Title (cruiser) and the Northeastern Title. From there he went onto the SWP and won the Juniorweight Title. And in September of 1998 he joined UEW and WWO in November of 1999. While his first sting in WWO floundered, Trixxster went on to become one of UEW's biggest stars, capturing the Cruiserweight, Television, American, and Ultimate Title all in the span of ten months. He was the fed's first Grand Slam champion and eventually his ego got the better of him. Trixxster went on a huge winning spree without being pinned up until July of 2001, where he was pinned twice in one match by two different opponents(it was a Pick Your Poision Match with three men involved each trying to score two victories), and after he evened things up at a fall a piece, Trixxster walked out on the match, the Ultimate Title, and the wrestling world forever. He gave his retirement speech a few weeks later and hasn't been seen since. That is, until now. Marvin Patch, not Trixxster, now comes to WWO with a totally different attitude. Gone is the laughing jester who would skip around with glee and punish his opponents with a variety of tricks. Gone is the trademark jester's outfit. Gone are the old catchphrases. Trixxster is dead, and all that is left is a nickname to remember the past with. A new man is in WWO, and this man is angry. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Very Good High-Flier Very Good Martial Arts Fair Power Poor Technical Good Submission Excellent Cheating Fair Brawling/Hardcore None Escapability Fair Stamina Good Ring Awareness Good Teamwork None Top Wrestling Moves A jumping DDT which is immediately followed up with a dragon sleeper, which is Patch's favourite move now. Jumping brainbuster. (Can only be done on cruisers obviously.) Spinning heel kick from the top rope. Shooting star press. Hook opponent in Tree of Woe position and then go outside the ring and grab opponent's arms and pull backwards on arms, causing opponent's back of head to crack into ringpost. Texas cloverleaf. Hurricanrana (any variation). Running powerbomb. (Can only be done on cruisers obviously.) Puts opponent into full nelson position and then leaps forward bringing his weight down and sending opponent crashing face-first into mat. Mafia kick that stuns opponent followed up with a quick magistrol cradle. Finishing Maneuver The Joke's On You -Your standard 450 splash from the top rope.
