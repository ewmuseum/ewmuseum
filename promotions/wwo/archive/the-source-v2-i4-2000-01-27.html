Much like we did with the World Wrestling Organization's tag teams in last week's edition of this rag, let's once again put that theory to the test with the top singles wrestlers in the federation, shall we? Exactly how do we stack up against the quote, unquote "best" in the sport? WWO - TOP FIVE SINGLES WRESTLERS: -------------------------------- 1) "MR. RATINGS" WARREN HAYES By the time you read this, he could very well be the World champion. And honestly, who could really say they didn't see it coming? Sure, Hayes basically had the ball waiting for him when he returned to the federation in the fall, but luckily, he had no problems running with it. Because of his most recent and smallest stint in the WWO, many question whether the Ratings Box has "paid his dues" or not. Bear in mind, folks, he led Simply The Best... he was at the forefront of Even Better, he wore tag team gold with Ric St... well, it really doesn't matter who he wore gold with, but he was once a tag champion. And to top it off, he had his own syndicated television show for many months in '98 and '99. The fact is, if the WWO were to go to war, would we want anyone else at the front of the line? 2) TYRONE "PURPLE" HAYES Hayes' antics never seem to get old... that is, unless they are directed towards you. The man that could quite possibly be considered the "walking heat machine" has done all there is to do in the WWO, that is, without winning a single title belt. Tyrone, while not always the most likeable guy around, he knows how to bring success, and would be the ideal guy to have on your side, if you're going to war. And in this case, the WWO wants the "Purple One" on their side. After all, he's a better ally than he is an enemy... depending on whom you ask. 3) "MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN Here is the one man gracing the WWO roster that has had a good amount of success outside the WWO, and has faced a handful of the "big names" from elsewhere. Because of his experience alone, Mike would be a great addition to the team, and could really add a great deal of knowhow to an already strong team. However, his differences with Tyrone inparticular might hurt the team ultimately, but he clearly deserves to be here. He has been an active wrestler in the WWO for the last 16 months, winning two coveted titles in less than a year's time. He did more in his first two months in the WWO than many ever accomplish in their entire tenure(s). 4) APEX There is a psychopath in every federation, for the most part. How many of them have illusionary managers and carry televisions with them to the ring? Apex, one of the more underrated competitors in the last year, would be a worthy member of "Team WWO" any day of the week. He really never seems to get the credit he deserves, but for for once, perhaps his efforts will be noticed. He truly is one of the best in the World Wrestling Organization. Unlike many, we aren't the least bit afraid to take a "rookie" with us into battle. 5) ULTIMATE THRASHER As long as he's not going by Ikonoclast, we should be fine. When Bryson and Warren stepped out, Thrasher is the one man who stepped up and in. When he wasn't feuding with Roundhouse, he was always raising hell to some degree. The rocker, while on the downside of late, would be a premiere member of the team, and deserves to be here as much as anyone. Honorable Mention: Roundhouse "Playboy" Jude St. Claire "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson Lord Keeblor Jack Lantern "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson OTHER FEDERATIONS - TOP FIVE SINGLES WRESTLERS: ---------------------------------------------- 1) "BLACKHEART" CASEY JAMES [EMWC] With the EMWC World Title eluding him his entire career, Casey recently had opportunity rapping away at his door, but he could not defeat his former Syndicate ally, Tiger Claw. James, a former SCRA World champion, while quite possibly being the greatest IIWF champion to ever live, the man just has not gone stale over the years, like the Kauffman's, the Deathbringer's, and the Kowalski's. He is more dominant now than he's ever been, even if his Syndicate is no more. 2) "MR. WORKRATE" TOMMY JAMES [GLCW] If Apex is underrated, you might have found someone in Tommy James that could make a strong case for being even _more_ underrated. He took GLCW by storm, but nevertheless, never really seemed to get the kind of respect he was looking for; perhaps being a bit overshadowed by the much talked about, Nyx Dunne. James was one of the most fighting champions ever - going to a time-limit draw with LWC great, "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson. James recently dropped his GLCW gold to "Violent" Victor Frost, but Tommy remains one of the major players in the sport... belt or not. 3) "DIAMONDBACK" CHRIS MYERS [EMWC] The original underdog, Chris Myers. They never said he could beat Tiger Claw. He did. They said the same when he locked horns with Hardin. He beat him, too. Myers, the first ever D-Cup winner, has ripped through both the EMWC and UWF, and is currently going to war with EMWC North American champion, Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. If he hadn't abruptly left the EMWC last year, he might be the top dog there today. There is no doubt about him having the talent. That's for sure. 4) "BLOOD ANGEL" SIMON EZRA [EMWC] Crazy? Crazy is an understatement for this man. Ezra, said by many to be the greatest of the greats, gets his kicks by causing harm to as many opponents as possible, especially if their name is Quinn Brown. He could be the EMWC World champion tomorrow and no one would make a fuss about it. Why? It's real simple - fear. There is not a man in the sport that's more feared than the "Blood Angel." It's only... him. 5) "CRIMSON" JOE REED [UWF] Whomever said the good guys never win must have never heard of Joe Reed. However, that statement may hold some truth after all. After years of being a fan favorite, Reed joined the heel Pride stable in the UWF, and has been running rampant through the league for many months now. His championship battles with Randall Osbourne in the UWF were quite the sight, as well as about any of his battles in the EMWC. He has held World championships in both, and continues to wreak havoc today. Honorable Mention: Mark Langseth [EMWC] "Sexy Baby" Kyle Backwood [UWF] William Craven [EMWC] Cadaver [NLWP] "Apostle" Mike Justice [NLWP] Tiger Claw [EMWC] WWO vs. The World (10 matches between each man): ----------------------------------------------- 1) "MR. RATINGS" WARREN HAYES vs. "BLACKHEART" CASEY JAMES How's this, Warren? You, the former frontman of Simply The Best and Even Better, against the head honcho of perhaps the greatest stable every assembled, the Syndicate. If you ever wanted to prove yourself to anyone, Warren, this would be your opportunity to do just that. The advantage for Hayes is the fact James' only ally is James' manager, Brian Lau. No Claw. No Hardin. No Thunder. But don't count Casey out of anything. He was the good guy once too, you know. But in a one-on-one bout, mix in the expected interference, and how can you even begin to guess who the better man would be? WINNER - DRAW (5-5) 2) TYRONE "PURPLE" HAYES vs. "MR. WORKRATE" TOMMY JAMES Think about it... Tyrone hears the word "workrate" and could probably write a book on all the puns and such he could come up with. Tommy, while he does not have too many allies, has a nice big family. Casey... Kyle... Rob, even! And with Rob already laying out Tyrone once before, this would surely be an advantage for the former GLCW World champion. But, Tyrone always seems to find a way to win with Johnstone at his side... then again, this is Tommy James, after all. Once again, where do we even begin to select a dominant winner? WINNER - DRAW (5-5) 3) "MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN vs. "DIAMONDBACK" CHRIS MYERS Two words - DREAM MATCH. Sebastian openly admits his idol in the wrestling world is none other than Chris Myers. This match nearly took place at the March to the Millenium supercard, but Sebastian and Myers each pulled out just after the encounter was announced. Sebastian and Myers have met in a tag team bout in the EMWC, but it was Andrew Tucker, Sebastian's partner, who pinned Myers, while Mike was being Epitomized by Serge Annis. If this match were to occur, however, the similarities would be tremendous, and despite each man's alignment, this would surely be a match full of great sportsmanship. The experience factor goes to Myers, as do most other categories that may aid in deciding the victor. But based on the success acclaimed in the recent past, Sebastian would be the one likely to pull out the most victories, but just barely. WINNER - "MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN (6-4) 4) APEX vs. "BLOOD ANGEL" SIMON EZRA Before asking if Apex could take Ezra, you first must ask if _anyone_ can take Ezra. Thus far, the only man that has come even remotely close is former EMWC World champion, Tiger Claw. Their Killing Box match was hands-down the Match of the Year in the EMWC. Apex may be a bit "out there", but Ezra is flat-out nuts. His trademark is to cut up his opponents with pieces of broken glass, so that is clearly a bit more extreme than talking to "invisible" managers such as Zombie Corners. In a fair, one-on-one encounter _with_ disqualifications, Apex would almost have to hope for a D-Q victory. Ezra... he's just scary, people. Apex would fair much better than the outcome states, but it's just a matter of being matched up against the craziest guy in the sport. The one thing we do know is that they would nearly kill each other, and it would really come down to who could take the most punishment. Ezra. WINNER - "BLOOD ANGEL" SIMON EZRA (7-3) 5) ULTIMATE THRASHER vs. "CRIMSON" JOE REED Thrasher, while being a two-time WWO World champion, would have quite the difficult time in the ring against Reed. First of all, the fans might be more interested in the catfight that is bound to occur on the outside of the ring between Sweet Melissa and the Lady in Red, Reed's fiancee. Thrasher has his following, but unfortunately, Reed is not Roundhouse. Because of that, Thrasher will have his problems, not to mention Reed's Pride stablemates. These men might also give Thrasher a D-Q win or two, but in short, Thrasher is sadly no match for Reed. WINNER - "CRIMSON" JOE REED (8-2) FREE AGENTS: ----------- 1) "MIDAS TOUCH" JOHNNY GLAMOUR [Formerly of GSWP, GLOW] If the WWO were to ever sign Glamour, there would be one _hell_ of a feud between Fang and Glamour. These two fought over the GSWP World Title for months, and that was perhaps the feud of the year. Fang came out the final victor, so you can see why Glamour would like to return. Not to mention the fact he's almost ready for active competition once again... 2) IVAN "THE IRON CURTAIN" RAMIUS [Formerly of NEW, INWI, SJPW, SCRA] This Russian was one sought after piece of talent last year, by a certain Mark Stone inparticular. Ramius, the #1 cruiserweight of 1998, had quite the stellar career in the NEW and SCRA, but never really reached his potential because of outside interests. Ramius, who is definitely looking to return somewhere, is a very good choice for the next recipient of a WWO contract sent out by President Stone. 3) "RODEO" NEVADA HARRIS [Formerly of SCW] It's not Hardin. It's not Thunder. It's not even Curtis. It's Nevada Harris, the man who teamed with "Ironman" Eddie Jacobs to take out "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder in SCW is a free agent once again, and I am sure he would love to bring his cigar smokin' carcass to the WWO, that is, if he is desired. 4) "MR. MAIN EVENT" CHRIS DOUGLAS [Formerly of UKCW, SCRA] Nothing has been heard from Douglas since his days in SCRA, unfortnately. He had quite the involvement with Ethan de Sade and Johnathan Mordenheim, and of course, he was the first-ever UKCW World champion, after defeating the Idol in the final rounds of the tournament. If anyone can match up to Warren Hayes, this is the guy. 5) TEX VIOLENCE [Formerly of EMWC, LWC] Sure, it's a longshot, but it might just be worth the effort. Tex absolutely tore up LWC, as well as the EMWC, for that matter. We all thought Thunder, Hardin, and Curtis were tough hombres, but boy were we ever wrong. Tex would stick his foot right up another man's cornshoot if given the chance, and if he is sought after, perhaps it might be done upon the entire WWO roster. If Stone could land 'ol Tex, the WWO would be in for one hell of a shake-up. So, how do we, as a federation, match up against the rest of the e-wrestling community? Get a few judges to judge everything fairly and evenly, and I say we can hang with just about anyone. Although, a couple of those free agents lifted above wouldn't hurt things. Let's just hope the recruitment department is listening... However, recently I was made aware of an article on the internet in which our hobby, e-wrestling, was being labelled as the reason a young boy had turned to drugs and crime. Now I am a Christian, and most of you know that, and I will _never_ push my beliefs upon you, but what I read was exactly that. Someone trying to use their Christian belief to accuse a hobby in which I participate as being the "devil's work", and trying to hold me in part responsible for a young man's choices. First, let me say that I do not support the federations which are in the webpage article, http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling/sucks , but for anyone to in essence put my hobby as part of the blame for a family member's wrong choices. They are no better than the people who were performing the Salem Witch Trials and the Spanish Inquisition. We're not all angels, and we know that, and yes we push the limits, but to be called responsible for a son's path of drugs and sloth. Folks, that is what we are being called. Responsible for a young boy's wrong decisions. Below is my response I sent to the owner of the web-page, Reverend J. Wheeler. ================================================== Dear Reverend Wheeler, First off, please do not take this letter as an attack on your Christianic faith. I am a Christian myself, and while there are certain things I do not approve of, I am tired of seeing individuals as myself be laden as hardened vindictive individuals because of, and you will have to forgive me for this, blind arrogance of yourself and some of the people who have written to you. Forgive me for saying so, but you yourself said in the opening of your page that at one time you were a professional wrestling fan. You like many of us, have grown tired of seeing what has been pushed in front of us on television wrestling. That is why many of us designed our own federations through the medium known as the internet. I do this myself, as I run my own organization, and I will be the first to say that sometimes the players who participate in our organization try to push the envelope. Sometimes they go beyond the realm of what you and others or even myself at times may find acceptable. But to my knowledge, none of the handlers (as they are kwown in e-wrestling) have begun using drugs or becoming criminals due to their particpation in a "mockery" of what is shown on television. Now I will grant that the examples that you listed are beyond the normal of good taste, and some are downright disgusting. However, you will find that organizations like those are a very slim minority of what is actually out on the internet. You took two of the most extreme examples and accepted them as the basis for everything, when in fact they are the far exception to the norm. Let me ask you Reverend, how would you feel if every christian priest, pastor, and believer was characterized as a sexual deviant because of one or two bad apples in the Christian field? Believe me, reverend, I have run into a few people who I knew fit in the various possible descriptions of "evil", who claimed to be Christian, so as such, now you are, by your logic and the logic of Miss Miggs, are as corrupt as any of the other 'Christians' out there, including myself. Now as for the letter of Miss Miggs, I will agree that there are people who are sick and evil, but i have to question the wisdom of her statement, and her immediate attachment to e-wrestling. "He was smoking marijuna, drinking, and even started shoplifting to get an adrenalin rush. " None of the above items are condoned by myself or any of a number of players in this game. Now granted, I know a few of the players who have done this, but they have been doing this long before they started participating in e-wrestling. To the majority of us, e-wrestling is simply a creative outlet to write and consider how we would do things differently than what we see on television. "when my brother turned 18 he left home and hasn't been back since. He's now living in a one bedroom apartment with three other boys. All they do every day is get high off cough syrup, shoot heroin, and play in "e-Feds" on the Internet. " I ask Miss Miggs, to prove to me that there is any corollation between e-wrestling as a hobby and her brother falling in with a bad crowd, which is what I believed what happened. And I do not blame e-wrestling for the Biggs family in failing to bring up their son in a proper environment, as harsh as that may sound. Because she is making a huge leap to this conclusion. Becaue I know many professional individuals, college graduates, successful business men, who take a little bit of time out of their busy schedule, just to relax in one way or another. I am sorry that her brother fell from the path of what is good and just, but to point the blame at a hobby? Perhaps Miss Miggs won't admit it, but there is only one person to blame, and that is her brother. He had choices in his life, and he ended up making the wrong ones. For that he has my prayers. But Miss Miggs and her family is trying to make herself and her family lighten their own conscience by passing the buck. Forgive me for saying so, Reverend, but Miss Miggs family failed to find a way to keep her brother on the straight and narrow, and now is looking for someone to blame. Who's next, Reverend? Do we blame the movie industry for producing slut films? Do we blame baseball for producing greedy selfish ball players? What about television game shows? They promote the selfishness among average individuals. Who's next Reverend? What will it take for Christian to stop blaming groups for individuals' failures? Perhaps when Christianity is found to be the next corrupting influence? And at the rate it's going, Reverend, that day won't be too far off, before Christians such as you and myself wil be the next great evil in the world. And Reverend, who do you think will be there to support us? The people that you've tried to categorize as the scourge?
