WWO bio card — Lion Tamer

1 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lion Tamer Wrestler Name: Unknown Date of Birth: Height: 6'7 Weight: 242 Alignment: Taoist Hometown: The Jungle Entrance Music: YtseJam by Dream Theater Manager: Tiger Lily Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ring Appearance Psychological Makeup Strengths Intelligence: Lion Tamer is a smart guy. And even more helpful he is very experienced. What he lacks in strength and wrestling ability he makes up with his knowledge of wrestling and just life all around. He knows how to avoid most of the tricks, and knows even more how to use them. Pride: Pride seems to be the downfall of most wrestlers. Not so in this case. Lion Tamer has too much pride to get beat without a fight. He hates losing, and will do what it takes to avoid embarassment. Whether this means cheating, kicking out when he should be pinned, walking out on a match, Lion Tamer will often find a way to do it. Cheating: Lion Tamer doesn't care a whole lot for the fans. And while having a strong set of morals, his morals are different then most. So cheating comes natural to him. His other two strenghts combine to make this strength one of Lion Tamer's most dangerous features. He has the experience and knowledge of when and how to cheat and he will do it anytime to avoid a loss. Add Tiger Lily into the equation, she has been with Lion Tamer for most of his career, and can be most helpful when it comes to cheating. Weaknesses Cockiness: Lion Tamer talks himself into trouble rather often. His amazing bragging ability is good at getting his opponents fired up. Teamwork: Just an all around problem. He has to much pride to work in a team. He usually doesn't make friends. Only if it reults in bigger goals will he join up with other wrestlers. When he does team up, he is not that great at it. Lion Tamer rarely even gets along with his loyal valet. Distraction: Lion Tamer can get distracted from time to time. He can lose easily site of his match if his heart is not set. He will often take underdogs lightly. Biographical Information Lion Tamer is a laid back guy who has a soft spot for being called Wrestler X. Wrestler X won world titles in DIWL and XeWA and now he wants to in one in the WWO. He kind of hates wrestling. He thinks the whole thing is fake, immature, and basically a waste of time. That is why he retired two years ago, despite enjoying a nice career. Lion Tamer is probably more experienced then most in the ring which is one of his main advantages. He thinks he is better then everyone. Lion Tamer isn't very far from a typical cocky heel. He has too much pride to back down from most matches even on his worse days. Really he is a Taoist, Selfish, and Miscreant wrestler. But he chose Taoist because it sounds funny. He's not even sure how to pronounce it correctly. Lion Tamer has come here because he was one of a selected few of XeWA/DIWL wrestlers. Although it was Apex who asked him to join, do not be confused. He hates Apex and Apex probably hates him. They have had a pretty lengthy feud in the past. This involved burning a hotel room down and all sorts of forgettable stuff. Both would probably say they won the feud. They may possibly find out once and for all in the WWO who is the better man. Lion Tamer also recognizes another man here. Dark Soul. These two wrestlers defintaely did NOT get along in the past. Even if they try to avoid each other as much as possible these guys will probably end up fighting a couple dozen times. And if history repeats itself Lion Tamer will end up at the bottom. Lion Tamer's only major injury came at hand to Dark Soul and friends. I do beive Apex was involved here as well. Tamer sufferred a pretty bad neck ingury and back pains. He has had plenty of time to heal though. His valet Tiger Lily has been there for him throughout the ages. They get along pretty well even if they don't act like it. And when it comes to managing she is not quite as innocent as she seems. She is almost as experienced as Lion Tamer when it comes to cheating. Besides his wrestling career and personality, which has been known to change from time to time, there is not much background on the man. Nobody really knows where he is from or what his real name is. It's not some corny "hailing from unknown" gimmick, he just doesn't care to reveal this information. If he even knows. He likes to play guitar. He likes to boss Tiger Lily, his valet, around. He likes to win. He likes funny little creatures. He likes being lazy, and it is late, so he is going to go to sleep now. See you in the ring, if you're lucky enough to touch this man.
