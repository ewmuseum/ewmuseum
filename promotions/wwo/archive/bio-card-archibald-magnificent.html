Archibald the Magnificent Wrestler Name: Archibald ??? Date of Birth: Height: 6'5 Weight: 255 pounds Alignment: Diabolic Hometown: Quebec City, Quebec, Canada Entrance Music: "Third Orchestral Suite : Gigue" from J.S. Bach Manager: Vincent Delacroix (manager) Francis Blake (bodyguard / butler) Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Archibald is well built, well muscled, lacking a bit of definition, but not that much. He has long hair, worn in a ponytail. Green eyes. He has no beard, but does have some sideburns. Ring Appearance Archibald the Magnificient comes ot the ring in a ring robe, gold with "THE MAGNIFICIENT" written on the back in black letters. He actually kind of look like a boxer when he gets out toward the ring. When he removes his robe (or before, since it's half-opened), he wears black tights, with a golden "M" on the back. His legs and the rest of his body is well muscled. He wears black wrestling boots. Psychological Makeup Strengths Likes to fight, lives to fight, so confident that he isn't affraid of anything. Knows surprisingly well his way around the ring for a relative rookie. A great cheater. Weaknesses Thinks that everything shall be given to him : recognition, victories, respect. Overrate himself frequently. Always looks for the easy way out, doesn't want to pay his dues. Biographical Information Archibald the Magnificiant is the child of a rich family, and possibly always got what he wished throught his life. He truly belives himself to live his name. He decided to become a wrestler, looking for easy glory, looking to prove himself to any doubters. He took along his bodyguard, butler, or whatever else, and also a former wrestler who suffered a back injury shortening his wrestling career, "Firestarter" Francis Blake. He toured the independants, but always had to live with the frustrations coming from the lack of exposure. Eventually, he met with former IWF/WOW Rage announcer, recently fired, looking to start a multifed stable, an "Order" as he called it. He offered Archibald the Magnificient his guidance, knowing his capacities from his days in the SLWA (St.Lawrence Wrestling Association) where he dominated a short-lived feud with surfer Johnny Destiny. The Magnificient accepted the offer, and together, they slowly are making their way to the majors.
