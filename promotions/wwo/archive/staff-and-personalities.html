Staff and Personalities

9 April 2001

Who runs the World Wrestling Organization? Honestly? The handlers of the World Wrestling Organization are good enough to run the entire federation on their own. But it takes someone to put it together. Front Offices/Behind the Scenes It starts with WWO President [name withheld]. President [name withheld] started as an assistant show producer until March 1997, when then President and still owner [name withheld] handed the reigns over to the "new man". [name withheld] kept the federation running for 4 years before suffering a nervous breakdown from the daily hustle and bustle. After a year of playing manager, [name withheld] came to the conclusion that the standard of excellence in professional wrestling wasn't there any longer. And someone had to do something to fix that, and thus began the rebirth of the World Wrestling Organization. To contact President [name withheld], send him an email from HERE Tracy Hudson Vice President, Tag Team Division A former wrestler in the WWO, This appointment came as a huge surprise to everyone who know [name withheld]'s philosophy and Hudson's personality. But [name withheld] has given his confidence in Hudson in keeping the excitement level up in the WWO Tag Team ranks. Now it's up to Hudson to do just that. Dave Olvera, Vice President, Talent Coordinator To complete the "sweep" of unusual surprise appointments, President [name withheld] immediately turned to former Source editor, David Olvera to reload the WWO roster. Frequently identified as a similar type of individual as Tracy Hudson, people began to wonder who VP Olvera would bring in, but so far, the new talent has been solid and VP Olvera has promised to keep the good talent coming in. WWO Commissioner Christopher Stonebreaker A former WWO superstar, former wrestling superstar. And now he's laying down the law in the World Wrestling Organization from the the other side of the ring ropes. WWO Light Heavyweight VP, Currently Unfilled This position was previously held by [name withheld], who had to give a leave of absence. As of yet, the position has not been refilled, and my even be dropped in the near future. On-Air Personalities To insure that the shows go off without a hitch, or a bare minimum, the World Wrestling Organization tries to get the best on-air personalities for the job, and arguably, it's not a bad group of people who make the shows run. Take a look at some of those people you see week in and week out. Mickey Ralph If there was an announcer who was a wrestling "mark", Mickey is it. Mickey isn't stupid of course, but a lot of things take him by surpise when they shouldn't. Of course, he's in on a lot of the happenings, but never lets on if he knows something or not. For the past year, Mickey's been working on a novel, "The Voice of Reason", an exclusive look at the backstage happenings in the World Wrestling Organization, from his own point of view. He hasn't decided to publish it yet, but if he does, some heads might be turning! Sam Bradley All we can say, is don't get on Sam's badside. Sam will use every name in the book to get under people's skin he hates, and he will brown-nose everyone he likes until they get tired of him. The heels of the WWO love Sam, because in his eyes, everyone's got it coming to them, and Sam is more than happy to see it given. During his vacation, Sam temporarily took over the duties for the "E-fed-erali" wrestling rating service, and got great reviews, but due to a lack of staff (you don't think it could have anything to do with his personality, do you?), the project was closed unceremoniously. Lenny Wacus Lenny is a straight shooter. He calls it as he sees it. He doesn't like foul play in the sport, but he knows it's going to happen. He knows that the workers make it ahead in the world, and he's not afraid to let anyone know w hen they seem to be slacking off. Lenny is President [name withheld]'s voice into ventures where [name withheld] cannot tread for various reasons. Lenny used his vacation to get away from professional wrestling and spend some time with his daughter and grandson. If it weren't for his huge respect for [name withheld], he might have turned down the job altogether. Johnny Calhoun Yes, folks, the worst dressed personality in the World Wrestling Organization is back! A quiet, but important part of the WWO team. Johnny is the WWO ring announcer, and probably the world's best poster child for the "don't do this" fashion patrol. Johnny is the ring announcer for almost every WWO event, and a "fixture" in the World Wrestling Organization. During his time off, Johnny expanded his wardrobe, which makes Goodwill happy, as they made a fortune of J.C. Unfortunately, the expanded wardrobe looks about as tacky as the original. Mackey Jay A roving reporter, who signed on with the WWO in particular for some indepth interviews with Rusty Bowman, Mackey has expanded his interviewing skills to cover the entire roster of the WWO. Unfortunately, many wrestlers enjoy using Mackey as a "verbal punching bag", giving him little or no safety in his interview segments. In his offtime, Mackey does some singing in various nightclubs, and hopes some day to land a record contract, but as of yet, his career remains with the World Wrestling Organization. Lisa Cultree Lisa was a supermodel working in Los Angeles, when one of the WWO staff members made note to President [name withheld] that we didn't have any "eye appeal" for the younger male demographic. Lisa was just getting off one of her fashion shoots, in which the WWO was a large contributor, and Lisa and President [name withheld] hit off a very successful combination, and a contract was signed, and Lisa became the permament cohost of the WWO's Live Mike program. Kimberly Campbell Known to the WWO at one point as Kimberly Cantrell, she originally became known to the WWO when doing a magazine interview with Apex, the WWO staff was amazed at her poise with the "disturbed" wrestler, and decided to offer her a part time contract with the WWO, and paired her with Mackey Jay as a match announcer. In the year of the WWO's hiatus, Kimberly had some family problems, and went through a divorce, reclaiming her maiden name. She still does the occasional interview, but as of now, she's apparently focused her attention on acting as a sort of "press agent" for Apex.
