James Knight - Tommy -------------------------------------------------------------------- Take It From Me by [name withheld] contact: [detail withheld] Its with a heavy heart, that I have to make this announcement. This is the last issue of the Source, from me. Now now, stop all your crying. I know it's hard to take, yes, I was a brilliant editor. Like all things in life and the WWO though, all good things must come to an end, or at least handed onto another person until they can no longer take it. Actually I have a confession to make, this is the very last Source, period. The end, no mas, un fin. I'd like to take this time to thank everyone right now... Jeremy, for being the straw that stirs my drink. Tamer, I don't care if you do have a real name, I just like calling you Tamer because you've always tamed my savage heart, I'll so very, very miss you. Yeti, Dale, Hootie, Dave, Chyrs, Mark, Rich, and Errol. If it weren't for losers like you all, I wouldn't have my weekends free to go out and drink it up like the unknown member of the Rat Pack and screw like a rabbit. Thanks guys, it just wouldn't have been as much fun, without you. Wow. All this being honest, it's really got me choked up. You know, I can't hide it anymore, I have to reveal the truth behind it all. Everyone, I am [name withheld]. That's right I been fooling you all, all along. There is no [name withheld]. It's just me. He's a figment of my imagination. Someone I just created, so I could have fun with the WWO, just so we could all enjoy this little thing called the WWO. I couldn't bare the load any longer. I mean really guys, this is one hell of a hard charade to carry. The fake photos of the person I pulled off www.reallifelosers.com to say it was me. The extra computer I used to mask my initial i.p. address. and the extra instant messenger programs. All the things I went through, just to carry the charade of me being a real person, and living in Mississippi. Jesus, who would admit living there? The thought, personally, disgusts me. Really, how much better could it be, it was all brilliant. Who would ever think it was all a charade? Honestly, I just got sick and tired of all the typing. You lazy bastards. Screw you all. I just got tired of it all. So there it is. The truth. So no longer is this just the final issue of the Source, its the end of the WWO all together. Enjoy this final issue of the Source, and the reviews of the final cards as well. If this is the first part of their two night event in San Francisco, my advice to everone is to stay home and to catch this in the $2 bin of your local videostore 5 years down the road. I mean really, you know it's a going to bad when only 2 matches are announced and the rest of the card is up in the air. Two matches were all that happened anyways. Here's the rundown. 1st round IP Tag Team Title Matches: Fire and Ice vs. The Marky Marvelous Troupe - I don't even have to tell you guys how strange the crowd looked tonight. I mean, I heard a rumor that this place was filled by them handing out comp tickets at all the local late night hot spots. Uh, this is San Franisco, I don't think it was too bright an idea. Either way, the opening match was announced and Lew Spectre came out with Michael Ice as the new Fire & Ice. Or is it the Firm v3.0 now? Their opponents were The Marky Marvelous Troupe. I'm not sure what happened. All I know is that there were pride flags waving all over the place and I missed the finish. I think I heard someone say how upset they were with the S&M show with the opening band. Opening band? Something tells me, this is going to get ugly. Blood Brothers vs. Dark Haven -The Blood Brothers are announced and their is a huge pop as the come out. This place went NUTS. Then Dark Haven came out and proceeded to kick the crap out of the Blood Brothers. Then, it really got ugly. The whole crowd went into a riot, it was awful. They stormed the ring and proceed to slap the living daylights out of Dark Haven. I heard one man screamed aloud how Dark Haven would make a great bunch of Cabana Boys. I heard another gathering of fans scream how dare these guys ruined the Village People concert before the other 3 could even make their on stage appearance. It was at this point, that the cops raided the place. I managed to sneak away and let me tell you. I hope for tomorrow night, the crowd is much, much different. Good thing WWO won't make this mistake again of distributing comp tickets to anyone to just fill the place. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Showcase Review by Donny McOysterpop "-sicles are great tasteing and great for testesterone.. cause I need all the help I can get" contract: [detail withheld] ===== WWO SUPERSTAR SHOWCASE ==== - Oh my god. What the hell happened to this joint? It looks like a mad mob raided the place last night! Well, its the final night for the WWO and as being one of the long time fans, I'm here to see it go out in grand style! I hope I'm not disappointed. Handicapped Match: Team NeX vs. Fydor Samsonov - The Russian continued his winning ways and beat both members of Team Nex. Great feat of strength? No, great feat of the vocals. Fydor Samsonov insisted on singing his rendition of the National Anthem of the former USSR (Former? Right.. that's the biggest April Fools' Day joke of all.. just watch!). Team Nex immeadiatly gave up. 1st round IP Tag Team Title Matches: Canadian Legacy vs. Midnight Stars - No match. Canadian Legacy came out, apologized for actually trying to make something of themselves and vowed to go back to being just Les Canadians. Don Cameron gave his final tearful farewell speech and vowed to never appear in wrestling ever again. He meant it. He swore that this was really finally it. Well, at least until he shows up to wrestle in OSW next week. -This left the Midnight Stars as the final IP tag camps. They hugged each other and walked out, swearing to defend the titles for as long as the WWO was around. Er, yeah. Best of three falls: Amazing Romanis vs. PAIN (winner gets world title shot at Renaissance.. loser faces Canadian Legacy or MIdnight Stars in IP tournament) -The most incredible match of the night. I have never, ever seen anything this spectacular in my life. 1st fall went to the Romanis. The 2nd fall went to Pain. The 3rd fall went all the way and the match was declared... a draw! The end result? Vegas Connection retires the WWO World Tag Champs. Leary was heard saying afterwards, "I've never been so proud of doing so little, yet attaining so much. See kids, sitting on yer' ass does work out for the better!" "Rubber Match" (Non-Title) Latex Lex vs. Tyrone Hayes - The 7th seal has been broken. My god, the unthinkable has happened. In this 'Rubber match" a pole was placed in the middle of the ring with a glow condom placed on top. First one to reach it, was able to use it. Latex Lex came out mask and all... and was up the pole and used the condom right away to poke Tyrone Hayes in the eye with pointy edges of the package. He fell on top of Tyrone Hayes for the pin and the unthinkable happened. He stood up, ripped off his mask and revealed himself to be, King $$$ Perfect (And if this doesn't prove this is a joke edition, I don't know what would!) and to answer the age old question he grabed the microphone and said, "It's because... I'm money, money, money babe!". When asked what he was going to do with the WWO belt he responded, "Have it bronzed you jackass!" (and this part .. well this part might just be true... he'd actually do it! I swear!) -So thats it, the WWO ends its glorious run, and in the end. King $$$ Perfect, walks away with the gold after all.
