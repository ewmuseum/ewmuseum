Fabulous Finds with Tyrone "Purple" Hayes

8 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~An old, but familar, voice comes over the television screen... along with what look like poorly made dolls from some poor, third world nation.~ [ Winston Allen Saed ]: Today on our program, find out how you too can own an amazing piece of hand crafted plastic goodness fashioned in the shape of one of your favorite WWO superstars! ~The small dolls are now shown on a revolving platform. The likenesses of "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes, Randy Bryson, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson, Fury, and Roundhouse are proudly displayed... in various unflattering positions.~ [ Winston Allen Saed ]: All this, and more today on... FABULOUS FINDS! And now your host... "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes! ~Golf claps accompany the announcer's sentence, and the familiar black man from Tuscaloosa, Alabama comes out on stage in what looks to be an orange and brown plaid suit raided from Todd "The Rod" Johnstone's closet. Tyrone's normally straight hair is slicked and combed in the style of bad used car salesmen the world over. He has on powder blue loafers and the burnt umber slacks he is wearing don't go all the way down his legs, showing off his white socks with blue strips and knee garders. Tyrone has an old 70's style small microphone in his hand and flashes a cheesy gameshow host smile.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Hi there folks, and welcome once again to Fabulous Finds! I'm your host with the most, "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes. Today we have another wonderous and exciting piece of crap for you to blow your children's college education on, so sit tight and prepare to be amazed. ~The audience begins to ooh and ahh like baby harp seals with fetal alchol syndrome.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: So without further ado, let's bring out the man who helped to bring these dolls to the forefront of our national consciousness... here is "Alligator" Todd Johnstone! ~More golf claps has a the rotund manager of Tyrone comes out decked out in a khaki explorer's uniform complete with pith helmet and wool socks. Todd has on a cheesy smile and is carrying a small brown box with him has he exaggerates his excursion to the stage table Tyrone is currently standing behind. Todd leaves the cover on the box, furthering the anticipation of the retarded audience.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: G'Day mates! How are all you people doing today? Well it ain't no matter to me, because today I've got the deal of all deals for you, right here on Fabulous Finds! ~Our audience cheers like baby harp seals with down syndrome. Tyrone waits until the crowd is electrically shocked into silence before raising his eyebrows in the stereotypical infomercial doubt manner.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Deal of all deals? What type of amazing, yet utterly useless, item are you trying to sell us? I think you're pulling my chain. [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Ain't none of that septic ruse here Tyrone. What I've got for you is TEN times better than what's currently on the market. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Is it actual talent for Warren Hayes? ~A few yelps and gasps escape from the crowd.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: No mate, I'm afraid that not even I can come up with something that implausible. ~Tyrone, Todd, and the audience share in a good, hearty laugh at the expense of Warren Hayes. The better of the two WWO Hayes, at least in his own mind, wipes a false tear away from his eye and pats Todd on the shoulder.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: All jokes aside Todd, what is this amazing discovery that you have chosen to show exclusively on Fabulous Finds? [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Well Tyrone, today I thought I'd show you something I found on my trip to the sweat shops of South Korea. Funny story about South Korea, seems they don't speak Japanese over in those parts. ~Tyrone does that Macauley Culkin face slap gag once again.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: You're kidding! I thought everyone over there spoke Japanese! [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Well mate, I reckon you were wrong then. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Well despite how amazing that fact is, I'm sure you've got something even more amazing to show us in that box! [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: You bet'cha Tyrone. In fact, what I have in this here box is so amazing, I almost didn't wanna show it on television! ~Tyrone tries to get a peek at what lies underneath the box cover, only to have his hand slapped away by Todd. The crowd is now showing more surprise and anticipation, waiting for the box's contents to be shown.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: All in good time, my American friend. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: C'mon Todd, the suspense is killing us, right folks? ~The docile audience begins to cry foul and looks to be close to a full revolt.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Alright, alright. What if I told you that you could have all the action, glory, and pretentiousness of the WWO's "Greatest" superstars right in your very own home. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: I'd be wondering if you're off your meds Todd... er I mean, WOW! You're kidding?! How is that possible? ~A buzz goes through the crowd... and after that bit of electro-shock, the crowd begins to ooh and aah some more.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Easy Tyrone, through the unethical use of child labor and terrible working conditions that South Korea is known for! Thanks to their sacrificing of children, today I can bring you WWO Wrassle Bud-E's! ~With all the style and flair of a used car salesman working on commission, Todd unveils several of the South Korean made WWO Wrassle Bud-E's. Sitting on a poorly made stand are crude likenesses of "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, Randy Bryson, Roundhouse, Fury, "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson, and Patrick Shade. Tyrone looks less than impressed, but nonetheless, goes on like the trooper he is. Hayes picks up the Patrick Shade doll, gives it a quick once over, then looks at the rest of the shoddy toys.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Now I know who Patrick Shade is, but who are the rest of these chumps? [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Well we all know who Patrick Shade is mate, but to answer your question, these are who Daesan Inc. of South Korea consider the top WWO superstars. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: What?! No Zac Schnieder? No Mason? No Tim Beck? Man, those Koreans are sure a stupid lot. [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Fear not mate, each and every one of these dolls comes with voice ablity, that way you can eventually learn to recognize these less than stellar "superstars" and have hours upon hours of fun listening to their inane babble. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Really? Why don't you show us this "talking" ablity, and maybe then I'll believe it for myself. [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Fair enough. Why don't we try this Warren Hayes fella. ~Todd picks up the makeshift Warren Hayes doll and deftly pulls the string sticking out of a very unflattering position. Tyrone holds his lame microphone up to the doll for people to here.~ [ "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes Doll ]: Oh, Oh, Oh! I am, 'ow you saying, Miss-terre Ratings, ze captAIN of ze love and ze sparkling example of le espirt du Quebec! [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: WOW! It sounds just like him... sorta... I guess... [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Wait a second mate, it gets better! ~Todd again pulls on the Wazza doll's string.~ [ "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes Doll ]: Sacre Merde! I am, 'ow is it, afraid to be facing ze Australian Roundhouse! I am wonter'ring 'ow ze WWO book-ers could make the miztake of 'aving moi face zat mon-stair! Zut allors! [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: See what I mean mate? ~Tyrone is busy putting the Johnny Detson doll and the Mike Sebastian doll in Ku Klux Klan wear and leather gear, respectively, to notice Todd... until Johnstone kicks him in the shins.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Ow... I mean, wow! But I'm still not convinced! [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: Well mate, I know for a fact that you have a wrassling match against that pillar of AWOL, Randy Bryson. So just to show you how authentic these lil babies are, I brought him along so you can try to understand your opponent. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: What's there to understand? As soon as he leaves, things get better, then he comes back to stink up the place. Stupid Stone... er I mean, thanks Todd! Let's have a listen. ~Todd grabs the Randy Bryson doll, in a flattering little blue pair of tights and a Detroit Red Wings jersey, and pulls his head, which is actually the cord to get him to speak.~ [ Randy Bryson Doll ]: Get your fu[TV Edit] hands off of me! Damn it! This is too much hard work, I'm gonna go on vacation like the loser I am! Glad I can get away with this crap time and time again. What the fu [TV Edit] you looking at? Don't you know who I am?! I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! I once was the offical jock strap rack for the Detroit Red Wings! I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! Get your goddamn, mother fu[TV edit] mug outta my face! Why? Cause I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Is that it? [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: I guess. Seems those Koreans didn't have much to go on when making Bryson. [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Well it's amazing... [ Randy Bryson Doll ]: Did I say you could talk, sh[TV Edit] for brains? No! So you'd better shut your mouth because I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! I once sodomized a pig on national TV! Why? Because I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson and I could get away with it! Quit breathing you overgrown heap of cow sh[TV Edit]! I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson and I say so, that's why! [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Um... how much for these AMAZING dolls? [ Randy Bryson Doll ]: I'll show you something amazing! I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! I fu[TV Edit] your mom raw from Monday til Sunday! Why? I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! That's why! I've broken every trick in the book because no one cared enough to watch me! Why? Because I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! ~Tyrone grabs the Bryson doll and drops it on the floor.~ [ Randy Bryson ]: Is that all you've got you sorry as[TV Edit] motherfu [TV Edit], sh[TV Edit], co[TV Edit], imbecile? Well it ain't enough to stop Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson! I think I'll go to Aruba now! Why? Because I'm... ~And now Hayes proceeds to stomp on the irritating doll.~ [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: $39.95 folks! Just 39.95 for these amazing replicas of your favorite WWO stars! ~The crowd begins to clap in approval... but a few electrical charges change their minds.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: I don't think the crowd likes that deal Todd... what else have you got to sweeten it up... [ Randy Bryson Doll ]: I've got something for you bi[TV Edit]! I'm Randy fu[TV Edit] Bryson... ~Hayes begins to repeatedily jump up and down on the Bryson doll. Little bits of Bryson fly out at the audience, much to their chagrin.~ [ Randy Bryson Doll ]: Is that all you've... sh[TV Edit] for brains? No! I'm Randy fu... book! ...no one cared... [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Shut the hell up you stupid pile of crap! Die! Die! Die! [ "Alligator" Todd Johnstone ]: How about $39.96? ~The lemmings that pass for an audience here cheer at the new, "low" price. Meanwhile, Tyrone has grabbed an emergency axe.~ [ "Honest" Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ]: Will you fu[TV Edit] die already you son-of-a-bi[TV Edit]! ~The Fabulous Finds logo flashes on the screen as this edition comes to an end.~ Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
