WWO bio card — "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson Wrestler Name: Tracy Hudson Date of Birth: June 13, 1974 Height: 6'1 Weight: 223 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Portland, Oregon Entrance Music: "Deep Six" By Big Black Manager: Moongirl Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Even though Hudson is 6'1 and 223 pounds, he's quite slender (or scrawny, if you will). However, he does have a very well defined upper body. He has what would be considered a very pleasant looking face. At least it'd be considered so if it wasn't filled with piercings all over his eyebrow, nose, lip, ears, and tongue. His piercings extend to his nipples, belly, and *ahem* "Tracy, Jr." His most distinguishing facial feature are his eyes, which are large and a deep green in color. His hair is shoulder length, and dyed black with red streaks through it.Fashion wise, Hudson tends to go for the gutter trash look. Old combat boots, cut off military style shorts, and old concert t-shirts are the order of the day. His idea of accessorizing is this: wear your leather jacket as much as humanly possible. And he does. Ring Appearance Psychological Makeup Strengths Power: Hudson is actually quite strong for his size. Because of this, he can go toe to toe with much bigger people, and look pretty good doing so. Provided the opponent isn't 400 pounds or over, Hudson can lift the man easily. All-round skill: Hudson is well versed in nearly every style of wrestling. He can exchanged skilled blows with martial artists. He can fly as well as the lucha style wrestlers. He can get into an all out brawl. He can fight his opponent's style of fight, and not be at much of a disadvantage. Psychological warfare: Outside the ring, Hudson can pick an opponent, find his buttons, and push them mercilessly. And he will. Inside the ring, Hudson knows how to get any opponent hopping mad, which may push him off of his gameplan, thus playing right into Tracy's hands. Weaknesses Inferiority Complex: Hudson may not know this consciously, but he subconsciously believes that he's quite a colossal failure. He sees his current place in the industry as a direct result of his not being good enough to compete. He even sees his chosen profession as a direct result of his failure to succeed as an artist. This doesn't manifest in every match he competes in, but sometimes, he'll make really stupid mistakes as he's about ready to win a match because of this. Killer instinct: Hudson likes to not really win. He likes to maim opponents. It isn't enough to win. He has to crush his foe utterly. As a result, he won't always go for the pin when he probably should, thus allowing said foe to mount a comeback. Bad knee: Like a ton of wrestlers out there, Hudson suffered a really nasty ACL tear to his left knee. Even though it's strong enough to wrestle with, it'll never be at 100%. A foe attentive enough to see this will go for said knee, which severly hampers Hudson's mobility and ability to withstand the pain with any submission move which would involve that leg. Biographical Information Tracy Hudson was born on June 13, 1974. As the child of a working lower class family, Tracy's childhood probably would have been regarded as mainly unimportant, were it not for one detail. See, it had become apparant, that even at the tender age of two years old, Hudson was a genius. At two, he taught himself how to read and write on his own. By the age of six, he was reading at a high school level, and involved with teaching himself a variety of skills, such as playing the guitar.Unfortunately, due to Tracy's father's rampant pattern of alcoholism and abuse, and his mother's willingness to put up with this, Tracy himself was something of a loner, and rather unwilling to share his talents with others. As is the case with such people, Tracy was a very sensitive young boy. And, when his parents finally divorced, the proceedings, and the custody battle that followed, turned Hudson intoa very enraged and hateful boy.He had no outlet to vent his growing hatred of everything. Because of this, he was prone to violent outbursts and got himself expelled from elementary school. He would go on to continue his education in a school for troubled children, but that actually made him worse, as the only thing he learned was an ability to hide his emotionaly problems long enough to insinuate himself into society.He could never quite keep his impulses contained for long though, and subsequently found himself in constant trouble with authority figures and even the law. Finally in high school, things seemed to come to a head.People had hoped that transferring Tracy to the same high school as his older brother Tyler would do him a world of good, as he would have a guiding hand to help steer him clear of trouble. In hindsight, had Tyler been something other than a recreational drug using punk, it probably would have helped Tracy somewhat. As it stands though, being within each other's sphere of influence on a constant basis made things even worse. Tracy and Tyler became skinheads, and got after school jobs as small time drug dealers and thugs-for-hire. His grades were always decent enough, but his extracurricular activities made him even more of an outcast as well as a target for the members of the more popular cliques.The Hudson Brothers then got arrested and expelled from high school on one fateful day, when one of the "jocks" from their high school singled Tracy out as a personal target. Tracy and Tyler's subsequent was quite efficient, swift, and almost fatal. Surprisingly, the charges against both Hudsons were dismissed, and Tracy found himself having to go to yet another new school. Tracy then smartened up, and learned to hide his activities very well from the authorities, while graduating from high school.It was in college when Hudson discovered his calling...sort of. Hudson was studying to become an artist at one of the more prestigious art schools of the Pacific Northwest. However, he had another love: fighting. He was training in Muay Thai boxing while fighting for money in more than a few illegal matches. It was at one of these matches that a local wrestling promoter spotted him and saw dollar signs in Tracy. Appealing to his pocketbook, this promoter was able to get Tracy into his wrestling school, and was also able to get Hudson into a regional fed. Sadly, it's been a downhill slide from there. From the regionals, Hudson was able to jump on board the WWO, where he became the United States Heavyweight Champion for almost a year. Sadly, he's never been able to put it all together. He had a run in EMWC, in which he was booked for some decent things. But Hudson screwed himself on that. In the GLCW, Hudson had a good angle going, and a lot of support from the people running the show. Again, Hudson did a Grade-A screwjob on himself there too. And why is this?It's simple. You see, Hudson has a problem: Hudson is a major league drug addict. Most of the things you saw him do in his promos were 100% authentic. As is the case with drug addicts, Hudson was very unreliable and something of a liability. So, Hudson's world began collapsing all around him, with Hudson way too messed up to realize it.Hudson finally understood the enormity of his situation when his manager (at the time) and good friend Danny Janssen informed Tracy that he was leaving the wrestling industry. This caused Hudson to to take a good long look at himself, and the type of life he was leading. This in turn caused Hudson to suffer a mild nervous breakdown.For almost six months, Hudson holed up in his home after his turn at inpatient rehab. While he was doing this, he decided to prioritize the things in his life. It was while doing this when he rediscovered the desire to be a wrestler once again. There was, as always, a problem with this. See, Hudson is now what we like to call "damaged goods". He thinks that no major fed in their right mind would take him in (and he may be right). But someone else figured out a way to work him back in Hudson's first major federation.Heh... betcha want to find this mysterious benefactor and kill him, don't ya? However, instead of going for a submission, Hudson picks the victim up in a urinage, and spinning 180 degrees, slams the victim on the mat. From there, Hudson can either go for a pin or a tap out.
