WWO bio card — Apex

28 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Apex Real Name: Luis Quesada Residence: Born in Phillidelphia, Pennsylvannia. Currently resides in Anchorage, Alaska Height: 6'3 Weight: 237 lbs Date of Birth: 06/07/76 Manager: Zombie Corners... possibly... Managerial Profile Agent: Charlie, the Old Bum. Managerial Profile Entrance Theme Music: "El Distorto de Melodica" by Everclear Alignment: Scrupulous Psychological Makeup Strengths Willpower: The old heart factor. Very few wrestlers want to win as much as Apex. And along those lines, very few wrestlers want to please the crowd as much as Apex. This desire leads Apex to do things that another person of his physical build simply couldn't do. Getting up from the third chair shot, for instance. Or escaping some devilish finishing submission. And so on. Ability to Improvise: Sometimes things just don't go like planned. Actually, in most cases, things don't. While Apex will go into every match with a basic plan, his success comes from being able to adjust his plan to meet that of his opponents. Recklessness: Apex will do whatever it takes, within reason, to get the job done. He will jump from the top turnbuckle to the outside, he'll do a moonsault off of the cage, and he'll risk his life to save a friend (real or imaginary) without a second thought. Weaknesses Easily Distracted: Apex isn't the type of guy to get crowd approval before doing each and every move. He would be, but the crowd always approves of pretty much everything he does. Instead, he's the kind of guy who gets into arguments with valets, bodyguards, managers, impartial referees, and so on. He's a complete sucker for the, "Oh, I'll follow your manager out of the ring to get one pathetic shot in at him, before you sneak up behind me and knock me out" move. Individuals outside the ring aren't his only distraction. In fact, they are probably much less of a factor than Apex 's invisible entourage. Arguments with the imaginary Zombie Corners (usually in the form of a ring post or turnbuckle) are common in Apex's matches. Slow Learner: Apex will fall for the same trick over and over. This applies to actual in-ring events and interview/backstage occurrences. For instance, if Apex was to face off against a wrestler with less than high morals. And this wrestler threw powder in Apex's eyes or used outside interference to his advantage, well, it'd probably never occur to Apex to look out for that when they next met. Recklessness: Like most people, his strengths are his weaknesses. In wrestling, it doesn't always pay off to be reckless. Some of his thoughtless actions, like leaping the cage, can be quiet effective and devastating. Others, like attacking 6 or 7 men at once, usually aren't so helpful. Biographical Information Apex was born into wrestling. His father was a huge draw for many small promotions throughout North America. To the casual observer, it seemed natural that Apex was physically gifted. It seemed natural that young Luis had an enormous interest in wrestling. It seemed natural to see Luis follow in his father's footsteps and start wrestling professionally at a young age. It seemed natural to see Luis take the name Apex and wrestle in several of the promotions his father had been in before. It simply seemed natural to see Apex come to be. Nothing is ever as natural as it seems, as Apex's life and career demonstrate. Through the eyes of the "non-casual" observer, Apex's life wasn't quiet so simple. Apex's mother and father lived together for several years. She traveled with him, and life was great. In Miami, they contributed yet another soul to the world, that of Miguel Quesada, Apex's younger brother. After that, Apex's father and mother married. Life was well. It didn't last long. Apex's father grew to be abusive, and frequently he beat Apex's mother. At around the age of 5, Apex began to try to attract his father's beating away from his mother, with some degree of success. Eventually, after much stress and much heartache, Apex's parents divorced. Apex and his younger brother were left with their mother, who finally settled in the suburbs of Atlanta. Apex's father went back on the road, and began wrestling full time again. His schedule made Apex's encounters with his father few and short-lived. Already fatherless, Apex's mother really never had time to be mother, as she was forced to work several jobs to support their lives. Left alone at home, with no parents to socialize him, Apex turned to the only other available outlet, the television. While the majority of television had little more than a numbing effect on him, the weekly broadcasts of a wrestling program greatly influence his thought process. Apex saw his father fighting with other men, saving his friends, and talking up a storm each week. Those things never bothered Apex. Seeing his father arrive at arenas in luxury cars, while his mother had to rely on mass transit. Seeing his father being escorted to the ring by seductive women, undoubtedly attempting to give Apex half-brothers. And seeing his father happy, while his mother cried herself to sleep every night, those things bothered Apex. Apex grew angry with time. Angry with himself, for not being able to help his mom. Angry with his mother, for bringing him into such a wretched world. And most of all, angry with his father, for never being a father. Wrestling journalists believe that by the age of 7, after years of holding up anger, Apex had decided to become a wrestler. It is all a common belief that the main motivation in his decision, was the opportunity to extract revenge from his father. There was also the issue of Apex's brother Miguel. In many ways, Apex raised Miguel. Given Apex's state of mind during that period, he most likely lashed out at his brother, and is probably responsible for his brother being in the "family" business. After several years at hating his brother for being a burden, he grew attached to him. Several years after that, around the age of 14, Apex took Miguel and left home and school. The homeless duo, after many more traumatizing events, ended up in Miami, Florida. Luis and Miguel, later to be known as Apex and his brother, through yet another series of traumatizing events, separated in that city. Less than a year after that, Apex entered the wrestling world at the bottom. He carried bags for various wrestlers for various promotions. A year after that, he was pushed into the ring, when one of the scheduled wrestlers was unable to make it to the venue. By the age of 16 he had won his first title. By 19, he was working for a fairly successful promotion in the northeast, and by 20, he was their main draw. Scouts for almost every major national promotion in North America had Apex picked out to be one of biggest names in wrestling by his 25th birthday. Life was once again well. When Apex was 21, the promotion he was employed and headlining for hired his father. Years of emotional and physical abuse came to the forefront of his life, and Apex snapped. Apex acquired several serious delusions about himself and life at this point, and has been mentally unstable since. He remembered his purpose in wrestling, in life, and took it upon himself to end his father's career. Apex was never the same. He retired at the age of 22, realizing he had nothing else to prove, nothing else to fight for. Before he achieved the age of 24, he was broke and living on the streets, this time in Norfolk, wondering where everything fell apart. Sometime during that year, a former colleague of his, a by the name of Night's Harbinger recognized him. Within weeks of this, he was training in the recruitment offices of one of the largest wrestling promotions in the nation, the WWO, on his way back to the only profession he's capable of. Some say his talk with Night's Harbinger brought him back into the business, some say his lack of financial resources. Regardless of his motives, Apex went on to lead a fairly respectable career in the WWO. He won his first title, the WWO Television title, within a year. Through his interaction with "Zombie Corners", his undead imaginary manager, whom he believes to truly exist, and his recklessness in the ring, Apex quickly became one of the crowd favorites. Some say he was on his way to higher gold. Unfortunately, the WWO experienced a fallout, and Apex was once again jobless. This time, thankfully, he had his agent, Charlie to help him organize his finances. And if he had checked his answering machine more often, he would have been the first person in line to re-sign, once the news that the WWO was reopening hit. Flying Legdrop Front layout suplex Fishermen buster suplex Powerbomb Flying Dropkick Moonsault Running Knee Attack: Apex whips his opponent into the ropes, bounces off the opposite ropes, and meets his opponent mid ring with a swift knee to the lower midsection. Superplex Distorted Flying Attack: Apex, who has no knowledge of actual 180 somersault splashes or corkscrew planchas, instead just jumps from the top rope at his opponent. During his descent, Apex turns, spins, and flails out with his limbs randomly. Finishing Manuever DDT - can perform effectively, any variant of this finishing move.
