WWO bio card — "The Millionaire Marvel", Eric Stryker

"The Millionaire Marvel", Eric Stryker Real Name: Eric Stryker Height: 6'5 Weight: 250 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Columbus, Ohio Entrance Music: "Don't Sweat The Technique" by Eric B & Rakim Manager: Physical Appearance Eric Stryker is an extremely well-built & muscular black man, with brown eyes. Due to his arrogance & cockiness, he's extremely concerned about how he looks. He maintains a clean shaven, bald head and a short-trimmed goatee. In order to maintain his appearance, Stryker shaves his head and face every day. He never shows any stubble or growth. Stryker also has a black lightning bolt tattoo that goes completely around his right bicep. When he's not wrestling, Eric Stryker is always dressed to impress. If he's not wearing an expensive, European suit, then he'll most likey have on some designer dress pants or khakis and accompanying, highly fashionable shirt. Also when he's not wrestling, Stryker will usually be wearing some assortment of expensive jewelry, including gold & diamnond encrusted watches, necklaces & ear rings. Stryker wears long, white wrestling tights with a dark blue lightning bolt down each leg. He wears normal wrestling boots, also in dark blue. Psychological Makeup Eric Stryker is your basic arrogant, cocky, self-centered, spoiled rich kid. He believes that his money, influence & reputation can get him anything he wants. He doesn't mind spending large amounts of money for what he wants, but he gets extremely frustrated and agitated if he doesn't get it. He loves flaunting his status at anyone who will pay him any attention. When competeing, Eric *loves* to embarrass his opponenets any way that he can and he is always willing to take the low road in order to beat someone. However, when things get tough for him and he doesn't see anyway out, it's not unlikely for him to panic and head for the hills. Strength Cheating- Stryker likes to use any advantage he can in order to gain the upper hand. He's very skilled at distracting the referee from his tactics. Not every match will be an all-out rule-breaking affair, but he knows how to take advantage of almost every opportunity. Technical Skills- Stryker has gained extensive knowledge of various wrestling moves and holds. In this facet of pro wrestling, he's one of the best. He uses his combination of power, technical skill and submission holds to try and catch his opponents off-guard. Weakness Extremely Overconfident- Stryker thinks he's the toughest thing to ever walk the earth, so when things don't go as he planned, he'll probably panic. Aversion to Hardcore/Brawling- Eric Stryker prefers not to engage in toe-to-toe brawling whenever he can avoid it. Despite his impressive size & strength, he just doesn't have what it takes to slug it out for a long period of time. He believes that these types of wrestlers are insane and might really try to kill him with one of their crazy stunts. Biographical Information Eric Stryker was born with the proverbial silver spoon in his mouth. His family heads up one of the largest corporations in the world, with several subsidiary companies under its control. Eric was also blessed with superb athletic ability. Those who were jealous of him always wanted to say that he bought his way onto every athletic team he played for, but Eric always proved not only that he legitimately belonged on those teams, but that he was usually the best athlete. After a very impressive college football career, Eric graduated and took control of Stryker, Inc., his family's international corporation. He began an illustrious and successful career as one of the youngest and mst impressive CEOs in the country. under his guidance, his family's company flourished. Eric developed a reputation as a merciless, cutthroat businessmn, who always wanted his opponents to suffer before he finished them off. About 4 years after taking over the company, Eric became bored. He disappeared from Wall St. for a year, running the business by phone & fax. When he resurfaced, Eric revealed that he had been in intense training under some of the best professional wrestlers that money could buy. He revealed to the world that after conquering the business community, he was going to conquer professional wrestling. With the financial backing of a huge corporation, Eric Stryker began his quest for the one type of gold that he never possessed in his young & privileged life.
