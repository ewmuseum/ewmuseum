And In Conclusion...

31 July 2002

Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

Jeez...it seems so hard.

Anyway, for this week's columns, I will bring up something brought up on the WWO Discussion Board (http://members5.boardhost.com/wwoboard/) and that is the titles and being defended. Now, I know, the roundtable discussed how the titles were being used, but we really didn't spend time on whether or not the titles should be defended with a much greater frequency. So, here _I_ go.

We'll work our way up. The Title of America's...this is a belt that to me should never go through the new Spotlight/Showcase combo without being defended. With the depth at that level behind Papa Legba (Extreme, Colby, Ace of Hearts, Marley, Tamer, Stryker, even Hopper), why not? The depth is unmatched by any level in the WWO. Now, there could be a growing concern that if the title is defended each week, it'll hinder a wrestler's ability to build a feud and have a big blowoff match, but I combat that by saying, that is possible, but the Title of America's should be a circulation belt. It needs to find itself a new owner every now and then. If someone can keep the belt despite the tough challenges, it is time to see that person rise up the card. Like, say Legba ran through all the man I mentioned. It's time he takes on the Detson's, Stringer's, and so forth to see if he is ready to charge up the roster.

The International's People Championship, to me, has been used well since the reopening. First, it was brought back with a great match (Quesada/Stringer) and then Stringer did well with limited material (after Stringer/Love/Tucker fell through, he followed with Stringer/Cole) and now is in the hands of someone who I feel should be allowed a lengthy title reign. We need to see if Apex can keep the title and allowed to build himself back up to what he was before losing two PPV matches to Tyrone Hayes. And I feel he can do that. He's the biggest face in the fed and should rise back to Hayes's top challenger soon.

The World Tag Team Titles have no heat. Absolutely none. I wouldn't mind P.A.I.N. or the Romanis taking the titles from them and adding even more depth to their golden feud. I also wouldn't mind Vegas Connection getting their act together, signing a contract extension, and bringing the titles back to the forefront of the Tag Team division. I mean, really, when was the last time we talked about those titles being the biggest thing in the division as it usually is? Back with P.A.I.N. lost the belts to Vegas Connection?

The World HeavyWeight Title has been hurt in recent weeks and the Latex Lex feud hurts short-term, but should help long-term. I say this because I've never been one for someone coming back and thrown into a title feud as we now have two examples of (Detson and Lex). But, hey, it gives Hayes something to do. I think maybe the title needs to find a new owner. I love Tyrone Hayes just as much as the next man, but with people like Chris Stringer, William Craven, half of his stable, and so forth sitting around with no title and a blockbuster feud (or coming from a blockbuster feud), is Hayes the right champion for today? Let's backtrack to the Holy Roller/Apex/Hayes feud. It wasn't that great _until_ Anarchy when the three put on a great match. It masked the fact that the title wasn't that big of a thing. Now, if Hayes keeps the title for the next cycle, I don't see bad things either, but I don't fall into this whole lack of challengers for Hayes has hurt him. No, there are plenty of challengers and Hayes is not exactly a guy like Roundhouse who doesn't put over his opponent. Hayes did a great job at putting Dark Soul and Steve Dumars as legitimate threats to the title post-Inquisition. I think the lack of challengers is, for a lack of a better word, bullshit. The challengers are there and even if they won't, Hayes would make them.

Well, that's my look at the titles. Thanks for reading the Source.
