WWO bio card — Sigma

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sigma Wrestler Name: Simon G. Mathews Date of Birth: Height: 6'1 Weight: 240 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: New Haven, Connecticut Entrance Music: Brain Damage by Eminem Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance He has brown hair, but has been know to dye it dark blue. His right eye has a pure red contact lens. Ring Appearance Even though he is not Asian he wears back Hamika paints (Haku/samurai-style paints ), a black sleeveless shirt with a the white Grecian letter, Sigma on the chest, over that he wears a black silk button T-shirt with white Kanji (Japanese characters) decorations like a jacket. Psychological Makeup On his campaign of pain, Sigma makes constant references to his childhood. He starts out calm, but as he remembers more and more from his painful past, he starts to get more and more emotional until he breaks down, becoming a ranting maniac, destroying and attacking anything in sight. Strengths Vicious: Sigma has a serious darkside, yes the side you usually see isn't it. When he begins a fight he starts out calm. But if the fight goes on for a long time, or if his opponent makes a point to taunt him, he will go into a frenzy. This is shown as would do all his moves with more intensity, and will attack relentlessly. Punching bag: All those years of getting beaten up by bullies finally had a plus to it. Sigma can take loads of punishment. Sometimes passing out before submitting. Martial Arts background: As part of his training, Sigma took up the art of Tae Kwon Do. He displays this in his kicks, and knee strikes Weaknesses Reckless: Sometimes the Sigma becomes too confident during his matches. If he has his opponent down and his opponent has a girl manager, Sigma will try and get his hands on her, chasing her around the ring giving his opponent time to recover. Also Sigma at times will not go for the cover, making a point to taunt his opponent, this time proves to be a big mistake. Too Emotional: Sometimes Sigma's emotions get the best of him, and he will cheat. If his opposite won't stay down, or Sigma has gotten way over his head, he will get a sheet of glass and try and breaking it over his opponent's skull, ending the match in a DQ. Or sometimes he will refuse to let go of a submission getting himself DQed. Not Knowing When to quit: Sigma is stubborn, he will typically refuse to give up, even when he should, even though Sigma does have limits. He might go after a 7 footer if he had made fun of Sigma. Or continue to kick out even though it is obvious that he will not win, causing himself to be more injured than he has to be. Biographical Information Simon is not what you call a happy person. When growing up all that seemed to happen to him was he got beaten up by bullies, rejected by girls, and finding himself all alone. His only friend was another outcast named Michael Donovan. When he was 17 wrestling caught the eye of Simon. But unlike most wrestling fans Sigma liked heels. His recent favorites were current day names such as Mankind (heel), Kane (heel), and Raven. They could share his pain, so Sigma wanted to be just like them. So when he got old enough, he went to wrestling school, even though no one there liked him, and graduated. He started out wrestling under the name ShatterdDream and was notorious for putting people through sheets of glass. After a year or so after getting nowhere, The Dream changed his ring attire and name to Sigma. Now that he is older, stronger, and more experienced it is time for Simga's revenge, and he has plans on inflicting pain on his discriminators (pretty people, popular people, etc.), in other words, the typical face. He has kept a lot of the style of his old alias "ShatterdDream", which means he likes putting people through sheets of glass, with his variation of a powerbomb, the "Glass Breaker". He especially likes putting women through glass as they have hurt him mentally so many times before. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Poor High-Flier None Martial Arts Very Good Power Good Technical None Submission Excellent Cheating Very Good Brawling/Hardcore Fair Escapability Good Stamina Good Ring Awareness Poor Teamwork Very Good Top Wrestling Moves Reality A very hard slap to the face. Sigma will rub his right palm then lift it up into the air, giving him a full swing for the slap. He will do this on dazed opponents, as an insult, though it still is an impact move. Reverse DDT Chains of Misery: Starts out as a standing Dragon Sleeper, but if the opponent refuses to submit, Sigma will drop them with a Reverse DDT, then still having the hold lock in will return to a standing Dragon Sleeper, the process will continue until the opponent submits or until Sigma grows tired of it. Martial Art style Kicks and Knee Strikes Single Leg Crab Ankle Lock Crossover Piledriver - Before applying the piledriver Sigma crosses opponent's arms underneath their chest then lifts them by their arms before slamming them head first to the ground various neckbreakers Dragon Sleeper Finishing Maneuver Primary Finisher: Tear Drop- A reverse Swinging Neckbreaker, starts out in a reverse DDT postition, and swings into a 3/4 neckbreaker. Sigma will actually signal when he's ready to use this move by putting his finger under his red eye and strolls it down his cheek like a tear Secondary Finisher: Glass Breaker- Modified Powerbomb (opponent is flipped onto Sigma's right shoulder before he is slammed) Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: Dark Haven Tag Team Partner: "San Malo" Michael Donovan Tag Team Moves Rejection (Sigma applies Dragon Sleeper, San Malo applies groin kick) Double Spin Kick Double Reverse DDT Double Team Backbreaker (Sigma lifts opponent onto his sholders, and drops him over San Malo's knee) Double Spear Takedown Tag Team Finisher Celestial Pain - Spiked Powerbomb followed by a double boston crab
