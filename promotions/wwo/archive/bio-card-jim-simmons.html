WWO bio card — Crimestarter Jim

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Crimestarter Jim Wrestler Name: Jim Simmons Date of Birth: January 1, 1971 Height: 6'5 Weight: 296 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Santa Monica, California Entrance Music: "Killing in the Name Of" by Rage Against the Machine Manager: Johnny "The Lawyer" Cochran Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Crimestarter Jim is a tall muscular man with a very good build in his upper arms and chest! Jim always makes sure he has a good tan. He has bleached blond curly hair that goes down just past his shoulders. He has a tattoo of himself on his left bicep and a tattoo of what appears to be a World Title on his left forearm! Ring Appearance Jim comes down to the ring in a black t-shirt with the words "Crimestarter Jim" in red on the front and the letters appear to be dripping in blood with a bullseye layered underneath the words. On the back it reads "If your not with me, your against me". Jim also wears a pair of black athletic tear away pants with a red stripe down each leg along with his black and red wrestling boots to the ring. Underneath the pants he wears a pair of black wrestling trunks with the words "Legend" across the butt in red. Most of the time, Jim just wears his athletic pants in his matches and takes off his shirt! Psychological Makeup Strengths Very Athletic: Jim can get the job done in the ring not only with power but he can move around very good and get technical when he needs to! Very sneaky: Jim is a person who has made an art of being able to cheat at anytime whenever he needs it. He always takes advantage of an open opportunity to bend the rules! Veteran: Jim is a veteran of the wrestling business and knows the ins and outs of the business. He knows how cruel this life can be and he always watches his back! Weaknesses Crowd hater: Jim has been known to worry about the crowd more than he does his opponent sometime. Jim will get involved with actions of the crowd and may sometimes actually take a fan out! Lose focus: Jim will sometimes forget about his opponent and admire himself or just act like he shouldn't even be wrestling his opponent because he thinks that the person isn't capable of producing a good match. Too assertive: Sometimes Jim will try and do too much in the ring and this can backfire on him and more than often it does. Biographical Information Jim Simmons was actually born in a small town in Indiana. He was born on the first day of January in 1971! His family was pretty wealthy and he was a kid who was pretty much able to get everything that he wanted. That doesn't mean that he didn't have to work for his things though. His family was very strict and Jim was forced to always do his share around the house and help out his family as much as possible. Around the year of 1982, the Simmons family had their ups and their downs but none was as big as Jim's father leaving the family to go to Las Vegas with a woman that he was working with. Jim was left by himself to take care of his mother who was working at nights to keep the family going since Jim's dad was the one who was making most of the money in the family as a stockbroker. Jim's mom was never home at night and never knew what Jim was doing. Jim took advantage of his mom not being home and went out and did his fair share of drinking and having fun with his friends but he never got too out of hand and didn't really do anything to hurt his reputation. Jim graduated from high school in 1988 and went on to go to college at Ball State University in Muncie Indiana. He went to school not knowing what he wanted to do with his life and that all changed one night when he saw a police officer in a high speed chase going after a felon. He saw the cop ram the offender's car several times before running him off the road and Jim knew immediately what he wanted to do so he quickly began studying law enforcement! He graduated from Ball State in 1992 and went on to law enforcement school for a couple more years and he finally moved away from Indiana and moved to Detroit where he became a police officer. It was during the first couple years of this where Jim became enthrawled with wrestling and he took up the sport and began training to maybe one day become a professional wrestler. Jim did very well as a police officer and had developed an exceptional looking body through his training. About 4 years into his police work, Jim was contacted by Playgirl to do a special pictorial called, "men on duty" for the magazine. Jim didn't even think twice about the shoot and made about $200,000 dollars for the shoot. He went back to police work and when the magazine came out 5 months later, the police chief saw the magazine and didn't like seeing Jim in the magazine wearing only his Detroit police badge and Jim was released from his duties as a police officer. This made Jim think twice about his career choice and he decided to step away from police work and try to fulfill his dream of becoming a professional wrestler and he made the transition to Crimestarter Jim. He started wrestling in small feds and was trying to build a name for himself and wasn't reallyhaving any luck. His luck changed when he was contacted by the CWC to join their federation, this was Jim's first real break and he tried to take advantage of the opportunity. His early wrestling days he portrayed a "Sexy Boy" type gimmick and as pretty successful with it. It was during his years with the CWC where he met a wonderful young woman named Monique. The two of them shared all the time together they could and got married about 6 months later. Monique began accompanying Jim to the ring. Jim's biggest accomplishment in the CWC was a World Title tourney where he was defeated in the middle rounds and after that took place the federation closed down and Jim moved to Santa Monica, California and bought a big mansion! Jim was then looking for work and he didn't have to wait long as the WWA came calling and they signed Jim immediately. Jim had proven that he was very capable of becoming a superstar in this business but no one seemed to like the "Sexy Boy" gimmick and that was especially true of the Fedheads in the WWA! Jim was almost suspended twice for rude behavior in his interviews. Before they could suspend him though, Jim got hurt and had to leave the federation for awhile. Jim returned about two months later but not as a wrestler, he returned with a different attitude and lost the "Sexy Boy" gimmick and he was now a man that meant business! He wasn't really wrestling though, he came back and started up a talk show segment that appeared on Monday Night Live's and WWA PPV's called the Interrogation Center. Some great events took place during the show but Jim had to leave to take care of his personal life! In August of that year though Jim made a surprising return in the main event and took the place of an injured wrestler and wrestled in an outstanding match. This return proved to be one that would change the shape of the WWA forever. Jim was meaner and badder than ever! About a month and a half later, the news came out about Jim and Monique having personal problems and this came to ahead one night on a Monday event when Crimestarter Jim took out his wife with a painful move that sent her to the hospital and she never returned to the WWA again and her and Jim seperated! Jim was getting into the business side of things now as he met Johnny "The Lawyer" Cochran and they developed a business called Crimestarter Jim, Inc. Fanclubs and a full-fledged marketing campaign came from this and these guys made Crimestarter Jim a household name.Jim went on about a month later and formed a stable called the "PTA" that was determined to take the WWA over. Jim recruited the most talented wrestlers that he could fine until he had an elite group of guys. After this was established Jim accomplished his greatest achievement ever, he won the WWA Heavyweight title tourney and became the WWA Champion for the first time. He was mister big time now and he wasn't going to look back. Just one night later, Jim came out and threw the WWA Heavyweight title in the trash and revealed the "PTA" Heavyweight title. Jim carried it around for a couple months until he was defeated for the title. Jim bounced back though and won the title back one month later to become the first and only person to hold the WWA Heavyweight title more than once. Jim held onto the title for 3 more months before losing it once again on a Monday Night Live right before the big Summer Survival PPV. Jim had the opportunity to regain the title at the PPV and he made good on that opportunity and regained the title once again, making him a 3 time WWA Heavyweight champion. Jim is still to this day the only person to win the Heavyweight title more than once. Jim was a mainstay in the WWA until last month when the WWA closed it's doors after a 3 and 1/2 year run. Jim is now looking for a federation now where he can try to build another legacy and make an impact on the wrestling industry!
