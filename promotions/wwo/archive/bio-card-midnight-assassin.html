WWO bio card — Midnight Assassin

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Midnight Assassin Wrestler Name: Sean Carter Date of Birth: Height: 6'0 Weight: 207 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: San Francisco, California Entrance Music: Braindead (Part 1) by Shizuo Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Midnight Assassin is a 23-year-old kid, with a perfection complex. He loves to be close to Adonis-level, which goes along with his handsome complexion, and a well-toned body that he constantly maintains. He has short blond hair, and a number of tattoos scattered about his body, most notably the Japanese symbol for "Power" on the right-side of his neck, a purple Devil woman on his inner left forearm and a black and blue dragon that runs up his right arm. Ring Appearance To the ring he usually sports black or blue moderately baggy jeans, Timberland boots, and a T-shirt depicting some sort of band or important rebellious figure in history, like Mumia, Malcolm, King, Che, etc., which he removes upon the entry of the ring. Psychological Makeup Midnight Assassin is a perfectionist yet an anarchist. He wants to be beyond perfection, yet abuses drugs like his real name was Tim Leary. He hardly thinks of ethical mannerisms, and just wants to see people's pain for his own personal pleasure. Something during his life had a huge impact on his psyche, but the impact proves to be more detrimental to people around him than Midnight himself. Strengths Durability Midnight is extremely tough. He can last as long as he has to during a match, be it five minutes or two hours. He hardly tires and will keep fighting until he is beaten within an inch of his life or he is the victor himself. He will wrestle only until his heart stops, and if asked, would wrestle as many matches as needed. Lack of Caring: Some call it fearlessness, but it's Midnight's state of mind. He really doesn't care about other people or who somebody thinks they are. If he feels someone is bothering him or rubbing him the wrong way, he'll hardly think twice about smashing their head in with a blunt object. He also has no caring toward winning titles, but most times their shininess attracts his eye. He loves harrassing people, beating people up, and generally causing mischief, but not for approval or anything of that sort. He does it because he knows he can, and hardly ponders upon reprocussions. Athletic Midnight loves to show off and be athletic. Big moves from the top rope, things people have never seen, just to make the crowd "ooh" and "ahh". Luckily, he treats his body like a work of art and works out regularly. While his affliction to drugs and alcohol minorly hamper his road to perfection, he constantly maintains a 8% body fat level, and diets regularly. Weaknesses Focus: If there's anything that attacts Midnight's attention in the middle of a match, his focus on the match goes from adequate to squat. It takes very little to distract Midnight; a video game, some booze, hell, it could probably even be a shiny object, and he forgets what he was previously doing. Some might attribute this to constant drug and alcohol abuse, others a short attention span. Undoubtedly it is probably a combination of both. Lifestyle: Midnight Assassin/Sean Carter loves partying. He loves drinking. He loves to go places in his jet. Sometimes, he goes places even when he has another committment. Midnight is hardly reliable as a partner, friend, or ally if you're not with him at all times at all hours of every single day of the year. His love of partying the night before gives him many problems the next day, and his fatigue or him being hungover definitely affects his ability to be at 100% and win. Overconfidence: Midnight is fearless, in essence. A lot of the times, however, he is fearless to the point where he feels he can beat anyone that comes in front of him. While the defeat never affects him, he tends to overestimate who he is and what he can do, and being too cocky ends up with him losing. Biographical Information Midnight Assassin has had a history of being in federations, but to really understand what is going through the young man's mind, you must travel back to view his very life. Coming from extreme wealth and born with basically a silver spoon stuck into his mouth, his whole life has been of the pampered sort. Growing up in San Francisco, the days were sunny, but many of Sean's days were the opposite. His father was a media mogul in the Bay Area, with his own Fortune 500 software company, Globotech Industries. His father expected Sean to take over the company, but their relationship grew more and more distant as Sean became rebellious. During his teen years Sean Carter would regularly go against his parents wishes and visit the streets of Haight and Ashbury, littered with Greatful Dead grafitti and leftover hippies from a forgotten era. During his visits, Sean would just hang out with them, and not go to his mansion for days on end. So, with an extreme choice, at the age of 18, his father pulled some strings and got him into the army. In his stay at a military base in Tuscon, Arizona, Sean suddenly learned the meaning of discipline, which was carried out extremely. Two months into his stay, something snapped in Sean's head, and he viciously began attacking fellow officers without warning. One, 19-year-old Thomas White was a victim of one of Sean's violent outbursts, giving White two broken ribs and breaking his collarbone. The attacks were growing more and more frequent, until the point where Sean was sent away for psychiatric evaluations. After his first few initial meetings, Sean was picked up by one of the branches of the government, and began visiting various countries around the world for about 2 years. What he was doing remains classified by the government for what they remarked as "safety precautiouns", but many remain sceptical. Some say he was sent on a soul searching mission, while others claim the military just wanted to get him as far the hell away as possible from them. Others believe that his monicker remains true to his nature and what he was sent around for, but the information still remains a mystery to those not linked near him. Then, at the age of 19, he suddenly disappeared off the face of the planet for quite a while. Finally surfacing at the age of 20, his government ties severed indefinitely, Sean met up with his future wife Maria Torres in a Manchester, England pub. After the two had wed, Sean decided to go back to see his family, after not speaking with them for over 2 years. However, upon his arrival, he had discovered his parents were killed in a plane crash over the Pacific a few months back. He was also informed that he was the new owner of Globotech Industries and all of his parents possessions. A year went by, and while the company made money for him, Sean and his friends just sat around, doing drugs and partying all night and day. Suddenly, Sean had some sort of half-baked epiphany while watching the tele, and decided he wanted to do something with his life. While watching a wrestling tape one of his friends brought over, at the age of 20, Sean Carter's new job suddenly hit him: professional wrestler. Donning a new persona, as the Midnight Assassin, he trained for nine months, finding schools across the continent and world that would take him in. Most times he would end up getting booted out, but retained most of the knowledge. Finally, he found a man by the name of Cassidy Jamison, and went under his tutelage for three months, learning the basics and learning what he needed to know. After finally deciding he was ready, at 21 Midnight and his friends joined up with the DIWL federation, storming it and causing minor havoc, and joined up with a group known as New Blood. After their dispersion, and the folding of the company, Midnight Assassin went back to the quiet life, sitting around his San Francisco mansion and doing nothing. Now, at the age of 23, Globotech Industries is starting to lose money. With his financial stability remaining for the most part stable, the company is waning, and Sean is unsure of his future. The Globotech house that his father built seems to be out of order, and Midnight is confused on his actions. Looking to do something, he has decided to take his mind off his problems and find an outlet for his frustrations. The WWO. However, his real intentions might lay on the fact that he has witnessed the presence of Dark Soul, whom the two have somewhat of a history between them. Why is Midnight back in wrestling? The mystery is only knowledge to him, and him alone.
