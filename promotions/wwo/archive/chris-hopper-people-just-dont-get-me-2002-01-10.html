Chris Hopper - People Just Don't Get Me

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

Allow me to quote a slice from the last "Source"....

*****BEGINS QUOTE*****

"Too Cool" Chris Hopper

What did he do in 2001? When he joined the WWO, most looked at him in the same format country folk look at a man from the city. He had all these accolades from other places and even in his introduction, he showed material from other places instead of his own fed. Since then, he has put together a good facedom run against the UEW.

What to expect in 2002? I still don't know what to think about Hopper. The UEW masked the fact that he still thinks of himself as a big shot to the wrestling world. The WWO is notorious for taking those chips off the shoulders of such wrestlers (see Andruw Tucker). The talent is obviously there, but WWO wrestlers for the large part are unique breed that could care less about non-WWO affairs.

*****END QUOTE*****

What chip is he talking about anyway? I'm pretty sure that I entered WWO on the pretense of performing for great audiences all over the Nation and abroad. I'm pretty sure I signed on to perform with very underrated....yet talented wrestlers on a nightly basis. I'm sure I signed on to work with one of the most talented booker in wrestling history. So let me ask the question one more time...

What chip is he talking about anyway?

I think this is one man's chance to call me full of myself without coming out and saying the words. I have never once tried to walk in and demand a shot at Hayes and the World Title. If I were the ego-maniac that this columnist (and many of the wrestling pundits as well) seems to believe I am, then why would I have defended the WWO against the UEW invasion? I think it would have made more sense for me to have led that team against the WWO since I would already feel above them all.

It just doesn't add up. Has anyone taken the time to get to know me? Nope. Knight never once has spent any time with me to get to know my motives and attitudes. I defended the WWO against people I worked with for over two years, and yet when the dust settles I'm full of myself? It seems everyone has mistaken my confidence for utter cockiness; so let me take this moment to fill you in on where I come from...

I grew up in a small southern Indiana town of about 3800 people. I wasn't rich, but I wasn't dirt poor either. My Father worked his ass off (in fact he still works, even though I am now able to afford an easier lifestyle for both my parents) for everything he ever got in life. My Mother was a loving housewife who stayed home and taught us all we needed to know in how to deal with others.

I guess that is where I get my work ethic and drive to be the best. Watching my parents always strive to make my sister's and my life better was something that inspired me to be the best in whatever I did. I grew up and played sports in high school. I stayed active in my local Christian Church. I dated my fair share of girls and did well in my studies. My life was average.

After I graduated high school, I got into wrestling. I started at the absolute bottom. I was in a federation where half the roster were the students who got free wrestling lessons in trade for getting their asses kicked in front of tiny crowds every week. I can remember learning my first 4 moves and that was all I knew how to do when I got thrown into the ring. I got my butt kicked on a regular basis. I broke a few bones and sprained a few ligaments; but I showed up every time out and took my lumps. Eventually I learned more moves and was allowed to win a few times. I knew I was moving up when I was allowed to take one of the youngsters under my wing and teach him what I had learned. Was there a raise in pay for that? No. I did it because that is what many of us should do. Help a youngster learn from my knowledge.

I caught a few lucky breaks and got to train in some amazing places in both the United States and Canada. I learned a lot and took a ton of beatings. I had some great runs in smaller feds and then got my "big break" in the NeCW and less than a year later found myself wrestling in the UEW. It has all led me to being here in the WWO.

Now many of you are thinking that I am telling this simply to get more airtime for my past and show how good I am. That is far from why I am doing it. The reason I share this with you is so you understand where I come from. I want the fans to realize that why I may have money and fame now, that I can relate to where they are. I still am a wrestling fan. I get Goosebumps every time I stand behind the curtain. I get chills when I hear the crowd chant my name. There really is nothing like it anywhere I have seen. Just because I am now a wrestler doesn't mean I lose my markish side.

I do this to entertain, to enthrall, to exhilarate. I won't lie and say the glory of winning titles and accolades is meaningless, because it does come into play. However seeing the crowd react when I step up on the turnbuckles or hearing them pop when I nail my finishing move.....well that is what it's all about.

So don't think I view myself as a big fish in a small pond. I'm happy. I'm motivated. I'm confident. I don't have a chip on my shoulder that needs knocked off. I just want a chance to entertain and excite the fans. Hopefully now you know me well enough to know where I come from because it's obvious that Knight just didn't get who I am.

Until next time, stay cool everybody!
