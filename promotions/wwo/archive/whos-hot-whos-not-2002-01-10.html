Who's Hot/Who's Not

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

So who is it that's skyrocketing up the charts in the World Wrestling Organization? And who's "Just there"?

Hot #5 - Matt Cole

It wasn't a great start for Cole in the World Wrestling Organization, as he was pushed into an immediate program with Chris Stringer, and it was pretty obvious that Cole has some work to do to get to "The Problem Child's" level. But not only did he admit that he have a let down, but his "Want a fight" speech, and the rescue of Jonas King and Lion Tamer from Xavier Fyre and Marvin Patch puts him in the Hot of the Week.

Hot #4 - Chris Stringer/Johnny Detson/Apex

I tried to determine which of the three guys in this list deserved the ranking.. and I couldn't come up with one. I was dying on the floor in laughter at the Liars' club (don't tell Chris I"m admitting to that), Detson following up on Tracy's "tribute" from the previous showcase, and well, Apex getting the best of both of them in the #1 IP contenders' match. So yeah, I'm chickening out and putting all three of them here.

Hot #3 - William Craven vs. Chris Hopper

Say what you will about your opinions of how well Hopper can get over... with everything that happened at Showcase, this match, despite the garbage feel to it (see NOT #3), was exactly what we were expecting.. brutal and just downright vicious. Add in Stryker's antics.. and it's got to make the Hot List.,

Hot #2 - Dark Soul

Someone _had_ to win, and someone _had_ to lose. Unfortunately for the fans of the WWO, it was Dark Soul who lost, but not without putting up one hell of a fight, and not without some last minute shenanigans. Dark, you'll be missed by many in the WWO.

Hot #1 - Tracy Hudson

Was there any doubt that Tracy would be number one this week? Considering what he just did? Depleted the World Wrestling Organization of one of it's two top faces just like that... sure it was questionable, but that's what makes Tracy so dangerous.

Not #5 - Ultimate Thrasher

We put Craven on this list last time, well now this time it's Thrasher who gets to join the "elite", having disappeared from television engagements, for no reason. I think the loss of Melissa is hurting him more than even "The Living Legend" will admit.

Not #4 - Fire and Ice

I tried to not put them on my list.. but how they got a tag title shot is beyond me. (And to think we complained about Olvera's booking.. what are you thinking Horan? Or are you?) Sure they nearly had the match won.. but come on.. if they had won it, could you say "transition"?

Not #3 - William Craven/Papa Legba extra-curricular activities.

I know this sounds odd, putting a wrestler on the hot and the not list, as Craven is. And I know that Craven's not exactly stable. But dammit, we're not a hardcore garbage federation. A panther by Legba, and an icepick by Craven. I'm sorry, but considering the otherwise fantastic old-school feel of showcase, their antics definitely put them on the NOT list this week... It may have been a "Holy ----" moment, but it's not wrestling...

Not #2 - Copycats...

I've said it time and time again, the Amazing Romanis are perhaps the best thing going in the World Wrestling Organization at this moment... between themselves and PAIN, you absolutely NEVER know what's coming next. Yet for some reason, Johnny Detson follows the exact lead of Tracy Hudson with a musical montage, Papa Legba plays the "referee" angle to screw Alex Extreme over.. come on guys.. I know there's originality in there... And I ain't seeing it.

Not #1 - Production Delays

We know the story, so I'm not going into it... let's just say it wasn't a good thing.
