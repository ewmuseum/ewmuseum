War of the Ages 3 (excerpt)

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. Three titles changed hands on this card, broadcast from Atlanta, GA; only the three title segments below are reproduced here in full. The rest of the broadcast remains held and is not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP [Fire and Ice challenge the Armed Forces:] Calhoun: Ladies and Gentlemen THE WINNERS OF THIS MATCH AND NEW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS. FIRE AND ICEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
SB: Yes, yes, yes, yes, the Forces didn't prevail. MR: Folks we've got new champs. Perkins with both belts in hand. WWO INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, "Latin Lightning" Al Perez, defends against "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson in a thirty-minute time-limit match that runs out with the four other Showmen and Hall of Fame stablemates already brawling on the floor:] SB: Not for much longer!! MR: Perez rolling Detson over after the lightning strike!! And we've got four men laying into one another here on the ring floor! Romulus, Tronson, Keeblor, and St. Claire all battling it out!! And Detson is out from the lightning strike!!
SB: Just deal with it Al. You can't score another two! MR: Sicoli pulling himself back into place for the count!! ONE!! TWO!! [Suddenly the bell begins ringing, just as Sicoli's hand hits the mat for the third count.]
MR: Did he get the pinfall? SB: Does it matter? Three to two at best!! MR: Sicoli's waving off the pinfall!! He didn't make it! SB: The hourglass got to Perez!! CALHOUN: Ladies and gentlemen, the time limit has expired, and therefore with the score of three to one, our NEW WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION'S INTERNATIONAL PEOPLE'S CHAMPION, "THE BLOCKBUSTER" JOHNNY DETSON!! WWO UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP [The reigning champion, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, turns on his own advantage against "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson, with a masked ally throwing Hudson the title belt itself mid-match:] MR: Hudson picking Sebastian up!!! PRODIGY DRIVER!! HE just planted Sebastian with the suplex tombstone!! And Hudson with the quick cover!! I don't believe this!!
ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREEE!!!! SB: What the.. this was a whole setup? MR: It looks like it!! And Lex .. lex is grabbing the United States championship title!! And.. I guess... I guess we've got a new champion!
SB: What do you mean? This wasn't a match!! Sebastian gave it over to Hudson! Calhoun: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match, and your NEW WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION UNITED STATES CHAMPION, "THE PRODIGY" TRACY HUDSON!!!
