WWO bio card — "Rattlesnake" Jake Thomas

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Rattlesnake" Jake Thomas Wrestler Name: Jake Thomas Date of Birth: Height: 6'6 Weight: 275 Alignment: Unprincipled Hometown: Dallas, Texas Entrance Music: Wanted Dead or Alive, by Bon Jovi Manager: None Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Jake Thomas is 6'6, 275. While not muscular, he is fairly tall and does have a decent build, with some weight added on too, fairly similiar to Barry Windhams'. He has short dirty blonde hair, a scruffy unkept, unshaven face and blue eyes. Ring Appearance Jake Thomas wears a dull, faded black duster to the ring, along with a black cowboy hat and a black sleevesless t-shirt with a rattlesnake's skeleton on it in striking position... he ditches those ringside. In the ring he wears a black bandana around his neck and wrists, black trunks, and a pair of black cowboy-style wrestling boots. Psychological Makeup Jake Thomas is just getting used to the idea that all the things he was brought up to believe in, was wrong. He's a little confused about what is right and wrong, and sometimes he finds it frustrating that he can't do what he used to do in the ring, but Michael Thunderwolf is showing him the way. He's begining to understand the right way to use his strength and size and understanding more and more the psychology of wrestling and the strategies behind it. He's far from being a master of it, but with time he'll learn and understand. Until then, he has Michael in his corner to steer him straight. Every now and then, Jake says screw it... and we'll see him haul off on an opponent with a right cross slug and he winks at Michael and just shrugs it off as oh well - just to let the opponents know - he's not all milk and cookies. But he's still on the up and up, and finding a true friend in Michael, will never cross him. Besides, Michael knows its wise to blow off a little steam now and then too. Strengths Brawling. While not a puncher, since hooking up with Michael Thunderwolf, Jake Thomas has no problem going toe to toe with someone and giving it right back to them in the ring and sees outside the ring as just another place to give you a lump on the back of your head if you choose to take it out there. Power moves. Jake Thomas likes taking the moves that everyone just uses casually and uses his size and strength to take that much more out of someone. Those elbows and forearms smashes he throws have a lot of power behind them, enough to wear someone out quick. Size. 6'6, 275. Jake Thomas is a big boy and not easy to move or throw around like some might think. They grow 'em big in Texas, and Jake is proof of that. A powerslam, or headlock with 275 pounds behind it or a suplex from 6'6 up is going to have that little extra advantage. Weaknesses Bum knee. Being 6'6 and 275 has its advantages, then again... it also has its disadvantages and as young as he is, being the size he is... Jake Thomas' knee takes a lot of abuse holding up his large frame - and doesn't do a good a job as it once used to, especially with the abuse it took in his fued with the James Gang. Lack of concentration. Jake Thomas often forgets that in tag team wrestling, helping your partners sometimes does more bad, than good... and is often a sucker for the double team and cheating tactics of some well coordinated heels. Temper. Jake Thomas quickly loses his cool, and as stated above - this often does the team little good. He'll jump in the ring and and try to straighten things out with, only to have the ref constantly escort him out of the ring and thus distracting him for the other side to further abuse his tag partner. Biographical Information Jake was brought into wrestling as a young adult, 18 by family friends. He was taken under the wing by the Texas wrestlers known as the James Gang and brought up on the heel side of the fence. Never seeing the harm in just having a little fun, he never understood why the fans always saw things differently. He was apart of the group for several years until when 25, a wandering wrestler, Michael Thunderwolf, came into the area and being 'injun... was naturally an immeadiate target of the James Gang. After interfering in several of his matches and then embarrasing him after one match he won, the James Gang set out to ultimately insult him by whipping him with a belt and trying to hang 'em with a rope. Michael later challenged the James Gang, and they chose Jake to teach him a lesson. After losing to Michael, Jake was beaten, and thrown out of the group for humiliating them by losing to the redskin. Michael already in the locker rooms came back out, made the save for Jake and they became friends and soon after tag team partners. Jack quickly became friends with Michael and soon learned more from him in the short time they were together than he ever did with the James Gang for the years he was with them. Michael gave Jake the confidence and skills he needed and they then went on to beat the James Gang in various tag matches, and finally took the Texas Tag titles from the James Gang to complete their vengeance. Seeing the potential in the tag team, Michael convinced Jake to take to the road with him, and find larger prizes than the Texas Tag titles. Jake agreed, wanting to see the world and sticking with his friend, Michael in hopes of learning more from him. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier Poor Martial Arts None Power Very Good Technical Very Good Submission Poor Cheating Poor Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability Good Stamina Very Good Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Excellent Top Wrestling Moves Elbowsmash. In the same way that Michael Thunderwolf replaces punches with chops... Jake Thomas tries to use the elbowsmash instead. Someone starts punching away at him, and Jake fires back with an elbow. When at the end of his rope, and going nuts on someone, unleashing a barage of elbowsmashes to the head and leaving the opponent stunned Jake pulls his fist all the way back... and nails him with... an elbowsmash. Clotheslines. Jake loves the clothesline to keep an opponent off his feet. Most often than not, it'll be a short-arm clothesline, just to keep things in control. When feeling awfully nasty, and damn po'ed with an opponent, Jake gets nasty, and after hitting a clothesline, he'll wait for an opponent to slowly get up to his feet, and nail him with a clothesline from behind. Now and then, when in firm control, he'll send his opponent to the ropes and comes off the other side with a flying lariat Bodyslams. Jakes like the power moves and he uses the bodyslam to take what he can out of someone. He'll keep picking them up like a sack of potatoes and dropping them flat on their back like an anvil, an does it over, and over. Now and then, he'll do a press slam... and feeling nasty... rather than slam them on their back, he just drops them in front of them face first, then does a kneedrop across the back of the head. Running Kneesmash. Jake Thomas takes his opponent, tosses him to the one side of the ring, and comes off the other side himself, and catches him with a high running kneesmash. Atomic Spinedrop. Jake Thomas has some height so he likes to use this move where he grabs the opponent for an atomic kneedrop, lifts him as high as possible up onto his shoulders, and then brings him all the way down, with no knee to stop him, just the mat. Spinebuster. Jake Thomas likes to take the guy whip him to the ropes, and catches him coming off around the upper part of the legs, not the waist, lifts him high, spins him around using the momentum of his opponent and just drops down on him with a spinebuster, much like Arn Anderson. High Vertical Suplex. Jake, being as tall as he is, thinks the suplex is something meant to show off his size... and does so accordingly. He'll take the person up with a suplex, hold them up for as long as he can... Powerslam. Being as large as Jake Thomas is, this would hurt a hell of alot, but rather than just have him use of all his energy up, Jake whips them to the ropes, and uses the opponent's momentum to catch and spin them around in a powerslam. Series of stomps, then double stomp to the stomach. When Jake Thomas gets infuriarted with an opponent and feels dis-respected and wants to get back at him, this is what he does... when his opponent is down and out and dazed, Jake starts to stomp every inch of his body up and down from the toes up to the head. He'll start on one side of the body and work his way all around until he comes full circle, then stops, and double stomps the guy across the stomach. Boothill Drop. Jake Thomas slaps the sleeper onto his opponent and just as that opponent is about to go out, he slips the one arm around the front of his neck and drops him with a Reverse DDT. Finishing Maneuver Texas Stampede Jake Thomas picks the guy up for what looks like a powerslam, but does it with the opponent facing out. He then charges to the corner and face first powerslams him into the corner, then takes a step or two out, while still holding the opponent, and then running face first powerslams him in the middle of the ring, and rolls him over for a pin. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: Blood Brothers Tag Team Partner: Michael Thunderwolf Tag Team Moves Michael Thunderwolf tags Jake Thomas in (who climbs and sits on the top turnbuckle), Michael takes his opponent, hoists him up for a face first suplex, but drops him across Jake's shoulders. Jake then comes off the 2nd rope with a Powerslam. Jake hooks the leg for the pin, and Michael guards for the save with a dropkick. Jake Thomas tags to Michael Thunderwolf and they both whip the opponent to the ropes, each grabs an opponent's leg on the way back, hoist him high in the air and Double Spinebuster him. They both then grab a leg as he lays flat on his back, then a double hamstring stretch going in opposite directions. Jake leaves, and Michael continues the abuse on the opponent. Jake Thomas tags to Michael Thunderwolf, then grabs him around the left leg and lifts him up (Like he's going to Leghook/Belly To Back Suplex him) and then drops and swings him forward so that he does a splash across the opponent's stomach and at the sametime Jake legdrops the opponent across the throat. This is usually for a quick surprise pin. Jake rolls off and makes the guard for the save with an elbowsmash, and Michael hooks the leg for a pin Jake Thomas tags to Michael Thunderwolf and they whip the oppenent to the corner and do a double hotshot into the top turnbuckle. Jake rolls off and makes the guard for the save with an elbowsmash, and Michael hooks the leg for a quick pin. Jake Thomas tags to Michael Thunderwolf and slaps on the Brazos Valley Backbreaker and Michael slams down 3 lightning quick elbowdrops to the back of the opponent's head. Jake drops his opponent and exits the ring, and Michael continues to punish the opponent. Tag Team Finisher Thundering Stampede. It's a combo of both their own finishing moves. Jake Thomas tags in Michael Thunderwolf and does his facefirst powerslam into the corner, than the middle of the ring and Michael Thunderwolf comes off the top rope with a flying legdrop across the back of his opponent's head. Michael Thunderwolf hooks the leg for the pin and Jake Thomas guards for the save with an elbowsmash.
