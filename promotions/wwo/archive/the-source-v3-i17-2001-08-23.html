Rest assured I didn't take one. Neither did anyone else. Hilarious of course, but am I the only one who thinks name dropping of certain people is getting a little overdone lately? All Tigress was was a pitiful attempt by the WWO in late '99/early 2000 to say "Hey look, who cares if we're pushing the wrong people or have no coherent storyline development or go a month between cards, we've got INTERGENDER wrestling! Rhaaaa!! A 140-pounder being put over a 230-pounder is perfectly believeable isn't it? Isn't it? No wait.....don't change the channel! Waaaaaa!!!". She was not a paragon of egotism like Sapphire or Andros, or a holy pillar of suckitude like a Mason, Vader, Eden or Erickson. Or a combination of both like...well, nevermind. Sure, has- beens deserve a little humilation, but taking every one of them to [name withheld]-esque levels is just, well, wrong. -We're live from the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
