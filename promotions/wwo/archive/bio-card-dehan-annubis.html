WWO bio card — "The Egyptian Enigma" Dehan Anubis

27 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Egyptian Enigma" Dehan Anubis Real Name: Dehan Anubis Height: 6'8 Weight: 312 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Hamunaptra, Egypt Entrance Music: "Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica Manager: N/A Physical Appearance Dehan is obviously of Middle East decent. His muscular, tanned skin is largely devoid of excessive body hair, save for a thin jet black braid that protrudes from the base of his skull. His ring attire consists merely of a pair of black tight, thigh length shorts, while a heavy dose of white tape covers the Enigma's wrists and ankles/heels respectively. Psychological Makeup Strengths Power - Anubis possesses almost unnatural strength. No warrior is too big for him to effectively manhandle, when he is suitably empowered by his beloved Deity. Imposing - Dehan's "sit up and glare" demeanour when he is knocked off his feet has a habit of stiking unease/fear into even the most hardened of opponents. Supple - For a big man, Anubis shows great flexibility. Weaknesses Tunnel Vision - When completely consumed by "Kail'Lin's voice", Dehan has a habit to block everything out around him; which can obviously make him suseptible to sneak attacks. No respect for authority figures - Dehan has no patience with Officials of any kind. Should they "disrupt" him too much, he'll "punish" them accordingly, irrespective of the consequences. Violently Antisocial - Apart from his tag team partner, Anubis vehemently shuns any other form of company, and this_can_ lead to many forms of backstage problems. Biographical Information Details of Dehan's past remain sketchy. All that is really known about him is the fact that the Religious Sect [Kult of Kail'Lin] that he was a part of since birth committed a ritual of mass suicide. The reason why he was spared from death still remains a mystery. Anubis has previously wrestled in WWEW [where he was crowned World Champion], and also in AIPW for a brief period of time. Wheelbarrow Suplex. Anubis' power makes this move effective against effectively anyone. Kail'Lin's Wrath - Cradle Piledriver with a 90 degree spin just before impact. A vice-like Clawhold to heart. Earringer, which then segueways into a Roundhouse Kick to ribcage. This is then immediately followed by a high impact Legsweep Chokeslam [Rock Bottom]. Top Rope Clothesline. Rydeen Bomb Heart Punch Standing Spinning Back Kick to jaw. Flapjack lift into a Jawbreaker. Finishing Maneuver The Cursed: A Gorilla Press into a Michinoku Driver Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Kai'Lin Collective Tag Team Partner: The Spectre Tag Team Moves Perdition: Double whip to ropes followed by a Double boot to midsection, and as Anubis propels himself backwards to the ropes, Spectre moves behind their prey and synches in a Full Nelson hold, which is followed by an Anubis Running Front Thrust Kick to prey's face that adds extra momentum to the Spectre Dragon Suplex. Blood Sacrifice: Double whip to ropes --->Spectre dropdown, and as foe steps over, he runs headfirst into a debilitating Anubis Earringer --->Spectre's Dragon Sleeper into an Inverted DDT. Sandwich Spear Tackles: Spectre hits high from the front, while Dehan hits low *legs* from the back at the very same instance. Wrath of the Heavens: Spectre places the prey in a Dragon Sleeper facing the corner, Annubis climbs to the top rope and hits a top rope heart puch on the prey's exposed chest. Curse of the Gods: A Spectre Shoulder Mount Crossed Arm Suplex---> an Anubis Bulldog Lariat from the top rope. Finishing Move Doomsday: Dehan brings the victim up for a powerbomb, but instead of smashing the opponent forward, Dehan propels himself backwards as the victim sits on his shoulders, while Spectre catches the opponent in a 3/4 facelock bulldog (a stone cold stunner).
