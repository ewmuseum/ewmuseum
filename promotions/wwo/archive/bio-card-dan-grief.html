WWO bio card — Sparkling Danny Grief

27 April 2001

Sparkling Danny Grief Real Name: Dan Grief Height: 6'3 Weight: 250 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Sydney, Australia Entrance Music: "I Can Make You a Man" - Rocky Horror Picture Show Soundtrack Manager: Psychological Makeup Strengths Agility / willingness to take risks from up top technical knowledge Stamina Weaknesses Has an unnatural 'ability' of being caught out of position or distracted. Especially on high-flying moves. Not a great brawler despite the family genes Biographical Information Sparkling Dan burst onto the scene in the WWO not long before it closed its doors Sparkling Dan is the younger brother of WWO superstar Big Dave Grief (Roundhouse) - one major difference between the two brothers is that Sparkling Dan is one of the most gifted technical wrestlers the WWO has ever seen. His combination of technical mat wrestling, submission moves and willingness to take to the air make his a true Super Star of the Future The other major difference is Sparkling Dan's ... shall we saw outlook on life. While big brother Dave loves the company of women while drinking beer - Sparkling Dan prefers the company of young men and drinking exotic cocktails . As masculine as Big Dave is, Sparkling Dan is just as effiminate His outlandish clothes (hats, feather boas, rings) will ensure that he always stands out in a crowd - never think he isn't a fashion plate - no matter what he is wearing you can be damn sure he'll accessories to the hilt - for example a bright purple jacket and hat would see that the pants were of a complimentary blue wile the band on the hat and sunglasses would also be the same Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Very Good High-Flier Very Good Power Fair Technical Very Good Submission Good Martial Arts None Cheating None Brawling/Hardcore None Escapability Fair Stamina Good Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Very Good Top Wrestling Moves Half Crab Bow and Arrow (standard and reverse) Airplane Spin Toss Cobra Clutch German Suplex Rolling Stepover Toe Hold Lou Thesz Press STF Three Quarters Nelson Standing Achilles Tendon Hold (Anklelock submission) Finishing Maneuver Harvey Wallbanger - Top Rope Frankensteiner Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Grief Brothers Tag Team Partner: Big Dave Grief Tag Team Moves Doomsday Device (the old S&D finisher - top rope bulldog etc to nail bloke sitting on shoulders of man in the ring) Spike piledriver drop toe hold to belly to belly suplex Tag Team Finishing Maneuver Good Grief - Big Dave Grief plants opponent with a tilt a whirl slam and Sparkling Dan Grief nails a back sommersault splash . Depth Charger - Sparkling Dan ties up opponent in a surfboard submission (Vertical or horizontal) while BDG lands a rather fat large (400lb) 2nd rope leg drop across the back on the opponents neck
