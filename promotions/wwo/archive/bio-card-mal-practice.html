WWO bio card — Dr. Mal Practice MD

27 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dr. Mal Practice MD Height: 6'9" Weight: 346 lbs. Alignment: Selfish Hometown: The Kevorkian Institute Of Painless Medicine Entry Music: "Humanitarian", a modified-lyric version of "Real American" by Rick Derringer Psychological Makeup Strengths Kevorkian Clutch. There is no more effective move in the universe, just ask Mal, he'll tell you! Seriously, a Kevorkian Clutch is a free pass to the hospital. Size. Mal's big and knows how to use that weight in technical wrestling. He's hard to physically handle in the ring because of his bulk and height. He almost always has leverage as well as power on his side. The good doctor is creative and sly about using this advantage to it's fullest extent. Unorthodox Style. Mal mat-wrestles exceptionally well. Mal gutter-brawls exceptionally well. Mal does moves noone else knows of. Mal counters in ways noone else has thought of. Mal uses his mind at all times. Mal cheats constantly. How do you counter this guy?! Answer: You don't. Not very well, anyway. Whether Mal has the advantage, or is trying to make a comeback, he throws his opponent off of his game better than 99% of wrestlers going. Weaknesses Mobility. Mal isn't going to win any Olympic track gold medals. In fact, he couldn't win any Special Olympic track gold medals. He's plodding and methodical. Getting out of the way of a move would be a big achievement for the good doctor. Aerial maneuvers don't exist for Mal. This REALLY becomes a disadvantage because he might actually TRY one! Crowd. Mal is easily distracted by the masses of "Practisites". He'll play to the booing crowd, yell, taunt, whatever. At very bad times to do so. :) Stamina. Tiring Mal out is an excellent strategy. Mal is methodical and doesn't over-exert himself, but if a wrestler comes in with a sound wear-down strategy it is probably going to work. Or, if he faces a wrestler he can't seem to put away, he's in some trouble. Biographical Information The man who would become known as Dr. Mal Practice grew up in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. Mal recieved a football and wrestling scholarship to the University of Iowa... he then did graduate work and recieved his medical degree from Northwestern University. Always a weird one, Mal's research pushed the limits of acceptable practice. He was always obsessed with finding new ways to do things, and he wasn't afraid to experiment with them. This was not always healthy for his patients and experimental subjects. Mal's "revolutionary theraputic techniques", as well as a growing idolization for the controversial Dr. Jack Kevorkian, got him kicked out of the AMA in September 1995. Mal became a zealot, adamant on perfecting and proving his "revolutionary theraputic techniques" to the world. Unfortunately, a man of his size could only legally attempt these techniques in a wrestling ring. So, he entered a federation called the UWF in late 1995, and grew to be one of the classic overacheivers in fed history, winning the TV title over a heavily favored Dr. Guildo in early 1997. He wanted to "heal" all the wrestlers of the injuries that they'd recieved throughout the course of their careers. In truth, Mal is money-hungry. The fanaticism is to hide his lust for cash, cash which losing his AMA license had cost him dearly. When the UWF closed in August 1997, Mal managed to use underhanded Legal tactics, and all the money he made in wrestling, to regain his AMA membership. But the wrestling bug had bit him. He came back in January 1998 to the short-lived WOLF, where he was ostensiably engaged in more experiments to "cure the wrestlers" of the toll that wrestling took on their bodies. Mal was never defeated in WOLF, which closed in March. Later in the summer, Mal was recommended for, and accepted, a UEW broadcasting position. He's become quite good at this line of work, but it made the wrestling bug bite him even harder. His UEW contract forbids him from competing there, so he's looked for a place where he can continue to compete. In 1998, he found a place, Hollywell Lanes Wrestling Association. After a few run-ins with the likes of Kingpin, Jack Banks, and Bedlam, the HLWA sadly closed down. But since then, Mal's taken personal credit for this, and also for running certain wrestlers out of the HLWA... including one Mr. Gabriel Whitecross. In late 1999-early 2000, Mal's unfounded boasting drew him into contention with the noted Keening Clan, a family of well-respected wrestlers (one of whom, Jason, was also in HLWA). So Practice used his commentator's position to verbally denegrate the entire family. One of the family's in-laws, Whitecross has taken great exception to Mal's comments. He's challenged Practice to 'put up or shut up'... and Mal saw the opportunity, finagled a few backdoor deals, and without Whitecross's knowledge, gained entry to the second UWF he had been in, run by [name withheld]. A serious injury in his loss to Whitecross eventually led to his dismissal from that company. Since then, he's been rehabbing his shoulder, waiting for another opportunity to get back into the sport. He has been training one of his 'medical assistants', Dr. Ohno Ow, who has immigrated in from China. The two have agreed to a tag team career, in the re-opening WWO... where Mal continues his pursuit of more money... WHOOPS, I mean medical excellence. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier None Power Very Good Technical Very Good Submission Excellent Martial Arts Poor Cheating Excellent Brawling/Hardcore Very Good Escapability Poor Stamina Poor Ring Awareness Fair Teamwork Good Top Wrestling Moves "Lethal Injection" - A hiptoss piledriver. Mal goes for a hiptoss/Biel throw, and sits down while angling the throw down, landing in a modified K-Driller. This is the setup for the Kevorkian Clutch. "Chiropractor's Nightmare" - A swinging neckbreaker where Mal steps off the second rope for added height and momentum. "Spinal Tap" - A vertical suplex lift into a swinging neckbreaker. Power Lariat (from heck:) STF Halfarm Camel Clutch (Sabu-style... a Camel Clutch with one arm hooked, to angle the hold and torque the neck) Crippler Crossface Backdrop Driver (high-angle belly-to-back suplex) Single Underhook Suplex Abdominal Stretch into a Pumphandle Slam Full Nelson into a Full Nelson Slam Finshing Moves Kevorkian Clutch: It is a Cobra Clutch... Mal then suplexes his opponent back, into a bridge... and HOLDS the Clutch on! The opponent's weight drives his body down, causing tremendous pressure on the neck... it's just a matter of whether he passes out before he taps out if the hold is completed. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: Physicians Advocating Innovative Neo-procedures (P.A.I.N.) Tag Team Partner: Dr. Ohno Ow Tag Team Moves Double Swinging Neckbreaker. Ohno actually leaps over Mal's back with this maneuver. Doctor's Orders - Mal lifts the opponent up for a back suplex, and Ohno catches the head of the opponent, executing a Reverse Neckbreaker along with the Back Suplex. Shock Therapy - Mal hoists the opponent over his knee after a side backbreaker. Dr. Ow launches a vicious Scissors Kick to the face of the opponent, flipping him off the knee and onto his face. Mal holds onto one of the guy's wrists as he flips, and crosses over into a crossface submission (Crippler Crossface) immediately. Ohno then turns, and smacks the guy's face and head with repeated hard thrust kicks, making the hold even worse. Defribluator - Dr. Ow hammers the opponent in the face with a rushing roundhouse kick, sending him snapping back into Dr. Mal, who snatches him with a reverse tilt-a-whirl suplex. Ow does not stop running, as he bounces off the far rope with a big windup kick to the head of the downed opponent. Traction - Dr. Ow places an opponent on the top rope, grabs his arm and head, and executes a martial arts shoulderthrow on the opponent off the ropes at Mal. Mal catches the upside down opponent and piledrives him out of the air. Tag Finishing Move The Cure - Dr. Mal lifts his opponent up onto his shoulders in a seated position, and stands in the corner facing out. Dr. Ow climbs to the top rope behind him, and hooks his head under his arm, as if for a reverse DDT. Ow executes a front flip Stunner (his finisher, Anathesia) off of Mal's shoulders. This is not the end of it, however, as Mal keeps ahold of the opponents' legs. He immediately throws the opponent back over his head in a Release Wheelbarrow Suplex... into the turnbuckles. The back of the opponent's head will impact the top of the ringpost, and he'll fall into the ring, VERY likely unconscious.
