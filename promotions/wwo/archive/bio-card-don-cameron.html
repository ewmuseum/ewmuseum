WWO bio card — "The Dominator" Don Cameron

27 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Dominator" Don Cameron Real Name: Don Cameron Height: 6'7 Weight: 295 Date of Birth: Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Amherst, Nova Scotia Entrance Music: "Back in Black" by AC/DC Manager: Psychological Makeup Strengths Strength #1: Ring Savvy/Experience: Cameron has been around for about 20 years and knows his way in and out of the ring with his eyes closed. He uses the crowd to his advantage as well as the referee. He prepares for each opponent differently. Strength #2: Stamina: Although he's wrestling in a tag team this time around, Cameron can go for 60 minutes straight in the ring without using many rest holds. He's a big man, but he's also very physcially fit. Strength #3: Agility: For a big man, Cameron can really get around. Going to the ropes is no biggie for him, as well as standing dropkicks. He moves quickly both in and outside of the ring. Weaknesses Weakness #1: Jack of all trades: While Cameron is skilled in many aspects of wrestling, he's never really excelled in one. Weakness #2: Listening to his heart, not his brain: Sometimes Cameron won't exactly think things through when doing something. He will be one man going after 5 who are about the same size of himself. Remember Han Solo yelling and chasing the stormtroopers down the hallway only to run into a hundred of them? That's Cameron. Weakness #3: Trust. Cameron trusts nobody. After being in the business for so long he's been stabbed in the back too many times for him to trust anybody. The only person he could possibly trust is Shade, who also trusts nobody. Style Notes: Biographical Information Don Cameron is a name that most people recognize around the e-wrestling world. Getting his start in the WCWA at the age of 20 (1982) he wrestled alongside his father, Angus, forming the tag team known as the Warriors of Rock. They were a team that nobody thought could go very far, but 8 years later, while wrestling as singles and as a tag team, they made it to a title match against the current champions. Alas, during the match Angus went down with an injury. Don stepped up and earned his nickname "The Dominator" from commentator Jack Snow when he single-handedly defeated both tag team champions (Arnold Swift and C.J. Young of The Supreme Destroyers). Soon after, disaster fell as the two were celebrating. While Don was helping Angus down the aisle to the back, a man in the crowd, obviously upset that his team had lost, fired a shot at the two. The shot hit Angus in the back of the head, killing him instantly. Don Cameron quickly fell apart and quit the WCWA, vowing never to return to the ring again. Four years pass and the tag team known as The Canadians (Marc Denis and Alain MacDonald), longtime friends of Cameron, ask him to be their manager in the CCW. Cameron, against his better logic, agrees and starts to get back into the 6'7", 295lbs form that he was in before, that of a monster. He made his debut as The Canadians manager in 1995 sporting a black armband (which he wears in the ring to this day) symbolizing his father's death.Three years pass and the CCW shuts down, but not before Don begins talking to a wrestler known as Hammer in the NWWF. Hammer convinces Don to return to the ring, and Don agrees, taking on the monikker of "The Dominator" Don Cameron, the name he remembers being called in that fateful match back in 1990.Cameron dominates in the NWWF, winning the World Title but then losing it in his last NWWF match to a wrestler by the name of Deuce Demon. Cameron then went on to the EIW where he met his first real enemy, a man by the name of Jack Mason. Winning a tournament to become EIW Galactic Champion, the war between Cameron and Mason begins, but then ends abruptly as the EIW closes due to lack of funding. Also in the EIW Cameron teamed up with newfound friend Blizzard Frost and won the EIW Tag Team Titles under the team name of The Storm, where they never lost the titles. After the EIW closed down, Cameron went looking for work. He wrestled in a few minor leagues and even received word that the WCWA was reopening. He couldn't force himself to go back to the league that had treated him so well, but had taken away so much. Instead Cameron received a call from the EWA, a fed that had rose to prominance in the mid-1990s, becoming one of the better small feds around with a small television deal. Cameron also found out that Jack Mason had also joined the EWA, so, to end their feud the two stepped into the ring. At this time, Cameron was 36 years old and not as swift as he once was. He only wrestled 7 matches in the EWA, winning five, including his last match against Jack Mason, in his first of many retirements. He defeated Mason to win the EWA World Title, then handed it back to Mason after the match. Cameron went on to become a commentator in the EWA for about a year, receiving the award for EWA Commentator of the Year, voted by the fans. He never wrestled another match in the EWA. Realizing that his career was over at the end of 1998, Cameron returned back home. That's when he received the call that changed his life.It was Mark Edwards, the man who was restarting the CCW, calling it the NEW CCW, the federation that Cameron made his comeback in. Cameron told Edwards that he WAS 37 years old at the time and not a spring chicken. Edwards said it didn't matter, he wanted Cameron. So Cameron signed on. The first NCCW Pay Per View was called Ignition, and in it was a Tournament for the NCCW World Title, one of the hardest titles to win. The buyrate of the Pay Per View was low, but the people who did buy it received one of Cameron's greatest performances. He was one of 16 wrestlers vying for the NCCW World Title that night. In his first match he defeated young star SuperStar; his second match he defeated the huge and powerful PhantomLord; he wrestled a classic match against Shane Stone in the semi finals, and then in the finals for the NCCW World Title, he wrestled the match of his career when he defeated CCW Legend "The Fox" Curt Crane to win the NCCW World Title. Also in the NCCW Cameron went on to win the tag team belts with friend "GQ" Geoffery Quartz (as Cameron being the face and GQ being the heel) and then he lost the World Title to "The Ultimate Destroyer" Katis after holding it for almost a year. Cameron regained the World Title at Fan Fest, and it was after that match that he met the man who would become his enemy for the rest of his wrestling career: "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre. Cameron lost the World Title to Assassin (with the help of Lew Spectre) and was not given a rematch. He retired from the NCCW without getting a rematch.And that's when the battles with Lew Spectre started. Being humiliated by Spectre and Assassin, Cameron announced his retirement again, at the age of 38. And that's when Marc Denis of The Canadians, the team that first brought Cameron back to wrestling, defended Cameron's honour in front of Lew's hometown of Florida, but was pummelled by both Lew and his stable, The Firm, to the delight of the Florida crowd.For weeks after, Lew berated Cameron at any chance he could. Finally Cameron couldn't take it anymore and returned to fight Lew in one last match, which would be Cameron's final NCCW match. A 60-minute Iron Man match. During that match, Cameron won it 3-2 on the strength of his finishing move, The Domination Factor, and then left the NCCW as a wrestler, with many a fan shedding a tear.Cameron returned as the manager of the Coast To Coast Connection (who were long time friends of Blizzard Frost, Cameron's old tag team partner). It was at this time that Alain MacDonald, the other half of The Canadians, who was also about the same size of Cameron, started impersonating him. And then Cameron disappeared. Many people weren't sure if the man who was Cameron now was REALLY Cameron or was it MacDonald? This Cameron was completely the opposite of what Cameron stood for (Cameron was called The World's Biggest Boy Scout by Lew Spectre). Finally at the Pay Per View called Re-Ignition, the faux (?) Cameron was unmasked by a man dressed completely in black, Hunter, which many people knew was Cameron's alter-ego at times. Hunter claimed to be Cameron and he unmasked Alain MacDonald to prove it. But then the faux Cameron was already involved in a tournament for a shot at the NCCW World Title. So Cameron decided to fight the fight that was started. In his first match he defeated Gauge Thompson and then had to face Lew Spectre in the second round. He defeated Spectre with some outside help to make it to the finals against upstart Tyro Marvel. He lost to Marvel in that match, in what some people would say would be the turning point in Cameron's career and the day that Cameron passed his torch onto Marvel. Yes, I said turning point. It wasn't revealed in the NCCW, but it was revealed in the CCW that the faux Cameron that MacDonald was representing... was REALLY Don Cameron, but he and MacDonald were fooling everybody as Cameron turned heel. Cameron then disappeared once more from the NCCW, mainly because he thought he deserved a title shot that he never received. He did return to become Commissioner of the league, but the NCCW closed it's doors months afterwards. Fast forward to the latter half of 2000. Cameron is 39 years old and has decided, with the help of Curt Crane, the CCW legend, to reopen the CCW. And that, my friends, is where Cameron showed how well we can become the heel when needed as he turned into the mega-heel President of the CCW. Where he stands now is one of the most respected wrestlers in the minor e-wrestling leagues. Playing the heel as well as the face, Cameron has only ventured into the big leagues once or twice to do a few cameos, always recognized by the fans. He appeared in a few interviews with Les Canadians in the WWO, which started the internet rumours that he was considering joining the WWO. As of today, Don Cameron owns a wrestling school in Riverside, California (called The Domination Factor) and that is where he was introduced to the man named Shade Roberts, who easily convinced Cameron to join the WWO in a tag team known as Roberts and Cameron, a pair of wily veterens who still have what it takes. Education: Graduated from Amherst Regional High School in 1982.....went to Mount Allison University from 1982-1985 and graduated with a degree in English and P.E. Wrestling Experience: Took up wrestling in 1984 with Mount Allison University....wrestled in the WCWA from 1986-1990....retired in 1990 at the age of 28 on the account of his father's death...returned to the ring in 1997 to wrestle in the National World Wrestling Federation, the Extreme Internet Wrestling and the Extreme Wrestling Alliance. He has retired from the NWWF and the EIW, and was a manager and a commentator for the now-defunct EWA. He was also in the fed known as Ultimate Superstar Wrestling. Was a wrestler/manager in the New Classic Championship Wrestling and now is dealing with the original CCW. Record in leagues: WCWA -- 25-6 NWWF -- 13-5 EIW -- 5-2 EWA -- 4-0-1 NCCW -- 12-3-0 USW-- 3-0-0 OVERALL RECORD -- 62-16-1 Titles Won WCWA Tag Team Championship - 1987 to 1990. Retired as champion. EIW Galactic Champion - 1997. Lost belt to Jack Mason. EIW Galactic Champion (2) - 1997. Retired as champion as league shut down. Co-holder of EIW Tag Team Belts - 1997. With Blizzard Frost as The Storm. Retired as champion. NWWF World Champion - 1997. Lost belt to Deuce Demon. EWA World Champion -- 1997 to 1997. Defeated Jack Mason for belt, and then handed it back over because it was his last EWA match. NCCW Heavyweight Champion -- 1998. Lost belt to "The Ultimate Destroyer" Katis. Co-holder of NCCW Tag Team Belts - 1998. With "GQ" Geoffery Quartz. Lost titles to team of J. Oliver Bradley and Gauge Thompson. NCCW Heavyweight Champion (2) - 1998. Lost Title to The Assassin. Personal life: Has been married to his wife [name withheld] for 12 years with two children -- [detail withheld].
