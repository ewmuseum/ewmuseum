"Bullet" Dwayne Rogers Wrestler Name: Dwayne Rogers Date of Birth: Height: 5'10 Weight: 230 Alignment: Unprincipled Hometown: Montgomery, Alabama Entrance Music: "Ramblin' Man" by The Allman Brothers Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Dwayne has solid build, nothing overdone, but a good trim but muscled look, think a young Brad Armstrong. He has black hair that goes down to his shoulders and a neatly trimmed beard. His righ shoulder is eborated with a tatoo of the Southern Flag Ring Appearance silver and blue is the order of the day, silver trunks and boots with blue trim. Biographical Information A young 21, Dwayne grew up in the Suth watching Southern wrestling and thrived on old tapes his father showed him of the Memphis and Continental areas. Training with former WICWF star Mike Malone while he attended College (graduating with a PE degree from Tenesse State) Dwayne is just starting out and lookign to make a name for himself. He is, at heart, a good old southern boy and is looking for a good time but he is also like every good face southern wrestelr because of his hot temper. as spent the last 6 motnhs training in Japan, of all places, with former SJPW star Kazuya Asakino.
