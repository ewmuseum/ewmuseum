WWO bio card — Pak

23 May 2003

Pak Wrestler Name: Jake McCloud Date of Birth: April 1, 1977 Height: 5'11 Weight: 198 Alignment: Taoist Hometown: Jersey City, New Jersey Entrance Music: "The Meaning of Life" by Offspring Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Pak is a pretty small guy, just a shade under six feet tall, and wee bit under two-hundred pounds, but he uses that to his advantage when he goes to the top rope. Pak has light brown hair that he wears in a tight crew cut. As, he wears a nice looking mustache and goatee combo. He doesn't really have any tattoos or scars. His eyes are a light blue. Ring Appearance In the ring, Pak wears a pair of green tights with bold, black stripes going down the sides. He also wears some black wrestling boots, elbow pads, and fingerless gloves. He also wears a gray Arabian belt that hangs down to his knees. Psychological Makeup Strengths Speed and Agility - Pak has a background in both track and acrobatics. He is usually the quickest man in the fight, and with several double jointed bones, he slip out of a person's grasp he they do get a hold of him. High Flier - There might not be another man who flies quiet like Pak. The air and distance he gets are just amazing. Weaknesses He may be the quickest man in the fight, but he surely isn't the strongest. His general strategy doesn't involve lifting. Bad Singles Competitor - In his entire career, Pak's maybe had four matches that didn't also involve Brick, so Pak's isn't really that used to fighting his own fights. So, if Pak is by himself, his general strategy is to end it quickly so he doesn't get worn out to bad. Biographical Information Pak was born as Jake McCloud, the son of Scottish emigrant, Alexander McCloud, is April 1, 1977. He grew up in New Jersey with his best friend, Beau Jackson, better known to the wrestling world as Brick. The two grew up loving pro wrestling; so it was no surprise that the two grew up to be professional wrestlers. While they are still new to the sport, they are known as a great up and coming team in the world of professional wrestling, especially now that the two are moving to the WWO. Known for their wacky stunts and even weirder in-ring antics, it seems that they fit perfectly into this strange circus that is the WWO, preferably as the clowns... Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Fair High-Flier Very Good Martial Arts Fair Power Poor Technical Good Submission Fair Cheating None Brawling/Hardcore Poor Escapability Very Good Stamina Good Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Excellent Top Wrestling Moves The Pak Smak - A Three-Quarter Nelson into a Forward Legsweep. All That and A Bag of Chips - A big roll into a Spear. Reverse Dropkick (Reno's Dropkick.) Sunset Flip Suicida (with the opponent standing on the apron.) Tornado DDT (leaping of the top rope, catching the opponent.) Running Spinning Leg Lariat Single Arm DDT Reverse Hurricanrana Victory Roll "Hockey~Sockey" - While the opponent is dazed and facing away from the turnbuckle, Pak climbs up to the second rope, leaps off while shouting something is sudo-Japanese (Like "Hockey-Sockey"), and locks in a Sleeper Hold while mounted on the opponent's back. Finishing Maneuver Bring It Down Legdrop: Pak climbs to the top rope, and executes a Shooting Star Back Flip into a Guillotine Legdrop. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Nex Tag Team Partner: Brick Tag Team Moves The NeXtreme 2000 (The NeX's original finisher. Brick lifts the opponent in a Power Bomb as Pak flies off the top rope with a Bull Dog Lariat.) Pak wrenches the opponents arm as Brick grabs the in a Front Face Lock as the two drop for a Single Arm DDT/DDT combination. Rolling Senton (Pak)/Legdrop (Brick) combination. Pak Single Arm Bulldogs the opponent, and in mid-air, Brick hits an uppercut. The Desperate Tag (Not so much a tag move than a desperation tag. Basically, Pak crawls up on the ropes until only his ankles and feet are the only thing still on the outside while he stabilizes himself while grabbing onto the ropes to his side. He then reaches out with one arm as he adds an extra five feet to the tag.) Tag Team Finisher The NeXtreme 2002 Brick lifts the opponent up in a Bear Hug while Pak climbs up to the second rope. Pak leaps off spiking the opponent with an Inverted DDT while Brick slams the opponent down with a Forward Spine Buster [A.k.a., The Rock's Spine Buster.]
