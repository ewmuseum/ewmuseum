WWO bio card — Ken Stone

Ken Stone Wrestler Name: Ken Stone Date of Birth: Height: 6'4 Weight: 256 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Hell's Kitchen, NY Entrance Music: Led Zeppelin's "Ocean" Manager: Marky Marvelous Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ken Stone's got a good build like all wrestlers typically do. He also has got a bit of a beer belly, but not in a grotesque way. He just doesn't happen to have some insane looking six-pack. Aside from that, just think of your stereotypical New Yorker, and you've pretty much got Stone pegged. He's got short, matted black hair, a rugged, stubble infested face, and cold glare. He has lips that pucker weirdly to talk (or "tawlk") in only a way that New Yorkers ("Yawkahs") can. Ring Appearance A wife beater, jeans, boots. Psychological Makeup Once again, think stereotypical New Yorker. He's got a short fuse that's easily lit, and he's not afraid to explode is anybody's face, anywhere. When he's in a bad mood, don't look at him funny, don't not look at him funny, don't stand around him funny, don't do anything funny basically. Strengths Attitude: Stone never has a wrestler's mentality. He's always got a fighter's mentality, and that can often work to his advantage. It means he's focused and determined. He's not going to come dancing down to the ring, do a little jig for the fans, bathe in the spotlight, and all this other stuff that other wrestlers might do. All that's on Stone's mind is the pin. Stubbornness: He'll never accept defeat easily. He fights 'til the end. He argues 'til the end. He holds grudges. He doesn't forget, and he makes sure he gets things done. He's like Santa Claus with excessive testosterone levels. Weaknesses Focus: When Stone's attention becomes glued to one particular subject, and focuses on it to greatly, things outside of his focus become a blur. He loses sight of other happenings around him, which can hurt him. Temper: Stone's short fuse can result in him getting into a lot of situations which it might not be best for him to get into. When he gets angry, all rationale goes down the toilet, even with his own teammate and "manager".
