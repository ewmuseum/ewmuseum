Joey Spencer Wrestler Name: Joey Spencer Date of Birth: Height: 5'10 Weight: 207 Alignment: Hometown: San Diego, California Entrance Music: "Get This Party Started" by Pink Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Short brown hair with blond highlights, brown eyes, good muscular definition, good-looking, tanned skin Ring Appearance baggy red jeans held up by a double-notched black belt, white wrestling boots, red elbowpads, kneepads (not visible) Psychological Makeup Joey...Joey's kinda stupid. There's no two ways around it. He's very competent in the ring, but outside, he's a freakin' dolt. Strengths Strong amateur background. The boy can flat-out wrestle. Charisma. He's very outgoing and friendly, which makes people enjoy his company. Calm under pressure. If things get rough, Joey stays calm. Weaknesses Not very bright. Joey won't outsmart too many people. Easily distracted. A good-looking woman, an amusing sign in crowd...Joey will find it and get distracted. Small. Joey's in fantastic shape, but he's still only 206 pounds. He will get thrown around by someone larger than him.
