Posted Thursday, January 10, 2002 by [name withheld]

On New Years night, a group of somewhat respected journalists (using that word lightly) gathered to discuss the Tag Team Division. A note to readers that yes, this was not a very organized discussion and I take the blame for that, but it is a learning experience so hopefully the next one will be different.

Key:

JamesKnight = James Knight, your well respected Source editor. He's a Libra, signal, and cries at Old Yellow.

Lenny_Wacus = Lenny Wacus, who is more notable for his Who's Hot/Not in issues of the Source.

DonnyMcClamrock = Donald (not Davey or Danny) McClamrock, a recent find by James Knight to temporary replace Gary Hammond for the Showcase Reviews.

FrmrTagVPDavidO = Dave Olvera, the former tag team VP of the WWO, a bitter bitter man.

DrMalPracticeMD = Part of P.A.I.N., Dr. Mal Practice, MD took part in the discussion as journalist because of his original scouting reports.

Steve Riq = Another newcomer to the WWO, Riq also works as a radio sports announcer after retiring from as a wrestler.
