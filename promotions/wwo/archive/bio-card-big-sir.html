WWO bio card — Big Sir

23 March 2002

Big Sir Wrestler Name: Unknown Date of Birth: Height: 6'11 Weight: 450 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Entrance Music: Singles: "Dallas" theme music Tags: "Nobody Does It Better" by Carly Simon into BTO's "Taking Care of Business" Manager: Richard Ray Vaughn III Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Big Sir is a giant in size/stature. He had long, dark brown hair, always pulled back into a ponytail. Facially his face is in a permenant snarl, lip curled over teeth. clean-shaved. Ring Appearance Big Sir is always dressed in a pair of black dress slacks, black boots, belt, a white business/dress shirt, tie, black jacket, and black firoda hat w/ a red band and an "Ace of Spades" tucked in it. Big Sir also carries a gold-plated tire iron with him whereever he goes. Psychological Makeup Strengths Impervous to Pain. Simply put, nothing seems to hurt him, or rather it seems to take a lot to do it. Strength. No one possesses more raw power. He's not a fat man, he's a GIANT man. Weaknesses Slooooow. Your not going to see him "hitting the ropes" or anything like that. This man is not a wrestler, he's a monster. If Big Sir gets caught in a double team, if he can't get out of it quick, he's in serious trouble. Agility. You won't see any dropkicks or top-rope moves here. It's just not in the big man's ability Biographical Information Not much is known about the mountian of a man called "Big Sir". He does not have a publically known "real name", nor has anyone ever really dug too deeply into his past. Then again, at 6'11, 450+ pounds, are YOU going to ask him questions??? For 7 years, Big Sir has been the "right-hand man" of Richard Ray Vaughn III, his self-proclaimed "Personal Insurance Policy." Due to his trademark gold-plated tire iron in leather-gloved hand, most would-be attackers of RRV3 and/or Lew Spectre fear "paying the premium" their Insurance Policy carries. No man has ever bested this giant, nor has anyone even faced him with a real physical challange. However, Big Sir has never been an "active wrestler". For the most part, he has stood ringside, protecting RRV3 and his main charge, Lew Spectre, for half a decade. However, opon entering this promotion, Lew was only offered a TAG contract, not a singles spot. Lew, in rage, decided to make the promotion and it's tag teams pay, by teaming with the only man who could/would watch his back for the last 5 years, Big Sir. Together,as "The Firm V2.0", they are on a mission to destroy the tag division it's self, and give Lew Spectre free passage into the singles ranks. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Poor High-Flier None Martial Arts None Power Excellent Technical Poor Submission Fair Cheating Good Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability Fair Stamina Very Good Ring Awareness Very Good Teamwork Excellent Top Wrestling Moves 1. Gorilla Press Slam <thrown onto partner, over top rope to floor, droped down to mat, etc> Bear Hug (when oppent starts to escape/rally, belly-to-belly overhead toss) Ear Clap <giant Vader ear-clap charge> Avlanche Splash in corner Big Boot to face Chokeslam <1 or 2 men at once> Doctor Bomb <Gutwrench pickup into powerbomb where you just throw oppent down/never leave feet> Stand on oppent <450+ pounds> One-arm vertical hanging <30-45 seconds> suplex "Big Shot" <BIG left handed punch, maybe that left hand glove is loaded?> Finishing Maneuver The Widowmaker Grabs oppent by throat. then brings his massive fist underhand and slams it down on the top of oppent's head in a KO style-blow. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Firm v3.0 Tag Team Partner: "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Tag Team Moves Big Sir gorrilla presses oppent from ring to floor. Then goes and distracts ref by messin w/ oppent's partner. Meanwhile, Lew Spectre beats and batters oppent on floor. "Blind Tag" If Lew is reeling/hurt, Big Sir may reach over and pull Lew Spectre out/tag his back and come in/clean house. Big Sir has a LOOONG reach. Big Sir/Lew have been known to play "pass the object" back and forth, tossing it and hiding it for one another so the ref can never actually see it. Tag Team Finisher None.. simply put, Specter always finishes a match. The point of this team is to showcase Lew Spectre, Big Sir is only a legality.
