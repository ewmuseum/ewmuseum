"San Malo" Michael Donovan Wrestler Name: Michael Donovan Date of Birth: April 26, 1974 Height: 6'0 Weight: 241 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Originally from New Haven, CT, now resides in Buenos Aires, Argentina Entrance Music: "Pretty Noose" by Soundgarden Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ring Appearance Psychological Makeup Strengths Real-life experience: Spent many years as a professional shoot-fighter in South America. The grueling experiences he had there give him an edge in the wrestling ring. Focus: Once he chooses a goal, he is almost unshakable. Rarely distracted by the crowd or by opponents managers Play Dead: Great at feigning weakness or injury in order to lull an opponent into a false sense of security. Sometimes goes as far as to have the referee stop the match, and then rise and beat down the distracted opponent Weaknesses Solitary: Very much the loner. Only known to work with his own tag team partner,and even then it is often not a close partnership. Outside Interference: More used to fighting in steel cages one-on-one, instead of a wrestling ring, so is easily taken by surprise by interference, weapons, etc. Berserkk: Refuses to conform to the rules of wrestling, and will go too far when applying beatings and submissions. Often get disqualified for closed-fist punches or refusing to release a hold Biographical Information Michael Donovan was the model child, good grades, strong ethics and a devout Christian. However, a mysterious event when he was 12 caused him to lose his faith, and become a troublemaking ne'er-do-well. His friend, a transfer student from Argentina, witnessed this transformation, and dubbed Michael San Malo, a Saint gone bad. He lived a pointless existence until the age of 16, when he decided to run away with his friend to Argentina. He lived for several years on the streets of Buenos Aires, sometimes supported by the friend's wealthy parents. However, one day he wandered into a small amateur fight club in search of food, and agreed to fight for a meal. He took a shine to the sport, however, and soon found himself making enough money to survive by fighting. Eventually, he saved up enough money to move to the US and join a small local circuit Shoot-fight League. However, one thing led to his switchover to professional wrestling, and that was the surprise reunion with an old friend from High School, the man now known as the Professional Wrestler, Sigma. Sharing similar childhood troubles, they decide to team up. Bitter against the USA, Donovan feels that its society turns its back on miscreants such as him, and the the countries he lived in in South America have a better more caring people. Using his South American fighting style, he is out to prove that he can win at anything using what he learned there.
