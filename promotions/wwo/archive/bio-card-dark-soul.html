WWO bio card — Dark Soul

30 May 2001

Dark Soul Real Name: Chris Warner Date of Birth: 8/7/76 Personal Reporter/Manager: John Collins - Managerial Profile Candy Malone - Managerial Profile Height: 6'5 Weight: 252 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: Amherst, Virginia Entrance Music: Pearl Jam - Light Years Physical Appearance With his long brown hair that finally ends half-way down his back in a ponytail, Dark Soul is far from the usual look of a wrestler. He wears a pair of torn jean shorts and usually a black t-shirt, which sometimes is the famous "Laters..." one. Psychological Makeup Strengths Threshold of Pain Dark Soul can take a lot of pain and keep coming back for more. At times, he will almost ask for pain (depending on his mood and feelings at the time) and wear a smirk as his opponent tries to hurt him. Most of the time, however, Dark Soul tries to make his opponent tire himself out trying to put Dark Soul away and when Dark sees a chance, he decides to try to take out his own version of pain on that opponent. Intelligence Dark Soul does his homework on every wrestler he faces, finding as much tape to watch and asking around to find out information. Dark Soul also tries to make sure he alters his game somewhat every match. He still wants the other wrestler to be forced to wrestle his game, but also wants to stay open to the other wrestler's style as much as possible. To Dark Soul, it's all a science. Experience Dark Soul has been wrestling off and since the beginning of 1998. In that time, he's wrestled from tag teams to hardcore matches to ironman submission matches and so on. There isn't a lot Dark Soul hasn't ran into. Furthermore, there isn't a lot Dark Soul hasn't found a way to defeat. Weaknesses Pride Most wrestlers consider pride a good thing, one that keeps them at the top of their game. However, for Dark Soul, he finds it a bane of his wrestling technique. Typically, he tries to not only beat a wrestler, but strip that wrestler of any respectibility in the match. Dark Soul sometimes forgets that the important thing is to win. Injuries Due to his all-or-nothing attitude, Dark Soul sustains injuries at least every other match usually. The injuries are typically a small sprain or a pulled muscle. Dark Soul has never missed a match because of an injury, but playing hurt does take its damage on him and makes him a little less stronger against opponents. Lack of Focus Dark Soul, even with his experience, will occasionally forget that the other wrestler in the ring should receive his undivided attention. If Dark Soul had a bad night on the town, that lingers through his head. If Dark Soul has a big match after his current opponent, he may look past him until either that opponent beats him handling to the point of almost pinning him (which Dark Soul has proved he can come back from) or when the bell has already ranged and the winner was not Dark Soul. He has intrusted John Collins to keep those loses to a minimal. Biographical Information Dark Soul comes from a small, one-parent home in central Virginia. Living most of his life as an outcast, allowing few friends in, Dark Soul left for college in 1991 at age 18, setting out to become a computer programmer for the new age of computers. However, money problems struck and Dark Soul was stuck with two years of college education, trying hard to gain a few scholarships. About this time, a fed known as the DIWL started up and offered free try-outs. Dark Soul showed up only because a friend asked him to. His friend did not make it, but Dark Soul was offered a contract and a trip to a wrestling school. While he never imagined himself in wrestling, he quickly signed the contract which would easily pay for his education. Once in the DIWL, Dark Soul started off slow, but rose to the top of the fed. The fans somehow started to rally behind the young star, but he hardly ever acknowledged them. Dark Soul also started a stable, at the time known by New Blood. Their trademark "New Blood Foreva" was a saying that many of the DIWL wrestlers wished they could follow under. After becoming the first person to win every single's Heavyweight Title offered (World, U.S., TV, Hardcore), Dark Soul and New Blood grew tired of wrestling and of the antics of the DIWL's owner. In the spring of 1999, at their first Pay-Per-View (New Blood Revenge), the group announced their retirement and went their separate ways. Dark Soul went back to college, but felt lost. He was not 18 and naive anymore, but 23 and alone. After his Junior year of college was finally finished, he returned home. In that summer, [name withheld], the WWO's President, called Dark Soul about a possible return to wrestling. It was a no-brainer as Dark Soul quickly signed a four-year deal worth seven figures. While in the WWO, Dark Soul defeated Latex Lex for the Television Title, but was criticized for sitting on the title and facing opponents of no close ranking. Silkk, who wasn't known as Miguel Quesada just yet, took the title from Dark Soul around Thanksgiving of 1999 and Dark Soul struggled to regain momentum to rise to the top of the fed. He came home to ask for the help of a child hood friend, John Collins, for help. At the beginning, Collins was nothing other than a personal reporter. At the end of Dark Soul's first attempt in WWO, he had agreed to be at ringside and be, in Dark Soul's word, "An Anger Management Tool." Dark Soul defeated Tigress, causing the woman to lose her first match. He also was in the top four of the WWO's last major Pay-Per-View, the Bash. He seemed to be heading back to the land of titles and gold, but then the WWO was retired. Returning home, Dark Soul fell in love with "that girl" as they say. She was the girl from the past that he never fully was over. However, Dark Soul now felt confident of their ability to last. But as the WWO started up again, she told him that she couldn't handle him wrestling. Dark Soul, torn between two loves, pondered his next move, but the girl quickly exited his life before he could actually choose, so with wrestling as the only thing left, he made his return to WWO known to [name withheld]. Wrestling Information He relies on speed and his ability to take pain and still fight back later in the matches. He is one of the few wrestlers who takes a beating and likes it. However, if he is pissed, he usually doesn't like to take a beating, but rather give one. Dark Soul will resort to chairs or anything else if it helps him get the win. He will also take to the top rope on occasion. Nothing has stopped Dark Soul's on-going quest to surprise the rest of the wrestling industry and he has stayed loyal with the WWO since he was originally signed on. Now, in his second tour of duty, Dark Soul sees no reason he might not surprise a few more people and win the World Title.
