WWO bio card — "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Wrestler Name: Lew Spectre Date of Birth: Height: 6'4 Weight: 265 Alignment: Selfish Hometown: Star Island, Florida Entrance Music: "Nobody does it better" by Carly Simon segued into BTO's "Taking Care of Business" Manager: Richard Ray Vaughn III Physical Appearance Standard Appearance 6'4, 260 pounds. Lew has long black stringy hair with the sides shaved. He also sports a goatee, vandyke <pice under lip> and pencil-thin mustache. Hazel eyes. Looks a bit like Zorro, or Jon Davis of Korn. Lew has Broad shoulders but his pecs aren't overtly cut. His abs, however, are Rick Rude-esque, cut and stacked. Lew has a very over-confident look to him, and while attractive, has a look of evil to him. Almost resembling Lucifier himself. Ring Appearance Lew wears long black tights w/ white boots. On the right leg it reads "Spectre" and on the ass of the tights "Simply the best". Lew wears a white singlet top that he usually takes down, and tapes up his wrists and left hand. Psychological Makeup Strengths Cunning. No man is more devious, more sharp-minded than Lew. He is always thinking, ususally 4-5 moves ahead. When it comes to having a game-plan, no one does their homework like Spectre. Lew Spectre SLEEPS in a ring, just so his awareness in a ring will be top notch. Always knows where he is. Mat Ability. Lew comes from a wealthy family. Because of this, he was able to spend his childhood/teens traveling the world, learning the ways of International Wrestling. Lew knows every hold, every move and every counter. Lew knows holds never before seen in the US, as well as holds he's invented himself. Endurance. Due to endless hours of conditioning, and a psychotic belief that he truly is a "wrestling god", Lew NEVER runs out of gas.He trains to wrestle 1-2 hour matches, and does in training regularly. Weaknesses Over-Confident. Lew believes he's the best. Because of this, he tends to expect to win. Especially after he's trained/studied an oppent for a match. Agilty. While not slow, Lew isn't the most agile of wrestlers. With the exception of the occsional top rope headbutt, Lew pretty much keeps his feet planted. Brawling. Lew is not a brawler. He can be vicious, but only with wrestling holds. It's rare Spectre ever throws a punch, and he doesn't take punches too well either. Biographical Information Of all the stars who have traveled the roads of the E-wrestling universe, none is quite the enigma Lew Spectre is. Not much is known about Lew's past, beyond that he is from South Florida, where he is EXTREMELY popular. Lew is also on the books at a multi-time champion in various amateur wrestling events in the US and abroad, however due to the fact he was homeschooled, Lew never competed in College wrestling. He traveled the world to learn the ways of the arts of wrestling, prepairing for a Pro career.Lew burst onto the E-wrestling world in 1993, a fresh face in a sea of "gimmicks" and "Characters". While his contemparies were busy painting their facesand doing dances, Lew wrestled. He refused to compete in "gimmick" matches, and hated wrestling in tag team events. Lew had stern beliefs and refused to compromise them. He hated gimmicks, and cruiserweight wrestlers. Lew hated brawlers and hardcore matches. He didn't understand midgets nor why women would be involved in the sport. Because these things insulted him so, Lew would regularly challange two cruiserweights at once, or take on a tag team single-handedly. Using his world-class mat skills and ring cunning, Lew always managed to find a way to best his oppents. Time after time Spectre managed to "slip through the cracks and best his oppents.When there was no competition left for "Pro Wrestlers" in the United States, Lew went to Japan. He went to England. Spain. South America. Germany. New Zeland. South Africa. The Samoan Islands. Lew traveled the world, to return home to a start-up company, the "NCCW". Disguested by the "hardcore and sideshow" antics of his fellow wrestlers, Lew went wild, scheming and executing the destruction of NCCW star after star, all within the confines of the rules. Lew formed two stables, "The Firm" and "The Firm V2.0/V2.5", but jealousy of Lew's grandor quickly broke both of these stables apart. Lew held the NCCW TV title for oer one year, until the promotion's folding. Durring this period, the NCCW "World" title had fallen into a near dormant state, and the TV belt pretty much became the "World" title. Lew, bored with the competition in the NCCW, began "indaving" other E-feds, and challanging their champions in "Title Vs. Title" matches. It was durring this period Lew also captured the MMWF, IPWA, BAL, FOW, and even Don Cameron's own NWWF's world titles. Taking these five titles, and his NCCW TV title, Spectre created the "Professional Wrestling World's Unified Heavyweight Championship". The NCCW became the CCW once again, and soon after that folded. Since then, Lew has traveled the world. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Poor High-Flier None Martial Arts None Power Fair Technical Excellent Submission Good Cheating Very Good Brawling/Hardcore Poor Escapability Very GOod Stamina Excellent Ring Awareness Very Good Teamwork Poor Top Wrestling Moves Butterfly Suplex-floatover into cradle pin Airplane Spin (Samoan drop, or sets oppent back down on feet and goes for big move or small packages them) European Uppercuts/forearms/headbutts (no punches) Extreme Closeup (Stun gun into the top turnbuckle) SoBe Suplex (Atomic drop pick up, bounced off top rope in middle, not turnbuckle, then droped back into a backdrop suplex, w/ fingers locked for a pin) Double-underhook DDT Chop-block to knee Figure-Four Leglock w/ Iron Claw applied to kneecap High vertical spinning suplex (pick up, hold for over a minute, then spin and drop) Hickory Nutcracker (headbutt from top or 2nd rope to groin) Finishing Maneuver Star Island Stranglehold A chickenwing with the left arm, a half-nelson with the right, right hand grabs wrist of left and left hand pushes aganst side of oppent's neck. right forearm pushes the other side of opponent's neck. This cuts off the flow of blood to oppents head, while the wing/nelson pulls rips at shoulders. Lew will also lock legs around opponent's waist to keep an opponent on the mat. If rolled backwards, Lew can even pin an opponent while in the hold. However, going for a pin up will almost always result in the chicken wing being released, allowing for a possible respite (as Lew would end up pinning himself in the process otherwise), and is therefore used primarily as a submission finisher. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: Firm V.3.0 Tag Team Partner: Big Sir Tag Team Moves Big Sir gorrilla presses oppent from ring to floor. Then goes and distracts ref by distracting opponent's partner. Meanwhile, Lew Spectre beats and batters opponent on floor. "Blind Tag" If Lew is reeling/hurt, Big Sir may reach over and pull Lew Spectre out/tag his back and come in/clean house. Big Sir/Lew have been known to play "pass the object" back and forth, tossing it and hiding it for one another so the ref can never actually see it. Tag Team Finisher No Tag Team Finisher, as Lew always finishes. The point of this team is to showcase Lew Spectre, Big Sir is only a legality. Therefore, there is no team finisher.
