WWO bio card — "20 Bux" Nate KnowItAll

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"20 Bux" Nate KnowItAll Real Name: Nathan Brawley Height: 6'3 Weight: 244 Date of Birth: Alignment: Taoist Hometown: Skin Deep, New Jersey Entrance Music: "Everything You Want" by Verticle Horizon Manager: Physical Apperarnce Olive skin, brown eyes, his hair is spiked on top and shaved on the sides; top is dyed red, He is well built but not chizzled, has a soulpatch (patch of hair under bottom lip) Ring Attire: Just a maroon t-shirt that says the word "Necropheliac" in black, black jean shorts, and red laced Dr. Marten boots Coming to the Ring: black sunglasses Psychological Makeup Likes to have fun and bad mouths opponent's none stop during the match. He'll say the stuff a typical loud mouth fan might pick up and tear apart. Other Nick-Names: "The Most Censored Man In Wrestling" and "Your Mother's Lunch" Strengths Experience: The man started his wrestling career a jacked up mug, so he's got the knowledge of a power wrestler. In Tai Pei, he studied judo and kick boxing. So, his days pit fighting should come in handy. He's also a student of Jason Agony's wrestling school, so you know he's gonna be doing all sorts of crazy stuff Spirit, Soul, What-have-you: Nate honestly feels that he is "The Most Censored Man in Wrestling." Feeling held back and unappreciated his entire carer the drive he has now to become the best (or at least the most entertaining) is un-parralleld. Like-ability: You will like Nate KnowItAll. There is no doubt about it. The man's promos, mic skills, cockiness, talent, and over-all appearance are going to make you become an instant "20 Bux" fan. There is no shame in having this man as your favorite wrestler. Just remember, stay loyal to Nate and Nate will stay loyal to you. Weaknesses He's a Guy: He loves women. He's not going to get side-tracked by a valet's feeble distraction, but if he has his eyes set on one valet in particular he may make more side-comments to her than actually wrestle. Oh and commitment to other men, has never stopped Nate from persual before, it probably won't now. Arrogance: Like most arrogant wrestlers, it may cost him in a situation...or two...or three... It's all about entertainment for Nate. As much as he loves himself, he also loves putting on a great show for the fans. Biographical Information "20 Bucks" got his start earning just that, on a nightly basis, in a small indy promotion called the TWF. He wrestled under the guise "Nathanial Necropheliac" and for the brief stint he was in this company he was barred from using his finishing move that was vulgarly named the "Blue Cootchie Licka." After building his name up a bit on the indy circuit, Nathan received a contract to the upstarting big budget federation, the COWC. Upon his arrival, Nate had added some muscle to his body and added to his repertoire of maneuvers, unfortunately though, his finisher was still banned.To the surprise of many, the company disbanded after only a short period of time. Many believe it was because their World Champion left the company high and dry. Could it have been because he was scheduled to defend the title against Nathanial Necropheliac? It's possible. Realizing the only place he might get respect is in the Far East, Nate packed his bags and spent some time in Japan and Taipei. There, He studied the martial arts, shedding the adage of muscle, yet bettering his over-all condition, also undertaking the name "Lucifer 4" to go along with the fresh new look. He wrestled and even partook in some of the underground pitfighting for his income. Upon his return to the states, he worked a few indy shows picking up some new found notoriety. Eventually a promoter for the GPW saw Nate wrestle and a signed contract came of it. Nate instantlly became the GPW's hottst rookie and fastest rising star. Unfortunatly, the GPW closed before Nate's in-ring debut could be made. But it would have been awesome! Four months later, here he is, the WWO's favorite son. And the "Blue Cootchie Licka" is still banned.
