Top 5

by Kimberly Campbell

"The WWO Hot Five & The 2001 WWO Hot List"

The WWO Hot Five is compiled for each issue of the Source by Kimberly Campbell. Everyone that reads the Source is encouraged to vote. To vote, simply send an email to [detail withheld], listing what you consider to be the top five WWO personalities, teams, stables, or angles. Comments explaining why you've chosen what you've chosen are also helpful and encouraged, but not required.

Before we get to the lists, I'd like to say thanks to everyone who voted. I really, really appreciate all votes. And as I've always said, the more votes I get, the more accurate this list will be. Don't like the list right now? Then by all means, please, vote and work at changing it.

But serious, thanks to everyone who voted. Some of you have been extremely loyal to the Hot Five for the last year. You know who you are.

Here are the WWO's current Hot Five, according to Source voters.

Honorable Mention: Chris Hopper (4), Chris Stringer (4)

5. Nate Knowitall Points: 5 Readers' Comments: Forget Tyrone he's really the man to beat!

4. William Craven Points: 12 Readers' Comments: Okay, so hardcore's not usual around here.. but when it happens, this is what we want to see. And it ain't pretty.. but hey, it got your attention right? | The guy is just on a tear. Any title holders should be in fear. | Undoubtedly the best monster heel I've seen in the WWO

3. Apex Points: 15 Readers' Comments: Title worthy yet again | He's half alive. | beat Detson & grabbed the #1 contender spot for the IP title

3. Dark Soul Points: 15 Readers' Comments: Gone, but it can't be for too long. The WWO needs him. | His career isn't alive. | Okay, he lost.. but he went out a fighter. Damn, I miss him already! | He gave everything for the WWO... including his career.

1. Tracy Hudson Points: 33 Readers' Comments: Has he ever been such a star as he is now? | His blood acts as if it is alive. | making Dark Soul retire is pretty big, IMO | Just made the WWO safe for the heels once again... Who's left? Apex? Think he can stop 'em all? I don't. Heels of the WWO, your new hero, THE PRODIGY! | Just made my dream come true | He showed a little humanity after killing DS's career, but I'm not buying for a second.

And there's the list. No huge surprises this week, with Hudson easily pulling away with the number one spot. He did, after all, end the career of a wrestler who many considered the number one face in the WWO. And while I'm talking about Dark Soul, I'd like to wish him the best of luck. You were a good guy.

Moving on... here's the 2001 WWO Hot List. Despite many of the votes not coming in until after the 1st of January, I've decided to include all of these votes on the 2001 list. I did this because all of these votes were based on actions that occurred in the year 2001. From here on out, all votes will go to the All Time WWO Hot List and the 2002 WWO Hot List, and of course, the weekly Hot Five.

As a small disclaimer, it should be mentioned that those individuals with the company through the entire year have had more time to accumulate points, than those that joined at later periods. So, if your favorite wrestler didn't make it or wasn't voted as highly as you would've guessed, consider the time at which they joined.

Without further ado, the 2001 Hot List, as voted on by Source readers.

21. Glen Peeps (26 points) 21. Alex Extreme (26 points) 19. Papa Legba (27 points) 18. Holy Roller (33 points) 17. Uncivil Disobedience (35 points) 17. Operation: Scorched Earth (35 points) 15. Vegas Connection (35.5 points) 14. Cherry Bomb (38 points) 13. Todd Johnstone (44.5 points) 12. Amazing Romanis (52 points) 11. Nate Knowitall (59 points)

And now the top ten.

Number 10: Miguel Quesada Points: 63

Miguel's Year at a Glance: At times, Miguel has not lived up to what everyone has expected. He's shown signs of greatness, especially during the feud between the Quesada + Dark Soul against Operation: Scorched Earth. At other times, he's shown signs that he's just not ready to take the WWO as a main eventer. He failed to control and lead his stable (Greatness Personified) and has had trouble deciding which side of the fence he's on. However, no one doubts his in-ring talent and technical abilities.

Miguel's Future: Well, it's in his hands. The feud with Knowitall has the potential to take the number 3 or 4 (clarified: 3rd or 4th importance) spot at the upcoming Pay-Per-View, thanks to other dwindling feuds. If this feud takes off and Miguel shows some stability and sticks to being a good guy, then he'll have a successful year that could include various title shots. If Miguel gets weak bellied again, and flops across the line, his credibility as a heat drawing wrestler could dwindle to next to nothing.

Number 9: Johnny Detson Points: 64

Johnny's Year at a Glance: When the WWO re-opened at the beginning of 2001, Johnny looked like the number 1 roadblock in Tyrone's road to the title. And may well have been, had he not mysteriously disappeared. But, Johnny's back and he looks pretty damn good... if you trust me (a lowly reporter). He's drawn incredible heat by attacking the WWO's president, [name withheld], and has now launched himself into a feud with 2 of the WWO's most consistent heat drawers: Apex and Chris Stringer.

Johnny's Future: The chances of Johnny Detson walking out of this feud with the IP Title are extremely good and I'd even give good odds to Johnny getting a chance at World Gold later this year. Even if he walks away from this feud without the title, he won't lose an inch of credibility, considering the men opposite him. Either way around, if Detson sticks around, he'll always be in the spotlight. He's just that good.

Number 8: Tracy Hudson Points: 93.5

Tracy's Year at a Glance: When WWO first re-opened, Hudson did a.. well, miserable job up front. And I guess he knew, because next thing you know, he's gone. Since then, he's came back... and he's doing what he does best: wrestle. I mean seriously, he's much better wrestler than booker. And since coming back, well, he's joined up with Tyrone Hayes, Nate Knowitall, and Johnny Detson to make one of the most ominous heel groups I've ever seen in the WWO. Just recently, he ended the career of WWO fan favorite, Dark Soul, and in doing that, sky rocketed up the Hot List.

Tracy's Future: If one considers the relatively short time Tracy's been wrestling this year, the number of points he's gained is unbelievable. In fact, he's going at a much faster pace than anyone above him in this list. And accordingly, it would not surprise me to see Tracy become the number man in the WWO in the year 2002. He's got experience, in-ring talent, and a tremendous amount of heat after forcing Dark Soul into retirement. Factor in the ever witty Moongirl, and you may be looking at the 2002 Hot List winner.

Number 7: PAIN Points: 107.5

PAIN's Year at a Glance: I would say that it is not stepping out on a limb to say that PAIN has been the WWO Tag Division this year. Not to discredit Vegas Connection, who have shown signs of brilliance here and there, and the Amazing Romanis, who have been coming on really strong recently... it's just that PAIN has been consistently the best tag team this year. They won the titles and in what turned out to be a great travesty, lost them just a month later.

PAIN's Future: In the year 2002, PAIN will become WWO Tag Team champions again. It's only a matter of time. These guys know how to get it done with interviews and in the ring.

Number 6: Ultimate Thrasher Points: 108.5

Thrasher's Year at a Glance: He started the year off, working with Johnny Detson, in what was a very entertaining angle, until Detson suddenly disappeared. Then, he wrestled Tyrone Hayes in one of the best matches of the year at the Inquisition PPV. And all of this was just the appetizer, because he really got started when he and Craven started going at it.

Thrasher's Future: Thrasher's future is in question. The feud with William Craven has lost a lot of heat and I don't really know where it's going. Thrasher showed that he definitely has it in him, despite being around forever, to be main event material. It's just a matter of whether he's around to continue to build his legacy, or if someone should start writing his biography now.

Number 5: William Craven Points: 127.5

Craven's Year at a Glance: He started off under the guise of Major Damage... and he hurt people. Then, he took off the mask, revealed himself to be Craven... and yes, he hurt some more people. And then he decided he needed a playmate, and he decided that Thrasher's longtime valet, Sweet Melissa was a good choice. And that started a serious war. And it looks like Craven won it.

Craven's Future: Craven can beat anybody in the WWO. I mean, anybody. He's a machine... a machine that likes to destroy things. Consequently, there isn't much doubt that he'll wreck havoc up and down the WWO in the year 2002. The problem is, that as he destroys more and more wrestlers, no one, and I mean, no one wants to get in the ring with the guy. If things go well for the ungentle giant, he could easily take the IP or the World Title.

Number 4: Chris Stringer Points: 128

Stringer's Year at a Glance: The guy came out of nowhere. The WWO was reopening, he was on the roster, and everyone was focused on Tyrone, Detson, Thrasher, and Apex... and well, the might have been better off what Chris. He's feuded with Apex, Miguel Quesada, Dark Soul, and well, in a way, he won. He was awarded the IP Title, and has held it since then, including defeating a very game Matt Cole. And that's completely bypassing all the other things he's done for the WWO, like bringing in Glen Peeps, Cherry Bomb, and William Craven. Now, the IP Champion is mixed up in a feud with Apex and Johnny Detson... and win or lose, this can only bode well for Chris Stringer.

Stringer's Future: Right now, thanks to his loyalty to the WWO, patience with the IP Title, and overall dominance, Chris Stringer is the best man to take on Tyrone for the World Title. The problem is, no one can take the IP Title from the guy... and until someone does, a world title feud doesn't seem likely. But, I suspect that this will not stop Stringer. Whether he loses it to Detson or Apex, or someone else, or heaven forbid, just vacates the title, eventually, Chris Stringer will be tired of small fries and take it to the next level.

Number 3: Dark Soul Points: 132.5

Dark Soul's Year at a Glance: Personally, I didn't think Dark Soul's year was starting off well. He did have have one of the most popular valets, Candy Malone, by his side, and he's always been a great wrestler... but he was jumping into a feud between the Quesadas and Operation: Scorched Earth and it looked like he might be stuck playing second fiddle. Then Dark Soul wrestled the recently unmasked Craven, and his stock skyrocketed. The match was a huge success and Dark Soul was quick to take advantage of it. He joined up with Apex and launched a series of successful selling ads, and continued to gain heat. His sister shows up soon after, with long time enemy Miguel Quesada, and a war erupts between them. Hudson enters the frey, and man, things got complicated. Dark Soul managed to beat Quesada, then turned head on to face Hudson. Unfortunately, Dark Soul lost a career match to Tracy Hudson recently, and his career was ended.

Dark Soul's Future: He's retired, so, it's hard to say. He might join up with another federation, in which case, I wish him the best of luck and suspect that he would do quiet well anywhere he chose to go. Or, maybe Dark Soul's boots are finally being put in the closet. And that'd be a shame.

Number 2: Apex Points: 180

Apex's Year at a Glance: I would say that Apex _almost_ lived up to his hype. As the WWO reopened, everyone had him written in as the WWO's Top Face. The man that would take the title from Tyrone (Yeah, everyone expected Tyrone to win the title). They were right on the first part. If one considers the entire year, Apex probably has had the most heat of all the faces in the WWO. However, despite two shots at Tyrone, Apex could not take the title. That's not to say that his year has been a failure. Dark Soul and his selling skits have been pretty popular and Apex has shown that he's still one of the best workers in the business. Now, he's drinking Apex Jr. Juice (which has been surprisingly well received) and feuding with Detson and Stringer.

Apex's Future: The upcoming feud with Detson and Stringer means a lot to Apex's career. Losing another title feud could be devastating. Winning the title feud could boost the man right back to where he was late 2001. Even if he loses some value, from losing the feud, Apex will still be a valuable asset to the WWO. Due to the lack of worthy faces in the WWO, several people backstage salivate at the thought of some of the possible feuds for Apex in the year 2002. Some of the names being dropped around are Miguel Quesada, Alex Extreme, and William Craven. All in all, I'd say Apex will have a solid year, but the World Title will probably just be out of his reach again.

Number 1: Tyrone Hayes Points: 308.5

Tyrone's Year at a Glance: He finally did it. He won the World Title. And along the way, had an awesome match with Ultimate Thrasher. Unfortunately, that's where the glory stops. Tyrone has defended the title here and there, and has looked good, but nobody has really stepped up to challenge him. He was tossed into a feud between Apex and Holy Roller. And while the final product of that feud was awesome, the road leading to that match at Anarchy left something to be desired. Now, Tyrone has formed a stable of some of the best heels in the WWO. But still, no one is there to challenge him.

Tyrone's Future: I've always thought Tyrone was a great wrestler. And I still do. But I don't see him keeping the belt too much longer. In many ways, the title is losing value, and it's sad, because it is no fault of his. Apex couldn't topple him and that doesn't leave much, especially with Dark Soul retiring. I suppose we have to look forward to William Craven or Chris Stringer stepping up and giving Tyrone some worthy competition. This of course, brings up the problem of two heels feuding. And as it has been said before, perhaps a face turn is the right move for Tyrone. None the less, I can't predict what Tyrone will do or who he'll do it to. I can, however, say that Tyrone, if he wants to, will have a great 2002. He probably won't keep the title past midyear, but he'll still be in the running for 2002 Wrestler of the Year.

There you have it. And don't complain to me, because, you people voted for it. All in all though, I think the list is pretty accurate. These point totals will be saved and each week all votes will be added to these totals. This list then becomes the all time list, and next year, assuming I don't get fired before then, around this time, you'll get the 2002 WWO Hot List, the Weekly Hot Five, and the WWO All Time Hot List.
