Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

-- I can't comment on how sweet [name withheld]'s ass is; mainly because APEX's head is always in the way when you try and look at it. Maybe someday he will move out of the way and give me a clear view.

-- One or both of the following teams may find their way back into the WWO. Armed Forced and/or Uncivil Disobediance. Somewhere, if he's sober enough, Hammond's marking out.

-- Lion Tamer has received a contract from Hanes to model their new line of faux leopeard-skinned underwear

-- Rumors backstage have the "higher ups" very pleased about the progress of some of the feuds right now. Craven/Thrasher (well, before Thrasher disappeared, anyway) and Hopper/Stryker are both getting really high marks. Rumor has it that the feud winners could be in line for World and IP title shots respectively in the next cycle. All eyes are on the Marley/Tamer feud as well because it coudl very well produce another title contender.

-- Rumor has it that a well-known team from before the WWO's closing is set to make a big return. Upon hearing this, the Romanis threatened to track down Precious Moments in retaliation.

-- Check your local gay porn section, Jake Thomas isn't knew to being ridden like a horse.

-- Staying in the tag team division, look for Vegas Connection to job the titles off and then be dismissed. Their contract demands are outrageous. Lester Leary wants his own hour-long variety show and Larry Lowbrow has said he'll never wrestle again in the WWO unless Cherry Bomb returns his phonecalls. Oh, and Candy Malone, Kimberely Campbell, Stacy Werner, MoonGirl, and sadly enough, even Candy Kisses.

-- Tyrone Hayes' reign may be about ready to end as well. Ratings are beginning to show that the fan base just isn't responding to Hayes as they did previously, but with new or recently elevated heavy hitters like Craven, Extreme, Hopper, and APEX stealing his thunder you can imagine how tenuous his situation is. Expect Hayes to drop the title at the PPV and then go into a short-lived hiatus to retool his gimmick and persona.

-- Apex seems to have lost his position as a key player in the Apex-Detson-Stringer feud. Look for Apex to drop out of the feud, to allow Detson and Stringer to mix it up freely.

-- The NAACP has offered assistance for Dark Soul. Jesse Jackson was rumored to have said, "Our brothers are still suffering from slavery."

-- On a side-note, Sean Collins was denied membership to the NAACP. He's at a loss to explain why. Which is understandable...since he's usually at a loss to explain anything.

-- A WWO janitor apparently walked in on Apex and WWO Reporter Kimberly Campbell arguing. The details of the argument were vague, but it seems as if Apex wasn't happy with the amount of attention that Ms. Campbell is getting from Papa Legba.

-- Expect a new title or a pair of new titles to be announced on the new WWO show, Spotlight. Early indications have the EXTREME TITLE!!! returning to active competition.

-- Tracy Hudson bitches that he was jipped, Dark Sould doesn't do windows.

-- William Craven, with Ultimate Thrasher's disappearance, is now looking for a new victim..er, feuding partner. Those who might be in need of a more up-to-date will are Chris Hopper (James Knight: "YAY!"), Matt Cole, or a title-carrying wrestler.

-- All the WWO women are in one mass PMS. First Melissa, now Moongirl. Look out, its a WWO Diva revolt.

-- The WWO front office realizes they are sitting on a gold mind. Expect Ace of Hearts and Chris Hopper to team up to face James Knight and Donny McClamrock in the coming weeks.

-- A few tag teams are planning to get together and join in a fued against The Upper Crust.

-- It's these times that make me sad to report anything. During the Packers/Rams game, Gary Hammond, my former boss, died. Time of death has been announced following Brett Farve's fifth of six interceptions.

And finally...

Ever wondered what aborted gmmicks were ALMOST seen on WWO television? Well allow me to share a few of them with our reading public:

* Ace of Hearts and Alex Extreme doing an "Ambiguously Gay Duo" type of gimmick, complete with costumes, capes, and all possible innuendos. Rumor has it that Ace is the one who pulled out. *snicker*

* APEX bearly did the porn-star gimmick early on. Rumor has it his goal was to be the IP Champion. However in this gimmick, he would have made "IP Champion" stand for "International Penis Champion".

* William Craven was set to play a "Jason from Friday the 13th" type of gimmick where he terrorized everyone with the most odd attacks in the history of sports enterta....wait a second....CRAVEN IS DOING THAT!
