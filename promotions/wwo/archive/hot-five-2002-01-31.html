Hot Five

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

The WWO Hot Five is compiled for each issue of the Source by Kimberly Campbell. Everyone that reads the Source is encouraged to vote. To vote, simply send an email to [detail withheld], listing what you consider to be the top five WWO personalities, teams, stables, or angles. Comments explaining why you've chosen what you've chosen are also helpful and encouraged, but not required.

One little tabulation note: Starting with this issue of the Source, votes that come in like:

#2: Amazing Romanis Vs. PAIN

Will count as 4 points per team. Before this issue, the points were split, but, I've had a change of heart. This will only be true of votes that make sense. For example.

#1 Tyrone Hayes & William Craven

This does not make sense. The two did not interact, and accordingly, the points would be split. Anyhow, just thought I'd let everyone know.

As usual, I'd like to thank everyone that took a few minutes out of their day to vote. I really appreciate it.

And, without further ado, here's the your Hot Five.

Honorable Mention: Chris Hopper (7), Nate KnowItAll (7), Tyrone Hayes (6), Johnny Detson (5)

Number 5 - Tied (Amazing Romanis & PAIN)

Note: Since many of the votes that were received for these two tag teams came as a joint vote (ie. #2 Amazing Romanis & PAIN), the comments for both teams will be listed below.

Points: 12 each Readers' Comments: How could they not both get number one this week? The jail fight and drug busts were great. "We knew those, Gypsies very, stoned. Can tell, from slurred SPEECH and, strange WORDS they in-VENT." | Drugs are bad, mmmmkay? | just proving they can do anything... anywhere... and making it look awesome. |

Number 3 - Latex Lex Points: 13 Readers' Comments: I like Sodomists. | It takes talent to return and make an impact like he did. Oh, wait, Detson and Hudson returned and made an impact just the same. | The scariest thing to grace the WWO is back. It's kinda scary. |

Number 2 - Tracy Hudson Points: 14 Readers' Comments: HE BEAT DARK SOUL! | I like Being an Ass. | He controls Dark Soul. That's impressive. | beating... then buying Dark Soul... he just rules on every level. Bringing Dark Soul back to insult him more, thats evil! | The man's evil... evil I tell you. |

Number 1 - Apex Points: 24 Readers' Comments: At last, Apex comes through with the title... | I like Apex Jr. Juice. | Champion...finally! |

And there it is. Apex gets the number one spot this week, although, if you combined the Amazing Romanis and PAIN's points, we technically have a tie for first. I would have actually considered doing this, but not all of the 12 points for each team came from joint votes, around 10 from each team did.

Also, I'd like to note that this was a pretty good week for the Upper Crust. Tracy Hudson took the #2 spot, with KnowItAll, Detson, and Hayes all in the Honorable Mention.

That's all I have. Remember to send your votes to [detail withheld]

Thanks for reading.
