New Generation ------------------------------------------------------------------ SIMPLY THE BEST EVER: HEELS by Gary Hammond Unlike some wrestling organizations out there, the WWO almost entirely throughout its history has placed an emphasis on storyline: the gigantic pathos play between the forces of good and evil. And although the increasingly jaded fan base of recent years has blurred the black-and-white definitions of face and heel to a point where the WWO no longer even officially uses them, the main reason we watch is to see the bad guy who's been scheming and running for so long finally "get his", or when the indomitable monster who's been chokeslamming and powerbombing everyone in sight finally gets cut down to size. Who have been the ten men in WWO history who have inspired the most loathing, whether from their plotting and planning or from their downright viciousness? Let's take a look: 10) JUDE ST. CLAIRE The "Playboy" or "Showman" never was able to quite fulfill his potential in the WWO due to various disputes with the front office which saw him leave the WWO on the verge on an IP title win only to return a mere month later, but during his all-too-brief tenure here he was able to make his mark as the best of the dozens who have tried the tired "rich boy heel" gimmick. Whether it was tormenting Al Perez along with bodyguard Romulus, making Apex lay down for a pin by giving him a fake Mr. TV, or taking one sip out of a bottle of Perrier before throwing it in the trash, Jude always found new ways to make the crowd scream for his head. 9) ROBERT D'ARTOIS This French blue blood snob hated Americans, and we hated him too. But it wasn't just xenephobia that made Robert d'Artois a great heel..it was his slow mat-based wrestling style, (which was anathema to the majority of marks in the HAAAAAAARRRRRRDCOOOOOORRRRRE era of 1997-98), his surrounding of himself with various bodyguards and assorted lackeys, and his tenacity....the guy recieved literally half a dozen title shots, losing all of them cleanly before FINALLY cheating his way to the World title at Battle of the Titans V. When he lost that belt to Fury and faded out of the WWO soon afterward, live audiences did not exactly shed a tear. 8) TYRANT Canadians.....a friendly people, right? If your idea of friendly is someone who slaps Sweet Melissa, shoves down vice-presidents, and tells America to kiss his ass, this Canadian fits right in with his countrymen. Otherwise, though, you'll join the legions of us who loved it when Fury, Thrasher or Spawn battered him from pillar to post...and seethed when he would inevitably emerge holding the IP title (or as he renamed it, the Canadian title) held high. The fact that he ended his WWO career as a JTTS takes some of the sheen off of him, but not that much. 7) NIGHT'S HARBINGER What could someone so pale and feeble-looking possibly do to get on this list? Try manipulating Fury into a heel turn, slapping Wuzho's valet Mikaella around, and flushing Genius' head in a toilet bowl, for starters. Perhaps the single most ruthless and vicious individual in WWO history, the only thing Harbinger seemed to enjoy more than dishing out pain was recieving it. His WWO career ultimately only lasted a few months, and there's no telling the carnage he would have wreaked had it gone on longer. 6) "BLOCKBUSTER" JOHNNY DETSON He'll take a beating from you for an entire match, and out of nowhere pull your tights or put his feet on the ropes to pin you....and spend months bragging about it afterward. Johnny Detson's arrogant, showman attitude and his constant surrounding of himself with stooges and other cohorts for protection have rankled legions of fans and good guys for years in the WWO, and continue to do so today, just ask Tyrone Hayes. From buying Fury's contract out to his current association with Ultimate Thrasher, the Blockbuster will stop at nothing to get what he's after. 5) FURY He entered the WWO as a babyface and rose to the top of the federation in meteoric fashion, but fan backlash due to his humorless interview style and easy wins over mediocre opposition led to him beginning to get large amounts of heel heat. So, it was decided a change was in order...and it worked. Boy, did it ever. In late 1998 Fury was re-invented as a baby-eating (not literally....or not officially proven anyway) badass with a brutal post-match beatdown on Tyrant, just for the hell of it...and then moved on to begin tormenting Wuzho and Al Perez, the WWO's top babyfaces at the time. Fury and Harbinger's brutality towards Wuzho and his valet Mikaella worked so effectively that when Wuzho suffered a freak injury and was forced to suddenly leave the WWO before their big blowoff match, "the abuse toward himself and Mikaella was too much for Wuzho to take anymore" was bought hook, line, and sinker by every mark out there as an excuse for his departure. 4) RANDY BRYSON He won the WWO World title....then turned around and laughed at everything the federation stood for. He fisherman-suplexed Mark Stone. He jumped to Iron's side in the WWO-WIF war....and then tried to cost Iron a career match. He pointed a gun at Zac Schneider's head (okay, maybe THAT wasn't a bad thing). He got Wuzho thrown in jail. He stole the World title belt from Ultimate Thrasher. Worst of all, he brought Iron BACK to the WWO! The hockey-playing, hard-partying Detroit native always did what he had to do to get under his opponents' skin....and then some. 3) "MR. RATINGS" WARREN HAYES I don't know what Warren Hayes had more of in late 1998-early 1999....lackeys who protected him in the Simply The Best stable, or excuses for not wrestling Roundhouse one-on-one. Either way, scheming and chicanery combined with Warren's unmatched cockiness and showboating (and the fact that he actually made Ric Studd appear relevant two years after his prime) made him perhaps the most despised individual on the WWO roster during both of his stints in the feder, while heading up three of the most hated heel stables in the federation's history. The only thing keeping Warren out of the top spot on this list is his late 1999 face turn, but in the end "Mr. Ratings" revealed his true colors and closed out his WWO stint doing what he does best. 2) UNCIVIL DISOBEDIENCE With tag team wrestling being somewhat overshadowed for much of the WWO's history, and with many teams contending for the World titles even at times when the division has flourished, it has been hard for any one team to really make their mark....but The Firm and Rax Justice have done so. Abusing female valets, rescheduling other teams' matches on a lark, engaging in street fight bloodbaths, tastelessly mocking the ages of their opponents and reviving the careers of moribund Fire & Ice? Just a regular day's work for this duo. They're such great heels that a large percentage of the fan base actually loves them now. 1) TYRONE "PURPLE" HAYES It's Tyrone. What more do I need to say? Who hasn't he parodied? Though he's tamed his act a little by necessity as time has gone on, it's very rare to see Tyrone on your screen and not either be laughing your ass off or wanting to punch him in the mouth. Usually it's the former, which is why Tyrone has gotten so incredibly over as a heel that he, too, is often now the recipient of more cheers than boos from the audience. But he has steadfastly refused to reinvent himself as a genuine good guy, and his continued association with Todd Johnstone and Latex Lex shows that we are guranteed to see plenty more antics from him in the future. And we don't want it any other way.
