Kyle Rogers Real Name: Kyle Rogers Height: 6'3 Weight: 264 Date of Birth: 1983 Alignment: Unprincipled Hometown: Mobile, AL Entrance Music: "Celtic Aggression" by Tonic Manager: Lexus Anderson Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Think of your "all-American" kid. That's Kyle. Short, neatly trimmed brown hair and blue eyes. He's got a great athletic build to him and great muscular definition. He's the picture-perfect athlete with the talent of a "pure" athelete. Go figure. Ring Appearance: Standing blue wrestling trunks with blue kneepads, blue elbowpads, white wristape, and white wrestling boots with blue laces and soles. Psychological Makeup Strenghts Pure athlete: He's just got a natural knack for sports and such. Wrestling is one of those. He has good power. He's got good speed. He's the perfect balance. Strength Has a lot more than people would think. Can easily match power with guys that're around 300 pounds and are bruisers. Looks can be deceiving. Speed While not as fast as a lot of the smaller guys, Kyle's got some damn good speed for someone his size and actually looks graceful coming off the turnbuckle. Weaknesses Experience: He lacks it. He hasn't been in pro-wrestling long enough to know the "dos" and "don'ts" as far as mistakes go. He'll walk right into a trap and not even realize it until its too late. He's green. Temper: He's the guy who has so many buttons that set him off, it's not even funny. He easily loses his temper and when that happens, he loses focus. Goes hand-in-hand with his weakness above. When upset, his above weakness actually gets worse. There is a hidden mean and selfish streak within Kyle. It's not come to the surface just yet, but it is there. He'll make some bad judgement calls in the name of glory that'll cost him big Biographical Information The history of Kyle Rogers is that of the proverbial bad seed saved by someone who actually gave a damn. Lance Van Majors was that person who gave a damn. Lance helped Kyle keep his nose out of trouble by getting him involved in a lot of different programs Thanks to Lance, Kyle was able to finish high school and then went right into training to become a pro-wrestler. He made his debut in WWO in April of 99 and wrestled there for a few months. Kyle and Lance soon left the promotion. Since then, Kyle hasn't actually wrestled. He's trained off and on, but otherwise, he's spent a majority of his time down in Mobile working odd jobs. WWO closed a few months later. Suddenly, the promotion came back into existence. Lance and Kyle decided to team up and give New Generation one more try as Lance was looming on finally retiring from wrestling. Yeah. Kyle and Lexus are still an item of sorts. It's just their both too shy to admit it. Ain't it sweet?
