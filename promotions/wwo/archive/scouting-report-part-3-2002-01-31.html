Scouting Report Part 3

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

As I have done to the singles roster, I will now look at the tag team roster. However, most of the roster is new and I can't give any real opinion on them so to those who have yet to be given a scouting report, sorry. Like I said, I _may_ put together a fifth scouting report (next Source, I tackle the valets, managers, bodyguards, and part-time players) for the newbies.

Amazing Romanis

What did they do in 2001?

Part of two teams to last the whole year (unless you want to try to convince me Final Justice lasted the whole year), the Romanis began the year with little heat as most didn't "get" them, but pulled off a good run at the World Tag Titles, lasting to the semifinals when P.A.I.N. finally dismissed them. Their Anarchy cycle was disappointing, but with the lack of interaction from at the time Les Canadians and Fire and Ice, can you expect different? But following Anarchy, they have since engaged in one of the WWO's best feuds with P.A.I.N. The Romanis brothers continue to be two of the rising stars in the WWO, both in the ring and out.

What to expect in 2002? The World Tag Titles won't have to be stole for these guys to have them around their waists. The talent is there, the heat is there, the circumstance hasn't presented itself just yet. But with their feud with P.A.I.N. becoming one of the best parts of WWO TV, can we not foresee a nice run with the titles and continued success in the revamped tag team division?

Blood Brothers

What did they do in 2001? Rivaled Midnight Assassin for long promos, but unlike MA, found success in the ring. Fought Totally 80's at Anarchy in one of the better Anarchy matches in my opinion. Now, they seem to be feuding with Dark Haven.

What to expect in 2002? Well, we have seen a bit of what the Blood Brothers will go through in 2002. Their old-school intensity brings depth to the rapidly growing tag team division and they've proved their in-ring talent. It's their out-of-the-ring entertainment that has yet to grab me.

Canadian Legacy

What did they do in 2001? Irritated me, basically. They had nothing to say, yet spent 15 minutes saying it. Another on-and-off tag team, they were basically handed a chance to feud for the Tag Titles and at year's end, couldn't care less.

What to expect in 2002? They seem to finally have direction...as heels, of course, because we all know nobody wants to be faces in the tag team division. They looked decent in the last Showcase so maybe their rustiness is fading. I'm really not a fan of these guys and I don't expect great things from these guys. Maybe they'll prove me wrong. If they can stay active for a couple of months straight, I might have to change my tune. Until then, though...

Dark Haven

What did they do in 2001? I'll be honest...these guys are growing on me. Blood Brothers bring depth and these guys bring "huh?" reactions from the fans, but they must be doing something right to be undefeated. They feuded with the since gone Damage Inc. (good riddance) for what seemed like forever. They seem to disappear and reappear a lot, but are among the toughest people in the WWO.

What to expect in 2002? They are my sleeper team. That is, if they don't break up. They have teased a break-up for so long that it would be useless to do it now. Feuding with the Blood Brothers will be their first serious challenge. I can't predict the one who will go over in that feud, but if done right, the tag team division might actually not be P.A.I.N. and the Amazing Romanis and a bunch of rejects for a change. Both teams possess the talent to add a mid-card and an upper-card to the division as opposed to a huge difference between divisions.

Fire and Ice

What did they do in 2001? We all thought "hey, finally, a face team" and then they did nothing until the year ended. So, alas, they weren't very active in 2001. They were the Latex Lex's of the tag team divisions...not literally, of course.

What to expect in 2002? We've seen more evidence recently that maybe they'll put some emotion into the sport again. If so, they certainly have the talent to rise. Course, they also need to find themselves a decent feud.

P.A.I.N. What did they do in 2001? They busted out in a big way when the WWO re-opened with their plucky humor and wrestling ability. In fact, it can be argued that the gap they put between themselves and the rest of the division as only now been cut by the Romanis. Ran through the WWO World Tag Titles Tournament to become the champs at Inquisition. Continued to feud with the Vegas Connection, losing the titles to the team on a Showcase in mid-cycle. About that time, they began a slow turn to face and found themselves teaming with the tweenerish Union Jack and superface Dark Soul at Anarchy against Greatness Personified. Team Dark Soul seemingly worked better as a team than the stable and P.A.I.N. had a million tricks up their sleeve in the cage. Following Anarchy, they began to feud with Amazing Romanis and continue to this day to be two of the biggest pranksters in the WWO.

What to expect in 2002? More coffee for Dr. Ow, more new procedures from Dr. Practice, and more fun for all. It would not surprise me if they joined World Leaders as the only multi-WWO Tag Team Champions.

Vegas Connection What did they do in 2001? There was a period when P.A.I.N. may have not been the favored team heading into the World Tag Titles tournament and these guys were the reason. They beat the dark horse team of Miguel Quesada and Dark Soul and gave P.A.I.N. a run for the titles before ultimately losing. However, in late July, they got their revenge by winning the titles from P.A.I.N. and following that by joining Greatness Personified, possibly what killed the team. After Anarchy, they settled into a heatless feud with the Canadian Legacy.

What to expect in 2002? Are they on the outs? The rumors are that maybe we'll see them drop the titles and then leave the WWO. When motivated, they can be a main event team. Right now, not one person cares about the tag titles. Let's hope they can change that.
