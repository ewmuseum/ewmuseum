WWO bio card — Niklos "Ricochet" Romani

27 April 2001

Niklos "Ricochet" Romani Real Name: Niklos Romani Height: 5'8 Weight: 217 Date of Birth: Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: The Gypsy Caravan Entrance Music: "Rebel Heart" by Corrs Manager: Physical Appearance Physical Appearance: Niklos is a less rugged version of his brother Ravnos. They share the family's trademark grey eyes and black hair, although Niklos is clean-shaven and sports longer, flowing locks. Other than that, the two are almost indistinguishable. Ring Appearance: Niklos prefers to wear loose fitting, lightweight pants (Arabian-style) and an open, dressy shirt; both are of varying colors and are generally somewhat extravagant. his boots are standard short wrestling boots (just above the ankle, like Steve Austin) to ensure a decent grip for aerial maneuvers, and a pair of elbow pads (either black or white, whichever better "goes with" his current attire) completes the outfit. Psychological Makeup While Ravnos left the company to persue other goals, Niklos remained behind and perfected his high-flying routine with his fellow performers (cousins, mostly). Unfortunately, times got hard and the company had to face a hard decision: either join up with a larger troupe (and face being lost in the shuffle as just another act) or fold. Being the proud gypsies that they were, the company folded and went their separate ways. Niklos was especially upset at this turn of events, for he was on the verge of becoming a headlining attraction. When added to his people's long-lived hatred of "the gaje", Niklos became an angry young man indeed. So when his fast-talking broher suddenly re-entered his life with an opportunity to both be back in the spotlight and to humilate gaje, Niklos jumped at the opportunity. Now, chip on his shoulder and fire in his cold grey eyes, Niklos is ready to show the WWO just how Amazing the Romanis can be. Strengths 1. Speed: Niklos share's his brother's natrual fleetness, as if it were bred into their family. It may have been, given what we know about the Romanis. 2. Agility: Years as a high-wire/trapeeze performer have given Niklos incredible nimbleness and the ability to pull off some truly crowd-pleasing aireal stunts. 3. High-impact offense: He may not hit every maneuver, but he certainly makes the ones he does hit with hurt. Weaknesses 1. Reckless: Niklos frequently acts before he thinks, and occasionally pays for it when one of his high-risk maneuvers backfires and he crashes into something hard. 2. Hot-headed: As if his recklessness wasn't enough, occasionally Niklos will just lose control and literally fly off the handle -- especially when being taunted. When combined with his penchant to leap before looking, this could spell major trouble for the young gypsy. 3. Green: What he has in raw talent he has to use to make up for his total inexperience. He uses the ropes for offense far more often then for defense, and sometimes doesn't pay attention to the ref's count when out of the ring. Ravnos is trying to help, but stubborness also runs in the Romani gene pool. Style Notes: Biographical Information Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Excellent High-Flier Very Good Martial Arts Poor Power Poor Technical None Submission None Cheating Fair Brawling/Hardcore Fair Escapability Good Stamina Excellent Ring Awareness Poor Teamwork Very Good Top Wrestling Moves High dropkick (from behind, preferably) Suicide dive Jumpnig knee strike Standing senton splash Float-over DDT "Human Cannonball" (tumbling shoulder tackle) Spining back elbow strike "High Wire" (rope walk) guillotine leg drop Handspring cross body slash Asai Moonsault Finishing Maneuver Romani Roll Niklos leaps to the top rope (not the turnbuckle, but the actual rope) and springs off with a flip, landing on the opponent's shoulders and rolling back into a huricanrana pinning combination. (The Dragon Rana in No Mercy is the basic idea.) Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Amazing Romanis Tag Team Partner: Ravnos Romani Tag Team Moves "Pickpocket DDT" -- Niklos grabs the opponent in a front facelock, and Ravnos lifts the opponent's legs into the air, parallel to the mat; Niklos then delivers an elevated DDT Niklos follows a Ravnos low blow with a flying bulldog Ravnos tilt-a-whirl slams Niklos onto a downed opponent, Niklos delivering a crushing leg drop in the process Ravnos temporarily blinds the opponent (either with a rake of the face or with the Gypsy Curse powder), allowing Niklos to get in a surprise attack from behind Stereo standing leg drops (Niklos to the head, Ravnos... lower) See Ravnos Romani's application for more tag team manuevers available to this team. Tag Team Finishing Maneuver "Working Without a Net" Niklos sits the opponent on the top turnbuckle and tags in Ravnos, who crouches down in front of the corner in a "ten fingers" pose. Niklos then runs the length of the ring, and is launched into the air by Ravnos, delivering a flying clothesline to the opponent that sends both the victim and Niklos sailing to the floor almost 10 feet below. The Romanis prefer a count-out victory (Ravnos, the legal man, is in the ring; Niklos tries to get up and to the corner), but if a pin is necessary (e.g. title match), one or the other will retrieve the usually KO'd opponent back into the ring; the Romani who actually does the retrieval depends on how good a landing Niklos had.
