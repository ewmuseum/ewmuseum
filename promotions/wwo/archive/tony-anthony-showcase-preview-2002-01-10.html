Showcase Preview

by Tony Antony

Okay dudes and dudettes its time once again for the mot kick ass article in all the land! I don't have my picks from last show but I'm totally sure that I got them all right! Once again I have proved that Tony Antony is totally the name that puts the HIP in HYPE! Okay now on to the article!

Last week I totally predicted that Hudson would win the Loser Leaves Town match and I so nailed it. Unfortunately that kind of match just wasn't for the right to party in Portland. Apparently it means that Dark Soul wrestle in the WWO no more, most foul! Just saying to Dark Soul that if he knows how to party he can jam with me any day. I have a Nintendo at the house and things get pretty wild sometimes. I threw a party last week, and while no one showed up, trust me it so kicked ass!

I also want to say that it is extremely hard to type with this cast totally itching. Its hard hard to concentrate with my cousin Dave banging his head against the wall non stop because Nebraska blew chunks against Miami! I mean he's totally acting like Winger isn't recording albums anymore, like that would EVER happen!

1) Midnight Stars vs Final Justice: Dude I've never even seen this Final Justice team, but I hear their talent is most foul. Plus throw in the fact that they are so ripping on Europe's the Final Countdown it makes me sick. WINNER: Midnight Stars.

2) "Russian Bear" FYDOR SAMSONOV and "Bullit" DWAYNE ROGERS: Russian Bear? Dude is that anything like a lost Care Bear because that show rocks. Er, I mean that show use to rock back when I was a kid! Yeah I totally dug Care Bears, you guys know that show rocked! You know you do! Winner: FYDOR SAMSONOV.

3) "Millionaire Marvel" ERIC STRYKER vs The Guardian: Okay once again the Big Book of Eighties Wrestling came through once again when I totally picked the Guardian to win his first WWO match! Dudes have been trying to tell me that I didn't get that one right, but dudes I so did! The book never lets me done. However the book totally goes again the dude this week. Not only does the book totally love millionaires, but it loves something else called Stryke Force which I can only totally assume is a kick ass corporation that Stryker owns. Throw in the fact that I totally dig Marvel Comics and this guy kicks ass! WINNER: Eric Stryker

4) Dark Haven vs Fire and Ice: Dude I totally know what you are saying. You wish Totally 80's was back and kicking serious ass in the tag scene! But due to one selfish, most foul man with a chair it totally will not be! Anyway I've said it once and I'll say it again, Pat Benetar kicks ass! WINNER: Fire and Ice

5) Xavier Fyre, Marvin Patch, and Rick Marley vs Lion Tamer, Matt Cole, and Jonas King: Dude this is the guy that totally put me on the shelf with a broken leg! This most foul individual says he totally did me a favor but he totally didn't! Is he paying my rent? My doctor bills? My credit card bills from the Tiffany Hotline? Ignore that last one. Anyway, I'm Tony Antony, and I totally don't see the need to hold a grudge. So here's the scoop on what's going down. Upon arrival a run away Mack Truck will come crashing into the most foul Marvin Patch, totally spilling his guts on the pavement. Dude you suck! WINNER: Anyone but Marvin Patch, or the team he's on.

6) Blood Brothers vs Canadian Legacy: Dude this Canada totally has something against people who entertain, basically because they totally can't! I do agree with the fact that the Blood Brothers kick ass! If and when Totally 80's return, which depends on Dave's suicide watch, I totally want to be in the ring with them. Since the Blood Brothers kick serious ass, and the Canada people do not.... WINNER: Blood Brothers.

7) The "Problem Child" Chris Stringer vs Apex: Dude its for a title so this is gonna kick ass! Well at least that's the way its being hyped up. You have a kick ass psycho in Apex, and a kick ass bad dude in Stringer. Plus you have a kick ass chick in Cherry Bomb, which I've been told was not the chick in the Cherry Pie video for Warrent. Dude let me tell you Warrent kicks ass! I was at their concert one time when suddenly... Okay I'll save it for next article. I once heard that AC/DC once sung a song called Problem Child. I never heard it but if AC/DC sang it, trust me it kicked ass. So since this match is too close to call I'll rely on that AC/DC tidbit. WINNER: Chris Stringer.

Dude that's it. Enjoy what is sure to be a kick ass Showcase I have to go take sharp instruments away from all the Cornhuskers now so peace out! I heard Run DMC say that once and it totally kicked ass!!!
