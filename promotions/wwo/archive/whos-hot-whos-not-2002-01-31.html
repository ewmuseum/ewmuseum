Who's Hot/Who's Not?

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

All I can say was... strange, stranger, and strangest... Maybe that should be the top 5 for the week? But as usual, there are some guys who turned it up a notch (whether for good or bad...) and the usual languishers..

So let's start with the guys who were on fire last week... and there were a ton to choose from, as whether or not you liked what happened.. it DID happen, and the "Politically Incorrect Showcase" will probably be talked about for ages!

Who's Hot

5. Lion Tamer

This one was a _tough_ call... Lex/Hayes was a hell of a promo and a brawl... but I had to give the edge to Tamer. Tamer's proven that there's probably no equivalent to himself when it comes to pissing off so many people in a wrestling match by not wrestling? And the "cat and the ball of string" was so cliched, you had to chuckle.. or at least I did... but Tamer not only had everyone on his team frustrated, but pretty much the entire opposition... 5 guys mad at you, because you don't do a single thing!

4. Tracy Hudson

Ouch.. you really want to get hated? Just ask Tracy how it was done. Not only does he beat Dark Soul, but only lets him participate in the WWO under "his" terms. I guess it's good that Tracy "Political Correctness be Damned" Tracy Hudson let us see Dark on TV, but isn't there a law against indentured servitude or something?

3. Apex

He finally did it! He won some serious gold... and over a guy that was otherwise unbeatable.. okay, sure there was all kinds of people involved.. but hey, at least for once Apex was on the "right" end of someone getting chair shotted for once...

2. PAIN/Romanis

Controversial... Absolutely... Dangerous ground to cover? Certainly! A match well worth it though? ABSOLUTELY!

And the number one hot for the week...

1. Canadian Legacy

Okay, again, it wasn't the most "politically correct" scene that Denis and McDonald pulled off.. but damn if it didn't work.. If Blood Brothers don't get a measure of revenge at some point, I think there might be a riot somewhere... And you thought only someone like Johnstone could get you in this much trouble? And the match between the two teams was hot as hell too!

Who's Not..

5. Yahoo

Just for screwing around so that certain front office people can't get in their accounts...

4. Nate Knowitall

Sorry Nate, but beating up on little kids isn't a good thing in anyone's book... although it was keeping with the "Politically Incorrect" theme of Showcase..

3. Midnight Assassin

Foxx claims that the year 2002 will be the year of the Assassin.. but we would have swore she said the same thing about 2001... We'll believe it when we see it.

2. Ultimate Thrasher

We found Thrasher, believe it or not.. he's at the FAYGO detoxification center in Toledo! Craven really did a number on Thrasher if he's looking for help in one of two cities the WWO labels as "hell" (Sacramento being the other.. and to think we have a pair of cards there.).

1. Brett Favre

6 INTERCEPTIONS? SIX?? What more needs to be said!
