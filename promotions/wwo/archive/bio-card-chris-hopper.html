WWO bio card — "Too Cool" Chris Hopper

23 May 2003

"Too Cool" Chris Hopper Wrestler Name: Chris Hopper Date of Birth: Height: 6'8 Weight: 288 Alignment: Scrupulous Hometown: Paoli, Indiana Entrance Music: Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ring Appearance Chris always comes down to the ring wearing sunglasses and has shot black hair. His goatee'd face smiles as he either has one of his merchandise shirts on to show off, or he may be wearing his leather jacket. If he is not wearing the jacket, then he carries it draped over his shoulder. He wears black or blue tights and even wears trunks on occasions. He always makes a point to spend time with his fans before and after every match. Psychological Makeup Hopper can be slightly cocky, but it always manages to be kept in check. He loves his fans and would never do anything to hurt them or their opinion of him. He believes the fans are the main reason this exists. He is normally a black and white kind of guy, but after the Rick Styles feud in UEW, he realizes grey does really exist in the business. He always works the crowd from the turnbuckles before the matches. Sometimes opponents will take advantage and attack while he is up top, but he always recovers. When he wins, he celebrates a little with the fans at ringside, but exits without being too cocky. He always gives high-fives as he enters the match and as he exits, win or lose. Strengths Pure Wrestling skill. Hopper is the complete package. He has martial arts training and also will pull out aerial moves to highlight is vast skill in submission and technical wrestling styles. He truly is unmatched in his pure skill inside the squared circle. Conditioning. Hopper is in tremendous shape. His ring conditioning and stamina lend him to be very quick and agile not only early on, but in the later stages of matches that mightmake other wrestlers his size tucker out. He works out religiously to maintain this conditioning and cardio fitness. It has made the difference in many a match in his career. Fan support. The fans truly love this guy. He attempts to be a good role model for the young people watching and he always shoots to be someone the fans can be proud of. He loves the fans and tries to give it his all every night. The fans see this and reward him with massive cheers everywhere he goes, even if it is not in a wrestling event. Weaknesses Can get distracted by fans and sometimes take a bit too long to cover afetr a big move. This is never match costing, but it can be pretty hazerdous. Tends to want to prove himself to the fans and also to everyone in the locker room that he is the big face guy. He will wrestle almost anyone if it gets him over as a face. This leads him to sometimes not be fully ready for a big time opponent he may not have heard of before. Prior injuries. It is well documented that Hopper has neck problems from some fights he had in UEW. These injuries will be something he will always try and protect at alltimes, but this also means he sometimes may get caught in a bad way as he tries to keep form being injured again. Biographical Information Chris was born in southern Indiana in 1974. He is the oldest of two children. He has worked hard for everything he has ever received in life. His family was never the richest, so Chris usually turned to sports to escape the perils that come with living in a poor community. As a youngster, Chris was always a bit larger than the other guys in his class. Chris never really worried about it, but he always feared that his size would present problems in the future because he was mostly fat. Between his seventh and eigth grade years, Chris had his growth spurt. He jumped from 5'4" to somewhere just over 6'1", a jump of about 9 inches or so. His weight didn't increase as much and he evened out to become a fairly good looking guy. By the time he was trying out for the Freshman Football teams, he was standing 6'5" and weighing in at just over 250 lbs. He not only made the team, but was promoted to varsity by the middle of the season. He ended up becoming one of the top football players in his region and led his team to the state title as a freshman, where he gained state finals MVP honors. He played both fullback and middle linebacker. This was the beginning of a career that saw him rack up over 6,500 rushing yards on offense and over 500 tackles (including 101.5 sacks!) in four years (59 total games) on the team. He was spotted one day by a guy named Randy who worked for the SWA as their champion, but said that if Chris was interested, he could get him a meeting with Mr. Eddie Yopp, the league's owner and head promoter. Hopper loved wrestling and jumped at the idea of wrestling professionally. When Chris met Mr. Yopp in Huron in late 1993, he wowed the promoter with his size. Chris had grown to be 6'8" and was weighing in at just under 290 lbs. Mr. Yopp asked him to try a wrestling school in Memphis instead of college and if he wasn't a top dog in the SWA by the time he was 20, then Chris would be released from his contract and he could go to college. Chris agreed and went to a high profile wrestling school in Memphis, TN in the spring of 1994 at the ripe age of 19. For the next six months, Chris trained harder than anyone else at the facility and it was noticed that he had gained more muscle and that his technical skills were beginning to be top notch. Hopper was told by Yopp that he woudl be given a 60 day active contract and Hopper began to make appearances on TV and worked on his surprisingly strong microphone skills. Hopper wrestled in a few matches and even jobbed to men that are now in the "big show". During his last month with the SWA, Hopper was becoming a huge fan favorite and Mr. Yopp was considering a title push. The SWA was beginning to grow in populairty and network affiliates were looking at it as a syndicated wrestling shows for Saturday afternoons. Hopper was approached by someone about the possibility of adding technical submission wrestling to his already vast skills in both wrestling and martial arts. The only catch to the deal was he had to move to Canada to continue his training there. So Chris was off to Canada for what he calls the "greatest experience of my young career". He trained with an entire family of successful wrestlers. He spent many months under their tuteledge and became a master of many submission and technical holds. Upon his departure he was widely considered the best technical wrestler of his generation of wrestlers. It was while in Canada that he happened upon a small regional league that gave him his first break. He got a chance to wrestle for Martin Solotki in the NARF (North American Roughneck Federation). This was a Canadian version of the Extreme Wrestling style. He was not very successful at the extreme style, and he felt it wasn't the best place to start so he left after only six weeks there. He waited for the last two weeks of his contract to run out and then signed a contract with the WWA in December of 1996. This is where Chris made his mark in the wrestling world because he could be himself and just go after people. He did and he eventually won both the US and World Titles before the league closed down in the spring of 1997. He had become exremely popular and in demand around the wrestling world. He went from place to place, but never was able to find a league that challenged him until September of 1997. That is when he received a call from a close friend that he teamed with in other leagues saying that he was having problems with a stable in his newest league. Chris jumped at the chance to help his friend and "Too Cool" debuted alongside The Gorilla against Hellspawn II in September of 1997. Their tag team, Paradoxx, took off and became the tag team of the year in the NeCW sweeping to two Unified Tag Team Title reigns. Hopper was becoming one of the top stars in the league and fans adored him. Of course all things can't last forever and Paradoxx split up and a bitter fued followed that resulted in an injury for Hopper in January of 1998. Hopper had gone on to win the TV title only to lose to the Gorilla the same night of his injury. Hopper's future was uncertain and he had no clue as to what was next. Chris turned down offers from the WWF, WCW, and IEWA to stay with the NeCW as they prepare to merge with the AeWF in March. Hopper was attending a card about a week before he would be allowed by doctors to return to the ring. He ended up in a special added challenge match against World Champion Gary Albright, and he won! Chris had become the NeCW World Heavyweight Champion! In a rematch, Hopper held on to the belt in a DQ win over Albright. Then abruptly, the NECW closed and Chris went on a two week vacation to figure out his wrestling future. Hopper is gearing up for a special challenge match against former NeCW Light Heavyweight Champion, Jinsei Shakanuzi in the World Cup of Wrestling Card to be held on May 8, 1998. But the question remains as to what his future in this sport will be after this special match in May. On March 28, 1998, Chris has joined a simmed fed called South-Eastern Championship Wrestling. He is doing well so far and is the number three contender after winning a match in each of the leagues first two shows. This was Chris' attempt to stay active and fit for when he returns to the "big time" later in the summer of 1998. During his tenure there he won numerous titles including a fued with Scimitar that had the World Heavyweight Title switch hands 5 times in less than a month! After achieving all he could in SECW, Hopper decided to jump back into the mainstream scene and he met with [name withheld], promotor and President of the UEW, one of the true "bog boys" of our sport. In the fall of 1998, Hopper made his UEW debut by tossing Creed off of a steel cage at the big "Rumble in the Bronx II" PPV event for UEW. He wold make his in-ring wrestling debut a few weeks later and actually began his career there with 3 consecutive pinfall wins. Then politics took over and Hopper was downed many times besides inside the ring, where he lost 3 of his next 4 and got injured in a battle royal that never counted in those match results. This injury gave him a leave of absence from the company and he eventually served them his notice in April of 1999. It wasn't long after the injury that Chris signed a short term contract with Jay Ralston. The promotor of that huge inter-fed event had began his own small promotion and Hopper was scheduled to be the mystery man at the PPV, "WRESTLEKADE", and wrestle in the World Title Tournament. He ha Moo Kame beaten in the semi-finals (after wrestling 2 matches already that night!) when King Snake came out and interferred in the match. Snake took offense to something Moo Kame said as he went towards Hopper and Snake nailed Kame with the Snake Bite, which got Hopper disqualified immediately and tossed from the tournament. Since then Hopper and Snake have fueded and now another man has entered the picture as Aries, God of War has came into IWE and began messing with Hopper. While in IWE, Hopper made numerous public appearances, but mainly those are more for the good perceptioon of wrestling rather than personal gain. After Aries burned down a house that had Hopper parafanalia in it, he was arrested after Hopper brought the tape of the fire being started to the District Attorney. Hoppper and 3 teammates went into battle at the IWE PPV, "SURVIVAL OF THE FITTEST". When the smoke cleared Hopper was one of three survivors from his team and 11 overall. In the final Hopper would win the battle royal and become the defacto number one contender for the IWE World Title. Hopper quickly won the big match against Kame and became the IWE World Champion. As time went by, Chris and IWE President Jay Ralston began to disagree on direction of the company, but before he cold resign, the league closed and Hopper is the IWE champion for life. Hopper also decided to return to UEW after a change in the front office was made and Mike Haynes finally had the support staff necessary to make a great promotion. Hopper's return came on the heels of the return of "Top Dog" Rick Styles and just before the returns of Mannifred Lester and Magnus Colby. With these four men back, the league was ready to return to it's glory days. Hopper quickly ran through his first few opponents, which included "STB" James Pillow, whom he accidentlly injured in the conclusion of their "I Quit" match. The injury to Pillow, incidentally, would be the main reason he left UEW. Hopper is now widely considered the top face in UEW and he has heat with a number of wrestlers. He spent two months earlier this year as UEW American Champion and has been on the losing end of three UEW Ultimate Title Matches. In July of 2000, he lost a three way "Pick Your Poisen" Match when Alex Extreme broke through and won his first Ultimate Title by defeating both Hopper and the defending Champion, Trixxter. His most recent shot was at UEW's Rumble In The Bronx" PPV. In this match he took on Alex Extreme and Doc Holliday in a Triathalon of Triumph Match with Extreme putting up his gold. Currently UEW is awaiting it's "Ides 2001" PPV in which Hopper was a solid favorite to walk out of the event with the league's top title. Now Hopper looks for new and added challenges as he has spare time during many weekends. An old friend contacted him about a league he was going to start in the mid-west and Hopper was curious. Not only about the potential of the area's rabid wrestling fans, but also that this friend is former NeCW chairman, Clint Fletcher. Hopper remembers that Fletch helped jump start his career and decided to sign on for this new opportunity. Hopper sees this as a way to work with old friends, but also to prove that he has not become "cultured" by his 2 years in the "big leagues". Despite solid successes early on, RCW became an environment that Chris no longer felt comfortable in. Ideas he conceived for himself were being taken and used for younger wrestlers by the staff and he felt that he had done his duty in lending "name value" to the early stages of the league. So after the on-air vigilantes attacked some of his friends, he put in his resignation and worked the final PPV. In that event he helped put over some new talent as well as current veteran star, Alex Extreme in the main event. He left the league only to be verbally attacked routinely in the months following his departure. Happy to be free from the "lower class" of wrestling leagues, Hopper centered himself on his UEW and UWF careers. UEW is going well, but UWF was just getting primed. He destroyed John Williams in a "Hell in a Cell" Match at the last PPV and earned a Rampage Title shot. Hopper defeated Eugene Robinson for the title, but had the decision reversed upon appeal from the wiley Robinson. Hopper has vowed to gain revenge for that and cost Robinson the title, but he awaits his chance to achieve his goal. Now Hopper has decided to take a road few saw coming. He has decided to throw his hat into the WWO ring and compete against many stars whom aren't as well known in the "mainstream". A Man like Tyrone Hayes, who was held down during his UEW stint, is the current WWO World Champion. Hopper knows he will be performing for an audience who hasn't seen much of him before and he is excited about the chance to expand his fan base. Can he continue his tradition of solid performing? Or will the talented, yet underrated roster of WWO chew him up and spit him out? The "King of Cool" is ready for all challenges as he steps up to the biggest plate of all!
