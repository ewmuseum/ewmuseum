Scott Bolan - Inside the Minds

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 10, [name withheld]

Well, it seems that I got a decent number of responses for the first question for Inside The Minds. Pretty good for a brand new idea. Hopefully for the second question, we can get the entire WWO roster!! The new question will be at the end of this article, but first, read the responses that were sent in.

Last Issue's Question: What has been your favorite moment so far in your wrestling career?

======================================================================

Apex: Defeating Night's Harbinger at the San Diego Zoo in the XeWA, back in April of '99. The match was brutal and painful, but strangely, it seemed to connect us. It resulted in us forming a great tag team and gave me a life long friend.

Cherry Bomb: Planning the whole Liars Club roast segment. Just really a hell of a lot of fun to do.

Sam Bradley: Are you kidding? Seeing Iron finally turfed out in the World Wrestling Organization is the greatest thing that could ever be seen. And to have it happen thanks to Mean Mason... just goes to show you never underestimate the power of the shoot-fights! Seeing Stone get clobbered never hurts either though, and anytime Thrasher got humiliated... those would be good runner-ups.

Johnny Calhoun: Harry Paumwelle finally getting his trap shut, by yours truly... with some help from Rick Marley.

Magnus Colby: That's an easy one. I'll never forget it. I had taken Alex Martinez down with a backslide, one of maybe half a dozen times in my career I ever used that move. When the bell rang, I could hardly believe it. I had rolled Alex up hoping to get a two count and stall to recover some energy. Apparently, Alex was even more worn out than I was. On that night, I beat Rick Styles and Damian Rose as well. To beat three guys like that... and at the same time walk out as Ultimate champion... pardon the pun, but that was absolutely THE ultimate.

Sean Collins: Bitch, when I kicked da chump Darky Soul's ass befoe da ref said he won cause da mutha cheated.

William Craven: My career. Longer than most seem to think. So much memory. So much time. In Atlantic City ... I was king. Time and again, I proved this. There was a time when I faced Steve Starr, in a pit. We fought as the men across the oceans fight, until one man was down, completely destroyed, unconscious. When I defeated him, I was given a precious moment in which anything I did was legal. That day ... I took Steve Starr's beauty. With a straight razor, I carved into his face the pattern of scars that covers my own. You can't see the marks now unless you stand face to face with Steve, but they are there. A memory of a time when I dominated him, totally. For you see, I didn't just take away his face. For a time, he thought he was me. He followed me. Everyone called him "Craven Two". He did as I said, both inring and backstage. I've fought many a battle more famous than this, against men feared the world over. I've defeated greater opponents, but nothing has ever been more satisfying... His face was cut. His head was shaved. Steve Starr ... my twin, my slave.

Dark Soul: When I won the World Title in a league named DIWL.

Tyrone "Purple" Hayes: That would definitely be the day I got my first paycheck from [name withheld] that didn't bounce... wait a minute, that hasn't happened yet. Well, I guess it would be... um, nevermind.

Tracy Hudson: My greatest moment in the WWO? well, there was this one time...I was standing in line at the hot dog stand, right, and these goth chicks came up to me and asked me if I was Tracy Hudson. I said yes, and then they asked me if I wanted to go in one of the back rooms of the arena to autograph their inner labia.So, I'm in the back with these chicks in the process of "giving them autographs", and we're rubbing soy sauce all over each other and stuff. I mean, things are getting hot and heavy, and I was starting to run out of ink for my pen, and then... well, they gave each other autographs while I reloaded my pen, and... Oh yeah. Kicking Dark Soul's stank ass out of the WWO was pretty cool too.

Todd "The Rod" Johnstone: It would be the time those worthless plebians actually hit me with a bottle they were throwing.

Tre Jordan: Well, when the chalupa came out, that was revolutionary to my career. So, that has to be my favorite.

Nate Knowitall: The first time I lost to Keeblor.

Zachary "SILKK" Mason: MY FAVORITEST MOMENTS WAS WHEN I TOOKD DOWN THAT LOOZER MIGUEL GAYSADA WITH MY BOOT!!! HOOTIE HOO!!!

Luke Morningstar: Being involved in the massive Sean Eden beatdown was really cool.

"Mr. America" Ilya Olenskaya: It is to be the time the Malissa is to say hallo to Ilya in Manestora.

Dr Ohno Ow: I have, to say it, TOSS up. Be-TWEEN winning WWO Tag TITLE, at IN-qui-sitTION, and HOUSE show in Cal-FOR-nia where I meet THREE lady fans who, GAVE me place to stay that, NIGHT. Chicks DIG man with, eyepatch. Need, say more?

Marvin "Trixxster" Patch: Being the first ever wrestler to win all four singles titles in UEW. In the span of ten months I dismantled and out-wrestled every opponent en route to winning the Cruiserweight, Television, American, and Ultimate Titles and becoming the first ever Grand Slam Champion. Despite the antics of my former persona, I *WRESTLED* for those titles, and anybody who says otherwise can kiss you know what.

Glen Peeps: Oh God, wow, there have been so many great ones... I guess when my buddy Chris gave me the cruiserweight belt. I mean, I earned it, and I looked good with it! Didn't I? Huh? Huh? That and doing the FBMS commercial with Tyrone. That was a lot of fun, too.

The Penguin: SQUWACK!

Dr. Mal Practice MD: Honestly, it was my singles title. In early 1997, in a fed called the Ultraviolent Wrestling Federation, I won the Television title in what was considered a complete upset. That's when I knew I really did have what it took to make it in this business; up until that point I had been mainly written off as a 'stupid gimmick'. What the heck does that even mean, anyway?!

Miguel Quesada: Winning the TV title from Dark Soul was great, being able to call myself a champion was something I'll never forget, but my absolute favorite would have to be wrestling Chris Stringer at Inquisition. Just hearing the fans cheering me on that loudly, and going out and having the best match of my career.. I don't care if I did lose, that was the greatest night of my life.

Mickey Ralph: I had a choice. As a play-by-play man, I see all sorts of matches. Hardcore, scientific, high-flying matches. But I think the match that I will remember more than any other is the five-of-nine match between Randy Bryson and Wuzho. That match had pretty much everything you could hope for... and considering the war it brought to a close, it ended the right way. No run-ins, no extra-curricular activities. Just two guys who were going to end it right then and there and kill each other in the process.

Amazing Romanis: With the exception of some minor dalliances, what you've seen from the Romanis is what you get. So far, the best moment in our careers would have to be when we embarrassed Fire and Ice in the cage at Anarchy. Oh yeah, those Canadian guys were in there too, but they embarrassed THEMSELVES, unfortunately.

Victor Schwartz: I dunno about you, but killing American Eagle was just the greatest thing in the world to me. Sure, it was Bill that did the beating, but I'm the one that set him on fire! Yeah.

Shelly: Without a doubt, Anarchy! It was my first time in front of a crowd that big, and it was my first Pay-Per View ever! I can't even begin to describe the rush I have just standing next to Nate, let alone accompanying him to the ring. Our entrance was nothing short of spectacular. Nate is sooo awesome. That was by far the best moment of my career. It was almost better than the sex. Almost. -xoxoxo

Christopher Stonebreaker: I have to admit being a bit selfish when I'm asked what my favorite moment in professional wrestling has been. To be honest, I have to say that my early career centered around fighting The Inner Circle in the IWF/WOW. And when I finally got a shot at The Prophet and Derrick Damann, and in the process won the I/W Heavyweight Championship, the pinnacle of my wrestling career had been reached.

Chris Stringer: Beating Miguel Quesada on Inquisition for the IP title. Just like the first time you have sex, you'll always remember your first title.

Lenny Wacus: Being from an older generation of wrestling fans, perhaps one of my all-time favorite memories is the kind that would surprise a few people. There's not often when a booking committee will "challenge" a wrestler for getting over-inflated in the ego department. So when Iron issued a promise that he'd leave the federation if he couldn't defeat 5 men in a gauntlet type scenario, it was a case of never being happier at seeing Randy Bryson cost Iron a match, and set off perhaps the greatest war in the World Wrestling Organization, as well as forever killing the career of Iron.

Stacy Werner: I haven't had that many great moments in my short career in the wrestling business, but I guess it'd have to be my debut in the WWO. Coming out before my brother's match and kissing Miguel, and announcing to the world who I was, it was just a fun night. I've never seen that many people before in my life. Plus, just seeing how upset the fans were at Miguel.
