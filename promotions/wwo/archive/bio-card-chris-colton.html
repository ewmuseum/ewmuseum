WWO bio card — "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton Wrestler Name: Christopher Lucas Colton Date of Birth: August 15, 1974 Height: 6'2 Weight: 228 Alignment: Miscreant Hometown: Riverside, California Entrance Music: "My Way" by Usher Manager: N/A Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Colton is very well put together. Though not a bodybuilding champion like his father, Chris still gives the appearance of someone who is in very good shape. He has a well- tanned body perfected on the beaches of the southern California Pacific coast. He has piercing green eyes that match his wrestling trunks, and a mop top of bleached blond hair. His skin is very well tanned, and on his upper right arm there is a tattoo of California, with a green star appearing right where Riverside is. Out of the way, and in in-ring interviews where he is not wrestling immediately after the interview, Chris will wear a variety of things, but on the whole he is well-dressed. This is the kind of man with the looks, body, and confidence that can make a T-shirt and jeans look like the cover of GQ. Most of the time he will wear silk dress shirts, and Dockers' slacks or other brand name clothes. Other times he will simply wear whatever C2C T-shirt is popular at the time and a pair of black jeans. He ALWAYS wears the Ray-Bans, with the only exception being when he is wrestling. At times they will be pushed up on his forehead, but they are always there. Ring Appearance Colton's wrestling attire is simply a pair of dark green tites and wrestling boots. His clothing on the way to the ring is the same thing, plus the trademark mirrored Ray- Bans Psychological Makeup Strengths Speed - He's only about 230 (a little less), and he has more quickness in his right index finger than most men do altogether. One of Colton's biggest tactics in a match is keep moving, wearing down an opponent with less stamina. Aerial Ability - Colton is not afraid to go up top, and once there, he's not afraid to do all kinds of crazy shit. He'll dive to the outside, into the ring, over the steel barricade, with flips, somersaults, planchas, whatever. Colton is fast and light, and that means he can hit hard and often. Experience - Colton has been around the game for a few years, and has spent a large amount of time in the ring. As a result, he feels very comfortable, and pretty much knows where he is at all time. This fact allows him to be VERY confident in the ring. Weaknesses Overconfidence - Sometimes, his comfort in the ring will exceed confidence and push forward into cocky and obnoxious. At this point is when someone he might have overlooked can come back and hurt him in a match. Strength - Colton is strong, able to bench over three hundred. But that doesn't always translate into the ring. It's not so much that he's not strong, but that he doesn't feel comfortable using power moves. So when he does, they're a lot less effective than they would be normally. Lack of Brawling Ability - Colton is not a hardcore guy, that was always Tony's area. If the opponent turns to the punches and just starts going off, Chris will undoubtedly try to turn it back to a wrestling match. Sometimes this is good-sometimes it's not. Biographical Information "Riverside" Chris Colton was born Christopher Lucas Colton on August 15th, 1975 in Riverside, California to Jackson Jeffrey (J.J.) Colton and Melissa Helen Colton. J.J. Colton was one of the top bodybuilders in southern California, and this was evident at an early age, as Chris was obviously stronger than a lot of kids in his class in kindergarten. Chris started playing baseball in 2nd grade, and quickly became one of the best shortstops in the Riverside Little League Baseball Association. Chris' strength was never more evident than in his first football game in 7th grade, playing for Rialto Middle School in Riverside. He played linebacker, and on his first play, he injured the other team's quarterback with a crushing tackle. However, Chris was never enamored with his strength. He was always more concerned on perfecting his finesse and increasing his speed. He joined the track team at River Heights High School in 9th grade, and was immediately varsity. His sophomore year he went to state, and he won the state competition in the 100-yard dash and 50- and 100-yard hurdles his 11th and 12th grade years. However, Chris' best sport was always wrestling. He started wrestling in the 8th grade, and won the San Bernardino County junior high championship for Rialto Middle School. In 9th grade he made varsity for River Heights and took third place at state in the 155-pound class. He bulked up a little bit, and in the 174-weight class, he took second place his sophomore year. He decided to stay at that weight for his junior year and ended up taking the state title again. As a senior, he was 6'4 and 225 lbs, after a growth spurt, so he was wrestling in the heavyweight division. He was a lock for the state crown, and he took it without losing a single match. He was then invited to the National High School Wrestling Tournament in New Orleans, Louisiana, and that was where he met Tony Richmond. Chris was a virtual unknown amongst the national wrestling experts, and was not expected to go far nationally. He was given a low seed, and in the second round, was matched up against Matt Marbles, a 238-pound senior from Prescott, Arizona. Marbles had won the national championship the previous year, and was generally considered to be the best heavyweight wrestler in the nation, already signed to wrestle at the University of Iowa the next year. In one of the most stunning upsets in high school wrestling history, Colton took the champ to three rounds, where he hung on till the final minute of the match, and was down by two points with just a few seconds left. Marbles almost had his shoulder pinned to the mat, but amazingly, Chris powered his way up, slipped a shoulder between Marbles' leg, and flipped him over, pinning him just before the towel hit the referee. Needless to say, Chris was happier than a football player at a cheerleading competition, but Marbles, who had not lost a match since his sophomore year, was mad and wanted revenge. Colton had traveled from California with the wrestle of the California squad, and they decided to go out and celebrate Chris' victory with a couple illegal drinks out on Bourbon Street. Chris came with them, but decided at 10:00 that he should go back to his hotel room to prepare for the next day. His friends didn't want to leave, so Chris walked back alone. Marbles' and some of his friends had followed Chris out, and followed him back. Chris decided to take a shortcut and ended up getting lost. He wandered into a dark alley, which had no exit other than the entrance. Marbles and his pals blocked Chris in and closed in on him. Chris tried to talk his way out of it, but there was no way. So he tried to fight them all off. There were 4 besides Marbles, and Chris managed to fight off three of them, until they smartened up and closed in on him at once. He landed a few good shots, but they began to pound on Chris relentlessly, and he dropped down to one knee. He was on his knees, fighting off the pain, when suddenly there was no more punches and no more kicks. He was bleeding very badly, and bruised, but no bones were broken, and he felt someone pull him to his feet. He swung at the unknown person, thinking it was one of his original attackers, but a strong fist closed around his. He looked up and a tall, large black man was smiling a small, quaint smile at him. Chris then allowed himself to be pulled back to his hotel. Chris got the man's number, who turned out to be Tony Richmond. They stayed in touch through the rest of Chris' senior year, and afterwards, the two hooked up and Chris taught Tony some of the finer points of wrestling. Tony helped Chris bulk up a little, and helped him hone in on his natural strengths, his agility and his endurance, not to mention his incredible speed. Tony had a friend, Trent Lassiter, aka Blizzard Frost, who was running an independent promotion, the Blizzard International Wrestling Company, or BIWC. Chris and Tony teamed up to win the promotion's tag belts. Blizzard, meanwhile, was winning the Internet belt in the NWWF, and then the Sunday Shocker Title in the EIW, not to mention his reign as the EIW Tag Champs with the legendary "Dominator" Don Cameron as the Storm. Trent took some time off to be with his pregnant wife, Crystal Lassiter, aka Crystal Queen, and gave his NCCW invitation to the tag team. Chris and Tony then embarked on what many consider one of the greatest tag team careers in the last 5 years, using the team nickname Coast to Coast Connection. They quickly captured the NCCW World Tag Team Titles, and every promotion they entered, they won the tag belts. This went on for about two years, before Tony's wife went into a coma with the birth of their first child. Tony promptly left the wrestling world, easily a multi-millionaire. Chris, who had never wrestled professionally without Tony, left as well and opened up a string of gyms throughout the Riverside/San Bernardino area. About 6 months later, Tony showed up at one of Chris' gyms unannounced, and told his friend that he was ready to go back to wrestling. And so they did. They entered the GPW and quickly won all of the tag straps there, and they also entered the WWO, which they looked very promising in before it's abrupt close. Chris and Tony continued entering small federations, continued dominating as usual. It was in one of these small federations that Chris Colton started to break off on his own. Tony was bored, and he wanted to spend more time with his wife and now 2 children. He and Chris were signed to the CWF as a tag team, but the front office had no problem allowing the charismatic Colton to work a few singles matches-after all, few can work the mic like Chris can. At any rate, personal alliances soon collided, and the front office soon found themselves with a match between Colton and their current World Champion. Nobody has asked for it, but Colton won it anyway. Now an accomplished singles and tag team wrestler, Colton continued to wrestle in several smaller promotions, even joining a larger one, the NNEW, with Tony, and quickly capturing their tag titles. However, the twosome's best opportunity arose when the WWO reopened it's doors, and invited the Connection back with open arms. At this time, Tony was the CEO of Newport Fashions, Inc., a clothing line which made all types of attire, 99% of it being black. At any rate, between that and his family, Tony didn't feel as if he had the time to give Chris 110% in the WWO, and he declined his half of the invitation. Colton then hooked up with another long-time colleague, Don Cameron, and accepted a singles invitation to the WWO.
