Battle of the Titans IV (excerpt)

18 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. Battle of the Titans IV ran twelve parts to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy; only the two title-deciding segments below were read in full for this pass and are reproduced here. The other ten parts, including the night's third title change, remain held and are not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO SIX-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP [Segment excerpted from Part 7 of 12. A stretcher-job angle and outside interference run through the match; the finish:] ...Lord Keeblor is motioning to the crowd!! DOWN!!!!!!!!! OH MY!!! Lord Keeblor just landed the KEEBLOR BOMB on Thompson!! Unbelievable!!!! Thompson I think needs medical attention! Lord Keeblor for the cover!!!!!! Who was that, and where is Roberts? In the back, on the outside!! COVER!!! 1, 2, 3!!!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS!!! NEW SIX MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS LADIES AND GENTLEMEN!!! LORD CRUISE, KNIGHT ROCKER, AND LORD KEEBLOR!!!! This WAS a great victory for these guys, but everyone must be thinking what is up with the CDO. A lot has happened in this one: the Dangerous Alliance defection, and this huge mastodon that took Roberts away! Unfortunately, the former Six Man Champs will have to wait quite some time for a rematch, as Andros, Grant, and Lariot get it first, as they defeated their opponents for the number one contender spot. WWO UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP [Segment excerpted from Part 10 of 12: Underground Kingpin, accompanied by Riddick Bowe, versus Andros of the Business Men, accompanied by Mr. Executive. Andros had wrestled earlier on the same card and entered still recovering. A long match followed multiple two-counts and escapes on both sides; the finish:] Andros hoisting Kingpin back up to a body slam position, but UK grabs on to the ropes, and won't let go! The referee kicks Kingpin loose! And Kingpin falls on top of Andros! The referee down to count! One... Two... Three!!!!!!! Yes! Kingpin able to lock the leg after the first count, and it looks like... Yes!! WE HAVE OUR NEW US CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!! You're winner of the match, and NEW WWO UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, UNDERGROUND KINGPIIIIINNNNN!!!!!!!! The referee handing the belt to UK, and he hoists it up in the air, and look at the look at Andros' face, he can't believe what just happened; he was just turning everything his way in the match, and out of nowhere, Underground Kingpin manages to catch a lucky break, and that's what it looked like it was going to take to get the win in this match, and it just happened to fall Kingpin's direction.
