Inebriated Press 31 March 2001 BILOXI, MS-From humble beginnings in the mid 90's, the World Wrestling Organization has risen to prominence, if not dominance, in the world of professional wrestling. Along the way, it has absorbed several small regional promotions to expand itself properly. The World Wrestling Organization has officially expanded their operations to levels which have been before unheard of in this sport. The WWO has completed a buyout option with a number of federations to create the sports largest conglomerate of federations. The WWO-Conglomerate had been in negotiations since their reopening with a number of federations and can finally announce the details of the merger. The Conglomerate includes the full purchase of the EMWC, UWF, UEW, GLCW, MVW, NEO, CSW, IWF/WOW, and every other wrestling organization on the planet. In addition, after some heavy donation to campaign funds, the United State Congress and President Bush enacted a law that forces all new wrestling organizations to fall in under the Conglomerate with a historic nearly unanimous agreement. Only Barney Frank cast the opposing vote, saying, "Hell, let's just get rid of professional wrestling altogether. What I do behind closed doors isn't considered professional, why should these guys get paid to do it?", before being reminded that he was drawing a salary in Congress, and immediately withdrew his opposing vote. Reports from the wrestling community is that this is a historic new era in the sport. Even some of the federation presidents were rumored to be heard in the background planning vacations to exotic locales as Tahiti, Monaco, Paris, and even Chris Blue said he was looking forward to taking a step up from his usual vacation sites, and plans on enjoying a vacation in Little Rock, Arkansas. "I was impressed, they even had indoor plumbing as one of their hot attractions, and I don't want to miss out on it!" Mike Beeby said that he was thinking about taking a break from his war-ravaged front offices and going to visit the peace and quiet of Afghanistan. "It's a nice peaceful change." [name withheld] was rumoured to be in a depression when he was told the CNN tower was not the tallest building in the world... "Now I've got a chance to prove that I'm finally right and everyone else is wrong." In addition, the Conglomerate has announced that it has taken creative control of all organizations as the "Fan Site" and "E-Wrestling University". The owners of the various news sites were the most vocal against the acquisitions, however, a trip to Disney Land changed the minds of many. [name withheld] said "I get a chance to visit the Magic Kingdom? Damn right I'm selling". Jacob Kane was the last one to sell, saying he wasn't going to sell a damn thing, until he got the chance to meet his idol, Goofy. After some last minute work with the Disney Corporation and Capital City, the promise was made and the deal was finalized. A number of wrestlers have been told that they need to start packing their bags, and that their contracts were being bought out "immediately". But no names were given, although inside sources say wrestlers such as Ric Studd, Iron, and King $$$ Perfect are on a permament blackball list. However, with the promise that various federations would be kept in operation in other locales, many of the stars of the sport would be kept on. Chris Courtade responded when he heard about the GLCW's relocation to Azerbaidzhan, "I may not know how to spell it, but hey, it's world travel, and I fly first class. It's a good life!". Many Australian wrestlers have been planned to be returned to the island due to a claim of "Dingo-Itis" by Tyrone Hayes. The Conglomerate promises that once the claim is disproven, and they believe it will be, they'll be given first class treatment in such locations as Indonesia, Malaysia, and Papau-New Guinea. Long time WWO announcer Sam Bradley, who acted as the mouthpiece for the World Wrestling Organization, was on his best behavior in the finance meetings. Bradley promised that everyone would be treated fairly, with the exception of all gypsies, saying that their thievery would not only hurt the Conglomerate, but all the federations under the conglomerate. "How would you feel if you knew that the minute you turned around, you had everything stolen from under you?" When asked if the WWO had just done the same thing with all these acquisitions, Bradley replied, "Don't tell me Stone's got gypsy blood in him too? Of course nothing he did would surprise me." Mr. Bradley later made sure that even women would get their proper time with the new company, after the usual compulsatory inteviews, which Mr. Bradley said he would be personally in charge of. When asked about what organizations would be closed, Stone responded by saying that he considered closing only two orgnaizations, the WWA and the WIF. However, after some further plans, Stone announced that [name withheld] would be put back in charge of the WWA, the federation he created, and Mr. Freeman promised, no more of these one-foot shennagins would ever happen again. The WIF however, was declared by Stone as being part of the city of Toledo, and he was petitioning the United States government to force the city of Toldeo to secede from the nation, noting that it was doing absolutely nothing but potmarking this great landscape. In addition, in a move that has industry insiders baffled and further the "acquisitions of professional wrestling", the WWO has taken over multimedia conglomerate AOL Time Warner, itself a new company formed by the recent merger of America Online and Time-Warner. The new company, to be known as WWO AOL Time Warner (WWOAOLTW on the New York Stock Exchange) will be one of the world’s largest businesses, with a yearly gross income larger than the Gross National Product of most Third World nations. "It is not our intention to create a monopoly here," said WWOAOLTW CEO Mark Stone at a press conference. "As with our, heh heh, arrangements with the WIF and other federations, we will take great precautions to keep all the companies under our umbrella separated, so that they may maintain the unique individuality that made them top in their fields." "That’s right," exclaimed Time/Source chairman Peter Snidegarb, holding up the newest issue of his magazine, adorned with Gary Hammond artwork and a layout by Kimberly Campbell. "As you can see, nothing has changed over here at Time. We’re still committed to journalistic integrity, the factual presentation of current events, and glowing reviews for the latest Union Jack match." While many in the wrestling community are praising the merger, it has its naysayers in the business world. Microsoft chairman Bill Gates, for one, is concerned about the lack of federal scrutiny given the deal. "What the fucking fuck?" he commented in a press release. "I take a shit and everyone yells ‘anti-trust’ and talks about splitting my company up, but this asshole Stone takes over the whole fucking goddamn world and no one bats an eye. What the cock is that horseshit??" Stone, for his part, claims that "we’re only here to serve the customer," adding that had Gates’s severed head not recently been impaled on a stake (next to the head of WWO founder [name withheld]) outside the WWOAOLTW Tower in Biloxi, Stone would have been happy to "open a dialogue" with Gates. President Bush, in his weekly radio address to the nation, urged Americans to "embrace the –what am I supposed to say? Ow! Stop poking me with that bayonet! -- embrace the new era in American business that commences with the – ow! -- merger of these fine companies." Stone insists that life will go on normally for all Americans under the benevolent protection of the Conglomerate-WWOAOLTW jihad. "Other than demanding that every household in the country to buy at least one Lord Keeblor Electric Water Pik, I will ask nothing of my subj – er, fellow citizens," adding that addressing him as "Lord God King Stone" is "strictly voluntary, at least until the third quarter of this year." ------------------------------------ RUMORS by Rumor Will -Tyrone "Purple" Hayes was found to be the illegitimate love child of Princess Diana and Chris Rock -As part of their alliance with Final Justice, Uncivil Disobedience may be allowed to "tag team" Valerie Dougan-Barney -Chris Stringer may be kicked out of Operation Scorched Earth for violating OSE's "Don't Ask, Don't Tell" policy -Look for the WWO to buy out a competing federation sometime after the first PPV. -The bounty on the head of Johnny Detson was not put out by Eric Stryker, but rather by Union Jack -On a recent visit to Los Angeles, Sparkling Dan Grief was arrested for indecent exposure -Shade Roberts and Don Cameron may be sent to Dr. Mal Practice for a personality transplant -Candy Malone may have been spotted coming out of the bedroom of Cherry Bomb -An unholy Malaysian ritual may force Steve Erickson and Sean Eden to merge into "Sucktron" -OSE may pick an unlikely target for their next attack -Ultimate Thrasher's guitar was reportedly stolen after the last Showcase and shipped to the Hard Rock Cafe in Orlando -A car owned by deceased wrestler "GQ" Elan Desire was found at the bottom of the Mississippi River. --------------------------------- SHOWCASE REVIEW by Gary Hammond -Tyrone Hayes gets ambushed by UTD and dragged into a locker room.
