Big Dave Grief Real Name: Dave Grief Height: 7'0 Weight: 400 Alignment: Anarchist Hometown: Syndey, Australia Entrance Music: "Mine" by the Hoodoo Gurus Manager: Psychological Makeup Strengths Besides a huge male organ and a very tolerant liver it would have to be his smart arse attitude Strength/power/brawling ability. Almost no one can go toe to toe with Dave Grief. Willingness to break the rules and no fear of hurting opponents. Unlike most people, to Dave, rules are meant to be broken, as long as the guy who gets it in the end deserves it. Weaknesses Speed - lets face it he's built for comfort and not speed Long matches will see him wear down. Make Dave chase you, and he will tire himself out faster than normal. Getting into fights better left alone. He doesn't care when he runs his mouth and how many people are willing to group up to get even with him for it, and it will get him in trouble if not just for sheer numbers. Biographical Information Where to start .... let's face it, Roundhouse was drinking long before SCSA, smoking loing before Paul Wight even worked out one end of the cigarette from the other and wenching before eitherthe WWF or WCW started showing tits and arse - the guy is a FUCKING TREND SETTING SHEEP SHAGGING LEGEND!!! The 'Big Unit' started as a manager/bodyguard/procurer of women with little or no morals for the World's Greatest Ever Tag Team, Search & Destroy. One thing led to another and he ended up with a bitchin' case of herpes - wait - that's another story ... The Big Unit soon found himself in the ring in a number of feds (I can't honestly remember which ones) but really began to shine in the WWO - feuds with Wazza, his cousin Tyrone Hayes and even Thrasher saw the Big Unit reaching the nose bleed causing height of main eventing .... Let's face it - the guy is a bloody laugh .. in WCW Sting worship led to such crap as Black Sting but in the WWO Roundhouse worship has spawn such all time favourites as FCR. Win, lose or draw, Roundhouse has brought out the best in his opponents and has well and truly earned his title of WWO Superstar. He still favours the typicla big man powermoves but will pull out the occasional technical move out of his arse - not so much to win the match with that style but more to make a point that he can do that type of shit when' he's desperate. Towards the end of the WWO he brought his younger brother Sparkling Danny Grief to the ring (Not that it was teh first time Sparkling Danny had been punched around the ring) - this time around the two Australians will spend some time competing in the tag team ranks Wrestling Attire - The Big Unit will wear his traditional long legg/singlet style wrestling trunks made of royal blue with the stars ofthe Southern Cross emblazoned across the chest. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil None High-Flier None Martial Arts None Power Excellent Technical Fair Submission Fair Cheating Good Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability Good Stamina Fair Ring Awareness Good Teamwork Good Top Wrestling Moves Southern Cross move onto the ring apron to really damage opponents heads/necks. Also done on the turnbuckles, as well as on the corner post itself for an extremely vicious strike. tilt a whirl slam assorted punches, kicks and headbutts pile driver power bomb Belly to Belly - including the twisting BTB, and his favorite, Over the Shoulder version. Belly to Back Suplex Attitude Adjuster - Requires the opponent to be outside the ring. Dave will pick the opponent up as if setting him up for a power slam, but instead literally drops him face fist on the ring apron. If he's really in a vicious mood, he'll perform the move on the ring steps. Finishing Maneuver The Southern Cross - Full nelson where he waves his opponent around like a rag doll before planting them with a face slam into the canvas, adding his own weight as the "coup de' gras" to the maneuver's impact. Tag Team Information Tag Team Name: The Grief Brothers Tag Team Partner: "Sparkling" Dan Grief Tag Team Moves Doomsday Device (the old S&D finisher - top rope bulldog etc to nail bloke sitting on shoulders of man in the ring) spike piledriver drop toe hold to belly to belly suplex or similar maneuver. Tag Team Finishing Manuever Good Grief - Dave will plant an opponent with a tilt-a-whirl power slam or back breaker, or other similar power move, and Sparkling Dan will then come off the top rope with a moonsault or back somersault splash. Depth Charger - Sparkling Dan ties up opponent in a surfboard submission (Vertical or horizontal) while BDG lands a rather fat large (400lb) 2nd rope leg drop across the back on the opponents neck
