Stables

13 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWO Stables The Alliance An attempt by some of the members of the WIF to break the World Wrestling Organization. Through a concentrated effort, the Alliance claimed all but one WWO title, before announcing they were leaving the federation and taking the WWO titles with them back to the WIF. However, not all of the members ended their WWO contracts, with Ultimate Thrasher, Search, and Destroy staying with the WWO, effectively dooming the stable's intent. Members - Steel Iron Ultimate Thrasher Tony Silver Lancaster Bomber Search Destroy Sapphire America's Most Dangerous One of the last "great" stables of the [name withheld] administration. The only stable known to hold all WWO singles titles in existance under one banner. Egos eventually got in the way of each other, and the stable self-destructed. Members -- Derrick Michaels King $$$ Perfect "Ravashing" Ric Studd Tony Tobis The Aristocracy A group of non-Americans, united and led by Robert d'Artois. He used the Aristocracy to claim a world championship, and once that was accomplished, the stable lost a reason of being and returned to Europe. Members - Robert d'Artois Jean Bonnemort Priam Ixion Manager - Mentor ??? Army of Aggression One of Andros last attempts to put a stable together, in part to get himself back to the top of the singles world Members - Andros Tyrant Ultimate Thrasher $tryke Business Men One of the first stables put together which involved Andros. Led by Mr. Executive, the Business Men showed that smaller stables could indeed become a dominant force in the WWO. Two versions of this stable existed, with "Solid Rock" joining the Business Men in the second version. Version 1 - Manager: Mr. Executive Andros Grant Lariot Version 2 - "Solid Rock" Rusty Bomwan Andros Grant Lariot Carnage-a-Trois A highly successful "troublemaking" stable which delights in causing trouble to both heels and faces. The Carnage-a-Trois is regarded as one of the biggest "heat-causing" stables, with incredible antics and words giving most anyone that opposing them a huge "rub". The stable was disbanded by Tryone Hayes after Latex Lex went into hiatus, and Hudson and James failed to live up to "his" standards Version 1 - Manager: Todd "The Rod" Johnstone Tryone "Purple" Hayes Latex Lex Version 2 - Manager: Todd "The Rod" Johnstone Tryone "Purple" Hayes Latex Lex "Prodigy" Tracy Hudson "Dios Loco" Rob James Continental Connection A concerted effort by the faces of the World Wrestling Organization at the time to unite and fight off a number of heel stables. The stable eventually fell apart when the heel stables self destructed, leaving too many faces in one group to deal with small numbers, and eventually Randy Bryson turning against many of his former stable mates. Members - American Eagle "Solid Rock" Rusty Bowman Rocky Johnson Andros Randy Bryson Ravashing Ric Studd Christopher Stonebreaker The Dangerous Alliance Effectively a "joke" of a stable, the DA was renowned for having three of the most incompetent wrestlers in the WWO, and was never a threat in itself. Members - The Genius The Perfect One Total Package Even Better The last rendition of the "Simply the Best" stable. (See below). The stable appeared to be one of the best "units" on paper, but never achieved the success it appeared primed for. Members - Randy Bryson "GQ" Elan Desire "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian "Pledge" Members (never had official membership) - Techno Nic B Vanilla Stalker Tyrant Fire and Ice One of the original stables of the WWO, and perhaps the first face stable to come into existance. However, it wasn't much of a force during the [name withheld] era, although it did claim the WWO Six-Man Titles. Members - "Iceman" Michael Ice "Red Hot" Alex Perkins "Frozen" Shade Roberts Fun Loving Criminals The first ever WWO stable to be consisted entirely fo tag teams. The stable was formed after Fire and Ice turned on the fans in a four corner match, claiming the WWO Tag Team Titles, while Uncivil Disobedience claimed to be the WWO IP Tag Team Champions. The stable dominated the tag team ranks for almost six months before Fire and Ice announced their hiatus. Members - "Iceman" Michael Ice "Red Hot" Alex Perkins The Firm Rax Justice Gentlemen's Club The reformation of the Showmen. The stable was eventually the same, with the replacement of Al Perez. Eventually this stable was shown to be an attempt to humiliate Mike Sebastian, due to actions in other federations. Original Members - "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian Jude St. Claire "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson After the assault on Sebastian, another member replaced Sebastian "Latin Lightning" Al Perez Thus reforming part of the Showman, however Perez retired shortly thereafter, and the Gentlemen's Club fell apart. Hall of Fame The second "all face" stable to form in the WWO (With the Connection being the first). The stable was destined to collapse when a World Title run got between the two heavyweights, Roundhouse and Ultimate Thrasher. With the departure of Rusty Bowman and Andros, Roundhouse sought to bring in Sapphire, which Ultimate Thrasher vehemently disagreed with, and he left the stable to join with Iron and Mean Mason in forming "NO!" Version 1 - Roundhouse Ultimate Thrasher "Solid Rock" Rusty Bowman Search Destroy Andros Version 2 - Roundhouse Sapphire Search Destroy Hell's Gate A small stable which managed some small success. The Lethal Weapons (Billy Club and Blade) held the WWO World Tag Team titles, although the competition was not regarded as some of the best, while Night Stalker won his first (and shortest) of two TV title reigns. Members - Night Stalker Billy Club Blade Hell On Earth One of the first truly dominant heel stables in the World Wrestling Organizations. The stable held the World Tag and World Heavyweight championship at the same time, one of the few stables that could make such a claim. The stable fizzled badly at the time of it's closure due to the failure to adjust to the new influx of talent. Original Members - Death Hell's Angel Mental Insanity The Master and Ruler of the World Captain America Eventually Mental Insanity retired from wrestling, and later on was replaced by KIng $$$ Perfect Jack "Tower of Terror' Harper The stable however failed to adapt to the change in power from [name withheld] to [name withheld] and collapsed. The IW Stable A small stable of individuals who invaded the WWO from the IWF/WOW. The highlight of the stable was The Prophet's victory over Solid Rock Rusty Bowman for the WWO United States Championship. Shortly thereafter, the groups attention was forced to return to the I/W and fizzled out in the WWO. Members - "The Future" Chris Johnson The Prophet Angel of Death Choir Master (Manager) Rick Wright (Bodyguard) Anthony Edwards (Manager) Lords of the Ring A small stable that not much information can be obtained on. Members - Lord Keeblor ???? ???? Maniac Bob's Crew A group of men who came over with the WIF after Stranglehold. While not interested in the same goal as the Alliance, and simply looking to wrestle, due to a lack of success, Maniac Bob's crew returned with the majority of the alliance back to the WIF. Members - Manager: Maniac Bob Bruiser Bob Hacker Dr. Doom Masked Bulldog NO! A last ditch attempt by Iron to get into the World Wrestling Organization. He latched on to the coattails of Ultimate Thrasher adn attempted to create havoc in the WWO. However, the entire stable collapsed within a couple of weeks, as Mean Mason joined only long enough to humiliate Iron, effectively destroying the stable. Members - Ultimate Thrasher Iron Mean Mason Royal Court A small stable led by American Eagle (under the name "King of Diamonds"). The stable lasting all of one match before falling apart due to a lack of talent. Members - Queen of Hearts Black Jack King of Diamonds Ace of Spades Ten of Clubs The Showmen The formation of a number of wrestlers who signed over from CWC. The Showmen were originally expected to be another failed invasion, but they quickly integrated themselves into the WWO instead of going up against it and became the second heel stable, right behind Simply the Best. The stable finally came to an end with Al Perez's face turn on St. Claire and Detson. Members - "Playboy" Jude St. Claire "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson "Latin Lightning" Al Perez The Ultimates Simply the Best Considered one of the greatest stables to ever step into the WWO, STB had a number of versions. Version 1 - "Mr. Ratings" Warren "Ravashing" Ric Studd "The Future" Chris Johnson "Longhorn" John Houston Version 2 - "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson "Playboy" Jude St. Claire Society of Notoriety The eventual reformation of STB and Even Better, this heel group was potentially the greatest stable, perhaps even surplanting CAT and STB, but the federation closed down before it's greatness could be reached. Members - "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes Doc Holliday Nevermind Ultimate Thrasher Brett Maverick Supremo Extremo A small stable that never amounted to much of a threat during it's time in the World Wrestling Organization, despite having former WWO World and IP champion Tony Tobis Members - Tony Tobis Wolverine Wolvenstein Thug Life Organization A failed attempt to bring a number of "cheap heat" seekers in order to get Michael Fulton over. The World Leaders managed to win the WWO World Tag Team titles, but the division was not that good at the time. This stable was generally considered a total failure. Members - Michael "Prime Time" Fulton Silk Napoleon Hussein Black Caesar Justin "Big Daddy" Fischer Trio of Terror A last ditch effort by King Perfect to form a stable to give him control over the singles ranks. The stable did not last long as the rookie Perfect brought in quickly realized that Perfect and Harper were a "dying breed" and quickly bailed on the duo. Shortly thereafter Perfect and Harper left the organization, realizing their time as the stars were up. Members - King $$$ Perfect Jack "Tower of Terror" Harper Ultimate Thrasher Uncivil Disobedience Originally a pairing of four who only cared about causing mischief and chaos, the tag team of Uncivil Disobedience, CJ Gesner, and Loaf united to form the Uncivil Disobedience stable. Gesner and Loaf left the federation shortly thereafter, and were replaced by Alexander Bennington and "Prodigy" Tracy Hudson, but the stable never lived up to it's intent. Version 1 - Rax Justice The Firm CJ Gesner Loaf Version 2 - Rax Justice The Firm Tracy Hudson Lord Alexander Bennington The Wrecking Crew This was the CWC's attempt to invade the World Wrestling Organization. The stable didn't last long, as even it's own members could not seem to get along, with most of the stable taking exception to the "stupid" actions of the LA Wrecking Crew. Members - LA Wrecking Crew "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson "Latin Lightning" Al Perez The Ultimates
