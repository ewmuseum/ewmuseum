WWO bio card — Papa Legba

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Papa Legba Wrestler Name: Xavier Dubois Date of Birth: Height: 6'4 Weight: 300 Alignment: Selfish Hometown: Montego Bay, Jamaica Entrance Music: "Bring the Pain" by Method Man Manager: Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Ring Appearance Wrestler Look: Papa is 6'4", 300 lbs, he wrestles in black pants with stylized white lightning on the legs. There are purple flames on the ankles and along the waistline. There is a white and purple skull on the left thigh. He wears purple "Kane" gloves, with white studs at the wrists (if you look closely, they're actually skulls too). His upper body is criss-crossed with tribal tattoos and scars from his tenure with "gran'lady", and he wears the white skull pendant around his neck. He NEVER takes it off; sometimes the eyes will glow purple as well. On his head, he wears a purple bandanna, covering short, thick dreads. He also wrestles barefoot, but wears purple ankle braces/athletic tape on both feet. When he enters the ring, the lights go dim, and dark, foreboding drums are heard. The big screen shows a graveyard with fog rolling low along the ground; the screen flashes and a white skull appears with glowing purple eyes. Deep, evil laughter sounds, the lights come up and "Bring the Pain" hits the PA system. Papa strolls out to the entrance way and down the ramp, never taking his eyes off his opponent. Once he reaches the apron, he will climb up, then slingshot/somersault flip himself over the top rope. Psychological Makeup Strengths Papa's Strength is his main asset, and he uses it to the best of his ability. He will get into the ring and brutalize his opponents, until they either quit, or are laid out flat on the mat, giving him the 1-2-3 He is a surprisingly agile individual, and uses it to his advantage, catching opponents off guard. Especially on those rare occasions when he goes to the top rope (a la Kane), the fans in the crowd are constantly stunned by what he is able to do. His intense gaze is an intimidating force as well, in and of itself. He will pysche out his foes by basically scaring the hell out of them! Even though he is not close to being the largest wrestler in the WWO, his sheer physical presence will sometime make him appear larger than he really is. After his time with his grandmother, he fears nothing any of these wrestlers can do. Weaknesses His speed is definitely an issue, but to counter-act this, he will try to corner his opponents so they can't get away from him, allowing him to brutalize them even further. Growing up in rural Jamaica, he had learned to watch his back, but a wrestling ring is a totally different story. While he doesn't trust anyone, he has not yet gained the level of ring awareness needed to remain insusceptible to run-in attacks. Occasionally, his rage will get the best of him, leaving him vulnerable to a foe that can weather his attacks and strike back through gaps in his defense. Biographical Information Xavier was born in a small village of coffee planters in Montego Bay. He worked in the fields early on, growing large and strong. After a while he got too big to work in the coffee bean fields and was sent out to do other manual labor. Going to school was important, but not a priority because of the money needed to support his family. His father was sick and could not work, and his mother worked 2-3 jobs a week just to pay bills. It was left up to Xavier to take care of his 5 younger brothers and sisters, who all called him "Papa" as a family joke. Working as a laborer, Xavier grew to be very strong with a powerfully built upper body. Many villagers thought him a man already at the age of 16. He was working in a ditch-diggingcrew one day, when he noticed one of the ladies on the water-line begin to choke. He looked around for someone to help, but no one would move, they all stared in horrid fascination. Not knowing what else to do, he clumsily tried to perform the Heimlich Maneuver, having just seen it in health class the previous week. However, his immense strength got the better of him, and he inadvertently broke three of her ribs, punturing her lungs and killing her almost instantaneously. He was barely even given a trial, and was thrown in jail that next day. While in jail, his mother scolded him for not thinking of the family first, and about the consequences of his actions. No one seemed to care that he was the only one who tried to help that girl. After his mother left, no one else would visit him. Once he realized that he was truly alone and that his family had deserted him, he vowed never to trust anyone else completely ever again. After about a year in prison, he was able to escape and he ran away to Kingston, to stay with his grandmother. His "gran'lady", was an avid practitioner of voodoo, and began to train him in the inner intricacies of the dark art. She was very blunt about what she was doing and knew all that he had been through. She told him herself to trust no one, not even her. "You can only count on yourself to get things done." She told him, "Use people along the way, but don't you trust nobody, not even your shadow". He stayed with his grandmother for 4 long years, learning whatever she would teach him, and even some things she wouldn't. She gave him a small skull pendant for his 21st birthday, and told him that it was time to leave. He should travel to the states and make a name for himself. Do all the things he couldn't do while picking coffee beans and doing manual labor in the back alleys of Jamaica. He left and never looked back. His time in prison and his years with his grandmother had hardened Xavier to the point where he no longer cared about anyone but himself. He formed the habit of talking to people while staring them directly in the eyes, without flinching or even seeming to blink. This intense, stony gaze served to intimidate more than one person he met throughout his travels. Once he arrived in the states, something inside him told him to travel east. He crossed over Florida and moved ever westward until he reached Biloxi, Mississippi. When the inner pull (which he later realized, came from the skull pendant) stopped, he was standing in front of WWO headquarters, with a duffel bag over one shoulder. Looking up at the sign, he knew that this was the place to start over, where his strength would be an asset, not a liability, where his speed and agility would help him to overpower smaller men...and it would all be LEGAL! He had seen wrestling briefly on TV in Jamaica, and had never really thought of becoming a professional wrestler, but the more he thought about it, the more it made perfect sense to him. Making up his mind, he walked through the front doors, exiting the sweltering heat of the Mississippi outdoors, and entering the cool interior of WWO headquarters. Walking up to the receptionist, he asked one question: "Where's the head man a dis place? I'm tinkin he-a wantin to see me. Who am I?" he chuckles, "I be Papa...Papa Legba, his newest recruit". Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Fair High-Flier Poor Martial Arts None Power Excellent Technical Poor Submission Good Cheating Very Good Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability None Stamina Very Good Ring Awareness Poor Teamwork Fair Top Wrestling Moves "Ragga Bomb" (Buh Buh Dudley's Sitdown Atomic Drop) Stalling Brainbuster 2-handed chokeslam 3rd rope cradle DDT Clinching Cradle Bodyslam Spinebuster (a la Farooq) Turnbuckle Powerbomb Moonsault (better from the 3rd rope, but will try an Asai from the 2nd - might not make it though) Guillotine Legdrop "Roots" (a TKO: fireman's carry to a swinging neckbreaker) this is the setup to the Voodoo Spell from Hell Finishing Maneuver "Voodoo Spell from Hell": After putting the Roots on an opponent, he will set them up on the 3rd rope, climb up after them and perform a super powerbomb from the top rope. Afterwards, he will stand up and lift the skull into the air and roar at the crowd before pinning his foe.
