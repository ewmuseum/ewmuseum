WWO bio card — Major Damage

1 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Major Damage Wrestler Name: Unknown Date of Birth: Height: 6'8 Weight: 320 Alignment: Diabolic Hometown: The Killing Fields Entrance Music: "Battle Hymn of the Republic" by Julia Ward Howe Manager: General Mayhem Managerial Profile Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Coming to the ring, Damage is every inch the nightmare. Hugely built up, and incredibly well-defined muscle bulges out from every part of his body. Deep striations in the muscle and traumatic scars add to his intensely monstrous appearance. In fact, it's hard to find a patch of skin anywhere that doesn't bear a scar of some sort on Damage's body. Of course, his flat black mask never comes off, so it's a bit hard to tell what his face looks like. Covering his back is a tattoo portraying a pair of entwined serpents. Their tails meet at his tailbone, and their heads cross over at the base of his neck. Fangs bared, they're pretty nasty to look at, to be sure. Ring Appearance Wrestling in the ring, Damage wears a pair of long army fatigue pants, and a plain white T-Shirt, cut off at the shoulder. Thick combat boots cover his feet, and have 3-inch lifts in them to exaggerate his height. Covering his head entirely is a flat black mask, without any holes for the eyes or nose. It would be easy for the casual observer to think him blinded by this headgear, but get close enough, and you'll know he sees you. You'll know because he'll grab onto you, and do some DAMAGE~! :) When coming to the ring, Damage wears a black US Military regulation Gas Mask. This is mainly for intimidation. Now, it is possible for Damage to wrestle in the Gas Mask, but, unless he's up against a jobber, this will only go against him. Once in the ring, he will usually remove the mask. Psychological Makeup Major Damage's ringwork centers entirely around his ability to both withstand and inflict tremendous amounts of punishment. In his mind he is weak, in his body he is strong, and this shows through in virtually every aspect of his public persona. Relying upon his manager (commanding officer?), General Mayhem for guidance, Damage seems more a tool than a man. Strengths Physical power Possessed of an intense upperbody strength Damage is quite capable of benchpressing something in the neighborhood of 450-500 pounds. This is usually used only to strike as Damage brings his martial-arts knowledge to bear on an opponent with punches, kicks, elbows, and knees. Stamina Damage has an incredible cardiovascular system and is capable of sustaining long periods of intense physical activity without tiring. Damage recovers from physical harm at a surprising rate. This allows him to go longer and wrestle multiple matches in a night with little weardown. Toughness Beyond having a Marathon runner's stamina Damage's body is toughened by extreme training. He is very difficult to injure weardown etc. This combined with a complete and utter inability to feel pain makes submission impossible on Damage, a knockout difficult, etc. He can be made to pass out, as this has happened before, but not to submit. Weaknesses No regard for personal safety Damage will do anything to win a match. This includes anything from psychotic-level suicidal actions in the ring (with little ability to execute such maneuvers) to purposeful self-mutilation with intent to frighten an opponent. When your opponent slices his palm open, then offers you his hand for a test of strength, are you going to grab that hand and play "mercy", or are you going to back away from the psychopath? It's this sort of thing that Damage counts on when wrestling. It can also cost him, as the loss of blood from a self-inflicted injury can weaken him, and his suicidal tactics can backfire, his huge frame hitting steel and giving his opponent the advantage. Easily distracted Just what it says. Damage can be pulled away from a match while he attempts to attack someone else. This sometimes leads to disqualifications and countouts. With a manager at ringside (Mayhem) to keep him in line, this effect is lessened, but it is still there, and without Mayhem in place, he will be subject to this limitation. Ring Savvy Some of what Damage does in the ring, maneuvers, using the ropes, foreign objects, etc, indicates that he has been in the business for some time now, however, he still hasn't learned to use the ring to his advantage defensively. Escaping a maneuver by grabbing the ropes is not something he would think to do. Additionally, he still does make rookie mistakes, such as going to the top rope when an opponent isn't weak enough to stay down, or stand still, to be struck by his attack. In short, there are a lot of holes in Damage's average gameplan. Biographical Information Damage is, by and large, a mystery. The informational vacuum built up around him is intentional. According to General Mayhem, his identity is "classified" as being "top secret". The trumped-up, obviously false history provided by Mayhem himself is that Damage is a super-soldier drafted into the Army and altered genetically by the Army R&D boys to be used as a weapon of the US Government. This work of fiction may seem ludicrous, but Damage seems to believe it 100%. Now, as sure as a pistol at his hip, Mayhem uses Damage as his own personal weapon, lashing out blindly at those that annoy him. It's a power play, pure and simple. Mayhem seeks glory and power in any venue. In this case, it seems that the WWO has become his playground. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Fair High-Flier None Martial Arts Good Power Excellent Technical Excellent Submission Very Good Cheating Poor Brawling/Hardcore Excellent Escapability None Stamina Excellent Ring Awareness None Teamwork None Top Wrestling Moves Brawling/stiff martial arts blows (forearms, kneeshots, shin kicks, legsweep). Mafia kick. T-Bone suplex. Also known as an Exploder Suplex. Wheelbarrow suplex. Applied to an opponent who is prone on the mat, face down, Damage grabs both legs and heaves the man back with a german suplex. Throatslam Suplex. Also known as a "Uranage". Dragonscrew Legwhip. Crucifix Kneebar. Cross armbreaker\headscissors combination submission. A Stepover toehold facelock (STF). Suicide dive (!!!). Finishing Maneuver The Dead Zone - An elevated Crossface Chickenwing submission. Major Damage first applies a chickenwing, then reaches around, clasping his hands together solidly behind the victim's neck, then LIFTS the man up into the air by that grip. The result is a nigh-inescapable, crippling submission hold that can knock a man out, break his arm or neck, and, in general, finish a match very very quickly.
