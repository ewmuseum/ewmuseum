We're now LIVE from the Arco Arena in Sacramento, California, where they have the top team in the West the last time I watched TV. Upon seeing that, on top of the Patriots winning a Super Bowl, I drove down to Wal-Mart to stock up on supplies in case of armageddon; but it had frozen over. Air raid sirens, explosions, fans cheering for Latex Lex, and all the other usual end-of-the-world signs ensue. Your announcers are Sam Bradley and Rich Manning. Bradley spends the next two minutes bullying around Manning. I seriously hope Manning grows a spine quickly, because if this turns into The Sam Bradley Show, I will quit this job. - A clip from our NEW new Tag Team Vice President, Josh Rodriguez. He's dressed like a teenage slacker, thus not exactly instilling me with confidence. However, he cut a better promo than most of the WRESTLERS this past week. He is reinstating the International People's Title with an eight team tag tournament at Renaissance. A new championship in WWO is major news, of course, that means Bradley must completely ignore it in favor of bullying Manning some more, because he's MUCH more important than a new championship. By the way, I hope that the IP titles get off to a good start, considering how incredibly muffed things got the LAST time it was reinstated. - Cut to Lion Tamer's dressing room.
