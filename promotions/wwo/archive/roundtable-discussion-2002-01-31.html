Roundtable Discussion

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Posted Thursday, January 31, 2002 by Knight

For the second week of the Roundtable Discussion, we talked about the Championships of the WWO. How they are being used and whatnot. A little more control during this discussion by me hopefully make it easier to read. Feedback would be nice at [detail withheld].

I was joined by: Donny McClamrock, our Showcase Reviewer and intern still waiting for his first WWO paycheck; Alan Parkhurst, a Source veteran who occasionally delivers his latest installment of High Notes; WWO newcomer Jeremy Small; and another WWO newcomer and co-host of the WWO's newest show, Spotlight, T.J. Smith.

Hope you enjoy.

<JamesKnight> So...here we go. Welcome to the second Roundtable discussion. It's on the Championships. We'll start with the mother of them all, the World HeavyWeight Title, currently held my Tyrone Hayes. Alan, your thoughts on the reign by Hayes? <AlanParkhurst> It has been uneventful, though part of that has to do with a lack of upper tier faces and lack of upper tier challengers all together. <JamesKnight> Donny, your thoughts on Hayes and the title? <AlanParkhurst> Very difficult to build an interesting title reign when most everyone has been pre-occupied and the undercard is still developing. <AlanParkhurst> Whoops, sorry <JamesKnight> No problem. <DonnyMcClamrock> I'll defer to Alan if he has more... <AlanParkhurst> I'm done. <DonnyMcClamrock> Okay. Well, up and down. I agree with Alan about the lack of upper-tier challengers for Hayes. There's probably only four people in the WWO right now who can give Hayes a run for his money, and two people are in his stable, and the other two are fellow heels, and they are all in their own feuds. The reign itself has been up-and-down, peaking at the Anarchy match. But, I believe, right now, it's at its low point. <DonnyMcClamrock> I don't think the belt should come off Tyrone, but he needs more credible challengers than Latex Lex. <JamesKnight> Jeremy, your thoughts on the whole title picture? All of the titles. (note, I will ask different questions...pm me if you have anything you really need to say about a particular question not directed to you). <JeremySmall> Well, let's see... <JeremySmall> As far as the World Title is concerned, there isn't mcuh more that needs to be said. I will add, however, that Tyrone's credible challengers need not be faces. EVERYONE wants the belt, and there's no honor among thieves. <JeremySmall> Apex's IP reign isn't even one card old, so there's no evaluation possible. I see good things on the horizon for it, however. <JeremySmall> The tag scene was covered pretty well last time, I think, so I won't touch upon it right now <JeremySmall> Personally, the TotA ranks are the ones that interest me the most right now * JeremySmall yields the floor for now <JamesKnight> TJ, you're new to the WWO, but if you had to choose one guy to be a "credible challenger" for Tyrone Hayes, ignoring current in-place feuds, who would that be? <TJSmith> Well, first off as a guy who is new to WWO, let me say it's great to see a guy like Hayes at the top. I have a great deal of respect for him, predating my time here in WWO. <TJSmith> As for contenders, unfortunately nobody really seems to be built up anywhere near his level. <TJSmith> I can't really even single out one guy from the mix. * TJSmith yields <JamesKnight> Alright...Jeremy touched on it, so let's go further into the subject. The Title of Americas. Alan, your thoughts? <AlanParkhurst> I would honestly say that where is the interest in the Title of the Americas? Other than the stryker feud, how often is it mentioned? <JamesKnight> Alex Extreme and Legba, I think, are having a good start to a feud. Let's see if anyone else agrees. Donny? <AlanParkhurst> I think that Fydor may be the next champion, but in actuality, there hasn't been much developement except for the odd match over it, and while Legba has done a good job with it, I cannot help but think that if Steve Dumars had stuck around, he it may have more shine to it to start. <JamesKnight> That's true. <DonnyMcClamrock> The TOTA title gets mentioned about as often as the World Title. <DonnyMcClamrock> My interest is in the feuds involved at that level. <DonnyMcClamrock> You've got Hopper/Stryker, which may turn into something. Extreme/Leg ba could get hot quick. Ace of Hearts vs. Cole is a chance for both of them to show their stuff against someone about their level. <DonnyMcClamrock> And I do like Fydor a lot. <DonnyMcClamrock> Not sure where his angle is yet, but he's got the talent, certainly. <DonnyMcClamrock> Oh, and Marley/Tamer, I still think that can be the feud of the cycle. * DonnyMcClamrock yields. <JamesKnight> What's everyone's feel on Apex's title reign? Starting with TJ. <TJSmith> I'm interested to see what Apex can do with it. He's not exactly a brand name elsewhere, but he is a guy who is quickly catching my eye. <TJSmith> It's always interesting to see which guys make the conversion from 'chaser' to champion, and I'm interested to see Apex as a champion and how he reacts. <TJSmith> I do think that if Apex can emerge as a legitamate threat, he can make a good challenge for guys looking to move to that world title level. <JamesKnight> Alan, think Apex can further the title along or do you think it's doubtful the title has seen its last title change before the PPV? * TJSmith yields <AlanParkhurst> Well, that's a two prong question/ <JamesKnight> I'm good at that. <AlanParkhurst> Apex has never had a long run with a title belt in the WWO, his only reign being a short TV title reign to get the belt off of a selfish Mike Sebastian during the dark days of Stone's drug binge booking in 1999. <AlanParkhurst> So I think this will be his major chance to prove he's more than just an imaginary person talking to bump taking machine. <AlanParkhurst> Of course who will challenge him if he does hold on to the title? I'm seeing a distinct lack of true middle card talent. <AlanParkhurst> This is much like a repeat of late 99, where there had been no building the IP title into a middle guard title. <AlanParkhurst> Some of the blame does fall on Stinger, who's involvement in building up major damage did severly tie up the IP title. <AlanParkhurst> But blame should squarely fall upon the shoulders of a good chunk of the roster, for not make a concentrated run at Stringer. <AlanParkhurst> And that's my opinion on those matters. <JamesKnight> I see my next roundtable discussion...the middle card...but Jeremy, you like Apex's chances at keeping the title and elevating himself to where he was pre-Inquisition? <JeremySmall> I do. He's over with the fans, he was in what, two of the nominees for match of the year -- including the winner -- and more importantly, he's got the talent to keep the chase interesting for anyone who wants to try to step up. <JeremySmall> Apex is god at making guys look good. Having him as IP champ is a nice way to bring some TotA-level guys to the midcard. <JeremySmall> (er... good, not god) <JamesKnight> Either way works for me. <JeremySmall> Heh... true. <JamesKnight> Donny, I know you're itching to get on the IP Discussion...thoughts on Apex, Stringer, and whatever else might be on your mind? <DonnyMcClamrock> Well, first, I'd have to agree with everyone else and say I'd be _shocked_ if the title came off Apex before the PPV. <DonnyMcClamrock> And, I think the scene developing around the IP title bears the most watching for clues as to who is going to make the jump up to the next level. Oddly, the losers of the 3-way feud that seems to be developing between Stringer, Detson and Apex may end up moving up, while whoever has the IP title will probably get another pretty decent run with it, barring disaster. <DonnyMcClamrock> Oh, and I'd like to disagree with Alan about one thing here. It wasn't Stringer's preoccupation with Damage that killed the IP heat. That build up was done before anyone knew the IP title was going to exist again. The Anarchy cycle, and Stringer's preoccupation with the War Games match, was what killed the IP heat. But I think this 3-way feud, among three of the finer talents in the WWO today can get it back. <AlanParkhurst> I mentioned the build up with Damage in a prior article. <AlanParkhurst> I suggest you re=read that one, long before the war games match. <JamesKnight> Okay, don't wait for me to give you the floor. <DonnyMcClamrock> Yeah, but by the time there was an IP title, Damage was already Craven. <DonnyMcClamrock> The cruiserweight title was a dismal flop, and the Damage buildup probably hurt that, but the CW division was flawed from the get-go. <JamesKnight> One last question...How's the future of the title scene look. This is to anyone. <JeremySmall> Most people are expecting a Tag Title change at the PPV. And I've heard rumors of more surprises in the Tag division, too. <TJSmith> Well, from my point of view, where there appears to be a lack of buildup for major title contenders in the next couple of months, I think the recent influx of talent bodes well for the long term health of WWO. <TJSmith> I also think having a second show will give more time for more people to shine. More time for angles and feuds to play out. <JeremySmall> Yes, that will be a big help <DonnyMcClamrock> The tag team scene will be interesting, especially with rumors that contract negotiations with the Vegas Connection are going especially rough running rampant. <DonnyMcClamrock> The World Title scene... I see Tyrone continuing to get kind of a "Flair" run. Beating back the upper mid-carders until one of the young lions finally breaks out of the pack. <DonnyMcClamrock> Hopefully, that'll happen sooner than later. <DonnyMcClamrock> And the TOTA... that title scene could get messy quick. But with all the good new talent in the WWO, it should be entertaining. <AlanParkhurst> I see a huge upset actually. <AlanParkhurst> Tyrone losing the World title for some reason, and building people up in the middle ranks.

Yeah, ended abruptly, but with a good thought on Tyrone Hayes. Anyway, thanks to Alan, Donny, T.J., and Jeremy (sounds like a bad boy band) for their involvement in the discussion.
