If you guys like this better, it will stay. Feedback is appreciated.) -Newcomer Matt Cole rides to the arena on his hog. He'd rather buy a ticket than sit with Mark Stone. I agree, the glare off of Stone's bald head makes following the card pretty tough. -62 dancing girls and 14 columns of pyro herald the enterance of the triumphant Tyrone and Todd. He runs down, well, all of his potential challengers. It's the guys he DOESN'T mention that I'm worried about. Crowd has pretty much fully turned against him again, BTW. I guess I should mention that I was there in person, it being a mere four-hour drive down beautiful I-65 from my house. -We're LIVE from Indianoplace, Indiana, home of teams which have never won a damn thing. Your hosts are Sam Bradley and Mickey Ralph. Shill-o-rama, and then... -"20 Bux" Nate Knowitall vs. American Eagle: Actually Eagle came out first. Crowd reaction: 85% silence, 15% "What the fuck is this old fart doing back?". Nate, on the other hand, draws pretty damn good heel heat. Nate ballshots him to take control and uses his typical martial arts crap, but misses a flying splash and Eagle takes control, slapping on a figure four that works about as well as you'd expect when the leg hasn't been worked on AT ALL. Yep, the Macarena is so out of style it's not even funny, the PS One is a dinosaur, the White House is Republican and Congress is Democratic, the Bulls are the worst team in the NBA and the economy is going down the shitter, but American Eagle is still a fucking idiot, it's nice to see that some things really DO never change. Nate quickly reestablishes himself, hitting the Bloody Whip for 2 4/5 just to be an ass, then hits an overhead belly-to-belly for the win. Nate draws almost Tyrone- level heel heat, getting garbage fired at him. Okay, I guess I should apologize for ranking Tucker ahead of him on my up-and-comers list. But he's still not good enough to carry Eagle to anything watchable. Rating: * Eagle was brought out of mothballs only in a failed attempt to lure back relative Rusty Bowman, and I have it on pretty good authority that the front office has noticed the massive suck heat Eagle drew during this match and has already had him taken out back and shot, or something to that effect. -Apex and Dark Soul sell the Grief Brothers, unfortunately it's only Charlie and John Collins disguised as the Griefs (I think you can guess which is the flamer). Even funnier than the Lachance sale. -Uncivil Disobedience vs. Damage Inc.: It's just SAD to see UCD come to the ring with no heat whatsoever. I mean, god, it was one thing to see the likes of Andros and Studd have their political power plays they pulled come back to bite them in the ass later in their careers as they disgracefully jobbed to everyone in the solar system, but this is UCD, who worked for and earned everything they got and never did anything to fuck anyone over...and yet it's happening to them too. Oh yeah, that other team in this match didn't draw heat for shit either. Sam invents the term "Eagle-ized" to describe has-beens, as Sanders and Rax start off and stay in for a long time, killing the crowd and the announcers so badly that Sam actually pulls "YOU'RE GAY!" out of mothballs. Firm gets tagged in, and double-clothesline knockout spot follows. Nyargh. I began leading a "boring" chant at this point, but I noticed on my tape that the fine fellows in the TV truck just cranked up the ring mic to block it out. Firm is up first, but HORRIBLY telegraphs an elbowdrop, and there's a tag to Harris, and Damage Inc. hits the cool legdrop-senton combo. Firm stops selling shortly afterward and hits a powerbomb for two, but gets caught in a Boston crab before tagging Rax back in, and he and Harris trade highspots and near-falls in a cool segment before Harris Northern Lights suplexes him to the floor, before hitting a plancha. Tag to Sanders, Pink Slip follows, but only gets 2 5/6 before Firm saves. After a pier-six, Harris takes out Firm and Rax gets Pink Slipped again to end it. Firm.. Rax...retire. Please. Soon. Or at least become managers or go to singles or do SOMETHING. Just don't disgrace yourselves any further. Damage Inc. has improved all the way to mediocre. Rating: ** -"Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton vs. "Millionaire Marvel" Eric Stryker: And the parade of JTTS continues. I feel sorry for Stryker and Knowitall, being low on the card with this tripe. I should point out that at this point, the crowd is as dead as it's been at any WWO show I've been to since the January '98 "Andros Revenge Tour". Stryker dominates early on, using the forearm~! and power moves before Papa Legba comes out. Colton fights back with a dropkick and a DDT, but gets caught on a flying bulldog attempt and tossed all the way to Cincinnati..well, okay, maybe just out to the ring floor. Colton takes a shitkicking on the outside, getting press- slammed on the steps, before being tossed back in the ring and Powerstryked to finish it. Legba doesn't attack. Rating: * Colton is off to a better start to his WWO tenure than Eagle, but not by much. -Mackey Jay catches up with Ultimate Thrasher backstage, and gets shoved down on his ass for his trouble. -Chris Stringer comes out with his entourage...Cherry, drool, my life is complete. He gets on the mic, and cue the "asshole" chant. He says that he doesn't have time to defend all three belts he won at Inquisition, so he awards Cherry the TV belt...well, hey, she's a more credible titleholder than anyone who had the belt after Latex Lex. (Take that, you Quesada-holics). He then awards Glen Peeps the Cruiser belt..and says he'll be defending it tonight. That was gold, especially the expression on Peeps' face when he found out he'd be wrestling tonight. -Pointless trios match: Stephen Morrolan, Michael Priest, and Ultimate Thrasher (w/Sweet Melissa) vs. Lion Tamer (w/Tiger Lily), Steve Dumars, and Midnight Assassin. Guess who drew heat here and who didn't. Dumars and Assassin make Morrolan their bitch, but Priest comes back against Midnight and Tamer won't tag in. Tag to Thrasher, who destroys Assassin and hits a superplex for two, but Dumars saves. Tag back to Morrolan, but MA reverses a hiptoss and tags Dumars in. Dumars almost finishes Morrolan off, but Tamer tags himself in and wastes time arguing with Dumars, letting Thrasher tag back in and hit Tamer from behind. Thrasher destroys him from pillar to post, and Tamer beautifully oversells everything, but ballshots Priest to take control when Priest is tagged in. Dumars won't let him tag out, but Assassin takes it, and although I didn't know it at the time, one of the seven signs of the apocalypse occurs as Mick uses the term "soupbone right" in reference to someone OTHER than Dave Grief (though he STILL doesn't explain what makes it different from a normal right hand). Sunset flip from Assassin gets 2 1/2, with help from Tamer, cueing a brawl between Tamer and Morrolan, and MA hits a moonsault for 2 1/2, breaking only when Morrolan goes for a legdrop and hits his own partner. Dumars pulls Tamer out of the ring, and Thrasher just stands there as Midnight hits a tornado DDT on Priest for the win, but gets racked by Thrasher immediately afterwards for some reason. Dumars chases Tamer back to the dressing room, and Morrolan and Priest pretty much just leave with their tails between their legs. My name is Michael, and jobbing is MY anti-drug! Match was acceptable, though nothing earth-shattering. Rating: **1/2 -I must point out the absolutely hilarious exchange that follows, as Mick points out how pissed Thrasher seems and Tyrone should probably watch out for him, when Sam fires back with "Nobodies that dumb to get his tail kicked by Tyrone twice in two shows... unless, we’re talking about the dumbest guy on earth. Which of course we are." -Cruiserweight title "match": Glen Peeps (w/Chris Stringer and Luke Morningstar) vs. someone: Stringer sits down to do color commentary, and Glen's opponent turns out to be....Niklos Romani, with Ravnos as well. Ravnos joins us for commentary as well, and this match supposedly came about just as basically a sparring session before the Romanis' match with Fire & Ice. Predictably, Niklos destroys Peeps with his usual cool highspots and Glen sells everything like he's being hit by a tank. Niklos could easily get the pin at about 324 different points in the match, but eventually they just decide the carnage has gone on long enough, and Morningstar drags Peeps out of the ring to give Niklos a countout win. Ooooooookay. Rating: * -Vegas Connection (w/Rimshot) vs. Final Justice (w/Valerie Dougan- Barney...drool): Wow, a match where nobody sucks! We still have those?? Barney and Leary trade dropkicks to start, then FJ cleans house and Hall controls with power stuff, but Lowbrow pulls Barney out of the ring and pummels him on the floor. Both VC guys take turns on Barney, but Lowbrow hits on Valerie, letting Barney make the comeback with a crossbody for two. Hot tag to Hall, double clothesline follows. Superplex-frogsplash combo follows, and Leary makes the save almost too late. Hall hits The Rage for two, but Lowbrow cheats to turn the tide. Desperate double-arm DDT leaves both guys down, but both finally tag out. Barney dominates Leary and a double Hotshot leaves Lowbrow dead, but Barney tries to pin the wrong guy and walks into Leary with the Golden Mic, and that's that. Leary gets on the mic afterwards and actually uses the term "blade" to put them down. Good, if cliched, match. Rating: *** -Thrasher and Melissa run into "20 Quid" Nate Knowitall, and Thrasher doesn't accept his challenge....instead he invites Nate to join him in a new stable, the Atlantic Connection. Nate accepts...and Thrasher turns on him milliseconds later, leaving him for dead. Confusing for those of you who don't get the Union Jack parody, but hilarious for those of us that do. -Main event: Dark Soul & "Smooth as Silk" Miguel Quesada (w/Candy Malone and John Collins) vs. William Craven & Chris Stringer (w/ Cherry Bomb, Glen Peeps, Luke Morningstar, and Dr. Victor Schwartz). Hey, what's this?? Oh yeah, it's heat. Lots of it. For everyone. You know, trying to decide exactly who is hotter among Candy and Cherry is like trying to figure out whether Molson is better than Labatt's: You'll never figure it out, but it's sure pretty damned fun. Vic gets on the mic beforehand and warns everyone about what's to come next, and Crisper orders both teams to just have one escort at ringside. Candy stays around for her team (yay) and Vic does for OSE (boo). Slugfest to start as the guys pair off into their Inquisition matchups, Craven clotheslines Dark out of the ring and smashes him into the rail, while Miguel hits a stinger splash on Stringer and tosses him out of the ring. Craven tosses Dark back in to officially start the match, and Dark eats turnbuckle a couple of times, then two WICKED chops and a spinning heel kick. A choke follows, and Dark comes back with a flying forearm, but Craven barely even moves. Jawbreaker and Russian legsweep follow, though, and Miguel gets tagged in, hitting a flying crossbody that finally puts Craven down. Miguel plays to the crowd, but turns around right into a Craven headbutt, then Stringer gets the tag and both OSE guys pound on him in the corner, but Miguel comes back with a baseball slide kick and a low legdrop. Ali punches follow, and Miguel showboats like crazy. He's still getting awesome face heat, Cristine or no Cristine. Tag to Dark, who hits a backdrop but misses a clothesline and takes a good bump over the top rope to the floor. Craven just KILLS Dark on the outside, press-slamming him right on the ring apron while Miguel doesn't even care. Back in the ring, Craven tags back in and a savate kick gets 2 1/2. Double shoulderbreaker from Craven, then a wheelbarrow suplex follows. Katahajime follows, but Quesada makes the save. Tag back to Stringer, but he eats foot on a corner charge, and Dark then hits a second- rope DDT leaving both guys down. Stringer tags out, but Miguel is busy hitting on a chick in the front row and Dark can't tag out. Craven goes for a Uranage, but Dark reverses to a Soul Crusher in desperation...but is too exhausted to make a tag. Candy has enough and slaps Miguel, then winds up in a catfight with whoever the chick is, to a big pop. Dark DDTs Craven and again looks for a tag, but finally sees Miguel at ringside, and starts yelling at whoever Miguel's girlfriend is..and Miguel nails him. OH MY GOD, QUESADA IS TURNING HEEL??? THAT JUST CAME OUT OF NOWHERE!!! They brawl on the outside, and Stringer just laughs his ass off.. but OSE gets ambushed by Thrasher, guitar in hand. Thrasher pounds on Craven, but Stringer nails him with the IP belt and he gets double-teamed until Cole vaults over the railing and attacks Stringer to make the save. Whew, three almost-sure-to-be-hot feuds set up in one match. Almost makes up for the rest of the card mostly sucking donkey balls. Rating: ***1/2 All these guys work better in singles than in tags. OVERALL: Yeah, the main event was good, but otherwise.....peeeeeeeeeyew. The crowd was dead for better than half of the show, and we have no idea what the next big main event feud was going to be. The "Tyrone saves Ilya" segment from Inqusition was damaging, as it dropped Tyrone below Stringer on the heel heat totem pole even before he won the World title, and will end up having long-reaching side effects if he winds up feuding with Holy Roller. We still have no credible face tag teams (the Romanis are edging that way, but making gentleman's agreements with Stringer won't help) and "psycho Thrasher" is something that's been done before and doesn't work. On the other hand, a long-needed purge of the lower card is finally in order, with several people who should have been fired long ago and one who should have never been brought back finally getting ye olde pink slip, and hopefully some new blood (Cole) is on the way to make things better soon. Legba-Stryker will be a good feud for the Americas belt and Dark-Quesada could be the final lift to superstardom that both guys need. But the WWO can't keep putting on lackluster shows like these week after week and not expect to see the momentum of a great show like Inquisiton fritter away. The Quesadas and Stringer can't save the show EVERY week....can they?
