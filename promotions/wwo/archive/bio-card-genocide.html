WWO bio card — GENOCIDE

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GENOCIDE True Name:Unknown Date of Birth: Height: 6' Weight: 217 Alignment: Aberrant Hometown: The Deserts of the Middle East Entrance Music: "Faceless Man" by Creed Manager: N/A Physical Appearance Standard Appearance Genocide is 6 feet tall and of light build. He is well-tanned and well-muscled. His face is never seen because of the mask he wears (see clothing). His eyes can be seen, however - and they are shining, black, and cruel. His hair extends a little below his shoulders and is, surprisingly enough given where he says he is from, blond. Ring Appearance Genocide wears loose-fitting white trousers. Sometimes he wears shirts of Middle Eastern style (think Indiana Jones), but usually that is not the case. He wears black, well-fitted wrestling boots. The most prominent feature is his black mask, which extends across and covers everything on his face below his nose. Psychological Makeup Raised in a harsh culture of "he who is stronger is better", Genocide actually possesses a remarkable intelligence. However, his childhood was a shattered mess, and hi world views came out twisted in the end. He still thinks of himself as a protector, but at the same time he is a man prone to violence. He recognizes it, have no doubt, but he lives with it, tacking it down to his nature. When not fighting, he is a man given to depression, and this is where his intelligence comes out. He sees everything around him as a symbol, and the combination of all these traits makes him a very bizarre individual. Strengths Intelligent wrestling. Genocide does not make great strategies or plans OUT of the ring... but IN the ring, he is a natural fighter. He is built for fighting - he doesn't KNOW what to do - he just DOES it. Thus, his reaction time is virtually non-existent. Aura. When you step in the ring against him, you are afraid. It's not a "I'm pansy and am afraid of him" type feeling - it's just a natural worry for one's own safety. Genocide is quick; he is brutal. His whole out-of-ring savageness translates into tremendous in-ring psychology, understanding perfectly how to make even the most insignificant match seem like a PPV headliner. Unpredictability. Perhaps the most important strength, Genocide's unpredictability - his sheer DIFFERENCE, a sense of being totally ALIEN and without emotion at times - makes it hard for others to say what he is and isn't going to do. Weaknesses Xenophobia. Well, perhaps not QUITE that, but Genocide is in a different land with different customs, and while he is a natural adaptor, he still isn't used to everything that comes with the US and pro-wrestling and pop culture. Add in an inherent distrust (NOT hatred) of those who are different (which is everyone) and you have a weakness. Lack of planning. Unlike a lot of other wrestlers, Genocide doesn't come in with a plan... he does what is natural to him, and against people who have (good )plans, this hurts him. Brutality/focus. Sometimes, he gets so caught up in his own bloodlust and is concentrating on one thing or person so much, Genocide leaves himself wide open for attacks. Biographical Information There is very little known about Genocide, mainly because he doesn't talk about it directly. He lets out little hints every now and then in his in-rings and flashes, but it can be gathered that he was born somewhere in the deserts of the Middle East as the son of a nomad womanand a rich merchant who used her as a concubine. He lived with the merchant, but he was shunned as he grew older for his strange, almost foreign, blond hair. His father was a man who had many enemies. Two of these were his older stepbrothers, though he didn't know of this. In a series of events, the brothers assassinated their father and framed Genocide who fled to the desert in his early teen years. There he was joined up with nomads who had known his late mother and grew up learning the hard ways of the desert. He eventually became the protector of the tribe, but before the age of 20, he left and travelled around the world to hone his already-impressive fighting skills, including an extended stay in Japan, China, India, and various African nations. He went back to his tribe, only to find a large amount dead from a rare contagious disease, and the rest a group of squabbling complainers. Disgusted, he left for America, where he joined a pro-wrestling indy federation as a first job. He comes to the WWO at the request of an indy booker. Wrestling Information Wrestling Styles/Grades Daredevil Excellent High-Flier Excellent Martial Arts Good Power None Technical None Submission Good Cheating Fair Brawling/Hardcore Good Escapability Very Good Stamina Fair Ring Awareness Fair Teamwork Poor Top Wrestling Moves Sandtwister - a trademark reversal. Genocide catches an opponent either in the air off a rope rebound and inverts him in the air, bringing him down in a side-suplex piledriver. Desert Storm - a swinging tornado DDT. Rain of Blood - a high-arch senton bomb into a rolling inside cradle (the last part is optional). Scimitar's Edge - Genocide grab's his opponent in a bulldog-start position, jumps so that his feet bounce off the top rope, flips in the air while still holding the head and delivers a DDT after the 180. Exile - from the start of an X-Factor position (holding the head in outstretched arms), Genocide slides THROUGH the opponents legs, hitting the person's head on the mat with vicious impact in the process. DDT (especially on chair or other object). Moonsault. Spear. Spinning heel kick combination (usually 3 kicks) followed by a dropkick with each foot targetting one knee of the opponent. Crucifix powerbomb. Finishing Maneuver The Xenocide: This is his primary finisher, intended for people less than 300 pounds. It is a tilt-a-whirl piledriver. For those more than 300 pounds, he uses the Desert Storm.
