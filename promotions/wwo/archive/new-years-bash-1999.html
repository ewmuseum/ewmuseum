New Year's Bash (excerpt)

1 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. New Year's Bash ran five parts to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy from the Boston Garden; only the title-deciding segment and the closing battle royal finish below are reproduced here in full. The undercard and the earlier rounds of the battle royal remain held and are not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] WWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP [The challengers, the Ultimates, take on the reigning champions, Uncivil Disobedience, with a biker gang's interference turning the match:] Ralph: Finally, we're getting the ringside area cleared, and Contessa with a few words for Christie, and look at the smile on the biker's face.
Bradley: Yeah, leatherface, enjoy it while you can. Can we say `lawsuit'? Harassment. Assault. And anything else I can think of to get your ass toted to jail! Then you can kiss anyone you wish!
[… the brawl continues around the ring as the challengers regroup …] Ralph: Justice down to canvas!! Justice was setting up for the splash but Duncan just pushed the man off!!
Bradley: Hit him again!! Hit him again! Ralph: Gesner and Davis battling on one corner of the floor! The masked man and Christie at the other end! Duncan is up to the top rope! Firm back around... TWIST OF FATE!! Duncan just nailed Firm with the Twist of Fate!!
Bradley: No!! No!! Get in there Gesner!! Get in there!! Ralph: He's busy on the floor with the Centurions!! ONE!!! TWO!! THREE!!! They got it!! The Ultimates score the win!!! What a major win by the Ultimates!! Duncan and Rock bailing out of the ring!! And there goes Gesner in!!
Calhoun: Ladies and gentlmen, your winners of the match, and NEW WORLD WRESTLING ORGANIZATION TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, "SUPERBEAST" MARK ROCK, "TURN OF FATE" MARSHALL DUNCAN, THE ULTIMATES!!! BATTLE ROYAL [The closing sixteen-man battle royal narrows to its final three: the reigning World Champion Fury, seated at ringside as a guest rather than a competitor, the gypsy wrestler Wuzho, and Night's Harbinger.] Ralph: Harbinger off the ropes... Kick to the midsection!!! And Wuzho setting up!! Wuzho: ARIMA!!!! Bradley: I'll be back!!! Ralph: Bradley's leaving the table again. I'm not sure what to make of this. Omen or prophecy of trouble?? And Wuzho has Harbinger up!! Quell the fire coming up....Wuzho going to the ropes!!! And down goes Harbinger!!! Harbinger dropped over the top rope and down to the floor!!! GOOD LORD!!!
[A possessed glint comes into Fury's eye.] Fury: The time has come...Wuzho will be no more... Ralph: Fury, where are you going? Fury get back here!! Calhoun: NIGHT'S HARBINGER has been elminated, and our survivior of this year's New Year's Bash, ladies and gentlemen, WUHZO!!!
[The champion folds up his chair, and walks toward the ring. The crowd begins to hush, as President Stone also makes his way up into the ring.]
Ralph: Fury up to his feet, Fury. This isn't why we had you down here! And Stone in the ring, waving some sorts of papers at Wuzho. But the gypsy not even paying the President any attention! And now Wuzho daring Fury to get in the ring. (c) World Wrestling Organization Productions, 1999 WWO - Simply the Best
