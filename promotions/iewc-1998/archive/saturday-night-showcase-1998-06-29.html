Brushing his jet-black hair out of his face, Van Strife yanks his arm away from a fan's outreached hand. And arriving in the ring, Setzer holds the title belt overhead before allowing the referee to take it.] BJ: We're set to go, Tressa stepping out of the ring. MJ: And mm, mm, mm, look at her go... BJ: Are you even going to pretend to call the match? MJ: Hey, I don't think she's wearing any... BJ: MATT! [Van Strife and Carey locked up as the bell rang, the standard collar-and-elbow tie up. Each man turned a bit, trying to gain an advantage. Carey slipped to the side, putting Setzer into a side headlock, but the Champ wasted no time in twisting Carey's arm into a wristlock, then manuevering up to a front facelock. A knee lift from Setzer, another, and still holding Carey around the neck, Van Strife twisted into a swinging neckbreaker.] MJ: You know, that's what I need. A valet. So I can dress her up in tight outfits like that. BJ: The match, Matt, the match... MJ: Match? [Carey was pulled up and led to the corner, where Van Strife laced several chops across the chest, the loud slaps echoing across the arena, bringing "whoos" from the crowd with each one. Grabbing him by the shoulder, Setzer whipped the challenger across the ring and into the opposite corner, following him in with a clothesline, moving aside as the momentarily stunned Carey fell to the mat. Setzer quickly covered him.] REF: ONE! KICKOUT! BJ: Setzer Van Strife going for the early pinfall, but Carey is not going down that easily. MJ: ...and look at her hair, man. I wonder where she gets it done... BJ: I give up. [Carey was again pulled up, but this time he was ready, slipping around into a cross-face, using his other arm to complete the hold with a chicken wing. Setzer wasted no time in going to the ropes, the referee ordering the break without hesitation, Carey letting go on a late three. Setzer got up and to the corner for a moment, but Carey stayed on him with a shoulderblock to the midsection. Van Strife was whipped across the ring into the turnbuckles, and as he stumbled back out, Carey rushed him with a shoulderblock that knocked the Champion on his back. Carey hooked the leg.] REF: ONE! T -- KICKOUT! [Van Strife tried to roll away, but Carey stayed on him, crossing his ankles and working up his opponent's prone body, until he had the STF locked into place. The referee immediately asked the Champion for a submission, but got none. Checking the hold to make sure nothing was illegal, he again asked Van Strife if he wished to surrender, but through gritted teeth, the "NO!" was clearly heard. Carey released the hold, changing to another tactic by lifting Setzer up around the waist and dropping him with a side slam. A snap suplex followed, and on impact, Carey slid over for the cover.] REF: ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! MJ: You're not going to get rid of the National Champion that easy... BJ: Oh, you ARE watching the match? [Both men back up, Setzer was led to the corner. Carey climbed the ropes, hooking his prey for a top rope DDT, but Van Strife came alive, wrapping both arms around Carey and turning right into a super spinebuster! As Carey tried to catch his breath, the Champion stepped out of the ring, only to springboard to the middle of the top rope, and moonsault onto the Hardcore one. The crowd gave a shocked reaction, and Setzer gave a lateral press.] REF: ONE! TWO! TH -- KICKOUT! BJ: What a move! MJ: The biggest man in the air is without a doubt "2 Smooth," and that's why he's got that Championship. [Carey and Setzer worked back to their feet near the ropes, and Setzer used a right hand punch to try to weaken his challenger, but Carey fought back. Rights and lefts flew, with Carey gaining the advantage. Setzer drew back and swung with a big haymaker punch, which Carey ducked under. He quickly grabbed the Champ by the throat, and surprised everyone by lifting him up for a chokeslam, dropping him out of the ring onto the arena floor!] BJ: CHOKESLAM! MJ: I don't think I've seen someone do THAT before! BJ: He just chokeslammed him right out of the ring! [Van Strife winced from the pain, arching his back, while Carey slipped between the ropes, and coming near. An eager fan near the guardrail held up some thin cardboard that read "Use My Sign!" So Carey grabbed it and smacked Van Strife, to no effect. Carey realized there was nothing IN the sign, it was just a sign. Scowling at the fan, Carey threw it aside as Setzer stood up in front of him.] Tressa: Catch! [A folding chair was tossed to Carey, but when he caught it, Setzer surprised him by dropkicking Carey right through the chair! The loud smack made the crowd surprised, but not as surprised as Carey, who was rolled back in the ring. Setzer chose another route into the ring, climbing the turnbuckles from the outside, posing for a moment before flipping backwards into a shooting star press, landing suqare on Carey!] REF: ONE! TWO! THRE -- SHOULDER UP! [Van Strife wasted no time in following up, delivering a crisp Russian legsweep. Both men were quick to get back to his feet, Setzer stopping to yell at Tressa who was up on the ring apron. Carey tried to approach, but got a rake in the eyes for his troubles. Staggering away but being pulled back from behind, Carey was on the receiving end of an atomic drop. He stumbled back against the ropes, where Setzer whipped him across the ring, raising his boot up for a Mafia kick into the jaw of the rebouding Carey.] MJ: Setzer going to work on Carey.. BJ: Tressa's up on the apron, the referee quick to order her down. MJ: Whoa! You can see her butt! BJ: Will you quit, you sound like Butthead! MJ: There's no need for namecalling! [Setzer went back to the match, dragging Carey to the corner, kicking him while he was down, then pulling him to a standing position. Two more lethal chops across the chest, and Van Strife whipped him across the ring into the other corner, running and leaping for a flying splash. The crowd reacted to the move, and Setzer tried to repeat, but Carey reversed the whip into the turnbuckles, rushing in for his own splash, but Van Strife backdropped him right out of the ring!] MJ: THAT'S your National Champion! BJ: Great timing by Setzer Van Strife, and Carey is very slow to get up! MJ: You can see him waiting for just the right moment - he's got something in mind, we're about to see a big move from the Champ. BJ: Carey on his feet, here comes Van Strife - Suicide Dive, NO! Carey dodged out of the way, and Setzer Van Strife just nailed that guardrail! Oh my God! [Carey shook his head, trying to clear the cobwebs, and taking advantage of the fallen champ. After several sharp kicks, he pulled up Van Strife, slamming his head into the rail, then whipping him down to another guardrail, nearly sending Setzer into the first row! The referee's count stopped when Tressa got up on the apron to distract him, and Carey took advantage of it by lifting up Van Strife, turning him around, and laying him out with a tombstone piledriver on the arena floor!] Crowd: "He's Hardcore! He's Hardcore!" [Carey went under the ring and pulled out a table as the fans went wild. Then he pulled out another table, setting it up alongside the other, but having some trouble with one of the table legs unfolding. Meanwhile, Setzer pulled himself up on the ring apron, realizing Carey hadn't noticed him, and ascending the turnbuckles to the top. Carey turned to find Setzer, just in time to see the National Champion come off the top with a corkscrew plancha, crashing Carey right through the nearest table, the crowd instantly on it's feet!] MJ: NO WAY! BJ: The hardcore style of Carey coming back to haunt him, and we see the airborne style of Van Strife that is his trademark. MJ: Now that I think about it, both of these guys are NUTS! BJ: Speaking of nuts, here's "Heartbreaker" Steve Wallace, and he's got a ladder! MJ: Don't tell me he's going to interfere in this match! BJ: He's setting up the ladder by the entrance, well away from the action. [Tressa screamed, running to try to help her man, but the referee had climbed out of the ring, trying to keep her at a distance. Meanwhile, after a long moment where neither man moved, Setzer slowly climbed out of the wreckage, Carey gingerly picking himself out of the lumber. Both men staggered around, both feeling the effects of the match. Van Strife finally managed to work his way over to Carey, grabbing him by the hair, only to get a back kick low blow, dropping Setzer to his knees.] Crowd: ooooooooh! [Wallace cheered from the ladder at that, laughing. But when Carey turned around, Setzer slammed his forearm into Carey's crotch!] Crowd: OOOOOOH! MJ: Speaking of nuts... BJ: Don't you EVEN go there! [Tressa finally got around the referee, pulling out her cellular phone, and smacking Setzer in the back of the head with it. Van Strife winced, already in pain from earlier shots to the skull. Carey, still moving around carefully, landed an overhand right on Setzer, then another, and another. He pulled Setzer up by his hair, slamming his forehead into the table, then lifting him up to slam him onto the remaining table.] BJ: Carey pulling out all the stops. MJ: These idiot fans are starting their hardcore chants again. Carey's getting up on the table? BJ: What's this going to be? Both men on top of the table. Carey pulls up Setzer - oh no, not a piledriver! RIGHT through the table! MJ: Both these guys are going to be picking splinters out of their bodies for a week! [The referee was eagerly encouraging both men back in the ring, all the while Tressa arguing with him to keep his attention, and prevent a possible countout. Carey dragged Van Strife through the shattered tables and rolled him back in the ring, climbing the turnbuckles from the outside. Without wasting time, Carey leaped from the top, dropping his leg right across the upper chest of Van Strife. The referee quickly slid back in the ring, just in time to make the count.] REF: ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! SHOULDER UP IN TIME! BJ: "Hardcore" Carey just a hair away from being the National Champion. MJ: I don't know what Wallace thinks of that, he's still up on top of that ladder. BJ: "Heartbreaker" Steve Wallace scheduled for a shot at the National title at Summer Bash on July 26th, surely he has an interest in who wins this match! Carey back to his feet, with a Fisherman's suplex on Setzer Van Strife! He's got it hooked! REF: ONE! TWO! THRE -- KICKOUT! [The frustration of the near-pinfalls evident, Carey pulled up Van Strife, lifting him by the waist and placing Setzer's head between his legs. Rage on his face, "Hardcore" Carey yanked the Champion up, raising him overhead, and driving him to the mat with a brutal power bomb! Shaking his head in approval, Carey picked up Setzer Van Strife again, this time hooking him for a Death Drop, laying out the National Champion!] BJ: This could be it, I think Van Strife may be out cold!
