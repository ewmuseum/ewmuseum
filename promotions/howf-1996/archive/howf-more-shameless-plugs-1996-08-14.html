HOWF More Shameless Plugs

14 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene shows Jerry Jenkins and Thomas "The Beer" Stein chatting at the HOWF control Desk] Jerry Jenkins: Hello everyone and welcome to another shameless plug for the HOWF. Thomas Stein: Will these things ever end? And will I ever get paid again? JJ: Serious questions Thomas, and they deserve some serious answers, but you're not gonna find them here. TS: Surprise, Surprise. JJ: Anyway, instead of us promoting the HOWF, I think we'll interview some former HOWF members and have them tell you what they think about the HOWF. (Scene flips to Jerry Jenkins and Thomas Stein at Disney World with Rey Misterio Jr.) JJ: So Rey, what did you think of the HOWF? RM: I tell you Jerry, that place has more non-stop exciting action then I've ever seen anywhere. All the moves you see me doing now in WCW and AAA, I learned those in the HOWF. TS: Is it true that the mask gives you super powers in bed. RM: Yes, but even this mask couldn't take me to the top of the HOWF, that's why I had to leave and join the WCW, the competition in the HOWF was just too tough. (Scene flips to show Jerry Jenkins with Bret Hart in the Canadian Wilderness) JJ: So Bret, what are your thoughts about the HOWF? BH: What is that some sort of hockey league or something? JJ: Uh, the HOWF, you know, High Octane Wrestling Federation, you were once a member (pokes Bret pointedly on the shoulder). BH: Oh yeah, the HOWF, good league, (Bret grins) I think I held every HOWF title at once at one point during my career didn't I Jerry? JJ: Ummm, yeah, I guess you did, uhh, any other comments. BH: Yeah, the HOWF is the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be. (Scene flips again to show Thomas Stein in a locker room with Ric Flair) RF: So who are you again? And how did you get in my locker room anyway? TS: Hey, wanna swig from my beer stein? RF: Sorry, I have a match coming up. TS: Oh don't worry, its a secret family recipe of Stein Herbs, its strictly medicinal. RF: Please go away before I call security. TS: You guys have had to do that a lot lately huh? (Scene flips once again to show Jerry Jenkins interviewing Jushin Liger) JJ: So tell us about your time with the HOWF? JL: HOWF Good. (Scene flips one last time, back to Jerry Jenkins and Thomas Stein) JJ: Well, there you have it folks, what more could you ask for in a league. TS: Please join, if you don't, I may never get paid. JJ: Yes, I think we all need to do our part to help support Thomas, anyway, for more info on joining the HOWF, please contact [name withheld] at [detail withheld] , you won't regret it. [name withheld] President - HOWF Striker Shirak - LAW Dala Khan - CWL
