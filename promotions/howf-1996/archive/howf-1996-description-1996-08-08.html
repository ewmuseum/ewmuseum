High Octane Wrestling Federation — Founding

"HOWF Description and Looking for new members"

8 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The HOWF still has openings in its inaugural 16-man, double elimination tournament to be held to determine the HOWF world champ. E-mail me at [detail withheld] if interested. A description of the league follows. HOWF Description and Rules The High Octane Wrestling Federation, or HOWF, is an e-wrestling league, if you don't know what this is, you might stop on by the usenet newsgroup rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, where the e-wrestling FAQ is posted quite frequently. The HOWF is a simulation based league, using the TNM version 6.2 Wrestling Simulator, created by [name withheld]. The league will use future versions of the Simulator as they become available. TNM allows for good wrestler pesonalization as well as a wide variety of gimmick and non-gimmick matches. Before I get going, I'd like to give you some words of wisdom from our Monday card writer, the man behind Loudmouth Donnie, [name withheld] "Like all e-mail wrestling leagues, The HOWF is supposed to be entertaining, you know fun? If you take stuff like this seriously then you are weird and strange and we don't want to know you. If you honestly find someone's flash offensive, mail the prez at [detail withheld] and say so, it will be given honest consideration." So above all else, we want you to have fun, and if anything interferes with that, we will do our best to remedy the situation, as soon as it is brought to our attention. You will only be allowed to have one wrestler. While the HOWF is a simulator based league, flashing is also very important, to put it bluntly, if you don't flash, you probably won't win very often. Those of you familiar with TNM realize that each wrestler recieves a push, from 1-100, which greatly affects his chances of winning. I will start each new wrestler with a push of 50. The push will fluctuate from week to week depending on flash activity. Basically, if you write fun and interesting flashes, with some degree of regularity (this does not mean you need to flash every day, people that do usually run out of things to say and start getting boring and repetitive, not always, but often this is the case), your push will increase. If you miss a week for vacation or exams or whatever reason, your push will suffer, but if you've proven to be a good flasher in the past, it won't affect you too much. For instance, if your push is at 85, and you don't flash for a week, its not going to drop down to a 50, it will drop to an 80 or so. If your push gets down to zero (hopfully this will not happen to anyone), which means no flashes for several weeks, you will be given an official warning, and if you do not flash in the next week, you will be removed from the league due to inactivity. I don't wish to be mean, but the point of e-wrestling is to get involved, if you don't say anything, no one cares about your wrestler, and you miss out on the best part of e-wrestling, the interaction between the wrestlers. The absolute maximum number of wrestlers I will carry at one time is 32 (the perfect number for single elimination tournaments :) Flashes are to be sent out to everyone in the league, myself included. For the first few weeks, I will ask everyone to send their flashes to me, and I will send them to the league, this is to make sure everyone gets them, and to make sure I see them. I will notify everyone when it is ok to send out your own flashes. Flashes should not contain other wrestlers without the wrestlers consent. If I see people attacking each other physically in flashes, I will ask that people disregard the flash and will warn the person not to do so again. Swearing will be allowed, but try to keep it reasonable, I've seen people swear every other word, and it just ends up being a horrible flash, and will probably get your push dropped instead of upped. I will be running 3 cards a week. I will be doing a Thursday Card, Loudmouth Donnie will also be running a card Monday, and [name withheld] will be running a Saturday card, as well as a weekly wrap-up show on Tuesdays. There will be one PPV every month. Some of the PPV's will be styled after existing PPV's such as the Royal Rumble, Lethal Lottery, Survivor Series, King of the Ring, Spin the Wheel - Make the Deal, and regular old super cards. I will also be running a special Alliance Cup Tournament every year, more on this later. If you wish to send in stuff for the cards, you may do so, this includes interviews, sneak attacks, etc. Send these to me or Donnie or Jon (depending on who's running the card), they must be in by the morning before the card. That is, for the Thursday cards, anything you want to do on this card must be sent to me by Wednesday before noon, eastern standard time.
Web Page: I will be creating a web page when I return to school, look for it to appear in early to mid September. It will hold all past cards, as well as current rankings, upcoming card schedules, and wrestler profiles.
Alliances: Alliances are allowed, but every member of your alliance must inform me of thier membership, and each Alliance must have a captain, who will be in charge of collating attacks and such. Tag Teams are the same as alliances, both members of the team must inform me that they are forming a team. I will then send out the tag team creation form, which will basically concern the finisher and team name.
Belts: There will be 3 singles belts, HOWF World Title, HOWF National Title, and HOWF Harcore title (will only be defended in gimmick matches). There will be one tag belt, the HOWF World Tag Team titles. The HOWF World Title will be defended at all PPV's, unless special circumstances prevent it. It will also be defended at least once a month on a regular card. The HOWF National title will be defended every week. It will be defended against a random opponent at least once a month. The Hardcore belt will have no schedule, and will be defended whenever the holder wants to defend it, or whenever President Jagermeister decides he wants it defended. The holder of the belt will always be allowed to determine the stipulations on the match, except on PPVs, where it is up to the President. The Tag Belts will be defended with the same schedule as the World Belt.
