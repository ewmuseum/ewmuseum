E-Fed Survey..........

2 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What makes an e-fed "good"? Well, here are the criteria I look for when judging. (NOTE: These aren't in order of importance.) 1.) Roster. This makes or breaks a fed, IMO. I look for original wrestlers like "Arkansas Razorback" and not twelve dozen "Stone-Cold Whatevers". Also, I don't like to see too many 7 foot monsters. Just not realistic. 2.) Rules. Again, with emphasis, RULES! There are a few major rules I like: 1. Cussing. It doesn't belong in e-feds. In real life...hell yeah. But, to quote "if it wouldn't pass on Saturday Night TV"... 2. No real wrestlers. Now, if a fed is made where you can use real wrestlers, but you must RP them well...fine. But otherwise, I'm all for original wrestlers. 3. No harassing other handlers. This is just plain immature. Leave the trash talk to roleplaying. It's just not fun when you have to put up with some jerk who keeps telling you how much you suck and the fed head won't do anything about it. 4. As to whether it's simmed or booked...I don't care. But if it's let people choose their own TNM moves, and not have to pick a "style." 3.) A good homepage. Nothing shows off a fed-head's desire to work hard than a well designed website. 4.) Last, but most definately not least, the roleplayers themselves. Who wants to get into a fed where no one RPs? Not I. Laterz, Phil WIWF Homepage - http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/5254
