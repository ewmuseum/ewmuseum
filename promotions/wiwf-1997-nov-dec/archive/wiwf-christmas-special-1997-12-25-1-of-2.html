(He flashes her an insincere smile.) TM: I'm not buying any of this crap. The people want to see what's going on! (The guard sighs, and snaps his fingers. Something doesn't check out here. Guys? Can you still hear me? Yes...I'm getting reports that those men ARE indeed FBI agents. This is something big. Hold on a sec... (In the distance, we can see several guards dressed similarly to the one who pushed Tana around rushing from the building pushing gurneys, upon which are bodies, into an unmarked van. He climbs into the back, and the van speeds away.) TM: ...isn't that? Tsutomo Kamiari, Antiseen's manager? (Screen goes to 'snow' as the signal is cut.) **************************** END CLIP ************************** TM: Yes, it seems that Sabbath has lost his life, another casualty in a strange year for the world in general. The wrestling world has lost a talented athelete, and he will indeed be missed. GO: I may not have liked him...but a man doesn't deserve to die. I don't know how Kamiari was involved...but I do hope that this mess is settled soon. RC: At least he and Sayaka are with God now, whereever their material bodies are.
