IPW is looking for members!

May 9, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

INDIANAPOLIS PRO WRESTLING I. What is IPW? II. How are matches decided? III. IPW rules IV. IPW shows and personalities V. Titles VII. Application ==================== WHAT IS IPW? Hi. My name is [name withheld]. Indianapolis Pro Wrestling is my attempt at an e-wrestling federation that mimics the feel of small independent wrestling groups like Ohio Valley Wrestling or All Pro Wrestling. All cards will be run out of Indianapolis, IN and the immediately surrounding area in small venues like high schools, flea markets and small baseball and racing facilities.
IPW is a booked fed, meaning the winners and results of all matches are decided by me, the owner. Everything will be decided based on promos, strategies and what would be best for angle development.
The basic style of IPW is similar to that used by many regional promotions from the 50s through the early 90s before the WWF and WCW killed everything else off. All angles and feuds will begin and end in the ring. The most important angles in IPW will be those for titles (which I'll get to in a minute), revenging losses or attacks and to just find out who's the best man.
No weddings, divorce announcements or book promotions will take place on IPW shows. There also won't be many broken tables, cages or other props used to take the place of good wrestling. As a small group, IPW cannot afford them, save for rare occasions.
==================== HOW ARE MATCHES DECIDED? I believe that a very important part of roleplaying is not just talking like your character, but acting like them too. Therefore, Indianapolis
Professional Wrestling will be somewhat unique in that strategies and promos share equal billing as far as importance to success. After all, there are wrestlers in real life that are great with the microphone but crap in the ring and vice versa.
Every IPW member will be expected to write roleplays regularly. There isn't a set schedule for how many or how long they must be. As one of my professors says in reference to essasys, "they should be like a woman's skirt.
Long enough to covr the material, but short enough to keep interest." If you have the time to write four 500 word roleplays a day, fine, but if they don't really say anything, they'll hurt you, not help you.
Strategies are important because they help me write matches. They can be anything you like, but should give me a good idea what out of the ordinary moves you might do, how you'll defend yourself from a manager, etc. You don't have to analyze counters for your opponent's entire moveset, but something like
"I will use punches alot" won't do. Not roleplaying will hurt your chances of winning, but there is no possible way to win if a strat isn't turned in. It's like an athlete that doesn't watch tape or practice. There's no way he can do very well.
Angles will also be an important part of how things work. I will contact members to start angles I think might be interesting for the fed, but also strongly encourage members to contact each other to set things up. Deciding on how things will happen in a series of matches between your wrestlers makes my job easier and ultimately leads to both of you coming out ahead.
All roleplays and strategies are to be emailed to me at [detail withheld] Strategies are due three days before the scheduled card. Roleplays will be distributed in bunches of 3-4 as I get them. If you cannot roleplay for a few weeks, tell me in advance and I will simply announce you on a tour of Japan and stop scheduling matches for you until you can return.
=================== IPW RULES 1. The deadline for any roleplays or strategies for a specific card are three days before it is scheduled to be released. If for some reason you'll be a day late or you know you won't have time this week, email me and I'll make sure nothing too horrible happens to you.
2. No racism in promos. You will be removed from the fed on your first offense, no questions asked. 3. The worst words you may use in promos are ass, damn and crap. In keeping with IPW's family image, the usual four and five letters are unacceptable. The first couple of times it happens I will merely mention during the show that your wrestler got fined half their share of the purse. After that it will start affecting your wins/losses. The occasional F-bomb dropping as part of a heated angle will be acceptable if we've talked about it.
4. Throwing your opponent over the top rope is illegal. IPW can't afford mats, so this is for the safety of its wrestlers. If you can come up with a plausable way to distract the referee in you strategy, more power to you.
5. Piledrivers are illegal. This dangerous maneuver is banned in 7 states, and IPW will not allow its usage. This is also just to go along with the regional feel; see #4 to get around it.
==================== IPW SHOWS AND PERSONALITIES IPW will run one show per week. It bears the simple title of Indianapolis Pro Wrestling because it isn't a T.V. show. Instead it is sold on the group's fictional website. Realistically, no small indy can start out with television coverage. However, bigger shows will run on local cable access approximatley every two months.
[name withheld]: That's me. In reality and in fantasy Robbins is IPW's owner, booker and commisioner. He will also serve as IPW play-by-play announcer.
Robbins is a fan of good wrestling, and that's why he started IPW. SIBLEY DE FANTASTICO: This masked man serves as color commentator at all IPW tapings. He hates both the faces and the heels and in fact wrestling itself, but enjoys seeing people get beat up.
STEVE ROBBINS: This veteran of many sports has played and umpired baseball and coached both soccer and basketball, always with spectacular results. Now, in need of money to pay for his son's college education, he adds pro wrestling referee to his many titles. He old, slow and doesn't know the rules that well.
In other words all he's really going to do is slowly count pins and DQ you the moment you touch him.
==================== TITLES Due to its small roster, IPW only has two titles. The holders of these belts are the undisputed best wrestlers in IPW and will always find themselves in main events.
