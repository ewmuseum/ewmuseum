W4F — Roleplay

Raizzor from the PWA, and into the W4F

22 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Scene: in a dark, forboding lair, a man crouches in the shadows, just out of the reach of the light, and he speaks........** Raizzor: For over 3 months, I have fought and clawed my way to the top of the PWA.. in less then 2 months, I have claimed 3 titles.. and by Sunday.. I should think a fourth.. the PWA world title. I am a man who stands by my word. and destroys any that should cross my path.. My strenghts lie in my ability to wrestle, fly.. brawl.....my power comes from the images of the
Phenom himself.. my belief in the destiny to survive and win. My life is in turmoil with the death of my Little Sister, Banshee.. to see my dearest
friends gone because of evil that plagues the night. I am here to beseach to those of you who believe you are the best.. to come to the W4F.. a new world.. to make it THE battle ground of your souls.... and also, it is time
I stepped into the light here in rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy and to make a name for myself......
**Raizzor steps into the light, he is 6'8.. about 260lbs, he is built, yet slim.. long black hair flows down his back..his eyes glower with a dark, yet almost benevolent intent.. a red scar tattoo sits above his red eye.. he wears all black, with blood red claw marks across his chest...he wears a trenchcoat (ex: Edge and Undertaker combined).**
Raizzor: There is this thing called the top 200. I know nothing about this, but it interests me.. to no end.. so, I will do what I must to become part of that elite group.. but i dare any of you to step into my arena.. to step into my world..
**Raizzor smiles** Raizzor: I dont' have an ego like many of those out there.. I have only history to back me up...
I have the record for quickest pin in the PWA.. I have the record of winning the tag-team titles from the ICONS all by myself.. I am a former Hardcore and Internet champion.. I won the Internet title in a 14 man over the top battle royal, where the last four must go into a tourny the same night.. So,
14 men, 2 pins later.. I am the champ.. this sunday, at who's the man, I will be there to take the gold and finally claim my spot as the Franchise of the PWA..
Not only that, by winning, I will have taken over the PWA .. and made it my own! So, this is a challenge.. not a request.. come to either or both of the following links.. and show me.. show the PWA and the W4F what your made of..
and maybe you will meet The Sharp One in the ring.. and learn the pain.. of the Guillotine Leg Drop.. or the Tombstone Shoulder Breaker.
see you in hell http://sunstar.dhis.org/index.html W4F http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Corner/5313/index.html PWA
