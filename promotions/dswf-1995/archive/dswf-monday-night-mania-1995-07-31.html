D.S.W.F. — Show Script

"DSWF Monday Night Mania"

31 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gavin:Welcome to the D.S.W.F. Monday Night Mania, tonight we have a great card for you, first off we have three of the first round
tournament matches, the first one is Diesel-vs-Steve Austin, the winner of this match will face the winner of Hart/Flair, the second match is Vader-vs-Yokozuna, the winner of this match will face the winner of the Underatker/Scorpio match, and the final match will be Shawn Michaels-vs-Steven Regal, the winner will face the winner of Luger/Raven match, & we also have a sppecial interview with Bret Hart. Rob:What a night of action we will see the great Ric Flair come down to ringside with Steve Austin, so let's get down to ringside.
Ring Annoucer:This match is for one fall, making his way from
Hollywood, CA. weighing 241 lbs. & accompanied to the ring by the one and only "Nature Boy" Ric Flair, Stunning Steve Austin, and his opponent from Las Veags, NV. weighing 356 lbs., "Big Daddy
Cool" Diesel.
----------------------------------------------------------------- This match was a great, Austin hit Diesel with a piledriver and numerous other moves, but the end came when Flair jumped on the ring apron and Diesel caught him, Austin came charging at Diesel, but Diesel moved out of the way making Austin hit Flair, this stunned Austin and when he turned around Diesel hit him with the
Jackknife, 1-2-3.
[Part 2 of this show is missing from the surviving corpus.]
Ring Annoucer:Here is your winner by way n pinfall, Yokozuna. Gavin:Who was that masked man? Rob:Who knows but the whole world saw big Yokozuna defeat that sorry excuse for a wrestler Vader, I guess Vader will leave now & now Yokozuna will have to face the winner of The Undertaker/2
Cold Scorpio match
Gavin:I don't think so, I think Vader is going to destroy whoever that masked man was, anyway I have to go up to ringside to conduct my interview with Bret Hart.
----------------------------------------------------------------- Gavin:Bret I have to ask you a question what do you think your chances are of beating the legendary Ric Flair?
Bret:We'll you know, Flair is a great wrestler, but so am I, all
I'm going to do is take it right to him and the best man will win, and I think I am the better wrestler then Flair, it's time for him to retire.
Gavin:What about the Austin/Flair connection, I mean you saw it tonight Flair came to ringside with Austin, what happens if
Austin comes to ringside with Flair?
Bret:Well you saw who won tonight, so I think there little plan backfired, but if he shows up with Flair I'll deal with it then.
Gavin:Final question, I know you and Diesel are good friends but what will happen if you beat Flair and have to face Diesel?
Bret:Well, both me and Diesel know that wrestling is our job and
we both want that title so we will most likely put our friendship behind us during the match, but after the match we'll end up staying friends.
----------------------------------------------------------------- Rob:What an idiot, wanting to stay friends with Diesel if they meet each other, what a dummy.
Gavin:Leave him alone, see he has friends you don't, so you wouldn't understand that. Rob:Look here comes the ring announcer again, so let's go down to ringside. Ring Annoucer:This match is for one fall, first making his way from England wighing 219 lbs., "Lord" Steven Regal and his opponent from San Antonio, TX. weighing 234 lbs., "The Heartbreak
Kid" Shawn Michaels.
----------------------------------------------------------------- This was a great match, Shawn was in control the whole match, he hit Regal with a forearm smash while he was coming out of the corner, then he nailed him with a elbow drop from the top rope, and finally he nailed him with a little chin music, 1-2-3.
----------------------------------------------------------------- Ring Annoucer:Here is your winner by way of pinfall, "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels.
Gavin:What a match and now Shawn will have to face the winner of the Luger/Raven match. Rob:Yes but wait to you hear next weeks matches, the first one is 911-vs-Sandman, second Bret Hart-vs-Ric Flair, and the third one Luger-vs-Raven, and there also will be an interview with Vader. Gavin:Don't forget fans to catch Saturday Night Brawling where you will see the Sabu-vs-Sid match plus a Diesel/Bret Hart-vs-Austin/Flair match. And Sunday Afternoon Bodyslam where you will see The Undertaker-vs-2 Cold Scorpio and you will also see Lex Luger face The Sandman, and 911, Bret Hart, and Raven will also be in action. So until next time bye everyone.
