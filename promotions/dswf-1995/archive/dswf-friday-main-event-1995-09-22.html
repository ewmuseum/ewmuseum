D.S.W.F. — Show Script

"DSWF Friday Night's Main Event"

22 September 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DSWF Friday Night's Main Event -------------------------------------------------------------------- Darrell>Welcome fans, we have a great show planned for you. Phil>Yeah, that's right! My boy Sabu will be here defending that I-C strap against 'Macho Man' Randy Savage, but my main man Raven is making his way to the ring.
Darrell>Great. Let's head down to the ring for our first match. =========================================================== Razor Ramon vs. Raven ========================================================= Razor starts the match off hitting a couple of go behinds and hair pulls, but the match really gets going when Raven walks up and just slaps the
<beep> out of Ramon, this causes Ramon to go off on on Raven hitting him with a fall away slam, throwing him into the ropes and delivering a chokeslam on him, Raven was out, he then picked Raven up and set him on the top turnbuckle, Ramon climbed up to the 2nd turnbuckle and was fixing to deliever a top rope backdrop when Arn Anderson jumped in the ring and
DDTed Ramon, the ref couldn't really do nothing because of the No DQ rule put in all the matches, Raven finally got his sense back and fell off the top turnbuckle, meanwhile inside the ring Anderson delivers a spine buster on Ramon, he then goes outside and graps a chair, folds it up and throws it in the ring, he picked Ramon up and DDTed him on the chair, at this time Raven was just getting back in the ring, Anderson took off, Raven went to cover Ramon, 1...2.. and all of a sudden the 123 Kid races in picks up Raven throws him in the ropes and delivers a spinning savat kick on him, puts Ramon on top of the dazed Raven and the ref counts 1...2...3
========================================================== Darrell>What a match, my broadcast partner is going down to the ring to get ready for his interview with the 'Macho Man' Randy Savage, so let's hand it to Phil.
=========================================================== Phil>My guest at this time will be in the main event at War Games Sunday
Night and will be facing my friend and I-C Champ tonight Sabu, please welcome the 'Macho Man' Randy Savage. Macho Man do you and your friends think you have a chance Sunday Night against the New Dungeon of Doom?
Randy>What do you think? We have Bret Hart, Shawn Michaels, Sting, and the big man Diesel, so to answer your question, yes, I do think we have a chance.
Phil>Those are some great wrestlers but they are nothing compared to the violent Dungeon of Doom, Kevin Sullivan, The Sandman, Vader, Cactus Jack,
& a man you will face tonight, the I-C Champ Sabu, who is Bret going to replace you with when Sabu gets through smashing you through tables?
Randy>See that's not a problem, Sabu's not going to do that, let me tell you how the match is going to go, the Macho Man climbs the top turnbuckle and flies off with the El....
Phil>Yeah Yeah whatever, we've got to go to commercial, thanks for your time Savage. ========================================================== Phil>What a jerk. Darrell>I thought he answered you questions honestly. Phil>Where the lights go? Darrell>Oh, it must be time for our next match, so let's get to the ring. ========================================================== The Undertaker vs. Kevin Sullivan ========================================================== The match didn't last long Kevin Sullivan came to ringside with Scott
Norton of all people, Undertaker made short work of this, he threw
Sullivan into the ropes and delivered a flying clothesline, picked the stunned Sullivan up and gave him a giant Chokeslam, and then he finished him off with the Tombstone piledriver. After the match Norton got up on the ring apperon and got in the Undertaker's face, Norton did nothing so the Undertaker turned away. Norton snuck in the ring he turned Undertaker around and delivered a powerbomb on him. All of a sudden 911 stepped over the ropes and chokeslamed Norton, by this time Undertaker rose up, Norton cut a trail leaving 911, Sullivan, & Undertaker in the ring, Sullivan rolled out of the ring. Undertaker & 911 had a stare down, Undertaker extened his hand & 911 shook it.
========================================================== Phil>Let's head to your War Games report with Darrell. ========================================================== Darrell>Hi folks! Welcome to your last War Games report for the the Main
Event, let's run down the matches.
1.The big War Games Survior Series match World Champ Bret Hart, US Champ Shawn Michaels, Randy Savage, Sting &
Diesel vs. Kevin Sullivan, I-C Champ Sabu, The Sandman, Vader, & Cactus Jack 2.DSWF TV Champ Shane Douglas vs. Tommy Dreamer in a texas death match
3.DSWF Tag Champs The Public Enemy vs, The Gangstas in a submission match
4.The Loser Leaves the DSWF match:Razor Ramon vs. Arn Anderson 5.Road Warrior Hawk vs. Harlem Heat's Booker T:If Harlem Heat's Booker T loses this match he and Stevie Ray must break up as a tag team.
6.The Undertaker vs. Ric Flair 7.123 Kid vs. Raven in a 2 out of 3 falls match 8.911 vs. Scott Norton 9.Alex Wright vs. Diamond Dallas Page ========================================================== Phil>Here comes my boy Sabu, folks you do not won't to miss one minute of this match, let's get down there.
========================================================== DSWF Intercontinental Champ Sabu vs. 'Macho Man' Randy Savage ========================================================== The match was good Sabu started it off with a slingshot kick to the head, followed by a trip to the top rope where he hit Savage with a Moonsault,
Sabu rolls out to the floor gets a chair and throws it in the ring, he throws Savage into a corner, he sets the chair up for sitting posistion, goes to the other corner of the ring and runs and jumps off the chair delivering a leapkick on Savage, he then sits Savage on the top turnbuckle and hits him with a top rope frankensteiner. After all of this Sabu didn't cover him, Sabu rolled outside and pulled a table from under the ring and pushed it inside the ring, set it up and laid Savage on top of it. Sabu climped to the top and was fixing to deliver the moonsault when he saw the
Undertaker at the entry way this distracted Sabu & allowed Savage to get his senses together,he rolled off the table and pushed Sabu's foot off the turnbuckle, he busted his crotch on the top turnbuckle, Savage then picks up Sabu and throws him off the turnbuckle, hit's him with a chair, thus causing Sabu to bleed, he laid Sabu on the table and climbed to the top turnbuckle and set up for the Elbow Smash, he flies through the air, but
Sabu rolls off the table before Savage hits him, Savage goes through the table elbow first Sabu rolls over and pins him, 1...2...3. After the match the paramedics took a look at Savage's arm and took him out of the arena on a stretcher.
========================================================== Phil>You see what you get when you mess with Sabu. Now he hurt his arm, poor baby. Ha! Ha! Ha!
Darrell>It's not funny, he could be permently injured. Phil>Now who can Bret Hart find to replace Savage? Everyone has a match for War Games. Darrell>Who knows? Folks tune in tomorrow when you will see Sting vs. The
Sandman, a special interview with Bret Hart, Diesel vs. Arn Anderson,
Tommy Drreamer vs. Cactus Jack, & The 123 Kid vs. Scott Norton. But now let's head down to our final match of the evening.
========================================================== 911 vs. 'Nature Boy' Ric Flair ========================================================== The match was pretty much lame to be honest, Flair did his walk arounds and struts. The fun came when 911 got Flair up in the Chokeslam posistion and was about to send him down when Norton ran in the ring and took out
911's leg with a chair, then Flair started to work on him to when all of a sudden the light's went out, and the Undertaker came down to ringside and the lights came back up, meanwhile Flair locks the Figure 4 onto 911, with his leg in so much pain he submitted, meanwhile outside the ring
Undertaker had just Chokeslamed Norton on the concrete. He then went after
Flair who broke the hold and cut a trail. Undertaker starts to leave the ring when 911 grabs the mike and says, "Hey, Undertaker! I saved you once tonight and you saved me once in return, I got a deal for you, me and you versus Flair & Norton this Sunday night. How bout it?" Undertaker walks over to the mike and says, "If they accept I will to." Then he walks away.
911 takes the mike back, "OK boys you want us come and get us accept the challenge by tomorrow night, if you have the guts Nature Boy & Norton" He drops the mike and limps out of the ring.
========================================================== Darrell>What a great night of action, right Phil. Phil>You bet two people hurt in one night, first Savage's elbow and now it looks like 911's leg, oh I love it.
Darrell>We hope you enjoyed the first internet broadcast, be sure to read tomorrow and Monday night. And be sure to read Fall Brawl, avaliable
Sunday Night. Bye for now.
