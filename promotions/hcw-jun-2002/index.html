Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HCW introduced itself on 7 June 2002 as “the newest G-Fed,” built on the shared Geeksoft “gfed” generator engine rather than a site of its own — the one recruitment post announcing it is its whole surviving record.

That post lists five regular championships and one seasonal championship, a weekly television event, monthly pay-per-views, and a roleplay requirement of at least once and no more than three times a week.

No roster, no card, and no closing notice survive; which of its five titles were ever contested, or whether HCW ran a single show on the Geeksoft engine, is not something its own surviving record answers.
