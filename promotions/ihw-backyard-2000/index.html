Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Insane Hardcore Wrestling billed itself as a backyard federation, staging its matches in the parks and backyards of Ohio rather than any arena. A newsgroup fan was already praising the concept days before the promotion's first card reached the public, treating it as a going creative enterprise with its own recipe of hardcore action, humor and deliberate immaturity.

The debut card, Backyard Brawl, opened a four-way Toledo City Title tournament across four three-way matches, its own hype billing every entrant a contender for a title that, by its own admission, did not exist yet outside the tournament itself. A second card, its own title a taunt aimed at a named rival, followed two and a half weeks later, advancing the same tournament through a ten-man qualifier, a semifinal, and a headline showdown between two of the promotion's own rising names.

A community directory later listed a federation under this name as still running in 2002, describing weekly broadcasts and a monthly pay-per-view under a dedicated staff on its own website, though nothing from that later run survives in this record and the site itself no longer resolves.

No further card or result from the promotion's own founding run survives past its second show, and how its Toledo City Title tournament was settled isn't recorded here.
