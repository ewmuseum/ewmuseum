Insane Hardcore Wrestling is here, and it's ready to take over the backyards and parks of Ohio like they've never been taken over before! This card,
Backyard Brawl!, is the first of many that will establish the IHW as THE craziest, most insane, most BRUTAL backyard federation in Ohio.
What can you expect on the first card? Well, how about FOUR matches? And how about ALL FOUR are THREE-WAY DANCES?! AND HOW ABOUT THEY'RE ALL FIRST ROUND
MATCHES IN THE IHW TOLEDO CITY TITLE?!?! That's right, the IHW Toledo City
Title Tournament will begin at Backyard Brawl!, and it's going to be more than just a brawl... IT'S GONNA BE A MASSACRE! Here's how the card shapes up as of now...
------------------- "The CWB" Mike Murray vs. "Tigerheart" Jason Irvine vs. The Orange Demon Three young men looking to make their mark on pro-wrestling step into the squared backyard in what is sure to be one of the best "technical" matches in
IHW history. "The CWB" is looking to bring a little philosophy into the IHW, and show that he's ready to "rule the world". "Tigerheart" Jason Irvine is searching for his hero, and in the meantime, using the IHW as a launching pad into the "sport" of pro-wrestling. And The Orange Demon... is orange. And formerly a beaver. All three of these athletes will try to take the others out in their pursuit of IHW greatness.
-------------- Jack "Off" James vs. "The Hardcore Sperm" Bubba "Cums" Lately vs. Retard Bob
This match will be messy, to say the least. First you have James, who jerks off in school bathrooms whenever he gets a chance to "get away". But he is a member of the infamous James Clan, so he can't be underestimated. Then you have "The Hardcore Sperm". Enough said. Finally, we got Retard Bob, the drooling, stumbling, silly little retard. Good lord. Who booked this shit?
Ah well, it looks like these three men will cum, drool, and who knows what else, their way to get into next round.
------------------ "Bitchwhipped" Bobby Hall vs. Negro Mutha Fucka vs. Blue, The Fat, Angry Dwarf
Two VERY angry men and one VERY angry woman make up this trio of terror... and the woman's not even in the match! Bobby Hall is pussy-whipped to the extreme, but who can blame him? Lisa is a FINE piece of ass. And if he doesn't win, he's not gonna get any. Wonder if she'll sleep with me if he loses?
Hummana... oh yeah, baby... come sit on papa's big, fat... AHEM. Then we got
Negro Mutha Fucka, one angry black dude. He doesn't like "whitey", and since
Hall and Blue are both white, they're targets number one and two. And finally, we have the oddest competitor in the IHW, Blue, The Fat, Angry Dwarf. Blue hates anyone that's not in his "clique", and loves his Twinkies. He also loves his computer. But there will be NO computer in this match, and that could be his downfall, as he must attempt to throw his girth around to win this epic encounter. Will this one be big? Hell, Blue being in the match GUARENTEES it.
-------------------- THE MAIN EVENT - Joebacca vs. "Stone Cold" Jase Russell vs. "The Taco Queen" Jess Flutebanger The big one. The whole enchilada. The big, steaming pile of shit. This will be the match that makes or breaks the IHW. Or... not. But regardless, SOMEONE is going to advance to the next round, as these three have quite a storied history. Joebacca is one hairy mofo. He's best friends with Jase. Jess wants his cock. Joebacca fears Jess. Jase... just wants to Stunner anything and everything. Who will walk out the winner? Will Jess get to bang a "flute"?
Will Joebacca eat some balls? Will Jase Stunner himself? YOU MUST GET THE TAPE TO FIND OUT! IHW: Backyard Brawl! COMING TO A VCR NEAR YOU, SOON!
