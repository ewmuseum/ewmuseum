Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Network Wrestling Alliance introduced itself 3 April 1997 as a deliberate blend: “NWA is going to be pretty much like the old NWA but not 100% like it… a mix between the typical type wrestling federations like WWF/WCW and mix it with a ECW type league.”

It billed itself a booked league, run through handler angles and handler roleplaying, and named itself a successor in spirit to two earlier federations, WIWF and NCWF, hoping to match their own standing in their prime.

It set expectations plainly for anyone joining: “only the TOP people who get involved will get far in NWA and those who dont are dismissed very quickly.”

No card, roster, or title of the Network Wrestling Alliance's own survives beyond this single founding post. Whether it ever matched the earlier federations it named as its models isn't in the surviving record.
