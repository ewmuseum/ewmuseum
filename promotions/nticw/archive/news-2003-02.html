News, February 2003

February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, February 19, 2003 Hedges signed Another former resurrection member has signed with NTICW... The 'all knowing - supreme being' Hedges has been confirmed to be competing amongst many others in the return of NTICW. Long time veteran of the sport and one the down right, most disturbed athletes in the profession. Many link and rumor Hedges to be very close to the CEO of NTICW and the motives of this man are still left unknown to why he has joined with NTICW. This man is returning to the ring after a long vacation, but if this man is half the contender of what he used to be then he will take this federation for everything it has. Another sick individual that will triumph through this federation. It will be very interesting for some and terrifying for others to find out exactly why this man is showing face and competing again. We will have to await and find out the first returning aired promotion of NTICW. Sunday, February 16, 2003 Large NTICW Update... The contracts have been flying around NTICW like they have been going out of style the last few days and we have a big update for you today. First off there has been a contract signed by the name of a young superstar named BLITZ. Finally making his chance into professional wrestling with fury and vengeance, the 'Hardcore Raver' will finally have the chance to show his stuff. Crossett seemed very confident about signing this new superstar to his roster. Going from one extreme to another... NTICW has signed long time veteran to the sport by the name of Kris Korbyn. Other wise known by Rampage, he was mostly known for holding multiple titles in NrCw where he was very successful. The tie where him and Crossett come in play. Crossett used to be on staff with the legendary indy fed NrCw. These two men have had confrontations previously in their careers, but have also been known to be on a very trusting level. Time will only tell if these two men have left their past as water under the bridge or not. Next on the lines of fire, an incredible cruiserweight has been signed to the roster by the name of Kid Extremo. This cat has more moves then Ron Jeremy has in bed except only in the ring. Kid may have a slight size disadvantage towards the rest of the roster, but you cannot defeat what you cannot catch. This will be a name you can only expect printed on every bloody piece of merchandise on the market when the fans see him back in action again. Former Resurrection member, the outcast of the group has signed his name back into professional wrestling. One name that was never thought to be seen competing again... The machine himself, 'the fallen angel' Chris Bones has been announced to be contending in NTICW with an extensive contract. This man is bad ass and can only be up to no good. When this name is heard, people cringe when they think of the thought or even the fact of which he really represents. The bad vibes will be flowing through NTICW like the smell of a cheap hookers perfume at a bachelor party. This is not the last heard of this man. Also fan favorite, Jason Oddysee has signed with the legendary NTICW. What has drove him to do such thing, Jason thought where else should a legend sign then with the building of the greatest federation known to this sport. Jason agrees it may of had a troubled past, but he knows that this is when the curse will be finally broken and he will not miss a single second of it. Oddysee is an overly skilled heavyweight to his business and should prosper very well in the near future. Friday, February 14, 2003 'The Rotton' signed! Another addition made to the NTICW roster when late this evening, we received verification that one named "The Rotton" Stevie DeLore signed his name in black ink to the legal contract. They say Crossett took liking for the character firstly for his admiration of TOOL, one of his favorite bands. Delore had the bad ass, but likable attitude and seemed like he had a large demand to prove himself to everyone. The middleweight wrestler from what is heard has very decent skills in all techniques of wrestling. Stevie Delore without a doubt will make impact in NTICW, but how large of one is solely upon himself. Tuesday, February 11, 2003 Another signed.. We have received verification of a superstar by the name of Grimey entering the federation after several minutes of negotiations. Nothing is still known of this character, but when leaving the NTICW headquarters he scratched his ass and lifted up his arm pit and gave a smell followed by a big grin on his face like he enjoyed his own stench. From what is heard, the man has hygienic problems and presents a wardrobe like a back alley drunk. When a NTICW official approached him behind the headquarters while digging through trash cans, he had this to say "My garbage damnit!" following with a mean growl. What CEO Crossett was thinking signing this odd character? Nobody will know until the return NTICW broadcast. At this time... We have no more signing news, but rumor has it that some huge names in the wrestling industry are just ironing out creases in their contracts as we speak. Monday, February 10, 2003 Contracts Signed?! It has been confirmed that the first, two NTICW superstars have signed their contracts early this morning when meeting with CEO Crossett and the mystery owner. The first was an athlete that goes by the name of Xero, who is very promising to say the least about this younger superstar. The scouts have said this man defines the meaning of hardcore, extreme entertainment. This man has been loved by the fans since the early start of his career. This will be his debut into professional wrestling and has everything to prove. The next of NTICW's competitors announced is the 'Soul of Ansai' Troy Trinity. One of the best in Thai Kick boxing and martial arts, he is also admired by his many fans that feel his pain and can understand his passion for this sport. Trinity has made brief debuts in LIFE and also Owl, he is ready to pick his pro wrestling career up a notch and has found the perfect, high impact federation to go about doing so. The white dragon has a flawless professional career and strong reputation in the making. This is all announced signed with NTICW so far, but we will keep you NTICW addicts informed of any more signings or news concerning the federation. NTICW lives! Sunday, February 09, 2003 Applications Open NTICW has started recruiting wrestlers to build the federation back into the best on the market and are only wanting serious athletes inquiring for a spot on the roster. It has been clearly stated that NTICW won't have any slackers along for the free ride with hope or chance they become someone off their money or time... At this time, members are being invited into the federation and NTICW will be reviewing any applications received, but is still not definite just anyone will receive a place on the limited roster. Ceo Crossett claimed earlier to our sources "The problem with a lot of federations these days, they are trying to rapidly expand and they push talent out the doors by bringing in another. NTICW plans to work with their selective roster and build them into the most loved or hated wrestlers imagined. Though the wrestlers will work for us, NTICW will also work for them in return with the mutual respect!" This is unofficially quoted, but from the best of sources... Its early to tell for sure, but from the sounds of things the spots on the roster will fill quickly and if serious it would be best not to wait regardless if you are the best or even a hot rookie trying to make a name for him or herself. We will keep you updated about the names signed on the dotted line and also rumor has it that applications may be closed after one week of having them open and remain invite only. NTICW CEO Confirmed Today it has been confirmed that Kyle Crossett has been announced the CEO of NTICW. Following the large amount of money offered and laid down on the table in recent weeks to Cade Johnson, another party has entered the picture and bought the federation and hired Crossett as his CEO. All though Crossett does not have ownership of the company, he is in better financial condition to have this fed lift off the ground faster and better then he ever hoped. Also he does not have to gamble the last of his personal bank accounts on this investment. The owner of the federation is still unannounced at this time, but from what is heard he is financially secured and very successful in the world of professional wrestling and many other companies that he owns and has shares in. Time will only tell who this 'big shot' really is... Recently, the newly hired CEO came out of meeting with the mystery owner and other out of character executives and was caught by a news reporter without much to say but did state a few words when the news reporter implied this was a cursed federation that should be left dead. "NTICW is anything but cursed and rest assure within the next two weeks you will all realize how well off this federation is. The hands that this past legendary company rests in are of the most strong and solid this business has to offer. The owner and myself will prove to all of you and turn heads in the process about NTICW and what you can hope to expect in the future and always" he stated very confidently. Following this statement, Crossett laughed at the news reporter and continued "Cursed?! You have to be [censored] kidding me!" he fled the scene in uncontrollable laughter and entered his awaiting limousine. The question really being, what should we expect? This whole situation seems like such a success before becoming one and also very shady at the same time. The mystery of this owner and no released information of when the federation will have its debut back into the business?! This whole ordeal seams like quite the sketchy one. Keep updated with NTICW to find out more in the coming weeks.
