Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New True Internet Championship Wrestling ran a show called Mayhem in December 2002 and wrote its own management as court reporting. Its vice-president was awaiting trial for attempted vehicular manslaughter against its president, who had been found in critical condition in a local hospital; its CEO of Operations had wrestled a tag match on the debut show and then disappeared, and the board would not comment.

In February 2003 it came back under a new CEO, Kyle Crossett, installed by an unnamed buyer, and the news page fills up with contracts — a veteran signing on, a hardcore raver signing on, a man called Hedges whose motives are left unknown. The fed relaunched by posting a story rather than a card: Chapter One is a wrestler waking up at twenty to two in the afternoon, missing the meeting about his own contract.

How it ended isn’t in the surviving record. The last things captured under the address are three empty wrestler-profile pages from June 2003 and, three years later, its host’s own placeholder.

Affiliations

Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW, Primetime, 2000–2004)

Owns NTICW (2002) (as of Oct 2001)
PCW CEO Kremmen von Smackdown, quoted outright: “I don’t think its any secret that Primetime Central owns NTICW... Yoder will go there to ensure that NTICW’s operations are running well and up to PCW standards.” NTICW functioned as PCW’s direct developmental/overflow fed and closed under its own last owner.

Old School Wrestling (OSW, Easton, 2002)

Crossover booking with NTICW (2002)
Jane Reagan personally recruited NTICW World Champion Scott Taylor into OSW mid-title-reign, a storyline beat that made both NTICW’s and OSW’s own news. NTICW appears frequently throughout OSW’s own record as a genuine peer/crossover fed, separate from PWA’s academy role.

Primetime Central (PTC)

Member of NTICW (2002) (2003)
Listed on Primetime Central’s own April 2003 affiliate wall, among eleven member feds sharing that same address. Corroborated from the fed’s own side: a PTC Forums link appears in the navigation of every captured page.

Primetime Wrestling Academy (PWA, Primetime, 2000–2003)

Fed by its academy NTICW (2002)
The academy's own December 2001 graduates list annotates Semore Smoke “*** Later became NTICW Champ,” and NTICW's own record holds him as a champion. A single annotation rather than an FAQ-level claim, so graded a step below the FAQ-named federations.

ewplanet.com

Community — NTICW (2002) was present here (February 2002 to August 2004)
A web address for it was posted on the eWPlanet forum 6 times between February 2002 and August 2004, according to Argyle's list of every address mentioned there.

NTICW (2000)

Predecessor to NTICW (2002) (2002-12)
The same name, a later fed: the NTICW of nticw.theewe.net calls itself the return of a jinxed fed that many owners had tried to start or revive, under new management at a new address, and crowned its own World Champion in a 2003 tournament rather than continuing the earlier NTICW's titles.
