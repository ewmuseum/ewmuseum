Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Xtreme Wrestling Federation wrote in Spanish. Its own roster page is titled Campeones, and the twelve XWF Superstars on it read C.R., Canadian Crippler, Canadian Perfect, Chivo, DJ, El Locon, Pyro, Raven, The Yure, DVD, La Envidia Mata and Limp Bizkit. Underneath them the same page carries a second, longer list headed GWA Superstars, sharing not one name with the first; nothing held says what it was doing there.

What the archive kept of the site is that page and a front page made entirely of pictures. The buttons name the sections — news, cards, results, rankings, records, champions, championship history, staff, rules, contracts, awards, polls, forums, an application — and every one of those pages is gone. Two of the graphics are show banners, and they are the only place the shows are named at all: a weekly called Tuesday Xtreme and a second called Saturday Brawl.

The one night the record can date, it did not happen here. On 20 April 2003 the promotion sent eleven wrestlers to Primetime Central's own New Era supercard, and the alliance gave its World Heavyweight Championship a match of its own on the card. C.R. defended it against Limp Bizkit under no-disqualification rules and was jumped from behind before the bell rang. The tag team champions worked him over, then the rest of the Corporation came down and Asesino put him through a table. Three men lifted him upright so a fourth could come off the top rope; he moved, and pinned the challenger while lying in his own blood. The commentary sent viewers to XWF's own programming to see how it ended, and pointed at a card called Payback where the champion would face Asesino.

Nothing of that programming survives, and neither does Payback, or the Anniversary show C.R. cut a promo about that night. Its own address stopped being anything but a redirect years before the crawler last passed, and the promotion had already left no results behind it.

Affiliations

Primetime Central (PTC)

Member of XWF (2003) (as of 20 Apr 2003)
Sent a delegation to PTC’s own “New Era” interfed supercard, 20 Apr 2003, whose Superstars page groups the whole card by member fed. This XWF is the Spanish-language Xtreme Wrestling Federation of geocities.com/xwf_pr, whose roster is a name-for-name match to the New Era delegation. Two other, unrelated promotions have also used the initials XWF.
