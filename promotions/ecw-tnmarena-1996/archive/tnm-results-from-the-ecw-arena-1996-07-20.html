ECW (TNM Arena Circuit, 1996) Card Archive

TNM: Results From The ECW Arena

20 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, here's my results from the ECW Arena, a card I just threw last night. Let me first tell you about what's going on in my fed. Shane Douglas is ECW World Champion after he defeated
Raven about a month ago (That's in TNM time). Hot on his trail is Tommy
Dreamer, a man who
Douglas USED to get a shot at Raven and take the title. About a week after he won the title, it was tag team action as Dreamer and Douglas teamed up against Raven and
Brian Lee. Near the
end of the match, Shane turned on Tommy and hit him with a keyboard, letting Raven and Lee,
ECW world tag Team Champions, get the win. The T.V. Title is now held by
Taz who just
defeated Pitbull #2 for it a few nights ago. Taz is one man in Bill
Alfonso's TRIPLE THREAT
stable, the other two members being Too Cold Scorpio and Rob Van Dam.
There has been some dissention lately as Too Cold wants a shot at his partners gold. Plus,
The Dudley family has split
as DeVon turned against Buh Buh Ray in a match where they were teaming.
Now, DeVon says he's bringing in a long lost Dudley for his tag match against Buh Buh and
Big Dick. Who will it
be? Here are the results:
The opener was the OFFICIAL in ring debut of the Samoan Gangsta Party
[Samoan #1, Samoan #2]. They faced Axl Rotten and ECW newcomer Mike Gemini (Made-up). I tried to give The Samoans a good push, but they had problems with Rotten and Gemini. They finally got the win at 12:18 after Samoan #2 hit the Samoan Flying Headbutt on Gemini. After the match, Da
Gangstas, who the Samoans attacked just one week ago, hit the ring and started kicking the crap out of the Samoans but The Eliminators and The Bruise Brothers hit the ring and all three teams triple-teamed Da Gangstas. Finally, after five brutal minutes and TWO
Total Eliminations on
Mustafa, The Pitbulls hit the ring and cleaned house, forming a new alliance with Da Gangstas.
Expect these teams to battle in the future. The next match was a very competitive match between Mikey Whipwreck and
Devon Storm. It
was back and fourth action with both wrestlers going for many aerial moves but finally, Mikey rolled Devon up in the Dandina Cradle and got the win at 12:33
Next, Hack Myers faced ECW newcomer The Convict (made-up). Hack pretty much dominated the rookie and got the win at 12:03 after the Death Drop.
After that , Joey Styles entered the ring and did an interview. Here it is: JOEY STYLES: And now, Extreme wrestling fans, frankly, because I have too, here is "Dancin'" Stevie Richards with his lackey, The Blue Meanie.... (Richards comes out without the Meanie) JS: So, Richards, where's your blue playmate??? RICHARDS: Joey, Jo Jo, my Blue Meanie, my lackey, has undergone some MAJOR reconstruction since the last time you saw him. JS: Did he get liposuction? SR: Funny, Jo Jo. No, the fact is that My Blue Meanie is no longer The
Blue Meanie. I want you
to meet the NEW Meanie. Here he is, along with his DIRECTOR, Miss patricia,
BLUEDUST!!!!!!!! (The Meanie and miss Patricia walk out. The Meanie is covered in Blue paint and wearing a blue cape and robe. He enters the ring)
BLUEDUST: <deep inhale> Enter the realm, of Bluedust!!! Miss Patricia, my director, direct me.
MISS PATRICIA: Do whatever the hell you want. (Bluedust first starts feeling himself up and then starts feeling on Joey
Styles jacket) JS: Hey Richards, get this fruitcake away from me!!!! (All of a sudden, The Sandman come running out of the back and clears the ring of Bluedust,
Richards and Miss Patricia with the Singapore Cain. Sandman grabs the mic) SANDMAN: YOOOOOO!!!! (Crowd goes nuts) Do you wanna see that fruitcake
Bluedust???? (Crowd chants "Hell no") Do you wanna see that putz Richards??? (Crowd continues chant) Do you wanna see me kick the ever-lovin' s**t out of Douglas tonight????
(Crowd chants "F**K HIM UP!!!") EXTREME intermission with the sweeper boy, who cleans up the ring of the crap that was left from the Samoan Gangsta Party match.
Back to EXTREME action as ECW T.V. Champion, Taz, defends his title against Louie Spinolli (aka Rad Radford). A very athletic match-up and Spinolli showed me alot as he avoided Taz's
Taz-mission twice but Taz overcame Spinolli after a Northern Lights Suplex. After the match, as Taz and Bill Alfonso were about to leave the ring, Too Cold Scorpio walked out. He grabbed the mic and said this:
"Taz, Bill Alfonso, I know you might not like what I gots to say, but it's gotta be said. Taz, you got a belt around your waist that I've worn FIVE times in the past. I respect you and you my partner, but I wants a shot at dat gold. So Taz, we can do dis the easy way or da hard way. You can give me a shot and we can still be partners or you can refuse me my shot, and things could get ugly. What you gots to say?"
(Taz snatches the mic from him) "You know something Too Cold Scorpio, I never liked you. Bill Alfonso wanted you in the Triple Threat so I put up with you. But Scorpio, you've crossed the line. Partners don't backstab partners, so, I'm not gonna give you the shot. I don't think you deserve it. So, now, let's get ugly!!!"
(Taz smashes Too Cold in the face with the mic and starts kicking him. Alfonso stands back stunned. All of a sudden, Rob Van Dam comes out and tries to pull Taz off of Scorpio. All of a sudden, some very familiar music starts to play and out of the dressing room comes....911!!!! The crowd goes nuts as 911 enters the ring and picks up Van Dam....
CHOKESLAM!!! He picks up Scorpio....CHOKESLAM!!!! Now Taz charges into him with a clothesline but
911 no sells it!!!!
All of a sudden, Alfonso jumps on 911's back and begins choking out 911 with Taz's orange towel. 911 goes down. Now, Van Dam and Taz start pounding on 911 as
Scorpio flees the ring) After that (If that wasn't exciting enough), was a highly competitive match between Chris Jericho and Japan native Rambo. It was a fair match as Rambo pinned Jericho after the Powerslam at
12:08 Then, it was time for EXTREME Lucha Libre. This Lucha match saw Blue
Panther and El Hijo Del Santo team up to face Psicosis and La Parka. It was truly an EXTREME match as there were many dives in and out of the ring and some dazzling top rope moves. The match ended after
Psicosis and Parka tried to double-team Blue Panther as Parka held Panther and Psicosis tried to hit him with a hockey stick. It went wrong when Panther ducked and Psicosis hit Parka. El Hijo Del Santo then rolled the falling Parka up from behind and got the pin at 17:04 After that, it was a match to settle a rivalry that started about six weeks ago when Tommy Cairo made his RETURN to ECW and was viciously sneak attacked by Jason from behind. The stipulation of this match? The loser must wear a dress. Cairo kicked
Jason's can all over the arena
and then dragged him back into the ring, delivered the Iron DDT and got the pin at 10:28. Then,
Cairo stood in the ring as Jason was starting to put on the dress but from behind, the team Jason used to manage, The Eliminators, attacked Cairo and give him a vicious beating, including TWO
Total Eliminations.
Next was the long-awaited match for the ECW World Title as "The Franchise" Shane Douglas faced off against long-time rival, The Sandman. The action carried it's way all over the Arena, with both men hitting each other with various foreign objects. Finally,
Douglas hit the Belly To
Belly Suplex and got the pin at 11:39
Next was another long-awaited match. The Dudleys showdown. It pitted Big
Dick Dudley and Buh Buh Ray Dudley against DeVon Dudley and his long lost Dudley mystery partner. DeVon announced who it was and to the crowds shock, it was Big Rick Dudley, a
Dudley that looked
exactly like Big Dick!!! Buh Buh was attacked before the bell by Devon as
Big Rick and Big
Dick had a monstrous battle on the outside of the ring. Too bad for Dick and Buh Buh, DeVon hit a Flying Dudley Butt on Buh Buh and got the pin at only 6:47. After the match, Big Dick picked up the mic and challenged Big Rick to a chokeslam vs. chokeslam match at the next card!!! Nothing was settled between this feuding family!!!
After that, it was Brian Lee vs. Tommy Dreamer in a steel cage weapons match. Every type of weapon you can imagine was put into the ring before the cage was lowered
(Heck, even a
refrigerator!!!). The match saw both men use the cage more then the weapons as both men were bloodied by the cage early. After about 10 solid minutes of back and fourth violence, Dreamer got the advantage when he hit Lee with a metal pole below the belt.
Dreamer then abused Lee some more and was ready to finish him off as he put the chair down, getting ready to DDT Lee on it. Just when it was about to happen, Raven came down to ringside and threw a FILE
CABINET (No Joke) into the cage. The Cabinet hit Dreamer in the back, causing him to fall and not execute the DDT. After about a minute of both men lying on the floor like bloody heaps, Lee got to his feet and saw his chance. He lifted Dreamer up and bulldogged him on the chair and got the three count at 12:34. That wasn't the end though. as right after that,
Raven and The Bruise
Brothers rushed down to ringside. They cut the cage door open with a lock cutter and rushed in.
Then, all four of them abused Dreamer for about five minutes until, low and behold, The Sandman rushed in and cained the crap out of all four of them. The card ended with Dreamer's music blasting over the loudspeaker.
What's next for ECW? The next card will be in two weeks (In real life time, that's two days) from
Long Island, NY. The main event will be Da Gangstas and Tommy Dreamer vs.
The Bruise Brothers and Raven in a six-man tag team match. Plus, The Sandman will face Brian Lee and that chokeslam vs. chokeslam match between Big Dick Dudley and Big Rick
Dudley PLUS
MORE!!! Overall: This card was a great new fued starter. There are many new fueds burning in ECW right now and I can't wait to throw the next card.
