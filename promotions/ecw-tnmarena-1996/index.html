Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This ECW circuit introduced itself on 19 July 1996 as a project run on a wrestling simulator, TNM version 6.2, using “all the real wrestlers from ECW” but building its own angles and cards around them rather than reporting the real promotion's own results. By its own account, when its one surviving card was thrown the following night, Shane Douglas was already its ECW World Champion, having beaten Raven for the title about a month earlier in the circuit's own time; Tommy Dreamer was chasing a rematch after Douglas turned on him mid-tag-match with a keyboard, costing Dreamer and letting Raven and Brian Lee, the circuit's own tag champions, take the win; Taz held the television title after beating Pitbull #2 for it, inside a stable, the Triple Threat, that was fraying as stablemate Too Cold Scorpio pushed for a shot at Taz's own gold; and the Dudley family had split, DeVon having turned on Buh Buh Ray and promising a mystery long-lost Dudley as a partner for the rematch.

The card itself opened with the in-ring debut of the Samoan Gangsta Party over Axl Rotten and a newcomer the operator marked as his own invented addition, and the post-match brawl that followed pulled in three more teams before ending in a new alliance between the Pitbulls and Da Gangstas. A mid-card interview segment turned the Blue Meanie into “Bluedust,” a paint-and-cape send-up introduced by his own manager before the Sandman ran in to clear the ring. Taz defended the television title against Louie Spinolli and kept it, and the aftermath brought the Triple Threat's own tension into the open: Scorpio demanded his shot, Taz refused it and attacked him instead, and 911 ran out to chokeslam both Scorpio and Rob Van Dam before Bill Alfonso and Taz turned on 911 together.

Shane Douglas defended the World Title against the Sandman and retained it. The Dudley Family's own showdown resolved DeVon's mystery partner as “Big Rick Dudley,” built, in the circuit's own words, to look exactly like Big Dick Dudley; DeVon's team won, and Big Dick closed the angle by challenging Rick to a chokeslam-versus-chokeslam match at the next card. The main event, a steel cage weapons match between Brian Lee and Tommy Dreamer, ran into interference from Raven, who threw a filing cabinet into the cage to break up a near-finish, and then from the Bruise Brothers before the Sandman returned to clear the cage of all four men as Dreamer's music played the show out.

The circuit's own operator promised a next card, in two weeks, from Long Island, headed by a six-man tag pitting Da Gangstas and Dreamer against the Bruise Brothers and Raven. Nothing in the surviving record shows whether that card, or any other after it, ever actually ran, or how or when the circuit came to an end.

This federation’s fiction portrays the ECW.
