NEWCOWF Triple Threat '97 Results, 29 June 1997

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks again to everyone that participated in the voting. You will find the results below. SANDMAN VS. CHRIS JERICHO The show opens with Metallica's "Enter Sandman" playing as Sandman comes to the ring and quicklye tries to cane Jericho, but he escapes under the ropes. The match begins with Jericho taking the advantage with some high flying moves. Jericho hits a suplex and attempts a lionsault but misses. Sandman takes him outside the ring and nails him with several chairshots followed by followed by breaking the cane over Jericho's head and tossing him back in the ring. Sandman hits the top rope leg drop for the pin at 8:42. Votes 13-2. WINNER GETS TITLE SHOT-HARLEM HEAT VS. GANGSTAS VS. THE PUBLIC ENEMY The match begins with Harlem Heat standing in the ring watching TPE and the Gangstas brawl throughout the building with chairs, trashcans, fans, brooms, mops and anything else they could get ahold of. They make it to the concession stand where TPE puts Mustafa through the condiment table. New Jack makes his way back to the ring where a well rested Booker T hits him with a Harlem Sidekick for the pin at 12:21. TPE returns to ringside and is attacked by the Gangstas with nightsticks and an unconcious Rocco Roc is tossed into the ring and pinned by Stevie Ray at 14:32. Votes HH8-TPE5-G2. BARBED WIRE WITH WEAPONS-TERRY FUNK VS. CACTUS JACK The ring ropes are wrapped with barbed wire and the following are palced in the ring-a jar of thumbtacks, a box of lightbulbs, a baseball bat, a hockey stick, a black studded glove, a brick and a 2X4 with nails in it. Funk and Jack begin brawling, Funk rips Jack's head open with the barbed wire. Jack grabs the jar of thumbtacks and shatters it over Funk's head. Funk kicks Jack in the stomach and piledrives him on the thumbtacks and begins beating Jack with the 2X4. Funk then goes for another piledriver but Jack backdrops him on to the lightbulbs and puts on the studded glove, but Funk hits him in the face with the brick and covers. 1...2...Jack appiles the mandible claw with the studded glove. Funk bleeds heavily from the mouth and taps out at 13:52. Votes 12-3. TV TITLE-RIC FLAIR VS. CHRIS BENOIT Back and forth mat wrestling continues for about 15 minutes. Flair gains the advantage and begins to work on Benoit's leg. Flair applies the figure four but Benoit gets to the ropes. Flair attempts the figure four again, but his kicked into the turnbuckles. Benoit hits the top rope headbutt. 1...2...3! NEW TV CHAMPION CHRIS BENOIT at 21:02. Votes 10-5. TAG TEAM TITLE-JOHN KRONUS & TOMMY DREAMER VS. SABU & ROB VAN DAM For over 15 minutes Kronus and Sabu exchange high spots in the ring while Dreamer and Van Dam brawl outside the ring. Jerry Lawler comes to ringside and joins Van Dam in attacking Dreamer. Sandman comes down and starts caning Lawler. Sabu puts Kronus through a table on the opposite side of the ring. Van Dam and Dreamer get in the ring and Dreamer hits a DDT on Van Dam, but the referee is distracted by Sandman and Lawler. The Insane Clown Posse (ICP) comes in and beats Dreamer in the head with 2-Liter bottles of Faygo. Van Dam hits the spilt-leg moonsault for the pin at 22:13. NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS-SABU AND ROB VAN DAM. Votes 12-3. SHOOTFIGHT-KEN SHAMROCK VS. TAZ. VS. VADER The wrestlers feel each other out for most of the first 10 minutes with lockups and takedowns. Vader gets frustrated and throws Shamrock in the corner and starts delivering hard punches to the face. Taz hits a belly to back suplex on Vader and makes him tap out to the Tazmission at 17:23. Shamrock and Taz exchange suplexes for over 5 minutes. Shamrock applies a torque leglock and Taz taps out at 24:21. Votes KS9-T5-V1. STREET FIGHT-NWO VS. NOD VS. The results of the last 2 matches were mistakenly left off. Here they are. STREET FIGHT-NWO VS. NOD VS. HART FOUNDATION NWO Team-Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, Syxx & Randy Savage NOD Team-Farooq, D-Lo Brown & Kama Mustafa (Ahmed Johnson is out due to knee surgury) Hart Foundation-Owen Hart, British Bulldog, Brian Pillman & Jim Neidhart The ring looks like a battle royal as all 11 wrestlers begin brawling. At 15:23 Neidhart is pinned by Kevin Nash follwing a powerbomb. The NOD brawls with the NWO using chains, chairs and boards, but being outnumbered 4-3 takes its toll when Farooq goes to pin Syxx and Savage hits him with the elbow off the top rope for the pin at 19:33. Votes NWO7-NOD5-HF3. WORLD TITLE-STEVE AUSTIN VS. STING VS. BRET HART Austin quickly attacks Hart and they brawl into the crowd. Sting stands like a statue in the ring with his bat. Austin and Hart battle back to the ring and Sting hits Austin with the bat and hits the Scorpion Death Drop on Hart and applies the Scorpion Death Lock. Austin gets up and hits the Stone Cold Stunner on Sting for the pin at 21:02. Hart hits Austin in the knee with the bat and starts stomping on the knee with the bat underneath it. Hart applies the Sharpshooter and keeps it on for 7 minutes until Austin reaches the ropes. Hart pulls Austin up and Austin hits the Stone Cold Stunner out of nowhere for the pin at 37:15. Austin retains the World Title. Votes A8-H3-S4. -------------------==== Posted via Deja News ====----------------------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Post to Usenet
