NEWCOWF Tournament Finals Results, 8 June 1997

8 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks again to everyone that has participated in the voting. I has taken a month but your votes have finally decided the World, Tag Team and TV Champions of the New Extreme World Championship Order Wrestling Federation. Here are the results from the final round Interview with Stone Cold Steve Austin- Announcer- "Tonight you will face the Giant for the World title. Do you have any worries about facing the big man. SCSA- "Hell no, I ain't worried about a damn thing because I brought a giant-sized can of whoop ass with me and I'm gonna pour it all over his big ass. I will be the World Champion, son, and that's the bottom line because Stone Cold said so." NEWCOWF TV TITLE-Ahmed Johnson vs. Ric Flair-Ahmed dominates the offense for most of the match. Johson sidewalk slams Flair and attempts to pick him up for the Pearl River Plunge, but Flair hits him with a crotch shot, clips his knee and applies the figure four for the pin at 21:37. Your first NEWCOWF TV Champion is the Nature Boy Ric Flair. Whooooooo! Votes 6-4. BONUS MATCH-Ken Shamrock vs. Vader with special referee Taz. After a brutal back and forth battle for about 15 minutes, Vader powerbombs Shamrock four times. Taz attempts to check on Shamrock, but is shoved by Vader. They shove each other several times and then Taz chokes out Vader with the Tazmission and declares Shamrock the winner at 17:23. Shamrock refuses to accept the victory and won't let Taz raise his hand. Shamrock turns to leave the ring and Taz chokes him out with the Tazmission and leaves Shamrock and Vader unconcious in the ring. Votes 6-1 (with 3 for Taz). NEWCOWF TAG TEAM TITLE-Doug Furnas & Philip LaFon vs. The Eliminators. This match was virtually two on one as John Kronus took on both Furnas & LaFon as the injured Perry Saturn stood on the ring apron. At the 10 minute mark, Saturn tagged himself in and attempted to fight off Furnas. Furnas grabbed Saturn's good leg but Saturn hit him with an enziguri with the cast for the pin at 13:42. The first NEWCOWF Tag Team Champions are the Eliminators. Votes 7-2. BONUS MATCH-Bret Hart vs. Sting. Sting and Hart battle back and forth for about 20 minutes. Sting takes the advantage and whips Hart into the corner for the Stinger Splash, but Bret pulls the referee in front of him. The Hart Foundation hits the ring, but Sting cleans house with his baseball bat. Meanwhile, Bret has grabbed a chair and clips Sting in the knee with it. Hart applies the Sharpshooter. Sting passes out from the pain and the referee stops the match and declares Hart the winner at 27:02. Votes 4-3. NEWCOWF WORLD TITLE-Stone Cold Steve Austin vs. The Giant. After a slow start, both men begin to brawl at the 7 minute mark. Austin sends the Giant into the corner and delivers several kicks followed by a double middle finger, after which the Giant grabs him by the neck and delivers several blows to the head. Giant chokeslams Austin and covers him. 1...2...AUSTIN KICKS OUT! Austin low blows The Giant and delivers a Stone Cold Stunner, but instead of going for the pin he picks him up and delievers a second SCS and picks him up again for a third SCS. Austin covers the Giant. 1...2...3! AUSTIN IS THE NEWCOWF WORLD CHAMPION!!! Time of the match 11:24. Votes 7-2. Post-match interview-Austin-"I don't know why you're all so happy, because I did by my damn self. Now..." Austin is interupted by Bret Hart on the video wall-"Why don't you shut the hell up. This is the biggest bunch of bullshit I've ever seen in my life. The only reason you have that belt is because I got screwed in my 1st round match with that painted up clown that I took care of tonight." As Austin watches Bret on the video wall, the crowd realizes that it is a tape as Bret sneaks up behind Austin with a chair and nails him in the head and begins to jam the chair into his knee. Sting drops down from the ceiling and KOs Hart with his bat. Sting pokes Austin in the chest with the bat, hands it to hime, turns his back and extends his arms. Austin nails Sting in the head with the bat and delivers the Stone Cold Stunner. Austin stands on the turnbuckle and holds the belt over his head as the show ends. Thanks again to everyone that participated and please participate in these upcoming NEWCOWF events (voting begins one week prior to event date).
