H.Closing ---------------------------------- A. Introduction 1.What is WHIP? * WHIP- is Womens Hardcore International Promotions, a wrestling organization for women only. WHIP is going to be officially opened on Feb 14th 2000 ( I know it's a Wednesday ) or the weekend of, depending on response and timeframe.
WHIP and their front offices are based out of New York City and shows will take place in the US and all over the world, ( Hence the use of "International " ).
Our 1st card will take place in NYC ( site T.B.D. ) and may have a Supercard feel , as long as we can secure "sponsors".
WHIP will have many different types of matches, it's not all extreme, or "hardcore" as the title says, but it will have an '95-'98 ECW feel to it. (
Which is basically todays WWF, but on a smaller scale. ) There will be a
*positive outcome to every match* rule in effect for all WHIP matches.... meaning you'll almost *never* get ripped off by a Countout, No Contest, or DQ ending, matches may be stopped, continued, or restarted due to certain circumstances, and the Higest ranking official, ( Fed President, Comissioner, and/or Head Referee ) will have full discretion over these *circumstances*.
Don't worry, there won't be 1000 run in's or senceless weapon use and brawling all over the arena on every match either. There's going to be normal matches, but there will be a few select matches like that showcased every card.
WHIP will start off in mid-small arenas and develop it's way towards Supercard
/PPV status 2.Who runs things?<Fictional> Who's running *The Whole F'n Show* you ask? Former EFW & EFWF Booker/Promoter/Announcer Paul E Furiously is back in action!... Yes, after close to a 4 year layoff from running EFW ( Extreme Fantasy Wrestling ), EFWF ( Extreme Female Wrestling Federation ) and co-ordination the development of The "Sister Leagues", Paul E's hit it big in Atlantic City, paid off his loans , and has found himself with a wadful of cash, the drive, and desire to see things work again, *The Right way*.
He's about 40 years old, been in the business, takes no crap, and is either loved or hated by the locker room for his laid back and let you do what you want.. until he has to get cocky, and arrogant in your face if you mess with him style.
He's a bit overweight, a bit balding, wears WHIP Gear proudly ( Hats & T's ) and does a lot of the developmental work ( Commercials, Merchandice ) for
WHIP. He wears a leather trenchcoat & jeans most of the time... he's one of the boys, and can act like it, or he can be the boss... it all depends on who's he's drinking with after the shows ;o).
3.Who runs things?<Real> That'd be me, the one and only [name withheld].. Pro Wrestler ( Indy worker ) in real life, former EFW & EFWF fedhead, matchwriter, handler of Heidi Leick, some of you hate me, some of you like me.
No big deal. All I know is I'm going to do my best for WHIP, this is a hobby of mine, and I like doing it.
I'll assemble a staff to run the flash show & help write matches, and get your
( the handlers ) input on things from time to time as to what you do and don't like about WHIP, and we'll see if we can work togtether, as this fed , WHIP, is for, above all people,.. you the handler.
I've seen too many great womens feds fold up, just wither and die, or go from a regular scheduele to having one show every 3 mos, where you cant get involved with the storyline. WHIP Will have storylines, there WILL be a reason for you working on PPV/Supercard shows, and I think you, the handler will enjoy it.
B.What are the Titles? 1.WHIP World Championship This is the most coveted title in WHIP, and there will be some kind of face off tourney for this leading up to our 1st Supercard... after 5 matches into the fed you'll qualify for a shot at this title, as long as your in the top 10. The
Champ can decide what rules they defend under ( within reason ). This belt must be defended once every month. Want to defend it more often? Go ahead with my blessing, but above all, it has to be defended at least once a month or it will be stripped away.
2.WHIP World Tag Team Championship We'll gauge intrest on The WHIP Tag Titles as we get into the swing of things,
I don't want it to be just a "paper" title, for 2 women who have no other options. I'd like to establish this division & it's feuds quickly and ferociously. Will be defended 1X a Month, with the opp to defend it more as we deem necessary.
3.WHIP TV Title Everyone Qualifies for TV Contender status as soon as they sign the contract,
THIS Title holder will defend EVERY WEEK.*unless injured * holding this belt gives you some claim to # 1 Contender status on the World Title.
You'll show your fortitude & the world what your woman is made of, and capable of. As the Champ, you can set stips & rules for your defence, (* again , within reason & storyline/angle* ) but be wary, Mr Furiously watches his title holders with an eagles eye.
Defend this belt wisely, and you could go from Supernobody, to Superhero in an instant. C.What are the Cards? 1.WHIP's Wrestling Show- Violence and Bloodshed WHIP's weekly show, I'm shooting to have the Shows to you on TUESDAYS. Deadline for Submissions, Strats & On carders * you must receive permission to do an on carder.* due by Saturday/Sunday12 Midnight EST. Afterwards, they will not count, except without previous permission.
With Announcers Jerry Styles, Noel Schmertner, and Heidi Leick. This is where your angles and storylines come to life, feuds get started and heat is built up for the Supercards. The matches are in full written PBP form. Each show will have between 3-5 matches. A lot of times the announcers will have done voice overs, as none of this is live, these are taped shows. We're not WWF, this is not RAW... you'll see commercials with your merchandice here, and for our next events, also there'll usually be the chance for at least 2 "in ring" promos to be done here, 1st come, 1st serve, *Angle builders get preference.
2.WHIP's Flash Show- The Crack of the WHIP These Shows will be out by SUNDAY before the V&B Show there's a deadline of
Saturday/Sunday 12 MIDNIGHT EST deadline for Submissions. Afterwards, they will not count, except without previous permission.
Hosted again by Jerry Styles, Noel Schmertner & Heidi Leick, there will be some commentary between flashes, and it'll give you the chance to set up angles, call out champs, introduce your character and further storylines.
* NO using other characters in your flashes in any fashion except saying thier name without thier & my explicit permission. 3.PPV's/Supercards- One every 3 months, and we'll build towards the 1st. We're not that big yet, but we'll be growing fast,.. and as we grow,and angles/storylines build, the
PPV's will MEAN something, instead of just having one every month to have one.
4.Update/Control Center The Update/Control Center will be like GDWA's CC, or Update, letting you know about matches, challenges, injury status, rankings, new additions & maybe a rumor mill. It'll be out at various times during the week, you'll always have
enough time to flash vs your opponnent. 5.Schedule- I'm shooting for a weekly show, & this is what I have planned as a scheduele for WHIP. Tueday Nights - V& B Airs, followed by a CC. Wed/Thurs/Fri/Sat- Update/CC. Sunday- Crack of the WHIP Flash Show Mon-? PPV/Supercard, 1 Every 3 Mos. D.WHIP Staff Members 1.Commentators Jerry Styles- He's the straight man, with a dry sense of humor, more the babyface of the bunch, a tell it like you see it PBP report from him, he's a veteran, and known as one of the best around,and he deseratly tries to keep his motley crue in the booth together, and on track.
Noel Schmertner- is the Heel/Color man. He'll have the strange comedy, and sometimes wont see the rulebreakers cheating, even though it's in his face.
The women hate him for the most part, cause he's overweight, thinks he's studly, and tries to hit on all of them, he's the victim of many a practical joke in the locker room.
Heidi Leick- Occasional commentator, has her own view of things, usually heel's off.. she won't usually get involved, as she's doing Announcing now to further her out of ring career.. sometimes she gets sidetracked, and Her and Noel can have some really heated arguments at times, as he loves to provoke Heidi.
Like I said, she won't usually get involved *unless provoked * if you provoke her enough... watch your ass.
2.Interviewers Jerry Styles- Jerry will always give you a no bones about it, hard hitting, fact finding Q&A session.. when the girls let him. He'll do most of the in ringers , whenever applicable.
Noel Schmertner- When Noel does backstage interviews, it's the goof of the century, the girls abuse him, or use him to just stand there and hold the mic,
Noel has sensless questions, and the girls tend to ignore him, play jokes on him, and abuse him.
Heidi Leick- Takes no crap.. If she's interviewing you, you're getting permission from me IRL, and I'm working with you on this. She does * Special* interviews.
3.Referees- Head Referee/Official- Mike Griswald- or "Iron Mike" as everyone calls him, he's apt to get involved and break up 2 on 1's, and he hates having to work under these every match will end in a * positive finish * rules, he calls things down the line, and he'll reprimand anyone he catches for rulebreaking, even though it's quite legal here.
He gets respect, the girls WILL argue with him but most of the girls are intimidated by him.
He's a former wrestler ( Amature & Pro ) that was forced out of the biz by a knee injury , he's 6'3, 240 lbs and in very good shape. He was a technical/tradionally trained wrestler, and calls it like he sees it, usually on the babyface/technician side though.
The other refs. Tracy Chiaramonte- Tracy's a rookie ref, with a penchant for losing control of the situation and letting things happen, She's got some heat with Mike Griswald, but the heel wrestlers generally like her, because they can get away with murder, one thing about her though, Tracy's ALWAYS in place to make the 3 count, for whoever's on the mat, making a lot of the heel girls angry... Tracy's a straight down the middle girl as well, and show's no favoritism.
5'2 , 110 lbs, Blonde hair, very attractive. Lisa James- The Heel ref. Put it this way, She can be bought. The Ex Wife of Paul E, who looks to screw him and his federation over at any given chance she can, he can't fire her, as a court order has her in his employment, at her profession when he met her.. and she was a "Foxy Boxing" referee.
She's stunningly attractive, 5'5, 125 lbs , w tan skin & brown hair, and everything that Paul likes now, she hates, and vice versa. She'll never be more than a referee... but she's in a position to throw a monkey wrench into the works.
4.Ring Announcers Noel Schmertner- Colorful personality & voice of WHIP, will do anything for a laugh and will definitly heel off on whatever crowd he's in front of.
Jerry Styles- Opens the show in the ring w Noel, sometimes will do main events
& PPV/Supercard RA'ing.
5.Bosses Owner/Booker/Promoter-Paul E Furiously Comissioner- TBD Head Official- Mike Griswald- "Iron Mike"- see above. E.What you must do. 1.Roleplays aka Flashes. Deadline for Flashes/Roleplays* you must receive permission to do an on carder.* due by Saturday/Sunday12 Midnight EST. Afterwards, they will not count, except without previous permission.
Put it this way, You don't Roleplay - You don't win, Ok? If you do not Roleplay, you will be warned1X for 1 show , jobbed and then cut from the roster if I get no reply.
If you're going out of town, or know you'll have no EM access, tell me, we'll work something out.
-No Attacking other wrestlers/managers/announcers or anyone else without getting permission. -Don't break kayfabe. This may be wrestling but it's Fantasy wrestling which means no mentioning any real wrestlers, & crap like Jobber, etc etc.
Also please don't mention other Fantasy federations unless it pertains to something important, like if you're facing someone in WHIP that you faced in another, fed then that's fine. Those titles mean nothing here, you're all starting from scratch except from some women who have a past that I recognize from certain feds I recognize.
-How many times can I Flash? There will be a one roleplay a week limit with one on carder ( w/ permission ), and one group flash will also be allowed, if you are in a stable.
If you don't have a match I'd say Flash anyway, and keep building some heat with someone... you may be able to do a locker room angle/flash.... Let me know ahead of time if you are going to do this as I don't want to have too many, and need to give permissions.
2.Strats- Deadline for Strats - They are due by Saturday/Sunday12 Midnight EST. Afterwards, they will not count, except without previous permission. Strats count for 50% of your match, and should not be too lengthy, I'll have your womans moveset and I'll use it, send me spots, what you want to set out to do, beginning, middle, end, any illegal/interference, and specific events & areas to work on, as well as counters & alternate finishers.
3.Angles Angles- are the Wildcard here. You can outflash, outstrat & outwork your opponnent, but if they have a great angle, I may use it. Because an interesting storyline helps build up much needed heat in feuds and makes people want to read the matches. Now, MOST OF THE TIME you'll be made aware previosuly.. as I want you all to get involved with other handlers, come up with ideas and go with them.
That's what makes this thing fun, and entertaining. There will be the occasional surprise ending though , and both handlers may be surprised at what ~I~ came up with.
F.How things are booked/decided- It's all decided & booked by me, through observation your input, participation
& activity in WHIP. Got a Question on why something happened the way it did?,Then ask me, my door is always open.

I'll let you guys and gals have fun, and I'll run a fair honest fed. I'll always be open to your suggestions & input on making WHIP better, in fact making it the BEST Womens E-Fed, and then the BEST E- fed we can possibly make... TOGETHER.
