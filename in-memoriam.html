In Memoriam

Commemorated, not just documented.

This commemorates the members of this community who died too soon, as remembered by the people who knew them. This is the only part of the museum that attributes real names of private individuals, when the community acknowledged those names. Any member of the memorial will be removed or anonymized upon request by family or friend without hesitation, and any name brought to us for memorialization will be honored with the same care, whether we knew them or not. The foremost priority of this museum will always be to honor the members of this list with love and respect.

ANDY DORAN · 1982–2015

An English teacher and football coach who “enjoyed a wrestling based creative writing circle.” He wrote as Marcus Broussard and “Cool Hand” Luke Kinsey, and served on EMWC's staff to its 2003 close.

CHAD DUPREE · 1975–2004

A roleplayer and a federation owner. EUWC gave his name to its annual tournament, the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, and the wider circuit renewed it year after year.

CHRIS WILLIAMS · d. 4 May 1999

Killed in an automobile accident. The wider e-fedding community he was part of has honored him every year since with the Chris Williams Memorial Cup.

DANIEL QUIRK · 1982–2005

He ran the EWF in the late 1990s and was CWF-Pro's original Punisher, a Hall of Famer there in 2001. He died after an accident in a professional match in 2005, aged 22; the second Punisher took the name in his honor.

ED WILSON · 1976–2001

The owner of the Cyber Wrestling League — in his own community's words, “a league that visually changed fantasy wrestling.”

FRANK A. MARANO JR. · d. 1993

He founded the Universal Wrestling Alliance, a play-by-mail league of the 1980s; a friend remembered him running eight regional UWAs at once, from Canada to Japan. His memorial cup, first held in 1993, was still being contested in 2010.

JAYMES FAULKNER · 1972–2022

A father, husband, brother, uncle and all round amazing person.

JEFFREY WARD · 1982–2026

He wrote Angelo Deville for most of a decade: the winner of PTC's third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, PTC's Wrestler of the Year for 2005 and, that spring, PRIME's Tag Team Champion beside Justin Williamson, Sean Simon Williams' character.

>=)

JOE SMALLZE · d. April 2002

Six years across the NAWA and Furiously Addictive Wrestling, in the period record's own words “one of the greatest competitors to ever roleplay.”

MIKE SANZONE · d. 2026

Handler of Diamond Rose in Empire Pro Wrestling, which dedicated a broadcast to him in May 2026. In his own community's words, his name “remained part of the fabric of the community for a very long time.”

PATRICK LITTLE · d. 31 December 1999

He created Internet Championship Wrestling in June 1998, then wrestled for GCW as Titan and Strider and served as its Commissioner. The man who built GCW wrote that “without him, there would be no us.”

PAUL SCANLAN · 1978–2011

He wrote Paul Cain and Damien Kahn across NWC: Atlantic, PRIME, CCW and Lone Star Wrestling. A fellow writer remembered him as “a champion in life” — PRIME dedicated an Intense Championship to his name.

RON OLSON · d. 2004

He wrote The Rock, a World Heavyweight title contender in the Almost Illegal Wrestling League, when a league lived on an Angelfire page and a weekly card. Killed in a car crash at twenty-two.

SEAN SIMON WILLIAMS · 1985–2018

An avid lifelong professional wrestling fan who also loved role-playing games and card games, and stood against injustice wherever he found it.

STEELMOOSE · d. 1996

The Extreme Wrestling Association dedicated its work to him in June 1996, soon after he died.

STEVEN W. PAGANO · d. 19 April 2001

He ran World Class Championship Wrestling, its vice president and then its chief executive, and lived “two years past the date doctors had given him.” His friends' memorial event became a tournament in his name.

TIM FITZGERALD · d. 20 March 2008

Creator of WrestleCrazy Entertainment and Mayhem Wrestling Entertainment. The roster he trained rebuilt his company in his honor, under the banner In Tim We Trust.
