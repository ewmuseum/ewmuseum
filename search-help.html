Search

The query language.

Every search box on this site speaks it — the global Search, every listing, every instrument. Terms combine with AND; the counter under the box always reads “N of M shown.”

Operators

word   substring match — crud finds Al Crud.
"exact phrase"   keeps its spaces — "steel cage".
field:value   match one field — promotion:uwf, participant:seymour-almasy (on Matches), stipulation:"steel cage".
field:a|b   either value — promotion:giwf|xuw.
-word, -field:value   exclude — -promotion:uwf, -"battle royal". -field:a|b is neither.
field:   field present, any value — gender: is everyone whose record gives one.
-field:   field absent — -gender: is everyone whose record never gives one.
> < >= <=   numeric compare — reigns:>3, year:>=2003. Dates compare by year: date:<2004.
field:2002..2004   inclusive range — same year rule for dates.

The global Search takes these fields: promotion, style, medium, language, country, year, date, reigns, opponent, opponents, who, stipulation, gender, kind, name, title, alignment, finisher, hometown. A field that belongs to one list page only works on that page: participant on Matches.

The second box on each page is a discovery aid: it lists that page's own field names (typed from its own rows, nothing hand-kept) and inserts a field: stub for you to finish. The “Fields this page can search” note under the box lists the same names. Your full query lives in the address bar as ?q=… as you type — copy the URL to share the exact view.

Recipes

Coverage   on Characters: gender:male, gender:female, then -gender: for everyone the record never gives either way.
Cross-promotion   on Matches: stipulation:cage promotion:giwf|xuw — every cage match across two feds.
A career, elsewhere   on Appearances: who:seymour-almasy -promotion:uwf — everywhere he turned up outside his home fed.
A window in time   on Reigns: year:2002..2004 promotion:uwf — every UWF reign begun in that stretch.
A record   on Characters and Units: wins:>=20, winpct:>=60, matches:10..50 — the record the museum counted from the match results it has parsed, never a record a fed declared; also losses, draws and nc (no contest). winpct is wins out of wins plus losses, given once there are 5 of those. The sort box beside the filter orders the list by most matches, most wins or best win share.
A scene   on Wings: language:pl for the wings a Polish-language community ran, country:DE for the wings run from Germany whatever language they ran in, and -language: for everything held in English — a wing states its language only when it is not English.
