Essays

This is the only part of the museum that steps out of the game. It's a collection of writing about fantasy wrestling, rather than participating within it. Some of this is unique to this site, but most originates with outside sites; in the latter case, we provide outbound links to those sites while they remain available online. Like everything else on this site, this remains a work-in-progress; if you have any recommendations, contact us at essays[at]ewmuseum.org or through the forum!
