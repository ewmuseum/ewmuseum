The Undertaker

The real professional wrestler — portrayed in FWF-1992's own November 1992 card as "Kane the Undertaker", the fed's own FAQ naming the practice out of fiction ("taking WWF gimmicks such as Hulk Hogan or the Undertaker and improving their workrate"). Served at promotions/fwf-1992/archive/. A real professional wrestler.
