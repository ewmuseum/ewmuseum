External figure

Lex Luger

The real professional wrestler — billed by FAW's own March–May 2001 roster as "TTP Lex Luger" and staged as himself across that fed's own show scripts, where he held its Universal Championship twice. The shared node for portrayals of him; the characters are the feds' own, and the feds' own fiction is not this museum's claim about the man.
