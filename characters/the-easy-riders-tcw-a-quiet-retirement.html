The Easy Riders

aka Easy Riders

Billy Fonda and Peter Hopper, Karen Killer's bikers, who first appear on Badge of Honor in July 2008 to ambush Painful Procedure (Randall Hopkirk and Billy Jack Shearer) and keep coming back to the ring while Killer shrieks directions from ringside, and in August 2008 beat Painful Procedure and the Young Guns, lose a title match to the New Wave and lose to Kingman & Alexander, and in September 2008 beat Rick Law & Rocky Golden and the Disrespected, then lose to the Machines by disqualification, and in October 2008 win the tag tournament under their new manager Carl Batch, beating the Rising Stars and the Keith Brothers, then lose to the New Wave at Crunch Time by disqualification, and in November 2008 beat Danny Fonzarelli & Koshiro Ino, Tooth & Claw and the Young Guns, lose to the Stone Brothers and, at A Little South Of Sanity, are eliminated first in the three-team tag title match, and in December 2008 beat the Keith Brothers, Hell Monkey & Teddy Powell and Team VIPER, attack the New Wave with brass knuckles after Carl Batch demands a no-disqualification title match and at Psycho Circus beat the New Wave in a hardcore match to take the tag belts.
