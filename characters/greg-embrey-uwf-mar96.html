Tenure detail
Lost his own UWF debut to "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb by pinfall (the Atom Splitter) on 17 November 2004, caught with a throat shot after pausing mid-powerbomb; accompanied to the ring by valet Farmer Johnson's Daughter. Lost to "Your Savior" Jamie Underwood by pinfall (a second split-legged moonsault) on 1 December 2004 after manager Farmer Johnson's own daughter distracted the referee, warned beforehand by Embrey himself to stay at ringside. Was left on the outside for the whole of Amazing Grace's own four-way singles debut match on 8 June 2005, taking a dropkick off the apron before Grace pinned Mitzi Sukkora for the win. Lost a Road to the Gold qualifier to "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian on 22 June 2005. Lost to Tumaffi by referee stoppage on 6 July 2005 after the big man ignored repeated warnings and kept striking him, then was slapped by his own valet, Farmer Johnson's Daughter, after the match. Tormented backstage on 23 July 2005 by valet "Angst" (Farmer Johnson's Daughter, revealed as Samantha Palmer), who framed his own losing streak as a debt owed to her that only "death" would settle.
