Biography (PURE (2002))

For many generations the Fujimoto family have been known for their ninjitsu heritage. Ninjitsu starting nearly 800 years ago the Fujimoto family have come a long way since then and with each new generation the legacy is past on from father to son. However that was not the case when it came to Hiro Fujimoto and his father Takata. Hiro was more of a modern man in society and saw the art of ninjitsu as nothing but a waste of time. He was more worried about getting through life successfully rather then having the knowledge of the ninja. So it was with much rebellion that he defied his father’s wishes and went on to live an average citizen’s life. As the years went by Hiro had become quite the success story as he became co-owner of a hug software company. He was making large amounts of yen and he had his decision to focus mainly on school and everyday life rather then listening to his father’s ramblings of the ninja to thank for it. If that wasn’t great enough already he had also fallen in love with a beautiful woman he had been dating since university, Chika Goto. The two love birds would be wed and much to the disgust of Takata he was happy for his son. Hiro had made all the right choices and he had big plans to start a family and raise his children the way he had grew up, he was in for a big surprise. On August 26, 1976, Hiro’s wife Chika would give birth to a healthy baby boy who weighed in at nine pounds and three ounces. Hiro was overcome with joy at the birth of his first son and he knew that his son would go on to be just as successful as him one day. The happy couple named their son Naoki after Chika’s deceased uncle who had died just a few months earlier in a car wreck. Takata was also present at the birth of his grandson and when he first held him he knew that young Naoki would indeed be successful but not in the way his father planned. Takata made it possible so that he would spend a lot of his time with Naoki as he grew up. Hiro was usually busy at work and Chika also had a part time designing cakes and pastries for parties and special events. She did all this right from her very own kitchen of the large house that Hiro was able to buy in Tokyo, Japan where all the big business was. So often when Chika became too busy she would leave Naoki with his grandfather, how was she suppose to know that a warrior was in the making right under her very own nose? Having lost his chance with his own son Takata was determined to pass on the legacy of ninjitsu to Fujimoto blood and lucky Naoki was “the chosen one” so to speak. Takata trained him from the time he took his first step and although the training started out pretty gentle it would get more harsh and challenging as Naoki grew. When Naoki was old enough his parents sent him to one of the best schools in the city but Naoki never did speak a word of his ninjitsu training. He would go to school and study very hard then after school he would head on over to his grandfather’s to resume last night’s training. Naoki was never a very popular kid in class nor did he ever try to be. He liked to keep to himself and he would rarely speak. Most of this came from his grandfather’s training in that a ninja is very stealthy and only the weak draw attention to themselves. Naoki was a very smart boy however and he did well in school thanks to his parent’s money and his great attention span. Both his parents would find out what he was up to after school when Naoki turned ten and his father was shocked beyond belief. Hiro’s own father had been training his son in the art of ninjitsu behind his back for ten whole years. Although Naoki did well in school he was very passionate of keeping up with his training and against his father’s wishes he wanted to grow up to be a ninja just like his grandfather. He stayed in school though so he kept his parent’s happy but he bled the blood of a ninja. As Naoki got into his teens his training sessions with his grandfather became more intense and more physical. Often returning home with many cuts, sores and bruises from head to toe. In high school he remained the mysterious, silent kid and because of that he was often flocked by girls who were turned on by his persona. Lucky for the other single guys though Naoki did not let female flesh detain him from his studies and his training. Around this time on January 14, 1992 Naoki’s mother would give birth to his little sister, Mai. Naoki cared very much for his sister and always kept a close eye on her. Naoki continued on with his training and even began fighting in tournaments with his grandfather in his corner. Naoki grew stronger and stronger and after high school at the age of eighteen he had left school to permanently focus on his training and tournaments. Mai was now attending the same school Naoki first started out in and Hiro and Chika were happy that at least one of their kids were following the plans they had in store. Naoki’s grandfather grew weak in age so he stepped down from physical training and just mentored Naoki on his mind and mental abilities. Naoki would train against other fighters each week at the martial art dojo just outside of town. Mai would sometimes come to watch her brother as well as both Naoki’s mother and father who had come to accept the life he chose even though they would still favour he would get a real job. On September 28, 1996, a few weeks after Naoki’s twentieth birthday Takata, Naoki’s grandfather would die in his sleep from a heart attack. Naoki was of course deep down sad but he never let his feelings get in the way of his training as his grandfather taught him. He trained harder and faster and no doubt he had surpassed his grandfather in skill. He had pretty much mastered both physical and mental aspects of a ninja at such a young age and his grandfather’s dream was met, the legacy was handed over. Naoki continued to train and earn money in tournaments. He eventually raised enough to buy himself a small apartment in Mishima, Japan but he couldn’t continue living like this. Sure he was making money but barely enough to fully live off of. He needed a job but he did not want to abandon his training. Thus on January 4, 1997, he came across pro-wrestling. A wrestler could make pretty good money, even more then a ninja down on his luck. This would also allow Naoki to keep up with his training and earn better money to support himself as well. For the next five years Naoki trained in a wrestling dojo and did a few smalls shows now and then for both fighting and wrestling to keep the cash flow going at least. After his five years of wrestling he was brought into his first federation on March 16, 2002. The Mishima Wrestling Alliance was a regional federation right in his very own hometown and it was very convenient for him. Naoki had since learned most of the basic moves of wrestling as well as some amazing high flying spots that he could pull off with ease. Ninjitsu was still his base though and it showed in all of his matches. He was one of the most talented workers on the roster. In the May of 2002 Naoki would come to meet Nicolas Grimes. Nicolas Grimes was originally a wrestler himself born in Salt Lake City, Utah of the U.S.A. Naoki found himself in a heated feud with this gaijin. Naoki was far more superior in skill then Grimes. Nicolas however was very charismatic and came off very arrogant whenever around someone. He often gloated about himself but once in the ring with Naoki he was quickly silenced with a swift kick to the jaw. Naoki got the pin fall ten seconds into the match making it the quickest match in MWA history. Naoki could not stand Grimes and he was just glad that he jus shut the white boy up for good. However fate would play against Naoki as the booker found the duo of Naoki and Nicolas Grimes to be quite the pair. He enjoyed their stint together so much as enemies that he actually made Nicolas Grimes Naoki’s manager and moth piece as Naoki was still his old quiet self. Naoki was not very impressed but he did not bother to argue as he had more important things to worry about so he was forced to tolerate the annoying presence of Nicolas Grimes. Grimes didn’t know when to keep his big mouth shut though and he would often get the two into trouble which Naoki would have to get them out of. Grimes was more of a liability then help but Naoki kept quiet. Naoki would go on to capture the MWA World Heavyweight Title in July and would hold the belt until the MWA eventually went bankrupt in October. Naoki was now desperate for work once again but he thought he would go on a more global basis. So Naoki went back to Tokyo for a month to visit his family and then he boarded a plane and flew to North America. Unlucky for him Nicolas Grimes decided to tag along to return to his homeland. Naoki had studied some English in school so he was fluent in the language but he still kept quiet. For the next few months Naoki and Nicolas Grimes would appear in random federations and wrestle matches for them all. This continued into 2003 until eventually both Naoki and Nicolas Grimes returned to Japan in January. A new federation had caught Naoki’s eye though, Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment or better known as PURE. A much larger company then the MWA and with even more skilled athletes that Naoki could test himself against. Nicolas Grimes got into negotiations with some of the PURE staff and was able to get an interview with the PURE owner, Mr. Jamar Wilson himself. Mr. Wilson was indeed impressed with Naoki and within a few short weeks Naoki found himself backstage a PURE event taping up his fists. Now finally in PURE Naoki hopes to test himself against the best PURE has to offer him in the ring while Nicolas Grimes is blinded by the stardom and merchandise. Will Naoki fit in within these new surroundings or will he find himself in troubled situations? It is all downhill from here.
