Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Fumihiro Ota

aka The Great Ota

A veteran ninja and former TCW Cruiserweight Champion who answers with gong bongs and smoke pellets, debuts as Max Mayhem's replacement in May and befriends Hugh Lee. With Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh he wins the Trios titles from The Firm at Hotter Than Hot in July 2010 and keeps them at Summernova and Eleventh Hour; he draws with Firebird at Summer Showdown in June, loses a three-way for the Heavyweight title at Summernova, and loses to Ricochet's Cross Armbreaker at Fall Of The King and to Toxin's cradle at Where It All Begins Again. Firebird names Evil Spirit as his former student. He loses to Jay Chord at Remember November in November 2010, loses the Trios titles at Season's Beatings and loses to Antonio in a qualifier at End Of Days. He is attacked by Firebird, Evil Spirit and Thrash in February 2011, beats Evil Spirit with the Ninja Strike at Third Time's The Charm and Primus Allen at March Madness. He loses to Hugh de Aske at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 in a Ninja versus Pirate Traditional title match, beats Jared Johnson at High Noon and JD Morgan at Summer Showdown, and loses a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews at Hotter Than Hot. He loses with the Young Americans to Jay Chord, Casey Valentine and Antonio at Summernova and, with Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh, beats the Canadian Blondes and Raphael at Back To School; at $1,500 a show he heads the narrator's list of expendables. He loses to Providence at Where It All Begins Again, is made into Team OMGsplosion with Hugh de Aske at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011, loses a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews at Season's Beatings after a Lockdown beating, teams with Randy Bumfhole and Kirk Jameson at End Of Days, and at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012 beats Providence in the first round and loses the semi-final to JD Morgan. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses the Triple Threat for the All Action title to JD Morgan at All Or Nothing, beats Eddie Powell at South East Crisis and Lenny Brown at Spring Fever with the Ninja Strike, and loses with Amazing Fire Fly to Primus Allen and Lenny Brown at May The Force Be With You on 5th May; the narrator notes that Black Eagle upset him at FCW's last show. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he appears backstage at South East Madness on 9th June, in the dark, to challenge Aaron Andrews, and loses the Heavyweight title match, the narrator's thirty-fourth defence for the champion, to the Twisting Face Crusher after thirty minutes, before taking off his hood in the ring and standing lost in thought. At Summer Showdown on 12th June he drops Donnie J with a Ninja Strike and joins The Fallen, painted in black and white with kanji symbols, and wishes to be called The Great Ota; he beats KC Glenn at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July and curb-stomps him, beats Amazing Fire Fly at Beach Blast on 12th July, loses with Providence to Kirk Jameson and JD Morgan at Summernova, beats Peverell and Jameson with Firebird at South East Grand Parade, loses with Andrews to Rich Money and Jameson at Iron, and draws with Randy Bumfhole at Back To School on 5th September. Andrews says he refused to join The Fallen unless Ota did. Written by AI, 2026
