Tenure detail
A former wrestling announcer the Phenomenons declared their best friend years ago; drafted into the Army against his will, twice lost his home to them, and by the time of their NAPW run has "accepted that willingly following them might be less destructive to his life than being surprised again." Currently their videographer, per the application's own account, "because the Phenomenons don't believe Canadians have video cameras."
