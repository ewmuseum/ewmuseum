Kingman & Alexander

aka Conquering Kings

Barry Kingman and Clark Alexander, a newish undercard tag team who run the Machines close in July 2008, beat them on Total Wrestling in Week 3 and lose to Tommy Cornell and Wolf Hawkins on Badge of Honor, and in August 2008 lose to Tyler & Huggins and to American Buffalo & Frankie Perez, then beat the Easy Riders and the British Lions, and in September 2008 beat the Fly Boys and, as the Conquering Kings, American Buffalo & Fumihiro Ota, before the Fly Boys and the Buffalo beat them down, and in October 2008 are in a no contest with the Fly Boys and lose to the Easy Riders, and in November 2008 beat Miyazaki & Ota, are ambushed by American Buffalo and lose to the Stone Brothers, and in December 2008 beat the British Lions and the Elite Express with the Crippler Bomb.
