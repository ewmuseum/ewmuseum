Troy Windham

Troy Windham carried a CSWA World Heavyweight Championship reign, and an AAWC Television title he was still defending in July 1997, into NFW's touring Brawl shows, where he won a No Disqualification match at Brawl 47 in Detroit in 2012 across a run that opened at the Season 2 Ultratitle Preseason in 2005.
