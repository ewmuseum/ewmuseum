Hot Property

"All That" Austin Lee and "Hotstuff" Stevie Knight, the reigning VXW Tag Team Champions, retaining the titles by disqualification against Purgatory and Team Brittania at Vegas Violence V, then losing a non-title four-way tornado tag to Purgatory at Fear Is Coming II. Both men entered the Rage In The Cage main event at Phear Phantastic 2005 as co-champions; Austin Lee pinned his own partner Stevie Knight for the match's own first fall, an outcome the broadcast leaves unresolved as to the tag titles' own status. The promotion's own management publicly rejected a stripping rumor on Setting Sun I, confirming Hot Property retained the titles; the team then survived a Tornado Tag Team title defense against The O'Malley Brothers on Christmas Comes Early that ended in a double-pin with no official decision rendered. Retained the VXW Tag Team Championship in a scored Irish Street Fight over The O'Malley Brothers on New Years Eve Of Destruction, Chris Wheeling's own hired men delivering the deciding points. Ran in against the O'Malley Brothers on Winter War II, then teamed with Thunder Valenkov on Winter War III in a losing effort against the Society of Doom that cost the trio a shot at the tag and 24 Hour titles at Ghost Town Blues. VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-05-17 – 2005-04-11
Tenure detail
Wins the VWF Tag Team Championship from the Rodeo Boys in a Flashback ambush on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminated from the Dog Day One tag team battle royal before winning the Dog Days Tag Team Elimination match. Wins the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round over Sonicman and Sebastian, then joins Flash in beating down the Rodeo Boys during a special Flashback on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) before competing in the sixteen-man tag scramble. Returns to the VWF at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), interrupting the retiring Sebastian and G-Gangsta's own post-match celebration and attacking them, only for both Legends to fight the pair off with a Legend Killer and a Gangsta Smash. Runs in on Mark McQueen during his own VWF Tag Team Championship challenge with Thunder Valenkov at End Of March (30 March 2005), Stevie Knight and Austin Lee hitting the Property Boom to help champions Team Brittania escape by disqualification. Wins a VWF Tag Team Championship match against Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov, the previous champions, at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), when Valenkov turns on his own partner McQueen; a returning Chris Wheeling reveals himself as the agent behind the turn and presents the team new contracts.
