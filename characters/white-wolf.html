Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Appearance: White Wolf is prone to changing a lot... Some may say he's lost it. Nowadays he is bald. A pale complexion complements his naturally dark circled eyes. His attire consists of a black bondage suit that is covered in black nylon straps and buckles hanging at random spots. White Wolf wears big black worn-out work boots. His hands are always covered with gloves made from bear skin. Many piercings cover his ear. Despite his demonic appearance White Wolf has an amazing smile, although very eerie. His dark blue eyes are very hypnotic. Yes... he is now bald... Gimmick: White Wolf is exactly the kind of person nobody wants as a friend or enemy. His gimmick is his upbringing by a nomadic tribe of Wolf worshipping cannibles. The tribe took their learnings from the Wolves, and there by lived with the Wolf and died with the wolf... Mysteriously possessing the body of a lost soul White Wolf inhabits the mind of a young man who lost everything he had. As for his personality, White Wolf is a still man, who enjoys the company of his Wolf Murdoc, and is annoyed easily by human interaction. A recovered alcoholic, White Wolf is apparently now back into his intense habbits of snapping at everyone around him. Background: Raised by the Mowglou tribe in the arctic. Trained by Elder Gray Wolf, White Wolf grew up in fear that his tribe might turn on him. However, White Wolf did possess parents at an infantile stage. However, his parents were killed and he was supposed to be raised by his father's brother. But White Wolf was not wanted, by anyone it would seem, and ran away when he became 4. He didn't really plan on getting far. In fact he was going to go home. Until he was kidnapped by a nomadic tribe and forced into a life of terrifying morals and barbaric ways of life.
