Announced himself as a former WWO Television Champion returning after three years away, accompanied by valet Talia DiMartino and bodyguard Swoll; won his own UWF debut over "Gunman" Danny Cole by pinfall (a shooting star press) on 17 November 2004. Beat Steve Ryott by pinfall (a cradle piledriver) on 25 May 2005. Talked back to Tumaffi backstage on 11 June 2005 after the big Samoan injured a try-out wrestler, then beat him by disqualification, luring him into hitting the ringpost with a chair and playing dead to fool the referee. Beat Edward Drake in the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament's own first round on 6 July 2005 with a cradle piledriver, then exposed Tumaffi hiding under a ringside table and showed him up in retaliation for a loss to Quesada at the Road to the Gold. Lost a random-partners tag match teamed with Jason Keening against Michael Augustine and Victor Frost on 23 July 2005, pinned by a cradle brainbuster after Augustine broke up an earlier three count. Lost a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament quarterfinal to "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun by submission (the Von Braun Leglock) on 18 March 2006, after a ringpost leg assault.
