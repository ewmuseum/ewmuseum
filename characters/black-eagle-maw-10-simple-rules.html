Black Eagle

A masked veteran who does not speak for himself, signed in June 2008 and managed by Sienna LeNoir, who finishes with the New Jersey Turnpike. He is beaten by Ultimate Phoenix in a Loser Leaves Town match at Fall Of The King in September 2009 and leaves the company, his contract up. Rip Chord brings him back at April Showers in April 2011 after his Loser Leaves Town defeat; he beats Jonnie Perez with the New Jersey Turnpike and Firebird, who forced him out in 2009, greets him with an embrace. He returns at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 with Sienna LeNoir at his side, loses to Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson with Firebird, loses a Heavyweight title challenge to Andrews on 12th May and the Dark Raptors three-way on 26th May, and loses to Hugh de Aske at Summer Showdown in their fight over Sienna, to Randy Bumfhole at Hotter Than Hot and to Chance Fortune at Beach Blast. Rumours he spreads about Antonio earn a stern warning from the narrator; at Iron he accepts Hugh's challenge for Sienna, loses to the Cutthroat Driver and then smashes Hugh with the ring bell as Sienna leans in for a kiss. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 Kirk Jameson beats him and he leaves on a gurney with Sienna LeNoir behind him; he fights Hugh de Aske to a draw at Where It All Begins Again for Sienna's love. With Firebird as the Dark Raptors he wins the All Action title for a few minutes at Remember November, spreads rumours about Jared Johnson that earn him a dressing down from the narrator, takes Sienna back on 26th December and loses to Fumihiro Ota in an Invitational qualifier. In the diary's pages from February 2012 he loses to Kirk Jameson at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February and is fined by the narrator for spreading rumours about Rock Downpour, is pinned by Randy Bumfhole's Bumfhole Buster when the Dark Raptors lose to KC Glenn and Bumfhole at All Or Nothing, and then has spinal surgery, the surgeon saying he should walk again in two months but that it may be a case of waiting to see whether he wrestles again, the flight from Tokyo after his GCG injury having aggravated it. In the narrator's April news his ex-girlfriend Raven Nightfall retires, and he upsets Fumihiro Ota at FCW's last show. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he returns freshly healed from spinal surgery at Summer Showdown on 12th June with Sienna LeNoir, Firebird's partner as The Dark Raptors, and the pair win the Tag Team titles from Smith & Singh; the narrator warns him, a night after his return, to stop messing about and living his gimmick, and Antonio resents the title he was handed. He loses to KC Glenn at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July and to JD Morgan at Beach Blast on 12th July, wins the All Action title backstage at Iron on 12th August with Firebird's help, only to be pinned for it the same night by Donnie J, and keeps the Tag Team titles against the West Texas Peacekeepers, the XX Factor and Spectrum Infinity. At Back To School on 5th September he taps out to Team Honor's Carolina Crossface, shoves Sienna away and is kicked by her; his contract then expires and, the narrator writes, he will not negotiate and leaves the company.
