UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-09-24 – 2005-08-06
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 17 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 9-6-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Teamed with his brother Chad as the Sons of Cacaphony, upsetting the former tag champions the Harlequins. Lost a non-title tag match to the Privateers on 25 October 2003 when Edward Drake, not the legal man, struck Chad with a hidden lead weight for an illegal pin. Teamed with brother Chad in the Tower of Doom on 15 November 2003, escaping the cage first among his own teammates but taking a battering from Sabbat Justice on the arena floor afterward; the pair's escape as a complete team won them the UWF North American Tag Team Championship away from their own side's champions, the Ghost Dancers. The first man eliminated from the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match, pinned by Bob Jablonski after manager Kari Stevens cracked him with a hockey stick behind the referee's back. Competed in the six-man tag on 10 March 2004, part of the losing side against Team Canada and Scott Daniels after two masked interlopers ambushed the match. Retained the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with brother Chad on 21 March 2004 over the Universal Powers, the Sky Hammer combination finishing Bobby Lincoln after both teams traded dives to the floor. Teamed with Michael Keening in a scrambled-partners tag on 5 May 2004 against Niklos Romani and Todd Franklin; the bout ended as a no-contest after Franklin turned on his own partner and finished Grimsson himself with a New Horizon Line. Lost the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with brother Chad on 28 August 2004 in a street fight against the Universal Powers, knocked out by an unidentified masked interloper from inside a locker-room closet before Todd Franklin's superkick finished him for the pin; the Privateers separately ambushed Chad at ringside during the same match. Teamed with brother Chad and Alex Martinez to beat the Universal Powers (Bobby Lincoln, Todd Franklin) and Randall Osbourne on 22 September 2004, combining with Martinez for a version of the Harlequins' own Reality Check on Todd Franklin for the pin. Teamed with brother Chad in a losing effort against "Hentai" Ryu Osawa and Michael Bonn by disqualification on 16 October 2004, the Sons of Cacaphony's own side disqualified after Erik attacked a non-competing Jacob Drake at ringside; the finish set off a building-wide brawl that ultimately knocked the broadcast off the air, Erik and Michael Augustine's own fight atop the production truck among the last scenes shown. Teamed with Chad Grimsson as the Sons of Cacaphony in a no-contest against Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian) on 17 November 2004, taken out by the Privateers' own High-Seas Shipwreck when they ran in to end the match. Ran in with brother Chad and Alex Extreme to save Lisa Drake from a beating by the Youngbloods on 27 November 2004, then helped carry her out on a makeshift gurney; threatened the Privateers with a UWF World Tag Team Championship challenge, set for Rise to Power alongside Chad. Retained the UWF World Tag Team Championship with brother Chad over the Privateers by pinfall (an inverted backslide DDT, the Metal Meltdown) on 28 May 2005, after interference from Fury (Rage, Spice) at ringside disrupted the challengers. Helped break up a post-match beatdown of Alex Extreme and Jason Keening on 11 June 2005 alongside Alex Martinez, then teamed with brother Chad in a non-title four corners tag main event, eliminated by disqualification after Caliban struck both Grimssons with a steel chair. Drank with the Mercenary, Alex Extreme, and Alex Martinez at a Memphis club on 22 June 2005, vowing revenge on the Hands of Death alongside brother Chad. Berated by Alex Extreme backstage on 20 July 2005 over the loss of the World Tag Team Championship. Kidnapped and chained up Caliban inside the Descent Into Hell structure on 23 July 2005, challenging the Hands of Death to defend the tag titles in that match at Gold Rush. Teamed with brother Chad and Corey Irons to beat the Hands of Death and Trey DaMann by pinfall on 3 August 2005, Irons pinning Caliban; called out the Hands of Death with Chad at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005 and was singed by Serge Annis's own pyrotechnics when no confrontation came.
