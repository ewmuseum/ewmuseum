Jack Harmen

aka High Flyer, Neighborhood Lunatic, the Neighborhood Lunatic

Worked Action cards as High Flyer in 2002 and 2003; won a War Games match at Crash TV 50 in 2009 and, in 2011, under order from NFW’s Board of Directors, dropped the ‘High Flyer’ ring name, going 29-21 across a run from 2007 to 2013; as High Flyer in PRIME held the 5 Star Championship for 166 days from October 2008 and, with Tony Davis as Team V.I.A.G.R.A., the Tag Team Championship three times, and was inducted with them into its Hall of Fame in 2012.
