Tenure detail
Runs in during Sebastian's Firestorm match against The Equalizer, sleeperholding Sebastian and shoving him into a faceplant, before teaming with The Equalizer in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Teams with The Equalizer in the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round, losing to Marcus O'Malley wrestling alone; boxes with Mark McQueen in the sixteen-man tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), while the arena's own video screens separately show an unidentified second Orion attacking Sebastian, an angle left unresolved. Eliminated from the Dog Days tournament in the Tag Team Elimination Match on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then wins the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal and the tag Pentagon of Pain with The Equalizer on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) to reach the Dog Days tag final; the broadcast again shows what the announcers call a second Orion active at ringside during the battle royal, as on Dog Day Three's own video screens, unresolved. Won alongside The Equalizer on Wrestle Style V.1, saw a signed tag match collapse before the bell on Wrestle Style V.2, then is credited with two separate opening-round VWF Heavyweight Title tournament results on Wrestle Style V.3: a count-out win over Mark McQueen billed "Orion #2", and a double-knockout draw with Jason Storm billed "Gods Tears" and named on commentary as Orion The Hunter without his mask. The broadcast needs 24 distinct tournament entrants for its own bracket to work, which is the strongest evidence yet that these billings may represent two figures rather than one; no name is given for either, and a single continuity record is kept pending further evidence. The broadcast again bills a second man under the Orion name across all three shows; registered once here per standing continuity presumption. Eliminated from a triple threat on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004) after interference, too injured to continue. Teams to a win over Jason Storm and Flash on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Counted out with Jason Storm during the seven-man mini rumble's own chaos on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004). Appears on a video screen threatening Yancy Kilpatrick's wife Melanie before a scheduled Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) match with Flash goes unwrestled, and wins the match by count-out after the scheduled opponent, attacked backstage minutes before the bell, never appears. Pulls The Equalizer out from under a Jason Storm cover attempt and restores the match's own original referee during the VWF Heavyweight Championship main event, both interventions helping The Equalizer take the title from Lark Fenriz. Wins the six-man tag for Trinity over Jason Storm, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Flash on March To Hell (21 October 2004), stopped from handing Orion #2 a pair of brass knuckles when Roadkill Rick pulls him off the apron; the same broadcast unmasks the previously unidentified second Orion figure by name as Pagan, now registered as a separate character. Wins a non-title count-out over The Maquilladora on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) that leaves the VWF Submission Championship with Maq under the promotion's own house rule against count-out title changes, boasting on air about recruiting The Equalizer and a masked partner into a new Trinity; chases Jason Storm up the ramp with that partner ahead of Storm's own post-show ambush of The Equalizer. Submits The Maquilladora with the Drunken Rage to win the VWF Submission Championship in the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), also breaking up a Fast Eddie Cutlass pin attempt on Maq earlier in the same match. Helps The Equalizer and Pagan attack a beaten Fast Eddie Cutlass, Lark Fenriz and Classy Joey Boy after Pagan's own video threat cancels their scheduled six-man tag on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Pitches Kingston Cash on a self-arranged VWF Hardcore Championship invitational and wins Cash's own recognition of the new title on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), then joins the post-match beatdown of Lark Fenriz and Fast Eddie Cutlass, scattered by an unidentified woman's own three wolves. Billed the new VWF Hardcore Champion on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after winning the title off-page at his own GT's Invitational; assaults backstage vendor Spaz and Sidious The Clown on the arena's own big screens with a lead pipe during the night's own no-contest main event until newly arrived guardian John Tyler orders him to stand down under threat of a double title strip and suspension. Already champion entering Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), defends the VWF Hardcore and Submission Championships together against Sidious The Clown and Lark Fenriz, losing the Submission Championship on a disputed submission call while the VWF Hardcore Championship goes unaddressed. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) as the surprise final three-man entry alongside Pagan and The Equalizer, eliminated by his own stablemate Pagan's clothesline meant for Thunder Valenkov. Defends the VWF Hardcore and Submission Championships together with Sidious The Clown against Lark Fenriz in a Scaffold Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005); Sidious kicks both other men off the scaffold together and Fenriz hits the ground first, but the broadcast states neither title changes hands, leaving Gods Tears with the VWF Hardcore Championship. Teams with The Equalizer against Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora at March To Madness (2 March 2005), the match declared a no contest after two masked men interfere on both sides; sucker-punched and attacked afterward by a returning Roadkill Rick during the Roll Of The Dice segment, setting a VWF Hardcore Championship defense against him. Retains the VWF Hardcore Championship against Roadkill Rick at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), fought to a draw when neither man answers referee's own ten count after a Cobra Clutch Suplex through a table. Retains again against Fast Eddie Cutlass at End Of March (30 March 2005), a time-limit draw. Retains the VWF Hardcore Championship without a change on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), attacking Sidious The Clown before the bell in the eight-man elimination match and later disqualified alongside Roadkill Rick in a chair-and-brass-knuckles standoff. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, eliminated alongside Adam Jennings on a Randall Crane double clothesline.
