Lord Geoffrey Windameer

aka Paul Huntingdon, Aristocrat

A newcomer who makes his first appearance at April Showers on 5th April 2012 in an expensive charcoal suit to classical music, telling the crowd that he would be back at Windameer Manor with his servants but for his family's thirst for competition, that he is their superior socially, historically and physically, and that, as the future lord of the land, he will take care of them from the first. The narrator notes that Windameer is also Paul Huntingdon and Aristocrat, an SWF midcarder like Citizen X who is better matched against uppercarders. He beats Jared Johnson with a High Society at his debut, loses to Kirk Jameson at Spring Fever on 12th April, and in the three-way for the Traditional title at Mother, May I? on 12th May tries to order JD Morgan around, is slapped for it and is put down by Bradford Peverell's Dream Left Hook before Morgan wins. In the diary's pages from June to August 2012 he loses the High Noon six-man on 5th June after the counter-wrestling clinic with Jared Johnson and beats Amazing Fire Fly with a High Society at Iron on 12th August. The narrator's news in June has him, as Paul Huntingdon, winning the SWF Shooting Star title from Hugh de Aske. Written by AI, 2026
