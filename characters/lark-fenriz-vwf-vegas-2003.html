2003-11-15 – 2005-03-17

Tenure detail
Confesses on the microphone during Firestorm (26 Feb 2004) to being Classy Joey Boy's own attacker at Ghost Town Blues; interferes in the Cutlass/Equalizer match but does not wrestle one himself that night. Interrupts the Kagedo/Sidious The Clown match and wins the Team Tournament Final alongside Fast Eddie Cutlass and The Equalizer on Firestorm (3 May 2004), securing a Gauntlet entry; commentary refers to him throughout as the reigning VWF Heavyweight Champion, apparently at odds with the vacancy declared the previous Firestorm. Retains the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship in a Japanese Death Match against Sidious The Clown, then wins the Gauntlet battle royal outright over Kagedo; commentary and the ring announcer continue billing him as the reigning champion throughout, an inconsistency the reigns of record do not corroborate. Retains a claimed VWF Heavyweight Championship over Kagedo with LOCO's interference on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then wins the Dog Day One singles battle royal and the Dog Days Singles Elimination match, knocking out Daniel Sinz with brass knuckles to pin The Maquilladora. Scratched from his own three-way match by majority owner Kingston Cash on Dog Days 2 (22 June 2004), sent backstage before the bell; returns to competition, without the title on the line, in the eight-man singles scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), targeted early and often by Morris Bass, Jason Storm and Sidious The Clown. Retains the VWF Heavyweight Championship over Daniel Sinz on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then wins the night's own Fourway Final on a disputed double-cover ruling by majority owner Kingston Cash to claim the Dog Day Four tournament; eliminated from the singles Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), spun into barbed wire by a kick meant for Jason Storm. The Heavyweight Championship main event on Wrestle Style V.1 ends without a finish when a returning Classy Joey Boy interferes; retains the title with the Sammael over a returning Billy Bones on Wrestle Style V.3, the opening defense of a new random-draw tournament. Wrestles The Predator Kevin Simmons to a time-limit draw in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004); both men advance. Retains in a fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) over Classy Joey Boy, Sebastian and The Assassin Marcus O'Malley, pinning Sebastian with the Sammael. Signs the contract for a Vegas Violence title defense against The Equalizer on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004); a video appearance by Jason Storm inserts him into the match as a third man, and the champion declares he no longer cares about the title, only about denying The Equalizer. Loses the VWF Heavyweight Championship to The Equalizer in a three-way also involving Jason Storm at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), pinned after a chair shot from guest referee Flash; Orion's own interference and a mid-match referee dispute between guest referees Classy Joey Boy and Flash both factor into the finish. Drives a forklift into the ring and canoe-paddles a trapped Flash bloody with barbed wire after Trinity's own six-man tag against Jason Storm, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Flash on March To Hell (21 October 2004), despite having no match of his own. Banned from the building at the top of March To Hell II (28 October 2004) after that attack, but slips in at the close of the night to pull The Equalizer from a brawl with Jason Storm and hit the Sammael on him. Enters the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004) uninvited despite the standing ban, press-slamming Iyasu Saku out for the final elimination to qualify for the Rage in the Cage, then loses the final fall of that cage match and his own VWF Heavyweight Championship challenge to The Equalizer, driven through two tables with a Face Plant after Pagan's own Final Judgement. Named on air ahead of a scheduled six-man tag against Trinity that never happens after Pagan's own video threat against Fast Eddie Cutlass on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Vows revenge in a taped promo, then pins Fast Eddie Cutlass with the Sammael to win a triple threat also involving Classy Joey Boy on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), the Trinity attack that follows scattered only by an unidentified woman's own three wolves. Wrestles Trinity to a no contest alongside Fast Eddie Cutlass on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after their own mystery third partner never arrives, nearly pinning The Equalizer before the interference began. Loses the VWF Hardcore and Submission Championship three-way to Sidious The Clown on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) after the referee mistakes his own tap on a nearby counter for champion Gods Tears's. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Classy Joey Boy's own Kiss of Death. Challenges Gods Tears and Sidious The Clown for the VWF Hardcore and Submission Championships in a Scaffold Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), hitting the ground first after Sidious kicks both other men off the scaffold together. Loses to the debuting Mad Bomber at March To Madness (2 March 2005), powerbombed after a small explosion near the entranceway knocks him down. Ambushed in the Orleans parking lot before St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) by the same two masked men who interfered in the Equalizer and Gods Tears's own tag match the week before, powerbombed onto a Hummer's hood and hospitalized with a pierced kidney; withdrawn from the scheduled Five Way elimination match.
