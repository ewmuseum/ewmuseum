The Hotshots

The tag team of Davis Wayne Newton and Cameron Vessey, beaten in May 2007 by Wanda Fish and Giant Oak and by Bulldozer Brandon Smith and Trent Shaffer. In October 2007 they work as Triple D's apprentices: they beat down Sam Strong, lose to The Dudez!!!1! at A Haunting in America, and are arrested on the Thursday of Week 3 for the "murder" of a pigeon. In October and November 2007 they are jailed for the murder of the pigeon Mr. Squiggles, found guilty and sentenced to 20 hours of community service, beaten bloody by Veteran Mentality, lose to them at Pilgrimage, are made members of Divinity there, win the World Tag Team Championship from The Valentines in 1:56 and keep it against them on the Monday of Week 3. In November and December 2007 they sit through community service as substitute teachers and in a nursing home, are chased by Jim Force and Mr. Snuggleforce, beat Veteran Mentality and Pure Evil, win a count-out over The Dudez!!!1!!!, lose a non-title cage match to them, and are told by Devine to win at Winter Epiphany.
