2003-10 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 1 parsed match, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 0-1-0
Tenure detail
Settles the Blair/Valenkov/Ric Beauty standoff on Firestorm (3 May 2004), naming Ric the current and Valenkov the former VWF 24 Hour Champion and booking a Triple Threat at the Gauntlet. Confronted backstage by a shadowy figure, later revealed as new majority owner Kingston Cash, over her own promise that Thunder Valenkov would be "taken care of." Wrestles for the New Age Army alongside The Legend of Xtreme, working over Kagedo's knee at ringside, in the ten-man tag loss to the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1; majority owner Kingston Cash announces the New Age Army's release from the VWF later the same broadcast. Rolls dice on behalf of client The Dark Monster Tai Angela at March To Madness (2 March 2005). Calls for the mic after Angela beats Capsman at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), warning Gods Tears his own time is nearly over. Distracts Roadkill Rick at End Of March (30 March 2005), helping Angela to a schoolboy win. Blackbird, manager to Tai Angela, is put in a chokehold at ringside by Kenny Kidd during the eight-man elimination match on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after signaling Angela that Revolution Randall Crane was making his own surprise return.
