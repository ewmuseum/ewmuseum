Sean Deeley

A young Canadian amateur champion turned professional whom Eric Tyler trained on his days off, who arrives on Badge of Honor in July 2008 to team with Joel Bryant against the New Wave, loses his first full match to Johnny Bloodstone and, as part of the School of Tradition, tries to help Tyler against Aaron Andrews at Summer Showdown, and in August 2008 beats the Cannonball Kid with the Front Choke Lock, helps the School of Tradition against Rick Law and loses with Joel Bryant to the New Wave at Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 beats Andrews & Minnesota with the Sure Shot armlock, earns a tag title rematch by proving to Scout he can break the Special Force, loses to Johnny Bloodstone and, with Joel Bryant, loses the rematch at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 draws with John Anderson for the International Championship, loses to Rick Law and, at Crunch Time, answers Anderson's open challenge and beats him by count-out. In November 2008 he loses with Freddy Huggins to Art Reed & Joey Minnesota, loses to Johnny Bloodstone and to Brent Hill, wins a six-man match with Chris Rockwell and Frankie Perez, is hit twice by Freddy Huggins' kick and is passed over by Eric Tyler for the All Action Championship four-way. In December 2008 he loses to Joey Minnesota, debuts the Sure Shot armlock in a no contest with Aaron Andrews, beats the British Lions with Art Reed, beats Joel Bryant and Freddy Huggins with the front choke lock, loses to Remo Richardson, turns away from the School of Tradition and, at Psycho Circus, pulls Jeremy Stone off Troy Tornado to break up a count.
