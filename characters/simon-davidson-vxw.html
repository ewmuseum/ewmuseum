Tenure detail
Debuts with brother Justin as Team Brittania at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), inserting the team into a VWF Tag Team Championship match already underway between champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov and challengers Frank O'Shea and Billy Bones; pins O'Shea to win the titles after Justin pulls Valenkov from the ring to stop a cover. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, pulling down Austin Lee off the apron behind the referee's back before being eliminated himself minutes earlier in a group toss by Thunder Valenkov.
