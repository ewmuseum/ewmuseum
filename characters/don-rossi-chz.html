Character · CHZ (1999)

Don "The Game" Rossi

The CHZ North American Champion, attacked backstage by Rage before retaining his title against Diablo by disqualification on the 22 March 2002 Wild show. He lost a North American Championship match to Rob Lewis at BreakOut 2002, won the Alpha Championship on 26 March 2003, lost it to the Darkness at May HayDay 2, and announced his retirement on 21 May.
