Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Roderick Remus

aka Rod Remus

A project the narrator brings in after Beach Blast in July 2010: his brother's motorbike accident in 2006 put the brother into retirement and left Rod on his own, and he had not had his head in the game since. He teams with Jean Cattley to beat Jonnie Perez and Kirk Jameson at Summernova, beats Perez with the Remus Clutch in the awful opener of All The Leaves Are Brown, and is one of Jared Johnson's trial partners in the Trios title match at Eleventh Hour in November 2010. He is still rusty in December 2010 and joins Jared Johnson and Nelson Callum as The Triple Threat at Season's Beatings, winning the Trios titles. He keeps the Trios titles with The Triple Threat until Summernova on 12th August 2011; on the tour shows he beats Max Mayhem and Matt Hocking and loses to Kashmir Singh and Kid Arachnid and, as Rod Remus, with Nelson Callum to the Internet Favourites. In the diary's pages for August 2012 the narrator reports that he left when his contract expired, ending the attempts to make silk purses from sows' ears; of his Trios partners Nelson Callum is also gone and Jared Johnson still works for MAW.
