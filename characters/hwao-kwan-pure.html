Biography (PURE (2002))

<i>There is not much really known about Kwan. The only thing the world knows about him is that he was born on January 2, 1978 in Seoul, Korea, around the age of ten he was abandoned by his parents who remain unknown to the world today.<p> Kwan took care of his self for a few years until he was old enough to go out and look for a job, and this is where all the fun started. He was hired by an asian man by the name of Lee. Lee took care of Hwao from there on and took him to Japan where Hwao was introduced to Shotokan Karate, a powerful type of Martial Arts. During his training Kwan entered a Martial Arts tournment. He made it into the semi-finals and he was eliminated after he was Poisoned by one of the fighters. Kwan gained a Disease after the poison and he suddenly became blind in the right eye, and that is why it is red today. But the blindness didn't make Kwan any weaker, in fact he bacame stronger. He gained a power that he didn't have and he hated the world for it. Lee claimed Kwan as his son. From there on Lee sent Kwan out to do all his dirty work such as kidnapping, killing and stealing and this was just the begining of how Kwan became the demon he is.
