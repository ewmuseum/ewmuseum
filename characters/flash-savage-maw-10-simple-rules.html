Flash Savage

aka Saville

The other Canadian Blonde and tag champion, a technical worker with a Savage elbow drop from the top. Trent Shaffer's Heart Burn pins him at Back To School in September 2010; he and Oscar Golden then win the Tag Team titles for a record third reign at Fall Of The King, and at Where It All Begins Again in October he climbs a ladder to retrieve the belts and keep them. A prank by referee Bret Graveson on him backfires on a November tour show. He pulls down the top rope on Brandon Smith at Remember November in November 2010 and wins with the Canadian Crab at Season's Beatings; the Blondes lose the Tag Team titles to Cattley and Singh at New Year, Old Rivalries. He wins the Tag Team titles with Oscar Golden at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, locks Kashmir Singh in the Canadian Crab to keep them in the Mother, May I? three-way and joins the defences through the summer; he loses to Ernest Youngman on the 18th September tour show. At Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March 2012 he brawls with the larger Brandon Smith, tires, and is finished by Smith's Backdrop Driver; at Third Time's The Charm he is drilled by KC Glenn's Tune Up The Sunshine Band.
