My name is Akai Nagato and I was born right here in Asahikawa, Hokkaido, Japan. Both of my parents were of Japanese descent; honorable descent might I add, but that didn’t mean a damn thing. In November of 1979 we moved to a small, rural town in northern Maine. The best job my father could get was as a Senior Executive for a small business. The pay wasn’t bad, the living conditions were good, but the hell I had to go through wasn’t worth half of it all. Growing up, I was the only foreigner in my school, which made me the center of ridicule. Even in the early grades of fourth and fifth, my life was a living hell. I was called a “Jap” and any other type of discriminatory name they could think of. Everyday after school I would have to take the long way, through the woods, so I wouldn’t be jumped and beaten up. My life, at the point, truly sucked. The only good thing out of all of it was the fact that somehow I kept my grades up. I was always at the top of my class and received many academic awards. When I reached high school, it got even worst. For example, in ninth grade, a Caucasian boy named Steven punched me right in the jaw. For no fucking reason and because he got me off guard, I could barely defend myself. Our math teacher just stood there and watched...bitch. He picked me up and I got one shot in, right to his temple. At that time, the teacher intervened and sent ME to administration. Needless to say I was the one who got suspended. That was not the only shitty thing about high school. Girls would ask me out then stand me up. My house would be egged and vandalized. My little sister had to be home schooled. I was even pushed down the stairs once...life, for me, SUCKED. I can not count how many times I held a razorblade in my hand and staring at my wrist. Looking for the “sweet spot” that would take me out of this God-awful existence. I hope all those bastards and bitches suffer the same way they made me, somewhere down the line. College was better because I made sure it was out of the state of Maine. In fact, I landed an academic scholarship at the University of Connecticut where my education helped me out a lot. I had my first girlfriend there, and she had experience almost the same exact putrid youth I did. She was a Vietnamese woman, and she was beautiful. I gave my body to her and she gave me hers. One night of passion was all that I needed to ‘enjoy’ where I was in life. She was my whole world but after college we went our separate ways. Again, I thought about ending it all because I had nothing to live for anymore. I was sure I would not get a job I was qualified for because of my origin. My family had moved back to Asahikawa and I was now alone. I was now beginning to grow cold, hateful, and emotionless. I was checking Japanese web sites (dubbed in English because I had not learned the language that should be predominant one) and came across a site for a wrestling organization known as PURE. The site said the federation was aimed at keeping to its roots, and it really got my interested. I mean, I was following professional wrestling throughout college but it wasn’t the same. That was sports-entertainment...this is wrestling. The very bottom of the site read, “wrestlers wanted. Asians are more than welcome, but we will not turn down any ethnicity.” I booked the first flight I could back to Japan. I signed up, was accepted, and felt this was exactly why I was put on this Earth. I decided to use myself as a character and for my in-ring name...use the word Enmity. Why the name Enmity? Well, that was exactly how I felt about the world and most of the Caucasian race. This was my time to lash out at the world in a very expressive form. It was also a way to show the country of Japan this is where I belong... After tearing through PURE for a few months, I decided it was time to move on to bigger and better things. Even though I was the first ever Gekito Cup Winner...TokyoPop’s main attraction...was about to kick Blitz’s ass...AND put Jamar Wilson out of commission...PURE was not necessarily the place for me. Well, also, it didn’t look like PURE was going to the top like it had hoped. And me only caring about myself, I wanted to move into a fed that already had a big spot in the wrestling world. And that is why, my friends, I left PURE and signed a contract with... RUSH Wrestling...
