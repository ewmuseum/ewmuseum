The Hands of Death

A pairing of Serge Annis and Caliban billed together under this name on a 29 May 2004 card banner, having already teamed once before beating Fury on 21 March 2004. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-03-21 – 2005-08-06
Tenure detail
First teamed on 21 March 2004, beating Fury (Rage and Spice). Billed together under this name on a 29 May 2004 card banner, beating the Fraternity Boys in a tag confrontation, then fighting the Ghost Dancers to a double countout in a formal tag match later the same card. Challenged for the UWF World Tag Team Championship in a Gold Rush triple threat on 5 June 2004 against champions the Privateers and the Ghost Dancers, taken out of the match by a triple Conchairto from the Fraternity Boys (Chris O'Brien and Brian McKenzie) before the Privateers stole the pinfall. Beat Kyle Lee and Alex Kidd in a steel cage main event on 28 August 2004, Caliban pinning Kidd with a Near Death Experience superplex-and-slam combination after Lee escaped the cage but could not return in time to help. Lost to Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian) by pinfall on 2 October 2004 after Tucker's frozen-fish distraction; beaten down afterward by the returning Prophets of Rage with their own tag title belts. Billed as the new World Tag Team Champions as of 20 July 2005, having taken the titles from the Sons of Cacophony on the previous week's own unread Rampage (9 July 2005); no surviving document in the held record states the match itself. Caliban was kidnapped and chained up inside the Descent Into Hell structure by Erik Grimsson on 23 July 2005, who challenged the team to defend the titles there at Gold Rush. Lost with Trey DaMann to the Sons of Cacaphony and Corey Irons by pinfall on 3 August 2005, Irons pinning Caliban; recorded a Descent Into Hell promo, assaulted Nina Grimsson, and brutalized a non-competing Alex Extreme on 6 August 2005, then answered the Sons of Cacaphony's own ringside challenge with a taunting Unitron segment rather than a confrontation, singeing Erik Grimsson with pyrotechnics.
