Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Casey Valentine

A Firm heel favoured by Rip Chord, half of C-V-2 with Cameron Vessey, who finishes with the Sweet Sweet Heartbreak and has a gong-banging match with Fumihiro Ota. With Jared Johnson he keeps the Tag Team titles against the Internet Favourites at Beach Blast in July 2010, Morgan & Benning at Iron and a three-way at Back To School, and loses them to the Canadian Blondes at Fall Of The King in September. Jean Cattley accuses him at Summernova of trying to take over The Firm; injured, he and Johnson lose to Morgan & Benning at All The Leaves Are Brown, and at Where It All Begins Again in October he loses to Cattley for control of the group, Primus Allen and Jared Johnson turn on him and Cattley expels him. Rip Chord and the narrator keep him on after he snubs a move to CGC; he loses to Kirk Jameson in a qualifier at End Of Days in December 2010, is named as a possible cut in the tew.com rumours of January 2011 and wins several tour shows in February and March, the narrator saying he plans a push for Valentine around the summer. He returns at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, tells Jean Cattley that he is better than ever, and beats his old mentor in a Loser Leaves Town match with the Sweet Sweet Heartbreak; he names the Second Sons with Erik Strong and Antonio. He beats Randy Bumfhole and Kid Arachnid with Antonio on 26th May, loses to Steve Flash at High Noon and again at Summer Showdown, which the narrator calls his best match in MAW, upsets Kirk Jameson at Hotter Than Hot and Brandon Smith at Beach Blast, wins the six-man at Summernova with Jay Chord and Antonio and beats the Young Americans with Antonio at Back To School. In the diary's pages from March to May 2012 he loses to Kirk Jameson at Great Lakes Crisis, insulting Katie Cameron beforehand, wins with Antonio against the Internet Favourites at Third Time's The Charm, loses to Smith & Singh at March Madness and May The Force Be With You, wins the three-way at Spring Fever by pinning Marc Speed and beats Brandon Smith at Great Lakes Madness. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he is caught with Smith's Inverted Piledriver in the three-way at High Noon on 5th June, beats Team Honor with the Sweet Sweet Heartbreak at Summer Showdown on 12th June, where he and Antonio are drawn into The Fallen's locker room by Father Johan, and beats Smith & Singh at Beach Blast; his team loses at South East Grand Parade and at Iron on 12th August.
