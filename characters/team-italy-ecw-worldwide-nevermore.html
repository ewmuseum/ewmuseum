Team Italy

The Italian squad for the Wrestling World Cup of July 2007, introduced on Revolution in June with Little Guido as captain beside Tony Mamaluke and Sal E Graziano (the diary's Big Sal). In Pool A they beat the sWo once, lose to them once and twice to Team USA, and are knocked out when the sWo take the match that would have kept them in the race and Cuervo's spray paint helps Team USA beat them in the last round.
