Biography (RUSH (2002))

Nobody who meets Cassius Deamont ever has to ask how good he is, because he tells them inside thirty seconds. The silver-haired Torontonian left Canada the first chance he got ("Why do you think I moved away from the place?"), picked up what he insists was a first-rate education, and came out the other side convinced that talent is the only thing that matters and that he has all of it. He goes nowhere without Kathryn, his girlfriend of ten years, and Erica, the two bisexual valets who laugh at his jokes and kick whoever he points at. Cassius walked into RUSH as the number one contender and opened the very first Adrenaline in Chicago by calling the whole locker room worthless and Jonathan Drachen a pizza delivery boy. By the end of the segment Drachen was flat from a Fall Before Me and Cassius was shaking hands with Chris Matthews. Add Steve Brent and part-owner Tim Walden and you had the Solution, who spent May handcuffing Drachen, Skye Stone, Hiro Saotome and Tex Kelley inside a steel cage and setting the ring on fire, blowing up another ring two weeks later, and putting owner Jeremy Howard to sleep with Cassius's trusty blackjack. The one thing Cassius could not solve was Maxx Crimson, who pinned him in Cleveland. The cracks started with the valets. Cassius asked Matthews to teach Kathryn and Erica self-defence, Matthews tried a chat-up line on them instead, and Howard played the tape to the whole arena. Somehow it held together at Ignition. Matthews lay down to send Cassius to the main event, Cassius smashed the knees of Drachen's manager Kahlil Anil in a corridor, and one blackjack shot from Walden later he was the first ever RUSH World Champion. He celebrated by blackjacking Drachen, Howard, Matthews and Brent. As champion he threw in with Boda and the I.R.S. Plan and hired Erica two bodyguards, Rich Cannon and Vulcan Flare. Matthews answered by kidnapping Kathryn and blowing up Cassius's limo. Cassius kept winning anyway, over Tex Kelley, over Matthews in Hartford and over El Spiffy!, usually with Boda standing somewhere nearby. At Crossroads in Washington, Boda proudly named him the new 25 percent owner of RUSH. Cassius had already sold the share to Rush Mason, which left Boda owning nothing at all. Kathryn came home that night, but Boda came to the first blood match with a towel and the blackjack, and Matthews walked out as champion. Boda then put Cassius through the announce table for good measure. The last anyone saw of him, the first RUSH World Champion was holed up above a Los Angeles nightclub with Kathryn, Erica and a pile of old PCW magazines, reading up on Boda and very nearly admitting that he might have picked his friends badly.
