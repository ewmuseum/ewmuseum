Runs in with a chair against Christian Demonstone before the bell on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004) and is presented afterward by Blackbird as the newest member of the New Age Army. Arrives at ringside as VWF 24 Hour Champion during the Kidd/Blair match on Firestorm (3 May 2004) and is blindsided by Thunder Valenkov, then needles his brother Rob into a Beauty Brothers tag match for the title at the Gauntlet. Wrestles for the VWF 24 Hour Championship on the Gauntlet, teaming with his own brother Rob against Kevin Blair and Thunder Valenkov; pinned by Valenkov to lose the title. Joins Dan The Man Aller on color commentary for New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), introduced by JD Southerns as a holdover from the 12 Days Tournament. Visits Classy Joey Boy backstage at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), blaming him for letting the company slip into government hands and promising the Beauty Brothers will win the coming auction. Joins his brothers Rob and Rod in attacking Classy Joey Boy in the Orleans parking lot before End Of March (30 March 2005). Joins his brothers Rob and Rod in a three-on-one handicap match against Classy Joey Boy on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), disqualified alongside them when Rod Beauty picks up the barred sledgehammer.

VXW (2001)

Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 3 parsed matches, VXW (2001)): 3-0-0
Tenure detail
Billed "The Bi-Polar Brawler" and "The 24 Hour Man," the same aka names as his already-held vwf-vegas-2003 and re-raw tenures; cuts a promo on Winter War II wearing a rival wrestler's own tournament medals, then wrestles and wins on Winter War III. Returns on Ghost Town Blues to beat Lean Darlene Orion by submission in a repeat Scott Starring Medal Challenge, then addresses the crowd as part owner of the VXW ahead of the unsanctioned Simmons/Kagedo main event. Refuses Chris Wheeling's demand for an immediate Twenty-Four Hour title rematch on Xtreme Machine I, citing Roman Anderson's own open title-shot contract, then argues in the ring with his brother Rod over the loss of Rod's own 49 percent VXW stake. Wins a comedic non-tournament exhibition bout over a hot dog vendor pulled from the stands on The Hype II, retaining Scott Starring's own Twelve Days Tournament medals. Does not appear on The Hype I. Cuts a Twelve Days Medal Challenge promo on The Hype IV and is attacked by a visiting Shootfire wrestler, Jeff Keenan, before retreating to the back without wrestling; does not appear on The Hype III or The Gauntlet, where his brother Rod reports he was unable to attend.
