Veteran Mentality

The tag team of Jim Force and Robert Oxford, first billed under a working title the narrator calls a placeholder; they beat The Towers of Power, Savage Fury and Maxmillion Productions and lose a World Tag Team Championship match to The Dudez!!!1! in April 2007. In October 2007, with the foul-mouthed parrot Mr. Pizzles in a cage beside them, they lose to Team Seduction on the Thursday of Week 1 (with Steve Flash), on the Monday of Week 2 (with Wanda Fish) and in the World Tag Team Championship match at A Haunting in America, and Force holds a funeral for a dead pigeon, Mr. Squiggles. In November 2007, after The Hotshots are given only community service for the murder of the pigeon Mr. Squiggles, Jim Force breaks down, beats them bloody on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 2, and he and Robert Oxford beat them at Pilgrimage; on the Monday of Week 3 Force looses the white tiger Mr. Snuggleforce on them. Force and Oxford lose to The Hotshots on the Thursday of Week 4, November 2007, chase The Dudez!!!1!!! through the halls and onto a beach for several shows and are booked against them at Winter Epiphany.
