Tenure detail
Distinct from his New Error stablemate Death, confirmed by FAW IS WAR (28 May 2001), which stages Mortician turning on Death mid-match; the previous Panic (24 May 2001) names only "Mortician" (not Death) as Gordone Junior's partner in a handicap match, now attributed here rather than to Death. Announces his withdrawal from New Error on FAW IS WAR, 28 May 2001. The rebrand takes hold here: billed "'The Real F'n Deal' James West," asking Tom The Bomb for a spot in the Taking Hardcore To The Extreme World Title match -- though the first name given (James) does not match his own announcement three days earlier (Jason), an inconsistency left unresolved rather than picked. Billed simply "The Mortician" again at Taking Hardcore To The Extreme (21 October 2001), with no reference to the announced "James/Jason West" rebrand -- read as the rebrand not sticking, though nothing on-air says so outright.
