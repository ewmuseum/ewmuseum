Shady K

One half of the former SWF team the tew.com notes call Death Row, a rising pair; Aaron Andrews buys them as "insurance" and introduces them at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011 as Lockdown, men who care only for cold hard cash. He crushes Hugh Lee with a K Killer that night, beats Team OMGsplosion with the K Krusher at Farewell Tour on 22nd November, and puts Marshall Dillon away with the K Killer at Remember November. With Knuckles he attacks Kirk Jameson before the Invitational qualifier at Season's Beatings on 12th December, taps to Jared Johnson's Carolina Crossface, and ambushes Randy Bumfhole and Fumihiro Ota at End Of Days. In the three-way for the Tag Team titles at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012 he takes a Cal Cutter from Kashmir Singh, and Lockdown are put out of the match and out of the promotion; Andrews tells them they are fired. The narrator reports at the end of January that Shady K has signed for RIPW on a pay-per-appearance deal. Written by AI, 2026
