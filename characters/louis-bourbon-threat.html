Louis Bourbon

aka The Sun King, Louis XIX, His Most Noble and Holy Majesty

"The Sun King" Louis Bourbon, a modern Frenchman who proclaimed himself Louis XIX, was carried to the ring in a royal carriage and finished with the Revokation of Nantes and a boot on the chest; two years later he resurfaced in the UWF without the crown, a “proud Frenchman” in Seth Silver's Uprising who beat Chris Storm with a top-rope guillotine leg drop. thReat (2002)

2003-07-05 – 2003-09-12
wrestler — "The Sun King", self-proclaimed Louis XIX of France
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 3 parsed matches, thReat (2002)): 1-1-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Debuted at Payback 2003 in a four-way exhibition that ended with every man counted out; carried to the ring in a carriage by four retainers on Sunday Night Flaw 23 (20 July 2003), where PhD interrupted him and was dropped with the royal scepter; last staged at House Show 02 (12 September 2003), where PhD made him tap to an ankle lock. His trainer on the card is Rene Rouge; the card gives his birth as 4 April 1978 in Calais and his real name as Louis Jacques Bourbon. The sources disagree and all stand: his application calls him a "Dignified heel" while the fed's singles roster of 26 September 2003 lists him under its neutral alignment marker; his application bills him 6'3" and 232 lbs. and names him Louis Rousseau at birth, the card 6'2 and 251 lbs.; his entrance music is "1812 Overture" in his application, "Danse Macabre" at Payback 2003, and "Beethoven's 5th Symphony: Allegro con brio" on Sunday Night Flaw 23 and 24; Sunday Night Flaw 24's narrative gives him the pinfall over DeVante Stevenson while the page's closing line reads "Winners: haTE via pinfall to progress." The fed's booker wrote on 23 September 2003 that the character was being written out.

UWF (2001–07)

2005-10-09 – 2005-11-16
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 2 parsed matches, UWF (2001–07)): 1-1-0
Tenure detail
Introduced at the Uprising's debut on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, 9 October 2005, by Seth Silver as “a proud Frenchman, and a truly excellent wrestler” whom “a company felt it necessary to make … portray a delusional heir to the Bourbon monarchy”; beat Chris Storm in an impromptu match at Live and Loud 5 on 30 October 2005 with the Top Rope Guillotine Leg Drop, after Seth Silver grabbed Storm's leg and a steel chair came into the ring; lost with Lance Williams to the Stars of the Proletariat on Wednesday Night Underground, 16 November 2005.
