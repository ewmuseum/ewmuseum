Tenures

VXW (2001)

2005-08-04 – 2007-02-07
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 19 parsed matches, VXW (2001)): 10-6-1, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Returned to VXW on New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, signing a Loser Leaves Vegas contract against Kevin Simmons and 'The Outlaw' Morris Bass for Ghost Town Blues and revealing a returning Travis LaGrange as his own mystery partner after Simmons and Bass had him outnumbered. Beat Bass by disqualification on Sin City Fights February 7 after a low blow, then hit the Hyper Driver once LaGrange's own taunting video insert had Bass distracted. Wins the VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), playing possum to dump The Maquilladora last, but majority owner Kingston Cash refuses to recognize him as champion unless he takes the belt from Kevin Blair by force. Blair no-shows a signed follow-up on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), ruled a no contest; Cash's new bodyguard G-Gangsta piledrives him on a chair afterward in a no-bell attack. Beats Gangsta in an actual bout on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) with the Hyper Drive, only for Blair to run in and chair him with Lucy; Cash books Blair against Jennings in an I Quit match for the title at Vegas Violence.

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-08-27 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 15 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 13-1-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Debuts as the newest Society of Doom member on Wrestle Style V.2, beating Sonicman by submission, then loses to Flash by count-out in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3 and is attacked after the bell with The Miracle. Wins the VWF 24 Hour Championship from Kevin Blair in an I Quit match at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), forcing the words out of a champion who refuses to relinquish his own belt even in defeat, then chairs a recovering Sebastian and G-Gangsta backstage. Jumps a kendo-stick-carrying Paddy O'Shea before The Society of Doom's own match on March To Hell (21 October 2004), both men escorted out by security, then retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship against Iyasu Saku by pinfall (Hyper Driver) the same show. Helps chase off RAR and the Rodeo Boys from a backstage beatdown of Kevin Simmons on March To Hell II (28 October 2004). Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship over The Phoenix Adam Lazarus in the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), pinning him after the cage's own second floor collapses onto the ring. Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship over Robbie Caldwell on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) after G-Gangsta is accidentally clocked mid-Buckshot, finishing Caldwell with the Hyper Driver. Eliminated first from the tag team title battle royal by the five-man Riders of the Apocalypse on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), pulling Caldwell out with him on the way down. Teams with Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov to beat a short-handed Riders of the Apocalypse on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), finishing Caldwell with the Hyper Driver off a Valenkov Thunder Bomb. Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship against the masked Masked Nevadan on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), the loser revealed as Billy Bones after the Hyper Driver, then fends off a post-match tazer attack from Robbie Caldwell with help from Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and eliminates Classy Joey Boy before Kagedo eliminates him. Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship over Robbie Caldwell in a Bring Your Own Weapons match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), finishing him with the Hyper Driver moments after ringside legend Johnny "Hands Of" Stone knocks Caldwell down and reclaims his own prosthetic leg, which Caldwell had been using as a weapon. Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship against Paddy O'Shea at March To Madness (2 March 2005) by disqualification after Frank O'Shea punches him to break up a near-fall, saved from a beatdown afterward by a returning Capsman. Retains again against the debuting Mad Bomber at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), the Bomber disqualified for refusing to empty his pockets for a bomb-sniffing dog. Retains a third time against the Mad Bomber at End Of March (30 March 2005), pinning him while he is stuck in a bulky training suit. Retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship without a challenge on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005): on the line, alongside the Submission and Hardcore Championships, in an eight-man elimination match, Jennings survives as the last man standing for his own side, pinning Tai Angela with a small package after ducking the Darkness and blocking a Hyper Driver.
