The Fallen

The group of Firebird, the former Ultimate Phoenix, who preaches that a man must fall to the bottom of his soul before he can understand goodness. Its three masked newcomers, Ricochet, Sharp and Toxin, ambush Fumihiro Ota, Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh at Mother, May I? in May 2010; Firebird himself breaks with The Watch at Summer Showdown that June, offers The Firm a truce at Beach Blast, and at Eleventh Hour in November has the three unmask as Marc Speed, Greg Ford and Joey Poison, while the newcomer Evil Spirit stands at his side. By April 2011 Firebird lists his followers as Thrash, Greg Ford, Marc Speed, Evil Spirit and JD Morgan, and says that another is to come: Thrash arrives at All Or Nothing in February, JD Morgan is signed to join them, Evil Spirit takes Firebird's side against his former master Fumihiro Ota, and Black Eagle is welcomed back at April Showers. At Mother, May I? on 26th May 2011 Firebird presents the converted Masked Patriot as Providence, the fourth of the four men, with Black Eagle and JD Morgan, who have "something greater"; Greg Ford and Marc Speed still work for the light in their own way. Firebird and Providence beat Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson at Summer Showdown, and a Fallen trio led by Morgan loses the Trios titles match against The Firm at Back To School. At Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011 Firebird announces, after Kirk Jameson has turned his back on the Fall, that The Fallen will no longer pursue one man and will be everywhere; JD Morgan breaks away to win the All Action title as his own man. With Black Eagle in hospital, Firebird calls in Father Johan at Spring Fever on 12th April 2012 as an ally to strengthen the ranks, and from May The Force Be With You on 5th May the two fight side by side. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 the name is Firebird's again, with Father Johan, Black Eagle and Providence; at Summer Showdown on 12th June Fumihiro Ota, who from Hotter Than Hot wishes to be called The Great Ota, attacks Donnie J and joins them, and Aaron Andrews shakes Father Johan's hand after swinging the Heavyweight belt into Kirk Jameson's head. The six men come to the ring together at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, Andrews saying that they will ensure he remains the Heavyweight champion; they fight Kirk Jameson's Knights Templar, Firebird and Ota blindsiding Jameson and Bradford Peverell at South East Grand Parade on 9th August, they lose Father Johan on 12th July, when he and KC Glenn fall from the turnbuckle, and they lose Black Eagle, with Sienna LeNoir, when their contracts expire in September; at Back To School on 5th September Andrews tells them that the group does not work without him as champion.
