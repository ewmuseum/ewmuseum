Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Robb Daniels was born on May 29th of 1980. The sibling/brother of former MPW Superstar and Tag Team Champion Eric Daniels. He grew up in the small metropolitan area of Lexington, Kentucky. Robb and Eric's childhood friends were former MPW Superstar Sean Ryan, and fellow MPW Superstar Jason Todd also known as Jefferson. They all four were vivid fans of Wrestling. As Young childeren they would watch it regulary, and pretend they were pro wrestlers. Having no knowledge that someday in there future, that actually would be there career. They attended Lookout Elementary, then moved on to Millard High School, they graduated from the consolidated school of East Ridge High. They attended the University of Kentucky, Robb then met his future wife but at the time girl friend Felicia Daniels. Robb Daniels broke into the Wrestling Scene with Jefferson in the FWEn.
