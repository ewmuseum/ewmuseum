Lex Taylor

aka "Lethal" Lex Taylor, LEX TAYLOR, "Lethal" LEX TAYLOR, Lethal Lex Taylor

Wrestled the CSWA from 1995 — put through a table before a bell rang, and in a roofed six-man cage beside Scotty Michaels and Shawn Matthews — held the AAWC Television title through a long stretch broken only once and lost it at Oktoberfest ’96 in a triangle match he had all but won, and arrived in the NFW six years later at FUTURESHOCK running in beside that same Shawn Matthews.
