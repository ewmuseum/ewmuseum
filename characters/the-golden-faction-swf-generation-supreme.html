The Golden Faction

The group Rocky Golden leads with Troy Tornado, Texas Pete and manager Kristen Pearce, who target Steve Frehley and Chris Morrisette. Johnny Bloodstone joins as the fourth man at Times of Trouble 2008, and Eric Eisen's offer to fold them into the Supreme Protectorate is refused on SWF Legends in June 2008. Troy Tornado, ordered to take the Supreme Championship from Christian Faith and hand it over, fails on SWF Legends in July 2008 and is attacked by the group; in Week 1 of August Rocky Golden says he is no longer a member. The group beat down Troy Tornado on SWF Legends in Week 2 of August 2008 and Rocky Golden beats the injured Ray Diaz at Welcome to the Jungle 2008; the three beat Robbie Retro and the Biggz brothers in Week 3, Texas Pete loses to Ray Diaz in Week 4, and in Week 1 of September Rocky Golden demands 'respect' from Eric Tyler.
