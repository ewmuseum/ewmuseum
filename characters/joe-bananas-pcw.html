Biography (RUSH (2002))

The sun had set, leaving a warm glow all across Kingston, as a baby boy was born. The hot, sticky air of that late August evening left the mother absolutely exhausted. She was in no mood for the screaming baby beside her, his round belly glistening in the dying sun and the tears trickling down from his eyes. The only comfort he could find was from a congratulatory basket of fruit on the bedside table. The early years of his life were spent crying for his parents, they were in no situation to look after the baby and had to work much more to try and supply for his greedy appetites. They had struggled to settle on a name for him, dozens of different ideas and suggestions had come and gone. Joseph was what they eventually called him, an old granddad had been named this and it seemed to suit the growing child. The very first day that young Joseph went out on his own, to play with the children outside, he ran back in as quickly as his stubby legs would carry him. He couldn’t stand the endless teasing and mocking. For lack of his mother most of the time, Joseph still sucked his thumb and had a generally less than independent character. With his parents gone more often than not, and no children befriending him at all, Joseph would find comfort in anything else he could find. He carried a pale blue blanket around with him, but it was torn and scratched by a cat from a nearby neighbour’s house. After continuously searching, and finding flaws in each or having it taken from him somehow, Joseph had one last attempt… fruit. It all started with a juicy mango, which had been intended for eating after his meal. Joseph cuddled it and talked to it, never being mocked by it. He continued his friendship with all the fruit he could find, greeting each one and talking at length with them about his problems and feelings. This lead to great trouble at school, his first day he had witnessed a banana being squashed and thrown against a wall. He strode up to the older boy and raised his fists. “Don’ beh foo, yah rahtid fruit killer!” Joseph screeched to the bewildered boy. Joseph was dead-set and punched the boy’s face with all his might, knocking him to the ground with a bloody nose. Incidents such as this continued, forcing Joseph into seemingly endless amounts of time in detention. He was a troublemaker, who never meant any harm. The children now had something greater to mock him with, his odd obsession with fruit. He was totally ignorant and unaware, he thought he was the only one in the right and everybody was insane. These fruit became more and more his best friends, and his only friends. As he paid more and more attention to his fruit chums, he knew less and less. Information would not stick in his brain; he barely had the basic knowledge needed. His parents were becoming more and more worried about Joseph’s problem. Child psychiatrists, doctors and many old wives’ home remedies made absolutely no impact. The best suggestion was to get Joseph involved in some sort of activity that would eventually deter his attention and bring him in to the real world. And so Joseph was forcefully enrolled into a small wrestling school that had recently opened over half an hours journey outside of Kingston. Joseph would make the journey every week, learn different holds and takedowns then make the journey back home. He was good at it, no doubt about that, but he wasn’t focused and didn’t put all of his energy into it as had been hoped by his parents. During the later half of his teenage years, Joseph did get into wrestling more and enjoyed doing it. He was one of the best in his class, but seen as the bottom for his problem with fruit. He was treated as if disabled, not all there in his mind. But nobody could hold him back, as he pushed harder and harder on himself. It lead to a transfer to an American school, and even less acceptance. Nobody understood why he talked to fruit, and why he thought they talked back to him. Considered a joke and pain point of mockery, Joseph Banner was nicknamed Joe Bananas. Giggles could be heard whenever he asked advice from a grape, or if he talked about his day with a raspberry. Joe Bananas was a joke to everyone, and has been ever since then. But his erratic and unpredictable style in the ring has lead him far, and has kept his loyal friendship with fruit stronger than ever.
