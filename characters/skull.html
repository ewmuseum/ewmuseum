Skull

Signed by the UWF in July 2003 with Legend in Training and Nyne, he was fired and banned in September 2004, then flooded the roleplay board with video-only "UWF Universal Promos" numbered to 147 (455, by the UWF head's own count); GTT4 ejected him that week, and the 2004 Impies named him Jobber of the Year as the "yutz" who got "kicked out of the UWF and GTT4 over Disturbed". Tenure detail
Dated from his signing, with Legend in Training and Nyne, on the 23 July 2003 UWF card (promotions/uwf/archive/wnu-2003-07-23.html) to his firing and banning in September 2004 (the GTT4 news item of 12 September 2004, promotions/ptc/news/news-skull-out-of-gtt4-2004-09-12.html, and the UWF head's own account in the same PTC forum thread, 14 September 2004). The 28 November 2004 card and the FANdemonium 2004 battle royal still bill him after the firing, the battle royal as a joke at his expense; the 9 and 23 January 2002 cards bill a Skull introduced as a former GIWF star and UWF newcomer from Belfast, Ireland.
