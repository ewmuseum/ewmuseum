Bulldozer Brandon Smith

A dark-match wrestler who loses to Citizen X in the National Championship's fifth defense. In October 2007 he keeps the Unappreciated Championship against Brendan Idol and Insane Machine and against Brett Hall in a street fight at A Haunting in America, after Hall's joyride on his motorcycle and the wrecking of Hall's car. In October and November 2007 he chases Thomas Morgan with a chain, is beaten down by Brett Hall and Brendan Idol, squashes Warlord Agony and keeps the Unappreciated Championship against Idol at Pilgrimage. In late November and December 2007 he beats Mean Jean Cattley, has his belt stolen by the Mantis and loses a dark match to Reaper. Written by AI, 2026
