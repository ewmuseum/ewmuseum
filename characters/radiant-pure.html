Biography (PURE (2002))

<i>In The Beginning...</i> On a tiny island, west of the Japanese main island of Honshu, a legacy would begin on the 21st of May 1620. Ichiro Kunamara had been thrown off a small boat housed by a nearly a dozen castaways from the Japanese town of Niigata. Kunamara had befouled the captain’s daughter, taking away her virginity while on a night of drinking through the port-town. The enraged captain, Kintaro Kawashima had ordered Kunamara to be drowned in what today is called the Sea of Japan. Luckily for Ichiro, he had managed to fight off the men of Kintaro Kawashima and float along in the cool Sea of Japan water until he reached an island off in the distance. Kunamara wondered many a night around the island, finding that the small island had plentiful fresh water, trees with fruit on them and several roaming livestock. Ichiro set to work building himself a small home, with the use of a Machete he had stolen from the boat. The construction of his home took Ichiro three solid weeks of construction to build himself a small house for him to sleep in. He had no way of travelling his way back to the mainland, unless he built himself a boat. So, Ichiro Kunamara went to work again. No rest period from between building his new home and constructing the small boat. The trusty Machete he had been using over the last few weeks was lashed into the nearby trees, Ichiro knocking down three trees to build himself the boat. The boat was only big enough to carry him, some food and the Machete. Ichiro set sail back to the main land, but he knew of the dangers lurking in the port-town of Niigata. Ichiro was known by many on the western coast of the main island. So, he had a choice. Ichiro pondered whether or not to arrive into Niigata and battle with the outlaws who had tried to cut his life short, or float South of Niigata toward the newly established Toyama. He decided to travel to Niigata, his life had been lacking excitement recently. It was very early July when Ichiro Kunamara snuck into the quiet town of Niigata, very early in the morning. He would have been very recognizable to the locals if he didn’t disguise himself by stealing a sheet of cloth that hung from the window of a house on the near the jetties. Ichiro tiptoed through Niigata in the dead of the night. He creaked open windows of closed food stalls to steal Bread, Rice, Additional Clothing, Woks for cooking, A couple more Machetes and Knifes, and a bigger boat that was abandoned, but in good condition, on the shores. He went unnoticed on this historical night. Not a soul in the port-town knew about his thieving, and would remain completely oblivious for years to come. Ichiro sailed back to his lonely island as the sun came up, watching the town of Niigata come to life as he floated away peacefully in the Sea of Japan. <i>...More To Come.</i>
