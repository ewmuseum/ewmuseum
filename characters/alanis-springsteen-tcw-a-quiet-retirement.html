Alanis Springsteen

Kingman & Alexander's manager, a woman Jason Azaria cannot name on air until he remembers she debuted as Alanis Springsteen, who walks to ringside as the British Lions are beaten in August 2008 and in September 2008 renames her team the Conquering Kings, telling them to draw courage from their namesakes. In November 2008 she walks to ringside with the Conquering Kings and panics as American Buffalo grabs her. In December 2008 she is at ringside for the Conquering Kings against the British Lions and Elite Express. Written by AI, 2026
