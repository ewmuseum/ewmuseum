Human Arsenal

A hard-hitting wrestler with the Ammo Dump whom Seduction sets on Darryl Devine in July 2007: he loses to Devine by disqualification, is pinned by William Hayes in the fatal four-way, and beats Muffin, Freddie Datsun and Duane Fry. In October and November 2007 he beats Steve Flash, Frankie Perez, Bruce the Giant and Peter Valentine, sides with Seduction against Triple D, loses the tag belts with Ota at Pilgrimage, and loses to Devine in a match of over twenty-three minutes on the Monday of Week 3. In late November and December 2007 he is injured by Devine, beats Rudy Velasquez, Freddie Datsun, draws with Datsun and Umaga, loses to Devine in 26:35, is wooed by the Masked Man, quits Divinity and returns.
