Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Kashmir Singh

A technical all-rounder who partners the Masked Patriot against Citizen X and finishes with the Cal-Cutter. He and Brandon Smith beat Ricochet & Sharp at Summer Showdown in June 2010; with Smith and Fumihiro Ota he takes the Trios titles from The Firm at Hotter Than Hot in July and keeps them at Summernova and Eleventh Hour. He loses to Firebird at Iron in what the narrator calls his best match in the company, and beats Sharp with the Bombay Duck at Fall Of The King and again at Where It All Begins Again. He and Brandon Smith lose the Tag Team title match with the Canadian Blondes at Remember November in November 2010, lose the Trios titles with Smith and Fumihiro Ota at Season's Beatings and, after Smith is hurt by the Blondes, bring in Jean Cattley as his partner and win the Tag Team titles at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011, keeping them to the end of April. With Cattley he loses the Tag Team titles at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 and is thrown to Antonio's Italian DDT; paired again with Brandon Smith as Singh & Smith, he comes back from a Broadway trip in pink boa, blonde hair and sequins for Hotter Than Hot, loses the title three-ways at Mother, May I? and Hotter Than Hot and to Ford & Speed at Summernova, and joins Smith and Fumihiro Ota to beat the Canadian Blondes and Raphael at Back To School. On the tours he beats Roderick Remus and Jared Johnson, loses to Antonio and Jared Johnson and, billed as "Sensational Singh", beats Raphael. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses to Aaron Andrews for the Heavyweight title in a twenty-one-minute match at All Or Nothing on 12th February, loses to JD Morgan for the All Action title at Third Time's The Charm, enters the Gold Rush match at April Showers, beats Greg Ford at Great Lakes Madness and loses to Antonio at Mother, May I? on 12th May. The narrator reports that he has re-signed with NYCW. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he and Brandon Smith keep the Tag Team titles in the three-way at High Noon on 5th June, lose them to the Dark Raptors at Summer Showdown on 12th June when Firebird pins him, lose to Always Breakin' Hollywood at Hotter Than Hot, to the Second Sons at Beach Blast, to the Canadian Blondes at South East Grand Parade, where Oscar Golden pins him after an O, Canada!, and to Ford & Speed at Back To School; at Back To School he sings with Sienna LeNoir, tells her she should go to New York for Broadway and the song writers he and Smith know, and stands with Donnie J between her and Black Eagle.
