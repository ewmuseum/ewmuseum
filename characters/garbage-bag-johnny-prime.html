Garbage Bag Johnny

The Dirtiest Dude in PRIME, winner of the 2009 Dual Halo and holder of the 5 Star Title in the summer of 2010, held the GCW United States Championship from 28 October 2008, was named Rising Star (2008), Most Missed Former Wrestler (2009) and appeared on 58 GCW cards from 2 July 2008 to 17 April 2011.
