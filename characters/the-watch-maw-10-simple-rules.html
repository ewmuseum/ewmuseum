The Watch

The stable Ultimate Phoenix presents at March Madness in March 2009 with Morgan & Benning, Kashmir Singh and the Masked Patriot, to police the company's rules and stand against The Firm. At March Madness in March 2010 Ultimate Phoenix, Kashmir Singh and the new Masked Patriot lose the first Trios title match to The Firm, and at Spring Fever in April the stable loses a five-match series to The Firm by three matches to two, which by the stake's terms ends it; Phoenix then attacks his own side with a chair. Ultimate Phoenix, unmasked as Firebird at Summer Showdown in June 2010, chairs his former Watch partners Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh. Written by AI, 2026
