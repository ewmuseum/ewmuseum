Tenure detail
Billed “The Fallen Angel” by the fed; the aka is not carried on this row because it auto-linked into ACW's own Simian Kade, whose billing carries the same moniker, and into two captions describing an unrelated finisher. Three surviving matches, all won: the triple threat that opened Rebirth's third hour, with the Best Moonsault Ever (the fed's own FoB: Rebirth, Hour 3, 20 September 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-rebirth-2005-09-20-9684.html)); the title defence against Matt Hardy on 13 October, with the Angel's Wings (the fed's own FoB Sacrifice, “A New Chapter”, 13 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-10-13.html)); and the 18:39 non-title main event against Matt Stanton on 19 November, with the Angel's Wings again (the fed's own FoB Sacrifice, “A Cannon's Past: Exposed”, 19 November 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-11-19.html)). The recap of 6 October records a fourth, retaining against Mark Garrett, and the show before that a loss to the same man. His own promos name the men he had beaten: Lee Nolan, Jeremiah Jacobs, David Jax, Mark Garrett, Balthamel, Steve Mitchell, Nathan Jones and “Underground superstar Body Breaker”; only Lee Nolan is otherwise unevidenced in FoB and no tenure was written for him on a champion's boast alone (the fed's own Sacrifice Recap Show of 8 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/sacrifice-recap-show-2005-10-08.html); the fed's own FoB Sacrifice, “A New Chapter”, 13 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-10-13.html)).
