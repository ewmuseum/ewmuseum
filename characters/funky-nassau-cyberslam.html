Tenure detail
The one office-holder the record follows across the whole run, from a single fed's commissioner to the President of the game.
Source: The 1998 rulebook names him twice - "CWF (Funky Nassau)" in its commissioner block, and in full in its own section on the indy feds: "Currently Nassau is the commissioner of the CWF fed and the president of the CWF league" (/CyberSlam Guidelines and FAQs, 1998). The staff panel on the Mail Box page lists "Nassau (CWF Commissioner)" (/CyberSlam Mail Box and staff panel). The front page of September 1999 reads "President: Funky Nassau" (/CyberSlam The Universe of Feds). He is still deciding the game's business in January 2001, a news post recording "an agreement with Funky Nassau" to fire an absent commissioner (/CyberSlam iMWA: Commish Skull Gone!?).
