Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Donnie J

A high-flyer "of CZCW fame" who makes his first appearance at April Showers on 5th April 2012 with a promise to put the fright into every champion in the company, and beats Citizen X with a Death On Miami Beach in the narrator's "bit too CZCW" match. At South East Crisis on 8th April Rip Chord puts the All Action title in a ladder match and Donnie J, at home among the ladders, hangs from the belt to win it from JD Morgan (the diary's own title records list "Donnie J April 2012 0"). At May The Force Be With You on 5th May he is beaten for it by Aaron Andrews in a title-versus-title match, regains it at Great Lakes Madness on 9th May in a two-out-of-three-falls match ("Donnie J (2) May 2012 7") and at Mother, May I? on 12th May loses the thirty-minute Iron Man match for the Heavyweight title to Andrews by the skin of his teeth. After that show the narrator notes that he has extended his contract with another company. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 the "Dogfight" keeps the All Action title against Father Johan in a pole match at High Noon on 5th June, Firebird in a submission match at South East Madness on 9th June, Eddie Powell and Citizen X at Summer Showdown, Firebird again at Hotter Than Hot, JD Morgan in a thirty-minute draw at Great Lakes Grand Parade, Powell and Rudy Velasquez at Summernova and Providence in a ladder match at South East Grand Parade, where he says he feels he needs to be the one to stop him. He agrees to help the faces against The Fallen only in the ring, loses the belt backstage to Black Eagle at Iron on 12th August and takes it back the same night, hitting Firebird with a metal briefcase; the narrator's news says that he has left 4C as its money troubles mount, and at Back To School on 5th September he loses a six-man with Morgan and Amazing Fire Fly, then stands with Kashmir Singh and Brandon Smith to protect Sienna LeNoir.
