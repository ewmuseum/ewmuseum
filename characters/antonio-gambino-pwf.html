Tenure detail
Billed age 28 per the card. Billed quote: "I show no mercy, and I take no shit" Bio: son of Mob boss Vincent Gambino. Rose through the pre-hibernation "old PWF" stable The Destruction Crew, staging a takeover of the group from its then-leader (billed "The Game," a different wrestler— not held elsewhere in this capture) alongside several PWF wrestlers including rival-turned-ally Diablo. The group was renamed the Ruff Ryders under Gambino, and included Destroyer, Devastator, the Top Doggs, and right-hand man Showstopper (none held elsewhere in this capture). Before feuding with Diablo's own Capital Punishment stable. Won the pre-hibernation PWF World Title from Diablo in a controversial match, lost it back to him in a Last Man Standing match. Wrestled in WVW (won its Intercontinental Title) after the PWF's first closure. At Saturday Night Havoc (24 March 2001), opened the show demanding the vacant World Title be handed back to him rather than contested in Michael Pellington's new tournament, and closed it beating Firestorm in the main event with the Gambino Facebuster.
