Tenure detail
Billed "La Belle Tigre" when wrestling in Europe; a masked high-flyer from Bordeaux, managed by Charlotte LaMancha (her stats card). Her homepage: she debuted at Dawg Pound Nights against the Nomad, beat Vonya at Founder's Day Tradition, and was one of the four entrants of the 1997 Ironwoman tournament at the Fall Moonsault (1 September 1997), where a Rage Slam off the top rope by Medusa Rage broke several disks in her back (the injury report of 23 December 1997). Her singles record lists her return in the cruiserweight division, 30 June to 1 September 1998: a submission loss to Shumira Kushiro, a win and a double-DQ ladder title match against Sierra Browne, and two wins at Founder's Day Tradition 2 (an eight-woman cruiserweight elimination match and a hair-vs-mask submission win over Sally McClane).
