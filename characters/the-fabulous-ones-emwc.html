The Fabulous Ones

The tag team of Agito Nakajima and Sho Satsuma, from Nagoya, Japan, managed by Ms. Miki, who eliminated the Fraternity Boys in the Hell On Earth cell on 9 August 1998 and lost to the Down Boys. Written by AI, 2026

Tenure detail
At Blood, Sweat, and Tears (9 August 1998): Ms. Miki introduces Agito Nakajima and Sho Satsuma as the Fabulous Ones, the Lady Thrillers from Nagoya, Japan, at a combined 525 pounds; the pair join the brawl in the match between Brian Lau and Kuyler Greyson, eliminate the Fraternity Boys in the Hell On Earth cell, and are the last team beaten by the Down Boys.
