Monsters Inc.

Abyss and Goliath, who take the tag belts from the Blue World Order at Guilty As Charged with a Double Chokeslam and keep them on Revolution against the Dudley Boys with the Iron Saints' help. In February and March 2007 Abyss and Goliath stay with Raven, who demands a tag title shot for his 'monsters' and is refused by Shane Douglas; at Baptism Of Fire Goliath is the first man eliminated from the Baptism Of Fire match, pushed into the flames by eight others, and Abyss is thrown over by Sabu and Tommy Dreamer. In April 2007 Monsters Inc help CM Punk beat Lance Storm and beat the Iron Saints on Revolution; in May they hold Kelly Thomaselli for Raven and, at Hardcore Heaven, are beaten by the Iron Saints, an upset, before chokeslamming them in a second attack with Vito. In June they beat Team England's Nigel McGuinness with a Double Chokeslam and lose to Paul Burchill and Davey Boy Smith. At Wrestlepalooza in June 2007 Goliath is found laid out in the car park and Abyss loses to Paul Burchill's Release German Suplex through tables; in July they lose to Mark Calaway (a Chokeslam on Abyss) and, in the handicap match Raven books against Vito, Vito wins with Calaway's help, and at Summer Assylum they lose to the Iron Saints in the six-man tag with CM Punk; in August Vito beats Goliath with a Shining Wizard. On Blood, Sweat and Beers in August 2007 Vito Thomaselli beats them with Shane Douglas's help; at Heatwave Goliath carries a key to the cage door of the Barbed Wire Cage Match so that they can enter, and Vito drives them out with a Double DDT; in September they stand by Raven and CM Punk, hitting Vito and Burchill with Double Chokeslams. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Abyss's Big Boot hits CM Punk by mistake and Vito Thomaselli's springboard somersault plancha takes both monsters down; in October they stand by Punk, and at Guilty As Charged they hold Vito for Punk's chair shots in the Last Man Standing Match (a Black Hole Slam by Abyss, a Chokeslam by Goliath) until the referee stops it. In November 2007 Abyss and Goliath interfere for Raven's Nest in the main events on Revolution, where Shane Douglas fights them off with a chair and Chris Jericho clears them with another; Abyss taps to Jericho's Liontamer in the six-man tag on Blood, Sweat and Beers. At November To Remember XV Abyss and Goliath escort the champion Raven to the ring and stand in front of his corner for the Loser Leaves ECW Match. On the Saturday show they beat down Paul Burchill with Raven's Nest until Sabu and The Sandman, in 'ECW Enforcer' shirts, clear Abyss and Goliath; on Revolution on the first Monday of December Abyss flattens Vito Thomaselli with a Black Hole Slam and Sabu chairs him out of the ring.
