JD Southerns

A newly hired backstage announcer and interviewer, introduced calling a remote lumberjack streetfight from the Las Vegas Mission at Vegas Violence III. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-10 – 2005-01-19
Tenure detail
Calls the Buried Alive match at Ghost Town Blues, then anchors the entire Firestorm (26 Feb 2004) broadcast from the desk. Anchors the desk for Wrestle Style V.1, V.2 and V.3 as the broadcast's own studio host. Continues anchoring the broadcast desk through March To Hell and March To Hell II. Anchors the desk for Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004) and New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), the last the broadcast's own first from the Orleans Arena; joined on color commentary for New Years Kick Off by a returning Ric Beauty.
