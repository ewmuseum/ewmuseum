The Jobber

aka Jobber

Became GLCW's first Television Champion at Chi-Town Showdown I on 10 October 2002, kept the title against Edmunds, James and Golem, and lost it to Jarod Poe at Cleveland Riptide on 21 February 2003; from July he made Scotty Sanders and Cameron Cruise submit, beat Dakota Smith and Nemesis and was thrown out of the Columbus battle royal by Manson.
