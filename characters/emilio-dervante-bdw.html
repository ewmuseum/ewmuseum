Tenure detail
Won BDW's own Static the week before the 7th Static (per his own roleplay #9's own framing) and, per that same roleplay, was arrested for the rape of a named victim afterward — released in time to wrestle. On the 7th Static he attacked champion Sean McClancy pre-bell over the World title, worked with a groupie to keep McClancy's manager Annie away from ringside, and was booked into a ladder match against McClancy for 27 June 2004 (Anger & Rage) after the scheduled non-title match was called off. Beat UnderGround King in the World Title tournament's second round on the 6th Static; at The Return I he was framed by Annie Leanan for an assault backstage, held by police, released on a lawyer's word, and pinned by Sean McClancy in the triple-threat final.
