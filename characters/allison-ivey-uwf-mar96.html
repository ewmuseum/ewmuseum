Tenure detail
The Youngbloods' manager, previously non-competing; made her own in-ring wrestling debut at Gold Rush 2004 against Angel, losing by pinfall (HeadHunter) after a hard-fought opening match. Attacked Angel with a chair after the bell alongside Edward Drake of the Privateers. Curbstomped Lisa Drake against the ropes on 27 November 2004 after Drake refused to rejoin the Youngbloods, then was cleared from the ring by a furious Nina Grimsson. Objected furiously in Commissioner Becky Byers's own office on 11 December 2004, first over the Youngbloods' ownership shares being staked on two upcoming matches, then over Lisa Drake being named Alex Extreme's manager against Michael Augustine. Complained to reporter Moe Owens on 6 July 2005 that the Privateers should not have to enter the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament for a World Tag Team Championship shot already theirs by right, then worked ringside during Edward Drake's own loss to Miguel Quesada. Ran interference at ringside for the Youngbloods during Jacob Drake's own Cruiserweight Championship defense over Zero-G on 20 July 2005. Distracted the referee at ringside for Michael Augustine during his own tag team win over Miguel Quesada and Jason Keening on 23 July 2005. Discussed challenger Rick Marley's chances with the Drake brothers backstage before Jacob's Cruiserweight title defense on 6 August 2005.
