Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Antonio

A cheating Italian heel, winner of the Rip Chord Invitational with the Italian DDT, who signs an extension, hires an assistant and takes Citizen X's money. He beats Fumihiro Ota in a qualifier at End Of Days in December 2010 and has his best match in the Invitational quarter-final against Kirk Jameson, but his contract comes up for renewal in February 2011 with Rip Chord pressing for a push, he loses a Tag Team title match with Raphael at Leap Into The Unknown, and at April Showers loses to Amazing Fire Fly and tells the crowd, "That's it – I'm done. We're done. Soon, everything's going to change..." He joins Erik Strong and Casey Valentine in the Second Sons at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, losing with Strong to Chance Fortune and Randy Bumfhole, and beats Joe Benning, Hugh Lee, Riley McManus and Kashmir Singh on the May tour shows. He wins with Valentine over Bumfhole and Kid Arachnid on 26th May, ambushes Bumfhole backstage at High Noon and holds him to a time-limit draw, loses to Steve Flash at Hotter Than Hot and with Strong to Flash and Bumfhole at Beach Blast, and pins Joe Benning with the Italian DDT at Back To School; Black Eagle's rumours about him earn Eagle a warning from the narrator. In the diary's pages from March to May 2012 he pins Max Mayhem at Third Time's The Charm, loses to Kashmir Singh's Bombay Duck at March Madness and beats Singh with an Italian DDT at Mother, May I? on 12th May; the narrator says he is on the roster out of loyalty to Rip Chord, and that Aaron Andrews's joke at his expense backstage was the third in five months. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he is drawn with Casey Valentine into a conversation with Father Johan in The Fallen locker room at Summer Showdown on 12th June, and the narrator tells him after the show that he has had one good match in six months, that Black Eagle has earned the title he would like and that he is still young enough to make it; he and Casey beat Smith & Singh at Beach Blast and lose at South East Grand Parade and Iron.
