Art Reed

Johnny Bloodstone's old training partner from the Stone family's summer sessions, whom Sam Keith taught the Dread Lock, a DaVE and Japan veteran who arrives on Total Wrestling in June 2008 to haul Sam Keith out of the ring and teams with Chance Fortune against the Machines, and in August 2008 wins the fans' web poll to face Edd Stone for the All Action Championship at Hotter Than Hell, where he beats Stone by count out without taking the title, and in September 2008 loses a submission match to Jeremy Stone, teams with the Young Guns to beat Edd Stone and the Enforcers and loses the rematch for the All Action Championship at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 loses to Eric Tyler, beats Merle O'Curle with the Dread Lock and, at Crunch Time, comes to Rick Law's rescue against Tyler and Acid. In November 2008 he beats Freddy Huggins & Sean Deeley with Joey Minnesota, wins the second All Action Championship contendership match with the Dread Lock, loses with the Keith Brothers to Chris Rockwell, Frankie Perez & Sean Deeley and at A Little South Of Sanity loses the four-way match for the title. In December 2008 he is beaten in the six-man match, beats the British Lions with Sean Deeley and with the Dread Lock and is eliminated from the ladder match. Written by AI, 2026
