Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 11 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 6-3-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
A member of Sabbat Justice, billed with his brother Ravnos as the Arcana; won a six-man tag by disqualification alongside Ravnos and Avanter when an opposing wrestler struck his own partner with a chair. Teamed with Ravnos as the Arcana in a Las Vegas Street Fight win over the Fraternity Boys on 15 November 2003, pinning Brian McKenzie with a swinging DDT. Teamed with Ravnos as the Arcana again on 17 December 2003, leaping off his own brother's shoulders to plant a forearm in Bobby Lincoln's face and preserve Ravnos's own pinfall over Todd Franklin, beating the Universal Powers. Forced into a scrambled-partners tag with a reluctant Todd Franklin on 5 May 2004 against Michael Keening and Erik Grimsson; the bout ended as a no-contest after Franklin turned on him mid-match with a lariat and a swinging full nelson. Teamed with brother Ravnos, now billed together as the Amazing Romanis, to win the UWF North American Tag Team Championship from the Universal Powers (Bobby Lincoln, Todd Franklin) by pinfall on 20 October 2004, pinning Bobby Lincoln with a neckbreaker delivered off his own brother's shoulders after a returning Vyolynce's own ringside chair distraction. Teamed with brother Ravnos as the Amazing Romanis to retain the UWF North American Tag Team Championship over the Prophets of Rage (Derek Rage, Shadoe Rage) by pinfall (Without A Net) on 17 November 2004, aided by a mystery burst of pyrotechnics and an eyeball graphic that rattled Shadoe Rage ahead of the finish. Fought the Hands of Death to a no-contest defense of the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with brother Ravnos on 1 December 2004, both men later hung by dog collars. On 11 December 2004, issued a joint challenge with Ravnos to whichever of the Hands of Death or Prophets of Rage survived their own Rise to Power dog collar match.
