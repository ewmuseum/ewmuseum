UBC (Underground Brawler's Club, 2002–2003)

2002-12-01 – 2002-12-21
Tenure detail
Dated from UBC's own news: the post of 1 December 2002 reads, "all four members of legendary wrestling faction, "Sudden Violence" (Chris Carter, Krisis, Violent B, and Paranoia), are in the UBC", and the post of 21 December 2002 reads, "Red Vizionz is the newest member of Sudden Violence". The stable carried across the same four men and gained a fifth, Red Vizionz, on 21 December 2002.
Source: UBC's own backstage database — its own news posts of 1, 15 and 21 December 2002; its own Stables page as captured on 29 May 2003 lists the same five
