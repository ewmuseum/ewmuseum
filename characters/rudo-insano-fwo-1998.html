Rudo Insano

aka Rudo, Terry Nodar

Machina's partner in the Lone Gunmen from the federation's first summer, twice a Tag Team Champion beside him and the team's first singles champion with the Cruiserweight title in March 2000, whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: in the story's later years he comes back under another name as the owner of the CWL, Penny Pureheart's husband, through the end of 2005.
