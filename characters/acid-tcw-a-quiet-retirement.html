Acid

A high flyer signed from DaVE who can mimic any other flyer, and who falls in with Eric Tyler's side at Total Mayhem 13, and in August 2008 beats Hell Monkey, helps Eric Tyler and Freddy Huggins beat TCW's three trainers and teams with UK Dragon as Acid Dragon, and in September 2008 loses with Eric Tyler to Rick Law and Hell Monkey, wins a four-way match by submitting Freddy Huggins with the Acidity Test and loses to John Anderson on Badge of Honor. In November 2008 he beats Steve Gumble and UK Dragon in a three-way match, is sent to the back by referee Eugene Williams and joins Eric Tyler & Freddy Huggins in a no contest with Koshiro Ino & Team VIPER. In December 2008 he is beaten in the six-man match won by Aaron Andrews, loses with the School of Tradition to Troy Tornado and the Young Guns, is beaten by the New Wave with Acid Dragon and is eliminated from the ladder match.
