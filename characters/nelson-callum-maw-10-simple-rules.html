Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Nelson Callum

A newcomer "working for beans" who comes to MAW after a miserable time in PSW. He debuts at Iron in August 2010 and loses to Jesse Christian's Human Torture Rack, joins Glamour And Devastation in a trios match at Where It All Begins Again in October, evening the match with an Ankle Lock on Hugh Lee, and is one of Jared Johnson's trial partners at Eleventh Hour in November. He wins a tour match over Amazing Fire Fly in December 2010 and joins Jared Johnson and Roderick Remus as The Triple Threat at Season's Beatings, winning the Trios titles. He keeps the Trios titles with The Triple Threat until Summernova on 12th August 2011, when Kid Arachnid pins him with the Cradle Piledriver; the narrator, who has booked Arachnid against Callum three times, wants "Arachnid + Callum = bad" tattooed on his forehead. He loses tour matches to Jonnie Perez, Kid Arachnid and Joe Benning. In the diary's pages from February 2012 the narrator is shown him through a cell hatch after an arrest for possession of illegal narcotics; he tells the press MAW has a zero-tolerance policy on performance-enhancing drugs and releases Callum from his contract with immediate effect. Callum and his lawyer arrive at MAW HQ muttering about unfair dismissal until Rip Chord goes ballistic, and the narrator blames him for the loss of the company's prestige. Written by AI, 2026
