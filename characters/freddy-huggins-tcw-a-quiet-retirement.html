Freddy Huggins

A former 'Teen Idol' of the All Action division managed by his twin sister Laura, who gets a darker streak in his team-ups with Eric Tyler. In September 2008 he wins by disqualification against the New Wave with Sammy Bach, loses with Eric Tyler to the Young Guns and is submitted by Acid in a four-way match when his sister Laura distracts the referee, punishing him for hitting Eric Tyler with the Huggins Kiss. In October 2008 he mistakenly hits Sean Deeley with the Huggins Kiss on Badge of Honor and is flattened by Remo Richardson when he interferes in the battle royal. In November 2008 he loses with Sean Deeley to Art Reed & Joey Minnesota, hits Sean Deeley with the Huggins Kiss twice, is punched by his sister, is chosen by Eric Tyler for the All Action Championship four-way and loses it to Frankie Perez at A Little South Of Sanity. In December 2008 he shoves his sister Laura, loses to Koshiro Ino, is felled by Troy Tornado's Star Maker and the Young Guns' double team, interferes in Sean Deeley's match with Remo Richardson with a Huggins Kiss and at Psycho Circus loses to Deeley.
