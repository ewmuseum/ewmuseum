Tyson Baine

aka BLZ Bubb

A Canadian monster formerly billed as BLZ Bubb, now an unlikely fan favourite who scares the bad guys, ambushed by American Buffalo and beaten by him at Where Angels Fear To Tread, then the winner of their Last Man Standing match at Total Mayhem 13, and in August 2008 is made official as the Syndicate's sixth man and beats Eddie Peak at Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 loses to Troy Tornado, draws in a tag with Jeremy Stone against Sam Keith and Eddie Peak and draws with Peak in a steel cage at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 destroys Sam Keith, Hades Bombs Mayhem Midden and Troy Tornado, is barred from title matches for six months and, at Crunch Time, loses the Chain match to Eddie Peak. In November 2008 he beats Stevie Grayson with three Hades Bombs, loses with Tommy Cornell to Rick Law and Troy Tornado and beats the American Eagle, and at A Little South Of Sanity helps Cornell against Troy Tornado until Rick Law drives him away with a baton. In December 2008 he blindsides Rick Law, beats Ricky Dale Johnson with the Hades Bomb, is struck by Rick Law's nightstick, beats John Anderson and Rick Law with Wolf Hawkins and at Psycho Circus is beaten by Law in a No Disqualification match.
