Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Cameron Vessey

Larry Vessey's son, the cocky youngster who yells at Rip Chord on the diary's first page, joins The Firm in February and teams with Casey Valentine as C-V-2. The diary's February 2010 page has him signed with USPW and leaving before the next show; at March Madness he is gone, and Casey Valentine carries the tag belts on without him. Returning from his loan to USPW at All The Leaves Are Brown on 12th October 2011, he appears hooded with a kendo stick to jump Chance Fortune and Zimmy Bumfhole beside Antonio and Casey Valentine, wins the Trios titles with them at Where It All Begins Again, and is thrown out of the battle royal for the new All Action title at Eleventh Hour on 12th November after Rip Chord tells him he never liked him.
