Dominic "Da Boss" Altobelli

aka Da Boss

The Family's own manager, briefcase in hand: he worked Da Boyz's corner at the second Summit and through the PWA's inaugural Tag Team Championship reign, put ten thousand dollars on the knees of the WeWF's tag champions live on that federation's own newsdesk, and turned up in the SWL that November in a white linen suit and spats doing all the talking for Jack the Ripper. SWL (1992)

1992 – 1992
manager — Jack the Ripper
Tenure detail
THE HELD PAIR IS SETTLED BY A DOCUMENT SERVED THE SAME DAY. The strong-joins pass filed this pair presumed-same and marked it thin, because the two records shared "no wrestler, no team and no fed" — only the billing and the gimmick. The WeWF's own newsdesk carries the missing link twice: its rumour mill of 30 January 1992 reads "Dominic ‘Da Boss’ Altobelli of the IWF and WeWF has let a $10,000 open contract on the knees of the Law after his Boyz failed to beat them for the fourth time", and its broadcast of 15 February 1992 runs a live interview with him under the same full name, introduced as the manager of "Da Boyz in the WeWF and Terry ‘the Hit Man’ Haynes and Big Tommy Gunn, the IWF's Chicago Torpedoes". That is one document giving the short billing on this row, the full name on the retired row and the team both records agree he manages — exactly the link the identity-join test asks for and the pair lacked. This row's own name is taken up from the retired one.
Source: the entrant promos posted for the league's own first round (Re: SWL First Round Match-Ups, SWF opening round. (SWL (1992)))
