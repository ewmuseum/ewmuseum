Always Breakin' Hollywood

aka ABH

Primus Allen and Lenny Brown, introduced by Haley Buck at High Noon on 5th June 2012 as her latest clients, "the future of professional wrestling, the essence of glamour", who spell out their initials as "A... B... H..." and say they are living large, "takin' what we wanna and winning every damn time". They beat the West Texas Peacekeepers and the Canadian Blondes in a three-way debut as a full-time team (Brown crushing Marshall Dillon with The Star Treatment), Team Honor at South East Madness, Smith & Singh at Hotter Than Hot and the XX Factor at Back To School on 5th September, and lose the Iron three-way to Team Honor; at Iron they offer Rudy Velasquez a place in their manager's new group and are turned away.
