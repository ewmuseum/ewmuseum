Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Appearance: Hot Mask is never seen without his...you guessed it...mask. A bright red and sparkly head disguise with a large white flame emblem that covers the face (save for the eyes and mouth holes.) Mask's build is very trimmed and lean, with noticable muscle tone that doesn't bear too much muscle mass. Skin is always lightly tanned and hairless, regardless of what time of year. Ring attire consists of his mask, a pair of matching bright red full-length tights with a white exclamation point design over the crotch area, standard white wrestling boots with an old-school 'HM' in red on the sides of the boots, and standard white elbow pads. Biography: Being 19 years old, you would think that an individual would have grown out of childish insults and catcalling, as well as being completely spoiled rotten and self-absorbed. Think again. Hot Mask is about as obnoxious as they come, with an ego that could make hot air balloons cower in fear and a tongue that could make a sailor blush. Taking male chauvenism to it's utmost extreme, Hot Mask is downright degrading, lewd, and immature when it comes to women, and is the antithesis of political correctness. Could also be accused of homophobia, but tends to have a somewhat homoerotic intimidation technique while wrestling in the ring. Despite his immaturity, Hot Mask is as crafty as a 30-year veteran when it comes to his ringwork. A mastermind at bending the rules and getting off scot-free, it is a rarity that he wins a match with a clean pinfall. It's near impossible for Hot Mask to have any fans, because hell...even complete assholes think this guy is a jerk. Add a somewhat squeaky voice and cockroach-type bounciness...and you've got a total package of grossly perverted and self-worshipping talent. As far as background goes, Caleb Mason is a relative unknown in the wrestling world. Wrestling in independents around the Kentucky area with virtually no gimmick whatsoever, Mason decided to seek higher ground. After much plotting, the character of Hot Mask was born. Entering the budding young organization run by Pierre Hyde, Neon Wrestling, Mask debuted and quickly became widely despised by the roster, management, and fans alike. Maintaining a flawless record, he was given a shot at the No-Holds-Barred title held by 'Sensational' Sean White, and defeated him...but the victory was in vain, as Neon Wrestling came to a close that night due to managerial problems. Mask proceeded to pack his bags and pawn the belt for a rare black market porno tape, and make his way to the Ultimate Wrestling Federation, where he currently resides. Manager: Skankalie Manager Description: Don't take Skankalie's name to heart, because to the eye she is anything but wretched. A very beautiful girl with short blonde hair and extremely cute facial features (think: a spitting image of Brittany Daniel from the movie Joe Dirt). However, by some strange force, she is completely under the control of Hot Mask. Forced to wear nothing but a potato sack to accompany him to his matches, and strapped to his side by a leash and collar, she is degraded on a consistant basis by the cocky young wrestler. Carries with her a loaded purse that often conceals some odd foreign object that Mask takes advantage of during matches.
