The West Texas Peacekeepers

aka West Texas Peacekeepers

Marshall Dillon and Bradley Blaze, two manly Texas cowboys whom the narrator pairs off in June 2011 because they are so alike. They debut on the 25th June tour show with a win over Simon Ice and Curtis Jenkins, challenge the Canadian Blondes for the Tag Team titles at Summer Showdown on 26th June, lose a Trios-title tour match to The Triple Threat with Amazing Fire Fly on 4th July, beat the Young Americans and Team XDW on 18th July, lose to VIP Wallace and Jefferson Stardust on 18th August, to Team XDW with The Quest on 1st September and to Ford & Speed's Fallen Chances at Iron. They lose to Ford & Speed at Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011, to Lockdown at Remember November and on 5th January 2012, to the Canadian Blondes at Season's Beatings and to the Second Sons at End Of Days. They lose to Ford & Speed at March Madness on 12th March 2012 and are in the four-team match at April Showers on 5th April. In the diary's pages from June to August 2012 they lose the High Noon three-way on 5th June, challenge the Dark Raptors for the Tag Team titles at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, and lose to Ford & Speed at Summernova on 5th August.
