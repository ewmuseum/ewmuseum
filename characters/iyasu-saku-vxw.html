Tenure detail
Joins Thunder Valenkov and Blackbird in a backstage ambush of Kevin Simmons on Firestorm (3 May 2004), then is challenged to a Hair vs. Title Submission Championship match at the Gauntlet. Already billed as VWF Submission Champion, retains against Kevin Simmons in a Hair vs. Hair match, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and loses the title by submission to The Maquilladora. Submitted to Kevin Simmons's Durham Deathlock for the New Age Army in the ten-man tag loss to the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1. Returns from a shock absence during Wrestle Style V.4's own Caldwell-Bass tournament match (10 September 2004). Pins Sonicman with the Roaring Elbow to open Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), applying the Good and Evil Crossface after the bell until a six-man brawl is ruled a no contest; his own wife Lindsay Simmons later distracts husband Kevin Simmons at ringside. Loses the six-man decider on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) when Kevin Simmons pins Robbie Caldwell, Morris Bass grabbing his own ankle to stop the save. Interferes in the Capsman-Evil Capsman-Gangsta-Sebastian match on March To Hell (21 October 2004), then loses the VWF 24 Hour Championship match to Adam Jennings by pinfall (Hyper Driver) the same show, the Riders of the Apocalypse unable to make the save. Loses to Classy Joey Boy on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), caught with the Kiss of Death out of midair; the Riders attack him afterward rather than CJB. Last man eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), pressed over Lark Fenriz's own head and out. Beaten down by stablemates Sebastian, G-Gangsta and Robbie Caldwell in an apparent staged play to draw out Paddy O'Shea on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Eliminated from the tag team title battle royal alongside The Maquilladora by a charging Roadkill Rick on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), after taking shots at Rick earlier in the match.
