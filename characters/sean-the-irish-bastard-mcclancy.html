Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy

aka Sean McClancy, Bres

A McClancy family brawler billed Bres across the GIWF's 1999 to 2001 run, where he held its North American, Television, Intercontinental and Hardcore titles, and later Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy, carrying three named finishers — Kissing the Blarney Stone III, the Celtic Driver and the Celtic Cross — to BDW's first World Heavyweight Championship and an on-screen marriage to 4WW's Patti Anderson.
