The Whole F**kin' Show

Rob Van Dam and Shawn Michaels, the Show Stealer and the Showstopper, who team on Revolution in November 2006, beat the Acolytes, take the Undisputed ECW World Tag Team Championships from Monsters Inc. at November To Remember with the 420 Splash, make Traci their manager and keep the belts through January and February 2007 while the two partners fall out over her. The partners fall out at Living Dangerously when Rob Van Dam steps off the apron and lets the Iron Saints pin Shawn Michaels for the belts, make up the next night on Blood, Sweat and Beers when Michaels explains himself, trap the Iron Saints with Traci's help, and win the belts back at Baptism Of Fire in the first Halflife Handicap match with a Five Star Frog Splash on Salvatore; in April Rob Van Dam and Traci kiss, and Rob Van Dam is booked against Chris Jericho for the Television Championship at Cyberslam. At Cyberslam in April 2007 Rob Van Dam beats Chris Jericho for the Television Championship in the Lumberjack Match and the team keep the tag belts in the Ladder Match against Los Increibles. In May AJ Styles's debut and Rob Van Dam's jealousy over Traci split the partners, and at Hardcore Heaven Shawn Michaels, bloodied and alone, keeps the tag belts over the Canadian Connection with a third Sweet Chin Music on Christian while Van Dam is away helping Traci; Van Dam then loses the Television Championship to AJ Styles, helped by the Canadian Connection's hockey sticks. In June Van Dam and Michaels refuse to tag each other and Michaels slaps Van Dam, then Traci locks them in a locker room and they make up. In June 2007 Van Dam and Michaels make peace with the Hardcore Innovators, trading hugs and signs on each other's backs, and at Wrestlepalooza keep the World Tag Team Championship over them when Michaels's Diving Elbow and Van Dam's Five Star Frog Splash win simultaneous covers, before the Dudley Boys, back in ECW, attack them. At Summer Assylum they win the Tables Match against the Dudleys with Michaels's Sweet Chin Music through a table on D-Von, are DDT'd by the Innovators afterwards, beat the Innovators on Blood, Sweat and Beers when Ultimo Dudley II hits Cactus Jack with a lid, and in August Traci is pranked by the bWo. At Heatwave in August 2007 they run out after the Innovators win the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles and attack Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer with a Superkick, an Enziguiri, a Flying Elbow Drop and a Five Star Frog Splash, telling them there is room for only one World Tag Team Champions; the Dudley Family and the Innovators then brawl with them in a no contest on Blood, Sweat and Beers. In September they keep the belts over the Dudley Boyz on Revolution with simultaneous finishers, Shawn Michaels pins D-Von with a Super Kick the week after, and they accept the Innovators' challenge for a Champions versus Champions match at Anarchy Rulz. When Michaels walks in on Traci changing and covers her as footsteps approach, Rob Van Dam takes the wrong meaning and the partners fall out for two weeks, Van Dam leaving Michaels alone against the Dudleyz in a non-title match, until they hug it out backstage on the last Monday before the pay-per-view. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 they lose the Champions versus Champions match by a no contest after the Dudley Family run in. In October Traci is carried out on a stretcher after the Dudleyz drop her onto the guard rail while Michaels and Van Dam are distracted, and the partners quarrel over which stipulation to ask for until Paul Heyman books a match with ladders, chairs, canes and flaming tables; at Guilty As Charged Michaels shoves D-Von through a burning table, then his Super Kick catches Van Dam in mid-air and Bubba Ray pins Van Dam, ending the reign. On Blood, Sweat and Beers Van Dam punches Michaels and later calls for a reunion, and Michaels hugs him and Super Kicks him again ('that Super Kick last night was no accident and nor was this'). In November 2007 Shawn Michaels apologises on Revolution for the Super Kick at Guilty As Charged and calls Rob Van Dam his brother; Van Dam asks for a one-on-one match and, when Michaels says that he cannot fight his brother, hits him with the Van Daminator and a chair, and on Blood, Sweat and Beers he appears at the entrance to distract Michaels in his match with CM Punk. Michaels refuses the match again, but the two save each other from the True World Order and beat it together on Revolution, and Paul Heyman, 'sorry to go over your head', books Rob Van Dam against Shawn Michaels at November To Remember XV; on the last Blood, Sweat and Beers before it they are on opposite sides of a tag match and still join to beat down Kurt Angle, and on the last Revolution they jump off opposite corners together onto the brawl that wrecks the ring. At November To Remember XV Shawn Michaels and Rob Van Dam walk to the ring together to Pantera and trade a contest of alphabet moves (RVD: 'It's November 2007, bro... they're on strike') before the match the diary rates A: Michaels beats Van Dam with a Sweet Chin Music that drives into his face the chair Van Dam is carrying, and both bow to the crowd. On Revolution on the first Monday of December Van Dam hugs Michaels backstage and says he wants a rematch, 'since we're both in Masters of Pain Qualifiers'; on the Saturday show Van Dam beats The Great Sasuke by knockout after the Van Daminator, Stevie Richards runs in and Michaels clears him with Sweet Chin Music.
