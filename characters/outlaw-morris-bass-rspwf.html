Tenure detail
Appeared only in a lengthy promo segment with Kevin Simmons on Sin City Fights November 18, celebrating his own solo Tag Team Championship win and helping straitjacket-Widowmaker a beaten Anthony O'Malley; defended the VXW Tag Team Championship against The O'Malleys on Sin City Fights November 27 and lost by disqualification when Simmons chair-shotted Marcus O'Malley. Wins the six-man decider on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), grabbing Saku's ankle to stop a save on Kevin Simmons's pin of Caldwell.

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-02-26 – 2004-11-08
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 12 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 8-2-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Runs in alongside fellow Society of Doom members Kevin Simmons and Kagedo to clear the ring after Ric Beauty's induction into the New Age Army on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004); does not wrestle a match that night. Wrestles his first attested vwf-vegas-2003 match, eliminated from the Dog Day One singles battle royal by Flash while attempting to powerslam The Maquilladora out. Wins the Dog Day Two singles bracket's first round over Setzer Van Strife, then the semifinal over Jason Storm with LOCO's help. Chokeslams majority owner Kingston Cash on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) after being warned off violence, then competes in the eight-man singles scramble. Wins the Dog Days Four Singles Six Man on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), loses the Fourway Final to a disputed double-cover ruling, then wins a singles elimination match over Lord Zero, Flash and Sidious The Clown and wins the Dog Days Four Tag Team Elimination Match's own semifinal spot for a short-handed Society of Doom that same night, wrestling three matches total; eliminated from the singles Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), dropkicked into barbed wire by Setzer Van Strife. Chokeslammed The Salvation for the Society of Doom's win on Wrestle Style V.1, saw a signed tag match collapse before the bell on Wrestle Style V.2, then beat Capsman with the Widowmaker in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Loses an Electric Ropes match to Robbie Caldwell at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) after Sid Starr, a ringside interloper billed from the WILD promotion, shocks him on the electrified ropes; runs in with the Society of Doom later the same show to save stablemate Kevin Simmons from a Kingston Cash chair attack during the Simmons-Saku ladder match. Wins by disqualification with Kevin Simmons over The Mighty Men on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Helps chase off RAR and the Rodeo Boys from a backstage beatdown of Simmons on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), then stands in for an injured Marcus O'Malley to successfully defend the VWF Tag Team Championship alongside Mark McQueen, submitting Billy Bones with the Devils Snare. Eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), knocked over the top rope alongside Fast Eddie Cutlass.
