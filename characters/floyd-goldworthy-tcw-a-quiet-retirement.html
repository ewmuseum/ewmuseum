Floyd Goldworthy

aka The Fast Buck

'The Fast Buck', a long-standing heel manager, who speaks for American Buffalo while Buffalo plots revenge on Tyson Baine. In September 2008 he also manages the Fly Boys, and looks horrified when American Buffalo turns on Kingman & Alexander with them. In November 2008 he is held against a wall by Mayhem Midden, who tells him that American Buffalo's contract is subcontracted through his manager's licence, and is taunted by Phil Vibert. In December 2008 he whispers that his clients are not out of the championship picture, is confronted by American Buffalo over his title shot and manages the Fly Boys against the New Wave and the Keith Brothers.
