Team Seduction

The group Seduction gathers in July 2007 to end Darryl Devine's career: Human Arsenal and Fumihiro Ota, joined by his former friend Jacob Jett; William Hayes, offered a place, turns on them. Arsenal and Ota, as Team Seduction, beat The Dudez!!!1! at Summer of Love in August 2007 to win the World Tag Team Championship. Arsenal and Ota keep the belts from The Dudez!!!1! in a ladder match at Blaze of Glory in September 2007, and the group with Triple D, Jett and Big Cat Brandon goes on as Divinity. In October 2007 Arsenal drops Sam Strong through his own desk with a C4 Suplex, and the team beats Veteran Mentality three times, the last at A Haunting in America. In November 2007 Arsenal and Ota side with Seduction against Triple D, who leaves her tied to a chair in the Masked Man's video to finish a match; they lose the tag belts to The Valentines at Pilgrimage when Devine turns on Arsenal, and Arsenal takes Devine to over twenty minutes on the Monday of Week 3. Arsenal and Ota lose to Umaga and Freddie Datsun on the Thursday of Week 3, November 2007 and beat The Best Tag Team Like Ever on the Monday of Week 4; the group takes Devine on after he injures Arsenal, and the author calls Arsenal's 26:35 loss to Devine on the Thursday of Week 2, December the new best match in USPW history.
