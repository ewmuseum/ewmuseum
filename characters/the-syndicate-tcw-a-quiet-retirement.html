The Syndicate

Tommy Cornell's stable, the holders of almost all of TCW's gold, with Sam Keith, Wolf Hawkins, the Machines and, from May 2008, Edd Stone; by Total Mayhem 13 it holds all four active championships, and by the diary's preview for the fourth week of July 2008 Sam Keith is, it seems, out of it, and in August 2008 signs Tyson Baine as its sixth man while the Machines break away, and in September 2008 adds Jeremy Stone and the Enforcers, Tommy Cornell promising that by July 2009 every championship in TCW will belong to it, and in November 2008 sees Wolf Hawkins refuse to take the Hard-Hitting Championship match and Tommy Cornell tell him the Syndicate needs a belt at A Little South Of Sanity, where Cornell loses to Troy Tornado and Hawkins is pinned, and in December 2008 sees Tommy Cornell and Wolf Hawkins quarrel over Aaron Andrews, Wolf Hawkins tell Brent Hill he is beneath him and Tyson Baine and the Enforcers brawl with Rick Law, until Cornell and Hawkins argue in the ring at Psycho Circus as Sam Keith watches.
