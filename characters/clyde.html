Biography (UWF (2001–07))

There is truthfully very little known about the demon named Clyde. That is more than likely for the best. What is known is that Clyde claims to be a walking incarnation of Death itself. No one has been able to refute these claims, as his ability to maim and slaughter to a ridiculous degree in very public avenues such as professional wrestling programs while somehow or another avoiding all forms of law intervention is well documented. For over 2000 years (to the best of his recollection), the burned man has wandered the earth, unable to find death for himself, cursed by his own immortality. So, it should come as no great shock to find he is an incredibly bitter individual who would like nothing better than to either find an effective method of suicide, or barring that, the eradication of all life on earth. Fueled by literally ages of hatred, he has every intention of accomplishing his goals......
