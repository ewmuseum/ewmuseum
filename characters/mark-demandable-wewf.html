That's right, Ramblin' with DeMandable is moving to the …” — and then this federation's own acronym, spelled with its middle two letters transposed, as that segment does twice (for the show and for its camera crew) against fifty-seven correct spellings elsewhere in the held record. The typo is the document's own and is served as written; it is described rather than repeated here so it does not read as a link to the unrelated frontier row that shares the transposed form. A show moves with the man who hosts it, and the WeWF's own This Week in Wrestling carries him on its desk conducting the same kind of interview.

UeWF (1991)

1992 – 1992
Tenure detail
THE LINE: a uewf tenure is warranted where a held document BILLS THE ENTITY AS THAT FEDERATION'S OWN, or STAGES IT FOR ONE OF THAT FEDERATION'S OWN CHAMPIONSHIPS. Entry to a tournament held at an interfederation event is not by itself enough — the Summit's tournaments were open to all comers and a bracket slot says only that somebody entered. WHAT SETTLES THIS ROW: he works for the federation on camera. The broadcast of 25 February 1992 cuts "to ringside at a UeWF Card" where "Vince McMayonasse and Mark DeMandable are doing play-by-play", and the interview segment of 15 February has him open with "I came all the way here from the UeWF, just to talk to you." Billed as that federation's own — as one of its broadcasters, which is the role recorded here. He wrestles no UeWF match.
Source: WeWF This Week in Wrestling
