The End

PURE's Junior Heavyweight Champion, billed as a blind man who claimed to be half Japanese and arrived by way of PCW, and the only wrestler the fed's own record ever names as holding one of its belts. Written by AI, 2026

Hometown   No Permanent Residence
Alignment   Face
Style   Developed as much for flash and flair as it is for damage, The End is an All-Rounder, with high-flyer preference (no brawling, no closed fists, no low blows, no eye rakes, no hair pulls, no biting, etc.). The End is very strong for 225 pounds, routinely lifting and tossing around men he physically shouldn't be able to (up to about 300 pounds, then it gets harder). This combined with his unnatural speed and agility make him a dangerous adversary. The End's blindness has made him modify his offense only to the extent that he does not change the attacks he does, but has turned to chain wrestling, turning one move into another into another, to keep him opponent in one spot and deal with them there.
Finisher(s)   1) Never Again 2) Forever More — 1) The End hits the One Last Time, then immediately rolls back into a Dragon Sleeper locked in from a Camel Clutch position. 2) The End goes to the apron, and as his opponent tries to stand, The End leaps at him/her with a springboard corkscrew shooting star DDT.

After a high angle springboard landing backflip into the center of the ring to show his agility, The End stretches for his match in his corner. <hr> <b>****Bio****</b> The End grew up without his parents, they died in a car accident when he was four. He doesn't dwell on it, per se, but he still remembers them fondly. The End's life up to the age of eighteen is hard to follow, due to records being lost. It is believed that he was an orphan in Virginia, USA until that point, but the facility closed under undisclosed circumstances, and nothing but a burnt wreck stands in place of it. Once The End got out of the orphange, he went to college and gained degrees in Biochemistry and Business Administration. He doesn't seem to care much for these fields, he probably just considered it something to do. We don't know how, but it is presumed that at some point he went to Japan. The End says that he did, and saw a Puroresu event. He credits that as being his inspiration to get into the sport of wrestling. The End came back to America, and apparently he gained employment in a garbage federation known as the HWF. We couldn't find many records from it as the federation has been closed for over a year, but we were able to verify his employment in the federation under the name 'Enigma.' This 'Enigma' would later surface in the UWWC, a bigger regional federation. Records from this federation show that The End was its star performer, losing only 3 professional matches, none of which were clean defeats. He is famous for being the only Grand Slam (or as the UWWC referred to him, the 'OmniChampion') Champion in the federation's history. The End changed his name to Seppuku during his tenure in UWWC and continued his rocket to regional stardom. When the UWWC closed last summer, The End came to the PTC looking to make a name for himself. He joined the PWA and within a week was promoted to EW, a developmental organization for PCW. There he won his first PTC title, the Primetanium Title, against a man who had cheated to hold it for eight weeks with a crooked promoter working to keep it on the champion. During his time in EW, The End's employer made the entire roster submit to house-arrest tagging. The End willingly took the house arrest implant, but misinformation resulted in a faultly experimental chip to be implanted in the back of his neck. When The End removed the chip himself, it caused him to go blind. The End's blindness, by all studies and medical reports, is irreversible and he must live with this condition for the rest of his life. The End was faced with a hard choice, to give up wrestling, or to continue and work through his disability, turning it into an advantage. He worked hard, and quickly adjusted to his lack of sight, and within but a few short weeks, The End was fully competent in blind-fighting, and he returned to wrestling ready to regain his lost time in the ring. With his honor code, innovative offense, and disability overcome, The End was one of EW's top stars until the federation was expelled from the PTC. At that point, The End decided to stay with the PTC, and his loyalty and talent paid off as PCW finally accepted him into its roster. The End has been signed with PCW for nearly 8 months now and continues to wrestle there, even winning the Cruiserweight Title in that federation, but The End's one true love has always been Puroresu wrestling, something he hopes to be more involved in now with joining PURE for the second time.
