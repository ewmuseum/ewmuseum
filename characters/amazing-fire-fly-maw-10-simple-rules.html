Amazing Fire Fly

A gangly Mexican lightweight in yellow and orange gear and mask, making his pro debut at Spring Fever in April 2010 as the Chord family's chosen challenger for the Heavyweight title; Jay Chord beats him with the Cradle Piledriver, and he is on the card for Summer Showdown. He is on the losing side of the Trios title match at Summer Showdown in June 2010, loses to Jefferson Stardust at Hotter Than Hot, to Firebird at Beach Blast, to Toxin at All The Leaves Are Brown in his best match to date, and with the Internet Favourites in a trios match at Where It All Begins Again, and is brutalised by Evil Spirit in the new masked worker's debut at Eleventh Hour in November 2010. He takes his first MAW win on the 4th December 2010 tour show, loses with the Internet Favourites at End Of Days, loses to Hugh de Aske in the 2011 qualifiers and beats Antonio with the Sky High Fire Fly at April Showers; the narrator notes in March that he has won the WAM TV title in Mexico. He beats Curtis Jenkins on 25th April, loses to Jared Johnson on 4th and 11th June, and with Kid Arachnid loses a Tag Team title challenge to the Canadian Blondes at High Noon, where the match is an experiment with "twinned masks"; he beats Jefferson Stardust, Simon Ice, Extreme Deluxe and Xavier Reckless on later tour shows and loses to Curtis Jenkins and Simon Ice. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses to Providence at Leap Into The Unknown, with Jared Johnson to Ford & Speed at All Or Nothing and to Raphael at Spring Fever, beats Primus Allen with a Sky High Fire Fly at South East Crisis on 8th April and is attacked afterwards by Lenny Brown, and with Fumihiro Ota loses to Allen and Brown at May The Force Be With You on 5th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he loses to JD Morgan at Summer Showdown on 12th June, to Eddie Powell's Motion Censor at Hotter Than Hot, to The Great Ota at Beach Blast, where he says it sickens him to see Ota with The Fallen, and to Lord Geoffrey Windameer at Iron on 12th August, after which he hits Black Eagle with a Cutter through a table for a two count that Firebird breaks up; he is in the Back To School six-man with Donnie J and Morgan. The narrator says he cannot let him go but that his hopes for a MAW star have gone, and that he has won a second Television title in WAM. Written by AI, 2026
