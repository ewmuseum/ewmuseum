The Young Guns

aka Young Guns

Harry Allen and Steve Gumble, former TCW tag champions with a new punk look, who join Painful Procedure. In September 2008 they beat Tyler & Huggins, with a finish the commentary calls the Blitzkrieg Bop, and Miyazaki & Ota, and help Art Reed beat Edd Stone and the Enforcers, and in November 2008 beat the Fly Boys and lose to the Easy Riders, and in December 2008 lose to the Elite Express and win with Troy Tornado over the School of Tradition's Acid, Eric Tyler and Freddy Huggins.
