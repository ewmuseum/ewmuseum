Shadow

Shadow, the WMW stable member who fought for The Kindred against Global's Manifest Destiny at Crossfire (2004) and was renamed Lia Ambrosi on air in GCW, held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship twice and the Cruiserweight Championship once, was named Wrestler of the Year (2007), Match of the Year (2006), Feud of the Year (2006), Writer of the Year (2006) and appeared on 48 GCW cards from 9 July 2006 to 4 February 2009.
