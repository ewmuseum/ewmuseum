UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2004-11-13
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 11 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 1-8-1, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Billed from Brookline, Massachusetts, a former UWF World Champion; teamed with Alex Extreme in a losing three-way main event for the UWF World Tag Team Championship, ruled a no-contest, on 9 August 2003. Teamed with returning legend Alex Kidd in a losing tag team main event against Scott Daniels and Tom Landis on 30 August 2003, pinned after Landis's exposed-turnbuckle Crossed-Arms Bomb on Kidd. Teamed with Alex Kidd, Paul Wong, and Youth Gone Wild to a count-out win over the Pride on 22 October 2003. Fought Vyolynce to a ten-minute time-limit draw on 12 November 2003, hitting the Fatal Hesitation for a pinfall the referee ruled came just after time expired. Challenged Chris O'Brien for the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World on 20 December 2003, losing when O'Brien caught him with an accidental Ace Crusher counter off Brown's own inverted DDT attempt. Interrupted the previous match to attack Scott Daniels with a baseball bat and challenge him to an Iron Man match at Eve of Destruction. Challenged Uriah Hawkins for the Rampage Championship in a three-way dance on 15 May 2004 also involving Troy Grace, losing when Hawkins caught both challengers together with a double DDT for the pin; chased Victor Frost through the arena afterward after Frost terrorized manager Victoria Marquess backstage. Lost his UWF North American Heavyweight Championship challenge to Victor Frost by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, shoved off the top rope by his own manager Victoria Marquess, who then revealed a secret alliance with Frost and helped him finish with the Violator Mark II. Teamed with Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker and Mike Sebastian) in a Meltdown main event loss to Tom Landis and Team Canada on 25 August 2004, kicking Kari Stevens's own thrown salt back into her face before Landis and Trevor Hextall's Game Misconduct combination floored Andrew Tucker for the pin. Lost a non-title three-way dance to Sabbath on 2 October 2004, pinned alongside Papa Legba, then shook Sabbath's hand after the bell. Lost a Tower of Doom cage match on 30 October 2004 alongside Uriah Hawkins, Alex Extreme, and Alex Martinez to the team of Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, Papa Legba, and Luke Kinsey, who escaped the structure first when Alex Martinez inadvertently threw Winston clear of the cage; joined the others in a post-bell beatdown of Winston with his own solid gold knuckles, welcoming a surprise-returning "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee back into the fold. Lost a UWF North American Heavyweight Championship challenge to champion "Violent" Victor Frost by pinfall (a high-elevation Death Valley Driver) on 13 November 2004, after Frost pulled his own manager Simone Sumner into the path of a missile dropkick to save himself; carried the injured Sumner to the back afterward.
