Blonde Bombshell

The valet who brings Johnny Bloodstone to the Where Angels Fear To Tread ring and goes with him on the shows that follow. In September 2008 she raises Troy Tornado's and Johnny Bloodstone's hands together at Destructive Energy after they make Tommy Cornell submit. In November 2008 she is back with Johnny Bloodstone, goes to ringside for Sam Keith, Matthew Keith and Greg Keith, and tells the Total Wrestling audience that she has two champions under contract and wants a third, saying she did not understand how good she had it in TCW the first time. In December 2008 she is at ringside for Johnny Bloodstone, Sam Keith, the Keith Brothers, Art Reed and Sean Deeley, and speaks at Psycho Circus for Johnny Bloodstone as the best of Sam Keith's trainees.
