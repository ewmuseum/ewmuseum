Baroness Emily

A demoted manager who aligns with Koshiro Ino at Total Mayhem 13 in a black leather bodysuit with his facepaint across the chest. In September 2008 she tells Chris Rockwell to meet Ino at Destructive Energy, is punched by Rockwell and watches him beat Ino. In November 2008 she leads Koshiro Ino to ringside, talks quietly with Ino and Shingen Miyazaki and is at ringside for Ino and Team VIPER. In December 2008 she is at ringside for Hidekazu, Koshiro Ino and Team VIPER. Written by AI, 2026
