THE 1991-TO-1996 JOIN. The same gimmick, under a name that is not a stock one, on the same newsgroup five years later: at Leprecharnage and again on the Monday Night Blow-Out of 22 April 1996 he leads a Second World War Imperial Japanese Navy tag team to the ring, taps a rattan cane against the guard railing, blinds an opponent with salt and calls the opposing manager Obayun. That is the Summit and SWL gimmick continued, not a coincidence of spelling — the same three-word invented name, the same Imperial Japanese heel faction, the same medium, and the same alliance's own orbit (this federation was a Summit Wrestling Alliance member). The federation's own broadcast spells it both Hojo Tojomoto and Tojo Hojomoto inside one match; the registered spelling is unchanged.
