Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Randy Bumfhole

A former SWF worker, dropped by Richard Eisen shortly before Spring Fever, who debuts there with Chance Fortune on 23rd April 2011 and whom the narrator calls possibly the most talented worker on the roster; his pay of $3,000 a show is the figure the rest of the roster will want matched. He joins Steve Flash's new Firm, beats Casey Valentine on 12th May, loses with Kid Arachnid to Valentine and Antonio on 26th May, is ambushed and held to a time-limit draw by Antonio at High Noon, beats Erik Strong at Summer Showdown and Black Eagle at Hotter Than Hot and, with Flash, beats Antonio and Strong at Beach Blast. He wins the Trios titles with Arachnid and Chance Fortune at Summernova on 12th August and pins Firebird with the Bumfhole Buster in the first defence at Back To School. In August the narrator reports that he has signed with TCW. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he beats Jay Chord in a twenty-five-minute match with the Bumfhole Buster, winning a Heavyweight title shot, which he cashes in at All The Leaves Are Brown for a time-limit draw with Aaron Andrews. With his brother Zimmy as The Fabulous Bumfholes he pins Andrews at Eleventh Hour on 12th November; the narrator wants him to sign for the next year, and tew.com reports Richard Eisen's plan to raid him. After the departures to the SWF he is the locker room's answer to Andrews, wins a rematch clause, beats Knuckles with Kirk Jameson and Fumihiro Ota at End Of Days and Nathan Coleman with Jameson on 5th January 2012, and loses the Heavyweight title match to Andrews at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January, cut over his left eye and with a wrecked ankle. In the diary's pages from February to April 2012 he loses to Firebird at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, beats Black Eagle with the Bumfhole Buster when he and KC Glenn beat the Dark Raptors at All Or Nothing, is beaten up backstage by Primus Allen and loses the Traditional title match to him at Third Time's The Charm on 5th March, is kicked out of the Gold Rush match by Aaron Andrews at April Showers and loses a steel cage Heavyweight title match to Andrews at Spring Fever on 12th April, injuring his knee. The narrator writes that he refused to re-sign and has left MAW: twenty-five matches at an average C for $3,000 a time. In the diary's pages from August to September 2012 he returns at South East Grand Parade on 9th August for a few shows, thanks to Rich Money and Kirk Jameson, to take revenge on Aaron Andrews, and wins the six-man there with Team Honor; the narrator's news has him signed to what appear to be the last days of 4C. At Back To School on 5th September he draws with The Great Ota, whom he calls his former friend, in a thrilling clash. Written by AI, 2026
