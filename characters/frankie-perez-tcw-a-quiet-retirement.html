Frankie Perez

A young submission wrestler with the Perfect Parity, hired to job his way to recognition, who impresses against Ricky Dale Johnson and slides in with a low blow at Total Mayhem 13, and in August 2008 beats Aaron Andrews and Danny Fonzarelli with the P-Clutch, teams with Chris Rockwell and American Buffalo, and is beaten by Sam Keith, and in September 2008 beats the Keith Brothers with Chris Rockwell and Clark Alexander, loses to John Anderson and to Koshiro Ino, then beats Danny Fonzarelli at Destructive Energy to have him shaved bald, and in October 2008 loses to Ricky Dale Johnson and Johnny Bloodstone, wins the number one contendership against Hell Monkey and Sammy Bach and, at Crunch Time, beats Edd Stone with the P-Clutch for the All Action Championship. In November 2008 he loses a non-title match to Rick Law on Badge of Honor, teams with Chris Rockwell and Sean Deeley to beat Art Reed & the Keith Brothers and at A Little South Of Sanity keeps the All Action Championship, beating Art Reed, Greg Keith and Freddy Huggins with the P-Clutch. In December 2008 he abandons Chris Rockwell, saying he only sides with winners, joins Remo Richardson and Phil Vibert, low blows Ricky Dale Johnson, beats Tooth & Claw and the Balcony Express with Remo Richardson and at Psycho Circus keeps the All Action Championship, beating Hell Monkey with the P-Clutch.
