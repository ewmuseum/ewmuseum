PRIME (2003)

Tenure detail
Billed as Sin City Championship Wrestling's own, "The Big Stack", a Las Vegas poker player riding with Alexandra Pierce's Dead Man's Hand, he came into PRIME for the two federations' war of 2008: turned away at the arena door with the Hand on ReVolution 170 (27 August), thrown out of the building after lariating Mary-Lynn Mayweather at ringside on ReVolution 171 (3 September), and lariating Captain Justice from behind on ReVolution 172 (10 September). At Cataclysm: PRIME vs. SCCW on 19 September 2008 he and Reginald V. Lovecraft, billed as SCCW's Strength in Numbers Champions, beat PRIME's Tag Team Champions, Team V.I.A.G.R.A., Kennedy pinning Tony Davis, and he ran interference in Alexandra Pierce's main-event cage match that night.
