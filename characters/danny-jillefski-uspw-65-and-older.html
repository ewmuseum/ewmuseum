Danny Jillefski

The on-screen authority figure beside Sam Strong, who makes matches for the shows and grants Freddie Datsun his shot at Bruce the Giant. In October 2007 he sets terms that keep Brett Hall and Bulldozer Brandon Smith apart, books Freddie Datsun against a mystery opponent, and runs the Monday of Week 3 as acting general manager while Sam Strong recovers. In November 2007, as the general manager, he books Pilgrimage, has Eddie Peak put Jacob Stranton in intensive care, tricks Sam Strong into signing USPW over to him, refs the World Championship finish in a referee's shirt, and as the company's new owner opens the Monday of Week 3 with Divinity around him. In late November and December 2007 he agrees under the Masked Man's arm to Strong's offer, has Devine thrown out of his office, sets up a hooded Casey Valentine for Devine, wears a referee's shirt to count Devine's pin on Human Arsenal, takes a phone call from Tommy Cornell, and tells a caller named Vibert that 'Operation Justice just got pushed forward'.
