Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Heidi Brooks

A highly respected former women's star whom the narrator hires in November 2009 as an extra backstage presence to calm the locker room; she helps him find the way round the standoff with Rip and Jay Chord, is the narrator's "Good old Heidi" when the crowd chants at Jay at End Of Days, and spends the following months teasing him about Katie Cameron. She attends the locker-room meeting after Remember November in November 2010 and keeps the narrator informed of the roster's contract news in the winter of 2011. In September 2011 she tells the narrator in tears that Rip Chord will not renew her contract because the company is running at a loss, and urges him to take Tommy Cornell's offer and get out before Rip runs MAW into the ground; she brings ice to the narrator's office after Jay Chord's fight with Hugh de Aske.
