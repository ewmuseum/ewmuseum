Tenure detail
A dark-match tryout for a spot on the VWF roster. Hot Property's Austin Lee and Stevie Knight publicly denounce LOCO before the Gauntlet's own Four Corners Tag Team Championship match, in which Hot Property are the last team eliminated, Lee's own partner Knight pinned after Billy Bones trips him. Wins the VWF Tag Team Championship from the Rodeo Boys in a Flashback ambush on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminated by Billy Bones in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal before Hot Property wins the Dog Days Tag Team Elimination match. Teams with Stevie Knight to win the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round over Sonicman and Sebastian, then joins Flash in beating down the Rodeo Boys during a special Flashback on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) before competing in the sixteen-man tag scramble. Wins a six-man tag on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), eliminated from the Dog Days Four Tag Team Scramble by his own partner Mark McQueen walking off, then bladed by The Equalizer in the tag Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Returns to the VWF with Stevie Knight as Hot Property at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), interrupting the retiring Sebastian and G-Gangsta's own post-match celebration and attacking them, only for both Legends to fight the pair off with a Legend Killer and a Gangsta Smash. Runs in with Hot Property partner Stevie Knight at End Of March (30 March 2005), hitting the Property Boom on Mark McQueen and costing champions Team Brittania a disqualification loss to McQueen and Thunder Valenkov. Helps Hot Property beat Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after Valenkov turns on his own partner. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, eliminated when Simon Davidson pulls him down off the apron despite Davidson's own elimination minutes earlier.
