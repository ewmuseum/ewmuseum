Eugene Williams

One of TCW's three referees, the one who works the opening matches and the International title match at Where Angels Fear To Tread. In November 2008 he ejects Acid from ringside, lets Rick Law & Troy Tornado go two on one against Tommy Cornell & Tyson Baine and sends the Badge of Honor six-man match to a no contest. In December 2008 he rules the Tommy Cornell and Troy Tornado match a no contest after twenty-one minutes. Written by AI, 2026
