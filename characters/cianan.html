Biography (PURE (2002))

Born Akira Witt, Cianan grew up in New York, having a rather rough child hood, due to his half breed heritage, in a mostly Italian neighborhood. As a child he was picked on, and beat up. Home life wasn't fun either, his father was a workaholic, and his mother was a stay at home Mom, both fought constantly and were verbally, mentally, and physically abusive to young Cianan. His only escape were his heroes of wrestling, which he longed to be. Cianan's father eventually had a breakdown, after a physical accident enabling him to work. His father went into a deep depression, and fell asleep, before slipping into a coma, leaving Cianan as the sole provider for his home. This put incredible stress on Cianan and his mother, who got more and more abusive, as Cianan started growing weary with both school and working to support two people at a young age. As soon as he turned 18, he left, never to return home. Cianan soon made his way to Japan, where he heard wrestling was in high graces at the time. He quickly joined the first fed that he was accepted to and began training. Though, due to his tainted herritage, his training was harsher then normal, but his will to prove them wrong, and earn there respect. His respect for man kind also went down, along with a small bit of bitterness that continued to grow, during his training. It escaladed to him finishing his training, in high regards, having earned the respect of his tormentors for the past years. In his first official match, he let himself slip, and during a tag match, broke his opponents neck, with a brain buster. He was immediately expelled from the promotion. So, Cianan went back home, to the USA. He struggled through, working with indy fed after indy fed, until coming across CCW. He quickly saw a Transatlantic Title match featuring Kensuke Suzahara, and joined the federation. After his joining, Kensuke disappeared. Cianan started off slowly, working a few feuds, and winning a few titles here and there. Coming close to gaining the CCW World Title #1 contenders ship. After failing, he soon grasped onto a strong position that elevated his career, the CCW Commissionership. He gripped it, and started using his power. While, at the same time joining Jesse Ramey, and Enigma in a stable called The Lost Souls. Thus, the embarrassment began. Due to problems with Koriann, Jesse started his cross dressing career. Cianan was horrified, and embarrassed at this course of events, and the frustration that he was putting forth more effort then his compatriots. He quickly proved that he could take care of the trash, by powerbombing Jesse Ramey over the ring to his 'death.' Aghast at his actions, all of CCW was now against him, and Cianan struggled to hold on. He fought person after person scrounging for more control, and to torture the enemies he made. Cianan turned Enigma's own mother against him, and took away his hard-core title. Cianan took Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes, Independent Title, and handed it over to Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen. He took Jason McAllister out of CCW. All the while, constantly being compared to Kensuke Suzahara, which drove him mad. He was obsessed with proving himself better than Kensuke. His chance came. Kensuke, and his friend Fanatik, finally came back to CCW after long absences. Cianan finally had his chances, but unable to get his hands on Kensuke, due to presidential restrictions. Kensuke won the world title, and the president.. Disappeared. Cianan grasped the reigns of CCW, and immediately took ultimate control. After losing a harsh match to Enigma, which nearly left Cianan crippled, he was challenged by Kensuke to a #1 contenders match. Cianan accepted, to a Singapore cane on a pole match, which ended quickly, with Kensuke's former girlfriend turning on him, and joining Cianan as a lover. Cianan had his title shot, but quickly lost in a submission match to Kensuke, which sent him over the edge. He was the submission wrestler, not Kensuke, it hurt his pride. Cianan hounded Kensuke, and finally got Kensuke in another match for the title.. A 60 Minute Iron Man Match. This time, Cianan came through for himself, and half crippled Kensuke and himself, winning the title. After a small embarrassment, losing the title on his first defense, He manipulated it back into his hands, without having to fight for it, and using the rule book. Cianan had everything. Soon, he had nothing. During a title defense against long time rivals Blayze and Enigma, a spot was blown (or not.) and Cianan's neck was broken, he fought on, and won his defense. But, quickly he was out, and emotionally torn, as his love, announced that she was pregnant.
