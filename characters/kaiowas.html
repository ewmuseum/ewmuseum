Tenure detail
His own name is glossed in the fed's own text as Bloody Vengeance, after a Ktulu tribal story he tells at length. The fed's own post-UnStAbLe segment has him at a fire on a hilltop with Xander Payne, Acaldema and Rainer, telling them a Ktulu war story and calling the four of them Stable Champions, while SurReal speaks for them in the arena as the self-proclaimed leader of the New Breed — a name the same passage also gives as the fed's own [d.W.o] and as the Fear Factory song SurReal exits to. The stable is recorded here as prose rather than as a unit, since nothing else in the held record names it and the name itself is not securely the faction's own. PTCpedia's own Kaiowas article (/promotions/ptc/archive/ptcpedia-kaiowas.html, captured 20 Aug 2010) claims he was the DWO's first two-time World Heavyweight Champion and holds that title's longest reign, bills him from Rio de Janeiro finishing with the Tribal Punisher, and has him lead the New Breed after SurReal left.
