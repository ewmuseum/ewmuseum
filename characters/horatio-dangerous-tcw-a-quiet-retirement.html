Horatio Dangerous

aka Harry 'Horatio' Dangerous

The diary's first-person narrator, a retired wrestler who takes over TCW's booking from Joel Bryant after the "Just Another Day?" pay-per-view in March 2008, takes the third seat on the commentary desk, hosts the Spotlight Interviews and sets down the shows as a book for TCW's own books line. In September 2008 he writes of a best-ever pay-per-view buyrate for Hotter Than Hell, a second developmental federation in Puerto Rico and the SWF's change of booker. In October 2008 he interviews Jeremy Stone over three weeks on Total Wrestling. In November 2008 he finds himself tied in a length of silver chain in his hotel room by Eddie Peak's prank, learns from a phone call that Jennifer Cornell has arranged the signing of seventeen women through Angel Athletic Association, renames the jobber Dave Wallace as the American Eagle, renegotiates Badge of Honor for another two seasons and calls the shows on commentary. In December 2008 he learns that his daughter Kate has signed with TCW, agrees to a return to the ring after ten years in an intergender tag with her, is beaten up by Chris Rockwell on commentary and in the match, interviews Texas Pete over three Total Wrestling shows and sees his sons Richard and Adrian weigh offers from other promotions.
