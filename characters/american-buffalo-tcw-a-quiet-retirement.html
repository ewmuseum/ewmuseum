American Buffalo

aka Morpheus

A hot-tempered brawler managed by Floyd Goldworthy, who Stampedes Tyson Baine through a barrier from the ramp to repay a betrayal the diary says came twelve years before, when Baine was BLZ Bubb and he was Morpheus. In September 2008 he loses to Koshiro Ino and, with Fumihiro Ota, to Kingman & Alexander, then shakes his head at Goldworthy and Stampedes the Kings with the Fly Boys. In November 2008 he blindsides Kingman & Alexander on Badge of Honor, loses to Koshiro Ino, loses with JD Morgan to the New Wave and is fined and warned by Mayhem Midden through Floyd Goldworthy. In December 2008 he demands his title shot from Floyd Goldworthy and loses to John Anderson after Wolf Hawkins's interference. Written by AI, 2026
