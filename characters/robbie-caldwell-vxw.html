VXW (2001)

2005-07-25 – 2006-11-06
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 14 parsed matches, VXW (2001)): 2-10-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Enters the Second Chance Battle Royal on Sin City Fights August 31, apparently eliminated by Anthony O'Malley while the referee's attention was diverted, then slides back in unnoticed and eliminates both O'Malleys himself, though the referee ruled the O'Malleys the winners regardless; meets Sebastian backstage on Sin City Fights September 11, then interferes in the Omega-Marcus O'Malley submission match with a tazer shot on Omega; runs in for Sebastian against Purgatory and the Polish Powers on Sin City Fights September 19, is named The Equalizer's own new tag partner against Sebastian at Vegas Violence, then interferes twice more, tazing Thunder Valenkov's challenger Ken Young's opponent and brawling with Omega after breaking up Omega's Ankle Lock From Hell on Cobra. Was turned on by his own tag partner The Equalizer during an abandoned six-man tag at Vegas Violence 2006, put through a table by Demonic and The Equalizer together. On the losing side of a disqualification loss with Roman Anderson to Trinity at Sin City Fights October 16, after The Equalizer sledgehammered Anderson's own injured shoulder before the bell. Beat Sebastian by a bridging pin at Sin City Fights October 24 with Miles McKinley's own run-in help, then was beaten down by Trinity afterward. Threw brass knuckles to a bloodied Roman Anderson to help him beat The Equalizer in a barbed wire Funhouse Match bout at Phear Phantastic 2006, then lost a streetfight to Sebastian, run down by The Equalizer's own car in the parking lot. Fights to a double count-out with Morris Bass in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), both chasing a returning Iyasu Saku; Kingston Cash rules both remain in the tournament. Disqualified from a Heavyweight title fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) for a second tazer shot on Morris Bass. Pinned by Kevin Simmons's Durham Deathdrop in a six-man decider on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004).

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-08-16 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 17 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 9-7-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Debuts as "Mr. Electricity" on Wrestle Style V.1, beating Rob Kidane, then wins again over Kenny Kidd on both Wrestle Style V.2 and the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Wins an Electric Ropes match over The Outlaw Morris Bass at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) after a ringside interloper billed from the WILD promotion, Sid Starr, turns the ropes' own power back on and costs Bass the win. Loses the VWF 24 Hour Championship match to Adam Jennings on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) after accidentally clocking stablemate G-Gangsta. Eliminated from the tag team title battle royal by Jennings on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004) after laughing at him mid-elimination. Pinned by Jennings in a short-handed six-man tag on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after Thunder Valenkov's own Thunder Bomb. Teams with Sebastian to beat CAPSMEN! on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), then tazes and briefly wears the VWF 24 Hour Championship belt after Adam Jennings's own title defense before being chased off. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Thunder Valenkov's own Urange Suplex. Loses the VWF 24 Hour Championship match to Adam Jennings in a Bring Your Own Weapons match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), beaten with the Hyper Driver moments after ringside legend Johnny "Hands Of" Stone knocks him down and reclaims his own prosthetic leg, which Caldwell had used as a weapon on Jennings. Beats Thunder Valenkov at March To Madness (2 March 2005) after fellow Riders of the Apocalypse members blind Valenkov with a smoke bomb and chair him from the apron, then smooths over a dispute with stablemate Kenny Kidd backstage. Beats Kenny Kidd at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) after first offering to take a fall, rolling him up once the offer turns into a real fight. Beats The Maquilladora at End Of March (30 March 2005) with Shock And Horror off the top rope after stablemate The Mad Bomber blinds her with a flash of light. Loses to The Maquilladora on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), then is the only member of the Riders of the Apocalypse to enter the closing Gauntlet battle royal, kicked off the apron by Thunder Valenkov.
