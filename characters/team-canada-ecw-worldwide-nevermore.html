Team Canada

The Canadian squad for the Wrestling World Cup, announced on Blood, Sweat and Beers in June 2007 by Don Callis, who has taken a leave from the Revolution announce desk to coach it: Lance Storm, Chris Benoit, Christian, Chris Jericho, Teddy Hart and Bobby Roode, with Christian and Jericho carrying Canadian flags to the ring. Joey Styles asks why Bret and Owen Hart were not picked, and Taz answers that they have their title business and do not see eye to eye. Canada beat Scotland twice and Japan once, tie Japan on points and lose the random draw for first place in Pool C, and are eliminated by Mexico in the quarter-final; Christian, whose last match is the pool win over Scotland, then leaves ECW for the WWA.
