2004-11-17 – 2006-05-27

Tenure detail
Won his own UWF debut over Zero-G on 17 November 2004 when the referee stopped the match after Zero-G passed out in the Figura-Cuatro Leglock; managed to the ring by Olivia Michaels. Beat Brick Rosen by submission (a figure-four leglock) on 1 December 2004 in his own second UWF outing, working over the leg throughout after his manager Olivia Michaels talked him up as a future champion. Turned heel on 28 May 2005, interrupting Luke Kinsey's own beatdown of Jamie Underwood alongside manager Olivia Michaels and joining in on Kinsey's own orders. Joined the Illuminati (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian, Corporate Leverage, Joey~!) in surrounding Alex Kidd after Luke Kinsey's own double-countout match with him on 11 June 2005, before Kinsey called the group off. Beat Youth Gone Wild in a Road to the Gold qualifier on 22 June 2005, forcing him to pass out to the Figura-Quatro Leglock. Beat "Hentai" Ryu Osawa in a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament first-round match by pinfall (a piledriver) on 20 July 2005, after Daniel Kidd ran in from the crowd to spear Osawa into the steel steps; manager Olivia Michaels accompanied him. Lost to Jamie Underwood by pinfall (the Jamie Driver) on 23 July 2005. Interfered for the Illuminati in the Underwood/Vyolynce match on 3 August 2005, pulling Jamie Underwood's leg from the floor for the pin; was beaten down alongside the Illuminati at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, then answered Jamie Underwood's own challenge by pulling manager Olivia Michaels into a chair shot and fireballing her, unmasking afterward as the returning "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun. Advanced in the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament on 18 March 2006, submitting Miguel Quesada with the Von Braun Leglock after repeated attacks on his own opponent's left leg. Beat Vyolynce by pinfall, billed throughout as "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun, in a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament semi-final on 27 May 2006 after secretly exposing a turnbuckle, advancing to a Luck of the Draw final against Youth Gone Wild.
