Matt Stanton

Tenure detail
The Foundation of Brutality built its 19 November show around him: five instalments of a video series called A Haunting Past, narrated by an unseen voice over TPG, Revenge, Underground and Carnage footage, telling his whole PWE career as a record of quitting — his TPG debut, his loss to John Bradshaw Layfield, his Pure Title loss to Tex Graham, his Pure Title win over Lord Greatest and the week he held it, and the Loose Cannons tag team. The fed's own roleplay forum then bills him in three December matches: a Newcomer Showdown with Teddy DiBiase, the four-way FoB Heavyweight Title match at Decimation, and a grudge match with Hoffman whose thread was still live on 14 December (the fed's own FOB: SACRIFICE roleplay forum as the board rendered it on 15 December 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/forum-fob-sacrifice-133.html)).
