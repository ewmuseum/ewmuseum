Kenny Mercury

aka The Shooting Star, The Technical Wizard

A UWF technical wizard who ran through the Intercontinental, Television, and Hardcore titles across four reigns and a run of feuds with Jamie Shock, Almasy, and Misfit that helped earn him the 2004 Impy for Best Heel — billed again, years later, as a returning name on the UiWF's own 2010 relaunch pitch.
