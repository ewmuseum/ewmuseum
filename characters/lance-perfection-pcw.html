BIOGRAPHY It all started January 15th 1983. A baby was born to Steven and Terri Heinrichsen, they named this baby boy Lance, Lance Heinrichsen. Lance also had an older brother by the name of Shawn. During Lance's birth his mother Terri Heinrichsen died just as he was placed into her warm loving arms. This day may have been the finish to this once loving family. The three men grew up in an old servant's house built in the late 1800's in front of a rich plantation owner's home. Fields where they used to grow crops and trees spread on for miles and miles as far as the eye could see. By this time Lance Heinrichsen was 5 where as his brother Shawn was 10. They mainly hung out together while their dad Steven Heinrichsen worked in a car repair shop. The family was dealt hardships but they pushed on as well as they could with out the loving mother and beautiful wife. Now the town they lived in wasn't very big. It was just outside of the regions between Arizona and California. Most people here were up to date on all the current business whether it had any thing to do with them or not. The word is that Steven Heinrichsen went mad over time. People began to disappear in this town of probably 800 people. Soon after Steven Heinrichsen too had disappeared leaving his two sons behind. When local officials asked the children of there fathers where a bouts the two children suspiciously clamed up not speaking a word of their father from that day forward. It wasn't to long before they placed to two young boys in an orphanage, Shawn was quickly adopted soon after arriving but Lance, Lance spent 5 years in that home till he was 10 years old with out his brother. But one day a rich family adopted Lance Heinrichsen taking them into their home. There the young boy grew up privileged with every thing a young man could want. He was extremely attractive and a ladies man, drove an incredible car and excelled in all sports his put his mind too. One day at 16 years of age, Lance Heinrichsen left school early during gym class. It was if something was calling him, summoning his soul in a direction that his body couldn't control. That day he found his brother back in his old hometown. This was around the time of the GOD'S HAND MURDERS! A week later after Lance Heinrichsens disappearance, Shawn Heinrichsen turned up missing. Lance too turned up missing neither to be heard from again. Like a family of ghosts vanishing into thin air. Weeks later the GODS HAND MURDERS Stopped and the investigating officer disappeared as well. About two years later, a young man resembling Lance Heinrichsen was seen wrestling on the Indy circuit cleaning up becoming one of the most decorated wrestlers to have ever lives. This young 18 year old mans name was Lance Perfection. When asked about his past Lance was more then happy to share his rich kid up bringing, and his wrestling stints in Japan and his short time in Mexico learning all the different styles to become successful. He spoke of the time he spent in the Dojo in Japan and in the Hart Dungeon in Calgary Alberta Canada, as well as his in the USA learning the style and what it takes to make the super big time. But strangely enough, when asked about family or and parents, the young super star seemed to clam up and dodge the question. As well as when asked his real name he would stop to think for a second and then answer. 'My name is Lance Perfection and that is all you will ever need to know.' We have watched this young 18 year old then now 19 year old pay his dues and win every thing he has ever deserved. People dieing to know the past of this kid dug through his files at one point a few months after he had signed with his first big name federation NTICW. But they found nothing more then incomplete material. The rest had been burned in an electrical fire at the hall of records. When they searched his name, they found he had legally changed it to Lance Perfection and that there had been a smudge but UN legible note of his former name, which no matter what they tried, could not even come close to making out to be read. Lance Perfection just recently signed with RUSH where he has been seen to have a successful future and maybe even found a home. He has made the big time and he knows it and will fight and do whatever he can to keep it. Whether Lance Perfection is Lance Heinrichsen or not will always is a mystery. But what the real mystery that people should be focusing on now is how much talent does this young 19 year really have and how much more will we see now in the big time then we have in his past accomplishments.
