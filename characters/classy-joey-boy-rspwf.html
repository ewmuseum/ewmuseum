VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 28 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 12-12-3, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Attacked by an unseen figure near a creek before Ghost Town Blues's own Pentagon match and hospitalized, unable to defend the VWF Heavyweight Championship; ruled out at least six weeks on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004), where the title is declared vacant pending a Gauntlet tournament, though he does not appear on that broadcast. Returns on Wrestle Style V.1, interfering with a sledgehammer in the Heavyweight Championship main event; cuts a lengthy promo on Wrestle Style V.2 disputing Lark Fenriz's claim to the title and asserting he was never properly stripped of it; wins over Kevin Blair by disqualification in the opening round of the new VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Wins by count-out over a no-show Jason Storm in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004) after The Equalizer attacks him from the crowd with the timekeeper's bell. Disqualified in a Heavyweight title fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) after Sebastian tricks him into a sledgehammer shot; hits Sebastian with the Kiss of Death and a Studd Splash regardless before officials pull the two apart. Teams with a returning Randall Crane against Sebastian and G-Gangsta in a handicap match turned even at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), won by count-out when the Legends flee the ring; later shares guest-referee duties, and blows, with Flash during the VWF Heavyweight Championship main event. Present for Kingston Cash's own backstage suspension of Flash on March To Hell (21 October 2004), then wrestles Lark Fenriz to a time-limit draw. Seconds The Maquilladora's own Submission Championship match and beats Iyasu Saku (Kiss of Death out of midair) to earn a Rage in the Cage spot on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), then feigns accepting Sebastian's own offer to join the Riders of the Apocalypse before powerbombing him and dropping Kiss of Deaths on G-Gangsta and Robbie Caldwell. Eliminated from the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), pinned by Fast Eddie Cutlass after Lark Fenriz's own Sammael. Named on air ahead of a scheduled six-man tag against Trinity that never happens on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Loses a triple threat also involving Lark Fenriz and Fast Eddie Cutlass on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), pinned by neither man in the final fall. Fights The Maquilladora to a double count-out draw on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005). Challenges The Equalizer for the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship alongside The Maquilladora on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), using his own 25% ownership stake to book himself into the match over majority owner Kingston Cash's objection. Loses to Sebastian and G-Gangsta in the Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) fatal four-way, then eliminates Billy Bones, Lark Fenriz, Sidious The Clown and Kenny Kidd in the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet before Adam Jennings eliminates him. Loses the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship match to champion The Equalizer and The Maquilladora in a Wheel Of Chaos match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) after a doctor, revealed afterward as Kingston Cash's own brother Terrance Cash, injects him mid-match. Teams with The Maquilladora against The Equalizer and Gods Tears at March To Madness (2 March 2005), the match declared a no contest after two masked men interfere on both sides; the IRS seizes his own 25% ownership stake in the VWF the same night, alongside Kingston Cash's majority share. Pulled from the scheduled Five Way elimination match at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) with a flare-up of a long-standing knee and ankle injury. Attacked in the parking lot by all three Beauty Brothers before End Of March (30 March 2005); John Tyler books a three-on-one match against them for The Gauntlet, allowing Classy Joey Boy a sledgehammer. Beats all three Beauty Brothers by disqualification in a three-on-one handicap match on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after Rod Beauty picks up the sledgehammer barred from the Beautys' own use, then enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal as its fourth entrant, thrown out by Justin Davidson.
