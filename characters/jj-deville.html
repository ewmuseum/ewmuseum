wrestler, CSWA World Tag Team Champion with Troy Windham (by his own account, won at ANNIVERSARY 2004 by pinning Craig Miles); special guest referee for the Presidential Championship three-way at PRIMETIME in Jacksonville
Tenure detail
Billed "The New Original" JJ DeVille. On 21 April 2004 he wears a title, recalls 'tagging with Troy Windham' and 'PINNING Craig Miles' at Anniversary, and tells the Diamond Cutters 'you aren't leaving with this'. On 27 May 2004 he recounts what followed: Troy Windham missed the next card and he lost alone against two men; on the next ON TIME he reversed a SlackKnife and pinned Troy Windham, his 'old mentor', in the main event; he would referee the Presidential Championship match (Eddie Mayfield, Steel Viper, Cameron Cruise) at PRIMETIME. No results page for these 2004 shows survives in the collection.
Source: the fed's own roleplays 'The New Original' (21 April 2004) and 'Right Down The Middle' (27 May 2004); Eddie Mayfield's 'Heel to the Chief' (26 May 2004); the build for PRIMETIME in Atlanta (May 2004) and PRIMETIME in Jacksonville (June 2004); the CSWA ANNIVERSARY 2005 video package, which shows Troy Windham holding the CSWA World Tag Team Titles with JJ DeVille
