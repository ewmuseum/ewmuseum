2003-09-12 – 2006-10-16

Tenure detail
Lost a handicap match alongside Blake Hamilton to The Outlaw Morris Bass at Sin City Fights October 16, both men finished off with a Widowmaker apiece, one delivered on the ring steps. Loses to Paddy O'Shea on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), caught with a top-rope Fist Drop after knocking O'Shea dizzy with an early flurry. Billed The Big V, submits to Kenny Kidd's Elko Lasso on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) after Billy Bones interferes from ringside, then loses a backstage challenge match to Capsman for Ghost Town Blues. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005), eliminates Evil Capsman, and is eliminated alongside CAPSMAN by Sidious The Clown and Kagedo before climbing back onto the apron to legdrop and pin CAPSMAN himself. Loses a Loser Is Winners Valet Match to CAPSMAN at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) after his own missed top-rope Twisting Corkscrew Legdrop bumps the referee and CAPSMAN schoolboys him once the referee revives, becoming CAPSMAN's own ringside valet. Rolls dice on the Orleans craps table at March To Madness (2 March 2005), chanting for a shot at Frank O'Shea. Faces Paddy O'Shea at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) in a match that ends without a decision when a returning Capsman runs in and chases off both O'Sheas, becoming Capsman's own ringside valet after the prior show's own Loser Is Winners Valet Match. Teams with Capsman against The O'Sheas at End Of March (30 March 2005), pinned after a top-rope fist drop from Paddy. Wins his first match, teaming with Capsman against The O'Sheas on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), pinning Paddy O'Shea after kicking him off a schoolboy attempt. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, tossing out debuting Stan Klenski before being eliminated himself minutes later.

Half of Dudes of Destruction on Xtreme Prejudice VI. Wrestles on the losing side of the six-man tag scramble on Sin City Fights August 31; distracts the referee to help fellow Dude of Destruction Blake Hamilton beat The Outlaw Morris Bass on Sin City Fights September 11, then loses a Russian Chain Match to Thunder Valenkov; wins Ring Two of the TV Title Open Match on Sin City Fights September 19, earning a VXW Spin The Wheel Television Championship shot against Justin Harper at Vegas Violence.
