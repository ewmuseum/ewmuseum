Tenure detail
SCCW's own backstage database (lot 0159), bio 3708 (singles) — its own biography card and the federation's own appearance record: 3 match and 2 segment appearances, 26 Aug - 31 Oct 2008, and 2 roleplays of his own. His SCCW card is this character, from three sides. SCCW's own card bills him “one half of Politically Incorrect, among the most dominant tag teams of the early 21st century”, “Considered retired as of 2004”, and Phillip Kennedy's own SCCW card names him as his Strength In Numbers partner — the same team the PCW team card names him a member of. Sean's own posts say whose he is: “I made Reginald Lovecraft and PI ran till OSW” (PTC forum topic 14253, post 333, 23 July 2005; in topic 9875, post 22, of 27 September 2004 he calls Politically Incorrect his best all-around tag team and says he invented Lovecraft); in “Your supporting characters” (topic 25888, post 13, 15 May 2009) he lists him among his own supporting cast: “Former one-half of Politically Incorrect, a dominant PCW/OSW/FSW tag team in the early 00s, Lovecraft returned at the behest of Alexandra Pierce … to be Phillip's tag team partner when the Dead Man's Hand held the Strength in Numbers championship.” Phillip Kennedy's own roleplay of 10 July 2009 (topic 26219, post 3) names him among the people who helped Kennedy get that far.
Source: SCCW's own backstage database (lot 0159), bio 3708; PTC's own forum, topics 14253 (post 333), 9875 (post 22), 25888 (post 13) and 26219 (post 3)
