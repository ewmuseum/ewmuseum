Tenure detail
Billed from Northwest Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, leader of the Youngbloods, accompanied by Allison Ivey; entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match, pinning Edward Drake with the Path of Bones nervehold and Chad Grimsson with an Impact DDT before being counted out alongside Edmond Winston IV after the two brawled out of the arena. Vice-President Jessica Marshall announced afterward that he would challenge Winston for the UWF World Heavyweight Championship at Eve of Destruction. Teamed with Alex Extreme against Edmond Winston IV, Jamie Underwood, and the Privateers on 15 May 2004; after the Privateers turned on Winston to side with Extreme, Augustine turned on Extreme in turn, joining the Privateers to beat him through two tables before Alex Martinez and the Sons of Cacaphony ran in. Announced with Allison Ivey on 19 May 2004 that he and the Privateers had purchased minority shares of the UWF; does not compete on this card. Lost the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World to Kyle Lee by pinfall in the Gold Rush main event triple threat on 5 June 2004, also involving Edmond Winston IV, pinned by a second Gone to Texas after the real Alex Extreme exposed an impostor (Job Holly) who had been interfering on Winston's behalf. His "Singles War" against Alex Martinez was never brought under control and ended without a decision on 18 September 2004 after the Privateers and a returning Alex Extreme turned the brawl into a four-man fight; manager Allison Ivey struck Martinez with a high kick during the match. Lost a non-title match to the One-Winged Angel by disqualification on 30 October 2004 after Erik Grimsson pulled him off the top rope to stop a chair-assisted diving attack; joined manager Allison Ivey and the Privateers in a post-match beatdown of Grimsson and Angel. Fought Scott "Hotspot" Daniels to a no-contest on 3 November 2004 after Kari Stevens and Allison Ivey both struck their own man's opponent with a chair and Amity ran in on both wrestlers, ending the match early. Challenged Alex Extreme to a match at Rise to Power on 27 November 2004, then looked on as Jacob Drake and Allison Ivey beat down Lisa Drake for refusing to rejoin the Youngbloods, restrained from joining in by Allison Ivey herself. Mocked the Illuminati and the new World champion for overlooking the Youngbloods on 8 June 2005, then lost a UWF North American Heavyweight Championship match to champion Papa Legba by pinfall (the Ragga Bomb); called out Legba for a grudge fight on 11 June 2005, naming him one of only two men to ever truly beat him. Beat Jason Keening with a cradle brainbuster in a random-partners tag match alongside Victor Frost against Keening and Miguel Quesada on 23 July 2005, then called out and accepted a Gold Rush title challenge from North American Heavyweight Champion Papa Legba. Eliminated fifth from the main event eight-man elimination match on 3 August 2005, pinned by Sabbath's Spinning Sabby Driver after himself eliminating Papa Legba.
