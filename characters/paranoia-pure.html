Biography (PURE (2002))

Set Up: Paradox - Para grabs the arm and Irish whips the opponent, but she pulls back. Then she slides underneath as the opponent comes back, holding the arm and pulling it between his legs, setting up for the Paranoia. Finisher: Paranoia - Straight Jacket Clutch Reverse Fisherman Suplex. From behind, Para reaches under hooks the opponent’s right leg and grabs the left arm with the same hooking arm. Then she brings the opponent’s right arm across his left and locks her hips, snapping the opponent over onto his neck and upper spine. Half the time she releases and then makes the pin, and the other half she bridges. Upgrade Finisher (super finisher/high impact) Anxiety – Swanton Roll into a Rana Face Driver. If the opponent kicks out of the Paranoia, Para goes to the top rope and clutches her head as she waits for the opponent to get into position. Then with the opponent standing facing her, she flips off with a Swanton Somersault with her arms stretched out in perfect formation. Then as he flips over she lands on the opponent’s shoulders legs first. From there she allows the momentum and gravity to carry her over, her legs locked as she falls back and drives the opponent face first into the mat, she slipping through his legs to pull him down more. After hitting the move she rolls the opponent over onto his back with her legs still around his neck, hooking both legs for the pin. Signature moves: Persistence- Front Shuffle Triple Strike Combination: Paranoia hops forward with a right snap kick into the knee of the opponent, followed by a left kick into the side of the hip, both to stagger the opponent to the right. Then Paranoia comes out with a jumping roundhouse kick to the head, knocking the opponent left onto his side. Aggression- A Hurricanrana into a Mounted Punch: Para jumps from either a standing position, running, springboard off the ropes, or off the turnbuckle with a hurricanrana. Once flipped over, Para kneels on the opponent and hits him with rights in the face. Resentment- Flying Cross Block Spin: After whipping the opponent into the ropes Paranoia catches them with a flying cross block to their chest, the impact sending the opponent down and spinning her own body in the air before she lands on her knees. Annoyance- Springboard Shooting Star Lariat: From the apron Para springs off and nails a back somersault, diving across the ring and coming down with a left clothesline across the throat of the standing opponent. In tag matches often done after the tag from her partner. Can also be done from inside the ring to outside. Neurotics- Corkscrew Moonsault Axe Kick: Para runs up the corner and flips back with a corkscrew moonsault, twisting around and nailing a hard heel axe kick into the face of the opponent. Obsession- Overhead Bridged Sleeper Hold: With the opponent face down on the mat Para locks her hands around their neck and flips over, bridging back to back on the opponent. She arches her back to tighten the choke submission. Intimidation- Throat Clutch Push into a Spinning Lariat: The opponent runs at Para, but she stops him with a tight grip on his throat. Then she shoves him away back first into the ropes, spinning around on her grounded leg and coming back with a knife edged lariat, dropping to her knee from the momentum. Anarchistic- Windmill DDT Combination: Starting from the top of the corner, Para sets the opponent up for a Tornado DDT. Swinging off, she kicks her legs out in a windmill pattern and DDT’s the opponent. Then she kicks her body over onto her feet and holds the arm, pulling the opponent up again. Then she runs up the ropes and springs off with another windmill DDT. A chain series, done from the corner to the ropes to the other set of ropes and back across the ring again as many times as she desires. Standard moves: Aerial: 450 splash, moonsault (standing, spingboard, tope). Technical: Falcon Arrow(spinning or standard), German Suplex, Back Drop Driver, Fisherman Suplex. Power: scoop powerslam, tilt a whirl side slam, falling powerslam (scoop to a horizontal position, then fall forward into a powerslam). Martial Arts: Roundhouse kicks, Spinning heel kicks, spinning wheel kicks, Tae Kwon Do Spin/reverse kicks.
