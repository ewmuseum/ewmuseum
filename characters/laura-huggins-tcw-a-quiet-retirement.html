Laura Huggins

Freddy Huggins's twin sister and manager, a business graduate in sharp executive clothes who turns to Eric Tyler's cause. In September 2008 she calls the submission when Johnny Bloodstone beats Sean Deeley and, to punish her brother for hitting Eric Tyler with the Huggins Kiss, distracts the referee while Acid submits him. In October 2008 she distracts referees for Sean Deeley, Eric Tyler and Acid and sees her brother's Huggins Kiss land on Deeley by mistake on Badge of Honor. In November 2008 she is given the choice between Sean Deeley and her brother Freddy for the All Action Championship four-way, distracts referees for Acid and Sean Deeley, punches Freddy in the crotch on Badge of Honor and sees Eric Tyler pick Freddy. In December 2008 she throws in the towel for Sean Deeley against the New Wave, takes up with Deeley and is shoved to the ground by her brother Freddy. Written by AI, 2026
