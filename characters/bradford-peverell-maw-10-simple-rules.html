Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Bradford Peverell

aka The Pev

A tall, rangy newcomer who rides a battered motorbike down the ramp at Summernova on 12th August 2011 to interrupt Providence, tells the crowd that where he comes from people like Providence are locked up and that he looked after his neighbourhood, and promises that people will know Bradford Peverell is a force to be reckoned with. He loses his debut to Providence's Eye Of Providence in a match the narrator scores for no chemistry; the narrator calls him The Pev. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses to JD Morgan for the All Action title at Leap Into The Unknown, beats Raphael with a Dream Left Hook at Third Time's The Charm and Eddie Powell at May The Force Be With You, loses to Firebird at March Madness and, at Mother, May I? on 12th May, loses a three-way for the Traditional title against JD Morgan and Lord Geoffrey Windameer. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he wins the six-man at High Noon on 5th June with Thea Davis and Jared Johnson, landing the Dream Left Hook on Raphael, loses to Father Johan's Crucifix Powerbomb at Summer Showdown on 12th June and suggests there that the four men who stand against The Fallen need a symbol. He loses a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, in what the narrator calls the worst main event in years, and with Kirk Jameson loses to Firebird and The Great Ota at South East Grand Parade on 9th August; Jameson credits him with saying that people need a symbol to rally behind.
