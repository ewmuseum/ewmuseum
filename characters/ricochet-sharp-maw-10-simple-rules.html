Ricochet & Sharp

aka Ricochet and Sharp, Ford & Speed, Ford and Speed

The two masked newcomers of The Fallen who work as a tag team: they lose to Smith and Singh at Summer Showdown in June 2010, beat Grayson & Shaffer at Iron in August, trading masks to confuse the referee, lose a three-way for the Tag Team titles to Valentine & Johnson at Back To School and take part in the Eleventh Hour match in November 2010 where, unmasked as Marc Speed and Greg Ford, they make their "debut" as Ford & Speed against Morgan & Benning and win with a Cross Armbreaker. As Ford & Speed they beat the Bailey Boys at Remember November in November 2010, lose to Morgan & Benning at End Of Days, beat Smith & Singh at All Or Nothing in February 2011, lose to Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson at Leap Into The Unknown and beat the Young Americans at April Showers. As Ford & Speed they tell the crowd on 12th May 2011 that they are no longer associated with The Fallen but follow its ideals, beat Stevie Grayson and Thomas Morgan that night, are beaten in the Hotter Than Hot three-way, and beat Smith & Singh at Summernova and the West Texas Peacekeepers at Iron with Fallen Chances. As Ford & Speed they beat the West Texas Peacekeepers at Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 with Fallen Chances, lose to Team OMGsplosion at Eleventh Hour, lose the Tag Team title three-way at Season's Beatings and the title match to Smith & Singh at End Of Days, and beat the Young Americans, Callum and Raphael on 5th January 2012 to earn a shot at the Invitational, where they lose the three-way with Smith & Singh and Lockdown. As Ford & Speed they beat Amazing Fire Fly and Jared Johnson at All Or Nothing on 12th February 2012 and the West Texas Peacekeepers at March Madness, and are in the Spring Fever three-way. As Ford & Speed in the diary's pages from June to September 2012 they lose the three-way at High Noon on 5th June, beat Kid Arachnid and Bradford Peverell at South East Madness, the West Texas Peacekeepers at Summernova and Smith & Singh at Back To School with Fallen Chances, the narrator saying they have lacked direction since being set loose from The Fallen.
