Tenure detail
Shown tied up backstage on a video screen at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), threatened by Orion #2 as a message to her husband Yancy Kilpatrick; the sight sends Flash running from his own scheduled match with Orion. Escapes captivity to help Pagan, revealed as her own kidnapper, cripple Jason Storm inside a cage trap at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), DDTing Storm's manager Alice before leaving with Pagan. Walks to ringside during the Pagan/Fast Eddie Cutlass Church Of Pain match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) and shares a wink with Pagan before enforcer Kagedo sends her away.
