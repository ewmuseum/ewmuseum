Biography (PURE (2002))

Inphino Blitz is a well defined martial artist usually seen wearing black martial arts slacks, black boots, and a tight black spandex shirt that covers most of his neck. His shoulder length hair is dark blue and black, and usually hangs over his face slightly, covering a portion of it, but he randomly tosses in streaks of color given the mood. Often he is seen wearing a black cloak around his shoulders. His eyes are dark gray, and his handsome face is usually solemn and brooding. His nails are painted black. Inphino is a wandering loner martial artist with a mysterious background and a strive to train and enhance his fighting technique. He's one of the breakthrough Japanese figures to introduce the 'Kin Kyu Hyaku Kyu Ju Ku Shiki' or the 'Forbidden 999 Style' into the Japanese culture. The Forbidden 999 Style, named for Inphino's martial arts form, is the equivalent to the Gothic movement over in the states. There have been many tales and stories telling about the Black Dragon Master. But there has yet to be any opportunity or chance to ask him if any of them are true. Inphino was one who grew up outside of modern society, dwelling in a secluded dojo until he was eighteen. Then from there he traveled the earth for battle, gaining many accolades as well as nurturing and developing his Forbidden 999 Style. Then he found Primetime Wrestling Academy, an institute of training in the US. After a few days, he was brought into Primetime Championship Wrestling alongside Ketsueki Karasu to form Hyakuhei. Later in his three-month career, he teamed with Miyagawa, Kensuke Suzahara, and Ming to form the Elements. In three months, he elevated from tag team specialist to World title contender through the 64-man tournament, the quickest rise through the rankings for any wrestler in PCW history. But then tragedy struck... Inphino was forced out of his title run as well as his position in PCW by one man... Akira Akiyama... Akiyama had devised a scheme to lure Inphino Blitz back to Japan, where the dark forces called the Void Elements were there waiting for him. He was presumed dead to the world, and Akiyama took his claim to his position within PCW. But little to their dismay that the Black Dragon Master was not entirely dead. The details are foggy, but Inphino somehow escaped death, and reclaimed his work visa so that he could return to the USA. By this time, Akiyama had become PCW Extreme Champion...so Inphino congratulated him...and moved on with his life. He found a small Indy federation called Hardcore Championship Wrestling, where he took his time to train, renew and raise his skills even further...waiting for the one day to return into PTC...and hopefully PCW. He is a silent man...but also an honorable and respectable one. He always shakes the hands of his opponents, and also helps them up after he has defeated them. But don't let that make you think he's a nice guy. He's extremely dark and hardly shows emotion.
