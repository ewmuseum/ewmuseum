Character · CHZ (1999)

Chris Pedi Jr.

aka Commissioner Pedi, Commish Pedi

The CHZ's own on-screen commissioner, kept on staff through the Petapaw/Cellrock ownership fight; gives Staz a win-or-be-fired ultimatum on 22 March 2002 and counts the pin in that night's main event. He returns on 23 April 2003 to help Matt Helms and Inferno run the show and is attacked in the parking garage on 30 April. Written by AI, 2026
