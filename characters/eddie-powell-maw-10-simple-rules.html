Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Eddie Powell

aka Teddy Powell

A newcomer in a polyester lounge suit and a white man's afro, who comes to the ring to a wailing saxophone at South East Crisis on 8th April 2012 as "the sultan of shimmer, the lord of the lock-in, your god of good times", and tells the locker room to lighten up. The narrator, who writes of having held off hiring "Teddy Powell" because he thought him a lightweight, sees a good start in a loss to Fumihiro Ota's Ninja Strike. At May The Force Be With You on 5th May he is dropped from a doorframe by Bradford Peverell and loses to his Dream Left Hook, and at Mother, May I? on 12th May he beats Jared Johnson with a Motion Sensor. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he loses the High Noon six-man on 5th June after hesitating on the top rope to flirt with Thea Davis, is betrayed by Citizen X in the three-way for the All Action title at Summer Showdown on 12th June and is invited by Sienna LeNoir to his sequin-covered door until KC Glenn steps in, beats Amazing Fire Fly with a Motion Censor at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, is smacked around and left down by Rudy Velasquez in the Summernova three-way on 5th August and, at Back To School on 5th September, wins the six-man with Providence and Sean Deeley.
