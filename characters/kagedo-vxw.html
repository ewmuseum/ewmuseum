VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-02-26 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 11 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 7-4-0
Tenure detail
Pins Rob Kidane with a Dragon Suplex, teaming with fellow Society of Doom member Kevin Simmons against The Enforcers on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004). Beats Sidious The Clown by schoolboy on Firestorm (3 May 2004) after Lark Fenriz's own distracting arrival. Wins a triple threat over Fast Eddie Cutlass and The Equalizer after an unidentified masked attacker's interference, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and is the last man eliminated, ruled out ahead of Lark Fenriz on a simultaneous over-the-rope fall. Challenges Lark Fenriz for a claimed VWF Heavyweight Championship on Firestorm (27 May 2004), losing after LOCO's interference, then eliminated jointly with Lord Zero in the Dog Day One singles battle royal. Signs a liability waiver to wrestle on his surgically repaired knee in the Society of Doom's ten-man tag win on Wrestle Style V.1, striking a side bet with Kingston Cash for the following week; loses the Company vs. Suspension match on Wrestle Style V.2 when his knee gives out, and is suspended six months without pay. Returns from his own suspension to make the save for a pinned Lark Fenriz on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), kicking down The Equalizer and diving onto Pagan, then is installed by new VWF guardian John Tyler as his own on-site enforcer over Trinity. Checks on a beaten Fast Eddie Cutlass and holds off Pagan on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005). Opens the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet against Classy Joey Boy on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and eliminates Mark McQueen, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Adam The Hype Jennings before The Equalizer eliminates him. Beats an impostor billed Scott Starring, revealed on the spot as Henry Fish, with a Dragon Suplex in an impromptu opening match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), then holds off Pagan's own Trinity stablemates Gods Tears and The Equalizer during Fast Eddie Cutlass's own Church Of Pain match. Beats Henry Fish, billed Scott Starring, at March To Madness (2 March 2005), a disqualification win after Fish kicks him low. His own scheduled VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship challenge against The Equalizer at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) is aborted when Gods Tears attacks him with a tire iron before he reaches the ring; teams instead with Roadkill Rick against The Equalizer and Gods Tears in a bonus tag match, winning by disqualification. Teams with Sidious The Clown against the Rodeo Boys at End Of March (30 March 2005), unable to save Sidious from tapping to the Elko Lasso. Competes on the losing side of the eight-man elimination match on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), schoolboyed and eliminated by Gods Tears.
