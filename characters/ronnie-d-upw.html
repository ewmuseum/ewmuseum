"Playboy" Ronnie D

"Playboy" Ronnie D was announced as UPW's own first 2017 Hall of Fame inductee days before the promotion closed, the induction itself never held. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UPW (2016)

2017 – 2017
Tenure detail
Billed a legendary cross-promotional draw out of fictional feds IIWF, IIeW and EMWC; competed in UPW's own Ultimate Rumble at Survival. Announced 11 April 2017 as the first of five inductees into UPW's own planned 2017 Hall of Fame class, to be formally honored at Slamfest VI — a card the promotion's own closure, twelve days later, meant never aired. Co-host Robert "The Gorilla" Smith separately recalled wrestling him "in NeCW back in the day" on the same broadcast — a further fed, not one the RSPWF placement itself tags, but consistent with the same well-traveled billing.
Source: UPW (2016) UPW Showdown; News & Rumors; UPW (2016) Survival (Preview Show)
