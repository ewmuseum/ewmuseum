Tenure detail
Billed from North Brookfield, Massachusetts, managed by Virginia St. Ursula; pinned Brian McKenzie with a top-rope 450 splash in a non-title cruiserweight contest. Fought newcomer November to a double-disqualification draw in a non-title match on 24 March 2004, now wrestling without a manager at ringside; Jamie Underwood and the One-Winged Angel both ran in before the finish. Lost the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World to One-Winged Angel by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, caught in an uranage counter off his own Deadfall attempt and then a top-rope Hell's Halo; demanded a title rematch backstage afterward while trashing his own locker room. Lost his own Gold Rush rematch for the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World to One-Winged Angel by pinfall on 28 August 2004, his own top-rope hurricanrana reversed into the Revelator; attacked Angel with the title belt afterward until a thrown water bottle drew him into the crowd, where "Hentai" Ryu Osawa clotheslined him and challenged him to a career-versus-career match, approved by Jessica Marshall for the following week. Lost a no-disqualification Career Paths match to "Hentai" Ryu Osawa by pinfall (an underhook sit-down powerbomb) on 18 September 2004, forced into a tag team with him under the match's own stipulation; manager Virginia St. Ursula pulled a chair away from him mid-match, and he stormed off after accusing her of betrayal. Teamed with "Hentai" Ryu Osawa, forced into the pairing by the terms of his own prior loss to Osawa, to beat the Sons of Cacaphony (Erik and Chad Grimsson) by disqualification on 16 October 2004 in the pair's own first match together. Lost a UWF Meltdown Championship challenge to Tigress by disqualification on 3 November 2004, after a six-wrestler UWF tryout group (revealed to be led by a disguised T.D. Swagger) ran in and beat him down. Stood at the entrance and refused to help tag partner "Hentai" Ryu Osawa as Team Canada beat him down after Osawa's own loss on 1 December 2004. Apologized to Chris O'Brien for a prior beatdown on 8 June 2005, with manager Virginia St. Ursula and tag partner Ryu Osawa looking on, and gave O'Brien a replacement laptop; O'Brien accepted the apology. Grabbed tag partner Ryu Osawa by the collar over a joking remark about manager Virginia St. Ursula's own legs on 22 June 2005, then accompanied Osawa to his own Road to the Gold qualifier. Lost to Youth Gone Wild in the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament's own first round on 6 July 2005, a hard-fought, mutually respectful contest that ended with a top-rope Wildflyer moonsault; told Virginia beforehand that he needed to prove he had changed since Rise to Power, and learned from Ryu Osawa that Osawa had won naming rights to their own still-unnamed tag team. Beat "Hellraiser" Tom Landis by pinfall (a Skyscreamer) on 3 August 2005 with an assist from Shaun Borders, who hurled Landis back into the move. Tripped Cornerstone Daniel Kidd during Juan Vasquez's debut match on 18 March 2006, turning back a Cornerstones interference attempt alongside tag partner Ryu Osawa.
