Da Boyz

Guido and Nunzio of the WeWF, led to the ring by Da Boss, who reached the second round of the Von Erich/Freebird Memorial tournament at the second Summit with Public Enemy for a third man and, the following June, won the PWA’s own inaugural Tag Team Championship and held it through most of the recorded summer.
