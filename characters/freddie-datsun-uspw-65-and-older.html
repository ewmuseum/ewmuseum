Freddie Datsun

The challenger who beats Bruce the Giant for the World Championship at Stars, Stripes and Slams and defends it against William Hayes, Peter Valentine and Umaga. In October 2007 he stands with Muffin against Divinity and the Wild Savages, beats Eddie Peak in a match the narrator rates an A* at A Haunting in America, and wins the three-way for the World Championship number one contendership on the Thursday of Week 3 with the help of a masked man. In October and November 2007 he beats Umaga on the Monday of Week 4 but has the belt taken from him by Triple D, beats Mean Jean Cattley, leads Team Datsun to a draw with Team Devine, and loses to Triple D at Pilgrimage for the World Championship when the Masked Man hits him with the Slick Trick and Danny Jillefski counts the pin. In late November and December 2007 he teams with Umaga to beat Team Seduction, loses to Big Cat Brandon in 15:56 (A), beats Fumihiro Ota, loses to Human Arsenal, draws with him by double disqualification and is booked with Umaga against Team Seduction at Winter Epiphany.
