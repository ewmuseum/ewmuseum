Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Kirk Jameson

A talented debutant who refuses to be pushed around by Jean Cattley and The Firm and pushes Cattley to the limit in August. He and Jonnie Perez lose to Roderick Remus and Jean Cattley at Summernova in August 2010, he beats Hugh de Aske with the Bullseye at Iron, loses to Primus Allen at Back To School, to Jean Cattley at Fall Of The King and to Firebird at Eleventh Hour in November, and signs a new contract in September at $1,500 an appearance, up from $600. He loses to Jay Chord at Season's Beatings in December 2010, beats Casey Valentine in a qualifier at End Of Days and Joey Poison at New Year, Old Rivalries, and on 21st January 2011 wins the Rip Chord Invitational by beating Antonio, Firebird and Hugh de Aske in the one night. He teams with Aaron Andrews, who advises him to believe in himself, beating Ford & Speed at Leap Into The Unknown; is pinned by Thrash with JD Morgan at Third Time's The Charm, draws with Morgan at March Madness and beats Robert Thomas at April Showers. The narrator learns in February that Rhode Island has approached him. He and Aaron Andrews beat Firebird and Black Eagle at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, loses to Jay Chord at Mother, May I? in a match that injures Jay's elbow and brings Rip's wrath on him, and turns down Firebird's offer that he "consider" the dark. Betrayed by Andrews at High Noon on 12th June and again at Summer Showdown, he is hurt with a gashed forehead and then a knee injury; he loses to Casey Valentine at Hotter Than Hot, returns at Iron on 26th August, driving a Corvette, to beat JD Morgan with the Bullseye, and beats Providence at Back To School by driving his skull into the exposed turnbuckle, Firebird noting that he is "coming along quite nicely". At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he drags Rip Chord across the announcers' table to demand Firebird, beats Black Eagle, and at All The Leaves Are Brown beats Firebird with a snap DDT and a brainbuster, drops the ring bell rather than strike him and wins with a cradle, then apologises to Brandon Smith and to Aaron Andrews and offers him his hand; Andrews refuses and Jameson cashes in the guaranteed shot of the Invitational title for Where It All Begins Again on 26th October, where Andrews beats him with a Twisting Face Crusher. Firebird says that the Fall has failed; Jameson becomes Kid Arachnid's replacement mentor, is beaten down by Lockdown and by Jared Johnson at Season's Beatings, teams with Randy Bumfhole and Fumihiro Ota at End Of Days and with Bumfhole at New Year, Old Rivalries (Bullseye on Nathan Coleman), and loses to Primus Allen for the Traditional title at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he beats Black Eagle with the Kirk-Hold at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February after refusing the advances of Sienna LeNoir, Citizen X at All Or Nothing and Casey Valentine at Great Lakes Crisis, with Katie Cameron now his manager, loses a submission match for the All Action title to JD Morgan at March Madness, beats Providence at April Showers, is stomped by Primus Allen afterwards, and beats Lord Geoffrey Windameer at Spring Fever on 12th April, where he promises to cleanse the company of Firebird, Black Eagle, Providence and his old friend Aaron. He and KC Glenn lose to Firebird and Father Johan at Mother, May I? on 12th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he loses to Firebird at High Noon on 5th June, draws with a clown-faced Providence at Summer Showdown on 12th June and warns Aaron Andrews about The Fallen's recruiting, only for Andrews to hit him with the Heavyweight belt, which puts him in hospital for a month. He returns at Beach Blast on 12th July to name the Knights Templar and beats Raphael with The Bullseye, teams with JD Morgan to beat Providence and The Great Ota at Summernova on 5th August and brings Rich Money, who has funded the help he has found, to the ring; he and Bradford Peverell lose to Firebird and Ota at South East Grand Parade on 9th August, he and Money beat Andrews and Ota at Iron on 12th August, and at Back To School on 5th September he beats Rudy Velasquez with the Kirkhold. The narrator renews his contract at a 20% pay cut, $2,000 an appearance for his previous $2,500, sends him to 4C in the trade that brings Money to MAW, and says he would cry if Jameson did not re-sign with NOTBPW.
