Tenure detail
Unmasked by Jason Storm mid-match and named on air as Pagan for the first time, fighting alongside Gods Tears and The Equalizer to beat Storm, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Flash for Trinity on March To Hell (21 October 2004); his sister Alice steps between the two men afterward. Helps beat down Trinity's own outgoing member Flash in the Flashback segment on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), brawls with Fast Eddie Cutlass outside the ring during Gods Tears's own Submission Championship match, and is speared, then Final Judgemented with his own finisher by a returning Adam Lazarus, during the chaotic post-show brawl that follows Jason Storm's own ambush of The Equalizer. Revealed as Melanie Kilpatrick's own kidnapper, badly beats and cripples Jason Storm inside a surprise cage trap sprung by Storm's own manager Alice at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004) -- slamming the cage door on Storm's neck and knee and dropping the cage's own weight onto his leg -- after Melanie escapes and turns on Alice with a DDT to side with her captor; also interferes in the Rage in the Cage's final fall with a Final Judgement on Lark Fenriz, helping The Equalizer retain the VWF Heavyweight Championship. Cancels his own scheduled six-man tag with a taped video threat against a beaten, cross-tied Fast Eddie Cutlass, then joins Gods Tears and The Equalizer in an unsanctioned beatdown of Cutlass, Lark Fenriz and Classy Joey Boy on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), all three of Trinity arrested afterward. Joins the post-match beatdown of Lark Fenriz and Fast Eddie Cutlass on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004) after Kingston Cash's own year-end Trinity suspension forces a triple-threat rebooking, chased off along with the rest of Trinity by an unidentified woman's own three wolves. Fights Fast Eddie Cutlass and Lark Fenriz to a no contest alongside Gods Tears and The Equalizer on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after their own mystery third partner never arrives. Chains, whips and crowns Fast Eddie Cutlass with a barbed-wire "Crown of Thorns" in a post-match beatdown on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), ejected from the building by John Tyler and forfeiting his own scheduled match with Lark Fenriz. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) as the surprise final three-man entry alongside Gods Tears and The Equalizer, eliminating Thunder Valenkov's own target Gods Tears before being eliminated himself. Loses to Fast Eddie Cutlass in a Church Of Pain match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) despite interference from Trinity stablemates Gods Tears and The Equalizer, who topple a ringside spotlight onto Cutlass and drag him toward the scaffolding before enforcer Kagedo intervenes; Cutlass breaks free, hits the Guarantee, and rolls Pagan off the scaffolding, the referee stopping the match when Pagan cannot breathe.
