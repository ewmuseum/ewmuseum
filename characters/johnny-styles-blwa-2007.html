Johnny Styles

aka Johnny Stylez, SBK

The wrestler SBK: 'Johnny SBK Stylez' returned at Halloween Massacre in Barely Legal Wrestling Alliance on 31 October 2007; a column on Wrestling Unplugged's news page follows him through other federations to March 2009; and in October 2009 he won the first 96 Revolution World title in a six-man cage and lost it to Nathan Lancaster on 3 November.
