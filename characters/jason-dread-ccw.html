Jason Dread

Jason Dread, who took the CCW Heavyweight Championship off Blayze at Revolution and held it into 2005, held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship twice, the HDTV Championship once and the Hardcore Championship once, was inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame in 2005 and appeared on 47 GCW cards from 30 August 2001 to 4 February 2009.
