Scottywood

Scottywood worked five Global cards from 1 June 2007 to 2 December 2007: a dark-match tag debut with Jaguar on 1 June, a loss to Joaquin Pierre and his release on 8 June, then the Graveyard Battle Royal of 10 November and two more cards. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

GW (Dallas, 2000–2007)

2007-06-01 – 2007-12-02
Tenure detail
Dated from the five cards that name him. The 8 June 2007 card has him released after a loss to Joaquin Pierre; he next appears in the Graveyard Battle Royal of 10 November 2007, speaking as a WMW Tag Team champion, and the 22 November 2007 hotlist says he came in through an open challenge and signed. The Global cards call him Scott "Scottywood" Wood (and, once, Scott Woodson).
