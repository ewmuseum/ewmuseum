Bio For every child in the world who grew up in a life of poverty and overcame it, there is a child who grew up in wealth, wishing he were someplace else. Raizo Tenzan was the latter of the previous statement. His family had speculative wealth, well the speculators were often correct. His father was worth more money then most could envisage. Conversely, the origin of this money was far from that of a hard working Japanese citizen. Junichi Tenzan, the esteemed and well-respected father of Raizo, was publicly perceived as a hard working businessman, a rags-to-riches story in the Fukuoka community. However, there are much deeper roots to Mr. Junichi Tenzan’s wealth. The roots would leave a scar on Raizo Tenzan’s life, not only as a young child, but it would affect him forever, an eerie shadow that he could never shake. Junichi Tenzan was the leading director of operations in the largest crime syndicate in Japan. During the year of 1978, the year of Raizo’s birth, Junichi Tenzan was in the midst of the largest scale counterfeit operation in Japanese history. He amassed a small fortune of three million United States Dollars, which he later had deposited in an overseas bank account. This was only the beginning… Junichi Tenzan would clime the ranks of the crime syndicate, and hoards millions upon millions of United States dollars. This all ceased, although shortly, when Raizo was born. Born July 2nd 1978, Raizo Tenzan, he immediately experienced the benefits of living a wealthy life, reaping the benefits of his fathers previous work. He was tutored by a Japanese teacher in the arts of English and became an articulate and confident speaker in both his mother tongue and English. Raizo Tenzan’s life was nothing short of blissful, his earliest years, up to five years old, were spent playing with an abundance of toys and learning the art of Japanese and English. Moreover, it would result in an abhorrent twist of fate that would inevitably change his life forever. From the age of five years and onwards Raizo would only know a world of pain and treachery. His father resumed his work, and Raizo experienced the cruel backlash of fury from his father’s hand. Something his mother must have previously experienced, she died a few days after Raizo was born. Apparently from the pain of child labor… How much do you trust your own father? They came and went, they came and died, they came and lived; however, the last clause was a rarity. They say the events that shape your personality, sense of morality and character occur when you are young. In Raizo’s case, he was becoming everything that could be viewed wrong in the world. He took on a sense of hate and disrespect for anyone he met outside his family. His perception of everything was blinded by obscene rage and hate. It somehow acted as a catalyst on his life. His size escalated above his father, and above the size of the people employed to look after him. Raizo was becoming a beast. At the age of fifteen, he was already 5’11”, five inches taller then his father. Every one of the events he witnessed, murder, treachery, deceit and an iron will made Raizo into an unyielding angry man. His teenage years were spent taking vicious beatings from his father, till his fifteenth birthday. There comes a day in every mans life when he must stand for what he believes in, it just so happened Raizo’s came a bit earlier in his life then most. On his fifteenth birthday, as his father was about to strike him, Raizo span to his right and smashed his father’s skull through the glass desk in his father’s office. Raizo was consumed with a feeling of joy, the pain he was inflicting was comforting. His father’s exploits had finally caught up on him, although from an unlikely source. Like every runaway child, Raizo sought refuge, and he found it at a local underground pit fighting syndicate, where he was taken in as a person to do small errands. He grew fond of the shootfighting that he was seeing, the vicious manner these warriors of the underground were doing battle. It had honour; it had everything his father had not. Raizo immediately began to train at a local ‘den’ learning his trade as a young fifteen year old, he began to train in every form of man to man combat the trainer knew. Mainly, suplex based, and power style fighting. His use of submissions intrigued Raizo, and he mastered several forms of submission moves. His big break came… a fighter had dropped out from a main event show. Raizo, now standing an impressive 6’3, 257lbs, at the age of 23 began his shoot fighting career. He was an immediate success, defeating the then undefeated Yoki Yoshi in a record 3:01 minutes, with a superb technical display. Life was looking up, Raizo made the life of a vagabond his own, he ventured from town to town, fighting fights, fighting for his own enjoyment, to gain respect from the masses He wanted to be feared… It was at this time he discovered the Puroresu form of fighting, the concept intrigued him, as did the prospect of an established pay. He figured the idea to be worth looking into for full time employment, his anger being fueled by the need to be respected, and for his father to know he is the better man. Raizo Tenzan is a young man fueled by hate and rage… only time will tell if this anger ever spoils over into the ring.
