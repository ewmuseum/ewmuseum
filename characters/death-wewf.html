Sword and Shield's own promo at the PWGeA SuperCard of 18 May 1991 addresses this team by name and dates their shared history to the other federation in the same breath as their debut against it: "Death and Destruction, we fought you countless times in the WeWF, and now it's here, in the PWGeA."
