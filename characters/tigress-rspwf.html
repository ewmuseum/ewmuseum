UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-10-22 – 2006-02-14
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 17 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 10-4-3
Tenure detail
Teamed with Leslie Hernandez and Angel as the Coven in a win over the Love Sisters and Comedy, stomping and taunting Comedy before tagging out. Reached the semi-finals of the 17 December 2003 Meltdown Championship tournament, surviving the battle royal, before Paul Wong caught her in midair off a Tiger Pounce attempt and drilled her with a Samoan drop for the pin. Beat "Twilight Angel" Sonya Benedict by pinfall on 10 March 2004 with a double Sumatran Headhunter, then interfered in Papa Legba's own Meltdown Championship defense against Alex Kidd later the same card; announced afterward, over drinks with Papa Legba, that she was splitting from Sabbat Justice to go independent. Ate takeout with her pet tiger Harley while discussing an upcoming match with Simon O'Neal and Donna Tetreault, then teamed with Papa Legba on 19 May 2004 to beat O'Neal and Tetreault for the UWF Meltdown Championship, using a handful of powder to blind the referee before Legba's top-rope splash finished it. Beat Scorpio by pinfall (the Poacher's Woe) and advanced to the UWF Meltdown Championship tournament finals on a bye after Papa Legba and Uriah Hawkins fought to a double countout, then fought Vyolynce to a twenty-minute time-limit draw. Won the UWF Meltdown Championship from Collision Holly by submission (the Marjara Mallaka) on 22 September 2004, sitting her full weight across Holly's legs until he tapped, after Vyolynce declined to compete in the advertised three-way and left the ring before the bell. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Youth Gone Wild by submission with an inverted dragon sleeper on 6 October 2004, accepting the challenger's own post-match handshake to a large ovation. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Bobby Nash by pinfall (the Sumatran Headhunter, using the ropes for leverage) on 20 October 2004, despite giving up a large size disadvantage. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn by disqualification on 3 November 2004, after a six-wrestler UWF tryout group (revealed to be led by a disguised T.D. Swagger) ran in and beat Bonn down. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Scorpio by submission (the Marjara Mallaka) on 17 November 2004. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Scott "Hotspot" Daniels in a draw on 1 December 2004 after both wrestlers were disqualified for a simultaneous low blow and eye-rake, an ugly title defense that began with Tigress blasting Daniels with the belt before the bell. Fought Marissa Monet to a double disqualification for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 25 May 2005 after both wrestlers shoved the referee, ending an ugly grudge match that began with a promo in which Tigress gutted a shark on camera to make her point. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Marissa Monet by submission (a modified Marjara Mallaka surfboard) in a No DQ rematch on 8 June 2005, after telling reporter Debbie Henshall she would rather have lost cleanly than share another double disqualification; President Becky Byers announced afterward that the defense was the Meltdown Championship's own last, with the title to be unified with the Rampage Championship the following week. Lost the UWF Meltdown Championship to Rampage Champion Marcus Nuit by pinfall (the 51st Drop) in a title unification match on 22 June 2005, ending the Meltdown Championship's own separate history; needled Nuit backstage beforehand over his own sentimentality about the belt. Rebuffed an awkward backstage approach from John DeWolfe about a possible Bod Squad partnership on 20 July 2005, while sharpening a katana in her own locker room. Challenged Marcus Nuit for the Unified Television Championship at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, losing by pinfall after a tense top-rope exchange; signed autographs for fans backstage beforehand. Teamed with Luke Kinsey in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, submitting a debuting Samantha Daniels with a double armbar rollup before being catapulted over the top rope by Jason Keening.
