Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Ricky Dale Johnson

aka RDJ

TCW's second-biggest paycheck, a brawler and long-time babyface who loses his last title shot against Tommy Cornell at Just Another Day?, is barred from challenging him and is named guest enforcer for the Cornell and Troy Tornado title match at Total Mayhem 13, and who becomes number one contender for the Hard Hitting Championship in July 2008 and loses to Sam Keith at Summer Showdown, and in August 2008 beats JD Morgan and American Buffalo and, with Danny Fonzarelli, Chris Rockwell and Frankie Perez at Hotter Than Hell. In September 2008 he beats Fumihiro Ota with the Leaping Lariat, loses with Johnny Bloodstone to Tommy Cornell and Wolf Hawkins and emails the narrator to ask what he is to do, which the diary says will be answered in 2009, and in October 2008 beats Frankie Perez, loses with Johnny Bloodstone to Jeremy Stone & Tommy Cornell, confronts Mayhem Midden about being overlooked and, at Crunch Time, beats Chris Rockwell and then pulls Remo Richardson out of the main event. In November 2008 he explains why he turned on Remo Richardson, draws with Eddie Peak against Marat Khoklov & Remo Richardson, is injured by Remo's chair, is given the women's division by the booking committee and at A Little South Of Sanity, fighting for TCW's honour, loses to Remo when Phil Vibert holds his boot. In December 2008 he beats the British Lions and Frankie Perez & Texas Pete with Danny Fonzarelli, is beaten by Jeremy Stone in the number one contendership match and by Tyson Baine, is beaten down by Baine and Remo Richardson and at Psycho Circus beats Richardson in a steel cage match.
