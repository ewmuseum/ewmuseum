Katy Hummer

WMW's Commissioner through the spring of 2003, secretly the leader of the heel stable The Court. Written by AI, 2026

WMW (2003)

2003-03 – 2003-05
authority-figure — Commissioner; leader of The Court
Tenure detail
Named WMW Commissioner ahead of Ides of March; secretly orders three security guards to block Sub-Beast's own corner in the main event, then reveals herself and the guards as a new stable, The Court, in the show's own closing angle. Benny Tyler's own "Trouble in Paradise" column recounts a prior rivalry with President Brad Johnson from a defunct fed, Maverick, where she first fielded a group called The Court against him; by 16 May 2003 Donovan Merlot is named the fed's new Commissioner in her place, with no document in the held record recording how the change happened.
