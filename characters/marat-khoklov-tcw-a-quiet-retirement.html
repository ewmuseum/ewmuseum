Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Marat Khoklov

A giant Russian whom Eric Tyler brings to Badge of Honor in September 2008, saying the School of Tradition has friends in Japan, though he holds no TCW contract: he flattens Rick Law there and, in an unsanctioned match at Destructive Energy, snaps Rocky Golden's baseball bat in two and beats him senseless. In November 2008 he walks out to the Psalm 69 theme to cut Eddie Peak down in his match with Eric Tyler, draws with Remo Richardson against Peak & Ricky Dale Johnson, beats Joey Minnesota with the Moscow Lariat and at A Little South Of Sanity wins a No Disqualification match against Peak.
