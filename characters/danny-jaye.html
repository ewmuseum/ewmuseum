Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Danny Jaye was first exposed to unarmed combat as a kickboxer in the United States Air Force. After his honorable discharge, he entered the arena of professional wrestling in an independent federation in his hometown of Memphis, TN. After this, he was picked up by the TCW. The rest, as they say, is history. Danny loves to pop the crowd. Sometimes, it seems, the more the crowd is into the match, the better he wrestles. His colorful style and unique personality are matched by his raw power and skill.
