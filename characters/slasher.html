Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Slasher hails from Des Moines, Iowa, and arrived on the scene during the days of the GIWF. He is not a rookie wrestler by far. He has never been the most decorated champion, but has heled titles. He has held the GIWF TV Title, GIWF THC Title, and the UWF THC Title. He has always been a force to be reckoned with, but his hardcore ways are also usually his undoing. Many a match has been lost due to him taking it way too far. His biggest claim to fame so far has been forming the extreme team known as the Drowning Pool with Unforgiven and Misfit. That group mutated into The Violent Reaction. Eventually this group left, and Slasher ventured to Japan to join wrestling federation known as DREAM, which quickly fell through. What does the future hold for Slasher? Only he knows.. but whatever it is.. he will make it for himself and will not be be stopped by anyone
