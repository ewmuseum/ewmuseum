UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2006-02-14
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 19 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 5-12-1, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Billed from Los Angeles, California, with his brother Michael Keening as the Ghost Dancers, the reigning UWF World Tag Team Champions; retained by default when their three-way title defense was ruled a no-contest on 9 August 2003. Lost the UWF World Tag Team Championship to Team Canada on 4 October 2003 after Kari Stevens pepper-sprayed him at ringside; the Privateers ambushed both brothers backstage afterward. Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with his brother from Team Canada by submission on 25 October 2003, after a contract clause kept the World Tag Team Championship off the line. Lost the UWF North American Tag Team Championship on 15 November 2003 when teammates Erik and Chad Grimsson escaped the Tower of Doom cage as a complete team before he and his brother Michael could. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match with brother Michael; tagged in late by Alex Extreme to help finish off Jacob Drake with a Hart Attack combination, winning the match and the right to help set his own side's Eve of Destruction stipulations alongside Extreme. Teamed with brother Michael and Erik Grimsson on 10 March 2004 in a six-man tag loss to Team Canada and Scott Daniels after two unidentified men in suits ambushed the match. Challenged Victor Frost for the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship on 24 March 2004, losing by pinfall (Death Valley Driver) after Frost blinded him with fingerpaint smuggled in by manager Simone Sumner. Teamed with brother Michael again as the Ghost Dancers on 29 May 2004, fighting Serge Annis and Caliban to a double countout after trading big moves at ringside; the Privateers ran in on the ensuing brawl, and commissioner Becky Byers added the Ghost Dancers to the Gold Rush UWF World Tag Team Championship match against champions the Privateers and Annis and Caliban. Teamed with brother Michael as the Ghost Dancers in a losing Gold Rush triple threat for the UWF World Tag Team Championship on 5 June 2004, his own brother pinned by Jacob Drake of the champion Privateers; assaulted backstage afterward by a mystery masked attacker while Michael was taken to a hospital for a back injury suffered in the match. Beat Scott "Hotspot" Daniels by pinfall (an inside cradle) on 28 August 2004 in his own first UWF singles match; attacked after the bell by Daniels and then by a masked man who revealed himself as "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas -- the same man who had attacked his brother Michael backstage at Gold Rush -- before Daniels drove Douglas from the ring. Lost a three-way dance to Scott "Hotspot" Daniels on 16 October 2004, pinned by a stolen cover after colliding head-first with Chris Douglas. Beat Chris Douglas's own bodyguard Titanicus by pinfall (a bridging Northern Lights suplex) on 27 November 2004 despite Douglas's own repeated ringside coaching and interference, only to be blindsided afterward by a Douglas Dragon suplex once the bout was won. Lost to Luke Kinsey by pinfall (a reverse cradle, feet on the ropes) on 11 December 2004 in a match Kinsey had proposed as a clean technical contest, then was whip-strapped by Chris Douglas and Titanicus afterward, setting up a strap match at Rise to Power; briefly reconciled with Rick Marley backstage. Lost to UWF newcomer Ethan de Sade by pinfall (the Ghostbuster) on 28 May 2005 after a pre-match beatdown from de Sade's father, Chris Douglas, and Douglas's own bodyguard, Titanicus; demanded a handicap rematch against both Douglas and de Sade from Jessica "Fatality" Marshall afterward, still bloodied. Traded insults with Alex Extreme outside a sports bar on 11 June 2005, then teamed with him in a tag loss to Chris Douglas and Ethan de Sade after Extreme accidentally clotheslined him, and brawled with Extreme afterward until Chris Douglas and de Sade jumped both men. Smashed a locker room mirror over his own rivalry with Alex Extreme on 22 June 2005, reassured by visiting uncle "Fireball" Ken Keening that Extreme's own past attack on him was not Jason's own fight to settle. Shared a Memphis jail cell with Alex Extreme on 6 July 2005 after their own brawl during the Road to the Gold, and learned from Ken Keening that Extreme's earlier attack on him had been staged to provoke Jason. Answered children's questions about his own feud with Alex Extreme at a community center appearance on 20 July 2005, calling Extreme "scum" and naming Chris Douglas and Ethan de Sade as also deserving a reckoning. Warned by Sabbath backstage on 23 July 2005 about the dangers of his own hatred for Extreme; lost a random-partners tag match teamed with Miguel Quesada against Michael Augustine and Victor Frost the same show, pinned by a cradle brainbuster. Eliminated second from the main event eight-man elimination match on 3 August 2005, chokeslammed by Tumaffi after sniping on-camera with teammate Alex Extreme. Teamed with Lisa Drake in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, eliminating Luke Kinsey and Tigress before being pinned by Alex Extreme after Kinsey's own post-elimination chair shots left both finalists dazed.
