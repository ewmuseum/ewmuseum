AGW (1997)

2002 – c. 2008
wrestler -- debuted at Ultimate Survivor 2002 eliminating three of the Order of Snakes single-handedly; AGW TV Champion and AGW Triple Crown Tag Champion (World, Anything Goes, and Unified Tag titles) as one-half of Damage Incorporated with Eric Von Doom; won the 2002 Rookie of the Year and 2003 Most Potential Slammy awards. His own bio card narrates an extensive kayfabe biography: raised by his father "Lord Stephen Regal" (a fictional former WCW Television Titleholder, never staged as an AGW character in his own right) after his mother's divorce, then by his uncle William Regal following his father's death; formed the early tag team Britannia with William Regal before Regal turned on him; married Jo Hardy, whose later infidelity drove her into William Regal's own company; opposed Mark Paris's stable The Clique on behalf of his idol Most Holy; feuded at length with AGW legend Smooth Operator, settled in a two-out-of-three-falls Insane Death Match; formed Damage Incorporated with Eric Von Doom after Smooth Operator's own defeat, capturing the AGW Tag Team Triple Crown as the first team to do so, before a Ghetto Boyz attack (a near-drowning) and its aftermath broke the team up; feuded with Mark Payne ("the Blonde Bastard"); and led the New Blood incursion revealed at Ultimate Survivor 2003 on Speed Racer's own invitation.
