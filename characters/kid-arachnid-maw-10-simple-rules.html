Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Kid Arachnid

aka KC Glenn

A young technician in a black Spider-Man mask who debuts at Beach Blast and beats Antonio on Fall Of The King. The masked wrestler who wins The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2009 is unmasked as Rip Chord's son Jay; the diary's own housekeeping note says Thom Morgan wore the mask until just before Remember November. The Arachnid gimmick returns on the 11th April 2011 tour show, when Kid Arachnid teams with Jonnie Perez against Flash Savage and Roderick Remus; the narrator's note says it is not Jay. A new Arachnid, whom Jay Chord says is not him under the mask, wins on the 25th April tour show and is called out by Jay Chord at Spring Fever, the match ending in a draw. He becomes the fourth man of Steve Flash's new Firm, loses with Flash to Erik Strong and Jay Chord and with Randy Bumfhole to Casey Valentine and Antonio, wins most of his tour matches (Greg Ford, Curtis Jenkins, Roderick Remus, Nelson Callum, Jared Johnson, Jefferson Stardust, Extreme Deluxe) and, with Chance Fortune and Bumfhole, takes the Trios titles from The Triple Threat at Summernova on 12th August, pinning Nelson Callum with the Cradle Piledriver. At Back To School Jay Chord attacks him and tries to tear off his mask; the narrator notes that a Japanese company wants to take him on tour. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he beats Antonio with the Cradle Piledriver; the narrator says the interest is in who is under the mask. After Jay Chord's departure Chance Fortune tells him at Farewell Tour on 22nd November, when he beats Citizen X, that he does not need the mask, and Kirk Jameson becomes his mentor; at Remember November he wins the All Action title with a chair backstage and loses it within minutes, then loses a tag match to Providence and Citizen X. He beats Citizen X in an Invitational qualifier at End Of Days, loses to Primus Allen for the Traditional title on 5th January 2012, and at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January beats Jared Johnson, Firebird and JD Morgan on the one night to win the tournament, removing the mask to say that his name is KC Glenn. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 KC Glenn, the Rip Chord Invitational champion, prepares to take his bow against Primus Allen and draws with him for the Traditional title at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, beats the Dark Raptors with Randy Bumfhole at All Or Nothing, beats the Canadian Blondes with Jared Johnson at Third Time's The Charm and Citizen X at March Madness, is entered in the Gold Rush match at April Showers, and loses to Firebird and to Father Johan, who invites him to join his family, and with Kirk Jameson to Firebird and Father Johan at Mother, May I? The narrator notes that his microphone skills have grown out of sharing promo time with Rip Chord on the tours, and that he can cash in his title shot at any time. In the diary's pages from June to July 2012 KC Glenn is the new GCG Openweight champion, and the narrator thinks seriously about how he might look with the MAW title round his waist. He loses with Bradford Peverell to Ford & Speed at South East Madness on 9th June, cashes in his Invitational guaranteed title shot against Andrews at Summer Showdown on 12th June and loses after a near-standstill twenty minutes, is felled with the belt by Andrews, beats Black Eagle with a Tune Up The Sunshine Band at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July and loses to The Great Ota at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, before coming into the ring with a chair to save Donnie J and JD Morgan. At Beach Blast on 12th July he and Father Johan fall from the top turnbuckle of the lumberjack match to the arena floor and are taken out on stretchers, and the narrator says he has broken through with someone else and left MAW.
