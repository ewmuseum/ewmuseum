Brendan Idol

A newcomer signed in September 2007 who practises American Idol auditions on karaoke and a teddy bear in each bathroom sink, loses to Jacob Jett on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 4, and challenges Bulldozer Brandon Smith for the Unappreciated Championship on the Monday of Week 1, October, which he loses. In October 2007 he loses to Phlex, sings a pop song to a stage of reporters, and beats Brett Hall by disqualification on the Monday of Week 3 to earn a challenge for the Unappreciated Championship at Pilgrimage. In October and November 2007 he beats Demon Spite, is beaten by Bulldozer Brandon Smith twice, joins Brett Hall in a beating of Smith, and loses the Unappreciated Championship match at Pilgrimage.
