Jeremy Stone

Edd Stone's big brother, a son of Dan Stone billed the Canadian Wrestling Machine and heir to the holds of the House of Stone, who debuts on Badge of Honor in September 2008 as the Syndicate's newest member and beats Art Reed with the Stone Hold, then teams with Edd to beat the Keith Brothers and, at Destructive Energy, loses to Sam Keith's Proton Lock in a No Holds Barred match for the Hard Hitting Championship. In October 2008 he beats Matthew Keith, explains in Horatio Dangerous' interview why he left the family company, beats Johnny Bloodstone & Ricky Dale Johnson with Tommy Cornell and, at Crunch Time, is the third man in the World Heavyweight Championship match that Troy Tornado wins. In November 2008 he promises that both Stone brothers will hold gold by the end of the year, loses to Troy Tornado's Star Maker, beats the Conquering Kings, the Easy Riders and Tooth & Claw with Edd Stone and is eliminated with him at A Little South Of Sanity. In December 2008 he beats Ricky Dale Johnson, loses to Rick Law by disqualification when Tyson Baine attacks, attacks Troy Tornado after a tag loss, loses the six-man match with Remo Richardson and Chris Rockwell and at Psycho Circus loses the three-way match to Troy Tornado.
