N.E.W.'s own commentary places him from Israel (Friday Night Slam Dance, 24 April 1998). Already N.E.W. Heavyweight Champion when King of the Mat 2 opens; its own highlight package credits the win to a steel-cage match over James Pillow. Retained the title against Patty Babes Carter at King of the Mat 2 (6 April 1998), then lost it to Vinny Vitrone in a Four Way Dance also involving James Pillow and Brass Knuckles at Friday Night Slam Dance (24 April 1998), suffering an in-match knee injury and being taken out on a stretcher.

WCWF (1996)

1996-08-05 – 1997-05-01
wrestler, WCWF World Heavyweight Champion (won the Friday Night Live battle royal of 9 August 1996)
Tenure detail
Billed Assaf "The Terminator" Levavy. Won the WCWF World Championship in the Friday Night Live battle royal of 9 August 1996, tossing Vader out last, and pinned Vader at Sunday Assault 2 (18 August 1996). Returned at March Madness (31 March 1997) and won against Lex Luger with the Terminator Plex; Warzone IV's title history has him beat Vader in a title-for-title match after his return.
Source: the fed's own Flash Flood bulletins (5 August-17 September 1996), Friday Night Live 1 (9 August 1996), Sunday Assault 2 (18 August 1996), March Madness (31 March 1997), Saturday Night Superstars (9 April 1997) and Warzone IV (1 May 1997)
