Merged from flint-and-steel-wewf. THE EVIDENCE. The Summit of E-Wrestling's own third night walks this team to the ring, "Led towards the ring by their shop steward, Molly Maguire… Representing the AFL-CIO", and the PWA's own 1992 record names "Molly MaGuire, shop-steward for Flint and Steel": one woman, one job, one named team, two federations. Thirty-eight newly served WeWF documents carry this team from the league's eighth turn to the last tournament of March 1992 with that manager in their corner throughout, through four Tag Team Championship reigns, and the newsdesk's own rumour mill of 30 January 1992 explains the gap in their own record — "Flint and Steel came back to the WeWF because they're both unemployed after the Millionaire bought their factory and closed it down." The retired row carried no members at all; this one carries both men.
Source: the WeWF's own "TWIW - Summer Recap of E-Wrestling - Pt.I" (27 September 1992) — its own closing line, "This takes the recap of the PWA through the submission of the Summit participants," confirms the whole card-by-card recap is PWA's own action, structured here under the anti-double-count rule rather than WeWF's (WeWF This Week in Wrestling: Summer Recap of E-Wrestling, Part I)
