Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-12-18 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 22 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 12-5-2, 3 no contest
Tenure detail
Guests on Flash's own new interview segment, THE FLASHBACK, interrupted by Sidious The Clown, then wins the Team Tournament Final alongside Lark Fenriz and Fast Eddie Cutlass on Firestorm (3 May 2004), securing a Gauntlet entry. Loses a triple threat to Kagedo after an unidentified masked attacker's interference, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and is eliminated by Kagedo and Marcus O'Malley together. Wins by disqualification against Sebastian on Firestorm (27 May 2004) after Sonicman's interference, then teams with Orion in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal, eliminated in a mutual elimination alongside him. Teams with Orion in the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round, losing to Marcus O'Malley wrestling alone; overpowers several rivals in the sixteen-man tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), attempting and failing to unmask the ring's own Orion after the video screens show a second Orion elsewhere. Disqualified alongside Billy Bones in the Dog Days Four Tag Team Scramble on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) after three chair shots, then wins the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal and the tag Pentagon of Pain with Orion The Hunter on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) to reach the Dog Days tag final. Won alongside Orion The Hunter on Wrestle Style V.1, attacked the referee before a signed tag match could begin on Wrestle Style V.2, then beat Real Deal in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Beats Blaze with a Face Plant onto Blaze's own steel chair in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), then attacks a no-show Jason Storm's scheduled opponent Classy Joey Boy from the crowd with the timekeeper's bell. Outlasts Robbie Caldwell, The Outlaw Morris Bass and The Predator Kevin Simmons in a fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) to earn the Vegas Violence title match, pinning Simmons with a Face Plant after Lindsay Simmons distracts her own husband. Signs the contract for that match on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004); Jason Storm crashes the signing by video and blows up his truck. Part of Trinity's six-man tag win over Jason Storm, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Flash on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Helps beat down his own ally Flash in the Flashback segment on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), then is ambushed by Jason Storm after demanding a fight of his own, beaten bloody with a chair before Lark Fenriz interferes with a Sammael. Retains the VWF Heavyweight Championship over Lark Fenriz in the final fall of the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), hitting a Face Plant through two tables after Pagan's own Final Judgement weakens the challenger. Helps Gods Tears and Pagan attack a beaten Fast Eddie Cutlass, Lark Fenriz and Classy Joey Boy after Pagan's own video threat cancels their scheduled six-man tag on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Runs in against Kingston Cash's own year-end Trinity suspension to join the post-match beatdown of Lark Fenriz and Fast Eddie Cutlass on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), scattered by an unidentified woman's own three wolves. Knocks Sidious The Clown from the ring and joins Gods Tears and Pagan's own no-contest brawl with Fast Eddie Cutlass and Lark Fenriz on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005). Retains the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship against The Maquilladora and Classy Joey Boy on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) after Sebastian's interference and a guest-referee stint from majority owner Kingston Cash. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) as the surprise final three-man entry alongside Pagan and Gods Tears, eliminated last by Thunder Valenkov. Retains the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship over Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora in a Wheel Of Chaos match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) after a doctor who slips into the cage stipulation to treat the bleeding champion instead injects both challengers, letting The Equalizer pin them once they collapse; the doctor is revealed afterward as Kingston Cash's own brother, Terrance Cash. Also joins Gods Tears in interfering for Pagan against Fast Eddie Cutlass earlier the same show, toppling a ringside spotlight onto Cutlass before enforcer Kagedo breaks it up. Teams with Gods Tears against Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora at March To Madness (2 March 2005), the match declared a no contest after two masked men interfere on both sides. His own scheduled VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship defense against Kagedo at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) is aborted before the bell when Gods Tears attacks Kagedo with a tire iron; John Tyler books a bonus tag match instead, and The Equalizer and Gods Tears are disqualified against Kagedo and Roadkill Rick when Gods Tears breaks up a near-fall with the tire iron. Retains the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), entering the wing's own closing Gauntlet battle royal first by his own request rather than last as tradition dictates and surviving as the sole man left in the ring after Thunder Valenkov, sent into a top rope that Hot Property forgets to pull down, goes over instead.
