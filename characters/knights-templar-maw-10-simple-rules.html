Knights Templar

The face stable that Kirk Jameson names at Beach Blast on 12th July 2012: because it is on a crusade of sorts and people call its members white knights, they are the Knights Templar, a name Jameson says a benefactor suggested after Bradford Peverell said that people need a symbol to rally behind; Jameson says they are not a cult. At Summer Showdown on 12th June Peverell, KC Glenn and Donnie J, who says he will help only in the ring, agree to stand united with Jameson against The Fallen; JD Morgan agrees at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July to help them when he is in trouble without signing up to anything, and the benefactor who funds the help they receive, Rich Money, is brought to the ring by Jameson at Summernova on 5th August; the diary does not print a full roll.
