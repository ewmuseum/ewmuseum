The Enforcers

aka Enforcers

Jack Marlowe and Julian Watson, the name Tommy Cornell gives the Syndicate's new tag team, who ambush John Anderson in September 2008, lose a six-man tag with Edd Stone to the Young Guns and Art Reed, and lose a tag title match on Badge of Honor to the New Wave when Scout locks in the Special Force, and in October 2008 help Edd Stone against Danny Fonzarelli and hurt Sam Keith, and in November 2008 lose to the Keith Brothers on Badge of Honor and, at A Little South Of Sanity, beat down John Anderson in the three-way International Championship match, and in December 2008 target the Keith twins in a Badge of Honor beatdown and join the brawl in Tyson Baine & Wolf Hawkins's win over John Anderson & Rick Law.
