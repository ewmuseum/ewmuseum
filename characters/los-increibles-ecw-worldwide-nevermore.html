Los Increibles

Rey Mysterio Jr. and Juventud Guerrera, who end their best-of-seven series at Anarchy Rulz with a hug and team up as Los Increibles on the first Revolution of October 2006, beating Michael Shane and Frankie Kazarian with a Double Springboard Legdrop, and win again with a Double Toprope Guillotina the week after. As part of Team Mexico they lose the Lucha Libre six-man tag at Living Dangerously; after the Ultimate Jeopardy match at Baptism Of Fire they turn on Eddie Guerrero with a Hurricanrana, and in April they beat Los Guerreros on Revolution with the Juvi Driver on Eddie and lose the rematch on Blood, Sweat and Beers. In April 2007 Los Increibles beat Los Guerreros on Blood, Sweat and Beers (Rey Mysterio Jr.'s Tornado DDT on Chavo) to earn a tag title match at Cyberslam, where the Whole F**kin' Show keep the belts in the Ladder Match. In May AJ Styles pins Juventud Guerrera on his debut, and in June Juvi beats AJ in a non-title match after AJ kisses Francine, while Rey Mysterio Jr. loses to AJ's low blow and small package. In June 2007 Rey Mysterio Jr. prepares for the World Cup with Team Mexico while Juventud Guerrera, who is not in the tournament, loses to AJ Styles with the low blow and the foot-on-the-rope finishes; in August Juvi beats Rey in the first match of their revived series with a 450 Splash. At Guilty As Charged in October 2007 Rey Mysterio Jr., saying that Juventud Guerrera 'was dead weight' and the reason they never won the tag titles, joins the four-way Stairway To Hell Match for the World Television Championship, and Guerrera, returning through the crowd with a chair, hits all four men; the next night he beats Mysterio with a 450 Splash. In November 2007 Rey Mysterio Jr. and Juventud Guerrera, who 'cannot stand one another', are booked together against Ultimo Dragon and Christopher Daniels on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and the alliance breaks down when Mysterio floors his own partner with a punch; Guerrera pins Daniels with a 450 Splash.
