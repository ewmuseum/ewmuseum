The Masked Patriot

aka Masked Patriot, "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith

A flag-waving face and the defender of truth, justice and the American way, who feuds with Citizen X for most of 2008 and works a flying tackle and the Backdrop Driver. In February 2010 he stakes his mask on a match with Primus Allen and loses; at March Madness in March 2010 he unmasks as former NFL star "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith, hands the mask to a fan, Masked Patriot #21, and carries on under his own name, beating Jean Cattley at Spring Fever and being left out cold by Ultimate Phoenix's chair. At Summer Showdown in June 2010, still the Bulldozer, he teams with Kashmir Singh to beat Ricochet & Sharp and is then chaired by the unmasking Firebird, his former partner in The Watch; with Fumihiro Ota and Singh he wins the Trios titles at Hotter Than Hot and keeps them at Summernova. At Iron in August he challenges Jay Chord in his main-event debut and loses to the Cradle Piledriver, partners Aaron Andrews at Fall Of The King, and at Where It All Begins Again in October beats Firebird with an Inverted Piledriver after pleading with his friend to give up the darkness. At Remember November in November 2010 Brandon Smith loses the Tag Team title match with Kashmir Singh when Flash Savage pulls down the top rope; the Trios titles go at Season's Beatings, and at End Of Days the Canadian Blondes demolish him with a spike piledriver after he and Singh win with Jean Cattley. Injured, he misses New Year, Old Rivalries, returns to lose to the Blondes at the Invitational, loses to Oscar Golden's Golden Shower at Leap Into The Unknown in February, beats Jefferson Stardust, Greg Ford and VIP Wallace on tour shows, loses to Primus Allen at Third Time's The Charm and then spears Flash Savage to save Cattley and Singh from the Blondes. As Singh & Smith he loses the Tag Team title three-ways of May and July 2011, Ford & Speed at Summernova, where they tease him about the straw-blonde hair he got from a stupid bet, and beats Team XDW at Iron with Singh; he beats Jared Johnson and Curtis Jenkins and loses to Greg Ford on the tour shows, signs to tour with EX2010 in June, and loses to Casey Valentine at Beach Blast after warning him about injuring his friend Kirk Jameson. At Back To School he, Singh and Fumihiro Ota beat the Canadian Blondes and Raphael. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he loses a Tag Team title three-way and a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews, and at All The Leaves Are Brown Providence, whom he had mentored, beats him; with Kashmir Singh he wins the Tag Team titles at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October and keeps them to the end of January 2012. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 Brandon Smith beats Providence at All Or Nothing on 12th February, beats the Canadian Blondes with Kashmir Singh at Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March, loses to Aaron Andrews for the Heavyweight title at Third Time's The Charm, enters the Gold Rush match at April Showers, loses to Casey Valentine at Great Lakes Madness and beats Marc Speed with an Inverted Piledriver at Mother, May I? on 12th May. As Brandon Smith, the Bulldozer, in the diary's pages from June to September 2012, he and Kashmir Singh keep the Tag Team titles in the three-way at High Noon on 5th June, Smith catching Casey Valentine with the Inverted Piledriver, say they have held the belts for eight months, and lose them to the Dark Raptors at Summer Showdown on 12th June. They lose to Always Breakin' Hollywood at Hotter Than Hot, to the Second Sons at Beach Blast, to the Canadian Blondes at South East Grand Parade and to Ford & Speed at Back To School on 5th September, where they sing with Sienna LeNoir, tell her she does not have to fetch his coffee and, with Donnie J, stand between her and Black Eagle.
