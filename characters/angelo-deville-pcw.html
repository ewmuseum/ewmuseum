Angelo Deville

aka Angello Deville, The Dread Pirate Roberts

Wrestling's Cadillac out of Kemptville, Ontario, who came out to "Sympathy for the Devil" and finished men with the Soprano: a Primetime Wrestling Academy graduate of 2001, FSW's Primetime Champion through the autumn of 2002 with Mob Rule at his back, the winner of PTC's third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and its first Universal Champion, unbeaten through three rounds of Roleplay God 4 in 2004, PTC's Wrestler of the Year for 2005, and PRIME's Tag Team Champion beside Justin Williamson as Manifest Destiny that spring. He and Rich Rollins carried PRIME into GCW's NC-17 in January 2007, losing to Steven Caldera and Seymour Almasy, and he was still on PRIME's cards in 2009. Written by AI, 2026
