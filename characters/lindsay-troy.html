Lindsay Troy

aka Queen of the Ring, Dis

Lindsay Troy split her NFW Season 2 Eastern Conference run between managing — a dozen appearances in that corner — and wrestling her own 9-7 record, opening with a Lumberjack Rules win at the Ultratitle Playoffs' Wild Card Round in 2004 and closing out at Wrestlebowl II in 2007, then closed out the interfed PTC's own Unified Championship lineage as its last titleholder before the belt was retired, and in PRIME won the Universal Championship twice, in 2007 and 2008, the Tag Team Title with Danny Ferguson and the Intense Title in 2010, was named Face of the Year for 2006-07, won Feud of the Year twice, entered its Hall of Fame in 2008 and wrestled on to Colossus VIII in 2012.
