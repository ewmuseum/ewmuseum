The Hart Foundation

Bret Hart and Owen Hart, the brothers who feud with the New Age Enterprise and Raven's Nest through February, March and April 2007: they guard each other against the Enterprise's numbers, with Owen banned from ringside for Bret's matches, and Owen is eliminated by Rhino near the end of the Baptism Of Fire match after Bret has been replaced in it by Paul Burchill. In April 2007 Raven fires Owen Hart as commissioner; Owen beats Kensuke Sasaki with a Sharpshooter in the semi-final of the Number One Contendership Tournament and loses the final to Rhino when the masked man's chair misses Raven and hits him, while Bret beats Goliath at Cyberslam for a title shot. In May the brothers fall out: after beating Paul London and Brian Kendrick with matching Sharpshooters Bret pulls Owen off a cover, and Paul Heyman makes their Hardcore Heaven match the number one contendership, which Owen wins with a Diving Headbutt; in June Heyman books Owen against Raven at Wrestlepalooza and Bret in a Triangle Match for the title. In June 2007 the brothers argue on Revolution over who will take the World Heavyweight Championship, Bret calling Owen's Hardcore Heaven win a fluke, and Bret beats Doug Williams with the Sharpshooter; at Wrestlepalooza they take turns with the Sharpshooter and the Ankle Lock in the Triangle Match with Raven, Owen makes Bret tap, and Raven then pins Owen with a Raven Effect onto a chair and keeps the belt. Bret does not appear again in this run; Owen beats Lance Storm with the Sharpshooter on Blood, Sweat and Beers after Summer Assylum, is mocked there by Chris Jericho, challenges Jericho for Heatwave, and in August beats Chris Hero with a Sharpshooter in the tag match with Jerry Lynn.
