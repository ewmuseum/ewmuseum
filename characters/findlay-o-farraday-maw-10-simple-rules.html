Findlay O'Farraday

A six-foot-eight, 350-pound monster who challenges Erik Strong in February and teams with Citizen X. At Beach Blast in July 2010 he accidentally boots his sometime partner Primus Allen while trying to save him from defeat, and Allen boots him in the head backstage and drops him; Jefferson Stardust and Kristen Pearce adopt him as the heavy lifter of Glamour And Devastation. At April Showers in April 2011 he wins for Glamour And Devastation with a Choke Sleeper on Max Mayhem, the narrator saying he is driven by a wish to impress Kristen. In June 2011 he asks for $500 a show, saying that he has been on a main show once in eight months and gets more reaction than Jefferson Stardust on tour, and the narrator refuses, offering $400; he leaves.
