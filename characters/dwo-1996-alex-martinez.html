UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-05-29 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 12 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 4-6-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Made his in-ring UWF debut teaming with Alex Extreme against Team Canada; ambushed by Tom Landis and Kari Stevens in the aisle before the bell and busted open with a chair, then legally pinned by Bob Jablonski after collapsing from exhaustion mid-comeback, never having tagged into the match. Beat Scott "Hotspot" Daniels by pinfall (a Firebomb Chokeslam) in a Gold Rush dream match on 5 June 2004, surviving a low blow, a brass-knuckles punch, two steel chair shots, and a Pridebreaker. Beat "Dark Horse" Randall Osbourne by pinfall (a Firebomb Chokeslam) on 25 August 2004 after surviving a Tree of Woe facebite; cut a backstage promo naming Osbourne's own past in a rival promotion beforehand. His "Singles War" against Michael Augustine was never brought under control and ended without a decision on 18 September 2004 after the Privateers and Alex Extreme turned the brawl into a four-man fight. Teamed with the Sons of Cacaphony to beat the Universal Powers and Randall Osbourne on 22 September 2004, days after a backstage tryout of Erik and Chad Grimsson ended in a three-way handshake; combined with Erik Grimsson for a version of the Harlequins' own Reality Check on Todd Franklin for the pin. Lost a Tower of Doom cage match on 30 October 2004 alongside Quinn Brown, Uriah Hawkins, and Alex Extreme to the team of Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, Papa Legba, and Luke Kinsey, inadvertently costing his own side the match when he hurled Edmond Winston IV clear of the top cage's own door, completing Winston's team's own escape; joined the others in a post-bell beatdown of Winston with his own solid gold knuckles. Teamed with "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme in an impromptu, no-contest challenge for the UWF World Tag Team Championship against the Privateers on 13 November 2004, hitting a Firebomb of his own on Jacob Drake before "Agony" Michael Augustine's interference ended the match. Challenged Kyle Lee for the UWF World Heavyweight Championship on 11 December 2004 and lost by pinfall (a surprise inside cradle) after his own left knee buckled mid-move, denying Luke Kinsey a title shot of his own at Rise to Power. Vowed on 11 June 2005 to stop brooding over a loss to Luke Kinsey, then lost by pinfall to "Violent" Victor Frost (a chair-assisted spike piledriver, feet on the ropes) after Roxy Mayhem's own early bell rang stole a Firebomb Chokeslam win from him; walked away from the crowd's calls for revenge with visible restraint. Drank with the Mercenary, Alex Extreme, and the Sons of Cacophony at a Memphis club on 22 June 2005. Won the Road to the Gold Battle Royal to become the number one contender to the World Heavyweight Championship, then shared a beer and a truce backstage with Chris O'Brien on 6 July 2005. Delivered a vengeful promo about Daniel Kidd's own pipe attack on his knee backstage to open the 23 July 2005 broadcast, then teamed with Brett Greene in the main event, saving him from a Pride beatdown mid-show before Scott Daniels turned on Greene late in their own tag match. Beat Daniel Kidd by disqualification on 3 August 2005 after Cornerstones interference, saved by a returning Youth Gone Wild; appeared on the Greene Room on 6 August 2005, promising Brett Greene a future title shot if he won the World Heavyweight Championship at Gold Rush. Opened Saturday Night Rampage on 18 March 2006 celebrating the World Heavyweight Championship alongside the Sons of Cacophony and Alex Extreme, promised Brett Greene the first shot at his title, then saw Becky Byers turn that into a four-way match the following week against Greene, Luke Kinsey, and Alex Extreme.
