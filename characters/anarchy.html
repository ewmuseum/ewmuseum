Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Joanna: Joanna managed Adien and then tag team partner Bahamut From the GWF to the now dead WWA. There they became World Tag team Champions till Bahamut decided it was time to retire. Adien, at a loss in singles went out in search foe his new, and current partner Reod Dai. Together she took them to the top again as the WWA World Tag Champions. She now is in search for a new fed that her team can dominate. What better fed than Anarchy. Joanna is about 5'6', wears biking shorts and tee shirt, and always has a Studded Leather Glove on her right hand. Adien Pryde Height: 5'10' Weight: 245lbs Entrance Music: 'Seek 200' by Information Society Wears: (Entrance) Black leather jacket with a green falcon carrying a sword on the back. (Ring) Black tights with ANARCHY in red down the left side. Singles Finisher: Falcon Bomb (Lifts his opponent up in the razors edge then flips them over the shoulder and catches their head and drives them down into a DDT.) Tag Team Finisher: Total Anarchy (Total Elimination, Pryde foot sweeps from the rear, Dai Spin Wheel kick from the front) Adien started his career in the GWF. Fresh out of collage, he found out the stark reality that not all wrestlers were honorable. After numerous back stabs, something in Adien snapped. Now a heel, Adien joined with Bahamut, a 7' giant and they were soon dominating the Tag Team scene and captured the Titles. Later, they joined the WWA after being lost in the GWF split. There they went to the top as Dragons Lair and won the WWA World Tag Team Titles. Several months after losing the titles, Bahamut retired. Joanna decided to get Adien a new partner. Enter Reod Dai, Pit fighter and amateur wrestler. Together they formed Anarchy, a team who didn't care who they wrestled, as long as they got what they wanted, any and all titles. Now he is her to rule the WWA again. Reod Dai Height: 6'2' Weight: 260lbs Entrance Music: 'Seek 200' by Information Society Wears: (Entrance) Long tattered leather trench, large wide brimmed hat, carrying a bo staff (Ring) Black tights with ANARCHY in red down the left side, and a red dragon on the right Singles Finisher: Dragon Strike(Blackmen's Lethal Kick to the face) Tag Team Finisher: Total Anarchy (Total Elimination, Pryde foot sweeps from the rear, Dai Spin Wheel kick from the front) Reod started in the small community of Morristown, MN. A population of 800 people was not his idea of a party place. He moved to Minneapolis at the age of 16 where he started to hang with a rough crowd. Here he learned the anything goes style of Pit Fighting. At age 17, Joanna entered his life. She liked what she saw and started to manage his career. For 2 years they traveled the country, winning match after match. Then it all ended on fateful night. Reod was slammed on his knee on the cement floor. His Knee was shattered, and so was his career. The next year, he was to spend in rehab, he told Joanna to continue her career. All through the year, Reod could thing of nothing but making a comeback. Joanna would keep him up to date on her life, including the new Tag Team she had in Pro Wrestling. Then one day, She came to him with an offer. One member of her team had retired. Joanna wanted Reod to fill his spot. He agreed on one condition. No other allies. From that moment on Anarchy was Born.
