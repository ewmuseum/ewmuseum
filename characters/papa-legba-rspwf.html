UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2004-12-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 18 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 10-4-1, 3 no contest
Tenure detail
A member of Sabbat Justice, accompanied by Tigress; his singles match against Cameron Black was ruled a no-contest after Serge Annis interfered. Returned on 22 October 2003 in a tag match against Randall Osbourne and Alex Extreme that ended without a decision after Osbourne turned on his own partner and the rest of Sabbat Justice joined a five-on-one beatdown. Reached the finals of the 17 December 2003 Meltdown Championship tournament as one of the battle royal's own final four, beat Victor Frost in the semi-finals with an inverted DDT, then beat Paul Wong in the finals with the Black Magic superbomb to become the first-ever UWF Meltdown Champion. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship over Alex Kidd on 10 March 2004 by pinfall (Ragga Bomb) with an assist from Tigress and Randall Osbourne. Ambushed Alex Extreme and Randall Osbourne with a chair before the bell of his own 21 March 2004 title defense, a scheduled three-way, then announced grieving over his recently-deceased father and postponed the match to a cage bout the following week without the bout ever starting. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship in the rescheduled steel cage match on 5 May 2004: Randall Osbourne never entered after Alex Kidd attacked him before the bell, and Legba climbed out of the cage to the floor ahead of Alex Extreme to win by escape. Retained the UWF Meltdown Championship again on 19 May 2004, teaming with Tigress to beat Simon O'Neal and Donna Tetreault in an intergender tag after a top-rope splash caught both opponents for the pin. Lost the UWF Meltdown Championship to Collision Holly by pinfall on 25 August 2004, caught with a second-rope bulldog after a long beating. Fought Uriah Hawkins to a double countout in a UWF Meltdown Championship tournament opener on 8 September 2004, both men brawling to the back past the referee's count. Lost a non-title three-way dance to Sabbath on 2 October 2004, pinned alongside Quinn Brown as Sabbath's Black Zero reverse hurricanrana caught him after Jessica Marshall goaded Sabbath into a rally. Escaped a Tower of Doom cage match on 30 October 2004 as part of the winning team alongside Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, and Luke Kinsey. Approached Chad Grimsson backstage on 27 November 2004 offering support after Lisa Drake's beating, framing himself as reformed from his own past with Sabbat Justice; the two agreed to a wary, tentative alliance. Beat Uriah Hawkins by pinfall (Roots, a fireman's carry slam counter) in a hard-fought grudge match on 11 December 2004, the two shaking hands afterward; signed a UWF North American Heavyweight Championship match against Victor Frost for Rise to Power beforehand.
