UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2004-08-25
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 6 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996), this run, placed by show date): 1-3-1, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Original member of the Dark Harlequins, billed as having held nine separate UWF championships; pinned Derek Irvin with the Sleepy Hollow Driver in a non-title singles match on 9 August 2003 and announced designs on the North American Heavyweight Championship. Challenged for that title against champion Eugene Robinson on 30 August 2003, fought to a draw after Derek Irvin's interference forced a double stoppage. Teamed with Robinson and Irvin in a losing six-man tag on 4 October 2003, disqualified when Irvin struck him with a steel chair; knocked Irvin back down with the Sleepy Hollow Driver after the bell. Lost to Victor Frost by pinfall on 12 November 2003. Entered the 17 December 2003 battle royal, eliminated together with Alex Kidd when Avanter snapmared Kidd into him. Returned on 29 May 2004 to challenge Uriah Hawkins for the UWF Rampage Championship, the match ruled a no-contest after Pierce Bleed attacked the referee while Tragedy still had the Tragic Ending locked on; told a reporter afterward that he needed no help from the promotion's ownership to win championships. Lost to "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme by pinfall on 25 August 2004 after an implant DDT and a springboard finisher; his wife, Comedy, accompanied him, and Rodney distracted him mid-match by slapping Comedy and fleeing.
