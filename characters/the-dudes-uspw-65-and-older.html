The dudes

aka The Best Tag Team Like Ever, TBTTLE

The pairing of Trent Shaffer and his partner Chance Fortune, whom the narrator has not yet named: billed only as the dudes, they challenge The Dudez!!!1! for the World Tag Team Championship in July and August 2007 and lose both matches. As The Best Tag Team Like Ever, or TBTTLE, they lose again to The Valentines at A Haunting in America in October 2007, and Shaffer and Chance Fortune challenge The Dudez!!!1! to a match at Pilgrimage. In October and November 2007 they trade insults with The Dudez!!!1! in a pick-your-poison series, beat them in a tag warm-up on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 2, and lose the I Quit Match at Pilgrimage when Trent Shaffer says he quits under Frankie Perez's P-Clutch.
