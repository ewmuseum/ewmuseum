King Krusher

aka KK, K.K.

GLCW's commissioner, introduced at First Wave in Detroit on 4 August 2002, who counted the Chi-Town Showdown I heavyweight final differently from its special referee, Maelstrom, locked Michael Manson in a backstage room at Kalamazoo Riptide and was handed the Tag Team Titles by the retiring Unholy Alliance at Columbus Riptide on 9 July 2003.
