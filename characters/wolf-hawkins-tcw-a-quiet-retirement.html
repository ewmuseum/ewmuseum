Wolf Hawkins

Tommy Cornell's protege and International Champion, trained by Cornell and a member of the Syndicate, who keeps the belt against Koshiro Ino at Where Angels Fear To Tread with Sam Keith's help and loses it to him at Total Mayhem 13, then loses to Sam Keith on Total Wrestling in August 2008 and goes to Hotter Than Hell as a challenger for the World Heavyweight Championship, which he fails to win, and in September 2008 beats John Anderson by count out without winning the International Championship, is hit with a chair by Stevie Grayson, teams with Tommy Cornell on Total Wrestling and joins Sammy Bach to beat the Disrespected by disqualification at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 loses with Tommy Cornell to Eddie Peak and Troy Tornado, wins the Hangtime match, is unsettled by Sam Keith's warning and, with Sammy Bach, beats the Disrespected at Crunch Time. In November 2008 he refuses to take his match for Sam Keith's title, saying he owes Sammy Bach a favour, catches Texas Pete from behind in John Anderson's title defence and announces that the International Championship is 'coming back home', beats the Keith Brothers and the Balcony Express with Tommy Cornell, loses to Rick Law and, at A Little South Of Sanity, is pinned by John Anderson in the three-way International Championship match. In December 2008 he beats Aaron Andrews with two Full Moon Risings after faking a knee injury, hits American Buffalo with a Full Moon Rising to help John Anderson keep the International Championship, draws with Anderson at the time limit, beats Anderson and Rick Law with Tyson Baine, quarrels with Tommy Cornell over Aaron Andrews and at Psycho Circus helps Cornell beat Andrews with a chair before losing to Anderson for the International Championship.
