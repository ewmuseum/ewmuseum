Hornet

aka the Greatest American Hero, the One and Only

Hornet arrived on NFW's Crash TV already a CSWA World, United States and World Six-Man Champion, an AAWC Universal and Unified Champion, and a three-time PRODIGY Unified World Champion, and went an even 7-7 there between 2003 and 2009, winning a Bunkhouse Rules Bring Your Own Bag match at Crash TV 16.
