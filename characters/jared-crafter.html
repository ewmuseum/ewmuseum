Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Hailing from Death Valley, this Gothic Warrior has struck fear into the hearts of all his opponents. Usually alongside other members of his "Forces of Darkness," Crafter has run roughshod over his competition, successfully winning every major title in every Federation he had been in before arriving in the GIWF. On his arrival, Crafter quickly established himself, leading his Forces of Darkness into battle against Maximum Damage. With his partner Riggs, Crafter soon took the World Tag Team titles from the RPA. The pair would continue their winning ways until Stable Wars 2001. The pair would unite with Mike Montgomery and a fourth, unknown individual and despite a strong showing from Montgomery and the FoD, it was the fourth man who would cost the threesome the victory. The team fell into obscurity after that, until their recent return to the UWF.
