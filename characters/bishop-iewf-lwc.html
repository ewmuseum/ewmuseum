Bishop

MLWO (1996)

1998-01-17 – 1998-03-28
Tenure detail
The Avatar of Extreme, introduced by the MLWO as 'He is a former IEWF World Champion, a LWC Silver City Champion' and, on 28 March 1998, as 'He is a former IEWF World Champion, the current LWC Silver City Champion'. Beat Luke Steele in a falls count anywhere match (17 January 1998) and Alex Reaver (24 January 1998); won the United States Title from Victor Vance and kept it against the Outcast on 28 March 1998.
Source: MLWO Saturday Slam #68; MLWO Saturday Slam #69; MLWO Saturday Slam #75
