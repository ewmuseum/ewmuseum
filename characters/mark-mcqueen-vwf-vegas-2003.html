VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-05-17 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 20 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 12-7-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Teams with Marcus O'Malley for the Society of Doom against Blaze and The Real Deal on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminates Orion in a mutual elimination in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Barred from competing in the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round by majority owner Kingston Cash over an unspecified contractual problem. Returns to box with Orion during the sixteen-man tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), credited alongside Marcus O'Malley as a winner when O'Malley unhooks the ring. Walks off during the Dog Days Four Tag Team Scramble on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), abandoning partner Austin Lee, then is busted open by The Reckoning and eliminated from the tag Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Won the ten-man tag for the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1, challenged for the VWF Tag Team Championship alongside Marcus O'Malley on Wrestle Style V.2 (won by disqualification, titles retained), then lost to a returning wrestler billed "Orion #2" by count-out in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Loses to Sebastian by rollup in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004). Wins with Marcus O'Malley over The Mighty Men by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) after being isolated and injured; is shown kidnapped on a video screen the prior week, forcing O'Malley from a Heavyweight title fatal four-way. Submits Real Deal with the Devils Snare to win the VWF Tag Team Championship with Marcus O'Malley from Blaze and Real Deal at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), billed Alcoholics Anonymous; the reinstated Rodeo Boys beat down both new champions immediately after. Survives the branding-iron chaos that costs Marcus O'Malley the use of his own arm, defending the VWF Tag Team Championship on March To Hell (21 October 2004) despite the Rodeo Boys' own interference. Successfully defends the titles again alongside a substitute Outlaw Morris Bass on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), submitting Billy Bones with the Devils Snare while an injured O'Malley cheers from his hospital bed. Gives a sullen, one-word prediction ("Pain") to Lean Darlene Orion and successfully defends his own half of the VWF Tag Team Championship over Robbie Caldwell in the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), retaining alongside new co-champion Kevin Simmons after O'Malley is pinned. Defends his half of the VWF Tag Team Championship in a straight singles match against The Reckoning on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) after The Salvation publicly refuses Kingston Cash's proposed handicap-match stipulation on The Mighty Men's own behalf, submitting The Reckoning with the Devils Snare. Retains automatically when mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov wins the battle royal for co-champion Kevin Simmons's own suspended half of the title on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004). Teams with Valenkov and 24 Hour Champion Adam Jennings to beat a short-handed Riders of the Apocalypse on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), not wrestling himself as Jennings pins Robbie Caldwell. Teams with Thunder Valenkov in a non-title tag against Fast Eddie Cutlass and G-Gangsta on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), ruled a no contest after the Riders of the Apocalypse run in on former member G-Gangsta; runs down with Valenkov afterward to chase off Robbie Caldwell and Billy Bones from the VWF 24 Hour Championship match. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Kagedo's own dropkick. Teams with Thunder Valenkov in a Rings Of Glory match against the Rodeo Boys at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), tapping out to Kenny Kidd's own Elko Lasso and eliminated from his own ring, but the VWF Tag Team Championship is retained regardless when Valenkov, already through Billy Bones, finishes Kidd alone with the Thunder Bomb. Challenges Sidious The Clown for the VWF Submission Championship at March To Madness (2 March 2005), caught in a Sleeper after an unexplained arena blackout and pinned. Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship with Thunder Valenkov at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) in an impromptu three-way after Team Brittania's own Simon Davidson pins Frank O'Shea instead of either champion. Challenges the champions Team Brittania for the titles at End Of March (30 March 2005) with Valenkov, winning by disqualification after the returning Hot Property run in on him. Billed alongside Thunder Valenkov as the VWF's own disputed tag team champions on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), pinned by Stevie Knight after Valenkov turns on him with a Thunder Bomb; does not appear again on the broadcast.
