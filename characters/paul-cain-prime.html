Tenure detail
His PRIME card tells of an earlier, short run: he came to rally PRIME's roster against Vampir's Dark Age, and it ended with Vampir "sacrificing Paul Cain at ReV 48 by carving the sign of the Pentagram on the 'Lethal Cocktail' chest", before he returned in 2005. The federation's own show archive dates ReVolution 48 to 24 November 2004, but the surviving review of ReVolution 49, a week later on 1 December 2004, stages the carving that night: after he and Black Angel lose a tornado tag steel cage match to Shadow and Ozric Mortimer, Dani Furher takes a lead pipe to his knee and Vampir cuts the pentagram into his chest with the knife the Dark Age had used the week before on Boda and Emilio Rage; the review of ReVolution 50 has Boda recall the Dark Age doing the same to Black Angel and Paul Cain. UltraViolence 2005's video package shows "Vampir, covering Paul Cain for the pin." The title history puts him in the five-way Alias Title match John Edwards won at Colossus II on 24 July 2005, a card not in the surviving show record; Edwards' own roleplay of 16 August 2005 recalls his Singapore cane there. ReVolution 75, on 14 September 2005, brings him back on screen beside Damien Kahn, having "shed the dark and depressive image". The show record carries him on sixteen cards from ReVolution 76 on 21 September 2005 to the Dual Halo finale at Culture Shock day two on 11 March 2007: a run to the 2005 Jewel in the Crown quarter-final, lost to Karina Wolfenden at ReVolution 79, and from October 2006 a return his card calls a "ticking timebomb".
