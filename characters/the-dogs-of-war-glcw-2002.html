The Dogs of War

"The Wolf" Chris McMillan and Jason Payne, old partners from the Basham-Schultz Wrestling Academy, who beat the Oak Street Beach Patrol and Martial Law in the spring, won the Ringlords I four-way on 9 June 2003, went to a double countout with the Krymson Dragons at Columbus Riptide and lost the vacant titles to The Movement at Wisconsin Dells.
