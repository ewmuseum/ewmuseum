UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-30 – 2004-11-03

Tenure detail
Billed from Kansas City, Missouri; lost a non-title singles match to Vyolynce of Sabbat Justice by referee stoppage after Vyolynce needled him over his girlfriend, Tawni Northern. Teamed with Alex Extreme against Papa Legba and Avanter on 22 October 2003 in a match that ended without a decision after Osbourne turned on his own partner mid-match and left, and Sabbat Justice beat Extreme down five-on-one. Attacked a returning Alex Kidd from behind backstage without provocation on 12 November 2003, then challenged Kyle Lee for the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship the same night, disqualified for repeatedly biting the champion's forehead. Attacked a returning Alex Kidd from behind backstage without explanation for a second consecutive show on 20 December 2003, sending Kidd to a local hospital. Teamed with Nina Grimsson later that night in a losing intergender tag match against Avanter and Angel, driving Grimsson himself down repeatedly with a full-nelson suplex and a full-nelson backbreaker until she began coughing up blood and the referee stopped the match. Threatened Alicia Kidd at ringside during Alex Kidd's own Meltdown Championship challenge on 10 March 2004, distracting Kidd long enough for champion Papa Legba to score the pinfall. Entered a scheduled three-way for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 21 March 2004 that never started after Legba attacked both challengers with a chair before the bell and left; a cage-match rematch was announced for the following week. Lost to Alex Kidd by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004 in a personal grudge match, caught with a full nelson bulldog after a Tree of Woe facebite and two Somersault Bulldogs; manager Cameo, Kidd's own ex-wife, was knocked unconscious by the first Somersault Bulldog. Lost to "Last American Badass" Alex Martinez by pinfall (a Firebomb Chokeslam) on 25 August 2004 despite a Tree of Woe facebite; manager Cameo's own distraction went for nothing. Lost a UWF Meltdown Championship tournament opener to Paul Wong by pinfall (a lariat) on 8 September 2004, seconded by Cameo. Lost alongside the Universal Powers (Bobby Lincoln, Todd Franklin) to the Sons of Cacaphony and Alex Martinez on 22 September 2004, seconded again by Cameo. Lost to Luke Kinsey by pinfall (the Lowlife Driver, an inverted DDT) on 3 November 2004 after nearly pulling Kinsey from the ring, seconded at ringside by Cameo.
