Jean Cattley

aka "Machine" Jean Cattley, Machine Jean Cattley

The company's veteran, leader of The Firm, who beats and ambushes anyone who resists it, finishes with the Mood Swing and wins his second MAW Heavyweight Championship from Erik Strong in October 2008. He fights Steven Parker to a time-limit draw at Fall Of The King in September 2009 and loses the Heavyweight title to him in an Iron Man match a month later; with Darryl Devine and Ryan Powell he wins the first MAW Trios titles for The Firm at March Madness in March 2010. The Firm loses the Trios titles at Hotter Than Hot in July 2010, and at Summernova he tells Casey Valentine that only he speaks for the group. He beats Kirk Jameson at Fall Of The King and the Masked Patriot at Back To School, and at Where It All Begins Again in October beats Valentine for control of The Firm, after which Primus Allen and Jared Johnson leave and he expels Valentine. He beats Joey Poison in a qualifier at Remember November in November 2010 and teams with Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh against Poison and the Canadian Blondes at End Of Days, then wins the Tag Team titles with Singh at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011. He loses a semi-final to Hugh de Aske at the Invitational, keeps the belts against Antonio & Raphael, the Blondes and Thrash and Firebird, beats Flash Savage at March Madness, and in April 2011 tells Rip Chord and the narrator that, at 33, he is taking a deal with the SWF. At Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 he and Singh lose the Tag Team titles to the Canadian Blondes, he refuses Casey Valentine's offer of an alliance and loses a Loser Leaves Town match to him after a Sweet Sweet Heartbreak, and after a celebration with Chance Fortune and Randy Bumfhole he says goodbye to the narrator in the car park after six years, handing Steve Flash the legacy of The Firm. The SWF calls him up in May with plans to revive his Mean Machine mask gimmick; by July he has lost to Kurt Laramee and been on the losing side of a four corners match. Written by AI, 2026
