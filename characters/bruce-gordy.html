Biography (UWF (2001–07))

1 Time (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship Bruce Gordy is a vey sadistic and extreme man. He is a hardcore specialist. He is dressed as a hardcore butcher but he is much more than just a wrestling gimmick. He is called the Innovator of Pain because he craves the feeling of inflicting pain on others and even himself. He is also called the Sole Survivor of the Elements of Destruction. The EoD was the greatest stable ever assembled. It started in GIWF with The Plague, TNT (Tommy N. Taylor), and Killer Dude. Then it grew a little and Bruce Gordy joined and later came Digital Mortality and Toxic Shock. But the first version was with Plague, TNT, Killer Dude, and Bruce Gordy. Gordy was a top contender in the GIWF for the THC and Television championships although he never suceeded in winning them. He feuded against Mike Montgomery and fought him in the famous cage match at the 3 STrikes and You're Out PPV. He infamously powerbombed Montgomery off the top of the cage. The EoD would continue to live on in different reincarnations like with Ian Thorn and Corrupt Judgment, but then Bruce Gordy returned and entered the UWF after a few years of silence. Bruce Gordy restarted the EoD with Killer Dude and Plague and Kevin Starr joined. He tried to get Jamie Shock formely known as Toxic Shock but he refused. Bruce Gordy feuded with Mike Montgomery affter Montgomery refused his friendship offer. It was nominated for feud of the year but the awards hve not been released yet so we'll just have to see if we won. The EoD got to the finals of Stable Wars 5 but were beaten evenutally by the worst stable ever with DJ Cool, Montgomery, and Wimp and Chimp Connection. Bruce Gordy would disappear eventually after the defeat but came back and won his life long goal of a championship when he won the THC title from Beelzebozo. Eventually Ben Jericho beat Gordy for the title, but now Bruce Gordy is still out there ready to inflict pain and win back his THC belt.
