Becky Byers

The UWF's own on-screen Commissioner, wielding just enough authority to unsettle even Marshall Law's own loyalists. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-12-01 – 2006-03-18
Tenure detail
Congratulated Tigress backstage on drawing Scott "Hotspot" Daniels for the Meltdown Championship on 1 December 2004. On 11 December 2004, confiscated the Youngbloods' own ownership shares from Edward Drake, staking their return on the Privateers' own tag title defense and Michael Augustine's own match against Alex Extreme; assigned Lisa Drake as Alex Extreme's manager against Allison Ivey's own objection; and announced a UWF World Heavyweight Championship shot for Luke Kinsey against "The Last American Badass" Alex Martinez, contingent on Martinez beating Kyle Lee that same night, over Jessica Marshall's own furious protest. Denied "Violent" Victor Frost another title shot on 28 May 2005 over his own "Graphic Violence" tour conduct, booked Chris O'Brien into a handicap match against the Hands of Death as punishment for an unprovoked chair attack, and announced a sixteen-man Cruiserweight Invitational tournament with a future title shot and a fifty-thousand-dollar prize. Announced after the Meltdown Championship's own main event on 8 June 2005 that it had been the title's own last defense, with Tigress set to face Rampage Champion Marcus Nuit the following week in a match to crown a Unified Television Champion; unveiled the sixteen-man Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament's own brackets on 11 June 2005, confirmed the unification match and new title belt, announced Kyle Lee's own indefinite absence with a chronic back injury, and set a forty-man Road to the Gold Battle Royal for the following week to crown a new number-one contender for the World Heavyweight Championship. Booked John DeWolfe into a three-on-two handicap match against Lisa Drake, Alicia Powers, and "Tornado" Tara Smith on 6 July 2005 over his own role in a Meltdown brawl, needling him over stolen office supplies and a hit-and-run on her own car. Told Alex Extreme backstage on 20 July 2005, alongside Jessica Marshall, that the board of directors wanted him in a high-profile match against Jason Keening with his career on the line; separately set a Blind Stipulation Match between Donna Tetreault and Stephanie Harper for Gold Rush. Signed Samantha Daniels, Scott Daniels's own daughter, to a UWF contract backstage on 23 July 2005, after Daniels fired the Pride-appointed lawyer sent to negotiate for her. Interrupted Scott Daniels's own Brawl From The Mall 6 promo on 6 August 2005 to ban the Pride, then every wrestler, from ringside at Gold Rush and to announce a special guest referee for the main event. Denied Victor Frost a title shot and booked him for a match the following week on 18 March 2006, settled a rematch-clause dispute with John DeWolfe over a Lisa Drake grudge match by approving a Women's number-one-contender battle royal in exchange, and set a four-way World Heavyweight Championship main event for the following week between Alex Martinez, Luke Kinsey, Brett Greene, and Alex Extreme after denying both Kinsey and Scott Daniels an immediate shot.
