Tenure detail
The join is not made on the name. Two independent primary sources meet in the same weeks. The NCCW's own vice-president lists Death and Destruction among the names that have wrestled in NCCW on 20 December 1998. The team's own handler first adds NCCW to the team's billing on 15 December 1998 (message 0cPTj_SUEdcJ), keeps it through 19 February 1999, and drops it by March 1999. The other NCCW (nccw-utm-1998, at utm.edu) had closed in April 1998, so only this wing fits the dates. The 1998 Tag Team 200 tags the team IWF/WOW only, and no NCCW card or title history held here names the team. The years run from the fed's opening year to the last billing.
Source: The fed's own recruitment post of 20 December 1998 ([NCCW] At least give it a read... — NCCW founding/recruitment ad) names Death and Destruction among those who have seen action in NCCW; the team's own handler bills 'Death & Destruction aka Perry Neptune & John Achillies' as 'IWF/WOW,NCCW' in his sign-off (GDWA Changes Name, 19 February 1999, 19 February 1999).
