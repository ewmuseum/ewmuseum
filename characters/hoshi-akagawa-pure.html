Biography (PURE (2002))

The following is my recollection of my life up to this point in time. But be warned. In this recollection of mine, there may be events of a sexual nature. Nothing of an extremely gross nature I do not think but an event or two which could be viewed as milady disturbing. If it is felt that the events which I speak of are too disturbing, just fast forward past that particular event or events. I will give proper warning of these so-called ‘disturbing events’ before I tell of them. With that said, let’s begin shall we? The beginning sounds like a good place to start. My life began on a day like today. October Thirteenth, 1981. Nothing special about the date in which I came into this world, although I was born eighteen days prematurely. I was destined to be born on Halloween, but was born on the unluckiest day of the month of October instead. The place of my birth is somewhat significant, though. I was born in Honolulu, Hawaii. When most people think about Hawaii they think of three things. Volcanoes, hot looking babes, and surfing. However, there is more to Hawaii that meets the eye, as I have found out later in my life. I was born with blonde hair and brown eyes.To move on, the first images that I can remember seeing are those of my parents, Alana and Koi Kalani. When I last saw them, they both had dark black hair, with my father's being short and my mom's being long, and brown eyes. To my knowledge, my parents were and always have been very devout Catholics. I’ll talk more about the crazy relationship between Catholicism and myself a little bit later. My parents gave me the name of Malia, which means “the perfect one” in Hawaiian. I happened to be the first and only child in my family, which is a good thing for me because I did not have to fight any brothers or sisters for the attention of my parents. As a child, I did several acting gigs. I was in several commercials and a movie or two. Unfortunately, my acting jobs kind of dried out once I hit puberty. Because of my parents’ religious ties, my family would go to mass every week at the local Catholic Church in Honolulu. Though it was kind of boring at first, but I quickly became intrigued with stories of Noah, Moses, David, and Jesus. I cannot really remember anything else important in my life until the age of nine. And now I should probably stop and take a moment to say that this is where the disturbing set of events begin. Skip past the next couple of portions if you do not wish to know about what is perhaps the most disturbing set of events that I have lived through in my short life. Now then. This is something I will never say again because it is so embarrassing for me to reveal it just once that I will never do it twice. Starting at around the time in which I was nine years old, one of the priests at the Catholic Church that I went to began to sexually abused me. I really cannot go into graphic detail because it is just too much for me to reveal. I refuse to say who the priest is because I was told by him that if I ever told that he would kill myself and the rest of my family. Unfortunately, there was no way I could stop him from abusing me sexually. This kept going on until I turned seventeen. Those are the disturbing set of events which I went though. At seventeen, I became pregnant. Of course, I never told my parents who really had gotten me pregnant. I told them that it was my boyfriend at the time who got me pregnant. They never asked any more questions about the father of the child and the boyfriend just went on with his life. Anyway, the baby was born on the February Twenty-Second, 1999. When he was born I gave him the name of Keoki, which means “George” in Hawaiian. He looked just like his father the first time I saw him and still does. I absolutely hate his father for what he did to me. However, don’t hate my son one bit for the actions of his father. I always have and will continue to love my son no matter what he or his father does or has done, whether it be to me or anyone else. Sure, he looks just like his father but I love him nonetheless. Soon after he was born, I began to take an active interest in wrestling professionally. I had enjoyed watching it as a girl. I had stopped watching it for several years because I basically grew out of it for awhile plus I was too busy with acting. But after Keoki was born, I began to watch it again and my interest in wrestling was rekindled. Once I turned twenty one, I decided to leave Hawaii for good and take Keoki with me. We have not returned since then. There just was not anything there for me anymore. Plus, I kind of felt as if I should get as far away from my terrible past as I possibly could. So I got on a flight to Tokyo, Japan of all places. It seemed like a mecca of wrestling to me and a great place to start out in the occupation. It has produced many of professional wrestling’s most talented wrestlers over the years. Since moving to Tokyo, I have tried to put my disturbing past behind me. I legally changed the names of myself and my son. We are no longer Malia and Keoki Kalani. We are now Hoshi and Renzo Akagawa. Once in Tokyo, I found a place to live and began training long and hard in both wrestling and martial arts. I’ve also tried to get back into acting. In any case, that’s basically where I currently stand as of today, December Twenty-Second, 2002. Where will I go and what will I do in the future? Only time will tell...
