Frankie Perez

The other half of the Dudez, who falls out with Trent Shaffer and fights him in March. In October and November 2007 he beats Trent Shaffer by disqualification, loses to Human Arsenal, is unmasked as one of the fake Masked Men, and beats Shaffer in the I Quit Match at Pilgrimage. In late November and December 2007 he and Mainstream Hernandez are framed by Seduction for the pigeon's death, lose to Wanda Fish and Phlex, beat Reaper in a dark match and win by disqualification over Jim Force.
