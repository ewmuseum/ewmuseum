Samurai

Billed with Kamakaze as the NeWA’s own reigning Tag Team Champions when the pair walked down the aisle at the first Summit of E-Wrestling in July 1991, he beat Centurion in his federation’s own first week that September, teamed with Bushi as the Imperial Storm, and was ranked that week’s own third Intercontinental contender.
