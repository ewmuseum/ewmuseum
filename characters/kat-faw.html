Tenure detail
Chris Thunder's own new manager/love interest, introduced to British Bomber for the first time backstage -- explicitly a different person from Kathryn Velmont-Thomas, Bomber's own manager, despite the similar name. Revealed to be living with Chris Thunder when Vincent (billed Spider) and Rick track her down at a New York apartment: she is Vincent's own long-lost sister, the Katrina Beck referenced the previous week. Spider immediately challenges Thunder to a hardcore match for Thunder's FAW International Championship at Deceit, with Kat/Katrina herself as an additional stake. This show's own text calls her "Kathryn" in the reveal dialogue and titles the following match "Spider vs Kathryn Velmont-Thomas" -- read here as the show's own name-confusion with the separately, continuingly-established Kathryn Velmont-Thomas (the xCw Hardcore Champion, still active elsewhere this same show's own commentary), not a merge of the two women; preserved as the primary source's own inconsistency rather than silently resolved. Breaks up with Chris Thunder backstage, then is seen with him again reconciled at the Crimson Lord/Lisa engagement party -- where she and Kathryn Velmont-Thomas both appear together in the same scene as visibly separate people, confirming the previous week's "Kathryn Velmont-Thomas" name in the match title against her was the show's own naming mix-up rather than an actual merge of the two characters. Attacked by her own brother Vincent mid-match at Deceit and installed afterward as his manager, per the match's own pre-set stipulation. Tells Vincent it's over between them as siblings after Deceit, comforted by Skipster; Crimson Lord intervenes and then piledrives both her and Vincent.
