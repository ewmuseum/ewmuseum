The lights cut out and strobes kick in as Yagyuu runs to the ring and slides in.. History: Born in Kobe, Japan Shin Yagyuu was trained from age five to be a machine of destruction. At 15 his father through him out of their home after Shin had stolen a loaf of bread for lunch. His father claimed Shin a disgrace to the legendary Yagyuu name. Next calling him Jubei reincarnated his father cut his own sons eye out. Shin left his home and family finding himself in multiple places over the next five years of his life. At twenty he fell in with the Yakuza and became their killing machine. During a job following his 21st birthday Shin was left behind during an assassination.
