Tenure detail
Debuted as a mystery opponent for returning "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict on 25 May 2005, winning by pinfall (a rolling Diamond Dust ace crusher) after selling a faked reconciliation hug into a suplex reversal; attacked by Benedict after the bell and busted open. Fled bloodied into the Love Sisters' own locker room on 11 June 2005 begging for help from "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict, then revealed the injury as a trick, ambushing both Love sisters alongside Benedict at the order of a man addressed as Caine. Kept Lolita Love's own kidnapped cat, Mr. Boo Boo, in a hotel room with "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict on 6 July 2005, plotting against the Love Sisters and a returning Naomi Ishikawa at Caine Tainer's own direction, then lost alongside Benedict to the Misfits (Dalbello Rage, Marissa Monet) in the Kindred's own debut tag match. Lured Lolita Love into a locker-room bathroom on 20 July 2005 and attacked her, revealing her own birth name as Akiko Masai and describing her own history with Caine Tainer and with Naomi Ishikawa, who she said had once shown her a different path; threatened Lolita with a shard of broken mirror alongside Myra Benedict and Caine Tainer. Appeared with Myra Benedict in taped footage torturing a captive Lolita Love on 3 August 2005; fought Sonya Benedict and Leanna Love to a no-contest in a handicap six-woman tag on 6 August 2005, then was knocked out with brass knuckles and dragged off by Leanna Love, who was talked out of cutting her with a katana by Lolita -- only for Lolita herself to take up the blade. Teamed with Sabbath to win the opening round of the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, struck by manager Caine Tainer backstage beforehand after trying to send him away. Teamed with "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict as the Kindred in a losing effort against "Firebird" Katie Smith and "Tornado" Tara Smith on 7 June 2006, pinned by Katie Smith's own Michinoku Driver.
