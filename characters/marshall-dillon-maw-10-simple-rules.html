Marshall Dillon

Half of the West Texas Peacekeepers, a tough cowboy from Dallas signed in June 2011 after a developmental deal with RIPW that did not work out (5.5/10). He is the victim of the Canadian Blondes' O, Canada! at Summer Showdown on 26th June, is pinned by Ford & Speed's Fallen Chances at Iron and wins the 4th July and 18th July Ontario tour matches with his partner. In the diary's pages from June to August 2012 he is crushed by Lenny Brown's The Star Treatment at High Noon on 5th June, loses a Tag Team title challenge to the Dark Raptors at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, and is pinned by Ford at Summernova on 5th August with Fallen Chances.
