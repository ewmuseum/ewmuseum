The Rhombus

A veteran Las Vegas tag team, VWF Tag Team Champions as far back as 2003 across a title lineage that also passed briefly through Randall Crane and Classy Joey Boy before ending for good at Vegas Violence III, when the returning stable The Circle cost them the belts against the Rodeo Boys; disqualified against the newly formed Dudes of Destruction on Xtreme Prejudice VI after a broken surfboard drew a referee's ire. VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2004-11-19
Tenure detail
Loses a six-man tag, all three men billed together as The Rhombus, to The Mighty Men and a returning Paddy O'Shea at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), Evil Capsman submitting to O'Shea's own Chicken Wing. Capsman and Evil Capsman fight G-Gangsta and Sebastian to a no contest on March To Hell (21 October 2004), broken up by Sonicman and The Maquilladora. Wins over The Mighty Men and a returning Paddy O'Shea on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), Capsman pinning O'Shea with the Capitol Punishment. Billed The Rhombus with a fourth man, The Maquilladora, in an eight-man elimination loss to the Riders of the Apocalypse on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), Sebastian pinning The Maquilladora after Robbie Caldwell's own outside tazer shot.
