Muffin

aka Alicia Strong

Sam Strong's daughter, hired in March 2007 against the narrator's own note that the company is male only. She asks for a mask and a new name, gets Muffin because old people like muffins, and becomes Darryl Devine's ally against Umaga, who beats her in a World Championship match in April. In October 2007 she turns with her father on Triple D, pins Big Cat Brandon at A Haunting in America with the Angel Driver, and is found out cold in a pool of blood backstage on the Thursday of Week 3. In October and November 2007 she draws with Eddie Peak and is thrown off the stage by Big Cat Brandon, appears tied to a chair in the Masked Man's video, and loses with her father to Big Cat Brandon and Peak at Pilgrimage, after which she sees him fired and swears to take the World Championship from Devine. In late November and December 2007 she beats Joey Minnesota in 21:53 (A), is saved by the Masked Man and then by Rick Law from Devine, beats Chance Fortune and teams with Law to beat The Best Tag Team Like Ever.
