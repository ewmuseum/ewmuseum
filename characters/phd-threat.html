Biography (UWF (2001–07))

 A Mind is a Terrible Thing to Waste  or  Youll Get Nowhere in Life, without a Proper Education  Sound familiar? Yeah, your parents probably told you this a million times while growing up. Well it was no different for Paul Dawson. No matter what path you find yourself taking, on your road to maturity. There are a few things that you can be sure of. It will take a lot of hard work and you need the proper education to get there. Whether you would like to become a Doctor, a Lawyer, etc. It takes if more work with your education to become an educator. You must keep everything on a professional level, yet a personal level to show your ethics in the educational system. Paul Dawson, son of Robert and Stacy Dawson. Younger Brother to Michael and older brother to Samantha Dawson. Born on February 23rd of the year 1974 at approximately 7:07 at night in a small town called Reading, Pennsylvania. Paul never really was too popular with his school mates, could it be from the middle child trauma. His older brother Michael never really cared to play with him. At the age of four Samantha was born, maybe that is why Paul was so pleased that he was going to have a little sister. Somebody he could finally play with. But to no avail, everything that little Paul wanted to do with Samantha, she was just too little to play. So nothing changed in hand for Paul. At five years of age, Paul finally went off for his first day of schooling. His mother took him to school by her car and took him up to his room. Little Paul could not have been more excited about it. Finally Paul would have kids his own age that he could play with and call his friends. Paul made it to his classroom and kissed his mom on the cheek and wished her good bye. He bolted right into the classroom eager to meet the other childeren. The First child he came to just looked at Paul without saying a word. Paul was a little discouraged at first. He then saw a group of kids as he then decided to walk over and talk to the kids. When he reached the crowd all the kids looked over at him, nervous as he was Paul made a mistake and said something stupid. All the kids looked at him and began to laugh. Nothing changed as the years went by, they got worse. Kids then began to make fun of Paul in class, throw pieces of paper among other things at him. Nothing he did could change the way the other kids felt about him. He got a part time job and bought fancy clothes, jewlery, and when he was old enough bought a nice looking car. But it still did nothing for him. So instead of trying to win a popularity contest he decided to focus more on his studies. Paul did his parents well, becoming an honor student. Things started to get better for Paul, showing his intelligence he made a few friends along the way. Just some of the smart quiet kids in school, but they were still friends. Things began to go down hill once again, when Paul was totally embarassed by one of the boys in his class in front of Debra Honer the girl of Paul's dream. Paul plain and simply just lost it and he attacked the boy. Not just with his fists but various objects around the room. The school's security had to come in and break everything up and take Paul away. With the actions of Paul, everything was taken away from him. As he was expelled from his school and his family moved away from their quiet little hometown. Paul leaving behind his friends, the girl of his dreams, his town.. his life. They moved out to Boston, Massachusetts. But Paul did not take this as an advantage to make a new. Become one of the popular guys. He just went to school and then came home and studied. The only other thing he would catch himself doing was dreaming off about his friends and Debra back at home. Where his personal level sunk to an all time low his educational level grew to a maximum. Paul finally graduated from high school, not even bothering to attend the graduation ceremony. Only picking up his diploma after the last day of school. But on a bright side of his schooling, Paul recieved a full scholarship to Yale University. Out of all the careers that he could of chosen getting a grade 'A' education from Yale, he decide to become a teacher. But why would Paul choose such a profession? Could it be because he wanted pay back from his horrible schooling life? Or could it be for the love of education, the thing that got him through all those years? None the less, Paul finally got his degree. He then began work being a professor for the University of Boston. Paul taught there for a good couple of years, becoming a highly regarded teacher to both the student and facilty. His classes were quickly filled up come class registration each year, being very difficult to get in his classes. Paul was finally back on his feet, it seemed like nothing could get him down. Until she walked into his life. Heather Tanser.. A freshman barely making it into the university, and was lucky enough to make the sign up for Paul's class. She attended the class all year, either showing up late or not showing up at all. Finally came finals, where Heather needed an 'A' to pass the class. She came to Paul proposing an 'offer' to him. Sadly, he accepted. The next day Paul was called into the Dean's office. In the office was the Dean at his desk, but standing by his desk was Heather and another man. Paul walked over to the Dean asking 'What did you want to see me about?'. The Dean then pulled a couple of pictures out of a file, Paul then looked down at them and looked right back up with a scared look on his face. Paul had been set up. Set up by Heather and this man that must of been hidden somewhere in the room with a camera. ' Mr. Dawson, I am afraid that I will have to let you go'. How could this happen, the only thing Paul ever loved was taken away from him like nothing. Paul couldn't hold in the anger as he attacked Heather and the man. Doing the same thing that he once did years ago. And the same degree happened to him. Mr. Dawson was escorted out of the University of Boston but this time put in jail. Paul had plenty of time to think about what he did, and what he now has to do. Education his whole life, was taken away what could his life truly be about now? After sitting awhile Paul was finally released by his uncle. His uncle then took him in while he was trying to sort out his life. His uncle ran a little wrestling training camp, he left the house at six in the morning and came home at ten at night. After a couple of days of seeing this Paul became curious of his uncle's profession. Paul then went with his uncle one day just to check things out. After watching for awhile Paul began to remember what exactly ruined his life.. And just what could make a new life for him. He then looked to his uncle to make his dream.. Reality. He then went under his uncle's wing and learnt the ropes of the ring. Finally Paul got his chance to show what he had in the wrestling world. With his credentials and having somebody in the business helps out. Paul finally made his big impact. PhD was now born into the world of wrestling.
