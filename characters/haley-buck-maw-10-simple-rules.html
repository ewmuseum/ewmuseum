Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Haley Buck

A "wench" who appears unnamed at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011, a sweet-looking girl in a long skirt and high-collared blouse with a skull-and-crossbones earring, as Hugh de Aske's new companion; she tells the Internet Favourites that her client would like to team with Fumihiro Ota, which makes Team OMGsplosion. Named at End Of Days on 26th December, in a power suit with padded shoulders, she tells the crowd that she arranged for Hugh to be fired because he expected her to do his laundry, and aligns herself with JD Morgan as his manager; the narrator notes that her earlier appearance was such a non-event that the heel turn gives her a second chance to make a first impression. She leads Morgan to the ring for The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012 and cheers him on from ringside through the final. In the diary's pages from March to May 2012 she faces down Kashmir Singh backstage at Third Time's The Charm, says Rip has been plotting something for the coming weeks, appears beside JD Morgan before the Gold Rush match at April Showers, where she shouts at him to make the cover on Primus Allen, and shrieks at ringside at South East Crisis and at Mother, May I?. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 she introduces Primus Allen and Lenny Brown at High Noon on 5th June as Always Breakin' Hollywood, her latest clients after a pirate and JD Morgan, and pushes them at South East Madness, Hotter Than Hot, Iron and Back To School, telling the XX Factor on 5th September that she could get them work as extras. She watches Morgan at Summer Showdown, urging him to join The Fallen for the money, hypes him at Hotter Than Hot, and at Summernova on 5th August says that he has dropped her for allying with the Knights Templar and brings out Sean Deeley, whom she manages against Morgan at South East Grand Parade and talks up again at Iron.
