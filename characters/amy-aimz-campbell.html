Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

aka Amy Campbell, Aimz

Amy “Aimz” Campbell, a high-flying hardcore wrestler from Halifax, Nova Scotia who finished with the Dead Aim and worked OSW's roster, held the SCCW Universal Championship three times — 421 days from August 2008 to September 2009 at the longest — and was named SCCW's 2008 Wrestler of the Year.
