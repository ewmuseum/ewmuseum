Tenure detail
Per the Hall of Fame: won the Intercontinental Title from JT Fringe, headed both the "New York Order" and the "Outlawz Revolution," and retired after losing to Staz in a Loser Leaves Town match. The Outlawz Revolution appears to be this stable's own earlier incarnation, predating the Outlawz reformation referenced in the 2002 news archive; nothing held directly links the two beyond the shared name. With JT Fringe, the tag team NY2K (December 1999).
Source: The fed's own Hall of Fame page, inducted 14 March 2000 (Hall of Fame); The Count's own 12 December 2001 promo names him among the CHZ's past legends (Wednesday Night Wild, 12 December 2001).
