Tenure detail
Challenges Lark Fenriz for the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship in a Japanese Death Match, going through the ring's own timed pyrotechnic explosion at the finish and losing by pinfall. Eliminated by LOCO in the Dog Day One singles battle royal. Loses to Jason Storm on Dog Days 2 (22 June 2004) after champion Lark Fenriz is scratched from their billed three-way. Competes in the eight-man singles scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), speared off the ladder by Setzer Van Strife. Wins the Dog Days Four Singles Six Man alongside Setzer Van Strife and The Outlaw Morris Bass on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), loses the Fourway Final, then is eliminated from the Dog Days tournament by The Outlaw in a singles elimination match the same night; brawls to a double countout with Daniel Sinz on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Works the New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) main event as special guest referee, knocked from the ring by The Equalizer and later assaulted on the arena's own big screens by Gods Tears with a lead pipe, prompting new VWF guardian John Tyler's own on-air intervention. Wins the VWF Submission Championship from Gods Tears in a three-way also involving Lark Fenriz on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) after the referee mistakes Fenriz's own tap on a nearby counter for the champion's. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Classy Joey Boy's own enziguiri. Retains the VWF Submission Championship in a Scaffold Match also involving Gods Tears and Lark Fenriz at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), swinging out on an overhead rigging line to kick both other men off the scaffold together; Fenriz hits the ground first and the match is awarded to Sidious, though the broadcast states neither the VWF Hardcore nor Submission Championship changes hands. Retains the VWF Submission Championship against Tazmanian Devil Mark McQueen at March To Madness (2 March 2005), putting McQueen out with a sleeper after an unexplained arena blackout leaves both men down. Defends the title against Fast Eddie Cutlass at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), declared a no contest after Gods Tears, Roadkill Rick, Adam Jennings and The Dark Monster Tai Angela all converge on Gods Tears mid-match. Teams with Kagedo against the Rodeo Boys at End Of March (30 March 2005), tapping out to Kenny Kidd's own Elko Lasso. Loses the VWF Submission Championship to Roadkill Rick, submitting to the Texas Speedbump, in an eight-man elimination match on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after Gods Tears attacks him from behind before the bell; also enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, eliminated when Classy Joey Boy flips him out of the ring off an errant handspring.
