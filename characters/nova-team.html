Nova

aka the Risen Star, the Eagle Star, the Starscreaming, Mushroom-Eating King of the Hallucination Nation

NFW's Rising Star, who replaced Kin Hiroshi partway through the 2006 Dupree Cup and carried NFW to the semifinals, Nova held the PRIME Universal Championship for 164 days from December 2006, the 5 Star Title twice and the Intense Title, was named Face of the Year for 2005-06 and Wrestler of the Year for 2006-07, and entered the PRIME Hall of Fame in 2007.
