Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Evil Spirit

A spooky-looking Japanese worker in a heavily stylised mask bearing a demon's face, leather ring gear and a heavy greatcoat, who debuts at Eleventh Hour in November 2010 to haunting Japanese music with a brutal win over Amazing Fire Fly and keeps beating Fly down after the bell. Firebird names him as Fumihiro Ota's former student, abandoned by Ota for seeking the darkness, and as the means by which he will bring Ota into the dark; he beats Matt Hocking on the 25th November tour show. He is Firebird's partner at Season's Beatings in December 2010, beating the Internet Favourites, loses tour matches to Hugh Lee and Riley McManus, beats Jonnie Perez in February 2011, beats Fumihiro Ota and the Masked Patriot with Firebird at All Or Nothing and is put on his last warning for spreading rumours about Jay Chord, then loses to his former master Ota at Third Time's The Charm. Firebird names him at Mother, May I? on 26th May 2011 as a man who has "gone from this place" to serve as an example; the narrator lists him among the June departures, saying that he put a lot of effort into Spirit and it never clicked.
