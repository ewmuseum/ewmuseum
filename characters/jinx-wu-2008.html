Jinx

The Devil's Jester, a painted-face clown who debuted at Wrestling Unplugged's first Parental Advisory on 22 September 2008, a friend and then foil of Requiem and a rival of DLS; he held the Elite title of the company that returned as Wrestling Unplugged in 2009 until Requiem beat him for it on 7 June, and was hurt in the Chaotic Cage of 18 October 2009.
