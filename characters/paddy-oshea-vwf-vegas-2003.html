VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-24 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 12 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 5-4-1, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Returns after a year-plus absence at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) as The Mighty Men's own mystery tag partner, submitting Evil Capsman with the Chicken Wing for the win over The Rhombus. Beats Sonicman on March To Hell (21 October 2004) with an assist from The Mighty Men, then walks to the ring carrying a kendo stick before The Society of Doom's own match against The Mighty Men, jumped by Adam Jennings and escorted out before reaching it. Pinned by Capsman's own Capitol Punishment on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), teaming again with The Reckoning and The Salvation. First man eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), dumped over the top by Sonicman. Wrestles Iyasu Saku to a no contest on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) after the returning Rodeo Boys run in following a staged Riders beatdown of Saku. Eliminated by mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov's own Urange in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004). Pins Lance Voltar with a top-rope Fist Drop on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005). Draws with Sonicman on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) after a pre-match saloon binge sets up a Rhyolite Saloon drinking match at Ghost Town Blues. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Thunder Valenkov. Wins the Rhyolite Saloon Match over Sonicman at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) with help from a bartender secretly working his corner, who punches Sonicman in the throat at the final pouring station and lets O'Shea finish his own fourth pitcher unopposed. Challenges Adam Jennings for the VWF 24 Hour Championship at March To Madness (2 March 2005), losing by disqualification after his own brother Frank punches Jennings to break up a near-fall. Faces Lance Voltar at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) in a match that ends without a decision when a returning Capsman runs in and chases off both O'Sheas. Teams with Frank against Capsman and Lance Voltar at End Of March (30 March 2005), pinning Voltar with a top-rope fist drop. Teams with his brother Frank against Capsman and Lance Voltar on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), pinned by Voltar after a schoolboy exchange.
