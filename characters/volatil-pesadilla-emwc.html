Volatil Pesadilla

At Blood, Sweat, and Tears (9 August 1998): shown in the locker room rubbing his son's shoulders and leading him out before the semifinal with Tiger Claw; at ringside he put a hand to his mouth when Claw hit the Syndi-Cutter, leaned in and pounded the mat while the referee lifted his son's arm, got into the ring to raise his son's arm after the win, and was pointed out at ringside for the final, where the announcers say he has trained his son all his life; after the final he supported his son and left with him.
