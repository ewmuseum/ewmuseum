Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Brody is the middle child of the O'Hara wrestling trio and the one to compete in singles wrestling, unlike his tag teaming brothers. He's got a good strong build. He has black hair, which is braided in thin braids that are parted the center of his head and flow down to about his jawline. He always has a 4 o'clock shadow on his face. He's fair skinned. In the ring, he wears black wrestling boots, small style tights, the speedo-esque ones, that are green, with a shamrock on it. The back reads 'O'HARA'. He wear torn blue jean shorts to the ring, but take them off right before the match starts. The O'Hara's were a fighting family in the poor sectors of the Irish hood in Boston. They were the offspring of poor Irish immigrants, and were raised to appreciate hard work and the rewards that it can bring. All the O'Hara's were streetfighters, and in were involved in organized crime for a spell. Evenutally, they got out and turned to Professional Wrestling for their main source of income. Brody is the strikes it out in singles competition, looking to one day become the UWF World Heavyweight Champion.
