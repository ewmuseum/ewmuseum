President J. Adam Hawkins

HIWF's own on-screen president, who rules on title matters, orders rematches, and strips champions who hold more than one title at a time. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

HIWF (1998–2001 continuation, self-dated Apr 1997 first card)

1998-07 – 1998-11
Tenure detail
Source: Referred to on air across the promotion's summer 1998 broadcasts (e.g. "July 27 Monday Mania"); issues the promotion's own out-of-character press release opening "Clash of the Titans II" (17 November 1998), ordering a World Heavyweight Championship rematch and stripping The Night Stalker of the Television Championship under a one-title-per-wrestler rule; is confronted on camera by Mr. Shh! demanding a title match be signed, and refuses.

HIWF (1998–2001 continuation, self-dated Apr 1997 first card)

2001-04 – 2001-09
Tenure detail
Source: Reopens the HIWF as "a separate entity" after the Supreme Wrestling Federation, into which the original HIWF had merged in August 2000 to form the Unified Wrestling Federation, folds on 1 April 2001; on air, fires and terminates the contracts of J and Shaggy (billed "aka Violent J and Shaggy 2 Dope") during The Stud's contract signing, then is beaten down by the pair until The Stud makes the save (27 April 2001); by the promotion's own 9 September 2001 broadcast has fined and indefinitely suspended debuting wrestler Dutch Dixon for refusing to accept a referee's decision.
