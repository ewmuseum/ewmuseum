Biography (PURE (2002))

Isao Asai was born and raised in Tokyo, one of the world's biggest cities, and so he has become accustomed to it's constant ebb and flow of change. He lived a life identical to most middle-class children in 70's Japan, and didn't really blossom as an individual until he got into the field of business, a profession he shared with his father. Here he spent the next decade of his life, becoming a dynamo in the field of consumer electronics. He had upper management written all over him, his future seemed assured, and that's when he 'discovered' wrestling. It started as just an entertaining way to spend an evening after work, but that single exposure was enough to instill a hunger in him for the sport like nothing he'd known before. He immediately searched down a trainer, and would alternate between work and training in a delicate balance that nearly cost him both. Finally, with his training complete, he made the hard decision between the business he'd grown up in, or the business he known he'd been born for. With a heavy but hopeful heart he resigned from the corporate world and immediately started working the Japanese Indy scene. Even as a rookie he still shined like few others, and immediately was picked up in a contract with the budding PURE franchise, who hoped to capitalize on young, fresh talent.
