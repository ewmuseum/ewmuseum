Edd Stone

A flying, unpredictable member of a wrestling family who fights as 'Edd-Fu', joins the Syndicate and takes the All Action Championship from Sammy Bach at Total Mayhem 13 with a top-rope Party's Over, and keeps it against Hell Monkey at Summer Showdown in July 2008, then against Art Reed at Hotter Than Hell in August 2008, when Reed wins only by count out, and in September 2008 keeps it against the Cannonball Kid and Art Reed, beats Hell Monkey in a non-title match and teams with his brother Jeremy to beat the Keith Brothers, and in October 2008 beats Danny Fonzarelli with the Enforcers' help, is eliminated from the Hangtime match and, at Crunch Time, loses the title to Frankie Perez. In November 2008 he is beaten by Greg Keith's NeutronPlex on Badge of Honor, helps Tommy Cornell and his brother beat Sam Keith & the Machines, teams with Jeremy Stone to beat the Easy Riders, Tooth & Claw and the Conquering Kings and is eliminated with his brother in the three-team tag title match at A Little South Of Sanity. In December 2008 he loses to Johnny Bloodstone and is eliminated in the ladder match won by Hell Monkey. Written by AI, 2026
