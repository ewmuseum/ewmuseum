The Greasers

entourage; the men who back Ponyboy Kertins
Tenure detail
Named on 7 February 1998 in a clip in which a man in a black hat, the mystery man promised for the Winter Carnival, is jumped by a carload of men he calls Socs and is saved by four men who run to help him ('Dese are de boyz I talkin' 'bout. De Greasers.'), and he counts himself among them ('We're considered as de bad boys, as the hoods, but what we do is fer kicks.'). On 16 February four Greasers, Darry, Soda, Steve and Two-Bit, lead "Golden" Ponyboy Kertins down the ramp with Cherry, each takes a corner of the ring and they pose around him as the fireworks go off.
Source: SCRA ScraPreview, 7 February 1998, Part 4; SCRA The Scraps, 16 February 1998, Part 3
