Tenure detail
Fought Nina Grimsson to a fifteen-minute time-limit draw, earning both women a three-way UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World shot against Stephanie Harper the following week. Lost that three-way title match on 25 October 2003, pinned by champion Stephanie Harper's Fallen Star. Lost to Tara Smith by pinfall on 12 November 2003, despite nearly winning with a Blissful Awakening fishermanbuster. Beat Brianna Landis, Tara Marshall's own personal assistant and sister-in-law, by referee stoppage on 21 March 2004 after Marshall sent Landis in as a last-minute substitute opponent; locked on a DDT into leg scissors until the official called the match. Beat "Wildfire" Leslie Hernandez by pinfall on 5 May 2004 with her feet on the ropes, then applied a post-match Morbid Sting until Arielle Starr ran in and traded blows with her. Lost to Arielle Starr by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, caught by the Bedazzler enzuigiri and a top-rope Skytwister Press after her own legdrop off the top rope missed. Returned from a hiatus following a loss to Arielle Starr at Gold Rush and lost to Moira Faith by pinfall (a rolling Diamond Dust ace crusher) on 25 May 2005, then attacked Faith after the bell, opening a cut above her eye. Broke into the Love Sisters' own locker room on 11 June 2005 and helped Moira Faith beat down both sisters ahead of their own Women's Tag Team Championship match, acting on the order of a man addressed as Caine. Plotted against the Love Sisters and a returning Naomi Ishikawa with Moira Faith over Lolita Love's own kidnapped cat, Mr. Boo Boo, on 6 July 2005, then lost to the Misfits (Dalbello Rage, Marissa Monet) alongside Faith in the Kindred's own debut tag match, pinned by Marissa Monet's Mischief Maker. Joined Moira Faith and Caine Tainer in cornering and threatening Lolita Love in a backstage bathroom on 20 July 2005, handing Moira a makeshift knife fashioned from broken mirror glass. Tortured a captive Lolita Love on camera with cousin Moira Faith on 3 August 2005; knocked out with a steel chair by cousin Sonya Benedict during a no-contest handicap six-woman tag on 6 August 2005. Teamed with Moira Faith as the Kindred in a losing effort against "Firebird" Katie Smith and "Tornado" Tara Smith on 7 June 2006, pinned by Tara Smith's own Arizona Rattler after a long heat segment on Katie Smith. Beat Samantha Daniels by pinfall (a heel kick into a rollup) in a return to singles competition on 28 April 2007, with Donna Tetreault's repeated ringside taunting breaking Daniels's own concentration late in the match; introduced afterward as the newest member of Amity by "Supervixen" Ami Tran, then joined Tran in accepting a future title challenge from Women's North American Champion Sonya Benedict, her own cousin. Was separately confronted backstage the same night by estranged brothers Jacen and Ronan Benedict, the War Hounds, who urged her to leave Darrin Kisler's Bloodhorn faction; she held a knife to Jacen's throat without following through and refused their appeal.
