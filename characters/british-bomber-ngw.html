Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 78 parsed matches, FAW (2001)): 30-43-0, 5 unresolved
Tenure detail
Hall of Fame inductee; billed measurements from his own Diabolical Day 2003 ring introduction, FAW's own Diabolical Day 2003 recap (promotions/faw/archive/diabolical-day-2003-2003-07-13.html). Loses to Chris Thunder, then is overheard on the phone with an unidentified man plotting a joint-stock deal tied to the Evil Intentions World Title match; forces Spawn to submit in the I Quit main event but is ultimately the one to say the words himself after Spawn locks in the 3rd Degree Burn. Wins the FAW World Heavyweight Championship in the Hell In A Cell/TLC main event, carried to the belt by new xCw co-owner Tom The Bomb after the cell floor collapses under him. Announces (via an xCw Smash clip) that his own fiancee Kathryn Velmont-Thomas has died in a car crash following an argument between them; interferes in the Rick/Skipster International title match, only for Skipster to turn on him. Returns from a self-described time-off dispute over an old knee injury (asked for time off after Evil Intentions, was refused, quit, worked NGW, then re-signed with FAW after Vendetta) in a promo on FAW Panic, 6 April 2003, read in full -- name-checks old friends Sean Mason, Chris Thunder and Jeff Harris.
