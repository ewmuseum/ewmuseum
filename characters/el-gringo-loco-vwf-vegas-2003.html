Tenure detail
Debuts a talk-show interview segment, THE FLASHBACK, under the new ring name Flash, hosting The Equalizer on Firestorm (3 May 2004). Loses the Dog Day Two singles bracket's first round to The Outlaw Morris Bass, then wins the VWF 24 Hour Championship match against Kevin Blair by count-out, the title not changing hands. Hosts a special Flashback attempting to mediate the Rodeo Boys and Hot Property on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), then joins Hot Property in beating down both Rodeo Boys before competing in the eight-man singles scramble. Wins a six-man tag on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) alongside Hot Property, then is eliminated from the Dog Days tournament by The Outlaw Morris Bass in a four-way singles elimination match the same night. The Heavyweight Championship main event on Wrestle Style V.1 ends without a finish when a returning Classy Joey Boy interferes; wins by count-out over Adam Jennings in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3, then attacks Jennings after the bell with The Miracle. Disqualified for a baseball bat attack in a VWF Heavyweight Title tournament triple threat on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004). Loses a tag match with Jason Storm to Orion The Hunter and Orion #2 on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Pinned by The Maquilladora with the Permanent Daylight in the seven-man mini rumble on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004).
