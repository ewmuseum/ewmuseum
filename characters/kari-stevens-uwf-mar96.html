Tenure detail
Demanded an in-ring debut from Commissioner Becky Byers rather than continue managing from ringside; beat "Mystical" Mitzi Sukkora by pinfall with a corkscrew 450 splash in her own first UWF match. Beat Angel by pinfall in her second UWF match on 29 May 2004, reversing a hotshot attempt into a rollup for the three count, then flattening her fallen opponent with a HeadHunter after the bell. Later joined Team Canada and Tom Landis in ambushing debuting Alex Martinez in the aisle ahead of his own tag match on the same card, without competing herself. Teamed with Team Canada (Trevor Hextall, Bob Jablonski) in a losing intergender six-way against Fury (Rage, Spice) and Ami Tran by pinfall on 6 October 2004, pinned by a version of the One-Winged Angel's own Revelator from Ami Tran. Lost a UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World challenge to champion Donna Tetreault by submission (a crossface, with salt and lemon juice rubbed into a cut over her own eye) on 30 October 2004, after Trevor Hextall's own salt throw was turned back on a prepared Tetreault. Interfered for the Pride during the Bluff City Streetfight main event on 27 November 2004, supplying a snow shovel and pulling Scott Daniels clear of a top-rope elbowdrop. Tied "Hentai" Ryu Osawa in the ropes to help Trevor Hextall finish him by pinfall on 1 December 2004. On 11 December 2004, presented "Dead End" Derek Martin to the crowd as the Pride's newest member, brawled at ringside with "Supervixen" Ami Tran during Martin's own no-contest with the One-Winged Angel, and distracted the referee for Scott Daniels's own interference. Pulled the ropes from Kyle Lee's reach during his own UWF World Heavyweight Championship defense against Scott "Hotspot" Daniels on 28 May 2005, then handed Daniels the title belt after the pinfall. Lost to Ami Tran by submission (the Woman's Scorn) on 8 June 2005 despite fighting through a tweaked knee. Traded banter with tag partner Amazing Grace backstage before the two fought the Wind Walkers to a no-decision on 20 July 2005, ended by Bod Squad interference. Walked to the ring with Scott Daniels and the Pride on 23 July 2005 and introduced the Cornerstones (Daniel Kidd, Tom Landis) as the group's own new tag team. Beat Stephanie Harper by pinfall (a swinging neckbreaker) on 3 August 2005 after mentoring debuting Samantha Daniels and clashing with Arielle Starr backstage; accompanied World Champion Scott Daniels to the ring for his Brawl From The Mall 6 promo on 6 August 2005. Told a distraught Samantha Daniels backstage at Cupid's Revenge on 14 February 2006 that she did not cost her father his own Gold Rush match, laying blame instead on Alex Martinez, special referee Juan Vasquez, and UWF president Becky Byers.
