Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Hugh de Aske

A piratical newcomer, also working for NYCW, who enters in a rowboat pushed by his crew; he loses a near fifteen-minute Invitational match to Stevie Grayson in November 2009, takes the MAW Traditional title from him at End Of Days with the Cut Throat Driver and keeps it against Fumihiro Ota, Hugh Lee, "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith (a time-limit draw) and Kirk Jameson, with Sienna LeNoir at his side, until Aaron Andrews wins it at Spring Fever in April 2010. He loses to Stevie Grayson at Summer Showdown in June 2010, signs a contract extension that summer, beats the Masked Patriot with the Cut Throat Driver at Summernova, loses to Kirk Jameson at Iron, and at All The Leaves Are Brown in October loses a steel-cage match to Aaron Andrews after Rip Chord promises him "a lot of booty" if he wins. At Where It All Begins Again he makes out with Sienna LeNoir in a backstage segment and then loses to Steve Flash, who reverses his Cutthroat Driver into a Flash Bang. He loses to Aaron Andrews in a Heavyweight title match at Remember November in November 2010 and, in the 2011 Invitational, beats Amazing Fire Fly, the Masked Patriot and Jean Cattley before losing the final to Kirk Jameson. At Leap Into The Unknown on 12th February 2011, after he and Sienna LeNoir cut a contract in half with a cutlass, he wins the Traditional title in a three-way with Jonnie Perez and Matthew Gauge, keeps it against Perez, Jean Cattley and Joe Benning, and, having "wised up to his true worth", is no longer available for the touring shows from March. Sienna LeNoir leaves him at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011: he beats Fumihiro Ota with the Cutthroat Driver to keep the Traditional title but runs after her, leaving the belt behind, and finds her in Black Eagle's dressing room. He is chaired by the Masked Patriot on 12th May, beats Providence on 26th May and Black Eagle at Summer Showdown, draws with Jay Chord at Hotter Than Hot, loses a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews at Beach Blast and the Traditional title to Primus Allen at Summernova. At Iron he beats Black Eagle for the right to speak to Sienna, only to be struck with the ring bell as he leans in for a kiss, and at Back To School he beats Jay Chord and is attacked by him in the locker room. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he loses to JD Morgan, distracted by Black Eagle being wheeled out on a gurney, and at Where It All Begins Again fights Black Eagle to a time-limit draw for the love of Sienna LeNoir; at Eleventh Hour on 12th November he teams with Fumihiro Ota as Team OMGsplosion, and after losing to Lockdown at Farewell Tour on 22nd November he leaves for the SWF. Haley Buck says on 26th December that she had him fired. Written by AI, 2026
