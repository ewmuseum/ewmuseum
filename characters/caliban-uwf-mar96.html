Tenure detail
Billed from Alaska; introduced destroying his own locker room before agreeing to team with Serge Annis against Fury (Rage and Spice), scoring the pinfall win with a superplex that drove Rage down across Annis's own outstretched knee. Teamed with Serge Annis again on 29 May 2004, billed as the Hands of Death in beating the Fraternity Boys (Chris O'Brien and Brian McKenzie) in a tag confrontation, pinning Brian McKenzie off a Near Death Experience superplex, then fighting the Ghost Dancers to a double countout in a formal tag match later the same card. Teamed with Serge Annis as the Hands of Death in a losing Gold Rush triple threat for the UWF World Tag Team Championship on 5 June 2004 against champions the Privateers and the Ghost Dancers; his own partner was taken out of the match by the Fraternity Boys' triple Conchairto before the Privateers stole the pinfall. Teamed with Serge Annis as the Hands of Death in a steel cage main event win over Kyle Lee and Alex Kidd on 28 August 2004, pinning Kidd with a Near Death Experience superplex-and-slam combination after Lee escaped the cage but could not return in time to help. His "Battle of the Sexes" match against Marissa Monet was thrown out without a decision on 18 September 2004 after tag partner Serge Annis ran in for a joint Near Death Experience; spat on the beaten Monet afterward. Lost alongside tag partner Serge Annis to Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian) by pinfall on 2 October 2004 after being distracted by a thrown frozen fish; beaten down afterward by the returning Prophets of Rage with their own tag title belts. Lost to Derek Rage by pinfall (the Hammer of God) on 20 October 2004 after an ally of Rage's, Pizzazz, jabbed a lit cigarette into his eye during a distracting brawl between Shadoe Rage and Serge Annis at ringside. Teamed with Serge Annis as the Hands of Death in a no-contest against Team Canada (Bob Jablonski, Trevor Hextall) on 13 November 2004, the match ruled a no contest after a mass brawl drew in the Sons of Cacaphony, Uriah Hawkins, Alex Martinez and Alex Extreme. Dismissed the Prophets of Rage's own history to their faces on 27 November 2004, calling Marissa Monet bait used to draw Shadoe and Derek Rage out; driven through a burning, chair-loaded table with a Flaming Godkiller by Derek Rage in the brawl that followed. A dog collar match against the Prophets was set for Rise to Power. Recalled his own past in Berwick, Nova Scotia in a vignette on 1 December 2004 before fighting the Amazing Romanis to a no-contest for the UWF North American Tag Team Championship alongside Serge Annis and hanging them by dog collars afterward. On 11 December 2004, helped Annis abduct Marissa Monet and Pizzazz, chokeslamming both off a skybox balcony onto a production table and hoisting them back up by their own chains. Arrived late, still bloodied from an earlier chair attack, to help Serge Annis beat Chris O'Brien by pinfall in a handicap match on 28 May 2005, throwing O'Brien off a balcony through a stack of tables along the way. Struck both Sons of Cacophony members with a steel chair to win a non-title four corners tag main event by disqualification alongside Serge Annis on 11 June 2005. Chained up inside the Descent Into Hell structure by Erik Grimsson on 23 July 2005, who used him to challenge the Hands of Death to defend the World Tag Team Championship in that match at Gold Rush. Pinned by Corey Irons after a chair shot on 3 August 2005; voiced a Latin battle vow via the Unitron on 6 August 2005 after partner Serge Annis's assault on Nina Grimsson and mocking of the Sons of Cacaphony.
