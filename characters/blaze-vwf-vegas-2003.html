VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-09-24 – 2004-10-12
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 16 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 5-11-0
Tenure detail
Teams with a new partner, The Real Deal, against the Capsmen on Firestorm (3 May 2004). Teams with the Capsmen and The Real Deal in the Gauntlet's opening tag match, on the losing side. Teams with the Real Deal against the Society of Doom on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminates Johnny Freil before his own team is eliminated in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Teams with The Real Deal to win the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round over Sonicman and Sebastian, then is beaten down and hospitalized by Hot Property after the bell, ending the team's own tournament run. Returns for the sixteen-man tag scramble and the Tag Team Elimination Ladder Match on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), eliminated when the ladder collapses. Eliminated from the Dog Days tournament in the Tag Team Elimination Match on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then goes over the top rope himself with tag partner Real Deal at the end of the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), honoring their own open challenge. Already billed VWF Tag Team Champion alongside Real Deal by Wrestle Style V.2, having regained the titles off-page since losing them earlier that spring; retained against the Society of Doom by disqualification, then beat Fast Eddie Cutlass with his own feet on the ropes in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Loses to The Equalizer in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), pinned on his own steel chair. Calls tag partner Real Deal away from a match with Mark McQueen on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004) after threatening a kidnapped McQueen on a video screen, part of a ploy against Marcus O'Malley's own Heavyweight title hopes. Retains the VWF Tag Team Championship with Real Deal by disqualification over Capsman and Evil Capsman on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004). Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship with Real Deal to Alcoholics Anonymous (Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen) at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004); lawyer RAR and majority owner Kingston Cash reinstate the Rodeo Boys on the spot to beat down the new champions immediately after.
