The Cult of Sasuke

A masked Super Juniors cult led by The Great Sasuke, who arrives on F' The Mainstream in September 2006 with five disciples vowing mayhem, strips the Iron Saints and the Dudley Boys to their underwear, stages ritual assaults on LA Park and Jushin Lyger, and wins LA Park for the group at Anarchy Rulz. In December 2006 The Great Sasuke renames the group the Sasuke World Order and announces that it will take over F' The Mainstream and then the world. In February 2007 the group, now the Sasuke World Order, tear off their shirts to show sWo logos spray-painted on their chests and The Great Sasuke loses to Big Stevie Cool at Living Dangerously; in March the cult drag Big Stevie Cool, Hollywood Nova and Da Blue Guy under the ring, and at Baptism Of Fire Stevie and Nova, now under Sasuke's spell, turn on Da Blue Guy and kneel to worship him. In April the Dudley Boys, as the self-styled enforcers of ECW, take on the cult, and Sasuke hangs life-size mannequins of the Dudleys from the rafters. In June 2007 the Sasuke World Order storm the ring on Revolution to claim a place as a twelfth World Cup team; in the pool stage they lose to Team USA twice, lose once to Italy when Oz's chair swing backfires and win once by swapping masks, and, with Cuervo's spray paint costing Italy its last match against Team USA, qualify by a single bonus point; they beat Samoa in the quarter-final with Oz's distraction, Cuervo's 2x4 and Sasuke's wits. They lose the semi-final to Japan when Tajiri, in Sasuke's mask, pins Chessman; after the tournament Sasuke beats Tajiri with Oz's lead pipe and the sWo paint the ECW flag, until two Chessmen and Big Stevie Cool's bWo invade and spray 'bWo' on the sWo, and in August Oz, tricked by the bWo into the ladies' locker room, is Super Kicked by Shawn Michaels. In August 2007 the sWo take on Espiritu and Escoria, who argue over 'We're overtaking' and 'We're taking over', brawl with the bWo on Revolution and lose to them before Heatwave, where Big Sasuke Cool beats Big Stevie Cool by hiding five extra Sasukes under the ring and a Michinoku Driver. In September Hollywood Nova, painted as Chessman, tricks the sWo, Sasuke pins Nova with a school boy, Oz is pinned by a Stevie Kick, Sasuke loses the International All Action Title Match to CM Punk when Monsters Inc. interfere, and Chris Hero pins Sasuke on F' The Mainstream. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 Big Sasuke Cool beats Big Stevie Cool with a Michinoku Driver after a low blow, as Chessman and four men dressed as Hollywood Nova run in. In October the sWo take BW Snow hostage and show him a Kevin Nash and John Cena match, are tricked when the bWo send Big Stevie Cool in as a masked 'Sasuke' to free him and show the real Sasuke 'The Best of Monday Night Raw', and lose on Blood, Sweat and Beers when a masked 'mole' attacks the bWo's men from under the ring. At Guilty As Charged, with the loser to disband, Big Sasuke Cool pins Hollywood Nova, Superkicks Chessman and leaves with Big Stevie Cool as the True World Order; the diary's Bam Bam Exam then lists Chessman, Cuervo, Escoria, Espiritu and Oz among the workers ECW is releasing.
