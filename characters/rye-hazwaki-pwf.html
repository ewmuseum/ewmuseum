Billed age 22 per the card. Billed quote: “You bring the word’s, I’ll bring the Fight!” Bio: born in Japan to an American father and Japanese mother, orphaned young; trained by a martial artist billed "Timmed" and worked as a bouncer alongside a man named Kult before joining the PWF. Interfered in Matt Van Dam's match against Genecide at Saturday Night Havoc, crotching MVD on the turnbuckle to cost him the win, then spent the rest of the broadcast fleeing the gWo (seen signing autographs, then driving past them in the parking lot). Won the US Title in a Double Jeopardy Ladder Match at War Games, 27 May 2001, with Havok's interference — the assist that formed the OutKasts.
