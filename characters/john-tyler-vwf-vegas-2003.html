VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2005-01-19 – 2005-04-11
Tenure detail
Introduces himself as a special guardian appointed over majority owner Kingston Cash's own objection during an FBI investigation into the VWF, orders a returning Kagedo installed as his own enforcer over Trinity, and threatens Gods Tears with a title stripping and suspension if he continues assaulting Sidious The Clown on the arena's own big screens. Overrules majority owner Kingston Cash to let Classy Joey Boy book himself into the Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) main event, then ejects Pagan from the building for the post-match attack on Fast Eddie Cutlass and adds Adam Jennings and a triple threat to the card. Answers Henry Fish's own demand for a Scott Starring title shot at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) by sending out Kagedo for an impromptu opening match. Announces on air at March To Madness (2 March 2005) that the IRS has seized the VWF over misappropriated talent funds traced to an offshore account under Kingston Cash's own name, fires Cash and takes temporary control of the company pending an auction, then books a five-man elimination match with a spot in The Gauntlet's own entry order at stake. Books an eight-man elimination match at End Of March (30 March 2005) after Kenny Kidd forces a submission win over Sidious The Clown, then discloses a legal challenge to the VWF Tag Team Championship disputing Team Brittania's own title win, declining to rule and leaving the question to the auction's own incoming owner. Presides over his own final broadcast as the VWF's custodian on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), addressing the crowd before handing the company over; a returning Jason Storm announces he has bought the promotion at the broadcast's close.
