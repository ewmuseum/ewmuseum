Slyk Tayshaun Dudley

aka STD, Anonymous Dudley, That Other Dudley

An unnamed Dudley who first walks out of the crowd on Revolution in September 2007 with a chair, blasts Shawn Michaels and Rob Van Dam, then turns on Bubba Ray and D-Von when they come to thank him, Spinning Powerbombing D-Von onto the steel, after which Rob Van Dam recovers to win the match with a Five Star Frog Splash; on Blood, Sweat and Beers he swings a flagpole at the rest of the family, and Becky Bayless names him: Slyk Tayshaun Dudley, 'STD' to his brothers, the seventh son of the missing Big Dick Dudley, who told him to 'find out the truth' from Uncle D-Von, who held some secret over his father. He makes his debut against Big Dick Jr. on Revolution, and the family beat him down and send him through a table with a Total Penetration, so that Chris Kay awards the match to Big Dick Jr. Written by AI, 2026
