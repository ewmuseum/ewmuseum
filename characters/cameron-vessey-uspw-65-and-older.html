Cameron Vessey

Larry Vessey's son, hired by Sam Strong in February, who loses to Jim Force on the first March show. In October 2007 he and Davey Wayne serve Triple D as apprentices, lose to the Dudez at A Haunting in America, and he is beaten by Umaga on the Monday of Week 3; his gambling and pawning of Wayne's belongings run through the Hotshots' backstage scenes. In October and November 2007 he sits in jail with Wayne for the murder of the pigeon Mr. Squiggles, is found guilty and given community service, is beaten bloody by Veteran Mentality, joins Divinity at Pilgrimage and wins the tag belts there with Wayne in 1:56. In late November and December 2007 he and Davey Wayne sit through community service, work for Seduction, switch the lights off on Devine and lose to Zeus Maxmillion. Written by AI, 2026
