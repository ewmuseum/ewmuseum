The Lone Gunmen

Machina and Rudo Insano's team from July 1998, joined in its matches by Sid Snow, Billy James and the Total Eclipse, twice the fWo Tag Team Champions — over Heaven's Demons in October 1998 and over Ultra Cool 2000 at Countdown '99 — and brought back together by fWo's staff after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001, in the story's August 2004 special.
