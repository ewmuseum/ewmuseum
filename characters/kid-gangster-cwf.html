CWF (1998–2003)

1998 – 2003-02-11
authority-figure and wrestler — co-owner who helped build the original CWF per its own History; returned at SeVeN, unmasked from a mystery vignette, to attack the new World Champion and force a signed contract for a match with owner Hellfire X
Tenure detail
History names him CWF's co-owner in the original run, who left to start his own fed with money made "wrestling in hundreds of federations," worked with Hellfire X across several later feds, and was explicitly not invited to the 2003 reunion over their "on going" feud — the costumed impostor at that show plays on the audience knowing this. His real return at SeVeN reveals a contract clause letting him book his own match, stipulations and all, against Hellfire X; whether that match ever happened is not attested anywhere in the surviving record.
Source: the fed's own History page (History); the Redz()ne-1 reunion show, where a costumed impostor crashes the commissioner reveal and is chokeslammed by Hellfire X (CWF (1998–2003) Redz()ne-1: The CWF/XWF Re-union Show); the SeVeN show script, where the real Kid Gangster unmasks after the World Championship match, attacks Mike Diamond, and reveals a signed contract for a title match against Hellfire X (CWF (1998–2003) SeVeN); the Redz()ne-2 card, which bills him tagging with Hardcore Z against Hellfire X and Mike Diamond (CWF (1998–2003) Redz()ne-2)
