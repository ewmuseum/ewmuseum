Rock Downpour

A former NYCW wrestler who joins MAW in the spring of 2009 and works the commentary desk with the narrator at Mother, May I?. He introduces Chance Fortune and Randy Bumfhole on commentary at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, signs a contract extension in May for $100 a show less than he was otherwise on, his second pay cut, and is on commentary at Summer Showdown and Beach Blast. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 Black Eagle spreads rumours about him and is fined; he commentates at Great Lakes Madness and Mother, May I?. In the diary's pages for June 2012 he is on commentary with Rip Chord and the narrator at South East Madness on 9th June, and at Summer Showdown on 12th June he screams the name of Fumihiro Ota.
