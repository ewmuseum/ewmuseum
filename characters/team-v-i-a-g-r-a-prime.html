Team V.I.A.G.R.A.

As Team V.I.A.G.R.A., managed by Mary-Lynn Mayweather, High Flyer and Tony Davis were on Action cards between 2002 and February 2003, then held the PRIME Tag Team Championship three times between UltraViolence 2008 and April 2009, were named Tag Team of the Year for 2007-08 and were inducted into PRIME’s Hall of Fame in 2012, the first seat of its final class.
