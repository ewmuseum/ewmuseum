Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Darryl Devine

aka Daryl Devine

A young wrestler Jay Chord brings in at Summer Showdown in June 2009 to take Greg Gauge's place; he finishes with the Devine Dream Drop, takes the MAW Traditional title from Stevie Grayson at Beach Blast and keeps it from Ultimate Phoenix at Iron. He loses the Traditional title to Stevie Grayson in October 2009, challenges Steven Parker for the Heavyweight title in November, loses to Erik Strong in the 2010 Invitational semi-final, and in March 2010 joins The Firm, whose Trios titles he helps win; the diary has Jean Cattley call him a follower who needs to be led. In June 2010 a young blond "Darryl" hands the narrator his notice because TCW are recruiting; the diary gives no surname, and Darryl Devine is the only Darryl on its roster. By Summer Showdown in June 2010 he has taken an offer from out west and left The Firm, Casey Valentine tells Jean Cattley; Cattley later blames Valentine for driving him out. He returns at Leap Into The Unknown on 12th February 2011 to accept Jay Chord's open challenge and wins with the Devine Dream Drop, saves Aaron Andrews from a beating at All Or Nothing and draws with Jay, and makes his last appearance for now at Third Time's The Charm on 12th March 2011, putting Jay over after Rip Chord's talk, the month after Tommy Cornell's orders made him balk at losing to Jay.
