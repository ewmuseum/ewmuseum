Tenure detail
Vampir Nosferatu's stable in PRIME, formed after The Great American Nightmare of 17 November 2004: the review of 1 December 2004 calls it 'the formidable new Dark Age group', with Shadow and Ozric Mortimer aligned alongside Vampir Nosferatu and Dani Furher, and Tiamat as Vampir's disciple. That night, after the steel cage main event, it electrifies the cage, cuffs Black Angel to it and cuts a pentagram into Paul Cain's chest with the knife it had used the week before on Boda and Emilio Rage; on 15 December 2004 it abducts Ignatius Lisieux and raises him on a cross above a burning Christmas tree. Vampir, its founder, had been one of the original members of PCW's Dark Age of 2001, a different stable.
Source: PRIME ReVolution 49; PRIME ReVolution 50
