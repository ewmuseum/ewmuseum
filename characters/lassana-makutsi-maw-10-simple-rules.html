Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Lassana Makutsi

A newcomer who debuts at Remember November on 26th November 2011 and loses to Roderick Remus in under six minutes, the narrator calling it a promising effort from a worker nobody knows. At Season's Beatings on 12th December he carries Providence through an Invitational qualifier like a seasoned veteran, the narrator praising his understanding of the psychology of a fight, and loses to the Eye Of Providence; he loses to Antonio on the 17th December tour show and to Greg Ford on the 24th, and at End Of Days he and Amazing Fire Fly lose to the Dark Raptors when Makutsi eats a Phoenix Firebird Splash. In January the narrator names him, with Raphael and an undebuted worker, among the works in progress. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses to Raphael at All Or Nothing on 12th February, to Providence at Third Time's The Charm, to Primus Allen in a Traditional title match at March Madness on 12th March, in which the narrator finds the two have tremendous chemistry, and, with KC Glenn, to the Second Sons in the Spring Fever three-way. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he loses the Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews, who picks him as an easy opponent, at High Noon on 5th June, and then teams with Jared Johnson as Team Honor, whom the narrator says have great chemistry; he loses to Always Breakin' Hollywood and the Second Sons, challenges Black Eagle at Iron on 12th August for the All Action title he calls dishonourably captured, is kicked in the stomach by Eagle and Firebird, and at Back To School on 5th September locks the Carolina Crossface on Eagle to win the Tag Team titles.
