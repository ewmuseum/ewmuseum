Jerry Kohler

aka Pops

A recurring UWF ringside official billed in-fiction as "Pops" Kohler, "the senile and pant-less referee," his own ring name a play on "Cherry Cola" and remembered for falling asleep mid-match and missing a count; at NAPW's Beach Blanket Bodyslams in July 2014 he was billed as the Phenomenons' social media spokesperson and made his wrestling debut as their substitute partner. NAPW (2005)

2014-07 – 2014-07
Tenure detail
Billed "Jerry 'Pops' Kohler", "the ancient former referee and current social media spokesperson for the Phenomenons", in Chuck Taylors and old school wrestling tights with a referee's black and white pattern, he makes his professional wrestling debut as the Phenomenons' substitute partner in the Tag Team title match against the A-Team and Gustav. The same billing and "Pops" moniker as the UWF's pant-less old referee, and the Phenomenons themselves, whose UWF tenure the registry holds.
Source: the Phenomenons' own NAPW entrance for Beach Blanket Bodyslams, 29 July 2014

ICW (Internet, 2012)

2012-08-19 – 2012-09-11
Tenure detail
Applies as "Jerry 'Pops' Kohler", a wrestling referee for forty-three years who falls asleep in matches and now, at 76, wants to win one match himself, with his grandson Johnny. The same name, the "Pops" moniker and the sleeping referee of the UWF record (#name-plus-moniker-rule); the same character wrestles at NAPW in 2014.
Source: /The Flying Kohlers (an ICW application) (the team's application) and /Flying Kohler Boys segment (ICW (Internet, 2012)) (the pre-match segment)
