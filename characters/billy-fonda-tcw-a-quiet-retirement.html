Billy Fonda

aka Grease Hogg

One of the Easy Riders, the bikers of Karen Killer's returning video, who attack Painful Procedure on Badge of Honor in July 2008; the announcers give his name as Billy Fonda and say he was formerly known better as Grease Hogg of the SWF's Dirty White Boys. In September 2008 he costs the Easy Riders a match against the Machines by hitting Brent Hill with a steel chair. In November 2008 he beats Danny Fonzarelli & Koshiro Ino, Tooth & Claw and the Young Guns with the Emergency Stop, loses to the Stone Brothers and, at A Little South Of Sanity, is eliminated from the three-team tag title match. In December 2008 he beats Hell Monkey & Teddy Powell and Team VIPER with the Easy Riders and, at Psycho Circus, wins the tag belts with Peter Hopper, choking Scout against the turnbuckle with Carl Batch's chain.
