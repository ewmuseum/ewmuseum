Derek Rage's own girlfriend and a Prophets of Rage regular, abducted and hung from a skybox balcony by the Hands of Death. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-12-11 – 2006-03-18
Tenure detail
Went missing from the Prophets of Rage's own dressing room on 11 December 2004 alongside Marissa Monet; both women were later revealed held captive by Serge Annis and Caliban in a skybox, dog collars chained around their necks, and were chokeslammed off the balcony onto a production table below, then hoisted back up by the same chains as Alex Kidd, Ryu Osawa, Paul Wong, and Stephanie Harper tried and failed to free them. Joined the Prophets of Rage's championship celebration on 18 March 2006, needling Nina Grimsson alongside Derek Rage.
