Tenure detail
Billed representing the Heroes of Sports Entertainment alongside Collision Holly; lost his own in-ring debut to "Prairie Fire" Mark Marleau by pinfall (the Saskatoon Special) after a late bearhug rally. Lost to a returning Steve Ryott by pinfall (a top-rope Ryott Bomb) on 25 August 2004 after taking an inverted DDT. Lost his UWF debut-tournament opener to "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas by submission (the End of the Show cobra clutch) on 8 September 2004. Beat Simon O'Neal by pinfall on 6 October 2004 after O'Neal collapsed attempting a bodyslam, an upset that stunned the crowd into silence. Lost to debuting "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb by pinfall (the Atom Smasher, a double underhook piledriver) on 20 October 2004.
