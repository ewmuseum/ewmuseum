The Canadian Blondes

The tag champions Oscar Golden and Flash Savage and their ninja-obsessed partner Hugh Lee, who hold the MAW Tag Team titles until the Beach Blast ladder match. They beat the Bailey Boys at Summernova in August 2010, win the Tag Team titles for a record third time at Fall Of The King in September, and keep them in a four-team ladder match at Where It All Begins Again in October and against Brandon Smith and Joe Benning on a November tour show. They keep the Tag Team titles at Remember November in November 2010 and Season's Beatings, spike-piledrive Brandon Smith after End Of Days, lose the belts to Cattley and Singh at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011, beat Smith & Singh at the Invitational and the Internet Favourites at All Or Nothing and lose a rematch to Cattley and Singh at Third Time's The Charm, where Oscar Golden flirts with a girl at ringside. They beat Cattley & Singh for the Tag Team titles at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, are brought home drunk by two policemen who are MAW fans, boast on 12th June of being four-time champions, debut the O, Canada! at Summer Showdown and keep the belts through the summer; at Back To School Raphael joins them in a six-man loss to Smith, Singh and Ota. They keep the Tag Team titles against the Young Americans and Smith & Singh on 26th September 2011 and on the 3rd and 24th October tour shows, lose them to Smith & Singh at Where It All Begins Again, beat the West Texas Peacekeepers at Season's Beatings, Peverell and McManus at End Of Days and the Internet Favourites on 5th January 2012, when Kristen Pearce joins them, and say that they have never had their rematch. At Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March 2012, in the Asylum in Michigan, they cash in their rematch four months after dropping the Tag Team titles and lose to Smith & Singh; they lose to KC Glenn and Jared Johnson at Third Time's The Charm and are in the four-team match at April Showers. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 they lose the three-way debut of Always Breakin' Hollywood at High Noon on 5th June, beat Spectrum Infinity with O, Canada! at Great Lakes Grand Parade on 9th July, lose to them at Summernova on 5th August with Kristen Pearce at their side, and beat Smith & Singh at South East Grand Parade on 9th August.
