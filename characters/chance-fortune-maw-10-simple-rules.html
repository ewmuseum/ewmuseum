Chance Fortune

A veteran whom the commentary introduces as a former TCW star and who walks out with Randy Bumfhole at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 to face Erik Strong and Antonio, the match ending on a Bumfhole Buster. He joins Steve Flash's new Firm, loses to JD Morgan on 12th May, beats "Lone Wolf" Robert Thomas with the Stroke of Luck on 26th May, loses to Erik Strong at High Noon, is ambushed backstage and beaten by Primus Allen at Summer Showdown, and beats Black Eagle at Beach Blast. With Kid Arachnid and Bumfhole he wins the Trios titles from The Triple Threat at Summernova on 12th August, and at Iron on 26th August takes his first Heavyweight title shot in the company and loses to Aaron Andrews. At the narrator's request he is one of the three veterans, with Flash and Bumfhole, who agree to be his ears and eyes in the locker room. At All The Leaves Are Brown on 12th October 2011 he brings Zimmy Bumfhole to The Firm's rescue; at Where It All Begins Again The Firm lose the Trios titles to the Second Sons, and at Farewell Tour on 22nd November he loses a Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews; the narrator then thanks him as he and Ernest Youngman and Hugh de Aske drive north to the SWF.
