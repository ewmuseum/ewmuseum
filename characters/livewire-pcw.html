Biography (RUSH (2002))

Robert Amanze was a young teenager who at the age of 17 had everything. Fast car,beautiful girlfriend, straight A student and soccer supertstar. He was doing very well at college but knew he wasn’t happy as his real love was soccer. He was on the books of London’s finest soccer team Arsenal, and felt that his future career lied here. So at the age of 18 decided to give up his studies and concentrate on soccer. He didn’t get the blessing of his parents but promised to prove them wrong and become a professional soccer player. Robert was already in the Arsenal youth team and managed within a year to get into the reserve team. He was then told on the eve of his 19th birthday that he was to be on the bench for the home game against London rivals Spurs. Robert was overjoyed but knew he was only in the squad due to a recent pile up of injuries. Robert however knew if he got the chance to come on would surely impress. Sadly however the morning of the game during a light training session Robert injured his left knee badly. He was trying very hard, some say too hard and fell awkwardly on his knee after a slide tackle. Robert immediately knew something was wrong and was told by the surgeon who operated on his knee that he would never play top level soccer again. Robert was devastated and felt that his life was over at the young age of 19. He decided with the advice of his parents to go back to school and do a course in marketing as he had a real knack for this in his youth. Sadly though he quit after just 6 months. It was quite clear he had not recovered from the disappointment at seeing his soccer career finish and decided to take some time out. One evening after a massive row with his parents Robert left home and decided to look for a flat. He moved up north and decided to get a part time job in his local pub. It was here one day while during his rounds he met up with his old school friend Mike Taylor. Mike was part of the same sports scene Robert was once part of at school and they talked intensely. Mike from what Robert could remember was once a very talented Greco-Roman wrestler. However as they carried on speaking Robert was informed Mike's promising career had been cut short by an recurring back injury. It was then that Mike informed Robert that he was now a scout for little known British wrestling federation The WFA (wrestling for anybody). It became quite obvious to Robert that Mike was looking for new talent as the British wrestling scene hadn’t really taken off yet. Mike spoke at how he could recall that Robert was once a very good athlete and asked if he was interested. Robert politely declined and said he had only seen wrestling briefly on t.v and was convinced the whole thing was just fake. Mike reassured Robert it wasn’t and that just some of the big matches in the us were fixed. Mike gave Robert his number and Robert politely excepted even though knowing he would probably never use it. One rainy day Robert was driving home from work when his car skidded into a nearby electric cable. He escaped unhurt but was told by doctors he was very lucky the cable hadn’t had been too high an voltage. Robert then knew his life wasn’t set out for nine to five jobs and decided to try and make something of his life. It was a week later when something of a ray of light came Robert's way. He was reading the newspaper when an advert for the WFA was plastered on the middle page. The WFA were coming down to London in three months and were suddenly in much demand. Wrestlers such as Chris Luck and the Unknown had brought back respect to British wrestling. Livewire couldn’t turn down this opportunity and decided to ring Mike who gladly offered Robert a trial and try and help him compete for the forthcoming London tour. Robert trained with Mike intensely during those three months and it came quite apparent that Robert was still an excellent athlete and was picking up the basic wrestling moves very easily. Robert was then given a trial a week before the tour and didn’t disappoint. He won the match against one of the feds useless jobbers by using a modified sleeper hold taught to him by Mike. After the match a tired, hurt and sweating Robert was told by the WFA president that he was in. Robert was overjoyed and instantly accepted the invitation to join the fed on their tour. Robert slowly moved up the ranks under his name as Robert Amanze but it became quite apparent WFA were still a small time fed and they were forced to let Robert go due to their small budget. Robert joined numerous small feds such as the PCW2, XWA and the NTICW to keep up his fitness and it was during one XWA house show that he was spotted by the RUSH president Jeremy Howard. Robert was told by him that he liked what he saw but felt for Robert to survive in RUSH he would have to get a new persona and gimmick as his current one just wasn’t good enough. Robert then thought of a time when he was given direction (after the car accident) and a name his sports teacher once gave him for his tenacity 'Livewire'. Jeremy Howard loved it and a formal contract was written and the rest as they say is history.
