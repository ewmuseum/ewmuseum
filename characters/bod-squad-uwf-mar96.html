the Bod Squad

A sister-in-law tag pairing holding the UWF Women's Tag Team Championship, retaining it by disqualification over Team BATTLE*DREAM. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-09-08 – 2005-08-06
Tenure detail
Sisters-in-law Tara "Sunburst" Marshall and Brianna Landis, already billed together as UWF Women's Tag Team Champions in the held record's prior broadcasts without any surviving document naming the match in which they won the title from its own last recorded holders, the Love Sisters. Retained the championship over Team BATTLE*DREAM (Reona Yoshida and Misaki Ishikawa) by disqualification on 8 September 2004, Marshall driving a chair into Ishikawa's spine to save Landis from a spike piledriver. Wrestled a non-title six-woman tag alongside a late-arriving Donna Tetreault against Sierra Browne and Team BATTLE*DREAM on 2 October 2004 and lost, Tara "Sunburst" Marshall tased mid-match by Misaki Ishikawa before Sierra Browne's Truth Superkick finished Brianna Landis for the pin. Lost a non-title match to "Tornado" Tara Smith and Alicia Powers by pinfall (a 450 splash) on 25 May 2005. Ran in during the Wind Walkers' own women's tag match against Amazing Grace and Kari Stevens on 20 July 2005, manager John DeWolfe directing the interference until security separated everyone; the match was declared a no-decision. Teamed with manager John DeWolfe to beat Lisa Drake and the Wind Walkers by pinfall on 6 August 2005, Brianna Landis handing DeWolfe the ring bell to strike Drake from behind for the winning fall.
