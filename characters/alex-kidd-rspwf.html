UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-30 – 2007-04-28
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 15 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 5-8-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
A three-time former UWF World Heavyweight Champion returning from a two-year absence; teamed with Quinn Brown in a losing tag team main event against Scott Daniels and Tom Landis, pinned after Landis's exposed-turnbuckle Crossed-Arms Bomb. Teamed with Paul Wong, Youth Gone Wild, and Quinn Brown to a count-out win over the Pride on 22 October 2003. Attacked from behind backstage without provocation by Randall Osbourne on 12 November 2003, then wrestled Avanter to a disqualification finish the same night when the Grimssons and Alex Extreme ran in three-on-one. Entered the 17 December 2003 battle royal; his own elimination went unannounced by name. Attacked again from behind backstage without explanation by Randall Osbourne on 20 December 2003 and taken to a local hospital, before either man could compete that night. Challenged Papa Legba for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 10 March 2004 with wife Alicia Kidd at ringside; lost by pinfall (Ragga Bomb) after leaving the ring to check on his wife against a threatening Randall Osbourne, an opening Legba and interfering Tigress exploited. Assaulted Randall Osbourne before the 24 March 2004 steel cage main event, driving the cage door into his knee and forcing Osbourne to leave without ever entering the match. Teamed with wife Alicia Powers to beat Tom Landis and Tara Marshall in an intergender tag match the same night after his own Somersault Bulldog set up her pinfall; Randall Osbourne then brought out Kidd's own ex-wife, Cameo, who low-blowed Kidd and hairpulled Powers down. Teamed with Paul Wong and "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee against Team Canada and Scott Daniels on 15 May 2004 in a six-man tag that ended without a decision amid a mass brawl; carried an injured Paul Wong from his own locker room to a hospital afterward alongside Kyle Lee, after Serge Annis and Caliban attacked him. Beat Randall Osbourne by pinfall (a full nelson bulldog) at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004 in a personal grudge match complicated by Osbourne's manager, Kidd's own ex-wife Cameo, surviving a Tree of Woe facebite and a Somersault Bulldog that also knocked out Cameo. Pinned by the Hands of Death's own Near Death Experience in a losing steel cage main event on 28 August 2004 after tag partner Kyle Lee escaped the cage but could not return in time to help. Lost a tag match alongside Uriah Hawkins to Scott "Hotspot" Daniels and a substituting "Dead End" Derek Martin on 18 September 2004, pinned by Martin's own End chokeslam despite having tagged out moments before. Teamed with Sabbath in a losing effort against Jamie Underwood and "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall on 30 October 2004, Underwood countering Sabbath's own Coffin Breaker II attempt for the pin; beaten down with steel chairs afterward alongside Sabbath. Lost a Tuxedo Death Match to Edmond Winston IV on 13 November 2004, unable to answer referee Ingraham's ten count after a cobra clutch suplex, in a brutal brawl that spilled from the ring to Winston's own limousine. Returned from injury to save the One-Winged Angel from a Pridebreaker on 11 December 2004, hitting a somersault bulldog on both Scott Daniels and Derek Martin before the Pride retreated; tried and failed afterward to free Marissa Monet and Pizzazz from their own skybox chains. Fought Luke Kinsey to a double countout on 11 June 2005, then was surrounded by the Illuminati before Kinsey called them off and let him leave with his wife and manager, Alicia Powers. Beat Andrew "Flash" Tucker by pinfall (a full nelson bulldog) on 20 July 2005. Responded to Illuminati taunts on 23 July 2005 by challenging Luke Kinsey and the whole stable to a Gold Rush match, saying he would take on all four if needed. Ran in to help fend off Illuminati interference in the Underwood/Vyolynce match on 3 August 2005; returned to challenge the entire Illuminati at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, teaming with the Fraternity Boys to run the group off after German-suplexing Luke Kinsey into a corner. Teamed with "Tornado" Tara Smith in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, eliminated by Brett Greene's superbomb after warning Greene backstage to take care of wife and tag partner Alicia Powers. Teamed with wife Alicia Powers to beat Kari Stevens and "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall on 7 June 2006. Ran in to help John Shock against Madison J. Valentine and Colby Greene after Shock's own three-way dance win on 28 April 2007, then was clipped from behind and put in a scorpion deathlock by a still-unidentified masked man before all three beat down both fallen men.
