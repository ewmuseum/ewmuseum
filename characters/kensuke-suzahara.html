Biography (PURE (2002))

<center><b><i>History</i></b></center> Kensuke Suzahara made his debut in CCW, expected to be nothing more than a gimmicky Japanese-gimmicked character with some martial arts skill to put over the established talent in CCW. He was Johnny Flame, destined to be nothing more than a comedy wrestler. An enhancement talent. A kid. Life's funny like that. Kensuke pulled up his boots, and managed to become a living legend in CCW, due to his demeanor as an old-school face. He formed the New Strangers, one of the longest-running stables in sports entertainment, and had MANY memorable feuds and title reigns. CCW entered an interfed called FedWars. Immediately, Alexander Miyagawa and Johnny Flame sought each other out, as they were two of the minority of Japanese wrestlers in the United States. A brutal war of words ensued, sparking off a bitter feud that would send Kensuke over to PCW first as Miya's ally...but later, as his enemy. He was fired shortly after their match at 'Back To Old School.' Reappearing a few months later, after an injury in CCW sent him packing, Kensuke went on a winning streak unparalleled in CCW history. At Group Therapy 2001, Kensuke won the CCW Heavyweight Title to the approval of millions of fans. But that's just not enough sometimes. Since then, Kensuke has been discontent with his lot in life, as while loved, he still did not get enouugh respect from the 'boys' backstage. Yet he still played to the fans that adore him. But recently...things have gone wrong. He lost the CCW Heavyweight Title to an archnemesis, Cianan. His most hated enemy, Neurosis came back, and usurped him as the biggest babyface in CCW. His girlfriend left him. And John Jupiter prevented him from going down in CCW history as the first ever Universal Champion. Backstage at CCW's pay-per-view event Irish Spring, Kensuke brutalized his tag team partner, Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen with a chair, and gave him his finishing move, the Stranger, onto an open chair. Kyle's wrestling career is over. Currently suspended from CCW. Currently in possession of a VERY MEAN streak. And in PURE for two reasons. (1) To get revenge on his former stablemates, the Elements (Miyagawa & Inphino Blitz). (2) To get back at the fans of Japan for 'turning on him,' he intends to prove success is his birthright, even though the business has been 'bad' to him. He is the messiah for the disenfranchised, the modern-day Hamlet. The most loved susperstar in CCW history is destined to become the most hated in PURE history. Do you think you can stop him? He doesn't. And neither does Alexander Miyagawa, the owner of PURE. Because Alexander has been taken out of commission, after Ikusa I. Who controls PURE?
