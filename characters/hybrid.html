Biography (UWF (2001–07))

He arrived in UWF upon GIWF's demise as El Metralla. After a reasonably impressive run, he had to leave to help his wife Julia care for their baby Ben during his partial illness. Metralla returned a couple of months later and denied vigorously that he was Adam Finn. This was revealed to be no lie, as Blade was revealed as the one under the mask (the second time around)! Blade and Hybrid formed a tag team known as "The Lost Prophets" and joined the UWF's new incarnation of The Youngbloods.
