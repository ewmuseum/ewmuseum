Dan Ryan

aka "THE EGO BUSTER" DAN RYAN, Ego Buster, 'Ego Buster' Dan Ryan, "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan, Frankie Scott, Phenomenal Frankie Scott, The Ego Buster

Dan Ryan's NFW World Heavyweight Championship reign lasted barely two months, won at Supercrash I in February 2008 — one stop in a wrestling career that ran from 1998 to 2013, touched the CSWA, EUWC, NAPW and half a dozen more promotions besides, and also carried him to the EUWC United States and World Tag Team Championships and the NAPW World Championship.
