Seymour Almasy

aka The Final Fantasy, almasyDEATH, Orphan, Emoticon, The Protagonist

Thirty title reigns under a straight-faced Final Fantasy X cosplay, World Heavyweight runs in the UWF and in ACW among them, the second ACW reign won on a 2009 return whose only stated goal, by his own card there, was to have fun, a 2003 sweep of Wrestler of the Year, Best Gimmick, and Best Mic-Worker, a masked half-year as Emoticon in which he took the PTC Infinite Gauntlet from Rich Rollins before unmasking on Global Wrestling's pay-per-view, the PTC Tag Team Championship won beside his ex-wife Aerith Heartilly as Unholy Matrimony, a 2005 arrival in GCW unbeaten in singles until Rich Rollins took their Mount Gagazet Death Match, a place in the Triangles match on the Atlantic Wrestling Club's last show, a 2011 return to PRIME, the one company he had promised never to work for, that left him its final Intense Champion, and a 2012 run as Orphan through four rounds of FWrestling.com's ULTRATITLE tournament before Castor V. Strife stopped him one match short of its final four — closing a career the Museum holds sacred and frozen exactly where Sean Simon Williams left it. Written by AI, 2026

He won the ACW Television Championship at breakOUT on 6 February 2005, was awarded the Scorpion Fighting Championship with it because the fed had merged the two belts, and split them again himself on Courage 72 — "the two titles will REMAIN SEPARATE!" — setting his own rule for which belt went up in which kind of match. I'm done."

Tenure detail
First seen in PRIME's segments of 14 September 2005 (ReVolution 75), attacking Karina Wolfenden as a GCW star ahead of PTC's Super Series; seen again at ReVolution 108 (13 October 2006), billed by its commentary as the reigning PTC Global Champion, and at ReVolution 110 (8 November 2006), charging the ring after Rich Rollins. His PRIME card counts 2010 as the first year since 2003 he made no televised appearance anywhere, and names PRIME the one company he had publicly promised never to work for. The show record also has him as a surprise entrant in the Dual Halo at Culture Shock 2008, Night Two, on 15 March 2008, billed by the commentators as the PTC legend and eliminated thirteenth, by Dusk's superkick. In 2011 it adds the ReVolution 243 Battle Royal on 1 April, in which he beat Chainz before Brian Postal pinned him; the opening of ReVolution 244 on 14 April, where he stood in the ring as the new Intense Champion between the Codemaster and Thanatos, and a double countout with Big Bear later that night; an appeal to pray for Japan at ReVolution 246 on 18 May; and, at ReVolution 247 on 16 June, a no contest with Brian Postal and a countout win over his own bodyguard Thanatos that put him in that year's Dual Halo.
