Maxmillion Productions

Zeus Maxmillion's film company, whose stars Rudy Velasquez and T-Rex also fight as a team. In October 2007 Zeus Maxmillion, Rudy Velasquez and T-Rex offer their services to Divinity, are given a match, and are then beaten by Triple D, Fumihiro Ota and Human Arsenal and told to stay away. In October and November 2007 Zeus Maxmillion and Rudy Velasquez lose a #1 contendership match to The Valentines when Fumihiro Ota kicks Casey Valentine, lose to Team Seduction in a match the narrator rates a B+, and are left with nothing to do, the narrator says, though fans like Maxmillion and dislike Velasquez, who gets a new look. Devine hires them in November 2007 to find the Masked Man; they tell him it is Wolf Hawkins, Devine turns on them and crushes T-Rex under a vending machine when it proves wrong, and they are booked against The Hotshots at Winter Epiphany.
