The H.I.T. — Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid

aka H.I.T., Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, Justin Williamson, The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid

A geometry pun played dead straight as a hardcore wrecking machine, Sean's second UWF creation alongside Almasy, with four reigns with the Hardcore title, one of them won by pinning a table. Before UWF he wrestled in IWO, whose own news of May 2000 had him promised a World Title match. At Epic Wrestling he won the North American Championship on 23 December 2001 in a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch of his own devising. In 2005 PRIME saw him purge the Trapezoid to wrestle as Justin Williamson, the Deville's Disciple, and win the Tag Team Championship with Angelo Deville as Manifest Destiny. Written by AI, 2026

Billed “The HIT” on the Ikusa IV card of 4 June 2002, in a six-man tag alongside Ali Khadaffi and Carl Kimbell. PURE's own columnist ranks him fifth in the Heavyweight division and second in the Jr. Heavyweight one, names him among the finalists of the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title tournament alongside Kensuke Suzahara and The End, and calls him an “IWO superstar known for his extreme love of dangerous matches and mathematics.” His run at the belt is dated by the fed's own scripts. At Ikusa, Expo '70 Stadium, Osaka, on 14 April 2002 he beat Cege Hayabusa in a Jr. Heavyweight Title tournament round with the Four Thirds Pi Times Radius Cubed. At Ikusa II, Matsumoto Stadium, Nagano, on 28 April 2002 he reached the three-way the fed billed on air as the crowning of its first ever champion, against Kensuke Suzahara and The End: he had Suzahara in the Word Problem and was closest of the three to winning it when a masked man pulled Suzahara out of the ring, and The End made him tap to Never Again to win it. At Ikusa V, Sapporo Dome, on 16 June 2002 he and Charlie Loc beat The End and Brian Simon with the belt on the line, finishing The End with a double powerbomb into a blue thunder facecrusher and covering him for the three, and the fed's own ring announcer called it: “Winner…and NEW PURE JUNIOR HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…THE HARDCORE ISOSCELES TRAPEZOID!!!” It is the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Championship his own PCW bio card claims, and the fed's own complete Junior Heavyweight Championship history numbers him its second champion: “Pinned The End during a six man tag team match to win the title at Ikusa III”, the shape of the Ikusa V bout under another show's name. The fed's own earlier paperwork bills The End because The End held the belt when it was written — the division rankings of 2 May 2002 and the Ikusa IV card of 4 June 2002 both predate the change — and its own champions page carries only the holder of the moment, in states that begin in January 2003. His own PURE roster card is the one document that reads against the reign: its PURE title-history field gives “None as of yet” and its PURE award field is blank. All of it stands. The same card confirms his masked run: its own bio text ends “Masking himself, he is now ready to face the best in PURE”, and PURE v2.0 Ikusa 3 wrestles him masked throughout (the text has Enmity grabbing “the back of his mask” and a cut opening “under the mask”). The card does not mention a win over Ivan Stanislav at PURE, which stands uncorroborated — PURE v2.0 Ikusa 3's own commentary says only that Stanislav is the one man ever to survive the Four Thirds Pi Times Radius Cubed, which is a kickout, not a win. The run reaches further than the Ikusa IV card alone shows: he appears at Gekito 1 on 14 August 2002 (billed for a post-show autograph signing, not on the card) and wrestles Enmity on PURE v2.0 Ikusa 3, a show the record can place no closer than 2002 — so the span closes at year precision. PURE's own news row of 7 January 2003 calls him “the former PURE athlete and now PCW breakout star Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid”, closing the run from the other side. The card's own “real name” field reads “Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid/Horribly Insane Trapezoid,” the phrase on his concept sketch. Three cards disagree gently on vitals: UWF bills him from Ashville, North Carolina at 230 lbs and Heel; PCW and PURE both bill Salt Lake City, Utah (221 lbs PCW, 222 lbs PURE), and PURE bills him Neutral where UWF bills Heel — his alignment shifting between feds and eras. PURE's own setup move, “Parallel Bases,” named without a number, plausibly the seed of UWF's later numbered “Parallel Bases IV.”

Biography (PURE (2002))

The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid has had a very interesting life, to say the least. He is a man of many talents, and almost as many quirks. When one lives in Mormon Salt Lake City, and is an atheist, one can expect many oddities. He was born to loving parents Jeffrey and Maria in a nice middle class neighborhood in Salt Lake City. Soon after his birth, the loving couple had family over at their house, to celebrate the HIT's birth. To this day, his first and last name are unknown. Since he has legally changed his name to The HIT, (Mr. Trapezoid for teaching) and his parents will not speak to the media, it is likely this information will remain forever unknown. While passing young HIT to an uncle, Jeffrey unfortunately bobbled the child, dropping the infant on the side of his head. Maria immediately called 911, and the paramedics arrived, rushing the baby to the hospital. At first, it appeared no real damage was done. However, in his emergency room, young Trapezoid woke up next to a second grade student, who was desperately studying his shapes. One had been carelessly left on his bed. Trapezoid saw it...and the image on it, an isosceles trapezoid, would change the HIT's life forever. In his early years, he believed that he WAS an isosceles trapezoid. He enjoyed counting games from the time he was six months old. He was well ahead of everyone else in his classes mathematically, studying such subjects as geometry and algebra when he was in 6th grade. He finished the most advanced calculus course his high school had to offer by 9th grade, leaving the school no choice but to bus him to Brigham Young University to take more advanced mathematics. Sadly, this would be the best college education he would get. His parents were too poor to send him to college, so he attended a local two-year community college, and graduated with honors, in math of course. His degree was in business, a subject he despised. However, he knew mathematics was his passion. So, he decided to use his degree to get something he always wanted.... A substitute teaching license. Going back to Brigham Young High School, The HIT stepped in as a substitute geometry teacher. His odd teaching techniques and bizarre behavior quickly made him a favorite substitute among students. While nearly being barred for life due to his substitute health teaching foray, after persuading the principal with what would come to be known as the SAS Postulate, The HIT was granted a permanent position as a 10th grade Geometry teacher, known to all as Mr. Trapezoid. Many of his students were wrestling fans, as was The HIT. For years, the HIT had been practicing on the side, joining various schools. He signed a contract with the IWO's IWO Minor League, or the IML. He then worked out a deal with the school, in which he would only teach one period a day to allow for his schedule. This makes each of his students extra special. He debuted in the IML to rave reviews, capturing the IML United States Title in a three way match against Lance McNally and the champion Phyre with a 450 splash. Soon after though, he got the call up to the IWO. Dropping the belt to The Executioner, he was ready for the next stage of his career. In the IWO, he came out fast. Many expected him to be an utter joke, yet he rose quickly through the ranks. Facing Jax Stone for the IWO North American title, he dropped one fall to Jax's Drop Like a Stone, yet took two straight falls for the title. He held this for about fifteen days, before dropping it to "The Real Heel" Evan Levine. From here, he was booked to take on Samuel Potright in a Trick or Treat match, the second of its kind. Trick or Treat II started out as being over the Extreme title, but quickly turned into a rivalry. The HIT emerged victorious in one of the bloodiest matches of all time, but his injury toll was staggering. Over 200 stitches, a broken leg, second degree burns over around 15 percent of his body..all of this forced him to vacate the Extreme Title a day later. While on the shelf for six months, the HIT's warped mind went even further off the deep end, as he formulated a plan to go after Potright. Competing under a mask as Why, he assaulted Potright, and kidnapped his lovely wife Beth. After using a knife to draw her blood, and then drink it from a glass, he convinced the then World Champion to accept Trick or Treat III for the World Title. After the most emotional, violent, and sadistic match in the history of professional wrestling, Sam Potright drove Trapezoid headfirst into the mat in overtime with War Within a Breath to defeat him. However, an executive quirk left The HIT World Champion, and Samuel unable to see Beth for the rest of his life. At this point, The HIT was heartbroken. He had the World Title...but he couldn't live with causing the emotional demise of his nemesis. He excersized a loophole in the match contract...sending him into retirement and surrendering the title, yet earning him Potright's undying respect...for then. As he recovered from this, even more insane match, he received a call from an unlikely man: Kent Anathason. He needed someone to join himself and Nuke in Primetime Championship Wrestling, to form a group called Neurosis. Neurosis formed in the summer of 2001, fighting groups such as the Dark Ages. Quickly, things degenerated, as Neurosis was thrown headlong into the path of The Red Army. Anthason and Nuke quickly left, despite HIT and Nuke teaming to nearly overthrow Da Westsidaz reign as tag team champions of PCW. This left the HIT alone to battle The Red Army. Despite much opposition from groups like The European Union, The HIT received no help in his battles. He eliminated Alexander Popov and Nikita Morozov from PCW, while capturing the PCW Cruiserweight Title from White Mexican, and the Television Title from Kid Wonder. Finally though, his run came to an end. In a retirement match against Rent-A-Hero, Ivan Stanislav, leader of The Red Army, planted HIT with The Red Scare, allowing RAH to pick up a win, and evict HIT from PCW. He immediately swore vengeance on both men. It didn't take him long to find a new federation, signing on with Epic Wrestling. In a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch, he won the promotions North American Title, holding it for two months before dropping it to nemesis Samuel Potright, and leaving. At this point, The HIT went looking back to PTC for work. He found PURE, a new Japanese promotion starting up. Seeing this as a perfect opportunity to test his skills, the HIT began a makeover. Masking himself, he is now ready to face the best in PURE. Can the HIT succeed here? We shall see... Description: The HIT has changed somewhat since his last appearance. A mask now covers his face. While his trademark red afro is still visable, a dark black mask obscures his features, a giant red 'X' drawn on it. He wears a red cape over his shoulders, and a red and black shirt, with a silver trapezoid on it. His pants are all silver, with black boots.
