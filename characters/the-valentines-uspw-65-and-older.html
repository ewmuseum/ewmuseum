The Valentines

The uncle and nephew Peter and Casey Valentine, who face The Best Tag Team Like Ever (Trent Shaffer and Chance Fortune) at Blaze of Glory in September 2007 and win by count-out when Peter, who has begged Shaffer off, hugs the referee. They beat The Best Tag Team Like Ever again at A Haunting in America in October 2007, while Peter keeps calling Casey his son. They become the #1 contenders on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 4, October 2007 when Fumihiro Ota costs Maxmillion Productions the match by disqualification, win the World Tag Team Championship from Team Seduction at Pilgrimage when Triple D turns on Arsenal, and lose it minutes later to The Hotshots. In late November and December 2007 Peter keeps insisting Casey is his son, Casey punches him and finally asks Jillefski for a match with him, and Peter wins in 4:43 by roll-up while refusing to fight back.
