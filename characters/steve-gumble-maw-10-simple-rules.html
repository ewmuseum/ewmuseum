Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Steve Gumble

A former TCW cowboy, signed a couple of hours before South East Crisis on 8th April 2012, who takes up Aaron Andrews's open challenge on the night and loses to the Twisting Face Crusher after almost thirty minutes in which he had Andrews on the verge of defeat with a suicide dive and a Savate Kick. At Spring Fever on 12th April he draws with Firebird, at May The Force Be With You on 5th May he loses to Father Johan's Crucifix Powerbomb, and at Mother, May I? on 12th May he loses to Providence's Eye Of Providence in a match the narrator says is interlinked with his plans for the masked man. In the diary's pages from June to August 2012 he draws with JD Morgan for the Traditional title at High Noon on 5th June, loses to Morgan at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, beats Citizen X with a Savate Kick at Beach Blast on 12th July, a rare win, and loses to Sean Deeley's Front Choke Lock at Summernova on 5th August, which the narrator says is perhaps his best and is his last match for the company; the narrator's news says that he has been signed away by the SWF.
