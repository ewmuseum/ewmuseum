Tenure detail
Wrestles Kenny Kidd to a no-contest on Firestorm (3 May 2004) after Thunder Valenkov's own interference, then is named alongside Rob Beauty's own brother Ric in a Gauntlet 24 Hour Championship tag match. Teams with Thunder Valenkov in a 24 Hour Championship tag match against the Beauty Brothers, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and pins Valenkov to win the VWF 24 Hour Championship himself. Attacks the Lynch Mob with his cane, Lucy, for a DQ win on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminates himself from the Dog Day One singles battle royal after assaulting a ringside official over his own 24 Hour Championship belt. Loses the Dog Day Two singles bracket's first round to LOCO, then is eliminated from the tournament, losing the VWF 24 Hour Championship match to Flash by count-out after walking off with the belt; the title does not change hands. Wrestles Daniel Sinz to a no-decision in a further 24 Hour Championship defense on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), both men walking away from the ring before a finish. Accidentally Psycho Flow DDTs tag partner Billy Bones on the outside of the ring during a six-man tag loss on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) while distracted by his own 24 Hour Championship belt. Refuses to surrender the VWF 24 Hour Championship belt when Kingston Cash declares the title no longer recognized on Wrestle Style V.1, striking Cash down; loses to a returning Classy Joey Boy by disqualification in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3 after attacking the referee rather than give up the same belt. Eliminated early from his own VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), fleeing to the back with the belt; Adam Jennings wins the battle royal and is announced champion, but Kingston Cash refuses to recognize the change unless Jennings takes the belt by force. Fails to appear for a signed follow-up against Jennings on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), ruled a no contest. Fails to appear again against Fast Eddie Cutlass on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), another no contest, then runs in with Lucy to chair Jennings after Jennings beats Kingston Cash's bodyguard G-Gangsta later the same show; Cash books Blair against Jennings in an I Quit match for the title at Vegas Violence. Loses the VWF 24 Hour Championship to Adam Jennings in an I Quit match at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), knocking out the referee and refusing to release his own grip on the belt even as Jennings works him over; finally forced to say the words after a powerslam over the top rope with both men still holding the title, then speared and sedated by ringside medics.
