Tenure detail
A former LEW Albuquerque Champion and Michael Bonn's own former MBC tag partner; beat Bonn by pinfall (an underhook sit-down powerbomb, the Ecchi Trigger Finger) in a no-disqualification Career Paths match, forcing the two into a tag team under the match's own stipulation. Manager Virginia St. Ursula, who had secretly arranged the match to confront Bonn's temper, pulled a chair away from Bonn mid-match to save him from a top-rope Deadfall; Bonn attacked Osawa again after the bell before storming off. Teamed with Michael Bonn, forced into the pairing by the terms of his own prior win over Bonn, to beat the Sons of Cacaphony (Erik and Chad Grimsson) by disqualification on 16 October 2004 in the pair's own first match together, after Erik Grimsson attacked a non-competing Jacob Drake at ringside. Was beaten down by Team Canada after his own loss to Trevor Hextall on 1 December 2004, saved by a returning "Showtime" Rick Marley. On 11 December 2004, tried to stop Trevor Hextall from using a lead pipe on Spice backstage during a Pride-Fury brawl and was jumped by Bob Jablonski for his trouble. Egged tag partner Michael Bonn on through an apology to Chris O'Brien on 8 June 2005, then traded online-game banter with O'Brien once the apology was accepted. Advanced in a Road to the Gold qualifier on 22 June 2005 when Niklos Romani failed to appear, accompanied to the ring by "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn and manager Virginia St. Ursula; needled tag partner Bonn beforehand over Virginia's own legs, drawing a choking grip from Bonn. Accompanied Bonn to his own Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament match on 6 July 2005 and revealed he had won naming rights to their own still-unnamed tag team. Had a football helmet placed on his own pet bird, Trice, by manager Virginia St. Ursula backstage before losing a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament first-round match to L. Dan Dee on 20 July 2005 by pinfall (a piledriver), after Daniel Kidd ran in from the crowd and speared him into the steel steps. Escorted valet Virginia St. Ursula ringside during the Landis/Bonn match on 3 August 2005 while pet bird Trice heckled the broadcast; opened Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005 with a comic bit blaming Trice for an unpaid restaurant bathroom visit. Threw Tom Landis off the top rope during Juan Vasquez's debut match on 18 March 2006, turning back a Cornerstones interference attempt.
