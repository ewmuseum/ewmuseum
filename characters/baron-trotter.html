wrestler, GWF International Xtreme Champion
Tenure detail
Held the International Xtreme Championship into the 2009 reunion tour; a billed 4 April 2009 defense against a returning Mark Madison was never captured with a result. At 20113 he interrupted and ended the Cruiserweight Championship match, then won a triple threat over XO Malone and Debolt Dragonsbane to become the Undisputed GWF International Xtreme Champion, and reached the closing show's own Money in the Bank ladder match.
