Jonathan Marx

aka "Gentleman" Jonathan Marx, Gentleman, Gentleman Jonathan Marx

'Gentleman' Jonathan Marx, announced as a newcomer in a promo at Milwaukee Riptide on 28 March 2003, beat Ryan Roberts, Billy Matthews and M.W. Grossard in the spring, lost to Dakota Smith at Akron Wired and to Maelstrom for the title at Joliet Wired, and pinned Dakota with Manson at Wisconsin Dells: 4 wins in 8 matches on its cards.
