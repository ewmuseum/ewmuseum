Tenure detail
Runs in during the Firestorm (26 Feb 2004) non-title Sinz/Blair match and is presented afterward as a new member of the New Age Army; does not wrestle a match that night. Blindsides arriving VWF 24 Hour Champion Ric Beauty during the Kidd/Blair match on Firestorm (3 May 2004), then is named in a Gauntlet tag match against the Beauty Brothers for the title. Pins Ric Beauty to reclaim the VWF 24 Hour Championship in a tag match against the Beauty Brothers, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and is pinned by his own tag partner Kevin Blair, losing the title again the same night. Returns to the VWF as a mystery entrant on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), his own work permit issue since resolved, and wins the tag team title battle royal by eliminating Sebastian last, joining Mark McQueen as VWF Tag Team Champion. Teams with McQueen and 24 Hour Champion Adam Jennings on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), flattening Robbie Caldwell with the Thunder Bomb before Jennings finishes him. Teams with Mark McQueen in a non-title tag against Fast Eddie Cutlass and G-Gangsta on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), ruled a no contest after the Riders of the Apocalypse run in on former member G-Gangsta. Wins the nineteen-man Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005), eliminating Paddy O'Shea, Sonicman, Robbie Caldwell, Pagan and The Equalizer to earn the final entry into The Gauntlet. Teams with Mark McQueen to retain the VWF Tag Team Championship over the Rodeo Boys in a Rings Of Glory match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), pinning Billy Bones to join his own partner's ring, then finishing Kenny Kidd alone with the Thunder Bomb after McQueen taps out to the Elko Lasso. Loses to Mr Electricity Robbie Caldwell at March To Madness (2 March 2005) after the Riders of the Apocalypse blind him with a smoke bomb and knock him out with a chair. Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship with Mark McQueen at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) in an impromptu three-way after Team Brittania's own Simon Davidson pins Frank O'Shea instead of either champion. Challenges the champions Team Brittania for the titles at End Of March (30 March 2005) with McQueen, winning by disqualification after the returning Hot Property run in on his own partner. Turns on his own tag partner Mark McQueen with a Thunder Bomb on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), letting Hot Property win the disputed VWF Tag Team Championship match, then re-joins returning agent Chris Wheeling. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal last, as tradition dictates for a non-champion, and is eliminated in the match's final fall when The Equalizer reverses an Irish whip into a top rope that Hot Property forgets to pull down.
