Jonathon Winters

A masked attacker who debuted by ambushing Kash and taking his WWA World Title League spot in August 2004, Jonathon Winters went on to hold the WWA United States, Extreme, and Television Championships across 2005 and 2006, becoming a double champion by beating Kid Cool for the Extreme title in the same tournament final, then in PRIME held the Alias Title for 73 days from July 2006 and the 5 Star Title twice, in 2006 and 2007.
