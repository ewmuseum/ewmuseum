Darryl Devine

aka Triple D, DDD, Darryl Daniel Devine

A lower-midcard wrestler with the Devine Dream Drop who becomes Darryl Daniel Devine, "Triple D", and is hounded by Peter Valentine for no reason he can find. He keeps the World Championship against Muffin at Blaze of Glory in September 2007, forms Divinity with Seduction and others, and beats Muffin down in a cage match. In October 2007 he keeps the World Championship against Frankie Perez, Bruce the Giant, Sam Strong and Davey Wayne with the help of the Hotshots, Seduction and a pulled pair of tights; a masked man appears above the entrance at A Haunting in America and again on the Monday, and Freddie Datsun walks off with his belt. In October and November 2007 he attacks William Hayes in Japan, stays in the ring to finish Umaga rather than rescue the kidnapped Seduction and Muffin from the Masked Man's video, loses Seduction and Team Seduction, beats Wanda Fish and Human Arsenal in singles, costs Team Seduction the tag belts at Pilgrimage and keeps the World Championship against Freddie Datsun there when the Masked Man hits Datsun and Jillefski counts the pin. In late November and December 2007 he hits Seduction, slams a door on Human Arsenal's forearm, pays Maxmillion Productions to unmask the Masked Man, drags Wolf Hawkins to the ring as the Masked Man and sees the real one revealed as Jack Giedroyc, beats Arsenal (26:35), Rick Law, Hawkins and Reaper, accepts Giedroyc's challenge for Winter Epiphany and wrecks Jillefski's office and Big Cat Brandon.
