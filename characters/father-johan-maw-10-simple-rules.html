Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Father Johan

An evil preacher with fiery red hair and a priest's collar, Firebird's "Father" in his address book, whom Firebird calls in at Spring Fever on 12th April 2012 while Black Eagle is incapacitated, and who arrives at May The Force Be With You on 5th May, kisses Firebird's ring on his hand and says Eagle's wound is grievous but survivable. He beats Steve Gumble with a Crucifix Powerbomb that night, KC Glenn at Great Lakes Madness on 9th May after telling him that he would be welcomed into the family, and with Firebird beats KC Glenn and Kirk Jameson at Mother, May I? on 12th May; he drives Sienna LeNoir from the room as a harlot. The narrator writes that he adores the character, having wanted an evil preacher for a while. In the diary's pages from June to July 2012 he loses a pole match to Donnie J at High Noon on 5th June, where he bludgeons Donnie with the title belt, and tells Firebird that The Fallen must recruit; he beats Bradford Peverell with the Crucifix Powerbomb at Summer Showdown on 12th June speaks to the Second Sons about joining, and with Providence beats Thea Davis and Jaime Quine at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July. At Beach Blast on 12th July the lumberjack match with KC Glenn ends when both men fall from the top turnbuckle to the arena floor; the narrator says his MAW career limps to an end, that the character was excellent but the skills mediocre, and that John "Father Johan" Greed has signed with the SWF.
