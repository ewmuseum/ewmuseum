Jason O'Neil

The Scarecrow from Cleveland, who teamed with Peter Maxwell as The Legal Department and wrestled twice for PRIME between November 2006 and March 2007, held the GCW Hardcore Championship from 1 September 2002, was inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame in 2009 and appeared on 30 GCW cards from 11 October 2001 to 5 March 2010.
