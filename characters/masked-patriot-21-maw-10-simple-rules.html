Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Masked Patriot #21

aka Providence

A fan in a stars-and-stripes mask, with a sign calling himself Masked Patriot #21, who is photographed with the Patriot at The Rip Chord Invitational in January 2010; the Patriot takes him under his wing, and when Brandon Smith unmasks at March Madness in March 2010 he becomes the new Masked Patriot in The Watch, loses to Primus Allen's Running Powerslam at Spring Fever and beats Raphael with a Patriot Slam at Mother, May I?. He takes Jay Chord close in a Heavyweight title challenge at Beach Blast in July 2010, with the Delaware crowd calling Jay a Commie, loses to Hugh de Aske at Summernova and to Jean Cattley at Back To School, and is the target of Jay's jibes about his mask and tights until Rip warns Jay off. He is forced to submit to Ricochet in a three-way with Kirk Jameson at Where It All Begins Again in October, and the narrator's roster overview calls him the "protégé" of Brandon Smith whom the game's auto-push put above his teacher. He beats Firebird with the Patriot Slam at Remember November in November 2010, beats Greg Ford at Season's Beatings and loses to Primus Allen at New Year, Old Rivalries and Hugh de Aske in the 2011 Invitational. Firebird's taunts make him hesitant, and he loses to Firebird in February, is thrown by Thrash with Fumihiro Ota at All Or Nothing, and at March Madness beats Thrash and refuses Firebird's offered hand. He loses to JD Morgan's Cross Atlantic Stretch at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, spears Thrash and chairs Hugh de Aske on 12th May, and at Mother, May I? on 26th May appears in a new black-and-red mask, re-christened Providence by Firebird, saying "I am Providence", and loses the Traditional title match to Hugh de Aske before bowing low to him. Sienna LeNoir comes with him, and he pins Max Mayhem with JD Morgan at Hotter Than Hot, beats Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson with Firebird at Summer Showdown, beats Bradford Peverell with the Eye Of Providence at Summernova, and loses to Kirk Jameson when Jameson drives his skull into the exposed steel at Back To School. As Providence he beats Brandon Smith at All The Leaves Are Brown on 12th October 2011 and Fumihiro Ota at Where It All Begins Again, beats Kirk Jameson and Kid Arachnid with Citizen X at Remember November, loses an All Action three-way to JD Morgan at End Of Days and loses a first-round Invitational tie to Fumihiro Ota on 12th January 2012; the narrator's note of 1st November calls him a personal project, an unhinged character he enjoys writing for. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 as Providence he beats Amazing Fire Fly at Leap Into The Unknown, loses to Brandon Smith at All Or Nothing, beats Jared Johnson at Great Lakes Crisis, Lassana Makutsi at Third Time's The Charm and Steve Gumble at Mother, May I? on 12th May, and loses to Kirk Jameson at April Showers; he tells Jameson that he lives by impulse and that his warning to stay out of his business is the last, and at Mother, May I? tells the crowd that everyone will get what is coming to them, "We'll make sure of that". In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 as Providence he turns up with a clown face painted on at Summer Showdown on 12th June, draws with Kirk Jameson and spears KC Glenn, and stands over the beaten faces with the rest of The Fallen when Aaron Andrews turns on them; he beats Thea Davis and Jaime Quine with Father Johan at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, Jared Johnson at Great Lakes Grand Parade, loses with The Great Ota to Jameson and JD Morgan at Summernova, loses a ladder match for the All Action title to Donnie J at South East Grand Parade, and wins the Back To School six-man with Eddie Powell and Sean Deeley. TEW.com, the narrator's news says, again calls him a star of the future, able to play heel and face roles and a wide variety of characters. Written by AI, 2026
