Tenure detail
SEAN'S CAPITAL PUNISHMENT IS THIS ONE, not the EWA of 1998 or the WeWF's six-man team of 1991. Statements from outside the fiction name the character as his, over seven years: Sean's own post of 20 October 2003 lists among what he is proudest of “Winning the IWO World Title as Capital Punishment” (PTC forum topic 688, post 10), and again on 29 November 2004, “I was in the OLD ass IWO as Capital Punishment and The HIT” (topic 11465, post 2), and on 15 September 2006, “well known as The HIT, Politically Incorrect, and Capital Punishment at various times” (topic 17800, post 23); a third party writes on 7 January 2004 of Sean as the “handler of the HIT and Capital Punishment and probably like ten other wrestlers as well” (topic 2144, post 5), and on 16 January 2004 calls the character “Cappy”, the man he fought in IWO (topic 2352, post 18); and Sean's own list of 15 February 2010 opens “Eligible characters: Capital Punishment, The HIT, Seymour Almasy, Kenjiro Ito, Logic, Politically Incorrect” (topic 27795, post 21). ACW's own text fits at both ends: its webshow of 7 May 2012 introduces “former IWO wrestling legend, Capital Punishment” in an IWO retro t-shirt, and its Two Nights recap of 21 February 2013 calls him “Cappy” and “The IWO Legend”. Those two ACW documents, which bound his run, are also two of the ACW documents already on Sean's memorial for Seymour Almasy's Orphan, so this hand was working ACW in exactly those months. The other two cannot be his: the WeWF's Capital Punishment is a six-man team of April and May 1991, when he was five or six, and the EWA's wrestled one autumn, September to November 1998, with nothing in its text tying it to him.
Source: the fed's own fan board thread "ACW Presents World.Wide.Wrestlecide: F*** You, Our Webshow Is Better!" (fwrestling.com forum id 541), posted 7 May 2012; PTC's own forum, topics 688 (post 10), 2144 (post 5), 2352 (post 18), 11465 (post 2), 17800 (post 23) and 27795 (post 21)
