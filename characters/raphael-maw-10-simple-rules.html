Raphael

Antonio's "personal assistant" with a Bluetooth earpiece and a Blackberry, who wrestles alongside him. A PPA deal with 4C is reported in February 2011; he and Antonio lose to Cattley and Singh at Leap Into The Unknown. He loses to Riley McManus, Kid Arachnid and Ernest Youngman and to "Sensational" Singh on the tour shows, beats Jonnie Perez and Ben Williams, is on the narrator's list of expendables for lack of direction, and joins the Canadian Blondes against Smith, Singh and Ota at Back To School. In the diary's pages from February to April 2012 he beats Wanda Fish and Thea Davis with Citizen X at Leap Into The Unknown, beats Lassana Makutsi at All Or Nothing, loses to Bradford Peverell at Third Time's The Charm and beats Amazing Fire Fly at Spring Fever on 12th April. The narrator checks his new contract in February; in May he notes that the midcarder has parted ways with the company on amicable terms. In the diary's pages from June to July 2012 he loses the High Noon six-man on 5th June to Bradford Peverell's Dream Left Hook, beats Thea Davis with an Italian DDT at South East Madness on 9th June, and loses to Kirk Jameson's Bullseye at Beach Blast on 12th July, the narrator's let-down; the narrator's news says that he has signed a pay-per-appearance deal with CGC.
