The Dudley Boys

The tag team that holds the ECW World Tag Team Championship through the diary's first ten shows, beating the Hardy Boys for it on the first and eighth. In March and April 2007 the Dudley Boys, billed as the enforcers of ECW, beat The Sandman on Blood, Sweat and Beers at Tommy Dreamer's booking, are eliminated together by Rhino at Baptism Of Fire, and beat Big Stevie Cool and Hollywood Nova with 3Ds on Blood, Sweat and Beers and Revolution while the Sasuke World Order hunt them. At Cyberslam in April 2007 the Dudley Boys take out Los Guerreros with 3Ds and a Spike Powerbomb through tables in the three-way dance for the tag number one contendership, but the Canadian Connection run in and pin them both; in May D-Von pins Chavo Guerrero Jr. with a 3D. The Dudley Boys return at Wrestlepalooza in June 2007 to attack the Whole F**kin' Show, and in July beat the Hardcore Innovators for the number one contendership with the help of Big Dick Dudley Jr. and Daizy, whom they introduce on Revolution as the children of their missing brother Big Dick Dudley. They lose the Tables Match for the World Tag Team Championship at Summer Assylum, then rename the old World Tag Team belts the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles, keep them over the bWo with the sWo's interference and over Balls & Axl, and are challenged by the Innovators and Los Guerreros. In August 2007 they keep the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles over Los Guerreros (a 3D on Chavo) on Revolution, over the Samoan Gangstas (a 3D on Anoai) on Blood, Sweat and Beers and over the Briscoe Boys in their last defence before Heatwave, all with their relatives' help, and the Hardcore Innovators then cane the second generation, DDT the two of them and walk off with the belts; at Heatwave Cactus Jack and Tommy Dreamer put D-Von through a table with a Double DDT and win the titles. In September they lose a tag title match to the Whole F**kin' Show on Revolution when simultaneous finishers beat Bubba Ray and D-Von, D-Von is pinned by Shawn Michaels's Super Kick despite interference from Big Dick Jr. and Ultimo Dudley II, Bubba Ray pins Michaels with a 3D in a non-title match, and the mystery Dudley, Slyk Tayshaun, attacks them both. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 the second generation of Dudleyz break up the Champions versus Champions match, Bubba Ray and D-Von put the Hardcore Innovators down with a 3D, and the Dudleyz take both sets of tag belts from ringside; D-Von beats Tommy Dreamer on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and on the first Revolution of October Shawn Michaels pins Bubba Ray with a Super Kick. Paul Heyman books the Three Way Dance for the tag titles at Guilty As Charged, and there the Dudley Boyz pin Rob Van Dam after Michaels's Super Kick takes him down and win the unified belts, which the Hardcore Innovators carry out of the arena. In November 2007 Bubba Ray and D-Von, standing with the Dudley Family, challenge the Hardcore Innovators, who walk around with all four tag belts, to a title match at November To Remember XV; Slyk Tayshaun Dudley then chairs the Dudley Family and the two of them, and D-Von tells him that he is fouling his own doorstep. On Blood, Sweat and Beers Paul Heyman announces the Undisputed ECW Tag Team belts and a match in which the Dudley Boyz and the Innovators unify the titles, and the diary's preview of the card bills it with the Dudley Boyz as the champions. At November To Remember XV (30 November 2007 in the diary's history) Bubba Ray and D-Von, with Big Dick Jr., Ultimo Dudley II and Daizy, beat the Hardcore Innovators in a weapons match: D-Von lifts Beulah McGillicutty for Bubba's Superbomb through a table, and a Dudley Death Drop through a flaming table pins Tommy Dreamer; they raise the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles, and Slyk Tayshaun Dudley then comes to the ring with a baseball bat and beats down every one of his relatives. On Revolution on the first Monday of December they defend the Undisputed belts against the Thomaselli brothers, the match is restarted as a Three Way Dance with Slyk and Dick Jr., and Slyk breaks up Bubba's pin on Brandon and makes Bubba tap to the Boston Crabs. The diary's next preview bills them as the 'Original Dudleyz'; on Saturday, with Ultimo Dudley II, they chair and powerbomb the Thomasellis through a table after the new champions' first defence.
