Team XDW

aka Team XDW II

Extreme Deluxe, Xavier Reckless and Super Sonic, the XDW survivors who, in the storyline the narrator describes in June 2011, assault MAW with backyard and hardcore tactics, with Daredevil Aero and Fearless Blue trying to stop them. They beat The Quest on 25th June and 15th September and the West Texas Peacekeepers and The Quest on 1st September, lose to the Young Americans on 11th August and are squashed by Smith & Singh at Iron; billed as Team XDW II they lose to the Internet Favourites on 11th July, and with the Young Americans they lose a three-way to the West Texas Peacekeepers on 18th July. The group is released with the rest of the third brand on 1st November 2011. Written by AI, 2026
