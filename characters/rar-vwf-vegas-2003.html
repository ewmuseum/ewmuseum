VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2004-11-08
Tenure detail
Takes a phone call as the Rodeo Boys' own lawyer on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), hinting at good news for his clients ahead of Vegas Violence. Announces the Rodeo Boys, Billy Bones and Kenny Kidd, are gone for good at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), then talks Kingston Cash into reinstating them on the spot, unleashing them on the new VWF Tag Team Champions. Protests when the referee confiscates Kenny Kidd's own cowbell during the VWF Tag Team Championship match and is tossed from ringside on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Demands the tag titles and a Rage in the Cage spot from Kingston Cash, then joins the Rodeo Boys in ambushing an injured Kevin Simmons backstage and low-blows Mark McQueen during the title rematch on March To Hell II (28 October 2004). Accompanies the Rodeo Boys to a Number One Contenders win at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004).
