Tenure detail
Billed from Cape Fear, North Carolina, accompanied by Angel; lost a non-title singles match to Sierra Browne. Teamed with Tigress and Angel as the Coven on 22 October 2003, pinning Leanna Love with a top-rope Santa Maria in a win over the Love Sisters and Comedy. Teamed with Angel as the Furies in a losing effort by disqualification against the Bod Squad (Bliss, Tara "Sunburst" Marshall) on 12 November 2003, refusing to release a leg-and-hair stretch on Bliss through the ropes. Lost to Donna Tetreault by referee stoppage on 24 March 2004 after being knocked out with a ringside bucket, now accompanied by Ravnos Romani rather than Angel. Lost to "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict by pinfall on 5 May 2004 despite twice nearly winning by small package; Benedict applied a post-match Morbid Sting until Arielle Starr ran in to break it up. Joked with the Amazing Romanis backstage about a soda can before their own tag win on 25 May 2005, then clashed in the locker room with newcomer Amazing Grace over the state of the women's division. Ran in to save Lisa Drake from a Bod Squad beatdown on 8 June 2005 after John DeWolfe's own dismissal of the women's division, then fought a tense backstage encounter with newcomer Amazing Grace before teaming with Drake against Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith in a match the Bod Squad broke up as a no contest. Ran down to help clear the ring of the Bod Squad during the Wind Walkers' own women's tag match on 20 July 2005, throwing Amazing Grace from the ring with a headscissors. Made the save for Arielle Starr against Amazing Grace and Kari Stevens on 3 August 2005, agreeing to team with Starr against the pair at Gold Rush. Teamed with Ravnos Romani in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, pinning Alicia Powers with a bridging German suplex before "Tornado" Tara Smith eliminated her.
