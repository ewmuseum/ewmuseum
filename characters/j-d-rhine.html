Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Growing up the only child of two prominent NYC Defense Attorneys, J.D. was able to ge the finest of anything that he had ever wanted. He was sent to the finest private schools that money could afford. In those very schools is where the foundation of his future would be cemented. In those schools he learned everything that would help him as he grew older. But he also learned things that were not found in any textbook he read. He learned that because of who his parents were and the lifestyle his family lived, he could basically get away with anything he wanted. He abused this priviledge all his life, buying his way out of trouble with other children, the schools, and even the police. Everything was going fine until his parents discovered his secret. One afternoon his parents walked in on him and few of his friends snorting lines of cocaine in their living room. His parents disowned him and forced him out on his own. Having nowhere to go and the little money his parents actually gave him before he left, he rented a room in a middle class hotel and began selling cocaine to make money. Soon he was able to afford his own apartment overlooking Times Square and he figured he'd once again become invincible. In August of 2000, J.D. was arrested in an undercover sting by the NYPD for distribution of a controlled substance. He was sentenced to six years in prison but was parolled after three for good behavior. He decided to change his life, he wanted to do something that was legal and at the same time very phsyical. One day he was walking down Broadway and noticed a flyer for a wrestling school. He joined the school and left one of the best students who had been through there in a while. His next move was to head to the big time.
