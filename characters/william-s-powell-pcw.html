RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004)

2004-04-11 – 2004-05-30
RPW's resident politician; a wrestler in one singles match
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 1 parsed match, RPW (RUSH Pro Wrestling, 2003–2004)): 1-0-0
Tenure detail
Arrives at the Dale F. Halton arena on 11 April 2004 to lead a protest, described as “One-half of the only team to capture the PCW, FSW, and OSE World Tag Team Championships”. On 25 April he hands the fed's owner a job application in the name of Reginald V. Lovecraft, a record RPW does not hold. On 2 May 2004 he beats Tessa Windsor, the number one contender to the Mid-South Championship, not cleanly. A scene of 30 May has him without a secretary and, as the text puts it, “No Lovecraft...yet.”
Source: RPW's own write-ups of its shows of 11, 18 and 25 April, 2 May and 30 May 2004 (bookings 2815 to 2819) and the federation's own backstage database; his own bio card there is a name only
