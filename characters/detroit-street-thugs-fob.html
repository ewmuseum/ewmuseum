Tenure detail
Brothers, billed Sexton, one with a sword. Won the number one contender's match at Rebirth against Future Perfect, finishing it beside a dumpster in the backstage area (the fed's own FoB: Rebirth, Hour 1, 20 September 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-rebirth-2005-09-20.html)); beat Trey Deep and Randy Orton on 6 October (the fed's own Sacrifice Recap Show of 8 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/sacrifice-recap-show-2005-10-08.html)); took the titles off The Butlers on 13 October with Detroit Rock City and were attacked by Fight Against Prejudice within a minute of winning them (the fed's own FoB Sacrifice, “A New Chapter”, 13 October 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-10-13.html)); and defended in 5:32 on 19 November (the fed's own FoB Sacrifice, “A Cannon's Past: Exposed”, 19 November 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/fob-sacrifice-2005-11-19.html)). Billed in the Decimation triple threat that ended the reign (the fed's own FOB: SACRIFICE roleplay forum as the board rendered it on 15 December 2005 (/promotions/pwe-inc/archive/forum-fob-sacrifice-133.html)).
