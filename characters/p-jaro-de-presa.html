Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Description: Hair color: Black Eye Color: Brown Fill in the rest of the details: Pájaro de Presa has a slender but athletic build, well-defined and very little body fat. He is lightly tanned and appears to have long, black hair. However, the color has been known to change to shades of white and green, leading some to theorize that it's a wig attached to his mask. His mouth is still visible, and runs constantly. Attire: Pájaro de Presa typically wears baggy black pants that resemble part of a karate gi. He wrestles shirtless with bizarre gloves or arm wrappings that look more like something seen in a goth club than a wrestling ring. His mask quite ornate, having various points that give it an avian look. The material is usually of a fancy design or metallic look. Resembles: Juventud Guerrera Gimmick: VERY cocky. He's prone to talking to fans, calling out the names of his moves, and pausing for effect. He also likes doing that 'pretend to blow snot on your downed opponent' bit that Benoit used to do. Before hitting the Death From Above, he holds his arms out wide, then crosses his hands over his chest, linking his thumbs so his hands make a sort of bird symbol, then leaps, spreading his arms again before connecting with his victim. He also likes to kick his foe a few times, get them staggering, flip them the 'Bird,' then floor them with another kick, like the spinning inferno. As a true rudo, Pájaro de Presa is more than willing to give another man a boot to the cajones if it will get him the win. History: Very little is known about Pájaro de Presa, which roughly translates to 'Bird of Prey.' He hails from Cancun, which might explain his irreverent attitude. Pajaro de Presa is the 'Rudo with Attitude-o.' Manic and flamboyant, he seems to have a better grasp on wrestling than he does on reality. He sees himself as an unbeatable luchador and an irrefutable ladies' man. He brushes off any attempts to find out about his past. He has never been seen without his mask. How he was trained, where he gained his experience, what his true goals are ... all are unknown. Which is just how Pájaro de Presa likes it.
