Team BATTLE*DREAM

A first formal pairing in this wing for countrywomen Reona Yoshida and Misaki Ishikawa, winning a four corners women's tag war to earn a title shot. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-08-25 – 2004-10-02
Tenure detail
First formal in-ring appearance under this name in this wing, winning a four corners women's tag war over Melody and Comedy, the Love Sisters, and Extreme Measures, Yoshida's own double DDT setting up the pinfall on Melody; earned a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship match against the Bod Squad the following week. Misaki Ishikawa's own earlier one-off uwf-mar96 appearance (19 May 2004) billed her representing "Project BATTLE*DREAM," her own home stable's name at DSW; this pairing reuses that same branding for her formal in-ring team with Reona Yoshida specifically in this wing. Challenged the Bod Squad (Tara "Sunburst" Marshall and Brianna Landis) for the UWF Women's Tag Team Championship on 8 September 2004 and lost by disqualification after Marshall drove a chair into Ishikawa's spine to save Landis from a spike piledriver; the title stayed with the champions. Teamed with Sierra Browne to beat Donna Tetreault and the Bod Squad in a non-title six-woman tag on 2 October 2004, Sierra Browne's own Truth Superkick finishing Brianna Landis for the pin. Reona Yoshida departed the UWF for Joshi Puroresu STARDOM by 11 June 2005; Misaki Ishikawa continued the pairing's own Women's Tag Team Championship reign under a new name, the Psychommunity, with older sister Naomi Ishikawa as her new partner.
