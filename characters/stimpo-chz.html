Biography (RUSH (2002))

Physical Description: Brendon has his share of muscles, but not as much as many of the RUSH stars. He wears his four packed abs proud and his calves are a medium type size. His face description is as follows, blue eyes, short blonde hair, a short horizonal scar under his left eye, a regular sized nose. Weighing in at a mere 187 lbs. and standing tall at 6'0'' Brendon could be called the cruiserweight of RUSH. Brendon's skin color is a mixture of both tan and white. Entrance: The crowd is silent. Suddenly trumpets sound playing 'Livin' La Vida Loca' by Ricky Martin blasts the stage. Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson shoots out from the entrance way wearing a short black hat covering his short blonde hair underneath. The crowd cheers on Stimpo for his dancing entrance. He comes out snapping his fingers and dancing to his own style of music. Wearing a skin-tight black shirt and black trousers with no print on them, he continues to do his latin style dancing to the up-beat song. The song reaches the chorus as Stimpo's shirt and pants rip off revealing his wrestling outfit consisting of no shirt and his black opaque spandex with 'Stimpo' printed on them in blue letters. Of course, not forgetting his lucky mask with the many attracting colors. His face his sheltered by the mask except for his blue eyes. While still dancing his way down the ring, the Latin PoP star does a neat spin while taking his hat off and frizbeeing it out into the crowd for one lucky fan's dream to come true. Stimpo finally dives under the bottom ropes ending the song and unfortunantly, his dancing. Wrestling Attire and Street Attire: While Wrestling: Brendon wears no shirt while wrestling but does wear black spandex with 'Stimpo' printed in blue letters, jaggedly designed going down each side. Brendon also wears the same colored boots as his spandex all laced up and ready to go. He never attends the ring without his lucky charm however, that being his mask. A perfectly shaped mask for Brendon's face making room for eye holes and room for his mouth. The mask has many different designs upon it including stripes of color across the mask, around the mask. (it looks similar to Rey Masterio's) Street clothes: Brendon covers his face once more by wearing his mask which he believes is his entire wrestling culture. He often wears his tights for wrestling all the time with no shirt on ready to wrestle. History: Born on September 28th, 1979 in his hometown of Rochester, New York, Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson was born. No one knew the future of this boy, how could they? He did have motives to join wrestling however, and it all started in his elementary years... When he was a wee little boy, Brendon always got his share of mockery from others. Most of these jokes were either short jokes or weak jokes or even jokes about his nickname, 'Stimpo,' each chipping away at Brendon a little at a time. Everyday during the third grade, Brendon would get into fights and always end up on the ground with a new bruise. Going through his elementary years, Brendon always swore to get his revenge on the bullies who always made fun of him. He finally got to high school, passing by flying colors I might add. High school did have it's other share of problems but this is when Brendon started taking culture classes for the culture of Mexico. He saw that many great wrestlers came from Mexico and thought why not give westling a try. Sure enough, Brendon started taking wrestling classes and joined the wrestling team at his High school. This began his problems, the teasing once again began. Other kids on the team claimed Brendon was too small and too weak, he may have fit this description being 5'4'' and weighing 105 lbs. at the time. But, Brendon would not have this going on, he decided that he would finally take his anger out onto wrestling opponents proving that he too could be one of the 'cool' kids in his grade. His first match came, he was put up against a man at least four times his size. 'Not a problem.' Brendon said to himself and went into that match with his head up high with his little muscles flexing. Surprisingly to his fellow classmates, the match lasted for quite a while but Brendon took what he had learned from both his Mexican culture class and his Wrestling lessons by performing what is now called a hurricanrana slamming the huge man down to the mat, head first. Though this move was illigal, Brendon was disqualified but it seemed that his wrestling teammates did not care, they still think today that Brendon is one of the greatest wrestlers in that High School. So Brendon had broken out of his shell in his wrestling abilities. He thought to himself that if he can't cut it in regular wrestling, why not join one of the modern federations for his style of wrestling. Sure enough, right outside of school door one day was a poster for applications to the DWA-Dark Wrestling Alliance. Brendon skipped school that day for a good reason, to apply to the Dark Wrestling Alliance at the age of 21, the age he is now. The application was of a long format but Brendon managed to get it done in under 20 minutes, then came his first match for the DWA. He ended up facing one of his long time friends from his High School who called himself, Zarcon. The match began as normal on March 10th and Brendon gave it all he had. The momentum went back and forth before Brendon finally performed what he used to call the Stimpo Splash, a 540 splash off the top rope. The crowd was stunned at the amazing feet that this new kid could get, he won his debut then went on to win about 8 more matches along with a few losses. Then, on April the 23rd of 2002, Brendon had a match on the DWA's pay per view called Hostile Takeover. The match was a triple threat between New-Blood (double champion, light heavy weight and hardcore.) and Inferno and of course, Brendon. The first fall of this match was for the light heavy weight title and the other fall was for the hardcore title. The match raged on for what felt to be at least an hour before Brendon finally broke out of his shell once more when both men were down, he climbed up to the top turnbuckle and smashed them both down with that Stimpo Splash. The referee counted the 1...2...3 that day, in which it changed 'Stimpo's' career. After the win, Stimpo came back to the ring the next night on Monday Night Manic and told the crowd that it was a new Stimpo. He came down the ramp with a small black shoe box. When he told the crowd of fans that he was about to start a new Stimpo, he pulled out from the box a mask! He claimed that this was Stimpo's new style, a Lucadore. Then it was announced that High Voltage Wrestling was looking to merge with another federation, sure enough, DWA jumped on the opportunity and Stimpo went for the ride. June 15th was the date the merge happened and after the first week of action, (Stimpo winning his first match over Jman.) the new HVW/DWA was sent straight into Primetime Central becoming a network promotion. Though, HVW was not what Stimpo was looking for in the federation he wanted. So, after about three weeks of action in HVW, (all wins.) Stimpo left HVW in hopes of climbing the WrestlingTattler ladder and maybe have a chance at getting into perhaps one of the top federations. Taking advice from friends, Stimpo entered himself into the Primetime Wrestling Academy to both develop his skills and maybe get into a larger federation. So his first match in the PWA was a 7-man cemetary match in which he was one of the last men to be eliminated. Taking the loss very well, Stimpo bounced back and won 3 straight matches. Then, Stimpo was ranked in the top ten for those three straight weeks and for the month of June, Stimpo was named the third best wrestler in the PWA. Then the time came for promotions. Stimpo waited in great anticipation, biting his nails as to what federation was promoting and who was in. The first to promote was NTICW, to Stimpo's depression, he was not on the list but then another federation claimed they were promoting. Stimpo looked at the name and it read in clear letters: CHZ. Stimpo's self esteem was sky high that day, then he listened and sure enough his name was the second on the list. He jumped up and down and packed his bags immediatly. He flew on the earliast fight to Canada where he became a star of the Canuck Heat Zone. Brendon only stayed in the CHZ for a mere three weeks until he stands here now, a proud wrestling member of the RUSH federation. In hopes of making an impact here in RUSH, Brendon may find himself up against WrestlingTattler's best. Though his name may not be known yet, Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson has been trying to get known for almost half a year. Yes, for 5 months now, Brendon has attempted to carry his career on sheer determination to get back at the ones who ridiculed him from the past.
