Thom Morgan

A Canadian babyface and frequent partner of Kirk Jameson against Jean Cattley, who beats Jean Cattley and Davis Wayne Newton with Kirk Jameson in May 2008. Named as a possible cut in January 2011, he and Joe Benning beat Ford & Speed at End Of Days; his contract is not to be renewed and he loses to VIP Wallace at March Madness in March 2011 as a send-off. Billed on the 12th May 2011 card as Thomas Morgan, he loses with Stevie Grayson to Ford & Speed and is let go in June as the first ex-champion the company has let go, after being with it almost from the first day.
