The Rodeo Boys

Kenny Kidd and Billy Bones' own tag team, first teaming in vwf-vegas-2003, then reuniting in VXW as previously three-time VXW tag champions, losing their first match back together to Team Essington, then losing a four-way tornado tag to Purgatory at Fear Is Coming II. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2005-01-19
Tenure detail
Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship to Hot Property in a Flashback ambush on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then wins the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Loses the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round to Marcus O'Malley wrestling alone, then is beaten down by Hot Property and Flash in a special Flashback before competing in the sixteen-man tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), Kenny Kidd falling from the ladder alongside Stevie Knight. Reported gone for good after Billy Bones's own contract release on Wrestle Style V.3, the pair are reinstated to the VWF on the spot at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) after lawyer RAR intervenes with majority owner Kingston Cash, then immediately beat down new VWF Tag Team Champions Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen with cowbells. Loses a VWF Tag Team Championship match by disqualification after Kenny Kidd brands Marcus O'Malley on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Loses the title rematch to a substitute Outlaw Morris Bass and Mark McQueen on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), Billy Bones submitting to the Devils Snare. Wins a weapons-advantage Number One Contenders match over The Mighty Men at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), Bones branding The Reckoning's face and pinning The Salvation. Interferes to help the Riders of the Apocalypse beat down Iyasu Saku and jump Paddy O'Shea on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), read by the broadcast's own commentary as the pair joining the group; helps eliminate Capsman first in the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse match before both members are eliminated themselves. Both eliminated separately from the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), Kenny Kidd by mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov and Billy Bones by a returning Sonicman. Billy Bones loses to Sonicman on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005); the pair works over The Reckoning's ankle together during Kenny Kidd's own title-feud win the same night.

VXW (2001)

2005-10-04 – 2007-02-07
Tenure detail
Announced their own retirement at Xtreme Machine I (8 March 2006, an earlier pass). Returned to make the save for a beaten Joey Young and Tom Rogers against POT on New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, then beat POT (Lark Fenriz and Foodstamp) by pinfall on Sin City Fights February 7, billing it their own 'last ride' and challenging the O'Malleys, Team Brittania and Hot Property to a one-night tag team tournament.
