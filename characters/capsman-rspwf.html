Tenure detail
Teams legitimately with Evil Capsman to beat Blaze and The Real Deal on Firestorm (3 May 2004). Teams with Evil Capsman to open the Gauntlet, pinning Blaze, then enters the battle royal and is thrown back out by Marcus O'Malley moments after entering. Makes the save for his partner Evil Capsman on Firestorm (27 May 2004) but is ruled too injured to wrestle the next scheduled match, then his own team is eliminated by The Salvation in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Teams with Evil Capsman as CAPSMEN! in the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round, losing to The Mighty Men, then competes in the sixteen-man Dog Days 3 tag scramble and is eliminated from the tournament in the Tag Team Elimination Ladder Match on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Loses to the Unholy Alliance on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then is eliminated from the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), thrown out by an already-eliminated Sonicman. Lost alongside Evil Capsman to Orion and The Equalizer on Wrestle Style V.1, then lost to The Outlaw Morris Bass in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Helps eliminate champion Kevin Blair early from the VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004). Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship match to Blaze and Real Deal by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) after The Mighty Men run in, then is beaten down by The Mighty Men regardless. Teams with Evil Capsman and Sonicman, billed together as The Rhombus, in a losing six-man tag against The Mighty Men and a returning Paddy O'Shea at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), Evil Capsman submitting to O'Shea's own Chicken Wing. Teams with Evil Capsman in a two-on-two against G-Gangsta and Sebastian on March To Hell (21 October 2004), the match ruled a no contest after Robbie Caldwell and Iyasu Saku run in for a four-on-two beatdown, broken up by The Maquilladora and Sonicman. Pins Paddy O'Shea with the Capitol Punishment for The Rhombus on March To Hell II (28 October 2004). Eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004). Eliminated early from the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), caught by the Rodeo Boys' own Double Eight Second Ride. Tries and fails to break up the Riders' own opening beatdown of Adam Jennings in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), eliminated late by a Sebastian clothesline. Teams with Evil Capsman as CAPSMEN! in a losing effort to Sebastian and Robbie Caldwell on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), the winners earning the right to book their own Ghost Town Blues matches; challenged backstage the same show by Lance Voltar for a match at Ghost Town Blues. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005), eliminated alongside Voltar by Sidious The Clown and Kagedo, then legdropped and pinned by Voltar himself. Beats Lance Voltar in a Loser Is Winners Valet Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) with a schoolboy after Voltar's own missed top-rope Twisting Corkscrew Legdrop bumps the referee, winning Voltar as his own ringside valet. Beats Billy Bones at March To Madness (2 March 2005) with a Bronco Buster, then runs in to break up Frank O'Shea's own beatdown of Adam Jennings, fighting off both O'Sheas with Lance Voltar. Runs in again during Paddy O'Shea's own match against Lance Voltar at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), chasing off both O'Sheas without a decision. Teams with Voltar against The O'Sheas at End Of March (30 March 2005), pinned after a top-rope fist drop from Paddy. Teams with Lance Voltar to beat The O'Sheas on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), pinning Paddy O'Shea after kicking him off a schoolboy attempt -- Voltar's own first win. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal as its tenth entrant, dropkicking out Stan Klenski before going over the top himself on his own flying bodypress, a spill that also carries out Hotstuff Stevie Knight and Justin Davidson.
