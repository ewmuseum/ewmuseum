Internet Favourites

aka The Internet Favourites

Max Mayhem and Hugh Lee, who top an internet poll of the company's most entertaining wrestlers in April 2009 and sign Dawn as their manager; they work the Beach Blast three-way and are booked against C-V-2 on the card for Fall Of The King. They lose a first title challenge to C-V-2 at Fall Of The King in September 2009, lose to the Canadian Blondes a month later and to Antonio & Raphael at March Madness in March 2010, and join Fumihiro Ota in April 2010 to challenge The Firm for the Trios titles. They challenge Valentine & Johnson for the Tag Team titles at Beach Blast in July 2010, in a three-way at Back To School and again at Fall Of The King, lose to Antonio & Raphael at All The Leaves Are Brown, and with Amazing Fire Fly lose a trios match to Glamour And Devastation and Nelson Callum at Where It All Begins Again in October. They lose to the Canadian Blondes on the 11th December 2010 tour show and to Firebird and Evil Spirit at Season's Beatings, beat Zeshin Makioka and Yusuke Takahama, Glamour And Devastation and Evil Spirit and Ultimo Soetsu on the 4th January 2011 tour show, lose to the Canadian Blondes at All Or Nothing and to Glamour And Devastation at April Showers, where Findlay O'Farraday clamps a Choke Sleeper on Max Mayhem. They beat Glamour And Devastation on 18th May, Team XDW II on 11th July, Roderick Remus and Nelson Callum on 11th August, Remus and Raphael on 4th September and Jared Johnson and Raphael on 18th September, and lose to Providence and JD Morgan at Hotter Than Hot and to Callum and Raphael on 11th September. They lose to the Canadian Blondes on the 24th October 2011 tour show, to Lockdown at Eleventh Hour on 12th November, when Aaron Andrews springs the match on them, and again to the Blondes on 5th January 2012. They lose to the Second Sons at Third Time's The Charm on 5th March 2012 and are in the four-team Tag Team number-one-contender match at April Showers on 5th April. In the diary's pages for June 2012 the narrator reports that Max Mayhem's contract has lapsed and that Hugh Lee has been re-signed for three months without a partner, so the Internet Favourites are no more.
