Tenure detail
Owner Edmond Winston IV's own protege, of the New Era; goaded into an impromptu match against Winston himself on 24 March 2004 after first being offered a staged loss. The bout ended without a decision when Sabbath, then Michael Augustine and Alex Extreme, then the Privateers all ran in in succession; Winston set up a handicap main event, the Youngbloods against his own New Era, for the following Rampage. Thrown from the ring early in that own handicap main event on 15 May 2004 by Michael Augustine and took no further part; pursued a legal scheme against Sabbath's custody of his own children, then beat Chad Grimsson by pinfall on 19 May 2004 with an assist from new girlfriend Sonya Benedict, who held Grimsson's leg down for the cover. Beat Sabbath by pinfall in a no-disqualification Gold Rush match on 5 June 2004 over custody of Sabbath's own children, pinning him after Sabbath's own son, Alex Price, turned on his father with a low blow and held down the cover. Won the UWF Rampage Championship from Uriah Hawkins by pinfall (a small package) on 28 August 2004, aided by a Papa Legba run-in and Ragga Bomb. Lost a UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World challenge to champion Kyle Lee by disqualification on 18 September 2004 after owner Edmond Winston IV interfered on his behalf, striking Lee with a chair and a top-rope ladder superplex. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship over Alex Extreme by pinfall (Signed, Sealed, and Delivered) on 2 October 2004, saved by Corporate Leverage and a returning Privateers five-on-one beatdown of Extreme's manager Rodney after the bell. Lost the UWF Rampage Championship to "Showtime" Rick Marley by pinfall (a small package) on 16 October 2004 after turning his back on the match to check on his own distracted "son"; assaulted afterward by a returning Sabbath, who broke off the attack on recognizing the boy as his own son. Revealed on camera afterward that his own months-long guardianship of the boy had been a deliberate scheme to torment Sabbath. Teamed with "Dead End" Derek Martin to beat Alex Kidd and Sabbath by pinfall on 30 October 2004, countering Sabbath's own Coffin Breaker II attempt into a sitting pin; joined Martin in a post-match steel chair beatdown of both fallen opponents and Sabbath's wife Sayaka. Interfered with a steel chair in the UWF Rampage Championship match between "Showtime" Rick Marley and Sabbath on 27 November 2004, busting Sabbath open, then accepted Sabbath's own Last Man Standing challenge for Rise to Power on the condition that Sabbath's win would mean Sonya Benedict's reinstatement. Told by owner Edmond Winston IV backstage that Winston himself would face Sabbath the following week to prepare Underwood for the pay-per-view. Beat "Long Haul" Greg Embrey by pinfall (a second split-legged moonsault) on 1 December 2004 after a distracting, non-contact appearance by Sabbath and interference from Embrey's own manager. On 11 December 2004, clashed with Jessica "Fatality" Marshall backstage over her own doubts ahead of his Last Man Standing match with Sabbath, vowing to be the Last Man Standing at Rise to Power after accusing her of favoring Luke Kinsey. Was smashed through a mirror by Edmond Winston IV, then beaten down twice more by Luke Kinsey's own growing faction on 28 May 2005, including a betrayal by Scott "Hotspot" Daniels and a heel turn by Strictly Business, and was declared "exonerated from all further UWF bookings" by Kinsey. Ran in after Alex Kidd's own win over Andrew Tucker on 20 July 2005 to attack Tucker's partner Mike Sebastian at ringside. Opened the 23 July 2005 broadcast declaring independence from retired mentor Edmond Winston IV and calling out the Illuminati by name, then beat L. Dan Dee by pinfall (the Jamie Driver). Lost a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament first-round match to Vyolynce by pinfall on 3 August 2005 after Illuminati interference; answered an in-ring challenge from Olivia Michaels on 6 August 2005, beating down L. Dan Dee until the masked wrestler pulled Olivia into the path of a chair shot and then fireballed her, unmasking afterward as the returning "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun.
