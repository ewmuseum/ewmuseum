Eric Cameron

One half of the Phenomenons across the GIWF and UWF, and for a few months in 2004 the other half of a different story entirely — signed to SCWF before being called up to WWA's own NWC brand and quietly disappearing from the active roster; in 2014, with Shane Stone, he won the NAPW World Tag Team Championship at Tagstravaganza.
