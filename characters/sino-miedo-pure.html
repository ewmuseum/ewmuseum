Biography (PURE (2002))

The Riven family is where everything begins for Andrew Riven. Andrew’s father, grandfather and great grandfather where all apart of All Canadian Wrestling, Canada’s top ranked wrestling promotion. They had passed the business down through the family. Everyone was involved someway form managing, wrestling, refereeing or even doing backstage work, every member of the Riven family was apart of the industry. The three old Riven’s though where all very big men. Each coming in over 275lbs each and they all used the same technical skills passed down to them. They where brawlers and mat technicians. But that was the end off the common bonds. Andrew Scott Riven was born to Lisa Smith-Riven and Alex Karl-Riven. Alex being a wrestler much like his father John and his father Scott was happy to have a soon he could bring up into his own eyes much like the rest of the family did. By age three Andrew was already starting to begin his training with his father. Doing the simple running and small weight lifting. By age seven Andrew had began to learn how to fall correctly on the mat and learn the basic moves. All three generations before Andrew helped out as they where all excited to push him farther. Andrew didn’t complain because he wanted to continue the family business. By age eleven problems began to form. Andrew couldn’t do the more powerful moves that his family want him to do and they all learn before and he couldn’t attempt any powerful ground moves with risking injury. Emotion’s starts to rise as Andrew had the wait of his family on his shoulder’s pushing him to move quickly. But all this was getting to Andrew and at age twelve he broke down. He couldn’t work anymore with his family. His father would constantly talk to him and encourage him back into the ring but the system was not right for Andrew. He continually told his family he was to small to perform and be a power wrestler. That’s when the Riven’s decided it was time for the chain to be broken and Andrew would have to train somewhere else. Knowing Andrew would have to start all over again if he went to a normal school in Canada or the United states, Andrew was sent down to Tijuana, Mexico famous for training wrestlers in the art of Lucha Libre. The whole Riven house hold got up and left Sarnia and moved down to Mexico so Andrew would have family near by and so the rest of the family could be an active role in his life. Private training began and Andrew learned at a normal rate. His teacher worked with him everyday and went over every key step. Andrew was finally happy he had found a style he could enjoy. By the age of twenty and spending eight years mastering his talent down to an art Andrew finally received his mask from his teach as a gift. He still wears the mask today. Andrew and the whole Riven family spent another year in Mexico so Andrew could spend time with other Luchadors but later on moved back up to Canada so Andrew could continue with the family business and join All Canadian Wrestling. Andrew became a huge talent and an entertainer larger then most. He was scouted but never signed and spent most in the crusierweight division, which was fine with Andrew. Classified as one of Canada’s only and best Lucha masters Andrew every day tries to impress the Canadian audience. But he is looking for a global view now and sets his eyes on PURE. He style maybe be different and maybe be small but Andrew has learned one thing from the generations before him and that is to entertain the fans.
