Tenure detail
Hall of Fame inductee per FAW's own Hall of Fame page (promotions/faw/archive/faw-hall-of-fame.html); separate roster bio cards survive under both billings. Revealed at FAW IS WAR, 6 November 2001, to have a brother, Rick, discussed backstage while planning to track down their birth parents. With Rick, breaks into an adoption agency and learns their parents are dead and that they have a sister, Katrina Beck -- Vincent's own birth surname given here as Beck; sets FAW IS WAR as the target date to find her. Finds his sister living with Chris Thunder in New York; challenges Thunder to an Anywhere Falls Hardcore match at Deceit for Thunder's own FAW International Championship, with his sister also at stake. Beats Axl Young, then tries and fails to reconcile with his sister backstage; attends the Crimson Lord/Lisa engagement party ahead of his own Deceit hardcore match with Chris Thunder. Wins the FAW International Championship from Chris Thunder in an Anywhere Falls match at Deceit, attacking his own sister Kat/Katrina mid-match and declaring her his manager afterward as the match's own stipulation. Beats Prayer, but is left kayfabe-injured by Crimson Lord (a tombstone piledriver) after trying to reconcile with his sister backstage; blames Skipster for the breakdown. Briefly installed as a third, chaos-agent referee in the SpaWn/British Bomber I Quit main event -- attacks Ben Preston, brawls with both combatants and JLC, then allies with JLC again to Venom both men before Spawn finishes Bomber. Fights to the top of the Hell In A Cell/TLC main event, taking British Bomber through the cell roof with a full nelson slam, before Tom The Bomb (revealed mid-match as xCw's new co-owner) chokeslams him out of it and helps Bomber up a ladder to grab the belt, denying Vincent the title. Unable to compete due to injury; hands his own FAW International Championship to his brother Rick to defend in his place, and closes the show attacking both Rick's opponent, Skipster, and British Bomber.
