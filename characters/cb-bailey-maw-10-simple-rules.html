Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

CB Bailey

aka Cheetah Boy

CB ("Cheetah Boy") Bailey, the other Bailey Boy, who comes off the top rope to pin Findlay O'Farraday and score the upset over Primus Allen and Findlay at the team's on-screen debut at Summer Showdown in June 2010. He pins Jefferson Stardust with a Flying Pounce at All The Leaves Are Brown in October 2010 and is in the ladder match for the Tag Team titles at Where It All Begins Again later that month. He is caught in a Canadian Crab by Flash Savage at Season's Beatings in December 2010 and leaves MAW in February 2011 with his contract expired. Written by AI, 2026
