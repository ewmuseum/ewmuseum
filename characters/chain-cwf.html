Tenure detail
History names him among the EPWA wrestlers Starter recruited when that fed folded (c. 1999) who "became CWF Legends." At the 2003 reunion he stalks Starter in the rafters as part of a six-way EPWA stand-off with Mr. X, Hardcore Z, Evil Santa and AJ; the Redzne-2 card bills him against Starter in a Cold Championship Tournament quarter-final whose own results were never captured.
