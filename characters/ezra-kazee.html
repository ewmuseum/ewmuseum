Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Once a hardened criminal wrestling under the name of Mr. Meaner (yes, he wrestled in the GIWF under this name) Kazee switched to who he is now after nearly losing his daughter and estranged wife in a house fire. He is a former championship amateur wrestler and has retained much of that knowledge. Knowing that he would never be strong, fast, or big, he continued on and learned submission maneuvers. While Kazee may not be fast, he has surprising quickness which can reverse a maneuver in an instant. 'The Common Man' Ezra Kazee is exactly what he says he is. Just a common man who has been given some great opportunities and will do whatever he can for his fans, whom he calls the Commonwealth. He lives by the idea of fairness, sportsmanship, and being the best person possible. His constant belief in these things gives the audience a refreshing sense and earns him the respect of friend and foe alike. However, with recent events, he has lost his mind so to speak, and now seems to have split personalities. He has reverted back to Mr. Meaner as well as a new persona named Fizbane the Fabulous. How does this all work out? Just keep watching and I'm sure you'll find out.
