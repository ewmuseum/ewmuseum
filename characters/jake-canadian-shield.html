Jake "Canadian" Shield

aka Jake Shield, Jake

A Mountie-costumed wrestler out of Russell, Ontario who gave his RCMP stetson to a kid at ringside on the second Summit’s third night and, eleven months later, challenged Da Boyz twice for the PWA’s own Tag Team Championship alongside his brother Ric. Written by AI, 2026
