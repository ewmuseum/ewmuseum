Biography (PURE (2002))

<p>Edward Mandrake was born in the quiet town of Guelph, Ontario to a well-to-do family. He had a good life, with many friends. Quite the popular person, in school. Elementary school proved that he was ranked within the 'genius' category, and was sent to the 'gifted' program at his school. That kind of alienated him from the rest, some were jealous and picked on him, but he was still popular enough to have back up.</p> <p>He entered High School, still a genius, and of course, he flew through all the advanced courses that he was given, and graduated with High Honors. He got employed at a local home improvement warehouse as a lot attendant (More affectionately known as a Buggy Bitch). He worked there for another two years, trying to build up the funds for college. However, one day, he was being a safety spotter for a forklift driver as he was pulling down some lumber. A nine-year old ran in the way of the forklift, Eddie ran into the zone and halted the forklift. Several loose boards fell off of the lift. The avoided the little kid, but clobbered Eddie right in the head, rendering him unconscious. <p>When he awoke, he was surrounded by family and friends. However, he felt different. His behaviour changed slightly. Like his watching and reading of dark comedies. Before the accident, he liked the more intelligent comedies. He now enjoyed watching people getting into accidents, getting hurt, and getting killed. It almost seemed euphoric. His parents were worried, and sent him to a shrink to find out what his problem was. Turns out the more recessive personality surfaced when his noggin got a floggin'. The plank of lumber acted as 'Jekyll's Potion'. When he returned to work, he didn't care about safety, either for other customers, or himself. He was fired when he decided to ride a quarter-pallet jack as a scooter through an area cordoned off due to heavy equipment being used. His lust for pain and suffering was evident when he castrated the man who his girlfriend cheated on with, with an olive fork.</p> <p>After about six months of this behavior, Eddie moved out on his own. He makes a living taking out ads for his services as a bounty hunter in several underground magazines. His method has grown much worse since his, what you would call humble beginnings. He likes to take prisoners, and decorate his hiding place with the body parts of his competitors in the bounty hunter market. His targets are usually skinned alive and sent to the bottom of Lake Ontario. However, it is time for him to select a new career, as the prices are beginning to increase significantly.</p> <p>The new career involved pro wrestling, where he could still practice his lethal craft, on a non-lethal level. He Entered PCW with hopes of eliminating other people and get away with it, legally! So enteres with very little pomp and circumstance, and most of his time there was rather quiet...even winning the PCW Cruiserweight Championship didn't increase his heat! Eventually, he ended up in limbo within the storylines, so PCW Kingpin Xavier Kannon suggested a tour with the Japanese Fed PURE. Eddie readily accepted to build his pro wrestling reputation. Later that week, he packed his shit, said good-bye to his manager in North America, Spider, and moved to Osaka. </p>
