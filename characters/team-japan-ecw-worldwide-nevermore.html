Team JAPAN

Jushin Lyger, Ultimo Dragon and Hayabusa, who come together in March 2007 when Hayabusa returns from injury to flip over the Canadian Connection and Team Mexico after a brawl, and are added by Paul Heyman to the Ultimate Jeopardy match at Baptism Of Fire; Hayabusa, Lyger and Ultimo Dragon are eliminated in turn, and the Canadian Connection win the match. The full Japanese squad for the Wrestling World Cup of July 2007 is Jushin Lyger (captain), Ultimo Dragon, Hayabusa, Kensuke Sasaki, Masato Tanaka and Yoshihiro Tajiri. They beat Scotland twice and split with Canada, finish level on points with them, and win the draw to top Pool C; they beat Ireland in the quarter-final and the sWo in the semi-final (Tajiri, in Sasuke's mask, pins Chessman), and lose the final to England when Paul Burchill, last of his team, beats Tanaka, Lyger and Ultimo Dragon.
