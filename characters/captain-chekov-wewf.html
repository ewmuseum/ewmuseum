Captain Chekov

aka Admiral Chekov

A Star Trek-pastiche promo gimmick, self-billed “from Star Fleet Headquarters,” who managed the Klingons and Photon Torpedo as “my Ultimate Trekkies” in the WeWF’s turn 6 and was billed Admiral Chekov ever after, seconding the same stable through the UFWS and both Summits of E-Wrestling and wrestling a rival manager on the last of them.
