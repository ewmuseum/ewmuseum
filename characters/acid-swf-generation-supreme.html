Acid

A short, stocky masked high-flier, a former DaVE star who has worked in Japan, whom Eric Tyler springs on SWF Legends in Week 2 of August 2008 as a third man in Kid Toma's Shooting Star Championship defence against Bart Biggz; he wins the belt at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 in his first pay-per-view match with the Acid Rain Bomb, and keeps it on SWF Legends against El Leon and Mainstream Hernandez, Remmy Skye and John Greed. Tyler names him the first of his watchmen and the pillar of the Sky Club Division, and the narrator notes that the little tank has the deepest voice imaginable. Written by AI, 2026
