Kid Cool

aka Kid Kool

A young wrestler who called out the fWo champion and Ice in his first appearance in July 1999 and held the Tag Team titles beside Ultra Violet as Ultra Cool 2000 and the Cruiserweight title for four days in 2000, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: a backstage hand, the federation's webmaster, from 2002 to 2004, and the acting General Manager, then owner, who ran its 2009 return.
