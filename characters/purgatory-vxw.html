Unit · VXW (2001), RAW Carolina (2000)

Purgatory

PsyKlone and Synn's own tag team, fighting through a punishment match, a number one contenders bout and a losing three-way title match at Vegas Violence V before a no-contest against Team Brittania, then winning a four-way tornado tag over Hot Property, Team Brittania and the Rodeo Boys at Fear Is Coming II. Won a second straight gauntlet fall over Team Brittania at Fear Is Coming III before losing to Marcus O'Malley and Kagedo, then lost a three-way tag to Tap Out City alongside Team Brittania at Phear Phantastic 2005. Teamed with Team Essington to beat a patched-together Rodeo Boys/Tap Out City side by submission on Christmas Comes Early, with a debuting Tanara Skott's own interference against Kenny Kidd setting up the finish. Survived a returning Sebastian's chair attack to win a triple threat by referee stoppage over the Rodeo Boys and Team Essington on New Years Eve Of Destruction, then beat Sebastian himself, in his own in-ring return, on Winter War I. Lost to Sebastian and a debuting Miles McKinley on Winter War II after PsyKlone's own blindside attack forced an illegal tag. Written by AI, 2026
