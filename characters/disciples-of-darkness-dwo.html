Tenure detail
Founded under Donny Diamond and relaunched under Damian's own leadership on the fed's own Travesty of 10 July 2003, the same broadcast on which Damian and Samantha claim to have killed Donny Diamond the week before. Tyrone Grace appears to accept Damian's own offer to join, then reveals the whole angle as a swerve to bring in three new members at once: Sean Quinn, Komodo Dragon (both debuting the stable that night) and Kuff McSlade, the reigning DWO Extreme Champion.
