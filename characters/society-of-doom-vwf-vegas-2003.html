Society of Doom

Kevin Simmons's own stable, billed together on the Gauntlet as McQueen and O'Malley beat Sonicman and the Original Masked Man in the group's first on-screen billing as a unit. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-05-17 – 2005-01-19
Tenure detail
Mark McQueen and Marcus O'Malley beat Blaze and The Real Deal on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then compete in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal, eliminated in a mutual elimination with Orion; leader Kevin Simmons announces his own in-character retirement the same Firestorm broadcast. Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen win the sixteen-man Dog Days 3 tag scramble together on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), O'Malley unhooking the suspended ring; O'Malley alone had wrestled the Dog Day Two tag bracket for the team the week before, McQueen barred from competing. Billed a Society of Doom member in his own Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) ring introduction, Adam Jennings wins the VWF 24 Hour Championship from Kevin Blair; The Outlaw Morris Bass runs in alongside Jennings, O'Malley and McQueen, all named on commentary as the Society of Doom, to save Kevin Simmons from a Kingston Cash chair attack during the night's own ladder match. Kevin Simmons and Outlaw Morris Bass win by disqualification over The Mighty Men on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen chase off a backstage beatdown of Simmons on March To Hell II (28 October 2004). All four -- Simmons, O'Malley, McQueen and Adam Jennings -- enter the Rage in the Cage together as the Society of Doom at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), Simmons pinning O'Malley to become the new co-holder of the VWF Tag Team Championship. Adam Jennings and Mark McQueen, both billed members, help fight off the Riders of the Apocalypse in a post-match brawl after Jennings retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Kevin Simmons and Marcus O'Malley are confirmed suspended from the VWF by New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), leaving only Jennings and McQueen active.
