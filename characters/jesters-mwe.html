JESTers

aka JEST'ers

Four WcE men with a bar-based web series and a merchandise line of their own, who talked MWE's General Manager into letting them throw out his announce team and call a retirement match themselves. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

MWE (2007)

2008-09-13 – 2010-08-06
a four-man WcE act that kept a standing guest presence on MWE's screens -- billed on camera as "WcE's JEST'ers" and as "David's cousin's stable" -- and took over an entire MWE broadcast segment at Crowning Glory 2009
Tenure detail
The dates span the earliest and the latest held MWE document that names the unit -- promotions/mwe/rp/pre-turmoil-turmoil.html and promotions/mwe/rp/elephant-in-the-room.html. They are a held-record span, not a stated formation or break-up; MWE's own record gives neither. The group is WcE's, and MWE's own text says so every time it bills them, but the unit is filed here because MWE's own documents are where it is staged -- WcE holds no document of its own.
Source: MWE's own served text throughout. Membership is spelled out twice: A Crash Course in Wrasslin' (MWE) (the fed's own board, s4.zetaboards.com/MWEntertainment, via the Wayback Machine) names "the JEST'ers, James Whitman (David's cousin), best friends Eddie Sanchez and Scott Jones, and Thomas Williams (James' best friend)", and The Final Flight of a Wasted Mind; David Whitman RP (MWE) expands the acronym itself -- "the JESTers' (James Eddie Scott Thomas) company; JEST Kiddin' inc." Staged on MWE's own cards at MWE Crowning Glory (the four walk out to "Welcome to the Jungle", have Ryan Adams and David Ferrera ejected from the announce desk on the General Manager's say-so, and run the broadcast for the David Whitman vs. Alejandro Rodriguez retirement match themselves) and MWE Crowning Glory II (all four picked out by the arena camera in the Hall of Fame audience, two of them in a pre-taped titantron segment). Billed from the ring at MWE Independence Day as "David's cousin's stable the JEST'ers". Thirteen further served MWE roleplays name the unit.
