Andy Murray

Pure Mental out of Glasgow, Andy Murray held the PCW Rising Star Championship for a fortnight in April 2002, and in GCW held the World Heavyweight Championship twice, was named Wrestler of the Year (2009 and 2010), Match of the Year (2009), Feud of the Year (2008 and 2009), Writer of the Year (2009 and 2010) and 2 other annual honours, was inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame in 2012 and appeared on 90 GCW cards from 18 December 2007 to 4 July 2011.
