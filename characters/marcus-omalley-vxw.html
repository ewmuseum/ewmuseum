VXW (2001)

Tenure detail
Charged Kevin Simmons on Sin City Fights November 18 over the chair shot that cost him the Rage In The Cage main event, but was clotheslined by a hiding Morris Bass before Simmons could be reached, taking a beating alongside brother Anthony. Confronted Simmons on camera before the main event on Sin City Fights November 27, then was chair-shotted by him during the VXW Tag Team Championship match, handing Bass a disqualification win that still cost him the title defense's own clean finish. Wins a triple threat over Flash and Orion The Hunter in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), Flash disqualified and Orion ruled too injured to continue. Runs in on Mark McQueen's behalf against Real Deal on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), drawing a disqualification and spearing Real Deal again after the bell; self-eliminates from a Heavyweight title fatal four-way the same night to save a kidnapped McQueen shown on a video screen. Wins with McQueen over The Mighty Men by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004).

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-05-03 – 2004-11-08
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 15 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 11-4-0
Tenure detail
Wins by disqualification over Sonicman on Firestorm (3 May 2004) after an unidentified masked man interferes; his first attested vwf-vegas-2003 tenure. Wins the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round alone, his partner Mark McQueen barred from competing, submitting Kenny Kidd with The Kill; loses the tag final to The Mighty Men blindfolded and handcuffed, then submits Sonicman with The Kill in a handicap match ordered on the spot. Wins the sixteen-man tag scramble with Mark McQueen on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), unhooking the ring alone. Wins the Dog Days Four Tag Team Elimination Match alone for a short-handed Society of Doom on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) after being knocked cold by his own tag partner The Salvation's loaded fist earlier the same night, then is eliminated from the tag Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) and helps strip majority owner Kingston Cash to reveal women's underwear. Won the ten-man tag for the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1, challenged for the VWF Tag Team Championship alongside Mark McQueen on Wrestle Style V.2 (won by disqualification, titles retained), then beat The Maquilladora with a T-Bone Suplex in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Wins the VWF Tag Team Championship with Mark McQueen from Blaze and Real Deal at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), billed Alcoholics Anonymous; the Rodeo Boys, reinstated on the spot by majority owner Kingston Cash at lawyer RAR's own urging, beat down both new champions immediately after. Branded on the lower back with Kenny Kidd's own branding iron and hospitalized with second- and third-degree burns defending the VWF Tag Team Championship with Mark McQueen against the Rodeo Boys on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Appears from his hospital bed on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), picking Outlaw Morris Bass to defend his own half of the titles in his place and introducing his new girlfriend Jessica Jennings on camera. Pinned by Kevin Simmons in the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), losing his half of the VWF Tag Team Championship.
