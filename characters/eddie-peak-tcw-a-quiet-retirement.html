Eddie Peak

aka The Great White Shark

'The Great White Shark', a former DaVE man and fan favourite who works as an ultraviolent brawler, who feuds with Wolf Hawkins and Tommy Cornell and plays rib jokes on the narrator, and in August 2008 beats Eric Tyler in a hardcore match on Badge of Honor, then loses to Tyson Baine at Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 draws with Tyson Baine in a steel cage at Destructive Energy after teaming with Sam Keith to draw with Jeremy Stone and Baine, and in October 2008 is attacked by Tyson Baine, splits Baine's scalp with a chain-wrapped fist, is eliminated from the battle royal and, at Crunch Time, beats Baine in the Chain match. In November 2008 he beats Eric Tyler by disqualification when Marat Khoklov attacks, draws with Ricky Dale Johnson against Khoklov & Remo Richardson, beats Merle O'Curle and flees a backstage brawl with Khoklov, then loses to Khoklov's Moscow Lariat in a No Disqualification match at A Little South Of Sanity. In December 2008 he beats Rhino Umaga, pins Chris Rockwell to win the six-man match and a place in the Triple-Threat defence, and at Psycho Circus is laid out by Tommy Cornell's Rough Ride and a belt shot as Troy Tornado keeps the title.
