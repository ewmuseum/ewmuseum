UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-09-24 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 18 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 12-5-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Trained by Simon O'Neal; pinned Brian McKenzie with a small package to win the Meltdown main event intergender tag match. Beat Tara Smith by submission (Caress of Steel) in a non-title singles rematch on 22 October 2003, wrestling without Simon O'Neal at ringside for the first time. Challenged Stephanie Harper for the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World on 15 November 2003, wrestling without trainer Simon O'Neal at ringside after Vice-President Jessica Marshall barred him; submitted to the Texas Cloverleaf. Beat "Alluring" Arielle Starr by pinfall on 10 March 2004 with a Domination Day suplex after Myra Benedict's knife-wielding distraction gave her the opening; Simon O'Neal coached her from ringside beforehand on countering Starr's high-flying style. Beat "Wildfire" Leslie Hernandez by referee stoppage on 24 March 2004 after knocking her out with a ringside bucket, applying the Caress of Steel once Hernandez could no longer defend herself; Simon O'Neal and the Amazing Romanis brawled at ringside throughout. Won the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World from Stephanie Harper on 15 May 2004 with a somersault plancha followed by a pinfall as the referee's count on both women lapsed; brass knuckles were found at ringside afterward. Teamed with Simon O'Neal against Tigress and Papa Legba for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 19 May 2004, losing when Legba's top-rope splash caught both her and O'Neal at once. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World over Stephanie Harper by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, using brass knuckles and then a foreign object hidden in her own bandana for the Caress of Steel; knocked out manager Simon O'Neal after he proposed marriage to her in the ring afterward. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship over Brianna Landis by pinfall (the Dominion Day windmill suplex) on 18 September 2004, aided by a distraction belt shot from Tara "Sunburst" Marshall. Wrestled a non-title six-woman tag alongside the Bod Squad against Sierra Browne and Team BATTLE*DREAM on 2 October 2004, sneaking into the match in a Greece cosplay disguise after her partners were forced to start a woman short; the match was lost when Sierra Browne's Truth Superkick finished Brianna Landis, and Tetreault ambushed Browne with the women's title belt afterward. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World over "Battle Princess" Misaki Ishikawa by disqualification on 16 October 2004 after Ishikawa was found with a taser at ringside; finished Ishikawa post-match with a Minnie Driver. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World over Kari Stevens by submission (a crossface, rubbing salt and lemon juice into a cut over Stevens's own eye) on 30 October 2004, having hidden a lemon wedge and salt of her own to counter Team Canada's own salt-throwing interference. A UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World defense against "Alluring" Arielle Starr on 13 November 2004 ended without a finish after Sierra Browne, Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith ran in and a chain-loaded glove shot meant for Starr struck Brian McKenzie instead; Tetreault fled with the title still around her waist. Interfered at ringside during the Browne-Harper number one contendership match on 27 November 2004, first tripping Sierra Browne into a rollup loss that Stephanie Harper refused to accept, then tripping Browne again once the match was restarted; fled after Harper and Browne jointly turned on her. Beat Sierra Browne and Paul Wong with Brian McKenzie by pinfall on 11 December 2004, planting smuggled brass knuckles on McKenzie mid-match after a search failed to find them on her; dined beforehand with McKenzie, who revealed plans to dump his own Anheuser-Busch stock. Drugged boyfriend Brian McKenzie's own drink before Chris O'Brien's handicap match with the Hands of Death on 28 May 2005, then watched the beating from the back with amusement. Watched boyfriend Brian McKenzie lose a Dance Dance Revolution challenge to Chris O'Brien at an arcade on 22 June 2005, then broke up a brewing argument between the two Fraternity Boys. Lost a number-one contenders match to "Mockingbird" Nina Grimsson on 20 July 2005 after the referee restarted the bout on Stephanie Harper's own insistence; was later told by Becky Byers and Harper that she would face Harper at Gold Rush under Blind Stipulation Rules. Backed boyfriend Brian McKenzie's backstage assault on Stephanie Harper on 3 August 2005; helped McKenzie ambush Paul Wong with a steel chair and brass knuckles on 6 August 2005, warning that Stephanie Harper's own Gold Rush stipulation match would not go her way. Teamed with Brian McKenzie to win the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament's opening round on 14 February 2006 over Dan Kidd and Kari Stevens and Lolita Love and DOOM!, then was eliminated in the finals alongside McKenzie by Brett Greene and Alex Extreme; earlier joined Chris O'Brien and Keith Marshall backstage plotting elaborate revenge on the returning Simon O'Neal. Set up and won a No Disqualification match against O'Neal on 18 March 2006, chair-shotting him from behind to steal the pinfall after Brian McKenzie had traded blows with him for much of the match.
