Kenjiro Ito

Sean Simon Williams' character — the Cocky Osakan, a strong-style wrestler out of Osaka who held Jolt Wrestling's Ambassador and Triple Crown titles, came into PRIME in 2006 beside Sonny Silver and twice challenged Adam there for the Internet Championship. In 2012, billed as ACW's, he teamed with Jack Harris against INFINITE's pair in IGC's first Intergalactic Championship qualifier. Written by AI, 2026

jOlt Wrestling (2001)

2005-01-23 – 2005-03-22
wrestler — the Jolt Ambassador Champion of 2005, won at WrestleSault I
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 1 parsed match, jOlt Wrestling (2001), this run, placed by show date): 0-0-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Debuts in a segment of his own, 'The Future', at Mardi Gras on 23 January 2005, with no match: 'I am Kenjiro Ito: future of wrestling.' On the first Tuesday Night Intense, 1 February 2005 at the CajunDome, he bets the Back Alley Brawler $5,000 on himself and beats Brandon Youngblood with Ito's Ice Age; the site's report of the next night's house show in Baton Rouge has him against Coburn P. Valencia, the crowd popping for his 'finishing top rope Blizzard Suplex'. He beats Geo Vacton in Block A of the Triple Crown Tournament in Detroit to reach the quarter-finals. At WrestleSault I in Montreal on 27 February, Jeff Garvin beats him in a 27:44 semi-final with the Memphis Death Certificate, and later that night he beats Chris Mezz with Ito's Ice Age to win the Ambassador Championship. With the Back Alley Brawler he humiliates DDR on the 8 March show; on 15 March in Providence, as champion, he wrestles Carson Nash to a no contest in the main event, issues an open challenge to any wrestler outside Jolt for The Retort in Boston on 20 March, and the next night defends the belt on PRIME Wrestling's ReVolution 58 in Anaheim, beating Amy 'Aimz' Campbell with Ito's Ice Age. The preview of the 22 March Tuesday Night Intense asks who faced him for the belt at The Retort.
