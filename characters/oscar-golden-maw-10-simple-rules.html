Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Oscar Golden

One of the Canadian Blondes and tag champions, who finishes with the Golden Shower and loses the MAW Tag Team titles in the Beach Blast ladder match. With Flash Savage the Canadian Blondes beat the Bailey Boys at Summernova in August 2010, win the Tag Team titles for a record third time at Fall Of The King in September and keep them in the four-team ladder match at Where It All Begins Again in October. He fights Jay Chord in the locker room after Season's Beatings in December 2010 and the Canadian Blondes lose the Tag Team titles at New Year, Old Rivalries on 12th January 2011; he beats Brandon Smith at Leap Into The Unknown with the Golden Shower. He pins Jean Cattley with the Golden Shower at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 to win the Tag Team titles with Flash Savage, and the two are brought home drunk, with the belts, by two policemen who are MAW fans. Jay Chord's shouting at him is quelled by the narrator; the Blondes boast on 12th June of being four-time tag champions, debut the O, Canada! on Marshall Dillon at Summer Showdown and use it on Aero at Iron, and he loses a tour match to Jonnie Perez on 25th September. In the diary's pages from June to August 2012 he is a Canadian Blonde who pins Kashmir Singh with an O, Canada! at South East Grand Parade on 9th August. Written by AI, 2026
