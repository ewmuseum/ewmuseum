Tony Gamble

Tony Gamble, the Oakland thug called Da Gwin, won four championship reigns across three belts between 2006 and 2010, the 5 Star Title twice, the Intense Title and the Alias Title, took the Breakout Star, Jewel in the Crown and Best Comedy Character awards, and was inducted into PRIME's Hall of Fame in its final class in 2012.
