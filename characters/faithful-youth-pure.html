Biography (PURE (2002))

Born on July 12, 1980 Andrew Riven or better known as Faithful Youth made his first appearance into the world. Coming from a small Canadian city, Andrew was different in many different ways. For starters he was very strong and powerful kid. He also was very intelligent and so when he did well in school other students wouldn’t dare touch him for they didn’t want to talk with Andrew. Andrew began to lift weights, run long distances, and train every day for something. His parents didn’t know what he was trying to become. His parents where normal people. His father and mother both worked and life went normal. Andrew’s inside life was a different story. He hated talking to people. He was very shy, and would get very nervous in front of large groups of people. His teachers suggest he write down his feelings on paper and communicate that way. It worked. He started off writing just normal sentences but later on wrote poetry, stories and even songs to show how he felt. At the age of 10, Andrew had his first poem published in a book. That was latter followed on by his own book of poems, a novel and a bunch of songs for local-city bands. But the good times would soon turn into dark times. The family went through financial troubles and was just getting by on what they had. Andrew had to do something. Knowing that money could be made as a writer he set out to create his own line of poetry books. Knowing that he would be taken seriously at the age of 12, Andrew created a second identity. It was then that Faithful Youth was born. Faithful for his faith in him self and youth because he was still a kid. He started writing and he spent all his free time making this big. By the age of 13 Faithful Youth’s first poetry book was on sale. Andrew made over $50,000 in the first year with the book. All that money went to his family. Andrew was still get stronger on the outside as well on the inside. By the age of 14 he was 200 pounds of pure power. He never hurt anyone but he just trained to build up a package in himself that would be unbeatable. At 16 the family was doing well. But it wouldn’t last long. Andrew needed something else. Looking at his size and strength the best choice would be either boxing or wrestling. He didn’t care for boxing as it had very little moves to remember. So he choice wrestling and all the money he made was sent back to his parents. So Andrew with no money for himself did the only other thing he knew how to do and that was write. After six years of wrestling in the Canadian circuit he stopped. His parents where more then grateful and offered money to Andrew. He didn’t take it saying that was for them. At age 21 Faithful Youth came back into the world. He put out his third poetry book and did a world tour promoting it. He still had some fear of standing in front of people but that was quickly overcome by reading his work to the people in the audience and 6 years of wrestling. But now at the age of 23, Faithful Youth wants to make the move from writing back into wrestling. The money was fine in writing but the excitement was almost gone. He now had one goal in mind. He was a major person in the writing industry but he didn’t want the years of his training to go to waist. He sets his eyes now on wrestling so he can once again amaze an audience only this time not with his words but with his actions.
