Son's of Thunder

aka Sons of Thunder

James and John, managed by Simon, who won the CHZ Tag Team Championship from Hell's Fury in the fed's first tables match on 9 November 2001 (a win the fed's own title history credits to the Disciples of Thunder), and who ran JT Fringe through a Coca-Cola machine backstage on 23 January 2002.
