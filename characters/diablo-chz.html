Diablo

Half of Team Extreme's Hell's Fury with Fire, CHZ Tag Team Champions from July to November 2001, and the CHZ Hardcore Champion that autumn, who lost a North American Championship strap match by disqualification on 22 March 2002. He set Counterfeit on fire at BreakOut 2002 and, as half of the AAOD with Lucifer, won the Tag Team titles at May HayDay 2 in 2003. Written by AI, 2026
