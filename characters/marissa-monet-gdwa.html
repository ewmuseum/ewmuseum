Tenure detail
The self-described "Great Black Shark" and power of the newly-introduced trio the Misfits, alongside Sierra Browne and Dalbello Rage; competed in her first structured uwf-mar96 match on 21 March 2004, a six-woman tag win over Fantasy Girl, Tesla St. James, and "Alluring" Arielle Starr, setting up Sierra Browne's Showstopper legdrop finish on Fantasy Girl. Attacked afterward at ringside by Tigress, seeking payback for an earlier ambush. Her "Battle of the Sexes" match against Caliban was thrown out without a decision on 18 September 2004 after Caliban's tag partner Serge Annis ran in for a joint Near Death Experience. Called out Caliban and Serge Annis on 27 November 2004 over their earlier attack on her, revealing Shadoe Rage had come to the UWF over it; the confrontation escalated into a brawl between the Prophets of Rage and the Hands of Death that left Caliban driven through a burning table. Went missing from the Prophets of Rage's own dressing room on 11 December 2004 alongside Pizzazz; both were later revealed abducted by Serge Annis and Caliban, chokeslammed off a skybox balcony onto a production table and hoisted back up by dog-collar chains.
