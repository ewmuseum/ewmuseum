Donnie J

Half of the Fly Boys, a high flyer with the shining wizard, who is fed to the Machines on Total Wrestling in June 2008 and taps to Sammy Bach's Bach on Your Back on Badge of Honor when his partner Jimmy P pulls the ropes out of his reach, then watches Jimmy P walk out on him in a loss to the New Wave in August 2008 and gets Mayhem Midden to book Jimmy against Johnny Bloodstone, and in September 2008 makes peace with Jimmy P under Floyd Goldworthy, loses to Kingman & Alexander, joins American Buffalo in attacking them and beats Narato & The Kid. In November 2008 he loses with the Fly Boys to the Young Guns and is eliminated in the six-man contendership match won by Art Reed. In December 2008 he is beaten by the New Wave's Wave of Mutilation and by the Keith Brothers' Proton Lock. Written by AI, 2026
