Accompanies brother Paddy at ringside at March To Madness (2 March 2005), punching Adam Jennings to break up a near-fall and cause the VWF 24 Hour Championship match's own disqualification finish, then rolls dice for a coming tag-team title shot. Teams with Billy Bones against champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov for the VWF Tag Team Championship at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), the match becoming a three-way when the debuting Team Brittania insert themselves; Team Brittania wins by pinning O'Shea. Teams with brother Paddy against Capsman and Lance Voltar on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), on the losing end when Voltar pins Paddy.
