Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Primus Allen

"The Rookie Monster", an outsider who runs through the Masked Patriot at End Of Days in December 2008, is taken into The Firm in place of Ricky Douglas and finishes with the Running Powerslam; he beats Erik Strong at The Rip Chord Invitational and is the Firm's enforcer through 2009. He wins the 2010 Rip Chord Invitational from Erik Strong in January 2010, beats the Masked Patriot in a mask-versus-career match at All Or Nothing in February and loses to Kirk Jameson's Kirk-Hold at March Madness. At Summer Showdown in June 2010 he steps into The Firm's Trios team for the departed Darryl Devine and wrestles twice that night, scoring a pin with the Running Powerslam and losing with Findlay O'Farraday to the Bailey Boys. He boots Findlay for a misfired kick after losing to Jesse Christian at Beach Blast, beats Aaron Andrews for the Traditional title at Iron, keeps it against Kirk Jameson and Joe Benning and loses it to Jonnie Perez at Where It All Begins Again in October, then powerbombs Casey Valentine and leaves The Firm, announcing that he is after the Heavyweight title. At Eleventh Hour in November he draws with Steve Flash and says he will cash in the Invitational's guaranteed title shot on Aaron Andrews at the 2011 Invitational. He beats Stevie Grayson at Season's Beatings in December 2010 and wins with Jay Chord at End Of Days; on 21st January 2011 he spends the title shot his Invitational crown carries on Aaron Andrews and loses, having beaten the Masked Patriot at New Year, Old Rivalries. The narrator can think of nothing to do with him: he beats Brandon Smith at Third Time's The Charm and loses to Fumihiro Ota at March Madness. He returns at Summer Showdown on 26th June 2011 by ambushing Chance Fortune backstage and beating him with the Running Powerslam, squashes Jonnie Perez at Beach Blast, and at Summernova on 12th August wins the Traditional title from Hugh de Aske with a Sitout Spinebuster, a title he keeps against Ernest Youngman at Iron and Steve Flash and Erik Strong at Back To School. The narrator, who has him on a list of expendables for lack of progress, hopes to keep him happy until October, when there may be room for a storyline. He beats Bradford Peverell at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2011 and Riley McManus at Season's Beatings to keep the Traditional title, partners Aaron Andrews in the loss to the Fabulous Bumfholes on 12th November, draws with Jared Johnson at End Of Days and beats Kid Arachnid on 5th January 2012 and Kirk Jameson at The Rip Chord Invitational. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he draws with KC Glenn at Leap Into The Unknown, beats Randy Bumfhole at Third Time's The Charm and Lassana Makutsi at March Madness, and loses the Traditional title at April Showers on 5th April when JD Morgan pins him in the Gold Rush match. He loses to Amazing Fire Fly at South East Crisis on 8th April, introduces his old friend Lenny Brown there and forms a tag team with him, beating Fire Fly and Fumihiro Ota at May The Force Be With You on 5th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he and Lenny Brown are introduced by Haley Buck at High Noon on 5th June as Always Breakin' Hollywood, "the future of professional wrestling", and beat the West Texas Peacekeepers and the Canadian Blondes, Team Honor at South East Madness with a Sit-Out Spinebuster on Jared Johnson and Smith & Singh at Hotter Than Hot with the same move on Kashmir Singh. At Iron on 12th August Allen and Brown lose the three-way to Team Honor and are turned away by Rudy Velasquez, who twists Allen's wrist, and at Back To School on 5th September they beat the XX Factor, Allen insisting that they take no pleasure in beating up women.
