"Epitome of Evil" Serge Annis

aka Serge Annis

The 290-pound "Epitome of Evil" from Oakville, Ontario, who made his EMWC debut on 9 August 1998 against North American Champion Gabriel Whitecross and set the cell on fire after the main event. Written by AI, 2026

Career, observed by the museum, W-L-D: 7-7-1, 2 no contest, 1 unresolved — from 18 parsed matches in 2 promotions. Counted from the match results the museum has parsed, not a record any fed declared.

Tenures

EMWC (1995)

1998-08-09 – 1998-08-09
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 4 parsed matches, EMWC (1995)): 1-2-0, 1 unresolved
Tenure detail
At Blood, Sweat, and Tears (9 August 1998): billed at 290 pounds from Oakville, Ontario, and entering to "Hands of Death" by Rob Zombie and Alice Cooper with fire around the ring, he was kept a secret as Gabriel Whitecross's challenger and the announcers recall his past in the UWF and the IIWF, where he was heavyweight champion; he dropped Whitecross with clotheslines and a plancha, took a Sacred Tailsman STF and two chair shots without falling, hit the Epitomizer chokeslam four times and was disqualified, then chokeslammed Mark Langseth through the announcers' table; in his locker room he told Lori Dane he spent twenty months in the IIWF and had come to the EMWC to start anew, and that the attack was a message; the announcers say Robert Donovan will meet him in the first match of the King of the Death Match tournament; after the main event he stood on top of the cell and set it on fire.

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-11-15 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 14 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 6-5-1, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Billed as the UWF's first-ever World Champion, back when the title was called the Universal Heavyweight Championship; scheduled for a singles match against Cameron Black, who appeared only by satellite to refuse the match, so Youth Gone Wild answered Annis's open challenge instead. Busted open by a top-rope DDT reversal, but won by Epitomizer chokeslam. Teamed with Caliban on 21 March 2004 to beat Fury (Rage and Spice), setting up the pinfall by pulling his own partner down to safety off a double-team attempt and clotheslining Spice over the top rope before Caliban's superplex finished Rage. Teamed with Caliban again on 29 May 2004, billed as the Hands of Death in beating the Fraternity Boys (Chris O'Brien and Brian McKenzie) in a tag confrontation, Caliban pinning McKenzie off a Near Death Experience superplex, then fighting the Ghost Dancers to a double countout in a formal tag match later the same card. Teamed with Caliban as the Hands of Death in a losing Gold Rush triple threat for the UWF World Tag Team Championship on 5 June 2004 against champions the Privateers and the Ghost Dancers, taken out of the match by a triple Conchairto from the Fraternity Boys (Chris O'Brien and Brian McKenzie). Teamed with Caliban as the Hands of Death in a steel cage main event win over Kyle Lee and Alex Kidd on 28 August 2004, helping set up Caliban's own Near Death Experience pin on Kidd after Lee escaped the cage but could not return in time to help. Lost alongside tag partner Caliban to Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian) by pinfall on 2 October 2004, pinned attempting a superplex after Tucker tripped his supporting leg; the returning Prophets of Rage then beat down both Hands of Death members with their own tag title belts after the bell. Never appeared for his own scheduled 30 October 2004 match against Shadoe Rage, vanishing from the entrance ramp mid-entrance during an extended mind-games standoff and forfeiting the bout. Teamed with Caliban as the Hands of Death in a no-contest against Team Canada (Bob Jablonski, Trevor Hextall) on 13 November 2004, before the two challenged the Prophets of Rage to name their own stipulation for a future match, then joined a mass brawl that drew in the Sons of Cacaphony, Uriah Hawkins, Alex Martinez and Alex Extreme. Needled Shadoe Rage on 27 November 2004 over an old IIWF World Championship loss to Steve "The Fury" Kowalski, boasting of having beaten the same man himself; the confrontation escalated into a brawl between the Hands of Death and the Prophets of Rage. A dog collar match against Shadoe Rage was set for Rise to Power. Fought the Amazing Romanis to a no-contest for the UWF North American Tag Team Championship alongside Caliban on 1 December 2004, provoking the Prophets of Rage and hanging the Romanis by dog collars afterward. On 11 December 2004, lured the Prophets of Rage to the ring, then revealed he and Caliban had abducted Marissa Monet and Pizzazz, chokeslamming both women off a skybox balcony onto a production table and hoisting them back up by their own collar chains. Beat Chris O'Brien by pinfall (the Epitomizer) alongside Caliban in a handicap match on 28 May 2005, retaliating for an earlier unprovoked chair attack from O'Brien; interrupted the Sons of Cacophony's own open challenge with Caliban beforehand, drawing the Prophets of Rage and the Amazing Romanis into the same confrontation and setting a fatal-four-way for the World Tag Team Championship. Won a non-title four corners tag main event with Caliban on 11 June 2005 by disqualification, chairshotting both Sons of Cacophony members after the Prophets of Rage and the Amazing Romanis had already been eliminated by countout. Lost with tag partner Caliban and Trey DaMann to the Sons of Cacaphony and Corey Irons on 3 August 2005; on 6 August 2005 recorded a promo inside the Descent Into Hell structure, assaulted Nina Grimsson backstage, and brutalized a non-competing Alex Extreme, throwing him through a Sears display window, before mocking the Sons of Cacaphony via the Unitron. Beat Alex Extreme by count-out in the main event on 18 March 2006 after driving him through two tables and hitting two Epitomizer chokeslams onto a steel chair, then inexplicably walked away mid-match; embraced road agent Joe Reed afterward, ignoring Jessica Marshall's demand to finish Extreme off, in a scene the broadcast read as a possible farewell.
