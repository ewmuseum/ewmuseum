Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Robert Thomas

aka "Lone Wolf" Robert Thomas, Bobby Thomas

"Lone Wolf" Robert Thomas, called Bobby Thomas by the narrator, who "has arrived" in MAW in October 2010: too expensive for the touring shows, short of the star quality to crack the top of the card, he plays a brooding loner and squabbles with Fumihiro Ota over who gets to use the boiler room. He is a debutant on the card for Remember November, against Matt Hocking in a Rip Chord Invitational qualifier. He wins a qualifier over Matt Hocking at Remember November in November 2010, but a booking in CGC takes him out of the Invitational itself and his quarter-final is played as a bonus match on the 18th January 2011 tour show, which Jean Cattley wins; his matches at Leap Into The Unknown and Third Time's The Charm are marred by bad chemistry, and the narrator thinks his loss to Kirk Jameson at April Showers is the first to get the best out of him. On 26th May 2011 he loses to Chance Fortune in an extended tryout that the narrator calls his first decent match, is listed among the expendables for lack of direction and low match quality, and after his dark matches at High Noon is added to the release list with Findlay O'Farraday; he leaves in June.
