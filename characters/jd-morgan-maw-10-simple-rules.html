Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

JD Morgan

A veteran with a technical, old-school style whom Rip Chord produces at All Or Nothing on 26th February 2011 as a challenger for Aaron Andrews: he dominates Andrews in a non-title match and beats him with A Shot Of JD, then joins Firebird, Evil Spirit and Thrash in a four-on-one beating of the champion that Darryl Devine breaks up with a chair. He and Thrash beat Andrews and Kirk Jameson at Third Time's The Charm, he goes to a time-limit draw with Jameson at March Madness, and at April Showers he loses to Andrews in a twenty-five-minute match that ends with Andrews's Rip Chord DDT. The narrator calls him The Fallen's most recent high-profile signing, and Firebird counts him among his followers. He beats the Masked Patriot with the Cross Atlantic Stretch at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, Chance Fortune on 12th May and Steve Flash with A Shot Of JD at Mother, May I? (the narrator's B-). He is Firebird's silent enforcer; at High Noon he eliminates Hugh de Aske and is knocked out on the exposed steel bolt by Aaron Andrews, loses to Fumihiro Ota at Summer Showdown, beats the Internet Favourites with Providence at Hotter Than Hot, loses to Jay Chord at Beach Blast and to Kirk Jameson at Iron, where Firebird watches over him. The narrator calls him third or fourth in his group and thinks he may have a future outside it. In the diary's pages from September 2011 he beats Hugh de Aske at Fall Of The King, Bradford Peverell at All The Leaves Are Brown and Ernest Youngman at Where It All Begins Again, helps Firebird to ambush Kirk Jameson, and wins the ten-man battle royal at Eleventh Hour on 12th November for his first MAW title, the All Action title, for which he is required to defend in randomly chosen matches; he loses it on the night of Remember November, regains it, and from End Of Days on 26th December has Haley Buck as his manager. He keeps it against Jonnie Perez and Providence, beats Riley McManus on 5th January 2012, Wanda Fish and Fumihiro Ota in The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January and loses the final to Kid Arachnid. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he keeps the All Action title against Bradford Peverell, Wanda Fish and Fumihiro Ota in a Triple Threat, Kashmir Singh and Kirk Jameson in a submission match, with Haley Buck at his side, wins the Gold Rush match at April Showers on 5th April, taking the Traditional title from Primus Allen, loses the All Action belt to Donnie J in a ladder match at South East Crisis on 8th April, beats Jared Johnson at May The Force Be With You and keeps the Traditional title against Bradford Peverell and Lord Geoffrey Windameer at Mother, May I? on 12th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he keeps the Traditional title against Steve Gumble in a draw at High Noon on 5th June, Amazing Fire Fly at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Gumble at Hotter Than Hot, Donnie J in a draw at Great Lakes Grand Parade, Black Eagle at Beach Blast, Sean Deeley at South East Grand Parade and Rudy Velasquez at Iron on 12th August. He turns down The Fallen's invitation to join, telling Father Johan he stands alone and Haley Buck that he will not pitch his tent on a viper's nest, hits Father Johan with an elbow at Great Lakes Grand Parade and agrees to help Donnie J and KC Glenn when he is in trouble, without joining the Knights Templar; Haley Buck takes on Deeley instead. At Summernova on 5th August he and Kirk Jameson beat Providence and The Great Ota, and at Back To School on 5th September Deeley pins him in a six-man match. Written by AI, 2026
