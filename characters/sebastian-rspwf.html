Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-09-12 – 2005-02-17
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 32 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 10-20-0, 1 no contest, 1 unresolved
Tenure detail
Unmasked as the Original Masked Man after losing to Sonicman on Firestorm (27 May 2004), becomes Sonicman's manager, then wrestles a mystery-opponent match against The Equalizer, losing by disqualification; teams with Sonicman in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal, eliminating both by his own mistake. Teams with Sonicman, losing the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round to Blaze and The Real Deal, then losing again to Marcus O'Malley in a handicap match. On Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) an unidentified second Orion attacks him in his own dressing room, shown on the arena's own video screens; later interferes in The Maquilladora's own match against Sonicman, locking a submission hold on The Maquilladora before Sonicman superkicks him. Loses the VWF Submission Championship when tag partner Sonicman turns on him in a triple threat also involving The Maquilladora on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) after a chair-shot run-in by The Equalizer, then is eliminated from the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal alongside Sonicman on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) by a second wrestler dressed as Orion, and attacks Kevin Blair backstage over the 24 Hour Championship, taking two Psycho Flow DDTs for it. Beat Sonicman on Wrestle Style V.1, challenged The Maquilladora unsuccessfully for the VWF Submission Championship on Wrestle Style V.2 after being blindsided by a foreign object, then beat Evil Capsman in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Beats Mark McQueen by rollup in the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament's second round on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004). Pinned by champion Lark Fenriz with the Sammael in a Heavyweight title fatal four-way on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), after tricking Classy Joey Boy into a sledgehammer disqualification. Loses the six-man decider on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) when Kevin Simmons pins Robbie Caldwell, then joins Iyasu Saku, Robbie Caldwell and a returning G-Gangsta in a four-on-one beatdown of Classy Joey Boy, finishing with his own Legend Killer Piledriver. Loses with tag partner G-Gangsta by count-out to Classy Joey Boy and a returning Randall Crane at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), fleeing the ring rather than face Crane; chair-shotted unconscious backstage afterward by a vengeful Adam Jennings. Refuses to wrestle Randall Crane on March To Hell (21 October 2004), stepping aside while Robbie Caldwell tazes and beats Crane instead, ruled a disqualification loss; also fights Capsman and Evil Capsman to a no contest earlier the same show, teamed with G-Gangsta. Interferes for Gangsta against Fast Eddie Cutlass and chairs Randall Crane after Crane's own disqualification win over Robbie Caldwell on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), then offers Classy Joey Boy the Riders of the Apocalypse's own open spot, taking two Kiss of Deaths and a powerbomb for the trouble. Eliminates himself from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), diving over the top rope to keep attacking an already-eliminated Randall Crane. Beats down stablemate Iyasu Saku in an apparent staged play to draw out Paddy O'Shea on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), then wins the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match, pinning The Maquilladora after Robbie Caldwell tazes her from outside. Eliminates Evil Capsman, Capsman and, betraying tag partner Sonicman with a second Legend Killer, Sonicman himself in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), before mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov eliminates him last. Loses a six-man tag alongside Robbie Caldwell to Mark McQueen, Thunder Valenkov and Adam Jennings on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after tag partner G-Gangsta fails to appear, then calls Gangsta out on the house mic and is beaten down and told of Gangsta's own resignation from the Riders of the Apocalypse. Teams with Robbie Caldwell to beat CAPSMEN! on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), earning the pair the right to book their own Ghost Town Blues matches. Hits back-to-back Legend Killers on Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora in the Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) fatal four-way before G-Gangsta's own Gangsta Smash finishes him for the loss; the two men are booked to retire against each other at Ghost Town Blues. Loses to G-Gangsta in a Steel Cage Double Retirement Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), pinned when Gangsta reverses a back body drop into a bridge, ending both men's own VWF careers; raises Gangsta's hand afterward and helps fight off a post-match attack from a returning Hot Property (Stevie Knight and Austin Lee) with the Legend Killer.

Tenure detail
The promotion's first 24 Hour Champion, in August 2001, and champion again that month; wrestled masked against Kerry Powell before his unmasking; BWF President from Halloween 2001. Lost to Jack Spade by disqualification, teamed with Tai Angela Classy Joey Boy Maquilladora, lost Maquilladora at Ghost Town Blues. Appears across VWF documents, February June 2002.
