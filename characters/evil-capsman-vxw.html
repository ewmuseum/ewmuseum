Tenure detail
Wrestled as The Rhombus's own "CAPSMAN," tagging with the real Capsman's regular partner Sonicman, before turning on Sonicman mid-match and unmasking as the impostor. Infiltrates The Rhombus's own corner disguised as Capsman again at Ghost Town Blues, costing Sonicman the match, then teams legitimately with the real Capsman as "Capsmen" on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004). Teams legitimately with the real Capsman to beat Blaze and The Real Deal on Firestorm (3 May 2004), scoring the pin with the Evil Capitol Punishment. Teams with Capsman to open the Gauntlet, pinning Blaze, then enters the battle royal and is eliminated by Kenny Kidd. Loses to The Mistress on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then his own team is eliminated by The Salvation in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Teams with Capsman as CAPSMEN! in the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round, losing to The Mighty Men, then competes in the sixteen-man Dog Days 3 tag scramble and is eliminated from the tournament in the Tag Team Elimination Ladder Match on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Loses to the Unholy Alliance on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) after offering The Mistress a rose, then is eliminated from the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal with tag partner Capsman on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Tapped out to Orion's Drunken Rage on Wrestle Style V.1 alongside tag partner Capsman, then lost to Sebastian in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Helps eliminate champion Kevin Blair early from the VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004). Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship match to Blaze and Real Deal by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) after The Mighty Men run in. Submits to Paddy O'Shea's own Chicken Wing, losing a six-man tag with Capsman and Sonicman (billed together as The Rhombus) to The Mighty Men and a returning O'Shea at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004). Rolls dice at March To Madness (2 March 2005), one of five men Kingston Cash books into a St Patricks Day elimination match. Attacked backstage by Paddy and Frank O'Shea before St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005) and hospitalized, withdrawn from the elimination match.
