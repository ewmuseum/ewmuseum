Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 36 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 14-20-0, 1 no contest, 1 unresolved
Tenure detail
Teams with Kenny Kidd to win the VWF Tag Team Championship in the Gauntlet's own Four Corners match, then enters the battle royal and is the first man eliminated, shoved off the top rope by his own opponent Kevin Blair. Loses the VWF Tag Team Championship to Hot Property in a Flashback ambush on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then wins the Dog Day One tag team battle royal with Kenny Kidd, eliminating Austin Lee. Teams with Kenny Kidd as the Rodeo Boys, losing the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round to Marcus O'Malley wrestling alone, then beaten down by Hot Property and Flash in a special Flashback and competing in the sixteen-man tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Accidentally Psycho Flow DDT'd by his own partner Kevin Blair in a six-man tag loss on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), submits to Marcus O'Malley's The Kill in the Dog Days Four Tag Team Elimination Match, then is thrown out with tag partner Kenny Kidd in the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Returns after months in an alcoholic rehabilitation facility to challenge Lark Fenriz for the VWF Heavyweight Championship on Wrestle Style V.3, losing cleanly to the Sammael; Kingston Cash reveals a contract stipulation releasing both Rodeo Boys, Bones and Kenny Kidd, from the VWF as a result. Brands Marcus O'Malley for a disqualification loss to the VWF Tag Team Champions on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Loses the title rematch on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), submitting to Mark McQueen's own Devils Snare after his own Eight Second Ride is kicked out of. Brands The Reckoning's face and pins The Salvation to win a weapons-advantage Number One Contenders match at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004). Helps eliminate Capsman first in the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) with the Double Eight Second Ride, before being eliminated himself by The Maquilladora off a missed Missile Dropkick. Eliminated by Sonicman's own High Back Body Drop in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004). Loses to Sonicman by Small Package on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), then works over The Reckoning's ankle with a chair from ringside during Kenny Kidd's own title-feud win. Interferes on Lance Voltar's behalf against Kenny Kidd on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), then wrestles the VWF 24 Hour Championship match under a mask as The Masked Nevadan, unmasked after Adam Jennings's own Hyper Driver retains the title, and joins Robbie Caldwell in a post-match tazer attack on the champion. Eliminated early from the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet by Classy Joey Boy's own clothesline on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005). Pinned by Thunder Valenkov in the Rings Of Glory match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), ending the Rodeo Boys' own half early and letting Valenkov join Mark McQueen's ring to finish off Kenny Kidd. Loses to Capsman at March To Madness (2 March 2005), pinned after a Bronco Buster. Teams with Frank O'Shea against the champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov for the VWF Tag Team Championship at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), the match becoming a three-way when the debuting Team Brittania insert themselves; Team Brittania wins by pinning O'Shea. Teams with Kenny Kidd as the Rodeo Boys against Sidious The Clown and Kagedo at End Of March (30 March 2005), forcing Sidious to tap. Loses to a returning Revolution Randall Crane by disqualification on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005) after going after Crane with a chair; does not enter the wing's own closing Gauntlet battle royal despite Robbie Caldwell's own demand for the match.

Tenure detail
Announced his own retirement from VXW at Xtreme Machine I (8 March 2006, an earlier pass).
