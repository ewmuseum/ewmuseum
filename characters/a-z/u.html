Ultimate Phoenix

aka Firebird
Forum2008–2012
Units: The Dark Raptors, The Fallen, The Watch
Title reigns: 1 (1 as part of a tag team)

MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

A masked high-flyer signed in July 2008 who lectures rivals on citizenship and freedom and finishes with the Phoenix Firebird Splash. He beats Black Eagle in a Loser Leaves Town match in September 2009, leads The Watch against The Firm for the first Trios titles in March 2010, loses The Watch's series at Spring Fever in April and turns on his own side with a chair. At Summer Showdown on 23rd June 2010 he swerves a chair attack on Ricochet to chair Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh, his former Watch partners, and pulls off his blue-and-white mask to show a black-and-red one with flames: "I am Firebird!" He preaches a crusade in which a man must fall to the bottom of his soul before he can find the light, draws with Fumihiro Ota at Summer Showdown, beats Amazing Fire Fly, Kashmir Singh, Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson in the following months, and loses to Brandon Smith's Inverted Piledriver at Where It All Begins Again in October 2010. He loses to the Masked Patriot at Remember November in November 2010, beats the Internet Favourites with Evil Spirit at Season's Beatings, and in the 2011 Invitational beats Riley McManus and Steve Flash before losing a semi-final to Kirk Jameson. He beats the Masked Patriot in February, introduces Thrash at All Or Nothing and loses to Aaron Andrews for the Heavyweight title at March Madness; at April Showers he and Thrash lose a Tag Team title challenge to Cattley and Singh, and he greets Black Eagle, whom he forced out in 2009, listing Thrash, Greg Ford, Marc Speed, Evil Spirit and JD Morgan as his followers. At Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 he and Black Eagle lose to Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson; at Mother, May I? he and Black Eagle, as the Dark Raptors, lose a Tag Team three-way when Oscar Golden crotches him on the top rope, and he presents the converted Masked Patriot as Providence, tells Kirk Jameson that the path is his own and offers him a way to the dark. At High Noon the Bullseye from the top rope eliminates him from the Gold Rush; he wins with Providence at Summer Showdown, and at Back To School he and JD Morgan lead a Fallen trio against The Firm for the Trios titles and he is pinned by Randy Bumfhole's Bumfhole Buster. At Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011 he tells Kirk Jameson that his only match is against his own mentor, Black Eagle, and after losing to Jameson at All The Leaves Are Brown laughs as Jameson refuses to strike him; at Where It All Begins Again he sits alone in the dark to say that The Fallen have failed and that "from now on, we will be everywhere". With Black Eagle he is billed as the Dark Raptors, beats the Young Americans and Lassana Makutsi and Amazing Fire Fly, loses the tag three-ways, helps Black Eagle to the All Action title, and loses to Kid Arachnid in the Invitational semi-final on 12th January 2012. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he beats Randy Bumfhole at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, Bradford Peverell at March Madness and KC Glenn at May The Force Be With You, draws with Steve Gumble at Spring Fever and, with Black Eagle in hospital, takes a singles run; he calls in Father Johan as an ally, takes Sienna LeNoir's service for himself and then sends her away, and with Father Johan beats KC Glenn and Kirk Jameson at Mother, May I? on 12th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 Firebird beats Kirk Jameson with the Phoenix Firebird Splash at High Noon on 5th June, loses a submission match to Donnie J when Sienna LeNoir throws in a towel at South East Madness, wins the Tag Team titles with Black Eagle at Summer Showdown on 12th June, loses to Donnie J at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July and, with The Great Ota, beats Peverell and Jameson at South East Grand Parade on 9th August. At Iron on 12th August he throws Donnie J into crates, helps Eagle win the All Action title, and then strikes Eagle in the throat in a corridor, meaning to capitalise; at Back To School on 5th September he loses the Tag Team titles with Eagle to Team Honor, and he calls himself the leader of The Fallen.
