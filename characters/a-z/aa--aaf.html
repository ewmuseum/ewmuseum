Forum2010–2012
Units: The Fallen
Title reigns: 3

MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

"Double A", a TCW star signed in February 2010 to replace Steven Parker, who loses to Champagne Lover in the contender's tournament, draws with Jay Chord for the Heavyweight title at April Showers (Rip Chord then rings the bell on his skull) and wins the Traditional title from Hugh de Aske with the Flying Body Press at Spring Fever, keeping it against Antonio in May 2010. He keeps the Traditional title in a triangle match against Casey Valentine and Jared Johnson at Summer Showdown in June 2010, against Firebird at Hotter Than Hot and against Toxin at Beach Blast, loses it to Primus Allen at Iron and to Firebird at Back To School, and with Brandon Smith beats Jay Chord and Firebird at Fall Of The King, after which the Board of Directors overrules Rip and orders his Heavyweight title match. He beats Hugh de Aske in a steel-cage match at All The Leaves Are Brown, wins the Heavyweight title from Jay Chord in a thirty-minute Iron Man match at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2010, by two falls to one, and keeps it against Joey Poison at Eleventh Hour in November. He keeps the Heavyweight title against Hugh de Aske at Remember November in November 2010 and Matthew Gauge at Season's Beatings, against Jay Chord in a street fight at New Year, Old Rivalries in January 2011 and Primus Allen at the Invitational, and signs a new deal for a full year in the face of Tommy Cornell's threat to pull him back to TCW. JD Morgan beats him in a non-title match at All Or Nothing in February and he is beaten down by Morgan, Firebird, Evil Spirit and Thrash until Darryl Devine drives them off; he beats Firebird at March Madness and JD Morgan at April Showers, and at the end of that match looks first at Rip Chord and then at Morgan. He and Kirk Jameson beat Firebird and Black Eagle at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 and he keeps the Heavyweight title against Black Eagle and Erik Strong in May. At High Noon on 12th June he wins the six-man Gold Rush by turning on his friend, dropping Jameson with a Standing Hot Shot onto an exposed steel turnbuckle bolt; at Summer Showdown he does it again, reopening the wound, and from then on he is the company's villain, who tells the Hotter Than Hot crowd that he will fight anyone but Jameson. He beats Fumihiro Ota, Hugh de Aske, Steve Flash, Chance Fortune and Ernest Youngman with the Flying Bodypress or the Twisting Face Crusher through September. In the diary's pages from September 2011 he keeps the Heavyweight title against Brandon Smith at Fall Of The King on 26th September, draws with Randy Bumfhole at All The Leaves Are Brown, and at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October beats Kirk Jameson, who cashes in the Invitational champion's title shot, telling him that he turned on him because he thought Jameson wanted his spot. He hires Shady K and Knuckles as Lockdown, pays them to guard him, cuts up Citizen X's shoes and is dressed down by the narrator, writes into a new contract the right to refuse any challenger, and beats Chance Fortune, Steve Flash and Fumihiro Ota. The narrator's January note says he is living up to his growing reputation as Jay Chord's successor by pranking Kirk Jameson. After Lockdown are fired at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012 he beats Randy Bumfhole in the main event, his biggest victory to date. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he keeps the Heavyweight title against Jared Johnson (the pair have zero chemistry, the narrator says), Kashmir Singh, Wanda Fish in her last match, Brandon Smith and Jim Force, has Randy Bumfhole thrown out of the Gold Rush match at April Showers on 5th April, in which he comes through without being pinned, and keeps the belt against Steve Gumble, Randy Bumfhole in a steel cage match on 12th April, his thirtieth defence, and Donnie J. At May The Force Be With You on 5th May he beats Donnie J in a title-versus-title match for the All Action belt as well, loses it back to him four days later at Great Lakes Madness, and beats him at Mother, May I? on 12th May in a thirty-minute Iron Man match by two falls to one. The narrator chews him out twice, once for being a dick backstage to Antonio and again for spreading a rumour about Jared Johnson. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he keeps the Heavyweight title against Lassana Makutsi at High Noon on 5th June, which he chooses to open the show, Fumihiro Ota at South East Madness on 9th June, in what the narrator counts as his thirty-fourth defence, KC Glenn at Summer Showdown on 12th June, Bradford Peverell at Hotter Than Hot on 5th July, in what the narrator calls the worst main event in years, Kid Toma on 12th July and Rich Money in a thirty-minute draw on 5th August. The narrator fines him for spreading more rumours. At Summer Showdown he swings the belt into Kirk Jameson's head and shakes Father Johan's hand to join The Fallen; at Iron on 12th August he and The Great Ota lose to Money and Jameson, Money pinning him with the Bank Roll, and at Back To School on 5th September he tells The Fallen that the group does not work without him as champion and loses a non-title steel cage match to Money.
