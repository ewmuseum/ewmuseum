Heard on the arena PA during the singles battle royal of 8 February 1998 as a shadow on the big screen, saying he has watched the league for a couple of weeks and is ready to 'kick some ass'; he names himself Ponyboy Kertins and says 'most people know me as being Golden, but thats a whole other story'.
