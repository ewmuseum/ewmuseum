Hessian

PCW Rising Star Champion for a single day in January 2002, across an eleven-card run that winter, Hessian held the PRIME Intense Title for 88 days in 2009 and the Universal Championship twice from November 2010, the second reign running to Colossus VIII, and was inducted into the PRIME Hall of Fame in its final class in 2012.
