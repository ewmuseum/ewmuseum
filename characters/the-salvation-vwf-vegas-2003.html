Tenure detail
Wins the Dog Day Two tag team tournament for The Mighty Men, pinning The Mistress in the first round with the Swing Low Sweet Chariot, then competes in the sixteen-man Dog Days 3 tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Wins the Dog Days Four Tag Team Scramble on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) with a loaded fist, knocking out his own tag partner Marcus O'Malley to do it, then is eliminated from the tag Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004). Pinned by The Outlaw Morris Bass's chokeslam for the New Age Army in the ten-man tag loss to the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1. Part of a six-man brawl with Capsmen, Iyasu Saku and Sonicman ruled a no contest on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Loses to Alcoholics Anonymous (Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen) by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) after The Mighty Men isolate an injured McQueen, then runs in to break up a Capsman near-fall during the VWF Tag Team Championship match, handing Blaze and Real Deal the disqualification win. Teams with The Reckoning and a returning Paddy O'Shea to beat The Rhombus (Capsman, Evil Capsman and Sonicman) in a six-man tag at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), O'Shea submitting Evil Capsman with the Chicken Wing. Clears house early with a running clothesline before The Reckoning's own accidental clothesline on the referee costs the team a disqualification loss to the Society of Doom on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Loses again on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), teaming with The Reckoning and a returning Paddy O'Shea against The Rhombus. Knocked out with a branding iron and pinned by Billy Bones in a losing, weapons-for-the-challengers-only Number One Contenders match at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004). Publicly refuses Kingston Cash's proposed tag-title handicap-match stipulation on The Mighty Men's own behalf on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), then helps clear the ring in the post-match brawl after Adam Jennings retains the VWF 24 Hour Championship. Eliminated from the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), triple-teamed by the Riders of the Apocalypse after tag partner The Reckoning leaves him alone to challenge Thunder Valenkov.
