Umaga

A Samoan power wrestler billed as undefeated, managed by Jacob Stranton, who squashes the older roster with the Savage Charge and challenges Freddie Datsun for the World Championship. In October and November 2007 he loses to Freddie Datsun, is kicked by Akima when Divinity pretends to take the Wild Savages in, loses a World Championship match to Triple D in an A*, beats Akima at Pilgrimage and is on Datsun's side by the Monday of Week 3. In late November and December 2007 he wins with Datsun over Team Seduction, beats Jacob Jett, draws with Fumihiro Ota and with Human Arsenal, and is booked with Datsun against Team Seduction at Winter Epiphany.
