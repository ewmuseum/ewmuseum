Interfered, with a masked ally billed as "Sinister Minster," in a June 2008 title match on behalf of The Movement. Won the GWF Tag Team Championship alongside Damian DeNiro, a reign recovered only as a flashback. Lost to The Reprobate in the FNB Reunion's own US Title tournament. At 20113, became Co-Cruiserweight Champion with Debolt Dragonsbane before the belt was unified into the International Xtreme Championship the same night.
