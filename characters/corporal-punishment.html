Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Born in Bolt West Virginia, the Corporal got his start in the real world by beating up weaker children in school. The neighborhood bully was the Corporals name to fame. Breaking childrens bikes and pushing children off the sidewalk in front of traffic was one of his most favorite things growing up. But that all changed when he enlisted in the United States Marine Corp. The Corporal fell right at home when he was placed in Special Forces. To him, there was nothing like being sent off to foreign countries to assassinate non-Americans. The best part of the ordeal was that he was doing it in the name of the good ole US of A. This career gave the Corporal an unsurpassed feeling of pride seeing as he was killing people in the name of patriotism. For 10 years, the Corporal became the most ruthless assassin the United States Marine Corp. ever trained. This would all end however. After the 1993 Russian blood bath incident, the Corporal was asked to step down as the captain of the S.S.A.R.C. for publicly admitting to the Russian media that he enjoyed listening to their fallen comrades scream while he cut of every left ear of theirs. He then pulled the ear-skinned necklace that he created from the fallen comrades detached lobes, and laughed as they began to scream in anger. The Corporal enjoyed the need for confrontation so much that instead of retiring from service, he transferred from military duty to the world of corrections. There he would find an even more compassioned love for sadistic-ness as the chief warden at the infamous Attica State Prison. For the first time, his ruthlessness could be done out in the open and the lack of compassion the public had in the prison industry, made the Corporal an even bigger public name. His tactics were not only ruthless and heartless, but the large majority of American citizens gave praise for the way he executed inmates for their crimes. The Corporal became hated amongst the inmate population, but respected amongst the uniform staff. Law enforcement agencies began using the Corporal as the standard way of doing things in the inner cities across the United States. The Corporal had found yet another perfect fit for his thirst of giving pain. It was not until 2000 that the Corporal became interested in the professional world of wrestling. His antics for cruelty made him a fan favorite no matter how ruthless and disloyal he was to those who professed good as their handle in the ring. The Corporal made a world-renowned name for himself in several small structured wrestling agencies. It was within 2 years that the Corporal handily began to dismantle small wrestling organization by literally destroying their competition in the name of MARTIAL LAW. The Corporal would conquer twelve wrestling federations before entering four federations that would mold his career as the cruelest and most sadistic name in wrestling. The TWF, UWF, HCWF and GIWF would welcome the Corporal with open arms solely for the purpose of humiliating the jobbers of professional wrestling. The Corporal would enter the most famous stable in wrestling history 'The Youngbloods' and as their chief enforcer the Corporal could blindly torment all that dared to stand in the Corporals way of enforcing Martial Law on those who apposed the YB way of family business. Having the slogan, Dont take it PERSONAL JUST TAKE IT CORPORAL STYLE! Corporal Punishment has created a fan base that resembles a neo-nazi movement. Instead of spreading racism and bigotry, the Corporal fan base spreads the thirst for pain and devastation. Known as Punishment-ites The Corporals loyal fan base travel far and abroad to ensure that when the Corporal enters the ring, the Corporal has thousands watching his back. And thousands ready to retaliate at his beckoned word. Thus why the Corporal is literally the most dangerous man in wrestling PERIOD.
