Art Reed

A technician the narrator signed in June 2007 and has stuck with a rapper gimmick: he hands out his album's CD, beats Bulldozer Brandon Smith for the National number one contendership, loses a National Championship match to William Hayes, splits matches with Fumihiro Ota, who attacks him, and teams with Triple D and Muffin. In October 2007 he helps Rudy Velasquez beat Steve Flash, then beats Flash at A Haunting in America, and is booked into a three-way with Jacob Jett and Wanda Fish for the National Championship at Pilgrimage. In October and November 2007 he beats Wanda Fish with Jacob Jett's help, loses to Phlex and Davey Wayne, beats Giant Oak, loses the three-way at Pilgrimage, is kept in Divinity by Seduction, beats Eddie Peak with the Dread Lock on the Monday of Week 3, and is destroyed by Peak and Big Cat Brandon, after which Devine puts him out.
