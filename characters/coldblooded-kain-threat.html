PWW (1997)

wrestler — one of the three men of America's Most Wanted, the stable Dr. Moreau led to the ring, with Congo and Northern Touch
Tenure detail
The federation's own broadcast books him in no match and stages him three times. First in the brawl that had spilled past the previous week's show going off the air, replayed to open this one: he hits the ring alongside Congo, takes a beating from Mav Richards and the Assassin, then holds his own manager back from Johnny Blaze and is led to the back with the rest of the stable. Then at the foot of the ramp with them, watching the opening contest that decided the stable's fourth member. Then in the ring afterwards, looking the winner over and walking out with him. The crowd's own signs read "AMW, the 3 baddest men on the planet." The commentary places a tag team of his with Army of One before an earlier closure of the federation, so he was in it before this card; the broadcast gives him no billing line, no vitals and no hometown. First and last seen are the one dated document's own date. Undated beside it: Jay Phoenix's own roleplay remembers wrestling him to a twenty-minute time-limit draw in Phoenix's first match for the federation, when Kain Threat was its Television Champion and had held the belt for months, and being clocked with that belt on the way out of the ring.
Source: The undated draw is from Jay Phoenix's own roleplay "No More Tears" (phoenix.rpcommunity.com, captured 10 June 2004; the same piece posted to rpcommunity.com's own Quill forum, captured 18 July 2004; rpcommunity mine).
