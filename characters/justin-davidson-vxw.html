VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2005-03-17 – 2005-04-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 3 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)): 2-1-0
Tenure detail
Debuts with brother Simon as Team Brittania at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), inserting the team into a VWF Tag Team Championship match already underway between champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov and challengers Frank O'Shea and Billy Bones; pulls Thunder Valenkov from the ring to stop a cover, letting Simon pin O'Shea for the titles. Defends the titles with Simon against McQueen and Valenkov at End Of March (30 March 2005), losing by disqualification when the returning Hot Property run in on McQueen; a legal challenge filed the same night disputes whether Team Brittania ever legitimately won the championship. Helps win the VWF Tag Team Championship match against a stand-in wrestling as the absent Van Wild on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), knocking the stand-in off the apron for partner Simon Davidson's cover. Enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, eliminating Classy Joey Boy before going over the top rope himself in a triple spill caused by CAPSMAN's own flying bodypress.
