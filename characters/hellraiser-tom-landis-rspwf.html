Tenure detail
Billed from Chicago, Illinois, a former UWF Heavyweight Champion of the World; challenged Paul Wong for the UWF Rampage Championship and lost when his own allies, Team Canada, interfered and were disqualified on 9 August 2003. Won the 30 August 2003 tag team main event with Scott Daniels over Quinn Brown and Alex Kidd, exposing a turnbuckle to hit Kidd with a Crossed-Arms Bomb. His 24 September 2003 singles match against Cameron Black was thrown out as a no-contest when Serge Annis attacked the Pride. Teamed with Scott Daniels and Team Canada as the Pride in a non-title, count-out loss on 22 October 2003, after Paul Wong turned Kari Stevens's own pepper spray back on him. Entered the 17 December 2003 battle royal, eliminated by Cameron Black with a right hand. Married to Tara Marshall; lost an intergender tag match with her on 24 March 2004 to Alex Kidd and Alicia Powers, pinned after Kidd's Somersault Bulldog set up Powers's own cover. Teamed with Team Canada (Trevor Hextall and Bob Jablonski) in a Meltdown main event win over Quinn Brown and Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker and Mike Sebastian) on 25 August 2004, his own Hextall-assisted Game Misconduct heart-punch flooring Andrew Tucker for the pin; manager Kari Stevens's own salt throw was turned back on her by Quinn Brown. Interfered repeatedly for the Pride during the Bluff City Streetfight main event on 27 November 2004, supplying weapons and blindsiding the One-Winged Angel, before a mystery attacker disguised as a fan shattered Landis's own crutch over his bad leg and DDTed him. Welcomed "Dead End" Derek Martin to the Pride on 11 December 2004 and taunted Shaun Borders, his own recent attacker, over the microphone. Beat a diminutive stand-in sent in Shaun Borders's own place by pinfall (a modified Diamond Dust) on 28 May 2005, having set up the mismatch himself; the genuine Shaun Borders never appeared. Chose Trey DaMann as Shaun Borders's own opponent on 6 July 2005, then attacked Borders with Borders's own Diamond Dust finisher after Borders appeared to have the match won, handing DaMann the pin. Lost to Rick Marley, Borders's own chosen opponent, by pinfall (the Limelight) in the companion match, after the Mercenary and Borders each separately interfered. Introduced as one half of the Cornerstones with Daniel Kidd on 23 July 2005, challenging Shaun Borders to a Gold Rush match; interfered at ringside during the main event tag match without officially entering it. Lost to "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn by pinfall on 3 August 2005 after Shaun Borders interfered at ringside and hurled him back into a Skyscreamer, then struck him with the Diamond Dust after the bell; his own Cornerstones interference later helped cost tag partner Daniel Kidd the main-card bout with Alex Martinez. Interfered as one of the Cornerstones in the North American Tag Team title match on 18 March 2006, then confronted Colby Greene on the interview platform and was driven off by a returning Madison J. Valentine; also cost Juan Vasquez's debut match a clean finish before Don't Go There turned the tables on him.
