Biography (RUSH (2002))

Matt Elia was the older of two children born to Alexander and Robin Elia. Both Alex and Robin’s family’s were immigrants from Italy. Alexander’s farther was a very rich diplomat and fled Italy once Mussolini began to take control of the country. Fleeing with his money Alexander SR. set up a very well to do business. After Alexander SR. death Alexander Elia Jr. took over the company as well as the inheritance of the billons of dollars that his father had earned. Now cut to a picture perfect meeting of Alex Jr. and Robin the daughter of the other local Boston construction company. As the two tied the knot so did the two companies and the Elia name became a household name…literally as thousands of apartments and homes began to pop up all over Boston with the Elia name imprinted in everyone. Now speed ahead 3 years to a hot and steamy September night in 1976. Sure enough after a long night of you know what Robin Elia walked out of the bathroom with that little pink line. Baby time….Matthew Ryan Elia was born on May 13, 1977. 3 years later on May 6 1980 Chris Elia was born. Cut ahead 13 years. Matt and Chris have been home schooled paid in full by their parents. Both children have received the intellect that most children would be jealous of. The only problem is that both children lack strong friends. Cut ahead 10 more years. Matt has just finished graduate school at Oxford University in England. Although Matt was smart and would most likely have been valedictorian of his high school class, (had he attended) his parent’s money attracted the looks of Oxford. Now surrounded by the smartest and brightest students in the entire world Matt began to become intimidated and would come close to flunking out. In fact the Elia fortune helped him stay in school as long as he did. Chris on the other hand was beginning school locally in Boston at the University of Harvard. He would eventually go on to graduate Suma Kum Luad of his class. Cut ahead 6 months. Matt and Chris have both decided to take their interest of pro wrestling to work. With the money that they have they both bought wrestling trainers and soon began to train. After lots of hard work…well hard work for the brothers…they found themselves wrestling in Indy gigs. Eventually they landed themselves in a developmental fed known as NZW which would eventually lead them into PCW. After a short stint in PCW the brothers were asked to move to PCW2. After decline the offer they moved to NTICW in which they found much success by capturing the tag tittles. After 2 more times of holding these titles the brothers would eventually go on to be one of the more successful teams of NTICW. After leaving NTICW for PCW again the brothers were again unable to find success and found themselves off the roster both nursing neck injuries. It seemed then that the brothers had disappeared from wrestling all together. But Matt reemerged in the PCW atmosphere. Matt began to produce an extremely popular column known as 20 Questions for Boobie Boy. The column became a cult classic and was read and enjoyed by many. Now after 6 months away from the ring Matt has decided to return. With brother Chris by his side for help Matt hopes to find renewed success in RUSH WRESTLING.
