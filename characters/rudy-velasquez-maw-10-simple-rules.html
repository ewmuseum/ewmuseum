Rudy Velasquez

A Latino high-flyer and brawler, raised in a street gang, whom Rip Chord announces at Summernova on 5th August 2012 as a signing of a few hours earlier and debuts in a three-way for the All Action title against Donnie J and Eddie Powell; he wrecks Powell, trades aerial exchanges with the champion and is beaten by Donnie's Death From Miami Beach. The narrator writes that he has been tracking him since day one and that only the company's heavy heel bias then stopped him bringing him in. At Iron on 12th August he turns down Primus Allen and Lenny Brown's offer, made for their manager, to be the "grit" of the group she is putting together, twists Allen to his knees, and loses to JD Morgan's A Shot Of JD in a Traditional title match; at Back To School on 5th September he taps out to Kirk Jameson's Kirkhold in what the narrator calls a compelling match, having said that what he does he does by himself.
