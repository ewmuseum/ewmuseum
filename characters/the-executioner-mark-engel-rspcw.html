"The Executioner" Mark Engel

aka Mark Engel, Mark "Samson" Engel

A founding wrestler and, from October 1994, the federation's own Chief of Broadcasting and head booker, whose ring name persisted from the tag-title match at the debut supercard through the injury angle that renamed the top title. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

RSPCW (1994)

1994-03 – 1994-07
Tenure detail
The gimmick is repackaged between April 1994 ("Samson", of the American Dream Team) and July 1994 ("The Executioner") -- an ordinary mid-run repackaging, not a distinct persona. From October 1994 the federation also bills the name as its own Chief of Broadcasting; the wrestler billing itself is unaffected.
Source: The 14 March 1994 live-draw roster call (billed "Mark 'Samson' Engel"); MegaWrestleFest, 10 April 1994 (tag title match, American Dream Team); the federation's own 22 July 1994 statement on Joe Petrow's retirement (billed "'The Executioner' Mark Engel"), all served on this wing.
