The wrestlers there were upset at the fact that he won the company and turn on him. Then left. The XXX is now his and his alone." Also, on the sale to Burns: "He did however sell Pres Burns the XXX efed in 2005." Sources disagree with the-flash-brothers-don-and-jerry-flash's own tenure note on the buy date (2003 vs. 2005) — both are recorded, neither resolved. The same page frames the takeover as a pattern rather than a one-off: "He did the same with the PWF back in the summer of 2003. He has told me that he makes hiself known by going after the top people in the fed. Namely the Presidents. He has closed down two efeds." OWNERSHIP, A THIRD ACCOUNT: his brother Bryan Williams' own served bio names a different owner entirely — "Blain Williams was the ower of the XXX and ran the company for Pres Burns as a farm efed" — their own father, who also trained them both. The two accounts fit if Bobby owned the independent XXX (from November 2002) and Blain ran the post-buyout farm XXX for the buyer; that reading is named here and NOT adopted, since neither page says so. Every XXX championship credit carried by any of this wing's three roster entities was counted against the five served XXX title ledgers, all of which are dated uniformly "1/2006 - 11/2007" and belong to the post-buyout farm era. EVERY CREDIT RECONCILES EXACTLY, leaving no residue that could belong to the independent promotion: Bobby Williams' own "XXX Champion (3)" against xxx-title.html's reigns 2, 11 and 14; his "XXX 2nd Champion (5)" against xxx-2nd-title.html's reigns 2, 4, 6, 15 and 17; his "XXX Tag Team Champion" against xxx-world-tag-team-title.html's reign 36 (with Jason Star). Bryan Williams' own "XXX Champion (4)" against reigns 1, 8, 10 and 13 — matching that ledger's own summary table, "Most Times as Champion: Bryan Williams, 4 times" — and his "XXX 2nd Champion (2)" against reigns 5 and 10. The Flash Brothers' own "XXX World Tag Team Champions (15)" against that ledger's own summary table, "Most Times as Champions: Flash Brothers, 15 times," and their "XXX 2nd Tag Team Champions (2)" against both of xxx-2nd-tag-team-title.html's own Flash reigns.
