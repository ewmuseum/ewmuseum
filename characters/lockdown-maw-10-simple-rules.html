Lockdown

aka Death Row

Shady K and Knuckles, the former SWF team the tew.com notes call Death Row, whom Aaron Andrews buys as his "insurance policy" and introduces at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011 as men who are motivated only by cold, hard cash. They beat the Internet Favourites that night, Team OMGsplosion at Farewell Tour on 22nd November, and, with Knuckles winning the All Action title, the West Texas Peacekeepers at Remember November; at Season's Beatings on 12th December they break up Kirk Jameson's Invitational qualifier and beat Jonnie Perez and Bradford Peverell, at End Of Days they ambush Randy Bumfhole and Fumihiro Ota, and on 5th January 2012 they beat the Peacekeepers. Brandon Smith, Kashmir Singh, Greg Ford and Marc Speed agree to put the tag champions' differences aside; at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January they are charged by all four men at the start of the three-way for the Tag Team titles and put out of the match and, Aaron Andrews says, out of the promotion.
