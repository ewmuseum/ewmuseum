Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Appearance: Jason has a built body with very little body fat. His body is somewhere between Billy Gunn and Lex Luger. Although hailing from England he has a rich tan. When wrestling he wears short black tights, black kneepads, black boots, and black elbow pads. He has sun-bleached hair that comes down to his shoulder blades, always pulled back in a ponytail. He is usually unshaven. On his way to the ring he wears a gown similar to Ric Flairs, but his are black. Gimmick/Personality: No real gimmick. He is very arrogant, cocky, and aggressive. Likes to insult his opponents. Basically the most typical professional wrestling heel you could imagine. Background: Jason was always a very competitive person. Born into an equally sports orientated family, he played some form of sport as soon as he could walk. For the physicality and grittiness of it, Jasons favourtie sport when he was younger was rugby union. With his height and frame he excelled in this sport and became very used to pushing his bodys performance to its limits. At the age of twenty-four the thirst for individual competition turned him to professional wrestling. He also loved the showmanship involved. From then on he has trained and wrestled, and has grown too big for the small ponds of independent federations. This has brought him to the big leagues.
