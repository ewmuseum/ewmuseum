Tenure detail
Introduced as the third member of the Misfits alongside Marissa Monet and Sierra Browne; made her in-ring debut in a six-woman tag win over Fantasy Girl, Tesla St. James, and Arielle Starr, absorbing a Bastard Grinder from St. James before the finish. Beat Lolita Love by pinfall (a super cradleplex) on 22 September 2004 after grinding down her legs for much of the match, then suplexed her over the top rope into sister Leanna Love's arms. Beat Angel by pinfall (a cradle-plex) on 3 November 2004 in a match egged on ringside by Sierra Browne, countering a Headhunter clothesline attempt for the finish. Played cards backstage with the Prophets of Rage on 11 December 2004, sent Marissa Monet out for muffins shortly before Monet went missing; locked the dressing room door as instructed once her brothers went looking for her. Put Marissa Monet through a pool workout ahead of her own UWF Meltdown Championship challenge to Tigress on 25 May 2005, coaching her to make the champion feel the loss of the title rather than settle for a countout or disqualification. Joined Marissa Monet in the Misfits' own backstage argument with Sierra Browne on 8 June 2005 over her handling by the promotion, then propositioned Sierra's own masseur once the room cleared. Rallied with brother Prophets of Rage members Shadoe and Derek, and Marissa Monet, against the Romanis' own interference on 6 July 2005, dubbing the alliance "Intelligent Violence," then beat "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict alongside tag partner Marissa Monet in the Misfits' own debut tag win over the Kindred (Moira Faith, Myra Benedict), earning a future UWF Women's Tag Team Championship match. Won the Women's Tag Team Championship with Marissa Monet from the Psychommunity by pinfall on 6 August 2005, then slapped the fallen Naomi Ishikawa during the celebration. Traded barbs with Nina Grimsson over Sierra Browne's own championship on 18 March 2006, then joined Marissa Monet in issuing an open Women's Tag Team title challenge.
