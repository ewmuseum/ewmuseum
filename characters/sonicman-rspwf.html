Tenure detail
Loses to Marcus O'Malley by disqualification on Firestorm (3 May 2004) after an unidentified masked man interferes on his behalf. Loses a tag match to the Society of Doom (Mark McQueen and Marcus O'Malley) after his own partner, the Original Masked Man, refuses to make a tag and walks out mid-match. Pins the Original Masked Man on Firestorm (27 May 2004), demanding the mask come off to reveal Sebastian, who becomes his manager; interferes in Sebastian's own match against The Equalizer for a DQ, then his own team is eliminated by Sebastian's mistake in the Dog Day One tag team battle royal. Teams with Sebastian, losing the Dog Day Two tag bracket's first round to Blaze and The Real Deal, then losing again to Marcus O'Malley in a handicap match. Loses to The Maquilladora by disqualification on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) in a non-title match after decking the referee. Turns on tag partner Sebastian to win the VWF Submission Championship from The Maquilladora in a triple threat on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004) after a run-in by The Equalizer, then is eliminated from the Wild Card Tag Team Battle Royal alongside Sebastian on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) by a second wrestler dressed as Orion. Lost to Sebastian on Wrestle Style V.1, then interfered in the VWF Submission Championship match to blind Sebastian with a foreign object before losing to debuting Adam Jennings by submission on Wrestle Style V.2. Eliminated from the VWF 24 Hour Championship battle royal on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), then eliminated from the seven-man mini rumble on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), beaten by Fast Eddie Cutlass's own reversed abdominal stretch into the Guarantee. Teams with Capsman and Evil Capsman, billed together as The Rhombus, in a losing six-man tag against The Mighty Men and a returning Paddy O'Shea at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004). Loses to Paddy O'Shea on March To Hell (21 October 2004) after The Mighty Men turn the tide with a ringside kick and powerbomb combination, then helps The Maquilladora clear a four-on-two beatdown of Capsman and Evil Capsman later the same show. Wins as part of The Rhombus over The Mighty Men and O'Shea on March To Hell II (28 October 2004). Eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004) by G-Gangsta's own Buckshot superkick. Eliminated second from the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), caught by Sebastian's own Legend Killer. Eliminates G-Gangsta and Billy Bones in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004) before tag partner Sebastian turns on him with a second Legend Killer and eliminates him. Loses to Billy Bones by Small Package on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005). Draws with Paddy O'Shea on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), the drunken finish setting up a Rhyolite Saloon Match at Ghost Town Blues. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Thunder Valenkov after a mid-match beer break. Loses the Rhyolite Saloon Match to Paddy O'Shea at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) after a bartender secretly working O'Shea's corner punches him in the throat at the final pouring station, letting O'Shea finish his own fourth pitcher unopposed.
