Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Mayhem Midden

A retired wrestler who walks out on the show in May 2008 as the Board's new representative, over Tommy Cornell's protest, to run matches the fans want, bar interference from the Syndicate and Painful Procedure at Total Mayhem 13 and make Ricky Dale Johnson its guest enforcer, and in August 2008 headbutts Wolf Hawkins, names Charlie Thatcher his enforcer, puts Edd Stone's challenger to a fans' web poll and sits at the announce desk for Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 waives a time limit, headbutts Edd Stone to stop a Syndicate brawl, makes the Rockwell and Ino and the Fonzarelli and Perez hair matches, will not sanction Rocky Golden's match with the contractless Marat Khoklov and takes the World Heavyweight Championship finish to the Board at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 announces a tag team tournament, bars Tommy Cornell and Tyson Baine from title matches for six months, makes the ten-man battle royal for the third place in the Crunch Time main event and calls Crunch Time on commentary. In November 2008 he delivers the Board's edict that Troy Tornado's and Johnny Bloodstone's belts are recognised and will be unified at Malice in Wonderland in January, puts Sean Deeley or Freddy Huggins into the All Action Championship four-way and the Stone Brothers into the tag title match, warns Floyd Goldworthy about American Buffalo's fines and calls A Little South Of Sanity on commentary. In December 2008 he orders the Board's matches for the third place in Troy Tornado's defence, sends Chris Rockwell home, calls shows on commentary and announces at Psycho Circus that the Board will introduce a Women's Championship for 2009.
