UWF (23 Mar 1996)

Tenure detail
Billed from Port-of-Spain, Trinidad, accompanied by her sister and videographer Indigo Browne; billed as the final LEW Women's Champion. Defeated Leslie Hernandez by pinfall on 9 August 2003. Defeated Angel of the Furies with the Truth superkick on 24 September 2003, opening the card with a pre-bell chair attack. Lost a non-title tag match with partner Marissa Monet to the Bod Squad on 22 October 2003, struck by her own dazed partner's chair shot before the finish; Monet assaulted her after the bell. Beat Tara Smith by pinfall (superkick) on 20 December 2003 after surviving four straight moonsaults, the fourth landing on Smith's own raised knees; announced afterward that she had already signed Stephanie Harper's open contract for the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World at Eve of Destruction, ahead of a furious Marissa Monet. Introduced on 21 March 2004 as the third member of a new trio, the Misfits, with Marissa Monet and Dalbello Rage; pinned Fantasy Girl with the Showstopper legdrop after a double uranage slam in a six-woman tag win over Fantasy Girl, Tesla St. James, and Arielle Starr. Teamed with Team BATTLE*DREAM (Reona Yoshida, Misaki Ishikawa) to beat Donna Tetreault and the Bod Squad in a non-title six-woman tag on 2 October 2004, finishing Brianna Landis with the Truth Superkick; ambushed by Tetreault with a title-belt shot to the shoulder after the bell. Beat "Superstar" Stephanie Harper by pinfall (a top-rope Sierra Slice) to become the number one contender to the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World on 27 November 2004, after Harper refused an earlier, tainted win handed to her by a meddling Donna Tetreault and the two restarted the match together. Vented to Moe Owens backstage on 1 December 2004 over Donna Tetreault's own interference in her number one contendership win, admitting the tainted result would always bother her even as she looked ahead to Rise to Power. On 11 December 2004, lost a mixed tag match alongside Paul Wong to Tetreault and Brian McKenzie by pinfall after Tetreault planted smuggled brass knuckles on McKenzie. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World over "Alluring" Arielle Starr by pinfall (a top-rope moonsault) on 28 May 2005 in her own first defense, then accepted a title challenge from newcomer "Supervixen" Ami Tran over her own Misfits stablemates' objections. Took a massage backstage on 8 June 2005 while sister Indigo reviewed tape of her own Arielle Starr title defense, brushed off the Misfits' own objections to a future title match with Ami Tran, then watched Tran's win over Kari Stevens from the aisle. Retained the Women's Heavyweight Championship over Angel on 23 July 2005 by pinfall (a superkick into the Truth neckbreaker), after opening the show with a promo challenging the rest of the women's division. Refused to celebrate the Misfits' Women's Tag Team title win alongside sister Indigo on 6 August 2005, superkicking Indigo when she tried to intervene.
