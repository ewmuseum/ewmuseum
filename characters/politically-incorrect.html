Politically Incorrect

William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft, PCW's own Tag Team Champions and part-owners, who held the FSW Tag Team Championship from 20 October to 27 November 2002 and OSW's United States Championship, as co-holders, from 25 May to 29 June 2003. Written by AI, 2026

Appears first as the masked Dead Presidents (Nixon and Kennedy) on 22 August 2002 and takes the FSW Tag Team Championship from Bad Frotitude in a chain match at Bad Moon Rising on 20 October 2002, where the masks come off: “WILLIAM S. POWELL!!”, “REGINALD V. LOVECRAFT!”, “AND THEIR CAMPAIGN MANAGER, ROGER SCHULTZ……THE NEW FSW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, POLITICALLY…INCORRECT!” They keep the titles on 30 October (Magnum Wolf pulls the referee out and signals for the disqualification: “PI RETAIN!”), 6 November (the timekeeper, on the time limit: “the winners of this match and still FSW Tag Team Champions”) and 13 November (beaten by disqualification: “still your tag team champions”), run the Supreme Court stable with Magnum Wolf and Big Daveylicious, and lose the titles on 27 November 2002, at Glory 20, to Jason McAllister and Neurosis (“The new FSW Tag Team Champions walk up the aisle”). The show is the fed's last in the record.
