Always being the lightweight underdog, no one dares have a problem when she works extremely stiff with her attacks. With her background in Wing Chun and Wushu strikes from Lotus done at full force are the same as a 200 pound man’s hits, though she has to let off with a few of her moves that would normally knock someone out.
