Team Honor

Lassana Makutsi and Jared Johnson, a newly formed pairing the narrator says have great chemistry teaming together. They lose to Always Breakin' Hollywood at South East Madness on 9th June 2012, to the Second Sons at Summer Showdown on 12th June, and then, with Randy Bumfhole, beat Black Eagle and the Second Sons at South East Grand Parade on 9th August, Johnson scoring with an MDK Powerbomb; at Iron on 12th August they win a three-way against the Second Sons and Always Breakin' Hollywood, and at Back To School on 5th September, against the Dark Raptors, whom they were not much fancied to beat, Johnson matches the Dark Raptors hold for hold, Makutsi takes to the air, and an MDK Powerbomb and a Carolina Crossface make Black Eagle tap out to win the Tag Team titles.
