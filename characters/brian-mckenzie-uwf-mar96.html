Tenure detail
A member of the Fraternity Boys alongside Chris O'Brien; lost a non-title cruiserweight contest to Michael Bonn on 30 August 2003, then lost the Meltdown main event intergender tag match in his hometown of Pittsburgh, pinned by Donna Tetreault after Simon O'Neal tricked his own partner, Tara Smith, into slapping him. Challenged the One-Winged Angel for the UWF Rampage Championship on 15 November 2003, pinned after being distracted by Virginia St. Ursula's bottle of Bailey's Irish Cream. Teamed with Chris O'Brien as the Fraternity Boys the same night in a Las Vegas Street Fight loss to the Arcana (Niklos and Ravnos Romani), pinned by a swinging DDT. Entered the 17 December 2003 battle royal, eliminated by Alex Kidd's skin-the-cat over the top rope. Assaulted backstage by Michael Bonn on 20 December 2003 while his own tag partner Chris O'Brien wrestled the Cruiserweight title match, dragged in front of the production camera and beaten with a flashlight; did not compete in a structured match that night. Returned on 29 May 2004 teaming with Chris O'Brien against Serge Annis and Caliban in a tag confrontation, pinned after Caliban caught him on an outstretched knee off a Near Death Experience superplex; too hurt to make his own scheduled UWF North American Heavyweight Championship challenge later the same night, replaced by O'Brien. Lost a UWF Meltdown Championship tournament opener to Vyolynce by pinfall (Oblivion) on 8 September 2004, then cost Vyolynce's semifinal opponent Paul Wong the match by running in and drawing a disqualification. Lost a UWF Rampage Championship challenge to champion "Showtime" Rick Marley by countout on 30 October 2004, walking out mid-match to save tag partner Chris O'Brien from a backstage beating by Michael Bonn. Gave girlfriend Donna Tetreault a foot rub backstage on 27 November 2004, then declared he would start booking his own matches after being goaded by tag partner Chris O'Brien and Tetreault herself. Worked out backstage with Keith Marshall on 1 December 2004 ahead of his own Rampage tag match, then joined Chris O'Brien in outrage over a new caffeinated Budweiser variant. On 11 December 2004, beat Paul Wong by pinfall with Donna Tetreault, pinning Wong himself after Tetreault planted a pair of smuggled brass knuckles on him mid-match; Chris O'Brien's own ringside protest sign was used as a weapon on Wong along the way. Was drugged by girlfriend Donna Tetreault before Chris O'Brien's own handicap match with the Hands of Death on 28 May 2005 and spent the rest of the broadcast passed out in her locker room. Ambushed Ravnos Romani at ringside with a steel-chair Conchairto alongside Chris O'Brien on 11 June 2005 during the non-title four corners tag main event, taunting the announce table about the Fraternity Boys' own need for attention. Lost a Road to the Gold qualifier to the Mercenary on 22 June 2005 after being jumped in the aisle by the Romanis and distracted mid-match by a confrontation between Chris O'Brien and Rick Marley; earlier lost a game of Dance Dance Revolution to Chris O'Brien at an arcade and punched a heckler. Assaulted Stephanie Harper backstage on 3 August 2005 after she interfered in a match involving girlfriend Donna Tetreault; brawled with the Illuminati alongside Chris O'Brien and Alex Kidd at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, then ambushed Paul Wong with Tetreault backstage. Teamed with Donna Tetreault to win the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament's opening round on 14 February 2006 with a Hart Attack clothesline on Lolita Love, then was eliminated in the finals alongside Tetreault by Brett Greene and Alex Extreme. Charged the ring to confront a returning Simon O'Neal on 18 March 2006 and brawled with him through most of Donna Tetreault's own No Disqualification match, pinned by O'Neal's loaded-glove fistdrop moments before Tetreault stole the win herself.
