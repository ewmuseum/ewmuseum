2004-06-11 – 2005-01-19

Tenure detail
Wins the Dog Day Two tag team tournament for The Mighty Men, pinning The Mistress in the first round and Marcus O'Malley in the final with the Swing Low Sweet Chariot, then competes in the sixteen-man Dog Days 3 tag scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Competes in the Dog Days Four Tag Team Scramble on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then is eliminated from the tag Pentagon of Pain alongside The Salvation on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004) after busting Mark McQueen open with smuggled brass knuckles. Wrestled for the New Age Army in the ten-man tag loss to the Society of Doom on Wrestle Style V.1. Part of a six-man brawl with Capsmen, Iyasu Saku and Sonicman ruled a no contest on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Loses to Alcoholics Anonymous (Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen) by disqualification on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004) after The Mighty Men isolate an injured McQueen, then runs in to break up a Capsman near-fall during the VWF Tag Team Championship match, handing Blaze and Real Deal the disqualification win. Teams with The Salvation and a returning Paddy O'Shea to beat The Rhombus (Capsman, Evil Capsman and Sonicman) in a six-man tag at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), O'Shea submitting Evil Capsman with the Chicken Wing. Accidentally clotheslines the referee trying to hit Kevin Simmons, costing himself and The Salvation a disqualification loss to the Society of Doom on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Loses again on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), teaming with The Salvation and a returning Paddy O'Shea against The Rhombus. Branded on the face with Kenny Kidd's own branding iron in a losing, weapons-for-the-challengers-only Number One Contenders match against the Rodeo Boys at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004). Loses a straight singles match to Mark McQueen by submission to the Devils Snare on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004) after Kingston Cash's own proposed tag-title handicap stipulation is refused by tag partner The Salvation. Challenges mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), eliminated by a big clothesline. Taps out to Kenny Kidd's own Ankle Lock on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after Billy Bones works over his ankle from ringside, suffering ligament damage expected to sideline him roughly eight weeks.
