Biography (PURE (2002))

What is important about him has already been said. Some heard the whispers, and some didn’t…some…were simply told. It is known that he was born in Cairo, Egypt and remained there for years until he had gained total awareness of the world about him. He studied for a scholarship in ancient history and went on to study archaeology. The man they call The Pharaoh is the only one who knows the truth, whatever that may be, and he doesn’t feel like telling it now – maybe he never will. He isn’t your average man. He is something you’ve never seen before. He’s out to prove a point to his Gods – he is his own new hope. He is Titu Xansiv...The Pharaoh
