The Amazing Bumfholes

aka The Immortal Bumfholes

The tag team of brothers Randy and Zimmy Bumfhole, who lost the tag belts to Sexual Aggression in February 2008 and chase them. The brothers rename themselves The Immortal Bumfholes on Supreme TV in June 2008, saying that immortality, not gold, is what they are striving for. They beat The Heartbreakers on Supreme TV in Week 3 of August 2008 and in Week 1 of September accept Rich Money and Remo's challenge for Under Control 2008, asking that the winners get a one-on-one World Heavyweight Championship shot.
