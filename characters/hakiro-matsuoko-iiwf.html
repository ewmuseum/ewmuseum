A 215-pound light heavyweight from Tokyo, Japan, billed the Angel of the Sun, who comes to the ring to Kodo drum music and wins the 25 May 1996 battle royal to become the first IIWF Intercontinental Champion, with Casey James as his corner man. On the card: won the IIWF Intercontinental Championship Battle Royal at Saturday Night (25 May 1996); lost at Saturday Night (1 June 1996) against Tiger Claw; won at Saturday Night (8 June 1996) against Venusian Death Cell.
