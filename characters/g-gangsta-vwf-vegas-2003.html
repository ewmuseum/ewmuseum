VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2003-07-11 – 2005-02-17
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 23 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 9-10-0, 3 no contest, 1 unresolved
Tenure detail
Returns as Kingston Cash's newly hired personal bodyguard on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004), piledriving Adam Jennings on a chair with the Queens Crusha in a no-bell attack before the Society of Doom runs in. Loses an actual bout to Jennings on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), then joins Sebastian, Iyasu Saku and Robbie Caldwell in a post-match beatdown of Classy Joey Boy, finishing with the Gangsta Smash. Dropped by Randall Crane's own Revolutions End Powerbomb before fleeing with tag partner Sebastian for the count-out loss to Classy Joey Boy and Crane at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004); found unconscious backstage afterward, Adam Jennings responsible. Teams with Sebastian in a no-contest two-on-two against Capsman and Evil Capsman on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Disqualified against Fast Eddie Cutlass on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) after Robbie Caldwell tosses him a tazer gun mid-match; Classy Joey Boy powerbombs him during a recruitment offer to join the Riders of the Apocalypse later the same show. Eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), pulled over the top rope by Fast Eddie Cutlass after biting him. Beats down stablemate Iyasu Saku in an apparent staged play to draw out Paddy O'Shea on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), then wrestles in the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match, eliminated by The Maquilladora's own cover. Eliminated by a returning Sonicman in the tag team title battle royal on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004). No-shows his own six-man tag alongside Sebastian and Robbie Caldwell on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), appearing only after the bell with his own former manager U-Trop to beat down Sebastian and announce his resignation from the Riders of the Apocalypse. Teams with Fast Eddie Cutlass in a non-title tag against Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), ruled a no contest after his own former Riders of the Apocalypse stablemates run in and stretcher him out. Pins Sebastian with the Gangsta Smash in the Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) fatal four-way, then tells Lean Darlene Orion he will retire against Sebastian at Ghost Town Blues. Beats Sebastian in a Steel Cage Double Retirement Match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005), reversing a back body drop into a bridging pin to end both men's own VWF careers; shakes Sebastian's hand afterward and helps fight off a post-match attack from a returning Hot Property (Stevie Knight and Austin Lee) with the Gangsta Smash.

Tenure detail
The VWF's first Triple Threat Champion, in August 2001, and BWF Champion that October; led the RCW from November 2001. Won Championship on 17 January, then successfully defended it a Last Man Standing match 2/3 falls Midtown Madness brawl ruled draw. Appears across VWF documents, February to June 2002.
