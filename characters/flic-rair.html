Flic Rair

aka "Nature Boy" Flic Rair, FLICCCCCC RAIRRRR

IWCCW World Champion with the CSWA in 1993 and its United States Heavyweight Champion from BATTLE OF THE BELTS X in November 1994, billed out of Charlotte, North Carolina and claiming to be the real AAWC World Heavyweight Champion two years after that, he chopped, strutted and hooked the figure four at Oktoberfest '96, and lost when a sidekick at ringside kicked a steel chair into his face.
