NBK

aka Natural Born KillaZ

The stable behind the CHZ's own early-2002 takeover storyline, revealed by Petapaw himself to be his own vehicle for retaking the promotion; Wild Dog and Knyghtmare billed its own members ringside, Petapaw and Soze billed the team "two members of the NBK" in the 22 March 2002 main event. In 2003 it is the nBk, a stable of eight that attacks Matt Helms with the ACC and buries him in a mock funeral on 21 May. Written by AI, 2026

Tenure detail
Blamed by the fed's own commentary for injuring The Count, having Fire fired, and turning Rage and Staz. The fed's own April 2002 pay-per-view was renamed NBK Pride in the stable's own honor (promotions/chz/archive/news-archive-2002.html, "PRIDE NOW NBK"). In 2003 the stable, written nBk, takes in Mad Max, Xstacy, Harry Manback, Rage, the Reaper, Archangel, the Darkness and Chris CK, and works with the ACC against Matt Helms.
Source: The fed's own news archive, which introduces the group unnamed in mid-February 2002 and reveals Petapaw as its own head at the fed's own February PPV (News Archive, February–March 2002); Wild Dog billed "from the NBK" and Knyghtmare billed "representing the Natural Born KillaZ" on the 22 March 2002 broadcast (CHZ Wednesday Night Wild); billed the NBK Pride main event opposite Matt Helms and Chazzy 2 Cool (NBK Pride Card). /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild; /CHZ May HayDay 2; /CHZ Wednesday Night Wild
