Lew Spectre

aka "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre

Won the NCCW Television Title on the fed's own titles page, defended it in the main event of the fed's own Battle in Seattle pay-per-view, and held it for over eighteen months without ever losing it before forfeiting the belt when the promotion folded and closing out the fed's run in a heated feud with World Champion "Dominator" Don Cameron. He then took "Strictly Business" to GPW, where he lost a World Heavyweight Title match to Eugene Robinson at Turkey Time Terror in November 1999, to Classic Championship Wrestling's 2001 return, to WWO, and to OSW Online, where he wrestled into February 2003.
