Vengeance

aka Skull DeBones, The Harbinger

The masked "Harbinger", once Skull DeBones, who hunts Jack Bruce's title, brutalises Darryl Devine and Gino Montero backstage and beats Christian Faith at The World is Watching. He beats Ray Diaz and chains him on SWF Legends in Week 2 of August 2008, loses to Christian Faith at Welcome to the Jungle 2008 and puts Faith through a table, beats Steve Flash in Week 3, and in Week 4 wrecks a backstage interview set in a fight with Mr. Jackson until security handcuff him and lead him away at Phil Vibert's order; SWF.com reports him on administrative leave.
