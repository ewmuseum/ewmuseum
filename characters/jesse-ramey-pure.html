Biography (PURE (2002))

Childhood On the day of January 6, 1976 Jesse Ramey was born in Cabell-Hunington Hospital in Huntington, West Virginia. His parents are Nancy and Clarence Ramey. He was not the only child in the family. He has one older brother, Chris. Jesse grew up in a normal family. He had a lot of the things that he wanted and made fairly good grade through school, but was known as a problem child since the day he started Kindergarten. The teachers all knew that Jesse had problems and sent him to some therapists, but Jesse was always the smart child and kept from being put on any kind of drugs by acting like the nicest child in the world while he was around the therapists and lied through his teeth all the time. His parents weren't necessarily proud of the things that Jesse did through his grade school years in school and had to take him from one school to the other because of his being kicked out so much. Jesse had to be kept away from family members, male cousins, during grade school because of him always getting into fights with them. Didn't matter what side of the family they were on; he always held a grudge with them for one reason or another. Jesse almost all the time disobeyed his parents and would never do any kind of work what so ever around his house. His parents didn't know what to do with him so they just let him be, until the day they found an ad for a Boot Camp in Virginia. His parents felt that since he was in his 6th grade year of school that it was time for him to get something more out of life than just sitting around doing nothing and sent him to the boot camp. Jesse wasn't really happy with the decision, but did as he was told because he had two choices at the time. Go to the Boot Camp or be sent off to Juvenal Hall. Jesse was then sent to the boot camp where he learned a lot of basic skills and learns a lot of extreme things. Times were very hard for Jesse during this time and they made him work harder than he had ever done in his entire life. At time he had almost come close to dying and no one cared. He would be out in the middle of a field unconscious from working so hard and having no water, but no one would do anything. They just left him lay until he came to or if he died. Jesse struggled to get out the, as he called it, 'Hell Hole'. But finally on the day that he would have graduated from 9th grade he was allowed to leave the Boot Camp and return for home. Jesse had acquired the skills of being able to take orders, being built and being able to work all day with a limited supply of nutrients, he had a large variety of fighting techniques in his mind and he didn't talk as much. Jesse arrived to his home where his parents and brother awaited with open arms. Jesse was enrolled back into his old high school were they placed him in the upper level 10th grade class. None of his friends recognized Jesse when they first saw him because of how much he had changed in the three years of his absents. Jesse was kept in the higher classes all the way through his High School years. During his 11th year of High School Jesse was introduced to the sport of Professional Wrestling by some of his best friends. At the time they had got to go see the WCW live at the Charleston Civic Center. Jesse fell in love with the sport the first time he laid eyes on it. He was then determined to make his way to becoming a wrestler. Jesse turned 18 and then graduated in the year of '96 and keeps his dreams of becoming a Pro-Wrestler. College and First Indy League Jesse started college at West Virginia State College six months after he had graduated from High School and start to peruse his teaching degree in grade school level class. He did not forget his dreams of becoming a Pro-Wrestler, but he did want to think of things in the right direction. If he did not succeed as a Pro-Wrestler, he could always have the teaching degree to fall back on. Jesse made a 3.0 average his first semester of the college year and he also had his job on the side as wrestling for an Indy league promotion called the RHW (Rock Hard Wrestling). Jesse made it halfway through college and he had made a lot of fans through the RHW. He was much better than he thought he could ever be at wrestling. The promoter of the RHW also told Jesse that he was top quality and he didn't deserve to wrestle for the likes of an organization as the RHW, but Jesse would only shake his head and laugh at the sound of what was coming out of the promoter’s mouth. The time had come for the day of Jesse's college graduation and the promoter of the RHW had a little of a graduation gift in order for Jesse. He had gotten Jesse a contract to a largely known wrestling organization in Tokyo, Japan. Jesse was so thrilled that he didn't even pay any mind to what was enclosed in the contract and just signed to the deal. What Jesse didn't know was that he had just signed a contract for three years of wrestling at only one hundred dollars a match. After reading the whole contract Jesse was seriously pissed and want to just rip the contract into a million pieces, but he also learned that it was an ironclad contract and that there was no negotiating on him leaving. Japan and JWO (Japan Wrestling Organization) On the day of July 30, 1998, Jesse got on a plane at the Charles Yeager Memorial Airport in Charleston, West Virginia. He had already started his career as a Japanese wrestle when he set foot on that plane. As soon as Jesse got seated on the plane he found out that he had been seated next to a man that had also fallen into the same trick that Jesse had been. The man went by the name of John Buchanan. Jesse and John hit it off from the start and decided that they would rather team together in the JWO than they would want the bookers to just place them with some Japanese crap wrestler. The two were on there long journey to the land of the rising sun and there was nothing much they could do about it. They were stuck in Japan for three years of worthless wrestling. Jesse and John arrived in Japan the next day and went to meet the promoter for JWO, but found out that he did not speak Japanese and neither did most of the wrestlers. This resulting Jesse and John losing a lot of matches and getting seriously injured all the time. Jesse and John regretted signing to those contracts without reading since the day the arrived in Japan, but regretted it even more after their first match. After about one year and a half Jesse and John were finally getting the hang of the native tongue and were actually winning a lot of matches. Sometimes even two or three a night. What they didn't know, but on December 27, 1998 a promoter for the UWL (Ultimate Wrestling League) from American had been in the crowd for a couple of their matches and had talked the JWO promoter into letting him take them back to America with him to wrestle for the UWL. Jesse and John were both very happy about this. UWL On the day of their arrival into the UWL the two had learned that the UWL was a very big major federation in the states and that they had two televised shows a week and a monthly pay-per-view. They had given John the gimmick of being the wanna-be African-American named FUBU and they let him start wrestling the night after his contract signing. Jesse on the other hand was given a while off and in the process of this time off they were using him as a human genie pig on some new muscle enhancing pills that were supposed to be safe and non-addictive. The pills on the other hand did what they said, but they also made Jesse huge. He was even more pumped then what he was. He may have been huge, but he was only in the Lightweight Division. Jesse had finally been given a chance in the UWL and he was booked in a Television Title match right off the bat. What the bookers didn't know was that Jesse was to win the match and that he would become the Television Champion the first day he was on the show. Jesse went on to obtain the UWL Television Title on two separate occasions, the Tag Team Titles on one occasion with his former stable mate Blackheart, and he held the number one contender ship of the World Heavyweight Title, but on the night of his Title match the UWL had went bankrupt and could not afford to do anything, but cancel the show and close down the federation. Jesse was left with billions and no job. Time off, Crazy, and CWA During most of 1999 Jesse was left without a job and had to stay in his little home located in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Jesse now calls Canada his home and only calls West Virginia his birthplace. Jesse was stuck in his little home with no job, no friends, no family and a little bit of a mind reaming. At one time he had threatened to take his own life and was sent to take calm down in a minimum security 'Nut House'. Jesse did not like the idea of this at all, but now he will say that it was worth it and that he needed to be looked away or he could have serious hurt himself. Jesse was released from the 'Nut House' on the day of April 16, 1999. He then was more determined then every to get back into the ring and found a home in the CWA (Championship Wrestling Alliance). There he stayed until the day that it closed. This brought him in even more millions and also helped him achieve the CWA Extreme Title on two separate occasions, brought him together with his friend John again and him and John held the Tag Team titles on one occasion and then Jesse went solo to claim his first World Heavyweight Title reign ever. After the night of Jesse winning the title the federation went under and shut down. It officially shut down on the day of October 30, 2000. JEW Days On the day of December 17, 2000 Jesse makes his debut on live television once again. After being off of television and out of wrestling for almost 3 months he returns to wrestling and joins up with a new wrestling federation called Just Electrifying Wrestling, JEW for short. Not only did he make a big bang back into the sport, but it also was at a Pay-Per-View and it was the JEW's first. Jesse was signed under a six month contract with the JEW and plans to claim his own in it as in his short time there now he has formed a dominating group with long time friend John Buchanan aka Fubu and a new comer Thug roC, he has also made connections with JP Shire and has plans on taking out the JEW President and his White Blood goons. Jesse also won the JEW King of Extreme 2001 title at the January Pay-Per-View on the 21st. Time slowly goes by for Jesse here in the JEW, but it is also a prosperous time, he has gained the Cruiserweight Title in a match against, Thug roC and he also won the United States Heavyweight Title in a Over the Top Rope Elimination match. Plus the unthinkable happened, he turned on his loyal friends to go and become one of the President's lackeys, as a member of the White Bloods. On the night of February 13, 2001, Jesse tosses down both of his titles in hope of a shot at the JEW World Heavyweight Title somewhere down the road. The days of Jesse Ramey begin in the JEW were finally becoming numbered for this talented young star. On the site of the JEW's third Pay-Per-View, The Great American Nightmare, Jesse Ramey's friend Tod 'The 1 Man Army' Strastky made his debut and Jesse and Tod started to take control, during Tod's first JEW match he captured the Hardcore Title, but things started to go down hill for the two. Tod was placed in another match were he was forced to job to Psycho and lose the Hardcore Title. Afterwards the JEW creative team decided that Jesse and Tod were a bit to over rated and were not getting over with the fans, so they were pretty much just placed on a shelf until the day of March 29, 2001. On that day, the two were seen in New York City, N.Y. where they both ripped up their contracts and became Free Agents. CCW Cyberspace Championship Wrestling seemed to be a great idea at the time of the JEW closing. Jesse was on a role with Fedwars and was getting into a very big Fedwars federation, but the worst only came. Jesse was slaking off. He was being beat by the worst in the business and wasn't even trying. His attitude in the ring was rubbing off on his 16 year old adopted daughter, Michelle Marie Ramey, and she herself joined with the CCW, and as said, she was also doing horrendous in the CCW, she had been in two Women's Title matches and lost in both. Jesse also has been in one Hardcore Title match and one Lightweight Title match, but he also lost in the two. Jesse has just recently re-signed a 150-day contract with CCW and hoping for the best. No titles had been won, but during a stint, Jesse had encountered a few new enemies in Phil Burner and his rogue Outlanders. In a way, Jesse was part of a turning point in the Outlanders rain of terror, no matter what anyone wants to think. Time has passed quickly in CCW. Not only did Fed Wars fold, but also in the process Jesse actually became popular. Joining the New Strangers stable. He fought alongside men that he could call friends, but trouble amongst the group occurred when Jesse wanted titles and the man standing in his way was Fenix, his stable mate, holding the Lightweight Title. Jesse won the Lightweight Title and then lost it to Fenix. Jesse then proceeded to take the Independent Title from Fenix and then lose it to Fenix once again. The stakes between the two were tied, until unfortunately, Fenix had to retire. Time went on and Jesse defected to a rogue heel stable, which he headed up, called The Lost Souls. Alongside Enigma, Jesse would go on to dominate most of CCW. The stable grows over a period of time, and Jesse, once again begins to go a little insane, or was it all part of his plan? Did he pretend to be insane only to come one step closer to his goal of being CCW Universal Champion? He lowered himself to actually compete in a Women's Title Evening Gown Match against Koriann, and actually come out on top, winning the Women's Title. After holding the title a short period of time, Jesse came to his senses and handed the title to his lover at the time, Damsel. After a stint as a cross-dresser and a failed Ladies' Man gimmick. Jesse was determined to get back on track, but that would all be cut short during his last PPV match in CCW. During the opening match against Blayze, Jesse was tosses off of a bridge by Commissioner Cianan. Jesse's body was never found and pronounced dead. RIP Jesse Ramey. Exposure and the OSW Jesse Ramey made his debut back into the Wrestling world on February 2nd, 2002 during the OSW Slam! Telecast. Many thought his being on the roster was a hoax, but Spider, the president of CCW had some heated things to say before the show went on the air, during the PTC Malice Radio Show. Saying that Jesse was a mere ingrate for doing this to CCW. Hinting that his return was indeed true. Jesse did in fact return during the first show, attacking former CCW rival Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes. The dreaded dress! Oh, yes, you heard that right. Not only did the rivalry between Toomes and Ramey continue, but also Toomes revealed Jesse's true weakness. The God-awful dress gimmick he was forced into playing out in CCW. The heated feud between these two men is continuing as the score is tied and looking to come to an end sometime soon, with the winner being the actual greater man. Time passes in the OSW and Brooklyn Born and Bred occurs. Jesse makes friends in Rhett Locke, Micheal Dame, and Scott Naket. The first OSW PPV comes soon, but how walks out the better man? Jesse Ramey or Tom Walczak? The PPV is long done, Michel Kreig made his debut to only cost Jesse his match and really, really piss Jesse off. After several weeks of going back and forth doing nothing with him-self, Jesse decided to take a stand, but was cut short by four masked men who have decided to make Jesse's OSW career a living hell. After finally revealing themselves as former CCW buddies, Jason McAllister, John Jupiter, Kensuke Suzahara, and Spider, a challenge was made for the PPV, Head on Collision and it was set to be a Street Fight. In the end, Team OSW came out victorious, but the mysterious I-Cybie caught Jesse's attention and sent him off into a deep dark alley, were the mysterious Vax character caught Jesse off guard with a couple ton of bricks from the air crushing Jesse and placing him on the injured list. When Jesse realized that OSW wasn't helping him and holding him back, he came to a decision that he wished to go back to Japan and train to come back full force in OSW, but spokesperson Shanda Russel didn't like the idea of this and shortly after an agreement was come to and Jesse was released from OSW and on the Wrestling Tattler free market. FSW Shortly after his firing, Jesse was approached by FSW representatives and was soon to be one of the first talents hired to the new Five Star Wrestling promotion. How will Jesse fair when Five Star Wrestling opens on July 1st, 2002? Going up against Ming and Asa Fountain on the opening FSW Glory card and what could be one of the greatest matches on the show, what could happen or be the out come of the match? After several weeks from the opening of FSW, things have begun to pick up pace, Jesse and Asa have found themselves being allies against a very unwilling team in Ming and Lo-Key. What does the road hold in store for the team of The Attitude and The Fro? After Lo-Key's departure from FSW and the introduction of an old rival of Jesse, the story continued with Bad Frotitude and the newly formed team of Michel Krieg and Ming. The aftermath of the first FSW PPV, Genesis, was nothing less than massive. Ming and Michel Krieg won the Tag Team gold and later on was challenged for the belts by the now number one contenders in Bad Frotitude. Once Bad Fro had gotten their chance at the Tag Team gold, an unknown two, who have been known as The Dead Presidents have been causing nothing, but havoc for both Ming and Krieg and Bad Fro. After many altercations and several weeks of painstakingly hard work, Bad Frotitude finally succeed in achieving their goal of holding the FSW World Tag Team Championship Titles and Jesse achieves his first actual piece of gold won in a Wrestling Tattler federation. The feud between Bad Frotitude and The Dead Presidents continues to heat up when Bad Moon Rising rolls around and everything is settled in a Tag Team Title Chain Match, or is it, when The Dead Presidents win the Tag Team Titles in a obvious display of cheating and also reveal themselves as the former PCW Tag Team Champions, Politically Incorrect. PI forms an alliance with the defunct Big Daveylicious and now turned Magnum Wolf to create The Court, leaving Bad Frotitude at a distinct disadvantage until former CCW Tag Team Champions, Jason McAllister and John Jupiter, reform the great Last Rites, but even then not all is right with things as Jesse once had a bitter past with the two and this spells trouble for everything as The Court continues their reign of terror in FSW. Abruptly things come to a close between Jesse Ramey and FSW as he is released from his contract for reasons that are unknown and will most likely never be known. PWA, The Move, and PURE After being let go by Five Star Wrestling and then several weeks later being asked to go to the Primetime Wrestling Academy to hone his wrestling abilities, Jesse was contacted by the new owner of the Puro based wrestling federation within the WT, amply titled PURE, in it’s now third run. Although several things had to happen in Jesse’s life to make all of this happen. One of the major things was having to leave all of his family and friends back in the United States as he seeks residency in Japan. After spending several weeks with his family and friends, Jesse got the word that he would be able to find some residency in the town of Mito, Japan. Jesse has been located there now for several weeks and is slowly beginning to work things out in PURE and the backstage area for the new opening, which should be amazing to say the least. After the first week of PURE, the heat is beginning to rise between 'Sinister' Steven Lee and Jesse, after a controversial win over Ramey, only more heat can come as the weeks progress. Everything came to a head at the first ever PURE PPV entitled Final Destinies when Steven Lee and Ramey fought to see who would continue on into the night to face the winner of the Enmity and Eddie Mandrake match to crown the first ever PURE Heavyweight Champion. Eddie beat Enmity and as soon as Jesse beat Steven Lee in a brutal match, Eddie came to the ring and in what could be claimed the best match of the year for PURE Jesse claimed victory over his friend Eddie to become the first ever PURE Heavyweight Champion, but with the brand extensions and Jesse now being a part of the PURE: World, who will stand up to take the challenge and try to take the championship away from him?
