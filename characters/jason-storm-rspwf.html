Tenure detail
Returns with a promo on Firestorm (27 May 2004) claiming a prior win over Lark Fenriz at a 'Lords of the Deathmatch' event in Havana, attacking Fenriz after his own title match, then wins the Dog Day One singles battle royal. Wins the Dog Day Two singles bracket's first round over Sidious The Clown after champion Lark Fenriz is scratched from their billed three-way, then loses the semifinal to The Outlaw Morris Bass. Competes in the eight-man Dog Day Three singles scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004). Competes in the Dog Days Four Singles Six Man and the Fourway Final on Dog Day Four (9 July 2004), then wins the singles Pentagon of Pain on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), sending Setzer Van Strife headfirst into the cage pole, to reach the Dog Days singles final. The Heavyweight Championship main event on Wrestle Style V.1 ends without a finish when a returning Classy Joey Boy interferes; fights to a double-knockout draw with an opponent billed "Gods Tears", named on commentary as Orion The Hunter without his mask, in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3; majority owner Kingston Cash rules both men advance. Fails to appear for a VWF Heavyweight Title tournament second-round match with Classy Joey Boy on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), counted out; The Equalizer attacks Classy Joey Boy from the crowd instead. Counted out again during the seven-man mini rumble's own chaos on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), then crashes the Fenriz-Equalizer contract signing by video, reveals a clause making the Vegas Violence main event a three-way, and sets fire to The Equalizer's truck. Competes in the VWF Heavyweight Championship three-way at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) alongside champion Lark Fenriz and The Equalizer, hitting the Perfect Storm on both men before Orion's own interference and a guest-referee chair shot cost him the finish; the Equalizer wins the title. Unmasks Orion #2 as Pagan during Trinity's own six-man tag win over him, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Flash on March To Hell (21 October 2004), then is accidentally chaired by his own partner Flash and pinned. Ambushes The Equalizer with a chair in an impromptu brawl on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) after accepting Classy Joey Boy's own booking, hitting the Perfect Storm before Lark Fenriz interferes; no decision is reached. Lured into an unsanctioned cage confrontation with Pagan at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), engineered by his own manager Alice; badly beaten and his leg broken when Pagan and an escaped Melanie Kilpatrick slam the cage door and drop the cage's own weight onto it, stretchered out. Returns after a five-month absence at the close of The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), announcing on air that he has bought the VWF; the on-screen VWF logo is destroyed and replaced with three new letters, V-X-W, as he declares the promotion is going "Xtreme."
