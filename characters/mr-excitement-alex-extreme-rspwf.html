UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2004-11-13
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 21 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 7-9-0, 5 no contest
Tenure detail
Billed from Chicago, Illinois; teamed with Quinn Brown in a losing three-way main event for the UWF World Tag Team Championship, ruled a no-contest, on 9 August 2003, and called out Serge Annis and Cameron Black afterward. Lost a non-title one-on-one bout to Kyle Lee on 24 September 2003 after injuring his arm on the ringsteps. Teamed with Randall Osbourne against Papa Legba and Avanter on 22 October 2003; Osbourne turned on him mid-match and left, and Sabbat Justice beat him down five-on-one with no decision ever rendered, ending with a chokeslam through the ringside table. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match; pinned Kyle Lee with a schoolboy rollup, then teamed with a tagged-in Jason Keening for a Hart Attack combination to pin Jacob Drake, winning the match as one of its own two survivors and the right to help set his own side's Eve of Destruction stipulations. Recovering in hospital as of 10 March 2004, spending Edmond Winston IV's money out of spite and reconciling uneasily with the Sons of Cacaphony and Michael Augustine against their common enemy. Entered a scheduled three-way for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 21 March 2004 that never started after champion Papa Legba attacked both challengers with a chair before the bell and left; a cage-match rematch was announced for the following week. Lost the rescheduled steel cage match on 5 May 2004 when champion Papa Legba climbed out of the cage to the floor first; fellow challenger Randall Osbourne never entered after Alex Kidd attacked him before the bell. Recruited by World Heavyweight Champion Michael Augustine into a reforming Youngbloods stable the same week, set to face Edmond Winston IV's New Era in a handicap main event the following Rampage. Teamed with Michael Augustine against Edmond Winston IV, Jamie Underwood, and the Privateers on 15 May 2004; the Privateers turned on Winston mid-match to side with him, but Augustine then turned on him in turn, and the Privateers and Augustine beat him through two tables before Alex Martinez and the Sons of Cacaphony pulled him clear. Hospitalized afterward. Cut a hometown promo threatening Michael Augustine and Edmond Winston IV on 29 May 2004, then teamed with debuting Alex Martinez against Team Canada; Tom Landis and Kari Stevens ambushed Martinez in the aisle before the bell, and Trevor Hextall legdropped Extreme to stop a save as Bob Jablonski pinned the exhausted Martinez for the loss. Rallied against both members of Team Canada before the beatdown continued after the bell, Scott Daniels and Landis going on to Pride-break his own cornerman Rodney. Beat Tragedy by pinfall (a springboard version of his own finisher, the Extreme) on 25 August 2004 after an implant DDT, following a backstage promo naming Winston, Augustine, and Daniels as the UWF's own real problem. Substituted for a medically-excluded Erik Grimsson and beat Todd Franklin and Jacob Drake by pinfall alongside Ravnos Romani on 18 September 2004 in a mismatched partners tag match. Lost a UWF Rampage Championship challenge to champion Jamie Underwood by pinfall on 2 October 2004 after Corporate Leverage and the returning Privateers overwhelmed him; his manager Rodney was later found brutally beaten near the loading dock by Michael Augustine. Lost a Tower of Doom cage match on 30 October 2004 alongside Quinn Brown, Uriah Hawkins, and Alex Martinez to the team of Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, Papa Legba, and Luke Kinsey, trapped in a jammed cage until Brown flipped the switch to free him; joined the others in a post-bell beatdown of Winston with his own solid gold knuckles.
