Wanda Fish

A woman who has wrestled all over the world and who debuts at Remember November on 26th November 2011 in a hooded sweatshirt as a backstage worker, pins Black Eagle in a pile of chairs to become the All Action champion (the diary's own title records call her the first female champion) and tells the locker room that she is "crossing the gender divide" and expects a lot of challenges; Knuckles takes the belt from her minutes later. At Season's Beatings she beats Citizen X in thirteen minutes with the Dish Of The Day, at End Of Days she beats Nathan Coleman in an Invitational qualifier, and on 5th January 2012 she and Jared Johnson beat the Second Sons, Wanda pinning Antonio. She loses to JD Morgan's Cross Atlantic Stretch in the first round of The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January. The narrator calls her a straight-up tremendous wrestler, and tew.com notes that she has talked openly about retirement. In the diary's pages from February 2012 she makes Thea Davis her protégé and loses with her to Raphael and Citizen X at Leap Into The Unknown, tells the narrator she will retire in a few weeks, loses a Triple Threat for the All Action title to JD Morgan at All Or Nothing and, in her last match, the Heavyweight title match to Aaron Andrews at Great Lakes Crisis on 2nd March, a B- match in which she very nearly won a three-count. The narrator, Rip Chord and others attend her retirement party; he says she cost $3,000 a time. Written by AI, 2026
