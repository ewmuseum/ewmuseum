Painful Procedure

The rock-band stable that broke up, reunites around Troy Tornado and takes in the Young Guns in May 2008, the Syndicate's chief opposition, and in August 2008 loses to the School of Tradition and is beaten up by the Easy Riders, Randall Hopkirk's injuries leading him to ask for a backstage role, and in late September 2008 ends when Randall Hopkirk retires from the ring, Billy Jack Shearer staying in Puerto Rico as a new father. Painful Procedure is billed again in December 2008: Troy Tornado and the Young Guns beat the School of Tradition's Acid, Eric Tyler and Freddy Huggins on Badge of Honor, a preview calls the Balcony Express its newest members, and Jack Bruce, its first lead vocalist, returns to argue with Troy Tornado and with Randall Hopkirk.
