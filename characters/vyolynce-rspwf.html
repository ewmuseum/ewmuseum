Tenure detail
A member of Sabbat Justice; forced a referee stoppage over Randall Osbourne with a dragon sleeper and body scissors combination after needling him over his girlfriend, Tawni Northern. Fought Quinn Brown to a ten-minute time-limit draw on 12 November 2003, moments after Brown's own Fatal Hesitation pinfall was ruled too late to count. Beat Brian McKenzie by pinfall (Oblivion) and Paul Wong by disqualification to reach the UWF Meltdown Championship tournament finals on 8 September 2004, then fought Tigress to a twenty-minute time-limit draw. Lost to "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall (a hanging brainbuster suplex) on 6 October 2004, after briefly reversing the Dead End with a standing neck vice. Beat "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb by pinfall (Oblivion, a sacrifice-the-knee driver) on 3 November 2004, sacrificing an injured knee twice along the way to slow the newcomer down. Lost the UWF Cruiserweight Championship challenge to champion One-Winged Angel by disqualification on 11 June 2005 after working over the champion's own left leg for much of the match and refusing to break a rope-break hold. Won a Road to the Gold qualifier over Scorpio by referee stoppage on 22 June 2005 after three consecutive Oblivions left Scorpio unconscious, the Amazing Romanis pledging their support to him beforehand. Won his own match against Shadoe Rage by disqualification on 6 July 2005, sans manager Angel who was still recovering from an earlier beating, after Niklos and Ravnos Romani disguised themselves as duplicate Vyolynces to even the odds, then was chair-shotted and piledrivered by Shadoe once the Romanis were beaten off. Beat Jamie Underwood by pinfall (a piledriver) in a Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament first-round match on 3 August 2005 after Illuminati members Andrew "Flash" Tucker and L. Dan Dee interfered at ringside; earlier sat silently through a backstage conversation with Niklos Romani.
