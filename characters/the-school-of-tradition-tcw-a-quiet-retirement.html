The School of Tradition

aka School of Tradition

Eric Tyler's stable, brought back in June 2008 around Acid, Freddy Huggins and his sister Laura after the New Wave turn him down, with the Tag Team Specialists billed as its resident team, at war with Aaron Andrews, Joey Minnesota and Rick Law; Tyler beats Andrews with a Tradition Lift at Excessive Force, and in July 2008 takes in Sean Deeley, who teams with Joel Bryant after Robert Oxford's knee injury, and in August 2008 assaults Rick Law, beats Narato & The Kid and loses both the title challenge against the New Wave and Eric Tyler's match against Law at Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 beats Andrews & Minnesota, draws with the Keith Brothers, brings in Marat Khoklov without a contract and loses the tag title rematch at Destructive Energy, and in October 2008 beat Art Reed and the Keith Brothers in a six-man tag but lose to the Machines, to the Keith Brothers and to the Rising Stars, Freddy Huggins' kick hitting Sean Deeley, and in November 2008 beat the Balcony Express, are told by Mayhem Midden to choose between Sean Deeley and Freddy Huggins for the All Action Championship four-way and fall out over the choice, and in December 2008 lose to the New Wave, see Laura Huggins throw in the towel, lose with Acid and Freddy Huggins to Troy Tornado and the Young Guns and watch Sean Deeley walk away from the group over Freddy Huggins.
