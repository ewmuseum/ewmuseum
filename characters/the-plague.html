Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Background Info: Grew up in the city and not knowing what he would like to do with his life decided to look around the wrestling industry to see if he had what it took to be a professional wrestler. He wasn't one of the in crowd in school so he was mostly a loner, he would sit at home watching wrestling on TV and all that drama got in his head and he decided he wanted to do that too. So after graduating high school he decided to go to a local wrestling school and learn a few of the basics. After two years of that he left the school and started searching for a home, an orginazation that would take him in. Application after application was filled out until a little company out of Chcago picked him up, The GIWF. and every since January of 2001 he has been wrestling professionally in The GIWF and after that closed The UWF. Accomplishments: GIWF Television Champion Held for 30 days. First UWF Hardcore Champion Held for 38 days before being stripped by Lew Spectre. Was the first UWF Shoot Wrestling Title for 35 days after being awarded the title by Spectre following his banning of hardcore matches in The UWF. The Plague is a former two time Shoot Wrestling Champion. His second reign as Shoot Wrestling Champion was for a total of 31 days. He is also a former two time UWF Hardcore Champ, his secod reign was for 63 days. He has also competed in an Inter-Federation PPV put on by The PTC in which he took part in a Scrapyard Hardcore Match. Held The UWF Intercontinental Title for 46 days. Third THC Title reign of 30 days
