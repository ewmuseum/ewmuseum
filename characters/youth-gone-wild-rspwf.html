Tenure detail
Teamed with Alex Kidd, Paul Wong, and Quinn Brown to a count-out win over the Pride, hitting a double elbowdrop with Kidd early in the match. Teamed with Eugene Robinson in a non-title losing effort against UWF World Tag Team Champions Team Canada on 12 November 2003. Answered Serge Annis's open challenge on 15 November 2003 after Cameron Black refused to appear, busting Annis open with a top-rope DDT reversal before losing to an Epitomizer chokeslam. Entered the 17 December 2003 battle royal; his own elimination went unannounced by name. Lost his own in-ring debut opponent's debut on 10 March 2004, pinned by "Insane Bronco" Shay Parker's Planting Flowers for the Dead after a top-rope elbow strike. Lost a UWF Meltdown Championship challenge to champion Tigress by submission (an inverted dragon sleeper) on 6 October 2004, extending a handshake to the champion afterward. Lost a UWF Cruiserweight Championship challenge to One-Winged Angel by pinfall (a top-rope german release suplex) on 25 May 2005 after a near-fall of his own on a Revelator-style counter. Lost a Road to the Gold qualifier to L. Dan Dee by submission on 22 June 2005. Beat "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn in the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament's own first round on 6 July 2005 with a top-rope Wildflyer moonsault, in a hard-fought, mutually respectful contest. Made a surprise return on 3 August 2005 to save Alex Martinez from a Cornerstones beatdown, only to be low-blowed and piledriven by Daniel Kidd afterward; recorded a backstage promo on 6 August 2005 telling Kidd he now remembered how personal the business could get.
