UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-07-23 – 2004-12-11

Tenure detail
Won the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World at Gold Rush 2003; lost the title in his first defense two and a half weeks later, breaking his arm on a botched moonsault fall against a same-night replacement challenger, Chris O'Brien. Billed as the reigning UWF Rampage Champion on 24 March 2004, retaining against "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall (a small package) after both men traded finishing-move attempts; "The Doomsayer" Kyle Lee ran off Martin's post-match chair attack. No surviving document in the held record states when or how Hawkins won the Rampage Championship from its own prior record holder, the One-Winged Angel. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship again on 15 May 2004 in a three-way dance, sneaking in behind challengers Troy Grace and Quinn Brown as they collided and catching both with a double DDT for the pin. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship again on 29 May 2004 when a title defense against Tragedy was ruled a no-contest after Pierce Bleed attacked the referee while Tragedy still had the Tragic Ending locked on; Bleed then challenged him to a title-versus-career match at Gold Rush with T.D. Swagger as special referee. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, pinning Pierce Bleed with a Suicide DDT after special referee T.D. Swagger restarted the match, having ruled out an illegal cordless-microphone shot; Bleed's own UWF contract was voided under the match's own title-versus-contract stipulation. Lost the UWF Rampage Championship to Jamie Underwood by pinfall on 28 August 2004, blindsided by a run-in Papa Legba's own Ragga Bomb moments before Underwood's small package; broke up Derek Martin's own backstage chair attack on Alex Kidd earlier the same show. Fought Papa Legba to a double countout in a UWF Meltdown Championship tournament opener on 8 September 2004, seconded by manager Holly Williams. Lost a tag match alongside Alex Kidd to Scott "Hotspot" Daniels and a substituting "Dead End" Derek Martin on 18 September 2004, seconded by manager Holly Williams. Won the main event over "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall (an Oklahoma side roll) on 2 October 2004, capitalizing on a leg he had worked over throughout the match after Martin's own knee buckled mid-charge; cut a pre-match promo on respect in professional wrestling, naming Martin directly. Lost a Tower of Doom cage match on 30 October 2004 alongside Quinn Brown, Alex Extreme, and Alex Martinez to the team of Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, Papa Legba, and Luke Kinsey, who escaped the structure first when Alex Martinez inadvertently threw Winston clear of the cage; joined the others in a post-bell beatdown of Winston with his own solid gold knuckles. Lost a UWF North American Heavyweight Championship challenge to champion "Violent" Victor Frost by pinfall on 17 November 2004, driving his own head into the mat attempting a Suicide DDT after Frost hung on to the top rope. Lost a hard-fought grudge match to Papa Legba by pinfall (Roots) on 11 December 2004, the two shaking hands afterward despite their own history.
