El Diablo

The Mexican Devil, managed by Angela, held the Rising Star, Television and Continental belts between 2001 and 2002 and fought Flaymin Aymin to second place in the 2002 Feud of the Year vote, then held the SCCW LiveWire Championship for 46 days in 2007 and its Gateway Championship for 23 in February 2008.
