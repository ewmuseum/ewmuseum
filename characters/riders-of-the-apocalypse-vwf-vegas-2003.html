Riders of the Apocalypse

A four-man stable of Sebastian, G-Gangsta, Robbie Caldwell and Iyasu Saku, feuding with the Society of Doom over the VWF 24 Hour Championship. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-10-21 – 2005-04-11
Tenure detail
Named as a four-man group on March To Hell (21 October 2004), interfering to help Sebastian and G-Gangsta against Capsman and Evil Capsman, then unable to save Iyasu Saku from Adam Jennings's own VWF 24 Hour Championship defense. Loses members to individual matches on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) -- G-Gangsta to Fast Eddie Cutlass, Robbie Caldwell to Randall Crane, Iyasu Saku to Classy Joey Boy -- and offers a fourth membership slot to Classy Joey Boy, who turns on Sebastian, G-Gangsta and Caldwell instead. Beats down stablemate Iyasu Saku in an apparent staged play on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), the returning Rodeo Boys jumping Paddy O'Shea afterward and, by the broadcast's own account, joining the group; loses the eight-man Rhombus/Riders of the Apocalypse elimination match's own final fall to Sebastian, the last man standing, and Robbie Caldwell loses the VWF 24 Hour Championship match to Adam Jennings the same night. Enters the tag team title battle royal five men strong on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), eliminated one by one until only Sebastian remains, beaten by mystery entrant Thunder Valenkov. G-Gangsta fails to appear for a six-man tag on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005), instead returning after the bell to beat down Sebastian and publicly resign from the group. Runs in on former member G-Gangsta -- teamed with Fast Eddie Cutlass against Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov -- on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), Sebastian, Robbie Caldwell and the Rodeo Boys stretchering him out of the building. Now four strong with Robbie Caldwell, Kenny Kidd, Billy Bones and the debuting Mad Bomber, sweeping March To Madness (2 March 2005): Caldwell beats Thunder Valenkov after Kidd distracts the referee and the Mad Bomber blinds Valenkov with a smoke bomb and chairs him, Kidd beats Fast Eddie Cutlass, and the debuting Mad Bomber beats Lark Fenriz. Demands matches for Billy Bones and The Mad Bomber from John Tyler backstage at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005); enters the wing's own closing Gauntlet battle royal with only Robbie Caldwell representing the group, Kenny Kidd having already been eliminated from the eight-man elimination match earlier on the same card and Billy Bones and The Mad Bomber never entering the battle royal at all.
