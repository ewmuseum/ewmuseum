Biography (RUSH (2002))

Fans. You know what they are, don’t you? The people who follow this sport like a religion, the people who pump money into the corporate wallets of the massive wrestling companies that pay us, the people who sacrifice their bodies for the fans to get paid. I’ve spent the last 6 years of my life doing this, breaking my arm twice, and breaking my ribs a total of 5 times. Fun, isn’t it? No, not at all. I don’t wrestle for anyone other than myself. Fuck the fans. I couldn’t care less about the people who live this sport from outside of the ring; they’re not the ones who bleed for a paycheck. The ‘fans’ aren’t the ones, who put their bodies on the line up to 6 nights a week for 30 minutes, are they? Hell no. The fans are only there for the cheap gimmick matches, that’s the only time those ungrateful bastards are ever happy with what we do. When they don’t get something they like from us, they bitch about it on their online ‘fan forums’...what a crock of shit. They’re only pleased when we split our heads open and bleed profusely. The ‘fans’ are never satisfied with what we do, no matter how much damn pain we put our bodies through, they’re not satisfied. So this is why I hate the fans. This is why I hate it when they cheer me. I don’t care for them or their damn opinions of me. When they cheer for me I put on a fake smile, and when they boo I really smile...because when they boo me I can really tell them what I think of their false opinions. If you don’t like this, then fuck you. I wrestle for one person, and his name is my name. I don’t wrestle to make you happy, I do it to make me happy? And you know what’s the best part of it?
