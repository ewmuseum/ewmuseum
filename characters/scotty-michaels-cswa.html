Scotty Michaels

aka SCOTTY MICHAELS, The Viewer's Choice Scotty Michaels, Superstar Scotty Michaels, Scott Michaels, "The Viewer's Choice" Scotty Michaels

Twice the United States/National Champion, he won the belt on Christmas Day 1995 without knowing it — piledriven and then draped across a man another wrestler had just laid out — and took it back at BATTLE OF THE BELTS XIV with a leg drop off the middle rope, after Teri Melton came off the top with a DDT on the champion.
