Tenure detail
Loses the Team Tournament Final on Firestorm (3 May 2004), pinned by Fast Eddie Cutlass. The Lynch Mob, defending champions, are eliminated first in the Gauntlet's own Four Corners Tag Team Championship match, submitting to Stevie Knight's sharpshooter. Teams with Setzer Van Strife as the Lynch Mob against Kevin Blair and The Maquilladora on Firestorm (27 May 2004), then eliminates Sidious The Clown and The Maquilladora before eliminating himself from the Dog Day One singles battle royal. Wins the Dog Day Two singles bracket's first round over Kevin Blair and Lord Zero, then helps The Outlaw Morris Bass win the semifinal over Jason Storm. Competes in the eight-man singles scramble on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004), then is eliminated from the tournament in the Dog Days Three Singles Elimination Match against Lord Zero after an unidentified masked man interferes.
