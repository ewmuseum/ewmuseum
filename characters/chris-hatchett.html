wrestler — staged as "Cutthroat" Chris Hatchet and as RiVeN, Celest's brother; the PWW World Champion on his 2000 visit, the Order of the Hammer's manager for the evening at Stable Wars 2000; wrestled for the GIWF in February 2001
Tenure detail
Five GIWF cards put him on screen. Global of 24 May 2000: Nephilim brings "Chris Hatchet" into the arena to meet the under-commissioner, and the commentary names him ""Cutthroat" Chris Hatchet, or more commonly known now as RiVeN. That's Celest's little brother". Stable Wars 2000: introduced as "the Professional World Wrestling World Champion, "Cutthroat" Chris Hatchet" and "manager for the evening" of the Order of the Hammer; he puts Ole Gus through a table. Suicide of 11 February 2001: "RiVeN, the brother of Celest and The Preacher" arrives during Celest's handicap match and attacks Benito Cavalera with him. Suicide of 18 February 2001: RiVeN pinned Benito Cavalera. Global of 21 February 2001: he rushes to the scene after Unforgiven's attack on Celest.The GIWF's own broadcasts name two debuts and both are recorded (#sources-disagree): Stable Wars 2000 as "the GIWF debut of Cutthroat Chris Hatchet", and the Mission IMPossible 2001 preview as "Chris Hatchett, or "RiVeN," made his GIWF debut in the handicap match that Thunder wouldn't show up for" (it names no date; his arrival on screen in Celest's handicap match is the Suicide of 11 February 2001, whose commentary says his earlier stay was "oh so brief").
