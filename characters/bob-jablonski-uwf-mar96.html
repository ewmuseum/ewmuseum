"Bonebreaker" Bob Jablonski

aka Bob Jablonski, Bobby Jablonski

One half of Team Canada and one of the UWF North American Tag Team Champions, whose interference cost Tom Landis a title match before the pair joined the main event's three-way brawl. Written by AI, 2026

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2005-05-28

Tenure detail
Billed with Trevor Hextall as Team Canada and the reigning UWF North American Tag Team Champions. Interfered in the Rampage Championship match, then competed in the main event three-way, ruled a no-contest, on 9 August 2003. Teamed with Hextall to pin Jason Keening on 4 October 2003, winning the UWF World Tag Team Championship to add to the team's own North American Tag Team title. Teamed with the Pride in a non-title, count-out loss on 22 October 2003. Lost the UWF North American Tag Team Championship to the Ghost Dancers by submission on 25 October 2003, after a fine-print contract clause kept the team's own World Tag Team Championship off the line. Teamed with Hextall to retain the UWF World Tag Team Championship over Eugene Robinson and Youth Gone Wild on 12 November 2003. Entered the Tower of Doom on 15 November 2003 alongside the Privateers, on the losing side as the Sons of Cacaphony became the first complete team to escape and won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match with Trevor Hextall as Team Canada; pinned Erik Grimsson after manager Kari Stevens cracked him with a hockey stick, then was pinned himself by Edmond Winston IV's Cobra Clutch suplex, which stole the fall from Winston's own teammate Chad Grimsson. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Scott Daniels on 10 March 2004 in a six-man tag win over the Ghost Dancers and Erik Grimsson, Daniels stealing the pinfall on Michael Keening after two unidentified men in suits ambushed the Ghost Dancers' side. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Scott Daniels again against Alex Kidd, Paul Wong, and "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee on 15 May 2004 in a six-man tag that ended without a decision amid a mass brawl. Teamed with Trevor Hextall as Team Canada again on 29 May 2004, ambushing debuting Alex Martinez in the aisle with Tom Landis and Kari Stevens before the bell, then pinning the exhausted Martinez, who never tagged into the match, for the win. Teamed with Trevor Hextall as Team Canada in a Gold Rush loss to Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker and Mike Sebastian) on 5 June 2004, his own tag partner pinned after a top-rope Frog Splash. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Tom Landis in a Meltdown main event win over Quinn Brown and Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker and Mike Sebastian) on 25 August 2004, Hextall and Landis's own Game Misconduct heart-punch combination flooring Andrew Tucker for the pin. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Kari Stevens in a losing intergender six-way against Fury (Rage, Spice) and Ami Tran by pinfall on 6 October 2004. Teamed with Trevor Hextall as Team Canada in a no-contest against the Hands of Death (Caliban, Serge Annis) on 13 November 2004, broke a hockey stick across Caliban's knee mid-match before the bout dissolved into a mass brawl. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Scott Daniels as the Pride, beating Amity (the One-Winged Angel, Rage, Spice) in the Bluff City Streetfight main event on 27 November 2004 after Derek Martin turned on the One-Winged Angel to score the pinfall. Joined Trevor Hextall in beating down a fallen "Hentai" Ryu Osawa after Hextall's own pinfall win on 1 December 2004, broken up by a returning "Showtime" Rick Marley. On 11 December 2004, brawled backstage with Spice in a Pride-Fury melee, then attacked an intervening Ryu Osawa. Was part of the Pride's own ringside entrance and locker-room scenes on 28 May 2005, celebrating the group's Rise to Power sweep and welcoming the returning Brett Greene alongside the rest of the group.
