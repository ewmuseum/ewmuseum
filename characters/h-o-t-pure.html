Biography (PURE (2002))

Chad Matthews ------------- “Chad Matthews…” “Who is Chad Matthews?” I hear you cry… as you look through these documents and begin to wonder. Well, Chad Matthews… for the time being anyway, is a nobody… plain and simple, he is a nobody… but, regardless of that wonderful little tidbit of information, I think that you all deserve to discover how this individual came to be the man that he IS today… Our story begins on Friday 18th August 1981… Yes, to those of you who have been able to look at the above details… that day just happens to be the ‘Birthdate’ of our little wannabe here… Anyway, the story starts on the day that Chad Matthews was brought into this world. His mother and father looked upon him and hoped that he’d be the best of their three sons… despite the advantage that they already had upon him, both Mr and Mrs Matthews were convinced that their son would be following the rest to higher plateaus before anyone would even come to recognize it. How ironic would that statement be later in his life… Chad progressed through his earlier years of life like any other kid would… well, any other kid that was able to enjoy the company of two older brothers. To introduce the ‘Brothers Grimm’, Joseph and Max were twins… Max being the older of the two, by all of an hour. Our ‘heroic’ figure of Chad tended to live in peace with his older siblings, although there would be times where altercations were inevitable… The Great Food Fight of Max’s 11th Birthday… The Punch-Up of Christmas 1990… And more famously… The Karaoke Brawl of Summer 1997… The later event will be detailed later on, but for now… we’ll go back to the relationship that existed between both Chad and his brothers. As it was said, the three siblings managed to live in peace for the majority of the time and like any unit, they would always try something together. The reason for this is still unknown, but like three best friends who would never leave the other behind… for some incidents, they would all give the task a shot, just to keep the balance of ‘Power’ equal. The first major task that all three brothers attempted to do was that of ‘Professional Wrestling’. Despite there being a three year gap between Chad and his brothers, by the time that they wanted to start working the business, Chad had developed into an eager sixteen year old chick magnet and his brothers had walked the steps to hitting their early twenties. Throughout the beginning of his training, Chad found it impossible to keep up with the things that were being done by everyone else… his body just didn’t seem to be ready to handle the strain and stress of fighting inside the ring… however, he persevered, using the goal of one day bettering his brothers as the only incentive needed to push his body through such vile actions… Remember how the parents of Chad had said how their son would rise through the ranks like nothing before him? Oh… he rose through the ranks of the academy alright… getting used to the basic spots and offence a lot quicker than his brothers and fellow classmates. After a year of training, everyone had decided that Matthews was ready to be used in their independent promotion, working the curtain jerkers to see what kind of effect the lad would have on the crowd… It was supposed to be the greatest night of his life… It was supposed to be the moment that he had always dreamed of… ever since stepping in that ring… It was supposed to be… the moment that his family would be proudest of him… Chad worked the match… he was strong, he looked alright… but a part of him was missing that night… that part of him that was family love. Chad knew his family didn’t make the show that night… he could see the gap that was supposed to contain their bodies in the crowd. Depressed… Distraught… Disturbed… Chad Matthews blew a spot in the bout… he was all set to take a DDT… but something happened, he lost his footing while trying to think of his family and was subsequently drilled headfirst into the canvas… After that night, Matthews decided to call what he thought was quits from the business… he just didn’t have the heart to keep wrestling if none of his family were going to be around to share this moment with him… It was six months after this night that he found a new love… a new passion, a new something that would give him the chance to shine without the worry of his parents… this was something that he didn’t need support of family for… it was something that he’d be able to express himself in… and gain recognition from more than just his forgotten family… but all of the country… Pop music… an ever-expanding empire, never willing to reject a talent that can bring money for their company… and fame for themselves. Chad had kept one thing secret from everyone when he was growing up… that secret was the fact that he could actually sing. People that sing in the showers… sing when listening to music with headphones… sing in general, Chad did everything of the kind when he was sure that nobody would be around to listen to him. March 25th 1999… This too would mark a significant date for Chad… not only would it establish the moment that “PoP Worldwide” was created, but it would also establish the date that Chad Matthews met a fledging artist like himself… the only major difference being that this man was Japanese… and not your average member of the human community… “Hallo… my name is Kamui Fukamachi… I’ve heard some girls talking about your… ability to sing… and I was wondering if you would be willing to join me…” Ok… Ok… so things didn’t happen as something as the ‘text extract’ above, but the basic jest of things happened and Kamui effectively asked for Chad to become his partner in business, touring the country… singing to the fans, trying to make something out of what they’d both considered to be wasted lives… June 15th 1999… July 22nd 1999… September 28th 1999… December 24th 1999… January 1st 2000… The above dates, just a few of the times that Chad and Kamui were asked to perform at various functions and what-not. Sure, these boys hadn’t marketed themselves as super material… proclaiming that they were going to be bigger than the World itself… but that didn’t stop them from proclaiming they were the biggest party act going at the moment! Most orders just assumed that the Canadian/Japanese combination would have been a lot funnier than talent flowing… but that was something that never seemed to come true at any of their shows. Chad Matthews had made something of himself… but even with all the success of being a Party Act… the part of him that longed for the business that was Professional Wrestling called out to him… it cried out for him to go back… An omen… A Sign… A Dealing of Fate? Nobody knows… not even Chad would be able to answer the question for you himself… but, what he can do, is look up to the Stars on any night and say the words… “My path is chosen… I did it alone… no longer will I hold myself on people… for it will be my undoing once more…” Will he come to realize a combination of Musical and Wrestling success in PURE? You’ll just have to stay tuned to find out... Kamui Fukamachi ------------ “Kamui Fukamachi…” Unlike his ‘Friend’… Kamui was nothing like the individual that Chad was… no way, no how… like two different books… they had different stories, they had different chapters… hell, the only thing about them that was the same was their gender… and possibly their love for everything that is PoP… This story begins in a small town in Japan, the actual name of the location is information that Kamui refuses to release… and continues to pass himself off as coming from America. Regardless, he was born to parents that died while he was at a young age. Did this event do as much as to haunt the young child? Of course it did… Of course it did… Kamui moved into an Orphanage at the age of three, taken in by ‘Police Officers’ that found him roaming the empty halls of the home that used to belong to his mother and father. Fukamachi was labelled as the “Kid with Big Dreams” while he was at the Orphanage… they accused him of being too adventurous… too hopeful… too ‘high’ on a future that many people believed would never change… for him, or themselves. They say that sometimes, you need ONE best friend… ONE person to help you up when you fall… At the age of ten, Kamui thought that he’d meet that one person… the one individual that would change the pathway of his life… that one soul who would never be able to desert him, not even in his darkest hour… After seven years in the Orphanage, Kamui had met Mai Fuma, a young girl who had also lost her parents… one way or another. Both aged ten, they became what was the best of friends… doing everything that they could think of together… She was his Rod of Power… He was her Tower of Hope… They meant so much to one another… They meant everything… Infact, Kamui became so infatuated with his friend, that he began to serenade her with songs that were sung by the other people around the complex. His graceful voice gave her something to feel… something to hold onto… while not in the physical sense, she took his ability… and enjoyed every second it was in motion… However… Like many things before this… like many stories before this one… When there are two people… happy… excited… caring about each other to points that were previously unimaginable… heartbreak is bound to strike… and for Kamui, it couldn’t have struck at a more horrid moment in his life… September 1992… A month that will be forever etched in the memory of Kamui… a month that will always stand the test of time, no matter how long our ‘hero’ decides to live for… Why the lack of a date? Simple… he doesn’t remember what day it happened… well, that, or the fact that he doesn’t want to remember the exact day that it happened. We could go into a lot of detail about the events of that time… but it wouldn’t be anything worthwhile… after all, whats done is done… and you can’t regret it… Aside from the horror behind this date… it was also a turning point for our fourteen year old hero… yes, he decided to take the one thing that bonded him to Mai… and developed from that day forth. For some reason, Kamui was confident that he could connect with people of the other-realm in one way or another… and he himself, tried to believe that his music was the bond that existed between both him and Mai… A fact of Fukamachi… A sugar coated dream… A non-existant hope? Whatever it is… it’s the one thing that Kamui has left with regards to Mai… his last ever item, his last connection with her. The time went by following that… Kamui perfected his singing talents… he also learned how to fight, namely to protect himself whenever that sticky situation would arise. Fukamachi wanted to be sure that nothing like Mai would ever be allowed to happen to him again… he wasn’t going to lose another friend… March 25th 1999… This too would mark a significant date for Kamui… not only would it establish the moment that he had made it to the country of Canada, but it would also be the first time that he met his future ‘Best Friend’ Chad Matthews. “Hallo… my name is Kamui Fukamachi… I’ve heard some girls talking about your… ability to sing… and I was wondering if you would be willing to join me…” Ok… Ok… so things didn’t happen as something as the ‘text extract’ above, but the basic jest of things happened and Kamui effectively asked for Chad to become his partner in business, touring the country… singing to the fans, trying to make something out of what they’d both considered to be wasted lives… June 15th 1999… July 22nd 1999… September 28th 1999… December 24th 1999… January 1st 2000… The above dates, just a few of the times that Chad and Kamui were asked to perform at various functions and what-not. Sure, these boys hadn’t marketed themselves as super material… proclaiming that they were going to be bigger than the World itself… but that didn’t stop them from proclaiming they were the biggest party act going at the moment! Most orders just assumed that the Canadian/Japanese combination would have been a lot funnier than talent flowing… but that was something that never seemed to come true at any of their shows. Kamui had made something of his life along with Chad, they’d both managed to conquer the world of Independent PoP… but what about the hidden agenda that had been carried with Chad? Matthews wanted to wrestle… Kamui decided that he wanted to just put his skills to use… Would it happen… would it ever? PURE is the place they are now… the next step of the story is down to them and them alone…
