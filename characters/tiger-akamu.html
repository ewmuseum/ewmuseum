Biography (PURE (2002))

Appearance: Akamu is very muscular and powerful. Broad shouldered and barrel chested. His skin is caramel tanned, his eyes almond shaped. He keeps his head shaved clean, as well as his face except for a thick goatee with his upper lip kept clean. His right arm is completely covered in tiger stripe tattoos. On his back he has a tattoo of a black tribal sun encircled by a dragon and a tiger chasing each other's tails. He has a kanji tattoo on each side of his neck, on is the symbol for 'warrior' and the other is 'tiger'. He is chiselled, little body fat and in peak physical condition. He is a true babyface, he is looking for the honorable fight. He had been in Japan, competing in puroresu for two years before looking to break into the US scene. His path has brought him back to Japan now as he competes in PURE. In ring attire: Mid thigh length black silk shorts, similar to many kick boxers, with Tiger across the back in gold kanji letters. Black fingerless gloves with wrist supports. He wrestles bare foot, his ankles taped heavily. After Akamu's birth his mother and father were together for some time, however his father's life in the Corp pulled him away and he didn't want to give his wife and kid the military life. He sent them money and had them stay in Manoa. Akamu grew up an agressive child, he excelled at all physical activities. When he was very young, his mother placed him in judo classes. Akamu fell in love and was soon learning jujitsu as well as karate. Through his training Akamu began to control his temper, and the neighborhood fights ceased. From the age of 8 he trained his arts until he turned 23 and went to Japan to join a small time Puroresu promotion. During middle school and high school Akamu excelled at wrestling and football. After graduation Akamu went to the University of Hawaii and wrestled. He graduated in 1999 at the age of 22 with a degree in philosophy. When Akamu was 18 his father returned home. Akamu shut him out because he felt abandoned, however through wrestling he and his father bounded. His father's advice helped to achieve a record of 67-9 while he was at UH. After graduation Akamu went to japan and hooked up with a puroresu promotion that had toured in Manoa before. It was here that Akamu Day became known as Tiger Akamu because of his ferocious style of combat. Shortly there after Akamu gained his tattoos. He wrestled there for two years. He came back to the states to make a name for himself but his path has brought him back to where he started.
