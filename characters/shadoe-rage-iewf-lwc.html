Tenure detail
One half of the returning Prophets of Rage; won his own first UWF match by forfeit on 30 October 2004 when scheduled opponent Serge Annis vanished from the entrance ramp during an extended mind-games standoff and never appeared for the bell. The Prophets' own dressing room was set ablaze afterward by a shipment of frozen fish, an apparent fan retaliation for an earlier parking-lot incident. Teamed with Derek Rage to beat Collision Holly and Job Holly by pinfall on 3 November 2004, blinding Collision Holly with his own cape for a turning lariat before the Prophets combined for the Apocalypse on Job Holly. Teamed with Derek Rage in a losing challenge for the UWF North American Tag Team Championship against the Amazing Romanis (Niklos and Ravnos Romani) by pinfall on 17 November 2004, pinned by the champions' own Without A Net after a mystery burst of pyrotechnics and an eyeball graphic rattled him ahead of a missed top-rope attempt. Traded history with Serge Annis over their old IIWF rivalry on 27 November 2004 before the Prophets of Rage and the Hands of Death brawled to a standstill, driving Caliban through a burning table with a Flaming Godkiller; a dog collar match against the Hands of Death was set for Rise to Power. Helped set a chair and table on fire at ringside during the Hands of Death's own no-contest with the Amazing Romanis on 1 December 2004. On 11 December 2004, searched frantically backstage for Pizzazz before Serge Annis revealed she and Marissa Monet had been abducted and hung from a skybox balcony, then led the Prophets of Rage to the ring at Annis's own summons. Promised revenge on the Hands of Death alongside brother Derek on 11 June 2005, then was eliminated by countout from the resulting non-title four corners tag main event after brawling with Ravnos Romani into the crowd. Beat Jacob Drake in a Road to the Gold qualifier on 22 June 2005 with the Angel of Death Drop. Rallied with the rest of the Prophets of Rage on 6 July 2005 against the Romanis' own interference, dubbing the alliance "Intelligent Violence," then won his own match against Vyolynce by disqualification after Niklos and Ravnos Romani disguised themselves as duplicate Vyolynces, finishing the masked man with a chair-assisted piledriver and the Angel of Death Drop once the Romanis were beaten off. Lost to Ravnos Romani by pinfall on 3 August 2005 after a low blow set up the Dance of Knives; ran in during the Women's Tag Team title match on 6 August 2005, helping drive Naomi Ishikawa down for the Misfits' own Spiked Mischief Maker. Sat brooding in the crowd throughout his own North American Tag Team Championship defense on 18 March 2006, leaving brother Derek to wrestle Don't Go There alone until the match was thrown out.
