Total Eclipse

A Tag Team Champion beside the Chameleon in the federation's first summer of 1998, then one of the Lone Gunmen, who wrestled for the team into 2000, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: he came back with the CWL's men in November 2002 and returned to the ring in 2005 and 2009.
