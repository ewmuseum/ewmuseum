RUSH (2002)

2002-05-02 – 2002-11-01
commentator
Tenure detail
RUSH's colour commentator from its first show. The fed's own staff page (about August 2002) lists Kris Raves as "Colour Commentator" beside James Williams, "Play By Play Commentator", and Adrenaline 1 (2 May 2002) opens on "the two man announce team of James Williams and the sadistic Kris Raves". His speaker lines are on the 21 structured cards, Adrenaline 1 to 6, Ignition 2002, Adrenaline 8 to 20 and Crossroads 2002 (a third voice, Jessica, shares the table on Adrenaline 1 to 6 and Ignition 2002), and on the two scripts that are not yet structured: Rising From the Ashes (13 Oct 2002) and the six-month anniversary show (1 Nov 2002, its replays and awards). Adrenaline 7 (20 Jun 2002) has no page and Adrenaline 21 to 23 have no result on file, so the run is dated by the pages that carry him, not claimed beyond them. Each of the 21 cards carries a commentary appearance; the two scripts carry none because they hold no structured card.
