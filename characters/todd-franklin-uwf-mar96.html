Tenure detail
Teamed with brother Bobby Lincoln as the Universal Powers; pinned by Fury's Motor City Massacre in their debut tag match. Teamed with Bobby Lincoln as the Universal Powers again on 17 December 2003, pinned by Ravnos Romani's small package in a loss to the Arcana. Teamed with Bobby Lincoln as the Universal Powers again on 21 March 2004, challenging the Sons of Cacaphony for the UWF North American Tag Team Championship in a losing effort that ended when both teams traded dives to the floor. Forced into a scrambled-partners tag with a reluctant Niklos Romani on 5 May 2004; turned on his own partner mid-match, chokeslamming and lariating him before finishing Erik Grimsson with a New Horizon Line, ending the bout as a no-contest and declaring the Universal Powers done being overlooked. Teamed with Bobby Lincoln as the Universal Powers again on 19 May 2004, beating the Harlequins after Niklos Romani's own pre-match chair ambush and an unidentified interloper's interference; finished Terror with the Universal Elimination. Won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with Bobby Lincoln from the Sons of Cacaphony on 28 August 2004 in a street fight, finishing a knocked-out Erik Grimsson with a superkick for the pin after an unidentified masked interloper ambushed him from inside a locker-room closet. Lost a mismatched partners tag match alongside Jacob Drake to Ravnos Romani and a substituting Alex Extreme by pinfall on 18 September 2004, blinded by the Gypsy Curse powder before Romani's brass-knuckled rollup. Lost alongside Bobby Lincoln and Randall Osbourne to the Sons of Cacaphony and Alex Martinez on 22 September 2004, pinned by a version of the Harlequins' own Reality Check from Martinez and Erik Grimsson. Lost the UWF North American Tag Team Championship alongside Bobby Lincoln to the Amazing Romanis (Niklos and Ravnos Romani) by pinfall on 20 October 2004, flattened by the Romanis' own Total Elimination combination before Niklos pinned Lincoln for the title change, aided by a returning Vyolynce's own ringside chair distraction.
