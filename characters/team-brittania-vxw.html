Team Brittania

Brothers Justin and Simon Davidson, fighting Hot Property to a draw and Purgatory to a no-contest before winning a three-way match by disqualification at Vegas Violence V without taking the VXW Tag Team Championship, then losing a four-way tornado tag to Purgatory at Fear Is Coming II. Won gauntlet falls over the Rodeo Boys, Purgatory and Team Essington at Fear Is Coming III before losing to Marcus O'Malley and Kagedo, then lost a three-way tag to Tap Out City alongside Purgatory at Phear Phantastic 2005. Fought the Rodeo Boys to a no-contest on Winter War III after Team Essington's own four-man run-in, earning a spot in a fatal four-way for the VXW Tag Team Championship at Ghost Town Blues. VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2005-03-17 – 2005-04-11
Tenure detail
Debuts by inserting itself into a VWF Tag Team Championship match already underway between champions Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov and challengers Frank O'Shea and Billy Bones at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), Simon Davidson pinning O'Shea to win the titles after Justin Davidson pulls Valenkov from the ring to stop a cover. Retains the titles by disqualification against McQueen and Valenkov at End Of March (30 March 2005) after the returning Hot Property run in on McQueen; a legal challenge filed the same night argues the team was never a signed, named participant in the St Patricks Day match and so could not legitimately win the championship, left for the VWF's incoming ownership to decide. Defends the titles, billed with a question mark over its own legitimacy, against a returning Henry Fish (billed Scott Starring) and an unidentified stand-in wrestling as the absent Van Wild at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), winning by pinfall; the team's own hired ring announcer, not the broadcast's regular ring announcer, declares the team champions afterward.
