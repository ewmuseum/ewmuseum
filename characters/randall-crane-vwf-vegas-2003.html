Tenure detail
Returns after a two-year absence at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004), teaming with Classy Joey Boy against Sebastian and G-Gangsta for a count-out win, hitting a Revolutions End Powerbomb on G-Gangsta before the Legends flee the ring. Awarded a disqualification win over Sebastian on March To Hell (21 October 2004) after Robbie Caldwell tazes and beats him down before the bell. Attacks Caldwell with a chain before their own rematch on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), disqualified for throwing off the referee and shoving Caldwell out to a technical win; Sebastian chairs him from behind afterward. Eliminated from the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004) by a Phoenix Adam Lazarus back body drop, medics attending his knee afterward. Returns after a five-month absence at The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), beating Billy Bones by disqualification when Bones goes after him with a chair, then enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal as its third entrant, eliminating Fast Eddie Cutlass and going over the top rope himself alongside The Maquilladora on the champion's own dive.
