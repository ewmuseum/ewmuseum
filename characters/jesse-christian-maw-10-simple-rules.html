Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Jesse Christian

A veteran, "despite his advancing years", who contacts Rip about a comeback in April 2010; he works April Showers as Kashmir Singh's partner, objects to being booked to lose, and beats Findlay O'Farraday with the Torture Rack at Spring Fever. He beats Primus Allen at Beach Blast in July 2010 when Findlay O'Farraday's boot misfires, beats Nelson Callum with the Human Torture Rack at Iron in August, and leaves the company after Where It All Begins Again in October 2010, the narrator noting that "the guy complained about losing too much".
