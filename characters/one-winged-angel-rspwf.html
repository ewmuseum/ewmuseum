Tenure detail
Billed from Austin, Texas, a fellow MBC wrestler of champion Paul Wong; pinned Wong with a top-rope moonsault elbowdrop (Hell's Halo) to win the UWF Rampage Championship. Retained the title against Simon O'Neal on 25 October 2003, reversing a corner whip into a body splash and flowing into a roll-up for the pin; his own tag team stablemates Rage and Spice, together billed as Fury, debuted the same night. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship again on 15 November 2003, hitting a top-rope moonsault elbowdrop (Hell's Halo) on Brian McKenzie after manager Virginia St. Ursula distracted McKenzie with a bottle of Bailey's Irish Cream. Retained the UWF Rampage Championship again on 20 December 2003, pinning Scott "Hotspot" Daniels with the Revelator after Quinn Brown's own entrance music distracted Daniels moments after he had knocked out the referee. By 24 March 2004 the title was billed on a different wrestler, Uriah Hawkins; no surviving document in the held record states the match, date, or method by which the reign changed hands. Won a four corners contenders match on 19 May 2004 over Chris O'Brien, T.D. Swagger, and November, lifting Swagger into a Revelator and driving him down across the other two fallen men for the pin, earning a UWF Cruiserweight Championship match against Michael Bonn at Gold Rush. Won the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World from Michael Bonn by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, reversing Bonn's own Deadfall into an uranage before finishing with a top-rope Hell's Halo, after cornerwoman Ami Tran and Amity stablemate Spice each ran interference at ringside. Retained the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World over Michael Bonn by pinfall on 28 August 2004, catching a top-rope hurricanrana attempt and turning it into the Revelator for the pin. Retained the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World over Chris O'Brien on 22 September 2004, a friendly contest between MBC friends that ended with the Heavenly Wind and Hell's Halo combination, cornerwoman Ami Tran dubbing it "Divine Intervention." Beat "Agony" Michael Augustine by disqualification in a non-title match on 30 October 2004 after Erik Grimsson pulled Augustine off the top rope to stop a chair-assisted diving attack; a mass brawl followed involving the Privateers and manager Allison Ivey, leaving Angel busted open. Led Amity (Rage, Spice) into the Bluff City Streetfight main event against the Pride on 27 November 2004, nearly winning with a cane-assisted DDT before Derek Martin turned on him mid-match, chairshotting him and hitting a Pridebreaker for the loss. Watched from the Fury locker room as Rage and Spice prepared for their own losing UWF World Tag Team Championship challenge on 1 December 2004, promising to "Harlequinize" Derek Martin. On 11 December 2004, fought Martin to a no-contest in a match that broke down after the referee was knocked out, hitting a Heavenly Wind Hurricanrana before Scott Daniels's own chairshot ended the contest as pure chaos; saved from a Pridebreaker by a returning Alex Kidd. Retained the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World over Youth Gone Wild by pinfall (a top-rope german release suplex) on 25 May 2005 in a hard-fought, mutually respectful contest, then launched Amity's own "I'm Rich, Bitch" North American tour with Rage and Spice. Challenged World Champion Scott Daniels to a rematch on 11 June 2005 and had it accepted for the following week, then retained the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World over Vyolynce by disqualification after Vyolynce refused to break a leg hold at the ropes. Arrived at Madison Square Garden with fellow Amity members on 28 April 2007 billed as the newly-crowned UWF Cruiserweight Champion of the World, then interrupted Luke Kinsey's own hasty exit after Kinsey's title defense to declare himself the next challenger for the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World.
