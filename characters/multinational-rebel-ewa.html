The Multinational Rebel

aka Irish Rebel, The Canadian Rebel

Tenure detail
Billed Irish Rebel from his own Bloodbath '98 debut, joining the Innovators of Violence after the 4 October 1998 Extreme Measures; billed "the Canadian Rebel"/"Multinational Rebel" from the 11 November 1998 Extreme Measures on, per the fed's own on-air identity-crisis gag. Won the EWA North American Championship at the 15 November 1998 Extreme Measures. Revealed as a fourth, masked Devastation Inc. member attacking Ronny Wonder at the 22 November 1998 Extreme Measures; lost the North American title to Johnny Sledge at Yuletide Beatings.
