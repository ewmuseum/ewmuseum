Tenure detail
A bodyguard with no card of his own in SCCW's database; every trace of him is inside Phillip Kennedy's story. Kennedy's SCCW card gives him a section — “As Phillip Kennedy's bodyguard, Vain can, will, and often does interfere in Phil's matches” — and the Temptation card of 11 October 2009 has him lead Kennedy to the ring as his “new bodyguard”, stand guard against Jared Sykes and club Sykes from the floor in the finish; he escorts Kennedy again on 8 November 2009, and the cards of 3 January and 21 February 2010 note his absence from Kennedy's side. Kennedy's own roleplays write him as the “former rising mixed martial artist turned pro-wrestler” who drinks with Kennedy in Las Vegas and whom the narration calls “The Pain Train”. PTCpedia's Kennedy article lists the 11 October 2009 win as “Phillip Kennedy (w/ Michael Vain)”. Held at presumed on this record: see the identity note.
Source: SCCW's own texts, served: Phillip Kennedy's own SCCW biography card (its MICHAEL VAIN section), the Temptation cards of 11 October 2009, 8 November 2009, 3 January 2010 and 21 February 2010, and Kennedy's own roleplays “Hero of the Day” (28 December 2009) and “Rebuy” (byline 24 July 2010)
