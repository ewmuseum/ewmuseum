Unholy Matrimony

Seymour Almasy and Aerith Heartilly's divorced-couple tag team, a pairing that had held the UWF World Tag Team Championship for four weeks in 2003 before it took the name and, in April 2005, the PTC Tag Team Championship on the judges' scores for a three-way pinata match no federation ever hosted — champions no longer a month later, when they no-showed the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. Tenure detail
The team's whole life, from the board's own posts. They signed up for the alliance's March 2005 tag division as "Unholy Matrimony", billed from the UWF, the sign-up naming them "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy and "The One Winged Angel" Aerith Almasy, and were put straight into a three-way for the PTC Tag Team Championship: "Six Kendo Sticks. Three tag teams. Two gold belts. One pinata. Zaro chance of anyone leaving with their dignity intact. This is... Purgatory with a Pinata~!", against the champions, billed Los Muchos Macho Mascadas (White Tiger and Pajaro de Presa, also of the UWF), and Soul Purpose (Noah Hanson and Fury, of WMW). One roleplay each was the limit. NO MATCH WAS EVER WRITTEN: no federation offered to host it after weeks of asking, and the championship changed hands on the judges' scores alone — two of the three judges placed Unholy Matrimony first and the third named only a second and a third place, and the ruling, posted 26 April 2005, reads "Sean is the new Tag Team Champion defeating the others." THE REIGN ENDED WITHOUT A MATCH OF ITS OWN EITHER: the Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean were booked against them through May 2005, and the Pirates' own side wrote a year later, listing his best matches, "PTC Tag Team Championship - Killer Pirates vs. Unholy Matrimony. Despite the no show by my opponents, I won the PTC Tag Team Championship belts". The alliance's own title history, posted 27 October 2005, places them sixth in the PTC Tag Team Championship's order, between the Masked Monkeys (Pajaro de Presa and White Tiger) and the Killer Pirates, and records the championship itself as discontinued and retired. THE REIGN IS RECORDED with the championship's other reigns, from 26 April 2005, the day of the ruling, to 28 May 2005, when the Killer Pirates took the belts uncontested: the history post gives an order and no dates — "I don't have dates. I just have orders of champions" — and both dates are PTC's own title log's, the first also the ruling post's. FOUR MENTIONS THAT ARE NOT THIS TEAM, checked and carrying no edge: Global Championship Wrestling's own roleplay "The Wheels Go 'Round 'n 'Round" of 1 October 2008 uses "that unholy matrimony" as the ordinary English phrase, for a wished-for combination of one wrestler's old ring skills with his current blood lust, and names neither member; so do FAW's "The Best Ideas are Often the Simplest" (16 October 2002, a stench), NAPW's "Phenoms Return" (April 2014, a suburban marriage) and a PTC roleplay of 9 January 2007 ("Lets make unholy matrimony with the belt on the line").

UWF (2001–07)

tag team — UWF World Tag Team Champions
Tenure detail
The UWF's cards field the pair as World Tag Team Champions under their own names, Seymour Almasy and Aerith Heartilly, not under the team name. They won the titles on 16 November 2003 and lost them to the British Blazers on 14 December 2003.
Source: The UWF's own Sunday Afternoon Supremacy cards: 16 November 2003 (UWF (2001–07) Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 16 Nov 2003, "And NEW Tag Team Champions: SEYMOUR ALMASY & AERITH HEARTILLY") and 14 December 2003 (UWF (2001–07) Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 14 Dec 2003); the title's own history dates the reign 16 November to 14 December 2003 (titles/uwf-world-tag-team.html).
