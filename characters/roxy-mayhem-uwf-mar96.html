Roxy Mayhem

"Violent" Victor Frost's new valet, debuting alongside his own "Graphic Violence World Tour 2005." Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2005-05-28 – 2006-03-18
Tenure detail
Debuted on 28 May 2005 accompanying "Violent" Victor Frost to the ring as he launched his own "Graphic Violence World Tour 2005," jawing with ringside fans in his place. Traded barbs with Sayaka Daikin-Price alongside Victor Frost backstage on 11 June 2005, then rang the ring bell early during Frost's own match against Alex Martinez to trick him into releasing a Firebomb Chokeslam, before Frost recovered to steal the pinfall. Backed Victor Frost's own complaints about Alex Martinez's Road to the Gold win to reporter Peter Phillips on 6 July 2005, then accompanied Frost to his own Unified Television Championship match against Marcus Nuit. Visited a hospitalized Victor Frost on 20 July 2005 and was blamed by him for having been scalped bald as well, in the aftermath of Sabbath's own attack. Interfered at ringside for Frost during his own tag team win over Miguel Quesada and Jason Keening on 23 July 2005. Was warned off accompanying Victor Frost to a strip club on 3 August 2005 and rebuked when she brought up the couple's own unspoken history, left alone in a hotel bathroom as he departed for the arena. Was publicly dumped by Victor Frost on his return to UWF television on 18 March 2006.
