UK Dragon

A masked Briton allied to JD Morgan who, as Acid's partner in the team called Acid Dragon, loses to the Disrespected on Badge of Honor in August 2008, then takes part in a four-way match with Aaron Andrews, Freddy Huggins and Shingen Miyazaki. In September 2008 he is one of four in a singles match won by Acid. In November 2008 he teams with Acid against Steve Gumble in a three-way match and takes Acid's Acid Rain Bomb when Gumble twists clear. In December 2008 he is beaten in the six-man match won by Aaron Andrews and loses to the New Wave with Acid. Written by AI, 2026
