UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2005-05-25 – 2007-04-28
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 5 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 4-1-0
Tenure detail
Appeared in a vignette on 25 May 2005 recounting a career cut short by years of overwork and injury, naming Verhoeven, Vitrone, and Eddie Jacobs among the opponents who left him scarred, and describing forfeited matches and unfinished business; did not wrestle. Delivered a rooftop monologue about returning from career-ending injuries on 8 June 2005, met a returning Sonya Benedict in the parking lot beforehand with valet Exotica at his side, then won his own first sanctioned UWF match against "Gunman" Danny Cole by submission (the Web of Death sharpshooter). Submitted "Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb with the Web of Death in a Road to the Gold qualifier on 22 June 2005, advancing toward the battle royal. Agreed backstage on 23 July 2005 to watch over Sonya Benedict against the Hands of Death at Chad Grimsson's own request, extracting a promise of reciprocal help against Trey DaMann. Overcame a pre-match backstage beatdown from Trey DaMann to team with the Sons of Cacaphony and pin Caliban for the win on 3 August 2005. Teamed with Sonya Benedict in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, pinned by a tandem shining wizard from Sabbath and Moira Faith after asking his own partner to focus on winning rather than her feud with Angel; shared a dockside scene beforehand recalling his late wife, Ashley, killed in a plane crash five years earlier. Beat "Violent" Victor Frost by pinfall (the Lariat) on 7 June 2006, reversing a dragon sleeper into a standing powerslam beforehand; delivered a promo before the match reflecting on a year of complacency since his own Gold Rush appearance. Sought out new outside consultant "The Doomsayer" Kyle Lee on 28 April 2007 for advice on breaking out of a career rut, gifting him a bottle of Jack Daniels; discussed the visit afterward with girlfriend "Twilight Angel" Sonya Benedict, who had a title belt of her own to show him. Did not wrestle.
