Appeared representing Project BATTLE*DREAM in a one-off interpromotional attraction match against UWF's own Fantasy Girl, carrying a taser to the ring (unused in the match) alongside sidekick Rocko; won by pinfall with the Skull Crash Buster. Teamed with countrywoman Reona Yoshida as the newly-billed Team BATTLE*DREAM in a four corners women's tag war on 25 August 2004, Yoshida's own double DDT setting up the pinfall on Melody for the win; earned a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship match against the Bod Squad the following week. Lost a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship challenge alongside Reona Yoshida to the champions, the Bod Squad, by disqualification on 8 September 2004, struck with a chair by Tara "Sunburst" Marshall to save the title. Teamed with Reona Yoshida and Sierra Browne to beat Donna Tetreault and the Bod Squad in a non-title six-woman tag on 2 October 2004, tasering Tara "Sunburst" Marshall mid-match before Sierra Browne's own Truth Superkick finished Brianna Landis for the pin. Lost a UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World challenge to champion Donna Tetreault by disqualification on 16 October 2004 after being found with a taser at ringside, despite having Tetreault beaten clean with the Skull Crash Buster moments earlier. Knocked out at ringside by Tara "Sunburst" Marshall with the Bod Squad's own championship belt on 1 December 2004 during tag partner Reona Yoshida's own loss to Brianna Landis; Marshall then used Ishikawa's own taser on a bystander identified only as Rocko.
