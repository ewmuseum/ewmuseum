Xavier Kannon

Wrestling's only Scientologist, Xavier Kannon was voted PCW's Wrestler of the Year and Heel of the Year for 2002, a season spent as World Champion, part-owner and Commissioner with Ellie Hall alongside, worked SCCW cards between July 2006 and June 2010, and was named Heel of the Year for 2007-08 in PRIME, where blinding Jonathan Rhine on ReVolution 163 was that season's Moment of the Year.
