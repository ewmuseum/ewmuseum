Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Tommy Cornell

TCW's English owner, its World Heavyweight Champion and the leader of the Syndicate, who holds almost all the gold with his stable, keeps Troy Tornado from a rematch until the Board's new representative makes it, and signs the diary's foreword as a book, and loses the World Heavyweight Championship to Troy Tornado at Excessive Force in June 2008, and beats Eddie Peak at Summer Showdown in July 2008 after a falling-out with Sam Keith, and in August 2008 signs Tyson Baine as the Syndicate's sixth man and, at Hotter Than Hell, is beaten by Sam Keith in a No Holds Barred match, leaving the ring holding his arm, and in September 2008 wins his Total Wrestling matches, promises that by July 2009 every championship in TCW will belong to the Syndicate and, at Destructive Energy, submits to Troy Tornado and Johnny Bloodstone in the three-way World Heavyweight Championship match, and in October 2008 walks off with the World Heavyweight Championship belt, is barred by the Board from title matches for six months, tests Aaron Andrews and Hell Monkey in the ring and, at Crunch Time, is beaten by Sam Keith in the Test of Steel. In November 2008 he beats Lobo Blanco and Chance Fortune in his tests, beats Sam Keith & the Machines with the Stone Brothers and the Balcony Express with Wolf Hawkins, loses with Tyson Baine to Rick Law and Troy Tornado, sends Marat Khoklov after Eddie Peak and at A Little South Of Sanity is beaten by Tornado in a non-title match despite Baine's help. In December 2008 he beats Rocky Golden in a brawl that echoes his match with Nemesis, beats Joey Minnesota as one of his tests, fights Troy Tornado to a no contest when Jack Bruce returns, falls out with Wolf Hawkins over Aaron Andrews and at Psycho Circus beats Andrews with Hawkins's chair before attacking Tornado and Eddie Peak with the retired TCW belt.
