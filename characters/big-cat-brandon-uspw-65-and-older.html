Big Cat Brandon

Danny Jillefski's bodyguard, brought in on the Monday of Week 3, July 2007: he flattens Bulldozer Brandon Smith, Wanda Fish and Giant Oak, takes part in the fatal four-way for the World Championship number one contendership and squashes Warlord Agony in August 2007. In October 2007 he slams Muffin's leg in a door, beats Rudy Velasquez, and loses to Muffin at A Haunting in America and to Freddie Datsun in a three-way on the Thursday of Week 3. In October and November 2007 he pounces Muffin off the stage, beats T-Rex, guards the door for Jillefski, and with Eddie Peak beats Sam and Alicia Strong at Pilgrimage. In late November and December 2007 he beats Peter Valentine and Bruce the Giant, wins with Joey Minnesota over Muffin and Datsun, pins Datsun in 15:56 (A), and is booked against Sam Strong for control of USPW at Winter Epiphany.
