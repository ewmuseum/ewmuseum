The Masked Patriot and Kashmir Singh

aka Smith & Singh, Smith and Singh

A babyface tag team the narrator finds excellent, introduced at Spring Fever against Citizen X and Findlay O'Farraday. With the Masked Patriot now wrestling as "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith, the pair beat Ricochet & Sharp at Summer Showdown in June 2010, and with Fumihiro Ota they are the Trios champions; the diary's own title records call them "Smith & Singh", and the narrator's roster overview says he has "formed a great tag team" for the coming months. As Smith & Singh they lose to the Canadian Blondes at Remember November in November 2010 and at the Invitational, lose to Ford & Speed at All Or Nothing in February 2011, beat Casey Valentine and Antonio on the 25th March tour show and fall to The Triple Threat with Amazing Fire Fly on 4th April. They lose the Tag Team title three-ways of 26th May and 12th July 2011, lose to Ford & Speed at Summernova, squash Team XDW at Iron and, with Fumihiro Ota, beat the Canadian Blondes and Raphael at Back To School; Singh's Broadway enthusiasm and Smith's dyed hair run through July and August. Billed by the diary as Smith & Singh, they lose a three-way at Fall Of The King on 26th September 2011, win the Tag Team titles from the Canadian Blondes at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October, and keep them against the Second Sons, the Dark Raptors and Ford & Speed, Ford & Speed, Firebird and Citizen X and, at The Rip Chord Invitational on 12th January 2012, Ford & Speed and Lockdown; Singh and Smith talk Broadway musicals through the winter. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 they keep the Tag Team titles against the Canadian Blondes at Great Lakes Crisis, the Second Sons at March Madness and the Second Sons again at May The Force Be With You, and before the Gold Rush match at April Showers agree to be friends and partners entering it but rivals bell to bell. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 they keep the Tag Team titles in the three-way at High Noon on 5th June, lose them to the Dark Raptors at Summer Showdown on 12th June, and then lose to Always Breakin' Hollywood, the Second Sons, the Canadian Blondes and Ford & Speed, the narrator saying they are doing the job to any heel team that needs a little love; at Back To School on 5th September they stand with Donnie J to protect Sienna LeNoir.
