Casey Valentine

Peter Valentine's 18-year-old nephew, sent to USPW to train with him, whom Peter insists is his newborn son: he beats Dallas McWade in his first match in September 2007, brushes off the dudes' mockery and, as one of The Valentines, beats Trent Shaffer and Chance Fortune by count-out at Blaze of Glory. In October and November 2007 he beats Maxmillion Productions with Peter by disqualification, loses to Fumihiro Ota, and wins the tag belts with Peter at Pilgrimage for a few minutes before losing them. In late November and December 2007 he is hooded and beaten by Devine, tells Peter he is his nephew, punches him and loses to him in 4:43. Written by AI, 2026
