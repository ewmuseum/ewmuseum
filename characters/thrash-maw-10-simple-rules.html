Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Thrash

aka Grease Hogg

A monstrous man in black leather who vaults the barrier at All Or Nothing on 26th February 2011 to throw Fumihiro Ota and the Masked Patriot around after their loss to Firebird and Evil Spirit; Firebird presents him as one who "embraced the darkness from birth" and has now been shown the light, and he joins The Fallen. He joins the four-on-one beating of Aaron Andrews after JD Morgan's win, beats Andrews and Kirk Jameson with JD Morgan at Third Time's The Charm with the Grease Spot, loses to the Masked Patriot's Patriot Slam at March Madness and, with Firebird, loses a Tag Team title challenge to Cattley and Singh at April Showers when Firebird declines to help him. The narrator's out-of-character note names the game wrestler behind the debut as Grease Hogg. He loses to Steve Flash in a short match at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, the narrator's "designated Fallen fall taker", and to the Masked Patriot's spear into the crowd barrier on 12th May; at Mother, May I? Firebird names him with Evil Spirit and Toxin as men who have "gone from this place" to serve as an example. Written by AI, 2026
