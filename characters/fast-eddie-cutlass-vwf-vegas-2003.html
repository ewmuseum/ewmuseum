Tenure detail
Pins LOCO with the Guarantee to win the Team Tournament Final on Firestorm (3 May 2004). Loses a triple threat to Kagedo after an unidentified masked man's interference, then enters the Gauntlet battle royal and is fired on the spot by new majority owner Kingston Cash, escorted out by security before the match's own finish. Wins a no-disqualification superplex match over a cooperative Lean Darlene Orion on Dog Day Five (16 July 2004), the first of majority owner Kingston Cash's own conditions for winning his job back. The Heavyweight Championship main event on Wrestle Style V.1 ends without a finish when a returning Classy Joey Boy interferes; loses to Blaze in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3 after Blaze used the ropes for illegal leverage on the pin. Wrestles The Maquilladora to a non-title time-limit draw on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Wins the seven-man mini rumble on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), reversing an abdominal stretch into the Guarantee on The Maquilladora after an unidentified woman's own interference; Kingston Cash books the win as a VWF Submission Championship match against The Maquilladora at Vegas Violence. Wrestles The Maquilladora to a no contest for the VWF Submission Championship at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) after Orion, Orion #2 and The Equalizer run in on both competitors. Loses with Jason Storm and Flash to Trinity in a six-man tag on March To Hell (21 October 2004). Beats G-Gangsta by disqualification and a follow-up Guarantee on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) after Gangsta reaches for a tazer gun. Qualifies for the Rage in the Cage by lasting to the final three of the twelve-man Phear Phantastic battle royal (8 November 2004), then wins a fall over Classy Joey Boy inside the cage after Lark Fenriz's own Sammael weakens him, both men eliminated from the match in the same moment. Named on air, tied to a cross and beaten by Pagan ahead of his own scheduled six-man tag against Trinity, which never happens, on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004). Loses a triple threat to Lark Fenriz's own Sammael on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004) moments after diving out of the ring to clothesline an interfering Pagan. Wrestles Trinity to a no contest alongside Lark Fenriz on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005) after their own mystery third partner never arrives. Teams with G-Gangsta in a non-title tag against Mark McQueen and Thunder Valenkov on Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005), ruled a no contest after the Riders of the Apocalypse run in, then is chained, whipped and crowned with a barbed-wire "Crown of Thorns" by Pagan in a post-match beatdown that ends with John Tyler ejecting Pagan from the building. Enters the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet on Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) and is eliminated by Kagedo. Beats Pagan in a Church Of Pain match at Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) by referee stoppage, fighting off interference from Pagan's Trinity stablemates Gods Tears and The Equalizer -- who topple a ringside spotlight onto him and drag him toward the arena scaffolding before enforcer Kagedo intervenes -- then hitting the Guarantee and rolling Pagan off the side of the scaffolding. Loses to The New Revolution Kenny Kidd at March To Madness (2 March 2005), caught in a small package. Challenges Sidious The Clown for the VWF Submission Championship at St Patricks Day Massacre (17 March 2005), declared a no contest after Gods Tears, Roadkill Rick, Adam Jennings and The Dark Monster Tai Angela all converge on Gods Tears mid-match. Challenges Gods Tears for the VWF Hardcore Championship at End Of March (30 March 2005), fighting to a time-limit draw. Competes on the losing side of the eight-man elimination match on The Gauntlet 2005 (11 April 2005), rolled up and eliminated by Tai Angela; also enters the closing Gauntlet battle royal, caught out of a flying bodypress and fallaway-slammed over the top rope by Revolution Randall Crane.
