Tenure detail
A UWF original billed as a former World Heavyweight Champion, two-time United States Champion, Road to the Gold winner, and a co-founder of the Pride roughly a decade earlier; returned from years away on 28 May 2005 at Scott "Hotspot" Daniels's own invitation, praised "Dead End" Derek Martin as his own successor, then flattened him with an inverted powerbomb (the Brett Bomb) after Martin agreed to train under him. Invited Alex Kidd, via a message passed through Alicia Powers backstage on 20 July 2005, to join him at a Gold Rush video-game press event. Accepted Derek Martin's own Gold Rush no-disqualification challenge on 23 July 2005 by attacking him immediately, and was saved from a Pride beatdown by Alex Martinez; his own longtime friend Scott Daniels then turned on him mid-main-event with a chair shot and a tandem Pridebreaker, and Martin scored the pin. Was confronted by Derek Martin in front of his daughter at the Eaton Centre food court on 6 August 2005; hosted Alex Martinez on the Greene Room the same night, securing a promise of a future title shot if Martinez won at Gold Rush. Teamed with Alicia Powers to reach the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament finals on 14 February 2006, eliminating Alex Kidd and "Tornado" Tara Smith before falling to Alex Extreme's spinning tombstone piledriver; was warned backstage beforehand by a protective Alex Kidd to look after wife and tag partner Alicia Powers. Hosted Trey DaMann on the Greene Room on 18 March 2006, recounting nephew Colby Greene's own rise in River City Wrestling before clotheslining the young braggart over the top rope; named to a four-way World Heavyweight Championship match the following week.
