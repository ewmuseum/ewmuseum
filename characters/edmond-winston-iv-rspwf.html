UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-10-04 – 2004-12-11
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 9 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 3-4-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
The UWF's own co-owner; personally wrestled protege Jamie Underwood on 24 March 2004 after first offering him a staged win, turning on him for real once Underwood accepted it. The match ended without a decision when Sabbath, then Michael Augustine and Alex Extreme, then the Privateers all ran in in succession; Winston set up a handicap main event, the Youngbloods against his own New Era, for the following Rampage. Teamed with Underwood and the Privateers against Michael Augustine and Alex Extreme in that own handicap main event on 15 May 2004; the Privateers turned on him mid-match to side with Extreme, triple-conchairtoing him and putting him through a table before Augustine turned on Extreme in turn. Delivered a chess-themed office monologue on 19 May 2004 naming Serge Annis and Caliban as the instrument of his revenge on the Privateers; does not compete on this card. Lost the main event to Kyle Lee by pinfall on 29 May 2004 with World Champion Michael Augustine as special guest referee, putting Lee into the Gold Rush main event triple threat against Winston and Augustine for the World Heavyweight Championship. Competed in the Gold Rush main event triple threat for the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World on 5 June 2004, losing when Kyle Lee pinned champion Michael Augustine after the real Alex Extreme knocked out both Winston and Augustine, exposing an impostor Alex Extreme (Job Holly) who had been assisting Winston. Escaped a Tower of Doom cage match last on 30 October 2004, and only after being hurled bodily out of the top cage's own door by Alex Martinez; his team (with Victor Frost, Papa Legba, and Luke Kinsey) was declared the winner once he landed outside the structure, though he was left unconscious and bleeding, then beaten further by a surprise-returning "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee and by all four opponents after the bell. Beat Alex Kidd in a Tuxedo Death Match on 13 November 2004 after Kidd failed to answer referee Ingraham's ten count following a cobra clutch suplex, in a brawl that spilled from the ring to his own limousine; dragged to safety afterward by a returning Scott Daniels and Team Canada before Kyle Lee and other fan favorites could reach him. Warned Jamie Underwood backstage on 27 November 2004 that Sabbath would beat him badly at Rise to Power, then agreed to face Sabbath himself the following week in a Hardcore Match to prepare Underwood; sparred with new signing Luke Kinsey over Marshall Law's own unpopularity. Lost a self-arranged Hardcore Rules match to Sabbath by pinfall (a Black Tie Suplex through a table) on 11 December 2004, a brutal contest that also put both men through a pane of glass and a section of hockey boards; watched Luke Kinsey's own world title opportunity develop from his own skybox during the main event.

Tenure detail
