Ricochet

aka Marc Speed

One of three masked newcomers who ambush Fumihiro Ota, "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh in the boiler room at Mother, May I? in May 2010; he debuts that night against Singh and wins with a Cross Armbreaker, and is on the card for Summer Showdown with Sharp. He loses to Brandon Smith at Summer Showdown in June 2010, beats Fumihiro Ota with the Cross Armbreaker in a twenty-minute match at Fall Of The King in September, and forces the Masked Patriot to submit in a three-way at Where It All Begins Again in October. At Eleventh Hour in November Firebird has him unmask as Marc Speed, "a man who lived life as though it would last forever". As Marc Speed, one half of Ford & Speed, he beats the Bailey Boys at Remember November in November 2010, loses a qualifier to Steve Flash at Season's Beatings and loses to Morgan & Benning at End Of Days; the team beat the Young Americans at April Showers. As Marc Speed he and Greg Ford tell the crowd on 12th May 2011 that they are no longer associated with The Fallen but still follow its ideals and will claim the Tag Team titles; he forces Stevie Grayson to tap to a Cross Armbar that night and beats Singh & Smith with a Cross Armbreaker at Summernova. As Ford & Speed he beats Amazing Fire Fly and Jared Johnson at All Or Nothing and the West Texas Peacekeepers at March Madness, and loses a singles match to Brandon Smith's Inverted Piledriver at Mother, May I? on 12th May. As Ford & Speed in the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he beats KC Glenn and Bradford Peverell at South East Madness on 9th June and the West Texas Peacekeepers at Summernova on 5th August, and Smith & Singh at Back To School on 5th September, all with Fallen Chances, and loses the High Noon three-way on 5th June.
