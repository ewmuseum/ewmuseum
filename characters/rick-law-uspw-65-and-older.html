Rick Law

A former USPW National Champion (the diary's Where Are They Now? column: the second biggest name to come out of USPW, whose feud with Rich Money led to both being signed by the Big Two) and Sam Strong's mentee, who returns on the Monday of Week 1, December 2007 in a police officer's uniform with a night stick and a ticket book (the author: he is not on a talent trade) to drive Jillefski's Big Cat Brandon and Akima off Sam Strong and Muffin, and joins the faces. He beats Akima by disqualification, teams with Muffin to beat The Best Tag Team Like Ever in 12:44, stops Devine from hitting Muffin and is framed by him with his own club on the Monday of Week 3 (disqualified in 17:59, A).
