The New Extreme Order

Nate Hatred, Chris Hero and James Mitchell, whom the diary bills as 'The New Extreme Order' when they walk to the ring on Blood, Sweat and Beers in October 2007 (and calls 'The New Blood' in the match before). Hero helps Hatred beat Paul Burchill at Anarchy Rulz, with the Decapitator Lariat, and the Sinister Minister, Hero and Hatred celebrate with a hug; Hero costs Bret Hart his match with Hatred and, in a handicap match with Hatred, rolls Bret up with his feet on the ropes; Hatred beats Sabu on Blood, Sweat and Beers and on Revolution with Hero's help, and Mitchell challenges Sabu to a Streak versus Career Match at November To Remember XV. In November 2007 Nate Hatred, with James Mitchell, interferes in the Mark Calaway and Sabu match on Revolution, beats Calaway and Sabu with Rhino on Blood, Sweat and Beers, and has Mitchell promise Sabu his 'day of judgement' in the Streak versus Career Match at November To Remember XV; Hatred beats Brandon Thomaselli with the Decapitator Lariat, and after Sabu beats Masato Tanaka he wraps barbed wire round Sabu's neck and hangs him over the ropes, the broadcast cutting away, and on the last Revolution a mannequin of Hatred hangs from barbed wire and burns in a corridor. Chris Hero, who calls himself 'The Saviour of ECW', beats Chris Jericho twice in three nights, Jerry Lynn and Sal Thomaselli, and with Punk beats Jericho and Vito on Blood, Sweat and Beers. At November To Remember XV Nate Hatred, with James Mitchell, beats Sabu in the Streak versus Career Match with a Big Splash onto barbed wire and a Decapitator Lariat and spits at Sabu's offered hand, and Chris Hero beats Chris Jericho with Hero's Welcome after a Liontamer. On the Saturday show Hero beats Tommy Dreamer in a Masters of Pain qualifier when Hatred knocks Dreamer out; on Revolution on the first Monday of December Hatred and Mitchell break up Cactus Jack's qualifier with Mark Calaway, Hatred hitting both men with Decapitator Lariats so that Cactus is ruled knocked out, and he floors both Innovators with a Double Decapitator. On the Saturday show Hero beats Eddie Guerrero with Hero's Sidekick, and Hatred beats Dreamer; Mitchell announces a handicap match against both Innovators at Got Blood?, and Hatred smashes a microphone handle into his own forehead until he bleeds.
