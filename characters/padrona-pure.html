Biography (PURE (2002))

Brought up to respect everyone and everything, Padrona began life much quicker then most boys his age. His parents had major goals and morals to set on their son at an early age, and by the age of four he was already starting to lift weights and run miles on end, trying to build his agility and strength. His parents saw this gift as an opportunity not only for them, but also for Padrona to become someone special in the world. They wanted him to use his gift as a way of showing his respect for his family. Padrona started taking martial arts and several other fighting and combat styles attempting to become a very skillful worker. Tournaments came and went, but no loss would keep this young child down. While other opponents got ear full from the trainers, Padrona would come alone and virtually break down his opponent on his own. At the starting of each match he would bow to his opponent in honor, then receive spit to the face or the middle finger. Padrona then moved onto larger competition, where money was involved. He kept his ways going and still received the same treatment. In one incident, he bowed to his opponent and received a stiff kick to the face. So Padrona placed a mask over his face, blocking out the window to the soul. Now his opponents would be fighting with no edge. Family and friends would cheer him on, but never gave more then respect to their son. Money was divided between family, travelling and charities. Padrona was brought up the right way and soon would give over five million dollars to local charities in Thailand. But soon, tragedy would strike, as Padrona’s body started to fade. His wrists started to give out and during one battle he collapsed both his lungs which almost ended his life. For the next five years while his body healed, Padrona studied the world and learned more about culture through out the world. He became interested on how the body works, and rituals of competition countries have. By 1995, Padrona was healed and ready to start again. Armed with a mask, wrist and shin plates and his intelligence, Padrona travels the world in search of new forms of competition. His respect for owners and political leaders has brought him great success. No matter what he is told to do, Padrona will comply as long as the task is honorable. He still gives a great deal to charities and to his family, keeping very little for himself. He now finds himself looked for a new form of honorable competition that the world has yet to see him in. <B>The History of the Mask</B> The mask Padrona wears is said to hide the ability Padrona possesses. In blocks out any mental feeds Padrona might be signaling and also stops eye movement from identifying the direction Padrona will take. The mask was a gift from his father as a child. It is believed to also symbolize the honor and respect Padrona has for his opponents. The mask adds the illusion to a face inside the mask. Padrona will never remove the mask, even out in the normal world. He is said to care so much for the world around him, that not showing his face means a sign of respect to the people around him. He bows to all, and rarely gets over abusive with anyone. The mask also shows that connection between his respect and honor for Thailand. <b>Respect and Honor</B> Padrona’s respect is said to be the key to his success. Respecting his opponent and honoring their method is the first step in learning how they move, think and fight. Padrona knows the world does not respect everyone, but with himself as the role model he tries to push those ways. Padrona’s respect and honor goes as for as to respect his enemies and the greedy at heart. Now one is perfect to him but that is respect is the sign of perfection. Now he looks at all as an equal until other wise. His main goal is to get the respect of his fellow opponents and be seen as a high respectable opponent. <b><U>Weaknesses</B></U> <b> Face</b> – Guards his mask at all costs, so his true identity is not reveled. <b> Strength</b> – His low strength can become a fact later on in matches when fatigue sets in. <b> Wrist</b> – Has weak wrists and can’t hold up opponents for long periods of time. <b> Lungs</b> – Weak lungs and can become short of breath quickly <b> Mind</b> – Can over think his strategy and miss key factors or take too long to execute a move. <b><U>Strengths</B></U> <b> Arms</b> – Has enough power to lift heavier men in quick motions. <b> Leg's</b> – Strong legs that allow faster movement. <b> Mind</b> – Uses intelligence as a weapon to out think his opponent. <B>Emotional:</B> Padrona style and way of life revolves around honor and respect for his opponent. There will be no trash talking from his mouth, as he prefers to rather defeat his opponent in an honorable fashion. Though he will get violent when need be, Padrona remains calm and collective even in the deepest defeats. He does not allow his emotions to control his actions, takes orders very easily, even if he has negative thoughts about the owner. Cheating is never an option and this may cause some loses if he spends time even thinking about the possibilities. He also is very involved in the culture of Thailand, which adds onto his values. <B>Physical:</B> Padrona uses key aerial moves, plus a large variety of ground base slams to grab an upper advantage. With a smaller size, he is very quick, but also has the power to lift heavier opponents. He takes calculated risks, and if need be, he may go out and take an extreme risk, only if the prizes is worth the risk. He may use his arm and shin plating as weapons during a violent struggle or if his opponent has cheated in a way before. He uses the classic bowing of the head to signify an acceptance.
