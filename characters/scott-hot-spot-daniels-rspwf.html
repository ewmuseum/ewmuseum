UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-30 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 17 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 9-4-1, 3 no contest
Tenure detail
Billed from Chicago, Illinois, a member of the Pride; teamed with Tom Landis to win the tag team main event over Quinn Brown and Alex Kidd, pinning Brown with the Before the Fall piledriver after a low blow. Also appeared on the following Meltdown, joining the Pride's attack on Serge Annis without competing in a structured match. Teamed with Tom Landis and Team Canada as the Pride in a non-title, count-out loss on 22 October 2003. Beat Paul Wong by pinfall (Pridebreaker) on 15 November 2003 with a low blow and his feet on the ropes, then challenged Quinn Brown to a sixty-minute Iron Man match at Eve of Destruction. Challenged the One-Winged Angel for the UWF Rampage Championship on 20 December 2003, knocking out the referee and appearing to score an unofficial pinfall of his own before Quinn Brown's entrance music distracted him long enough for the champion to retain with the Revelator; Brown then attacked him with a baseball bat and challenged him to an Iron Man match at Eve of Destruction. Teamed with Team Canada (Trevor Hextall and Bob Jablonski) on 10 March 2004 in a six-man tag win over the Ghost Dancers and Erik Grimsson, scoring the pinfall on Michael Keening after two unidentified men in suits ambushed Team Canada's opponents; also met privately and cordially with new owner Edmond Winston IV, who told him his own claim on Michael Augustine's UWF World Heavyweight Championship came first. Teamed with Trevor Hextall and Bob Jablonski again against Alex Kidd, Paul Wong, and "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee on 15 May 2004 in a six-man tag that ended without a decision amid a mass brawl; accepted debuting Alex Martinez's own in-ring challenge for Gold Rush after Vice-President Jessica Marshall made it a pay-per-view attraction. Lost to Alex Martinez by pinfall in a Gold Rush dream match on 5 June 2004 despite a low blow, a brass-knuckles punch, two steel chair shots and a Pridebreaker, caught with a Firebomb Chokeslam after a second Pridebreaker attempt was blocked. Lost to Jason Keening by pinfall (an inside cradle) on 28 August 2004, then attacked him after the bell with a Pridebreaker until a masked "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas -- Michael Keening's own attacker from Gold Rush -- interrupted; drove Douglas from the ring himself, calling it his own house rather than Keening's. Beat Alex Kidd and Uriah Hawkins by pinfall alongside a substituting "Dead End" Derek Martin on 18 September 2004 after tag partner Tom Landis was hospitalized in a backstage crowbar attack by "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas. Fought Chris Douglas to a double disqualification on 2 October 2004 under special guest referee Jason Keening, who ended the match himself and clotheslined both men from the ring after being caught between their blows; offered "Dead End" Derek Martin a spot in the Pride later the same show, which Martin turned down. Won a three-way dance over "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas and Jason Keening on 16 October 2004, stealing a pinfall on Keening after Keening and Douglas collided head-first. Fought "Agony" Michael Augustine to a no-contest on 3 November 2004 after Allison Ivey and Kari Stevens both struck their own man's opponent with a chair and Amity (One-Winged Angel, Spice, Rage) ran in on both wrestlers, ending the match early. Challenged the One-Winged Angel to a steel cage match at Rise to Power on 27 November 2004, then led the Pride to a win over Amity in the main event Bluff City Streetfight after new addition Derek Martin turned on the One-Winged Angel with a Pridebreaker for the pinfall. Fought Tigress to a double-disqualification draw for the UWF Meltdown Championship on 1 December 2004 after both wrestlers traded low blows and eye-rakes. On 11 December 2004, presented "Dead End" Derek Martin to the crowd as the Pride's own newest member, accepted a Ballbreaker Cage Match challenge from the One-Winged Angel, then interfered in Martin's own no-contest with Angel by chairshotting him off the top rope and joining the beatdown until Alex Kidd ran in. Won the UWF World Heavyweight Championship from Kyle Lee by pinfall (the Before the Fall piledriver) on 28 May 2005 under an Edmond Winston IV charter amendment naming him the automatic number-one contender, aided by Kari Stevens's interference, then blindsided Winston with a Pridebreaker after thanking him; introduced returning UWF original Brett Greene to the Pride beforehand and watched Greene turn on new recruit "Dead End" Derek Martin with a Brett Bomb. Celebrated his own UWF World Heavyweight Championship win on 11 June 2005 with confetti and streamers, framed it as three years of scheming against Edmond Winston IV, then accepted a rematch challenge from the One-Winged Angel for the following week. Introduced the Cornerstones as the Pride's own new tag team on 23 July 2005, then turned on longtime friend Brett Greene mid-main-event with a chair shot and a tandem Pridebreaker/the End combination, allowing Derek Martin to score the pin. Assigned Kari Stevens to mentor his debuting daughter Samantha on 3 August 2005; cut a Brawl From The Mall 6 promo on 6 August 2005 dismissing his own roster and warning Gold Rush challenger Alex Martinez, before UWF president Becky Byers banned the Pride and then every wrestler from ringside and named a special guest referee for the Gold Rush main event. Demanded his own rematch clause be honored on 18 March 2006, was denied an immediate World Heavyweight Championship shot by Becky Byers, and had his own microphone cut after arguing; booked instead for Luck of the Draw 2006.
