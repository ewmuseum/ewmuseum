The Mask

aka Stanley Ipkiss

Won Ultimate Championship Wrestling's North American Title in July 2005 and its World Title three weeks later, then carried both onto PWE when the company closed and spent his first promo there explaining, to a demon who had never heard of it, what UCW had been; the last the record sees of him is a TPG Pride Title Showcase in May 2007, which he lost.
