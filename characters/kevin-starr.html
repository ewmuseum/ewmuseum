Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Kevin Starr was born awesome. I mean, just look at how many wins he has in his record on the UWF website. Simply Awesome! Kevin Starr discovered his awesomeness somewhere around the age of 3, when he first discovered his talent for juggling brownies. After that he took off. Becoming more awesome, and more awesome everyday. Growing up in school, he earned the nickname, Kevin 'Awesome' Starr. That's how awesome he was. He ruled the hallways, strutting by the other kids who would envy him and whisper 'Someday, I wish I could be as awesome as him' Kevin Starr was a natural at being awesome. So awesome, he called all the non awesome kids, emosewa, which is awesome backwards. Kevin Starr was in no way a bully though, he would give the emosewa's 3 seconds to empty their pockets full of money before he pounded them. Unlike the others who simply pounded them, THEN asked for their money. Buttholes is what they were. After a few years in school, Kevin 'Awesome' Starr got tired of the bullies beating up the emosewa's. So he got all the emosewa's together and started a Revolution against the bullies of the school... of course at a small price of the emosewa's lunch money. But soon enough, after over 7 minutes of intense training, Kevin led all of 500 emosewa's to the playground after school to face the bullies who were already waiting. The emosewa's had at least 300 kids over the bullies. It was a stare down at first, the bullies standing on one side of the playground, the emosewa's standing on the other, with Kevin out in front. Not a sound was heard, the only movement was that of a ball of tumbleweed rolling across the pavement in between the two armies. Then, Kevin turns to his emosewa's and gave a speech, to hopefully motivate them... 'Listen up! Just look out there... look at THEM! Look at their fat, dirty, brainless heads! For years they have been stealing your lunch money, stealing your homework, throwing you in trash bins, and breaking your pencils. They have gotten the best of you for FAR TOO LONG! It's time to put your foot down and show them that doing all of those things is wrong! That you are not their plaything! That you are all tired of their bullying! Now let's show them that you to have fists of your own! CHAAAAARRRGGGEE!!!' And with that, Kevin 'Awesome' Starr raised his arm, pointing at the bullies as emosewa after emosewa ran past him, charging straight towards the bullies and even though heavily out numbered, running straight toward the emosewa's! After a few seconds of pointless fighting, every single last emosewa lay on the ground in heavy pain, as the bullies walk around the battlefield asking each of the emosewa's for their leftover lunch money. Kevin Starr stands at the edge of the playground and shrugs... well at least he tried. Well anyway, Kevin Starr learned a very important lesson after this. He not only learned that emosewa's can't fight, but that he is not only awesome but... REALLY awesome. Kevin's awesomeness level only went up from there as he graduated school and got into the wrestling business.
