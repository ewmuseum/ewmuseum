Vivica J. Valentine

Vivica J. Valentine, who buried Bryan Mayhem alive at Great American Nightmare 2007 and worked four PRIME shows between 2006 and 2010, held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship twice and the United States Championship twice, was named Wrestler of the Year (2008), Match of the Year (2010), Feud of the Year (2008), was inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame in 2012 and appeared on 127 GCW cards from 9 July 2006 to 17 March 2012.
