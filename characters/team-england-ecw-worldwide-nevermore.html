Team England

The England squad for ECW's Wrestling World Cup in July 2007, announced in June 2007: Steven Regal, released from his Federation contract, introduces himself as its active team captain and recruits Paul Burchill, who agrees, and the developmental talents Nigel McGuinness and Doug Williams. Williams and McGuinness come out with steel chairs to even the numbers against Raven's Nest on Revolution, lose to CM Punk and Vito on Blood, Sweat and Beers and, after a no contest on Burchill's match, lose again to Vito and Monsters Inc on the next Revolution. In the World Cup of July 2007 England win the first Pool B match, over India, lose to Mexico twice and to India once (Raven and Punk distract Paul Burchill), and scrape into the quarter-finals one point ahead of India; they beat Team USA when Burchill beats Jay Briscoe, Christopher Daniels and Jerry Lynn after Kurt Angle's punch, beat Mexico when Burchill beats Rey Mysterio Jr. and Eddie Guerrero with a Double C-4, and win the first Wrestling World Cup at Summer Assylum when Burchill, last of the three left, beats Masato Tanaka, Jushin Lyger and Ultimo Dragon; Paul Heyman presents the trophy. Davey Boy Smith is on the squad throughout. Written by AI, 2026
