UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-10-04 – 2006-06-07
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 13 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 3-10-0
Tenure detail
Billed from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing 121 pounds, representing the Bod Squad; entered mimicking the look and old entrance of former friend Erin McCoys. Lost by pinfall (Burn Cycle) in a non-title women's contest, then ambushed McCoys after the match with a kick-start DDT and a double armscissors and legscissors hold. Teamed with Bliss as the Bod Squad on 22 October 2003, again entering in the same borrowed look and calling herself the "New Blonde Bombshell," in a win over Marissa Monet and Sierra Browne. Lost a non-title women's contest to Lolita Love on 17 December 2003, her own Ray of Sunshine submission hold reversed into a rollup for the pin. Married to Tom Landis; lost an intergender tag match with him on 24 March 2004 to Alex Kidd and Alicia Powers when Powers's own Somersault Bulldog assist set up the pinfall on Landis. Attacked Alicia Powers with Brianna Landis after Powers's own win on 25 August 2004, chased off by Tara Smith. Lost to Tara Smith by disqualification on 28 August 2004 after striking her with a tag team title belt in front of the referee, then continued the beatdown with Landis until Alicia Powers ran in. Retained the UWF Women's Tag Team Championship with tag partner Brianna Landis over Team BATTLE*DREAM by disqualification on 8 September 2004, driving a chair into Misaki Ishikawa's spine to save Landis from a spike piledriver. Lost a non-title six-woman tag alongside Brianna Landis and a late-arriving Donna Tetreault to Sierra Browne and Team BATTLE*DREAM on 2 October 2004, tased by Misaki Ishikawa mid-match; Sierra Browne's Truth Superkick finished Landis for the pin. Confronted Commissioner Becky Byers backstage on 27 November 2004 over a referee's own beating at the hands of the Bod Squad's challengers, only for Byers to announce a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship defense against Team BATTLE*DREAM ("Tornado" Tara Smith, Alicia Powers) at Rise to Power. Knocked out Misaki Ishikawa at ringside with the Bod Squad's own championship belt during Brianna Landis's own win over Reona Yoshida on 1 December 2004, then used Ishikawa's own taser on a bystander identified only as Rocko. Lost with tag partner Brianna Landis to "Tornado" Tara Smith and Alicia Powers by pinfall on 25 May 2005, telling Landis beforehand that she had "worked something out" with an unnamed man. Joined Brianna Landis in ambushing Lisa Drake from behind at John DeWolfe's direction on 8 June 2005, then broke up Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith's own match against Drake and Leslie Hernandez, ending it as a no contest. Accompanied John DeWolfe and Brianna Landis to the ring on 22 June 2005 to belittle the women's division, backing DeWolfe's own praise of the Bod Squad before being run off by Lisa Drake, Alicia Powers, and "Tornado" Tara Smith. Lost to Alicia Powers by disqualification on 6 July 2005 after Brianna Landis interfered twice, having told husband Tom Landis beforehand that she meant to end Alicia's own wrestling career. Ran in with Bod Squad partner Brianna Landis during the Wind Walkers' own women's tag match on 20 July 2005, attacking Tara Smith before Lisa Drake, Arielle Starr, and Leslie Hernandez helped clear the ring. Teamed with Brianna Landis and John DeWolfe to beat Lisa Drake and the Wind Walkers by pinfall on 6 August 2005. Lost to Fantasy Girl by pinfall (a second-rope fisherman buster) on 7 June 2006, then was attacked further after the bell -- a top-rope double moonsault kneedrop, a Death Valley Driver off the ring apron, and a 450 splash onto her own stretcher -- when Fantasy Girl unmasked as her own sister-in-law, Brianna Landis.
