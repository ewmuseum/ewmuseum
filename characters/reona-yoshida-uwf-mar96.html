Tenure detail
Billed from Nagoya, Aichi, Japan, weighing 137 pounds; lost to Angel by pinfall (a reverse Ace Crusher, the Headhunter) after injuring her own shoulder on a missed springboard plancha. Countrywoman Misaki Ishikawa ran in to pull Angel off a post-match submission hold. Teamed with countrywoman Misaki Ishikawa as the newly-billed Team BATTLE*DREAM in a four corners women's tag war on 25 August 2004, hooking a double DDT on Melody and Lolita Love and pinning Melody for the win; earned a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship match against the Bod Squad the following week. Lost a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship challenge alongside Misaki Ishikawa to the champions, the Bod Squad, by disqualification on 8 September 2004 after nearly finishing Brianna Landis with a spike piledriver. Teamed with Misaki Ishikawa and Sierra Browne to beat Donna Tetreault and the Bod Squad in a non-title six-woman tag on 2 October 2004, Sierra Browne's Truth Superkick finishing Brianna Landis for the pin. Co-won a women's three-way dance over Brianna Landis and Tara Smith on 20 October 2004 after she and Smith each locked on a separate submission hold on an unconscious Landis at the same moment, the referee declaring both women winners and awarding each a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship match against the Bod Squad. Lost to Brianna Landis by pinfall (a high moonsault press) on 1 December 2004 after leaving the ring to check on a blindsided Misaki Ishikawa, giving Landis the opening to finish the match. By 11 June 2005 had left the UWF for Joshi Puroresu STARDOM in Japan, ending the Team BATTLE*DREAM pairing; did not appear on the broadcast that announced it.
