Strictly Business

A pair of former tag champions returning from retirement to beat Team Canada at Gold Rush 2004, having staged a wheelchair-bound retirement angle beforehand. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-06-05 – 2004-11-17
Tenure detail
First formal in-ring appearance under this name in this wing: staged a wheelchair-bound retirement announcement, were mocked by Team Canada (Trevor Hextall and Bob Jablonski), then revealed the ruse and beat the team by pinfall, Tucker scoring the fall on Hextall with a top-rope Frog Splash. Teamed with Quinn Brown in a Meltdown main event loss to Tom Landis and Team Canada on 25 August 2004, Tucker pinned after Landis and Trevor Hextall's own Game Misconduct heart-punch combination. Beat the Hands of Death (Serge Annis, Caliban) by pinfall on 2 October 2004, Tucker distracting Caliban with a thrown frozen fish and tripping Annis off a superplex attempt for Sebastian's own cover. Challenged the Privateers for the UWF World Tag Team Championship by pinfall on 6 October 2004, Sebastian pinned off a DDT while Tucker was held down by an illegal grip on his own feet from Jacob Drake.
