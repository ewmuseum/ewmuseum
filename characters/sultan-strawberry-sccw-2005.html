Tenure detail
SCCW's own record makes him half of the tag team Kings of Popsicles with King Blueberry (SCCW bio 3758; 14 July to 1 September 2009), and the name of the team is the whole of the receipt from outside, twice over. Contemporary: in a thread Sean himself opened on 21 September 2009 (PTC forum topic 26810, post 1), another handler's answer on his own Baron von Blackberry (post 3) says the character “was developed as part of the Kings of Popsicles team, but it fell apart pretty quickly because the guy who posted this thread had so little interest in actually pushing his own guy”, and greets him by first name. Ten years on, in the thread “Gut Reactions and trivia” (topic 29032, post 11, 28 June 2019), the handler of the King Blueberry persona answers a question about Sean: “Sean and I had a tag team together. I was King Blueberry, and he was Sultan Strawberry. I think we called ourselves the 'Kings of Popsicles.'” His own SCCW card (the Strawberry Emirate; “The Smoothie Criminal”, “Short Cake Kid”) is a fruit-costume gimmick of exactly the kind the post describes.
Source: SCCW's own backstage database (lot 0159), bio 3620; PTC's own forum, topic 26810 (post 3) and topic 29032 (post 11)
