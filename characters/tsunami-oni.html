Tsunami Oni

aka Lucharesu

Held the Intercontinental and World Heavyweight titles simultaneously, logged the longest single reign in UWF history, won both the Regal Rumble and the inaugural Contest of Champions, earned a 2003 Hall of Fame induction under the Lucharesu banner, swept the 2004 Impies for Wrestler of the Year and Best Face, and was accompanied through most of it by his own valet and on-screen partner, Shiatsu — later Kitsune Oni. Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Tsunami Oni... When he burst on the scene, he was a masked supervillain determined to take over the world with his foul-mouthed dominatrix sidekick, Shiatsu. While he never did take over the world (although he did steal the launch codes to America's nukes when Dubya left them in a White House bathroom, subsequently using one to blow up the Loch Ness Monster), he did manage to capture the TV, Intercontinental, and World titles (the last two he held simultaneously for a brief time before 'losing' the IC belt in a Paper, Rock, Scissors Match against his then-ally, the Prodigy) as well as winning the notorious Contest of Champions. Some career highlights include... 1. Having PeTA activists in bunny suits assault Seymour Almasy 2. Beating Digital Mortality with an aluminum baseball bat while singing 'Take Me Out to the Ballgame.' 3. The Paddle on the Pole match between Shiatsu and the Dominatrix. 4. FAT MURPHY~! and the Table, Ladders, and Cats match. 5. The Evilutionary War and the subsequent ladder match against Unforgiven. 6. Using ShadowBlade's head as a tee after running him over with a golf cart. 7. The formation of the Network of Operatives for the Global Overthrow and Obliteration of Democracy, better know simply as N.O.G.O.O.D. 8. The debut of the Flying Monkey Bomb, greatest triple-team move ever, on Moraless (with the assistance of the Local NVA Company.) 9. Creating an imperfect duplicate of himself known as Bizarro Oni. Following Live and Loud in 2003 (in which he won the right to be called Greatest Cruiserweight Ever), Oni unmasked and vanished. He recently returned with a new look and slightly changed attitude, but still a success, winning the fifth Regal Rumble at Terror in Tokyo. Now more of an anti-hero that a supervillain, he allied with Unforgiven, Slasher, and Misfit in the drowningPOOL. Although the dP faded, Tsunami Oni's success did not and he has recently won the UWF World championship for a second time. Strengths - Quite possibly the UWF's greatest high-flyer. Supremely confident and daring. Cunning strategist and master at rule-bending (not to mention rule-breaking). Weaknesses - Usually gives up size and strength to his opponents. High-risk moves have resulted in multiple knee surgeries. Past betrayals have made him an untrustworthy ally.
