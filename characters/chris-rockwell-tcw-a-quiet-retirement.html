Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Chris Rockwell

aka Lobster Warrior

'The Pride of New York', a former masked wrestler of seven years (the Lobster Warrior) who bursts into TCW in June 2008 by beating Ricky Dale Johnson with the Furusawa Armbar, sides with Wolf Hawkins, challenges Koshiro Ino for the International Championship and beats him by disqualification at Excessive Force; the author's own note says he is the one piece of characterisation he takes outright from the Philly Pro diary, and in August 2008 beats Art Reed, Johnny Bloodstone and Rocky Golden, then is kept off the card at Hotter Than Hell by Mayhem Midden for refusing Koshiro Ino a rematch and loses a tag match with Frankie Perez, and in September 2008 draws with Sam Keith for the Hard Hitting Championship, beats Joey Minnesota, loses a three-way match to Troy Tornado and, at Destructive Energy, beats Koshiro Ino after feinting at Baroness Emily, and in October 2008 tries to help Frankie Perez against Ricky Dale Johnson, goes to a double disqualification with Sam Keith, beats Eddie Peak and Sam Keith with Tyson Baine, is eliminated from the battle royal and, at Crunch Time, loses to Johnson. In November 2008 he beats Matthew Keith, hits Johnny Bloodstone with brass knuckles, beats Danny Fonzarelli with the Furusawa Armbar, wins a six-man match with Frankie Perez and Sean Deeley and another with Tommy Cornell & Wolf Hawkins, challenges Remo Richardson and Phil Vibert and at A Little South Of Sanity loses to Johnny Bloodstone for the Canadian Belt, then brawls with Remo. In December 2008 he introduces Lauren Easter, is chosen for the six-man match for the third place in Troy Tornado's defence and loses it, wins two intergender tags with Easter, attacks Horatio Dangerous on commentary and is sent home by Mayhem Midden, interferes in the cage match at Psycho Circus and is mocked by Phil Vibert.
