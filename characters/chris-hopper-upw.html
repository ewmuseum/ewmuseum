Tenure detail
Named 13 December 2016 as the UPW minority owner who purchased a promotion called GEW (its library, roster and intellectual property, minus its women's division, folding into UPW — Windham Brody's own prior GEW Television Championship among the assets that followed). Thomas Grissom later credits him with originally signing Rick Styles "at a considerable amount of money." Appeared live at the Survival preview desk alongside the regular announce team, discussing the Ultimate Rumble field and UPW's own move away from TTW's old double-elimination Rumble format; recalled sharing old UEW Rumble experiences with Robert Smith.
