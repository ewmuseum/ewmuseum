Reaper

aka Jay Darkness, The Angel of Death

A tall, white-eyed, black-robed wrestler with long black hair streaked blood red and a scythe, who first walks into the ring on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 2, December 2007 as the Masked Man's 'associate' and 'The Angel of Death' (the author tells a reader he is the former Jay Darkness, repackaged, in a part first meant for Hell's Bouncer). He knocks out Big Cat Brandon, Akima and Jacob Jett, takes Devine by the throat on the Monday of Week 3, loses a dark match to Frankie Perez the same night, beats Bulldozer Brandon Smith in a dark match on the Thursday of Week 3 with Fade To Black, and loses to Devine on the Monday of Week 4 in 9:35.
