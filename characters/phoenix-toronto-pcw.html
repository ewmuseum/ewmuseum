Tenure detail
PCW's own cards bill him from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, entering to Linkin Park's "One Step Closer" (Friday Fusion 20, 2 February 2001, and on), finishing with Into the Ashes and setting it up with the Cry for Help. He wrestled for Asylum beside Miyagawa through February to April 2001 (Sabotage 1, 11 March 2001: "Miyagawa and Phoenix representing Asylum"), was "the former Asylum member" by Turmoil 31 (1 May 2001), fought for PCW2 at Gold and Glory (10 June 2001) and turned on Miyagawa at Back to Old School (16 September 2001). PCW's database books his appearances on bio 708, the card that carries Jay Phoenix's own fields; his own card, bio 10733 (a PCW2 card), books none. Turmoil 62 (26 December 2001) and Friday Fusion 67 (11 January 2002) are his by a presumed reading: Turmoil 62 names Miyagawa as "Phoenix's former mentor, and the man Phoenix tried to forcefully retire", and on Fusion 67 he sides with Clyde's crew against Asylum.
