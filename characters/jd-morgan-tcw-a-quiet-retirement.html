JD Morgan

A former three-time DaVE Extreme Champion who debuts in August 2008, sponsors the British Lions and UK Dragon, and loses to Ricky Dale Johnson and Art Reed. In September 2008 he loses to Rocky Golden and to Aaron Andrews. In November 2008 he loses to Sam Keith's Proton Lock in a wrestling exhibition, then to the New Wave with American Buffalo. In December 2008 he is beaten by Koshiro Ino's Kobra's Bite on Badge of Honor. Written by AI, 2026
