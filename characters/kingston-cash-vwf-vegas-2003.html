Tenure detail
Appears unnamed in two earlier backstage segments, as a shadowy figure dealing with Blackbird and Lark Fenriz, before his on-air introduction. Confirms Sebastian as Sonicman's new manager and enters Sonicman in the Dog Days tournament as a singles competitor. Scratches champion Lark Fenriz from his own match, bars Mark McQueen from the tag bracket over an unspecified contractual problem, then has Marcus O'Malley blindfolded and handcuffed for the tag final before ordering him into a handicap match against Sonicman and Sebastian; also announces Kevin Blair's VWF 24 Hour Championship defense against Flash as that week's elimination match, all on Dog Days 2 (22 June 2004). Chokeslammed by The Outlaw Morris Bass on Dog Day Three (1 July 2004) after warning him off violence, and removed from the arena on a stretcher; the night's tag and singles elimination matches are decided by random draw in his absence. Rebrands the broadcast Wrestle Style and strips Kevin Blair of recognition as 24 Hour Champion on Wrestle Style V.1; beats Kagedo in their Company vs. Suspension match on Wrestle Style V.2 after Kagedo's own knee gives out, suspending him six months without pay, then announces a random-draw VWF Heavyweight Title tournament; presides over the tournament's opening round on Wrestle Style V.3 and reveals a contract stipulation releasing both Rodeo Boys from the VWF after Billy Bones's title match loss. Rules that a double count-out and a title-match time-limit draw both leave every wrestler in the Heavyweight tournament on Wrestle Style V.4 (10 September 2004), then refuses to recognize Adam Jennings as new 24 Hour Champion despite his own battle royal win, unless Jennings takes the belt from Kevin Blair by force. Hires G-Gangsta as his own personal bodyguard against Jennings on Wrestle Style V.5 (17 September 2004). Announces a career-stakes ladder match between Iyasu Saku and Kevin Simmons for Vegas Violence on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), books Fast Eddie Cutlass's mini-rumble win as a Submission title shot, and hosts the Fenriz-Equalizer contract signing that Jason Storm crashes. Hosts Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004): sets up Kevin Simmons's own career-stakes ladder match with a rigged choice of envelopes, then has him beaten down by Sebastian, G-Gangsta and Robbie Caldwell before the Society of Doom makes the save; accepts lawyer RAR's offer to reinstate the Rodeo Boys, who immediately beat down the new VWF Tag Team Champions. Suspends Flash and books him against Lark Fenriz on March To Hell (21 October 2004), then overrules Classy Joey Boy's own 25% ownership stake by asserting his own 75% control. Turns Mark McQueen's title defense into a handicap match by adding Billy Bones on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), then announces a twelve-man, two-story Rage in the Cage with the final three spots decided by battle royal. Makes the Number One Contenders match a weapons match for the Rodeo Boys alone at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004), then applauds Trinity's own closing celebration. Proposes a tag-title handicap-match stipulation for Mark McQueen against The Salvation on Holiday Havoc I (19 November 2004), refused on the spot. Agrees to recognize Gods Tears's own self-arranged VWF Hardcore Championship invitational and suspends Trinity from the VWF through the end of the year on Holiday Havoc II (3 December 2004), re-booking their own scheduled six-man tag as a triple threat between Lark Fenriz, Fast Eddie Cutlass and Classy Joey Boy. Sees his own authority publicly overridden by newly arrived VWF guardian John Tyler on New Years Kick Off (19 January 2005). Confronts Classy Joey Boy and John Tyler backstage over the Road To Rhyolite (27 January 2005) main event booking, then dons a referee's shirt to work a stretch of the VWF Vegas Heavyweight Championship match. Opens Road To Rhyolite II (7 February 2005) with a warning to Yancy Kilpatrick's own ownership group, John Tyler and Classy Joey Boy, sets Classy Joey Boy and Kagedo to open the Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet, and signs an impostor using the ring name Scott Starring to a VWF contract. Opens Ghost Town Blues (17 February 2005) boasting of the show's own record buy rate, dismisses Henry Fish's own demand for a Scott Starring title shot by sending out Kagedo, works a guest angle into the Wheel Of Chaos main event through his own newly revealed brother Terrance Cash, and vows afterward that Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora will never hold a VWF title again while he owns 75% of the company. Books a five-way elimination match for St Patricks Day Massacre at March To Madness (2 March 2005), then is confronted by John Tyler on air, who announces the IRS has seized the VWF over misappropriated talent funds diverted to an offshore account bearing Kingston Cash's own name; Cash is fired and escorted out of the Orleans, his own 75% ownership stake seized pending a future auction.
