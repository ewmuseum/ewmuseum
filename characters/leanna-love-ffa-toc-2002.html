Tenure detail
Teamed with sister Lolita Love and Comedy against the Coven (Tigress, Leslie Hernandez, and Angel); pinned by Leslie Hernandez's top-rope Santa Maria. Billed with Lolita as the reigning UWF Women's Tag Team Champions, though the title itself was not on the line in this three-on-three match. Lost a non-title singles match to Lisa Drake by rollup on 15 November 2003 after Tara Marshall ran in from the crowd and knocked out cornerwoman Lolita Love with the ring steps. Teamed with sister Lolita Love as the Love Sisters in a losing four corners women's tag war on 25 August 2004 against Melody and Comedy, the debuting Team BATTLE*DREAM, and Extreme Measures. Lost to "Mockingbird" Nina Grimsson by pinfall (the Dragon Spike) in a lumberjill match on 20 October 2004, ending the pair's own long friendship-turned-rivalry. Was beaten down backstage by Moira Faith and Myra Benedict ahead of her own Women's Tag Team Championship challenge on 11 June 2005, refused medical treatment, and lost the title match anyway alongside sister Lolita. Confronted younger sister Lolita on 6 July 2005 over letting the Kindred escape with information on their kidnapped cat's captor, warning her against getting close to Moira Faith alone. Told Moe Owens that sister Lolita would be sidelined by the Kindred's own torture on 3 August 2005; fought Angel and the Kindred to a no-contest alongside Sonya Benedict on 6 August 2005, then captured and pistol-whipped Moira Faith with brass knuckles before sister Lolita talked her out of using a katana on her.
