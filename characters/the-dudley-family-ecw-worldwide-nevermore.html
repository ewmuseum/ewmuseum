The Dudley Family

The 'first family of Dudleyville': Bubba Ray and D-Von Dudley, who introduce their 'second generation' on Revolution in July 2007, Big Dick Dudley Jr. and Daizy Dudley, the 'nephew' and 'niece' of the Dudley Boyz and children of the missing Big Dick Dudley, and at Summer Assylum a third, Ultimo Dudley II. With the second generation at ringside the Dudleys win the number one contendership, lose the Tables Match at Summer Assylum to the Whole F**kin' Show, rename the old World Tag Team belts the Dudleyville Hardcore Titles, and defend them against the bWo, Balls & Axl and, on the previews, Los Guerreros and the Hardcore Innovators. In August 2007 the family (Bubba Ray, D-Von, Daizy, Big Dick Dudley Jr. and Ultimo Dudley II) interfere in the Dudleyz's title defences and lose a match to the Hardcore Innovators on Blood, Sweat and Beers when Ultimo Dudley II is pinned after a Double DDT; at Heatwave they tell the Innovators in a backstage promo the story of Big Daddy Dudley's grizzly bear and the table, before the Dudleyz lose the belts. In September they beat the Briscoe Boys, the F.B.I. and the Headhunters on F' The Mainstream and lose to the bWo, Daizy beats Traci in a catfight match when Big Dick Jr. hits her with a Total Penetration Chokebomb, Kelly Thomaselli beats Daizy to qualify for the Lioness' Den, and an unnamed Dudley, Slyk Tayshaun Dudley, a son of Big Dick Dudley, attacks the family with a chair and a flagpole, saying D-Von knows a secret that made his father leave, until the family beat him and put him through a table. At Anarchy Rulz in September 2007 the family run in on the Champions versus Champions match and carry off both sets of belts. In October they ask Paul Heyman for a Flaming Tables Match, Big Dick Jr. and Ultimo Dudley II lose to the Whole F**kin' Show and to the Hardcore Innovators (Dreamer's DDT on Ultimo Dudley II), Big Dick Jr. is pinned by Shawn Michaels and by Slyk Tayshaun Dudley, who kicks him low after calling out 'is that Big Dick?', and at Guilty As Charged they help the Dudley Boyz win the Three Way Dance before the Innovators leave them laid out backstage and take the belts. In November 2007 Daizy tells the Hardcore Innovators that the belts they carry belong to her uncles, and the family run out to the Innovators after they beat the Thomaselli brothers, until Slyk Tayshaun Dudley, a cousin who calls them a disgrace, chairs Daizy, Big Dick Jr. and Ultimo Dudley II and then Bubba Ray and D-Von, and says that he is entering the International All Action Title Tournament and means to find out the secret his father kept. He beats Scorpio Sky after the Second Generation run in and are chaired, clears the family again when the Second Generation lose to the Innovators with a Double DDT on Revolution, and on the last Revolution before the pay-per-view takes a five-on-one beating from the whole family until the Innovators arrive with Singapore canes. At November To Remember XV Big Dick Jr. costs his cousin Slyk Tayshaun Dudley the International All Action Championship with a Total Penetration, and Daizy and D-Von tell the camera that the family will 'take out the Hardcore Innovators'; Slyk then batters every one of his relatives, even Daizy, with a baseball bat and says that he will find out the secret about his father. On the Saturday show Ultimo Dudley II trips Slyk into Dick Jr.'s Total Penetration through a table, and Dick wins the falls-count-anywhere match. On Revolution on the first Monday of December Slyk interrupts his uncles' title defence with the bat, Dick Jr. blocks it with a chair, and in the restarted Three Way Dance Slyk and Dick Jr. win the Undisputed ECW Tag Team Titles; on Saturday Slyk, in a backstage scene, asks Dick Jr. and Daizy to join him in finding the family secret, with the belts as leverage, and the new champions beat the Thomasellis with Daizy's help before the Original Dudleyz (Bubba Ray, D-Von and Ultimo Dudley II) destroy the brothers.
