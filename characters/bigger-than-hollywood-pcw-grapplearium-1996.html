Bigger Than Hollywood

The promotion's Tag Team Champions who unified the title with DPW's own belts at the Grapplearium in April 1997, later losing and regaining the combined championship by Spring Showdown. In the Longhorn Wrestling Council of 1998 the name was carried by Mark Cutter and B. Astard. Written by AI, 2026
