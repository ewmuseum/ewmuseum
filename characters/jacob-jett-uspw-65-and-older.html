Jacob Jett

Darryl Devine's best friend and former tag partner from Canada, who signs with USPW in July 2007 to stand by him against Seduction's campaign, beats Java, Warlord Agony and Demon Spite, loses a National Championship match to William Hayes, then turns on Devine for Seduction's money and loses to him in August 2007. In October 2007 he loses with Bulldozer Brandon Smith to Brett Hall and Wanda Fish and loses to Fish at A Haunting in America, and is booked into a three-way for the National Championship at Pilgrimage. In October and November 2007 he teams with Art Reed to beat Fish and Giant Oak, loses with Reed to Fish and Phlex, loses the National Championship three-way at Pilgrimage, and is unmasked there as the fake Masked Man who celebrates with Triple D and Jillefski. In late November and December 2007 he beats Peter Valentine, loses to Umaga and is thrown over the top rope by Reaper. Written by AI, 2026
