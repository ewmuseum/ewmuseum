Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Steve Flash

A veteran past forty-five, whom the narrator introduces at Back To School in September 2010 as the challenger the Board of Directors has chosen for Jay Chord; he schools the champion for a quarter of an hour before Jay's Cradle Piledriver beats him. He beats Toxin with the Flash Bang at Fall Of The King, reverses Hugh de Aske's Cutthroat Driver into a Flash Bang at Where It All Begins Again in October, and goes to a time-limit draw with Primus Allen at Eleventh Hour in November. The narrator's roster overview says he will use Flash as "awesome, get little or no mic time, and be worshipped by everyone". He beats Marc Speed in a qualifier at Season's Beatings in December 2010, teams with Aaron Andrews against Jay Chord and Primus Allen at End Of Days and loses, and loses a quarter-final to Firebird at the Invitational on 21st January 2011. He beats Robert Thomas at Third Time's The Charm and loses to Jay Chord at March Madness. The narrator wants to do more with him than good matches. He beats Thrash at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 and, asked by Jean Cattley to keep The Firm's legacy going, gathers Chance Fortune, Randy Bumfhole and, as the fourth man, Kid Arachnid in the old Firm's dressing room to take on the Second Sons and The Fallen. He loses with Arachnid to Erik Strong and Jay Chord on 12th May and to JD Morgan at Mother, May I? in the narrator's B-, beats Casey Valentine at High Noon and Summer Showdown and Antonio at Hotter Than Hot, wins with Randy Bumfhole at Beach Blast and introduces his "nephew" Ernest Youngman. At 45 he loses a Heavyweight title challenge to Aaron Andrews at Summernova and a three-way for the Traditional title at Back To School, and with Fortune and Bumfhole promises to be the narrator's ears and eyes in the locker room. He beats Jared Johnson at All The Leaves Are Brown on 12th October 2011 and Roderick Remus at Where It All Begins Again, and loses to Aaron Andrews's sleeper hold for the Heavyweight title at Remember November on 26th November; the same night he tells the narrator that, at forty-five and a grandfather, he is leaving for the SWF for the money.
