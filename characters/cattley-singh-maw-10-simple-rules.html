Cattley & Singh

aka Cattley and Singh, Singh and Cattley

Jean Cattley and Kashmir Singh, put together on 12th January 2011 at New Year, Old Rivalries: Smith & Singh have earned a Tag Team title shot, the Canadian Blondes have injured Brandon Smith, and Singh, noting that the contract named no individuals, brings in Cattley as his partner. They beat the Blondes for the belts, Cattley pinning Flash Savage with the Mood Swing, and keep them against Antonio & Raphael at Leap Into The Unknown in February, the Blondes at Third Time's The Charm in March and Thrash and Firebird at April Showers; in April 2011 Cattley tells the narrator and Rip Chord that he is leaving for the SWF. The diary's own title records list the reign as "Cattley & Singh January 2011 3". They lose the Tag Team titles to the Canadian Blondes at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011, Oscar Golden pinning Cattley; Casey Valentine and Antonio attack them and Erik Strong makes his return, and Cattley then loses a Loser Leaves Town match to Valentine and leaves MAW.
