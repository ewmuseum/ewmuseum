Jay

aka Jacob Stranton

The diary's first-person narrator, a man who bought the head booker's job at an auction for one dollar and spends the first months trying to find a replacement so he can be fired. On screen he is Jacob Stranton, a manager brought in by the New Talent Initiative, who manages Umaga and leaves a retirement-home pamphlet on every wrestler they beat down. In November 2007 the narrator, told by Sam Strong that he will be paid ten dollars for an A show, gets it for Pilgrimage and learns his cheque has gone to his apartment; he is made to promise Giant Redwood screen time. On screen, as Stranton, he is put into intensive care by Eddie Peak and misses Pilgrimage. In late November and December 2007 the narrator writes the year-in-the-job entry (he has made $10 and 'stolen' $102.35), jokes about his girlfriend and the dog's house, and in an out-of-character note moves the diary's real-time anniversary and plans for the Winter Epiphany year-end vote. On screen, as Stranton, he supports Umaga and throws Peter Valentine out of his locker room. Written by AI, 2026
