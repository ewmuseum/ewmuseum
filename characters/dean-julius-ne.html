Character · New ERA of Wrestling (2004)

Dean Julius

A colour commentator who sat in on New ERA of Wrestling's RAUCOUS, took the RAPTURE colour seat, and moved permanently to the RAUCOUS desk when RAPTURE was cancelled. Written by AI, 2026

Tenures

New ERA of Wrestling (2004)

2005-01-29 – 2006-12-31
Tenure detail
The broadcast's own dialogue on this show already anticipates a recurring RAPTURE seat for Julius; this tenure records only the one confirmed appearance on this show.
Source: New ERA of Wrestling's own RAUCOUS broadcast (29 January 2005): sits in for one match, Cameron Cruise vs. Mr. Entertainment, while Tom Gheorghe calls the rest of the show alone and Nick Jive is off-air; on-air billed by Gheorghe as 'future RAPTURE color commentator, Dean Julius.' He takes the colour seat for a full RAPTURE on 28 May 2005, the broadcast making it Nick Jive's last night on the show. He returns on the 19 September 2005 RAUCOUS and joins the desk full time, telling Gheorghe and Jive on air that he has "a two year contract to announce" and that with RAPTURE off the air he is moving to RAUCOUS; he calls that whole show apart from one match he walks out on. New ERA's own 2006 broadcasts carry the character further: RAUCOUS of 3 February 2006, 31 December 2006; RAPTURE of 3 July 2006, 22 July 2006, 17 December 2006; BattleBRAWL 2 of 19 September 2006.
