Tessa Windsor

Tessa Windsor, a two-time XCWF Women's Champion in 2002 and a Boston-billed high-flyer, held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship once, the United States Championship once and the Cruiserweight Championship once, was named Feud of the Year (2010) and appeared on 92 GCW cards from 9 July 2006 to 17 April 2011.
