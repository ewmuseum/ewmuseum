Tenure detail
Reappears alive at the close of the Ghost Town Blues Submission Championship match, presumed dead since a prior in-fiction plane crash; does not compete. Released from his VWF contract before Firestorm (26 Feb 2004) to search for his missing wife, and does not appear on that broadcast. Returns during the seven-man mini rumble's own chaos on Wrestle Style V.6 (29 September 2004), part of the interference that surrounds a black-masked man's partial unmasking as Roadkill Rick. Walks toward the ring during the chaotic post-show brawl on March To Hell II (28 October 2004) and is speared by a returning Phoenix Adam Lazarus before reaching it.
