Team Essington

A masked luchador and a big man from West Virginia, debuting under the management of Essington Sports Inc with wins over Javier King and Matt Payne, over Dudes of Destruction and a student pairing in a three-way, and over the reunited Rodeo Boys. Lost a gauntlet fall to Team Brittania at Fear Is Coming III (their own final match together); released from Essington Sports Inc soon after and replaced under the same team name by a wholly new pairing, a seven-foot Mr. Smith and a four-foot Mr. Jones, who lost a debut match to the Rodeo Boys by disqualification at Phear Phantastic 2005. A masked third man, Mr. Jefferson, debuted on Setting Sun I and helped Smith beat Kenny Kidd; the now three-man team, fielding Smith and Jefferson, then teamed with Purgatory to beat a patched-together Rodeo Boys/Tap Out City side by submission on Christmas Comes Early. Fielded Mr. Jefferson, Mr. Smith and Mr. Jones in a chaotic triple threat against the Rodeo Boys and Purgatory on New Years Eve Of Destruction, then beat the Rodeo Boys outright on Winter War I after the referee missed a Rodeo Boys tag. Beat a debuting masked lucha team, Los Diablos, on Winter War II, then ran in as a four-man unit, fielding a newly introduced fourth member, Mr. Sal, to break up a Rodeo Boys/Team Brittania match on Winter War III and demand a VXW Tag Team Championship shot.
