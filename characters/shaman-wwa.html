Shaman

A desert-born powerhouse who held the WWA World Heavyweight Championship twice, held the GCW United States Championship twice, the HDTV Championship once, the Hardcore Championship once and the Vanguard Championship once, was named Radio Personality of the Year (2009) and appeared on 116 GCW cards from 25 September 2006 to 17 March 2012.
