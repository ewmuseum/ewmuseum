Tenure detail
Billed from London, England, one of the Privateers with brother Edward Drake and sister "British Bad Girl" Lisa Drake; tripped Erik Grimsson as he tried to break up his brother's pinfall over Chad Grimsson. Also interviewed backstage this same night alongside Edward, sparring with Allison Ivey over the Youngbloods and the World Tag Team Championship picture. Entered the Tower of Doom on 15 November 2003 alongside Team Canada, briefly brawling with teammate Bob Jablonski mid-match; on the losing side as the Sons of Cacaphony escaped the cage first and won the UWF North American Tag Team Championship. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match as the last man standing on Edmond Winston IV's own side, surviving a long string of near-falls from both Keening brothers before Alex Extreme and a tagged-in Jason Keening finished him with a Hart Attack combination. Announced with Edward on 19 May 2004 that the brothers had bought minority shares of the UWF alongside Michael Augustine and Allison Ivey. Teamed with Winston and Jamie Underwood against Michael Augustine and Alex Extreme on 15 May 2004, turning on Winston mid-match to side with Extreme before Augustine turned on Extreme in turn; no fall was ever scored. Retained the UWF World Tag Team Championship with brother Edward in a Gold Rush triple threat on 5 June 2004, pinning Michael Keening of the Ghost Dancers after the Fraternity Boys' triple Conchairto took the Hands of Death's Serge Annis out of the match. Lost a mismatched partners tag match alongside Todd Franklin to Ravnos Romani and a substituting Alex Extreme by pinfall on 18 September 2004. Retained the UWF World Tag Team Championship with brother Edward over Strictly Business (Andrew Tucker, Mike Sebastian) by pinfall on 6 October 2004, illegally holding down Tucker's own feet to protect Edward's own cover on Sebastian. Berated sister Lisa for leaving the family to team with Nina Grimsson on 27 November 2004, then clotheslined and beat her down when she refused to rejoin the Youngbloods, joined by Allison Ivey; helped announce a UWF World Tag Team Championship defense against the Sons of Cacaphony for Rise to Power. Retained the UWF World Tag Team Championship with brother Edward over Fury by pinfall (a flying kneedrop) on 1 December 2004. On 11 December 2004, defended his own attack on sister Lisa to a still-upset Edward backstage, then was present when Becky Byers took the Youngbloods' own ownership shares hostage against the Privateers' own upcoming title defense.
