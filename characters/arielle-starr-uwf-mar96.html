Tenure detail
Normally teams as one of the Sun Belt Angels; lost to Donna Tetreault by pinfall (Domination Day suplex) on 10 March 2004 after Myra Benedict's ringside knife distracted her from a Starrstruck attempt. Teamed with Tesla St. James and Fantasy Girl on 21 March 2004 in a losing six-woman tag against the Misfits (Marissa Monet, Sierra Browne, and Dalbello Rage), describing the match beforehand as payback for years of run-ins with all three. Lost to Alicia Powers on 19 May 2004 in Powers's own singles debut, her leg buckling mid-suplex and leaving her open to a Michinoku Driver; myra Benedict threw a knife into her dressing-room door earlier that night. Beat Myra Benedict by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, countering a second Shadow's Bane kick with the Bedazzler enzuigiri before finishing with a top-rope Skytwister Press. A UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World challenge against champion Donna Tetreault on 13 November 2004 ended without a finish after Sierra Browne, Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith ran in on Tetreault's behalf; Starr was struck by a chain-loaded glove shot meant for the fleeing champion. Lost a UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship challenge to Sierra Browne by pinfall (a top-rope moonsault) on 28 May 2005. Ran down to help clear the ring of the Bod Squad during the Wind Walkers' own women's tag match on 20 July 2005. Confronted Kari Stevens backstage over an attack on Stephanie Harper on 3 August 2005, was blindsided by Amazing Grace, and was saved by Leslie Hernandez, the two agreeing to team against Stevens and Grace at Gold Rush.
