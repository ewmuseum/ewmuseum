Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Sharp

aka Sammy The Shark, Greg Ford

The silver-clad one of three masked newcomers who attack Fumihiro Ota, Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh at Mother, May I? in May 2010; he loses to Smith's Backdrop Driver in his first match and is on the card for Summer Showdown with Ricochet. He loses to Kashmir Singh's Bombay Duck at Fall Of The King in September 2010 and again in a rematch at Where It All Begins Again in October. At Eleventh Hour in November Firebird has him unmask as Greg Ford, known to some as Sammy The Shark, a gambler who "gambled everything, and won". As Greg Ford, one half of Ford & Speed, he loses a qualifier to the Masked Patriot at Season's Beatings in December 2010; he beats Matt Hocking in February 2011 and Kashmir Singh in March, loses to Brandon Smith and leaves NYCW. As Greg Ford he beats Brandon Smith on 4th May and Amazing Fire Fly on 11th May, loses to Kid Arachnid on 25th April, and takes part in Ford & Speed's wins over Stevie Grayson and Thomas Morgan, Smith & Singh and the West Texas Peacekeepers. As Ford & Speed he pins Amazing Fire Fly with Fallen Chances at All Or Nothing and beats the West Texas Peacekeepers at March Madness, and loses to Kashmir Singh at Great Lakes Madness. As Ford & Speed in the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he and Ricochet lose the three-way at High Noon on 5th June and beat KC Glenn and Bradford Peverell at South East Madness on 9th June, the West Texas Peacekeepers at Summernova on 5th August and Smith & Singh at Back To School on 5th September, the narrator calling them talented workers who rarely drop out of the D range.
