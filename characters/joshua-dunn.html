Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Before the UWF Joshua was a Junior Highland Games Champion, and came to the UWF in the early months of the fed.after a short spell of nothing spectacular, Dunn was kidnapped by the Local NVA Company and brainwashed to carry out their evil bidding and thus becoming a communist and never being able to overcome those drugs the NVA used on him. Shortly afterwards He won the shoot title off of Mike montgomery in the first ever written match of the UWF (i think). After getting screwed out of Stable wars for the first time, Dunn didn't do much, appart from a feud with kevin starr who held the THC title at the time, in a title vs. title match up, Kevign ended up the shot champion and Dunn the THC Champion and after a spell of time i don't remember that well (including trying to turn BBB against state trooper roger rogers, which almost worked) left the fed. Returning in the build up to the next stable wars under the banner of the Prime Alliance (ugh) he joined the side of Digital Mortality again along with Jamie Shock and Ruff Ryder, getting screwed out of his second consecutive Stable Wars. After that time Dunn went on to win the IC title for the first time in a ladder cage match against Kevin Starr and Mike Montgomery inserting himself in the match by kidnapping Starr's nephew. Going to prison for that, he was leater released by Mike Montgomery, he joined him all that time ago to become the Players Club. soon, somehow, he came across Digital Mortality's woman who had left him some time ago, inraging his former partner and creating a bitter feud between the two escalating into a fight for the IC title which Digital Won. Not long afterwards, using Justine as leverage, Joshua entered against Digital Mortality for the IC title and won, and sending digital out of the fed for a few moths, apparantly never to be seen again. No one ever took the IC title off of Dunn in the next 140 days, not even the once great Seymour Almasy, Which Dunn won in the Mount Gagazet II deatmatch. After Beating Almasy in his own stipulation, Dunn then vacated the title in search of better things. The Players Club lay dormant for a while, as with Mike Montgomery gone, no one knowing where, He was left in charge of the Club, and a newer form of the Prime Alliance reformed and started their campaign to be the best damn stable in the UWF. After Recruiting Ezra, the long forgotten Players Club was thrust back into the lime light as the only teaming able to combat the Prime Alliance. With Mike returned and Joshua at the helm, They waged a war agains thte Prime Alliance which finalised on one SAS with a three part main event, seeing the Players Club white wash the Prime alliance 3-0, and the disgraced Jamie Shock left the federation. After a spell of having no competition what so ever, holdng the IC title and Tag title's the players Club really did reign supreme, until the return of Digital Mortality and Moraless who swore they would dismantle the UWF and rebuild it again. Not going to allow this to happen, a new battle was waged, this time between the Players Club and the Ressurection. losing all their collective gold, and gaining the World Title, thanks to Mike Montgomery, the Players Club seemed in trouble, especially when Resurrection member #3 was introduced as Bondage the Sex Fiend. Dunn brought it upon himself to disgrace the Resurrection and stop their gloating by way of the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet, a Hardcore Match, followed by a submissions match, followed by a last man standing match, against the entire Resurection. Only a draw was managed against the Scotsman as he won 1, lost one, and drew one. This tit for tat escalated into Stable Wars VI, where both the Resurrection and The Players Club made it to the Finals, Digital, Moraless, Bondage the Sex Fiend and Malcolm Kenedy, against Joshua Dunn, Ezra Kazee Kevin Starr and Nick Persia. Of course the wild card of the match was Richard Ray Vaugn's Super Squadron (or whatever they were called) and this came down to just Digitla Mortality and Joshua Dunn. Dunn finally managing to over come past difficulties and beat Digital to win his first Stable Wars (and screwing Digital out of his third). His victory stunted as the KnightMare Brigade stormed in on his celebrations and publicly humiliated him, shaving off all his hair and gluing a mop to his head. Dunn vowed revenge against them after having the mop surgically removed from his head, leaving it horribly scarred, and no he volutarily wears head bandages to keep it out of sight of the public.
