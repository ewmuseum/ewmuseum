THE LINE: a uewf tenure is warranted where a held document BILLS THE ENTITY AS THAT FEDERATION'S OWN, or STAGES IT FOR ONE OF THAT FEDERATION'S OWN CHAMPIONSHIPS. Entry to a tournament held at an interfederation event is not by itself enough — the Summit's tournaments were open to all comers and a bracket slot says only that somebody entered. WHAT SETTLES THIS ROW: his own manager announces his withdrawal from the federation in that federation's name — Ace Diamond, interviewed on the WeWF's newsdesk of 15 February 1992: "I have decided to pull my Brimstone Baxter and Cannonball Calhoun out of the UeWF." You cannot be pulled out of a federation you were not in. The same manager's letter of 4 March lists among his stable's credentials that Baxter "finished in the top 5 at the Royal Rumble", which is the Summit's event and would not on its own have earned this tenure.
