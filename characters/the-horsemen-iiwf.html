The Horsemen

Tenure detail
The IIWF's ruling heel stable, led from the Iron Den by Flare with Miss Victoria Secret at his side; the World Heavyweight Champion the Outlaw belongs to it, Brad Kinder (Bodybag) is the muscle, and Jack Haley, the former Green Giant, is unveiled as the fourth Horseman, Blackjack Haley, in the Report of 11 June 1996. The page names the pairing: "ladies and gentlemen I give you the fourth Horseman... Blackjack Haley!!".
