Tenure detail
A former UWF Cruiserweight and Rampage Champion, managed by Tesla St. James; pinned Alex Extreme with the Gone to Texas in a non-title one-on-one bout. Won the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship from Eugene Robinson with a Superfly Splash off the top of a steel cage on 25 October 2003, a stipulation Vice-President Jessica Marshall set specifically to hinder him. Retained the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship by disqualification against Randall Osbourne on 12 November 2003 after Osbourne repeatedly bit his forehead. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match, non-title; rolled up Alex Extreme for an elimination before Edmond Winston IV accidentally struck him in the face with a Diamond Dust meant for Michael Augustine, and Extreme later rolled him up for his own elimination. Jessica Marshall announced afterward that he must defend the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship in a gauntlet against Eugene Robinson, Tragedy, and Victor Frost at Eve of Destruction, after publicly accusing him of attacking President Sarah Reed. Broke up "Dead End" Derek Martin's backstage attack on Tesla St. James on 15 May 2004, agreeing to a Gold Rush street fight against him; teamed with Alex Kidd and Paul Wong against Team Canada and Scott Daniels in a six-man tag that ended without a decision amid a mass brawl, then helped carry an injured Wong to a hospital afterward. Interrupted Michael Augustine's own open-mic promo on 29 May 2004; after Vice-President Jessica Marshall offered him a place in the Gold Rush main event triple threat if he could beat Edmond Winston IV that same night, did exactly that by pinfall (Gone to Texas) with Augustine himself as special guest referee. "Dead End" Derek Martin blindsided him with the End immediately after the bell. Beat "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall (Gone to Texas) in a Gold Rush street fight on 5 June 2004, then won the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World later the same card, pinning champion Michael Augustine with a second Gone to Texas in a triple threat also involving Edmond Winston IV, after the real Alex Extreme exposed an impostor (Job Holly) who had been interfering on Winston's behalf. Escaped the cage first in a losing steel cage main event alongside Alex Kidd against the Hands of Death on 28 August 2004, but could not return in time to stop Serge Annis and Caliban's own Near Death Experience pin on Kidd; fell from a ladder set outside the cage after Annis took away his own steel chair. Retained the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World over Jamie Underwood by disqualification on 18 September 2004 after owner Edmond Winston IV struck him with a steel chair, then superplexed him from the top rope through a steel ladder propped against the ring apron. Returned from a long absence to team with Tesla St. James in a win over Alex Extreme and a substituting Nina Grimsson on 27 November 2004, pinning Extreme with the Gone To Texas; cut a pre-match promo vowing revenge on Edmond Winston IV ahead of their own Rise to Power ladder match for the World Heavyweight Championship. Retained the UWF World Heavyweight Championship over "The Last American Badass" Alex Martinez by pinfall (a surprise inside cradle) on 11 December 2004 in the main event, denying Martinez the title and, with it, Luke Kinsey's own shot at it for Rise to Power. Lost the UWF World Heavyweight Championship to Scott "Hotspot" Daniels by pinfall (the Before the Fall piledriver) on 28 May 2005, an impromptu title defense forced by an Edmond Winston IV charter amendment naming Daniels the automatic number-one contender after Rise to Power; Kari Stevens's own interference tipped the finish. Returned on 28 April 2007 as outside consultant to a newly-formed Metroplex Broadcast Consulting group, counseling Corey Irons on finding his own motivation and reuniting backstage with "Mockingbird" Nina Grimsson; did not wrestle.
