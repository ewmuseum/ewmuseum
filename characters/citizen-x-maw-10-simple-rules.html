Citizen X

An anarchist heel who flattens the Masked Patriot with a cowbell and the Flaming Anarchy; out of character a shy man, he leaves MAW for a development deal in Rhode Island in September 2008. He returns to MAW from the SWF at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011 after three years away with a speech about the lie of democracy and beats Riley McManus with the Flaming Anarchy. Aaron Andrews cuts up his shoes in a prank and is dressed down by the narrator; X teams with Providence at Remember November, loses to Wanda Fish at Season's Beatings and to Kid Arachnid at End Of Days, and on 5th January 2012 loses with Firebird to Smith & Singh, Brandon Smith finally beating him. In the diary's pages from February to April 2012 he beats Wanda Fish and Thea Davis with Raphael at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, loses to Kirk Jameson at All Or Nothing and to the newcomer Donnie J at April Showers on 5th April. In the diary's pages from June to July 2012 he is the third man in the three-way for the All Action title at Summer Showdown on 12th June, betraying Eddie Powell and losing to Donnie J's Death On Miami Beach, hears Firebird's talk of falling until KC Glenn takes Firebird down, and at Beach Blast on 12th July loses to Steve Gumble's Savate Kick after printing anarchist leaflets in which Gumble says "anarchy" is spelled with a "p". The narrator says that his contract runs out shortly and that he will not renew it; the August news says that he has signed with CZCW. Written by AI, 2026
