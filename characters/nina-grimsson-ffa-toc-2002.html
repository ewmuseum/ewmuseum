UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-09-24 – 2005-08-06
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 10 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 4-5-1
Tenure detail
Fought Myra Benedict to a fifteen-minute time-limit draw in a number-one-contender's match, earning a three-way UWF Women's Championship shot against Stephanie Harper the following week. Attacked afterward by Tigress and Leslie Hernandez of Sabbat Justice's Coven. Wrestled that promised three-way title match on 25 October 2003 with open disinterest, making no attempt to prevent Stephanie Harper's pinfall over Myra Benedict. Teamed with Randall Osbourne on 20 December 2003 in a losing intergender tag match against Avanter and Angel; driven down twice by Osbourne's own tag partner Avanter with a full-nelson suplex and a full-nelson backbreaker, she began coughing up blood and the referee stopped the match after Angel applied the Altar of Fire. Taken to a local hospital for observation afterward. Ambushed backstage with a chair by "Twilight Angel" Sonya Benedict on 24 March 2004; beat Benedict by disqualification on 5 May 2004 after Benedict busted her open with a fluorescent light tube, saved by Tesla St. James and Lisa Drake. Teamed with Lisa Drake as Extreme Measures in a losing four corners women's tag war on 25 August 2004 against Melody and Comedy, the Love Sisters, and the debuting Team BATTLE*DREAM, continuing to brawl with Yoshida and Ishikawa after the bell. Teamed with Lisa Drake as Extreme Measures in a losing tag match against Alicia Powers and Tara Smith on 22 September 2004, pinned alongside Drake by Smith's own top-rope Arizona Rattler. Beat Leanna Love by pinfall (the Dragon Spike) in a lumberjill match on 20 October 2004, ending the pair's own long friendship-turned-rivalry. Talked a reluctant Alex Extreme into accepting her as a late substitute for the injured Lisa Drake, then lost alongside him to Kyle Lee and Tesla St. James by pinfall on 27 November 2004. Earlier saved Lisa Drake from a beating by Allison Ivey and Jacob Drake, clearing Ivey from the ring with a string of headbutts and helping carry Lisa out on a makeshift gurney. Told Moe Owens in a backstage interview on 28 May 2005 that she was refocusing on the singles ranks after a non-title win over Sierra Browne in Bangor, and revealed that her own feud with Sonya Benedict, including a real attack on herself, had been an elaborate scheme against Edmond Winston IV and Jessica Marshall. Caught up with Sonya Benedict backstage on 11 June 2005 over drinks, thanking her for the real chairshot she once took to help sell their shared scheme against Edmond Winston IV and Jessica Marshall. Beat "All Night" Wendy Minow by pinfall (a Dragon Spike kick) on 6 July 2005 in a return to singles competition, telling reporter Moe Owens beforehand that she meant to work her way back up the rankings rather than demand a title shot from Sierra Browne. Beat Donna Tetreault by pinfall (a Dragon Spike kick) in a Women's Heavyweight Championship number-one contenders match on 20 July 2005 after the referee restarted the bout on Stephanie Harper's own insistence, ruling Tetreault had used the ropes for leverage on an earlier rollup. Was interviewed by Moe Owens on 6 August 2005 and then attacked backstage by Serge Annis and Caliban of the Hands of Death, in retaliation for her brothers' own actions against the team.
