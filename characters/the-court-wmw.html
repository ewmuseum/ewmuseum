Tenure detail
Revealed at Ides of March when three security guards blocking Sub-Beast's own corner tear off their shirts, Katy Hummer named as its own leader; Jordan Hammer and Adam King wrestle under its own banner at High Stakes. By High Stakes the commentary also describes a "strong bond" tying Dess, Archangel and a newly arrived Volcano to the group, none of whom is recorded here as a member on the strength of that alone; Volcano turns on the group at Blood Factory, revealed as a Black Rider underneath his own Court shirt.
