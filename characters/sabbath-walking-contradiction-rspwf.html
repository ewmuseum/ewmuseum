UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-06-05 – 2006-02-14
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 11 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 3-7-0, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Made his own first documented UWF appearance in the held record at Gold Rush 2004 (his own dialogue frames it as a return to the promotion after a year-plus away), losing a no-disqualification match to Jamie Underwood over custody of his own children, pinned after his own son, Alex Price, turned on him with a low blow and held down the cover. Lost to "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall (The End) on 25 August 2004 after a missed shooting star press; his wife, Sayaka, accompanied him. Won a non-title three-way dance over Papa Legba and Quinn Brown on 2 October 2004 with the Black Zero reverse hurricanrana, spurred into a rally by Jessica Marshall's own ringside taunting; shook Brown's hand after the bell. Teamed with Alex Kidd in a losing effort against Jamie Underwood and "Dead End" Derek Martin by pinfall on 30 October 2004, pinned after Underwood countered his own Coffin Breaker II attempt; beaten down with steel chairs afterward alongside Kidd, his wife Sayaka struck with a chair as well. A UWF Rampage Championship challenge to champion "Showtime" Rick Marley ended in a no-contest on 27 November 2004 after Jamie Underwood interfered with a steel chair, busting Sabbath open and working over his own knee brace; accepted Underwood's own Last Man Standing challenge for Rise to Power afterward, adding a stipulation of his own: beat Underwood and Sonya Benedict is reinstated. Appeared in the ring with a Singapore cane during Jamie Underwood's own match on 1 December 2004 without touching him, rattling Underwood before departing. On 11 December 2004, cut a promo on a Cleveland beach warning Edmond Winston IV of "two early Christmas presents," then beat him by pinfall (a Black Tie Suplex through a table) in a brutal Hardcore Rules match that saw both men driven through tables, a pane of glass, and a section of hockey boards. Lost the UWF Rampage Championship challenge to Marcus Nuit by pinfall on 28 May 2005 after Nuit reversed his own Coffin Breaker into a cradle, then called out Victor Frost's own "Graphic Violence" tour as self-pity, refused a fight in return. Joined the broadcast booth as a guest commentator on 11 June 2005 for Victor Frost's own match against Alex Martinez. Ran in during Marcus Nuit's own Unified Television Championship defense against Victor Frost on 6 July 2005, attacking Frost and causing the disqualification after Frost dismissed him backstage earlier as no threat. Competed in a fatal four-way for the North American Heavyweight Championship on 20 July 2005, pinned along with Rick Marley and Ethan de Sade when champion Papa Legba retained over de Sade. Warned Jason Keening backstage on 23 July 2005 that his own hatred of Alex Extreme could consume him, describing his own recent custody fight and gratitude toward Keening and the Grimssons for their support. Eliminated Chris Douglas and Michael Augustine in the main event eight-man elimination match on 3 August 2005 before being pinned last himself by Victor Frost's Death Valley Driver, after Frost tripped tag partner Tumaffi's own top-rope splash attempt onto him. Teamed with Moira Faith to win the opening round of the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006 with a tandem shining wizard on Corey Irons, rebuffing manager Caine Tainer's own attempt to dictate the match beforehand.
