Jay Phoenix

aka Firestorm, Inferno, Meltdown, the Eternal Flame, Aquilia De Flagaro, Ember

The Eternal Flame — the GIWF North American Champion who renamed that belt the "Native American Title" and shared the 2000 Feud of the Year with Luca the Imp, the UWF's 28-day World Heavyweight Champion and a 2003 Hall of Famer, 4WW's first North Wind Champion and twice a Golden Turnbuckle Tourney finalist — who, masked as Ember, held the WWA World Championship and came to PRIME in 2006, then shed the mask there to win its first Merry Melee in 2008 and stand as the sole survivor of Power Play's fourth match in 2009. "Five", his Infinite Gauntlet 32 entry on PTC's board (May 2007), is the fullest thing of his own there: five chapters named for the stages of grief, set against the Ember contract he denied signing and what he called PRIME's "fake 'outing'" of him. It ends with his childhood friend Rick James reaching him in time, and a paid photographer catching the two of them in the doorway. Written by AI, 2026

PRIME (2003)

2006-11-08 – 2009-12-20
wrestler — masked as Ember from November 2006; entered the 2007 Dual Halo finale under his own name; won the first King of Kings Merry Melee in 2008; sole survivor of Power Play's fourth match and a three-time 5 Star Championship challenger in 2009
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 30 parsed matches, PRIME (2003)): 10-20-0
Tenure detail
PRIME's own biography card for this character (bio 167) carries the hometown Flagstaff, Arizona, the nickname The Eternal Flame and the entrance Phoenix Rising by Annihilator exactly as this record holds them, and its other-federations line claims the 4WW North Wind Championship this record carries, the PWW and UWF Grand Slams and the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. The card stages Ember, the masked billing he first wore in PRIME, as a spirit that possessed him — the never-born soul of his own twin brother — and says he spent nearly two years under that mask. As Ember he was disqualified for throwing a fireball at Jonathon Winters in the first round of the Jewel in the Crown tournament (ReVolution 110, 8 November 2006), confronted Winters, his old WWA enemy, on ReVolution 111, won a four-way for the number-one contendership to the Intense Championship on ReVolution 113 and lost the Intense Championship three-way at King of Kings on 17 December 2006. PRIME's news of 3 February 2007 held Jay Phoenix to the contract he had signed as Ember ("as Ember he recieved a HUGE signing bonus"). Under his own name he lost a four-way for the number-one contendership to the Alias Championship, won by Easton Hall, on ReVolution 120 (22 February 2007), and entered the Dual Halo finale at Culture Shock on 11 March 2007, billed "No longer under the mask of Ember"; Jason Snow's Snake Eyes eliminated him after Tony Gamble pushed him into it. Held to that contract by C.P. Cantrell, he came back on ReVolution 175 (8 October 2008), lost to Enemigo III by count-out when he walked out, then lay down for the pin when Cantrell ordered the match restarted; PRIME's news of 14 October 2008 advertised his debut at Great American Nightmare and noted he had already competed "under the mask of Ember (despite not seeming to realize it at the time)", and there, on 19 October, Cantrell put him in a handicap match against DUI, and Colby Korver lay down for his pin. He won his Jewel in the Crown first-round three-way on ReVolution 177 (7 November 2008), lost a three-way won by High Flyer on ReVolution 181 (12 December 2008) and won the first Merry Melee at King of Kings on 28 December 2008, eliminating Jason Natas first; its prizes were a guaranteed late entry into the next Dual Halo and the use of Chandler Tsonda's interns for a show. In 2009 he made peace with Jonathon Winters over the Ember years (ReVolution 184, 28 January) and lost a 5 Star Championship ladder match to High Flyer (ReVolution 186, 18 February); he gave up his late Dual Halo draw for a match with Hunter Sabuani, lost it at Culture Shock on 13 March, entered the Dual Halo first the next night and was pinned by the returning Jason Natas. At ReVolution 200 (8 July) he was the sole survivor of Power Play's fourth match, pinning Karina Wolfenden last, then eliminated Natas again in the closing battle royal and lasted to its final three, which carried him an Intense Championship shot. He lost an I Quit match to Natas at Colossus VI (16 August) under terms Natas set, that the man who quit would leave PRIME, and on ReVolution 204 (2 September) turned the result on Natas, who had said the words first. He lost the three-way in which Tony Gamble took the 5 Star Championship from Jason Snow (9 September) and a 5 Star Championship challenge to Gamble at Great American Nightmare (18 October), was made to lie down for Tyler Nelson in a tag match on ReVolution 213 and so joined Nelson's Army, and was last seen losing to Troy Douglas at King of Kings on 20 December 2009. PRIME's news of 28 December 2009 reports that, with Nelson released, he asked for his own release.
Source: PRIME's own backstage database (lot 0159) — his PRIME biography card, the federation's own show record and its news of 3 February 2007, 14 October 2008, 27 July 2009 and 28 December 2009

PWW (1997)

1997 – 2004-04-19
wrestler — the only grand slam champion in the federation's history, twice World Champion; bought the federation in 2002 and reopened it as owner and CEO in 2004
Tenure detail
The PWW is the federation this character's own record starts in — "the first place that I wrestled professionally," in his own words on the federation's 2004 interview teaser, signed by its president Alan Miller. The staff page names him the only man ever to hold all five PWW titles plus the Master of the Mat tournament and the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. The 1997 start is the federation's own founding year (27 July 1997, its own welcome page) and his own signed account on its closing notice of 19 April 2004: "In 1997 the PWW originally opened, and not too long after that I joined myself". No card dates his debut, and his own later accounts of it differ; they are recorded here rather than smoothed (#sources-disagree). "Created him for efedding in 1996 for the PWW" (PTC forum, "Longest Character Run...", topic 12366, post 24, 19 January 2005); "Started off in 1997 or late 1996 in a fed called the PWW" ("The Start of Your eFed Career", topic 5701, post 37, 21 May 2004); "I first joined an e-fed (PWW) in either late 1996 or early 1997 - depending on how you look at it. When I joined I didn't know that I had been accepted so went a while without doing anything" ("Small Survey", topic 23302, post 28, 16 June 2008); "I started efedding in late 1997 in my first fed, the PWW" ("Glory Days", topic 1646, post 1, 10 December 2003); and "PWW - 3+ years 1998 until the very end" ("Whats the longest you ever been in the same fed?", topic 2301, post 3, 14 January 2004). The character dates from 1996 in his own words; his start on the PWW's roster stays at the year the federation's own founding date and his own closing notice agree on.
