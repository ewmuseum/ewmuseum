Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Jared Johnson

An indy standout from North Carolina, a technical whiz, who turns out at April Showers in April 2010 to be Casey Valentine's partner after Cameron Vessey's departure; as Valentine & Johnson they keep the MAW Tag Team titles against Morgan & Benning and Antonio & Raphael, against Morgan & Benning again and against Shaffer & Grayson at Mother, May I?. With Casey Valentine he keeps the Tag Team titles against the Internet Favourites at Beach Blast in July 2010, Morgan & Benning at Iron and in a three-way at Back To School, carries Fall Of The King's three-way for the champions and loses the belts to the Canadian Blondes; at Where It All Begins Again in October he leaves The Firm and the partnership, saying he wants a partner who does not always have one eye on the next big score, and at Eleventh Hour in November he brings in Roderick Remus and Nelson Callum as trial partners in a Trios title match, losing in two falls. He loses Traditional title challenges on the December 2010 tour shows, then at Season's Beatings forms The Triple Threat with Roderick Remus and Nelson Callum and wins the Trios titles with the Carolina Crossface. He loses a run of tour matches to Kid Arachnid and Kashmir Singh and wins others over Amazing Fire Fly, Kashmir Singh, Matt Hocking and Ernest Youngman; at High Noon on 12th June he loses to Fumihiro Ota in a match the narrator takes as a sign of good things to come for him, and the notes keep calling him awesome, once warning that he was used too much. In the diary's pages from September 2011 he loses to Steve Flash at All The Leaves Are Brown and to Zimmy Bumfhole at Where It All Begins Again, beats Ernest Youngman at Eleventh Hour and Amazing Fire Fly at Remember November, beats Kirk Jameson with the Carolina Crossface at Season's Beatings and then fights Lockdown off to help him, draws fifteen minutes with Primus Allen for the Traditional title at End Of Days, and on 5th January 2012 teams with Wanda Fish to beat the Second Sons. He loses to Kid Arachnid in the first round of The Rip Chord Invitational; the narrator names him as one of his two projects for the coming months. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he loses to Aaron Andrews for the Heavyweight title at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February, to Ford & Speed with Amazing Fire Fly, to Providence at Great Lakes Crisis and to Lord Geoffrey Windameer at April Showers, wins with KC Glenn against the Canadian Blondes at Third Time's The Charm, loses to JD Morgan at May The Force Be With You in what the narrator calls a last throw of the dice, and loses to Eddie Powell at Mother, May I? on 12th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he wins the High Noon six-man on 5th June with Thea Davis and Bradford Peverell, and as half of Team Honor with Lassana Makutsi loses to Always Breakin' Hollywood and the Second Sons, is beaten by Providence's Eye Of Providence at Great Lakes Grand Parade, and with Randy Bumfhole wins the six-man at South East Grand Parade with an MDK Powerbomb. At Iron on 12th August the same move on Casey Valentine wins Team Honor the three-way, and at Back To School on 5th September it leaves Black Eagle open to the Carolina Crossface for the Tag Team titles.
