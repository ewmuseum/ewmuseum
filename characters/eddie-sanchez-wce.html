WcE (2004)

wrestler -- a WcE Hall of Fame inductee, one half of the tag team Florida's Finest, and the E of the JESTers (James, Eddie, Scott, Thomas)
Tenure detail
Source: MWE's own served text. The WcE Hall of Fame induction is stated by the character himself in a pre-taped titantron segment on MWE Crowning Glory II (the fed's own board, s4.zetaboards.com/MWEntertainment, via the Wayback Machine): "Remember when we were inducted into the WcE Hall of Fame James?" MWE's own returning star bills him from the ring on MWE Independence Day as "one half of Florida's Finest, one quarter of David's cousin's stable the JEST'ers, a WcE Hall of Famer." MWE's own served text, which is the whole of the surviving record for this character. The unit and its membership are spelled out twice in MWE's own words: A Crash Course in Wrasslin' (MWE) (the fed's own board, s4.zetaboards.com/MWEntertainment, via the Wayback Machine) -- "David Whitman and his friends the JEST'ers, James Whitman (David's cousin), best friends Eddie Sanchez and Scott Jones, and Thomas Williams (James' best friend)" -- and The Final Flight of a Wasted Mind; David Whitman RP (MWE), which expands the acronym outright: "the JESTers' (James Eddie Scott Thomas) company; JEST Kiddin' inc." The roster recital in Going Home; Dedicated to Tim Fitzgerald (MWE) lists the name among WcE's own. His first match is separately recited, out of character, in Something To Remember Me By...; This IS for you Tim... (MWE).

MWE (2007)

2008-08-08 – 2009-08-30
recurring on-camera guest from WcE -- a JESTer, staged in MWE's own arena on three cards: a pre-taped titantron segment at Crowning Glory II, a Pay-Per-View crowd interview at Independence Day that ended with him beaten out of the building, and guest commentary at Crowning Glory 2009
Tenure detail
The dates span the earliest and the latest held MWE document that stages or names this character; MWE's own record states neither a debut nor a departure for him.
Source: Three served MWE shows. MWE Crowning Glory II (the fed's own board, s4.zetaboards.com/MWEntertainment, via the Wayback Machine) -- the titantron segment with James Whitman during David Whitman's Hall of Fame induction, and the arena camera's own pass across the four JEST'ers in the ceremony audience. MWE Independence Day -- Alejandro Rodriguez picks him out of the crowd by name, billed as above, brings him into the ring, takes his answer, then attacks him and has security drag him up the ramp; the General Manager's own protest afterwards calls him "a member of the general public." MWE Crowning Glory -- the JEST'ers' four-man walk-out, the ejection of MWE's own announce team, and his seat on guest commentary for the whole David Whitman vs. Alejandro Rodriguez retirement match. Sixteen served MWE roleplays name or stage him besides.
