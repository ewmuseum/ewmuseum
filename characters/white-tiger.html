Biography (UWF (2001–07))

White Tiger is the nephew of Uncle Oscar, the original White Tiger. He has trained dilligently and his years of hard work and his unbeatble spirit gotten him... nowhere. Although he can always be counted on to put up a good fight, his win-loss record leaves something to be desired. Then again, maybe losing to World champs like Ruff Ryder and Jamie Shock ain't so bad. White Tiger took some time off to concentrate on his film career with Mucha Macho Movies, maker of B-grage action flicks. He has recently returned to the UWF, this time as a member of the Masked Monkeys, Richard Hayes' crack security team. So far, he's managed to get his butt kicked by Jay Vyper and the drowning POOL, but, without question, there will be even more butt-kickings in the future.
