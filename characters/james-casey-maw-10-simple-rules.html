Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

James Casey

The diary's first-person narrator: a former SWF midcarder whose career ended when Rip Chord tore his knee in a drunken match, who retired to North Carolina and is called back to be MAW's road agent, where he books the shows, handles the contracts and fines misbehaving staff a quarter of their pay for the local orphanage. In September 2010 he walks to the ring to his old SWF entrance music, for only the second time in nearly three years, to introduce Steve Flash as Jay Chord's challenger, and at Fall Of The King he announces that the Board of Directors has ordered Aaron Andrews's Heavyweight title match. He is visited at home in a storm by Katie Cameron after her split from Jacob Jett, takes her to dinner on New Year's Eve 2010, and fines Jay Chord for the locker-room fight; he refuses Richard Eisen's offer of the SWF's head booker's job in March 2011 and is attacked by Rip Chord in April. Rip Chord turns on him in the summer of 2011: he sits in his chair and blames him for Jay's injured elbow, hires sixteen workers behind his back and, in July, orders Jay onto commentary in his place, which the narrator takes as Rip phasing him out. After the dog dirt in a cap at Summernova he asks Steve Flash, Chance Fortune and Randy Bumfhole to be his ears and eyes in the locker room; he signs Ernest Youngman at $800 a night, fires Jay in September, loses Heidi Brooks's contract and Erik Strong, and takes a telephone offer from Tommy Cornell to become TCW's head road agent. In the diary's pages from September 2011 he fines Jay Chord a quarter of his show fee after Fall Of The King, takes Jay off the card for All The Leaves Are Brown, and on 26th October quits when Rip Chord demands that the main event finish be reversed; after a fight in the office he breaks his cane over Rip's head, talks him round and withdraws his resignation, saying he would rather stay, with an idea for a new title. He sits in on the release of eleven workers on 1st November, books the Farewell Tour and sees Chance Fortune, Ernest Youngman, Hugh de Aske and Steve Flash leave for the SWF, considers Tommy Cornell's job offer again, argues for cutting the touring shows and for four monthly shows, and learns from tew.com that Richard Eisen admires him. In January 2012 he plans a holiday with Katie Cameron. In the diary's pages from February to May 2012 he hears Wanda Fish's decision to retire, faces the press after Nelson Callum's arrest, and sets out in a note on the monthly Great Lakes or South East show the plan to reach Cult status on tew.com by the end of the year, with the profits of January and February behind it. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 he fines Aaron Andrews for his rumours, re-signs Hugh Lee for three months, drinks a half-pint of vodka at his desk over the loss of KC Glenn until Rip Chord screws the cap back on, and, with Rip, vows to break Richard Eisen in two; at 12:05am on a night in August he is woken by Rip's telephone call and takes an apology from Jay Chord in Los Angeles, with Katie Cameron beside him; he writes that he would cry if Kirk Jameson did not re-sign with North of the Border Pro Wrestling, and explains the storyline he had planned for Sienna LeNoir and Black Eagle.
