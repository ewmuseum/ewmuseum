Big Money Incorporated

The stable of Rich Money, Brandon James and Remo, led by manager Emma Chase, that targets Christian Faith, Steve Frehley, Lobster Warrior and Jack Bruce. The stable breaks up on SWF Legends in July 2008, when Emma Chase takes her client Brandon James to the Legends brand to face Christian Faith for the Supreme Championship and tells Rich Money and Remo they were never her clients.
