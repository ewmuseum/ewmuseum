UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2004-10-30 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 8 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 4-2-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
Jessica Marshall's mystery Tower of Doom partner and the UWF's own newest signing, billing himself as the man who beat Chris Blue and ended Chris Courtade's career; escaped the cage on 30 October 2004 as part of the winning team alongside Edmond Winston IV, Victor Frost, and Papa Legba in his own first UWF match, though Winston used him as muscle against Alex Extreme and Alex Martinez along the way. Beat "Dark Horse" Randall Osbourne by pinfall (the Lowlife Driver, an inverted DDT) on 3 November 2004 in his own first singles match for the wing, mocking the former heavyweight champion as washed up beforehand. Beat Steve Ryott by pinfall (the Cherry Pop Drop, a double underhook facebuster) on 27 November 2004, mocking the newcomer beforehand as a nobody and surviving a springboard dropkick exchange along the way. Beat Jason Keening by pinfall (a reverse cradle with his feet illegally on the ropes) on 11 December 2004 after proposing a weapons-free technical contest himself; learned backstage that Becky Byers had booked him against "The Last American Badass" Alex Martinez at Rise to Power, with a UWF World Heavyweight Championship shot attached if Martinez beat Kyle Lee that same night. Accepted Edmond Winston IV's own gift of the New Era on 28 May 2005, immediately putting Jamie Underwood through a bomb-squad beatdown with his own new recruits Corporate Leverage and Joey~!, then turned Strictly Business against Underwood as well and renamed the faction the Illuminati. Fought Alex Kidd to a double countout on 11 June 2005 after both men refused to break outside the ring, then called off the surrounding Illuminati and let Kidd leave unharmed, calling the veteran's own showing worthy of respect. Mocked Alex Kidd and Alex Extreme in an Illuminati promo backstage on 23 July 2005 alongside Andrew Tucker, then lost to Extreme by count-out after walking out of their match at Joey~!'s prompting. Led the Illuminati in mocking the Pride, the Youngbloods, and a returning Alex Kidd at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, then was beaten down alongside Strictly Business by Kidd and the Fraternity Boys. Teamed with Tigress in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, interfering with a chair on both remaining finalists after his own team's elimination and inadvertently helping Alex Extreme pin Jason Keening. Demanded a World Heavyweight Championship shot on 18 March 2006, citing his own pinfall win over Alex Martinez at Rise to Power, and was named to a four-way title match the following week against Martinez, Brett Greene, and Alex Extreme after Becky Byers denied him the sole shot.
