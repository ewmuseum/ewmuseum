Team Extreme

Don Rossi's CHZ stable of autumn 2001, with Matt Helms, Kris Snyder, Darryl Rossi and Hell's Fury among its members, which cast out Extreme Chancelz after his chair shot cost Rossi the Intercontinental Title on 9 November 2001. Written by AI, 2026

CHZ (1999)

stable
Tenure detail
Don Rossi's stable in the autumn of 2001; after Rossi left the CHZ, Darryl Rossi sought the members' backing as its new leader (12 December 2001). Named again in March 2002, with Madd Maxx.
Source: Fire and Diablo billed Team Extreme members, 21 September 2001 (CHZ Friday Night Wild); Don Rossi's Team Extreme meeting, 23 September 2001 (CHZ House Show); Extreme Chancelz cast out and hunted, 9 November 2001 (CHZ Friday Night Wild); Darryl Rossi "usually is with the rest of TEAM EXTREME", 9 December 2001 (CHZ House Show); Darryl Rossi seeking its leadership, 12 December 2001 (CHZ Wednesday Night Wild); "The next match pitted Madd Maxx, of Team Extreme and the Black Dragon" (CHZ Wednesday Night Wild).
