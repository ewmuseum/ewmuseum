The Dark Raptors

aka Firebird and Black Eagle

Firebird and Black Eagle, billed as The Dark Raptors in a three-way for the Tag Team titles against Smith & Singh and the Canadian Blondes at Mother, May I? on 26th May 2011, where Oscar Golden crotches Firebird on the top rope and the Blondes retain. As Firebird and Black Eagle they lose to Aaron Andrews and Kirk Jameson at Spring Fever; after the three-way they stand with JD Morgan and the converted Masked Patriot, presented as Providence, as the four men who "have something greater". They beat the Young Americans at Eleventh Hour on 12th November 2011, where Firebird announces that they are the champions-elect, lose the Tag Team title three-way at Season's Beatings when Singh pins Firebird, and beat Lassana Makutsi and Amazing Fire Fly at End Of Days. They lose to KC Glenn and Randy Bumfhole at All Or Nothing on 12th February 2012, when Bumfhole pins Black Eagle; Eagle's spinal surgery that follows leaves Firebird on a singles run and the tag division with more heel teams than face ones. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 Firebird and Black Eagle win the Tag Team titles from Smith & Singh at Summer Showdown on 12th June as part of The Fallen, keep them against the West Texas Peacekeepers on 9th July, the XX Factor on 5th August and Spectrum Infinity on 12th August, and lose them to Team Honor at Back To School on 5th September, when Eagle taps out.
