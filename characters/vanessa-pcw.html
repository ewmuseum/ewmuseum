Jason Wulf's valet, who led him to the ring on a chain. PCW's own cards show her from his debut on Turmoil 7 (7 November 2000) and Fusion 8 (10 November 2000) to the Creatures of the Night's tag match on Fusion 23 (23 February 2001); the fed's database books her on nine cards from 5 January 2001. The database card's tenth booking, Fusion 101 of 20 December 2002, is another Vanessa (vanessa-matthews-rush-2002).
