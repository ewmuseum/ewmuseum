Tenure detail
The fed's own commentary on the 7th Static treats him as previously fired ("I thought he was fired?") before he attacks Co-Owner Jeffery with a chair; security removes him from the arena. Named in Revelation's 2003 roleplay (/promotions/bdw/rp/bdw-roleplays-1-2003.html) as the man who hurt Apocalypse and who was to face Chezina on Sanitarium; his own 2004 card says the company closed after he had signed with it, and that he went back to prize fighting before returning. Lost to Emilio Dervante in the World Title tournament's second round on the 6th Static (23 May 2004) and was fired by Co-Owner Jeffery after the match.
