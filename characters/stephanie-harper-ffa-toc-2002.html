Tenure detail
The reigning UWF Women's Heavyweight Champion of the World, won at Gold Rush 2003 per her own promo; retained against Bliss on 30 August 2003, then appeared on the following Meltdown to promise a three-way title defense against the winner of Nina Grimsson vs. Myra Benedict, which ended in a draw. Retained the title in that promised three-way dance on 25 October 2003, pinning Myra Benedict with the Fallen Star while Nina Grimsson wrestled with open disinterest. Retained the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World again on 15 November 2003, forcing challenger Donna Tetreault to submit to a center-ring Texas Cloverleaf. Lost the UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World to Donna Tetreault on 15 May 2004: after being caught by a somersault plancha alongside cornerman Simon O'Neal, she was rolled back into the ring and pinned as the referee's count on both women lapsed; brass knuckles were found at ringside afterward. Lost her UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World rematch to Donna Tetreault by pinfall at Gold Rush on 5 June 2004, in a match with both women's own cornermen (Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal) handcuffed together at ringside; knocked out by a foreign object hidden in Tetreault's own bandana. Lost a UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship of the World number one contendership match to Sierra Browne by pinfall (a top-rope Sierra Slice) on 27 November 2004, refusing an earlier win manufactured by a meddling champion Donna Tetreault and asking for the match to be restarted rather than accept it. Recorded a message from home on 1 December 2004 wishing Sierra Browne luck against Donna Tetreault at Rise to Power, reiterating her own unfinished business with Tetreault over past attacks on Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal. On 11 December 2004, joined Alex Kidd, Ryu Osawa, and Paul Wong in a failed attempt to free Marissa Monet and Pizzazz from their own skybox chains. Told UWF reporter Moe Owens on 8 June 2005 that her issue with Donna Tetreault was one of principle rather than titles, accusing Tetreault of driving a wedge between the Fraternity Boys the same way she once split Simon O'Neal and Paul Wong. Convinced the referee to restart Nina Grimsson's own number-one contenders match against Donna Tetreault on 20 July 2005 after Tetreault won a rollup using the ropes for leverage; later joined Becky Byers in setting a Blind Stipulation Match against Tetreault for Gold Rush. Lost to Kari Stevens by pinfall on 3 August 2005 while still hampered from a backstage assault by Brian McKenzie earlier that night. Counseled Paul Wong backstage on 7 June 2006 over his own long rivalry with the Fraternity Boys, urging him not to let standing by tag partner Chris O'Brien push him into doing wrong himself; did not wrestle.
