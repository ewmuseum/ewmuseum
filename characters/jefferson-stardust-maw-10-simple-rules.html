Jefferson Stardust

A mink-coated rock-star gimmick with an entrance as long as his matches, first with the valet Kristen Pearce and later with the manager Jakki. He beats Amazing Fire Fly with a Star Burst at Hotter Than Hot in July 2010 and then teams with Findlay O'Farraday and Kristen Pearce as Glamour And Devastation. The narrator notes that at April Showers in April 2011 he is mostly hiding behind Findlay O'Farraday. Three and a half years after his arrival the narrator still calls him awful and lists him among the expendables for $150; on the tours he beats Matt Hocking and Ben Williams and loses to Max Mayhem, Kid Arachnid, Amazing Fire Fly, Ernest Youngman and Daredevil Aero.
