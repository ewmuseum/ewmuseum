Biography (UWF (2001–07))

Reod started in the small community of Morristown, MN. A population of 800 people was not his idea of a party place. He moved to Minneapolis at the age of 16 where he started to hang with a rough crowd. Here he learned the anything goes style of Pit Fighting. At age 17, Joanna entered his life. She liked what she saw and started to manage his career. For 2 years they traveled the country, winning match after match. Then it all ended on fateful night. Reod was slammed on his knee on the cement floor. His Knee was shattered, and so was his career. The next year, he was to spend in rehab, he told Joanna to continue her career. All through the year, Reod could thing of nothing but making a comeback. Joanna would keep him up to date on her life, including the new Tag Team she had in Pro Wrestling. Then one day, She came to him with an offer. One member of her team had retired. Joanna wanted Reod to fill his spot. He agreed on one condition. No other allies. From that moment on Anarchy was Born.
