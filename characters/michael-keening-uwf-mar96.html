Tenure detail
Billed with his brother Jason Keening as the Ghost Dancers, descendants of the Paiute nation from Los Angeles; also confronted Serge Annis over an old injury to their brother-in-law Gabriel Whitecross. Lost the UWF World Tag Team Championship with his brother to Team Canada on 4 October 2003 after Kari Stevens pepper-sprayed Jason Keening at ringside; the Privateers ambushed both brothers backstage afterward. Sabotaged the Privateers' cricket bats in a woodchipper before winning the UWF North American Tag Team Championship with his brother from Team Canada by submission on 25 October 2003, after a contract clause kept the World Tag Team Championship off the line. Lost the UWF North American Tag Team Championship on 15 November 2003 when teammates Erik and Chad Grimsson escaped the Tower of Doom cage as a complete team first; he and his brother Jason escaped afterward. Entered the 20 December 2003 twelve-man elimination match with brother Jason; pinned Jacob Drake with a Gourdbuster before eventually being pinned himself by Drake's own Gourdbuster in return. Teamed with brother Jason and Erik Grimsson in a six-man tag loss to Team Canada and Scott Daniels on 10 March 2004 after two unidentified men in suits ambushed the match; Daniels stole the pinfall off him. Beat "Insane Bronco" Shay Parker by pinfall on 21 March 2004 with a top-rope somersault splash, following an old backstage confrontation with Alex Extreme over Extreme's own alliance with Michael Augustine. Teamed with Erik Grimsson in a scrambled-partners tag on 5 May 2004 against a reluctant pairing of Niklos Romani and Todd Franklin; the bout ended as a no-contest after Franklin turned on his own partner and battered all three other wrestlers. Teamed with brother Jason again as the Ghost Dancers on 29 May 2004, fighting Serge Annis and Caliban to a double countout after a suicida dive and other big moves at ringside; the Privateers ran in on the ensuing brawl, and commissioner Becky Byers added the Ghost Dancers to the Gold Rush UWF World Tag Team Championship match against champions the Privateers and Annis and Caliban. Teamed with brother Jason as the Ghost Dancers in a losing Gold Rush triple threat for the UWF World Tag Team Championship on 5 June 2004, pinned by Jacob Drake of the champion Privateers after a Near Death Experience-style superplex from Serge Annis left him unable to continue; collapsed backstage afterward with a back injury and was taken to a hospital.
