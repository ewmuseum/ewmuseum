Biography (RUSH (2002))

Tyler was born in Milwaukee, Wisconsin on February 14th (Valentine’s Day), 1979. At the age of three, his mother, Dana, died during a tragic bicycling accident, leaving Tyler and his father, Aaron, to fend for themselves. This sparked something in Tyler that changed his life forever. He became far more removed from the outside world and was very introspective in his thoughts and words. At one point, he even considered taking his own life. However, while going through rehabilitation after a horrendous knee injury in April 2000, he found the will to continue on living, and became generally happy once again, renewing ties with his family, gaining many new friends, and honing his wrestling skills tremendously. After recently feeling himself about to go into a bout with depression, he decided to get hypnotized to remove any bad memories he had. However, the “hypnotists” – two midget con artists named Tweedledee and Tweedledum – played a practical joke on him, making him believe he was a living, breathing, talking peanut butter and jelly sandwich. Backstage at the final NTICW Mayhem, a stray puddle of slippery soap would prove to be the most important moment of Valentine's recent life. Upon falling unconscious, an angel - or so we believe - visited him in his sleeping and offered him the chance to break the hypnosis and find out who he really is. He accepted. And now, Valentine must go through the struggle of entertaining millions of people while battling to find his true identity. Then came Crossroads, RUSH Wrestling's second Pay Per View, in which Valentine finally realized that it's not his past that matters to him, or anyone else for that matter, but the present. Career History Tyler's first real wrestling endeavour was in NTICW. He came out as a rising star in the new Cruiserweight division, which was considered one of the best things to happen to NTICW at the time, becoming the first-ever NTICW Cruiserweight Champion on May 19 at Battle in Brazil. He would hold the belt for all but the final week before NTICW merged with RUSH. Tyler Valentine officially became a member of the RUSH roster on June 18, 2002 when NTICW owner Chris Sloboda sold his, along with eighteen other wrestlers', contract over to Jeremy Howard. Tyler debuted on RUSH Television on July 11, 2002, teaming with Chris Matthews and Tex Kelley in a main-event six-man tag team match against Cassius Deamont, Brock Newbluud, and Boda. The match was going poorly for the 'good guys' until former NTICW wrestler D-Kay Calyx entered the fray. Calyx teased a save only to take out Kelley with the Chi-Town DDT. Newbludd took out Matthews with The Last Call and Boda delivered the Bounced Czech to Valentine, sealing a victory for Deamont, Newbludd, and Boda. After the match, Calyx further attacked Valentine with the Calyx-O Drop as Adrenaline 9 went off the air, setting up a rivalry that is still going on, fueled by the revelation that Calyx holds a manila folder that supposedly contains secrets of Valentine's past. On Adrenaline 11, Valentine tried to capture this folder but D-Kay's locker room, but was unsuccessful, being chased out his locker room with a baseball bat. On Adrenaline 12, Valentine challenged D-Kay to a ladder match - at the top of the ladder is the fabled manila folder. Come Crossroads, Valentine finally got his hands on the manila folder, only to rip up it into tiny little pieces in front of thousands of confused fans. His reason? Who cares, he says. All Tyler knows is that the only thing that matters now is the present, and anything from his past is simply inconsequential. After Crossroads, Tyler Valentine noticed something very strange in his best friend, Chris Matthews - he was becomming greedier and more distant by the day. This all led up to each other foiling each other's title matches with Brock Newbludd, and the falling out of their doomed friendship. The fact that Brock did nothing to help Valentine from these attacks didn't sit too well with him either. This rivarly is to be settled once and for all at Rising From the Ashes, on October 13th.
