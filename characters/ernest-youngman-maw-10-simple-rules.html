Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Ernest Youngman

aka King Midas, The Assassin of the Squared Circle

An eighteen-year-old from the independent scene, called the Assassin of the Squared Circle and King Midas, whom the narrator signs in August 2011 for $800 a night after talking Rip Chord into watching him; his announcement that he was going pro was on his blog. Steve Flash presents him to The Firm at Summernova on 12th August as his nephew, whom he trained himself, and he loses his debut to Erik Strong; at Iron he is fed to Primus Allen for the Traditional title in an extended squash and at Back To School he takes a Heavyweight title shot against Aaron Andrews, losing both. On the tour and Moment Of Madness shows he beats Jefferson Stardust, VIP Wallace, Curtis Jenkins, Flash Savage, Simon Ice and Raphael and loses to Jared Johnson, the notes calling the September shows "the Ernest Youngman show". He loses to Jared Johnson at Eleventh Hour, to JD Morgan at Where It All Begins Again and, in a submission match for the All Action title, at Farewell Tour on 22nd November, after which the narrator says goodbye to him as he leaves for the SWF with Chance Fortune and Hugh de Aske.
