Tenure detail
Watched 'The Outlaw' Morris Bass lose the VXW Tag Team Championship to a returning Adam Jennings's own distraction at New Year's Eve of Destruction 2006, then plotted a Loser Leaves Vegas Ghost Town Blues match against Jennings and a mystery partner (revealed as a returning Travis LaGrange) at the same contract signing. Taunted Darlene Orion backstage before Sin City Fights February 7 with a mind-games threat against two people close to Jennings. VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005)

2004-02-26 – 2004-11-08
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 12 parsed matches, VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005), this run, placed by show date): 8-2-1, 1 no contest
Tenure detail
Teams with fellow Society of Doom member Kagedo against The Enforcers on Firestorm (26 Feb 2004); also runs in later that night to help clear the ring after Ric Beauty's induction into the New Age Army. Submits newcomer Johnny King on Firestorm (3 May 2004) after a backstage ambush by Thunder Valenkov, Blackbird and Iyasu Saku, then earns a Hair vs. Title Submission Championship match against Iyasu Saku at the Gauntlet. Helped submit Iyasu Saku for the Society of Doom's win on Wrestle Style V.1, saw a signed tag match collapse before the bell on Wrestle Style V.2, then beat Sonicman with the Durham Deathdrop in the opening round of the VWF Heavyweight Title tournament on Wrestle Style V.3. Wins a ladder match against Iyasu Saku at Vegas Violence IV (12 October 2004) to avoid retirement and earn the first Rage in the Cage berth, grabbing his own envelope after the Society of Doom runs in to stop a Kingston Cash chair attack. Wins by disqualification with Outlaw Morris Bass over The Mighty Men on March To Hell (21 October 2004) after The Reckoning accidentally clotheslines the referee, then helps neutralize the Riders of the Apocalypse during Adam Jennings's own VWF 24 Hour Championship defense the same show. Ambushed backstage by RAR and the Rodeo Boys on March To Hell II (28 October 2004), too injured to wrestle his own scheduled match against Billy Bones. Pins Marcus O'Malley in the Rage in the Cage at Phear Phantastic 2004 (8 November 2004) to become the new co-holder of the VWF Tag Team Championship alongside Mark McQueen.
