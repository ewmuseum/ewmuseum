Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Thea Davis

A flame-haired young woman introduced at Leap Into The Unknown on 5th February 2012 as the protégé of Wanda Fish, who has her training on a heavy punch bag and calls her the most talented worker of her age she has seen in years, male or female. The narrator says he had thought of bringing her in under a hood for an eventual reveal and has instead made her Fish's protégé, since Wanda is not getting any younger; as the lowest member of the roster she takes the fall when she and Fish lose to Raphael and Citizen X, pinned with Flaming Anarchy. In March the narrator's survey of the regional companies says that the women's company AAA could cause MAW trouble by taking her, and that, high as he is on her prospects, it would not be a major loss; after Mother, May I? he notes that she has extended her contract with another company. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 she wins the High Noon six-man on 5th June with Bradford Peverell and Jared Johnson, yanking Eddie Powell off the top rope when he stops to flirt with her, and loses to Raphael's Italian DDT at South East Madness on 9th June, an opener the narrator designs to limit costs. At Hotter Than Hot on 5th July she takes a new regular partner, Jaime Quine, as The XX Factor; they lose to Providence and Father Johan, to the Dark Raptors for the Tag Team titles at Summernova on 5th August and to Always Breakin' Hollywood at Back To School on 5th September, where Davis tells interviewer Jenny that they will always try their best.
