Character · TCW: A Quiet Retirement (2008)

Aaron Andrews

A 21-year-old brawler whom Eric Tyler calls the worst of the chancers, who has lost to nearly everyone and is rescued from Tyler by Joey Minnesota, and in August 2008 is billed as the first graduate of TCW's fast-track training programme, beats Freddy Huggins, Shingen Miyazaki and UK Dragon in a four-way match and spinebusters Sean Deeley to help Rick Law at Hotter Than Hell, and in September 2008 beats the British Lions with Joey Minnesota, loses to the School of Tradition and beats JD Morgan with the spinebuster, and in October 2008 loses to Eric Tyler in a Streetfight, impresses against Tommy Cornell, is eliminated from the battle royal and beats the School of Tradition with Joey Minnesota. In November 2008 he beats Jack Marlowe with a Northern Lights suplex and loses to Remo Richardson's Destroyer, after which Phil Vibert tells him he will not be his second client. In December 2008 he loses to Sammy Bach, wins a six-way match with a spinebuster, debuts the Game Breaker, is called to the ring by Tommy Cornell as one last test, loses to Wolf Hawkins and, at Psycho Circus, loses to Cornell after Hawkins hits him with a chair.
