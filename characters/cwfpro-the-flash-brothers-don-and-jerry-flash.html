Their own Earlier Career line separately dates their DEPARTURE from the XXX to 2001 ("They left the XXX efed after talking with R@ven in 2001"), before the 2003 buy this same passage states — read as the same wrestlers' XXX run ending before the promotion itself changed hands, not a contradiction. Sources disagree with bobby-williams's own tenure note on the buy date (2003 vs. 2005) — both are recorded, neither resolved. "They started out in 1997 as rookies in a few efeds winning minor titles in the PWE, JJP, GWC, & RFP. They left the XXX efed after talking with R@ven in 2001." R@ven is the buyer's own scout (his own served bio: "He was a scouter for the cwf.pro"), and the same page's next section confirms what the talking was for: "They were set to join the CWF-Pro in 2001, but the cwf.pro clsed after the cross over with the tdw." That gives this wing its only firmly dated in-era EVENT of any kind — a departure in 2001, inside the independent XXX's own attested 2000-2003 window, two years before the earlier of the two disputed buyout years. Note also that the four efeds named as their 1997 starting points (PWE, JJP, GWC, RFP) are NOT the XXX; nothing here moves this wing's own founding date earlier than Bobby Williams' own 2000.
