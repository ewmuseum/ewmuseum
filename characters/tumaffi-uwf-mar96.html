Tenure detail
Introduced by an in-house vignette on 1 December 2004: took offense at a narrator's script framing him as a primitive island cannibal, smashed the UWF's own editing studio to pieces while trying to cut his own promotional video, and was billed as "coming soon to the UWF" ahead of an announced debut. Has not yet wrestled a match in the held record. Beat Zero-G by pinfall (the Polynesian Burial, a Samoan drop) on 25 May 2005 in his own first UWF match, shrugging off a flurry of dropkicks and forearms beforehand. Was warned backstage on 11 June 2005 about injuring a try-out opponent in an unscheduled dark match, mocked "Showstealer" Miguel Quesada for interjecting, then lost their singles match by disqualification after Quesada tricked the referee into believing Tumaffi had used a chair. Beat "Long Haul" Greg Embrey by referee stoppage on 6 July 2005, ignoring repeated warnings and berating the official after the bell; hid under a rolling table at ringside during Edward Drake's own Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament match against Miguel Quesada, only to be exposed and shown up by Quesada. Survived the main event eight-man elimination match on 3 August 2005 alongside Victor Frost, eliminating Jason Keening and Alex Extreme himself and splashing onto the pile that finished Papa Legba. Won the Unified Television Championship from Marcus Nuit by pinfall (the Polynesian Burial) on 18 March 2006, and had the ring announcer read out an on-air rename of the title to one of his own choosing.
