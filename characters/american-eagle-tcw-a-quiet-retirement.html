American Eagle

aka Dave Wallace, Air Attack Weasel

A masked wrestler created in November 2008 for Dave Wallace, who had worked in TCW's developmental territory under the ring name Air Attack Weasel: a tennis player turned wrestler whom the narrator, on the advice of his son Adrian, calls a jobber for life, unrivalled at selling and willing to take any stunt, and whose mask the company keeps as it keeps MAW's Kid Arachnid. He debuts on Badge of Honor as half of Tooth & Claw with Lobo Blanco, loses to the Easy Riders and, on Total Wrestling, to the Stone Brothers and, alone, to Tyson Baine's Hades Bomb, and takes a Precision Driller in the six-man contendership match won by Greg Keith. In December 2008 he loses with Lobo Blanco to the Elite Express and to Frankie Perez & Remo Richardson, after countering a powerbomb into a pinfall that Remo powers out of before two.
