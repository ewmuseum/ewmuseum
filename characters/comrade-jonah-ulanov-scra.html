Tenure detail
Opens the Comrades Inc. segment of 8 February 1998 with 'Peoples of SCRA lend me your ears' and speaks for the team; he clicks the lighter that sets the gasoline-filled pool, and the ring, on fire. On 9 February he climbs the top rope to distract the referee while his team attacks Chris Eagle, jumps down from the apron waving the Ukraine flag and sits at the announce desk to say they kicked the German Eagles.
Source: SCRA Winter Carnival, Part 2; SCRA The Scraps, 9 February 1998, Part 4; SCRA The Scraps, 16 February 1998, Part 4; SCRA The Scraps, 16 February 1998, Part 6
