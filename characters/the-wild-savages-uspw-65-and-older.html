The Wild Savages

Jacob Stranton's two Samoans, Umaga and Akima, whom he bills as a destructive force that will take the tag belts from Team Seduction or the World Championship from Triple D; they beat Pure Evil on the Family Night of Thursday, Week 3, September 2007 and Akima beats Bruce the Giant on the Monday of Week 3. In October 2007 they attack Bruce the Giant at the food table week after week, lose to Muffin and Freddie Datsun, beat Sam Strong down on the Thursday of Week 2, and lose to Bruce the Giant and their brother Giant Tana at A Haunting in America. In October 2007 Jacob Stranton leads Akima and Umaga to the ring to join Divinity, which turns out to be a trap: Akima kicks Umaga and is welcomed in his place. In November Umaga loses a World Championship match to Triple D, Stranton is put into intensive care by Eddie Peak, and Umaga beats Akima at Pilgrimage. Written by AI, 2026
