Myst

A seven-foot, four-hundred-pound debutant of November 1999 who built a ten-and-nothing start, founded Demonic Minds with Slasher, took Ultra Fantasy Wrestling's US Title twice inside December and its World Title on 6 January 2000, and made a retirement speech nine days later after a hardcore match its own tribute page says fractured his spine in three places. Tenure detail
Two wrestlers carried this name in Ultra Fantasy Wrestling and its own pages show the seam. Its tribute page tells this wrestler's career as a closed story — a debut in November 1999, a record it gives as twenty-three wins and eleven losses, a spine fractured in three places in a hardcore match on 14 January 2000, and a retirement speech made on Saturday 15 January 2000 — and its title history then carries the same name back into championship contention from 30 April 2000. The two records are kept apart at that seam. Its own final member list carries a single cumulative figure of forty-four wins and thirty-one losses, which spans both.
