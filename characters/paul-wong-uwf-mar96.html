Tenure detail
Billed from Denver, Colorado, weighing 276 pounds; entered as the reigning UWF Rampage Champion. Retained the title by disqualification over Tom Landis, then was beaten down by Team Canada until Alex Kidd made the save. Retained again on 30 August 2003, pinning Cameron Black with a tilt-a-whirl powerslam after Serge Annis's appearance froze Black in place. Lost the UWF Rampage Championship to the One-Winged Angel by pinfall on 4 October 2003. Teamed with Alex Kidd, Youth Gone Wild, and Quinn Brown to a count-out win over the Pride on 22 October 2003, turning manager Kari Stevens's own pepper spray back on Tom Landis. Lost to Scott Daniels by pinfall on 15 November 2003 after a low blow and Daniels using the ropes for leverage. Entered the 17 December 2003 Meltdown Championship tournament as the reigning UWF Rampage Champion's own former MBC rival, eliminated last from the battle royal by Victor Frost; beat Tigress in the semi-finals with a Samoan drop, then lost the finals to Papa Legba, pinned by the Black Magic superbomb. Teamed with Alex Kidd and "Doomsayer" Kyle Lee against Team Canada and Scott Daniels on 15 May 2004 in a six-man tag that ended without a decision amid a mass brawl; Serge Annis and Caliban ambushed and hospitalized him in his own locker room after the match. Beat "Dark Horse" Randall Osbourne by pinfall with a lariat on 8 September 2004, then lost the tournament semifinal to Vyolynce by disqualification after Brian McKenzie's run-in. Returned from a concussion to team with Sierra Browne against Donna Tetreault and Brian McKenzie on 11 December 2004, nearly finishing McKenzie with a gorilla press before Tetreault knocked him out with smuggled brass knuckles for the loss. Took a call from a nephew stuck at boarding school on 8 June 2005, unable to reach the boy's own father, Simon O'Neal. Rescued Stephanie Harper from a backstage assault by Brian McKenzie on 3 August 2005; lost the Brawl From The Mall 6 opener to the Mercenary by pinfall on 6 August 2005, then was ambushed backstage by McKenzie and Donna Tetreault with a steel chair and brass knuckles. Brawled with Chris O'Brien at ringside throughout Donna Tetreault's own No Disqualification match with Simon O'Neal on 18 March 2006. Reflected in a backstage conversation with Stephanie Harper on 7 June 2006 on his own long rivalry with the Fraternity Boys, recalling his own past powerbomb on Gus Zampitella and worrying aloud about how far the feud with Brian McKenzie and Chris O'Brien would go; did not wrestle.
