UWF (23 Mar 1996)

2003-08-09 – 2006-03-18
Observed by the museum, W-L-D (from 11 parsed matches, UWF (23 Mar 1996)): 3-6-0, 2 no contest
Tenure detail
"The Drunken Icon," a member of the Fraternity Boys; volunteered from the crowd as a same-night replacement challenger for an injured Sean Watts and pinned champion Uriah Hawkins to win the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World on 9 August 2003. Defended the title against Michael Bonn in his hometown of Pittsburgh on 4 October 2003; the referee waved off a Bonn pinfall for a foot on the ropes, and no decision was ever rendered after Bonn beat down the referee, McKenzie, and O'Brien. Teamed with Brian McKenzie as the Fraternity Boys in a Las Vegas Street Fight loss to the Arcana on 15 November 2003, handcuffed to the crowd barrier by Virginia St. Ursula and Michael Bonn before the finish. Retained the UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World again on 20 December 2003, pinning Quinn Brown with an accidental Ace Crusher counter; Michael Bonn interrupted the pre-match introductions by beating a captive Brian McKenzie on camera, and the two brawled through the arena for the rest of the broadcast, ending with Bonn piledriving O'Brien onto a steel chair in the lobby. Shared a strip-club scene with a visibly drunk Victor Frost on 19 May 2004 ahead of their own upcoming Gold Rush match, then lost a four corners contenders match to the One-Winged Angel, driven down by a Revelator alongside two other fallen men for the pin. Substituted into a tag confrontation against Serge Annis and Caliban on 29 May 2004 alongside Brian McKenzie, breaking a Jalapeño Pepper Beer bottle over Caliban's head before Caliban pinned McKenzie for the loss; substituted again for the injured McKenzie in the UWF North American Heavyweight Championship match later the same night, beating champion Victor Frost by countout when Frost abandoned the match to chase a returning Quinn Brown, the title not changing hands. Lost a UWF Cruiserweight Championship of the World challenge to champion One-Winged Angel on 22 September 2004 in a friendly MBC-reunion contest, caught with the Heavenly Wind and Hell's Halo combination; embraced the champion afterward. Baited tag partner Brian McKenzie backstage on 27 November 2004 over his own lack of ring time, then threw a cup of Chai in McKenzie's face; needled Donna Tetreault about her relationship with McKenzie before wishing the couple well. Held up a protest sign at ringside during Donna Tetreault and Brian McKenzie's own mixed tag win on 11 December 2004; McKenzie grabbed the sign and used it on Paul Wong. Ambushed Caliban with a steel chair backstage and brawled with Serge Annis through a door on 28 May 2005, then lost a handicap match to both Hands of Death members by pinfall (the Epitomizer) after a wild brawl that put both men through a table off a balcony. Accepted a backstage apology and a replacement laptop from Michael Bonn on 8 June 2005, with Virginia St. Ursula and Ryu Osawa looking on, then traded online-game banter with the pair; ambushed Ravnos Romani at ringside with a steel-chair Conchairto alongside Brian McKenzie on 11 June 2005 during the non-title four corners tag main event, until Niklos Romani drove both Fraternity Boys off with a springboard splash. Beat Brian McKenzie at Dance Dance Revolution in an arcade on 22 June 2005 and traded barbs with Donna Tetreault over training methods. Shared a beer and a truce with Alex Martinez backstage on 6 July 2005 after their own best-two-out-of-three-falls Road to the Gold final. Sang a comedic hockey song backstage ahead of facing Marcus Nuit on 23 July 2005, then lost the Cruiserweight Invitational Tournament/Unified Television Championship match by pinfall (a Falcon Arrow). Joined Brian McKenzie and Alex Kidd in running off the Illuminati at Brawl From The Mall 6 on 6 August 2005, smashing beer bottles over Luke Kinsey and L. Dan Dee during a CareerBuilder.com-themed confrontation. Joined Donna Tetreault and Keith Marshall backstage at Cupid's Revenge on 14 February 2006, plotting elaborate revenge on the returning Simon O'Neal over a shell-shocked Brian McKenzie. Brawled with Paul Wong at ringside throughout Donna Tetreault's own No Disqualification match with Simon O'Neal on 18 March 2006.
