Wild Dog

One of the CHZ's own self-declared "Sickest Bastards Alive," a member of the NBK who won a hardcore triple threat over Hell's Keeper and 'Psycho' Cody Hawk on the 22 March 2002 Wild show. In 2003 he won the Omega Championship match against Gus Theo on 26 March, kept the title by disqualification against Lucifer on 9 April, and won the vacant title in a ladder match against Chazy 2 Cool at May HayDay 2.
