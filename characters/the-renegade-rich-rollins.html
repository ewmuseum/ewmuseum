Rich Rollins

aka The Renegade

Rich Rollins, who won the PTC Global and Extreme Championships together in a Scavenger Hunt Match, held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship twice, the United States Championship once and the HDTV Championship once, was named Match of the Year (2001), Feud of the Year (2007), Writer of the Year (2001), was inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame, attested by 2005 and appeared on 67 GCW cards from 1 February 2001 to 28 July 2011.
