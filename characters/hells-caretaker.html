Hell's Caretaker

aka HELL'S CARETAKER

Doc Silver's man in both feds: he brawled Anarky's CSWA debut to a double countout neither man seemed to mind until Silver called him off, won a grudge tag after Silver stripped a top turnbuckle down to bare steel, and walked out with Silver's group at the end of the Oktoberfest '96 Television title match to raise the new champion's hand and hand him a bottle of Poland Spring.
