EFW (1997–98)

1997-10-27 – 1998-12-27
Tenure detail
"The Amazing" Mick Mann, a masked high-flyer from Venice Beach, California: challenged the Dragon for the Cruiserweight title in a steel cage at Extreme Halloween (4 November 1997), entered the Cruiserweight battle royal of 11 November, teamed with Glass Joe and a third partner as the Innovators of Violence against the HOA at the Survivor Series pay-per-view (1 December 1997), lost a loser-unmasks match to Patriot-X later that month and was billed in the four-way for the vacant Cruiserweight title at Seasons Beatings (29 December 1997); back a year later in SEXtreme with Glass Joe, teaming with Stupendous Steve on the Seasons Beatings 98 card (27 December 1998).
