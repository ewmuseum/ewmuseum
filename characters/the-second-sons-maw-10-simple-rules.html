The Second Sons

Erik Strong, Antonio and Casey Valentine, who name themselves at Spring Fever on 23rd April 2011 after Casey Valentine's Loser Leaves Town win over Jean Cattley, telling Cattley that they are the men who should be in charge of the company now. Jay Chord refuses to join them and warns them to change the name, and the narrator's e-mails tell Jay to think about what "Second Sons" could mean in the company. They feud with Steve Flash's new Firm through the summer: Strong and Chord beat Flash and Kid Arachnid on 12th May, Valentine and Antonio beat Randy Bumfhole and Arachnid on 26th May, the three-match series at High Noon on 12th June ends one win each and a time-limit draw, Flash beats Valentine again at Summer Showdown and Antonio at Hotter Than Hot, where the Sons ambush Flash and Bumfhole after the bell, and Antonio and Strong lose to Flash and Bumfhole at Beach Blast. Jay Chord, Valentine and Antonio, billed as the Second Sons, beat Fumihiro Ota and the Young Americans at Summernova on 12th August, and Valentine and Antonio beat the Young Americans at Back To School; in September Strong tells the narrator that he has an offer to go back up north. With Jay Chord and Erik Strong gone, Antonio and Casey Valentine are joined by Cameron Vessey, on loan from USPW, at All The Leaves Are Brown on 12th October 2011, where the three jump Chance Fortune and Zimmy Bumfhole; they win the Trios titles from The Firm at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October, Valentine pinning Kid Arachnid, and lose them when Rip Chord retires the belts at Eleventh Hour on 12th November, JD Morgan throwing out all three in the battle royal for the All Action title. Antonio and Valentine lose to Smith & Singh for the Tag Team titles at Remember November, beat the West Texas Peacekeepers at End Of Days, and lose to Wanda Fish and Jared Johnson on 5th January 2012; Valentine says that the pair will rely on themselves. In the diary's pages from March to May 2012 they beat the Internet Favourites at Third Time's The Charm, lose a Tag Team title match to Smith & Singh at March Madness, win a four-team match at April Showers on 5th April to earn a title shot, win the Spring Fever three-way and lose the title match to Smith & Singh at May The Force Be With You on 5th May. In the diary's pages from June to September 2012 they lose the High Noon three-way on 5th June, beat Team Honor at Summer Showdown and Smith & Singh at Beach Blast, and lose at South East Grand Parade with Black Eagle and in the three-way at Iron on 12th August.
