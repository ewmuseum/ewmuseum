Tenure detail
Billed from London, England, representing Extreme Measures alongside tag partner Nina Grimsson; pinned Leanna Love with a rollup after Tara Marshall ran in from the crowd and knocked out Leanna's cornerwoman Lolita Love with the ring steps. Lost to "Twilight Angel" Sonya Benedict by disqualification on 19 May 2004 after her own Extreme Measures stablemates, Nina Grimsson and Tesla St. James, beat Benedict unconscious with her own Singapore cane. Teamed with Nina Grimsson as Extreme Measures in a losing four corners women's tag war on 25 August 2004 against Melody and Comedy, the Love Sisters, and the debuting Team BATTLE*DREAM, continuing to brawl with Yoshida and Ishikawa after the bell. Teamed with Nina Grimsson as Extreme Measures in a losing tag match against Alicia Powers and Tara Smith on 22 September 2004, pinned by Tara Smith's own top-rope Arizona Rattler after Powers's Michinoku Driver. Confronted the Youngbloods (Allison Ivey, Michael Augustine, Edward and Jacob Drake) after refusing to rejoin her brothers' stable on 27 November 2004; blindsided and beaten by her own brother Jacob and curbstomped by Allison Ivey before Alex Extreme and the Sons of Cacaphony ran in to clear the ring. Carried out on a makeshift gurney, forcing Nina Grimsson to take her place in a scheduled mixed tag match. Announced her return to the singles ranks on 8 June 2005, was interrupted mid-interview by a UWF executive, John DeWolfe, who dismissed the women's division as a sideshow, then was ambushed from behind by the Bod Squad after slapping him; Leslie Hernandez made the save. Confronted John DeWolfe alongside Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith on 22 June 2005 over his own treatment of the women's division and Brianna Landis. Cheered on the Wind Walkers from the corner during their own women's tag match on 20 July 2005, then helped fight off a Bod Squad run-in. Discussed the upcoming six-person tag against the Bod Squad and John DeWolfe with the Wind Walkers on 3 August 2005; lost that match by pinfall on 6 August 2005 after Brianna Landis handed DeWolfe the ring bell to strike her from behind. Teamed with Jason Keening in the Cupid's Revenge Blind Date Tournament on 14 February 2006, eliminated by a small package from Alex Extreme; ran a tag-team drill with Keening beforehand and pressed him about his own long grudge with Alex Extreme.
