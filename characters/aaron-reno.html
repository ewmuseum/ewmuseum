Aaron Reno

aka AARON RENO

One half of the Vegas Connection, he took the CSWA's United States Heavyweight Championship in Boston in June 1996 and held it five weeks, refused a win handed to him when an opponent landed neck-first through a ringside table, and made his AAWC return at Oktoberfest '96 only to take a Fall of Saigon from the top rope after the match was already over.
