Character · fWo (1998), CWL (2001)

Penny Pureheart

A reporter who came to the federation in November 1999 to write an exposé on it and kissed Machina as the century turned, and whom fWo's staff carried on after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001: in the story's November 2002 she was revealed as the one under Machina's mask, winning the World Heavyweight Championship in his memory and giving it up a week later as belonging to Erik Kelly, and in its August 2004 special she is the CWL's researcher and its owner Rudo's wife, dying there of an illness. Tenures

fWo (1998)

1999-11-24 – 2000-02-23
a reporter writing an exposé on the federation (1999–2000)
Tenure detail
14 shows on the write-ups, 24 November 1999 to 23 February 2000, none of them in a match. She covers the federation with a tape recorder from November 1999 (4662 ¶2; 4664 ¶19), protests outside its Countdown '99 show and kisses Machina at midnight as the century turns (4673 ¶45, ¶52). The federation's own retired-roster index of February 2001 lists her as "(deceased)", a label from the story: she is on screen in 2002. The character's run while Ed Wilson handled it, which ends with his death in May 2001.
Source: fWo's own show write-ups (donated by Ben H. through Argyle, lot 0186; fed 47 in Argyle's database, bookings 4660 to 4689): first named at Wednesday Warfare, 17 November 1999 (4658 ¶7, "she's doing an expose for 20/20 on what it's like to be in a wrestling promotion"), first on screen at Wednesday Warfare, 24 November 1999 (4660 ¶14, ¶34); last among the protesters outside Wednesday Warfare, 23 February 2000 (4689 ¶37).

fWo (1998)

2002-11-27 – 2002-11-27
in the federation's later story, revealed in November 2002 as the one under Machina's mask
Tenure detail
Credited in one show, without a match. Written after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001. In the story she is the one under Machina's mask when he wins the World Heavyweight Championship on 20 November 2002 (4848 ¶390–391); a week later she unmasks, tells the crowd that the man who wore the mask died years before in an accident and that she had worn it for a time in his honour, and gives the title up as belonging to Erik Kelly (4849 ¶51–57). That death is the story's own.
Source: fWo's own show write-ups (donated by Ben H. through Argyle, lot 0186; fed 47 in Argyle's database, bookings 4848 to 4849): under the mask at Wednesday Warfare, 20 November 2002 (4848 ¶390, a night credited to Machina); unmasked at Wednesday Warfare, 27 November 2002 (4849 ¶51–60).

fWo (1998)

2004-08-01 – 2004-08-01
in the story's later years, the CWL's researcher and its owner's wife, in the federation's August 2004 special
Tenure detail
Credited in one show, without a match. Written after Ed Wilson's death in May 2001. The special closes on her death from illness ("She had been in a lot of pain for the last two years", 5024 ¶903; ¶923): the story's own. It recalls that "she had won the fWo World Heavyweight Championship in his name" wearing Machina's mask (¶923). The sinking-liner tale that opens and closes it (5024 ¶4–34, ¶784–814) is a story Machina tells.
Source: fWo's own show write-ups (fed 47 in its current holder's backstage database, bookings 5024): Inbetween: The legend of the Lone Gunmen, 1 August 2004 (5024: at a diner with Sid Snow and Rudo's later self, "Three years ago...", accepting his offer, "we try it again together", ¶873–900; "in the years of the CWL she had always been by the side of her husband and had worked tirelessly to research the history of the promotion", ¶923).
