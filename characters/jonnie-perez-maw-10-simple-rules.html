Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Jonnie Perez

A hot young prospect billed as the brother of Frankie Perez, one of three newcomers the narrator brings in after Beach Blast in July 2010. He and Kirk Jameson lose to Roderick Remus and Jean Cattley at Summernova, he loses a poor opener to Remus at All The Leaves Are Brown, and at Where It All Begins Again on 26th October 2010 taps out Primus Allen to the P-Clutch in his third MAW match to win the Traditional title, which he keeps against Raphael, Curtis Jenkins and Antonio on the November tour shows. He keeps the Traditional title against Jared Johnson, Roderick Remus, Matthew Gauge and Jefferson Stardust in December 2010 and January 2011 and loses it to Gauge at the Invitational on 21st January; he loses the three-way at Leap Into The Unknown, loses to Hugh de Aske in February and to Black Eagle at April Showers. He beats Nelson Callum on 11th and 25th May, Extreme Deluxe on 4th July and Oscar Golden on 25th September, loses to Raphael and VIP Wallace and is squashed by Primus Allen at Beach Blast. In the diary's pages for August 2012 the narrator reports that he left the company when his contract expired, calling him the reason for MAW's existence as a way of seasoning future stars, though he had no improvement in him, and noting that he had ten defences of the Traditional title.
