Smith, Ota & Singh

aka Ota, Smith and Singh, Ota, Singh and Smith, Smith, Singh and Ota, Ota, Singh & Smith, Smith, Singh & Ota

The "multinational face trio" of "Bulldozer" Brandon Smith, Fumihiro Ota and Kashmir Singh, who beat The Firm for the MAW Trios titles at Hotter Than Hot in July 2010, keep them against Ricochet, Sharp and Toxin at Summernova in August, and against Jared Johnson, Nelson Callum and Roderick Remus at Eleventh Hour in November. The diary's own title records list the team as "Smith, Ota & Singh". They lose the Trios titles to The Triple Threat at Season's Beatings on 12th December 2010, when Firebird distracts Ota; at End Of Days Brandon Smith and Singh, with Jean Cattley, beat Joey Poison and the Canadian Blondes. Smith, Singh and Fumihiro Ota reunite for a six-man at Back To School on 12th September 2011, beating the Canadian Blondes and Raphael when Ota pins Oscar Golden with the Ninja Strike.
