Biography (PURE (2002))

Vilas Lavagna grew up in a normal house, with a normal family and normal friends. Life went as usual for Vilas and things really didn’t get to him. School was no trouble and he usually got high marks when he was interested in the subject. But that’s really where it all ended. Vilas parents where very religious people and took Vilas to church every week. At first when he was younger he thought he had to go. But as the story went on Vilas started to question what was being said. Should he praise some over powering being that no one has seen? Should he give all his free moments to the church? She he say his prayers every night and hope for them to be answered? Vilas didn’t agree with what was being told. He didn’t believe in god, or in fate. He wanted to control his own life and not have something do it for him. His parents at first thought it was just a phase he was going through. But after months of questioning everything that was being said in church his parents finally had a long conversation with him. The told him that he had to believe in the great power or he wouldn’t move onto another life. Vilas at the time didn’t really care of the next life because he was still very young. For months he argued with his parents about everything he was being told. He would continually ask if anything is the world was real or was it all just made up. With that notion, Vilas parents quickly went to see the church and get an answer. They said he was possessed by the devil and must leave if they wanted to get into heaven. So without any thought Vilas parents told Vilas to leave at the age of 14. Vilas did as they wish saying he couldn’t live with people brainwashed by a system. Vilas travels brought him to Japan. Of course he couldn’t escape the religions there but he put up with them. There in Japan he started his own group of people who much like him shared the same values as he. Named the realist they quickly gained in numbers. People finally came out and spoke freely about their beliefs. But some people couldn’t live with them. So they naturally destroyed there meeting places and punished members whom where involved. Vilas was a major target but went into hiding once members started to be killed off. During that time Vilas trained himself and others in a type of submission martial arts. One of their members was skilled in this art and shared it with the group just in case. With no meeting place and members killed, financial problems started to occur. They couldn’t afford to leave. So Vilas ended the group and told everyone to use their skills and use them for good. But try and get your message across. Most of them didn’t. They just went back to the normal system. But a few managed to get into some martial arts tournaments and other fighting leagues around the world. Vilas on the other hand stayed in Japan and worked for a small puroese wrestling promotion and used his submission moves to get an advantage over other competitors. He grew in popularity but like always he was forced to leave when his beliefs where told to everyone. So now Vilas is again alone and looking. He still will not give up his realistic ideas and beliefs and will work for anyone caught in the system. His main objective is to find more who believe the same and form an even stronger group then before and maybe re-call his old friends back. He doesn’t want to scare anyone but only get his point across. He has been mistaken for a gothic man or a Satan follower but even that isn’t one of his beliefs. But as of now he is out on his own. Out for respect and pride for his own opinions
