Biography (PURE (2002))

Shaun Styles was born in Brooklyn, NY. His life was filled with hardships that he had to overcome to get where he is at today. When he was born his father left the family to get himself another bitch to knock up and leave. Life was hard for Styles as a kid growing up without a father but he managed. Things seemed to just keep on getting worse and worse and worse. At the age of eight his mother died of cancer. Now he had no place to go and nothing to look forward to. Styles ended up moving in with his grandparents who were less than thrilled to take care of him and raise him. This is why it was so uncomfortable living there for such a long time. He did not get much of an education and could not afford to go to college. When he reached the age of 18 his grandparents told him to find his own life and stop expecting everybody to help him. With that said Shaun Styles was off on his own again and once again did not have a single clue of what to do. In the end Styles decided to collect all of the money he had saved from working half assed jobs and decided to go around the world to find his niche. After a while of traveling he had returned with a newfound talent: Fighting. That seemed to be the only thing he's good at. This 'skill' was discovered in China during his journey. He ended up in a fight with a mechanic over car problems, which led to a dispute. The fight ended in a KO. Shaun took the well-built man out without taking a punch. Jade Taylor was in the crowd during the fight. He saw what went down and offered Shaun a job. A job to be a boxer. No it wasn't professional but it paid very well nonetheless. Jade Taylor was running an underground boxing promotion. Every once in a while a fair amount of people would meet, bet, and witness a boxing match. This was the business that Shaun Styles was involved in now. Jade Taylor and him grew to become very good friends. The best of. His success all came down to one fight. Shaun Styles vs. J.T. Thunder. Jade Taylor was in discussions with the opponent for several nights before the match and they have come up with an agreement. J.T. Thunder was supposed to take the fall. Shaun Styles and Jade Taylor chose a select few and told them of this lucrative proposition. The main man they came to was Don Laproni, the head of the Laproni family. A very dangerous man. They had promised him that this was a very safe investment. He believed them. Everything just went downhill after that. J.T. Thunder broke the deal. Shaun Styles was knocked out in the first round. That was the end of his career. It might even be the end of his life. So here he is now, a young man of 21, completely broke, and in debt to some very dangerous people. What do you do? ...You wrestle.
