The Valkyries

Ranked third among the federation's own ladies tag teams in January 1992 at 1-1, and the team that handed the HellKats their first loss in ten to take the Ladies Tag Team Championship on 19 February. Written by AI, 2026

Tenure detail
Three of the newly served WeWF newsdesk editions — 30 January, 15 February and 25 February 1992 — reprint the UeWF's OWN published weekly rankings in full, singles, tag and ladies, and this entity stands in them under its own name. The tenure is not overstated; it is now independently attested from the other wing's shelf.
