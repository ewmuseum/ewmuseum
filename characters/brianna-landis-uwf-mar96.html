Tenure detail
The Women's Tag Team Co-Champion Tara Marshall's personal assistant and sister-in-law; ambushed "Poison Bliss" Myra Benedict at Marshall's own instruction in place of a scheduled Bod Squad opponent, and lost by referee stoppage after Benedict locked on a DDT-and-leg-scissors chokehold. Lost to Alicia Powers by pinfall (a Michinoku Driver) on 25 August 2004; joined Tara Marshall's post-match beatdown of Powers until Tara Smith ran in to chase the Bod Squad off. Retained the UWF Women's Tag Team Championship with tag partner Tara "Sunburst" Marshall over Team BATTLE*DREAM by disqualification on 8 September 2004, surviving a near-finish spike piledriver before Marshall's interference. Lost a UWF Women's Heavyweight Championship challenge to champion Donna Tetreault by pinfall (the Dominion Day windmill suplex) on 18 September 2004 after tag partner Tara "Sunburst" Marshall knocked her from the top turnbuckle with a belt shot. Lost a non-title six-woman tag alongside Tara "Sunburst" Marshall and a late-arriving Donna Tetreault to Sierra Browne and Team BATTLE*DREAM on 2 October 2004, finished by Sierra Browne's own Truth Superkick. Knocked unconscious in a women's three-way dance on 20 October 2004 attempting a top-rope tornado DDT on Tara Smith, who ducked clear; Smith and Reona Yoshida each locked on a separate submission hold afterward and were both declared winners. Backed up tag partner Tara Marshall in a backstage confrontation with Commissioner Becky Byers on 27 November 2004, only for Byers to announce a UWF Women's Tag Team Championship defense against Team BATTLE*DREAM ("Tornado" Tara Smith, Alicia Powers) at Rise to Power. Beat Reona Yoshida by pinfall (a high moonsault press) on 1 December 2004 after tag partner Tara "Sunburst" Marshall knocked Misaki Ishikawa out at ringside with the Bod Squad's own title belt, drawing Yoshida briefly from the ring. Lost with tag partner Tara "Sunburst" Marshall to "Tornado" Tara Smith and Alicia Powers by pinfall (a 450 splash) on 25 May 2005 after nearly finishing Powers herself with a fireman's-carry frog splash. Joined Tara "Sunburst" Marshall in ambushing Lisa Drake from behind at John DeWolfe's direction on 8 June 2005, then broke up Alicia Powers and "Tornado" Tara Smith's own match against Drake and Leslie Hernandez, ending it as a no contest. Accompanied John DeWolfe and Tara Marshall to the ring on 22 June 2005, kissed on camera by DeWolfe before the trio was run off by Lisa Drake, Alicia Powers, and "Tornado" Tara Smith. Interfered twice in Tara Marshall's own loss to Alicia Powers on 6 July 2005, breaking up pin attempts before the match was thrown out. Pulled Alicia Powers off the ring apron during the Wind Walkers' own women's tag match on 20 July 2005 as part of a Bod Squad run-in that led to a no-decision. Learned partner John DeWolfe had not gotten the Bod Squad out of their Brawl From The Mall match on 3 August 2005; handed DeWolfe the ring bell to finish Lisa Drake for the win in that six-person tag on 6 August 2005. Revealed on 7 June 2006 to have been wrestling as the masked Fantasy Girl, unmasking after pinning sister-in-law Tara Marshall and attacking her further with a top-rope double moonsault kneedrop, an apron-to-floor Death Valley Driver, and a 450 splash onto her stretcher; husband Tom Landis pulled her away as EMTs moved Marshall. Carried husband Luke Kinsey's own UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World belt to ringside for his title defense against "Nighthawk" Michael Bonn on 28 April 2007, mocking challenger Bonn beforehand as an unworthy opponent; the match ended in a double countout.
