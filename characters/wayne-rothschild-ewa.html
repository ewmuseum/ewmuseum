The dedicated bio card gives a kayfabe "Real Name" of Wayne Rothschild (matching the ring name) and a birthdate of 6 September 1972 — character content per the fed's own bio-card template. Won the EWA North American Championship, then was crippled and retired, then became a Vice President and eventually President of the fed after President Artful's departure; the 3 June 1999 site credits him as owner and cites "President Rothschild" as the contact for fed business. He also won the EWA World Tag Team Championship alongside Dean McGrath and the North American ("Cyber") Championship at the 21 September 1998 Extreme Measures. Announced his engagement to Crystal Laine at the 6 January 1999 Extreme Measures; she was murdered on-air four days later at Yuletide Beatings.
