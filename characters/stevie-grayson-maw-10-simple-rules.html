Character · MAW: 10 Simple Rules (2008)

Stevie Grayson

Trent Shaffer's new partner and tag champion from Fall Of The King, a veteran with the 460 Degree Splash. He wins the first MAW Traditional title in March 2009 and loses it to Darryl Devine at Beach Blast. He regains the Traditional title from Darryl Devine at Where It All Begins Again in October 2009, loses it to Hugh de Aske at End Of Days, wins the number one contender's final over Champagne Lover at March Madness in March 2010 and loses to Jay Chord for the Heavyweight title that night. He beats Hugh de Aske with a Shining Wizard at Summer Showdown in June 2010, with Jenny by his side, teams with Trent Shaffer from Beach Blast in July, and at Eleventh Hour in November is set up by Aaron Andrews as Jay Chord's opponent, jumps the champion as he arrives and beats him with a 460 Degree Splash. He loses to Primus Allen at Season's Beatings in December 2010 and to Matthew Gauge at New Year, Old Rivalries, and to VIP Wallace at All Or Nothing in February 2011 in what the narrator expects to be his last match for MAW, his contract not being renewed. He and Thomas Morgan are told on 12th May 2011 that this is their last chance to save themselves, complain about everything and lose to Ford & Speed's Cross Armbar; the narrator and Rip agree they are canned, and he leaves in June, his old baseball cap later turning up filled with dog dirt.
