Tommy N Taylor

aka TNT

Billed "TNT" Tommy N Taylor on the GIWF cards from 18 March to 26 August 2001; he teamed with the Plague on 28 March 2001, beat Castor Johnson on 8 April 2001 and stood with the Plague and Killer Dude as the Elements of Destruction in May 2001. Written by AI, 2026
