How it's built

The tiers, the map, the search. Written by AI, 2026

Three things worth knowing before you dig in: how deep a promotion's own record was worked, what the pieces of the registry are and how they connect, and how to find anything in it.

The tier ladder

Every wing carries one. It describes the work done, not the promotion itself — and each rung earns the promotion more written prose.

Tier 0 — Attested   The promotion is named and sourced; nothing else is held. A one-line placard is all the prose it carries.
Tier 1 — Collected   Everything recovered is mined, verbatim-served, dated, and linked — not read show by show. The epitaph and document captions are written here.
Tier 2 — Structured   Key shows (title changes, PPVs, standout cards) are structured into matches, appearances, and reigns. The house-style write-up is written here.
Tier 3 — Full-crawl   Every surviving show is read and structured, and only then is that promotion's own Storylines written.

The knowledge map

Everything in the registry is one of these, connected to the others as shown.

Wing (a promotion)   a federation — the hub its characters, units, shows, titles, awards, and roleplays all connect back to.
Character   a wrestler, one row, with a tenure per promotion it appeared in.
Unit   a group of characters — a tag team, a stable, a faction — with tenures of its own.
Show   a card. Holds matches and appearances.
Match / Appearance   one bout, or one name on a card — the atomic facts a show is made of.
Title / Reign   a championship — belt, tournament, or rumble — and its ledger of who held it and when.
Award / Bestowal   an honor a fed gave out, and its ledger of who received it and when.
Roleplay   a served promo, linked to who wrote it and who it involves.
Relationship   a tie between two characters (feud, ally, manager).
Memorial   a page for a real person, dead and welcomed, linked to the characters they played.
Frontier   a promotion known to have existed, not yet held.
Known, not held   a show or roleplay proven to have existed but not recovered — filed in its own instrument, marked uncollected.

The instruments

Every list on this site is a live, filterable search over the registry, not a hand-kept page.

A wing's own page links straight into each instrument, pre-filtered to it. Every box speaks one query language, documented with examples at search syntax. Behind every instrument sits a complete, no-JS register — the completeness fallback, not the instrument. Entities register alphabetically (characters, units, wings, championships, awards); events register chronologically (shows and roleplays, by year).

The AI flag

AI marks prose the Museum's own automation wrote. Human-written prose carries an attribution of its own — a credit, never a real name outside a memorial.
